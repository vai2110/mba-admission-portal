What should you know about SDMIMD Mysore?

Quick answer

SDMIMD offers a two-year full-time residential PGDM in Mysuru. The institute's current admissions page says PGDM 2027 admissions are open, with a published 2026–28 programme fee of ₹14.10 lakh including tuition and hostel stay.

₹14.10LPublished 2026–28 PGDM fee incl. hostel
₹15.25LHighest CTC shown on 2027 admissions site
₹10LAverage CTC shown on 2027 admissions site

SDMIMD states that its PGDM is AICTE-approved, NBA-accredited and has MBA equivalence through AIU. Its current programme page accepts several major management entrance scores.

*Fee is stated for the 2026–28 batch and is subject to revision for 2027–29.

How does SDMIMD PGDM admission work?

SDMIMD accepts valid CAT, XAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA or MAT scores. Graduates and final-year students from any discipline can apply, subject to the institute's academic conditions.

Eligibility

  • Graduation/final-year graduation in any discipline from a recognised institution.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and degree as stated by SDMIMD.
  • A valid score in one of the accepted entrance exams.

Selection process

Shortlisting

Academic consistency, competitive-exam score, extracurricular participation and work experience may be considered.

Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are interviewed; online interviews can be requested according to the institute.

Offer

Selected candidates receive the admission offer and payment schedule.

Confirmation

Seat confirmation follows the institute's prescribed fee and score-submission requirements.

What is the SDMIMD cutoff?

CollegeDecoded currently uses 75 percentile as an indicative MAT reference for SDMIMD in its cutoff dataset. This is a research reference, not an official admission cutoff.

Read the cutoff correctly

SDMIMD evaluates applicants through a broader profile that includes academics, entrance score, extracurriculars and work experience. A single percentile should therefore not be treated as a guaranteed admission threshold.

SDMIMD Mysore PGDM FAQs

What is the SDMIMD Mysore PGDM fee?

SDMIMD currently lists ₹14.10 lakh as the fee for the 2026–28 two-year residential PGDM, covering tuition and hostel stay. Food is charged separately on the basis of distributed expenditure and is currently estimated at about ₹35,000 per year. The institute states that the fee is subject to revision for the 2027–29 batch.

Which entrance exams does SDMIMD accept?

SDMIMD accepts valid CAT, XAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT, ATMA and MAT scores for the PGDM. Candidates who have not yet appeared for an entrance examination can still apply and submit their examination registration or score details later, subject to the institute's admission rules.

What is the SDMIMD Mysore cutoff for PGDM admission?

SDMIMD has not published a single fixed official cutoff percentile that guarantees admission. For admission research, a 75 percentile MAT reference is used as an indicative minimum benchmark for SDMIMD. This should not be treated as an official guaranteed cutoff: the institute's selection process also considers academic consistency, competitive-exam performance, extracurricular participation and work experience, followed by the Personal Interview.

What are the latest SDMIMD placement figures?

The current SDMIMD admissions material reports a highest CTC of ₹15.25 LPA, an average CTC of ₹10 LPA and a median CTC of ₹9.8 LPA. It also reports a ₹13 LPA average for the top 10% of the batch. Students should check the latest institute placement report for the exact graduating cohort and methodology before using these figures for comparison.

What is the SDMIMD selection process?

SDMIMD shortlists applicants using consistent academic performance, competitive-exam score, extracurricular participation and work experience where applicable. Shortlisted candidates then appear for a Personal Interview, after which selected candidates receive an offer and complete the prescribed fee and score-confirmation process.
