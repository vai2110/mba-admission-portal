01 · Why we started

The problem isn't lack of information.
It's knowing what to do with it.

Students already have access to college websites, counselling portals, rankings, placement reports, PDFs, news articles and online discussions.

The challenge is putting all of that information together and understanding what actually matters for a decision.

CollegeDecoded was created to make that research process simpler — bringing relevant information together, organising it and explaining it in a way students can actually use.

02 · The idea

Less searching.
More understanding.

We don't want students to spend hours opening tabs just to answer basic questions about a college.

We want CollegeDecoded to become a place where students can discover options, compare what matters and understand the information behind a decision.

03 · What we believe

Three ideas guide
how we build.

01

Data over claims.

We look beyond promotional statements and focus on information that can genuinely help students evaluate their options.

02

Context over numbers.

A cutoff, fee or placement figure means little without understanding what it represents and where it comes from.

03

Clarity over complexity.

Research shouldn't require students to decode complicated admission information across dozens of pages.

04 · How we research

From information
