What Does CollegeDecoded Do?

CollegeDecoded publishes student-focused information on college admissions, fees, eligibility, cutoffs, courses, seats and placements. The editorial team researches information and presents it in a format intended to help students compare programmes and understand admission requirements.

Where official information is available, CollegeDecoded prioritises university, institute, admission-authority and official placement sources.

How We Research & Review Information

01 · RESEARCH

Find the source

Identify the relevant university website, admission brochure, examination notice, placement report or other primary source.

02 · VERIFY

Check the details

Cross-check important information such as eligibility, fees, dates, seats, selection process and placement figures against the available source.

03 · EXPLAIN

Make it useful

Organise verified information into clear sections, tables and explanations that students can use while researching a college.

04 · UPDATE

Review changes

When important official information changes, the relevant page can be reviewed and its update date refreshed.

How Does CollegeDecoded Verify Information?

Not every college page has the same source availability. Our approach is to use the strongest available primary source for each claim and avoid presenting an unverified figure as an official fact.

1. Start with the source

We look first for the relevant official university, institute, admission authority, examination notice, prospectus or placement document.

Official Fact vs. CollegeDecoded Analysis

OFFICIAL FACT

What the institution publishes

Examples include an admission date, eligibility requirement, fee amount, seat intake or placement statistic stated in an official source.

ANALYSIS

What CollegeDecoded explains

We may organise, compare or explain published information. Such interpretation is kept distinct from the underlying institutional fact.

Our Source Policy

  • Official university and institute websites
  • Admission brochures and prospectuses
  • Controller of Examinations notices
  • Official counselling and admission authorities
  • Official placement reports and notices
  • Government or regulatory sources where relevant

When a figure or claim cannot be verified from an available primary source, the page should avoid presenting it as an official fact.

What Happens When Information Changes?

01

New official information appears
For example, a university publishes a revised admission notice, fee schedule or placement report.

02

Relevant page is reviewed
The affected information is checked against the new source.

03

Content is updated
Material changes are incorporated and the page's visible update date is refreshed.

Content Update Policy

College pages display a visible last updated date. The date is intended to indicate when the page content was last materially reviewed or updated. It is not automatically changed for every technical or deployment change.
