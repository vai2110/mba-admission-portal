Who Is CollegeDecoded?

CollegeDecoded is a higher-education information platform focused on helping students research colleges, courses and admission opportunities.

Our editorial work covers admissions, eligibility, fees, cutoffs, courses, seats, selection processes and placements. We research information from universities, institutes, examination bodies, counselling authorities and other relevant primary sources, then present it in a student-friendly format.

Our goal is not to replace an institution's official information. It is to make that information easier to find, understand and compare.

How We Research & Verify College Information

We use a source-first workflow. The exact source depends on the claim and the admission or placement cycle, but the process follows four core steps.

01 · SOURCE

Find the primary source

We look first for the relevant official university, institute, admission notice, prospectus, examination authority, counselling notice or placement report.

02 · VERIFY

Check the claim

Important details such as eligibility, fees, dates, seats, selection rules and placement figures are checked against the available source.

03 · EXPLAIN

Add useful context

We organise verified information into clear explanations and comparisons while keeping institutional facts separate from our interpretation.

04 · UPDATE

Review material changes

When relevant official information changes, affected content is reviewed and the page's material update date is refreshed.

Our Source Policy

We prioritise the strongest available primary source for each important claim.

  • Official university and institute websites
  • Admission brochures and prospectuses
  • Official examination and admission notices
  • Official counselling authorities
  • Official placement reports and notices
  • Government or regulatory sources where relevant

Important: If a figure or claim cannot be verified from an available primary source, we do not present it as an official institutional fact. Where appropriate, estimates or analysis are clearly identified as such.

Editorial Updates

College information changes frequently. When material official information changes, the affected page is reviewed and its visible update date is refreshed.

If you spot an outdated or incorrect detail, we welcome corrections supported by the relevant official source.
