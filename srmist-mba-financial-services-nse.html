About the Programme

SRMIST lists MBA Financial Services with NSE as a Kattankulathur management programme for students interested in financial services and capital markets.

Eligibility

The MBA eligibility requirement on the current SRMIST management admission page is a bachelor's degree with minimum 50% marks.

Admission

SRMJEEM and PI are used for MBA admissions. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct PI consideration.

Fee

The current SRMIST management admission page lists ₹5,75,000 for MBA Financial Services with NSE at Kattankulathur.

Placements

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement figures reported ₹10.5 LPA average salary and ₹1 crore per annum maximum salary, with 1,311 companies and 13,069 offers.

FAQs

₹5.75 lakh at Kattankulathur.
