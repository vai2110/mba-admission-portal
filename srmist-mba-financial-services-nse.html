What Is MBA Financial Services with NSE at SRMIST?

SRMIST lists MBA Financial Services with NSE as a specialised management programme at Kattankulathur. It is aimed at students interested in financial services and capital-market oriented careers.

Eligibility

SRMIST's MBA eligibility is a bachelor's degree with a minimum 50% marks, subject to the programme conditions.

Admission

SRMIST uses its management admission process with SRMJEEM and PI; eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct PI consideration.

Official Fee

The Kattankulathur MBA Financial Services with NSE fee listed by SRMIST is ₹5,75,000. This figure is specific to this programme and campus.

What Is the Finance Focus?

The programme is relevant to students targeting financial services, capital markets and finance roles. Students should compare the NSE-linked structure and curriculum with SRMIST's general MBA before deciding.

Placements

Use the dedicated SRMIST placements page for university-level placement figures. It should not be read as a guaranteed package for this specialised MBA.

Financial Services MBA FAQs

What is the official fee for this programme?

₹5.75 lakh at Kattankulathur, as listed on SRMIST's management admission information.

Is this the same as the general MBA?

No. It is a specialised MBA focused on financial services and includes NSE in the programme title.

Can CAT be used for admission?

CAT is among the listed scores eligible for direct consideration for the PI, subject to SRMIST's conditions.
