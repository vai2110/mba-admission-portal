What Is MBA Financial Services with NSE?

SRMIST lists this as a specialised MBA option at Kattankulathur. The programme is relevant to students targeting financial services and markets-oriented roles and should be compared with the general MBA before admission.

Who Can Apply?

SRMIST lists a minimum 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree for its MBA portfolio. Preference is given to candidates with work experience.

How Does Admission Work?

The standard management route is SRMJEEM followed by Personal Interview. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for PI.

What Entrance Score Is Needed?

SRMIST's official AICTE disclosure lists minimum desirable attainment figures: CAT 65 percentile+, MAT 75 percentile+, XAT 65 percentile+, GMAT 640+, NMAT 180+, CMAT 75 percentile+ and TANCET 80 percentile+.

These are not guaranteed final cutoffs. SRMIST says selection also depends on profile and pre-admission rounds.

What Will You Study?

The programme should be evaluated for its finance and financial-services content, market orientation and practical exposure. The NSE reference in the programme title is particularly relevant to students considering market-linked careers.

Who Should Choose This MBA?

Choose it if finance, financial services and markets are your primary career interests. If you want a wider management path, compare the general MBA; if you prefer data-driven management, compare Business Analytics.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported average salary ₹10.5 LPA and maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum. These are university-level numbers, not a guaranteed package for this specialisation.

FAQs Students Ask

What is the KTR fee?

₹5.75 lakh as listed in the SRMIST management admission table.

What is the minimum eligibility?

A bachelor's degree with minimum 50% aggregate.

Can CAT candidates apply?

Eligible CAT scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

Is CAT 65 percentile a final cutoff?

No. It is a minimum desirable attainment figure in the official disclosure.

Why is this fee higher than the KTR general MBA fee?

The published KTR fee is ₹5.75 lakh for this specialised programme versus ₹4.50 lakh for MBA Business Administration.
