Who is eligible for MBA-HHM and who should choose it?

The 2026-28 admission policy makes this programme more specialised at the entry stage than the flagship MBA. Eligible backgrounds include healthcare and allied fields such as medicine, pharmacy, paramedical and nursing, along with engineering, science, law/legal studies and BBA with a healthcare specialisation. BA, BCom and BBA candidates without a healthcare specialisation are not listed as eligible in the current policy.

Strong fit

Students who want hospital administration, healthcare operations, health services, healthcare consulting or health-tech management.

Why specialisation matters

You enter with sector context that can be valuable when solving healthcare-specific operational and strategic problems.

Think twice if…

You want maximum flexibility across unrelated industries; the flagship MBA is the more natural comparison.

How does MBA-HHM admission work in 2026?

The official 2026-28 policy lists 90 seats and uses CAT followed by the CAP 2026 personal-interview process and final merit evaluation. The policy publishes category-wise reference cutoffs for CAP shortlisting, while noting that the final cutoff is subject to the CAT result and candidate pool.

What will you study in MBA-HHM?

The official programme structure totals roughly 108–114 credits. Year I carries 60 core-course credits, while Year II combines track-specific and general electives with industry/community components.

What should a healthcare manager actually be able to do?

The programme should be judged by whether it helps you understand healthcare operations, service quality, finance, people, strategy, technology and the interaction between hospitals, insurers, diagnostics, pharma and other stakeholders.

Why are industry immersion and community projects important?

Healthcare management cannot be learned entirely from business cases because patient services, capacity, quality and public-health delivery involve operational realities. The programme includes a six-credit industry immersion and a three-credit community project.

Industry immersion

Use the experience to understand hospital processes, service operations, quality and management decision-making.

Community project

Students engage with nearby semi-urban/rural communities to understand health problems and the reach of public-health schemes.

Career value

These experiences can help turn healthcare theory into evidence you can discuss in interviews and future roles.

What should you consider about fees and ROI?

The institute publishes a separate 2026-28 fee structure for MBA-HHM. Because the fee is batch-specific, students should use the current official document instead of carrying forward an older figure.

Calculate full cost

Include tuition, academic charges, hostel, mess/living costs, deposits and other institute charges.

Compare the right outcome

Healthcare-management ROI should be compared with the roles you actually want, not with the highest package in an unrelated sector.

Consider background advantage

A student with healthcare exposure may extract more value from sector-specific learning than someone seeking a general corporate career.

Check placement evidence

Use the latest programme-specific placement report where available and do not assume the consolidated institute average applies equally to every role.

MBA-HHM FAQs

How many seats are there?

The 2026-28 admission policy publishes 90 seats.

What is the eligibility?

UG 50% is listed, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD. Eligible academic backgrounds are specifically defined in the policy and include healthcare, engineering, science, law and BBA-Healthcare; BA/BCom/BBA without healthcare specialisation are not listed as eligible.

Which exam is required?

CAT 2025 is the entrance route in the 2026-28 policy, followed by the CAP 2026 PI process.

Is MBA-HHM only for doctors?

No. The policy also lists pharmacy, paramedical, nursing, engineering, science, law/legal studies and BBA with healthcare specialisation among eligible backgrounds.

How many credits does the programme have?

The official programme page lists a total of about 108–114 credits, including core, electives, industry immersion and community project components.
