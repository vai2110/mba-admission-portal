Who is eligible for MBA-HHM and who should choose it?

The 2026-28 admission policy is more specific than the flagship MBA. Eligible backgrounds include healthcare and allied fields such as medicine, pharmacy, paramedical and nursing, along with engineering, science, law/legal studies and BBA with a healthcare specialisation. BA, BCom and BBA candidates without a healthcare specialisation are not listed as eligible in the current policy.

Strong fit

Students targeting hospital administration, healthcare operations, health services, healthcare consulting or health-tech management.

Why specialise?

You enter with sector context that can help when solving healthcare-specific operational and strategic problems.

Think twice if…

You want maximum flexibility across unrelated industries; compare the flagship MBA carefully.

How does MBA-HHM admission work in 2026?

The official 2026-28 policy lists 90 seats and uses CAT followed by the CAP 2026 PI process and final merit evaluation. The policy publishes reference cutoffs but notes that final cutoffs depend on the CAT result and candidate pool.

What will you study in MBA-HHM?

The official programme structure totals roughly 108–114 credits. Year I carries 60 core-course credits, while Year II combines track-specific/general electives with industry and community components.

Community project3 creditsConnects management learning with real health problems and public-health delivery.

What should a healthcare manager be able to understand?

Healthcare management requires more than hospital administration. Students need exposure to operations, service quality, finance, people, strategy, technology and the interaction among hospitals, insurers, diagnostics, pharma and other stakeholders.

Why do industry immersion and community projects matter?

Healthcare management cannot be learned entirely through generic business cases. Patient services, capacity, quality and public-health delivery create operational realities that are difficult to understand without field exposure.

Industry immersion

Use the experience to understand hospital processes, service operations, quality and managerial decisions.

Community project

Students engage with nearby semi-urban/rural communities to understand health problems and public-health schemes.

Interview value

Field experience gives you concrete healthcare examples to discuss during internships and placements.

MBA-HHM FAQs

How many seats are there?

The 2026-28 admission policy publishes 90 seats.

What is the eligibility?

UG 50% is listed, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD. The policy specifically defines eligible academic backgrounds.

Is MBA-HHM only for doctors?

No. The policy also lists pharmacy, paramedical, nursing, engineering, science, law/legal studies and BBA with healthcare specialisation among eligible backgrounds.

Which exam is required?

CAT 2025 followed by the CAP 2026 PI process.

How many credits does the programme have?

The official structure lists about 108–114 credits including core courses, electives, industry immersion and community project components.
