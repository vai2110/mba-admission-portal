Chitkara University Admission Process 2026

Start by selecting the exact programme, reading its 2026 eligibility criteria and completing the university application. The selection route differs across disciplines, so applicants should not assume that one entrance-test rule applies to every programme.

Best approach for applicants

Choose the programme first, then verify eligibility, required subjects, entrance requirement, interview requirement, fee and scholarship conditions from the official 2026 programme information.

Eligibility

Eligibility is programme-specific. For example, the 2026 Business School material lists Class XII with at least 50% for the five-year Integrated Programme in Management and BBA Liberal Education, with a personal interview. Engineering and healthcare programmes can have different subject and percentage requirements.

Programme areaTypical eligibility focusCheck before applying

Documents for Admission

  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets/certificates where applicable
  • Graduation documents for postgraduate programmes
  • Valid identity proof and photographs
  • Entrance-test scorecard if required
  • Category, domicile or scholarship documents if applicable

The final document list should be taken from the programme's application instructions.

Selection Process

Depending on the programme, selection may involve academic merit, personal interview, entrance examination or other programme-specific assessment. The 2026 Business School document explicitly lists a personal interview for several listed programmes.

Student Discussion: Admission Decisions

Applicants should compare the total cost, programme duration, internship opportunities, placement outcomes and scholarship probability rather than choosing only on admission ease. A lower entry barrier does not automatically mean better career fit.

Admission FAQs

Are all Chitkara programmes admitted through the same exam?

No. Admission requirements are programme-specific and can include academic eligibility, interviews and other selection routes.

What should I verify before paying the fee?

Verify the exact programme fee, additional charges, refund policy, scholarship conditions and current admission status.
