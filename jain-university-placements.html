Jain University Placements: Why Programme-Level Data Matters

JAIN publishes placement information across individual schools and programmes. A university-wide highest package can therefore be misleading if it is used to estimate the likely outcome for every student.

What should you compare besides the highest package?

Look at placement percentage, average or median package, number of offers, role quality, recruiter mix and the batch/year behind the figure.

Jain University Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at Jain University?

The latest programme-level figures used on this page show ₹81.25 LPA for B.Tech in 2024-25. Other programmes have different highest packages.

What is the average package for MBA at Jain University?

CMS Business School currently reports an average MBA package of ₹8.66 LPA, with ₹12.89 LPA as the average package for its top 100 offers.

Does Jain University have good BTech placements?

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology reports 98.02% placement success and ₹8.24 LPA average salary for B.Tech in 2024-25.

Are the highest package figures guaranteed?

No. Highest package is an individual/top outcome, not a guaranteed salary. Students should evaluate average/median packages, placement rate and roles as well.
