UPES Dehradun: What Students Should Know

UPES is a multidisciplinary private university in Dehradun with 100+ programmes and seven schools listed on its admissions platform. Its portfolio spans engineering, computer science, business, design, law, health sciences and liberal studies. The university highlights 19,000+ students, 1,500+ faculty and 150+ specialisations on its admissions page.

Is UPES only an engineering university?

No. Engineering and computer science are major areas, but UPES also has business, design, law, health sciences and liberal studies programmes. This makes the university relevant to students with different career goals.

Student View & Practical Inference

UPES offers a very broad programme portfolio, so the university name alone is not enough to judge an individual course. A student comparing UPES with another university should compare the exact specialisation, total fee, internship structure, recruiter list and role outcomes.

Good fit if

  • You want a specialised programme rather than a generic degree.
  • You value industry-linked curriculum and international pathway options.
  • You want access to multiple schools and multidisciplinary opportunities.

Check carefully if

  • You are selecting only because of the highest package headline.
  • Your chosen course has substantially different outcomes from the university-wide figures.
  • You need a low-cost degree and have not calculated the complete residential cost.

UPES Dehradun FAQs

What are the popular courses at UPES Dehradun?

Popular areas include B.Tech, BBA, BCA, MBA, B.Des and law programmes, along with courses in health sciences and liberal studies.

What is the UPES B.Tech eligibility?

Regular B.Tech programmes generally require 50% in Class X and XII along with 50% in PCM in Class XII. Specific programmes can have higher requirements.

What is the UPES MBA eligibility?

UPES lists 50% in Class X, XII and graduation for MBA programmes. Certain EY-partnered MBA programmes require 60% in graduation.

Does UPES have good placements?

UPES states 92% placements in 2024-25 and 800+ recruiters on campus in 2025. Programme-level outcomes should be checked before making a decision.
