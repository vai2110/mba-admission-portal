UPES B.Des Overview

UPES School of Design offers B.Des programmes designed around areas such as industrial and product design, communication design, fashion, interaction and other creative disciplines. The university also highlights global pathway options.

Popular UPES B.Des Areas

Industrial & Product Design

Focus on product systems, materials, digital twins, consumer products and emerging hardware ecosystems.

Communication Design

Visual communication, branding, digital experiences and creative problem-solving.

Global Pathway

International progression options are available for selected design programmes.

UPES B.Des Eligibility

UPES lists a minimum 50% marks in Class X and XII for B.Des admissions. The selection route can include UPESDAT or CUET depending on the programme.

UPES B.Des Fees

Current fee listings place B.Des tuition at about ₹14.48 lakh across listed B.Des courses. A current international pathway listing shows ₹5.52 lakh first-year UPES fee for a B.Des Global Pathway programme before partner-university costs.

Check the exact specialisation:

Design fees can include consumable/material charges. UPES currently lists an additional ₹12,800 per year consumable fee for B.Des students.

UPES B.Des Admission 2026

UPES lists UPESDAT and CUET among B.Des selection routes. Admission alerts show UPESDAT conducted through online slots on specified dates.

UPES B.Des Placements

UPES School of Design publishes programme-level placement information. A current UPES design programme page reports indicative placement outcomes including average CTC of ₹6.48 LPA and highest CTC of ₹15.17 LPA for an industrial/product-design programme. Outcomes vary by design discipline and cohort.
