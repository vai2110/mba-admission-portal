Fee Categories Can Change the Cost Dramatically

The same degree can have different tuition depending on the admission category. B.Tech, for example, distinguishes State Merit, All India Merit and NRI categories. MCA distinguishes Regular and Cost Sharing. This is why “CUSAT B.Tech fees” or “CUSAT MCA fees” without a category can be misleading.

Best practice

Always record programme + campus/unit + category + academic year before comparing fees with another college.

Additional Charges Students Often Miss

  • Establishment / one-time charges
  • Library and laboratory fees
  • Course-specific material or value-added charges
  • Management Students Association fees for MBA where applicable
  • Programme-specific training, placement or practical charges
  • Hostel rent and related residential costs

Scholarships and Financial Support

CUSAT's student ecosystem includes scholarship/fellowship opportunities and a Student Aid Fund. The university's 2026–27 budget documents an enhanced ₹30 lakh Student Aid Fund allocation to support economically disadvantaged students. citeturn0search35

For international students under Study in India, the 2026 prospectus states that selected students receive a 50% tuition-fee scholarship, subject to the programme and scheme conditions. citeturn0search34

Students should not assume a scholarship from general eligibility alone; apply through the relevant university/scheme process and verify continuation conditions.

CUSAT Fees FAQs

What is CUSAT B.Tech fee per semester?

For 2026, the fee structure lists ₹28,800 per semester for B.Tech SOE/CUCEK State Merit and ₹55,000 for All India Merit, before additional applicable charges.

What is CUSAT MBA fee?

The 2026 fee structure lists ₹35,200 tuition per semester for MBA Regular All India, with additional student-related charges.

Why is CUSAT MCA Cost Sharing more expensive?

It is a separate fee category. The 2026 structure lists ₹38,900 tuition per semester versus ₹13,100 for MCA Regular All India.
