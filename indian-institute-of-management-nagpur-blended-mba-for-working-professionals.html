Quick Answer

The Blended MBA for Working Professionals at IIM Nagpur is designed for individuals seeking to enhance their leadership and strategic decision-making skills without career disruption. The programme utilizes a blended learning format, integrating core management disciplines with contemporary topics like digital transformation, artificial intelligence, and sustainability.

Programme Overview

The Blended MBA for Working Professionals is tailored for career-focused individuals. It aims to develop business acumen and strategic leadership capabilities. The programme structure allows professionals to pursue advanced management education while maintaining their current employment.

Curriculum Focus

The curriculum is designed to bridge foundational management knowledge with modern business requirements. Key areas of integration include:

  • Core Management Disciplines
  • Digital Transformation
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Sustainability

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this programme for?

It is designed for working professionals seeking to develop leadership and strategic decision-making skills without disrupting their careers.

What is the format of the programme?

The programme is delivered in a blended format, combining various learning modes to suit the needs of working professionals.
