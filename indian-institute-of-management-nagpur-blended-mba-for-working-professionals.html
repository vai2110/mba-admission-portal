How does the Blended MBA work?

IIM Nagpur designed the Blended MBA for Working Professionals for participants who want to strengthen leadership, strategy and business decision-making without leaving employment. It is separate from the Executive MBA (Hybrid); compare eligibility, teaching format, curriculum and fees separately.

The curriculum is organised into eight terms, with core management courses in the first four terms and electives plus a field project later. Confirm the current intake calendar and required campus visits before applying.

What does the 84-credit curriculum include?

The published curriculum totals 84 credits: 48 core credits, at least 30 elective credits and a 6-credit field project. IIM Nagpur states that one credit equals 10 hours of classroom teaching. Core areas include statistics, economics, accounting, marketing, operations, finance, HR, strategy, research methods and MIS.

Elective areas include finance and accounting, marketing, operations, decision sciences/information systems, strategy and entrepreneurship, economics, organisational behaviour/HR, and public policy/governance. Course availability can vary by term.

What is the published fee and how current is it?

The official fee document for the 2025–27 batch lists ₹12,10,000 total, including an offer-acceptance fee of ₹1,00,000 that forms part of the first instalment. The schedule includes tuition, study materials, campus visits and administrative charges.

This is a previous-batch benchmark, not a confirmed fee for a later intake. Ask IIM Nagpur for the current fee, payment dates, refund rules and any additional costs. Do not use the Executive MBA Hybrid fee on this page.

No, they are separate programmes.

Core management subjects followed by electives and a field project.
