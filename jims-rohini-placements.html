JIMS Rohini Placements: latest reported outcome

Quick answer

For the reported 2024-26 PGDM cycle, JIMS states that more than 90% of students were placed and 190 companies participated. Its 2026 admission communication highlights a ₹24 LPA highest package.

These figures should be read together. The highest package represents a top-end outcome, while average and median salary provide a better picture of the broader placed cohort.

Which companies recruit from JIMS Rohini?

JIMS reports recruiter participation across sectors including banking and financial services, FMCG, IT, consulting, e-commerce, retail and other corporate functions. Recruiter mix can change from one placement season to another, so students should treat a recruiter list as evidence of hiring access rather than a guarantee of a particular job.

Does specialisation affect placement fit?

Yes. JIMS offers different management areas, and students should choose based on the role they want rather than assuming that every specialisation produces the same career path.

Choose Finance if…You want banking, financial analysis, credit, risk or research-oriented roles.
Choose Marketing if…You are targeting brand, digital, product, sales or customer-growth roles.
Choose HR if…You want talent, HR operations, people analytics or organisational roles.
Choose PGDM-IB if…You specifically want international business, trade or cross-border commercial exposure.

JIMS Rohini Placement ROI

The published programme fee for 2026-28 is ₹10.75 lakh, while the reported PGDM median salary is ₹9.83 LPA. This is a useful first comparison, but it is not a simple one-year payback calculation.

What supports ROI

Strong Delhi-NCR location, 90%+ reported placement coverage, 190 participating companies and a median close to the reported average salary.

What to calculate

Include living/commuting costs, taxes, loan interest, scholarship, opportunity cost and your actual target role when comparing B-schools.

Best comparison method: compare JIMS with alternatives on median salary + placement coverage + total cost + scholarship + role quality, not only the highest package.
