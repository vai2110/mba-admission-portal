JIMS Rohini Placements: latest reported outcome

Quick answer

JIMS Rohini's current placement page reports a 90%+ placement rate for the 2024-26 PGDM batch, with a ₹9.95 LPA average salary, ₹9.83 LPA median salary and 190 companies participating. JIMS's 2026 admission communication also highlights a ₹24 LPA highest package.

The detailed placement page lists individual 2024-26 offers up to ₹23.46 LPA, including an international offer. Because JIMS uses different reporting pages and batch labels, the table below keeps the source and batch name visible instead of mixing figures.

How to read the numbers: the highest package is a top-end outcome. For a realistic comparison between B-schools, give more weight to the median salary, placement coverage, number of participating companies and sector mix.

Primary sources: JIMS placement highlights and JIMS placement record.

What placement support does JIMS Rohini provide?

JIMS's Corporate Resource Management Centre (CRMC) is responsible for placement activities. The institute describes support that includes CV preparation, interview preparation, soft-skills workshops, special lectures, pre-placement talks and corporate outreach. A student placement committee also works with CRMC and alumni/recruiter networks.

Before placements

CV preparation, skill development, interview preparation, workshops and interaction with recruiters.

During the placement cycle

Corporate outreach, company interactions, placement information and opportunities based on recruiter requirements.

Important: placement assistance is not the same as a guaranteed job. JIMS's own published figures are generally stated for eligible/interested students.

JIMS Rohini Placement ROI: is the fee justified?

JIMS Rohini Placement FAQs

What is the JIMS Rohini placement rate?

JIMS currently reports 90%+ placement for the 2024-26 PGDM batch. Its batch table separately lists 100% for 2023-25, 2022-24 and 2021-23 for eligible/interested students.

What is the highest package at JIMS Rohini?

JIMS's 2026 admission communication highlights ₹24 LPA. Its detailed 2024-26 placement page separately shows a highest individual offer of ₹23.46 LPA.

What is the JIMS Rohini placement salary range?

JIMS states that domestic packages typically ranged from ₹7–17 LPA, while international placements ranged from ₹20–25 LPA, depending on sector and location.

Which sectors hire from JIMS Rohini?

Which companies recruit from JIMS Rohini?

Does JIMS Rohini offer finance placements?

Yes. JIMS lists finance-related roles such as Credit Analyst, Financial Modelling, Valuations, Risk Advisory, Financial Research and Wealth Management.

Is JIMS Rohini good for marketing placements?

Marketing is a major placement area. Recent placement highlights include marketing offers at Urban Company and other employers, with individual offers reported up to ₹23.46 LPA in the detailed 2024-26 page.

Does JIMS guarantee placement?

No. Placement assistance is provided, but a job is not guaranteed. JIMS generally states its placement figures for eligible/interested students.

How many companies visit JIMS Rohini?

JIMS reports 190 companies in its recent placement reporting; its earlier batch tables report 190+.

What is the JIMS Rohini placement trend?

The published median salary rose from ₹8.30 LPA for 2021-23 to ₹9.05 LPA for 2022-24 and ₹9.83 LPA for 2023-25. The latest 2024-26 narrative also reports a ₹9.83 LPA median.

How should I compare JIMS placement with another MBA college?

Compare median salary, placement coverage, batch size, total fee, scholarship, sector distribution and the roles offered. Do not compare colleges using the highest package alone.
