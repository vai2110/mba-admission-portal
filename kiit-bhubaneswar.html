KIIT Bhubaneswar: Overview

Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT), officially KIIT Deemed to be University, is a multidisciplinary university located at Patia, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Its academic portfolio spans engineering and technology, management, law, biotechnology, medicine, dentistry, nursing, humanities and other disciplines. KIIT's current official admission ecosystem is organised around KIITEE for most programmes, while MBBS and BDS admissions are through NEET.

For students comparing private universities in India, KIIT is particularly relevant when the shortlist includes B.Tech, computer science and technology programmes, management, law, biotechnology or health sciences. The university's current official pages report NAAC A++ accreditation, NIRF 2025 rank 17 among Indian universities, and international accreditations including ABET and IET. citeturn1search2turn1search0

Student decision point

KIIT is a large multidisciplinary university, so the university-level reputation should not be treated as a substitute for programme-level comparison. KIIT Admission 2026: KIITEE, Application & Counselling

For KIIT's 2026 admission cycle, the official admission policy states that admission to courses is through KIITEE 2026, while MBBS and BDS admission is through NEET 2026. The KIITEE 2026 portal states that there is no application or examination fee for KIITEE. citeturn0search1turn0search4

KIITEE 2026 Application Process

  1. Complete the online KIITEE application form.
  2. Upload the required photograph, signature and qualifying documents as specified by KIIT.
  3. Appear for the applicable KIITEE examination.
  4. Check the result/rank and follow the counselling and seat-allocation schedule.
  5. Attend counselling with the required original documents and admission amount.

How KIITEE Counselling Works

KIIT states that counselling and seat allocation are merit-based on entrance-examination performance. Counselling is conducted according to the published schedule and stops once the KIITEE-2026 seats are filled. Document verification is performed during counselling/admission. citeturn0search7

KIIT Bhubaneswar FAQs

What is KIIT Bhubaneswar famous for?

KIIT is known for its multidisciplinary university structure, engineering and technology programmes, management, law, medical and other professional programmes. Its official pages currently report NAAC A++ accreditation, NIRF 2025 rank 17 among Indian universities, and NIRF top-50 positions in Law, Medical, Dental, Engineering and Research. It also reports ABET and IET accreditation/recognition for relevant programmes. citeturn1search2turn1search12

Is KIIT a private university?

KIIT is a deemed-to-be university under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. KIIT's official accreditation page describes it as an A Category deemed university and states that its courses conform to UGC guidelines, with professional courses recognised by their respective regulatory bodies. citeturn1search0

What is the NIRF rank of KIIT in 2025?

KIIT was ranked 17th among Indian universities in the NIRF 2025 University category. KIIT's official ranking page also lists 14th in Law, 24th in Medical, 26th in Dental, 36th in Engineering and 43rd in Research in NIRF 2025. citeturn1search3turn1search12

What is the admission process for KIIT in 2026?

For the 2026 cycle, KIIT states that admission to its courses is through KIITEE 2026, while MBBS and BDS admissions are through NEET 2026. The KIITEE portal describes online application, entrance examination, result/rank, counselling and seat allocation as the core process. KIIT states that counselling and seat allocation are merit-based on entrance-examination performance. citeturn0search1turn0search4turn0search7

Is KIITEE 2026 application free?

Yes. The official KIITEE 2026 portal states that there is no application or examination fee for KIITEE 2026. This should not be confused with later counselling/admission payments or programme fees, which are separate. citeturn0search4

What is the eligibility for B.Tech at KIIT?

For B.Tech 2026, KIIT states that applicants who passed 10+2 in 2024 or 2025 or are appearing in 2026 can apply, subject to the other criteria. Candidates need at least 60% in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics taken together and must meet the stated schooling and age conditions. B.Tech Biotechnology has a Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics/Biology criterion. citeturn0search0

CSE, IT and several computer science variants are listed at ₹2.20 lakh per semester, while several core engineering groups are listed at ₹1.75 lakh. B.Tech Biotechnology is listed at ₹1.90 lakh per semester.
