Programme Overview

The DMS IIT Delhi full-time MBA is a two-year management programme within IIT Delhi. Admission uses CAT-based shortlisting followed by a personal interview.

Tentative Admission Dates: 2027–29

Not officially announced. Based on the latest 2026–28 cycle, plan for late Nov 2026–early Feb 2027 applications, March 2027 interviews and May 2027 results. These are planning estimates, not official dates.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree or equivalent requiring at least three years after 10+2 is eligible. The 2026–28 criteria require 60% aggregate / 6.00 CGPA; SC/ST/PwD candidates receive a 5 percentage-point relaxation. Final-year candidates can apply subject to institute conditions.

Selection Process

Shortlisting

For 2026–28, CAT contributed 65/100 marks to the shortlisting index. Academic marks, work experience, gender diversity and the published institute component supplied the remaining marks.

Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for the personal interview at IIT Delhi.

Final index

The published 2026–28 final index gives 40 marks to Interview & ECA and 40 to CAT, with the remaining marks from profile components.

The 45.00 General shortlisting index is a composite score, not a 45-percentile CAT cutoff.

What the fee covers

The ₹12 lakh amount is programme tuition payable over four semesters. Other institute and residence charges are separately listed.

What the fee does not cover

Do not treat tuition as an all-in living cost. Mess, personal expenses and other living costs should be planned separately unless the applicable offer states otherwise.

FAQs

Is work experience compulsory?
No. Work experience contributes profile marks but is not a basic eligibility requirement for the full-time MBA.
What is the CAT weight?
For 2026–28, CAT carries 65 marks out of the 100-mark shortlisting index.
What is the MBA tuition?
DMS states ₹12 lakh for the full-time MBA tuition. Other applicable institute and residence charges are separate.
Are 2027–29 dates announced?
No. Dates shown on this page are tentative planning estimates.
