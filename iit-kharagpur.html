Admission Process: What Applicants Need to Do

VGSoM's 2026-28 MBA admission uses CAT, application rating, WAT and PI. The published eligibility requires a qualifying engineering/technology/pharmacy bachelor's degree or a master's in Science/Economics/Commerce with Mathematics or Statistics at bachelor's level, with 60% for General/EWS/OBC and 55% for SC/ST/PwD.

Application sequence

  1. Check the programme-specific eligibility before paying any application fee.
  2. Take the required entrance examination or prepare the institute-specific application route.
  3. Submit the institute application within the notified window with academic, category and work-experience documents where applicable.
  4. Complete the PI, WAT, written test or other selection stage applicable to the programme.
  5. Review the final offer, fee schedule and joining conditions before accepting the seat.

How to Decide Between the Programmes

Choose by career stage

Use the full-time MBA/PGP route for early-career management entry and an executive programme when the official eligibility is designed for working professionals.

Choose by admission route

Check whether the programme uses CAT, another entrance test, a common process or an institute application.

Choose by total cost

Include tuition, accommodation, mess, deposits and any programme-specific mandatory charges.

Choose by placement scope

Where placement is combined across programmes, treat the reported numbers as institute-level or combined outcomes rather than course-only salary guarantees.
