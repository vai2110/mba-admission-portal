Quick Answer

The Career Development Services (CDS) office at IIM Visakhapatnam manages industry relations, student preparation, and the recruitment calendar. Official placement reports and specific historical salary metrics are currently unavailable in the provided documentation.

Placement Process

The CDS office facilitates the recruitment cycle through structured engagement. Key components include:

  • Placement Preparation: Skill-building workshops and training.
  • Industry Engagement: Corporate interactions and guest lectures.
  • Placement Committee: Student-led coordination of recruitment logistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find the latest placement reports?
Official placement reports are hosted on the IIM Visakhapatnam website under the Career Development Services section.
Does the institute provide placement support for executive programs?
Executive programs (e.g., EMBA, CFO, CHRO programs) are designed for working professionals to enhance leadership competencies and are not part of the traditional campus placement process.
