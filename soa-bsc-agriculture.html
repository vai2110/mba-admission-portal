SOA B.Sc Agriculture: Quick Answer

SOA's Institute of Agricultural Sciences offers B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture and postgraduate agriculture programmes. The undergraduate programme is designed for students who want to study crop science, soil, agronomy, horticulture, agricultural economics and related areas after Class 12.

Why Study B.Sc Agriculture at SOA?

Students should compare agriculture colleges on farm/practical exposure, laboratories, faculty, field learning, fee, curriculum and access to postgraduate research. SOA's programme sits within a larger multidisciplinary university ecosystem.

B.Sc Agriculture Subjects & Curriculum

Agriculture education combines classroom science with field and practical work. Typical areas include agronomy, soil science, horticulture, plant breeding and genetics, entomology, plant pathology, agricultural economics, extension and farm management.

Crop

Agronomy

Crop production, field management and sustainable agricultural practices.

Soil

Soil science

Soil properties, fertility and crop-nutrition principles.

Plants

Horticulture

Fruit, vegetable, ornamental and plantation crop systems.

Business

Agricultural economics

Farm economics, markets, extension and agribusiness concepts.

Practical & Field Learning

Agriculture is a field-oriented degree. Students should assess farm exposure, laboratories, crop demonstrations, soil testing, practical classes and opportunities to work on agricultural projects when choosing an institution.

Higher Studies After B.Sc Agriculture

SOA lists M.Sc agriculture and horticulture specialisations. Students can also pursue postgraduate agriculture, agribusiness, agricultural economics, food systems or related research programmes depending on eligibility.

SOA B.Sc Agriculture FAQs

What is the eligibility for B.Sc Agriculture at SOA?

SOA's 2026 criteria require the prescribed 10+2 PCM/PCB/PCMB combination, aggregate and age conditions.

Is SAAT required for B.Sc Agriculture?

The programme is included in SOA's SAAT admission system.

How long is B.Sc Agriculture?

The programme is B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture; students should check the current programme brochure for the exact duration and semester structure.

What are the career options after B.Sc Agriculture?

Common areas include farm management, agribusiness, agricultural extension, technical services and higher study/research.

Does SOA offer M.Sc Agriculture?

Yes. SOA's programme list includes M.Sc agriculture/horticulture specialisations.
