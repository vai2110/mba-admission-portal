LPU M.Tech: Who Should Consider It?

LPU offers two-year M.Tech programmes in areas including computer science, data science and analytics, machine learning and AI, information security, VLSI, power systems, structural engineering, biotechnology and other engineering disciplines.

Quick student verdict

M.Tech makes the most sense when you want deeper technical expertise, a research-oriented pathway or a specialised engineering role. Compare the exact specialisation, faculty/research opportunities, fees and expected career outcomes before enrolling.

LPU M.Tech Eligibility 2026

Requirements vary by specialisation. Current programme information commonly requires a relevant bachelor's degree with around 55–60% marks, while accepted routes include GATE and LPUNEST for applicable programmes.

LPU M.Tech Fees 2026

Current reporting places M.Tech tuition broadly between ₹3.20 lakh and ₹4.00 lakh for two years, depending on specialisation. CSE, Data Science, ML/AI and Information Security are commonly reported around ₹1.00 lakh per semester; several core branches are around ₹80,000 per semester.

LPU M.Tech Scholarships

Scholarships can be linked to LPUNEST, qualifying performance and other categories. GATE applicants should also check whether their score affects scholarship or admission treatment.

LPU M.Tech Research Opportunities

Students considering research careers should evaluate faculty projects, laboratories, publication opportunities, industry collaborations and the dissertation topic before joining. An M.Tech is more valuable when you use it to produce demonstrable technical or research work.

M.Tech Projects & Industry Exposure

Choose projects that solve a real technical problem rather than only reproducing classroom exercises. For data/AI students, build deployable models; for CSE, software or systems projects; for core branches, lab, simulation or industry-linked projects.

LPU M.Tech Placements: What Students Should Know

Placement outcomes vary significantly by specialisation. Current third-party reporting cites a median salary around ₹8.1 LPA for several M.Tech routes, but this is not a guarantee and should be checked against current official programme-specific data.

LPU M.Tech Career Scope

Career options include advanced engineering, software, data science, AI/ML, cybersecurity, semiconductor, R&D, design and technical consulting roles. Students targeting academia or research can also consider doctoral study.

LPU M.Tech FAQs

What is the duration of M.Tech at LPU?

Two years and four semesters for the regular full-time routes.

Does LPU accept GATE?

GATE is an accepted route for applicable M.Tech programmes.

What is the M.Tech fee?

Current reporting places tuition around ₹3.20–4.00 lakh for two years depending on specialisation.
