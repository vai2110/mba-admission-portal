What is the MNNIT Executive MBA fee?

A current numeric Executive MBA fee could not be verified from the official School material reviewed. Unlike the regular MBA, no reliable current fee bifurcation was found that can be safely presented as an official 2027 figure.

Students should use the Executive MBA admission notification and latest institute fee notice when published. We have deliberately not inserted an old or third-party amount here.

MNNIT Executive MBA FAQs

How many seats are there?

The School lists an intake of 30.

Is work experience considered?

Yes. SMS explicitly includes work experience among the selection factors.

Is this the same as the regular MBA?

No. The School lists Executive MBA separately from the two-year full-time MBA.
