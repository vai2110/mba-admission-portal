What is the MNNIT Executive MBA?

School of Management Studies lists an Executive MBA with an intake of 30. It is a separate route from the 77-seat two-year full-time MBA.

The department states that Executive MBA admission is based on marks earned through a Written Test, Interview, Academic Qualification and Work Experience.
