SMS MNNIT lists an Executive MBA with an intake of 30, separate from the 77-seat two-year full-time MBA.

The department explicitly considers academic qualification and work experience in selection. Check the next official notification for the exact qualifying degree and experience conditions.

What is the MNNIT Executive MBA fee?

Reported fee: ₹2.10 lakh for the three-year part-time MBA/Executive MBA listing. This is indicative, not a confirmed 2027-29 institute fee. The official SMS page confirms the Executive MBA and intake of 30, but does not publish a numeric fee in the accessible material.

MNNIT Executive MBA FAQs

How many seats are there?

The School lists an intake of 30.

Is work experience considered?

Yes. SMS explicitly includes work experience among the selection factors.

Is this the same as the regular MBA?

No. The School lists Executive MBA separately from the two-year full-time MBA.
