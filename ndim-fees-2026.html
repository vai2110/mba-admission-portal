NDIM Fees

NDIM Fees: Quick Answer

Published 2026-28 figure

NDIM's official fee page lists ₹6.90 lakh for Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh for Year 2, making the two-year PGDM course fee ₹13.75 lakh. The institute says this course fee is inclusive of all charges except optional hostel and international study tours.

NDIM Hostel Fees 2026

NDIM says it has tied up with multiple hostel accommodations for boys and girls within walking distance of the campus, with married-student accommodation also available. The published fee page states that twin-sharing accommodation with furniture, air cooling, attached bathrooms, Wi-Fi and common TV starts at ₹9,500 per month including meals.

Actual charges can increase for single occupancy, air-conditioning, family accommodation or other additional amenities. Free to-and-fro transport to campus is stated by the institute.

Budgeting point:

₹9,500/month is a published starting rate, not a universal hostel price. Your actual two-year living cost depends on room type, amenities and changes in hostel rates.

NDIM Scholarships & Rebates

NDIM states that it has created a ₹2.5 crore pool for scholarships and/or rebates. Scholarship consideration is linked to high percentiles in CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT or ATMA and/or a strong academic record in Class 12 and graduation.

Published scholarship criteria

  • High percentile in accepted management entrance exams
  • Strong Class 12 and graduation record
  • Scholarship amount varies according to the exam and percentile

Published rebate categories

  • Wards of defence/paramilitary personnel
  • Siblings of NDIM students/alumni
  • NDIM BBA/BCom students
  • Students from J&K and Northeastern states
  • SC/ST/OBC/EWS and wards of a single parent

Students must first apply to NDIM and qualify through the institute's GDPI process before scholarship/rebate benefits are considered under the published rules.

NDIM Fee Refund Policy

NDIM's fee page states that refund applications are processed as per AICTE guidelines. Students who withdraw should therefore check the applicable AICTE refund rules and the institute's current admission documentation for the relevant batch.

NDIM Fees FAQs

What is the NDIM PGDM fee for 2026-28?

The published course fee is ₹6.90 lakh for Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh for Year 2, totalling ₹13.75 lakh.

Is hostel included in NDIM PGDM fees?

No. NDIM states that hostel charges are separate. The published starting twin-sharing rate is ₹9,500 per month including meals, subject to accommodation and amenities.

Does NDIM provide scholarships?

Yes. NDIM states a ₹2.5 crore scholarship/rebate pool and lists entrance-score and academic-performance criteria along with several rebate categories.

Is the NDIM international study tour included?

No. The published course fee specifically excludes optional international study tours.
