NDIM Fees: Quick Answer

Published 2026-28 figure

NDIM's official fee page lists ₹6.90 lakh for Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh for Year 2, making the two-year PGDM course fee ₹13.75 lakh. Optional hostel and international study tours are separate.

NDIM Hostel Fees 2026

NDIM states that hostel accommodation is available through tied-up options near the campus. The published twin-sharing starting rate is ₹9,500 per month including meals. Actual cost can rise with single occupancy, AC, family accommodation or other amenities.

NDIM Scholarships & Rebates

NDIM states a ₹2.5 crore scholarship/rebate pool. Consideration is linked to high CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT/GMAT/ATMA performance and/or strong Class 12 and graduation records. Published rebate categories include defence/paramilitary wards, siblings, NDIM BBA/BCom students, J&K/Northeastern students, SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates and wards of a single parent.

NDIM Fee Refund Policy

NDIM states that fee refunds are processed as per AICTE guidelines. Students withdrawing from admission should check the current batch documentation and applicable AICTE rules.

NDIM Fees FAQs

What is the NDIM PGDM fee for 2026-28?

₹13.75 lakh: ₹6.90 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh in Year 2.

Is hostel included?

No. NDIM states hostel is separate; a published twin-sharing starting rate is ₹9,500 per month including meals.

Does NDIM offer scholarships?

Yes. NDIM states a ₹2.5 crore scholarship/rebate pool with entrance-score, academic and category-based criteria.
