Graphic Era B.Com (Hons.): What Students Should Know

GEU's commerce portfolio includes B.Com (Hons.), professional-certification-linked variants and a Financial Analytics and Business Intelligence pathway. The regular B.Com (Hons.) is designed around commerce, finance and business skills.

Quick answer

This is a strong starting route for students interested in accounting, finance, banking, taxation, analytics or later professional qualifications and MBA study.

B.Com (Hons.) Eligibility

GEU's commerce course pages state Class XII pass from any Indian Board or equivalent foreign board as the academic requirement for its B.Com pathways.

B.Com (Hons.) Admission Process

The official course pages state that admission is merit based on the qualifying examination. Provisional admission may also be available under the university's published process.

B.Com (Hons.) Fees

GEU publishes programme-wise fee structures. The latest detailed B.Com (Hons.) fee document available for reference shows tuition of ₹71,700 per semester in Year 1 before scholarship, with scholarship and payment slabs based on academic performance/CUET criteria. B.Com Specialisations & Professional Pathways

GEU lists options including Banking & Finance, Accounting/Auditing/Taxation, Financial Analytics & Business Intelligence, ACCA, CPA and CMA-linked pathways. The choice should follow the career direction you want rather than only the course title.

B.Com Careers & Placement Context

Potential career paths include accounting, audit, taxation, banking, finance operations, financial analysis and business analytics. Professional qualifications such as CA, CMA, CPA, ACCA and CFA can also be considered depending on eligibility and career plan.

B.Com (Hons.) FAQs

Who can apply for B.Com (Hons.)?

GEU's course pages state Class XII pass from any Indian Board or equivalent foreign board.

Is admission entrance-exam based?

The course pages state that admission is merit based on the qualifying examination.

Does GEU offer analytics-focused commerce?

Yes. GEU lists B.Com (Hons.) in Financial Analytics and Business Intelligence in knowledge partnership with EY India.
