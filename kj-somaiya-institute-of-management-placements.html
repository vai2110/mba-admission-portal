Which Recruiters Hire from KJ Somaiya?

The institute's placement ecosystem covers multiple management functions and industries. Its professional-development model includes guest lectures, competitions, corporate projects, summer internships and final placements.

Students should evaluate recruiter quality by role, function and batch rather than treating the recruiter count alone as a salary indicator. The current public placement page is strongest for the headline highest/median numbers and recruiter participation.

KJ Somaiya Internship Highlights

For the 2025-27 internship cycle, KJSIM reports 126 recruiters, a ₹3 lakh highest stipend, and average top-10%, top-20% and top-30% stipends of ₹1,43,950, ₹1,14,394 and ₹94,220, respectively.

The institute describes summer internships as full-time practical exposure and also highlights corporate projects and industry engagement as part of its professional-development model.

KJ Somaiya Placement Trends

KJSIM's public placement material uses different reporting formats across programme and batch pages. The latest MBA highlight panel gives the 2024-26 highest and median CTC plus recruiter count. Older numbers should only be compared when the batch scope and metric definition are identical.

Why no artificial year-wise salary table?

A year-wise table mixing different reporting scopes can create a false trend. This page keeps the latest official figures together and avoids filling missing years with third-party estimates.
