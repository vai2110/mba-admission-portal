What Are the Latest Official KJSIM MBA Placement Figures?

For MBA 2024–26, KJSIM reports a ₹29.30 LPA highest CTC, ₹12 LPA median CTC and 210 recruiters. The latest 2025–27 internship highlights list 126 recruiters and a highest stipend of ₹3 lakh.

The current official placement page does not show a comparable overall-batch average CTC in the same panel. We therefore keep highest and median separate rather than calling the highest package an average.

Which Recruiters and Job Functions Should Applicants Check?

KJSIM describes placement preparation through guest lectures, corporate projects, competitions, summer internships and final placements. The official highlights reviewed provide recruiter participation totals, but not a complete public role-wise hiring matrix.

Before using a recruiter list to judge ROI, ask: which function hired, how many students were selected, what was the fixed component, where was the role based, and was the offer for the flagship MBA or a specialised programme? Recruiter presence alone does not establish the quality or salary of every role.

What Internship Opportunities Does KJSIM Report?

For the 2025–27 internship cycle, KJSIM reports 126 recruiters, a ₹3 lakh highest stipend, and average stipends of ₹1,43,950 for the top 10%, ₹1,14,394 for the top 20% and ₹94,220 for the top 30%.

These are segment averages, not the average stipend for the entire batch. Students should use them as upper-cohort reference points and check the official placement brochure for programme and role context.

Can KJSIM Placement Trends Be Compared Year by Year?

Only compare years when the programme, batch size, metric definition and reporting scope are consistent. The current official highlights reviewed give the MBA 2024–26 highest and median CTC and recruiter count, plus 2025–27 internship data.

A missing average or previous-year metric should not be estimated from the highest package. For current comparisons, use the KJSIM overview and fee snapshot alongside the placement data.

Is the KJSIM MBA Placement Outcome Worth the Programme Cost?

Use the median CTC as your starting point, then compare the role, fixed salary, city, growth path and your full cost of attendance. The published 2026–28 flagship MBA programme fee is ₹23.875 lakh before hostel and personal living costs.

KJSIM Placement FAQs: Salary, Recruiters and ROI

What is the latest highest package at KJ Somaiya?

KJSIM reports ₹29.30 LPA as the highest CTC for MBA 2024–26.

What is the median package at KJSIM?

The official MBA 2024–26 placement highlight reports ₹12 LPA median CTC. This is more useful for typical-outcome planning than the highest CTC.

Does KJSIM publish the average MBA package?

The current official placement highlights reviewed do not show a comparable overall-batch average CTC in the same panel. We do not infer one from the highest or median figure.

How many recruiters participated in MBA placements?

The official placement highlight reports 210 recruiters for MBA 2024–26.

What is the highest internship stipend?

For the 2025–27 internship cycle, KJSIM reports a highest stipend of ₹3 lakh. The top 10%, 20% and 30% averages are segment-level figures, not batch averages.

Should I compare the highest package with MBA fees?

No. Compare the programme fee and additional living costs with median CTC, role quality, fixed pay and your financing plan. The 2026–28 flagship MBA fee is ₹23.875 lakh before hostel.
