Latest placement snapshot

The current official placement page reports 100% placement, 200+ recruiting companies, a ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC and ₹14.80 LPA average package. These figures are useful starting points, but a student should still examine the underlying placement report before treating them as a personal salary expectation.

Placement outcomes need programme context

IIM Bodh Gaya's recent cohorts include the flagship MBA, MBA in Digital Business Management/AI and MBA in Hospital & Healthcare Management. The institute's 2025 convocation communication reported programme-level averages of ₹12 LPA for MBA, ₹12 LPA for DBM and ₹11 LPA for HHM for that graduating cohort. The current placement page presents a newer consolidated headline view.

Why this distinction matters

Do not mix a current consolidated placement figure with an older programme-specific figure. Use the newest audited report for the exact batch and programme you are evaluating.

For programme-level career fit, compare the MBA, MBA-DBAI and MBA-HHM pages.

How does the placement process work?

The official placement process describes a structured sequence in which recruiters interact with students before screening and final selection.

Pre-placement interaction

Companies conduct presentations or pre-placement talks so students can understand the organisation and roles.

Screening

Depending on the recruiter, aptitude tests, case studies and group discussions may be used.

Final selection

Personal interviews, offer rollout and acceptance complete the recruitment cycle.

The institute also runs a Summer Internship Programme for first-year PGP students, giving students industry exposure before final placements.

How should you use placement data while choosing the institute?

Use the average for ROI

Compare average compensation with total programme cost rather than using the highest CTC as the denominator.

Use percentiles for realism

Top-25/50/75 data provides a better sense of the distribution than a single highest package.

Check your target role

A strong overall average does not guarantee that your preferred role, function or sector will recruit in the same volume.

Check the batch

Placement numbers change with cohort composition and market conditions. Prefer the latest audited report.

Placement FAQs

What is the latest average package?

The current official placement page reports ₹14.80 LPA.

What is the highest package?

The current official placement page reports a highest CTC of ₹45.63 lakh.

Does 100% placement mean every student gets the same salary?

No. Placement rate and compensation are different metrics. Salary varies by role, recruiter, student profile and selection outcome.

Are the placement reports audited?

The institute provides audited final placement reports for multiple batches and identifies some newer reports as audit in progress.
