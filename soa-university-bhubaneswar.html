SOA University Bhubaneswar: Overview

Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) is a deemed-to-be university in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. It has a multidisciplinary academic structure spanning engineering, computer applications, management, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, law, agriculture, veterinary sciences, hospitality, paramedical studies and research.

For a student comparing SOA with another university, the most important point is that admission, fees and career outcomes differ significantly by institute and programme. The right comparison should therefore be course-specific rather than based only on the overall university ranking.

Quick answer: Is SOA a good university?

SOA is a strong option for students who want a multidisciplinary private/deemed university in Bhubaneswar and whose target programme has a suitable fee, admission route, curriculum and outcome profile. Its NIRF 2025 positions are particularly strong in dental, law, medical, university and engineering categories.

Accreditation, Status & Recognition

SOA describes itself as a deemed-to-be university and its official website lists NAAC A++ accreditation and Category-I autonomy. Its NIRF presence across several disciplines provides an additional national benchmarking reference.

Campus, Infrastructure & Student Life

SOA operates a multidisciplinary campus ecosystem with academic buildings, laboratories, libraries, sports and student facilities. Medical and health-science students also have access to the university's hospital and clinical ecosystem, while engineering students study within the Institute of Technical Education and Research.

For a course decision, focus on the facilities relevant to your programme rather than generic campus claims. Engineering applicants should inspect labs and curriculum; medical and dental applicants should assess clinical exposure; management applicants should examine curriculum, internships and placement reporting.

SOA University Placements

SOA placement outcomes differ by discipline. NIRF 2025 reported 1,802 of 1,903 engineering graduates placed in 2023-24 with a ₹6 lakh median salary. Management reported 175 of 180 graduates placed with a ₹5.20 lakh median.

Who Should Choose SOA?

SOA can make sense if you want a broad academic ecosystem in Bhubaneswar, are comfortable with its programme-specific fee, and have a clear course target whose curriculum and outcome data fit your goals.

Compare alternatives first if your decision is driven only by a university-wide ranking, a single highest-package claim, or a generic campus promise. For B.Tech, MBBS, BDS and MBA, compare the relevant discipline data, total cost and admission route separately.

SOA University FAQs

What is SOA University famous for?

SOA is known for its multidisciplinary portfolio and strong NIRF positions, particularly in dental, law, medical, university and engineering categories.

Is SOA private or government?

SOA is a deemed-to-be university in Bhubaneswar; it is not a government university.

What entrance exam is required for SOA?

It depends on the course. SAAT is used for several programmes, B.Tech has an applicable JEE Main/SOA route, and MBBS/BDS require NEET and the prescribed counselling process.

What is the SOA B.Tech fee?

Published annual fees vary by branch, from ₹2.35 lakh for Civil/Electrical/Mechanical to ₹3.35 lakh for listed CSE specialisations such as AI/ML, Cyber Security, Data Science and IoT.

What is the SOA placement salary?

There is no single university-wide salary. NIRF 2025 reports a ₹6 lakh engineering median and ₹5.20 lakh management median for the latest cohorts cited on this page.

Where is SOA University located?

SOA is located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
