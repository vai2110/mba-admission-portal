SOA University at a Glance

Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) is a deemed-to-be university in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. It offers programmes across engineering, management, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, law, agriculture, veterinary sciences, hospitality, computer applications and research.

Which entrance exam does SOA accept?

The route depends on the programme. SAAT covers several programmes, JEE Main is used for applicable B.Tech admissions, while MBBS and BDS admissions are through NEET and the prescribed DGHS counselling route.

SOA Placements

SOA placement outcomes vary sharply by discipline, so a single university-wide package can be misleading. NIRF 2025 reported 1,802 placements among 1,903 engineering graduates in 2023-24, with a ₹6 lakh median salary. Management reported 175 placements among 180 graduates with a ₹5.20 lakh median.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SOA a university or college?

SOA is a deemed-to-be university located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.

What is the admission process at SOA?

It depends on the course. SAAT is used for several programmes, JEE Main is accepted for applicable B.Tech admissions, and NEET with DGHS counselling is used for MBBS and BDS.

What is SOA University famous for?

SOA has a multidisciplinary academic portfolio and strong NIRF positions across university, engineering, medical, dental and law categories.
