BMU Hostel Fees

Published hostel charges with food vary by programme and accommodation. For many UG programmes, tower double occupancy is ₹2.36 lakh per year and triple occupancy ₹2.25 lakh. MBA accommodation is listed separately at ₹2.45 lakh single, ₹2.40 lakh double and ₹2.30 lakh triple per year.

Scholarships

BMU offers merit, sportsperson, Haryana domicile, equity and defence ward scholarships or fee waivers. The university advertises awards of up to 100% for eligible students.

How to Calculate Your BMU Budget

Do not budget only for tuition. Add hostel and food, travel, books and personal expenses, and then subtract any confirmed scholarship. For MBA and residential programmes, compare the total two-year cost with realistic placement outcomes rather than only the highest package.

Fees FAQs

What is BMU MBA tuition?

₹7.55 lakh per year, or ₹15.10 lakh for two years, according to the current published fee page.

Does BMU hostel fee include food?

The published hostel tables describe the listed annual academic-period charges as accommodation with food.

Does BMU offer full scholarships?

BMU advertises scholarships and fee waivers of up to 100% for eligible categories.
