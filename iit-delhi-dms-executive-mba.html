Programme Overview

DMS describes the Executive MBA as a two-year degree programme for experienced professionals. Classes are held from 6:15 PM onwards at IIT Delhi. The programme is designed to be pursued alongside professional work.

Tentative Admission Dates: 2027–29

2027–29 dates are not announced. Based on the 2026 cycle, the planning pattern is January–April applications, May written test, May/June interview, June results and July classes. These are tentative planning windows.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 60% marks is required. For SC/ST/PH candidates, the qualifying-degree requirement is relaxed to 55% or 5.50 CGPA. Applicants also need a minimum of 3 years of work experience.

Admission Process

1. Application

Apply online with the required fee and supporting information.

2. Written Test

The test assesses verbal, analytical/data interpretation, quantitative and business-awareness abilities.

3. Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for the personal interview at IIT Delhi.

4. Offer

Final selection considers the published admission index and the candidate's professional/academic profile.

What the fee covers: programme tuition and the separately notified institute charges.

What it does not cover: do not treat the programme fee as an all-in living cost.

Placements

No placement support is provided. DMS states that Executive MBA participants are expected to already be working professionals. This is therefore different from the full-time MBA's campus-placement model.

FAQs

Is work experience compulsory?
Yes. DMS requires a minimum of 3 years of work experience.
Is this a degree programme?
Yes. DMS states that the Executive MBA is a degree programme offered by the Department of Management Studies, IIT Delhi.
Is hostel provided?
No. DMS states that hostel facility is not extended for this programme.
Is placement support provided?
No. DMS explicitly states that placement support is not provided for the Executive MBA.
