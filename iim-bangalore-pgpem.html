IIM Bangalore PGPEM: What the Programme Is Designed For

The Post Graduate Programme in Enterprise Management (PGPEM) is IIM Bangalore's two-year weekend MBA for working professionals. The programme is designed for mid-level and senior executives who want to build broader management capability without leaving their jobs. IIMB says the programme complements participants' existing functional knowledge and experience with contemporary management knowledge.

2 yearsWeekend MBA format
6+ yearsMinimum full-time experience after graduation for 2027-29
750 hoursExpected contact hours, with flexibility to complete requirements over up to five academic years
Key distinction: PGPEM is not simply a part-time version of the two-year PGP. It is specifically structured around professionals continuing their careers, with weekend classes, live projects and an Action Learning Project that connects classroom learning to workplace problems.

IIM Bangalore PGPEM Eligibility 2027-29

The 2027-29 admission document makes work experience the most important eligibility differentiator. Candidates must have substantial post-graduation professional experience in addition to the academic and test requirements.

RequirementPGPEM 2027-29 criterion
Academic qualificationBachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA.
SC / ST / PwDAt least 45% marks or equivalent CGPA.
Professional qualification routeCandidates without a bachelor's degree who completed CA/ICWA/CS after Class XII can also apply, subject to the same minimum percentage/CGPA requirement.
Work experienceMinimum 6 years of full-time work experience after bachelor's degree as on March 31, 2027.
Accepted testsCAT, GMAT, GRE or IIMB Test.

What work experience is eligible?

IIMB specifies full-time work experience after obtaining the bachelor's degree. Part-time work and internships, paid or unpaid, undertaken during a programme of study are not counted as eligible work experience. Applicants should be prepared to submit employment letters or payslips showing the joining date.

Required application documents

  • SSC, HSC and bachelor's degree transcripts
  • Bachelor's degree certificate
  • GMAT/GRE scorecard, where applicable
  • Statement of purpose covering achievements and future goals
  • Work-experience letters / first and last payslips
  • Applicable category and disability documentation
  • Two online reference letters, including preferably one referee who can evaluate job performance

IIM Bangalore PGPEM Admission Process 2027-29

IIMB's 2027-29 PGPEM selection process is conducted in two rounds. Candidates can apply in either round but should apply in only one round.

Choose an accepted test routeApplicants can use CAT, GMAT, GRE or the IIMB Test. The test selected at application is used for assessment under the applicable rules.
Submit the online applicationThe application fee is ₹1,500 and is non-refundable. Complete academic, work-experience, SOP and reference sections before submission.
Meet the screening requirementsOnly complete applications meeting the minimum academic, experience and other criteria move to the next stage.
Attend the interview and writing testShortlisted candidates are called for an interview and Writing Ability Test. The current schedule lists Bengaluru as the interview city.
Composite-score evaluationAfter interviews, IIMB assigns each candidate a composite score. Offers depend on the composite score, available seats and applicable minimum cutoffs.

IIM Bangalore PGPEM CAT, GMAT, GRE & IIMB Test

PGPEM is unusually flexible in its accepted test routes: candidates can apply through CAT, GMAT, GRE or the IIMB Test. This gives experienced professionals more than one way to demonstrate readiness for the programme.

Test2027-29 ruleImportant detail
CATAcceptedApplicants must indicate PGPEM in the CAT 2026 application. CAT 2026 scores are used for the PGPEM Round-2 assessment.
GMATAcceptedOfficial score report / test-taker copy is required as applicable. IIMB publishes specific validity windows for each application round.
GREAcceptedOfficial score report / test-taker copy is required as applicable. IIMB publishes specific validity windows for each application round.

About the cutoff: IIMB's current PGPEM admission page does not publish a single CAT/GMAT/GRE percentile cutoff that guarantees an interview or offer. The final decision is based on a composite score after the interview stage. Students should therefore avoid treating any unofficial "safe score" as an official cutoff.

IIM Bangalore PGPEM Curriculum & Weekend Learning Format

PGPEM uses a four-term academic structure in each academic year. PGPEM-specific courses are scheduled on Fridays and Saturdays. In the second year, students with flexible work schedules may also take electives offered across other IIMB postgraduate programmes.

Core management areas

TermCore courses
Term 1Managerial Economics; Designing Organizations; Decision Sciences
Term 2Financial Reporting & Analysis; Marketing Management; Managing People & Performance in Organizations
Term 3Microeconomics; Business Law; Corporate Finance
Term 4Operations Management; Competition and Strategy; Cost Management; Corporate Governance & Ethics

Projects and application-based learning

Each student is expected to complete the required 750 contact hours within a maximum of five academic years. The curriculum includes live projects, and the Action Learning Project allows students to take a turnkey problem from their organisation, analyse it and make recommendations. This is particularly relevant for professionals seeking immediate workplace application.

Elective flexibility

The elective basket spans decision sciences, finance, marketing, information systems, operations, strategy, entrepreneurship, organisational behaviour and other management areas. IIMB states that the list is tentative and can change by year based on student interest.

IIM Bangalore PGPEM Career Outcomes: What Official Evidence Shows

PGPEM is designed around career progression for working professionals rather than conventional campus placement for fresh graduates. IIMB's programme description explicitly positions the programme for mid-level and senior executives, while official ranking releases provide outcome indicators.

#72Global position in Financial Times Executive MBA Ranking 2025
#2India position in FT Executive MBA Ranking 2025
82%Salary percentage increase reported for PGPEM alumni in IIMB's FT EMBA 2025 release

Why there is no conventional placement table here

IIMB's current official Career Development Services placement materials list dedicated placement reports for PGP, PGPBA and EPGP, but do not provide a comparable PGPEM-specific salary table. It would therefore be misleading to copy EPGP or PGP/PGPBA salary data onto this page.

What the published career evidence suggests

The FT EMBA 2025 release says PGPEM achieved a Career Outcomes score of 87.1 out of 100, measuring graduates promoted and mean salary increase. The same release reported an 82% salary percentage increase for PGPEM alumni, up from 68% in the previous ranking release. These are career-progression indicators, not campus placement salary figures.

Decision implication:

PGPEM should be evaluated on promotion potential, role expansion, leadership capability, peer network and the ability to apply learning while remaining employed—not on a conventional highest-package metric.

Who Should Apply for IIM Bangalore PGPEM?

Strong fit if you

  • Have at least six years of qualifying full-time experience after graduation.
  • Want to continue working while pursuing an IIMB MBA.
  • Want broader management capability beyond your current functional specialisation.
  • Can apply projects, strategy and management frameworks directly to workplace problems.

Think carefully if you

  • Are a fresher or have less than six years of qualifying experience.
  • Need a full-time residential MBA with a summer internship and campus placement focus.
  • Are primarily comparing programmes by average or highest salary packages.
  • Cannot commit to the Friday/Saturday academic schedule and associated workload.
Student decision rule: PGPEM is most relevant when your objective is career progression without career interruption. If you want to leave work and make a major pre-experience or early-career transition, IIMB's PGP or EPGP may be a more relevant comparison depending on your experience level.

Frequently Asked Questions About IIM Bangalore PGPEM

What is IIM Bangalore PGPEM?

PGPEM is a two-year weekend MBA programme for working professionals, designed for mid-level and senior executives.

What is the eligibility for IIM Bangalore PGPEM 2027-29?

A bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent CGPA, 45% for SC, ST and PwD applicants, at least six years of full-time work experience after graduation as on March 31, 2027, and a valid CAT, GMAT, GRE or IIMB Test score are required.

Can I apply to PGPEM through CAT?

Yes. Candidates can apply through CAT 2026, provided they indicate PGPEM in the CAT application. IIMB also accepts GMAT, GRE and the IIMB Test.

What is the latest IIM Bangalore PGPEM fee?

The latest published fee is ₹20.11 lakh tuition plus a ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit for the 2026-28 batch, before applicable taxes and levies.

Is PGPEM a full-time MBA?

No. PGPEM is a two-year weekend MBA designed for working professionals. PGPEM-specific classes are scheduled on Fridays and Saturdays.

Does IIM Bangalore publish PGPEM placement packages?

The current official placement materials do not provide a PGPEM-specific salary table comparable to EPGP. IIMB instead publishes career-outcome evidence such as promotion and salary-increase indicators through external rankings.
