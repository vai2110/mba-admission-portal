IIM Sirmaur MBA Tourism Management: Programme Overview

The MBA Tourism Management at IIM Sirmaur is a two-year, full-time residential programme with a sector focus on tourism, travel, hospitality, aviation, events and allied services. The first year develops core management capabilities; the second year adds sector-focused courses and electives.

The institute positions the programme around applied learning, fieldwork, industry-oriented projects, a mandatory summer internship and exposure to evolving tourism business models.

Who should choose this programme?

Choose it if you want an IIM management education but are specifically interested in tourism, travel, hospitality, aviation, events or related service businesses. If you want a completely general MBA, compare it directly with IIM Sirmaur's flagship MBA.

IIM Sirmaur MBA Tourism Management Admission Process

1. CAT-based shortlisting

Eligible candidates are shortlisted for PI using the published CAT criteria.

2. Personal Interview

The PI is part of the common selection process and contributes 20% to the final composite score under the 2026 policy.

3. Final merit list

The final score combines CAT, PI, academics, work experience, gender diversity and Trailblazer score. A degree in tourism/hospitality management is among the backgrounds recognised under the Trailblazer component.

MBA Tourism Management: Career Scope

The programme is designed for careers in tourism enterprises, destination organisations, hospitality, aviation, events, policy institutions and entrepreneurship. The sector-specific curriculum can be useful for students who want to build domain expertise alongside management capability.

General MBA vs Tourism MBA

If you are unsure whether you want a tourism/service-sector career, the general MBA offers broader management positioning. If you already want tourism, hospitality, aviation or events, the specialised curriculum can give you a more relevant domain foundation.

IIM Sirmaur MBA Tourism Management FAQs: Questions Students Actually Ask

Is the 85 percentile CAT cutoff enough to get MBA Tourism Management at IIM Sirmaur?

It is the published General male overall shortlist cutoff for the 2026-28 process, not a guaranteed admission score. General female candidates had an 80 overall cutoff, with 60 sectional in each CAT section. Final selection also considered PI, academics, work experience, gender diversity and Trailblazer score.

Does MBA Tourism Management make sense if I want a regular corporate MBA job?

The programme is still an MBA, but its second-year structure is specifically oriented toward tourism, travel, hospitality, aviation and related services. Its published curriculum includes sector subjects such as Tourism Management, Hospitality Management, Strategic Airport Management, AI Applications in Tourism and Aviation and Digital Tourism Marketing and Analytics. Students seeking a completely general management profile should compare it with the flagship MBA.

What is the total fee for IIM Sirmaur MBA Tourism Management?

The published 2026-28 fee is ₹21,00,000 across six terms, plus ₹30,000 refundable caution money. The fee table includes admission fee, tuition, hostel, alumni membership and mediclaim. Personal expenses should be planned separately.

Does a tourism or hospitality degree give an advantage in admission?

It can contribute through the Trailblazer component because the 2026 policy specifically lists a degree in Tourism/Hospitality Management among the recognised backgrounds. Trailblazer carries 5% of the final composite score. It is only one part of the selection formula and does not replace CAT or PI performance.

What kind of subjects will I study in MBA Tourism Management?

You study both management and domain subjects. The official brief lists areas including marketing, finance, quantitative analysis, operations and organisational behaviour, along with tourism management, hospitality management, digital infrastructure for tourism and travel, strategic airport management, AI applications in tourism and aviation, tourism futures and digital tourism marketing and analytics.

How is MBA Tourism Management different from MBA TTHM at IIM Sirmaur?

MBA Tourism Management is a sector-focused programme delivered through IIM Sirmaur's management-and-tourism curriculum. MBA TTHM adds a nine-month international component in Mauritius, with Term 4 at the University of Technology Mauritius and Term 5 at the University of Mauritius. Both have the same published 2026-28 fee of ₹21 lakh plus ₹30,000 refundable caution money.

Are the 2027 admission dates official?

No. The 2027 dates shown on this page are tentative planning estimates because the complete 2027-29 admission calendar has not been published. Students should use the official IIM Sirmaur/CAP announcement once the cycle opens.
