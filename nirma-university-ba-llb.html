Nirma BA LLB (Hons.) Overview

The Institute of Law offers five-year integrated programmes including B.A., LL.B. (Hons.), B.Com., LL.B. (Hons.) and B.B.A., LL.B. (Hons.). The programme is approved by the Bar Council of India and combines legal education with humanities, commerce, management and social sciences.

What entrance exam matters?

For the 2026-27 general route, admission was based on the inter-se All India merit rank of CLAT 2026.

BA LLB Eligibility

Candidates must have passed HSC with a minimum 45% aggregate for the published 2026-27 route. Category-specific rules and seat distribution should be checked in the current prospectus.

Nirma BA LLB Admission Process

Students appear for CLAT, apply to Nirma within its application window and are considered through the published All-India merit process. Admission also includes counselling and fee payment after merit declaration.

BA LLB Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

For 2026-27, the official calendar listed online applications from August 15, 2025, CLAT 2026 on December 7, 2025, correction/last opportunity on April 15, 2026, merit declaration April 17 and counselling April 19.

2027 planning: CLAT and Nirma's application calendar should be tracked separately; exact 2027 dates are not yet official.

BA LLB Career & Academic Path

The integrated structure can prepare students for litigation, corporate law, legal research, compliance, policy, public-sector examinations and higher legal education. Internships, moots, research and practical exposure are important in building a law profile.

Nirma BA LLB FAQs

What entrance exam is required for Nirma BA LLB?

For the published 2026-27 general admission, CLAT merit was used.

What is the BA LLB eligibility?

HSC with minimum 45% aggregate under the published 2026-27 criteria.

How much is the Nirma BA LLB fee?

Tuition is ₹3.75 lakh per year; the official 2026-27 listed total payable is ₹4,25,700 including the specified charges.

How long is BA LLB at Nirma?

Five years across ten semesters.
