ICFAI University Hyderabad: What Is IFHE?

The ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education (IFHE) is a deemed-to-be University established under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. Its Hyderabad campus brings together management, technology, law, architecture and social-science education. For management aspirants, the key brand to understand is ICFAI Business School (IBS) Hyderabad, which offers the full-time MBA.

Quick student verdict

IFHE is best evaluated programme-by-programme. If your goal is an MBA, compare IBS Hyderabad on total fee, placement outcomes, batch size, career roles and the value you place on its case-based, residential learning environment rather than treating every IFHE programme as equivalent.

Why students consider IFHE

  • Large multidisciplinary university campus in Hyderabad.
  • IBS Hyderabad offers a dedicated two-year MBA.
  • Case-based and industry-oriented management learning.
  • Residential facilities and student activities on campus.
  • Multiple academic routes beyond management.

What you should verify

  • Whether you mean IFHE, IBS Hyderabad or an online/distance programme.
  • Current MBA fee and additional living expenses.
  • Admission route and accepted test scores.
  • Programme-specific placement data instead of generic university claims.
  • Hostel availability and charges before budgeting.

IFHE Hyderabad Schools and Popular Courses

IFHE's academic structure includes the ICFAI Business School, ICFAI Tech School, ICFAI School of Architecture, ICFAI Law School and ICFAI School of Social Sciences. Current programmes include MBA, Executive MBA, BBA, B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA, law, architecture, economics, psychology and doctoral programmes.

IBS Hyderabad MBA: Why It Gets Most Attention

The IBS Hyderabad MBA is a two-year full-time programme. IFHE's current admission information states eligibility of graduation in any discipline with 50% or above marks and English as the medium of instruction. Candidates can be considered through IBSAT as well as valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores, subject to the applicable admission route.

ICFAI University Hyderabad Admission 2026

Admission depends on the school and programme. For IBS Hyderabad MBA, the current selection route includes IBSAT 2026 or qualifying CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores, followed by the selection process. The selection process for the MBA includes Micro Presentation and Personal Interview, with the final decision also considering past academic performance.

Important distinction

Do not use the MBA admission process for B.Tech, BBA, law or other IFHE programmes. Each school can have its own entrance test, eligibility and application timeline.

ICFAI University Hyderabad Fees 2026

For the IBS Hyderabad MBA, the published fee is ₹17.01 lakh for two years, with ₹8.505 lakh per year. A refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit is additional and living expenses are extra. IFHE's 2026 mandatory disclosure also reports the same ₹17.01 lakh total programme fee for MBA.

IFHE / IBS Hyderabad Placements

Placement figures should be read specifically for IBS Hyderabad MBA. Publicly reported 2025 data puts the average CTC at about ₹9.82 LPA and the highest domestic CTC at ₹21 LPA. NIRF-based data reported for the management category shows 1,078 students placed out of 1,230 graduates in the relevant 2025 reporting cycle, with a median salary of ₹9 lakh.

IFHE Hyderabad Campus and Hostel

The IFHE campus is at Donthanapally, Shankarapalli Road, Hyderabad. IBS Hyderabad states that hostel facilities are available to MBA students, while day scholars can use city-to-campus transport on a payment basis. Campus facilities include academic databases, sports, recreation and health support.

Because the MBA fee does not include living expenses, students should calculate the full cost of attendance rather than comparing tuition alone. The hostel and campus guide covers the practical details.

Student Discussion: What Should You Check Before Choosing IFHE?

Student and applicant discussions around IBS Hyderabad commonly focus on ROI, batch size, role quality, the difference between average and highest packages, campus life and whether the programme is a good fit for a student's target function. These are discussion themes, not official statistics.

Questions worth asking

  • How does the average package compare with the total cost?
  • Am I comfortable with a large MBA cohort?
  • Do I prefer Hyderabad's location and residential campus?

Verify before paying

  • Latest placement report and median salary.

  • Hostel availability and actual living cost.

Who Should Choose ICFAI University Hyderabad?

IFHE can make sense for students who want a large private university ecosystem in Hyderabad and, for management, are specifically interested in the IBS Hyderabad MBA. It is less suitable to evaluate purely on brand or highest package; the better decision comes from matching programme, career function, cost and expected outcomes.

FAQs About ICFAI University Hyderabad

Is ICFAI University Hyderabad the same as IBS Hyderabad?

IBS Hyderabad is the management school within IFHE. IFHE is the university-level institution, while IBS Hyderabad is the business school offering the full-time MBA.

What is the MBA fee at IBS Hyderabad?

The current published fee for the IBS Hyderabad MBA is ₹17.01 lakh for two years, excluding living expenses and with a refundable caution deposit payable separately.

What is the MBA eligibility?

IFHE states graduation in any discipline with 50% or above marks, with English as the medium of instruction, subject to its admission rules.

What is the average package at IBS Hyderabad?

Reported 2025 placement data puts the average CTC around ₹9.82 LPA, while the highest domestic CTC was ₹21 LPA. Students should also consider median salary and role quality.

Does IFHE provide hostel facilities?

Yes. IBS Hyderabad states that hostel facilities are available to MBA students. Availability, deposits and charges should be checked for the relevant intake.
