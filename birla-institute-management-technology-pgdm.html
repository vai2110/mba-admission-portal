What is BIMTECH PGDM?

BIMTECH PGDM is a two-year, six-trimester, full-time residential management programme. It is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

The programme combines core management courses with electives, summer internship, live projects, simulations, alumni mentoring and skill-based learning in areas such as data analytics and technology.

Who is eligible for BIMTECH PGDM?

Candidates need a graduation degree in the 10+2+3 pattern with at least 50% aggregate marks. Final-year students appearing in their first attempt in Summer 2027 can also apply, subject to securing 50% and submitting the result by 31 October 2027.

Shortlisting considers entrance score and past academic record. The selection stage consists of Extempore and Personal Interview. Final selection also considers work experience and other applicable parameters.

What CAT percentile is needed for BIMTECH PGDM?

BIMTECH does not publish a single fixed final CAT/XAT cutoff for PGDM on the current admissions page. Meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee a shortlist or admission.

As a planning reference, the institute's admission merit scholarship starts at 85 percentile in CAT/XAT, rises to ₹3 lakh at 90–94.9 and ₹5 lakh at 95+. These are scholarship bands, not admission cutoffs.

What do you study in BIMTECH PGDM?

The first two trimesters cover core management such as marketing, finance, accounting, economics, operations, HR, statistics and information systems. Later trimesters add electives, NTCCs, data analytics with Python, business simulation, rural immersion and a dissertation.

Elective areas include Marketing & Retail, Finance & Insurance Risk Management, OB & HR, Operations & Decision Sciences and IT.

What are BIMTECH PGDM placements?

In the official 2023-25 final placement report, 96.79% of PGDM students were placed. PGDM highest Indian CTC was ₹22 LPA, average CTC ₹10.90 LPA and median CTC ₹10 LPA.

BIMTECH PGDM FAQs

Is BIMTECH PGDM equivalent to MBA?

Yes. BIMTECH states that its PGDM is recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

Is BIMTECH PGDM residential?

Yes. BIMTECH states that all its programmes are residential and hostel stay is compulsory.
