MCA at BBAU

BBAU lists MCA as a two-year, four-semester programme with 50 regular seats plus 10 paid seats. It is offered by the Department of Computer Science.

MCA Eligibility

The programme catalogue accepts BCA, Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent. It also allows B.Sc., B.Com or B.A. graduates with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, subject to the stated bridge-course condition. The listed minimum is 50%, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

MCA Admission 2026

BBAU's 2026-27 PG cycle publishes score lists, merit lists and spot rounds. The exact MCA route should be checked in the current PG prospectus and university notice because admission rules can change between sessions.

MCA Career and Placement Context

MCA students should assess placement opportunities at the university through the Central Training & Placement Cell and compare the current employer mix, internships and role quality. Do not use the university's best package as an MCA-specific salary expectation unless the source identifies the programme.

BBAU MCA FAQs

What is BBAU MCA duration?

MCA is listed as a two-year, four-semester programme.

What is the BBAU MCA fee?

The programme catalogue lists ₹3,000 per semester for regular seats and ₹25,000 per semester for paid seats.

Can a B.Com graduate apply for MCA?

Yes, the published eligibility allows B.Sc., B.Com and B.A. graduates with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, subject to the stated conditions.

How many MCA seats are there?

The current listing shows 50 regular seats plus 10 paid seats.
