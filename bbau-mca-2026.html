BBAU MCA 2026: Quick Snapshot

BBAU's MCA is a two-year postgraduate computer applications programme offered by the Department of Computer Science. The current listing provides 50 regular seats and 10 paid seats. The programme is particularly relevant for graduates who want to move into software, IT, data, systems or technology-support careers through a postgraduate computing qualification.

BBAU MCA Eligibility 2026: Can B.Com or BA Students Apply?

Yes, the current programme listing is broader than only BCA graduates. It accepts BCA, a bachelor's degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent, and specified B.Sc., B.Com and B.A. graduates who have Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, subject to the university's stated conditions/bridge-course requirement. The listed minimum is 50%, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Most important eligibility check

If your bachelor's degree is B.Com, B.A. or B.Sc., do not stop at the percentage requirement. Verify the Mathematics condition and the exact bridge-course/subject rule in the current BBAU prospectus.

What Should You Learn During an MCA?

An MCA should move you beyond basic computer familiarity into applied computing. Students should expect core areas such as programming, data structures, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering and web/application development, followed by advanced or elective topics and project work. The exact BBAU syllabus should be checked in the current department curriculum.

For better placement readiness, students should build at least one programming stack, practise SQL, understand Git and APIs, complete deployable projects and gain exposure to data structures and problem solving. If targeting data/analytics roles, add Python, statistics, Excel/BI and portfolio projects.

BBAU MCA Career Scope and Placement Reality

An MCA can lead toward software development, QA/testing, application support, database roles, IT operations, web development and selected data/technology positions. Employers generally evaluate skills and projects in addition to the degree.

BBAU's university-wide NIRF disclosures can provide context, but they combine different programmes. The 2026 overall submission reports 1,337 students placed across the reported two-year PG cohort in 2024-25 with a ₹10.523 lakh median. That is not an MCA-specific placement figure, so students should not present it as an MCA salary guarantee.

BBAU MCA FAQs

What is BBAU MCA duration?

MCA is listed as a two-year, four-semester programme.

What is BBAU MCA fee?

Regular tuition is ₹3,000 per semester; the separate paid-seat fee is ₹25,000 per semester.

Can B.Com students apply for BBAU MCA?

Yes, the published eligibility includes B.Com graduates with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, subject to the stated conditions.

Can BA students apply for BBAU MCA?

The current listing also includes B.A. graduates with the required Mathematics background, subject to the university's conditions.

How many MCA seats are there at BBAU?

The current listing shows 50 regular seats plus 10 paid seats.
