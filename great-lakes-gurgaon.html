Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA: What Students Should Know

Great Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon offers the one-year PGPM for professionals and the two-year PGDM for freshers and early-career candidates. The institute highlights industry interaction, live projects, internships and CXO exposure as part of the learning experience.

PGPM is designed for candidates with 2+ years of work experience, while PGDM is for freshers and candidates with strictly less than 36 months of experience under the published eligibility rules.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA Admission Process

Eligibility

PGPM accepts GMAT, CAT, XAT and NMAT under the current 2027-28 rules. PGDM accepts GMAT, CAT and XAT. A bachelor's degree and applicable academic/work-experience requirements are required. citeturn0search7turn0search2

Selection

Applicants submit the online form, undergo profile screening and complete the published interview stages. PGDM includes an AI-proctored interview followed by a personal interview. citeturn0search2

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA FAQs

What is the latest Great Lakes Gurgaon placement average?

₹16.2 LPA for PGPM Class of 2026 and ₹11.8 LPA for PGDM Class of 2025.

What are the total published fees?

₹22.50 lakh for PGPM 2026-27 and ₹19.82 lakh for PGDM 2026-28.

Are there costs beyond the published fee?

Yes. Laptop, personal travel and miscellaneous expenses are additional; non-vegetarian food is optional and extra.

Are the 2027 dates official?

No. The 2027 dates on this page are explicitly marked tentative because the complete 2027 calendars are not yet published by Great Lakes.
