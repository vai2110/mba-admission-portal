Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA: What Students Should Know

About Great Lakes Gurgaon

Great Lakes Institute of Management’s Gurgaon campus is located in the Delhi NCR corporate hub. The institute describes the campus as a 7.5-acre residential campus designed around faculty mentorship, peer learning, management and leadership development and industry proximity.

What makes the Gurgaon campus different for an MBA applicant?

The location is relevant for students who value access to the wider Delhi NCR corporate ecosystem. Great Lakes highlights industry interaction, CXO exposure, live projects, internships and experiential learning across its management programmes.

Quick answer: Is Great Lakes Gurgaon worth considering?

It is worth shortlisting if your programme fit matches your work experience and career objective, and if the published fee and placement outcomes work for your ROI expectations. The PGPM is the accelerated option; the PGDM is the longer route for freshers and early-career candidates.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA Admission Process

PGDM admission process

For PGDM, the current official process is: submit the online application and application fee, undergo application screening, complete the AI-proctored interview, attend the personal interview, receive the admission decision, complete document verification and confirm admission through fee payment and enrolment.

PGPM admission process

For PGPM, Great Lakes uses a holistic evaluation covering test scores, academic performance, work experience, personal and professional track record, management aptitude, competencies, diversity and interview performance. The process includes an AI-proctored video interview followed by the personal interview.

What is actually evaluated?

Does Great Lakes Gurgaon have a management quota?

The institute states that it does not admit students under management quota or any other form of reservation and warns applicants against third parties offering such seats.

What is included?

The published fee includes academic expenses and reading material. Residential accommodation is mandatory, with twin-sharing AC accommodation. Vegetarian meals are included. Non-vegetarian food is optional and charged extra.

Scholarships and financing

For PGPM, Great Lakes currently states that it offers Diversity & Merit Scholarships, including 40 scholarships worth ₹5 lakh each, with at least 30% reserved for female candidates. It also lists the Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship with a 100% tuition fee waiver for exceptional candidates. Scholarship terms can change and students should verify the live official policy before relying on them.

ROI note

Compare the published fee with the relevant programme's placement outcomes rather than comparing headline package figures alone. PGPM and PGDM have different programme lengths and candidate profiles.

Great Lakes Gurgaon Campus & Student Life

The Gurgaon campus is a fully residential environment with academic, accommodation, dining, fitness and wellness facilities. The official campus page describes a 7.5-acre campus with on-campus hostels, classrooms, library, sports facilities and a Spatial Reality Lab.

Residential facilities

  • Twin-sharing air-conditioned hostels
  • Study desks, storage and housekeeping
  • 24/7 high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Vegetarian meals included in the published fee
  • Medical support and regular housekeeping

Learning & recreation

  • Interactive classrooms and library
  • AR/VR devices and Spatial Reality Lab
  • Gym and outdoor sports
  • Cricket, football, basketball, badminton and volleyball
  • Industry and CXO interactions

The campus's proximity to Delhi NCR's corporate ecosystem is particularly relevant if you value industry exposure and networking alongside classroom learning.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA FAQs

What is the latest Great Lakes Gurgaon placement average?

₹16.2 LPA for PGPM Class of 2026 and ₹11.8 LPA for PGDM Class of 2025.

What is the total fee for Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA programmes?

The published 2026-27 PGPM fee is ₹22.50 lakh and the published 2026-28 PGDM fee is ₹19.82 lakh.

Does Great Lakes Gurgaon accept CAT?

Yes. CAT scores are accepted for both PGPM and PGDM under the current published admission rules, alongside other programme-specific tests.

Can a fresher apply to Great Lakes Gurgaon?

Yes. Freshers can apply for PGDM. PGPM is intended for candidates with 2+ years of work experience.

Is the Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

Great Lakes states that its PGDM is an MBA-equivalent programme as validated by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), and the programme is approved by AICTE.

Is hostel accommodation mandatory?

Yes. The published fee documents state that the programmes are residential and hostel accommodation is mandatory, with twin-sharing AC accommodation.

Are there scholarships for Great Lakes Gurgaon?

Great Lakes currently lists Diversity & Merit Scholarships for PGPM, including 40 scholarships worth ₹5 lakh each and a Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship with a 100% tuition fee waiver for exceptional candidates. Verify current terms before applying.

Are the 2027 Great Lakes Gurgaon admission dates official?

No. The 2027 dates shown on this page are explicitly tentative planning estimates because the complete official 2027 calendars are not yet published.
