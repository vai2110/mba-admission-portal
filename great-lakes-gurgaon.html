Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA: What Students Should Know

Great Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon offers the one-year PGPM for professionals and the two-year PGDM for freshers and early-career candidates. The institute highlights industry interaction, live projects, internships and CXO exposure as part of the learning experience.

The key decision for an applicant is programme fit: PGPM is the accelerated route for candidates with 2+ years of work experience, while PGDM is designed for freshers and candidates with less than 36 months of experience under the published eligibility rules.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA Admission Process

Eligibility and test score

PGPM accepts GMAT, CAT, XAT and NMAT under the current 2027-28 eligibility rules. PGDM accepts GMAT, CAT and XAT. A bachelor's degree and the applicable academic/work-experience requirements are also required. citeturn1search9turn1search2

Application and selection

Applicants submit the online form, pay the application fee and undergo profile screening. The published PGDM process includes an AI-proctored interview followed by a personal interview, with evaluation based on test scores, academics, experience and overall profile. citeturn1search2

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA FAQs

What is the latest Great Lakes Gurgaon placement average?

₹16.2 LPA for PGPM Class of 2026 and ₹11.8 LPA for PGDM Class of 2025.

What are the total published fees?

₹22.50 lakh for PGPM 2026-27 and ₹19.82 lakh for PGDM 2026-28.

Are there costs beyond the published fee?

Yes. Great Lakes identifies personal laptop, travel/personal expenses and optional non-vegetarian food as additional costs; some international immersion expenses can also be extra.

Are 2027 admission deadlines announced?

Complete 2027 deadlines are not yet published on the current official pages. Previous-cycle dates are therefore shown separately as planning references rather than confirmed 2027 dates.
