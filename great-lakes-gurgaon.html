Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA: What Students Should Know

Great Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon offers two principal full-time management programmes: the one-year Post Graduate Program in Management (PGPM) for experienced professionals and the two-year Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) for fresh graduates and candidates with up to three years of work experience.

The Gurgaon campus is positioned in the corporate hub of Delhi NCR and the institute highlights live projects, internships, CXO interactions and industry engagement as part of the learning experience. Great Lakes Gurgaon is ranked 50th in the Management category in NIRF 2025. citeturn1search5

The most important student decision is not simply whether Great Lakes is suitable, but which of the two programmes fits your work-experience stage. PGPM is the accelerated one-year MBA route; PGDM is the two-year route for freshers and early-career candidates.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA Admission Process

Step 1: Check programme fit

PGPM requires 2+ years of work experience, while PGDM is intended for freshers and candidates with less than 36 months of experience under the published eligibility rules. citeturn1search2turn0search1

Step 2: Apply online

Applications are made through the Great Lakes website. The current published application fee is ₹2,200. Great Lakes states that it has not authorised third parties to initiate applications on its behalf. citeturn1search2

Step 3: Screening and interviews

The evaluation considers standardised test scores, academic record, work experience, interview performance, leadership potential and diversity of experience. PGPM uses an AI-proctored video interview followed by a personal interview. citeturn1search2

Step 4: Final selection

The admissions panel evaluates overall profile and programme fit rather than relying only on an entrance-test score.

Who Should Apply to Great Lakes Gurgaon?

Consider PGPM if

  • You already have 2+ years of full-time work experience.
  • You want a one-year MBA rather than a two-year programme.
  • You are looking for a career transition or acceleration.

Consider PGDM if

  • You are a fresher or have less than 36 months of experience.
  • You want two years for internships, projects and broader management preparation.
  • You want time to choose a specialisation before the second year.

Great Lakes Gurgaon MBA FAQs

Does Great Lakes Gurgaon offer an MBA?

Great Lakes describes PGPM and PGDM as MBA programmes on its Gurgaon website, with PGPM as a one-year MBA and PGDM as a two-year MBA programme.

What is the Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM fee?

The published 2026-27 total fee is ₹22.50 lakh, including academic fees, accommodation, caution deposit and alumni subscription.

What is the Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 total fee is ₹19.82 lakh.

What is the latest PGPM placement average?

₹16.2 lakh CTC for the PGPM Class of 2026, according to Great Lakes Gurgaon.
