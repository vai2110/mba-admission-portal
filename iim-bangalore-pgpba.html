IIM Bangalore PGPBA: What the Programme Is Designed For

The IIM Bangalore Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) is a two-year, full-time residential MBA programme focused on using analytics and artificial intelligence to solve complex business problems. The programme runs for six trimesters and includes a summer project between the first and second year.

2 yearsFull-time residential MBA leading to an MBA in Business Analytics
6 trimestersStructured across core management and analytics-focused learning
Analytics + AISecond-year core includes Predictive and Generative AI and Big Data Analytics & NLP
Course-level decision point: PGPBA is not a narrowly technical data-science degree. The curriculum combines management subjects with quantitative, analytics and AI courses, so it is better suited to students who want to use analytics for business decisions and eventually move into analytical leadership or cross-functional management roles.

IIM Bangalore PGPBA Eligibility 2027-29

IIM Bangalore has published the eligibility rules for the PGPBA 2027-29 admission cycle. The academic route is deliberately broad: applicants do not need a specific undergraduate discipline, but they must satisfy the minimum academic requirement and have a valid CAT score.

Does your undergraduate stream matter?

No specific bachelor's discipline is prescribed in the published eligibility criterion. However, students should distinguish eligibility from competitiveness: PGPBA's admission process also evaluates academic performance, CAT sectional performance and work experience, so simply meeting the minimum graduation percentage does not indicate a likely shortlist.

Can final-year students apply?

The 2027-29 admission document lists a bonafide certificate for candidates studying in the final year among the required documents. Such applicants should therefore ensure their university documentation can establish their current academic status and later completion of the qualifying degree.

IIM Bangalore PGPBA Admission Process 2027-29: Step-by-Step

The PGPBA admission process has two selection stages. The first stage creates a shortlist and a pre-PI score. Shortlisted candidates then participate in a Personal Interview and Written Ability Test, after which IIMB calculates a post-PI composite score for final offers.

Check eligibility and take CAT 2026You must meet the academic/professional eligibility and have a CAT 2026 score valid on the application date. IIMB requires positive raw scores in all CAT sections for candidates to be considered for the first shortlist.
Submit the online PGPBA applicationThe application is the starting point of the IIMB PGPBA admission process. The 2027-29 application window is scheduled from 1 October 2026 to 14 January 2027.
Complete the reference requirementTwo online reference letters are mandatory. Referees should preferably be a current/past employer or a teacher from an academic institution where the candidate studied. The application cannot be submitted until the reference section is complete.
Clear the first-shortlist requirementsIIMB first applies minimum CAT section requirements. Candidates in the first shortlist are then evaluated through the pre-PI scoring framework.
Reconfirm interest if shortlistedBefore calling candidates for the PI, IIMB will write to first-shortlist candidates and ask them to reconfirm their interest in PGPBA 2027-29. Candidates who reconfirm are considered for the PI/WAT shortlist using the pre-PI score.
Attend PI and WATThe Personal Interview and Written Ability Test are held on the same day and at the same location. The WAT is approximately 30 minutes and is evaluated on the content and style of the note.
Final selection through post-PI scoreIIMB combines PI, WAT, CAT, academic scores and work-experience components to create the post-PI composite score. Final offers are made category-wise on this score.
Important for applicants:

IIMB explicitly states that the published process is applicable to the 2027-29 cycle only and may be changed for future cycles. Use the official admission document as the final authority when the application window opens.

How work experience is scored

For the pre-PI score, IIMB gives 10x/36 when qualifying work experience is between 0 and 36 months, and a maximum of 10 points at 36 months or more. The 2027-29 document specifies the reference point as months of work experience up to July 2025; applicants should follow the exact cycle-specific calculation in the official document.

Special automatic PI qualification routes

Among first-shortlisted candidates, the top-ten candidates in each of the total CAT score, CAT DILR score and CAT QA score categories automatically qualify for the PI stage. IIMB then evaluates these candidates in the same way as other PI candidates.

Financial aid for PGPBA students

IIMB states that financial aid is assessed using two weighted scores and can be awarded at 100%, 80%, 60%, 40% or 20% levels. Students should check the institute's financial-aid page for the applicable assessment process and conditions for their batch.

IIM Bangalore PGPBA Curriculum: Analytics, AI and Management

The PGPBA curriculum is designed around a management foundation followed by deeper analytics learning. This structure matters for students deciding between an MBA in Business Analytics and a purely technical analytics or data-science programme.

Electives and specialisation choice

Students must take at least four analytics-domain field electives worth 12 credits across Terms IV, V and VI. The elective basket includes areas such as Business Analytics and Intelligence, Data Visualization and Storytelling, Generative Artificial Intelligence, Sports Analytics, Marketing Analytics and Spreadsheet Models for Business Decision Problems. The exact elective list can change by academic year.

Summer internship

The summer internship is scheduled between the first and second year with a firm in the Analytics domain. It is optional for students who have at least 34 months of work experience before enrolling.

IIM Bangalore PGPBA Placements & Career Outcomes

PGPBA participates in IIM Bangalore's placement ecosystem alongside the PGP. IIMB's 2026 final and lateral placement update for the combined PGP and PGPBA Class of 2026 reported 596 students appearing for placements, 664 offers and 177 firms. Management Consulting accounted for 45% of offers, followed by Technology/Product Management at 15% and Investment Banking at 11%.

What roles can PGPBA support?

The programme's curriculum is particularly relevant for students targeting analytics-led management, product and technology, consulting, business intelligence, marketing analytics, decision sciences and other roles where quantitative analysis is combined with business judgment. The programme is not positioned by IIMB as a single-function degree, so students should choose it based on the type of work they want to perform rather than the course title alone.

Who Should Choose IIM Bangalore PGPBA?

Strong fit if you want

  • An MBA degree with a stronger analytics and AI orientation than a general management MBA.
  • To combine management fundamentals with machine learning, predictive and generative AI, NLP, optimization and data analysis.
  • Analytics-domain summer internship exposure and the option to select analytics field electives.
  • Career flexibility across analytics-linked management, consulting, technology/product and other business functions.

Think carefully if you want

  • A purely technical data-science programme focused primarily on coding and statistical modelling.
  • A course where admission depends mainly on crossing a published CAT cutoff.
  • A programme with a guaranteed analytics-only job outcome. IIMB's placement ecosystem covers many management functions.
  • A low-intensity MBA. The curriculum combines quantitative courses, core management subjects, projects and a demanding selection process.
Student decision rule: Choose PGPBA when your long-term goal is to sit at the intersection of business + analytics + AI. If your target is purely technical data science, compare PGPBA against specialised technical programmes before applying. If your target is broad management with analytics capability, PGPBA is structurally aligned with that goal.

Frequently Asked Questions About IIM Bangalore PGPBA

What is the eligibility for IIM Bangalore PGPBA 2027-29?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required; SC, ST and PwD applicants need at least 45%. Completed CA, CS, ICWA(CMA) or FIAI professional degrees are also accepted under IIMB's stated conditions. A valid CAT score is required.

What is the IIM Bangalore PGPBA CAT cutoff?

The published first-shortlist minimum for the 2027-29 process, based on CAT 2025 cutoffs, is 85 overall for General category candidates with 80 in each CAT section. The interview-call scores may be higher.

Is work experience mandatory for IIM Bangalore PGPBA?

No. The eligibility criteria do not require prior work experience. However, work experience contributes up to 10 points to the pre-PI score and is evaluated again in the post-PI process.

Are two reference letters required for PGPBA?

Yes. Two online reference letters are mandatory, and the application cannot be submitted until the reference section is complete.

What is the latest IIM Bangalore PGPBA fee?

The latest published domestic PGPBA fee is ₹26 lakh for the 2026-28 batch, plus a ₹30,000 general caution deposit and ₹30,000 mess advance per term. The 2027-29 fee should be confirmed from IIMB when published.

When does IIM Bangalore PGPBA 2027-29 admission begin?

IIMB lists 1 October 2026 as the application opening date and 14 January 2027 as the application closing date. Interviews are scheduled for February/March 2027, with offers in the second week of April 2027, subject to change.
