IIM Udaipur GSCM: Programme Overview

The One-Year MBA in Global Supply Chain Management is a full-time residential programme for experienced professionals. It combines management fundamentals with intensive supply-chain specialisation, live projects and industry interaction. The current programme is a 12-month format scheduled for April 2026 to March 2027.

Supply chain depth

Strategy, inventory, logistics, procurement, demand forecasting, risk and global sourcing.

Analytics + technology

Advanced analytics, IoT, AI, digital supply networks and operations research.

Industry exposure

Live industry projects, practitioners and a capstone simulation.

IIM Udaipur GSCM Admission Process

  1. Complete the online application.
  2. Submit the eligible GMAT, GRE or CAT score information.
  3. Eligible candidates are considered for shortlisting.
  4. Shortlisted candidates appear for a Personal Interview.
  5. Admission offers are based on the overall candidate profile and performance in the selection process.

2026–27 application cycle

IIM Udaipur published four application cycles for the 2026–27 batch. The final cycle closed on January 31, 2026, and the programme commencement date was April 7, 2026.

IIM Udaipur GSCM Curriculum

The programme consists of four terms that progressively move from management fundamentals to supply-chain specialisation, advanced topics, industry projects and capstone simulation.

Term 1: Management Fundamentals

Financial Reporting and Analysis & Cost Management, Business Statistics, Economics for Managers, Marketing Management, Organisational Behaviour, Operations Management, Corporate Finance, Operations Research-I, Managerial Oral Communication and Emerging Trends in Supply Chain Management workshop.

Term 2: Supply Chain Management & Related Capabilities

Supply Chain Management, Strategic Management, Advanced Analytics-I, IoT, Artificial Intelligence and Future of Work, Digital Supply Networks, Logistics: Operations & Analysis, Operations Research-II and Emerging Trends workshop.

Term 3: Advanced Management & Supply Chains

Multi-sided Platform Business Strategy, Project Management, Written Managerial Communication, Industry Projects, Global Trade and Supply Chains, Supply Chain Analytics, Service Operations, Sustainable Supply Chains, Operations Strategy, Strategic Sourcing and Procurement, and Strategic Human Resource Management.

Term 4: Simulation, Electives & Project Conclusion

Capstone Enterprise Simulations, Industry Projects, Entrepreneurship and programme-specific electives.

Projects, Capstone & Experiential Learning

GSCM includes a live supply-chain project designed around real business problems. The curriculum also uses industry projects and a capstone enterprise simulation to bring together analytical, operational and strategic learning.

Examples of elective directions

Advanced Analytics-II, Database Management for Supply Chain, Negotiations in Sourcing, Lean and Six Sigma, Production Planning and Demand Forecasting, Supply Chain Consulting, Supply Chain Finance, Revenue and Pricing Management and regulatory frameworks are among the listed tentative electives. Specific offerings may vary by year.

GSCM FAQs

Is GSCM a one-year MBA?

Yes. It is a 12-month full-time residential MBA programme.

Does GSCM require prior supply-chain experience?

No. Prior supply-chain experience is not required, although applicants must satisfy the overall work-experience requirement.

Is there a summer internship?

No. The one-year GSCM programme does not include a summer internship.

Which exams are accepted?

GMAT, GRE and CAT are accepted subject to the current validity rules stated by the institute.
