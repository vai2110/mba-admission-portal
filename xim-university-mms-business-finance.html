What Is XIM MMS-BF?

The Master of Management Studies in Business Finance is a two-year postgraduate management programme offered by XIM University's School of Commerce. It is designed around business-finance knowledge and is relevant to students seeking finance-oriented management careers.

Best fit

MMS-BF is worth considering if you want finance depth but also want a management-school environment. Compare the curriculum and career outcomes with an MBA in Finance before deciding.

MMS-BF Eligibility 2026

XIM's published eligibility is more specific than its general MBA programmes. Applicants can come from B.Com with Accounting/Finance, BBM/BBA, B.Sc Mathematics/Statistics, B.A Economics, B.Tech or professional qualifications such as CA, CS or CMA, with at least 55% aggregate marks.

MMS-BF Admission 2026

The 2026 XIM admission information lists XAT, CAT, GMAT, XGMT and NMAT for MMS-BF. Candidates should follow the programme-specific application instructions and current admission bulletin.

See the XIM admission guide for the central process and timelines.

MMS-BF Fees 2026

The published 2026 course fee is ₹6 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.20 lakh in Year 2. Additional charges include development fund, course materials, IT, alumni activities, career advisory services and refundable deposit.

MMS-BF Career Options

Potential career areas include corporate finance, financial analysis, banking, investment-related roles, risk, treasury, business finance and finance operations. The exact role mix depends on the student's profile, internships and recruitment cycle.

Relevant skills

  • Financial analysis
  • Accounting and reporting
  • Corporate finance
  • Excel and data analysis

Before applying

Check whether you want a finance-specialist route or the broader management orientation of MBA-BM.

Who can apply for MMS-BF?

Eligible backgrounds include B.Com Accounting/Finance, BBM/BBA, B.Sc Mathematics/Statistics, B.A Economics, B.Tech and CA/CS/CMA routes, with at least 55% as published.

What is the MMS-BF fee?

Is MMS-BF residential?

XIM states that MBA and MMS-BF programmes are residential.
