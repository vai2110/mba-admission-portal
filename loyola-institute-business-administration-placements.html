LIBA Placement Statistics 2024-25

LIBA's official Mandatory Disclosure 2025-26 states that the institute has maintained 100% placements since inception of the full-time PGDM programme. For 2024-25, it reports a minimum salary of ₹7.5 LPA, maximum salary of ₹21 LPA and average salary of ₹11 LPA.

LIBA Salary Trend: How to Read It

The official figures show that the maximum package reached ₹21 LPA in both 2023-24 and 2024-25, while the average moved from ₹11.21 LPA to ₹11 LPA. For applicants, the average is generally more useful for budgeting and ROI comparisons than the highest package alone.

LIBA Recruiters & Industries

LIBA's official PGDM site lists 60+ recruiting companies and highlights organisations such as Deloitte, KPMG, EY, Accenture, JPMorgan, HSBC, Barclays, HDFC, ICICI, Tata Motors, Asian Paints, Schneider, Cognizant, Infosys, Hexaware, Mahindra and BMW.

The recruiter mix spans consulting, IT, BFSI, FMCG and manufacturing, giving students options across both business and functional careers.

What Roles Can LIBA Graduates Target?

The current programme structure covers Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Business Analytics and IT & Operations. Role outcomes therefore vary by specialisation, internships, academic performance and recruiter demand. Students should inspect the role offered, fixed/variable pay and location before comparing two offers only on CTC.
