Christ University BCA: Quick Answer

For students

The BCA (Honours/Honours with Research) is a 4-year, 8-semester programme. The standard eligibility is a pass at +2. Christ's 2026 UG selection list places BCA in the ET + SA + MP + PI route at Bangalore Central and other listed campuses.

What Does Christ BCA Teach?

The BCA curriculum covers programming, databases, software development and newer computing areas. The current programme structure also provides pathways involving AI and Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity and other technology areas depending on the programme variant.

Students can choose between the regular BCA, Honours, Honours with Research and specialisation pathways where offered.

Who Can Apply for BCA?

The central eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12 for BCA. The programme page also accepts recognised boards and provides specific provisions for international curricula.

Unlike some engineering courses, the central BCA eligibility does not require PCM. Still, students should verify the exact campus/programme variant before submitting the application.

What Is the BCA Selection Criteria?

Christ's 2026 UG ET document lists BCA under ET + SA + MP + PI. The university describes SA as a written assessment, MP as a 90-second presentation and PI as the interview stage.

Selection schedules are programme- and campus-specific. The final admit card is the controlling document for the candidate's venue and date.

Christ University BCA Fees

For the Central Campus 2026 BCA listing, the published programme fee is ₹1.74 lakh per year for Karnataka students and ₹1.94 lakh per year for other Indian states, plus ₹8,000 non-refundable registration fee.

What Can You Specialise In?

Current BCA programme information includes technology areas such as software engineering/full-stack development, AI and data science, cloud computing and cybersecurity. The exact options depend on the campus and BCA variant.

Students targeting software jobs should prioritise programming, data structures, databases, development projects and internships. Students targeting data/AI roles should add statistics, Python, machine learning and project work.

What Are the Career Options After BCA?

Common roles

  • Cloud/Systems roles

Higher studies

BCA also provides a route to MCA, MSc and other postgraduate computing programmes. Build a strong technical portfolio during the degree rather than relying only on the BCA title.

Christ University BCA FAQs

Does Christ BCA require Mathematics in Class 12?

The central eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12 for BCA and does not state a universal Mathematics requirement. Check the exact programme page for any campus-specific condition.

Is BCA at Christ University 3 years or 4 years?

The current standard BCA listing describes an 8-semester, 4-year Honours/Honours with Research programme. Some programme variants may show different structures.

What is the BCA fee for other Indian states?

The Central Campus 2026 listing shows ₹1.94 lakh per year for other Indian states, plus ₹8,000 registration fee.

What is the BCA selection process?

The 2026 UG entrance-test document lists BCA under ET, SA, MP and PI for the relevant campuses.
