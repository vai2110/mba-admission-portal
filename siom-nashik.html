SIOM Nashik: College Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Operations Management (SIOM), Nashik is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University) focused on operations, supply chain and technology-oriented management education. Its flagship MBA in Operations Management is designed exclusively for engineers and covers areas such as supply chain management, project management, technology management, dynamic systems modelling and quality management.

SIOM also offers an MBA in Agri Operations Management for graduates interested in operations and supply-chain careers in agro-based and allied sectors. The institute's pedagogy includes project-based learning, SAP-ERP training, Six Sigma certification, industry visits, workshops and industry interaction.

Is SIOM Nashik a good choice?

SIOM is particularly relevant if you are an engineer targeting operations, supply chain, procurement, consulting, manufacturing, logistics or technology-linked management roles. Non-engineering graduates should look at the Agri Operations Management route rather than assuming the flagship Operations Management eligibility applies to every programme.

SIOM Nashik MBA Courses & Eligibility

MBA Operations Management

Designed exclusively for BE/BTech graduates. Focuses on operations, supply chain, project, technology and quality management.

MBA Agri Operations Management

Open to graduates and focused on agro-based and allied sectors, agri supply chain, procurement, warehousing and value chains.

MBA Operations + Durham Dual Degree

SIOM's MBA Operations Management with MSc Management (Supply Chain Logistics) at Durham University Business School; introduced from 2026-27.

MBA Operations Management eligibility

Candidates must be engineering graduates (BE/BTech of any stream) from a statutory university with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year engineering students can apply subject to meeting the required marks. Foreign-university qualifications require an AIU equivalence certificate.

MBA Agri Operations Management eligibility

Graduates from a recognised university or institution of national importance with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST candidates, can apply.

SIOM Nashik SNAP Cutoff

SIOM requires SNAP for the regular MBA admission route, but the current official programme page does not publish a fixed overall SNAP percentile cutoff. Therefore, the portal should treat any percentile used for shortlisting as an expected/indicative figure rather than an official SIOM cutoff.

How should students interpret the cutoff?

A competitive SNAP score improves the probability of being shortlisted, but the final outcome also depends on the institute's selection process, programme eligibility and GE/PI performance. Do not treat a third-party percentile estimate as a guaranteed SIOM cutoff.

SIOM Nashik MBA Fees, Hostel & Scholarships

For the 2026-28 batch, SIOM's approved fee structure lists ₹9.50 lakh annual academic fees for MBA Operations Management. Hostel, mess and refundable deposits are additional. SIOM Nashik Campus & Learning

SIOM is located at A-23, Shravan Sector, Cidco, New Nashik, Nashik. The institute's operations-focused learning model combines classroom teaching with live projects, SAP-ERP training, Six Sigma, industry visits, workshops and guest lectures.

Operations focus

Supply chain, process improvement, procurement, quality, technology and operations strategy.

Industry exposure

Live projects, industry visits, workshops and CEO/industry interaction.

Certifications

SAP-ERP training and Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification are part of the learning ecosystem.

Who Should Apply to SIOM Nashik?

Good fit if
  • You are an engineer targeting operations or supply chain.
  • You want careers in manufacturing, logistics, consulting, IT, FMCG or e-commerce.
  • You prefer a technical + management-oriented MBA.
  • You are interested in procurement, demand planning, quality, process improvement or analytics.
Check alternatives if
  • You are a non-engineering graduate looking for the flagship Operations Management MBA.
  • You want a generalist MBA with broad finance/marketing/HR specialisations.
  • Your target role is unrelated to operations, supply chain or adjacent business functions.
  • Your total budget does not fit SIOM's current residential fee structure.
Decision rule

Compare SIOM with other SNAP colleges using your engineering background, target role, SNAP score, total two-year cost and programme-level placement outcomes—not just the highest package.

SIOM Nashik FAQs

Who is eligible for SIOM MBA Operations Management?

Engineering graduates (BE/BTech of any stream) with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST candidates, are eligible. Final-year engineering students can also apply subject to meeting the required marks.

Does SIOM Nashik accept CAT?

The regular SIOM admission route is through SNAP. Candidates register for SNAP and apply for SIOM in the same application process.

What is the SIOM Nashik MBA fee?

For the 2026-28 Operations Management batch, the approved academic fee is ₹9.50 lakh per annum. Hostel, mess and refundable deposits are additional.

What is the SIOM Nashik SNAP cutoff?

SIOM requires SNAP but does not publish a fixed overall percentile cutoff on its current programme page. Treat third-party cutoff figures as indicative rather than official.

What is the highest package at SIOM Nashik?

The latest SIOM placement summary currently available on its website reports a highest CTC of ₹21.60 LPA.

Is SIOM Nashik only for engineers?

The flagship MBA Operations Management is designed exclusively for engineering graduates. SIOM's MBA Agri Operations Management accepts graduates from recognised universities with the required marks.

Does SIOM provide hostel accommodation?

Yes. SIOM publishes hostel and mess charges for the Nashik campus. The 2026-27 three-sharing hostel fee is ₹1,05,900 and the refundable hostel deposit is ₹20,000.

When does SIOM MBA 2027-29 start?

The current official admission schedule lists 1 June 2027 as the programme commencement date.
