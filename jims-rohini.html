What is JIMS Rohini known for?

Quick answer

Jagan Institute of Management Studies (JIMS), Rohini is a Delhi management institute offering AICTE-approved PGDM programmes in PGDM, PGDM-International Business and PGDM-Retail Management. The institute says these PGDM programmes are equivalent to an MBA by AIU.

JIMS has operated in Rohini since 1993 and focuses on industry-oriented management education, corporate interaction, internships, live projects and specialised tracks. Its 2026 admissions material highlights AI, digital skills, critical thinking, leadership and decision science in the curriculum.

NIRF's 2025 Management ranking places Jagan Institute of Management Studies Technical Campus at Rank 43 nationally. Students should still compare programme-level fees, selection rules and placement outcomes before making a final choice.

For the 2026 PGDM intake, JIMS states that applicants should have a minimum three-year bachelor's degree or equivalent in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks. Final-year candidates can apply subject to submission of the required documents later.

JIMS Rohini cutoff: what percentile should you target?

JIMS's mandatory disclosure provides historical programme-level entrance benchmarks. For the PGDM programme, the published 2023-25 benchmark was 75 percentile in CAT, 580 composite score in MAT, 60 percentile in XAT and ATMA, and 70 percentile in CMAT. These are historical disclosed cutoffs, not a promise of the 2026 selection threshold.

JIMS Rohini Fees 2026-28: exact PGDM fee

Current official fee

For Batch 2026-2028, JIMS lists a total PGDM/PGDM-IB/PGDM-RM fee of ₹10,75,000, plus ₹2,000 lifetime alumni membership and ₹5,500 refundable security deposit. Total cash outlay including those listed charges is ₹10,82,500.

The same fee structure is published for PGDM, PGDM-IB and PGDM-RM. JIMS also provides documentation facilitation for education-loan applications.

Scholarships for 2026-28

JIMS publishes a merit-cum-means scholarship scheme. Depending on entrance score or academic performance, published awards include ₹3 lakh for CAT/XAT above 90 percentile, ₹2 lakh for 85–89.9 percentile, ₹1 lakh for 75–84.9 percentile, ₹50,000 for qualifying MAT/CMAT performance, and ₹1 lakh for above 80% in Class 10, 12 and graduation. An EWS/means category can provide 20% of tuition fee subject to conditions.

JIMS says its 2024-26 PGDM batch recorded 90%+ placement, with 190 companies participating. The reported median was ₹9.83 LPA and average ₹9.95 LPA. These cohort-level figures are more useful for ROI decisions than a highest-package headline alone.

Prominent recruiter categories listed by JIMS include BFSI, FMCG, IT, consulting, e-commerce, retail and other corporate sectors. Actual role and salary outcomes vary by programme, specialisation, prior profile and placement cycle.

ROI view: At a listed academic fee of ₹10.75 lakh before the separate alumni/security charges, a reported median salary of ₹9.83 LPA makes JIMS worth evaluating for students seeking a Delhi/NCR management career. But students should compare the complete cost of living, opportunity cost and their own target role—not simply divide fee by highest package.

JIMS Rohini FAQs

Is JIMS Rohini good for MBA?

JIMS offers PGDM programmes rather than a conventional university MBA. The institute states that its PGDM is equivalent to an MBA by AIU. Its 2024-26 placement page reports a ₹9.83 LPA median salary and 190 participating companies.

What is the JIMS Rohini PGDM fee for 2026-28?

The official 2026-28 fee is ₹10.75 lakh, plus ₹2,000 lifetime alumni membership and ₹5,500 refundable security deposit.

What is the JIMS Rohini CAT cutoff?

The mandatory disclosure lists 75 percentile as a historical PGDM benchmark. It should not be treated as a guaranteed 2026 final cutoff.

Does JIMS accept XAT and CMAT?

Yes. JIMS's published admission procedure accepts CAT, XAT, MAT and CMAT scores, with its broader admission information also referencing other eligible tests.

What is the JIMS Rohini highest package?

JIMS's 2026 admission material highlights a ₹24 LPA highest package. The more useful cohort-level figures currently published are ₹9.95 LPA average and ₹9.83 LPA median for the 2024-26 PGDM placement cycle.

Does JIMS provide scholarships?

Yes. For 2026-28, JIMS publishes merit-cum-means scholarships linked to CAT/XAT, MAT/CMAT, academic performance and EWS/means criteria.

What are the PGDM specialisations at JIMS?

JIMS offers the flagship PGDM plus PGDM-IB and PGDM-RM. The programmes cover management areas including Finance, Marketing, HR, International Business and Retail-related domains.

Is JIMS Rohini AICTE approved?

JIMS's mandatory disclosure lists PGDM, PGDM-IB and PGDM-RM among its AICTE-approved programmes and says the three PGDM programmes are NBA accredited.

What is the JIMS Rohini placement rate?

JIMS currently reports 90%+ placement for the 2024-26 PGDM batch, with 190 companies participating.

Where is JIMS Rohini located?

The campus is at 3, Institutional Area, Sector-5, Rohini, near Rithala Metro Station, Delhi-110085.
