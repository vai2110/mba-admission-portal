How to choose the right MBA budget

1. Know your total budget

Include tuition, hostel, mess and living costs for the entire programme.

2. Compare value, not just fees

Look at placement consistency, curriculum, brand value and learning opportunities.

3. Match it with your career goals

Choose a college that fits your budget and supports the roles and industries you want to target.
