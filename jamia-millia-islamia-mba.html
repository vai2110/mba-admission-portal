JMI MBA Course: Quick Overview

What is the JMI MBA?

It is a regular, full-time, four-semester Master of Business Administration programme offered by the Department of Management Studies. The 2026–27 prospectus lists 60 seats and an annual fee of ₹26,875.

ParameterDetails
DegreeMaster of Business Administration (M.B.A.)

Duration4 semesters

JMI MBA Eligibility 2026

The official 2026–27 entrance-test schedule states that applicants for the MBA must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade. The proof of the qualifying degree is required at the time of admission.

Academic qualificationBachelor's degree in any discipline
Minimum marks50% aggregate or equivalent grade

No engineering-only restriction is stated in the cited JMI admission schedule for the MBA.

JMI MBA Admission Process 2026

The 2026–27 university schedule places the MBA under programme code M55 and specifies a JMI entrance test. The exam was scheduled for 2 June 2026, 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM, in multi-city mode.

Step-by-step

  1. Register for the MBA through the JMI admission system.
  2. Appear for the university entrance test.
  3. Check the university's interview/group-discussion and selection notices, where applicable.
  4. Complete document verification and admission fee formalities after selection.

The exact post-test selection workflow and dates should always be checked in the latest JMI admission notice because the university can issue programme-specific updates.

JMI's University Prospectus 2026–27 lists the regular M.B.A. with a total fee payable per year of ₹26,875. The prospectus states the annual figure; it does not present a single consolidated two-year fee in the same row.

JMI MBA Curriculum

The DMS 2025–26 placement brochure publishes the full-time MBA course structure. The first year builds the management foundation; the later semesters add strategy, corporate analysis, projects and electives.

JMI MBA Placement Snapshot

The latest DMS placement brochure available on the official JMI website reports placement statistics for the 2023–25 batch: ₹24 LPA highest CTC, ₹10.8 LPA average CTC and ₹10 LPA median CTC. The brochure covers DMS programmes collectively in its placement material, so these figures should not be presented as an MBA-only guarantee.
