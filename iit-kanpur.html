Overview

Quick answer: DoMS IIT Kanpur offers a full-time MBA built around management education within IIT Kanpur's technology and research ecosystem. The 2026–28 admission cycle required CAT 2025, followed by the institute's shortlisting, GD/PI and merit process. The 2026–27 admission process is now complete according to the department's latest notice.

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), formerly the Department of Industrial & Management Engineering, offers an MBA with compulsory and elective coursework, a summer term and a capstone project. The programme covers areas including marketing, finance, HR, economics, operations, IT/systems, service management, public policy, sustainability and energy policy.

Courses, Curriculum & Specialisation Areas

The MBA is structured around 216 credits, including core coursework, electives, a summer project and a capstone. The academic model gives students room to build a management profile while using IIT Kanpur's quantitative and technology-oriented environment.

The qualifying degree must involve at least three years of education after 10+2, with Mathematics as one of the subjects. Final-year candidates can apply provisionally, subject to completing the qualifying degree requirements by the institute's deadline.

Selection components

The published merit framework uses CAT score, Class X/XII performance, undergraduate performance, work experience and PI, with the institute also providing for gender-diversity consideration. For the 2026–28 process, the published final merit weightage was CAT 20%, Class X/XII 20%, work experience 10%, PI 35%, and undergraduate degree 15%.

What the trend tells students

The latest batch shows a major rise in the highest CTC to ₹36 LPA, while the average and median are below the 2022–24 reported figures. This is a reminder that the highest package alone is not a reliable measure of placement quality. Median CTC and the concentration of offers are more useful when evaluating realistic outcomes.

Recruiters & roles

The latest official brochure lists recruiters/associations including ICICI Bank, Wells Fargo, Deloitte, Cognizant, Accenture, Infosys, HDFC Bank, TVS, Sobha Realty, LUMIQ, IndiaMART and others. Roles span management, analytics, finance, consulting, operations, technology and related business functions.

Placement process

DoMS describes a structured process involving company invitations, job announcement forms, pre-placement talks, student registration, tests/interviews/group discussions where applicable and final selection. Its placement week is generally scheduled in the first week of December. The institute also follows a one-person-one-job policy, subject to its stated dream-company provisions.

Campus & Student Life

Students study within IIT Kanpur's residential campus ecosystem, with access to academic infrastructure and the P.K. Kelkar Library. The MBA's technology-oriented environment can be particularly relevant for students interested in analytics, digital business, operations, technology management and quantitative decision-making.

Who Should Choose IIT Kanpur DoMS?

Good fit

  • Students comfortable with quantitative and analytical coursework.
  • Aspirants wanting an IIT ecosystem with a management degree.
  • Students interested in analytics, technology, finance, operations or consulting.
  • Candidates who value strong academic and research infrastructure.

Reconsider if

  • You want a purely marketing-oriented MBA with little quantitative work.
  • You are selecting a school mainly on the basis of the single highest package.
  • You are uncomfortable with the CAT + academic + PI based selection structure.

Decision factors to compare

Compare IIT Kanpur DoMS with other MBA options on average and median CTC, fees and living costs, role mix, batch size, location, academic intensity, alumni network, work-experience fit and the exact admission criteria—not just brand name or highest package.

FAQs

What CAT percentile is required for IIT Kanpur MBA?

For the 2026–28 admission cycle, the published benchmark was 80 percentile for GEN/EWS/OBC(NCL) and 55 percentile for SC/ST. These are qualifying/shortlisting benchmarks, not guaranteed final-call or admission percentiles.

Does IIT Kanpur DoMS accept CAT?

Yes. CAT 2025 was the qualifying examination for the 2026–28 MBA batch.

What is the latest average package at IIT Kanpur DoMS?

The latest official 2023–25 placement report/brochure reports an average CTC of ₹17.2 LPA, median CTC of ₹16.5 LPA and highest CTC of ₹36 LPA.

What is the IIT Kanpur DoMS MBA selection process?

The published process combines CAT, academic performance, work experience and PI. For the 2026–28 cycle, the final merit weightage was CAT 20%, Class X/XII 20%, work experience 10%, PI 35% and undergraduate degree 15%, with a gender-diversity provision.

Is Mathematics compulsory for IIT Kanpur MBA eligibility?

Yes. The 2026–28 eligibility rules required Mathematics as one of the subjects in the qualifying education after 10+2.

Is work experience mandatory for IIT Kanpur MBA?

No. Work experience is a component of the merit framework, but the published eligibility criteria do not make prior full-time work experience mandatory.

Is IIT Kanpur MBA worth it?

It can be a strong option for students who value the IIT ecosystem, analytical learning and strong reported placement outcomes. The decision should still consider total cost, role preferences, admission competitiveness and the latest placement distribution rather than the highest package alone.
