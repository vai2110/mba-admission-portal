Quick Answer

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) at IIT Kanpur offers a full-time Master of Business Administration (MBA), along with M.Tech and PhD programmes in Management Sciences. For the 2026–28 MBA batch, CAT 2025 is the qualifying examination. The MBA is a four-semester programme with a summer term, compulsory and elective courses, and a capstone project.

IIT Kanpur Management Sciences: Overview

DoMS, IIT Kanpur evolved from the Department of Industrial and Management Engineering and combines management education with IIT Kanpur's analytical and technology-oriented ecosystem. The department's current programme portfolio includes MBA, M.Tech, PhD and e-Masters programmes. Its academic and research areas include operations research and management, decision support systems, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, entrepreneurship and energy.

IIT Kanpur MBA Admission 2026–28

For the 2026–28 batch, applicants must appear for CAT 2025. The admission process has four stages: online application, CAT-based shortlisting, Group Discussion and Personal Interview, followed by final merit-list preparation.

The bachelor's degree must represent at least three years of education after 10+2 and must include Mathematics as one of the subjects. Final-year candidates may apply provisionally, subject to completion of the qualifying degree before joining.

Selection Process

  1. Submit the online application after CAT results.
  2. DoMS shortlists candidates using the CAT percentile and its category-wise criteria.
  3. Shortlisted candidates participate in GD and PI.
  4. Final merit is prepared from the prescribed composite score.

The published MBA FAQ lists the composite components as CAT 20%, Class X/XII academic performance 20%, work experience 10%, Personal Interview 35% and undergraduate performance 15%, with a gender-diversity provision for women candidates.

Application fee: ₹2,000 for General/OBC(NCL)/EWS candidates and ₹1,000 for SC/ST/DAP candidates.

IIT Kanpur MBA Curriculum

The MBA curriculum has 216 total credits and a four-semester residential requirement. Core courses include Marketing Management, Accounting and Finance, Organisation Structure and Design, Economic Analysis for Management, Quantitative Methods for Decision Making, Production and Operations Management, and Socio-Political and Ethical Aspects of Business.

Students can choose electives from DoMS and other IIT Kanpur departments. The programme also includes management seminars, a compulsory summer project and a two-semester capstone project focused on solving real business problems with guidance from faculty and industry stakeholders.

IIT Kanpur Management Placements

IIT Kanpur's official Annual Report 2024–25 reported program-wise placement outcomes for the 2024–25 recruitment season as of 18 May 2025. MBA placement was 68% in the English annual-report figure. The report also recorded 426 placements among 593 registered students across the combined M.Tech, MS(R), MSc, MBA, M.Des and PhD group.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIT Kanpur offer an MBA?
Yes. DoMS IIT Kanpur offers a full-time Master of Business Administration (MBA) programme.
Is IIT Kanpur MBA a specialised MBA?
No. DoMS describes it as a general management MBA. Students can choose electives across multiple management domains.
Is CAT mandatory for IIT Kanpur MBA?
For the 2026–28 batch, CAT 2025 is the qualifying examination.
Can non-engineers apply for IIT Kanpur MBA?
Yes. The official FAQ states that the MBA is not restricted to engineering graduates. The qualifying degree must satisfy the published eligibility requirements, including Mathematics at 10+2 level.
Does IIT Kanpur have M.Tech and PhD programmes in Management Sciences?
Yes. DoMS also offers M.Tech and PhD programmes in Management Sciences, in addition to MBA and e-Masters programmes.
