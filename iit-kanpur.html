Identity & Overview

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur integrates quantitative methods, strategic management, and organizational behaviour within the institute's engineering and science ecosystem. IIT Kanpur is an Institute of National Importance established in 1959.

Admissions

Admissions are managed by the Office of Academic Affairs. The application portal for the July 2026 semester is open from March 19, 2026, to April 15, 2026.

Selection Logic

Selection is based on academic performance, research potential, and interview performance. Official cutoff data is not publicly disclosed.

Eligibility

Applicants must meet department-specific criteria. Final year students graduating by July 27, 2026, are eligible to apply.

Research

The department focuses on multidisciplinary research. Key areas include quantitative methods, strategic management, and organizational behaviour.
