Quick Answer

IIT Kanpur offers postgraduate management education primarily through the Department of Management Sciences, focusing on M.Tech and PhD programmes. Admissions are conducted online via the institute's portal, typically between March and April. Eligibility generally requires a relevant degree with a minimum of 55% marks or 5.5 CPI.

College Overview

Established in 1959, the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur is an Institute of National Importance. The Department of Management Sciences contributes to the institute's mission by offering advanced research-oriented postgraduate programmes. The institute maintains a robust academic environment with over 570 faculty members and a 1,055-acre campus.

Admissions & Eligibility

Admissions for the July semester are processed through the online PG admission portal. Applicants must hold a relevant degree with at least 55% marks or 5.5 CPI (with relaxations for reserved categories as per GoI norms).

ProgrammeSelection Basis
M.Tech / PhDAcademic review, GATE/JRF scores, and interviews

Application Fee: ₹500 for General/EWS/OBC; ₹250 for SC/ST/DAP. Fees are non-refundable.

Placement Performance

IIT Kanpur maintains a strong industry interface. In the 2025–26 placement season, the institute recorded 672 job offers on Day 1, representing a 16% increase over the previous year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIT Kanpur offer a traditional MBA?
Official information regarding a standalone MBA programme is not provided in the current PG admission brochures; the focus is on M.Tech and PhD in Management Sciences.
Is financial support available?
Yes, Institute Assistantships are provided to non-sponsored PhD, M.Tech, and MS (By Research) students.
