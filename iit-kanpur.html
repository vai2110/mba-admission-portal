Overview

Quick answer: DoMS IIT Kanpur offers a full-time MBA within IIT Kanpur's technology and research ecosystem. The 2026–28 cycle required CAT 2025 followed by shortlisting, GD/PI and merit assessment. The 2026–27 admission process is now complete.

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), formerly the Department of Industrial & Management Engineering, offers compulsory and elective coursework, a summer term and a capstone. Areas include marketing, finance, HR, economics, operations, IT/systems, service management, public policy, sustainability and energy policy.

Placement interpretation

The latest batch's highest CTC rose sharply, but average and median were below 2022–24 figures. Students should therefore judge outcomes using median and distribution, not only the highest package.

Recruiters & roles

Recent official material lists ICICI Bank, Wells Fargo, Deloitte, Cognizant, Accenture, Infosys, HDFC Bank, TVS, Sobha Realty, LUMIQ and IndiaMART among recruiters/associations. Roles span analytics, finance, consulting, operations, technology and business functions.

Placement process

DoMS describes company invitations, job announcements, pre-placement talks, student registration, tests/interviews/group discussions and final selection. Placement week is generally scheduled in the first week of December.

Campus & Student Life

Students are part of IIT Kanpur's residential campus ecosystem with access to academic infrastructure and the P.K. Kelkar Library. The environment can suit students interested in analytics, technology management, operations and quantitative decision-making.

Who Should Choose IIT Kanpur DoMS?

Good fit

  • Comfortable with quantitative coursework.
  • Want an IIT ecosystem with an MBA.
  • Interested in analytics, technology, finance, operations or consulting.

Reconsider if

  • You want a purely marketing-oriented MBA.
  • You judge schools mainly by highest package.
  • You dislike CAT + academic + PI based selection.

Decision factors

Compare average and median CTC, fees, living costs, role mix, batch size, location, academic intensity, alumni network and work-experience fit.

FAQs

What CAT percentile is required?

For 2026–28, the published benchmark was 80 percentile for GEN/EWS/OBC(NCL) and 55 percentile for SC/ST.

Does IIT Kanpur DoMS accept CAT?

Yes. CAT 2025 was the qualifying exam for the 2026–28 batch.

What is the latest average package?

₹17.2 LPA average CTC for the 2023–25 batch, with ₹16.5 LPA median and ₹36 LPA highest.

What is the selection process?

CAT, academics, work experience and PI form the published merit framework, with CAT 20%, Class X/XII 20%, work experience 10%, PI 35% and UG 15%.

Is Mathematics compulsory?

Yes. Mathematics is required as one of the subjects in the qualifying education after 10+2.

Is work experience mandatory?

No. It contributes to merit but is not mandatory under the published eligibility criteria.

Is IIT Kanpur MBA worth it?

It can be strong for students seeking an IIT ecosystem and analytical management education. Compare total cost, roles, competitiveness and placement distribution before deciding.
