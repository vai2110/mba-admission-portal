Manipal University Jaipur: What Kind of University Is MUJ?

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) is a multidisciplinary private university within the Manipal education ecosystem. Its current academic portfolio spans engineering, management, computer applications, architecture, law, media, humanities and sciences. The university homepage currently reports more than 14,000 students and 700 faculty, so applicants should evaluate it as a large residential university rather than a single-school B-school or engineering college.

The useful student question is not simply whether MUJ is “good”. It is whether its programme, admission route, annual fee, hostel cost, curriculum and placement evidence match the student's target career. The same university has materially different economics and selection rules for B.Tech CSE, MBA, BBA and BCA.

Quick decision rule

Start with the exact programme. MUJ's programme-specific pages publish different eligibility, entrance routes and fees; therefore a university-level placement or fee number should not be assumed to apply identically to every course.

B.Tech at MUJ: CSE Is Not the Only Engineering Choice

MUJ offers B.Tech options including Computer Science Engineering, CSE & Biosciences, Electrical & Computer Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, VLSI, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Chemical, Civil, Robotics & AI and other specialised routes. B.Tech is generally four years.

How CSE admission works in 2026

For MET 2026, the official eligibility requires 10+2/equivalent with Physics, Mathematics and English as compulsory subjects plus one listed optional subject, with at least 50% combined in Physics, Mathematics and that optional subject. MUJ also lists merit-based admission routes: CSE candidates can qualify through 70% PMX in Class XII, or SAT 75% / JEE Main 90 percentile together with 60% PMX, subject to the stated conditions and seat availability.

CSE students can use electives from semester IV in areas such as Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, AI, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Computer Vision, Web Technologies, Full Stack Development and IoT. Project-based learning starts from semester III.

MBA, MBA Business Analytics and Integrated MBA: Which Route Fits?

MUJ's general MBA is a two-year programme under the TAPMI School of Business. The published eligibility is a bachelor's degree with at least 50% and a valid CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT/GMAT score. Selection considers academics, entrance performance, personal interview, presentation and English comprehension.

MBA Business Analytics has a more explicit analytics orientation. Its published curriculum includes Data Analysis in Excel, Python for Data Analytics, inferential statistics and hypothesis testing, DBMS with SQL, exploratory data analysis, data visualisation and BI, predictive analytics with introductory machine learning, big-data analytics/data strategy and dissertation work. This makes it a materially different option from a generic MBA.

The Integrated MBA combines undergraduate and postgraduate management education in a 10-semester route with specialisations including Marketing, Finance, HR and Business Analytics. The published annual tuition is ₹2.20 lakh.

BCA and MCA: Curriculum Evidence for Computer-Application Careers

MUJ's BCA portfolio includes BCA, BCA-Cyber Security and BCA-Data Science. The published BCA fee is ₹1.77 lakh tuition per year, plus ₹10,000 registration and ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit at admission.

The MCA is a four-semester programme. Its published curriculum moves from discrete mathematics, web technologies, C, RDBMS and data visualisation to networks, Java, operating systems and data structures; semester III includes Unix/Shell, Android, software engineering/project management, electives and a minor project, followed by a major project in semester IV.

MUJ Placements: Separate University-Level Facts from Programme-Level Reality

MUJ's admissions page currently publishes 95% campus placement, 200 companies visiting campus, 50 Fortune 500 companies visiting, ₹1.06 crore maximum compensation and ₹9 lakh average compensation. These are university-level headline figures; they should not be interpreted as the average or maximum specifically for B.Tech CSE, MBA or BCA unless a programme-specific report says so.

The placement ecosystem lists recruiters across technology, consulting, finance and engineering, including Accenture, Adobe, Amazon, Bosch, BlackRock, Capgemini, Cisco, Cognizant, Deloitte, Dell, EY, Goldman Sachs, IBM, Infosys, JP Morgan Chase, KPMG, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Oracle, PwC, SAP, TCS, Wipro and Zscaler. MUJ Jaipur FAQs

What is the fee of B.Tech CSE at MUJ?

The current CSE page lists ₹4,87,000 tuition per year, plus ₹10,000 one-time registration and ₹15,000 refundable caution deposit. Hostel is additional.

Does MUJ accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

MUJ lists merit-based B.Tech admission in addition to MET 2026. For CSE, one listed route is JEE Main 90 percentile with at least 60% PMX in Class XII, subject to the programme conditions and seat availability.

What is the MBA eligibility at MUJ?

The general MBA requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50% and a valid CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT or GMAT score. Selection also considers academics, interview, presentation and English comprehension.

How much is MUJ hostel fee in 2026–27?

The published double-occupancy total including refundable security is ₹2,08,225: ₹1,67,372 accommodation, ₹15,378 housekeeping and ₹25,475 security.
