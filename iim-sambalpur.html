IIM Sambalpur: What Students Should Know

Indian Institute of Management Sambalpur is an Institute of National Importance offering management education with a strong emphasis on innovation, inclusion and integrity. Its flagship two-year residential MBA is designed around management fundamentals, experiential learning, summer internship exposure and second-year electives.

IIM Sambalpur Admission: CAT, Eligibility & Selection

For the regular MBA and MBA-BA programmes, the 2026-28 admission policies use a three-stage process built around CAT 2025, academic profile, relevant work experience, gender diversity and Personal Interview.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Sambalpur?

For the 2026-28 MBA process, the published minimum overall CAT percentile is 90 for General, 80 for EWS, 75 for NC-OBC, 55 for SC and 40 for ST/DAP. For General candidates, the published policy reduces the minimum to 85 for male candidates with at least one year of relevant work experience and for all female candidates. These are minimum shortlisting criteria, not guaranteed admission scores.

Campus, Curriculum and Learning Experience

The regular MBA is a two-year full-time residential programme. The first year provides a common grounding in functional areas of management, followed by a summer internship. In the second year, students can choose electives across areas such as economics, financial management and accounting, information systems, operations, OB and HR, marketing, and policy and strategy.

Learning elementWhat it offers
Core curriculumCommon management foundation during the first year.
Summer internship8-10 week experiential opportunity after the third term, as described by the institute.
ElectivesFunctional choices across finance, marketing, operations, HR, information systems, economics and strategy.
PedagogyCase discussions, problem solving, simulations, projects, presentations, technology-enabled learning and self/peer learning.

Who Should Choose IIM Sambalpur?

Strong fit

  • Students seeking an IIM-brand residential MBA with broad functional exposure.
  • Candidates who value experiential learning, live projects and summer internship exposure.
  • Applicants who want flexibility across finance, marketing, operations, HR, systems and strategy.
  • Students interested in a dedicated business analytics pathway can consider the MBA-BA programme.

Think carefully if

  • You are choosing only on the IIM label without checking roles, median salary and career fit.
  • You want a programme focused almost entirely on one niche rather than general management.
  • You are uncomfortable with a residential programme and its associated total cost.
  • You want an analytics-specialist curriculum; compare the general MBA with the MBA-BA before deciding.

IIM Sambalpur FAQs Students Actually Search

Is IIM Sambalpur a good IIM?

IIM Sambalpur is an Institute of National Importance and was ranked 34th in the NIRF 2025 management rankings. Its fit should be judged using programme design, placements, career goals, cost and location rather than the IIM label alone.

What is the IIM Sambalpur MBA cutoff?

For the 2026-28 MBA admission policy, the published minimum overall CAT percentiles are 90 for General, 80 EWS, 75 NC-OBC, 55 SC and 40 ST/DAP, subject to the policy's conditions and possible revision.

Does IIM Sambalpur have an MBA in Business Analytics?

Yes. IIM Sambalpur offers a two-year MBA in Business Analytics focused on analytics, data-informed decision-making and strategic management.

What is the highest package at IIM Sambalpur?

The latest official final placement report for the 2023-25 MBA batch records the highest offered CTC at ₹48.60 LPA.

What is the average package at IIM Sambalpur?

The latest official final placement report for the 2023-25 MBA batch records an average CTC of ₹15.65 LPA and median CTC of ₹15.03 LPA.

Is IIM Sambalpur MBA residential?

Yes. The regular MBA is described by the institute as a two-year full-time residential programme.
