IMI Bhubaneswar Placements FAQs

What is the average package at IMI Bhubaneswar in 2026?

The latest official portal reports ₹14.35 LPA average CTC for the 2024-26 batch. The previous 2023-25 final report recorded ₹12.74 LPA.

What is the highest package at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The latest official figure is ₹41.36 LPA for the 2024-26 batch. The 2023-25 report recorded ₹40.31 LPA.

Does IMI Bhubaneswar have 100% placement?

The institute reports 100% placement for the 2024-26 batch, and its recent placement reports also show 100% for the 2023-25 and 2022-24 batches.

What is the median salary at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The NIRF 2025 submission reports a ₹16.60 LPA median salary for graduates in 2023-24. The institute's 2023-25 final placement report separately reports ₹12.48 LPA median for that placement batch; students should check the reporting cohort before comparing.

What is the top quartile average package at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The current 2024-26 placement portal reports a ₹21.07 LPA top-quartile average. This is not the average package of the full batch.

Which companies recruit from IMI Bhubaneswar?

The 2023-25 final report highlights ICICI Bank, Infosys, PwC, Kotak Mahindra Bank, SBI Life, HDFC Bank, Capgemini, IDBI Bank and Deloitte, among 130+ recruiters.

Is IMI Bhubaneswar placement data trustworthy?

The strongest approach is to compare the institute's placement report with its NIRF submission. Both are useful but use different definitions: institute reports commonly highlight CTC and placement statistics, while NIRF reports graduating students, placed students and median salary.

Does IMI Bhubaneswar provide good placements for freshers?

The PGDM accepts fresh graduates and the latest published placement statistics report 100% placement for the 2024-26 batch. Freshers should still compare role mix, median salary, location and recruiter quality instead of using only the highest package.
