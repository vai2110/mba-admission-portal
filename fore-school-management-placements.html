These figures cover the combined placement process for PGDM, PGDM(IB), PGDM(FM) and PGDM(BDA), rather than one individual programme.

How large was the placed batch?

The final placement report for 2023-25 covers 466 students across the four main PGDM programmes. FORE reported 138 corporate partners, including 45 new recruiters.

The reported recruiter mix matters when evaluating the college because the placement pool is shared across the programmes.

What salary range did FORE report?

Salary metricReported figureHow to use it
Highest CTC₹29 LPATop offer, not typical salary
Average CTC₹16.4 LPAMean across the reported placement pool
Median CTC₹15.2 LPAMiddle value of the placement distribution

For ROI decisions, median CTC is often more useful than the highest package because it gives a better indication of the middle of the placed cohort.

Which companies recruit from FORE?

The official placement material lists recruiters including Bain & Company, JPMorgan Chase, KPMG, PwC, EY, Deloitte USI, Genpact, Gartner, Flipkart, MakeMyTrip, Mercedes-Benz, ICICI Securities, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, Asian Paints, ITC, Godrej Properties, Marsh McLennan, Acuity Knowledge Partners and others.

The institute's placement team page also lists the 2024-25 recruiter universe, so students should distinguish between companies participating in a year and companies recruiting every year.

What happens during FORE summer internships?

FORE describes the Summer Internship Programme as part of the course curriculum. The internship generally runs for 8 weeks, beginning around the second week of April and ending around the second week of June.

  • Students work with industry organisations.
  • The internship can be used to evaluate an industry segment and build a case for a PPO.
  • Students submit an internship report with company feedback.
  • Students are evaluated through presentations of their internship projects.

What do FORE placement numbers actually tell a student?

The ₹16.4 lakh average and ₹15.2 lakh median should be read together. The highest ₹29 lakh package represents the top offer, while the median shows the middle of the placement distribution.

Good for comparison

  • Median CTC
  • Average CTC
  • Sector mix
  • Recruiter diversity

Do not use alone

  • Highest package
  • One recruiter name
  • One exceptional offer

For programme choice, compare these placement outcomes with the programme you intend to join, its fee and the career function you want after graduation.
