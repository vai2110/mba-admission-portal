How does the FORE placement process work?

  1. Corporate outreach: the Placement Office identifies and invites organisations for summer and final placements.
  2. Pre-Placement Talks: recruiters explain the organisation, culture, roles and expectations.
  3. Student evaluation: recruiters conduct their stated assessment and selection process.
  4. Final selection: selected students receive formal employment offers.

FORE's Career Division also provides career counselling by faculty and trained professionals across functional areas.

What about summer internships at FORE?

The Summer Internship Program is part of the PGDM curriculum and normally runs for 8 weeks, from around the second week of April to the second week of June. Students submit an internship report and company feedback is used in evaluation.

The 2025-26 brochure describes SIP exposure across consulting, FMCG, BFSI, IT, e-commerce and manufacturing. The institute also describes internships as a potential route to pre-placement opportunities.

What do FORE students say about placement preparation?

FORE's official Placement Speaks section includes experiences from PGDM, PGDM(IB) and PGDM(BDA) students. Students have discussed interview preparation, corporate interaction, internships and roles such as Business Analyst-Intern and Creative Intern.
