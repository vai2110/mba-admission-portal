TSM PGDM

What Is TSM PGDM?

TSM's PGDM is its flagship two-year full-time residential management programme with 180 students across six trimesters. The programme combines core management subjects with live projects, analytics labs, mentorship, business simulations, case studies and a career-focused internship.

TSM's PGDM is an autonomous diploma programme approved by AICTE, and TSM states that its equivalence with an MBA is certified by the Association of Indian Universities. That distinction matters: students receive a PGDM qualification rather than a university-affiliated MBA degree, but the programme is designed as a full postgraduate management qualification.

TSM PGDM Curriculum and Learning Model

The first year builds business fundamentals before students move into specialisation and advanced application. TSM highlights core business disciplines, analytics, live business problems and case-based learning. The programme includes an 8–10 week industry internship and culminates in capstone work, business simulation, electives and placements.

Students choosing a specialisation should map the subjects to the roles they want after graduation. For example, a finance aspirant should examine whether investment analysis and financial modelling match their target roles, while an operations aspirant should evaluate supply chain and project-management exposure.

Internship and Experiential Learning

TSM describes an 8–10 week summer internship with real-world project work, corporate mentorship, an internship report and presentation. The programme also includes live projects, industry visits, business simulations and case studies. These components are particularly important because management education value is not determined only by classroom subjects; the quality of applied exposure can affect interview readiness and role fit.

PGDM Admission 2026

TSM requires a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks and a valid CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT or ATMA score for PGDM. The selection process includes group discussion, personal interview and academic record evaluation. TSM's current programme information states that applications open in September and final admissions run through May.

PGDM Career Outcomes

TSM's placement support includes resume building, interview coaching and recruiter engagement. Its published role examples include management trainee, business analyst, marketing executive, financial analyst and HR associate. Students should use the latest placement report to assess compensation, role mix and recruiter participation before making a final ROI decision.

Is TSM PGDM residential?

Yes. TSM describes the full-time PGDM as a residential programme and its fee structure includes accommodation and mess.

What is the TSM PGDM duration?

The PGDM is a two-year programme divided into six trimesters.
