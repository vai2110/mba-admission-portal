TSM PGDM: Programme Structure and Qualification

TSM's PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential programme with an intake of 180 students across six trimesters. It is the institute's flagship management programme and is designed around core management knowledge, analytics, functional specialisation and experiential learning.

The PGDM is an autonomous diploma rather than a university-affiliated MBA degree. TSM states that its PGDM equivalence with MBA is certified by the Association of Indian Universities. This distinction matters when students compare qualification type, curriculum autonomy and university affiliation.

TSM PGDM Curriculum: From Data Analysis to Capstone

The published PGDM course structure shows a clear progression. The first year covers organisational behaviour, managerial economics, data analysis, accounting, marketing, operations, corporate finance, strategic management, HR, optimisation and applied business analytics. The later trimesters shift toward electives, internship, simulation, capstone and industrial project work.

TSM PGDM Specialisations: What Each Track Covers

The current PGDM page lists Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics. TSM provides subject examples that help students understand what those labels mean in practice.

MarketingBrand Management, Digital Marketing and Sales Management.
FinanceInvestment Analysis, Risk Management and Financial Modeling.
HRTalent Management, Performance Management and Leadership.
OperationsQuality Management, Project Management and Logistics.
Business AnalyticsData-driven management and analytics-oriented business decision-making.

Do not choose a specialisation only from its name. Match the subjects with the roles you want. Finance candidates should examine modelling, valuation and analytical exposure; analytics candidates should examine data, visualisation and business problem-solving evidence; marketing candidates should examine digital, customer and brand applications.

Internship, International Exposure and Other PGDM-Only Components

TSM's official programme comparison identifies several components that distinguish PGDM from MBA: a mandatory international study tour, mandatory domestic outbound training, a mandatory capstone simulation and a longer summer internship. The current PGDM programme page describes an 8–10 week industry internship with real-world project work, corporate mentorship, report and presentation; TSM's PGDM-vs-MBA comparison separately states a three-month SIP. Because the official pages use different wording, students should verify the exact duration for their admission batch before relying on one number.

TSM PGDM Admission 2026

TSM currently requires a recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks and a valid CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT or ATMA score. Selection information includes group discussion and personal interview, alongside academic profile evaluation.

TSM's admissions information indicates that applications open around September and final admissions continue through May. Applicants should use the live admission notice for exact deadlines because the cycle can change.

PGDM Placements and Career Roles

TSM's placement ecosystem includes resume preparation, interview training, recruiter engagement, career mapping and alumni mentorship. The institute cites role examples such as management trainee, business analyst, marketing executive, financial analyst and HR associate. The actual outcome depends on student profile, specialisation, recruiter demand and the placement cycle.

Use the latest official placement record to evaluate salary distribution, role quality and recruiter mix. Do not treat the highest CTC as the expected salary for every PGDM student.

TSM PGDM FAQs

What is TSM PGDM intake?

The current official PGDM page lists an intake of 180 students.

What is the duration of TSM PGDM?

It is a two-year full-time programme organised across six trimesters.

Is TSM PGDM residential?

Yes. TSM describes the PGDM as a full-time residential programme and its published fee includes accommodation and mess.

What are TSM PGDM specialisations?

Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics are listed on the current programme page.

Is TSM PGDM equivalent to MBA?

TSM states that AIU has certified equivalence with MBA. Structurally, PGDM is an autonomous diploma whereas MBA is a university-affiliated degree.

What is the TSM PGDM fee?

The latest published TSM fee table shows ₹15.70 lakh including academic fee, accommodation/mess and refundable caution deposit.
