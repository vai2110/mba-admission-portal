What Is B.Tech ECE at SRMIST?

Electronics and Communication Engineering is the engineering choice for students interested in electronics, communication systems, embedded technology and hardware-software interfaces. It is a different career starting point from CSE, even though coding can also be part of an ECE career.

What Is the Eligibility?

SRMIST's regular B.Tech eligibility requires at least 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory and one prescribed third subject. The published age condition is 16 years 6 months on 31 July of the relevant year.

How Do You Get Admission?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route. The 2026 process had three application phases. Counselling then uses rank, programme/campus preference and seat availability.

What Is the B.Tech ECE Fee?

Fees are programme- and campus-specific. A concrete official SRMIST reference is ₹4,25,000 annual fee for B.Tech CSE with Specialization in Big Data Analytics at Kattankulathur. This is not an ECE fee and should not be copied to ECE.

What Is the SRMIST B.Tech ECE Cutoff?

SRMIST does not publish a consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used here. An internet estimate should not be labelled the official ECE cutoff.

What should you use?

Use SRMJEEE rank and the programme/campus options actually available during counselling.

What Do You Study in ECE?

ECE builds knowledge around electronics, digital systems, signals, communication and related embedded applications. Students should check the exact SRMIST curriculum for the current programme before deciding.

Who Should Choose ECE?

ECE suits students who genuinely like electronics and communication and are willing to study both hardware and software concepts. It can also support software-oriented careers, but students choosing it only for coding should compare it carefully with CSE and IT.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum and average salary ₹10.5 LPA. NIRF 2026 engineering data reports a ₹6.32 lakh median salary for the reported four-year UG engineering population in 2024-25.

These are not ECE-only statistics.

FAQs Students Ask

Does B.Tech ECE use SRMJEEE?

Yes. SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route.

What is the regular B.Tech eligibility?

SRMIST states 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory.

Does SRMIST publish an official ECE closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Can ECE students get software jobs?

Yes, depending on skills, electives and the role requirements. ECE also provides routes into electronics, embedded and communication careers.

Can I use the CSE fee as the ECE fee?

No. The ₹4.25 lakh annual figure is for a specific CSE with Big Data Analytics listing at Kattankulathur.
