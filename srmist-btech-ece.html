B.Tech ECE at SRMIST

Electronics and Communication Engineering covers electronics, communication systems and related embedded and technology applications. SRMIST includes ECE among its B.Tech engineering offerings.

Eligibility

Applicants need the prescribed Class 12 PCM qualification and SRMIST's applicable B.Tech aggregate requirement.

Admission Through SRMJEEE

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal SRMIST B.Tech entrance route. Counselling follows the entrance process.

Fees

B.Tech fees are programme- and campus-specific. The exact ECE fee should be taken from the current SRMIST programme listing.

ECE Cutoff

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was not found in the official sources used for this page. Unofficial closing ranks are therefore not presented as official cutoffs.

Placements

Official university-level 2023 placement figures reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, ₹1 crore per annum maximum salary and ₹10.5 LPA average salary.

FAQs

Yes, SRMJEEE is the principal SRMIST UG engineering route.
