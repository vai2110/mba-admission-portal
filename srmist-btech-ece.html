B.Tech ECE at SRMIST

Electronics and Communication Engineering is aimed at students interested in electronics, communication systems, embedded technology and related hardware-software applications.

Eligibility

SRMIST prescribes a Class 12 PCM background and the required aggregate for B.Tech admission. Match the current eligibility to the exact programme entry.

How Does SRMIST B.Tech ECE Admission Work?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal engineering entrance route. Counselling uses rank, programme/campus preferences and seat availability.

Fees

The ECE fee is programme- and campus-specific. Do not use the ₹4.25 lakh annual fee listed for Kattankulathur CSE with Big Data Analytics as an ECE fee.

SRMIST B.Tech ECE Cutoff

No consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was found in the sources used for this page. Unofficial rank lists are not shown as official cutoffs.

What Do You Study in ECE?

Core areas typically include electronic circuits, communication, digital systems, signals and embedded applications. The exact subjects should be checked in the current SRMIST curriculum.

B.Tech ECE FAQs

Does SRMIST publish an official ECE closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Is ECE suitable for students who also like coding?

Yes. ECE includes electronics and communication foundations and can also lead to embedded, semiconductor, software and technology roles depending on skills and electives.

Is SRMJEEE used for ECE?

SRMJEEE is the principal SRMIST UG engineering admission route.
