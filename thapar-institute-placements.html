Thapar Placement 2025: What the Headline Says

TIET's homepage currently highlights a highest package of ₹1.23 crore. This is a headline outcome and should not be interpreted as the typical salary received by the batch.

How students should read it

Use highest package to understand the ceiling. Use average and median, where officially available, to understand typical outcomes. For engineering, also compare branch and role distribution.

Engineering Placement Analysis

TIET's engineering placement market covers software, analytics, consulting, core engineering, electronics, manufacturing and other technology roles. Outcomes can differ substantially by branch and student profile.

LMTSM MBA Placements

MBA placement data should be evaluated separately from engineering. Students should use the latest LM Thapar School of Management placement report for recruiter mix, average/median salary, functions and placement participation.

Typical Career Directions to Evaluate

Software & Data

Development, analytics, data engineering and technology roles.

Consulting / Business

Business analysis, consulting, product and management-oriented roles.

How to Evaluate Placement ROI

Calculate ROI using the complete programme cost, realistic salary outcome, loan interest if applicable and career growth. A ₹1.23 crore headline offer cannot be used as a representative benchmark for every TIET student.

Good comparison

  • Average vs median
  • Branch-wise outcomes
  • Role quality
  • Placement participation

Avoid

  • Highest package alone
  • Old placement numbers
  • Unverified social-media claims
  • Mixing MBA and engineering data

Student Discussion: What Placement Data Doesn't Tell You

Students often care about whether the role is software or core, location, work-life balance, career growth, batch competition and whether an offer is actually aligned with their target career. These are discussion themes, not official placement claims.

Thapar Placements FAQs

What is Thapar's highest package?

TIET's current homepage highlights a ₹1.23 crore highest package figure.

Is the highest package the average salary?

No. Highest package and average/median salary are different measures.

Should engineering and MBA placement data be combined?

No. They should be analysed separately because recruiter pools, roles and compensation structures differ.
