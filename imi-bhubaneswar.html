What is IMI Bhubaneswar known for?

Quick answer:

IMI Bhubaneswar is a management institute offering a two-year, AICTE-approved PGDM. Its 2026 admission portal lists an intake of 180 students and accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT scores as part of a holistic selection process.

The institute is accredited by NBA, AMBA and NAAC. It is part of the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group and is located at Gothapatna, Bhubaneswar.

IMI Bhubaneswar Admission 2026

The 2026-28 PGDM cycle accepted CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT 2026 or GMAT scores. Applicants needed a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks; candidates graduating in 2026 could also apply.

Selection is holistic: past academics, work experience where applicable, test score, a video assessment and personal interview are considered. The institute explicitly states that there is no fixed cut-off.

IMI Bhubaneswar Fees 2026

The published 2026-28 PGDM programme fee is ₹14.98 lakh. This includes ₹12.08 lakh tuition, learning material, student activities and welfare, library resources, IT infrastructure, alumni membership and a ₹15,000 security deposit.

Hostel and mess are separate. For 2026-27, hostel is ₹1.45 lakh for boys' twin AC common-bathroom rooms and ₹1.45–1.60 lakh for girls depending on bathroom type; mess is ₹80,000.

IMI Bhubaneswar Campus and Hostel

The institute is located at IDCO Plot No. 1, Gothapatna, Bhubaneswar. The PGDM is residential, with hostel options and separate mess charges. Students should compare the total two-year cost rather than tuition alone.

IMI Bhubaneswar FAQs

What is IMI Bhubaneswar famous for?

IMI Bhubaneswar is primarily known for its two-year PGDM and management-focused academic environment. Its current PGDM intake is 180 students, and the programme is accredited by NBA, AMBA and NAAC.

Is IMI Bhubaneswar good for an MBA or PGDM?

It can be a relevant option for students targeting management careers, particularly when the fee, location and placement outcomes fit their goals. The institute's latest portal reports 100% placement, ₹14.35 LPA average and ₹41.36 LPA highest CTC for the 2024-26 batch.

What is the NIRF ranking of IMI Bhubaneswar?

IMI Bhubaneswar was ranked #94 in NIRF Management 2025. Its 2024 NIRF rank was #61, so students should look at both the latest rank and the trend rather than quoting an older rank as current.

What is the average package at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The latest official admission portal reports ₹14.35 LPA average CTC for the 2024-26 batch. The previous 2023-25 final placement report reported ₹12.74 LPA average.

What is the highest package at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The latest official portal reports ₹41.36 LPA for the 2024-26 batch. The 2023-25 final placement report reported ₹40.31 LPA.

Does IMI Bhubaneswar have 100% placement?

The institute's current placement statistics report 100% placed for the 2024-26 batch. The 2023-25 final placement report also states 100% placements.

Which exams are accepted by IMI Bhubaneswar?

For the 2026-28 PGDM cycle, the institute accepted CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT 2026 and GMAT within the specified validity window.

Does IMI Bhubaneswar have a fixed CAT cutoff?

The institute's own FAQ says there is no fixed cut-off. Shortlisting considers test scores along with academics, work experience and other profile factors, followed by video assessment and interview.
