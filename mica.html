MICA Ahmedabad: What Students Should Know

MICA, Ahmedabad is a specialist management institute focused on strategic marketing, communication and applied creativity. Its School of Management offers the flagship PGDM-C and PGDM programmes, while the institute also runs FPM and certificate programmes under its two-school structure.

180Approved annual intake for PGDM-C
60Approved annual intake for PGDM
15:1Student-to-faculty ratio stated by MICA
What makes MICA different from a conventional MBA college?

MICA combines management education with cultural intelligence, strategic marketing, communication, creativity and increasingly data, technology and AI. That makes it particularly relevant for students targeting brand, marketing, communication, media, consumer insights and newer analytical management roles.

MICA Admission: Eligibility, MICAT and Selection Process

For the flagship PGDM-C and PGDM programmes, the admission route combines a management entrance score with MICAT and a later assessment stage. MICA's official process lists CAT, XAT or GMAT scores, MICAT and Group Exercise/Personal Interview as key components.

How does MICA selection work?

StageWhat it means for applicants
ApplicationApply to MICA and select the relevant programme/school.
CAT/XAT/GMATSubmit a valid score used in the School of Management evaluation.
MICATComputer-based assessment covering psychometric, descriptive and aptitude components.
GE & PIShortlisted candidates go through Group Exercise and Personal Interview.
Final meritMICA uses a holistic evaluation to determine the final selection.
What is MICAT?

MICAT is MICA's entrance assessment designed to evaluate psychometric attributes, creative and descriptive ability, verbal ability, quantitative ability, data interpretation, divergent and convergent thinking and general awareness, depending on the applicable programme and stage.

MICA Fees and Scholarships

MICA's official programme information lists the complete two-year residential PGDM-C and PGDM programme fee at ₹28 lakh, with optional international immersion carrying separate charges.

Does MICA offer scholarships?

Yes. MICA states that merit-cum-means scholarships are available for eligible programmes. Its published scholarship criteria include family income, academic performance and attendance requirements. Students should evaluate the applicable scholarship rules separately from the headline programme fee.

Who Should Choose MICA?

Strong fit

  • Students who want a specialised career in marketing, brand, communication or consumer strategy.
  • Applicants who enjoy combining creativity with business and analytical thinking.
  • Candidates interested in media, entertainment, sports, digital, advertising or consumer-facing businesses.
  • Analytical students who want to explore AI, data and technology through the PGDM pathway.

Think carefully if

  • You want a traditional generalist MBA with limited emphasis on marketing and communication.
  • Your career target is unrelated to MICA's core industry strengths.
  • You are comparing colleges only on highest package rather than role quality and career fit.
  • The residential programme cost is materially above your comfortable budget even after financial aid.

MICA FAQs Students Actually Search

What is MICA famous for?

MICA is especially known for strategic marketing, communication, brand management, media and applied creativity. Its newer PGDM positioning also brings data, AI and technology into management education.

Is MICA an MBA college?

MICA's flagship School of Management programmes are PGDM-C and PGDM, rather than a conventional MBA degree. They are AICTE-approved postgraduate management programmes.

What is the MICA PGDM fee?

MICA currently publishes ₹28 lakh as the complete two-year residential fee for the PGDM and PGDM-C programmes, with optional international immersion charged separately.

Is MICAT compulsory for MICA PGDM admission?

MICAT is a core part of the School of Management selection process for PGDM-C and PGDM applicants, alongside CAT, XAT or GMAT and the subsequent assessment stages.

What is the highest package at MICA?

MICA's current admissions page reports a highest CTC of ₹40.91 LPA for its 2025 placement snapshot. Placement figures should always be read with the corresponding batch and report context.

Does MICA offer PGDM without a marketing focus?

MICA's PGDM has a more analytical and technology-oriented positioning, with focus areas including business analytics and consulting, data science and AI, BFSI and fintech, and technology-driven business strategy.
