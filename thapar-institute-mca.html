TIET includes MCA among its postgraduate programme offerings. The programme is intended for graduates seeking advanced computer applications and software-oriented education.

MCA Career Scope

MCA graduates can target software development, testing, database, cloud, analytics, application support and business-technology roles. Practical skills in programming, SQL, data structures, cloud and projects are important for placement readiness.

Student Discussion: MCA vs MTech

The better choice depends on background and career target. MCA is generally more application/software oriented, while MTech is usually more specialised engineering/technical education. Compare the exact curriculum and eligibility before choosing.
