# Top 100 MBA Colleges in India Hub

Published hub: `top-mba-colleges-in-india.html`

## Source
NIRF 2025 Management ranking, Ministry of Education, Government of India:
https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/ManagementRanking.html

## Data layer
`mba-colleges-data.json` contains the corrected college/campus mapping used by the hub. Tied NIRF ranks are retained as separate institutions (rank 46 and rank 57).
