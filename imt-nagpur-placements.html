IMT Nagpur Placements: What the Official Data Says

IMT Nagpur's 2025-27 admissions FAQ reports placement highlights for the 2022-24 batch. The highest pay package was ₹36.33 LPA, while the Top-10 and Top-20 average packages were ₹17.16 LPA and ₹14.47 LPA respectively.

These figures are historical batch outcomes. They should not be presented as guaranteed 2026 packages. The institute also states that the placement process is standard for students of all programmes.

What should students compare besides the highest package?

Look at median/average outcomes where available, role quality, recruiter mix, internship conversion, specialization relevance and the share of students placed. A single highest package can be an outlier.

IMT Nagpur Recruiters & Job Roles

IMT Nagpur's published material and alumni ecosystem show careers spanning BFSI, FMCG, manufacturing, consulting, automobile, IT/ITES and other sectors. Alumni records include organizations such as Accenture, BNP Paribas, BMO Financial Group, Berger Paints, Bridgestone and many others.

Common role families

Analyst, financial operations, platform solutions, project management, sales, marketing, consulting, analytics and general management roles can emerge across batches.

What changes the outcome?

Specialization, internship performance, academic profile, interview readiness, role preference and market conditions can materially affect individual placement outcomes.

Summer Internship & Pre-Placement Opportunities

IMT Nagpur describes the Summer Internship Programme as an important part of the first-year experience. Its 2025-27 FAQ notes that the 2022-24 batch received 8 pre-placement offers from summer internships.

Students should therefore treat the internship search as part of the placement journey rather than as a separate activity.

Programme-wise Placement View

IMT Nagpur Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at IMT Nagpur?

The official 2025-27 FAQ reports ₹36.33 LPA as the highest pay package for the 2022-24 batch. This is a historical batch figure, not a guaranteed package for the 2026 placement cycle.

What are the Top-10 and Top-20 average packages?

For the 2022-24 batch, the official FAQ reports a Top-10 average of ₹17.16 LPA and a Top-20 average of ₹14.47 LPA. These figures describe the highest-performing groups and are not the average salary of the entire batch.

Does IMT Nagpur have one placement process for all PGDM programmes?

Yes. The institute says the placement process is standard for students of all programmes. Institute-wide placement figures should therefore not automatically be presented as separate package figures for Core PGDM, Marketing or Financial Management.

What sectors and roles can students target?

Published material and the alumni ecosystem indicate opportunities across BFSI, FMCG, manufacturing, consulting, automobile, IT/ITES and other sectors. Roles can include analytics, finance, sales, marketing, consulting, operations, project management and general management depending on the recruiting cycle and student profile.

Do summer internships lead to pre-placement offers?

They can. IMT Nagpur’s 2025-27 FAQ says students from the 2022-24 batch received 8 pre-placement offers from summer internships. An internship is an important part of the placement journey, but an offer is not guaranteed.

Should I judge placements only by the highest package?

No. Compare average/median figures where available, role quality, recruiter mix, internship outcomes, specialization relevance and placement coverage. Different official documents cover different batches, so always read the batch year with each salary number.
