IMT Nagpur Placements 2024-26: latest official figures

Latest official reference:

IMT Nagpur's current admissions page reports a ₹24 LPA highest package and ₹12 LPA average package for Batch 2024-26, with the average marked ongoing. It also reports an average PPO package of ₹16 LPA.

Because the average is marked ongoing, students should treat it as a current published reference rather than a final audited batch statistic.

What roles can IMT Nagpur students target?

IMT Nagpur's programme mix supports career paths across finance, BFSI, marketing, sales, analytics, operations, HR, consulting and general management. Actual recruiter demand changes every placement season.

Summer Internship and PPOs

The institute's FAQ says the Summer Internship Programme is conducted after the first year for about eight weeks. It also states that the 2024-26 students received 12 pre-placement offers from summer internships.

For a student comparing B-schools, internship conversion is useful because it shows whether the summer internship pipeline can translate into full-time opportunities.

Does IMT Nagpur publish separate placement packages for each PGDM?

The institute states that the placement process is standard for all three PGDM programmes. Therefore, the headline ₹24 LPA/₹12 LPA figures should not be labelled as separate Core, Marketing or Financial Management packages unless a specific official programme-wise placement report provides that breakdown.

Students should instead evaluate role and recruiter relevance to their chosen specialization.
