TAPMI's official 2025 placement reports provide separate outcomes for the MBA Core, MBA-BKFS, MBA-HRM and other programmes. The figures below are the published 2025 programme results and should be read separately from the 2025-26 placement snapshot in the 2026 prospectus, which was still marked “in progress” when published.

Best way to read TAPMI placement data:

Start with the average CTC and role mix for your programme. Then look at the highest CTC as an upside indicator. Do not treat the highest package as the salary you should expect.

TAPMI Placement Recruiters and Job Profiles

The recruiter mix spans BFSI, consulting, IT/analytics, FMCG, retail, professional services and other sectors. Examples in the official reports include Deloitte, EY, JPMorgan Chase & Co., Wells Fargo, HSBC, Infosys, Cognizant, PwC, Tata Capital, CRISIL, CARE Ratings, Arcesium, KPMG, JLL, Godrej & Boyce and others.

MBA Core roles

The MBA Core report shows placement activity across IT & analytics, consulting, BFSI, FMCG & retail and other sectors. This makes the programme comparatively flexible for students who are not yet committed to one function.

MBA-BKFS roles

Published job profiles include Financial Analyst, Credit Analyst, Financial Operations, Risk Advisory, Global Finance & Business Management, Product Analyst, Relationship Manager, Wealth & Investment Management, Management Trainee and related roles.

MBA-HRM roles

Published HRM profiles include HR Generalist, HR Specialist, HR Advisory, HR consulting, Talent Acquisition, People Success, Knowledge Management and HR management-trainee roles.

What TAPMI Placement Numbers Do Not Tell You

Useful signals

  • Average CTC gives a better centre point than the highest package.
  • Programme-wise outcomes reveal the difference between Core, BKFS and HRM.
  • Sector and job-profile data helps you judge career fit.
  • PPO data can indicate internship-to-job conversion opportunities.

Do not assume

  • ₹32.77 LPA is a typical MBA salary.
  • 100% placement means every student received the role they wanted.
  • One year's recruiter list will repeat unchanged.
  • A higher average automatically makes a programme better for your target career.

Freshers vs experienced candidates

The 2025 reports also show programme-level differences. MBA Core reported average CTC of ₹14.05 LPA for freshers and ₹13.94 LPA for experienced students; BKFS reported ₹13.84 LPA and ₹15.53 LPA respectively; HRM reported ₹13.29 LPA and ₹12.32 LPA respectively. These are historical batch outcomes, not guarantees for the next batch.

TAPMI Placements FAQs

What is the average package of TAPMI MBA?

For the 2025 reports, MBA Core average CTC was ₹13.99 LPA, MBA-BKFS was ₹14.73 LPA and MBA-HRM was ₹12.86 LPA.

What is the highest package at TAPMI?

The 2025 MBA Core report recorded the highest CTC of ₹32.77 LPA among the three programmes compared on this page.

Does TAPMI have 100% placement?

The 2025 reports recorded 100% placement among interested students for MBA Core, MBA-BKFS and MBA-HRM. This should not be interpreted as a guarantee of a preferred role or salary.

Which TAPMI programme has the best placement?

It depends on the career you want. BKFS had the highest average CTC among Core, BKFS and HRM in 2025, while Core had the highest maximum CTC. HRM is designed for specialised HR careers.

Which companies recruit from TAPMI?

Official reports list recruiters including Deloitte, EY, JPMorgan Chase & Co., Wells Fargo, HSBC, Infosys, Cognizant, PwC, Tata Capital, CRISIL, KPMG and many others.
