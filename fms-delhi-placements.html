FMS Delhi Placement Overview

The latest detailed placement report available from FMS covers the 2024-26 MBA batch. The placement process had two stages: lateral placements in December 2025 for students with more than 18 months of work experience before the MBA, followed by final placements for the remaining students in January 2026.

The report records 307 students in the batch, 304 students appearing for placements, 287 students placed and 3 students taking a placement holiday. It also records 119 participating companies and 103 companies that rolled out offers.

What this page uses: the official FMS 2024-26 final placement report is the primary source for the placement figures. FMS also describes its placement system on the official website as covering summer internships, lateral placements and final placements across consulting, IT/product/operations/analytics, finance, general management/strategy and sales & marketing.

Average CTC

Average CTC rose from ₹29.20 LPA in 2024 to ₹32.27 LPA in 2026. The report therefore shows a higher mean compensation level in 2026 than in the previous two years.

Median CTC

Median CTC moved from ₹29.00 LPA in 2024 to ₹30.00 LPA in 2025, then stood at ₹29.59 LPA in 2026. The median did not increase every year in the same direction as the average.

What do the FMS Delhi placement numbers mean for students?

1. Average and median tell different stories

The 2026 average CTC of ₹32.27 LPA is above the median of ₹29.59 LPA. This gap means the mean is being pulled upward by higher salary outcomes; students should not interpret the average as the salary every student receives.

2. The highest CTC is an upper-end outcome

The ₹1.10 crore highest CTC is useful as a record of the top reported outcome, but it is not a measure of the typical placement result. The median and salary-band distribution provide more context for the broader batch.

3. Role choice matters

The report shows recruitment across consulting, finance, marketing, product/IT/analytics and general management. A student's placement outcome therefore depends not only on the institute-wide salary headline but also on the function, role and company selected during the process.

4. Work experience is not a prerequisite for the batch

Freshers made up 54% of the 2024-26 batch, while the batch profile reported an average work experience of 19.5 months. This shows that the placement cohort included both freshers and experienced candidates.

5. Recruiter participation has expanded

The report highlights a 32% increase in recruiters over the previous year, along with increased finance offers and growth in Product/IT/Analytics/Operations offers. These are report-level changes, not a guarantee of the same mix in the next batch.

Data consistency note: The report's placement-overview narrative says 99 companies extended offers, while the placement-statistics panel states 103 companies rolled out offers. This page uses the statistics-panel figure of 103 for the headline metric and preserves the distinction rather than silently reconciling the two figures.

FMS Delhi Placements FAQs

What is the FMS Delhi average package in 2026?

The official FMS final placement report for the 2024-26 MBA batch reports an average CTC of ₹32.27 LPA. This is the mean compensation figure reported for the placement outcomes covered by the report. Students should read this figure along with the median CTC of ₹29.59 LPA and the salary-band distribution because an average can be affected by higher-value offers. The report also records a top-100 average of ₹45.19 LPA, which represents a separate subset and should not be treated as the average for the entire batch.

What is the FMS Delhi median package in 2026?

The official 2024-26 placement report reports a median CTC of ₹29.59 LPA. Median means the middle point of the reported salary distribution, so it provides a different view from the ₹32.27 LPA average. In the year-wise comparison, the median was ₹29.00 LPA in 2024, ₹30.00 LPA in 2025 and ₹29.59 LPA in 2026. This shows that the median did not move in exactly the same direction as the average over these three reported placement years.

What is the highest package at FMS Delhi?

The official FMS final placement report for the 2024-26 batch reports the highest CTC as ₹1.10 crore. This is an individual upper-end placement outcome and should not be interpreted as the expected salary for the batch as a whole. For a broader picture, the same report gives an average CTC of ₹32.27 LPA, a median of ₹29.59 LPA and a top-100 average of ₹45.19 LPA. Looking at these figures together gives more context than using the highest package alone.

How many students were placed at FMS Delhi?

The 2024-26 final placement report lists 307 students in the batch, of whom 304 appeared for placements and 287 were placed. The report also records 3 students taking a placement holiday. These figures describe the placement participation reported by FMS for this batch. The report separately lists 119 participating companies and 103 companies that rolled out offers in its placement-statistics panel.

What percentage of FMS Delhi students received offers above ₹25 LPA?

The official report states that 74.9% of the batch received offers above ₹25 LPA. The report also provides a salary-band table showing 83 students in the ₹25–29.9 LPA band and 132 students in the above-₹30 LPA band, for 215 students across those two bands. Because the report does not explicitly state that the salary-band table and the 74.9% headline use the same denominator, these figures should be read separately rather than combined into a new percentage.

Which domains recruit from FMS Delhi?

The 2024-26 report covers recruitment across consulting, product/IT/operations/analytics, marketing, finance and general management. The report lists 45 consulting offers, 51 product/IT/analytics offers, 39 marketing offers, 49 finance offers and 12 general-management offers, along with separate PPO counts. It also describes roles such as strategy analytics, product management, product design, operations, growth and AI-focused roles in the product/IT/analytics area, while finance roles included corporate finance, wholesale credit risk, front-end investment banking and equity research.

Does FMS Delhi have placements for freshers?

Yes, the 2024-26 batch profile includes a substantial fresher group. 54% of the batch were freshers, while the remaining students were distributed across different work-experience bands. The reported average work experience for the batch was 19.5 months. This means the placement cohort included both candidates entering the MBA without prior full-time work experience and candidates with prior professional experience. The placement report, however, does not provide a separate overall average CTC for freshers in a tabular form, so this page does not infer one.

Should students compare FMS using average CTC or median CTC?

Both figures are useful because they describe different aspects of the placement distribution. Average CTC is the mean of the reported compensation outcomes, while median CTC represents the midpoint of the distribution. For FMS's 2024-26 batch, the average was ₹32.27 LPA and the median was ₹29.59 LPA. Students can also look at the salary bands, which show 37 students below ₹20 LPA, 35 between ₹20–24.9 LPA, 83 between ₹25–29.9 LPA and 132 above ₹30 LPA. This combination gives more context than relying on one headline number.

Is the ₹1.1 crore highest package the salary most FMS students receive?

No. The ₹1.10 crore figure is the highest reported individual CTC, so it represents the upper end of the outcomes rather than a typical salary. The official report gives a median CTC of ₹29.59 LPA and an average CTC of ₹32.27 LPA for the 2024-26 batch. It also shows the distribution of 287 placed students across four salary bands. These broader measures are more useful for understanding how compensation was distributed across the batch than the highest package alone.
