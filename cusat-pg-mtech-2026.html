AI & Data Science: For Students Wanting a Technical Specialisation

The M.Tech CSE with Data Science & Artificial Intelligence is materially different from a general computer science master's because the specialisation itself signals an advanced AI/data direction. Candidates should examine the department's curriculum, laboratory work and project topics before choosing it over a general CSE route.

Cyber Security: A Specialist Computing Route

CUSAT lists M.Tech CSE with specialisation in Cyber Security. Students considering it should evaluate whether they enjoy systems, networking, secure software, cryptography and security operations rather than choosing the specialisation only because cybersecurity is a growing field.

Business Analytics & Intelligence: Technology + Business

The M.Tech in Information Technology with Business Analytics and Intelligence is useful for candidates who want to combine technical computing with analytics and business decision-making. It can be compared with CUSAT's MBA for students deciding between a technical analytics career and a broader management route.

Simple decision rule

Choose the M.Tech analytics route if you want to remain technically close to data/IT. Choose MBA if your long-term target is broader business leadership, consulting, marketing, finance or general management.

M.Tech Admission: GATE Preferred, DAT for Non-GATE Candidates

The CUSAT admissions portal states that preference is given to candidates with a valid GATE score in the relevant subject. If eligible GATE candidates are unavailable, non-GATE candidates can be considered through a Department Admission Test (DAT) conducted by the department. citeturn2search2

Some PG programmes also have CUET-reserved seats, so candidates should read the exact programme notice rather than assuming a single route for every M.Tech.

M.Tech Fees: Do Not Use One Generic CUSAT PG Fee

CUSAT's 2026 fee structure contains separate rows for individual M.Tech programmes, executive and part-time formats and different admission categories. Fee varies by programme and category, so the official 2026 fee document should be used for the final calculation. citeturn2search11

Cost check before admission

Add tuition + establishment + library/lab + programme-specific charges + hostel/living expenses. GATE scholarship/stipend possibilities, if applicable, should be verified separately rather than assumed.

CUSAT M.Tech FAQs

Does CUSAT M.Tech accept GATE?

Yes. The admissions information states that preference is given to valid GATE scores in the relevant subject, with DAT used for non-GATE candidates where applicable.

Which CUSAT M.Tech is best for AI?

The M.Tech CSE specialisation in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence is the most directly named AI/data route in the 2026 prospectus.

Does CUSAT have M.Tech Cyber Security?

Yes. The 2026 prospectus lists M.Tech CSE with specialisation in Cyber Security.
