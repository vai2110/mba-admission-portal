B.Tech Information Technology at SRMIST

IT is the engineering pathway for students interested in software, information systems and technology applications. This page focuses on IT rather than repeating the wider SRMIST course catalogue.

Eligibility

Applicants need the Class 12 PCM background and aggregate prescribed by SRMIST for the selected B.Tech programme.

How Does SRMIST B.Tech IT Admission Work?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal B.Tech entrance route. Counselling allotment depends on rank, programme/campus preference and seat availability.

Fees

SRMIST fees are programme- and campus-specific. An IT fee should be taken from the exact current programme entry; the ₹4.25 lakh annual figure used elsewhere on this site belongs specifically to B.Tech CSE with Big Data Analytics at Kattankulathur.

SRMIST B.Tech IT Cutoff

SRMIST does not provide a consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used here. No unofficial closing rank is labelled as official.

What Do You Study in IT?

The IT pathway generally centres on programming, databases, software development, systems and information technology applications. The current SRMIST curriculum should be used for the exact semester-wise subjects.

Placements

See the SRMIST placements page for consolidated salary and engineering placement statistics.

B.Tech IT FAQs

Is B.Tech IT the same as CSE at SRMIST?

No. They overlap in computing fundamentals but are separate programme choices. Compare the exact curriculum and available campus before choosing.

Is there an official SRMIST IT cutoff?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Does SRMJEEE apply to B.Tech IT?

SRMJEEE is the principal SRMIST UG engineering admission route.
