B.Tech Information Technology

IT is a computing-focused B.Tech pathway at SRMIST for students targeting software, systems and information technology careers.

Eligibility

SRMIST B.Tech programmes require the prescribed Class 12 PCM qualification and programme-specific aggregate. Check the current programme notice for the exact requirement.

Admission

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal SRMIST B.Tech admission route, followed by counselling.

Fees

Annual tuition varies by programme and campus. Use the exact SRMIST IT programme listing for the applicable fee rather than a generic B.Tech estimate.

Cutoff

SRMIST's official sources used for this page do not provide a consolidated branch-wise closing-rank table. No unofficial rank is labelled as an official cutoff.

Placements

Official 2023 university-level figures: 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, ₹1 crore per annum maximum salary and ₹10.5 LPA average salary.

FAQs

Which entrance exam is used for SRMIST B.Tech IT?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal entrance route.

Is the IT cutoff officially published?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was not found in the sources used.

Does fee vary by campus?

Yes. Programme and campus must be matched before comparing fees.
