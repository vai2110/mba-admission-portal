What Is B.Tech IT at SRMIST?

Information Technology is a computing-oriented engineering route. Students comparing IT with CSE should look at the exact curriculum, specialisation options, campus and fee rather than treating the two programme names as interchangeable.

Decision point

IT can suit students who want a technology-focused degree, but the right choice depends on the exact SRMIST programme and campus being offered.

What Is the Eligibility?

SRMIST's regular B.Tech eligibility requires at least 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory and one prescribed third subject. The published age condition is 16 years 6 months on 31 July of the relevant year.

How Do You Get Admission?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route. The 2026 process had three application phases. Counselling then uses the SRMJEEE rank, programme/campus preference and seat availability.

What Is the B.Tech IT Fee?

SRMIST fees are programme- and campus-specific. A concrete official reference is ₹4,25,000 annual fee for B.Tech CSE with Specialization in Big Data Analytics at Kattankulathur. That figure should not be copied to IT.

What Is the SRMIST B.Tech IT Cutoff?

SRMIST does not publish a consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used for this guide. An internet estimate should therefore not be labelled the official IT cutoff.

What should you use?

Use SRMJEEE rank and the programme/campus options available in counselling. Keep counselling closing rank separate from scholarship rank bands.

What Do You Study in IT?

An IT curriculum is centred on programming, databases, software systems, networking and application development. For SRMIST, compare the exact syllabus before deciding between IT and CSE.

Who Should Choose IT?

IT can fit students who prefer software and technology but want to compare a focused IT pathway with broader CSE options. Select only after checking the actual curriculum and campus availability.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum and average salary ₹10.5 LPA. NIRF 2026 engineering data reports a ₹6.32 lakh median salary for the reported four-year UG engineering population in 2024-25.

These are not IT-only figures. Use the reporting scope before making a branch decision.

FAQs Students Ask

Does B.Tech IT use SRMJEEE?

Yes. SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route.

What is the regular B.Tech eligibility?

SRMIST states 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory.

Does SRMIST publish an official IT closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Can I use the ₹4.25 lakh CSE fee for IT?

No. ₹4.25 lakh is the annual fee for the specific CSE with Big Data Analytics listing at Kattankulathur.

Is IT the same as CSE?

No. They overlap in computing, but the exact curriculum and specialisation structure can differ.
