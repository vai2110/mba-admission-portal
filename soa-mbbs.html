SOA MBBS: Quick Answer

SOA offers MBBS through the Institute of Medical Sciences and SUM Hospital in Bhubaneswar. Admission is through NEET and the prescribed counselling process; SAAT is not the MBBS admission route.

Important

The fee, seat and counselling rules for medical admissions are different from SOA's SAAT programmes. Always use the current NEET/DGHS and SOA medical admission information.

Why Consider MBBS at SOA?

The main factors are the medical institute, hospital ecosystem, clinical exposure, fee, seat availability, NEET route and postgraduate environment. SOA's Medical category ranked 15 in NIRF 2025.

SOA MBBS Admission 2026

MBBS admission requires a valid NEET qualification and participation in the counselling route applicable to SOA's seats. SOA's admission information identifies NEET and DGHS as the route for MBBS and BDS.

  1. Appear for and qualify NEET.
  2. Register for the applicable counselling process.
  3. Fill choices and participate in seat allocation.
  4. Complete SOA reporting, document verification and fee formalities if allotted.

SOA MBBS Seats

NIRF 2025 reported an approved UG intake of 250 seats for the 2023-24 reporting year. This is a year-specific disclosure and should not be presented as the confirmed 2026 sanctioned intake without current confirmation.

SOA MBBS Course Structure

MBBS is a long-form medical programme combining foundational medical sciences, clinical subjects, practical training, hospital exposure and internship. Students should review the current regulatory curriculum and institute schedule for the exact semester/phase-wise subject structure.

What students study

  • Foundational anatomy, physiology and biochemistry.
  • Pathology, pharmacology, microbiology and related medical sciences.
  • Clinical subjects across medicine, surgery and allied specialities.
  • Practical and clinical learning followed by compulsory internship requirements.

Clinical Exposure & Career Path

For MBBS, the hospital environment is as important as classroom teaching. Students should assess patient exposure, departments, clinical teaching, internship structure and access to postgraduate learning when comparing medical colleges.

SOA MBBS Career & Placement Outcomes

NIRF 2025 reported 148 of 148 UG medical graduates placed in the 2022-23 reporting cohort with a median salary of ₹9 lakh. PG medical reporting for 2023-24 shows 106 of 106 graduates placed with a ₹12 lakh median.

These figures should be interpreted differently from engineering placements because medical career paths commonly include internship, clinical practice and postgraduate education.
