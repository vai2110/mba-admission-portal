SOA MBBS: Quick Answer

SOA offers MBBS through the Institute of Medical Sciences and SUM Hospital, Bhubaneswar. Admission is through NEET and the prescribed counselling route; SAAT is not the MBBS admission route.

Admission route

SOA's current admission information states that MBBS and other listed medical programmes are admitted through NEET and DGHS.

SOA MBBS Admission 2026

Candidates must qualify NEET and participate in the counselling process applicable to the SOA MBBS seats. The university's SAAT process does not apply to MBBS.

SOA MBBS Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy the prevailing medical-admission rules for 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology and English, and must qualify NEET. Category-specific rules are governed by the current counselling authorities.

SOA MBBS Seats

NIRF 2025 reported an approved UG intake of 250 seats for 2023-24. This is a year-specific NIRF disclosure and should not be treated as the 2026 sanctioned intake without current confirmation.

SOA MBBS Career Outcome

NIRF 2025 reported 148 of 148 UG medical graduates placed in 2022-23, with a median salary of ₹9 lakh. Medical placement reporting should be interpreted separately from engineering and management outcomes.
