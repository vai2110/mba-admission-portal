Is ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE a good choice?

Quick answer

The four-year B.Tech CSE is designed around core computer science with electives in AI/ML, data science, IoT, cloud computing, security, networking and related areas. Admission is through JEE Main and centralised JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

The programme started in 2017 and is intended to prepare students for industry as well as emerging computing domains.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE admission 2026

Admission is through JEE Main followed by JoSAA/CSAB counselling. The institute does not run a separate entrance examination for B.Tech CSE.

What do you study in ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE?

The programme combines core CSE with electives and supporting humanities, management and science subjects. The institute highlights specialisation areas including AI and Machine Learning, signal/image/video processing, computer architecture and systems, IoT, networks, theoretical computer science and data science.

This makes the course relevant for students targeting software engineering, data, AI/ML, cloud, cybersecurity and higher studies.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE fees 2026

CSE falls within the B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG fee group in the official 2026 fee notice. For a new admission, the notice lists ₹95,000 tuition and ₹1,05,000 total institute fee per semester. Hostel and mess are additional components.

The listed admission-year grand total for the group is ₹1,68,750, including institute fee, hostel, mess, one-time payments and refundable hostel-mess security as specified by the institute.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE FAQs

How can I get B.Tech CSE at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

You need to qualify JEE Main and participate in JoSAA/CSAB counselling. The institute's B.Tech CSE page explicitly states that admission is through JEE Main followed by centralised counselling.

What is the B.Tech CSE fee at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For new 2026 admissions, the B.Tech fee group has ₹95,000 tuition and ₹1,05,000 total institute fee per semester. The official admission-year grand total including hostel, mess, one-time payments and security is ₹1,68,750.

What is the average package for CSE at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The 2023–24 annual report reports ₹22.11 LPA average CTC for B.Tech CSE, with 51 students placed.

What is the highest package for CSE at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The 2023–24 annual report lists ₹65 LPA as the highest CTC for B.Tech CSE. This is the highest reported outcome for that session, not the expected package for every student.

What specialisations or electives are available in ABV-IIITM CSE?

The institute highlights elective areas including AI/ML, data science, IoT, networks, security, computer architecture, theoretical computer science and signal/image/video processing.

Is ABV-IIITM Gwalior CSE better than the integrated B.Tech+M.Tech programme?

They suit different goals. B.Tech CSE is a four-year undergraduate degree with flexibility in computing electives. The integrated B.Tech+M.Tech is five years and can suit students seeking deeper postgraduate technical study. Compare duration, career goal and the programme-specific placement data.
