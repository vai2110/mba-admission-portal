College Overview

About IIM Lucknow

IIM Lucknow offers a broad two-year PGP/MBA ecosystem alongside specialised and executive programmes. It is a strong traditional IIM option for candidates targeting consulting, finance, general management, analytics, marketing and operations.

Why students shortlist it

Strong fit for candidates seeking a top-five NIRF management institute with broad career access and a mature alumni and recruiter network.

Decision lens

Use ranking as a starting point; make the final decision using programme fit, fees, career outcomes, admission probability and personal preference.

Batch Profile & Student Life

Students should evaluate batch size, academic background, work-experience mix, campus environment and student-led activities using the latest annual and placement reports.

Evaluate these strengths

  • Peer-learning quality
  • Alumni and recruiter access
  • Campus and academic ecosystem

  • Living and accommodation cost

Who Should Apply?

Strong fit if

  • Strong fit for candidates seeking a top-five NIRF management institute with broad career access and a mature alumni and recruiter network.
  • You have a clear career reason for choosing the institute.
  • The programme structure matches your target role.

Think twice if

  • You are choosing only because of the rank.
  • You are relying on the highest CTC for ROI.
  • The programme's specialisation does not match your goal.
