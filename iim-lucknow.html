Latest available shortlisting benchmark

IIM Lucknow's 2026–28 MBA policy set the minimum CAT threshold for General candidates at 85 percentile in each section and 90 overall. These are minimum thresholds for consideration, not a guaranteed interview call or final admission score. The 2027–29 policy has not been announced yet.

Admission 2027

For the 2027–29 batch, candidates will use CAT 2026 and then follow IIM Lucknow's programme-specific shortlisting and WAT-PI process. The 2026–28 policy is the latest available policy reference until the next admission policy is released.

Eligibility for PGP / MBA

  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA; the minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.
  • The bachelor's degree must represent at least three years of education after 10+2 or equivalent.
  • Final-year bachelor's candidates and candidates awaiting results can apply subject to IIM Lucknow's documentation and completion conditions.
  • There is no separate work-experience requirement for the flagship PGP/MBA, although work experience can contribute to the selection score.

How the 2026–28 MBA selection worked

  1. Meet the CAT sectional and overall minimum thresholds for the relevant category.
  2. IIM Lucknow calculates a WAT-PI shortlist composite using CAT, Class 12, graduation, work experience and diversity factors.
  3. Shortlisted candidates submit the WAT-PI form within the stipulated deadline.
  4. Candidates appear for the Writing Ability Test and Personal Interview.
  5. For the MBA final score, the 2026–28 policy assigned 30% to CAT, 10% to academics, 5% to diversity, 5% to work experience, 10% to WAT and 40% to PI.
2027–29 status

IIM Lucknow has not released the 2027–29 MBA admission policy yet. Once released, the CAT thresholds, shortlist weights and final-selection weights should be checked again.

2026–28 PGP / MBA fee

The official IIM Lucknow fee structure totals ₹20.75 lakh for the 2026–28 PGP, including admission, tuition, infrastructure, refundable deposits and alumni membership charges. Mess charges are payable separately on actual basis.

What is not included

Mess charges are payable by students on an actual basis in every term. Personal expenses, travel, optional activities and other individual living costs are not part of the ₹20.75 lakh figure.

ROI context

For the latest 2024–26 PGP and PGP-ABM final placement cycle, IIM Lucknow reported an average salary of ₹33.2 lakh and median salary of ₹32.9 lakh. Placement outcomes vary by role and are not guaranteed.

Curriculum

The two-year PGP uses a core-management foundation followed by electives. IIM Lucknow's programme structure covers major functional areas including finance and accounting, marketing, operations, strategy, human resources and analytical tools, with elective choices expanding in the second year.

Core areas

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

IIM Lucknow provides need-based financial assistance based on family income. The institute states that students meeting the applicable income criteria can apply, irrespective of caste, creed, gender or religion. Eligibility and disbursal rules are announced after admissions, the scholarship is for one year, and disbursal is term-wise subject to academic requirements.

Students can also explore scholarships offered by Central and State Governments and other bodies through the National Scholarship Portal and relevant government portals.

Campus & Hostel

The IIM Lucknow campus is spread over about 200 acres at Prabandh Nagar and hosts the PGP and PGP-ABM programmes. Institute facilities include academic buildings, library and computing resources, sports and recreation facilities, health services and student residential infrastructure.

The institute's own directory lists dedicated PGP hostels on the Lucknow campus. Students should confirm room-allocation and accommodation charges for their batch before budgeting because hostel and mess arrangements can change.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Lucknow MBA 2026?

For the 2026–28 PGP/MBA admission process, the official minimum for General candidates is 90 overall with 85 in each CAT section. This is only the minimum eligibility/shortlisting threshold. IIM Lucknow uses a composite process, so a 90 percentile should not be interpreted as a realistic call guarantee.

What is the fee of IIM Lucknow MBA 2026–28?

Students should compare the official total with the ₹33.2 lakh average and ₹32.9 lakh median placement outcomes from the latest cycle.

Is IIM Lucknow worth the fees?

Fee value depends on the role a student targets, the total cost of attendance and the placement outcomes relevant to that role. The latest final placement report should be read alongside the full fee structure.

IIM Lucknow vs IIM Indore: which is better?

Compare the two programmes using admission criteria, fees, location, curriculum and programme-specific placement reports. Both institutes use CAT-based admission processes but their shortlisting and final-selection criteria differ.

What is the average package at IIM Lucknow?

The latest 2024–26 placement cycle reported about ₹33.2 lakh average and ₹32.9 lakh median salary, with the highest domestic offer at ₹1 crore. Median is more useful than the highest package for estimating what a typical student may receive.

What is PGP-ABM at IIM Lucknow and who should choose it?

PGP-ABM is the agribusiness management route. It is better suited to students interested in food, agriculture, rural markets, supply chains and related consulting/corporate roles. Compare its curriculum, eligibility and programme-specific placements with the flagship PGP before applying.

Should I choose IIM Lucknow PGP-SM?

PGP-SM has its own curriculum, eligibility requirement and placement reports. Its programme-specific outcomes should be reviewed separately from PGP and PGP-ABM.
