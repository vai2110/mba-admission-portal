SIBM Pune MBA: What Applicants Need to Know

SIBM Pune's flagship MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme with 240 seats. The current programme offers specialisations in Marketing, Human Resource Management, Finance, Operations and Supply Chain Management, and Data Analytics. The programme is structured across four semesters and includes a summer internship. SIBM Pune's official programme page confirms the current structure and specialisations.

Is SIBM Pune a SNAP-based MBA?

Yes. Applicants for the flagship MBA must appear for SNAP and separately register with SIBM Pune. A SNAP score alone does not automatically qualify a candidate for the institute's next selection stage.

How to Get Admission to SIBM Pune MBA

Step 1: Meet the academic eligibility

Candidates need a graduation degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. The minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year students can apply, subject to obtaining the required qualifying marks.

Step 2: Take SNAP

SIBM Pune requires applicants to appear for the Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP). Candidates must register for SNAP and separately register with SIBM Pune by paying the additional institute application fee of ₹1,000 plus GST, as stated by the institute.

Step 3: Clear the SIBM Pune shortlist

SIBM Pune shortlists candidates based on SNAP scores. The institute does not publish an official fixed SNAP percentile cutoff on its MBA admission pages. Therefore, an unofficial percentile should not be presented as an institute-declared cutoff.

Step 4: Attend GE-PI

The current admission page states that shortlisted candidates move to the institute's selection process, which includes Group Exercise and Personal Interview (GE-PI). The process is currently described as online on the updated admission page.

Step 5: Check the merit list and complete fee payment

SIBM Pune publishes the merit list on its website. Selected candidates then have to complete fee payment within the published deadline. The institute advises applicants to monitor its website because separate individual communication may not be sent to every applicant.

Important admission point

Meeting the graduation eligibility requirement and taking SNAP does not guarantee admission. SIBM Pune uses SNAP for shortlisting and a subsequent institute-level selection process for final admission.

SIBM Pune SNAP Cutoff: What Is Officially Known?

SIBM Pune's official MBA pages specify SNAP as the entrance examination and state that shortlisted applicants are selected on the basis of SNAP scores. However, the institute does not publish a fixed official SNAP percentile cutoff on the cited admission pages.

SIBM Pune MBA Fees 2026-28 and Total Cost

The official 2026–28 fee structure lists ₹6.55 lakh academic fee per installment, payable four times across the two-year programme. The published first-year hostel and mess charges are separate components.

What is the minimum known cash requirement?

The first-year published instalments total ₹16,26,900. Adding the second year's academic fee of ₹13.10 lakh gives a known two-year outlay of ₹29,36,900 before Year-2 hostel and mess charges. This includes the ₹40,000 refundable deposits. Excluding those deposits, the known non-refundable/usage-linked amount is ₹28,96,900 before Year-2 hostel and mess.

Do not treat ₹26.20 lakh as the complete residential cost.

₹26.20 lakh is the academic fee. The complete cost of attendance is higher because hostel and mess charges are additional. The exact two-year residential cost cannot be fixed from the current official fee sheet because Year-2 hostel and mess charges are to be communicated later.

Who Should Apply to SIBM Pune MBA?

Strong fit

  • Applicants seeking a two-year residential MBA through SNAP.
  • Students who want to specialise in Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations & Supply Chain Management or Data Analytics.
  • Candidates comfortable with a selection process that goes beyond the entrance test.
  • Applicants who want to evaluate a programme using official placement outcomes and total residential cost.

Check carefully before applying

  • Do not use an unofficial SNAP percentile as if it were SIBM Pune's published cutoff.
  • Do not compare ₹26.20 lakh academic fees with another institute's all-inclusive residential fee without adjusting for hostel and mess.
  • Year-2 hostel and mess charges are not fixed in the current fee sheet.

SIBM Pune MBA FAQs

What is the SIBM Pune MBA admission process after SNAP?

Applicants first appear for SNAP and separately register with SIBM Pune. Candidates shortlisted on SNAP scores move to the institute's selection process, which currently includes Group Exercise and Personal Interview. The merit list is then published by SIBM Pune. Clearing SNAP itself does not guarantee the next stage or admission.

What is the official SNAP cutoff for SIBM Pune MBA?

SIBM Pune does not state a fixed SNAP percentile cutoff on its current official MBA admission page. The institute says that shortlisting is based on SNAP scores. Therefore, a percentile quoted by a coaching website should not be described as SIBM Pune's official cutoff.

How much does SIBM Pune MBA cost for two years?

The published 2026–28 academic fee is ₹26.20 lakh. First-year hostel is ₹1.573 lakh for three sharing and first-year mess charges total ₹1.196 lakh in the published schedule. Two refundable deposits of ₹20,000 each are also listed. Year-2 hostel and mess charges are to be communicated later, so the exact final residential cost cannot yet be stated from the official fee sheet.

What is the latest SIBM Pune MBA placement package?

In the official 2025 Campus Recruitment Programme report for the MBA 2024–26 batch, the highest CTC was ₹74.84 lakh, average CTC was ₹28.18 lakh and median CTC was ₹27 lakh. More than 110 recruiters participated.

Does SIBM Pune MBA accept CAT?

The official admission process for the flagship MBA specifies SNAP as the entrance test. Applicants must appear for SNAP and separately register with SIBM Pune. CAT is therefore not the stated entrance route for the flagship SIBM Pune MBA.
