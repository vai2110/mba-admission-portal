Programme Overview

IIM Kozhikode's Post Graduate Programme in Liberal Studies & Management (PGP-LSM) is a two-year, six-term, full-time postgraduate programme that combines liberal studies with core management education. Its design is intended for students who want to develop managerial judgment beyond purely technical or quantitative business problem-solving.

The programme provides liberal-studies and core-management coverage in the first year, followed by advanced courses and electives in the second year. IIMK also includes international and rural immersion and a master's thesis as distinctive elements of the programme structure.

What makes PGP-LSM different from a regular MBA?
The programme deliberately combines management education with perspectives from liberal studies. For students, the practical distinction is that the learning approach places greater emphasis on judgment, context, interpretation and multidisciplinary thinking alongside core management principles.

Eligibility for IIM Kozhikode PGP-LSM 2026–28

For the 2026–28 admission cycle, IIM Kozhikode requires candidates with a graduation or post-graduation degree to have at least 60% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA. Candidates with CA, CMA (ICWA) or CS who do not have a graduation degree need at least 50% aggregate marks in the relevant professional qualification.

A valid CAT, GRE or GMAT score from the period specified in the official policy is also required. CAT applicants must have a positive scaled score in all sections of CAT 2025. Candidates awaiting final-year results may apply subject to the provisional-admission conditions in the official policy.

Entrance Exam & Selection Benchmarks

PGP-LSM accepts CAT, GRE and GMAT for the 2026–28 cycle, subject to the score-validity and programme-specific conditions published by IIM Kozhikode. CAT candidates must obtain a positive scaled score in all CAT 2025 sections.

Instead of presenting an unofficial “expected cutoff”, this page follows the official admission policy. Students should distinguish between the minimum conditions needed to enter the selection process and the competitive score/profile required to receive a shortlist.

A high “IIM Kozhikode PGP-LSM cutoff” search may refer to either eligibility thresholds or actual shortlist competitiveness. IIMK's final shortlist depends on multiple factors, including test performance and profile parameters, so a single percentile cannot be treated as a guaranteed admission score.

IIM Kozhikode PGP-LSM Admission & Selection Process

IIM Kozhikode follows a three-stage selection process for PGP-LSM admissions.

Stage 1: Registration

Candidates who meet the eligibility conditions complete the programme-specific registration process. Registration is not an interview call.

Stage 2: Shortlisting

Candidates are shortlisted using an Aggregate Index Score (AIS). IIMK's selection process considers aptitude-test performance, academic performance, work experience, academic diversity and gender diversity.

Stage 3: Writing Task & PI

Shortlisted candidates participate in the Writing Task and Personal Interview. The final merit list is prepared using the parameters specified in the official selection process.

Who Should Apply for IIM Kozhikode PGP-LSM?

Students who value multidisciplinary learning

A strong fit for candidates who want management education informed by humanities, social sciences and broader liberal-studies perspectives.

Future managers facing complex decisions

Useful for students who recognise that many management problems involve incomplete information, competing stakeholders and contextual judgment.

Candidates seeking a distinctive MBA alternative

Suitable for applicants who want a management degree but do not want their learning experience to be limited to a conventional functional-specialisation approach.

Freshers and professionals with academic curiosity

The programme can suit candidates who are comfortable exploring ideas across disciplines while building a management foundation.

Who Should Think Twice Before Choosing PGP-LSM?

  • Candidates looking primarily for a narrowly specialised programme in finance, marketing, analytics or operations.
  • Students who want only a conventional MBA curriculum and have little interest in multidisciplinary or liberal-studies learning.
  • Applicants selecting the programme only as an alternative route to the IIM Kozhikode brand without understanding its academic orientation.
Realistic view: PGP-LSM should be chosen for its academic and career fit. Its interdisciplinary design is a strength for the right student, but it may not match someone who wants a highly focused functional specialisation from day one.
