How do you compare MBA placement reports correctly?

First match the programme type, graduating batch and placement year. A full-time MBA, executive programme and specialised PGDM can have different student populations and should not be treated as equivalent.

Why do roles matter as much as salary?

A high CTC is not useful if the job does not match your goal. Check whether the report shows consulting, product, finance, sales and marketing, operations, analytics, technology or general management roles.

  • Consulting: strategy, implementation, technology or operations?
  • Finance: corporate finance, risk, markets, sales or operations?
  • Analytics: modelling and decision support, or mostly reporting?
  • Marketing: brand, product, digital or sales?
  • Operations: supply chain, procurement, manufacturing or process improvement?

Recruiter logos show participation only as defined by the report; they do not prove a recurring role or multiple offers.

How do you calculate MBA return on investment?

Include tuition, compulsory fees, accommodation, food, travel, laptop, books, non-refundable deposits, loan interest and income foregone. Compare the total with realistic post-MBA outcomes—not the single highest offer.

  1. Record the current programme fee and exclusions.
  2. Estimate living costs for the full duration.
  3. Use median salary and role mix where officially published.
  4. Remember CTC is not take-home pay.
  5. Compare the result with your current salary and alternative programmes.

A payback estimate is only a planning aid, not a guarantee of ROI.

Where should placement data be verified?

Prefer a dated institutional report for the exact programme and batch. Look for methodology notes, salary definitions and student denominator. If only an old report exists, label the year. If median is not published, state that instead of estimating it from the average.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is median or average salary better?

Median often better represents the middle outcome; check both when published.

Does highest package show what most students earn?

No. It is the largest reported offer and may be an outlier.

What does 100% placement mean?

Check whether the denominator is all students, eligible students or only those seeking placement.

Is CTC in-hand salary?

No. CTC can include variable pay, benefits and deferred components.

How do I verify data?

Use the dated official report for the exact programme and batch.
