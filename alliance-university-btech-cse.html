Alliance University B.Tech CSE: Is This Course Right for You?

Alliance University's B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is a four-year undergraduate programme built around programming, algorithms, system design and practical project work. Alliance also highlights design projects, capstone projects, internships and hackathons, with emerging areas such as machine learning, IoT, embedded systems and cybersecurity available through the curriculum and electives.

The practical choice is between a broad CSE base and a more specialised AI/data route. CSE gives wider software and technology flexibility, while AI & Data Science is more focused on data, analytics and machine learning.

4 YearsRegular B.Tech duration
8 SemFull-time academic structure
5 RoutesJEE Main, SAT, COMEDK, CET and AUEET listed
Best suited for

Students targeting software engineering, AI, data, cloud, cybersecurity or product-engineering careers and who want a broad computer science foundation.

B.Tech CSE Eligibility at Alliance University

The current official CSE programme page states that regular-entry applicants need 10+2 with a minimum of 50% marks in Physics and Mathematics plus one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology or a technical vocational subject. Alliance also lists a lateral-entry route for eligible applicants.

Is Maths compulsory?

Yes. Mathematics is a compulsory foundation for the programme according to Alliance's current CSE FAQ.

Can diploma students apply?

A lateral-entry route is listed. Check the current programme page for the relevant diploma branch and marks requirement before applying.

Before you apply

Match your Class 12 subjects and marks with the current CSE eligibility table, then choose the entrance route for which you have a valid score or eligibility.

Alliance University B.Tech CSE Fees 2026: Fee Bifurcation

The current official CSE page lists a ₹25,000 registration fee and a published ₹18,00,000 total programme fee for Indian nationals. The page also displays instalment amounts. Alliance University B.Tech CSE FAQs

What is the duration of B.Tech CSE?

The regular programme is four years across eight semesters.

Can I get B.Tech CSE admission without JEE?

Yes. Alliance lists AUEET, CET, COMEDK and SAT alongside JEE Main.

What is the B.Tech CSE fee?

The current official page states ₹18 lakh total and ₹25,000 registration fee for Indian nationals. Confirm the final payment schedule in the offer letter.

Is Maths compulsory?

Yes. Mathematics is compulsory for B.Tech CSE.

What is the B.Tech placement package?

Alliance reports ₹60.1 LPA highest, ₹5.7 LPA average and ₹5 LPA median for B.Tech for academic years 2024-25 and 2025-26.
