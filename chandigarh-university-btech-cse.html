Chandigarh University B.E. CSE: What You Are Actually Choosing

Chandigarh University's B.E. Computer Science & Engineering is the university's core engineering route into computing. The 2026 curriculum page lists the standard B.E. B.Tech CSE or BCA at CU?

Choose B.E. CSE if you specifically want an engineering degree and are comfortable with engineering mathematics, systems and technical coursework. Choose BCA if you prefer a three-year computer applications route. The difference matters for both curriculum and some job eligibility requirements.

Chandigarh University B.Tech CSE Admission 2026

CU's current admission policy states that CUCET is mandatory for B.E. and several other listed programmes, with an exception for candidates scoring more than 90% in the qualifying examination or holding a JEE rank up to 50,000. Students should verify the latest programme notice before relying on an exemption.

Does JEE Main guarantee B.E. CSE admission at Chandigarh University?

No. The current policy describes an exemption from the CUCET requirement for candidates up to a stated JEE rank threshold. Admission still depends on meeting the university's programme eligibility and completing the admission process.

Chandigarh University B.Tech CSE FAQs

What is the Chandigarh University B.Tech CSE fee?

The mandatory disclosure lists ₹1.46 lakh for standard B.E. Computer Science and Engineering. Specialised CSE programmes have different listed fee figures, so students should not use ₹1.46 lakh for every CSE option.

Is CUCET compulsory for B.Tech at Chandigarh University?

CU's current policy states that CUCET is mandatory for B.E. and several other listed programmes, with exceptions including qualifying marks above 90% or a JEE rank up to 50,000. Confirm the current policy before applying.

Is Chandigarh University CSE good for placements?

CU has a large university-wide placement network and reports 10,000+ offers and a ₹59.9 LPA highest national package for its current placement cycle. Students should evaluate CSE-specific recruiters, roles and salary distribution rather than treating the highest package as a typical outcome.

Which CSE specialisation is best at Chandigarh University?

There is no single best option for every student. Core CSE gives broader flexibility, while Data Science, Cyber Security, Cloud and Full Stack routes make more sense when the student already has a target domain and is willing to build relevant projects.
