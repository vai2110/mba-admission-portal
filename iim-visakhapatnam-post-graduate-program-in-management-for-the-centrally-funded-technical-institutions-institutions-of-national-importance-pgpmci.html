Programme Overview

The PGPMCI is a specialized degree-granting programme offered by IIM Visakhapatnam, an Institution of National Importance under the IIM Act 2017. It is designed specifically to cater to candidates from Centrally Funded Technical Institutions (CFTIs) and Institutions of National Importance (INIs).

Curriculum & Specialisations

The programme focuses on advanced managerial learning and strategic decision-making. Specific specialisation tracks and detailed curriculum modules for PGPMCI are currently not published in the official online programme documentation.
