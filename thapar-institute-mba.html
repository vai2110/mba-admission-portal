LM Thapar School of Management MBA Overview

The MBA is offered by LM Thapar School of Management at TIET. It is a two-year full-time management programme designed for students seeking careers across business functions. The 2026-28 admissions cycle is currently listed by the school.

Thapar MBA Eligibility 2026

Applicants should meet the programme's graduation and test-score requirements specified for the 2026-28 cycle. The school should be treated as the final authority for exact eligibility, work-experience and document rules.

Accepted MBA Entrance Tests

The 2026 admission page lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT and TMAT as accepted routes. Candidates should submit the score applicable to their admission route and follow the active application instructions.

Thapar MBA Selection Process

Selection is based on the applicable entrance score and the institute's subsequent evaluation process. Applicants should prepare for profile questions, academics, work experience where relevant, career goals and current business awareness.

Thapar MBA Fees 2026-28

The published academic fee schedule totals ₹14.10 lakh across four semesters: ₹3.90 lakh in semester 1, ₹3.60 lakh in semester 2, ₹3.75 lakh in semester 3 and ₹3.75 lakh in semester 4.

Thapar MBA Placements

Placement outcomes should be assessed separately for LMTSM MBA rather than using TIET's engineering headline. Check the latest school-level placement report for recruiter mix, average/median salary, role quality and placement participation.

Student Discussion: Is Thapar MBA Worth It?

The MBA decision should be based on total cost, target function, expected salary, location and alternative admits. A candidate comparing private B-schools should calculate ROI using realistic average or median outcomes rather than a highest package.
