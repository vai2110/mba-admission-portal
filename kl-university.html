KL University: What Students Need to Know

KL Deemed to be University (KLEF) has its main campus at Vaddeswaram in Andhra Pradesh and offers programmes across engineering, management, science, arts and commerce, law, architecture, pharmacy and agriculture. The university's current admissions site lists 2026-27 admissions and describes the Vaddeswaram campus as its main campus.

Is KL University good for engineering?

Engineering is one of KLU's major programme areas, with B.Tech options including CSE, AI & Data Science, CSIT, ECE, EEE, IoT, Mechanical, Civil and Biotechnology. The university also reports engineering-specific placement outcomes, so students should compare branch-wise fees, curriculum and placement data rather than relying only on the university-wide headline package.

How to Get Admission to KL University

Step 1: Choose the programme and campus

KLU operates the Vaddeswaram main campus and additional Hyderabad campuses. Programme availability differs by campus, so confirm the exact campus attached to your application.

Step 2: Check the programme eligibility

For B.Tech, KLU states 60% or above in the qualifying examination and 60% in group subjects, with PCM eligibility for engineering programmes and PCB eligibility for Biotechnology/B.Pharmacy routes as specified on its admissions portal.

Step 3: Take the applicable entrance test

Depending on the programme, this may be KLEEE, KLECET, KLMAT, KLSAT or KLHAT, while the university also recognises specified external examination routes for some programmes.

Step 4: Apply, attend counselling and complete admission

The university's 2026-27 portal lists registration, verification, application submission and fee payment as the core online application steps. Counselling and course commencement are scheduled according to the applicable admission process.

KL University Campus and Facilities

The Vaddeswaram campus is the university's main campus. KLU describes it as a 100+ acre green campus and lists smart classrooms, laboratories and research centres, student activity facilities, project-based labs, practice-school exposure, global certifications and innovation-oriented learning among its academic environment.

What stands out

  • Large main campus at Vaddeswaram
  • Engineering and multidisciplinary programme breadth
  • Research, innovation and entrepreneurship infrastructure
  • International collaborations and study opportunities

Check before deciding

  • Campus and programme availability are not identical across all four campuses.
  • Fees, hostel charges and scholarships can change by intake.
  • Placement headlines should be read with the relevant year and eligibility conditions.

KL University FAQs

What is the main campus of KL University?

The main campus of KL Deemed to be University is at Vaddeswaram in Andhra Pradesh.

Which entrance exam is required for B.Tech at KL University?

KLU lists KLEEE as its engineering entrance examination and also recognises JEE Main, JEE Advanced, specified state-level engineering examinations and Uni-GAUGE for B.Tech eligibility.

What is the highest package at KL University?

KLU's current admissions website reports a highest package of ₹75 LPA. The figure should be read as a university-reported headline and checked against the relevant placement year and programme.

Does KL University offer B.Tech AI and Data Science?

Yes. KLU offers a four-year B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science and publishes a programme-specific curriculum and eligibility page.

Is KL University accepting applications for 2026-27?

Yes. KLU's admissions portal states that admissions are open for the 2026-27 academic year.
