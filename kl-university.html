KL University: What Students Need to Know Before Applying

KL Deemed to be University (KLEF) is a multidisciplinary deemed university with its main campus at Vaddeswaram in Andhra Pradesh. Its current admissions portal also lists Hyderabad campuses at Aziznagar, Bachupally and KLH GBS Kondapur.

For a student, the important point is that KLU is not a single-course institution. It offers engineering, management, computer applications, science, architecture, law, pharmacy and other programmes. That makes the choice of programme, campus and admission route more important than judging the university only from an overall ranking or placement headline.

What happens after submitting the form?

The current portal asks applicants to provide working contact details, submit the application and pay the ₹1,000 non-refundable application fee. Entrance examination, counselling and course commencement dates are scheduled according to the applicable admission cycle and norms.

Do not assume that selecting a programme preference in the online form permanently fixes your branch. KLU's portal states that the preference is for statistical purposes and the final programme choice is made during counselling.

KL University Eligibility and Entrance Exams: What Score Do You Need?

B.Tech eligibility

KLU's current admissions information states that B.Tech applicants should pass 10+2 or an equivalent examination with 60% or above in aggregate and 60% or above in group subjects. Students with PCM are eligible for all listed B.Tech programmes including Biotechnology, while PCB is accepted for Biotechnology and B.Pharmacy as specified by the university.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

For B.Tech, KLU lists KLEEE, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, recognised state-level engineering entrance examinations including EAMCET, and Uni-GAUGE where applicable. This means a student does not necessarily have to rely only on KLEEE, but the exact route should be matched to the programme and admission cycle.

Management and other programmes

The current portal identifies KLMAT for management admissions. Other programmes may use merit, a university test and/or Personal Interview. Students should not transfer the B.Tech eligibility rule to MBA, BBA, BCA or other courses without checking the relevant programme.

Does KLU offer scholarships?

KLU states that merit-based and need-based scholarship opportunities are available. For engineering, the current programme information links scholarship consideration to factors such as entrance-examination performance and academic performance. Always check the scholarship conditions attached to your offer rather than assuming the headline scholarship applies automatically.

KL University Placements: What Do the Headline Numbers Mean?

KLU's current admissions material reports ₹75 LPA highest package, ₹8.1 LPA average package, 7,000+ placement offers, 5,000+ students placed in 2024 and 487+ companies visiting in 2024. These are useful scale indicators, but a university-wide number should not be treated as a CSE-, AI&DS- or MBA-specific salary.

The right question is not only “What is the highest package?” It is “What are the outcomes for the exact programme I want to join?” Compare median/average where available, recruiter roles, branch-wise placement data, placement eligibility and total programme cost.

KL University Campus and Facilities: Which Campus Will You Study At?

The Vaddeswaram campus is KLU's main campus. KLU's current admissions portal also lists Hyderabad campuses at Aziznagar, Bachupally and KLH GBS Kondapur. Campus matters because the same university name can represent different programmes, facilities and fee structures.

Before paying an admission fee, confirm the campus printed on your offer, programme location, hostel availability, commute/residential arrangements and whether your preferred course is offered there. KLU describes its main campus as 100+ acres and highlights laboratories, research centres and student facilities.

KL University FAQs: Questions Students Usually Ask Before Applying

Is KL University accepting applications for 2026-27?

Yes. KLU's current admissions portal lists applications for the 2026-27 academic year. Exam, counselling and commencement dates vary by programme and phase.

What is the application fee for KL University?

The current online application fee listed by KLU is ₹1,000 and is non-refundable.

What is the B.Tech eligibility at KL University?

KLU states 10+2/equivalent with 60% or above in aggregate and 60% or above in group subjects for B.Tech. Subject combinations and programme-specific conditions also apply.

Can I get B.Tech admission with JEE Main?

Yes. KLU lists JEE Main among accepted B.Tech admission routes, along with KLEEE, JEE Advanced, recognised state-level engineering exams and Uni-GAUGE where applicable.

What is the fee for B.Tech AI & Data Science?

The official 2025-26 programme page lists ₹1,53,500 for the first semester at Vijayawada and ₹1,61,000 at Hyderabad, including the ₹16,000 admission fee. The university states that these figures are subject to change for 2026-27.

What is the highest package at KL University?

KLU's current admissions material reports ₹75 LPA as the highest package. This is a university-level headline and should not be treated as a typical or guaranteed salary.

Which is better at KLU: CSE or AI & Data Science?

CSE provides a broader computer-science foundation, while AI & Data Science starts with deeper focus on AI, machine learning and data. Your intended career direction should decide the choice.
