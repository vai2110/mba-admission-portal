KL University: What Students Need to Know

KL Deemed to be University (KLEF) has its main campus at Vaddeswaram in Andhra Pradesh and offers programmes across engineering, management, science, arts and commerce, law, architecture, pharmacy and agriculture.

KL University Admission 2026-27: What to Track

KLU's admissions portal lists applications for the 2026-27 academic year. Entrance and selection routes vary by programme, so students should check the specific programme notification before applying.

How to Get Admission to KL University

Choose the programme and campus

Confirm the exact programme, campus and intake before applying because programme availability and admission routes can differ.

Check eligibility and entrance requirements

For B.Tech, KLU states minimum qualifying requirements and applicable entrance routes including KLEEE, while specified external examinations are also recognised for some routes. Management admissions use the applicable KLMAT/selection route.

Apply and complete the admission process

Follow the programme-specific application, verification, counselling and fee-payment process published by KLU.

KL University Rankings and Recognition

KLU's admissions website reports the 2025 NIRF positions as 26th among universities, 35th in engineering, 46th overall and 70th in management.

KL University Placements: Latest Highlights

KLU reports ₹75 LPA as the highest package and ₹8.1 LPA as the average package, along with 7,000+ placement offers and 5,000+ students placed in 2024.

KL University Campus and Facilities

FAQs

Which are the popular courses at KL University?

B.Tech CSE, B.Tech AI & Data Science, B.Tech ECE, MBA, BBA and BCA are among the major programmes highlighted on this page.

What is the eligibility for B.Tech at KL University?

Students need to meet KLU's qualifying-examination and subject requirements for B.Tech admission. The exact requirement should be checked against the current programme notification.

What is the fee for B.Tech AI & Data Science at KL University?
