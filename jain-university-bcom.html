Jain University BCom Overview

The School of Commerce at JAIN offers Bachelor of Commerce programmes in honours/honours with research formats and specialised routes, combining academic foundations with practical exposure and industry-oriented learning.

Who should choose BCom?

BCom is a natural fit for students interested in accounting, finance, taxation, business analytics, banking, corporate roles or professional qualifications such as CA.

BCom FAQs

Does Jain University offer BCom Honours?

Yes. The School of Commerce lists Bachelor of Commerce Honours/Honours with Research among its regular programmes.

Which BCom specialisation is best for finance?

Finance Management, Accounting & Taxation and FinTech are relevant starting points depending on the career direction.

What is the BCom placement package at Jain University?

The current BCom page reports ₹24.80 LPA highest CTC and ₹6.03 LPA average CTC for 2024-25.

Can I pursue CA after BCom?

Yes. BCom can provide a relevant academic foundation for CA and other professional qualifications, subject to current professional eligibility rules.
