Jain University BCom Overview

The School of Commerce at JAIN offers Bachelor of Commerce programmes in honours/honours with research formats and specialised routes. The university describes its commerce curriculum as combining academic foundations with practical exposure, certifications and industry-oriented learning.

Who should choose BCom?

BCom is a natural fit for students interested in accounting, finance, taxation, business analytics, banking, corporate roles or professional qualifications such as CA.

BCom Specialisations & Options

Current commerce programme information includes areas such as Accounting & Taxation, Finance Management, International Business, Marketing Management, Business Analytics and FinTech. The school also lists certified programmes in capital markets and entrepreneurship/business management.

BCom FAQs

Does Jain University offer BCom Honours?

Yes. The School of Commerce lists Bachelor of Commerce Honours/Honours with Research among its regular programmes.

Which BCom specialisation is best for finance?

Finance Management, Accounting & Taxation and FinTech are relevant starting points. Your choice should depend on whether you prefer corporate finance, accounting/taxation or finance technology.

What is the BCom placement package at Jain University?

The current BCom page reports ₹24.80 LPA highest CTC and ₹6.03 LPA average CTC for 2024-25.

Can I pursue CA after BCom?

Yes. BCom can provide a relevant academic foundation for CA and other professional qualifications, subject to the professional body's current eligibility and examination rules.
