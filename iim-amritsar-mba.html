IIM Amritsar MBA Overview

The IIM Amritsar MBA is a two-year management programme and the institute's flagship full-time offering. The 2026-28 admission process had two stages: pre-PI shortlisting followed by Personal Interview. IIM Amritsar states that selection criteria are finalised annually, so the 2026-28 weights should not be treated as fixed for 2027-29. citeturn0search0

IIM Amritsar MBA Eligibility

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements prescribed for the relevant CAT admission cycle. Final-year candidates may be considered subject to completion and minimum-mark conditions specified by IIM Amritsar. citeturn0search0

IIM Amritsar MBA Selection Process

  1. Take CAT and meet the applicable minimum section-wise and overall percentile requirements.
  2. Build the pre-PI composite score using the parameters notified for that cycle.
  3. Attend the Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  4. Final ranking uses the notified pre-PI and post-PI components.

For 2026-28, CAT carried 50% of the pre-PI score; Class 10 and Class 12 each carried 10%, graduation 5%, gender diversity 10% and work experience 15%. The post-PI score gave 70% weight to PI, with 10% each for work experience, gender diversity and academic diversity. The aggregate score was 70% pre-PI and 30% post-PI. citeturn0search0

IIM Amritsar MBA CAT Cutoff

For MBA 2026-28, the General-category minimum shortlist requirement was 90 overall, with 80 QA, 75 DILR and 80 VARC. EWS also had a 90 overall requirement; NC-OBC, SC, ST and PwD thresholds were lower. citeturn0search0

These are minimum shortlisting thresholds. They are not the same as a final admission cutoff because candidates are ranked using a composite score and PI performance.

IIM Amritsar MBA Fees

IIM Amritsar's official fee page currently lists fee structures for the 2024-26, 2025-27 and 2026-28 MBA batches. The 2027-29 fee should be confirmed once the institute publishes that batch's structure. citeturn0search1

IIM Amritsar MBA Placements

For the 2024-26 MBA batch, IIM Amritsar reports a highest CTC of ₹43 LPA. The average CTC for the top 50 students was ₹18.60 LPA with a median of ₹18 LPA; for the top 50% of the batch, average CTC was ₹16.30 LPA and median CTC ₹15.25 LPA. The graduating cohort had 256 students. citeturn0search2

IIM Amritsar MBA FAQs

Is 90 percentile enough for IIM Amritsar?

90 percentile was the published General-category minimum overall percentile for the 2026-28 first shortlist. It does not guarantee a PI call or final admission because the institute uses a composite score. citeturn0search0

Does IIM Amritsar consider academics?

Yes. The 2026-28 selection process explicitly included Class 10, Class 12 and graduation performance in the pre-PI score. citeturn0search0
