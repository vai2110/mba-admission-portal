What is the IIM Amritsar MBA?

IIM Amritsar's flagship MBA is a full-time two-year residential programme in General Management with a summer internship. The institute says each academic year normally runs across three terms.

The admission process is important to understand before applying: IIM Amritsar does not make the decision on CAT alone. The latest policy combines CAT with academics, work experience and diversity factors before the PI, followed by a heavily weighted Personal Interview.

IIM Amritsar MBA Admission 2027: Eligibility & Selection Process

Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy the CAT eligibility rules and the institute's admission policy for the relevant cycle. Final-year candidates may be considered subject to the institute's stated degree-completion and minimum-mark conditions.

Selection Process

  1. Appear for CAT.
  2. Meet the applicable section-wise and overall minimum percentile requirements.
  3. Compete for the pre-PI shortlist using the notified composite criteria.
  4. Attend the Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  5. Final merit is prepared using the notified pre-PI and post-PI components.

IIM Amritsar MBA Placements 2024-26

The latest official final-placement update reports a ₹43 LPA highest CTC. The top 50 students recorded ₹18.60 LPA average and ₹18 LPA median CTC; the top 50% recorded ₹16.30 LPA average and ₹15.25 LPA median. The graduating cohort comprised 256 students.

The current official report does not publish an overall-batch average in the same format, so this page does not incorrectly label ₹18.60 LPA as the average salary of the entire batch.

IIM Amritsar MBA FAQs

Is 90 percentile enough for IIM Amritsar MBA?

90 was the published General-category minimum overall percentile for the 2026-28 first shortlist. It does not guarantee a PI call or admission.

What is IIM Amritsar MBA fees?

The official 2026-28 programme fee is ₹19.50 lakh for two years. The fee notice shows ₹21.50 lakh total payment including listed caution and mess deposits.

What is the highest package at IIM Amritsar?

₹43 LPA was the highest CTC reported for MBA 2024-26.
