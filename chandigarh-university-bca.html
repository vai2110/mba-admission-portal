Who should choose BCA?

BCA can be a strong route for students who want software, applications, web, data or IT careers but do not want a conventional engineering degree. The value of the programme depends heavily on the technical skills, projects and internships you build alongside the degree.

The degree alone is not the career

For a computing career, treat programming, databases, problem-solving, Git, projects and internship experience as part of your education rather than optional extras.

Which BCA options are available?

CORE

BCA

The standard computer applications route for broad IT and software fundamentals.

DATA

BCA Hons. Data Science

More suitable for students interested in data, statistics and analytics-oriented computing.

DESIGN

BCA UI/UX

Combines computing with interface and user-experience design interests.

IMMERSIVE

BCA AR/VR

For students interested in immersive applications and related technologies.

How does admission work?

The current admissions ecosystem lists BCA among open programmes. Unlike B.E. and MBA, CUCET is not universally mandatory for every programme; students should follow the current programme-specific admission criteria.

For the exact 2026 application status, eligibility and documentation, use the university's current admissions status and admission-criteria pages.

What does BCA cost?

The current mandatory disclosure lists ₹78,000 for Bachelor of Computer Applications (Hons./Hons. with Research). Specialised BCA variants have different listed figures, including ₹85,000 for the Data Science route and ₹80,000 for AR/VR and UI/UX options.

Check the exact variant before paying

The specialisation you select can change the fee. Scholarships and other charges can also alter the effective cost.

What will you study?

The 2026 curriculum catalogue includes BCA, BCA AR/VR, BCA UI/UX and BCA Hons./Hons. with Research Data Science schemes. The right curriculum should give you a foundation in programming and computing before adding the specialisation layer.

Programming

Build strong coding fundamentals and problem-solving ability.

Applications

Projects should turn classroom concepts into usable software or digital products.

Specialisation

Choose data, AR/VR or UI/UX only if it aligns with your intended career direction.

What can you do after BCA?

Software / Web

Build coding, development and project skills for entry-level software roles.

Data

Use statistics, SQL, Python and analytics projects for data-oriented roles.

Design

UI/UX route can lead toward interface and product-design roles when supported by a strong portfolio.

Higher study

MCA or related postgraduate study can deepen technical capability and broaden eligibility.
