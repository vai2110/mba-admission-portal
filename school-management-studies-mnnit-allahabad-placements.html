How are MNNIT MBA placements?

The latest official placement highlight published by the School of Management Studies covers the 2024-26 MBA batch. It reports a batch size of 67 students, highest package of ₹13.96 LPA, median package of ₹9 LPA and average package of ₹7.76 LPA.

The report also shows recruitment across BFSI, IT & ED Tech, FMCG/Consulting and Consulting & Healthcare.

What do MNNIT MBA placement numbers mean for a student?

Is ₹13.96 LPA the typical MNNIT MBA salary?

No. ₹13.96 LPA is the highest package in the 2024-26 placement highlight. The average is ₹7.76 LPA and the median is ₹9 LPA. A student should therefore use the median and average to understand the broader salary outcome.

Why is the median higher than the average?

The reported median of ₹9 LPA is higher than the ₹7.76 LPA average. This can happen when the salary distribution contains lower values that pull the average down. It is another reason not to judge placements from the highest package alone.

Is MNNIT MBA good for finance?

The latest official sector split is led by BFSI at 60%, and the recruiter list includes ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank and other financial-sector employers. The programme also offers Finance as a specialisation area.

Does MNNIT MBA have analytics exposure?

Yes. SMS curriculum material includes analytics-oriented electives such as financial analytics, marketing analytics, HR analytics, data analytics, business intelligence and supply-chain analytics.

How should ROI be judged?

MNNIT is a public technical institute, so the MBA's fee structure is materially different from high-fee private B-schools. Students should compare the complete academic + hostel/mess cost with the latest median and average package rather than comparing only highest CTC.
