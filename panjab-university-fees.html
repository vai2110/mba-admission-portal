How much is the fee at Panjab University?

There is no single PU fee

Panjab University's fee varies by course, department, academic level, professional programme and study mode. A BA student, B.Pharmacy student, MBA student and M.Tech student do not pay the same fee.

The university and individual departments publish course-specific fee notices. Students should therefore use the exact programme notice for the final payable amount rather than relying on a single university-wide number.

Why management fees look very different across PU programmes

UBS's 2026 handbook illustrates the difference clearly. For regular MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), the tuition component is ₹31,675 per year, while MBA Entrepreneurship and MBA Business Data Analytics carry substantially higher tuition components.

Panjab University hostel fees

Hostel fees are separate from academic tuition and depend on the hostel, course, year and applicable university charges. Hostel allotment is also subject to availability and eligibility.

Students should not add an old hostel figure to a 2026 fee calculation unless the current hostel notice confirms it.

Panjab University Fees FAQs

Does Panjab University have one common fee structure?

No. Fees are course- and department-specific.

What is the cheapest course at Panjab University?

There is no reliable single answer without comparing the current fee notices for all programmes. Many traditional university programmes have different fee structures from professional courses.

Is hostel fee included in tuition?

No. Hostel and mess costs are separate and subject to allotment and current hostel rules.

What is the UBS MBA fee?

For 2026, UBS lists ₹31,675 per year tuition for MBA/MBA-IB/MBA-HR, with additional published case-unit charges. Other MBA variants have higher tuition.
