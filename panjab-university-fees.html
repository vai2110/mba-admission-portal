How much does a Panjab University MBA cost?

2026 fee snapshot

The 2026 UBS handbook lists ₹31,675 per annum tuition for MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), ₹1,35,135 per annum for MBA(EP), and ₹2,00,000 per annum for MBA(BDA). These are tuition components, not necessarily the complete amount a student pays.

UBS separately lists case-unit charges that cover items such as teaching materials/case units, business club, computer charges, alumni relations and placement-related charges. The handbook also notes that tuition is being finalised by PU authorities and is subject to change.

Scholarships and fee concessions

Fee support can depend on university rules, category and the specific concession notified for the admission year. The UBS handbook explicitly directs candidates to Panjab University's Handbook of Information for fee concessions.

Possible support routes

  • SC/ST/PwD category concessions where applicable
  • University fee concessions under notified rules
  • Other category-specific benefits mentioned in the PU admission handbook

Important

Do not assume a scholarship is automatic. Check the current PU rules, documentary requirements and the applicable programme before budgeting.

Panjab University MBA ROI: why the fee changes the decision

UBS's 2024-25 reported average CTC was ₹12.95 LPA, while the highest reported CTC was ₹25.11 LPA. Against the published tuition of the regular MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), the tuition-to-salary relationship is unusually favourable on paper.

Strong ROI case

Regular MBA tuition is very low compared with many private B-schools, while UBS has established recruiters and a sizeable corporate-placement ecosystem.

Don't oversimplify ROI

CTC is not take-home salary, placement percentages vary by year and programme, and living/other expenses still matter. Use average or median outcomes rather than the highest package alone.

Panjab University MBA Fees FAQs

What is the Panjab University MBA fee in 2026?

For MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), the 2026 UBS handbook lists ₹31,675 per annum as tuition. Additional case-unit and university charges are separate.

What is the MBA BDA fee at UBS?

The 2026 handbook lists ₹2,00,000 per annum tuition for MBA(BDA), with separate case-unit charges.

What are the additional charges for regular MBA?

The handbook lists ₹19,000 for year 1 and ₹26,500 for year 2 as the case-unit package, including specified placement, teaching-material, club, computer and alumni components.

Is hostel fee included?

No. Hostel and living costs should be budgeted separately and depend on current PU hostel rules and availability.

Can UBS fees change?

Yes. The 2026 handbook says the tuition is subject to finalisation by PU authorities and notes that fee-related changes can be made according to university rules.
