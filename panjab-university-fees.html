How much is the fee at Panjab University?

There is no single Panjab University fee.

The 2026 PU Handbook lists fees separately by department and programme. The academic total can include tuition, maintenance/user charges and contributions to university funds, so tuition alone is not the complete payable amount.

The figures on this page use the official Handbook of Information 2026, Part C wherever the programme is explicitly listed.

Source: Panjab University Handbook of Information 2026 — Part C, Fees.

Panjab University Fees FAQs

What is the fee structure of Panjab University?

PU does not have one common fee structure. Fees vary by programme, department and campus. The official 2026 Handbook Part C lists separate academic totals; for example, UBS MBA is ₹2,00,025 and UIET B.E. Information Technology is ₹1,36,855 for the listed 2026–27 academic charges.

What is the Panjab University MBA fee in 2026?

The official 2026–27 fee table lists the UBS MBA academic total at ₹2,00,025. The listed components are ₹1,93,380 tuition, ₹1,580 maintenance/user charges and ₹5,065 contribution to funds. The payment is shown as ₹1,03,335 + ₹96,690.

What is the B.E. or engineering fee at Panjab University?

It depends on the engineering programme. For 2026–27, the official fee table lists ₹1,36,855 for UIET B.E. Information Technology and ₹1,50,880 for UIET B.E. Biotechnology. These are academic totals; hostel and mess are separate.

What is the LLB fee at Panjab University?

The official 2026–27 fee table lists ₹69,290 as the academic total for the three-year UILS LLB and ₹1,25,055 for the five-year BA LLB (Hons). The exact payable amount should be checked against the current programme fee notice.

Is hostel included in Panjab University fees?

No. The academic fee and hostel are separate. Hostel allotment and charges depend on the applicable hostel rules, while mess, books, travel and personal expenses are also outside the academic total.

Does Panjab University charge the same fee for every course?

No. Professional and specialised programmes can cost much more than general programmes, and regional-centre fees can also differ. Comparing only a university-level “average fee” can therefore be misleading.

Can Panjab University fees be paid in instalments?

Some programme fee tables show instalment-wise payment. For example, the 2026–27 UBS MBA academic total is split into ₹1,03,335 and ₹96,690. The applicable instalment schedule is programme-specific and should be followed from the current fee notice.

What other expenses should I budget for besides tuition at Panjab University?

Plan separately for hostel, mess, books or equipment, laptop needs, transport, relocation and personal expenses. These costs are not included automatically in the academic totals shown in the fee handbook.
