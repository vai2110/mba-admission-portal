How much is the fee at Panjab University?

There is no single Panjab University fee.

The 2026 PU Handbook lists fees separately by department and programme. The payable amount can include tuition, maintenance/user charges and contributions to university funds, so quoting tuition alone can understate the actual academic fee.

The figures below use the official 2026 Handbook of Information, Part C wherever the programme is explicitly listed. Fees are shown as the published academic total for the stated session, not as an estimated university-wide average.

Panjab University hostel and living costs

Hostel should not be added to the academic total unless a current hostel fee notice confirms the amount for the selected programme and hostel. Mess, books, laptop, travel and personal expenses are also separate.

For a realistic budget, students should calculate academic fee + hostel + mess + books/equipment + travel/personal expenses.

Panjab University Fees FAQs

Does Panjab University have one common fee structure?

No. Fees are department- and programme-specific. The 2026 Handbook Part C lists separate fee structures for different courses.

What is the Panjab University MBA fee in 2026?

The official 2026-27 fee table lists UBS MBA academic charges of ₹2,00,025 for the year, including ₹1,93,380 tuition, ₹1,580 maintenance/user charges and ₹5,065 contribution to funds.

What is the UIET B.E. fee in 2026?

For B.E. Information Technology, the official 2026-27 academic total is ₹1,36,855. Different UIET branches can have different fee totals.

Is hostel included in Panjab University fees?

No. Hostel and mess expenses are separate from the academic fee total and depend on allotment and current hostel rules.
