What does BHU BCom Hons cover?

BHU offers B.Com. (Hons.) and B.Com. (Hons.) Financial Market Management under the NEP curriculum. The programmes are designed around commerce, finance, markets, technology and business skills.

The 2026 admission notice uses CUET-UG for both programmes.

What is the BHU BCom eligibility?

The 2025 official bulletin states that candidates should have passed 10+2/equivalent with Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Finance, Financial Markets Management or a related vocational subject, with 50% aggregate and a pass in the concerned subject.

The 2026 notice requires Accountancy/Book Keeping, Business Studies and General Aptitude Test for the listed BCom routes.

How do you get BCom admission at BHU?

  1. Appear in CUET-UG with the required papers.
  2. Register for BHU CAP-UG.
  3. Fill programme/location preferences.
  4. Track allotment rounds.
  5. Pay the prescribed fee and complete reporting.

What is the BHU BCom cutoff?

The official 2025 Round 1 General-category cutoff for B.Com. (Hons.) at the Faculty of Commerce was 548.985.

This is a programme and round-specific score. It is not a universal BHU BCom cutoff for every college or category.

What is the BHU BCom fee?

The 2025 official bulletin lists the Faculty of Commerce regular B.Com. (Hons.) fee at ₹1,150 per semester. Paid-fee and affiliated-college structures are different.

What are BCom placements at BHU?

BHU's NIRF-based university figures report ₹8 LPA median for UG 3-year programmes and ₹9 LPA for UG 4-year programmes. These are not BCom-specific placement numbers.

See BHU Placements for the NIRF and official Annual Report evidence.

What can you do after BCom Hons?

Typical paths include accounting, banking, finance, taxation, audit, financial services, business operations, market research and higher studies such as MCom, MBA, CA, CMA and other professional qualifications.
