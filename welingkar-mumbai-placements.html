Welingkar Mumbai Placement ROI: Is It Worth the Fee?

The flagship PGDM tuition is around ₹14 lakh over two years at the ₹7 lakh annual brochure rate, before living and hostel costs. A student should therefore compare the total investment against the programme's average and median salary, not the highest package.

For specialised programmes, use the programme-specific placement numbers because averages can differ materially across Healthcare, Retail, Rural, Media, E-Business, Business Design and Analytics.

Before accepting your seat, check

  • Final fee in the admission offer.
  • Programme-specific median and average salary.
  • Actual role and location.
  • Fixed versus variable compensation.
  • Hostel and Mumbai living costs.
