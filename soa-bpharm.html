SOA B.Pharm: Admission 2026, Eligibility, Fees & Career

SOA offers B.Pharm through its School of Pharmaceutical Sciences.

SOA B.Pharm Eligibility 2026

SOA's 2026 criteria require 10+2 with English and the prescribed Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and/or Biology combination, with the applicable aggregate and age requirements.

SOA B.Pharm Admission

B.Pharm is among the programmes offered through SOA's SAAT admission system. Check the current 2026 round for application and result dates.

SOA B.Pharm Fees

Use the official 2026 SOA fee-structure page for the current academic fee and any hostel, mess or other charges.

Career After B.Pharm

Relevant career areas include pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality control, quality assurance, formulation, regulatory work and research. Graduates can also pursue M.Pharm and related postgraduate study.

FAQs

What is the eligibility for B.Pharm at SOA?

10+2 with English and the prescribed PCM/PCB/PCMB combination and aggregate under SOA's 2026 criteria.

Is SAAT required for B.Pharm at SOA?

B.Pharm is included among the programmes offered through SAAT, subject to the applicable admission round.
