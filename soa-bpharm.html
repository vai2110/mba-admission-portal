SOA B.Pharm: Quick Answer

SOA offers a four-year B.Pharm through its School of Pharmaceutical Sciences. The programme is included in SOA's SAAT admission system and is designed for students entering pharmacy after Class 12.

Why Study B.Pharm at SOA?

The decision should be based on pharmacy facilities, curriculum, practical exposure, fee, admission route and postgraduate options. SOA also lists M.Pharm programmes, giving students a direct higher-study pathway within the university.

Programme

Four-year B.Pharm

A professional undergraduate pharmacy degree after Class 12.

Admission

SAAT route

B.Pharm is included among SOA's SAAT programmes.

Progression

M.Pharm option

Postgraduate pharmacy specialisation is available through M.Pharm programmes.

SOA B.Pharm Eligibility 2026

SOA's published criteria require 10+2 with English and the prescribed Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and/or Biology combination, along with the applicable aggregate and age conditions.

B.Pharm Subjects & Course Structure

B.Pharm combines pharmaceutical sciences with laboratory and practical learning. The curriculum generally progresses from foundation sciences to pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, pharmacognosy, quality and professional practice.

Foundation

Pharmaceutical sciences

Basic science concepts applied to medicines and healthcare.

Core

Pharmaceutics & chemistry

Drug formulation, dosage forms and pharmaceutical chemistry.

Clinical

Pharmacology

Drug action, therapeutic use and safety concepts.

Practical

Laboratory work

Hands-on practical learning supports the theory curriculum.

Higher Studies After B.Pharm

Students can pursue M.Pharm specialisations, research, regulatory or management-oriented postgraduate education depending on their career target. SOA itself lists M.Pharm options, while other postgraduate routes can be considered nationally.

Scholarships & Financial Support

SOA publishes scholarship and fee-support information through its admission ecosystem. Eligibility can depend on category, merit, programme or admission conditions, so confirm the current rules for the 2026 batch.

SOA B.Pharm FAQs

What is the eligibility for B.Pharm at SOA?

10+2 with English and the prescribed PCM/PCB/PCMB combination, plus the published aggregate and age requirements.

Is SAAT required for B.Pharm at SOA?
