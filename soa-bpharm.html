SOA B.Pharm: Quick Answer

SOA offers B.Pharm through its School of Pharmaceutical Sciences. The programme is included in SOA's SAAT admission system and is designed for students entering pharmacy after Class 12.

SOA B.Pharm Eligibility 2026

SOA's 2026 criteria require 10+2 with English and the prescribed Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and/or Biology combination, along with the applicable aggregate and age requirements.

SOA B.Pharm Admission 2026

B.Pharm is among the programmes offered through SOA's SAAT system. Check the current admission round for application, test and result dates before submitting documents.
