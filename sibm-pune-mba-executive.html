SIBM Pune MBA Executive: Programme Overview

The MBA Executive at SIBM Pune is designed for working executives. The 2026–28 programme has a duration of 24 months and an intake of 90 seats. The programme is structured to allow candidates to continue working while studying.

Classes are conducted on weekends, Saturday and Sunday, from 9 AM to 5 PM, according to the institute's official programme information.

Who Is Eligible for SIBM Pune MBA Executive?

For the 2026–28 intake, applicants need a graduation degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance with 50% marks or equivalent grade, with the minimum reduced to 45% for SC/ST candidates. Applicants must also have at least one year of full-time work experience after graduation as on May 31, 2026, in a registered firm, company, industry, educational, government or autonomous organisation.

How to Get Admission to SIBM Pune MBA Executive?

The MBA Executive has a separate admission process from the flagship MBA. Candidates register online and pay the application fee. The institute states that selection is based on a Personal Interview.

Application sequence

  • Complete the online application form.
  • Submit the online application fee.
  • Attend the Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  • Wait for the merit-list/selection communication.
  • Complete fee payment within the institute's stated deadline.

What Does SIBM Pune MBA Executive Cover?

The programme has 100 credits. Students can build expertise across Marketing, Finance, Operations and Supply Chain Management, Analytics and IT, with major-minor specialisation options under the programme structure. The curriculum includes areas such as Machine Learning, Data Mining, IoT, Mobile Analytics, Security Analysis and Portfolio Management, Operations Strategy and Control, Technology in Supply Chain and International Marketing.

SIBM Pune MBA Executive Admission Dates

The official 2026–28 programme page records registration from March 10, 2026 and a programme commencement date of June 20, 2026. The page states that interview, result and fee-payment dates are communicated separately to candidates.

SIBM Pune MBA Executive FAQs

Is work experience required for SIBM Pune MBA Executive?

Yes. For the 2026–28 batch, the official requirement is at least one year of full-time work experience after graduation as on May 31, 2026.

Does SIBM Pune MBA Executive require SNAP?

The official executive programme selection procedure is based on Personal Interview. Its application process is separate from the SNAP-based flagship MBA.

What is the SIBM Pune MBA Executive fee?

The 2026–28 academic fee is ₹7.20 lakh across four instalments, plus a ₹10,000 refundable institute deposit.
