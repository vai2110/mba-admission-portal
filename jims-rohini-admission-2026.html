JIMS Rohini Admission 2026: current status

Current cycle

JIMS is publishing 2026 admission information for its two-year full-time PGDM programmes. The official GD/PI page currently lists an online GD/PI slot on 16 June 2026 at 9:00 AM. citeturn3search2

The institute offers PGDM, PGDM-International Business and PGDM-Retail Management. JIMS states that the PGDM is equivalent to an MBA by AIU. citeturn1search1

JIMS PGDM Eligibility 2026

Candidates need a minimum three-year bachelor's degree or equivalent in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks. Final-year degree candidates can also apply subject to completion and submission of the required documents. citeturn1search3

Eligible profile

Graduate in any discipline, 50%+ aggregate, or final-year candidate who can satisfy the documentation requirements.

Do not assume

Meeting the minimum 50% rule does not guarantee admission. Entrance performance, academics, PI and GD/SOP are considered.

JIMS PGDM Selection Process 2026

JIMS's published autonomous-PGDM selection framework allocates weight to entrance score, academic/professional performance, PI, GD and SOP.

In the PI, JIMS assesses areas such as communication, general awareness, academic knowledge, vision and commitment. GD evaluation includes leadership, communication and clarity of thought. citeturn1search3

JIMS Rohini Cutoff 2026: what should you target?

JIMS's mandatory disclosure lists 75 CAT percentile as the historical PGDM benchmark for 2023-25. It also lists 60 XAT, 70 CMAT, 60 ATMA percentile and 580 MAT composite score. citeturn2search26

Practical advice

Use the published cutoff as a floor for planning, not as a safe-score guarantee. A stronger percentile can improve the competitiveness of your overall application because JIMS considers multiple selection components.

JIMS Admission FAQs

What is the minimum percentage for JIMS PGDM?

JIMS states a minimum of 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification.

Does JIMS accept CAT?

Yes. CAT is among the entrance tests listed by JIMS for PGDM admissions.

What is the JIMS CAT cutoff?

The mandatory disclosure lists 75 percentile as a historical PGDM benchmark. It is not a guaranteed 2026 final cutoff.

Does JIMS accept CMAT?

Yes. CMAT is accepted and the historical disclosed benchmark is 70 percentile.

What is the JIMS application fee?

The current application page lists ₹1,000 for the PGDM application and says the fee is non-refundable.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes, subject to completing the degree and submitting the required mark sheets/pass certificate later.

How is JIMS PGDM selection done?

Published selection criteria consider entrance score, academic/professional performance, PI, GD and SOP.

When is JIMS GD/PI 2026?

The currently published online GD/PI schedule lists 16 June 2026 at 9:00 AM.
