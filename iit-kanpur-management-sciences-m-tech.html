Quick Answer

DoMS IIT Kanpur offers an M.Tech in Management Sciences for applicants with a four-year B.E./B.Tech degree. For 2026 admissions, DoMS accepts valid GATE scores from 2024, 2025 or 2026 in any stream. The GATE requirement is waived for eligible IIT B.Tech graduates and specified sponsored/part-time candidates.

Programme Overview

The M.Tech in Management Sciences is part of DoMS IIT Kanpur's postgraduate management-science portfolio. The programme is suited to engineering graduates interested in areas such as data analytics, supply chain management, operations research, consulting, business analytics, finance and related quantitative management domains.

IIT Kanpur M.Tech Eligibility 2026

  • A four-year B.E./B.Tech degree in any discipline.
  • Minimum CPI of 5.5/10 or 55% marks for the 2026 DoMS criteria.
  • A valid GATE score from 2024, 2025 or 2026 in any stream for regular candidates.

For B.Tech graduates from IITs, the GATE requirement is waived if the candidate has a minimum overall CPI of 6.5 and a CPI of at least 8.0 in the last two years of the B.Tech programme. Sponsored and part-time candidates can also fall under the specified GATE-waiver route, subject to employment and experience conditions.

IIT Kanpur M.Tech Admission Process 2026

  1. Apply through the IIT Kanpur PG admission portal within the notified application window.
  2. Regular GATE-based candidates are screened using the applicable GATE score and category criteria.
  3. Eligible candidates without a GATE score under the permitted waiver categories may be considered through the department's prescribed selection process.
  4. Admission offers are issued through the applicable IIT Kanpur admission/offer mechanism.

For 2026, the DoMS announcement specified an application deadline of 13 April 2026 for Indian national candidates. Applicants should always verify the current PG admission notice before applying.

Curriculum & Areas of Interest

DoMS combines quantitative and management-oriented training. The department's wider academic strengths include operations research, operations management, decision support systems, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, entrepreneurship and energy. Current M.Tech student profiles also show interests including data science, data analytics, supply chain management, operations research, consulting, business analytics and finance.

M.Tech Management Sciences Placements

M.Tech placement outcomes are distinct from MBA outcomes. IIT Kanpur's 2024–25 Annual Report reported a 73% placement rate for M.Tech in its program-wise placement figure as of 18 May 2025. The institute also reports department-wise and programme-wise outcomes through its annual placement reporting.

Do not use the MBA median salary of ₹19.90 lakh as an M.Tech salary figure; that number belongs to the NIRF Management 2025 submission for the 2-year PG management programme.

Financial Assistantship

DoMS states that financial assistantship is available to eligible non-sponsored M.Tech students. Exact assistantship terms and semester fees should be checked in the latest IIT Kanpur fee and PG admission notices because these can change by academic year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIT Kanpur M.Tech in Management Sciences require GATE?
For regular candidates, a valid GATE score is required. DoMS accepts GATE scores from 2024, 2025 or 2026 for the 2026 admission cycle.
Can IIT graduates apply without GATE?
Eligible IIT B.Tech graduates can receive a GATE waiver if they meet the prescribed CPI conditions.
Which GATE streams are accepted by DoMS?
For 2026, DoMS states that GATE scores from any stream are accepted for its M.Tech in Management Sciences.
Is M.Tech in Management Sciences the same as an MBA?
No. The M.Tech is a postgraduate management-science/technology degree, while DoMS separately offers a full-time general-management MBA.
