Quick Answer

The M.Tech programme in Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur is a postgraduate degree. Admissions are conducted online via the official portal, typically opening in March for the July semester. Financial assistantship is available for non-sponsored students.

Programme Overview

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur offers M.Tech and PhD programmes. The curriculum is designed to integrate engineering rigour with management principles. The institute provides a robust research environment supported by 570+ faculty members and extensive campus facilities.

Eligibility Requirements

Candidates must hold a relevant degree with a minimum of 55% marks or 5.5 CPI. Relaxations are provided for SC/ST/DAP candidates as per Government of India norms. Final-year students are eligible to apply for provisional admission.

Admission Process

Admissions are managed through the online portal at IIT Kanpur PG Admissions. The application fee is ₹500 for General/EWS/OBC and ₹250 for SC/ST/DAP candidates. Selection is based on academic performance, entrance scores (where applicable), and interviews.

Placement Highlights

IIT Kanpur maintains a strong industry interface. In the 2025-26 placement season, the institute recorded 672 job offers on Day 1, marking a 16% increase over the previous year. Top recruiters include Accenture, BlackRock, HSBC, and Qualcomm.
