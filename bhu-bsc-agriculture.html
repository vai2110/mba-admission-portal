What is BHU BSc Agriculture?

BHU offers B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture as a four-year undergraduate programme. The course is relevant for students interested in crop science, agricultural production, soil, plant science, farm management, agribusiness and related research.

What CUET subjects are needed for BHU BSc Agriculture?

The 2026 official notice allows multiple CUET combinations: Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics; Biology/Biotech/Biochem + Chemistry + Physics; or Agriculture + Biology + Chemistry.

Always match your Class 12 subjects to one of the permitted combinations before applying.

How does BHU BSc Agriculture admission work?

  1. Appear for CUET-UG with a permitted subject combination.
  2. Register for BHU CAP-UG.
  3. Submit BSc Agriculture preferences and documents.
  4. Track allotment and pay the admission fee after selection.

What is the BHU BSc Agriculture cutoff?

The cutoff varies by category, round and seat location. The current 2026 portal displays live programme-level cutoff rounds, while historical PDFs can be used to understand competition. No single 2027 cutoff is officially announced in advance.

What is the BSc Agriculture fee at BHU?

The exact fee should be taken from the current Institute of Agricultural Sciences/BHU admission fee schedule. BHU does not have one common fee across all UG programmes.

What are BSc Agriculture placements at BHU?

There is no university-wide BSc Agriculture salary figure in the official sources used here. BHU's NIRF data provides broad UG placement medians, while faculty/unit reports can show more specific outcomes.

See BHU Placements for the combined NIRF and Annual Report evidence.

What can you do after BSc Agriculture?

Career paths include agriculture officer and government roles, agribusiness, farm management, agri-input companies, food and supply-chain businesses, research, extension services and higher studies in agriculture and allied fields.

BHU BSc Agriculture FAQs

Can a PCB student apply for BHU BSc Agriculture?

Yes. The 2026 notice lists Biology/Biotech/Biochem + Chemistry + Physics as one permitted option group.

Can a PCM student apply?

Yes. Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics is another listed option group.

Is BHU BSc Agriculture through CUET?

Yes, followed by BHU's undergraduate CAP process.
