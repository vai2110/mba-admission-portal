Quick Answer

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur offers a PhD programme. Official information regarding a dedicated MBA degree is currently unavailable. The institute's 2025-26 placement season recorded 672 job offers on Day 1.

Admissions

The department invites applications for its PhD programme. Admissions are conducted via the official PG Admissions portal. Financial support (Institute Assistantship) is available for non-sponsored students.
