Quick Answer

IIT Kanpur offers postgraduate management education primarily through M.Tech and PhD programmes in Management Sciences. The institute maintains a robust placement cell, recently recording 672 job offers on Day 1 of the 2025-26 season.

Programme Overview

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur provides advanced research and academic training through M.Tech and PhD programmes. The curriculum is designed to foster innovation and analytical rigour, supported by the institute's interdisciplinary research environment.

Admission Process

Admissions are conducted online via the official portal. Key details include:

  • Eligibility: Generally requires a relevant degree with a minimum 55% marks or 5.5 CPI (relaxations apply for reserved categories).
  • Selection: Based on academic performance, GATE/JRF scores, and interviews.
  • Application: Typically open March-April for July semesters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIT Kanpur offer a traditional MBA?
The Department of Management Sciences primarily offers M.Tech and PhD programmes. Please refer to the official PG Manual for specific degree nomenclature.
Where can I find official placement data?
Official placement reports and NIRF management data are available at iitk.ac.in/nirf.
Is financial support available?
Yes, Institute Assistantships are provided to non-sponsored PhD, M.Tech, and MS (By Research) students.
