Quick Answer

IIT Kanpur's DoMS MBA recorded a 68% placement rate in the 2024–25 recruitment season, according to the institute's Annual Report 2024–25 figure reported as of 18 May 2025. For the latest completed management-placement year available in the NIRF 2025 submission, 44 graduates were placed and the median salary was ₹19.90 lakh.

IIT Kanpur MBA Placement Trend

The NIRF-submitted median salary increased from ₹15.14 lakh in 2021–22 to ₹17.25 lakh in 2022–23 and ₹19.90 lakh in 2023–24. The number of students placed varies by cohort and should be interpreted alongside the number of graduates and students pursuing higher studies.

IIT Kanpur MBA Placement Process

DoMS works with the institute's placement ecosystem to facilitate company engagement. The official MBA placement process includes company invitations, job announcement forms, pre-placement talks, student registration, campus interviews and final selection. Companies may use aptitude/technical tests, personal interviews or group discussions depending on the role.

DoMS states that placement week is generally scheduled in the first week of December, in consultation with the Student Placement Office, IIT Kanpur.

Recruiter & Career Ecosystem

DoMS describes its management education as interdisciplinary, with academic strengths across analytics, operations, finance, marketing, economics, entrepreneurship, energy and related fields. The department's official website also records MBA student participation and achievements in national case competitions, reflecting the programme's wider career-development ecosystem.

Recruiter names should be taken from individual official placement reports rather than treated as a permanent recruiter list because participation changes by batch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the latest IIT Kanpur MBA placement rate?
The IIT Kanpur Annual Report 2024–25 reports 68% MBA placement as of 18 May 2025.
What is the IIT Kanpur MBA median salary?
The NIRF 2025 Management submission reports a ₹19.90 lakh median salary for the 2023–24 graduating cohort.
Does IIT Kanpur publish MBA placement reports?
Yes. DoMS maintains an archive of MBA summer and final placement reports.
Are institute-wide IIT Kanpur placement figures the same as MBA placement figures?
No. Institute-wide figures combine multiple degree programmes. MBA-specific figures should be taken from the DoMS MBA reports or NIRF Management submission.
