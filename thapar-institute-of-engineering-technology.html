Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology: Overview

Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala, is a deemed-to-be university with academic offerings across engineering, technology, management, sciences, humanities and doctoral education. The institute currently highlights a 250-acre campus and 16 centres of excellence.

Quick student verdict

TIET is particularly relevant for students comparing established private/deemed engineering institutions. The right choice depends on programme, branch, total cost, placement profile and the type of campus environment you want.
