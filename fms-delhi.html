FMS Delhi MBA Overview

Faculty of Management Studies (FMS), University of Delhi was established in 1954. Its flagship MBA Full-Time programme is a two-year programme designed around a strong analytical foundation and academic flexibility. The first year covers core management areas and ends with a summer internship, while the second year allows students to build their academic and professional focus through core and elective courses.

For Indian applicants, admission to the full-time MBA is based on CAT. The 2026-28 information bulletin used a weighted CAT score for shortlisting, followed by a Statement of Purpose discussion, extempore and Personal Interview for shortlisted candidates.

At a glance: FMS is part of the University of Delhi and its full-time MBA is a residential programme. The latest official bulletin available for the 2026-28 batch lists 251 sanctioned seats and a semester fee of approximately ₹60,818, subject to revision.

FMS Delhi MBA Admission Process 2027-29

1. Register for CAT 2026

For the full-time MBA, FMS uses CAT for the Indian applicant route. The 2026-28 bulletin specifically states that applicants who did not take CAT 2025 would not be considered for admission. The equivalent requirement for 2027-29 should be checked in the next bulletin.

2. Apply separately to FMS

Taking CAT does not replace the FMS application. Candidates need to complete the separate FMS MBA application within the institute's published window.

3. Shortlisting through a weighted CAT score

For the 2026-28 cycle, FMS calculated a weighted CAT score using VARC 40%, DILR 30% and QA 30%. The bulletin also states that an additional 5 marks were added to the weighted score for women candidates for interview shortlisting.

4. Interview process

Shortlisted candidates go through three individual components: Statement of Purpose discussion, Extempore and Personal Interview. The 2026-28 bulletin does not list a group discussion as a selection component.

FMS Delhi CAT Cutoff 2027: What the Data Shows

FMS does not publish a single guaranteed CAT percentile cutoff for admission. Its published process uses a weighted CAT score for shortlisting. Therefore, raw CAT percentile should not be presented as the official FMS cutoff.

Latest published selection mechanism

CAT sectionWeight in FMS weighted CAT score
VARC40%
DILR30%
QA30%

For the 2026-28 cycle, public reporting of the official shortlist gave category-wise composite-score cutoffs, including 46.739 for General, 37.031 for EWS, 35.189 for OBC, 29.370 for SC and 23.140 for ST. These numbers are composite scores, not CAT percentiles. The published FMS selection framework explains how that composite is constructed.

How to read the cutoff: a candidate cannot convert the composite score into a fixed “required percentile” without knowing the CAT sectional scores and the applicable shortlisting rules. FMS gives VARC the highest sectional weight at 40%.

What students report in Reddit discussions

Recent Reddit discussions from CAT aspirants commonly mention 99+ CAT percentile when discussing FMS calls, but these are individual experiences rather than an official cutoff. One June 2026 discussion included a user reporting that 99.5 was considered a practical target, while another discussion showed candidates discussing calls around the high-99 range. Such comments are useful for understanding candidate expectations, but they are not a substitute for FMS's composite-score calculation.

For 2027-29: the exact shortlist composite cutoff will only be known after FMS releases the 2027 shortlist. Until then, use the latest composite cutoff as historical context and treat Reddit/Quora discussions as anecdotal rather than official data.

FMS Delhi Campus Life

FMS is located in the University of Delhi system in New Delhi. Its official information describes learning resources across the North and South Campuses, while the current student-life section highlights academic, cultural, social and student-led activities.

Academic and technology facilities

  • North and South Campuses with Wi-Fi-enabled learning resources.
  • Dedicated computer centre and access to business, finance and research databases.
  • Finance Lab with Bloomberg terminal and databases/software including CMIE Prowess, EViews, SmartPLS, NVivo, STATA and IBM SPSS Statistics.
  • North Campus library with more than 40,000 books and journals, along with access to the wider Delhi University Library System.

Hostel accommodation

Non-Delhi students of full-time FMS programmes can apply for accommodation in specified University of Delhi hostels. The latest bulletin lists seven men's hostels and four women's hostels in its accommodation section. Hostel allotment depends on the University's process and availability; the FMS bulletin does not guarantee a hostel seat for every student.

Clubs and student activities

FMS currently lists student bodies including the Finance Society, Marketing Society, Consulting Club, HR and OB Society, Product and Operations Society, Entrepreneurship Cell, Sports Society, Scholastic Council, Media Relations Cell and Alumni Relations Cell.

The Management Science Association coordinates student activities for the full-time MBA and currently lists an annual MSA fee of ₹52,000 per student, subject to revision.

FMS Delhi MBA FAQs

What is the FMS Delhi MBA fee for 2027-29?

The final 2027-29 fee has not been published in the sources reviewed. The latest FMS Information Bulletin for 2026-28 lists the MBA semester fee at approximately ₹60,818. Four semesters at that rate work out to about ₹2.43 lakh, but this is only a planning calculation because FMS can revise fees. The MSA fee and hostel-related costs are separate.

What is the eligibility for FMS Delhi MBA?

Candidates need a Bachelor's Degree after 12 years of formal schooling. The latest bulletin specifies 50% marks for the unreserved category, passing marks for SC/ST and 45% for OBC Non-Creamy Layer, EWS, PWD and CW as per University rules. Final-year students can apply subject to the conditions in the bulletin.

Does FMS Delhi accept XAT, GMAT or NMAT for the full-time MBA?

For the regular full-time MBA, the published admission bulletin uses CAT for the Indian applicant route. The 2026-28 bulletin states that applicants who have not taken CAT 2025 will not be considered. The executive programmes have separate admission requirements.

What is the official CAT cutoff for FMS Delhi?

FMS does not publish a single raw CAT percentile as a guaranteed admission cutoff. For shortlisting, it uses a weighted CAT score with VARC 40%, DILR 30% and QA 30%. For the 2026 shortlist, the reported General composite cutoff was 46.739, with separate composite cutoffs for other categories. A composite score should not be confused with a CAT percentile.

Why do students say 99+ percentile is needed for FMS?

Because FMS shortlisting is highly competitive and its CAT sectional scores are converted into a weighted composite. Recent Reddit discussions among CAT aspirants frequently mention high-99 percentiles when discussing FMS calls. These are community reports, not an official FMS cutoff, and the exact CAT percentile corresponding to a shortlist can vary with sectional performance and the applicant pool.

What is the FMS Delhi admission process?

The broad sequence is: take CAT → submit the separate FMS application → shortlist based on the weighted CAT score → Statement of Purpose discussion → Extempore → Personal Interview → final merit list. The latest bulletin gives the final-selection weights as CAT 50%, Class X 10%, Class XII 10%, SOP discussion 10%, Extempore 5% and PI 15%.

What are the latest FMS Delhi placement statistics?

The uploaded FMS final placement report for the 2024-26 batch reports an average CTC of ₹32.27 LPA, median CTC of ₹29.59 LPA, top-100 average CTC of ₹45.19 LPA and highest CTC of ₹1.1 crore. It records 287 students placed, with 119 companies participating.

How many students are there in the FMS MBA batch?

The latest official 2026-28 bulletin lists 251 sanctioned seats for the MBA Full-Time programme. The uploaded 2024-26 placement report records 307 students in that particular batch, so the placement-report batch size should not be treated as the current sanctioned intake.

Does FMS Delhi provide hostel accommodation?

Yes, but hostel accommodation is not automatic. The FMS bulletin says non-Delhi students of full-time programmes can apply for accommodation in specified University of Delhi hostels. Allocation depends on the University's applicable process and availability.

What is student life like at FMS Delhi?

FMS's official student-life pages describe a mix of academic activities, industry workshops, guest lectures, student societies, cultural events and national-level fests. The institute lists functional societies covering finance, marketing, consulting, HR and OB, product and operations, entrepreneurship, sports and other student activities.

When will FMS Delhi MBA 2027-29 applications open?
