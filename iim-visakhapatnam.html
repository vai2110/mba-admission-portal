Quick Answer

IIM Visakhapatnam is an Institution of National Importance. The flagship Executive MBA (EMBA) is a two-year, blended-mode programme. Admissions are based on a composite score of entrance exam scores, work experience, and personal interviews. Official fee structures and placement data are not explicitly published in a consolidated table format on the public portal.

Admissions & Selection Logic

The admission process evaluates candidates using multiple parameters to ensure a comprehensive assessment. Selection is based on a composite score derived from entrance examination scores, work experience, and personal interview (PI) performance.

Academic Programmes

IIM Visakhapatnam offers a diverse portfolio of programmes:

  • Degree Programmes: PGP, EMBA, Doctoral (PhD), Executive PhD, PGPMCI, EPGDPM, PGDMEx.
  • Executive Education: Includes specialized certificates in AI-Driven General Management, Operations Management & Analytics, Chief Human Resource Officer, Chief Financial Officer, and Strategic Investment Banking.

Placement Overview

Official placement statistics for the last three years are not available in the provided public documentation. The institute maintains a Career Development Services (CDS) department to facilitate placement processes, but detailed year-wise comparative reports are not currently accessible on the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of the EMBA programme?

The EMBA programme is a two-year course comprising 740+ hours of interactive executive learning.

Are there loan facilities available?

Yes, IIM Visakhapatnam provides loan facility support through State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, and Credilia.

Where can I find official fee tables?

Official fee bifurcations are not publicly available in a consolidated table format on the institute's website.
