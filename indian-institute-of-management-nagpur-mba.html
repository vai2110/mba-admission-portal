Programme Overview

IIM Nagpur offers a two-year full-time residential flagship MBA programme. The curriculum integrates lectures, case studies, projects, and simulations. The programme includes an international immersion, field immersion, and a summer internship.

Admissions Policy 2026-28

Batch Size: 340 students.

Selection Process: Three stages including CAT 2025 screening, Personal Interview (PI), and final selection based on a composite score.

Fees: Total programme fee is Rs. 21,00,000. Acceptance fee is Rs. 1,00,000 (adjusted towards Term I).

Curriculum

The curriculum covers core management areas including Decision Science, Finance & Accounting, Marketing, Organizational Behaviour, and Strategy. Electives include Business Data Mining, Corporate Valuation, FinTech, and Supply Chain Management.
