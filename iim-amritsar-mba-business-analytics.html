What is IIM Amritsar MBA Business Analytics?

IIM Amritsar runs a dedicated MBA in Business Analytics (MBA-BA) and publishes a separate admission process and fee structure for it. The programme is a natural fit for students who want management training with a stronger analytics orientation rather than a purely general-management route.

The 2026-28 admission policy used CAT-based shortlisting followed by PI and considered academics, work experience and diversity factors alongside CAT.

IIM Amritsar MBA-BA Admission 2027

The latest published MBA-BA policy follows the same broad two-stage structure as the flagship MBA: first shortlist candidates using the notified CAT and composite criteria, then assess shortlisted candidates through Personal Interview.

Who should choose MBA-BA?

  • Students targeting analytics, business intelligence, product, consulting or data-led management roles.
  • Students who want an MBA credential while retaining a clear analytics positioning.
  • Candidates comfortable with CAT-based selection and the institute's composite evaluation.

IIM Amritsar MBA Business Analytics FAQs

What is the IIM Amritsar MBA-BA cutoff?

The 2026-28 published General-category minimum first-shortlist threshold was 90 overall with 80 QA, 75 DILR and 80 VARC.

What is the MBA-BA fee?

The official 2026-28 programme fee is ₹19.50 lakh over two years, with listed deposits taking the total payment shown to ₹21.50 lakh.

Is MBA-BA better than the general MBA?

It depends on your target. MBA-BA is more logical for students already targeting analytics-led roles; the general MBA offers broader functional flexibility.
