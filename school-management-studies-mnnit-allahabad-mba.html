What is MNNIT MBA?

MNNIT MBA is a two-year full-time programme offered by the School of Management Studies. The School was established in 1996 and currently lists 77 seats. SMS states that candidates are shortlisted on CAT percentile for GD and PI.

Who is eligible for MNNIT MBA?

The next 2027-29 brochure should be treated as the final authority for eligibility. MNNIT's regular MBA is an entrance-test-based programme followed by GD and PI, and category-wise reservation rules apply.

Do not copy an older third-party percentage as the current eligibility threshold unless it matches the latest official MNNIT notification.

How to get admission to MNNIT MBA?

  1. MNNIT's central admission information lists CAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and XAT as accepted routes for regular MBA admission.
  2. Submit the MNNIT/SMS application within the notified window.
  3. SMS highlights CAT-percentile-based shortlisting for GD and PI.
  4. Attend GD and PI if shortlisted.
  5. Complete admission formalities after selection.

What is the MNNIT MBA CAT cutoff?

There is no permanent official closing percentile published by SMS. The School says CAT percentile is used for shortlisting candidates for GD and PI.

Student-reported cutoff reference

Recent Reddit discussions are more useful here than a generic “expected cutoff” paragraph. One discussion has an applicant reporting 97 CAT percentile; the same thread later has a student joining the MBA batch through CMAT with 92 percentile. What is the MNNIT MBA curriculum like?

The programme uses case-based teaching, presentations, group discussions, role plays, workshops, field surveys and project work. Advanced areas include financial analytics, marketing analytics, HR analytics, data analytics, business intelligence and supply-chain analytics.

MNNIT MBA FAQs

What is the official MNNIT MBA cutoff?

MNNIT does not publish a permanent closing percentile on the School page; it states that CAT percentile is used for shortlisting to GD and PI.

What student-reported scores are available?

Recent Reddit reports include 97 CAT percentile and 92 CMAT percentile from applicants in an MNNIT MBA discussion. These are not official cutoffs.

What is the 2026 reported MBA fee?

₹84,000 in Semester 1 and ₹79,000 in each of Semesters 2–4, plus a separately reported ₹25,000 mess charge per semester.
