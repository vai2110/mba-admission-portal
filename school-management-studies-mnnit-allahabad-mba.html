What is MNNIT MBA?

MNNIT MBA is a two-year full-time programme offered by the School of Management Studies. The School was established in 1996 and currently lists 77 seats for the full-time MBA.

The programme combines management fundamentals, research-based work, industry exposure, electives and summer internship. SMS states that candidates are shortlisted on CAT percentile for GD and PI.

Who is eligible for MNNIT MBA?

The exact 2027-29 eligibility conditions should be checked against the next official MNNIT admission brochure. The institute's current admission page confirms that the regular MBA is an entrance-test-based programme followed by GD and PI. MNNIT applies category-wise reservation and programme-specific admission rules.

Students should not rely on older third-party eligibility tables when the institute publishes the new cycle brochure.

How to get admission to MNNIT MBA?

  1. Appear for one of the entrance tests listed by MNNIT: CAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA or XAT.
  2. Complete the MNNIT School of Management Studies MBA application within the notified window.
  3. Wait for the institute shortlist. SMS MNNIT highlights CAT-percentile-based shortlisting, while the institute admission page lists multiple accepted entrance tests.
  4. Attend Group Discussion and Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  5. Complete document verification, fee payment and institute registration after selection.

The School's current page states CAT-percentile-based shortlisting followed by GD and PI. MNNIT's central admission page also lists CAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and XAT as accepted routes for regular MBA admission.

What is the MNNIT MBA CAT cutoff?

There is no permanent published CAT percentile that guarantees admission. SMS MNNIT states that candidates are shortlisted on the basis of CAT percentile for GD and PI.

Because only 77 seats are listed for the full-time MBA, a candidate should treat the shortlist threshold as a competitive filter rather than as a guaranteed admission score.

What should you do with cutoff numbers found online?

Separate official MNNIT shortlisting criteria from unofficial estimates. A CAT percentile reported by a student or education portal can be used as a reference point, but it should not be presented as an official MNNIT closing cutoff.

What is the MNNIT MBA curriculum like?

The programme uses case-based teaching, presentations, group discussions, role plays, workshops, field surveys and project work. Students encounter subjects such as managerial economics, accounting, operations research, research methodology, computer applications, financial management, HR, marketing, MIS and organisational behaviour.

Later semesters add professional and open electives, industry survey, a six-to-eight-week summer internship and a research project. Current curriculum material also includes areas such as financial analytics, marketing analytics, data analytics, business intelligence and supply-chain analytics.

What is the MNNIT MBA placement record?

The School's official 2024-26 placement highlight reports 67 students in the batch, ₹13.96 LPA highest package, ₹7.76 LPA average package and ₹9 LPA median package. The top 10% average is also shown as ₹9 LPA.

The official highlight shows BFSI as the largest sector, followed by IT & ED Tech and FMCG/Consulting. Recruiters shown include ICICI Bank, EY, HDFC Bank, TCS, Accenture, Volvo, IndiaMART and others.
