PDEU M.Tech Admission 2026

PDEU's published M.Tech rules divide admissions into government seats through ACPC and All India seats handled by PDEU. The rules have used GATE for an early merit-based phase, followed by a regular process for eligible candidates.

Specialisations include areas such as Petroleum Engineering, VLSI Design, Energy Systems, Data Sciences, Cyber Security, Electrical Power Systems and other engineering disciplines. Always check the current programme-specific eligibility.

How to apply to PDEU?

  1. Choose the exact school and programme.
  2. Check the seat category: Gujarat/ACPC, All India, NRI or another applicable category.
  3. Keep entrance-exam scorecards, Class 10/12 marksheets, graduation records where applicable and identity documents ready.
  4. Register through the relevant PDEU online admission portal.
  5. Complete the application, upload documents and pay the applicable fee.
  6. Track merit lists, counselling, interview or GDPI notices as applicable.
  7. After an offer, complete fee payment and reporting within the stated deadline.
Do not mix counselling systems.

ACPC, PDEU's university-level process and School of Management MBA selection are separate mechanisms. A student should follow the route attached to their seat category.

PDEU Admission FAQs

Is PDEU admission open for 2026?

PDEU's main website currently promotes Admissions 2026. The exact programme-wise application status and deadline should be checked on the relevant official admission page.

How can I get admission to PDEU B.Tech?

Follow the current B.Tech admission notice and the correct seat category route. PDEU has used JEE Main/Gujarat entrance routes, with ACPC involved for applicable Gujarat seats.

Does PDEU accept JEE Main?

Yes. PDEU's official undergraduate admission page lists JEE Main for B.Tech admission. The exact use of the score depends on the admission category and current rules.

What was PDEU B.Tech cutoff 2025?

In the official 2025 Round 1 All India cutoff, Computer Engineering was 310 and CSE/Business Systems was 366 for General, while ECE was 882 and ICT was 938. These should be used only as previous-cycle benchmarks.

What exams are accepted for PDEU MBA?

Published School of Management material has considered CAT, XAT and NMAT for MBA. Some older documents mention additional tests, so the current 2026 notice should be treated as definitive.

What is PDEU MBA eligibility?

Published rules require a recognised bachelor's degree with 50% marks, with a relaxation to 45% for reserved categories. Final-year students can apply subject to the cycle conditions.

Does PDEU MBA have GDPI?

Yes. Published selection criteria include written ability, group discussion and personal interview after entrance-score shortlisting.

When should I apply for PDEU MBA?

The 2025 cycle's reported application deadline was 28 February. Use January-February as a preparation window, but follow the current official notice for the actual 2026/next-cycle deadline.

How does PDEU M.Tech admission work?

PDEU's rules use ACPC for government seats and a PDEU process for All India seats. GATE-qualified candidates can enter the merit-based phase; non-GATE candidates may be considered under the regular process if eligible.

Can I get PDEU admission without JEE Main?

This depends on the programme and seat category. Do not assume the JEE Main route applies to every PDEU course; MBA, M.Tech and science programmes have their own admission mechanisms.

Does PDEU have an entrance test for MBA?

PDEU MBA selection is primarily based on recognised management entrance scores in published cycles, followed by institute-level selection components. Check the current 2026 rules for the accepted exams.

Where is PDEU located?

PDEU is located at Raisan, Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
