SOA BBA & Integrated MBA: Quick Answer

SOA offers BBA and a five-year Integrated MBA through the Institute of Business and Computer Studies. Both are management-entry programmes after Class 12 and are included in SOA's SAAT programme ecosystem.

Why Consider Management at SOA?

Students choosing a management programme after Class 12 should compare curriculum, annual fee, programme duration, internships, placement evidence and flexibility for postgraduate study. SOA's NIRF 2025 Management rank was 66, while the published programme fees differ between BBA and Integrated MBA.

SOA BBA & Integrated MBA FAQs

What is SOA BBA eligibility?

10+2 in any stream with 50% aggregate, with the published category relaxation where applicable.

What is SOA BBA fees?

The published annual academic fee is ₹1.90 lakh, subject to the current fee schedule and additional charges.

What is SOA Integrated MBA fees?

The published annual academic fee is ₹2.50 lakh, subject to the current 2026 schedule and additional charges.

Is BBA better than Integrated MBA?

BBA offers a more flexible undergraduate route, while Integrated MBA suits students who want a continuous five-year management pathway.

Does the SOA MBA placement figure apply to BBA?

No. The cited NIRF Management data is postgraduate-category reporting and should not be treated as a BBA-specific outcome.
