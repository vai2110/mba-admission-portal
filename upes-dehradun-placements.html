UPES Placements: Latest Reported Numbers

UPES currently states that 92% of students were placed in 2024-25 and that the university had 800+ recruiters on campus in 2025. It also displays a ₹1.3 crore highest package secured by an alumnus.

92%Placements in 2024-25
800+Recruiters on campus in 2025
₹1.3 CrHighest package secured by an alumnus
Important interpretation:

The ₹1.3 crore figure is explicitly described as secured by an alumnus. It should not be treated as the typical package for current students. Course- and school-level outcomes are much more useful for decision-making.

Student decision rule:

Before paying fees, ask for the latest programme-specific placement report, number of students in the denominator, median/average CTC, fixed pay, role mix and the number of offers actually accepted.

Recruiters, Roles & Career Outcomes

UPES placement stories and recruiter references include organisations such as Microsoft, SLB, Accenture, ONGC, Bain & Company and others. The exact recruiter and role mix changes by school and cohort.

What to check

  • Exact job title and function.
  • Fixed pay versus total CTC.
  • Location and joining conditions.
  • Number of offers for your programme.

Do not assume

  • University-wide highest package is your likely salary.
  • B.Tech CSE data applies to BCA.
  • MBA outcomes apply equally to every specialisation.
  • Placement percentage alone indicates role quality.

Internships & Career Preparation

UPES states that summer internships are mandatory for graduate and postgraduate students, with durations ranging from eight weeks to six months. Its EDGE programme includes employability preparation, aptitude training, mock interviews, resume and LinkedIn workshops, industry mentoring and career guidance.

Student Questions & Practical Inference
