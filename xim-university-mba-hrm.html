XIM MBA-HRM: Programme Overview

MBA-Human Resource Management is a two-year specialised management programme offered by the School of Human Resource Management at XIM University. It is designed for students who want to build careers in human resources, talent management, organisational development, compensation, employee relations and people strategy.

Best fit

MBA-HRM is a better fit than MBA-BM when your intended career is clearly people-function focused. If you want a broad general-management route, compare it with the XIM MBA-BM programme.

MBA-HRM Eligibility 2026

The published XIM criteria require a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 55% aggregate marks from a recognised university. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the stated completion requirements.

MBA-HRM Selection Process

Selection is not based only on the entrance score. XIM shortlists candidates and evaluates them through its interview/selection process, with academic profile and other relevant factors considered. Check the current call letter and admission bulletin for the exact stages.

What to prepare

  • HR fundamentals and business awareness
  • Current affairs and organisational issues
  • Clear career motivation
  • Academic and work-experience questions

Do not assume

  • A high test score alone guarantees admission.
  • Work experience is mandatory.
  • HR roles are limited to recruitment.

MBA-HRM Careers & Placements

Typical career areas include talent acquisition, HR business partnering, learning and development, compensation and benefits, employee relations, organisational development and people analytics. XIM announced 100% summer placements for the MBA-BM and MBA-HRM 2025–27 batch in April 2026.

Placement reality

Summer placement success is a positive signal, but it should not be treated as a final-placement guarantee. Evaluate role quality, recruiter mix, salary distribution and the career path attached to the role.

See the XIM placements guide for broader placement context.
