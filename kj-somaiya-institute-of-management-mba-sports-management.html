KJ Somaiya MBA Sports Management: Quick Answer

For 2027-29

KJSIM's MBA Sports Management is a two-year full-time programme with a 30-seat intake. It combines core management learning with sports-specific modules and industry exposure. The institute also offers one full-tuition scholarship for a deserving Sports Management student based on sports achievement, academics, leadership and communication.

MBA Sports Management Important Dates

The current 2027-29 admission page lists 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM as the application deadline for MBA-SM.

MBA Sports Management Eligibility

KJSIM's management-programme eligibility requires a minimum three-year graduation with 50% aggregate marks from a recognised university. Final-year students can apply subject to the institute's conditions.

How Do You Get Admission to KJSIM MBA Sports Management?

For 2027–29, the application deadline currently listed is 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM. The institute considers the entrance score, academic history and applicant profile; sports participation can contribute to the selection score.

  1. Submit the separate MBA-SM application on the KJSIM portal.
  2. Provide a valid accepted entrance score: CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT 2027, MAT 2026, ATMA 2027, PERA-CET 2027 or CUET 2027, as listed for this programme.
  3. Upload academic records and sports achievements/participation certificates, if applicable.
  4. Complete the programme's PSA and PI stages and any further assessment notified by KJSIM.
  5. Check the merit list and seat allotment; offers depend on merit position and available seats.
  6. Pay the admission fee through the candidate portal within the offer deadline.

MBA Sports Management Curriculum

The curriculum blends general management with sports-specific subjects. KJSIM lists areas such as advanced Excel, business communication, human behaviour and HR in sports, sports marketing research, financial management in sports, legal and ethics in sports management and related specialised subjects.

The programme also provides international immersion opportunities, industry visits, volunteering, practical workshops, masterclasses and conferences/conclaves.

Sports Management Career Opportunities

The programme is designed for careers across sports organisations and allied businesses. Potential paths include sports marketing, sponsorship and licensing, event management, club/team management, digital marketing, communication, sports retail, sports technology and brand partnerships.

The institute says students can work across managerial functions while applying those skills to sports properties and entities.

Who Should Choose MBA Sports Management?

Strong fit

Students who specifically want a career in sports business, events, leagues, clubs, sponsorship, sports marketing or allied sports companies.

Compare carefully

If you want a conventional finance/consulting/operations MBA without a sports focus, the flagship MBA may offer broader optionality.

MBA Sports Management: Student FAQs

Do I need to be a professional athlete to apply for MBA-SM?

The institute's selection criteria award weight for sports participation at different levels, but the programme eligibility itself is based on graduation marks. Submit certificates for the level of participation or achievement you have; do not assume only national-level athletes can apply.

How many seats are available in MBA Sports Management?

KJSIM lists 30 seats for MBA-SM.

Which entrance exams are accepted for MBA-SM?

The current admission page lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT 2027, MAT 2026, ATMA 2027, PERA-CET 2027 and CUET 2027.

How much is the MBA-SM fee?

The published 2026–28 fee total is ₹15.13 lakh over two years. Hostel and living costs are separate.

Does KJSIM offer a scholarship for Sports Management?

The programme page describes one full-tuition scholarship for a deserving Sports Management student, subject to the institute's published eligibility and selection conditions. Confirm the current application requirements directly with KJSIM.

What careers can MBA Sports Management lead to?

The programme is intended for sports-business roles across sports organisations, leagues, clubs, events, sponsorship, marketing, operations and related businesses. Check the programme curriculum and internship opportunities before choosing it over a general MBA.
