KJ Somaiya MBA Sports Management: Quick Answer

For 2027-29

KJSIM's MBA Sports Management is a two-year full-time programme with a 30-seat intake. It combines core management learning with sports-specific modules and industry exposure. The institute also offers one full-tuition scholarship for a deserving Sports Management student based on sports achievement, academics, leadership and communication.

MBA Sports Management Important Dates

The current 2027-29 admission page lists 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM as the application deadline for MBA-SM.

MBA Sports Management Eligibility

KJSIM's management-programme eligibility requires a minimum three-year graduation with 50% aggregate marks from a recognised university. Final-year students can apply subject to the institute's conditions.

How to Get Admission to MBA Sports Management?

For the current cycle, KJSIM lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT and CMAT 2027 as accepted entrance routes. Sports Management shortlisting considers the applicant profile, entrance score and academic history.

  1. Submit the KJSIM application.
  2. Provide an accepted entrance score.
  3. Clear programme-specific shortlisting.
  4. Complete PSA/PI/WAT components as notified.
  5. Receive the merit and seat-allocation decision.

MBA Sports Management Curriculum

The curriculum blends general management with sports-specific subjects. KJSIM lists areas such as advanced Excel, business communication, human behaviour and HR in sports, sports marketing research, financial management in sports, legal and ethics in sports management and related specialised subjects.

The programme also provides international immersion opportunities, industry visits, volunteering, practical workshops, masterclasses and conferences/conclaves.

Sports Management Career Opportunities

The programme is designed for careers across sports organisations and allied businesses. Potential paths include sports marketing, sponsorship and licensing, event management, club/team management, digital marketing, communication, sports retail, sports technology and brand partnerships.

The institute says students can work across managerial functions while applying those skills to sports properties and entities.

Who Should Choose MBA Sports Management?

Strong fit

Students who specifically want a career in sports business, events, leagues, clubs, sponsorship, sports marketing or allied sports companies.

Compare carefully

If you want a conventional finance/consulting/operations MBA without a sports focus, the flagship MBA may offer broader optionality.
