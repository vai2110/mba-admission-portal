What is ABV-IIITM Gwalior known for?

Quick answer

ABV-IIITM Gwalior is an autonomous Institute of National Importance funded by the Ministry of Education. Its academic model combines information technology, engineering and management, with programmes ranging from B.Tech and five-year integrated degrees to M.Tech, MS, MBA and Ph.D.

The institute was established in 1997. Its current departments include Computer Science & Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Management Studies and Engineering Sciences.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior Admission 2026

B.Tech and five-year integrated programmes use JEE Main followed by JoSAA/CSAB counselling. The institute's 2026 admission notice for these first-semester programmes was issued for CSE, EEE, Mathematics & Scientific Computing and integrated IT programmes.

For MBA, the institute's 2026 notice required a valid CAT score, with GD/PI conducted by ABV-IIITM. M.Tech admissions can use GATE/CCMT and institute-level vacancies; the institute also published a GATE/Non-GATE admission round in July–August 2026.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior Fees

The official 2026 fee notice lists a ₹1,68,750 grand total for a new B.Tech/IMT/IPG admission when institute fee, hostel, mess, one-time payments and refundable hostel-mess security are combined for the stated admission charges.

For MBA, the same notice lists ₹1,53,750 for a new admission under the same combined first-payment structure. The fee is not simply tuition: hostel, mess and one-time payments materially affect the amount payable.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior campus and hostel

The institute is located on Morena Link Road in Gwalior. Separate hostel accommodation is available for boys and girls. The official 2026 fee notice includes hostel room/electricity and mess charges separately from institute fees.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior FAQs

Is ABV-IIITM Gwalior an IIIT and an Institute of National Importance?

Yes. ABV-IIITM Gwalior is an autonomous Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It was established in 1997 and focuses on the integration of IT, engineering and management.

What is ABV-IIITM Gwalior famous for?

The institute is particularly known for its technology-focused undergraduate and integrated programmes, especially B.Tech CSE, five-year B.Tech+M.Tech and B.Tech+MBA programmes. Its curriculum explicitly combines IT with management and emerging technology areas.

Is ABV-IIITM Gwalior good for CSE?

Its B.Tech CSE programme covers areas such as AI/ML, data science, IoT, cloud computing, security and networking. The 2023–24 annual report lists ₹22.11 LPA average CTC and ₹65 LPA highest CTC for B.Tech CSE placements.

Does ABV-IIITM Gwalior accept JEE Main?

Yes. B.Tech CSE, B.Tech EEE, B.Tech Mathematics & Scientific Computing and the five-year integrated IT programmes use JEE Main followed by JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

What is the highest package at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For the 2023–24 placement session, the institute's annual report lists ₹65 LPA as the highest CTC for B.Tech CSE, B.Tech+M.Tech and B.Tech+MBA. This is a programme/session-specific figure, not a guaranteed package.

What is the average package at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

It varies by programme. In the 2023–24 report, the average CTC was ₹22.11 LPA for B.Tech CSE, ₹22.68 LPA for B.Tech+M.Tech and ₹16.18 LPA for B.Tech+MBA.

What is the fee of ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For new 2026 admissions, the official fee notice lists a combined ₹1,68,750 for B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG and ₹1,53,750 for MBA under its stated institute, hostel, mess, one-time and security-payment structure.
