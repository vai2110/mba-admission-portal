What is PDEU Gandhinagar known for?

Quick answer

Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), formerly Pandit Deendayal Petroleum University, is a private university in Raisan, Gandhinagar with a strong identity around energy, engineering, technology and management. It operates schools including Technology, Petroleum Technology, Management and Liberal Studies.

PDEU is NAAC A++ accredited with a CGPA of 3.52/4. In NIRF 2025, it was ranked 98 in Engineering and 89 in Management. The university also highlights research centres, industry projects, internships, international exposure and an innovation/incubation ecosystem.

The important student decision is programme-specific: B.Tech admission, MBA admission and other UG/PG routes do not follow one identical process. Check the exact school and admission category before comparing fees or cutoffs.

PDEU Admission 2026: How does admission work?

B.Tech

PDEU's undergraduate engineering admission page lists JEE Main/Gujarat entrance routes for B.Tech. Gujarat seats may involve ACPC, while PDEU also conducts its own admission process for applicable All India categories. Exact rules differ by seat category and year.

MBA

The School of Management uses national management-test scores. Published admission material has considered CAT, XAT and NMAT, followed by written ability, group discussion and personal interview components. Check the current MBA notice for the 2026-28 cycle.

M.Tech

PDEU's published M.Tech rules use GATE for the GATE route and a separate university process for eligible non-GATE candidates; Gujarat government seats are handled through ACPC.

PDEU FAQs: High-intent student questions

Is PDEU a good university?

PDEU can be a strong private-university option for students interested in engineering, energy, technology and management. Its NAAC A++ accreditation and NIRF 2025 ranks of 98 in Engineering and 89 in Management provide useful context, but branch-level placement and total cost should drive the final decision.

What is PDEU famous for?

PDEU is particularly known for its energy and petroleum heritage, engineering programmes, School of Technology, School of Petroleum Technology and School of Management. It has also built research, innovation and industry-linked initiatives around energy and technology.

What is the B.Tech admission process at PDEU?

The route depends on seat category. PDEU's admission material uses JEE Main/Gujarat entrance routes for B.Tech, with ACPC involved for Gujarat government seats and university processes for applicable All India categories.

Does PDEU accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. PDEU's undergraduate admission page has listed JEE Main for B.Tech admission. The exact counselling and seat-category rules should be checked in the current admission notice.

What is PDEU B.Tech fees?

The latest directly accessible official B.Tech rule sheet available for reference lists ₹1,24,500 tuition per semester for All India/Gujarat seats, plus other fees and refundable deposits. Because that document is for 2024-25, it should not be treated as the final 2026-27 fee.

What is PDEU MBA eligibility?

Published MBA rules require a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks, with a relaxation for reserved categories. Final-year candidates may apply subject to the cycle's conditions.

Which exams are accepted for PDEU MBA?

Published School of Management material has considered CAT, XAT and NMAT, while some historical cycles also listed MAT/other tests. Use the current 2026 MBA notice for the definitive list.

What is PDEU MBA placement salary?

NIRF 2025 reports a ₹8.50 lakh median salary for the School of Management's PG 2-year cohort in 2023-24, with 117 of 121 graduates placed. This is more useful for ROI analysis than a highest-package claim.

What is PDEU's NIRF rank?

In NIRF 2025, PDEU ranked 98 in Engineering and 89 in Management. These are category-specific ranks and should not be interpreted as a universal rank for every PDEU programme.

Does PDEU provide hostel facilities?

Yes. PDEU has residential facilities, but hostel fees and availability can change by year. The official 2024-25 B.Tech rules listed ₹96,000 annual hostel fee plus a ₹5,000 refundable deposit, with accommodation subject to availability.

Where is PDEU located?

Pandit Deendayal Energy University is located at Raisan, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, near the Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar urban region.

When should I apply to PDEU?

Start tracking the official admission page before the relevant entrance-exam results. The 2025 B.Tech Round 1 window was in early June, while the 2025 MBA application deadline was reported in late February. These are planning references, not fixed future deadlines.
