What is IRMA PGDM(RM-X)?

PGDM(RM-X) is IRMA's Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Rural Management-Executive). It is designed for professionals seeking management education connected to rural markets, cooperatives, development and underserved segments.

Who should consider it?

It is relevant for professionals who want to deepen management capability without treating rural management as a narrow agriculture-only subject. The official programme ecosystem connects business, social-sector and rural-transformation challenges.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) Eligibility

Eligibility is programme-specific and IRMA maintains a separate current eligibility notice for PGDM(RM-X). Because professional-experience and qualification rules can change by batch, applicants should use the current official notice before applying.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) Selection Process

IRMA publishes a separate selection process and admissions calendar for the executive programme. Candidates should not transfer the flagship MBA(RM) selection formula to RM-X.

Important

Always verify the current batch's qualification, experience, test/interview requirements and final-selection criteria from IRMA's official admissions notice.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) Fees

IRMA maintains a dedicated fee-structure page for PGDM(RM-X). The exact fee should be taken from the current batch's official notice because executive-programme fees can differ from the flagship MBA(RM).

Learning & Career Orientation

The executive programme sits within IRMA's broader rural-management ecosystem. The institute's work spans cooperatives, agribusiness, rural finance, social enterprise, sustainability, development and public-policy-linked management.

PGDM(RM-X) Placements

IRMA publishes a separate placement page for RM-X. The latest official page available reports an average package of ₹13.67 LPA for the second self-sponsored batch and lists recruiters including Samunnati Financial Services, ITC, GCMMF/Amul Dairy, TVS Srinivasan Service Trust and Jai Kisan.

Career Options After Executive Rural Management

Potential roles reflected in the official placement examples include projects/management, operations, sales and marketing, CSR, IT, finance and account management. Career direction depends on prior experience and the organisation you join.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) FAQs

Is PGDM(RM-X) meant for fresh graduates?

It is an executive rural-management programme; applicants should check the current official eligibility notice for the required professional background.

Does RM-X have separate placements?

Yes. IRMA maintains a separate PGDM(RM-X) placement page.

What is the latest official average package for RM-X?

The official page currently reports an average package of ₹13.67 LPA for the second self-sponsored batch.

Is RM-X the same as MBA(RM)?

No. MBA(RM) is IRMA's flagship two-year residential management programme, while PGDM(RM-X) is the executive programme with separate admissions and programme requirements.
