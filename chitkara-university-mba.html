Chitkara University MBA Overview

Chitkara offers full-time MBA programmes with a range of management specialisations and a minor in AI in Business. The programme is positioned around industry collaboration and practical business learning.

Who is the MBA suitable for?

Graduates looking for a private-university MBA in the Chandigarh region and interested in management, analytics, technology-enabled business or sector-focused specialisations can consider it.

Eligibility & Admission

Eligibility and entrance requirements are intake-specific. Candidates should use the current 2026 MBA admission instructions for accepted examinations, academic requirements, application stages and interview details.

Career & Placement Considerations

Chitkara's career material emphasises industry connections and campus recruitment. MBA aspirants should evaluate outcomes by specialisation, batch and role rather than relying only on the highest package.

For ROI, compare tuition and hostel cost with median/average outcomes, recruiter mix, internships and the roles relevant to your target function.

MBA FAQs

Does Chitkara offer a full-time MBA?

Yes. Chitkara lists full-time MBA programmes with multiple specialisations.

Does the MBA include AI?

The university lists full-time MBA programmes with a minor in AI in Business.
