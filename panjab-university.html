What is Panjab University Chandigarh known for?

Quick answer

Panjab University (PU), Chandigarh is a multidisciplinary public university with 74 teaching and research departments, 15 centres/chairs and a 550-acre campus. It is not a management-only university: its academic portfolio spans arts, science, engineering, law, pharmacy, business, languages, education and social sciences.

PU was established in 1882 as the University of Punjab at Lahore and is now based in Chandigarh. The university reports NAAC A++ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.68 and was ranked 35th among universities in NIRF 2025.

The university also has 202 affiliated colleges across Punjab and regional centres at Muktsar, Ludhiana and Hoshiarpur. Students should distinguish between programmes offered at the Chandigarh campus, regional centres and affiliated colleges.

Panjab University Fees

There is no single “Panjab University fee”. Fees vary substantially by programme, department, professional course and whether the student is studying at the main campus, regional centre or through CDOE.

For example, the 2026 UBS handbook lists regular MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR) tuition at ₹31,675 per year, while MBA Entrepreneurship and MBA Business Data Analytics have higher programme-specific tuition. These figures illustrate why fees must be checked course-by-course.

Students should budget separately for tuition, examination/university charges, hostel, mess, books, transport and programme-specific charges.

Panjab University campus and student life

PU's main campus covers about 550 acres across Sector 14 and Sector 25, with academic, administrative, residential, sports and recreational infrastructure. The university also has a large affiliated-college network and regional centres.

Hostel availability, department location, commuting and programme-specific facilities should be checked separately for the course selected.

Panjab University FAQs

Is Panjab University a government university and is it UGC recognised?

Panjab University is a public university based in Chandigarh. It is a central institution in the Indian higher-education system and its degrees are awarded by the university. Students should still check the current programme notice for any course-specific regulatory or professional approval requirements.

What is Panjab University famous for?

PU is a multidisciplinary university rather than a single-stream college. It has 74 teaching and research departments and programmes across engineering, science, pharmacy, law, management, commerce, arts, humanities, languages and education. This breadth is one of its main strengths.

What is the NIRF rank of Panjab University in 2025?

Panjab University was ranked 35th in the NIRF 2025 University category and 57th in the Overall category. It also reports NAAC A++ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.68.

Does Panjab University accept CUET for admission?

There is no single CUET rule for the entire university. Admission is programme-specific. PU uses different routes including merit, PU-CET and institute or counselling processes for different courses. Students should check the exact course's 2026 admission notice instead of assuming CUET applies to every programme.

What is the average package at Panjab University?

There is no reliable single university-wide average package because placement reporting differs by institute. For example, UIET reports ₹9.11 LPA average CTC for 2023–24, while NIRF reports a ₹12.42 LPA median for the UBS management cohort graduating in 2022–23. These figures should be compared only with their source and programme context.

Which course is best in Panjab University?

There is no one best course for every student. Engineering, management, law, pharmacy, science and humanities have different career outcomes. A sensible comparison should consider the course syllabus, admission route, total fee, placements or higher-study outcomes, campus, career goal and your eligibility.

Is Panjab University good for engineering?

UIET provides a strong engineering option within PU, and its official 2023–24 placement data reports 314 offers, 50+ companies and ₹9.11 LPA average CTC. Students should compare branch-wise outcomes rather than using one university-wide placement number.

How much does it cost to study at Panjab University?

There is no common fee. For 2026–27, the official fee handbook lists different totals by programme; for example, UBS MBA is ₹2,00,025 for the academic year, while UIET B.E. Information Technology is ₹1,36,855. Hostel, mess and personal expenses are separate.
