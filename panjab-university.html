What is Panjab University Chandigarh known for?

Quick answer

Panjab University (PU), Chandigarh is a multidisciplinary public university with 74 teaching and research departments, 15 centres/chairs and a 550-acre campus. It is not a management-only university: its academic portfolio spans arts, science, engineering, law, pharmacy, business, languages, education and social sciences.

PU was established in 1882 as the University of Punjab at Lahore and is now based in Chandigarh. The university reports NAAC A++ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.68 and was ranked 35th among universities in NIRF 2025.

The university also has 202 affiliated colleges across Punjab and regional centres at Muktsar, Ludhiana and Hoshiarpur. Students therefore need to distinguish between programmes offered at the Chandigarh campus, regional centres and affiliated colleges.

Panjab University Fees

There is no single “Panjab University fee”. Fees vary substantially by programme, department, professional course and whether the student is studying at the main campus, regional centre or through CDOE.

For example, the 2026 UBS handbook lists regular MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR) tuition at ₹31,675 per year, while MBA Entrepreneurship and MBA Business Data Analytics have higher programme-specific tuition. These figures illustrate why fees must be checked course-by-course.

For a university-level decision, students should budget separately for tuition, examination/university charges, hostel, mess, books, transport and programme-specific charges.

Panjab University Placements: why there is no single university-wide package

Placement outcomes at PU are programme/department dependent. UBS has its own management placement process, while engineering, pharmacy, law and other schools can have different recruiters and outcomes. It is therefore misleading to quote one “Panjab University average package” for every student.

AreaHow placement should be evaluated
Management / UBSInstitute placement reports, recruiter mix, average/median/highest CTC and placement coverage
Engineering / UIETBranch-wise placement, recruiters, average/median and batch size
PharmacyProgramme-specific NIRF/department data; higher studies can be significant
LawRecruitment + litigation/chambers + internships + higher studies
Arts/ScienceHigher studies, research, competitive exams and programme-specific employment rather than one central package

The university's NIRF submissions show materially different outcomes across programme categories. The dedicated placement page therefore separates department-level evidence instead of creating a misleading university-wide salary number.

Panjab University campus and student life

PU's main campus covers about 550 acres across Sector 14 and Sector 25, with academic, administrative, residential, sports and recreational infrastructure. The university also has a large affiliated-college network and regional centres.

The Chandigarh campus environment can be particularly relevant for students comparing public-university education with private residential universities. Hostel availability, department location, commuting and programme-specific facilities should be checked separately.

Panjab University FAQs

Is Panjab University a government university?

Panjab University is a public university established under the Panjab University Act and is based in Chandigarh.

What courses is Panjab University famous for?

PU has strong and diverse offerings across management, engineering, law, pharmacy, science, arts, humanities, languages and other disciplines. It should not be viewed as an MBA-only university.

What is the Panjab University NIRF rank?

PU was ranked 35th in the University category and 57th in the Overall category in NIRF 2025.

How does Panjab University admission work?

It depends on the programme. PU operates separate UG and PG admission portals, while entrance-based courses such as B.Sc (Hons), B.Pharmacy and several professional programmes have their own admission processes.

Does Panjab University have placements?

Yes, but placement is largely programme/department dependent rather than one centralized university-wide placement outcome.
