What is Panjab University known for MBA?

Quick answer

For management aspirants, the key Panjab University destination is University Business School (UBS), which offers the flagship MBA along with MBA in International Business, Human Resource, Entrepreneurship and Business Data Analytics. The flagship MBA admission is based on CAT followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

UBS is a university department rather than a standalone private B-school. That matters for students comparing cost, academic environment and placement process. The 2026 UBS handbook lists a very low published tuition component for the regular MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), while case-unit and university charges are separate.

Do not confuse Panjab University with Chandigarh University. Panjab University is located in Chandigarh and is a public university established in 1882.

Panjab University MBA Admission 2026

For the regular UBS MBA programmes, the 2026 admission handbook states that candidates need a recognised bachelor's degree with the prescribed minimum marks and must appear in CAT 2025. The selection process then includes GD and PI. Category-specific eligibility relaxations apply as per university rules.

Campus, student activities and academic environment

UBS operates within Panjab University's Chandigarh campus ecosystem. Its published material highlights active cells and clubs including the Placement Cell, Alumni Relation Cell, Business Cell, Entrepreneurship Development Cell, Investment Club, Consulting Club and Environment Club.

Students should also consider the wider university ecosystem, access to Chandigarh's corporate market and the relatively low tuition of the regular UBS MBA when evaluating total ROI.

Who should choose Panjab University UBS for MBA?

Strong fit if…

  • You want a low-cost public-university MBA with established corporate recruitment.
  • You are comfortable with CAT + GD + PI rather than a direct private-college admission route.
  • You want specialised options such as HR, IB, Entrepreneurship or Business Data Analytics.

Think carefully if…

  • You want a highly residential private-B-school experience with a single integrated campus model.
  • You are comparing only highest packages; average/median and placement coverage matter more.
  • You need a fixed 2026 fee beyond the published components; university charges can change.

Panjab University MBA FAQs

Is Panjab University good for MBA?

UBS is an established public-university management school with a strong placement history and unusually low published tuition for its regular MBA programmes. Suitability depends on your CAT score, target role and preference for a public-university environment.

Which entrance exam is required for UBS MBA?

For the regular UBS MBA programmes in the 2026 cycle, the handbook specifies CAT 2025, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

What is the Panjab University MBA fee?

For MBA/MBA(IB)/MBA(HR), the 2026 handbook lists ₹31,675 per annum as tuition, with separate case-unit and other university charges.

What is the latest UBS placement package?

For 2024-25, reported placement data shows ₹25.11 LPA highest CTC and ₹12.95 LPA average CTC, with 105 students placed and an 85% placement rate.

Is Panjab University the same as Chandigarh University?

No. Panjab University is a public university in Chandigarh. Chandigarh University is a separate private university in Mohali, Punjab.
