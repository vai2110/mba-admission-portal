LPU Placements: Current Snapshot

LPU's official placement page currently reports a large recruiter ecosystem. It highlights 2,225+ recruiters, 6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies, 400+ recruiters that also recruit from IITs/IIMs/NITs and an average salary of ₹10.23 lakh for the top 25% of placed students.

Placement metricPublished figureHow a student should read it
Recruiters2,225+Shows scale of recruiter engagement, not a guarantee for every course.
Fortune 500 offers6,000+Indicates access to large corporate recruiters; offers are distributed across years/programmes.
IIT/IIM/NIT recruiter overlap400+Indicates recruiter breadth.
Average salary, top 25%₹10.23 lakhThis is specifically for the top 25% of placed students, not the university-wide average.
The most important warning

Do not convert the ₹10.23 lakh top-25% figure into “average LPU package”. It is a selected-cohort metric. Your decision should use programme-specific and typical outcomes wherever the university publishes them.

LPU Placements 2025-26: Latest Reported Highlights

LPU's July 2026 placement update reported that more than 2,000 students had secured offers of ₹10 lakh or above. The update also reported a top-10% average of ₹14 lakh and a highest offer of ₹60 lakh from Microsoft. It mentioned 6,000+ offers and several other high-value technology offers, including ₹48.6 lakh at Amazon and ₹43.5 lakh at CRED. An MBA student was reported at ₹40.85 lakh.

Reported outcomeFigureHow to interpret it
Students with ₹10L+ offers2,000+Strong headline outcome, but not the percentage of the entire university graduating class.
Top 10% average₹14LTop-cohort average, not all-student average.
Highest offer₹60LHighest reported offer; exceptional outcome.
MBA reported offer₹40.85LShows a high-value management outcome, not a typical MBA package.

Do not use the highest package as your salary expectation

The highest offer is useful for understanding the ceiling of the recruiter ecosystem. It is not a forecast of what an average student will earn.

LPU Average Package: How Should You Read the Numbers?

Students commonly search for “LPU average package”. The problem is that one university-wide average can hide huge differences between B.Tech CSE, management, computer applications, design, law and other programmes. A top-25% average is also fundamentally different from a median.

MetricWhat it tells youWhat it does not tell you
Highest packageUpper end of the placement range.Typical salary.
Top 10% averagePerformance of a high-performing subset.Median or average of the whole batch.
Top 25% averagePerformance of the upper quarter of placed students.Outcome for every student.
Median salaryMiddle point of the salary distribution when available.Role quality or growth by itself.
Placement percentageShare of eligible students placed under the stated definition.Whether the job is your preferred role.
Best question to ask the placement team

“For my exact programme, what percentage of eligible students were placed, what was the median salary, what were the top recurring roles, and how many students entered core roles versus sales/support roles?”

LPU Placement Process: How Does It Work?

LPU's Division of Career Services coordinates placement support and campus recruitment. Students generally need to meet the eligibility criteria specified by each recruiter and participate in the university's recruitment process.

StageStudent action
Career preparationBuild resume, aptitude, communication and technical/domain skills.
Eligibility checkCheck company-specific CGPA, backlog, degree and skill criteria.
RegistrationRegister for eligible drives through the university's placement system.
AssessmentComplete aptitude, coding, group discussion, case or role-specific assessments as required.
InterviewAttend technical, HR, business or domain interviews.
OfferSelected students receive the employer's offer subject to company terms.

LPU Placement Eligibility

LPU states that students who opt for placement support and meet the minimum eligibility criteria defined by respective companies can participate in campus recruitment drives. There is therefore no single placement eligibility rule for every recruiter.

Possible criterionWhy it matters
Academic performanceSome companies set CGPA/percentage cut-offs.

Degree / branchTechnical recruiters can specify eligible branches.
SkillsCoding, analytics, communication or domain skills may be tested.

Internships and Live Projects at LPU

LPU highlights internships, training, live projects and industry exposure as part of its career ecosystem. For students, internship quality matters because it can affect both skill development and final placement readiness.

Good internship outcome

  • Real business/technical problem.
  • Named project contribution.
  • Measurable result.
  • Mentor or manager reference.
  • Portfolio evidence.

Do not optimise for brand only

  • A famous brand with no meaningful work can be weaker than a smaller firm with real responsibility.
  • Choose an internship aligned with your target job.

Programme-wise Placement Reading

Do not compare every LPU programme using one placement figure. A B.Tech student and an MBA student are recruited for different roles and through different assessment processes.

For B.Tech / MCA / BCA

  • Ask about software eligibility.
  • Check coding-test patterns.
  • Compare technical role versus support role.
  • Build a strong GitHub/project portfolio.

For MBA / BBA

  • Check function-wise roles.
  • Separate sales from analytics/consulting roles.
  • Ask about fixed versus variable pay.
  • Compare internship-to-PPO opportunities.

Is LPU Placement ROI Good?

ROI should be calculated using net education cost and the realistic salary/role outcome for your programme. Scholarship can make a major difference. A ₹10 lakh package is not equivalent to ₹10 lakh take-home, and variable pay or location costs also matter.

A practical ROI checklist
  1. Calculate total tuition after scholarship.
  2. Add hostel, mess, laptop and living costs.
  3. Estimate a realistic—not highest—starting salary.
  4. Check role quality and career progression.
  5. Compare with two or three alternative colleges.

LPU Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at LPU?

LPU's 2025-26 update reported a highest offer of ₹60 lakh, with Microsoft named for that offer.

What is the average package at LPU?

The official placement page currently reports ₹10.23 lakh as the average salary of the top 25% placed students. This is not the average for every student.

Are placements guaranteed at LPU?

No. Students must meet recruiter-specific eligibility and clear the selection process.

Which companies recruit from LPU?

LPU highlights a wide range of technology, consulting, BFSI, consumer and other recruiters, including Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, Cognizant, Bosch, Capgemini, IBM, Wipro and others.

Does every programme get the same package?

No. Salaries, roles and recruiter eligibility vary substantially by programme and student profile.
