Overview

DMS IIT Delhi is the Department of Management Studies at IIT Delhi. Its flagship full-time MBA is a two-year programme. DMS also offers MBA (Telecommunication Systems Management) and Executive MBA programmes.

Admission Process

1. Apply

Submit the DMS application in the notified window with CAT and academic details.

2. Shortlisting

CAT, academics, work experience, gender diversity and other published profile components are considered.

3. Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for the personal interview.

4. Offer & registration

Selected candidates complete fee payment, document verification and registration.

Eligibility — MBA

The 2026–28 criteria specify 60% aggregate / 6.00 CGPA in graduation, with a 5 percentage-point relaxation for SC/ST/PwD candidates. Final-year candidates can apply subject to institute conditions.

Fees

The official DMS admission page states ₹12.00 lakh tuition for the entire full-time MBA, payable over four semesters. Its published 2025–27 example lists ₹3,00,000 tuition + ₹22,400 other charges + ₹8,600 institute residence fee per semester for General/OBC/EWS.

Tuition₹12.00 lakh total
Other charges₹22,400/semester in 2025–27 example
Institute residence fee₹8,600/semester in 2025–27 example
Refundable deposit / messNot separately stated on the cited MBA admission page

What the fee covers

The ₹12 lakh figure is programme tuition; institute and residence charges are separate published line items.

What the fee does not cover

Do not treat tuition as an all-in living cost. Check hostel, mess and personal expenses separately.
