Overview

Admission Process

1. CAT

Take CAT and use the score for the DMS application cycle.

2. Apply to DMS

Submit the institute application with CAT, academic and profile details in the notified window.

3. Shortlisting

DMS calculates its published shortlisting index and invites shortlisted candidates.

4. Personal Interview

Attend the interview and complete document requirements.

5. Offer & admission

Selected candidates complete fee payment, verification and registration.

Eligibility — MBA

The 2026–28 criteria require a bachelor’s degree/equivalent involving at least three years of education after 10+2 and 60% aggregate / 6.00 CGPA. SC/ST/PwD candidates receive a 5 percentage-point relaxation to 55% / 5.50 CGPA. Final-year candidates and candidates awaiting results can apply subject to DMS conditions. The entrance test used for the published cycle is CAT.

Fees

DMS states ₹12.00 lakh tuition for the entire full-time MBA, payable over four semesters. The fee details for the 2025–27 batch separately list other institute and residence charges.

Tuition — General/OBC/EWS₹3,00,000 per semester; ₹12.00 lakh for four semesters
Other charges₹22,400 per semester for the 2025–27 batch
Institute residence fee₹8,600 per semester when hostel accommodation is allotted
SC/ST/PwD tuition₹0 tuition for SC/ST/PwD candidates in the 2025–27 batch; other applicable charges remain payable

Fee calculation

For the 2025–27 batch, General/OBC/EWS candidates had ₹3,00,000 tuition + ₹22,400 other charges per semester. An institute residence fee of ₹8,600 per semester applies when hostel accommodation is allotted. Across four semesters, the tuition and other-charge components total ₹12.90 lakh; residence charges are additional where applicable. This is not an all-inclusive living-cost estimate.

What the fee covers

The ₹12 lakh figure is programme tuition. Other institute and residence charges are separately stated by DMS. The exact amount and payment mode for a new batch are specified in its admission offer.

What the fee does not cover

Do not treat tuition as an all-in living cost. Mess, personal expenses and other living costs should be planned separately unless the applicable offer explicitly includes them.

FAQs

What is the DMS IIT Delhi MBA fee?
DMS states ₹12 lakh tuition for the full-time MBA. Other institute and residence charges are separate.
What is the minimum graduation percentage?
The 2026–28 criteria require 60% aggregate or 6.00 CGPA, with a 5 percentage-point relaxation to 55% / 5.50 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
Is the DMS shortlisting cutoff of 45 a CAT percentile?
No. For 2026–28, 45.00 was the minimum MBA shortlisting-index cutoff for the General category. The index combines CAT and profile components, so it should not be interpreted as a CAT percentile.
Does DMS IIT Delhi guarantee hostel accommodation?
No. IIT Delhi’s current postgraduate guidance says hostel accommodation is constrained and admission does not guarantee an on-campus hostel seat.
What does the DMS MBA curriculum cover?
DMS describes the MBA curriculum as covering areas such as Information Systems, Finance, Marketing, Strategic Management, Human Resource Management and Operations Management, with electives and project work.
Does DMS use CAT?
Yes. CAT contributed 65 marks in the 2026–28 shortlisting index.
Is 2027–29 admission open?
No. The 2027–29 dates on this page are planning estimates based on the latest published cycle and are not official dates.
