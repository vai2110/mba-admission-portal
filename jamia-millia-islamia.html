Jamia Millia Islamia: quick facts students usually need

What is Jamia Millia Islamia? Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) is a central university in Jamia Nagar, New Delhi. It was founded in 1920 and is recognised as a central university by an Act of Parliament. Its programmes cover arts, humanities, languages, social sciences, sciences, engineering, education, law, architecture, dentistry, management and other professional areas.

How to apply for Jamia Millia Islamia admission

JMI applications are submitted online through the university admission portal. The exact form, entrance route and document list depend on the programme.

  1. Open admission.jmi.ac.in and select the relevant academic session.
  2. Open the current prospectus and locate the exact programme name and programme code. Check whether admission is through JMI's test, CUET, JEE Main, NATA, NEET, NCET or another route.
  3. Register with a working email address and mobile number. Use the same details throughout the application and admission process.
  4. Enter personal, academic and category details exactly as shown on official documents. Check name, date of birth, marks and subject combinations before payment.
  5. Upload the photograph, signature and supporting documents in the file format and size specified on the portal.
  6. Pay the application fee through the available online payment method and save the submitted form and payment receipt.
  7. Download the admit card when released. Check the reporting time, test centre, permitted documents and instructions.
  8. After the test or national exam result, check the JMI admission portal for interview lists, merit lists, waiting lists, counselling and document-verification instructions.
  9. If selected, complete admission formalities and pay the course fee within the deadline stated in the selection notice.
Do not miss this: JMI's prospectus says that once the application fee is paid, the form cannot be edited through the normal edit option. If a correction is needed, follow the specific email procedure and deadline stated in the prospectus. A correction request is not automatically accepted.

JMI cutoff, merit list and selection list

JMI does not have one university-wide cutoff. The cutoff or closing score depends on the programme, entrance test, category, seat matrix, applicant performance and selection stages. For CUET/JEE/NEET-based programmes, the applicable national test score and JMI's programme-specific merit process matter.

  • Check the programme code and entrance test before comparing cutoffs.
  • Read the selection list and waiting list for the correct academic session.
  • Where JMI conducts an interview or group discussion, a written-test score alone may not determine final selection.
  • Do not treat an unofficial coaching estimate as an official closing cutoff.
  • JMI's admission portal publishes results, notices and selected-candidate lists: official portal.

For a reliable cutoff comparison, use the official list for the same programme, year and category. If JMI has not published a closing score, it should be reported as unavailable rather than guessed.

JMI campus, hostel, facilities and student life

The university is located in Jamia Nagar, South-East Delhi. JMI's official overview describes the campus as partly residential and spread over 239 acres. Academic facilities include faculty buildings, laboratories, libraries, research centres and other learning spaces. The university also has sports, co-curricular and cultural activities.

Does JMI provide hostel accommodation?

JMI has university hostel facilities, but a seat should not be assumed for every admitted student. Hostel allocation is governed by separate eligibility, availability, application and allotment rules. Check the current hostel notice and fee schedule from JMI rather than relying on old hostel fee figures.

Is Jamia Millia Islamia in Delhi?

Yes. The main campus is at Jamia Nagar, New Delhi – 110025. Applicants should check the address and reporting venue in their admit card or department notice because an entrance test or admission activity may be held at a different centre.
