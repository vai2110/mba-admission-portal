Jamia Millia Islamia: University Overview

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) is a central university located in Jamia Nagar, New Delhi. Its academic portfolio spans multiple disciplines, including management, tourism and hospitality, healthcare management, engineering, humanities, law and social sciences. The university's official website and current prospectus should be treated as the primary sources for programme availability, admission notices and fee updates.

For applicants considering management education, the relevant academic umbrella is the Faculty of Management Studies (FMS), which brings together three departments with programmes across business management, tourism and hospitality, and healthcare-related management.

Faculty of Management Studies at JMI

The Faculty of Management Studies was established in 2022. It has three constituent departments: Department of Management Studies; Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management; and Department of Hospital Management and Hospice Studies. The faculty's programmes span management, tourism, hospitality, hospital and healthcare management, and pharmaceutical management.

Its academic approach combines management foundations with applied learning, research, industry interaction and discipline-specific professional training. Programme-level admission requirements and fee structures differ by department and course, so applicants should verify the current prospectus rather than assume that all programmes share the same process.

Campus, Accreditation and Recognition

In the NIRF 2025 Management category, Jamia Millia Islamia is ranked 28th. This is the Management-category position, not the university's overall NIRF rank. Use the year and category whenever quoting the number.

JMI is a central university and its official website identifies the institution as NAAC A++ accredited. Applicants should consult official university publications and current national ranking releases for dated ranking positions; a ranking should always be stated with its category and year.

Which JMI department should you check?

Management applicants should compare the Department of Management Studies with the Healthcare and Hospital Management and Tourism and Hospitality departments. These are separate departments with different eligibility rules, seat counts, selection processes and fee structures. Do not treat every programme under the Faculty of Management Studies as one MBA admission route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jamia Millia Islamia a central university?

Yes. JMI is a central university established by an Act of Parliament.

When was the Faculty of Management Studies established?

The Faculty of Management Studies was established in 2022.

How many departments are under JMI's Faculty of Management Studies?

There are three: Management Studies, Tourism and Hospitality Management, and Hospital Management and Hospice Studies.

Does JMI offer a full-time MBA?

Yes. The Department of Management Studies lists an MBA (Full Time) programme with a four-semester duration. Confirm current intake and fees in the latest prospectus.

Where can I find JMI MBA placement information?

Use the dedicated JMI MBA placements page and the official Department of Management Studies placement brochures.
