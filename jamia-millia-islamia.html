Jamia Millia Islamia MBA: Overview

Quick answer:

The JMI MBA is a 4-semester full-time programme offered by the Department of Management Studies. For the 2026–27 admission cycle, the university scheduled its MBA entrance test for 2 June 2026. The programme has 60 seats and the 2026–27 prospectus lists an annual fee of ₹26,875.

JMI's Faculty of Management Studies describes the Department of Management Studies as offering MBA programmes across full-time, International Business, Entrepreneurship & Family Business and Executive formats. The dedicated MBA page here focuses on the flagship full-time MBA.

JMI MBA Admission Schedule 2026

The official 2026–27 entrance-test schedule lists the MBA under programme code M55. The entrance test was scheduled as a multi-city examination on 2 June 2026 from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM. JMI states that the list of candidates called for interview is to be displayed within 20–25 days of the entrance test, with the selected-candidate list displayed within 10 days of the last interview/group discussion date.

JMI MBA Eligibility & Admission Process

Eligibility

For MBA, MBA (International Business) and MBA (Innovation & Entrepreneurship), JMI's 2026 entrance-test schedule specifies a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade.

Admission process

  1. Apply through the JMI university admission system for the MBA programme.
  2. Appear for the JMI entrance test on the notified date.
  3. Follow the university's subsequent interview/group-discussion and document-verification process wherever applicable.
  4. Complete admission formalities and fee payment after selection.

The university's programme page identifies the MBA as a regular, full-time programme of four semesters. The 2026 schedule should be treated as the controlling source for the admission cycle rather than older admission pages.

JMI MBA Entrance Test & Cutoff

JMI's official 2026–27 schedule confirms a university entrance test for the MBA; it does not publish a fixed CAT/XAT/CMAT percentile cutoff for the flagship MBA in the cited admission notice. Therefore, this page does not present coaching-site estimates as an official JMI cutoff.

JMI MBA Curriculum

The official 2025–26 DMS placement brochure describes the full-time MBA as a two-year programme built around foundation subjects, advanced management courses, electives and project work. The published course structure includes the following areas.

SemesterRepresentative subjects / components
IManagement Concepts & Organisational Behaviour, Quantitative Methods, Managerial Economics, HRM, Business Communication, Financial Accounting, Marketing Management, Information Systems & Emerging Technologies in Management
IIAccounting for Managerial Decision-Making, Business Analytics, Economic Environment of Business, Financial Management, Management Science, Business Research Methods, Operations Management, Organisation Structure, Dynamics & Change
IIIBusiness Policy & Strategic Management, Comprehensive Corporate Analysis, Legal Environment of Business, Summer Training Project and electives
IVBusiness Ethics & Corporate Governance, Dissertation Project, E-Business and electives

Elective areas shown in the brochure include Finance, Marketing, Human Resources and Operations, with subjects such as Financial Modelling, Security Analysis & Portfolio Management, Digital Marketing, Strategic HRM and Quality Management.

JMI MBA Placements

The latest DMS placement brochure currently available on JMI's official website is the 2025–26 Placement Brochure. Its placement-statistics page reports outcomes for the 2023–25 batch: highest CTC ₹24 LPA, average CTC ₹10.8 LPA and median CTC ₹10 LPA. The same brochure says the department's current MBA batch profile is 186 students across the full-time MBA, MBA (International Business) and MBA (Entrepreneurship & Family Business) cohorts, so placement figures should not be assumed to represent only the 60-seat regular MBA intake unless the source explicitly says so.

Highest CTC₹24 LPA
Average CTC₹10.8 LPA
Median CTC₹10 LPA

For broader historical outcomes, NIRF Management 2025 reports for the PG two-year programme group: 97 students placed out of 103 graduates in 2023–24, with a median salary of ₹12.60 lakh. NIRF data is reported at the programme-group level and should not be rewritten as an MBA-only placement figure.

JMI MBA FAQs

Does JMI offer an MBA?

Yes. The Department of Management Studies offers a full-time MBA. The 2026–27 prospectus lists 60 seats.

What is the eligibility for JMI MBA?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade, as specified in the 2026–27 entrance-test notice.

Does JMI MBA accept CAT?

The 2026–27 admission schedule specifies a JMI university entrance test for the MBA. It does not list CAT as the entrance route for this programme.

What is the JMI MBA fee?

The 2026–27 prospectus lists ₹26,875 as the total fee payable per year for the regular MBA.

What was the latest published JMI DMS placement package?

For the 2023–25 batch, the official 2025–26 DMS placement brochure reports ₹24 LPA highest, ₹10.8 LPA average and ₹10 LPA median CTC.
