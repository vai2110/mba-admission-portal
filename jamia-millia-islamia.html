Where Should You Check Programme Fees?

Fees vary by programme and admission category. Use the current JMI prospectus and the relevant department admission notice to confirm the payable amount for the programme and academic session. Do not assume the regular MBA fee applies to executive, international business, entrepreneurship, tourism or healthcare programmes.

Which Faculty of Management Studies Programme Is Relevant?

The Faculty of Management Studies covers distinct academic areas: the Department of Management Studies, Tourism and Hospitality Management, and Hospital Management and Hospice Studies. Each has its own programme mix and admission requirements. Use the department-specific guides below instead of treating them as one MBA curriculum.

Management Studies · Tourism and Hospitality · Hospital Management

Where Can You Find Department-wise Placement Information?

Jamia has separate academic departments within its Faculty of Management Studies. Placement outcomes should therefore be checked against the relevant programme and department report rather than treating every programme as one MBA placement cohort. The dedicated JMI placements page explains the publicly available reporting and its limitations.
