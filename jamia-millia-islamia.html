Jamia Millia Islamia MBA: Overview

Quick answer:

The JMI MBA is a 4-semester full-time programme offered by the Department of Management Studies. For 2026–27, the university scheduled the MBA entrance test for 2 June 2026. The programme has 60 seats and the prospectus lists an annual fee of ₹26,875.

JMI MBA Admission Schedule 2026

The official 2026–27 schedule lists MBA under programme code M55. The entrance test was scheduled for 2 June 2026 from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM in multi-city mode. JMI states that interview shortlisting and selected-candidate lists follow the notified schedule.

Stage2026–27 schedule
MBA entrance test2 June 2026, 2:30 PM–5:30 PM
Interview shortlistWithin 20–25 days from entrance test
Selected candidatesWithin 10 days from the last interview/group discussion

JMI MBA Eligibility & Admission Process

Eligibility

Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade, as specified for the 2026–27 admission cycle.

Admission process

  1. Apply through the JMI admission system.
  2. Appear for the JMI entrance test.
  3. Follow the university's interview/group-discussion and selection notices, wherever applicable.
  4. Complete document verification and admission formalities after selection.

JMI MBA Entrance Test & Cutoff

JMI's 2026–27 schedule specifies a university entrance test for the MBA. It does not publish a fixed CAT/XAT/CMAT percentile cutoff for the flagship MBA in the cited notice. This portal therefore does not present unofficial coaching estimates as an official JMI cutoff.
