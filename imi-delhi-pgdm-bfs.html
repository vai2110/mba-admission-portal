IMI Delhi PGDM B&FS: What the Programme Is Built For

IMI Delhi's PGDM in Banking & Financial Services is a dedicated two-year programme designed for careers in banking, financial services, investment, risk, fintech, insurance and related finance functions. The programme is affiliated with the CFA Institute University Affiliation Program and provides access to an eight-terminal Bloomberg lab.

Who should choose B&FS?

Choose B&FS if finance is your intended career domain and you want a curriculum built around financial markets, banking, fintech, risk and investment rather than a general-management programme with finance electives.

IMI Delhi PGDM B&FS Admission: Eligibility & Selection

For the recent cycle, IMI Delhi required a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent. CAT, XAT and GMAT were accepted for the two-year programme route. The institute states that there is no fixed cutoff and uses holistic evaluation.

ParameterRecent requirement
GraduationMinimum 50%
Entrance testsCAT / XAT / GMAT
CutoffNo fixed published cutoff
SelectionTest score + profile + video assessment + PI
FocusFinance and banking suitability alongside overall profile

The video assessment tests thought process and communication. The personal interview considers business awareness, career goals, attitude, people skills, communication and personal/professional initiatives.

IMI Delhi B&FS Curriculum: Finance, Banking & Analytics

The programme's curriculum covers financial modelling, fintech, insurance, data analytics, financial markets and services, investment management, financial derivatives, risk management and other banking and financial-services subjects. The institute also provides research resources such as Bloomberg terminals and databases.

AreaWhat students learn
BankingBank management, financial services and banking operations
MarketsFinancial markets, securities, derivatives and investment management
AnalyticsBusiness analytics and data-driven finance
FintechTechnology-led financial services and emerging models
RiskRisk management, regulation and financial decision-making

A dedicated two-month summer internship between the first and second year adds industry exposure. The institute also highlights access to an eight-terminal Bloomberg lab exclusively for programme participants.

Bloomberg Lab, Resources & Campus Experience

For finance-focused students, the eight-terminal Bloomberg lab is one of the programme's most distinctive resources. IMI also provides access to research-based content through resources such as WOS, Scopus, CMIE Prowess and Statista.

The Delhi campus gives students access to the broader NCR business environment, while the institute's international linkages can support exchange and short global immersion opportunities.

Why the resource stack matters

Finance students should evaluate not just the name of the programme but whether they will actually use market terminals, databases, modelling, analytics and industry projects during the two years.

Who Should Choose IMI Delhi PGDM B&FS?

Good fit

  • You want a finance/BFSI career.
  • You are interested in markets, investment, risk or fintech.
  • You want Bloomberg and finance-specific learning resources.
  • You are comfortable with a specialised career path.

Think twice

  • You are unsure about finance and want broad functional flexibility.
  • You dislike quantitative or market-oriented subjects.
  • You are choosing the programme only because of its highest package.
