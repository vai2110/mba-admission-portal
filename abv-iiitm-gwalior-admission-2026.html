How does ABV-IIITM Gwalior admission work?

The route depends on the programme.

B.Tech and integrated IT programmes use JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB. The two-year MBA uses a valid CAT score followed by GD/PI. M.Tech admissions use GATE/CCMT and, where seats remain, institute-level GATE/Non-GATE rounds.

ABV-IIITM B.Tech and IPG Admission 2026

The institute's first-semester 2026–27 admission notice covers B.Tech CSE, B.Tech EEE, B.Tech Mathematics & Scientific Computing, Integrated B.Tech IT + M.Tech IT and Integrated B.Tech IT + MBA.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior MBA Admission 2026

The 2026 MBA notice required a first-class bachelor's degree with at least 60% marks or 6.50 CGPA, Mathematics/Statistics as one of the courses, and at least three years of education after 10+2. Admission uses a valid CAT score plus institute-conducted GD/PI, with academics and work experience also considered.

The main application deadline published for the 2026–27 MBA intake was 16 May 2026. A spot-round notice was subsequently published in June 2026.

ABV-IIITM M.Tech and MS Admission 2026

For 2026, the institute published an M.Tech GATE/Non-GATE odd-semester admission round from 16 July to 5 August 2026, covering CSE, Information & Cyber Security, IT, Wireless Networks & Computing, IC Design & Technology and Autonomous Systems & Machine Intelligence, subject to available seats after CCMT.

How to apply to ABV-IIITM Gwalior

B.Tech / IPG

Register for JEE Main, qualify, participate in JoSAA/CSAB and complete institute reporting as instructed.

MBA

Use the institute MBA application after meeting eligibility, submit CAT details, then follow GD/PI and selection instructions.

Do not use the MBA application route for B.Tech/IPG admissions. Each programme has its own authority and schedule.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior Admission FAQs

How can I get admission to ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech?

For B.Tech CSE, EEE and Mathematics & Scientific Computing, admission is through JEE Main followed by JoSAA/CSAB counselling. You do not apply through the MBA or institute-level MBA portal.

What is the admission process for ABV-IIITM Gwalior B.Tech CSE?

Qualify JEE Main, participate in JoSAA counselling and select ABV-IIITM Gwalior if your rank and seat availability permit. Any subsequent institute reporting requirements are given in the 2026 admission notice.

What is the eligibility for ABV-IIITM Gwalior MBA?

The 2026 notice required a first-class bachelor's degree with at least 60% marks or 6.50 CGPA, Mathematics/Statistics as one course and at least three years of education after 10+2. A valid CAT score is also required for the selection process.

Does ABV-IIITM Gwalior accept CAT for MBA?

Yes. The institute's two-year MBA admission process uses a valid CAT score, followed by GD/PI conducted by ABV-IIITM Gwalior. Academic qualifications and work experience are also given weightage.

When does ABV-IIITM Gwalior MBA admission start?

The institute says its MBA admission process starts around February/March for the July session. For the 2026–27 cycle, the formal advertisement was dated 15 January and the application deadline was 16 May 2026.

Is there an institute-level M.Tech admission at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

Yes. After the CCMT process, the institute can publish vacancy-based institute-level rounds. In 2026 it published a GATE/Non-GATE round from 16 July to 5 August, subject to vacant/supernumerary seats.

When should I expect ABV-IIITM Gwalior admission dates?

B.Tech/IPG dates are linked to the JEE Main and JoSAA/CSAB cycle, while MBA applications generally begin around February/March. If a future programme schedule is unavailable, these are useful planning windows, but the institute's notification is final.
