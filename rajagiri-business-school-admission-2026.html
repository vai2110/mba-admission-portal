Rajagiri Business School Admission 2026: current status

Quick answer

RBS opened applications on 20 December 2025. The official admission page lists 25 June 2026 as the tentative closing date. Shortlisted candidates are invited for GD, PI and Business Awareness Test, with previous academics, work experience and non-academic achievements also considered at different stages.

RBS uses phased GDPI rounds based on the entrance exam reported by the candidate. Dates can change, so applicants should verify the current official notice before acting.

RBS PGDM Eligibility 2026

Candidates need a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks or a master's degree. Final-year students completing graduation in March-May 2026 were eligible to apply.

The qualifying bachelor's degree can be from any discipline, subject to RBS's recognition and documentation requirements.

How to apply for RBS PGDM 2026

  1. Open the official RBS admission process page.
  2. Use the linked online application portal.
  3. Select the programme(s) you want to apply for.
  4. Enter graduation and entrance-test details accurately.
  5. Upload marksheets/certificates and requested documents.
  6. Pay the ₹1,000 application fee.
  7. Monitor your registered email for GDPI communication.

Rajagiri Business School Cutoff 2026

RBS does not currently publish a fixed official 2026 percentile cutoff on its PGDM admission page. It states that shortlisting is based on eligibility and entrance examination scores.

For that reason, this page does not present an unsupported “official cutoff”. Students should aim for the strongest possible accepted score and prepare for GD, PI and BAT because the entrance test is only one part of the selection journey.

Rajagiri Business School Admission FAQs

What is the eligibility for RBS PGDM 2026?

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks or a master's degree is required. Final-year students completing graduation in March-May 2026 were also eligible.

Which entrance exams are accepted by RBS?

RBS lists CAT, CMAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT and GMAT for PGDM admission 2026.

What is the RBS application fee?

The application fee is ₹1,000.

Does RBS conduct GD and PI?

Yes. Shortlisted candidates are invited for GD and PI, and the stated process also includes a Business Awareness Test.

Can I submit more than one entrance-test score?

Yes. RBS says candidates can report scores from more than one listed entrance exam, but GDPI can be attended only once for 2026 admissions.

What is the official RBS cutoff for 2026?

RBS does not publish a fixed 2026 cutoff percentile on the current admission page. It states that shortlisting is based on eligibility and entrance-exam scores.

When does the RBS 2026 application close?
