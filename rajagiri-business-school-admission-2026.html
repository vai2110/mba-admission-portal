Rajagiri Business School Admission 2026: current status

Quick answer

RBS opened applications on 20 December 2025. The official PGDM admission page lists 25 June 2026 as the tentative closing date. Admission is based on entrance-test performance followed by the RBS selection process, including GD, PI and Business Awareness Test.

*RBS states that dates can be changed during the process. Always use the current official admission notice before submitting an application.

Who is eligible for RBS PGDM 2026?

Applicants need a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks in aggregate for all parts of the examination, or a master's degree. Students completing graduation in March-May 2026 were also eligible to apply for the 2026 intake.

The rule is broad: the qualifying bachelor's degree can be from any discipline, subject to the institute's recognition requirements.

Rajagiri Business School Cutoff 2026

RBS does not publish a fixed 2026 cutoff percentile on its current PGDM admission page. It states that shortlisting is based on eligibility and entrance-exam scores.

Therefore, do not publish a made-up “official cutoff”. Students should target the strongest possible score in CAT/CMAT/MAT/KMAT/XAT/GMAT and prepare for GD, PI and BAT because the process does not end with the entrance test.

Rajagiri Business School Admission FAQs

What is the eligibility for RBS PGDM?

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks or a master's degree is required.

Which exams are accepted for RBS PGDM 2026?

CAT, CMAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT and GMAT are listed by RBS for PGDM admission 2026.

What is the RBS application fee?

The application fee is ₹1,000.

Does RBS conduct GD and PI?

Yes. Shortlisted candidates are invited for Group Discussion and Personal Interview, and the admission process also lists a Business Awareness Test.

Can I submit more than one entrance score?

Yes. RBS says candidates can report scores from more than one listed entrance exam, although GDPI can be attended only once for 2026 admissions.
