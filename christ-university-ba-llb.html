Christ BA LLB: Quick Answer

For applicants

BA LLB (Honours) is a 5-year integrated programme with 10 semesters. The central eligibility requirement is at least 45% in Class 12. The 2026 UG law selection list uses ET, SA, MP and PI for BA LLB at listed campuses.

What Is BA LLB at Christ?

BA LLB combines undergraduate arts/social-science learning with professional legal education. Christ's law programme includes areas such as constitutional law, corporate and commercial law, criminal law and justice, legal aid and practical legal training.

The integrated format is intended for students who want to enter legal education directly after Class 12 rather than completing a separate bachelor's degree first.

What Is the BA LLB Selection Process?

Christ's 2026 Bangalore Central UG selection document lists BA LLB under ET + SA + MP + PI. The ET is the entrance stage, SA assesses written skills, MP is a 90-second presentation and PI is the interview.

The final venue and date are controlled by the e-admit card. Candidates should not assume the same centre or date across all campuses.

Is There a BA LLB Cutoff?

Christ publishes an academic eligibility threshold rather than a single universal final closing-rank table for BA LLB. The relevant published minimum is 45% in Class 12.

Do not convert the 45% eligibility mark into a guaranteed admission cutoff. Selection also depends on the prescribed entrance and assessment process.

What Do You Study?

The programme combines law with interdisciplinary learning. Christ's published programme information highlights corporate and commercial law, constitutional/public law, criminal law, legal aid, internships, moot-court and practical legal training.

Students interested in corporate law should compare the programme's internships and commercial-law exposure; students targeting litigation should pay attention to mooting, research and courtroom-oriented training.
