GGSIPU MCA Overview

GGSIPU MCA is a postgraduate computer applications option within the university ecosystem. Current 2026 notices show MCA admissions through NIMCET and GGSIPU CET routes, with separate round-wise cutoff and counselling updates.

GGSIPU MCA Admission 2026

The 2026 counselling notices include fresh registration for MCA through NIMCET/CET, followed by centralized online counselling, choice filling and sliding/seat-allocation stages as notified. Applicants should follow the route applicable to their score.

GGSIPU MCA Eligibility

Eligibility is programme-specific and can include a qualifying bachelor's degree plus required mathematics/computer-related subjects or other conditions. Always check the 2026 PG brochure for the exact programme code before applying.

GGSIPU MCA Cutoff

GGSIPU publishes separate MCA cutoff reports for NIMCET and CET. Compare the closing rank by admission route and round; the two routes should not be treated as interchangeable.

Cutoff tip

Use the exact route, round, category and institute when evaluating your chances.

GGSIPU MCA Fees

MCA fees vary by the institute or University School. Check the current fee notification for the exact campus and include tuition, university charges, hostel and other recurring costs in your budget.

GGSIPU MCA FAQs

Does GGSIPU MCA accept NIMCET?

Current 2026 counselling notices include MCA through NIMCET as well as CET routes, subject to the applicable programme rules.

Are NIMCET and CET cutoffs comparable?

No. They are different admission routes and should be evaluated separately.
