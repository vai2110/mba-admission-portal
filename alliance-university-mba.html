Alliance University MBA: What Does the Programme Offer?

Alliance University's MBA is a two-year full-time management programme. The current curriculum combines core management subjects with AI, data-driven decision making, digital business and elective specialisations.

The programme is particularly relevant for students who want a management degree in Bengaluru and want to combine business fundamentals with analytics, technology or functional specialisation.

2 YearsFull-time MBA across four semesters
32,000+Alumni globally, as reported by Alliance
₹36 LPAHighest MBA package reported for 2024-25/2025-26
Who should consider this MBA?

Graduates looking for a broad management foundation with options in finance, marketing, HR, operations, business analytics, AI and digital transformation.

Alliance University MBA Admission 2026

The current MBA programme pages describe a multi-stage selection process rather than a simple percentile cutoff. The process includes application, an Alliance Entrance Assessment, oral extempore presentation and personal interview assessment.

Alliance University MBA Eligibility & Entrance Exams

Current Alliance MBA programme material states a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks, with applicable relaxation for eligible categories. The exact programme page should be checked for any format-specific condition.

Which exams can help with MBA admission or scholarships?

Alliance's current MBA scholarship tables reference CAT, XAT, GMAT and NMAT at different score thresholds. These scores can also strengthen the application profile, but the university's current selection process may still include its own assessment, extempore and interview.

Alliance University MBA Fees 2026: Fee Bifurcation

Alliance's current MBA programme pages show different fee structures depending on the MBA format. Several current specialised MBA pages show a ₹25,000 registration fee + ₹4.5 lakh + ₹4 lakh + ₹4 lakh, giving a stated total of ₹13 lakh. MBA FinTech currently shows a stated total of ₹14 lakh with a different final instalment.

What the fee comparison tells you

The MBA is not priced as one universal programme. Your specialisation can change the total cost.

Budget separately

Hostel, food, travel, personal expenses and any programme-specific external costs should be checked separately.

Always confirm the fee attached to your exact MBA offer rather than using the lowest published MBA figure as your budget.
