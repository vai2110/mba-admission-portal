Does PUMBA accept CAT or CMAT for MBA admission?

For the regular MBA admission route, the official PUMBA programme information states that admissions are strictly based on merit and the institute's official selection procedure through MH-MBA-CET. Therefore, students should not treat a CAT or CMAT score as a substitute for the Maharashtra MBA CET route for the regular PUMBA MBA. Always check the latest PUMBA admission notice for the applicable academic year before applying.

PUMBA MBA Eligibility

The exact eligibility and admission instructions are issued for each academic cycle. PUMBA's official admission notices should be treated as the controlling source for the current year's requirements, registration steps and document rules. Students should complete the qualifying degree and examination requirements before participating in the admission process.

PUMBA MBA Admission Process 2026-27

The official PUMBA website is currently publishing MBA 2026-27 admission instructions, institute-level round notices and vacancy updates. The admission journey should therefore be read as a cycle-specific process rather than a permanent cutoff or date.

  1. Appear for the applicable Maharashtra MBA entrance process.
  2. Register through the admission process prescribed for the academic year.
  3. Follow the university/CAP or institute-level instructions applicable to the candidate.
  4. Complete document verification and fee payment after selection.
Important: PUMBA's official website currently lists 2026-27 MBA admission notices. Dates, rounds, vacancies and admission instructions can change during the cycle. Do not rely on an old cutoff or third-party admission promise.

PUMBA MBA Fees

PUMBA does not publish one permanent fee figure that should be treated as valid for every admission cycle. Fees are subject to university norms and the applicable admission notice. For this reason, CollegeDecoded is not displaying an estimated fee as an official number. Students should verify the fee payable for their specific admission year directly with PUMBA/Savitribai Phule Pune University.

PUMBA Placements

PUMBA operates a Placement & Corporate Relations Cell that coordinates summer internships and campus recruitment. The official PUMBA placement pages describe the cell as an interface between the department and industry and provide placement-related information through the institute's own portal. CollegeDecoded has not inserted an unsupported current average or highest package figure where a current official placement report was not available.
