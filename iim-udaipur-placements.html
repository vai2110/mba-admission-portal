IIM Udaipur reports placement outcomes separately for its Two-Year MBA and its one-year MBA programmes. The latest figures currently visible on the institute's admissions portal are from different graduating batches, so a single “IIM Udaipur average package” is misleading.

IIM Udaipur Placement Salary: How to Read It

Salary figures should be read alongside the programme, graduating batch and metric definition. For example, the GSCM 2024–25 audited report provides average, median and highest maximum earning potential, while the current IIM Udaipur admissions snapshot gives average and highest figures for the Two-Year MBA and DEM.

MetricWhy it mattersStudent use
Average maximum earning potentialShows the mean reported earning-potential figure for the cohort.Useful for broad benchmarking, but not the expected salary for every student.
MedianShows the middle of the reported salary distribution.Useful for understanding a more typical outcome where published.
Highest maximum earning potentialRepresents the top reported outcome.Should not be treated as the likely salary of a typical graduate.

IIM Udaipur Placement Process

The placement calendar varies across programmes. For the one-year GSCM programme, the institute states that placements typically begin in October, with companies able to conduct pre-placement presentations from September.

Pre-placement phase

  • Company and role presentations
  • Student interaction with recruiters
  • Role and eligibility communication
  • Applications for relevant openings

Selection phase

  • Recruiter-specific screening
  • Assessments, interviews or case processes as applicable
  • Final offers from participating recruiters
  • Programme-specific placement reporting

The exact process, schedule and eligibility can differ by recruiter and programme; students should not assume that the GSCM calendar is identical to the Two-Year MBA.

IIM Udaipur Internship and PPO Context

The Two-Year MBA includes a compulsory corporate summer internship after the first year. This internship sits between the first-year academic phase and the second-year elective-heavy phase and can provide practical exposure before final placements.

Two-Year MBA

Mandatory summer internship after Year 1. Students should evaluate the internship by function, project ownership, industry and relevance to their final-placement goals.

One-Year GSCM

The GSCM programme does not have a summer internship. Its one-year format instead uses industry projects, live supply-chain work and experiential learning within the academic schedule.

PPO caution: A pre-placement offer is not a universal outcome across programmes. Where an institute publishes PPO numbers, they should be read against the specific batch and programme.

Is IIM Udaipur Good for Placements?

IIM Udaipur can be a strong fit for students who value a management-focused curriculum and want access to roles spanning consulting, analytics, operations, supply chain, product and digital business. The right programme matters as much as the institute name: GSCM is built around supply-chain careers, DEM around digital enterprise, and the Two-Year MBA provides the broadest management route.

Strong fit if you want

  • A career transition through a full-time MBA
  • Access to multiple management functions
  • Analytics and experiential learning
  • Programme-specific career pathways

Check carefully if you need

  • A salary figure for the exact graduating batch you are targeting
  • Placement data for one specific function
  • A particular recruiter or sector guarantee
  • A direct comparison using only the highest package
Best way to compare: Match the programme, batch year, salary metric, recruiter mix and role mix before comparing IIM Udaipur with another B-school.

IIM Udaipur Placements FAQs

What is the IIM Udaipur average package?

IIM Udaipur reports different figures by programme and batch. The current admissions snapshot lists ₹18.77 LPA average maximum earning potential for the Two-Year MBA 2022–24, ₹22.54 LPA for GSCM 2023–24 and ₹21.72 LPA for DEM 2023–24.

What is the highest package at IIM Udaipur?

The highest figure in the current admissions snapshot is ₹47.66 LPA maximum earning potential for the Two-Year MBA 2022–24. GSCM 2024–25 separately reports ₹40.18 LPA.

Which companies recruit from IIM Udaipur?

Recruiters vary by programme and year. The latest GSCM audited report includes Accenture, British Petroleum, Cognizant, Delhivery, DHL, Infosys, Kinaxis, Tech Mahindra, Tiger Analytics and other organisations across multiple sectors.

Does IIM Udaipur have a summer internship?

The Two-Year MBA has a compulsory summer internship after the first year. The one-year GSCM programme does not include a summer internship.

Are IIM Udaipur placement figures the same for all MBA programmes?

No. The institute publishes programme-specific outcomes. Two-Year MBA, GSCM and DEM should be evaluated separately.

Where should students check placement reports?

Students should use the latest official IIM Udaipur placement material for the exact programme and graduating batch instead of relying on an older third-party salary figure.
