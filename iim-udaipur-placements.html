IIM Udaipur Placement Process

The placement experience is best understood as a combination of summer internship recruitment after the first year and final placement recruitment during the second year. Students should evaluate the process alongside their target function, prior work experience, academic profile and the type of role they want after MBA.

Summer internship

  • Mandatory component of the Two-Year MBA.
  • Completed after the first academic year.
  • Provides practical exposure before the second-year elective-heavy phase.

Final placements

  • Recruitment for full-time roles after programme completion.
  • Relevant for consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, product and general management aspirations.
  • Role quality should be assessed alongside compensation.

IIM Udaipur Placement Salary: How to Evaluate the Numbers

Students commonly search for average, median and highest salary. For a reliable comparison, use the institute's placement report for the exact batch and check whether the number refers to the flagship Two-Year MBA or a one-year MBA programme. IIM Udaipur also publishes separate programme information for DEM and GSCM, so their outcomes should not be merged with the flagship MBA without qualification.

IIM Udaipur Placements FAQs

Does IIM Udaipur have campus placements?

Yes. The Two-Year MBA has a structured placement ecosystem, alongside the compulsory summer internship after the first year.

Is IIM Udaipur placement data the same for every MBA programme?

No. The institute has separate one-year MBA programmes such as DEM and GSCM, and their career context should not automatically be merged with the flagship Two-Year MBA.

Should I compare average or median salary?

Use both where available, but median is generally more informative about the middle of the cohort. Always check the exact graduating batch and programme.
