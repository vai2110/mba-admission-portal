Great Lakes Chennai Placements: Latest Official Picture

Great Lakes publishes separate placement outcomes for PGPM and PGDM. The latest completed reports currently available on the institute website are PGPM Class of 2026 and PGDM Class of 2025. Their salary numbers should therefore be compared with the batch year clearly stated.

Which Great Lakes programme has the higher reported average CTC?

In the latest completed official reports available, PGPM Class of 2026 reports ₹18.1 LPA average CTC, while PGDM Class of 2025 reports ₹15 LPA. These are different cohorts and different programme structures, so the numbers should not be treated as a like-for-like guarantee for future batches.

How Students Should Read Great Lakes Placement Data

What looks positive

  • Both flagship programmes report strong average CTCs relative to their programme type.
  • Recruiter diversity extends beyond a single sector.
  • Consulting, analytics, technology and product roles are visible in PGPM outcomes.
  • PGDM has meaningful representation from finance, marketing, consulting and operations.

What to verify before deciding

  • Average CTC is not the same as your expected salary.
  • Role quality and location matter as much as headline CTC.
  • Placement outcomes differ between PGPM and PGDM and across cohorts.
  • Check the latest report for the exact programme and batch before paying fees.

Students comparing Great Lakes with another B-school should compare average and median CTC where available, role mix, recruiter quality, batch size, placement participation and the percentage of students receiving offers—not only the highest package.

Great Lakes Chennai Placements FAQs

What is the average package at Great Lakes Chennai?

The latest official PGPM Class of 2026 report lists ₹18.1 LPA average CTC, while the latest completed PGDM report available lists ₹15 LPA average CTC for the Class of 2025.

What is the highest package at Great Lakes Chennai?

The latest official PGPM Class of 2026 report lists ₹43 LPA. The PGDM Class of 2025 report lists ₹39.3 LPA.

Which has better placements, PGPM or PGDM?

The latest reported average CTC is higher for PGPM, but the batches are different. Students should compare the role mix and their own experience level rather than using salary alone to choose the programme.

Does Great Lakes have consulting placements?

Yes. Consulting is particularly prominent in PGPM 2026, where Great Lakes reports 56% of roles in consulting and 37% of the cohort employed in business consulting.

Does Great Lakes offer finance jobs?

Yes. Finance was the largest functional category in the PGDM 2025 report at 33% of offers, while PGPM also reported management accounting and financial-services-related opportunities.

Are Great Lakes placement figures guaranteed?

No. Published placement statistics describe past cohorts and should not be treated as a guaranteed salary or placement outcome for a future student.
