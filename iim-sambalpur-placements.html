IIM Sambalpur Placements: Latest Official Data

The latest final placement report published by IIM Sambalpur for the MBA 2023-25 batch reports ₹48.60 LPA highest CTC, ₹15.65 LPA average CTC and ₹15.03 LPA median CTC. A total of 216 companies participated, including 144 new companies.

Average vs Median vs Highest Package

Students should not judge the placement season only by its highest package. The average and median provide a much better starting point for an ROI decision.

Why is median important?

Median CTC reduces the impact of a few unusually high offers. For a student deciding whether the ₹21.13 lakh MBA cost is worthwhile, the median and role quality are more useful than the ₹48.60 LPA headline alone.

What Do IIM Sambalpur Students Get Hired For?

Strong career pathways

  • General management and leadership-track roles
  • Finance and BFSI
  • Consulting and business analysis
  • IT, analytics and technology consulting
  • Product management
  • Sales, marketing and customer roles
  • Operations and HR

What the placement data does not tell you

  • It does not guarantee a specific role for an individual student.
  • Highest CTC is not the typical salary.
  • Recruiter count does not equal offer count.
  • Domain share does not reveal the seniority or location of every role.
