Why should students look at median CTC?

The median helps show what the middle of the placed cohort received and prevents the highest package from becoming the only indicator of placement quality. For ROI decisions, average and median CTC should be considered alongside the total programme cost and the type of roles offered.

What Do IIM Sambalpur Graduates Get Hired For?

Career strengths visible in the report

  • General management and leadership roles form the largest reported domain.
  • Finance, consulting and IT/analytics provide multiple analytical career pathways.
  • Product management is represented as a separate hiring domain.
  • Sales and marketing remain an important part of both final and summer placement outcomes.

How to read the placement data

  • The highest CTC is an outlier and should not be treated as a typical salary.
  • Median CTC is important for understanding the middle of the placed batch.
  • Domain distribution matters if you have a specific career target.
  • Compare salary against the complete two-year cost, not tuition alone.

MBA-BA Placement Context

IIM Sambalpur's MBA in Business Analytics is a newer programme. The latest final placement report published by the institute is for the MBA batch, so it should not automatically be presented as a dedicated MBA-BA placement report.

What should an MBA-BA applicant check?

Look for programme-specific recruiter lists, analytics/product/consulting role counts, median CTC and placement outcomes once the institute publishes a sufficiently large MBA-BA placement cohort. Until then, use the institute-level MBA report only as context, not as a direct guarantee for MBA-BA outcomes.
