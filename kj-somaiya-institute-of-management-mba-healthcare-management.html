KJ Somaiya MBA-HCM: Quick Answer

For 2027-29

KJSIM's MBA Healthcare Management is a two-year full-time programme with a 30-seat intake. The institute describes it as a specialised management programme covering hospitals, pharmaceuticals, medical devices, healthcare analytics and policy. Shortlisting is profile-based.

MBA Healthcare Management Important Dates

The current 2027-29 KJSIM admission page lists 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM as the application deadline for MBA-HCM.

MBA Healthcare Management Eligibility

Applicants need a minimum three-year graduate programme with 50% aggregate marks from a recognised university. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's conditions.

KJSIM says the programme is open to graduates from varied backgrounds, while healthcare-related backgrounds such as pharmacy, biotechnology, dentistry and physiotherapy can be particularly relevant to the programme.

How Does KJSIM Select MBA Healthcare Management Applicants?

MBA-HCM is a profile-based programme. The institute evaluates the entrance score alongside academic profile, healthcare-related additional qualifications and performance in its assessment stages. It is not the same selection formula as the flagship MBA.

  1. Apply online for MBA-HCM by 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM.
  2. Submit a valid accepted entrance score: CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT or CMAT 2027.
  3. Complete profile-based shortlisting and submit the candidate profile/documents when invited.
  4. Attend the programme's PSA and competency-based PI; complete WAT as notified.
  5. Check the merit result and seat-allotment offer online.
  6. Pay the first-year fee through the institute portal after receiving the offer.

MBA Healthcare Management Curriculum

KJSIM's HCM curriculum is designed around the business side of healthcare. The institute highlights areas including hospital administration, pharma and medical-device management, healthcare analytics, quality, digital healthcare, operations and supply chain, and healthcare policy.

MBA-HCM Career Opportunities

The official programme page lists career paths across hospital administration, digital healthcare, healthcare marketing, HR, product management, patient-care support, finance, healthcare chains, data analytics and informatics, quality, consulting, operations, supply chain, sales and business development.

The programme also has an academic collaboration with K J Somaiya Medical College, Hospital and Research Centre, according to the institute's FAQ.

Who Should Choose MBA Healthcare Management?

MBA Healthcare Management: Student FAQs

Is KJSIM MBA Healthcare Management only for doctors?

No. The institute's eligibility allows graduates from any discipline who meet the minimum 50% aggregate requirement. Healthcare-related academic or professional experience can be relevant to the programme, but a medical degree is not stated as a universal requirement.

How many seats are available in MBA-HCM?

KJSIM lists an intake of 30 seats for MBA Healthcare Management.

Which entrance exams are accepted for MBA-HCM 2027–29?

The current admission page lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT and CMAT 2027. Check the official page for GMAT score validity and NMAT best-of-two-attempt rules.

What is the MBA-HCM selection process?

Shortlisting is profile-based. The published selection components include entrance score, competency-based PI, PSA, academic profile, work experience, HCM-related additional qualification and WAT.

What is the MBA-HCM fee and is hostel included?

The published 2026–28 fee total is ₹23.875 lakh, matching the flagship MBA fee schedule. Hostel is listed separately and should be added to the cost of attendance.

What jobs can I target after MBA-HCM?

KJSIM lists career areas such as hospital administration, digital healthcare, healthcare marketing, product management, healthcare analytics and informatics, quality, consulting, operations and supply chain. The actual role depends on hiring demand and the student's profile.
