LIBA Chennai: What Students Should Know

Loyola Institute of Business Administration (LIBA) was established in 1979 and operates from the Loyola College Campus in Nungambakkam, Chennai. LIBA describes itself as a Jesuit business school committed to excellence with ethics, professional competence and social responsibility.

The flagship full-time PGDM is a two-year residential programme spread across six terms. LIBA's current PGDM site lists specialisations in Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Business Analytics, and IT & Operations, with 126 credits including core courses, electives and a summer project.

Quick answer: why do students shortlist LIBA?

The strongest reasons are its established Chennai location, Jesuit management-education ethos, CAT/XAT route, broad management specialisations and a long-standing placement record. Students should still compare the fee with the latest official placement outcomes and their target role before applying.

LIBA Admission 2027: Process & Entrance Exam

Application route

LIBA's current admissions portal shows registration open for the 2027-2029 full-time PGDM. Applicants should use the live LIBA admissions portal for the application deadline, required documents and subsequent selection instructions rather than carrying forward dates from an older cycle.

Selection

The current full-time route is based on CAT 2026 or XAT 2027 scores, followed by LIBA's institute-level selection process. Exact selection-stage dates should be taken from the current admission communication issued to applicants.

Application checklist

  • Academic records from Class 10 onward.
  • CAT/XAT score details.
  • Valid identity and contact details.
  • Category or other supporting documents where applicable.
  • Work-experience evidence, if applicable.

LIBA FAQs

Which entrance exams does LIBA accept for the full-time PGDM?

The current 2027-29 admission listing accepts CAT 2026 or XAT 2027 scores.

What is the official PGDM fee at LIBA?

The current PGDM site lists the total programme fee as ₹18,89,700.

What was LIBA's average salary in 2024-25?

LIBA's official mandatory disclosure reports an average salary of ₹11 LPA for 2024-25, with a maximum of ₹21 LPA and minimum of ₹7.5 LPA.

Is work experience compulsory for LIBA PGDM?

No. LIBA's current PGDM information states that prior work experience is preferred but not mandatory and fresh graduates can apply.

Is LIBA's PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

LIBA's current PGDM site states that its AICTE-approved PGDM is recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.
