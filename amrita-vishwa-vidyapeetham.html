What is Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham known for?

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multidisciplinary university with campuses and schools offering programmes in several academic areas. The official admissions portal is organised by discipline and programme, so eligibility, selection tests, fees and deadlines are not identical across the university. A student applying for B.Tech, medicine, nursing, a science degree or an MBA should use the relevant programme page rather than assume one admission rule applies to every course.

For management aspirants, Amrita School of Business lists MBA options across Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Kochi and Mysuru. Bengaluru also offers an MBA–MS in Business Analytics dual-degree route with the University at Buffalo, USA. Explore the dedicated Amrita MBA programme guide and MBA–MS dual-degree guide for programme-specific details.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

The official MBA page lists Amrita CAT (ACAT), CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and GRE. NMAT is accepted for Amrita School of Business, Coimbatore; KMAT is accepted by the management departments at Kochi and Amritapuri. Acceptance can be campus-specific, so confirm the rule for your preferred campus before submitting a score.

Step-by-step application process

  1. Open the official MBA application portal and create an account using your email and contact details.
  2. Verify your email and complete the online form with personal, academic and campus/programme preferences.
  3. Choose an accepted entrance-test route. If using a national test, enter the relevant score or registration details as instructed.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹1,880, including GST, and upload the required academic and supporting documents.
  5. Wait for shortlisting based on academic performance and entrance-test scores. Shortlisted applicants attend a personal interview, online or offline as communicated.
  6. If selected, review the offer letter and complete the prescribed fee payment by the deadline shown by the university.

Quota and agent warning: Amrita’s official MBA FAQ states that it does not offer management-quota admissions for the MBA. The university also warns applicants not to use unauthorised admission agents. Apply through official university channels only. The overview page does not publish a reliable category-wise seat matrix; contact the relevant campus for any seat-allocation question.

Extra requirements for the MBA–MS dual degree

Applicants to the Bengaluru MBA–MS programme with the University at Buffalo must meet additional English-proficiency requirements for the MS component. The official page lists TOEFL, IELTS, PTE or Duolingo English Test, along with minimum score requirements and a separate deadline. Review the current official MBA admission page carefully: missing the MS requirement may affect eligibility for the additional MS degree, even if the student continues in the MBA programme.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. The official MBA page describes merit-based scholarships for a limited share of the sanctioned intake. Selection considers Class 10, Class 12 and undergraduate academic performance, entrance-test performance and the interview. The page states that first-year scholarships are offered to the top 10% of sanctioned intake; second-year continuation is linked to a CGPA of 8.5 or above and other conditions, with the number of awards limited. Scholarship slabs and campus-specific fee tables may differ, so check the latest official fee/scholarship document. Do not assume a scholarship is automatic or guaranteed solely from an entrance-test percentile.

What about rankings and accreditation?

Amrita’s official MBA admissions page describes Amrita School of Business, Coimbatore, as being among India’s Top 26 business schools and confirms AACSB accreditation for the Coimbatore school. These are specific claims about the business school and should not be treated as the same thing as a university-wide NIRF rank or a campus-wide accreditation statement.

The university is also described on its official pages as NAAC A++ accredited. When using rankings in an admission decision, check the ranking agency, category and year together—for example, university versus management category—and verify the current listing directly with the ranking authority. This page does not assign a new rank where a current, category-matched official figure has not been verified.

What should you compare before deciding?

  • Whether the programme and specialisation match your career goal.
  • The campus-specific total cost, including accommodation and deposits.
  • Median salary, placement percentage and batch year—not only the highest CTC.
  • Scholarship eligibility and renewal conditions in writing.
  • Commute, accommodation, food, student support and the campus environment.

What is campus life like?

Amrita offers campus options in different cities, including Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Kochi and Mysuru for MBA applicants. The environment and daily living costs can vary considerably by city and campus. Official MBA information refers to hostel accommodation and facilities such as Wi-Fi, gym, sports, recreation spaces and campus activities, but availability, room type and charges should be confirmed with the campus you select.

Hostel, transport and facilities checklist

  • Hostel: Ask whether a room is guaranteed, whether the quoted charge includes mess, and what the refund rules are.
  • Connectivity: Check the campus address, nearest railway station/airport and daily transport options using the campus-specific information.
  • Learning facilities: Ask about library access, computer labs, study spaces, Wi-Fi and availability outside class hours.
  • Health and safety: Confirm campus medical support, emergency procedures and student support contacts.
  • Student experience: Ask current students about workload, internships, clubs, events, food, rules and actual monthly expenses.

For an accurate four-year cost of an undergraduate course—or the full two-year cost of an MBA—request a campus-specific estimate from the university. Accommodation and facilities may differ by campus and are subject to availability.

Frequently asked questions

Is Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham accepting MBA applications for 2027?

The official MBA page lists MBA 2027 admissions as open. It currently lists 31 January 2027 as the Amrita CAT application deadline and 7 February 2027 as the exam date. Verify the live official page for updates.

What is the eligibility for Amrita MBA 2027?

The general MBA page states that applicants need a recognised undergraduate degree under the 10+2+3 pattern and at least 60% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and undergraduate studies. Final-year students may apply subject to the stated completion deadline. Campus-specific conditions should be checked before applying.

Which entrance exams does Amrita accept for MBA?

ACAT, CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and GRE are listed on the official MBA page. NMAT is listed for Coimbatore, while KMAT is listed for Kochi and Amritapuri. Confirm campus-specific acceptance.

What is the MBA fee at Amrita?

The official page currently lists regular fees of ₹6 lakh to ₹12 lakh depending on campus. Hostel and mess costs, deposits and other charges may be additional; verify the current fee document for your selected campus.

Does Amrita offer MBA scholarships?

Yes. Merit-based scholarships are available to a limited share of students and depend on academic record, entrance-test performance, interview and renewal conditions. Check the latest official scholarship rules.

What are the placement statistics for Amrita MBA?

The official page reports 93.62% students placed for 2024, highest CTC of ₹24.84 LPA for 2025, and average and median salary figures of ₹8.34 LPA and ₹7.60 LPA in its FAQ. The average and median figures are not clearly tagged with a batch year on that page, so request the latest placement report for a like-for-like comparison.

Does Amrita have MBA management quota seats?

The official MBA FAQ says that management-quota admissions are not offered for the MBA. Apply through the university’s official admission process and avoid unauthorised agents.

Where can I apply or ask admission questions?
