Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multi-campus, multi-disciplinary research institution accredited with an 'A++' grade by NAAC. The university offers management education through its Department of Management.

MBA Programmes

The university offers the following postgraduate management programmes:

  • MBA: Master of Business Administration
  • Dual Degree: MBA - MS in Business Analytics (in collaboration with University at Buffalo, USA)
