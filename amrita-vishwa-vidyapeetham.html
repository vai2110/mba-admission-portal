Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multidisciplinary institution with schools and programmes across a broad range of academic areas. Its admissions are organised by programme and discipline, so there is no single entrance examination, eligibility percentage or fee that applies to every course. A B.Tech applicant, a medical aspirant and an MBA candidate should use the relevant school’s current admissions notice.

For management applicants, Amrita School of Business lists MBA options across Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Kochi and Mysuru. The Bengaluru school also offers an MBA–MS in Business Analytics dual degree in collaboration with the University at Buffalo, USA. Use the dedicated MBA programme guide, MBA–MS dual-degree guide and placements guide for more focused information.

Student takeaway: Campus matters. Entrance-test acceptance, tuition fees, scholarships, hostel availability and placement context can differ by campus and programme. Verify the details for your exact course before making a decision.

How much should an MBA student budget in total?

For a two-year programme, begin with the published programme fee, then add accommodation, mess, deposits, travel, laptop, books, personal expenses and any programme-specific charges. The university’s page lists hostel charges at approximately ₹1.2 lakh per year, subject to revision. If that amount applies for both years, it would add about ₹2.4 lakh before any increases or other charges. That makes the indicative fee-plus-hostel calculation approximately ₹8.4 lakh for Mysuru, ₹10.6 lakh for Amritapuri/Kochi, and ₹14.4 lakh for Bengaluru/Coimbatore, before mess, deposits, travel and personal expenses. These are planning calculations, not official all-inclusive totals.

Before you pay: Ask the campus for a written fee breakup that distinguishes tuition, caution deposit, hostel, mess and other charges. Confirm refund rules and instalment deadlines in the offer documents.

Amrita MBA Admission Process 2027

1. Check academic eligibility

The official MBA admissions page states that applicants must hold a recognised undergraduate degree under the 10+2+3 pattern and have at least 60% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and undergraduate studies. Final-year undergraduate students may apply if they complete examinations and viva-voce, where applicable, by 30 June 2027; they may use results from their latest completed semester while applying.

2. Choose an accepted entrance-test route

The official page lists ACAT 2027, CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and GRE. Amrita School of Business, Coimbatore also accepts NMAT, while the management departments at Kochi and Amritapuri accept KMAT. Confirm the exact test accepted by your preferred campus and whether a score is valid for the current intake.

3. Complete the application

  1. Open the official MBA application portal and create an account.
  2. Enter personal details, academic history, test information and campus preferences accurately.
  3. Upload the requested marksheets, certificates, score details and other supporting documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹1,880 including GST.
  5. Save the application confirmation and check the registered email for updates.

4. Shortlisting and personal interview

Amrita says shortlisting considers academic performance and entrance-test scores. Shortlisted candidates attend a personal interview online or offline, as communicated by the university. Keep your academic documents and test registration/score records available and prepare to discuss your goals, academic interests and career plans.

5. Offer, fee payment and enrolment

Selected candidates receive offer letters by email. Read the offer carefully for the campus, programme, scholarship (if any), fee amount, payment deadline, document verification and refund conditions. Do not treat an application submission or interview invitation as confirmation of admission.

Management quota and agents

The official MBA FAQ states that Amrita does not offer management-quota admissions for the MBA. Apply through official university channels and avoid agents claiming to guarantee admission or a scholarship.

Additional requirements for the MBA–MS dual degree

The Bengaluru MBA–MS in Business Analytics programme with the University at Buffalo includes additional English-proficiency requirements for the MS degree. The official programme page lists accepted tests such as TOEFL, IELTS, PTE or Duolingo English Test and specifies minimum scores and deadlines. These additional requirements apply to the MS component; review the live programme page before applying.

Amrita MBA Scholarships and Fee Waivers

Amrita School of Business describes merit-based scholarships for a limited proportion of the sanctioned intake. Selection considers Class 10, Class 12 and undergraduate academic performance, entrance-test performance and the interview. The official page states that first-year awards are offered to the top 10% of sanctioned intake. Scholarship slabs and criteria can differ by campus and programme, so students should use the campus-specific fee tables rather than assume one waiver structure applies everywhere.

Amrita Campus Life, Hostels and Facilities

Amrita has campuses in multiple cities, so the day-to-day student experience depends on the location and school. MBA applicants can compare Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Kochi and Mysuru. Hostel and mess arrangements, room types, transport, campus activities and monthly expenses may vary; confirm them directly with the campus.

Hostel and living costs

The official MBA page lists hostel charges at approximately ₹1.2 lakh per year, subject to revision. Before budgeting, ask whether the amount covers the room only or includes mess, utilities, deposits and other services. Confirm room availability, sharing arrangement, curfew/visitor rules, refund policy and whether accommodation is guaranteed for first-year students.

Academic and student facilities

Use the campus-specific facilities page to verify library hours, digital resources, computer labs, Wi-Fi, classrooms, sports and recreation spaces, medical support, student counselling and clubs. Facilities listed for one school or campus should not automatically be assumed to be available in the same form at every location.

Location and transport

Compare travel time from your home, nearest railway station or airport, public transport and the cost of getting around. Bengaluru and Coimbatore have different housing and commuting patterns from Amritapuri, Kochi and Mysuru. If you plan to live off campus, include rent, food, commute and deposit costs in your total budget.

Questions to ask current students

  • How manageable is the academic workload and trimester schedule?
  • Are internships and placement preparation accessible across all specialisations?
  • What is the actual monthly expense beyond tuition and hostel?
  • How responsive are academic advisors, career services and student support teams?
  • What are the campus rules around attendance, accommodation, events and travel?

Amrita Rankings and Accreditation

Amrita’s official pages describe the university as NAAC A++ accredited. The MBA admissions page also describes Amrita School of Business, Coimbatore, as among India’s Top 26 business schools and identifies AACSB accreditation for the Coimbatore school. These claims refer to different levels—the university and a specific business school—and should not be conflated.

For ranking comparisons, always note the ranking agency, category and year. A university-level rank is not the same as a management-category rank, and a business-school accreditation is not a placement outcome. This page does not assign a NIRF rank without a current, category-matched ranking entry verified for the relevant year.

How to assess a ranking: Check the published methodology, the exact campus or school covered, the ranking year and whether the ranking measures teaching, research, graduation outcomes, perception or placement-related indicators.

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham FAQs

Is Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham accepting MBA applications for 2027?

The official MBA page lists MBA 2027 admissions as open. It currently lists 31 January 2027 as the Amrita CAT application deadline and 7 February 2027 as the exam date. Verify the live portal for changes.

What is the eligibility for Amrita MBA 2027?

The official page requires a recognised undergraduate degree under the 10+2+3 pattern and at least 60% aggregate in Class 10, Class 12 and undergraduate studies. Final-year students may apply subject to the stated completion deadline.

Which entrance exams does Amrita accept for MBA?

ACAT 2027, CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and GRE are listed. NMAT is accepted at Coimbatore, while KMAT is accepted at Kochi and Amritapuri. Confirm the rule for your selected campus.

What is the MBA fee at Amrita?

The official page lists regular MBA fees of ₹6 lakh to ₹12 lakh depending on campus. Hostel and other costs may be additional, and the latest campus fee sheet should be checked before paying.

Does Amrita offer MBA scholarships?

Yes. The university lists merit-based scholarships for a limited share of intake. Eligibility can depend on academic results, entrance scores, interview and renewal conditions including CGPA.

What are the placement statistics for Amrita MBA?

The official page reports 93.62% placed for 2024 and a highest CTC of ₹24.84 LPA for 2025. Average salary ₹8.34 LPA and median salary ₹7.60 LPA are listed in the FAQ without a clearly stated batch year. Request the latest campus-specific placement report.

Does Amrita offer MBA management-quota seats?

The official MBA FAQ states that management-quota admissions are not offered for the MBA. Apply through official university channels.

Where can I apply for admission?
