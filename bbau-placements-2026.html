How to Read BBAU Placement Data

The most useful BBAU placement figures are the official NIRF disclosures: number of students placed, graduating cohort and median salary. Median is more informative than a single highest-package claim because it represents the middle of the reported placed cohort.

Latest Official NIRF Submission

BBAU's 2026 NIRF submissions include 2024-25 placement outcomes. Across the university, results differ substantially by programme, so students should always open the NIRF category relevant to their course.

Important:

Placement data is programme/category specific. A university-wide highest package should not be presented as the expected salary for every BBAU student.

BBAU Placements FAQs

What is BBAU MBA median salary?

The official NIRF Management disclosure reports ₹15.135 lakh for the 2024-25 graduating cohort in the reported two-year management programme data.

What was the BBAU UG 3-year median salary?

The 2023-24 UG three-year disclosure reports a median of ₹11.851 lakh with 151 students placed out of 180 graduates.

Does BBAU have a placement cell?

Yes. BBAU operates a Central Training & Placement Cell and publishes placement-related activity and NIRF outcome data.

Should I trust BBAU highest package figures?

Use the highest package only as one data point. Median salary, placed count and programme-specific outcomes are better for judging likely outcomes.
