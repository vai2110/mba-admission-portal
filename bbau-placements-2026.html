How to Read BBAU Placement Data

“BBAU highest package” is a popular search, but the highest salary is a weak standalone measure because one outlier does not describe the typical outcome. Official NIRF disclosures provide three more useful pieces of information: how many students graduated, how many were placed and the median salary among the reported placed cohort.

Median is especially useful because half of the reported placed salaries are above it and half below it. Even median data must be read by programme/category: an MBA management median should not be copied into a B.Tech or MCA article.

UG 4-Year Placement Context

The official NIRF 2026 overall submission reports 4-year UG outcomes of 135 placed students at a ₹9.846 lakh median in 2022-23, 151 at ₹11.851 lakh in 2023-24 and 160 at ₹12.735 lakh in 2024-25. These figures provide useful university-level context for four-year programmes, including engineering, but they are not a branch-specific B.Tech salary table.

PG 2-Year Placement Context

The overall NIRF 2026 submission reports 1,294 PG two-year students placed at a ₹7.215 lakh median in 2022-23, 1,302 at ₹9.524 lakh in 2023-24 and 1,337 at ₹10.523 lakh in 2024-25. This broad category covers multiple postgraduate disciplines, so it should not be used as an MCA- or MBA-specific placement number.

For MBA applicants

Use the separate management NIRF disclosure where available. It reports the much more specific 2024-25 management median of ₹15.135 lakh for 74 placed students.

What BBAU Placement Data Means for Different Students

CourseWhat you should ask before joining
MBASpecialisation-wise roles, recruiter mix, internship conversion and management cohort outcomes.
B.TechBranch-wise recruiters, software/core role split, placement participation and internship opportunities.
MCAIT/software recruiters, coding expectations, project quality, internships and programme-specific outcomes.
BBA/B.ComEntry-level role types, higher-study progression and how many students pursue MBA/professional qualifications.

A student should also ask whether “placed” means the student accepted an offer, whether multiple offers are counted, and how many students chose higher studies. These questions help put the headline placement percentage into context.

BBAU Placements FAQs

What is BBAU MBA's median salary?

The latest NIRF Management 2026 disclosure reports ₹15.135 lakh for the 2024-25 reported two-year management cohort, with 74 students placed out of 76 reported graduates.

What is BBAU's highest package?

A single highest package is not the best benchmark for judging placement quality. This page prioritises official placed counts and median salaries because they describe the cohort more meaningfully.

What is the BBAU UG median salary?

The reported UG three-year cohort had a ₹11.851 lakh median in 2023-24, with 151 students placed out of 180.

Does BBAU have a placement cell?

Yes. BBAU has a Central Training & Placement Cell and publishes placement-related information and statutory outcome disclosures.

Are BBAU placement figures course-specific?

Some official NIRF submissions are category/cohort specific rather than individual-course specific. Always check the source category before applying a number to B.Tech, MBA, MCA, BBA or B.Com.
