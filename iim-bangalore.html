IIM Bangalore MBA: Student Decision Snapshot

IIM Bangalore is best suited to students looking for a rigorous two-year MBA with strong career mobility across consulting, technology and product, finance, banking and investments, consumer businesses and general management. The key differentiator is not simply the CAT cutoff or the institute brand; the programme combines a broad curriculum, a large peer group and a placement ecosystem with substantial role diversity.

IIM Bangalore MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility, CAT Cutoff & Selection

The PGP 2026–28 admission process uses a two-stage evaluation. The first stage combines CAT and profile factors to prepare the pre-PI shortlist; the second stage uses the interview, WAT and additional academic and profile components for final selection.

Basic eligibility

Applicants need a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD applicants. Eligible professional qualifications such as CA, ICWA and CS are also recognised under the stated admission conditions.

Important for CAT aspirants

The official admission process states that these cutoffs are used to prepare the first shortlist and that the scores of candidates called for interviews are higher. Students should therefore treat the published cutoff as a qualifying threshold, not a safe target.

What changes after the interview?

At the post-PI stage, the personal interview carries the highest weight, followed by CAT, WAT, academics and work experience or professional-course components. This is why a strong CAT score alone cannot be used to predict final admission.

IIM Bangalore MBA Fees 2026 & Other Popular Programmes

For the 2026–28 domestic PGP batch, IIM Bangalore lists a two-year programme fee of ₹26 lakh. The fee structure should be read together with deposits and recurring mess advances when estimating the full cash requirement.

PGPBA ₹26 lakh for 2026–28 domestic batch 2 years Business analytics and AI-led management careers

What is included in the PGP fee?

IIMB states that the PGP fee covers tuition, library, internet, case permission royalty, course material, hostel room rent and service charge, medical insurance, personal accident insurance and alumni activity. A one-time general caution deposit of ₹30,000 is separate, and students also pay a mess advance of ₹30,000 each term.

Fee vs investment decision

For a two-year residential MBA, compare the fee with the role outcomes you want and the income or experience you may give up while studying. This produces a more useful decision than comparing only average or highest packages.

What this means for freshers and professionals

The official profile confirms that the programme includes freshers as well as candidates with substantial prior experience. For applicants, this means work experience can add value in admissions and peer learning, but it is not a prerequisite for entering the two-year PGP.

Learning model

The two-year residential MBA runs across six trimesters with a summer project between the academic years. The programme combines core management learning with electives and broader opportunities for peer learning, exchange, student activities and engagement with the IIMB ecosystem.

Financial Aid & Scholarships

IIM Bangalore provides need-based financial aid and also lists merit-based and merit-cum-need scholarships. The financial-aid process evaluates financial circumstances rather than using academic performance alone.

Who Should Choose IIM Bangalore?

Strong Fit

  • You want a rigorous MBA with access to consulting, product, technology, finance and broad management roles.
  • You have strong academic consistency or a profile that can compete in a multi-factor selection process.
  • You value peer diversity, electives and career flexibility.
  • You are prepared for both the academic intensity and the opportunity cost of a full-time residential MBA.

Think Carefully If

  • You are treating the published CAT cutoff as a realistic predictor of final selection.
  • You have not considered the full two-year financial and opportunity cost.
  • You want a narrowly specialised course without evaluating the broader MBA structure.
  • You are choosing only on institute rank without comparing the programme with your target role and career stage.

Student decision: IIM Bangalore is particularly strong for candidates seeking broad post-MBA optionality. The best decision is one that connects your target role with the institute's placement mix, curriculum and peer environment—not simply its ranking or entrance cutoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IIM Bangalore MBA fee for the 2026–28 batch?

The published two-year domestic PGP fee is ₹26 lakh. A one-time general caution deposit of ₹30,000 is separate, and students pay a mess advance of ₹30,000 each term.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Bangalore?

For the General category, the published minimum for the first shortlist is 85 overall, with 80 in VARC and 75 each in DILR and QA. The official process notes that interview-call scores are higher than these minimums.

Does IIM Bangalore consider Class 10 and Class 12 marks?

Yes. School and higher-secondary performance are part of the pre-PI and post-PI evaluation under the published 2026–28 PGP admission process.

Can a fresher get into IIM Bangalore PGP?

Yes. The official PGP 2026 batch profile includes 82 students with no prior work experience, while the programme also includes students with multiple years of experience.

Is IIM Bangalore good for consulting?

Consulting was the largest career pathway in the official 2026 PGP and PGPBA placement update, accounting for 45% of total offers.

How many offers were made in IIM Bangalore's 2026 placements?

The official placement update reported 664 offers from 177 firms for 596 students appearing for placements in the PGP and PGPBA Class of 2026.

What is the difference between PGP and PGPBA at IIM Bangalore?

PGP is the broad two-year MBA in management, while PGPBA is a two-year MBA with a stronger business analytics and AI orientation while retaining a management foundation.
