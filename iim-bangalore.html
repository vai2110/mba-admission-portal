College Overview

About IIM Bangalore

The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) is a leading management institute located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Its flagship Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) leads to a Master of Business Administration (MBA).

The PGP is a two-year full-time residential programme spread across six trimesters, with a summer project between the first and second year. The programme combines core management education with electives, experiential learning and opportunities for international exposure.

Why IIM Bangalore stands out

IIMB's PGP combines a rigorous management curriculum, experiential learning, student-led activities and exposure to contemporary business issues. The institute also has multidisciplinary Centres of Excellence covering areas such as capital markets, entrepreneurship, public policy and supply chain management.
