College Overview

About IIM Bangalore

The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) is a leading management institute located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Its flagship Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is a two-year full-time residential programme leading to the Master of Business Administration (MBA).

The PGP runs across six trimesters with a summer project between the first and second year. IIMB also offers a separate two-year MBA in Business Analytics and programmes designed for experienced professionals.

MBA Admission 2026–28

Admission route

IIM Bangalore's flagship PGP is a two-year full-time residential MBA. Domestic applicants are admitted through the institute's PGP admission process, which uses CAT along with other profile and selection parameters.

Basic Eligibility

The PGP requires a bachelor's degree. Candidates should check the current IIMB admission policy for the applicable minimum marks, category-specific provisions and final-year conditions.

Entrance Exam

CAT is the entrance examination used for the domestic PGP admission process.

Selection Process

IIM Bangalore evaluates candidates using CAT performance and additional academic and profile-related parameters as specified in the official admission policy. Shortlisting and final admission should not be understood as being based on CAT percentile alone.

Important for applicants

Use the official IIMB PGP admissions page for the latest admission dates, eligibility rules, shortlist methodology and programme-specific requirements.

Other Popular Programmes at IIM Bangalore

IIMB offers several degree-granting management programmes. The right programme depends mainly on academic background, work experience and career stage.

PGP in Business Analytics (MBA BA)

Two-year full-time residential MBA focused on using analytics and AI to solve complex business problems.

Best suited for: Students interested in management roles with a strong analytics orientation.

Official Programme →

Executive PGP in Management (EPGP)

One-year full-time residential MBA designed for experienced professionals, with a minimum of five years of post-graduation work experience.

Best suited for: Professionals targeting senior management roles.

Official Programme →

PGP in Enterprise Management (PGPEM)

Two-year programme designed for working professionals, allowing participants to continue working while attending on-campus weekend sessions.

Best suited for: Working professionals seeking management education without leaving their jobs.

Official Programme →

PGP in Public Policy & Management

Degree programme focused on public policy and management, designed for professionals working in or around public administration and policy.

Best suited for: Candidates interested in public policy and governance-oriented careers.

The ₹26 lakh programme fee covers tuition, library, internet, case permission royalty, course material, hostel room rent and service charge, medical and personal accident insurance and alumni activity. Taxes, where applicable, are extra.

Financial aid

IIMB provides need-based financial aid and also lists merit and merit-cum-need scholarships for eligible PGP students. Students should check the current official financial-aid policy rather than assuming every scholarship is automatic.

How to think about ROI

For IIM Bangalore, ROI should be evaluated using total programme cost, financing or opportunity cost, placement outcomes, career function, location and the student's own post-MBA goals rather than salary alone.

Batch Profile & Student Life

Current PGP Cohort

IIMB reports 540 students enrolled in the 2026 PGP cohort. The programme is residential and runs across six trimesters over two years, with a summer project between the first and second year.

Campus & Student Experience

IIM Bangalore's campus supports academic, professional and extracurricular activities through facilities such as the library, student centre, sports and cultural activities, clubs and other student services.

Beyond academics

IIMB describes student life as extending beyond the classroom through clubs, social projects, action-oriented courses, student self-governance and experiential learning.

Student & Community Insights

Student-led ecosystem

The Student Affairs Council is a key interface between students, faculty and administration and coordinates academic, extracurricular and day-to-day student activities.

Clubs, activities and peer learning

IIMB's official student-life material highlights clubs, sports, cultural activities, social initiatives and peer interaction as important parts of campus life. Student-run initiatives such as Vikasana also provide opportunities to work on social-impact projects.

Student support

The Student Welfare Office provides wellness and mental-health support, while the MITR peer-support system connects incoming PGP students with senior-student buddies.

Who Should Apply?

PGP / MBA

  • Students seeking a rigorous two-year residential MBA.
  • Candidates comfortable with a highly competitive CAT-based admission process.
  • Students targeting consulting, finance, technology/product, marketing, operations or entrepreneurial careers.
  • Candidates who value peer learning, student-led activities and a strong corporate ecosystem.

Consider another IIMB programme if...

If you already have substantial work experience, compare the PGP with EPGP or PGPEM. If your career goal is strongly analytics-oriented, compare the PGP with the MBA in Business Analytics.

FAQs

What is IIM Bangalore's flagship MBA programme?

The Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP), a two-year full-time residential programme leading to an MBA degree.

Which entrance exam is used for the IIMB PGP?

CAT is used for the domestic PGP admission process.

How much is the IIM Bangalore PGP fee for 2026–28?

IIMB lists the two-year PGP fee for the domestic 2026–28 batch as ₹26 lakh, with a ₹30,000 general caution deposit. Mess advance is additional.

Does IIM Bangalore offer an MBA in Business Analytics?

Yes. The PGP in Business Analytics leads to an MBA in Business Analytics and is a two-year full-time residential programme.

Does IIM Bangalore have programmes for working professionals?

Yes. IIMB offers programmes including EPGP and PGPEM designed for experienced professionals.
