IIM Bangalore MBA: What Applicants Need to Know

Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) offers a two-year, full-time residential Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) leading to the Master of Business Administration (MBA). The current PGP runs across six trimesters with a summer project and is designed around conceptual, analytical and leadership development.

IIMB also offers a two-year full-time MBA in Business Analytics (PGPBA), a one-year full-time residential MBA for experienced professionals through EPGP, and a two-year weekend MBA for working professionals through PGPEM. These programmes serve very different student profiles, so the right choice depends strongly on work experience and whether you can leave your job.

How to Get Admission to IIM Bangalore MBA

Step 1: Meet the academic eligibility

For the flagship PGP, applicants need a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates. IIMB also permits specified professional qualifications such as CA/ICWA/CS under the conditions stated in the admission document.

Step 2: Take CAT

Domestic PGP admission is based on the Common Admission Test (CAT). The CAT score is one component of a broader evaluation rather than the only factor used for final selection.

Step 3: Clear the first shortlist

IIMB prepares a first shortlist using section-wise and aggregate CAT requirements along with the institute's selection methodology. Academic performance, work experience or professional course and gender diversity are also considered at the pre-PI stage.

Step 4: Attend WAT and Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates proceed to the second stage, which includes a Written Ability Test (WAT) and Personal Interview (PI). IIMB then combines the prescribed post-PI components to prepare the final composite score.

Step 5: Final offer

Final offers are made on the basis of the post-PI score and applicable category-wise process. Merely meeting the minimum CAT percentile or academic eligibility does not guarantee an interview call or admission.

IIM Bangalore CAT Cutoff: What Is Officially Published?

IIMB publishes minimum percentile requirements for the first shortlist, but it also makes clear that candidates actually called for interviews have higher scores. For the latest admission document available for the 2026-28 cycle, the published minimum aggregate percentile was 85 for General, 75 for NC-OBC/EWS, 70 for SC, 65 for ST and 60 for PwD.

IIM Bangalore MBA Fees: Detailed Fee Structure

For the domestic PGP 2026-28 batch, IIMB publishes a two-year fee of ₹26,00,000 plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit. The published fee covers tuition, library, internet, case permission royalty, course material, hostel room rent, service charge, medical insurance, personal accident insurance and alumni activity. A mess advance of ₹30,000 is payable each term.

Who Should Apply to IIM Bangalore MBA?

Strong fit

  • Candidates targeting a rigorous two-year residential MBA through CAT.
  • Students who want broad management exposure before choosing electives and career domains.
  • Applicants comfortable being evaluated on academics, CAT, work experience/professional qualification, WAT and PI.
  • Experienced professionals who specifically want to compare the one-year EPGP or weekend PGPEM with the flagship PGP.

Check carefully before applying

  • Do not treat the published minimum CAT cutoff as a safe admission percentile.
  • Do not compare PGP fees with EPGP or PGPEM without considering programme duration and what the fee includes.
  • PGPEM is designed for working professionals and does not provide placement services like PGP/EPGP.

IIM Bangalore MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Bangalore MBA entrance exam?

Domestic admission to the flagship PGP is based on CAT. PGPBA also uses CAT for domestic admissions.

What is the IIM Bangalore MBA fee?

The published PGP 2026-28 fee is ₹26 lakh for two years, plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit and mess advance of ₹30,000 per term.

What is the latest IIM Bangalore MBA placement average salary?

The official 2026 placement report records ₹35.35 lakh average salary and ₹34.75 lakh median salary for the PGP and PGPBA 2024-26 batch.

Does IIM Bangalore publish a CAT cutoff?

Yes. IIMB publishes minimum section-wise and aggregate percentile requirements for its first shortlist. These are not guaranteed interview-call or admission cutoffs.

Which IIM Bangalore MBA is for experienced professionals?

EPGP is a one-year full-time residential MBA requiring at least five years of post-graduation work experience. PGPEM is a two-year weekend MBA requiring at least six years of post-graduation work experience.
