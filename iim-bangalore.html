IIM Bangalore MBA: What Applicants Need to Know

Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) offers a two-year, full-time residential Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) leading to an MBA. The programme runs across six trimesters and includes a summer project.

IIMB also offers the two-year MBA in Business Analytics (PGPBA), one-year full-time EPGP for experienced professionals and the two-year weekend PGPEM. These programmes have different eligibility, entrance requirements and career formats.

How to Get Admission to IIM Bangalore MBA

1. Check academic eligibility

For the flagship PGP, applicants need a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates, subject to the current admission document.

2. Take CAT

Domestic PGP admission uses the Common Admission Test (CAT). CAT is one part of IIMB's selection process.

3. Clear the first shortlist

IIMB applies published section-wise and aggregate CAT requirements and considers other prescribed factors such as academics, work experience/professional qualification and diversity.

4. Attend WAT and PI

Shortlisted candidates proceed to the institute's Written Ability Test and Personal Interview stage as specified for the admission cycle.

5. Receive the final offer

The final composite score uses the components prescribed in the relevant admission policy. Meeting the minimum CAT percentile does not guarantee an interview call or admission.

IIM Bangalore MBA Fees: 2026-28 Published Structure

For the domestic PGP 2026-28 batch, IIMB publishes a two-year programme fee of ₹26,00,000, plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit. A mess advance of ₹30,000 per term is payable separately.

Who Should Consider Which IIM Bangalore MBA?

PGP may suit applicants who

  • Want a two-year full-time residential MBA.
  • Are applying through CAT.
  • Want broad management exposure before selecting career domains.
  • Can commit to a full-time residential programme.

Check before applying

  • Do not use the minimum CAT cutoff as a guaranteed-call percentile.
  • Compare fees only after checking what each programme includes.
  • EPGP and PGPEM have different work-experience expectations and formats.

IIM Bangalore MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Bangalore MBA entrance exam?

Domestic admission to the flagship PGP is based on CAT. PGPBA also uses CAT for domestic admissions.

What is the IIM Bangalore MBA fee?

The published PGP 2026-28 programme fee is ₹26 lakh for two years, plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit and mess advance of ₹30,000 per term.

What is the latest IIM Bangalore MBA average salary?

The official 2026 placement report records an ₹35.35 lakh average salary and ₹34.75 lakh median salary for the PGP and PGPBA 2024-26 batch.

What is the IIM Bangalore CAT cutoff?

For 2026-28, the published minimum aggregate percentile was 85 for General, 75 for NC-OBC/EWS, 70 for SC, 65 for ST and 60 for PwD. These are minimum requirements, not guaranteed admission cutoffs.

Does IIM Bangalore have an MBA for experienced professionals?

Yes. EPGP is a one-year full-time MBA for experienced professionals, while PGPEM is a two-year weekend MBA for working professionals. Their eligibility and entrance requirements differ from the flagship PGP.

Does IIM Bangalore publish a highest package?

The reviewed official 2026 placement report does not publish a highest CTC figure. This page therefore does not estimate or import a third-party number.
