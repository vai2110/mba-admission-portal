What is the right order for MBA admissions?

Start with target programmes rather than registering for every exam. Colleges accept different tests, require separate forms and use different selection stages. A valid entrance score does not help if a college's own application deadline has passed.

  1. Define target roles, location and budget.
  2. Check academic eligibility, final-year rules and work experience.
  3. Match target programmes to accepted exams.
  4. Track exam and institute deadlines separately.
  5. Prepare for essays, written tests, group exercises and interviews.
  6. Compare offers using curriculum, total cost and placement evidence.

What eligibility should you verify?

Most full-time programmes require a recognised bachelor's degree, but minimum marks, category relaxations and final-year completion deadlines differ. Executive programmes may require work experience or current employment.

Prepare marksheets, degree/provisional certificates, test scores, experience letters and category documents where relevant. If your marks are close to the threshold or your degree needs equivalence, ask admissions for written clarification before paying a non-refundable fee.

Which entrance exam should you take?

Choose based on your college list. CAT is used by IIMs and many other programmes; XAT, SNAP, NMAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA and GMAT are relevant to different institutions. Acceptance and score-validity year vary by programme.

How do shortlisting and final selection differ?

A minimum exam cutoff is often only a screening threshold. Institutes may also consider academics, work experience, diversity, essays, written tests, group exercises and interviews. The final-selection formula may differ from the shortlist formula.

Prepare to explain your career goals, academic choices, work experience and why the programme fits. Never assume a high percentile guarantees admission.

What should you check before accepting an offer?

  • Exact programme title, campus and credential awarded.
  • Full fee, deposits, instalment dates and refund rules.
  • Hostel, rent, meals and travel costs.
  • Latest placement report for the same programme and batch.
  • Scholarship terms and whether an award is confirmed.
  • Document-verification and joining deadlines.

Use official application/payment channels and keep offer letters and receipts. Avoid agents promising guaranteed admission or placements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply before final-year results?

Many programmes permit it subject to completion and document deadlines; check each programme's rules.

Is an entrance score enough?

No. Separate forms and further selection stages may be required.

Should I apply to every college accepting my exam?

Prioritise by goals, eligibility, budget and location.

How should I compare offers?

Compare curriculum, total cost, median salary/roles, location and financial aid.

Are dates the same each year?

No. Use current official admission calendars.
