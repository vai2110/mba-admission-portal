Programme Overview

IIM Nagpur offers a two-year full-time residential flagship MBA programme. The curriculum integrates lectures, case studies, projects, and simulations across areas including Finance, Marketing, Strategy, and Decision Science.

Admissions 2026-28

The batch size is 340 students. The total programme fee is Rs. 21,00,000.

Selection Process

Admission follows a three-stage process: CAT 2025 screening, PI shortlisting, and final selection based on a weighted score (CAT 45%, PI 25%, Academic Performance 6%, Work Experience 9%, Academic Diversity 5%, Gender Diversity 10%).
