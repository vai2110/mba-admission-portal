Which placement data should you use?

Use IIM Nagpur's official placement report for the flagship MBA and the relevant graduating batch. Keep it separate from the Executive MBA (Hybrid), Blended MBA for Working Professionals and other executive programmes because candidate profiles and programme structures differ.

A useful report identifies batch year, students seeking placement, salary metrics and function/industry distribution. If a current official report does not publish a metric, do not estimate it from a recruiter announcement.

Which career roles should you investigate?

Review the report's actual function-wise data for consulting, finance, marketing, sales, analytics, operations, general management and technology-related roles where reported. The exact mix should come from the latest IIM Nagpur report, not assumptions about every MBA class.

  • Check job-function counts, not just recruiter names.
  • Match roles to your target career and previous experience.
  • Check whether domestic and international offers are distinguished.
  • Keep summer internship/PPO information separate from final placements.

How should you interpret salary metrics?

Median salary describes the midpoint of a reported cohort; average salary can be influenced by a small number of high offers. Highest CTC is an outlier metric, not a typical outcome. Check the number of eligible students and those who sought placement, then review roles and industries.

  1. Open the latest official MBA report.
  2. Check the cohort and denominator.
  3. Compare median and average where both exist.
  4. Review role distribution and total education cost.

What else should you compare before joining?

Combine placements with admission criteria, curriculum, campus, fees and your target role. If you have significant work experience, compare the flagship full-time MBA with the institute's executive offerings separately. The right programme depends on whether you want a full-time career switch or study alongside employment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I verify IIM Nagpur placements?

Use the institute's official placements section and the latest report for the flagship MBA.

Can executive MBA outcomes be used for the flagship MBA?

No. They are different programmes and cohorts.

Is average salary better than median?

Use both when available; median often represents the middle outcome more reliably.

Does highest CTC represent a typical student?

No, it is the largest reported offer.

What if median is not published?

State that it is unavailable in the report reviewed.
