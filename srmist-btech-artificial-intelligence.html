B.Tech Artificial Intelligence at SRMIST

SRMIST offers AI-focused engineering options within its technology portfolio. The exact programme title and campus should be matched to the current engineering prospectus before applying.

Eligibility

B.Tech applicants need the prescribed Class 12 PCM background and aggregate required by SRMIST for the selected programme.

How Does SRMIST B.Tech AI Admission Work?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal engineering entrance route. Counselling then considers rank, programme/campus preferences and seat availability.

Fees

SRMIST publishes fees by programme and campus. An exact AI-specific fee should not be inferred from another branch. Check the current AI programme entry for the payable amount.

SRMIST B.Tech AI Cutoff

No consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was found in the sources used here. Unofficial rank lists are not presented as official cutoffs.

What Do You Study in AI?

The AI pathway focuses on computing plus artificial-intelligence areas such as machine learning, data-driven methods and intelligent systems. The exact curriculum depends on the programme title and campus.

Placements

For university-level placement numbers and engineering NIRF data, use the dedicated SRMIST placements page.
