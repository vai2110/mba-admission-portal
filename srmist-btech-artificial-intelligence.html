B.Tech Artificial Intelligence

SRMIST offers AI-focused engineering programmes covering artificial intelligence and related computing areas. The exact programme title and campus should be checked in the current engineering prospectus.

Eligibility

Applicants need the prescribed Class 12 PCM background and programme-specific aggregate required by SRMIST for B.Tech admission.

Admission Through SRMJEEE

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal entrance route for SRMIST B.Tech. After the entrance examination, eligible candidates participate in counselling and select available programme-campus options.

Fees

SRMIST publishes fees by programme and campus. The annual tuition should be checked against the exact AI programme rather than using a generic B.Tech figure.

Cutoff

No consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was found in the official sources used for this page. Unofficial ranks are not presented as official cutoffs.

Placements

Official SRMIST university-level 2023 placement figures reported an average salary of ₹10.5 LPA, maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum, 1,311 companies and 13,069 offers.

FAQs

Is SRMJEEE used for B.Tech AI?

Yes, SRMJEEE is the principal SRMIST UG engineering route.

Does SRMIST publish an official AI closing rank?

A consolidated official closing-rank table was not found in the sources used.

Can the programme be assumed to be available on every campus?

No. Programme availability is campus-specific.
