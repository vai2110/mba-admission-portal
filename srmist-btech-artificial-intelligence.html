What Is B.Tech Artificial Intelligence at SRMIST?

AI is a computing-focused engineering choice for students interested in machine learning, data-driven systems and intelligent applications. SRMIST has multiple computing pathways, so the exact programme title and campus should be checked before application.

Do not copy CSE details blindly

Fee, intake, campus availability and curriculum can differ by programme. A CSE fee or closing-rank estimate is not automatically an AI figure.

What Is the Eligibility?

SRMIST's regular B.Tech eligibility requires at least 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory and one prescribed third subject. The published age condition is 16 years 6 months on 31 July of the relevant year.

How Do You Get Admission?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route. The 2026 process had three application phases. After the entrance exam, counselling uses rank, programme/campus preference and seat availability.

StageWhat you do

What Is the Fee?

AI-specific fees are programme/campus-specific. A concrete official SRMIST reference is the Kattankulathur B.Tech CSE with Specialization in Big Data Analytics listing at ₹4,25,000 per year. That is not the AI fee.

What Is the SRMIST B.Tech AI Cutoff?

SRMIST does not publish a consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used here. Therefore, this page does not label an unofficial internet rank as the official AI cutoff.

Use this instead

Use your SRMJEEE rank and the options actually available during counselling. Do not confuse scholarship rank bands with counselling closing ranks.

What Will You Study?

The AI pathway combines computing foundations with AI-oriented study. When comparing the exact curriculum, look for programming, data structures, databases, machine learning, data handling, projects and relevant electives.

Who Should Choose AI?

AI is a good fit if you are comfortable with mathematics, programming and technical learning and already want an AI/ML-oriented career. If you want broader computing flexibility, compare the exact AI and CSE curricula before deciding.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum and average salary ₹10.5 LPA. NIRF 2026 engineering data reports a ₹6.32 lakh median salary for the reported four-year UG engineering population in 2024-25.

Neither is an AI-only salary. Use the programme scope before interpreting the numbers.

FAQs Students Ask

Is SRMJEEE used for B.Tech AI?

Yes. SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route.

What is the B.Tech eligibility at SRMIST?

SRMIST states 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory.

Is there an official AI closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Can I use ₹4.25 lakh as the AI fee?

No. ₹4.25 lakh is the annual fee for the specific CSE with Big Data Analytics listing at Kattankulathur.

Should I choose AI over CSE?

Compare the exact curriculum, campus, fee and your career target. AI is not automatically a better choice for every student.
