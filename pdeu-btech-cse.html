PDEU B.Tech Admission 2026

PDEU's official undergraduate admission page has listed JEE Main and Gujarat entrance routes for B.Tech. The exact route depends on the seat category. Gujarat government seats can involve ACPC, while PDEU handles applicable university-level All India admissions.

Basic eligibility

Students need the required Class 12 subjects and minimum marks under the current admission rules. Check the exact 2026 notice before applying because category and programme conditions can change.

PDEU Engineering Placements: What should a CSE aspirant look at?

Official NIRF benchmark

PDEU's NIRF 2025 Engineering submission reports a university-wide UG 4-year median salary of ₹6.42 LPA for 2023-24, with 618 of 1,112 graduates placed in minimum stipulated time.

This is not a CSE-only median. PDEU's placement page separately reports a historical ₹38 LPA highest offer to four CSE students by JP Morgan Chase. The correct way to judge CSE ROI is to compare median/typical outcomes, role quality and total cost, not only the highest offer.

PDEU B.Tech CSE FAQs

Is PDEU good for B.Tech CSE?

PDEU is a credible private-university option for engineering and technology, with NAAC A++ accreditation and an Industry 4.0-oriented School of Technology. For CSE, the final decision should compare branch-level outcomes, fees and your alternatives.

What is the PDEU CSE cutoff?

In PDEU's 2025 Round 1 All India cutoff, Computer Engineering closed at General rank 310 and Computer Science & Business Systems at 366. These are previous-cycle Round 1 ranks, not 2026 closing ranks.

Does PDEU accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. PDEU's official admission page has listed JEE Main for B.Tech. The exact seat allocation route depends on the admission category.

What is PDEU B.Tech CSE fees?

The latest accessible official B.Tech rule sheet is for 2024-25 and listed ₹1,24,500 tuition per semester for All India/Gujarat seats. It is a historical benchmark, not a confirmed 2026 fee.

What is the PDEU CSE highest package?

PDEU's placement page highlights a historical ₹38 LPA highest package offered to four CSE students by JP Morgan Chase. This should not be treated as the typical salary.

What is PDEU engineering median salary?

NIRF 2025 reports ₹6.42 LPA median for PDEU's UG 4-year engineering cohort in 2023-24.

Which is better at PDEU: Computer Engineering or CSBS?

Computer Engineering is the more directly core-computing route, while CSBS blends computing with business systems. Your choice should depend on curriculum, career target and the cutoff you can achieve.

Is PDEU private or government?

PDEU is a private university established under Gujarat state legislation. It is not an IIT/NIT or a government engineering college.

Does PDEU provide hostel?

Yes. Residential facilities are available, subject to availability. The 2024-25 B.Tech rules listed ₹96,000 annual hostel fee plus a refundable deposit, subject to revision.

Where is PDEU B.Tech campus?

The university is at Raisan, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, in the Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar region.
