IIM Kashipur Executive MBA Overview

Quick answer

IIM Kashipur's Executive MBA is a two-year programme for professionals with at least three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience after graduation.

The programme is intended for candidates who want to strengthen management capability while bringing prior professional experience into the classroom. The institute maintains dedicated eligibility, course structure, fee and student-profile pages.

Executive MBA Eligibility

The current official eligibility page requires a bachelor's degree recognised by UGC/AIU with minimum aggregate marks of 50% for General candidates, 47% for NC-OBC and 45% for SC/ST/DAP.

Candidates also need a minimum of three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience after graduation.

Experience note

The work-experience requirement is post-graduation. Applicants should use the institute's current eligibility page to verify how their specific employment history is counted.

Executive MBA Selection Process

IIM Kashipur states that selection is based on a valid CAT/GMAT score from the last two years or marks obtained in the Executive Management Aptitude Test (EMAT) conducted by IIM Kashipur, followed by Personal Interview.

EXAM

CAT / GMAT / EMAT

A qualifying score is one component of the selection process.

INTERVIEW

Personal Interview

Interview performance is considered along with the candidate's overall profile.

FINAL MERIT

Composite Profile

Final merit considers test score, academics, work experience and Personal Interview performance.

Who Should Consider the Executive MBA?

Strong fit

Working professionals with at least three years of post-graduation experience who want an IIM learning environment without treating the programme like a conventional fresh-graduate MBA.

Executive MBA FAQs

What is the minimum work experience?

Three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience after graduation.

What is the minimum graduation percentage?

50% for General, 47% for NC-OBC and 45% for SC/ST/DAP candidates according to the current official eligibility page.

What exams can be used?

A valid CAT/GMAT score from the last two years or IIM Kashipur's EMAT can be used as specified by the institute.
