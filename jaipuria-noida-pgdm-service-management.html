Jaipuria Noida PGDM Service Management: What It Means

PGDM (Service Management) is a two-year full-time programme focused on management in service businesses. Jaipuria describes it as an MBA-equivalent PGDM that combines functional management with service concepts such as service operations, customer experience and service strategy.

What makes Service Management different from the core PGDM?

The service lens is the key difference. Students can still build depth in marketing, finance, HR, analytics, operations or strategy, but the programme uses service-sector contexts. It can therefore suit students interested in consulting, BFSI, retail, hospitality, customer experience and other service-heavy industries.

Curriculum & Dual Specialisation

The official programme describes a curriculum covering core management subjects and a dual-specialisation structure. Students can choose from functional areas such as Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations Management, Business Analytics, Business Strategy and Economics, subject to the institute's final elective basket.

Service-sector capabilities

  • Customer experience management
  • Service operations
  • Service marketing
  • Service strategy and business development

Functional depth

  • Business analytics and operations analytics
  • Marketing and digital marketing
  • Finance and valuation
  • HR and organisational behaviour

The programme also includes internships, live projects and industry-oriented learning. Electives may change according to academic and industry requirements.

Who Should Choose PGDM Service Management?

Good fit

  • You want consulting, analytics or service-sector roles.
  • You are interested in customer experience and service operations.
  • You want a management degree with dual specialisation flexibility.

Compare alternatives if

  • You already know you want a highly specialised finance or analytics career.
  • Your alternative B-school has stronger role-specific placements.
  • The ₹16.50 lakh tuition makes your expected ROI uncomfortable.

PGDM Service Management FAQs

What is the fee for Service Management at Jaipuria Noida?

The official 2026-28 fee is ₹16.50 lakh, plus the refundable security deposit and separate hostel/mess costs.

Which exams are accepted?

CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT are listed by Jaipuria for the 2026 admission cycle.

What is the difference between PGDM and Service Management?

Service Management applies management concepts more directly to service businesses while retaining broad functional specialisation options.

Is Service Management equivalent to MBA?

Jaipuria states that its AIU-recognised PGDM programmes are equivalent to MBA.
