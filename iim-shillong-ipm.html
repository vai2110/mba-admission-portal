IIM Shillong IPM: What the Five-Year Programme Includes

IIM Shillong's IPM is a five-year integrated management programme designed for students entering after Class XII. The first four years are built around the undergraduate management stage, while the fifth year is the MBA stage. The programme offers structured exit pathways, allowing students to earn a qualification earlier if they meet the academic requirements.

5 YearsIntegrated management pathway
70%Minimum Class X and XII marks
MathsRequired in Classes XI & XII
Who is IPM for?

IPM is designed for students who already know they want a management career and prefer an integrated route from school to an MBA rather than completing a separate bachelor's degree followed by an MBA.

IIM Shillong IPM Eligibility 2026-31

For IPM 2026-31, a candidate must have been born on or after August 1, 2006. There is a five-year age relaxation for SC/ST/DA candidates. The candidate must have at least 70% marks in Class X and Class XII, or 65% for SC/ST/DA candidates, and must have studied Mathematics or Applied Mathematics in both Class XI and Class XII.

IIM Shillong IPM Admission Process 2026

Domestic applicants are shortlisted for Personal Interview using a valid IPM AT 2026 score. The institute's 2026 policy says the IPM AT is conducted by IIM Indore. International applicants can use SAT subject to the institute's separate requirements.

Application fee

The application fee for 2026 is ₹2,000 and ₹1,000 for SC/ST/DA/EWS candidates. The fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.

Important:

IPM AT is not the same as CAT. Students applying to IIM Shillong IPM should prepare specifically for the entrance-test pattern used for IPM admissions and then for the PI stage.

IIM Shillong IPM Fees: Current Published Structure

IIM Shillong's IPM finance page currently publishes tuition and non-academic expenses for the first three years, while Years IV and V are linked to the prevailing PGP fee for the respective years. Therefore, a single five-year rupee total cannot be responsibly presented as a fixed 2026-31 amount.

The institute states that non-academic expenses include hostel, IT infrastructure, student welfare and health insurance. The Year I fee includes the acceptance fee. Alumni fees of ₹10,000 and a refundable ₹20,000 caution deposit are payable at admission in addition to the listed fees. Mess bills are payable as actuals in advance.

Costs not included in the published estimate

  • Mess bills beyond the listed fee.
  • Travel and stay for local/global immersion, charged as actuals.
  • Study-abroad costs, charged separately.
  • Any applicable fee revision when the student enters the PGP/MBA stage.

IIM Shillong IPM Structure & Exit Options

The IPM is built in two connected phases. Years 1-3 lead to the BBA stage, the fourth year continues the undergraduate management pathway with BBA (Honours), and the fifth year is the MBA stage.

End of Year 1Certificate in ManagementEarly exit option subject to academic requirements.
End of Year 2Diploma in ManagementSecond structured exit point.
End of Year 3BBAUndergraduate degree route.
End of Year 4BBA (Honours)Honours undergraduate pathway.
End of Year 5MBACompletion of integrated management programme.
Why exit options matter:

The flexibility is useful if your academic or career plans change. However, students choosing IPM primarily for the MBA should evaluate the full five-year financial and time commitment rather than assuming they will necessarily complete all five years.

Campus, Hostel & Student Life

IIM Shillong's IPM is fully residential, and students are required to stay in institute hostels. The institute positions residential life as an integral part of the learning experience.

The Shillong setting provides a campus environment distinct from metro-city business schools. Students should consider the climate, distance from home, connectivity and the fact that much of the programme's academic and social life happens within the residential ecosystem.

Good fit if you want

  • An integrated management route after Class XII.
  • An IIM campus environment from the undergraduate stage.
  • Residential peer learning and structured exit options.

Think carefully if

  • You are unsure about pursuing management after Class XII.
  • You strongly prefer a metro-city undergraduate ecosystem.
  • You want maximum flexibility to switch disciplines after school.

IIM Shillong IPM FAQs

What is the IIM Shillong IPM eligibility?

For 2026-31, candidates need at least 70% in Class X and XII, or 65% for SC/ST/DA, must have studied Mathematics or Applied Mathematics in Classes XI and XII, and must meet the age requirement.

Which exam is accepted for IIM Shillong IPM?

Domestic applicants need a valid IPM AT 2026 score conducted by IIM Indore. International applicants can use SAT subject to the institute's rules.

Is IIM Shillong IPM residential?

Yes. The institute states that IPM is fully residential and students must stay in institute hostels.

How much does IIM Shillong IPM cost?

The institute currently publishes ₹3.05 lakh tuition plus ₹76,000 non-academic expenses for each of Years I-III. Years IV and V are charged according to the prevailing PGP fee, so the final five-year cost is not fixed on the current public fee page.

Can I exit IIM Shillong IPM before five years?

Yes. The programme provides structured exit options after Years 1, 2, 3 and 4, subject to academic requirements, with qualifications ranging from Certificate in Management to BBA (Honours).
