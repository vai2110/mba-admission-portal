GGSIPU Law FAQs

Is GGSIPU law admission the same for every course?

No. BA LLB, BBA LLB, LLB and LLM can have different eligibility and admission routes.

Should I compare affiliated law colleges separately?

Yes. Compare faculty, approvals, fees, placements, internships, campus and location at institute level.
