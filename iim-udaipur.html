IIM Udaipur MBA: Key Facts for Applicants

IIM Udaipur's two-year MBA is a six-term residential programme with a mandatory summer internship after the first year. The institute's current curriculum uses a flexi-core structure that allows students to choose courses aligned with their interests and career goals.

What changed in the 2026 admission cycle?

IIM Udaipur moved out of the Common Admission Process (CAP) and conducted its MBA selection independently for the 2026–28 batch. The institute's process uses CAT, an online registration stage, profile-based PI shortlisting and a final composite score.

How to Get Admission to IIM Udaipur MBA

The 2026–28 process has four practical stages: CAT, online registration, PI shortlisting and final merit-list preparation. The online registration after the CAT result is mandatory for candidates who receive the institute's invitation.

Step 1: Meet the CAT eligibility threshold

For the General category, the published minimum is 94 overall with 75 in each of VRC, DILR and QA. The minimum varies by category. Meeting this threshold only makes a candidate eligible for the registration/shortlisting process; it does not represent a safe admission score.

Step 2: Complete IIM Udaipur online registration

Eligible candidates receive an email invitation to complete the mandatory online registration after CAT results. Registration itself does not mean that a PI call has been received. Candidates who do not register within the stipulated timeline are not considered further.

Step 3: PI shortlisting

IIM Udaipur shortlists candidates using their CAT 2025 score and profile. The profile includes Class 10, Class 12, graduation, work experience and gender-diversity information. Candidates invited to PI complete the application form and select preferred PI locations.

Step 4: Personal Interview and final merit list

The final composite score uses 55% normalised CAT score, 25% PI and 20% profile. Within the profile component, academic profile carries 6 points, work experience 6 points and diversity 8 points. IIM Udaipur states that CAT score, rather than CAT percentile, is used in the final composite calculation.

What students should budget separately

The published regular fee schedule should not be treated as the complete personal cost of attending an MBA. Students should separately plan for laptop/computer, travel, clothing, stationery, laundry and other personal expenses. These costs vary by student and are not included as a fixed amount in the published fee table.

IIM Udaipur MBA FAQs

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Udaipur MBA?

For the General category in the 2026–28 admission policy, the minimum is 94 overall with 75 in VRC, DILR and QA. This is a qualifying threshold for the process, not a guaranteed admission score.

Does IIM Udaipur use CAP for MBA admission?

No for the 2026–28 batch. IIM Udaipur moved out of CAP and conducted its own registration, PI shortlisting and final merit-list process.

What is the IIM Udaipur MBA fee for 2026–28?

The published schedule totals approximately ₹21.01 lakh across the offer-acceptance payment and six terms. This includes tuition, hostel, mess, the refundable caution deposit and the alumni fee where applicable.

What is the final selection weightage at IIM Udaipur?

The final composite score gives 55% to the normalised CAT score, 25% to the PI and 20% to profile. Profile consists of academic profile, work experience and diversity.

Does IIM Udaipur consider work experience?

Yes. Work experience is part of the profile used for PI shortlisting and carries 6 points within the 20-point profile component of the final composite score.
