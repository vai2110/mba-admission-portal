IIM Udaipur MBA Admission 2026: Fees, CAT Cutoff, Eligibility, Dates & Placements

IIM Udaipur MBA: What Applicants Should Know

IIM Udaipur's Two-Year MBA is a full-time residential programme spread across six terms. The current programme includes a mandatory summer internship after the first year and a revised curriculum built around compulsory, flexi-core, experiential and elective courses.

How the current IIM Udaipur MBA is structured

The first year focuses on management fundamentals. Terms I and II contain compulsory core courses, while Term III introduces flexi-core and experiential courses. The second year is primarily elective-based and includes a compulsory Capstone course.

The institute's revised curriculum adds flexi-core and experiential learning and incorporates feedback from faculty, alumni and recruiters.

What changed for MBA admission?

For the 2026–28 batch, IIM Udaipur conducted its own MBA admission process rather than using the Common Admission Process. The published process has four stages: CAT, online registration, PI shortlisting and final merit-list preparation.

How to Get Admission to IIM Udaipur MBA

The domestic admission process is not a single CAT-score-based selection. Candidates first need to satisfy the published CAT and academic eligibility requirements. Eligible candidates are then invited for online registration, followed by PI shortlisting and final merit-list preparation.

Step 1: Meet the CAT and academic eligibility requirements

For the 2026–28 cycle, IIM Udaipur used CAT 2025. Candidates must meet the category-wise overall and sectional CAT thresholds and the programme's graduation eligibility conditions before moving into the institute's process.

Step 2: Complete the mandatory IIM Udaipur registration

Candidates who meet the stated eligibility and CAT thresholds receive an email from the Admission Office to complete the mandatory online registration after CAT results. During this stage, candidates can update Class 10, Class 12, graduation and work-experience information. For the 2026–28 policy, work experience was considered up to 31 January 2026.

Step 3: Shortlisting for Personal Interview

IIM Udaipur uses CAT performance and profile information to shortlist candidates for PI. The profile includes academic performance, work experience and diversity-related components. Candidates invited for PI complete the required application information and choose from the available interview locations.

Step 4: Personal Interview

The PI is a separate component of the final selection. IIM Udaipur states that candidates who do not obtain the minimum PI score set by the institute will not be considered for the final merit list.

Step 5: Final merit list

The final composite score for the 2026–28 policy comprises 55% normalised CAT score, 25% PI and 20% profile. Within the profile component, academic profile carries 6 points, work experience 6 points and diversity 8 points. IIM Udaipur states that the CAT score, not CAT percentile, is used in the final composite calculation.

What should a CAT aspirant infer from the 94 percentile cutoff?

94 percentile is the published minimum for General candidates, not a guaranteed admission target. IIM Udaipur notes that the institute may modify cutoffs and that PI-call scores may be higher than the stated minimum. The final score also includes PI and profile.

Why CAT percentile alone cannot predict final admission

The final merit calculation is based on normalised CAT score, PI and profile. Two candidates with similar CAT percentiles can therefore have different final composite scores because their underlying CAT scores and profile components differ.

What is included in the published fee schedule?

The 2026–28 fee schedule explicitly lists tuition, hostel and mess charges, along with the caution deposit and alumni fee where applicable. The term-wise amounts are the correct basis for estimating the institute-billed cost for the two-year programme.

What expenses are extra for students?

Students should separately budget for personal expenses such as a laptop/computer, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry. These are not given as fixed amounts in the regular 2026–28 fee schedule and should not be added as an invented fixed figure to the official course cost.

Financial assistance for IIM Udaipur MBA students

IIM Udaipur states that students with annual household income below ₹6 lakh are eligible to apply for financial assistance, while students above that level may also apply if they can demonstrate severe financial difficulty. Assistance is provided through tuition-fee waivers based on assessed need.

IIM Udaipur MBA Curriculum and Summer Internship

The academic structure is relevant when comparing IIM Udaipur with other two-year MBA programmes because the institute has introduced flexibility before the second year rather than keeping the first year entirely fixed.

First year: core, flexi-core and experiential learning

Terms I and II contain compulsory core courses across foundational and functional management areas. Term III contains flexi-core and experiential courses. Skill-based courses include Spreadsheet Modelling, Managerial Oral Communication and Written Analysis and Communications.

Summer internship after Year 1

At the end of the first three academic terms, students undertake a two-month corporate internship. The institute describes the internship as an application-oriented component in which students work on projects and tasks in a corporate setting.

Second year: electives and Capstone

The second year is built around electives across major management functions and multidisciplinary areas. IIM Udaipur lists courses such as Digital Strategy and Digital Transformation, Management Consulting and International Business in Practice. A compulsory Capstone course uses simulated decision-making scenarios to apply management concepts.

Is IIM Udaipur MBA Right for You?

Potentially strong fit

  • You want a two-year residential MBA with a structured first year and elective flexibility in the second year.
  • You are comfortable with an admission process where CAT, PI and profile all matter.
  • You want an MBA with a mandatory corporate internship after the first year.
  • You want to compare the institute using an official term-wise fee schedule.

Check carefully before applying

  • You are relying on the 94 percentile General cutoff as a guaranteed admission target.
  • You require three consecutive years of comparable Two-Year MBA placement statistics.
  • You are comparing the two-year MBA with IIMU's one-year programmes without considering their work-experience requirements.
  • You have not budgeted separately for personal expenses outside institute charges.
Student decision

IIM Udaipur should be evaluated on the combination of its independent admission process, six-term curriculum, summer internship, published fee structure and programme-level placement evidence.

IIM Udaipur MBA: Frequently Asked Questions

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Udaipur MBA?

For the 2026–28 General category, IIM Udaipur published a minimum of 94 overall with 75 in VRC, DILR and QA. This is a qualifying threshold, not a guaranteed final-admission score.

Does IIM Udaipur use CAP for MBA admission?

No for the 2026–28 batch. IIM Udaipur conducted its own registration, PI shortlisting and final merit-list process.

What is the IIM Udaipur MBA fee for 2026–28?

The published schedule totals ₹21,01,008 across the offer-acceptance payment and six terms. This includes tuition, hostel, mess, the refundable caution deposit and the alumni fee where applicable.

What is the final selection weightage at IIM Udaipur?

The final composite score gives 55% to normalised CAT score, 25% to PI and 20% to profile. Profile consists of academic profile, work experience and diversity.

Does IIM Udaipur consider work experience?

Yes. Work experience is part of the profile and carries 6 points within the 20-point profile component of the final composite score.

What is the latest official IIM Udaipur MBA placement figure?

The current official admissions portal reports ₹18.77 LPA average maximum earning potential and ₹47.66 LPA highest maximum earning potential for the Two-Year MBA 2022–24.

Does IIM Udaipur offer one-year MBA programmes?

Yes. The institute lists one-year MBA programmes in Global Supply Chain Management and Digital Enterprise Management. The 2026 admissions portal lists graduation plus at least three years of work experience for these programmes.
