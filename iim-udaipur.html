IIM Udaipur MBA: What Applicants Should Know

IIM Udaipur's Two-Year MBA is a full-time residential programme spread across six terms. The current programme includes a mandatory summer internship after the first year, flexi-core and experiential learning, elective flexibility and international exposure. Source: IIM Udaipur Two-Year MBA programme page.

How to Get Admission to IIM Udaipur MBA

The 2026–28 domestic process followed CAT → mandatory IIMU registration → PI shortlisting → final merit list.

1. Meet eligibility and CAT thresholds

Candidates must satisfy the published academic and CAT requirements. The institute states that its admission criteria may be modified during the process.

2. Complete mandatory IIMU registration

Eligible candidates were invited by email after CAT results to complete mandatory online registration. This was a pre-interview requirement and did not itself mean a PI call.

3. PI shortlisting

Shortlisting used the CAT 2025 score and profile information including Class 10, Class 12, graduation, work experience and gender diversity.

4. Personal Interview

PI locations included Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai and the IIM Udaipur campus. Candidates had to provide location preferences.

5. Final merit list

The final composite score gave 55% to normalised CAT score, 25% to PI and 20% to profile. Profile points were Academic 6, Work Experience 6 and Diversity 8. The final calculation uses the CAT score, not CAT percentile.

IIM Udaipur MBA Curriculum & Summer Internship

The programme spans two academic years and six terms, with a revised curriculum built around compulsory, flexi-core, experiential and elective learning.

First year

Terms I and II focus on compulsory management foundations. Term III introduces flexi-core and experiential courses. Skill-based courses include Spreadsheet Modelling, Managerial Oral Communications and Written Analysis and Communications.

Summer internship

Students undertake a mandatory summer internship after the first year, applying classroom learning to corporate work.

Second year

The second year provides broader elective flexibility and includes a compulsory Capstone component.

Financial Assistance & Scholarships

IIM Udaipur states that students with annual household income below ₹6 lakh are eligible to apply for institute financial assistance. Students above that level may also apply where severe financial difficulty can be demonstrated. Assistance is based on assessed need and can take the form of tuition-fee waivers.

Students should also review applicable government scholarship schemes and education-loan options referenced by the institute.

IIM Udaipur MBA FAQs

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Udaipur MBA?

For 2026–28, the published General-category minimum was 94 overall with 75 in VRC, DILR and QA. This is a qualifying threshold, not a guaranteed final-admission percentile.

Does IIM Udaipur use CAP for MBA admission?

No for the 2026–28 batch. IIM Udaipur ran its own registration, PI shortlisting and final merit-list process.

What is the total IIM Udaipur MBA fee for 2026–28?

The published schedule totals ₹21,51,258, including the ₹1,00,000 offer-acceptance payment and six term payments. The schedule includes a ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit.

What is the final selection weightage?

The 2026–28 final composite score gives 55% to normalised CAT score, 25% to PI and 20% to profile.

Does work experience matter for the Two-Year MBA?

Work experience is not a basic eligibility requirement, but it carries 6 points within the 20-point profile component in the 2026–28 selection formula.

What is the latest official placement figure?

The latest comparable NIRF data reviewed reports ₹17.10 LPA median salary for 2024–25, with 325 of 328 graduates placed. Separately, the institute placement portal reports ₹18.77 LPA average and ₹47.66 LPA highest maximum earning potential for the Two-Year MBA 2022–24.
