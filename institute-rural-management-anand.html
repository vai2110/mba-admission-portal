IRMA Anand: What Students Should Know

The Institute of Rural Management Anand (IRMA), established in 1979, is known for management education focused on rural transformation, cooperatives, agribusiness, development, sustainability and inclusive business. IRMA's official programme pages continue to identify the flagship programme as Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Rural Management) - PGDM(RM).

The flagship PGDM(RM) is a two-year fully residential programme. IRMA is therefore better evaluated as a specialist management school rather than as a generic MBA where the only comparison point is the highest salary package.

Quick answer: who should consider IRMA?

IRMA is a strong fit for students targeting agribusiness, dairy/cooperatives, rural finance, BFSI, FMCG, CSR, social business, development consulting and related management roles. Students seeking a purely generalist urban MBA should compare recruiter mix, role profile and compensation with general-management schools before deciding.

IRMA Anand FAQs

What is the IRMA fee for 2026-28?

The reported 2026-28 total programme fee for Indian Nationals is ₹18.70 lakh, comprising ₹13.60 lakh tuition and ₹5.10 lakh in reported library, computing and materials charges. Students should verify the final institute fee notice before payment.

What was the IRMA average package in 2025?

The 2025 mean CTC was ₹15.64 LPA and the median CTC was ₹15.80 LPA.

What was the highest package at IRMA in 2025?

The highest reported CTC for the 2025 flagship batch was ₹31.84 LPA.

What was the last date to apply to IRMA in 2026?

The last date for the 2026 cycle was January 19, 2026.

When will IRMA 2027 applications open?

The 2027-29 schedule was not official at the time of this update. For planning, candidates can expect an application window around late 2026 to January 2027, based on the previous cycle. Treat this as tentative.

Is IRMA only for agriculture students?

No. The flagship programme accepts graduates from diverse disciplines subject to the published eligibility criteria.

Does IRMA have a management quota?

IRMA's published admission information states that there is no management quota for its flagship programme.
