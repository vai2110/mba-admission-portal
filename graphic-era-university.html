Graphic Era University: What Students Should Know

Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) is located at 566/6, Bell Road, Society Area, Clement Town, Dehradun, Uttarakhand. Its current official website describes a multi-disciplinary university with 25+ departments and 135+ programmes. The programme portfolio covers undergraduate, postgraduate, certificate and PhD education across fields including engineering, computer science, management, commerce, biosciences, law, design, hospitality, nursing and allied health.

For a student, this breadth matters because the admission route and fee structure differ significantly by course. GEU's official fee portal publishes separate 2026 fee structures and scholarship slabs for individual programmes. For example, BCA and BBA use Class 12/CUET-based slabs, while MBA has separate graduation/entrance-test slabs and B.Tech CSE has a distinct published fee structure.

Quick answer: What is Graphic Era University best known for?

GEU has a particularly broad portfolio, with strong visibility around technology and engineering, computer applications, management, commerce and professional health programmes. Students should compare the exact course rather than relying on a university-wide ranking or package.

Graphic Era University Admission Process

The university's admission system is course-specific but generally follows an online application, document submission, evaluation under the relevant programme criteria, provisional/final admission and fee payment. Many GEU course pages explicitly state that merit is prepared on the basis of the qualifying examination.

What you should check before applying

For UG applicants

  • Check Class 12 subjects and minimum marks.
  • Check whether CUET/JEE or another score is relevant to your course.
  • Compare scholarship slab with the published fee.

For PG applicants

  • Check degree eligibility and course-specific entrance requirements.
  • Do not assume MBA admission rules apply to other PG programmes.
  • Confirm the latest fee before paying.

Scholarship reality check

GEU's 2026 fee pages commonly state that 25% of seats are available for scholarship on a first-come, first-served basis. The university also lists a 5% scholarship for wards of defence personnel and an additional 10% scholarship on net tuition fee for girls candidates, subject to the applicable programme policy.

Budget beyond tuition:

Students should also budget for hostel/accommodation where required, food, transport, books, laptop, personal expenses, examination/library/insurance/student-welfare charges and any programme-specific industry integration or global-immersion fee.

Graphic Era University FAQs

Is Graphic Era University only good for MBA and engineering?

No. GEU is a multi-disciplinary university. Its official programme list includes engineering, computer applications, management, commerce, health sciences, law, design, hospitality, humanities, biosciences and other disciplines.

Which courses are most useful to compare before applying?

Start with the course that matches your intended career: B.Tech CSE for technology, BCA for computer applications, BBA for business, B.Com (Hons.) for commerce/finance, BPT for physiotherapy and MBA for postgraduate management. The comparison table above is designed for this first-stage decision.

Does GEU have one common fee for all courses?

No. GEU publishes separate fee structures by programme. Scholarship slabs and one-time/other charges also vary by course.

Is the ₹61.99 lakh package the average salary at GEU?

No. It is the university's highlighted highest package figure. GEU's own placement showcase associates that cited offer with a B.Tech CSE student. It should not be interpreted as the typical salary for every programme.

Are the 2027 admission dates official?

No. Where GEU has not published a university-wide 2027 calendar, the dates shown on this page are clearly marked tentative planning estimates. Students should replace them with the official programme notification when released.
