Graphic Era University MBA: What Students Should Know

Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) is located at 566/6, Bell Road, Clement Town, Dehradun. Its Department of Management Studies was established in 2003 and currently lists four postgraduate management programmes: MBA Business Analytics with industry partner Grant Thornton, MBA IMPACT, MBA in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, and the general MBA.

The university's general MBA is a two-year programme. The official programme information describes an industry-oriented curriculum covering areas such as accounting, economics, marketing, organisational behaviour and strategy, with opportunities for internships, projects and specialisation.

Quick answer: Is Graphic Era a good MBA option?

It can be a relevant option for students who want a university MBA in Dehradun with multiple management pathways and a strong focus on industry exposure. Your decision should depend on the exact programme, fee after scholarship, placement role you target and the difference between university-wide placement claims and MBA-specific offers.

Graphic Era University MBA Admission Process

The university's admissions workflow involves selecting the programme, starting the online application, uploading qualifying documents, reviewing and submitting the form, paying the application fee and completing course-fee payment after admission/provisional allotment.

Management-specific evaluation

The Department of Management states that candidates may go through an aptitude test and personal interview/self-introduction video. Valid CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT or NMAT scores may provide exemption from the aptitude test under university policy.

Graphic Era University MBA Fees 2026

The general MBA fee page publishes fee slabs based on graduation percentage or CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT percentile. Scholarships

GEU states that 25% of seats are available for scholarship on a first-come, first-served basis. The fee page also lists a 5% scholarship for wards of defence personnel and an additional 10% scholarship on net tuition fee for girls candidates, subject to the stated policy.

Important:

The published fee is not the same as your total personal budget. Add travel, books, laptop, food/personal expenses and any programme-specific/global-immersion costs not covered by the fee schedule.

Graphic Era MBA Placements

GEU reports a university-wide placement ecosystem with 6,200+ total offers, 200+ companies visited and a 100% placement-success statement across departments. These are university-wide figures and should not be interpreted as the average MBA package.

The official MBA placement records page currently lists individual MBA offers. The highest listed MBA offer is ₹15.40 LPA for an MBA AI&DS student with GMR in 2025. Other listed 2025 MBA offers include ₹10.20 LPA at BNY Mellon, ₹10 LPA in marketing and ₹8.20 LPA in HR. The page also lists 2026 MBA offers, including ₹8.83 LPA at Ameriprise Financial.

Placement reality check

MBA Curriculum & Learning

The general MBA curriculum covers management foundations and allows students to build domain expertise through areas such as finance, marketing, HR, business analytics, digital marketing, international business, logistics and supply chain management, entrepreneurship and other sector-oriented specialisations.

The AI & Data Science MBA goes much deeper into Python, statistics, databases, big-data analytics, machine learning, business intelligence, data engineering, generative AI and AI in business transformation. The Business Analytics programme with Grant Thornton similarly combines management subjects with programming, machine learning, deep learning, NLP and analytics.

Campus & Student Life

The university's campus is at Clement Town, Dehradun. GEU describes student life around academics, infrastructure, events, sports, clubs and global exposure. The university also states that students from 65+ countries, 28 Indian states and 8 Union Territories contribute to its international and diverse campus environment.

Location advantage

Dehradun provides a quieter university-city environment while remaining connected to North Indian education and business hubs. For an MBA student, however, location should be weighed against the specific programme's industry exposure and placement outcomes.

Graphic Era MBA FAQs

Is Graphic Era MBA good for a fresher?

Yes. The general MBA is a two-year programme and the university states that fresh graduates as well as working professionals are eligible. Your choice should depend on the specialisation and placement role you want.

Does Graphic Era accept CAT, MAT, XAT and CMAT?

The management department states that candidates with valid CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT or NMAT scores may be exempted from the aptitude test as per university policy.

What is the MBA fee at Graphic Era University?

The 2026 general MBA fee varies by scholarship/payment slab. The official fee page also lists one-time admission, enrolment and refundable caution charges plus other charges.

Is the ₹61.99 lakh package an MBA package?

No. GEU's ₹61.99 lakh figure is a university-wide highest package and its own placement page identifies the cited ₹61.99 lakh offer with B.Tech CSE. MBA-specific listed offers are substantially different.

Which MBA should I choose if I want analytics?

Compare the dedicated Business Analytics programme with Grant Thornton and the MBA in AI & Data Science. The latter has explicit coursework in Python, machine learning, data engineering, GenAI and AI in business transformation.
