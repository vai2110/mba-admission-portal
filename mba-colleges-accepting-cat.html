Methodology & data note

Official admission information verifies CAT routes for FMS, IIFT, IIT Madras DoMS, SPJIMR and IMT Ghaziabad. IIFT states that its 2026–28 full-time MBA admissions use CAT 2025 along with other selection parameters; IIT Madras requires the CAT scorecard for domestic MBA applicants; SPJIMR accepts CAT for its PGDM; and IMT Ghaziabad accepts CAT for its full-time PGDM programmes.
