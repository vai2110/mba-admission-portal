MBA Programme

Programme Overview

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur offers a rigorous postgraduate MBA programme designed to develop leadership and analytical skills. Admissions are conducted once a year, specifically for the first semester.

Admission Details

Admissions to the MBA programme are processed through the official IIT Kanpur PG Admissions portal. Candidates must submit a separate application form for the department during the announced application window.

MBAManagement SciencesOnce a year (First Semester)

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the minimum marks/CPI requirements as specified in the official PG manual. For detailed eligibility requirements, including specific academic background and entrance test scores, please refer to the official eligibility page.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are MBA admissions conducted?

Admissions to the MBA programme are conducted once a year, during the first semester.

How do I apply for the MBA programme?

Interested candidates must register and submit their applications through the official IIT Kanpur PG Admissions portal during the active application window.

Where can I find the detailed eligibility requirements?

Detailed eligibility criteria, including minimum CPI and academic qualifications, are available on the official PG Admissions eligibility page.
