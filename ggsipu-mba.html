GGSIPU MBA Overview

GGSIPU offers MBA education through its University Schools and participating institutes. The admission route is programme-specific; current 2026 notices show MBA counselling through CAT, CMAT and CET routes. Students should therefore identify the exact MBA programme and participating institute before comparing options.

Who should consider GGSIPU MBA?

Students seeking a Delhi-NCR MBA and willing to compare institute-level fees, placements, commute and admission route rather than evaluating the university as one homogeneous MBA campus.

GGSIPU MBA Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the MBA programme and admission route. Candidates should check the current PG/management admission brochure for the qualifying degree, minimum marks, entrance score requirements and category-specific rules applicable to their programme.

MBA fees vary by University School and affiliated institute. The 2026-27 UG brochure separately publishes fee structures for University Schools, government institutes and self-financing institutes; the relevant PG fee notification should be checked for the exact MBA option.

GGSIPU MBA Cutoff

GGSIPU publishes round-wise cutoff reports. Current 2026 notices include separate cutoff reports for MBA through CAT, CMAT and CET. A useful comparison is the closing rank/score for the exact round and admission route, not a generic university cutoff.

Cutoff tip

When comparing colleges, record the programme, admission route, round, category and institute together. Otherwise two cutoff numbers may not be comparable.

GGSIPU MBA Placements

MBA placement performance should be evaluated institute by institute. Compare batch size, eligible students, placement rate, average/median salary, highest salary, recruiters and role mix wherever the exact institute publishes these figures.

GGSIPU MBA FAQs

Which entrance exams are accepted for GGSIPU MBA?

For 2026, GGSIPU counselling notices show MBA routes through CAT 2025, CMAT 2026 and CET 2026. The applicable route should be confirmed for the specific programme.

Is GGSIPU MBA the same across all colleges?

No. Participating institutes can differ in curriculum, fees, seats, location and placements, so institute-level comparison is essential.

How should I compare GGSIPU MBA placements?

Use average and median salary together with placement rate, batch size, role mix and recruiter quality instead of relying only on the highest package.
