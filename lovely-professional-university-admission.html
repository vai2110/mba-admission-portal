LPU Admission 2026-27: Is Admission Open?

LPU's official admission portal confirms that the 2026 admission process has started. Because LPU offers 150+ programmes, there is no single deadline that applies to every course and every phase.

Most important advice

Do not use an old universal “LPU last date” for every programme. Open the exact course page, check the current phase, eligibility and test requirement, and then submit the application.

LPU Admission Dates 2026-27

LPU Admission Fee & Provisional Payment

LPU's admission portal states that a ₹10,000 provisional admission payment is adjusted against the semester programme fee, subject to stated exceptions. This is different from the complete tuition fee.

Do not confuse application and admission payments

Before paying, check what the amount represents, whether it is refundable, and how it is adjusted against the final fee.

Common LPU Admission Mistakes to Avoid

Do this

  • Check the exact programme page.
  • Compare fee after scholarship.
  • Save payment receipts.
  • Check reporting instructions.

Avoid this

  • Relying on an old deadline.
  • Assuming every course accepts the same exam.
  • Paying before checking the refund terms.
  • Assuming the highest placement applies to your course.

LPU Admission FAQs

Are LPU admissions open for 2026?

Yes. The official LPU admission portal states that the 2026 process has started.

What is the last date for LPU admission?

There is no single universal domestic deadline for all programmes; dates vary by programme and phase.

What is LPUNEST?

LPUNEST is LPU's entrance and scholarship test used for multiple programmes.

How much is the provisional admission payment?

LPU currently states ₹10,000, adjusted against semester programme fee subject to stated exceptions.
