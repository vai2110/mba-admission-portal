Goa Institute of Management: Is GIM a Good B-School?

Goa Institute of Management (GIM), established in 1993, is a fully residential management institute in Goa. GIM is AACSB accredited and says it has 95+ full-time faculty, 7,500+ alumni and 200+ companies visiting across programmes. Its current portfolio includes PGDM, PGDM-HCM, PGDM-BDA and PGDM-BIFS, along with other programmes.

Quick student verdict

GIM is especially relevant if you want a residential B-school with a strong campus experience, multiple specialised PGDM options and a structured admissions process. The main decision point is ROI: the published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh before personal expenses, so compare the total cost with the salary and role you realistically target.

Why students consider GIM

  • AACSB-accredited management institute.
  • 50+ acre residential campus in Goa.
  • Flagship PGDM plus HCM, BDA and BIFS options.
  • Achiever's Round for strong profiles.
  • ACCESS scholarships and full tuition waivers for eligible students.

What to check before joining

  • Total cost beyond academic fee.
  • Role-wise placement outcomes.
  • Whether a specialised programme fits your target career.
  • Exam score and profile strength for shortlisting.
  • Alternative B-school calls at a similar fee.

GIM Admission 2027-29: Dates, Eligibility & Selection

GIM has already published key 2027-29 admission deadlines. Applications close on 4 January 2027, while the institute lists February 2027 for evaluation, March 2027 for final admission results and June 2027 for the academic session.

Stage2027-29 timeline
First Mover22 October 2026
Early Bird19 November 2026
Achiever's Round3 December 2026
CAT 2026 deadline23 December 2026
Last date4 January 2027
EvaluationFebruary 2027
Final admission resultsMarch 2027
Academic sessionJune 2027

For the full-time PGDM programmes, GIM requires at least 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree; SC/ST candidates have a 45% minimum under the stated criteria. Candidates completing degree requirements by June 2027 may apply. CAT 2026, XAT 2027 or eligible GMAT scores are accepted. BDA applicants also need the GIM Analytics Aptitude Test (GAAT).

Regular selection gives 40% weight to objective assessment, 15% academics, 10% work experience, 30% face-to-face evaluation and 5% profile/diversity. The Achiever's Round is profile-based and can shortlist candidates without initially considering CAT/XAT/GMAT scores.

GIM Fees 2026-28 & Scholarships

The latest detailed official fee page publishes ₹21.45 lakh as the academic fee for the 2026-28 batch for PGDM, HCM, BDA and BIFS. A 2027-29 academic fee has not been published yet, so ₹21.45 lakh should be treated only as the latest benchmark.

ACCESS Scholarship

For PGDM, GIM offers up to 45 scholarships of ₹5 lakh each. BDA has up to 21 such awards and BIFS up to 7. HCM has up to 7 GIM-MBBS scholarships of ₹5 lakh each.

Full tuition fee waiver

Students with family income below ₹8 lakh per year can be considered for a full tuition fee waiver, subject to financial-need assessment and a maximum of 5% of admitted students.

GIM states that fees once paid are not refundable if a student discontinues the programme. The caution deposit is refundable after exit subject to dues, with ₹5,000 credited as GIM Alumni Association membership on graduation.

For the 2024-26 PGDM batch, GIM reports a highest package of ₹35 LPA, 50 PPOs and 110+ recruiters. The PGDM batch profile listed 284 students, with 46% freshers. Sector participation included financial services, IT, banking, consulting and FMCG/FMCD.

Who Should Choose GIM?

Strong fit if

  • You want a residential management campus with strong student life.
  • You prefer a broad PGDM or a focused HCM/BDA/BIFS pathway.
  • You have a competitive CAT/XAT/GMAT profile or a strong achievement profile.
  • You can justify the total investment through your target role.

Compare alternatives if

  • Your budget is substantially below the total GIM cost.
  • You are choosing only because of the Goa location.
  • Your target career has a much stronger specialised pipeline elsewhere.
  • You are relying only on the highest CTC to estimate ROI.

Goa Institute of Management FAQs

What is the GIM fee?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh for PGDM, HCM, BDA and BIFS, before personal expenses. The 2027-29 fee has not yet been published.

What exams does GIM accept?

For full-time PGDM programmes, GIM lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027 and eligible GMAT scores. BDA applicants additionally need GAAT.

What is the GIM 2027-29 last date?

The current official admission page lists 4 January 2027 as the last date, with earlier First Mover, Early Bird, Achiever's Round and CAT deadlines.

Is GIM residential?

Yes. GIM describes its full-time PGDM programmes as residential and operates a fully residential campus.

Does GIM offer scholarships?

Yes. GIM offers programme-specific scholarships including ACCESS awards and full tuition fee waivers for eligible students.
