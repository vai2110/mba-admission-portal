NMIMS Placement Highlights 2025-26

Quick answer

SBM NMIMS Mumbai reports 190 recruiters for MBA Core and 37 recruiters for MBA HR in the 2025-26 placement season. Average CTC is ₹24.6 LPA for MBA Core and ₹19.08 LPA for MBA HR.

Recruiter Ecosystem

SBM NMIMS Mumbai describes its placement ecosystem as a dedicated placement and corporate-relations function supported by faculty, student placement committees and corporate partners. Recruiter participation varies by programme and placement season.

MBA CORE

Broad role mix

The flagship MBA connects students with opportunities across functions and industries through the annual final placement process.

MBA HR

HR-focused roles

MBA HR has a separate placement cohort and dedicated programme leadership within SBM.

CORPORATE RELATIONS

Year-round interface

The placement office works with recruiters and industry partners around hiring, corporate interaction and student preparation.

NMIMS Placement Process

Placement is managed through the School of Business Management's placement and corporate-relations function. Students participate in recruiter processes according to the eligibility and role requirements communicated for each company.

What applicants should compare

Average CTC is useful, but a better ROI assessment also considers programme fee, role quality, sector mix, location, recruiter diversity and the difference between programme-level and campus-wide statistics.

NMIMS Placement Reports

The official placement page currently provides a Final Placement 2025-26 section and also maintains previous placement-report references. For historical comparisons, use the programme-specific official reports rather than third-party package lists.
