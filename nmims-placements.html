Quick Answer

NMIMS offers MBA, MBA Tech, and MBA Pharma Tech programmes across multiple campuses (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Indore, Navi Mumbai, and Shirpur). Admissions are merit-based, primarily utilizing the NMAT by GMAC exam followed by a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).

Overview

SVKM's NMIMS is a Deemed to be University with a 44-year legacy. It operates across seven campuses and offers diverse management programmes. The university is recognized for its industry-aligned curriculum and professional management education.

Admission Process

Admissions at NMIMS are strictly merit-based. The university does not accept capitation fees and operates without an agent network.

  • Entrance Exam: NMAT by GMAC.
  • Selection Stages: Entrance test followed by Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).
  • Eligibility: Candidates must meet the specific programme requirements as detailed in the official admission handouts.

Placement Process

NMIMS maintains a strong corporate interface to facilitate student placements. The university provides official placement data through its annual NIRF reports. Please note that specific year-wise placement statistics and salary packages are not detailed in the provided source material; interested candidates are encouraged to refer to the official NIRF data portal on the NMIMS website for audited placement outcomes.
