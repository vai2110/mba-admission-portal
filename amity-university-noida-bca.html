BCA at Amity University Noida

BCA provides an undergraduate route into computer applications, programming, databases, software development and related digital roles. Amity's Noida programme portfolio includes computer applications and computer science pathways.

BCA Eligibility

Eligibility varies by the exact BCA programme. Students should verify the Class XII subject and minimum-percentage requirements in the 2026 programme prospectus before applying.

BCA Admission 2026

Applications are submitted online through Amity's admission process. Selection can be programme-specific, so applicants should follow the exact instructions attached to the chosen BCA programme.

BCA Fees

The current 2026 fee depends on the exact programme and category. Use the official prospectus rather than an old third-party fee estimate.

BCA Career Scope

Typical pathways include software support, web development, testing, data and application support, IT operations and further study through MCA or specialised technology programmes.

Student Discussion: BCA or BTech?

Students comparing BCA with BTech should consider the role they want, mathematical and technical depth, duration, total cost and whether they plan to pursue MCA or another postgraduate qualification.

Amity Noida BCA FAQs

Is BCA offered at Amity Noida?

Yes. Computer Applications is among the academic areas offered at the Noida campus.

Can BCA students pursue MCA?

Yes. BCA can provide a foundation for postgraduate computer-application study, subject to the future programme's eligibility requirements.

Is hostel available?

Yes. Amity Noida provides on-campus hostel accommodation for boys and girls.
