NMAT 2026 Overview

NMAT by GMAC is a computer-adaptive management entrance exam used by participating business schools in India and other countries. For the 2026 cycle, the exam delivery window in India is November 2 to December 20.

Students can choose an available test date and, where offered, the delivery mode. The exam also allows up to three attempts in a testing cycle, including the first attempt and up to two retakes.

Student tip:

NMAT registration and B-school applications are separate. A student can register for NMAT but still needs to apply separately to the MBA programmes they want to join.

NMAT 2026 Eligibility

GMAC allows test takers who complete registration, pay the fee and follow the published exam rules to sit for NMAT. However, admission eligibility is decided by each participating B-school.

Important:

Do not treat NMAT eligibility as admission eligibility. Check every target school's official programme page before applying.

NMAT 2026 Syllabus

Language Skills

Reading comprehension, para forming, error identification, prepositions, sentence completion, analogies and related verbal skills.

Quantitative Skills

Number properties, arithmetic, algebra, probability, data sufficiency and data interpretation through caselets, tables, graphs and charts.

Logical Reasoning

Critical reasoning, analytical puzzles, deductions and other logical reasoning areas.

Preparation focus:

Because the sections are individually timed, practise each section separately rather than relying only on full-length mocks.

How Many Attempts Are Allowed in NMAT?

Students can take NMAT up to three times in a testing cycle: the first attempt plus up to two retakes. A valid-score retake must be scheduled with the required gap between attempts under the current GMAC rules.

Smart use of retakes:

Do not treat all three attempts as casual practice. Build a plan around the first attempt, review your score and use a retake when it fits your target-school strategy.

NMAT Cutoff: What Students Should Know

There is no single cutoff that applies to every NMAT-accepting B-school. Each institute and programme can set its own score or percentile requirement, and the requirement can change by admission cycle.

What to checkWhy it matters
NMAT score requirementSome schools publish a minimum score or use it as a screening criterion.
ProgrammeThe same institute can have different requirements for different programmes.
Selection processShortlisting may also consider academics, work experience, interview or other components.
Application deadlineNMAT registration does not automatically submit your B-school application.

NMAT Admission Process

  1. Register and schedule NMAT.
  2. Take the exam and receive your score.
  3. Send scores to selected schools.
  4. Submit separate applications to target B-schools wherever required.
  5. Meet the school's shortlist and programme requirements.
  6. Complete the institute's selection process, which may include interviews or other stages.
  7. Receive the admission decision and complete enrolment formalities.
Remember:

NMAT is an entrance test, not a common counselling process. Each participating B-school controls its own admission rules and deadlines.

How to Prepare for NMAT 2026

1. Build section-wise speed

Language Skills has 28 minutes, Quantitative Skills 52 minutes and Logical Reasoning 40 minutes. Practise with the same section-level time limits.

2. Prioritise accuracy

There is no negative marking, but the adaptive format still requires disciplined question selection and time management.

3. Practise with official material

GMAC provides an Official Guide, study plan and practice exams to help students understand the actual test experience.

4. Plan your attempts

If you may use a retake, decide in advance what score improvement would justify another attempt and leave enough time between attempts.

NMAT 2026 FAQs: Frequently Asked Questions

When is NMAT 2026 conducted?

The 2026 exam delivery window is November 2 to December 20, 2026.

What is the last date to register for NMAT 2026?

Registration closes on October 10, 2026. Scheduling closes on October 22, 2026.

How many questions are in NMAT?

NMAT has 108 questions: 36 each in Language Skills, Quantitative Skills and Logical Reasoning.

Is there negative marking in NMAT?

No. The current official NMAT information states that there is no negative marking.

How many times can I take NMAT?

Up to three times in a testing cycle, including the first attempt and up to two retakes.

Can I choose the order of NMAT sections?

Yes. Candidates can choose the section order at the beginning of the exam.

When do I get my NMAT score?

An unofficial score is shown after the exam, while the scorecard is generally available within 48 hours, subject to test-delivery/security checks.

Does registering for NMAT apply me to NMIMS or other colleges?

No. NMAT registration and B-school applications are separate. You must follow each school's application process and deadline.
