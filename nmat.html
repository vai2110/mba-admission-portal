NMAT 2026 Overview

NMAT by GMAC is a management entrance examination designed for graduate business and management admissions.

The exam provides flexibility because candidates can choose their exam date and, where available, the delivery mode and retake options.

Important:

NMAT registration and applications to individual B-schools are separate processes. Candidates should check each institution's admission requirements.

NMAT Eligibility

  • Eligibility is determined by the individual business school.
  • Candidates should hold or be completing an eligible bachelor's degree.
  • Academic requirements can vary between participating institutes.
  • Some programmes may have additional requirements.
Important:

Clearing NMAT does not by itself guarantee admission. Always verify programme-specific requirements with the B-school.

NMAT Attempts

GMAC currently allows candidates to take the NMAT exam a maximum of three times in a testing cycle, including the first attempt and up to two retakes.

Retake strategy:

Use the first attempt as a serious attempt, analyse your performance, and use the retake opportunity only if it can materially improve your application.

NMAT Admission Process

  1. Register for NMAT.
  2. Take the examination.
  3. Send scores to selected B-schools.
  4. Complete separate institute applications where required.
  5. Meet shortlist criteria.
  6. Participate in the institute selection process.
  7. Complete admission formalities.

How to Prepare for NMAT

Practise under time pressure

NMAT requires consistent speed because each section has a defined time limit.

Use adaptive-test strategy

Focus on accuracy and avoid spending excessive time on one question.

Practise all three sections

Do not neglect Language Skills, Quantitative Skills or Logical Reasoning.

Take official practice tests

GMAC provides official practice resources that can help candidates understand the test experience.

NMAT 2026 FAQs

Who conducts NMAT?

NMAT is conducted by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC).

How many questions are in NMAT?

The current structure contains 108 questions.

Can I take NMAT more than once?

Yes. GMAC currently permits up to three attempts in a testing cycle.

Is there negative marking?

The current NMAT information states that there is no negative marking.

Can NMAT be taken online?

NMAT is available at test centres and, subject to availability, online at home.
