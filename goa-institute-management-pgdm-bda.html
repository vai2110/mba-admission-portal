GIM PGDM BDA: Is It a Good Analytics Management Programme?

GIM's PGDM in Big Data Analytics is a two-year full-time residential programme designed around statistics, data management, business intelligence, machine learning, artificial intelligence, deep learning and related tools. GIM describes the programme as hands-on and oriented toward solving actual industry problems.

Who should choose BDA?

BDA is a better fit for students who genuinely enjoy quantitative and analytical work and want management roles connected to data. It is not simply a general MBA with one analytics elective; the programme has a stronger technical and quantitative orientation.

BDA shortlisting considers profile, competitive exam score and GAAT. The regular selection framework gives 40% to objective assessment, 15% academics, 10% work experience, 30% face-to-face evaluation and 5% profile/diversity.

GIM GAAT: What Does the Analytics Aptitude Test Cover?

GAAT is an institute-level aptitude test required for PGDM-BDA applicants. GIM lists Linear Algebra, Calculus, Technology & Business Awareness and Basic Coding among the preparation areas. The test is therefore materially different from a conventional general-management aptitude test.

Prepare for

  • Linear Algebra
  • Calculus
  • Technology & Business Awareness
  • Basic Coding

Important

Do not prepare for BDA admission using only CAT/XAT-style verbal and logical reasoning. GAAT is an additional part of the BDA process and should be prepared separately.

GIM BDA Career Scope & Placements

GIM reports placement access across its full-time programmes. BDA is designed for students targeting analytics and technology-enabled business careers. The wider GIM placement ecosystem includes IT/ITES, financial services, consulting, banking and other sectors.

Career reality

The value of BDA depends heavily on your technical capability. Students who can combine analytics skills with business communication and domain understanding are better positioned for data-oriented roles than candidates who treat the programme as a conventional MBA.

GIM BDA FAQs

What is the GIM BDA fee?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh. The 2027-29 fee has not yet been released.

Is GAAT compulsory for BDA?

Yes. GIM states that BDA applicants need to appear for the GIM Analytics Aptitude Test.

When is GAAT 2027?

GIM currently lists 31 January 2027 for GAAT.

What does GAAT test?

GIM lists Linear Algebra, Calculus, Technology & Business Awareness and Basic Coding among the areas.

Does BDA require an engineering degree?

No. GIM's general eligibility is based on graduation and accepted examinations, while specific profile-based Achiever's Round criteria may favour certain backgrounds.
