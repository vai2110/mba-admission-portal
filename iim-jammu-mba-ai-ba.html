IIM Jammu MBA AI & Business Analytics Overview

The Dual Degree MBA in AI & Business Analytics is offered by IIM Jammu and the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand. The 2026-28 batch is expected to be around 60 students. Students complete the first year at IIM Jammu and, subject to meeting the prescribed requirements, the second year at AIT Thailand.

Who is this programme for?

It is designed for students who want management education combined with substantial AI, analytics, programming, data and technology exposure, rather than a conventional general MBA.

MBA AI & Business Analytics Curriculum

The first year includes 64.5 core course credits plus a 3-credit summer internship. The curriculum includes programming for analytics, business analytics, business intelligence and visualisation, AI/ML, natural language processing, data management, generative AI and agentic AI for business.

Second Year at AIT Thailand

Students who successfully complete the first-year requirements and meet the English-proficiency requirements can proceed to AIT Thailand for the second year. The AIT component comprises 33 credits across five terms and is equivalent to 48 course credits under the programme structure.

Important

This is a dual-degree structure, not simply an exchange semester. Students should budget separately for AIT fees, living expenses, travel, visa and related costs.

MBA AI & Business Analytics FAQs

What is the batch size?

The 2026-28 batch is likely to be around 60 students.

What is the General CAT cutoff?

The published PI-shortlisting threshold is 75 overall for General male candidates and 70 for General female/transgender candidates.

Where is the second year studied?

The second year is at AIT Thailand, subject to completing IIM Jammu's first-year requirements and fulfilling English-proficiency requirements.
