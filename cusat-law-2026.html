CUSAT Law Eligibility 2026: Qualification Requirements

The prospectus describes an aptitude test common to BBA LL.B (H), B.Com LL.B (H) and B.Sc LL.B (H) Computer Science. It comprises 150 objective questions, with 4 marks for a correct answer and 1 mark deducted for an incorrect answer.

Why this matters

The negative marking makes accuracy important. Candidates should prepare for speed but avoid random guessing.

How many years is CUSAT integrated law?

Does CUSAT have B.Sc LL.B?

What is the CUSAT law entrance test pattern?
