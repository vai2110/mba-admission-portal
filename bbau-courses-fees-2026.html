How to Compare BBAU Fees Properly

TuitionCompare semester tuition first; then check examination, hostel, mess, registration and other charges.
Paid seatsSome programmes have separate paid-seat fees. Never use the regular-seat figure for a paid-seat comparison.
Return on costFor professional courses, compare department-level placement outcomes rather than only the university's overall reputation.
Current noticeUse the 2026-27 prospectus or fee notice for the amount payable in your admission cycle.
