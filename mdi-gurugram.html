CAT cutoff: MDI is composite, not CAT-only

MDI does not publish one fixed official CAT percentile cutoff. For 2026–28, CAT 2025 carries 70% weight in Stage-I shortlisting, alongside academics, diversity and work experience. External analyses put the recent General shortlisting band around 95–97, with 96 reported for CAT 2025; treat this as an indicative competitive benchmark, not an official cutoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MDI Gurgaon fee for PGDM 2026–28?

The 2026–28 fee for PGDM, PGDM-HRM and PGDM-Business Analytics is ₹26,55,310. This includes ₹19,23,010 tuition, boarding ₹2,08,400, lodging ₹2,19,500, learning material ₹1,24,000, activities ₹62,500, library ₹75,400, alumni fee ₹15,000 and refundable security deposit ₹27,500.

What CAT percentile is required for MDI Gurgaon?

MDI does not publish one fixed CAT cutoff. For 2026–28, CAT 2025 carries 70% weight in Stage-I shortlisting along with academics, diversity and work experience. An online '96 percentile cutoff' is therefore best treated as an indicative competitive benchmark, not a guarantee.

Is MDI Gurgaon worth ₹26.55 lakh?

MDI becomes particularly attractive for students who value Delhi-NCR corporate access, a mature recruiter network and specialisations such as HR, analytics and international business. The fee is substantial, so compare the actual median/average outcome of your programme with your target role.

What is the average package at MDI Gurgaon?

The placement cycle had 555 students placed, 147 recruiters and ₹53.60 lakh highest CTC, but the highest CTC is not a typical outcome.

MDI Gurgaon vs IIMs: which should I choose?

MDI can be a sensible choice when Delhi-NCR location, corporate access or a specialised programme matters more to you than the IIM label. If you have an IIM convert in a comparable fee range, compare programme-specific median salary, recruiter mix and career function.

Is MDI PGDM-IB worth the higher fee?

The 2026–28 PGDM-IB combined two-year fee is ₹32.54 lakh, including ₹26 lakh tuition and the listed boarding, lodging, learning material, activities, library, alumni and security components. It is worth considering if international business is a real career goal; otherwise the flagship PGDM may offer a simpler ROI comparison.

Is MDI Gurgaon good for freshers?

Yes, especially for students targeting consulting, BFSI, FMCG, technology, analytics or HR. The important point is to choose the programme according to your intended function rather than assuming every MDI programme has identical outcomes.
