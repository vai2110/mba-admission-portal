IIM Kashipur Overview

Quick answer

IIM Kashipur offers a two-year residential MBA through CAT-based admission, a separate two-year MBA (Analytics), and executive programmes for working professionals. Its latest audited final placement report covers the 2023-25 MBA and MBA (Analytics) batch.

IIM Kashipur is located at Kundeshwari in Kashipur, Uttarakhand. Its academic portfolio combines the flagship MBA with analytics-focused management education and executive programmes. The institute also maintains dedicated placement reports, academic calendars and programme-specific admission policies.

What should an MBA applicant compare?

The useful comparison points are not just CAT percentile. Applicants should evaluate the published selection route, total fee, residential structure, programme curriculum, summer internship exposure and audited placement outcomes.

IIM Kashipur MBA Admission 2026
