Quick Answer

IIM Kashipur offers full-time residential MBA and MBA (Analytics) programmes, alongside Executive MBA and Executive MBA (Analytics) options. Admissions are managed by the institute's academic office. Official fee and selection details are available on the respective programme pages at the official website.

Institute Overview

IIM Kashipur is a premier management institute providing a range of academic programmes including MBA, MBA (Analytics), and Executive MBA. The institute emphasizes a blend of theoretical management frameworks and practical, data-driven analytical skills.

Academic Programmes

MBA (Analytics)

A two-year, six-term residential programme. The curriculum includes core management and analytics courses, electives, a summer internship, and a dissertation component in the second year.

Executive MBA (Analytics)

A two-year, six-term blended learning programme designed for working professionals. It requires a bachelor's degree (min 45% marks) and 3 years of full-time work experience.

Admissions & Eligibility

For the Executive MBA (Analytics), candidates must hold a full-time bachelor's degree with at least 45% marks (42% for NC-OBC, 40% for SC/ST/DAP) and possess a minimum of 3 years of full-time post-bachelor work experience. Internships and apprenticeships are not counted toward the experience requirement.
