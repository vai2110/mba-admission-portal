About MDI Gurgaon PGDM

MDI’s PGDM is the general-management programme. Students should use the programme brochure and current curriculum information to verify the term-wise core and elective choices before selecting it.

Programme fit

General management. Compare the curriculum, eligibility, cost and programme-specific placement data before selecting it in the MDI application form.

PGDM Eligibility & Admission 2027–29

At least 50% in Class 10 and 12 and at least 50% in a recognised bachelor’s degree of minimum three years. CAT 2026 is required for Indian applicants.

Cutoff note

MDI does not publish a fixed numeric CAT cutoff in the current admission notice. The 96 percentile shown in the 2024–26 placement report is the batch’s average CAT percentile, not an official cutoff or guaranteed call threshold. MDI’s admissions FAQ says aggregate and sectional CAT cut-offs are considered.

PGDM Fees: What Is Included and What Is Extra?

Published fee reference: ₹26,55,310 (published 2026–28 fee). MDI may revise fees; confirm the 2027–29 schedule before payment.

The published 2026–28 fee includes tuition ₹19,23,010; boarding ₹2,08,400; lodging ₹2,19,500; learning material ₹1,24,000; student activities ₹62,500; library ₹75,400; alumni fee ₹15,000; and refundable security deposit ₹27,500.

PGDM: Student FAQs

What is the MDI PGDM fee?

MDI’s published 2026–28 fee for PGDM is ₹26,55,310. It includes the listed tuition, boarding, lodging, learning material, student activities, library, alumni fee and refundable security deposit. Check the 2027–29 fee notice before payment.

What is the MDI PGDM average package?

The 2024–26 official placement report records ₹29.50 LPA average CTC and ₹29.00 LPA median CTC for PGDM. These are batch-specific outcomes, not a salary guarantee.

What is the MDI PGDM admission route?

For Indian applicants in the 2027–29 cycle, MDI requires CAT 2026. Shortlisting and final ranking use the institute’s published academic, diversity, work-experience and assessment weightages.
