MCA at SRMIST

MCA is a postgraduate computer applications programme for graduates targeting software, applications and technology roles. SRMIST offers MCA within its computing/academic portfolio.

Eligibility

Eligibility is programme- and campus-specific. Check the current SRMIST MCA admission notice for the required bachelor's qualification and marks.

Admission

Admission route depends on the current SRMIST programme notice and campus. Candidates should use the university's official Directorate of Admissions information.

Fees

Regular MCA fees vary by programme/campus. SRMIST also separately lists distance education MCA at ₹75,000 total; this should not be confused with regular campus MCA.

Placements

Official university-level 2023 placement figures reported ₹10.5 LPA average salary and ₹1 crore per annum maximum salary, with 1,311 companies and 13,069 offers.

FAQs

Is distance MCA the same as regular MCA?

No. SRMIST publishes separate distance and campus programme information.

Is ₹75,000 the regular MCA fee?

No. ₹75,000 is the official distance education MCA total listed separately.

Are placement figures MCA-specific?

No. The cited figures are university-level.
