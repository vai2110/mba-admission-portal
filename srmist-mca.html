MCA at SRMIST

MCA is a postgraduate computer applications route for graduates interested in software and computing careers. This page focuses on MCA rather than repeating SRMIST's full course catalogue.

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility is programme-specific. Check the current SRMIST MCA entry for the required bachelor's qualification, subject conditions and aggregate.

How Is MCA Admission Done?

SRMIST publishes programme-specific admission routes. Do not automatically apply the B.Tech SRMJEEE or MBA SRMJEEM route to MCA.

MCA Fees

Regular MCA fees are campus/programme specific. SRMIST separately lists distance education MCA at ₹75,000 total; that figure is not the regular campus MCA fee.

What Do You Study in MCA?

The programme is centred on computer applications, programming, databases, software systems and related computing areas. Use the current curriculum for exact semester-wise subjects.

MCA Cutoff

A single official university-wide MCA closing cutoff is not presented here because admission can be programme and campus specific.

Placements

Use the dedicated SRMIST placements page for university-level placement data. Those figures are not an MCA-specific salary guarantee.

SRMIST MCA FAQs

Is ₹75,000 the regular MCA fee?

No. It is the separately listed total fee for distance education MCA.

Is MCA admission through SRMJEEE?

Not automatically. MCA has its own programme-specific admission information and should be checked separately.

Does SRMIST have MCA on every campus?

Programme availability is campus-specific; verify the current listing before selecting a campus.
