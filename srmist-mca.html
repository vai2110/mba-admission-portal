What Is MCA at SRMIST?

MCA is a postgraduate computer-applications route for graduates who want to move into software, IT and computing careers. It is a different pathway from B.Tech because it starts after graduation and has a shorter 2-year duration in the current SRMIST listing.

Who Can Apply?

SRMIST's published MCA material includes BCA, B.Sc. Computer Science/IT/Computer Technology and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent. Certain B.Sc./B.Com./B.A. backgrounds are also accepted where the stated Mathematics or related-subject condition is met.

How Is MCA Admission Done?

The current Science & Humanities admission portal lists MCA through the SRMIST admission/e-counselling route. Students should select the exact current MCA programme entry and complete its eligibility and document requirements.

What Is the MCA Fee?

The official Science & Humanities portal lists MCA as a 2-year programme and shows more than one fee configuration across its MCA entries. One current listing shows ₹1,75,000. Other official portal entries display ₹1,25,000, ₹1,00,000, ₹90,000 and ₹1,10,000.

Official portal listingPublished fee
MCA – Computer Applications₹1,75,000 in one listing
Other MCA entries₹1,25,000 / ₹1,00,000 / ₹90,000 / ₹1,10,000

Because the official portal contains multiple MCA entries, the exact current programme/campus listing must be matched before payment. The ₹75,000 distance-education figure is a separate programme and should not be used for regular MCA.

What Is the MCA Cutoff?

MCA does not use the B.Tech SRMJEEE cutoff framework. A single university-wide MCA closing cutoff is not presented here because the programme-specific admission route applies.

What Do You Study in MCA?

SRMIST describes MCA as a 2-year programme with theoretical and application-oriented study relevant to software development, computing, communications/networking and commercial problem-solving.

Who Should Choose MCA?

MCA is useful for graduates who want a postgraduate computing qualification rather than another undergraduate engineering degree. It is particularly relevant when your bachelor's background satisfies the published computer/Mathematics conditions.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's university-level placement figures cover multiple programmes, so they should not be treated as an MCA-specific package. The official 2023 university-level communication reported average salary ₹10.5 LPA and maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum.

FAQs Students Ask

What is the MCA duration?

The current SRM Science & Humanities portal lists MCA as a 2-year programme.

Who can apply after B.Com or B.A.?

Certain B.Com/B.A. backgrounds can apply where the published Mathematics, Business Mathematics, Statistics or related condition is satisfied.

Is ₹75,000 the regular MCA fee?

No. ₹75,000 is associated with the separate distance-education MCA listing. Regular MCA listings show different fee configurations.

Why do official MCA fee listings show different amounts?

The SRMIST portal displays multiple MCA programme/campus listings with different published fee figures. Match the exact current entry before payment.

Is MCA admission through SRMJEEE?

No. MCA follows its own SRMIST programme-specific admission/e-counselling route.
