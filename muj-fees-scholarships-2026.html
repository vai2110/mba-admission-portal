UG Scholarships: Merit and Special Categories

MUJ's scholarship framework includes TMA Pai Engineering Scholarships, TMA Pai Merit Scholarships, Rajasthan Merit Scholarship and additional categories such as sibling, differently abled, defence/martyr wards, single mother/orphan and sports-related support.

For the TMA Pai Engineering Scholarship, MUJ publishes two scholarships per B.Tech programme and criteria involving a top-8000 MET rank and parents' combined annual income below ₹12 lakh. The TMA Pai Merit Scholarship includes 100%, 50% and 25% tuition-waiver categories for top performers under the published rules, with continuation requiring CGPA of at least 8.5 and no backlog.

MUJ Fees & Scholarship FAQs

What is the total MUJ hostel fee for 2026–27?

The published double-occupancy total including refundable security is ₹2,08,225.

Does MUJ provide 100% MBA scholarships?

The current MBA scholarship table publishes tuition waivers of 20%, 30%, 40% and 50% based on the stated entrance percentile or UG CGPA criteria. Other scholarship schemes may have separate rules.

Is MUJ caution money refundable?

MUJ's central fee policy says the caution deposit is refundable on completion of the programme. Registration fee is non-refundable.

Are hostel and tuition paid together?

No. Hostel is separately managed and MUJ's hostel fee page provides a separate booking/payment process.
