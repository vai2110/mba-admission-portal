MUJ Fees & Scholarship FAQs

What is the total MUJ hostel fee for 2026–27?

For double occupancy, MUJ publishes ₹1,67,372 accommodation + ₹15,378 housekeeping + ₹25,475 refundable security = ₹2,08,225.

What MBA scholarship waivers are published?

The current MBA scholarship table publishes 20%, 30%, 40% and 50% tuition waivers based on the specified entrance percentile or UG CGPA criteria.

Is MUJ caution money refundable?

MUJ's central fee policy says the caution deposit is refundable on completion of the programme. Registration is non-refundable.

What is a realistic first-year residential budget?

Use the programme admission amount plus the ₹2,08,225 hostel total as a starting point. For example, B.Tech CSE works out to about ₹7.20 lakh before personal living expenses and future fee changes.
