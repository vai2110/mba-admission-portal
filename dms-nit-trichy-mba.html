What is the eligibility for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

For the 2026-28 MBA intake, GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL candidates needed a full-time regular bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 60% marks or 6.5 CGPA. SC/ST/PwD candidates needed 55% marks or 6.0 CGPA. Candidates also needed a CAT 2025 percentile. Final-year students could apply if they fulfilled the stated requirements on completion of the degree.

How do you get admission to DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

DoMS uses CAT for MBA shortlisting and does not accept MAT, XAT or other management entrance scores for this programme.

What CAT percentile is required for DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS NIT Trichy does not state a fixed minimum CAT percentile in the 2026-28 admission brochure. It says candidates are shortlisted using CAT overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievement.

This means there is no official number you can treat as a guaranteed DoMS NIT Trichy CAT cutoff. A higher CAT percentile strengthens your shortlisting position, but final admission also depends on academics, work experience and the personal interview.

For 2026-28, CAT 2025 was the required exam. If you are targeting the next cycle, use the CAT percentile from your scorecard and compare your profile against the full selection criteria rather than relying on an unofficial single cutoff number.

What is considered after CAT?

The 2026 brochure says shortlisting considers CAT overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievement. The final selection adds professional work experience and personal interview performance.

What is the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA fee?

NIT Trichy publishes a separate institute fee structure for students admitted in 2026. The official department FAQ also records an approximate annual figure of ₹1,08,700 institute fee + ₹50,000 hostel and mess = ₹1,58,700 per year, but that FAQ is an approximate reference and should not replace the latest fee notice.

What specialisations can you choose?

DoMS lists Human Resource Management, Marketing, Finance, Production and Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management. The programme combines core courses with electives and functional-area learning.

What is the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA curriculum like?

The programme runs for two years across six trimesters, with each trimester lasting about 10 weeks. The department's 2026 brochure highlights classroom teaching, case discussions, group work, presentations, simulations, guest lectures, industrial visits and mini projects.

Students build foundations in leadership, communication, strategy, finance, marketing, operations, HR, analytics, IT consulting and entrepreneurship before moving deeper into functional and elective areas.

Does DoMS NIT Trichy MBA include a summer internship?

Yes. The 2026 department brochure specifies a two-month summer internship at the end of the first year. The internship is intended to connect classroom concepts with corporate work and practical problem-solving.

How are placements at DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS states that it has a 100% placement record in recent years. The department also publishes a dedicated MBA placement brochure for 2025-26.

See the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA placements page for the placement-specific figures and recruiter information available from the department.

DoMS NIT Trichy MBA FAQs

Does DoMS accept XAT or MAT?

No. The official DoMS FAQ says CAT is the entrance score accepted for MBA shortlisting.

How many seats are available?

The MBA Admission 2026 FAQ states 115 seats, inclusive of reservations proposed by the Ministry of Education.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes, provided they have CAT 2025 and meet the stated undergraduate eligibility requirements on completion of their degree.

Is the MBA residential?

Hostel accommodation is not compulsory according to the department FAQ, although hostel stay is recommended.

When does the MBA academic year normally start?

The department FAQ says the academic session normally commences around the second week of July.
