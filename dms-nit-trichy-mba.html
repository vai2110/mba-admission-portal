Who is eligible for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

For the 2026-28 MBA batch, eligibility depends on category. The degree must be a full-time, regular undergraduate degree in any discipline. A previous-year CAT score cannot be used.

Applicant categoryMinimum UG requirementCAT requirement
GEN / GEN-EWS / OBC-NCL60% marks or 6.5 CGPACAT 2025 percentile
SC / ST / PwD55% marks or 6.0 CGPACAT 2025 percentile
Final-year UG studentCan apply if the stated degree requirement is met on completionCAT 2025 percentile required
  • No age limit is specified in the DoMS FAQ.
  • CGPA cannot be converted into percentage for this admission; if both are shown on the marksheet, the CGPA is considered.
  • Applicants must satisfy the eligibility conditions when admission is verified. A discrepancy can lead to rejection or cancellation.

How does the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA admission process work?

The 2026 process is straightforward: CAT → NIT Trichy application → shortlist → Personal Interview → final selection. DoMS conducts its own MBA admission process after CAT.

StageWhat you need to doWhat DoMS checks
1. CATAppear for CAT 2025CAT overall percentile
2. MBA applicationApply online through the notified NIT Trichy portal and pay the application feeEligibility and application details
3. ShortlistingWait for the shortlist published by NIT TrichyCAT overall percentile + UG academic achievement
4. Personal InterviewAttend the offline PI at the allotted/selected centreInterview performance
5. Final selectionComplete admission formalities if selectedCAT overall percentile + UG academics + work experience + PI

Entrance exams accepted: CAT is the MBA entrance route. The official DoMS FAQ does not list MAT, XAT, ATMA or other management exams as alternatives.

What CAT percentile is required for DoMS NIT Trichy?

There is no fixed CAT percentile published as the 2026-28 admission cutoff. The official brochure says candidates are shortlisted using their CAT 2025 overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievement. It does not give a numeric minimum CAT percentile for shortlisting.

What does this mean for you?

Do not read a number such as 80, 85 or 90 percentile as an official DoMS cutoff. Your CAT percentile is one part of the shortlisting decision, and UG academics are also considered.

What do recent applicants report?

Student discussions can be used as a competitiveness reference, but they are not official cutoffs. Recent Reddit threads include applicants reporting 84.51, 85.83 and 73.63 percentile while discussing DoMS NIT Trichy. These are individual profiles, not admitted-student cutoff data.

Bottom line: a higher CAT percentile is helpful, but there is no official “safe cutoff” published by DoMS. Shortlisting and final selection depend on the factors stated by the institute.

What factors decide the final DoMS NIT Trichy MBA selection?

The brochure separates the process into shortlisting and final selection. This distinction matters if you are trying to understand why CAT alone does not decide admission.

Shortlisting

  • CAT 2025 overall percentile
  • Undergraduate academic achievement

Final selection

  • CAT 2025 overall percentile
  • Undergraduate academic achievement
  • Professional work experience
  • Personal interview performance

The brochure does not publish a separate weightage percentage for each of these factors. Therefore, this page does not assign artificial weights.

What about DASA fees?

For DASA (SAARC), the fee notice lists $2,000 tuition per semester plus the rupee-denominated institute charges. The four-semester tuition is therefore $8,000, with ₹1,51,700 of the listed rupee charges across the four semesters. For DASA non-SAARC, the tuition is $4,000 per semester, or $16,000 for four semesters, plus the listed rupee charges.

Important: The ₹2,91,700 and ₹1,51,700 totals above are calculated by adding the four semester totals printed in the 2026 fee notice. They are not estimates.

Which documents are required for the MBA application?

The 2026 brochure gives a specific upload checklist. Keeping these files ready before the application can prevent last-minute issues.

Academic & identity documents

  • Aadhaar card
  • CAT 2025 score card
  • 10th and 12th marksheets
  • UG marksheets / consolidated grade sheets
  • Degree or provisional certificate, if available
  • Transfer certificate, if available

Category & profile documents

  • GEN-EWS / OBC-NCL / SC / ST certificate, where applicable
  • Professional work-experience certificate, if applicable
  • PwD certificate, if applicable
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Signature

Documents are uploaded in PDF format where specified. Final-year candidates waiting for results must upload the latest semester marksheet together with previous semester marksheets as one PDF.

GEN-EWS and OBC-NCL certificates for the application cycle had to fall within the dates specified in the brochure. Provisionally selected GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL candidates were also required to submit the latest certificate during physical reporting.

Does DoMS NIT Trichy MBA have a summer internship?

Yes. The 2026 department brochure specifies a two-month summer internship after the first year. This is an important part of the two-year MBA because students get a chance to apply classroom learning in a professional setting before returning for the second year.

What are the placement prospects after DoMS MBA?

DoMS states that it has maintained a 100% placement record in recent years. For the detailed placement discussion, salary data and recruiter information available for the department, visit the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA placements page.

DoMS NIT Trichy MBA FAQs students actually ask

Can I apply with CAT 2024 or an older CAT score?

No. The 2026 brochure specifically says candidates with a CAT percentile from before 2025 were not eligible for that admission cycle. CAT 2025 was required.

Does DoMS accept XAT, MAT or CMAT?

No. The DoMS FAQ identifies CAT as the entrance score for MBA shortlisting. The 2026 admission brochure also requires CAT 2025.

Is there an official CAT cutoff such as 80 or 90 percentile?

No fixed numeric CAT cutoff is stated in the 2026-28 brochure. Shortlisting uses CAT overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievement.

Does a high CAT percentile guarantee admission?

No. Final selection also considers undergraduate academic achievement, professional work experience and personal interview performance.

How much should I budget for the 2026-28 MBA?

For the standard Open/Open-EWS/OBC-NCL and listed equivalent categories, the four semester totals in the 2026 fee notice add up to ₹2,91,700. The SC/ST total printed across the four semesters adds up to ₹1,51,700. Hostel and mess charges are separate from this institute fee notice.

What is the application fee?

₹1,550 for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL and ₹1,050 for SC/ST/PwD candidates for the 2026 admission cycle. The application fee is non-refundable.

Where were interviews conducted for the 2026 admission cycle?

The brochure proposed five locations: Noida/Delhi NCR, Nagpur, Kolkata, Chennai and Tiruchirappalli.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes. Final-year UG students with CAT 2025 could apply, provided they met the required degree percentage/CGPA by the time of admission.

Do I have to submit a hard copy of the application?

No. The 2026 brochure says a hard copy of the application is not required.

Is hostel compulsory?

No. The DoMS FAQ states hostel accommodation is not compulsory, although it is recommended.
