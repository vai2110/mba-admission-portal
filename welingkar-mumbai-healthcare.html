PGDM Healthcare Management: Who Is It For?

WeSchool's Healthcare Management programme is designed for management careers in healthcare businesses. The official brochure says suitable backgrounds include MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BPharm, allied health, life sciences, engineering and other graduates with interest in healthcare.

Do I need a medical degree?

No. The official brochure specifically states that students from all educational backgrounds can join and do well if they are interested in healthcare; a minimum 50% bachelor's degree is required.

Who Should Choose Healthcare Management?

Good fit

  • You want healthcare business rather than clinical practice.
  • You have a healthcare/life-science background or strong sector interest.
  • You want hospital, pharma or health-tech management roles.

Think twice if

  • You want a generic MBA with maximum cross-sector flexibility.
  • You are not interested in healthcare as an industry.
  • You have a stronger specialised healthcare management call.
