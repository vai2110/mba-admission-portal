What is the DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

DoMS NIT Trichy's flagship MBA is a two-year full-time programme organised across six trimesters. The 2026 admission brochure identifies the intake as 115 seats, including reservations.

The programme combines core management subjects, electives and a summer internship. The department lists Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Production and Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management among its academic areas.

How Do I Apply for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

DoMS uses CAT for MBA admission. For the 2026 intake, candidates applied through NIT Trichy's online admission portal and submitted the required information and CAT score.

What CAT Percentile Is Required for DoMS NIT Trichy?

There is no fixed final CAT cutoff published by DoMS on its MBA admission page. The department says applicants are shortlisted using CAT overall percentile, while the final selection also considers academics, professional work experience and the personal interview.

That means there are two different questions: meeting eligibility and being competitive for a shortlist. The official 2026 brochure required a CAT 2025 score, but it did not publish a category-wise final closing percentile.

If your CAT percentile is higher, your shortlisting position is generally stronger, but DoMS does not state that a particular percentile guarantees admission.

How Is the Final MBA Selection Done?

According to the official admission information, final selection uses four broad parameters: CAT overall percentile, personal interview, academic performance and professional work experience.

The 2026 brochure also states that shortlisting considered CAT overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievements. The interview is therefore an important part of the final decision rather than a formality after CAT.

How Is the DoMS MBA Structured?

The MBA follows a trimester system with six trimesters. The academic structure includes core courses, electives and a summer internship. The trimester format means students complete academic terms more frequently than in a conventional two-semester annual structure.

The programme is designed to cover general management before allowing students to build depth through electives and the department's listed specialization areas.

Does DoMS NIT Trichy Accept XAT or MAT?

No. The department FAQ says DoMS accepts CAT scores only for MBA admission.

Can a final-year student apply?

Yes. The 2026 admission brochure allowed final-year undergraduate students with CAT 2025 to apply, subject to the required graduation criteria.

How many MBA seats are available?

The 2026 admission FAQ states 115 seats, inclusive of reservations.

Is work experience compulsory?

The published final selection parameters include professional work experience, but the 2026 eligibility criteria do not state that prior work experience is compulsory for applying.

Is the MBA residential?

DoMS describes the MBA as a two-year programme and its FAQ recommends hostel accommodation, but hostel stay is not compulsory.
