Nirma BBA (Hons.) Overview

The four-year BBA (Hons.) is offered by Nirma University's Institute of Management. The programme is designed around business education, leadership, communication, analytical decision-making and industry exposure.

Is IPMAT compulsory?

For the 2026 All-India admission route, the published eligibility requires candidates to have passed SSC and HSC/10+2 with 60% each and to have appeared in IPMAT for the corresponding academic year. Category-specific rules can differ.

BBA (Hons.) Eligibility

For the published 2026-27 general route, candidates need 60% in both SSC and HSC/10+2 or equivalent and must appear in IPMAT. The published age condition says the candidate should not be more than 20 years as on July 1 of the admission year.

Nirma BBA Selection Criteria

ComponentWeight
IPMAT score50%
Personal Interview30%
Class XII score20%

This formula means a high Class 12 score alone does not guarantee selection. IPMAT performance and interview performance are major parts of the final merit.

BBA (Hons.) Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

For 2026-27, Nirma lists its online application from February 4, IPMAT application opening February 2, IPMAT on May 4, and the extended All-India Nirma application deadline of May 31, 2026. Interview dates were to be announced.

2027: Use the 2026 pattern only for planning. Exact 2027 dates should be taken from Nirma's fresh admission notification.

BBA Career, Internships & Higher Studies

The programme can lead to roles in business analysis, marketing, finance, sales, operations, consulting and entrepreneurship, depending on electives and skills. The institute states that many students receive internships and some secure pre-placement offers based on internship performance.

Who should choose it?

It suits students who want a structured management foundation after Class 12 and are comfortable with an entrance-test-plus-interview selection model.

Nirma BBA (Hons.) FAQs

What is the Nirma BBA selection weightage?

IPMAT 50%, Personal Interview 30% and Class XII score 20% for the published 2026-27 route.

What percentage is required for Nirma BBA?

The published eligibility requires 60% in both SSC and HSC/10+2 for the relevant route.

What is Nirma BBA fee?

For 2026-27, tuition is ₹3.89 lakh per year plus ₹7,000 annual examination fee.

Is Nirma BBA four years?

Yes, the BBA (Hons.) programme is listed as four years.
