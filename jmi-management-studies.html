Department of Management Studies at Jamia Millia Islamia

The Department of Management Studies (DMS) at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, traces its origins to the Centre for Management Studies established in 2003. It is now part of the Faculty of Management Studies (FMS), established in 2022. The department offers postgraduate management programmes and doctoral study, with a focus on business fundamentals, managerial decision-making, innovation and industry relevance.

For applicants: DMS is the department to explore if you are looking for JMI MBA admission, MBA (Full Time), MBA International Business or MBA Innovation & Entrepreneurship. These programmes do not necessarily share identical fees or admission rules; check each programme's current notice.

JMI MBA Admission: Eligibility and Selection

Admission requirements are programme-specific. For the MBA (Full Time), JMI's published admission schedule for the relevant session should be used to confirm the accepted degree, minimum marks, entrance-test pattern, selection stages and application dates. Do not assume CAT is the admission route unless the current official notice explicitly says so.

What should applicants check before applying?

  • Whether the programme is regular, self-financed or part-time.
  • Minimum graduation marks and accepted equivalent grades.
  • Whether admission is through a JMI entrance test, GD/PI or another notified route.
  • Application deadline, admit-card schedule and test centre instructions.
  • Reservation rules, document verification and fee-payment deadlines.

JMI MBA Syllabus and Specialisations

The MBA curriculum builds a foundation in management concepts, quantitative methods, economics, accounting, organisational behaviour, human resource management, marketing, finance, information systems, analytics, operations and business research. Later semesters include strategic management, corporate analysis, business law, summer training and a dissertation/project, as set out in the department's published programme material.

Major functional areas

  • Finance: financial management, accounting, investment and risk-related electives.
  • Marketing: marketing management, digital marketing, brand management and customer relationship management.
  • Human Resources: organisational behaviour, strategic HRM, labour relations and HR analytics.
  • Operations: operations management, quality, procurement and inventory-related subjects.

Elective availability can vary by programme and academic year. Refer to the latest syllabus document for the definitive semester-wise paper list.
