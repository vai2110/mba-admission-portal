Methodology & data note

CollegeDecoded does not assign a single ROI score. We use programme-fee information and reported placement outcomes as comparison points. Fee structures can vary by category, hostel, mess and other charges, while placement figures may refer to different graduating batches.

  • FMS currently states approximately ₹60,818 per semester for its MBA programme.
  • IIT Bombay SJMSOM lists ₹15.55 lakh for the 2026–28 general-category programme and reports ₹25.82 lakh average CTC for 2024–25.
  • JBIMS and PUMBA are shown with specific published fee-year conditions rather than an assumed current fee.
