What is JIMS Rohini PGDM?

Quick answer

JIMS Rohini's PGDM is a two-year full-time management programme. JIMS states that its PGDM is equivalent to an MBA by AIU and is designed around academic learning, industry interaction, live projects, internships and corporate readiness.

The 2026 programme highlights future-ready learning in AI, digital skills, critical thinking, leadership and decision science. Students can build careers across Finance, Marketing, HR and other business functions.

JIMS PGDM curriculum: what will you study?

The programme combines core management subjects with specialised learning, experiential modules, internships, industry sessions and self-development activities. The practical advantage is that students can build both functional business knowledge and employability skills.

FinanceUseful for banking, financial analysis, valuations, risk and corporate finance roles.
MarketingUseful for sales, brand, digital marketing, consumer insights and product roles.
HRUseful for talent acquisition, HR analytics, HRBP, L&D and transformation roles.
Analytics & digitalUseful for data-driven business, decision science and digitally enabled management roles.

JIMS PGDM Scholarships 2026-28

JIMS publishes a merit-cum-means scholarship scheme. The highest listed CAT/XAT award is ₹3 lakh for above 90 percentile. Other categories include ₹2 lakh for 85–89.9 percentile, ₹1 lakh for 75–84.9 percentile, ₹50,000 for qualifying MAT/CMAT scores and ₹1 lakh for above 80% in Class 10, 12 and graduation. An EWS/means category can provide 20% of tuition fee subject to conditions. citeturn3search6

Students generally need to maintain specified academic performance and attendance to receive/disburse the scholarship instalments.

JIMS PGDM ROI: is the fee justified?

Why ROI can work

The current fee is ₹10.75 lakh before the separate alumni/security charges, while the reported 2024-26 median salary is ₹9.83 LPA. Delhi/NCR location can also reduce relocation friction for students already based in the region.

What to compare

Compare median—not just highest—salary, placement coverage, living costs, specialisation fit and your expected role before deciding.
