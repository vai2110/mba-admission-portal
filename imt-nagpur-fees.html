How much does IMT Nagpur cost?

Quick answer:

The official 2026-28 fee information states a total of ₹16 lakh for the two-year PGDM, including hostel, mess, books and admission fee under the published residential structure.

The programme fee applies to PGDM Core, PGDM Marketing and PGDM Financial Management. The published academic fee is ₹15.27 lakh, with other listed components making up the ₹16 lakh total.

The merit scholarship provides a 40% waiver on academic fees; the female scholarship provides a 20% waiver on academic fees. TFW waives the academic fee but includes a stated ₹12,000/month academic amenities charge. A candidate eligible for more than one scheme must opt for one.

What should a student budget beyond the ₹16 lakh?

The institute's published FAQ says the ₹16 lakh total covers hostel, mess, books and admission fee. Therefore, unlike many residential B-schools, students do not need to add a separate standard hostel-and-mess estimate to the headline fee.

Still, keep a personal budget for travel, laptop/device needs, discretionary spending, internships or exchange-related expenses and other individual costs not stated in the fee schedule.

IMT Nagpur Fees FAQs

What is the total IMT Nagpur PGDM fee?

The official 2026-28 total is ₹16 lakh, including hostel, mess, books and admission fee under the published residential structure.

Is hostel included in IMT Nagpur fees?

Yes. The official FAQ states that the ₹16 lakh total includes hostel and mess. The published residential structure is based on double occupancy.

How much is the academic fee?

The 2026-28 fee schedule lists ₹15.27 lakh as the academic fee over two years.

Does IMT Nagpur offer scholarships?

Yes. The institute lists a 40% academic-fee merit scholarship for qualifying high scorers, a 20% academic-fee scholarship for female students and a Tuition Fee Waiver scheme subject to eligibility and limits.

What is the fee after the 90 percentile scholarship?

The official page lists ₹9,89,200 as the total payable for the stated merit scholarship, including hostel, mess, books and admission fee.
