BMU Hostel Overview

BMU provides residential accommodation on campus. The published fee structure lists tower accommodation for many UG programmes and apartment accommodation for MBA students, with food included in the stated annual academic-period charges.

Should you stay on campus?

For a residential private university in Gurugram, campus living can reduce daily commuting and make it easier to participate in academic and student activities. The additional cost should be included when comparing total programme expenses.

Campus Life

BMU's residential model is designed around academic and extracurricular engagement. Students can use the campus environment for peer interaction, clubs, events, projects and career activities without a daily commute.

What to Check Before Booking

Ask the university

Confirm room allocation, inclusions, food schedule, maintenance process, internet facilities and refund rules.

Budget realistically

Include hostel, food, personal expenses, travel and annual fee revisions when calculating total cost.

Hostel FAQs

Does BMU hostel fee include food?

The published hostel tables describe the listed annual academic-period charges as accommodation with food.

What is the MBA hostel fee?

Published MBA accommodation charges are ₹2.45 lakh single, ₹2.40 lakh double and ₹2.30 lakh triple per year with food.

What is the UG hostel fee?

Published tower accommodation is ₹2.36 lakh for double occupancy and ₹2.25 lakh for triple occupancy per year with food.
