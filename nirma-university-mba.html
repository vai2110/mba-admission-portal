Nirma University MBA Overview

The Institute of Management, Nirma University offers MBA programmes including MBA and MBA (HRM), with separate programmes such as Family Business & Entrepreneurship. The regular MBA intake listed for 2026 is 300 including the stated seat categories.

What is the selection process?

General-route candidates are shortlisted using CAT and then evaluated through Theme Development & Presentation (TDP) and Personal Interview, along with academics/profile.

Nirma MBA Eligibility

A three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate or equivalent CGPA from a recognised university is required. Final-year candidates may apply subject to the university's conditions.

Nirma MBA Selection Criteria & CAT Cutoff

The official admissions material describes CAT-based shortlisting followed by TDP and PI. The current admission landing page presents the final composite as CAT 60%, PI 20%, TDP/Extempore 10% and academics 10%, with an additional provision for relevant work experience. The exact year's prospectus should be treated as final.

A published 2024 cutoff reference on the programme page was 81 percentile. This is a historical reference, not a guaranteed 2027 cutoff.

Nirma MBA Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

The 2026 cycle listed application opening on August 1, 2025, application deadline January 15, 2026, shortlisting in the fourth week of January, selection in the first week of February, offers in late February/early March and programme commencement in the third week of June 2026.

2027 planning: expect a similar CAT-to-selection sequence, but exact 2027 dates are not official until Nirma publishes them.

Nirma MBA Placements

The Institute of Management's published 2024-25 figures list a highest package of ₹70 LPA subject to confirmation, average package of ₹11.10 LPA, average for top 100 of ₹14.74 LPA and average for top 50 of ₹17.08 LPA.

The institute reports corporate linkages with more than 300 companies. Its placement ecosystem spans IT, FMCG, manufacturing, telecom, banking, insurance, consulting and other sectors.

Placement reality check

Do not compare the ₹70 LPA maximum with the average package. A maximum package is an outlier outcome; your likely salary depends on profile, role, recruiter and batch.

Nirma MBA FAQs

What CAT percentile is needed for Nirma MBA?

The programme page cites an 81 percentile cutoff reference for 2024. Treat this as historical, not a guaranteed 2027 cutoff.

Is Nirma MBA CAT-based?

Yes, the general MBA route uses CAT for shortlisting followed by TDP and PI and other composite-score components.

What is Nirma MBA fees?

The published tuition reference is ₹6.40 lakh per annum, with additional student-development and living costs.

What is the average MBA package?

The published 2024-25 average package is ₹11.10 LPA; top-50 and top-100 averages were higher.
