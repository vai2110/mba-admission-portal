IIM Ahmedabad Placement Overview 2025–26

The 2025–26 final placement process for IIMA's Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) Class of 2026 concluded with all students placed across 26 cohorts. The process began with lateral placements from January 5 to 15, 2026, followed by the final placement process organised around industry and business-function cohorts.

The latest cycle also saw wider participation from analytics and IT consulting, fintech, healthcare, renewable energy, consumer services, manufacturing, conglomerates and other emerging areas.

Key developments in the latest placement cycle

  • More than 150 dream applications were made by students after receiving initial offers.
  • The private equity, venture capital and asset management cohort recorded a 45% increase in offers.
  • Analytics and IT consulting offers increased by 150%.
  • Consumer services offers increased by 150%.
  • Pharmaceuticals and healthcare offers increased by 60%.
  • Renewable energy and greentech offers increased by 50%.
  • Fintech offers increased by 36%.

IIM Ahmedabad Salary Packages 2026: Mean, Median and MEP

The 2025–26 audited salary figures are not yet available. Until IIMA publishes the next IPRS report, the latest audited PGP salary data available is for the Class of 2025.

₹1.10 Crore Maximum reported domestic MEP Audited PGP report · 2025
How IIMA reports salary: IIM Ahmedabad reports placement compensation using Maximum Earning Potential (MEP). The institute states that MEP represents the earning potential from the components included in the offer and is different from the conventional way some institutes present CTC. For this reason, students should always check the reporting methodology before comparing IIMA's salary figures directly with another B-school.

IIM Ahmedabad Placement Trends Over the Years

The latest audited PGP reports show some year-to-year movement in salary outcomes. The table below focuses on the reported domestic MEP figures and should be read alongside the methodology used in each year's IPRS report.

Placement Year Mean MEP Median MEP Maximum Domestic MEP
2023 ₹37.15 LPA ₹31.58 LPA ₹1.46 Crore
2024 ₹33.23 LPA ₹32.00 LPA ₹56 LPA
2025 ₹35.50 LPA ₹34.59 LPA ₹1.10 Crore
How to read the trend

Mean MEP increased from 2024 to 2025, while the median also moved upward. The maximum offer, however, is much more volatile because it can be influenced by a small number of high-value offers. For students, mean and median are generally more useful indicators of the broader salary outcome than the single highest offer.

What Do IIM Ahmedabad Placement Figures Mean for Students?

Consulting remains a major career route

The 2025 function-wise placement data shows consulting as the largest accepted-offer category. The 2025–26 cycle also saw strong participation from BCG, McKinsey, Bain, Accenture Strategy, EY-Parthenon, Kearney and other consulting firms.

Finance remains highly significant

Finance was the second-largest function in the 2025 accepted-offer data. The latest placement cycle also featured Goldman Sachs, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley, Blackstone, Avendus Capital and other financial-sector recruiters.

Career options are becoming broader

The 2025–26 release shows stronger offer growth in analytics and IT consulting, consumer services, healthcare, fintech, renewable energy and manufacturing. This indicates that recruitment is not limited to traditional consulting and finance roles.

Look beyond the highest package

The ₹1.10 crore maximum MEP in the 2025 audited report represents the upper end of the placement distribution. Mean and median figures provide a better starting point for understanding the broader salary outcome.
