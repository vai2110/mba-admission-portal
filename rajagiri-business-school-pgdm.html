What is the Rajagiri Business School PGDM?

Quick answer

The RBS PGDM is a two-year full-time programme spread over six trimesters with a summer internship. It is AICTE approved and recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU.

The programme combines compulsory foundation learning with electives and gives students the flexibility to build a dual specialisation. RBS also emphasises experiential learning, cases, simulations, mentoring and industry interaction.

RBS's elective structure can change, so students should verify the current list of available electives before making a final choice.

Does RBS PGDM provide international exposure?

RBS highlights international tie-ups and partnerships as part of its learning ecosystem. The school also holds AACSB accreditation, while its PGDM has EFMD and ACBSP accreditation references.

International exposure can strengthen cross-cultural and global-business understanding, but it should be treated as an opportunity rather than a guaranteed overseas placement.

How is learning delivered at RBS?

RBS highlights cases, simulations, experiential learning and mentoring. The objective is to develop competencies such as conceptual clarity, decision making, problem solving, communication and independent/team-based working.

The Office of Corporate Relations also supports career counselling, pre-placement training, industry interaction, summer internships and final placements.

RBS PGDM Fees and Scholarships

The current tuition reference is ₹7.50 lakh for two years. RBS's official older fee structure shows ₹4 lakh for the first three trimesters and ₹3.5 lakh for the final three; the dedicated fee page clearly labels this as the older published schedule.

RBS also lists scholarship opportunities, including Diversity, Career & Talent and Director/Dean's scholarships. Eligible candidates can receive substantial tuition waivers.

RBS PGDM Placements: what is the latest benchmark?

Latest NIRF benchmark

For the 2024-25 graduating cohort, NIRF data reports 201 placed out of 215 graduates and a ₹7.50 LPA median salary.

The separate 2023-25 placement report lists 249 placed among 272 students who opted for placements, 170 recruiters, ₹16.13 LPA top-10 placement, ₹11.26 LPA top-50 placement, ₹9.28 LPA top-100 placement and 21 international placements.

RBS PGDM FAQs

Is RBS PGDM equivalent to MBA?

RBS states that its AICTE-approved PGDM is recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU.

How long is the RBS PGDM?

The programme is two years full-time, spread over six trimesters with a summer internship.

Does RBS offer dual specialisation?

Yes. RBS provides elective flexibility through which students can pursue dual specialisation.

What is the RBS PGDM fee?

The current tuition reference is ₹7.50 lakh for two years, with hostel and other expenses additional.

What is the latest RBS placement median?

The latest NIRF data for the 2024-25 graduating cohort reports ₹7.50 LPA.

Is RBS PGDM residential?

RBS describes its PGDM as a fully residential two-year programme. Hostel and related residential costs should be checked in the current fee communication.
