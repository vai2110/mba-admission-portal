What is the RBS PGDM?

Quick answer

The Rajagiri Business School PGDM is a two-year full-time programme spread over six trimesters and a summer internship. It is AICTE approved and recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU.

The programme combines compulsory foundation courses with electives that build job-specific skills. RBS says students can select courses from a wide range of electives and pursue dual specialisation.

Does RBS PGDM provide international exposure?

Yes. RBS states that the PGDM provides opportunities for global exposure through international tie-ups and partnerships. The programme is also positioned within a school holding AACSB accreditation, while the PGDM itself is accredited by EFMD and ACBSP.

International exposure should be treated as an opportunity rather than a guaranteed overseas job or placement.

How is teaching conducted at RBS?

RBS highlights cases and simulations as innovative instructional practices. The programme is designed around applying concepts to decision-making and problem-solving rather than relying only on classroom theory.

The placement ecosystem also includes career counselling, pre-placement training, recruiter engagement, summer internships and final-placement support through the Office of Corporate Relations.

RBS PGDM Fees and Scholarships

Current 2026 course listings report ₹7.50 lakh tuition for the two-year PGDM. Hostel charges are separately reported at about ₹1.88 lakh for two years. Confirm the final fee schedule directly from RBS before payment.

Scholarship support can materially change the cost. RBS lists 25–50% tuition waivers under its Diversity and Career/Talent scholarship categories, while its Director/Dean's Scholarship offers a 75% tuition waiver for eligible CAT 2025 candidates at 95 percentile or above, subject to the published conditions.

RBS PGDM Placements: what should students expect?

Latest benchmark

NIRF 2026 data for the 2024-25 graduating cohort reports 201 students placed out of 215 graduates with a ₹7.50 LPA median salary.

The separate 2023-25 placement report lists 170 recruiters, 249 placed students among 272 who opted for placements, top-10 placements of ₹16.13 LPA, top-50 placements of ₹11.26 LPA, top-100 placements of ₹9.28 LPA and 21 international placements.
