CMAT 2027 Overview

The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a national-level management entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

CMAT scores are used by participating management institutes for admission to MBA, PGDM and other management programmes, subject to the eligibility and admission requirements of individual institutes.

CMAT 2027 status:

The official NTA material currently available does not provide the CMAT 2027 examination schedule. Candidates should check the official NTA CMAT website for the latest notification.

Candidates should check the official CMAT 2027 Information Bulletin when released for the definitive eligibility requirements.

CMAT Marking Scheme

The latest official CMAT pattern uses objective-type questions. Candidates should verify the final marking scheme in the CMAT 2027 Information Bulletin before the examination.

  • The latest published scheme awards 4 marks for a correct answer.
  • The latest published scheme deducts 1 mark for an incorrect answer.
  • Unattempted questions do not receive marks.

CMAT 2027 Registration

CMAT registration is conducted online through the National Testing Agency's official CMAT website.

How to Register

  1. Visit the official NTA CMAT website.
  2. Create your candidate registration account.
  3. Enter personal and academic details.
  4. Upload the required documents and photograph/signature as prescribed.
  5. Select the required examination details.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee.
  7. Submit the application form.
  8. Download and retain the confirmation page for future reference.

CMAT Admission Process

Qualifying in CMAT does not automatically guarantee admission to a management institute.

Typical Admission Stages

  1. Appear for CMAT.
  2. Receive the CMAT score/percentile.
  3. Apply separately to participating institutes where required.
  4. Meet the institute's shortlist criteria.
  5. Participate in the institute's selection process.
  6. Complete document verification and fee payment after selection.

Selection components may include academic performance, CMAT score, personal interview, group discussion, written assessment, work experience or other institute-specific criteria.

How to Prepare for CMAT 2027

1. Strengthen Quantitative Aptitude

Build strong fundamentals in arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data interpretation before moving to timed practice.

2. Practise Logical Reasoning

Solve different types of arrangements, puzzles, analytical reasoning and logical deduction questions.

3. Improve Reading Speed

Regular reading can help with comprehension, vocabulary and time management in the language section.

4. Cover General Awareness

Follow important national, international, business and economic developments.

5. Don't Ignore Innovation & Entrepreneurship

This section makes CMAT different from several other MBA entrance examinations. Prepare its concepts separately.

6. Take Mock Tests

Full-length mocks help identify weak areas and improve speed, accuracy and question selection.
