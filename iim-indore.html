College Overview

About IIM Indore

IIM Indore's flagship PGP is a two-year MBA route with a broad general-management orientation. The institute also runs the five-year IPM and specialised/executive programmes.

Why students shortlist it

Strong fit for students who want a broad IIM MBA, a residential campus and career access across consulting, finance, general management, analytics, marketing and operations.

What should you budget?

PGP 2026–28 official schedule: ₹12,00,000 course fee per year + ₹50,000 refundable caution deposit + ₹54,000 mess deposit in Year 1; Year 2 adds ₹11,800 alumni fee including GST. The two-year course fee alone is ₹24 lakh; with the listed first-year deposits and second-year alumni fee, the published academic/mandatory components total ₹24.65 lakh before any actual insurance/personal expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

These questions focus on the decisions students usually need to make before applying, shortlisting or accepting an offer.

What is the fee of IIM Indore MBA 2026–28?

The official PGP page lists ₹24,11,800 as the two-year course fee for resident Indian students. It includes lodging and alumni fee but excludes mess, insurance and textbooks. This is the number students should use instead of older ₹20–21 lakh figures found on third-party sites.

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Indore MBA?

For PGP 2026–28, the official minimum for General/EWS candidates is 90 overall and 80 in each section. For PGP-HRM, the minimum is also 90 overall, but the sectional requirements differ. These are minimums, not safe scores.

What is the average package at IIM Indore?

The 2025 PGP/IPM placement report records ₹29.57 lakh average, ₹27 lakh median and ₹70 lakh highest package, with 225+ recruiters. Consulting was the largest reported domain at about 35.4% of offers.

IIM Indore vs IIM Lucknow: which is better?

IIM Lucknow has a stronger traditional top-IIM positioning for many consulting and finance aspirants, while IIM Indore offers a broad PGP and strong consulting outcomes. If both convert, compare the role mix and your personal career goal rather than relying only on rank.

Is IIM Indore PGP-HRM worth choosing?

PGP-HRM is a specialised MBA route. Its 2026–28 official course fee is ₹10.35 lakh per year, plus ₹50,000 refundable caution deposit and mess-related charges. It makes sense for students who genuinely want HR, people analytics, talent or organisation roles—not for someone using HRM simply as an alternative IIM entry.

What is the IIM Indore IPM fee?

For domestic applicants, the institute currently lists ₹6 lakh per year for the first three years, subject to revision. The fourth and fifth years are charged at the prevailing PGP fee. The first three years include lodging, lectures, internet and library access.

Is IIM Indore good for freshers?

Yes. The PGP is a broad management programme and does not require prior full-time work experience. Freshers should nevertheless compare the opportunity cost of two years against their target career and not assume that an IIM automatically guarantees a high-paying role.
