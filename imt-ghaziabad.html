College Overview

Institute of Management Technology, Ghaziabad is a management institute located in Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR. The institute was established in 1980 and offers multiple management programmes, with its two-year full-time PGDM being a major route for management aspirants.

IMT Ghaziabad states that its full-time PGDM is residential and that the curriculum incorporates industry inputs, business simulations, AI, analytics and sustainability-oriented learning. The institute is AACSB-accredited and also lists AICTE approval and other recognitions on its official programme information.

Admissions & Eligibility

For the two-year full-time PGDM programmes, IMT Ghaziabad currently states that applicants need a minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks, or they may apply in the final year of graduation subject to the institute's conditions.

IMT also offers PGDM variants including Marketing, Financial Management, Banking & Financial Services and the Dual Country Program. Applicants should check the exact programme-specific requirements before submitting an application.

Fee Structure

For the 2026-28 batch, the official IMT Ghaziabad fee document lists a total of ₹16,00,000 for the PGDM, PGDM (Finance), PGDM (Marketing) and PGDM (Logistics & Supply Chain Management) fee structure, including the listed admission, academic, alumni and refundable caution-money components. Fees can be revised, so the official fee document for the relevant batch should be treated as final.

Placement Statistics

IMT Ghaziabad's latest placement communication published in January 2026 reported that, up to 31 December 2025, 400 students had been placed with 110 participating companies, including 25 new recruiters. The same official communication reported a highest CTC of ₹35 lakh, an average CTC of ₹18.89 lakh and a median CTC of ₹17.56 lakh.
