BIM's programme is designed to develop depth in a chosen management area while maintaining cross-functional business understanding. The structure also includes practice-oriented activities, personality development and industry interaction.

What does the BIM MBA curriculum cover?

The academic guidelines include a mix of management fundamentals and contemporary business subjects. Examples include data science tools, operations research, financial markets and treasury management, business research methods, technology-enabled business management, digital business modelling and new venture planning.

BIM Courses FAQs

How many specialisations are offered in BIM MBA?

BIM lists Marketing Management, Finance and Economics, Operations and Supply Chain Management, Human Resources Management, and Digital Business & Analytics.

Does BIM offer MBA in Business Analytics?

BIM offers Digital Business & Analytics as a management specialisation. Its curriculum also includes data science tools and digital business modelling.

Is BIM MBA good for finance?

BIM has a Finance and Economics specialisation and a Financial Trading and Analytics Centre with Bloomberg terminals and an NSE Smart Trading Simulator. It is relevant for students interested in finance, markets and analytics.

Can engineering students study at BIM?

Yes. BIM's eligibility is open to graduates from any discipline with the required marks. Its published student profiles include B.E./B.Tech graduates across several engineering branches.

Is BIM MBA residential?

Yes. The MBA is a two-year full-time residential programme.

What is the π-shaped programme at BIM?

It is BIM's programme architecture intended to combine strong depth in a chosen area with broader cross-functional management capability. Students should think of it as a specialisation-plus-breadth model rather than a single-subject MBA.
