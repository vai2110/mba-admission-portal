What is MICA PGDM?

MICA's PGDM is positioned for analytical and technology-oriented management careers. The current programme description emphasises applied AI, human-centred design, data-driven decision-making and business strategy.

How is MICA PGDM different from PGDM-C?

PGDM leans towards data, analytics, AI and technology-driven management, while PGDM-C focuses more strongly on strategic marketing, communication, brand, content and culture. Both remain rooted in MICA's interdisciplinary approach.

MICA PGDM Eligibility and Admission Process

Applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree under the 10+2+3 system or equivalent qualification. MICA's School of Management selection process combines CAT/XAT/GMAT performance, MICAT and the GE/PI stages.

MICA PGDM Specialisations and Curriculum

MICA currently lists these focus areas for the PGDM:

Business Analytics & Consulting

For students interested in using data and analytical thinking to solve business problems and support decisions.

Data Science & AI

A compulsory specialisation focused on data, AI and technology-enabled management.

BFSI & Fintech

For careers at the intersection of financial services, technology, analytics and strategic management.

Technology-driven Business Strategy

For students who want to connect technology adoption with business strategy and organisational decision-making.

What is distinctive about the PGDM?

The programme combines analytical capability with human-centred design and MICA's broader emphasis on cultural understanding. It is intended for non-technical managerial careers in AI, business analysis, data science and analytics, AI product management and AI governance.

MICA PGDM Fees and Scholarships

The published complete fee for the two-year residential PGDM is ₹28 lakh. Optional international immersion carries separate charges.

MICA provides merit-cum-means financial support for eligible students. Scholarship evaluation includes financial need, academic performance and attendance conditions as applicable.

MICA PGDM Placements and Career Outcomes

MICA's placement ecosystem includes consulting, analytics, consumer technology, BFSI, FMCG, marketing and other sectors. The current admissions page reports a ₹40.91 LPA highest CTC and ₹19.22 LPA average CTC in its 2025 placement snapshot for the PGP programmes.

Who Should Choose MICA PGDM?

Good fit

  • You want a management career involving data, AI, analytics or technology strategy.
  • You want to combine analytical thinking with human-centred business understanding.
  • You are interested in business analytics, consulting, fintech or AI product/governance pathways.
  • You want a specialised programme with strong marketing and consumer context.

Reconsider if

  • You want a pure technical master's rather than a management programme.
  • You are looking for a traditional generalist MBA with broad functional specialisations.
  • You have no interest in analytics, technology or data-driven decision-making.
  • You are choosing the programme only because MICA has a strong brand name.

MICA PGDM FAQs

What is the MICA PGDM fee?

The published fee for the complete two-year residential PGDM is ₹28 lakh, with optional international immersion charged separately.

What is the MICA PGDM eligibility?

Candidates need a recognised bachelor's degree under the 10+2+3 system or equivalent qualification and must meet MICA's published academic conditions.

What are the MICA PGDM specialisations?

MICA currently lists Business Analytics & Consulting, Data Science & AI, BFSI & Fintech, and Technology-driven Business Strategy. Data Science & AI is described as a compulsory specialisation.

Is MICA PGDM suitable for analytics careers?

Yes. The current programme is explicitly positioned around applied AI, business analytics, data science and AI, consulting and technology-driven strategy.

Is MICAT required for MICA PGDM?

MICAT is a core component of MICA's School of Management selection process, alongside CAT, XAT or GMAT and the GE/PI stages.
