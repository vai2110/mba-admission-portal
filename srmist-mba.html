What Is the SRMIST MBA?

SRMIST's MBA portfolio includes MBA Business Administration across Kattankulathur, Ramapuram and Vadapalani, along with focused management programmes. The general MBA is useful when you want broader specialisation flexibility.

Who Can Apply?

SRMIST lists a minimum 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree for the MBA portfolio. Preference is given to candidates with work experience in the published management admission information.

How Does MBA Admission Work?

The standard route is SRMJEEM followed by Personal Interview. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

What Entrance Score Does SRMIST Look For?

The official AICTE mandatory disclosure lists minimum desirable attainment figures: CAT 65 percentile+, MAT 75 percentile+, XAT 65 percentile+, GMAT 640+, NMAT 180+, CMAT 75 percentile+ and TANCET 80 percentile+.

These are not guaranteed qualifying cutoffs. The disclosure states that selection also depends on profile and pre-admission rounds.

What About MBA Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported average salary ₹10.5 LPA and maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum. These are university-level numbers, not an MBA-only average.

MBA vs Business Analytics vs Financial Services

Compare the course you will actually study, not just the title. A general MBA gives wider specialisation choice; Business Analytics narrows the focus towards analytics; Financial Services with NSE is more finance/markets oriented and has a higher listed Kattankulathur fee.

FAQs Students Ask

What is the minimum MBA eligibility?

A bachelor's degree with minimum 50% aggregate.

Can CAT or XAT be used instead of SRMJEEM?

Eligible CAT/XAT and other listed national-test scores can receive direct consideration for the PI, subject to SRMIST's conditions.

Are CAT 65 or XAT 65 final cutoffs?

No. They are minimum desirable attainment figures in the official disclosure, not guaranteed qualifying cutoffs.

Is the MBA fee the same at all SRMIST campuses?

No. The published MBA Business Administration fee is ₹4.50 lakh at KTR, ₹3.25 lakh at Ramapuram and ₹3 lakh at Vadapalani.

Does work experience help?

SRMIST's management admission information states that preference is given to candidates with work experience.
