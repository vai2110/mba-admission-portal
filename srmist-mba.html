MBA at SRMIST

SRMIST offers MBA Business Administration at Kattankulathur, Ramapuram and Vadapalani, along with specialised MBA programmes. This page covers the general MBA route; specialised programmes have separate guides.

Who Can Apply for SRMIST MBA?

Applicants need a bachelor's degree with a minimum 50% marks, subject to the current programme conditions.

How Does SRMIST MBA Admission Work?

SRMIST lists SRMJEEM + personal interview. Candidates with eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT or TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

The management admissions page lists Business Analytics, Banking & Financial Services, Healthcare & Hospital Management, AI & Data Science, Digital Marketing, Financial Services with NSE, Sports Management, Logistics & Supply Chain Management, Project Management and Family Managed Business, among others.

Read the dedicated MBA Business Analytics and MBA Financial Services with NSE pages for programme-specific details.

Placements

Use the SRMIST placements page for university-level salary and NIRF data. A university-wide figure should not be treated as a guaranteed MBA package.

SRMIST MBA FAQs

What is the lowest official MBA Business Administration fee among the three listed Chennai campuses?

The official listed fee is ₹3 lakh at Vadapalani, compared with ₹3.25 lakh at Ramapuram and ₹4.50 lakh at Kattankulathur.

Can a CAT score be used for SRMIST MBA?

Yes. SRMIST lists CAT among the national-level scores eligible for direct consideration for the PI, subject to its stated conditions.

Is SRMJEEM mandatory if I have CAT or XAT?

Eligible CAT/XAT and other listed scores can receive direct consideration for the PI, so the applicant should follow the current admission instructions for the applicable route.
