MBA at SRMIST

SRMIST offers MBA Business Administration and specialised MBA programmes. Kattankulathur, Ramapuram and Vadapalani have different fee levels for listed MBA programmes.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks is the stated MBA eligibility requirement on the current SRMIST management admission page.

How to Apply?

SRMJEEM followed by personal interview is the SRMIST route. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

MBA Cutoff

SRMIST's AICTE mandatory disclosure lists minimum desirable scores of CAT 65 percentile+, MAT 75+, XAT 65+, GMAT 640+, NMAT 180+, CMAT 75+ and TANCET 80+. These are minimum desirable attainment figures, not guaranteed selection cutoffs.

Placements

Official SRMIST 2023 university-level figures reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum and average salary ₹10.5 LPA.

FAQs

Can CAT be used for SRMIST MBA?

Yes. Eligible CAT scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

What is the Kattankulathur MBA fee?

The current management admission page lists ₹4.50 lakh for MBA Business Administration at Kattankulathur.

Are AICTE scores the same as admission cutoffs?

No. SRMIST describes them as minimum desirable attainment, not qualifying scores.
