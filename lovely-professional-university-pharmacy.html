LPU B.Pharm: Programme Overview

LPU's Bachelor of Pharmacy is a four-year undergraduate programme covering pharmaceutical sciences, drug development, formulation, quality control and safe use of medicines. The programme is intended for students interested in pharmacy, healthcare, research and pharmaceutical industry careers.

Quick student verdict

B.Pharm is a specialised professional degree. It makes sense when you genuinely want pharmacy or pharmaceutical-sector work. Compare practical exposure, laboratories, regulatory recognition, fees and career pathways rather than choosing only on university size.

LPU B.Pharm Eligibility 2026

The degree combines pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmaceutics, pharmacology, pharmacognosy, medicinal chemistry and other professional subjects with laboratory and practical learning.

B.Pharm Practical Training & Laboratories

Pharmacy students should expect substantial laboratory work. Practical competence matters for formulation, analysis, quality control and professional confidence. Use internships and projects to understand how manufacturing, QA/QC and clinical environments actually operate.

Skills to build

  • Laboratory discipline.
  • Drug formulation basics.
  • Quality-control methods.
  • Documentation and compliance.
  • Scientific communication.

Do not overlook

Industrial exposure and professional documentation can be important differentiators when many graduates have similar academic qualifications.
