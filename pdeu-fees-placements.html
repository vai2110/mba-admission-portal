PDEU Hostel & Mess: What should you add to your budget?

PDEU provides residential facilities, but hostel accommodation is subject to availability and fees can be revised. The 2024-25 B.Tech admission rules listed ₹96,000 annual hostel fee plus a ₹5,000 refundable hostel deposit. Electricity was extra based on actual consumption.

Older international/DASA documents show different hostel and mess amounts, reinforcing that hostel charges should always be taken from the current admission year rather than copied from an old article.

PDEU ROI: How should students calculate it?

Use

  • Current tuition + hostel + living cost.
  • Median salary rather than highest offer.
  • Placement rate and graduating cohort size.
  • Role quality and location.

Don't use alone

  • Highest package.
  • Old fee PDFs without the academic year.
  • University-wide placement numbers as CSE-specific results.
  • Marketing claims without NIRF or programme evidence.

For example, comparing a historical B.Tech fee benchmark of ₹1.245 lakh tuition per semester with the NIRF UG median of ₹6.42 LPA is only a rough planning exercise. The real four-year cost and current fee must be verified before deciding.

PDEU Fees & Placements FAQs

What is PDEU B.Tech fees?

The latest directly accessible official B.Tech rule sheet is 2024-25 and lists ₹1,24,500 tuition per semester for All India/Gujarat seats, plus enrolment and refundable deposits. Treat this as a historical benchmark until the 2026 fee is confirmed.

What is PDEU MBA fees?

A 2025 secondary listing reported approximately ₹8.1 lakh tuition for MBA. Older official policies differ, so the current 2026 School of Management offer document should be used for payment.

What is PDEU hostel fee?

The 2024-25 B.Tech admission rules listed ₹96,000 per academic year plus a ₹5,000 refundable hostel deposit. Fees are subject to revision and availability.

What is PDEU engineering average package?

The most comparable official figure is the NIRF median salary: ₹6.42 LPA for UG 4-year engineering graduates in 2023-24. Median is not average.

What is PDEU highest package?

PDEU's placement page highlights a historical ₹38 LPA CSE offer from JP Morgan Chase to four students and other school-specific highest offers. These are not typical salaries.

What is PDEU MBA placement salary?

NIRF 2025 reports a ₹8.50 LPA median salary for the 2023-24 School of Management cohort.

What is PDEU MBA placement percentage?

117 of 121 MBA graduates were placed in the 2023-24 minimum-time cohort reported to NIRF, about 96.7%.

Is PDEU placement good for CSE?

PDEU reports strong individual CSE offers, including a historical ₹38 LPA JP Morgan Chase offer. But the NIRF median is university-wide engineering data, so students should request or compare current branch-level placement information before paying.

Does PDEU provide hostel?

Yes, residential facilities are available subject to seat availability. Current charges should be checked with the admission/hostel office.

Is PDEU worth the fees?

It depends on branch, current fee and alternatives. The latest NIRF data shows ₹6.42 LPA UG engineering median and ₹8.50 LPA MBA median, but the correct ROI calculation requires current total cost and programme-specific outcomes.

What is PDEU NIRF Engineering rank 2025?

PDEU ranked 98 in Engineering in NIRF 2025 with a score of 45.97.

What is PDEU NIRF Management rank 2025?

PDEU ranked 89 in Management in NIRF 2025 with a score of 46.81.
