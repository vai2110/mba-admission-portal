IIM Indore Placements: Student Decision Snapshot

The latest consolidated official report available for IIM Indore records final placements for the 2023-25 batch. It reports 225+ recruiters, an average package of ₹29.57 lakh, a median package of ₹27 lakh and a highest domestic package of ₹70 lakh. Consulting accounted for 35.4% of offers. Finance accounted for 14.5%. General management, HR and operations accounted for 26.5%. IT and analytics accounted for 10.4%, while sales and marketing accounted for 13.2%. The report states that more than 49% of the batch secured roles in consulting and finance combined.

Recruiters, Job Roles & Career Functions

The official report lists recruiters across consulting, finance, general management, HR, operations, IT and analytics, and sales and marketing. Consulting recruiters include Accenture Strategy, BCG, Deloitte, EY, Kearney, KPMG, PwC and Vector Consulting Group. Finance recruiters include Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan Chase, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Nomura and Yes Bank. Other listed recruiters include Amazon, Microsoft, Reliance Industries, Tata Administrative Services, HUL, PepsiCo, Samsung and Tata Consumer Products.

Functions and roles reported

The report records roles such as business valuation and modelling, corporate finance, deal strategy, digital transformation, operations transformation, business strategy, founder's office, investor relations, supply chain management, data science, data analytics, product management, project management, B2B sales, growth marketing and strategic partnerships.

Placement Analysis: What the Official Data Shows

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.
