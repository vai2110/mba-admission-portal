IIM Amritsar Executive MBA Overview

The IIM Amritsar Executive MBA is a two-year degree programme designed for working professionals. The institute's current criteria require a bachelor's degree, at least three years of progressive managerial, entrepreneurial or professional work experience after graduation, and a valid CAT/GMAT/GRE/GATE score or the IIM Amritsar Admission Test (IAAT).

IIM Amritsar Executive MBA Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline with consistent academic performance.
  • Minimum 3 years of progressive work experience after graduation.
  • Valid CAT, GMAT, GRE or GATE score, or IAAT as the alternative institute test.

The institute's current admission criteria also specify score-validity rules for GMAT, GRE and GATE for the stated admission cycle.

IIM Amritsar Executive MBA Selection Process

Shortlisted candidates are assessed through an online Personal Interview. The institute's FAQ states that candidates can be shortlisted using CAT/GMAT/GRE/GATE/IAAT scores and are then assessed comprehensively.

IIM Amritsar Executive MBA Important Dates

Dates can change by intake. Students should use the official Executive MBA application portal for the active cycle rather than carrying forward dates from an earlier intake.

IIM Amritsar Executive MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Amritsar Executive MBA fee?

The official programme-fee page currently lists ₹12 lakh, excluding the ₹2,950 application fee.

How much work experience is required?

The current eligibility criteria require at least three years of progressive work experience after graduation in a managerial, entrepreneurial or professional role.

Is CAT mandatory for the Executive MBA?

No single test is mandatory if you use an accepted alternative. The institute lists CAT, GMAT, GRE and GATE scores and also provides IAAT as an alternative admission test.
