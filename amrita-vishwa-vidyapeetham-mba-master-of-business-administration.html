Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multi-campus, multidisciplinary research institution accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC. Its management-study options include a Master of Business Administration (MBA) and a dual-degree MBA–MS in Business Analytics programme described as being offered in collaboration with the University at Buffalo, USA.

These options may suit different goals. A general MBA is worth exploring if you want broad management education; the MBA–MS in Business Analytics option is more specifically oriented towards business analytics. Compare the curriculum, learning format, campus, total cost and career outcomes for the exact programme and intake you are considering rather than assuming the two routes have identical requirements.

What should you check before applying?

First, confirm the academic eligibility criteria for the specific programme. Do not assume a minimum percentage, accepted undergraduate disciplines or work-experience requirement without checking the current official admission notice. Next, verify which entrance-test scores are accepted, whether the university has its own assessment or interview, and how the final selection is calculated.

Keep your academic transcripts, degree or provisional certificate, identity documents, entrance-test details and any other documents listed in the current application instructions ready. Check application dates, document-upload rules and whether separate applications are required for the two programme options. These are items to verify, not a claim that every document or selection stage applies to every applicant.
