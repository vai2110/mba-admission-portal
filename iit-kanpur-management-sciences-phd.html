Quick Answer

The PhD programme in Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur is a research-intensive degree. Admissions are conducted online via the institute's portal, typically with application windows opening in March for the July semester. Eligibility requires a relevant degree with a minimum of 55% marks or 5.5 CPI. Financial support is available for non-sponsored students via Institute Assistantships.

Programme Overview

The Department of Management Sciences at IIT Kanpur offers a rigorous PhD programme designed to foster advanced research and academic leadership. The programme is supported by the institute's robust research infrastructure and interdisciplinary environment.

Eligibility Requirements

Candidates must meet the minimum academic standards set by the institute. Generally, this includes a relevant degree with at least 55% marks or 5.5 CPI (on a 10-point scale). Relaxations apply for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/DAP) as per Government of India norms. Final-year students may apply for provisional admission.

Admission Process

Admissions are managed through the online PG admission portal. The process typically involves:

  • Online application submission during the notified window (e.g., March-April).
  • Review of academic performance, research potential, and relevant test scores (e.g., GATE/JRF where applicable).
  • Departmental selection process, which may include interviews.

Application fees are ₹500 for General/EWS/OBC candidates and ₹250 for SC/ST/DAP candidates.

Placements

IIT Kanpur maintains a strong industry interface. In the 2025-26 placement season, the institute recorded 672 job offers on Day 1, marking a 16% increase over the previous year. This reflects the high demand for IIT Kanpur graduates across various sectors.
