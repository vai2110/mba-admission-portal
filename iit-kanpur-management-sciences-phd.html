Quick Answer

The DoMS PhD programme is designed for advanced research in Management Sciences. IIT Kanpur's current eligibility routes include master's-level qualifications in management or relevant engineering/technology disciplines, as well as specified bachelor's and master's routes that require qualifying national-level test credentials where applicable.

Programme Overview

The PhD in Management Sciences covers research across the department's management and quantitative disciplines. DoMS lists research strengths in operations research and management, decision support systems, organisation sciences, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, entrepreneurship and energy.

IIT Kanpur PhD Eligibility

The official DoMS/DoAA eligibility rules provide multiple qualification routes. The applicable route depends on the applicant's prior degree.

Applicant backgroundPublished requirement
Master's in Management / relevant Engineering or TechnologyMaster's degree with marks/CPI not below the specified minimum
4-year Bachelor's in Engineering or ScienceMinimum 75% marks / 7.5 CPI, with a valid GATE/CAT/GMAT score unless the specified CFTI waiver applies
Master's in Science / Arts / Commerce / Chartered AccountancyMinimum 65% / 6.5 CPI in master's, first division in bachelor's, plus JRF/NET or qualifying CAT/GMAT/GATE/national-level test as specified

The official eligibility page should be checked for the exact qualifying-test conditions applicable to the candidate's degree and institution type.

IIT Kanpur PhD Admission Process

  1. Submit the online application during the notified PhD admission window.
  2. DoMS screens applicants against the programme-specific academic and qualifying-test requirements.
  3. Shortlisted candidates proceed through the department's written-test/interview process where prescribed.
  4. Final selections are announced by IIT Kanpur for the applicable semester.

For the 2026–27-I semester, IIT Kanpur announced that the written test and interview were held on 11 May 2026, followed by the selected-candidate announcement.

PhD Research Areas in Management Sciences

  • Operations research and operations management
  • Decision support systems and analytics
  • Finance and financial markets
  • Marketing and consumer research
  • Economics
  • Organisation sciences and human-resource related research
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Energy and regulatory research

Applicants should review current faculty profiles and research groups before selecting a research direction.

Financial Support

IIT Kanpur provides Institute Assistantship support to eligible non-sponsored research students subject to the applicable institute rules. The amount, continuation conditions and fee structure should be verified from the latest IIT Kanpur PG/DoMS notices.

Career Outcomes

A PhD in Management Sciences is primarily research-oriented, so its outcomes should not be evaluated using MBA placement metrics. Graduates may pursue academic, research, policy, analytics and specialised industry roles depending on their research area.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the research scope of the DoMS PhD?
The programme covers all areas of Management, with research strengths spanning operations, analytics, finance, marketing, economics, organisation sciences, entrepreneurship and energy.
Can an engineering graduate apply for the PhD?
Yes. IIT Kanpur provides an eligibility route for engineering/technology graduates subject to the prescribed academic and qualifying-test conditions.
Is CAT accepted for PhD in Management Sciences?
CAT can be a qualifying credential for specified eligibility routes, including the published route for a four-year engineering/science bachelor's degree.
Are assistantships available?
Eligible non-sponsored students may receive Institute Assistantship subject to IIT Kanpur rules.
