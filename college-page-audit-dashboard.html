Audit Rules Applied to Every Page

  • Hero must follow the locked SIBM Pune structure: concise H1, intent-led supporting line, location + official website; no invented logo/stat cards.
  • Content depth must meet the IIM Ahmedabad benchmark without copying generic wording.
  • Admission process is the primary student-intent section: eligibility, application, selection, shortlisting, interviews/tests and final offer.
  • Fees must distinguish published academic fees from additional student-incurred costs and show total course-period cost where official data supports it.
  • Placements must use actual official numbers, latest highlights and up to three years of comparable statistics; never fabricate missing figures.
  • Important admission dates must use the upcoming cycle where published; otherwise clearly label previous-cycle/tentative planning dates.
  • SEO/AEO/GEO: search-intent H2s, long-tail coverage, answer-first blocks where useful, clean metadata, canonical, structured data and local/entity signals.
  • Use paragraphs, bullets and tables deliberately; add <hr/> between consecutive H2 sections; avoid generic FAQs.
  • All factual claims must be supported by official college/institute sources unless the page explicitly labels a non-official inference.
  • Course cards and placement buttons must resolve to the correct dedicated internal page, not a generic official-site redirect.
