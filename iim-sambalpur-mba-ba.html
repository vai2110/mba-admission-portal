IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA: Programme Overview

The MBA in Business Analytics is IIM Sambalpur's specialised management programme focused on business analytics, data-informed decision-making and strategic management. The first year is conducted at the IIM Sambalpur New Delhi Centre. The second year can be pursued at the same centre or, for eligible students selected for the dual-degree route, at the Asian Institute of Technology in Thailand.

What makes MBA-BA different from the regular MBA?

The MBA-BA is more specialised. It combines management with business intelligence, data visualisation, machine learning, AI, econometrics and analytics-oriented decision-making. Students who want analytics-heavy roles should compare it directly with the broader IIM Sambalpur MBA.

IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA Eligibility

The 2026-28 admission policy states that a candidate must have at least one year of relevant work experience, except candidates with a four-year undergraduate degree. The programme uses CAT 2025 for initial shortlisting.

IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA Curriculum

The programme's first year carries 60 credits and is structured across three trimesters. The curriculum combines management fundamentals with analytics and technology-oriented subjects.

Curriculum areaExamples of subjects
Business foundationBusiness Economics, Financial Reporting and Analysis, Financial Management, Marketing Management, Operations & Supply Chain Management.
AnalyticsIntroduction to Business Analytics, Business Intelligence & Data Visualization, Decision Sciences, Applied Econometrics.
AI & technologyFoundations of Algorithm Design and Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Managerial Computing.
ManagementOrganisational Behaviour, Human Resource Management, Strategic Management, Entrepreneurial Mindset and Action.

Second-year academic direction

The second year can be completed at IIM Sambalpur's Delhi Centre or through the optional AIT Thailand dual-degree pathway, subject to the applicable selection and eligibility conditions. The institute lists electives in areas such as finance, economics and other business functions.

MBA-BA Career Scope and Roles

The curriculum is designed for students who want management careers where data, analytics and strategic decision-making are central. Likely role families include business analytics, data-driven consulting, product, finance, risk, operations and technology-enabled management.

Strong fit for

  • Students targeting business analytics and data-driven management roles.
  • Candidates interested in AI, machine learning, BI and decision science.
  • Professionals who want to combine management with technical analytics skills.
  • Students interested in the optional international dual-degree pathway.

Think carefully if

  • You want a broad generalist MBA with minimal analytics depth.
  • You dislike quantitative, statistical or technology-oriented coursework.
  • You are choosing the programme only because of the dual-degree option.
  • You have not checked the separate cost and eligibility requirements for the AIT route.

IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA FAQs

What is IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA?

It is a two-year MBA in Business Analytics focused on business analytics, data-informed decision-making and strategic management.

What is the IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA cutoff?

The published 2026-28 minimum overall CAT percentiles are 85 General, 80 EWS, 75 NC-OBC, 55 SC and 40 ST/DAP.

Is work experience mandatory for IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA?

The 2026-28 admission policy requires at least one year of relevant work experience, except candidates with a four-year undergraduate degree.

Does IIM Sambalpur MBA-BA offer a dual degree?

Yes, the programme provides an option to pursue the second year through an AIT Thailand dual-degree route, subject to eligibility and merit-based selection.

Is MBA-BA better than the regular IIM Sambalpur MBA?

Neither is universally better. MBA-BA is the more specialised choice for analytics and data-driven careers, while the regular MBA provides broader functional flexibility.
