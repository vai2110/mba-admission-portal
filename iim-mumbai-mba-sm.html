IIM Mumbai MBA in Sustainability Management (MBA-SM): Overview

MBA-SM is a two-year management programme positioned for students interested in sustainability-linked business, ESG, responsible operations and management decision-making.

Quick answer for applicants

This page focuses on the information students usually need before applying: eligibility, entrance route, selection process, fee structure and officially published placement outcomes.

Eligibility Criteria

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required (45% for SC/ST/PwD), subject to the current official admission policy. Final-year students may apply under the published conditions.

Entrance Exam & Cutoff / Shortlisting

CAT is required for the current domestic admission route.

Important:

Where the institute does not publish a fixed programme-level final cutoff, this page does not invent one. Minimum eligibility and actual shortlist competitiveness are different.

Admission & Selection Process

Candidates are shortlisted and selected under IIM Mumbai's current official policy. Minimum eligibility alone should not be interpreted as a guaranteed interview call or final offer.

Placements: Latest Official Data & Trend Analysis

Who Should Consider This Programme?

A relevant option for students who want management training linked with sustainability, ESG and responsible business rather than a conventional general-MBA positioning.

Strong fit

  • Your career goal broadly matches the programme's stated positioning.
  • You meet the academic and experience requirements.
  • You have evaluated the full fee and opportunity cost.

Think carefully if

  • You are choosing only on the basis of a college brand without understanding programme differences.
  • You are using combined placement figures as if they were guaranteed programme-specific outcomes.
  • You have not compared the selection route with your profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main factors students should compare before applying?

Eligibility, entrance route, selection criteria, fee structure, programme format and the coverage of the official placement report should be compared together.

Is the published cutoff enough to guarantee admission?

No. A qualifying threshold or minimum eligibility level is usually only the first filter. Final admission depends on the institute's complete selection process.

Are placement figures always programme-specific?

No. Some institutes publish combined placement reports for multiple programmes. This page labels combined data accordingly and does not present it as programme-specific unless officially separated.
