What is JIMS PGDM-IB?

Quick answer

JIMS PGDM-International Business is a two-year management programme designed for students who want to work around international markets, trade, MNCs, export-import, global supply chains and cross-border business.

JIMS's published 2025-27 course structure says the programme offers two specialisations—Marketing and Finance—and includes core papers, IB specialisation papers, electives, summer internship and open electives. citeturn2search8

JIMS PGDM-IB Admission 2026

Applicants need a three-year bachelor's degree or equivalent with at least 50% aggregate marks. JIMS uses entrance-test performance along with academics/professional performance, PI and GD/SOP components in its published autonomous-PGDM selection framework. citeturn1search3

Accepted testsCAT, XAT, MAT and CMAT are listed in the core selection framework.
ApplicationThe PGDM application fee is ₹1,000 and one application can cover more than one programme.
GD/PIThe currently published 2026 online GD/PI schedule lists 16 June 2026 at 9:00 AM.
Final decisionDo not evaluate only on exam score; JIMS uses multiple selection components.

Important distinction

International exposure does not automatically mean a foreign semester or guaranteed overseas placement. Check the current brochure for the exact destination, duration, cost and eligibility of any immersion opportunity.

Who should choose PGDM-IB?

Choose PGDM-IB if

You are interested in MNCs, international trade, export-import, global supply chains, foreign markets or cross-border business.

Compare regular PGDM if

You are still unsure about your function and want maximum flexibility across general management careers.

JIMS PGDM-IB FAQs

What is the JIMS PGDM-IB fee?

For 2026-28, JIMS lists ₹10.75 lakh plus ₹2,000 lifetime alumni membership and ₹5,500 refundable security deposit.

What are PGDM-IB specialisations?

The published course structure lists Marketing and Finance as the two specialisations.

Is JIMS PGDM-IB equivalent to MBA?

JIMS states that its PGDM programmes are equivalent to an MBA by AIU.

What jobs can I get after PGDM-IB?

Potential roles include international sales, export-import, documentation, logistics, supply chain, market research, finance and management trainee roles.

Does JIMS accept CAT for PGDM-IB?

Yes. CAT is included in JIMS's published PGDM selection framework.

What is the JIMS PGDM-IB duration?

The programme is two years full time.

Does PGDM-IB have an internship?

Yes. The published course structure includes a summer internship project and viva.

Is PGDM-IB better than regular PGDM?

Neither is universally better. PGDM-IB is more suitable when international business is a clear career goal; regular PGDM offers broader flexibility.
