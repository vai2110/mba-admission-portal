Most important caveat

MUJ's admissions page presents these as university-level placement statistics. A student should not turn ₹1.06 crore into an “expected package”, nor assume ₹9 lakh is the BCA, MCA, MBA or CSE-specific average.

Recruiter Mix: Technology, Consulting, Finance and Engineering

The official recruiter list includes Accenture, Adobe, Amazon, Bosch, BlackRock, Capgemini, Cisco, Cognizant, Deloitte, Dell, Deutsche Bank, EY, Genpact, Goldman Sachs, Google, HCLTech, IBM, ICICI Prudential, Infosys, JP Morgan Chase, KPMG, L&T, Mahindra & Mahindra, Meesho, Microsoft, Micron, NVIDIA, Nokia, Oracle, PayPal, Persistent, Philips, PwC, SAP, Schneider Electric, Seagate, Synopsys, TCS, Tech Mahindra, TVS Motors, UltraTech, Wipro, Yamaha and Zscaler.

This is a useful breadth signal: the university is not dependent on one sector. However, recruiter presence is not the same as role availability for every course, so students should ask which recruiters participate in their programme's placement process.

Internships: An Often-Ignored Part of Placement ROI

MUJ's official placement statistics page states that paid internships can offer monthly stipends ranging from ₹15,000 to ₹1,25,000. The range is broad, so it should be treated as evidence that paid opportunities exist rather than an expected stipend for every student.

For engineering students, project-based learning and industrial exposure matter because recruiters often assess demonstrable technical work. For MBA students, internships should be judged by function—finance, marketing, analytics, consulting, operations—not just by stipend amount.

How Placement Evidence Should Be Read by Programme

B.Tech CSELook for software, product, cloud, cybersecurity, data and engineering roles. The CSE page displays alumni examples including Shell, Accenture, Siemens and Zscaler.
MBAFocus on role function and recruiter quality—finance, marketing, consulting, operations and analytics—rather than the university maximum alone.
BCA / MCALook for software, application, IT services and data roles. MUJ's programme page shows examples from Accenture, TCS, EY India, Vinove Software and CodefyLabs.
BBAEvaluate internship-to-job pathways and whether recruiters offer roles aligned with business, marketing, finance or analytics interests.

Placement ROI: Compare Salary Against the Full Education Cost

For a residential programme, ROI should include tuition plus hostel and living expenses. For example, B.Tech CSE currently lists ₹4.87 lakh annual tuition, while the 2026–27 double-occupancy hostel total including refundable security is ₹2.08 lakh. A student should therefore avoid comparing a package only with tuition.

Similarly, MBA applicants should account for ₹5.10 lakh annual tuition for the general MBA or ₹5.73 lakh for MBA Business Analytics, plus hostel and other living costs. Scholarships can materially change the net cost and should be included when the student actually qualifies.

MUJ Placement FAQs

What is the average package at MUJ?

The university's admissions page currently publishes ₹9.0 lakh average compensation as a university-level figure. It should not automatically be treated as the average for a specific programme.

What is the highest package at MUJ?

The current university-level headline is ₹1.06 crore maximum compensation. Maximum compensation is an outlier metric and should not be used as the expected salary.

How many companies visit MUJ?

MUJ currently states that 200 companies visited campus and 50 Fortune 500 companies visited.

Does MUJ offer paid internships?

The official placement statistics page states that paid internships can carry monthly stipends of ₹15,000 to ₹1,25,000.
