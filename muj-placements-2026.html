Most important caveat

These are university-level headline statistics. ₹1.06 crore is not an expected package, and ₹9 lakh should not automatically be presented as the BCA, MCA, MBA or CSE-specific average.

Recruiter presence is not the same as an offer. A useful placement page should therefore distinguish recruiter breadth from programme-level placement outcomes.

Internships: An Often-Ignored Part of Placement ROI

MUJ's official placement statistics page states that paid internships can offer monthly stipends ranging from ₹15,000 to ₹1,25,000. The range is broad, so it is evidence that paid opportunities exist rather than an expected stipend for every student.

For engineering students, project-based learning and industrial exposure matter because recruiters assess demonstrable technical work. For MBA students, internships should be judged by function—finance, marketing, analytics, consulting or operations—not only by stipend.

Placement Reading by Programme

MBAFocus on role function and recruiter quality—finance, marketing, consulting, operations and analytics—rather than the university maximum.
BCA / MCALook for software, application, IT services and data roles. Programme pages show examples from Accenture, TCS, EY India, Vinove Software and CodefyLabs.
BBAEvaluate internship-to-job pathways and whether recruiters offer roles aligned with business, marketing, finance or analytics interests.

Placement ROI: Compare Salary Against the Full Education Cost

For a residential programme, ROI should include tuition plus hostel and living expenses. B.Tech CSE currently lists ₹4.87 lakh annual tuition, while the 2026–27 double-occupancy hostel total including refundable security is ₹2.08 lakh.

General MBA tuition is ₹5.10 lakh per year and MBA Business Analytics is ₹5.73 lakh. Scholarships can materially reduce net cost where the student actually qualifies.

MUJ Placement FAQs

What is the average package at MUJ?

The admissions page currently publishes ₹9.0 lakh average compensation as a university-level figure. It should not automatically be treated as the average for a specific programme.

What is the highest package at MUJ?

The current university-level headline is ₹1.06 crore maximum compensation. Maximum compensation is an outlier metric, not an expected salary.

How many companies visit MUJ?

MUJ currently states that 200 companies visited campus and 50 Fortune 500 companies visited.

Does MUJ offer paid internships?

The official placement statistics page states that paid internships can carry monthly stipends of ₹15,000 to ₹1,25,000.
