Chitkara University Punjab: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Chitkara University, Punjab is a private university on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway near Rajpura, Patiala. Its portfolio spans engineering, computing, business, health sciences, pharmacy, design, law, hospitality, media and other applied disciplines.

The important point for a student is that Chitkara should not be evaluated only at university level. Admission rules, fees, curriculum, internships and placement outcomes can change significantly by programme. This guide therefore gives the university-wide context first and links to dedicated course pages for the highest-intent choices.

Is Chitkara University good?

Chitkara University Admission 2026

There is no single admission rule for the whole university. Engineering, computing, management, health and other schools can use different eligibility and selection criteria.

QuestionWhat the student should know
Where do I apply?Through the official Chitkara admissions portal.
Is one entrance exam compulsory?No. The route is programme-specific. Engineering uses JEE Main 2026 or a Class 12 marks route.
Application feeThe current portal lists ₹1,100 for other programmes and ₹2,100 for Global/Doctorate programmes.

Who Should Apply to Chitkara University?

Potentially strong fit

  • You want a multidisciplinary private university with many specialisations.
  • You value practical learning, industry exposure and structured career support.
  • You are interested in international exchange or 2+2 pathway options.
  • You are willing to evaluate the exact course rather than the university headline.

Do not skip these checks

  • Exact 2026 fee and additional ETS/CAS charges.
  • Programme-specific eligibility and selection process.
  • Course-level placement outcomes and median salary.
  • Hostel cost and total annual living expense.

Chitkara University FAQs Students Actually Ask

Is Chitkara University in Chandigarh?

The Punjab campus is near Rajpura on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway. Chitkara also has an information centre in Chandigarh.

What is Chitkara University's NIRF ranking?

Chitkara's 2026 materials cite NIRF 2025 rank 78 in the University category and rank 16 in Pharmacy.

Does Chitkara accept JEE Main?

Yes. The 2026 engineering admission document provides a JEE Main route alongside a Class 12 marks route.

What is the Chitkara University placement package?

There is no single package that represents every student. The NIRF median for four-year UG programmes rose from ₹8.00 LPA in 2021-22 to ₹8.50 LPA in 2023-24, while two-year PG median rose from ₹7.50 LPA to ₹7.75 LPA over the same period.

Is Chitkara University private?

Yes. Chitkara University, Punjab is a private university established under the Chitkara University Act.
