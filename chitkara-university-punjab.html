Chitkara University Punjab: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Chitkara University, Punjab is a private university established under the Chitkara University Act and located on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway near Rajpura, Patiala. The Punjab campus offers multidisciplinary undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, business, computing, health sciences, pharmacy, design, law, hospitality and other areas.

Is Chitkara University good?

It can be a strong shortlist for students who want a private university with a broad programme portfolio, industry-oriented curriculum, global pathways and an established campus placement ecosystem. The more useful decision, however, is course-specific: compare the exact fee, admission route, curriculum, internships and placement outcomes before applying.

Chitkara University Admission 2026

Chitkara's 2026 admissions are programme-specific. The university's application portal is active, and its published fee/admission documents set different eligibility and selection routes across schools.

Student questionShort answer
Where do I apply?Through the official Chitkara admissions portal; the application requires the qualifying-examination name/details and an applicable application fee.
Is there one entrance test?No. The route varies by programme. For engineering, the 2026 rules use JEE Main or Class 12 marks; other programmes can use merit, interviews or programme-specific criteria.
What should I check first?Eligibility, admission route, programme fee, scholarship conditions and the current counselling/application schedule.

Chitkara University Fees: Do Not Use One Generic Number

Chitkara's official 2026 fee documents show substantial variation by programme. For example, the published 2026 MBA fee ranges from ₹1.65 lakh to ₹2.25 lakh per semester for some Chitkara Business School MBA tracks, while the BCA page lists ₹70,000 for each of the first two semesters including ETS/CAS and ₹65,000 for later semesters. Engineering has its own 2026 fee structure.

Because fees can include programme fee, Educational Technology Services (ETS) and Career Advancement Services (CAS), students should use the exact course document rather than a single “Chitkara University fees” figure.

Chitkara University Placements

Chitkara describes its placement ecosystem as supported by industry partnerships, Career Advancement Services and a large recruiter network. University materials also state that more than 2,000 recruiters visit annually; placement outcomes vary significantly by course and graduating batch.

What is the Chitkara placement package?

There is no single package that represents every Chitkara student. Use course-specific placement information, recruiter profiles, internships and the latest batch data rather than treating a highest-package claim as the expected salary.

Chitkara University Campus & Student Experience

The Punjab campus is on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway and is positioned roughly 30 km from Chandigarh according to Chitkara's institutional material. The university offers residential facilities, student clubs, sports, laboratories, innovation facilities and global-exposure opportunities.

Students considering residential study should separately evaluate room type, hostel rules, food, transport, campus access and current charges rather than relying on general campus descriptions.

Chitkara University FAQs

Is Chitkara University in Chandigarh?

The Punjab campus is on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway near Rajpura, Patiala. Chitkara also maintains an information centre in Chandigarh.

What is Chitkara University's NIRF ranking?

Chitkara's 2026 materials cite an All India Rank of 78 in the NIRF 2025 University category.

Does Chitkara accept JEE Main?

Yes. The 2026 engineering admission document provides a JEE Main-2026 route alongside a Class 12 marks route.

Is Chitkara University private?

Yes. Chitkara University, Punjab is a private university established under the Chitkara University Act.

Is Chitkara University good for placements?

It has an established campus recruitment ecosystem, but students should compare the latest course-specific outcomes, internship exposure and recruiter mix before judging placement value.
