TAPMI MBA Core: What the Programme Is Actually Designed For

TAPMI's MBA Core is its flagship general-management programme. The 2026-28 prospectus describes it as a two-year programme across six trimesters, with Year 1 building foundations in strategy, marketing, operations, HR, accounting, finance, economics, information systems and analytics, followed by more specialised and experiential learning.

The programme is aimed at students who want broad management capability rather than a narrow first-job specialisation. TAPMI also uses cross-functional experiential courses including BrandScan, SEVA, SMIC, SAMNIDHY, Integrative Thinking Simulation and OMEGA.

Who should choose TAPMI MBA Core?

Choose the Core MBA if you want flexibility across consulting, BFSI, IT/analytics, marketing, operations or general management and do not want to lock yourself into HR or finance at the time of admission.

TAPMI MBA Admission 2026-28: Eligibility, Cutoff & Selection

TAPMI requires at least 60% in graduation from a recognised university; final-year candidates can apply subject to maintaining the required graduation score. The 2026 process used CAT 2025, XAT 2026 and GMAT scores for the general admission route, with profile-based shortlisting alongside the test score.

Entrance testsCAT, XAT or GMAT for the 2026 core route

Final weightageEntrance 35%; academics 15%; GD/WAT/PI 45%; gender diversity & extracurriculars 5%

Does TAPMI have a fixed CAT cutoff?

No single permanent cutoff should be treated as a guaranteed-call number. TAPMI states that the entrance cutoff can vary by year and programme depending on the applicant pool and programme suitability. This means students should assess their complete profile rather than targeting an assumed one-number cutoff.

Important distinction:

A cutoff used for shortlisting is not the same as a score that guarantees final admission. TAPMI's final selection gives 45% weight to GD/WAT/PI and also evaluates academics and profile factors.

Scholarships for TAPMI MBA

The Dr. Ramdas M. Pai Scholarship for Excellence in Academics & Practice can provide up to 100% tuition fee waiver for eligible candidates. TAPMI also lists a 20% Scholarship for Achievers for 5% of approved intake across MBA programmes. The actual award depends on the published scholarship policy and candidate eligibility.

TAPMI MBA Curriculum: What You Will Study

The flagship MBA follows a six-term structure. Year 1 establishes broad functional competence; later terms add advanced management learning, electives and experiential work. The 2026-28 handbook describes a summer internship and a broad course basket covering business fundamentals and applied management.

StageLearning focus
Year 1Strategy, marketing, operations, HR, accounting, finance, economics, information systems, analytics and quantitative foundations.
SummerSummer Internship Programme / experiential industry exposure.
Year 2Advanced functional learning, electives and cross-functional application.
Experiential coursesBrandScan, SEVA, SMIC, SAMNIDHY, Integrative Thinking Simulation and OMEGA.
Why the curriculum matters for placements:

The Core MBA keeps multiple career paths open, but your eventual placement role will depend on your electives, internship, skill profile and recruiter demand. Do not assume the general-management label itself guarantees consulting or finance roles.

TAPMI Manipal Campus, Hostel & Student Experience

TAPMI's Manipal campus is residential. The institute describes five hostel blocks with attached bathrooms, 24-hour hot water, Wi-Fi, lifts and backup power. Men's and women's hostels have air-conditioned single and double rooms.

The Manipal location gives the programme a university-town environment. For students who value campus interaction and a contained residential cohort, this can be an advantage. Students who want a metro-city ecosystem should compare that factor before accepting an offer.

International exposure

TAPMI's internationalisation initiatives include student and faculty exchange partnerships. The institute lists universities such as the University of Akron, Montpellier Business School and Loughborough University, with participation subject to nomination and programme arrangements.

Is TAPMI MBA Core a Good Fit for You?

Good fit

  • You want broad management exposure before specialising.
  • You are considering consulting, BFSI, IT/analytics, marketing or general management.
  • You prefer a residential university-town environment.
  • You want experiential courses and industry exposure built into the curriculum.

Think twice

  • You need a metro-city campus for daily corporate access.
  • You are choosing purely because of the highest package.
  • You need a highly specialised programme such as pure finance or HR.

TAPMI MBA Core FAQs

What is TAPMI MBA fees for 2026-28?

The published tuition is ₹9.75 lakh per year, or ₹19.50 lakh for two years. Hostel and mess charges are currently listed as to be updated.

What is the TAPMI MBA CAT cutoff?

TAPMI does not publish one permanent universal cutoff for the Core MBA. Shortlisting depends on the valid entrance score and the overall profile, and the cutoff can vary by year.

Does TAPMI MBA accept XAT?

Yes. XAT was accepted for the 2026-28 admission cycle along with CAT and GMAT.

Is TAPMI MBA residential?

Yes. The Manipal MBA is conducted in a residential campus environment with institute hostel facilities.

What is the latest TAPMI MBA average package?

The 2025 MBA Core placement report recorded an average CTC of ₹13.99 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹32.77 LPA.
