What is Christ University?

Quick answer

Christ University is a multi-campus deemed university with major study options in management, commerce, computer applications, engineering, law, sciences and humanities. For 2026 admissions, the university publishes programme-specific eligibility, fees and selection rules rather than one common admission test for every course.

The university's official admission material separates undergraduate entrance-test programmes, non-entrance programmes and MBA admissions. This matters because a student applying for BBA or BCA may face a different process from a student applying for MBA.

The safest way to compare Christ University is programme by programme: first check the campus, then eligibility, fee, entrance/selection route and the programme-specific deadline.

What Is the Eligibility for Christ University?

Eligibility is course-specific. The university's central eligibility page states that BBA/BCA/BHM and several other UG programmes accept a pass in Class 12, while BTech requires Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with the prescribed marks conditions. BA LLB and BBA LLB require at least 45% in Class 12. PG programmes generally require a recognised bachelor's degree, commonly with 50% aggregate.

For UG applicants

  • Check Class 12 subjects before applying.
  • Do not assume a general "pass in +2" rule applies to specialised courses.
  • Check whether the programme is ET or non-ET.

For MBA applicants

  • Minimum 50% in the UG degree.
  • A valid accepted test score is required for the 2026 MBA route.
  • The test score is an eligibility condition; Christ states it does not itself determine the final selection.

What Is the Christ University Selection Criteria?

For many UG entrance-test programmes, the official 2026 selection document lists Entrance Test + Skill Assessment + Micro Presentation + Personal Interview. BBA and BCom programmes are included in the published Bangalore Central ET list.

For MBA, Christ lists GD, MP, Written Assessment, PI and academic performance. The university also states that the minimum test score is an eligibility requirement and that the actual test score has no bearing on the selection process.

What Is the Christ University Cutoff?

There is no single Christ University cutoff because admission rules are different for each programme. A student applying for MBA should look at the accepted entrance-test score first, while UG applicants should check the academic eligibility and the selection process for the exact course.

For MBA, the 2026 eligibility thresholds were CAT 60 percentile, XAT 60 percentile, CMAT 60 percentile, ATMA 60 percentile, MAT 600, GMAT Focus 450 and GRE 295. These numbers tell you whether you meet the minimum test-score requirement; they do not mean that a student at that score will receive an offer.

For BBA, BCA, BCom, BTech and other UG programmes, Christ does not provide one university-wide closing cutoff. Your relevant benchmark is the eligibility requirement plus the programme's entrance test and selection stages. If you are checking a specific course, use its dedicated page for the applicable admission criteria.

What Are Christ University Placements Like?

Christ's official 2024-25 annual report states that the MBA programme had 327 participating companies, 911 offers among 946 eligible students, a 99% placement rate, ₹10.7 lakh average salary and ₹33 lakh highest domestic package.

These are MBA-specific figures, not a university-wide average. The dedicated placement page separates MBA and other programme-level information.

Christ University FAQs

Does Christ University have one entrance exam for every course?

No. The 2026 admission structure separates entrance-test and non-entrance programmes, while MBA has its own accepted-test and selection process.

Is CAT enough for Christ University MBA admission?

No. A valid CAT score meeting the stated eligibility threshold is only the test-score requirement. The MBA selection process also includes GD, Micro Presentation, Written Assessment, Personal Interview and academic-performance assessment.

What is the minimum CAT percentile mentioned by Christ for MBA eligibility?

For the 2026 MBA route, Christ specifies CAT 60 percentile as an eligibility threshold. It is not a final selection cutoff.

Can a student from any Class 12 stream apply for BCA?

The central eligibility page lists BCA as requiring a pass in Class 12. Individual programme/campus pages should still be checked for the exact 2026 intake and selection route.

Does Christ University publish course-wise fees?

Yes. Fees are published on individual programme pages and can vary by campus, category and programme.

Are Christ University placement figures university-wide?

Not always. The 2024-25 annual report figure of 99% placement, ₹10.7 lakh average and ₹33 lakh highest domestic package is specifically reported for the MBA programme.
