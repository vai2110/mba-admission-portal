What does XIME Bangalore offer in 2027–29?

XIME's admissions portal lists a Bangalore PGDM intake of 240 and a programme fee of ₹13,50,000 for 2027–29. XIME also offers programmes in Kochi and Chennai; do not apply Bangalore-specific fee figures to those campuses.

The admissions page highlights ₹10 lakh average CTC, ₹9 lakh median CTC and ₹20.5 lakh highest CTC as current outcome indicators. Check the associated batch and placement notes before using them for ROI calculations.

Who is eligible to apply?

The current admissions portal lists a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate, with 45% for SC/ST candidates. It accepts applicants from any discipline subject to detailed programme rules. The portal states that candidates with uncleared backlogs are not eligible.

For 2027–29, the portal says the degree must be completed before 25 June 2027 and proof of the final result should be available by 30 September 2027. Confirm these dates on the live portal before relying on them.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

The portal lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA and GMAT for Bangalore PGDM. KMAT is listed for Kochi only and TANCET for Chennai only. Campus-specific exam acceptance matters.

  1. Select Bangalore as your programme preference.
  2. Confirm score validity and accepted exam.
  3. Complete online registration, mobile verification and the application form.
  4. Pay the application fee and monitor the GDPI phase.
  5. Review the offer and fee schedule before accepting.

How should you compare fees and placements?

Use the ₹13.50 lakh 2027–29 programme fee plus accommodation and living expenses as the starting budget. For ROI, compare total cost with median CTC and actual job roles. CTC is not take-home pay, and the highest package is not the expected salary for every student.
