What is BIT Mesra Ranchi known for?

Quick answer:

Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra is a private deemed university founded in 1955 at Mesra near Ranchi. It offers engineering, technology, management, pharmacy, architecture, science and other programmes.

For students, BIT Mesra is particularly known for its engineering and technology ecosystem, residential campus and research orientation. Its undergraduate B.Tech portfolio includes CSE and AI & ML among other engineering branches.

BIT Mesra FAQs

Is BIT Mesra a government college?

No. BIT Mesra is a private deemed university, established in 1955 and located at Mesra, Ranchi.

Is BIT Mesra good for B.Tech?

It is a well-established engineering and technology institute. The decision should be based on branch, JEE Main rank, total cost and branch-level placement outcomes.

Does BIT Mesra accept JEE Main?

Yes. JEE Main is the principal entrance route for several undergraduate engineering admissions, with the exact counselling and seat-allocation process determined by the current admission notification.

What is the BIT Mesra CSE cutoff?

The 2025 General All India final reported closing rank was 23,596 in the published round-wise dataset. The cutoff changes by year, round, category and quota.
