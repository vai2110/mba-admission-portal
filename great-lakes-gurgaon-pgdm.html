Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) is Great Lakes Gurgaon's two-year full-time management programme for freshers and early-career professionals. The institute describes it as a programme for candidates with up to three years of work experience.

The first-year curriculum is common to students, followed by specialisation exposure before the second year. The programme also includes a summer internship, projects, mentoring and industry interaction.

Candidates with 2–3 years of work experience may apply for both PGPM and PGDM, with programme fit considered during evaluation.

Miscellaneous Charges and Estimated Additional Cost

The ₹19.82 lakh published fee should not be treated as the complete personal budget. Great Lakes excludes personal laptop expenses and personal travel/miscellaneous expenses. Non-vegetarian food is optional and charged extra. Certain outsourced housing or catering services may also involve separate charges.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM?

The first year builds management fundamentals before students move toward specialisation exposure in the second year. Great Lakes also highlights summer internships, projects, mentoring and industry interaction.

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Summer internship
  • Projects and live business exposure
  • Specialisation choice
  • CXO and industry interactions

Specialisation decision

Do not choose the programme only by the headline salary. Your internship and second-year specialisation can shape the roles you are prepared to target.

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM FAQs

What is the PGDM duration?

Two years, full-time.

What is the PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 total fee is ₹19.82 lakh.

What extra costs should students budget for?

A laptop, personal travel and miscellaneous expenses are outside the published fee. Non-vegetarian meals are optional and extra.

Are 2027 admission dates official?

No. The 2027 dates shown on this page are clearly marked tentative because Great Lakes has not yet published the complete 2027-29 calendar.
