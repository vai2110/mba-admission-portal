Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) is Great Lakes Gurgaon's two-year full-time management programme for freshers and early-career professionals. The institute describes it as a programme for candidates with up to three years of work experience.

The first-year curriculum is common to students, after which students get the opportunity to choose a specialisation before the second year. The programme also includes a summer internship and extensive projects. citeturn0search6

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM Admission Process

1. Take an accepted management entrance test

Great Lakes accepts GMAT, CAT and XAT scores for the PGDM route under the published eligibility rules. citeturn0search1

2. Apply online

The application is submitted online through the Great Lakes website. The current published application fee is ₹2,200.

3. Application screening

Great Lakes evaluates standardised test scores, academic consistency, work experience and overall profile.

4. AI-proctored interview and PI

The admissions process includes an AI-proctored video interview followed by a personal interview. Candidates are assessed on communication, thought process, awareness, goals and other personal and professional attributes. citeturn0search1

5. Final selection

Offers are made after the holistic evaluation and programme-fit assessment.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM?

The first year is common across students, giving them time to build management fundamentals before choosing a specialisation for the second year. Great Lakes also highlights summer internships, projects, personal mentoring and industry interaction.

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Summer internship
  • Projects and live business exposure
  • Specialisation choice before second year
  • CXO and industry interactions

Specialisation decision

Do not choose the programme only by the headline salary. Your second-year specialisation and internship can materially shape the roles you are prepared to target.

Who Should Choose Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM?
