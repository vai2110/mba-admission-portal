Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) is Great Lakes Gurgaon's two-year full-time management programme for freshers and early-career professionals. The programme is designed for candidates with less than three years of work experience and is approved by AICTE.

The first year builds common management fundamentals, followed by specialisation exposure before the second year. The programme also includes a summer internship, projects, mentoring and industry interaction.

Student takeaway

Choose PGDM when you want a two-year management journey rather than the accelerated one-year PGPM. The longer format gives you more time for internship, projects, specialisation and placement preparation.

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM Admission Process

Step 1: Take an accepted entrance test

Great Lakes accepts GMAT, CAT or XAT for the PGDM under the published admission rules. CAT and XAT are used for screening applications; the institute conducts its own admissions process.

Step 2: Submit the online application

Eligible candidates apply online through the Great Lakes website. The currently published application fee is ₹2,200.

Step 3: Application screening

The institute evaluates entrance scores, academic performance, work experience and the overall profile. College or university rank holders may receive additional weightage under the published criteria.

Step 4: AI-proctored interview

Shortlisted applicants complete an AI-proctored video interview that evaluates thought process and communication.

Step 5: Personal Interview

The personal interview considers areas such as general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, people skills, communication and personal, extracurricular or professional initiatives.

Step 6: Offer, verification and enrolment

Selected candidates receive an admission decision and then complete document verification, fee payment and enrolment as instructed by the institute.

Documents to keep ready

Academic details and transcripts, valid entrance-test information, personal profile details and one recommendation with the recommender's email ID are listed by Great Lakes for the application. Additional documents may be requested later.

What does the fee include?

Tuition includes reading material such as case studies, textbooks and online resources. The residential programme includes mandatory twin-sharing AC accommodation and vegetarian meals. Non-vegetarian food is optional and charged extra.

What is not included?

The institute specifically identifies personal laptop expenses and personal travel and miscellaneous expenses as excluded. Some non-academic housing or catering services may also be outsourced and charged through the relevant service providers.

Do not budget only ₹19.82 lakh.

₹19.82 lakh is the published programme fee. A student should keep a separate personal budget for laptop, travel, stationery and optional expenses.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM?

The PGDM combines management fundamentals with experiential learning, industry engagement, projects and a summer internship. The programme is structured so students build a common foundation before moving toward specialisation exposure.

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Summer internship
  • Projects and live business exposure
  • Specialisation exposure
  • CXO and industry interactions
  • Optional global learning opportunities

Career decision

Use the two-year format to test career hypotheses through coursework, internship and projects before locking into a target role.

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGDM FAQs

What is the PGDM duration?

Two years, full-time and residential.

What is the PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 total fee is ₹19.82 lakh.

What entrance exams are accepted?

GMAT, CAT and XAT are accepted under the published PGDM rules.

Can freshers apply?

Yes. Freshers and candidates with strictly less than 36 months of work experience can apply under the published criteria.

Is there a GD round?

The current official FAQ describes the selection process as an AI interview followed by a personal interview and states that there is no Group Discussion round.

Are 2027 admission dates official?

No. The 2027 dates on this page are clearly marked tentative because Great Lakes has not published the complete 2027-29 calendar.
