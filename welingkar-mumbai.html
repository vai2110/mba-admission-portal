Welingkar Mumbai: What Students Need to Know

Prin. L. N. Welingkar Institute of Management Development & Research, popularly known as WeSchool, is a management institute in Matunga, Mumbai. The Mumbai campus offers the flagship two-year PGDM alongside specialised programmes including e-Business, Business Design, Healthcare, Retail, Rural, Research & Business Analytics and Media & Entertainment. It also offers the Maharashtra-regulated MMS programme.

Is Welingkar Mumbai a good B-school?

It can be a strong option for students who value Mumbai's location, specialised PGDM choices and a relatively broad management curriculum. The key decision is programme-specific: compare the relevant placement numbers, total cost, role mix and your alternative calls rather than using one campus-wide highest package.

Welingkar Mumbai Campus & Student Life

The Matunga campus is centrally located in Mumbai and benefits from the city's business ecosystem. The institute's Learning Resource Centre is spread over 7,000 sq ft and provides access to more than 110,000 books/e-books and over 17,000 e-journals through online databases.

Why Mumbai matters

  • Strong access to BFSI, consulting, media, retail and corporate ecosystems.
  • Central Matunga location.
  • Multiple specialised management programmes.

What to evaluate

  • Programme-specific placement outcomes can differ materially.
  • Total cost includes living/hostel expenses.
  • Highest salary should not be treated as typical salary.

Is Welingkar Mumbai Worth It?

Welingkar Mumbai is most compelling when you use its programme diversity strategically. The flagship PGDM offers broad management depth, while programmes such as E-Business, Business Design and Research & Business Analytics are useful for students with a more defined career direction.

The strongest comparison should be programme-specific: fee after scholarships, average/median salary, role quality, location, batch size and your alternative calls.

Welingkar Mumbai FAQs

What is Welingkar Mumbai PGDM fee?

The 2026-28 PGDM brochure lists ₹7 lakh per year, subject to change.

What exams does Welingkar accept for PGDM?

CAT 2025, XAT 2026, GMAT from 2023 onward, ATMA 2026 and CMAT 2026 are listed in the 2026-28 brochure.

What is the highest package at Welingkar Mumbai?

The 2026 brochure reports ₹40 LPA maximum salary for PGDM batch 2023-25.

Does Welingkar offer MMS?

Yes. Welingkar Mumbai also offers the two-year full-time MMS programme under the Maharashtra admission framework.

Which Welingkar programme should I choose?

Choose flagship PGDM for broad management, E-Business for digital/technology-oriented business, Business Design for innovation/design, and Research & Business Analytics for analytics/research-focused careers.
