Who should choose MBA-DBAI?

This programme is for a graduate who wants management education but expects technology, data and digital business models to be part of everyday decision-making. It is not a substitute for a computer-science degree; the value lies in connecting business problems with digital and AI capabilities.

Strong fit

Students targeting digital transformation, business analytics, product, fintech, digital marketing or technology consulting.

Not a coding-first degree

The programme is management-led. Choose engineering or a technical master's if your primary goal is deep software or algorithm development.

Best advantage

You learn to frame technology opportunities in business terms and business problems in technology terms.

What is the eligibility and admission route?

The 2026-28 official admission policy lists a recognised undergraduate degree with 50% marks, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. It publishes 90 seats and uses CAT followed by the CAP 2026 personal-interview process and profile evaluation.

2026 admission pointWhat the policy says
Academic eligibilityUG with 50%; 45% for SC/ST/PwD.
Seats90.
TestCAT 2025.
Interview routeCAP 2026 PI process; the policy notes offline mode and coordination by the participating IIMs.
Cutoff caution

The policy publishes category-wise CAP reference cutoffs and notes that the final cutoff is subject to the CAT result and actual candidate pool. Treat a reference cutoff as a screening benchmark, not as a guaranteed admission score.

What will you study?

The programme is designed around the intersection of management and new-age business capabilities. IIM Bodh Gaya describes it as a two-year full-time residential MBA in Digital Business Management, with domain-oriented learning in areas such as analytics, fintech, digital marketing and management consulting.

Learning layerWhat it should help you do
Management foundationsUnderstand finance, marketing, operations, strategy and organisational decisions.
Digital businessAnalyse platforms, digital products, transformation and technology-enabled business models.
AnalyticsUse data to support managerial decisions and evaluate business performance.
AI orientationUnderstand where AI can create value, change workflows and alter competitive strategy.

Which career tracks can this programme support?

Business analytics

Suitable for candidates who enjoy quantitative problem solving and want to work with data in a commercial context.

Fintech

Useful for students interested in the intersection of financial services, technology, platforms and digital products.

Digital marketing

Relevant for careers where customer acquisition, digital channels, experimentation and analytics intersect.

Management consulting

Can support transformation and technology-led consulting roles when paired with strong case and communication skills.

Product management

Build the ability to translate customer needs, data and technology constraints into product decisions.

AI-enabled strategy

Focus on business adoption, operating-model change and value creation rather than pure model development.

What is the MBA-DBAI fee?

The official 2026-28 fee document lists a two-year course total of ₹16.70 lakh before refundable caution and mess/laundry deposits. Tuition is ₹1.60 lakh per term, with additional academic, hostel, career-development and other charges in the term schedule.

Budget itemHow to treat it
Tuition₹1.60 lakh per term in the published 2026-28 schedule.
Academic chargesSeparate institute charges included in the published term totals.
HostelSeparate residential cost; include it in total ROI.
Caution deposit₹25,000 published as a refundable deposit.
Mess/laundry depositSeparate deposit; the amount can vary by term/vendor charges.

What career outcomes should you evaluate?

The current institute placement snapshot reports ₹14.80 LPA average CTC and ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC across the consolidated placement ecosystem. For MBA-DBAI, the more important question is which digital, analytics, consulting, fintech or product roles appear in the relevant programme cohort.

Good outcome signal

Recruitment into analytics, product, digital transformation, fintech or technology consulting roles.

What to verify

Role titles, recruiter mix and programme-specific placement evidence for the batch you will join.

Use the placements page for the institute-wide recruiter mix and placement process, but do not automatically apply the consolidated average to every role.
