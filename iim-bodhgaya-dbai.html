Who should choose MBA-DBAI?

This programme suits a graduate who wants management education but expects technology, data and digital business models to be part of everyday decision-making. It is management-led, not a replacement for a computer-science degree.

Strong fit

Students targeting digital transformation, analytics, product, fintech, digital marketing or technology consulting.

Not coding-first

If your primary goal is deep software engineering or algorithm development, a technical degree may be more appropriate.

Core advantage

You learn to connect business problems with digital and AI capabilities and explain technology through a management lens.

What is the 2026-28 eligibility and admission route?

The official 2026-28 policy lists a recognised undergraduate degree with 50% marks, 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates, 90 seats, CAT 2025 and a CAP 2026 PI process.

What will you study?

The programme sits at the intersection of management and new-age business capabilities. Official programme material describes a two-year full-time residential MBA in Digital Business Management with domain-oriented learning around analytics, fintech, digital marketing and management consulting.

Learning layerWhat it should help you do
Management foundationsUnderstand finance, marketing, operations, strategy and organisational decisions.
Digital businessAnalyse platforms, products, transformation and technology-enabled business models.
AnalyticsUse data to support managerial decisions and evaluate performance.
AI orientationUnderstand AI value creation, workflow change and competitive strategy.

What should you look for in projects and electives?

Choose projects that force you to solve a real business problem with data, digital products or technology. The strongest learning value comes when the programme moves beyond terminology and makes you explain measurable business impact.

Which career tracks can MBA-DBAI support?

Business analytics

For candidates who enjoy quantitative problem solving and want to use data in commercial decisions.

Fintech

For careers at the intersection of financial services, platforms and technology.

Digital marketing

For customer acquisition, experimentation, digital channels and marketing analytics.

Consulting

For transformation and technology-led consulting with strong case preparation.

Product management

For translating customer needs, data and technology constraints into product decisions.

AI-enabled strategy

For business adoption, operating-model change and value creation rather than pure model development.

What career outcomes should you evaluate?

The current consolidated placement snapshot reports ₹14.80 LPA average CTC and ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC. For MBA-DBAI, the more important evidence is the role mix: digital transformation, analytics, product, fintech, technology consulting and related business roles.

Good signal

Recruitment into roles where digital, data or technology is central to business value creation.

What to verify

Role titles, recruiter mix and programme-specific placement evidence for your batch.

Use the placements guide for recruiter and process context, but do not automatically apply the consolidated average to every role.

How does MBA-DBAI admission work and what CAT cutoff should you target?

The 2026-28 MBA-DBAI policy requires a recognised undergraduate degree with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD), 90 seats and CAT 2025 followed by CAP 2026 PI. The admission funnel is similar to the flagship MBA, but the programme's profile and career fit should be judged separately.

How should you interpret the CAT cutoff?

The 2026 IIM Bodh Gaya MBA-family EOI cutoff for General-GN was 90 overall with 65 in each CAT section, while the programme admission policies publish a 95 overall/75 sectional reference cutoff for General candidates. For future aspirants, 95+ is a sensible target and 97+ is safer; this is an editorial inference, not an official future cutoff.

Important:

A cutoff gets you into the consideration funnel. It does not tell you whether MBA-DBAI is the right career choice or guarantee an offer.
