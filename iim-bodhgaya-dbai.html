Who is this programme really for?

The MBA in Digital Business and AI is for students who want a management qualification but do not want their career story to remain disconnected from technology-led business change. The programme is positioned around the intersection of management, digital transformation and AI.

Good fit

Students targeting digital transformation, product, analytics, technology consulting, AI-enabled business and strategy roles.

Not a pure technical degree

The programme is management-led. Students should not choose it expecting the depth of a conventional computer science or engineering degree.

Best advantage

The combination can help candidates explain technology in business terms and business problems in technology terms.

What should you expect academically?

The programme's positioning means the student experience should be evaluated around three layers: management fundamentals, digital business understanding and AI/data-enabled decision making.

LayerStudent outcome
ManagementUnderstand strategy, marketing, finance, operations and organisational decisions.
Digital businessUnderstand how digital platforms, products, transformation and technology alter business models.
AI & analyticsDevelop the ability to interpret data and AI opportunities in managerial decision-making.

What does it cost?

The official 2026-28 fee document gives a two-year course total of ₹16.70 lakh for MBA-Digital Business and AI before refundable caution and mess/laundry deposits. The published term schedule includes tuition of ₹1.60 lakh per term, hostel rent, academic charges, career development and other programme charges.

Budget beyond tuition

The fee document separately lists a ₹25,000 caution deposit and mess/laundry deposits; these should not be confused with the academic fee.

Career scope after MBA-DBAI

The programme is best evaluated by role fit rather than by the word “AI” alone. Potential directions include digital strategy, product management, business analytics, transformation consulting, technology consulting and AI-enabled business functions.

Product

Translate customer and business requirements into digital product priorities.

Analytics

Use data to support commercial and strategic decisions, working with analytics teams.

Consulting

Work on technology transformation, digital operating models and AI adoption questions.

The current consolidated placement page reports ₹14.80 LPA average CTC and ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC for the institute's current placement snapshot. Use the latest programme-specific placement report where available.
