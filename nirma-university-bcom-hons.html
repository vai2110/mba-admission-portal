Nirma B.Com (Hons.) Overview

Nirma University's Institute of Commerce offers a four-year, eight-semester B.Com (Hons.) programme. The official programme description highlights accounting and commerce with minor specialisations including BFSI, Economics, Data Analytics and Marketing.

What makes this course different?

It is a four-year honours programme with a broader commerce foundation and opportunities to build domain depth through minors/specialisations.

B.Com (Hons.) Eligibility

The programme is open to students who have passed Class XII/equivalent examination under the university's admission rules. Candidates from non-Gujarat boards may need an eligibility certificate and related documentation.

Nirma B.Com Admission Process

Students apply through the Nirma admission portal, provide qualification details after results are available and complete the required fee payment and document verification. The official programme page provides course-specific merit and application instructions.

B.Com (Hons.) Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

The 2026-27 application form was available from January 16, 2026. The general-category application was later marked closed, with merit declaration listed as TBA.

2027 planning: January-February is a reasonable planning window based on the 2026 cycle, but exact dates should be taken from the 2027 notice.

B.Com Career & Placement Scope

The institute cites recruiters including Bank of America, Federal Bank, Karnataka Bank, KPMG, PwC and EY. Career paths can include accounting, audit, banking, finance, analytics, marketing and business roles, depending on skills and specialisation.

Students targeting CA/CMA/ACCA or higher studies should evaluate the academic structure and professional-exam compatibility alongside placement outcomes.
