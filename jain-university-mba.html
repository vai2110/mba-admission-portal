Jain University MBA: Course Overview

The MBA is offered through CMS Business School, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University). The current admission page describes a two-year, full-time, four-semester MBA at the Sheshadri Road campus in Bengaluru, with contemporary specialisations and industry-oriented learning.

MBA Specialisations at Jain University

The current programme page lists single-specialisation options including Aviation and Aerospace Management, Business Analytics, Digital Business and Marketing, Entrepreneurship and New Venture Creation, and Family Business Management. The university also presents finance, HR, international finance/accounting and other management pathways in its current talent pool.

MBA Eligibility

The university's PG eligibility page states an aggregate of 50% in a bachelor's degree in the respective discipline or equivalent 10+2+3 qualification. Final-semester/year students can also apply subject to the stated rules.

Jain University MBA Admission 2026 & JET

MBA selection includes the JAIN Entrance Test process: written test, essay writing, presentation and personal interview. Candidates with specified valid scores can be exempted from the written test.

Jain University MBA Fees

The current official MBA admission page does not publish a single fee figure on the page and directs applicants to the admissions office for programme fee information. Students should therefore avoid relying on old third-party fee figures when applying for 2026.

What should you budget for?

Ask the admissions office for the current 2026-28 tuition, registration/application charges, hostel if required, scholarships and any additional programme costs. Compare the total two-year cost with the programme's latest placement outcomes.

MBA FAQs

Is CAT compulsory for Jain University MBA?

No. The university accepts several competitive exams and also conducts JET. Candidates meeting the published MAT, NMAT, CAT, GMAT, CMAT or XAT thresholds can be exempted from the written test.

What is the Jain University MBA eligibility?

The university states 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree or equivalent 10+2+3 qualification, subject to programme rules.

What is the Jain University MBA highest package?

The current CMS Business School MBA page reports a highest package of ₹24.7 lakh per annum.

What is the average MBA package at Jain University?

The current MBA page reports an average package of ₹8.66 LPA and ₹12.89 LPA as the average package for the top 100 offers.

Where is the Jain University MBA campus?

The current MBA admission page lists Sheshadri Road, Bengaluru as the campus.
