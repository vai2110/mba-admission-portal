What Does the Three-year Salary Trend Tell Students?

  • Highest CTC increased to ₹43.55 LPA in 2023–25, above ₹40.50 LPA in 2022–24 and ₹41.13 LPA in 2021–23.
  • Average CTC decreased across the three reported batches: ₹25.82 LPA in 2021–23, ₹23.40 LPA in 2022–24 and ₹22.53 LPA in 2023–25.
  • Median CTC moved from ₹24.45 LPA to ₹22.05 LPA, then to ₹22.51 LPA. The latest median is slightly above the previous batch's median, but below the 2021–23 figure.
  • Placement rate was 100% for the 2021–23 and 2022–24 batches, and 98% for 2023–25. Do not read the earlier rates as a guarantee for future batches.

For a college decision, compare the salary trend with programme fees, your likely target domain, and the roles you want. These reports do not provide a complete distribution of salaries by role or individual profile, so they cannot predict what a particular student will earn.

Average CTC vs Median CTC vs Highest Package: What Should You Compare?

  • Average CTC: total reported CTC divided by the number of relevant offers/students under the institute's calculation method. A few high offers can pull the average upward.
  • Median CTC: the midpoint of the reported salary distribution. It is often useful alongside the average when estimating a more typical outcome.
  • Highest CTC: the single highest reported package. It does not tell you how many students received offers near that level.
  • Placement rate: the rate reported for that graduating batch. The public summary does not provide a complete breakdown of students seeking placements, opting out, or role-level outcomes.
CTC may include components that are not paid as monthly fixed salary. Before comparing offers, check fixed pay, variable pay, joining bonus, stock/equity, benefits and location. The three reports do not provide enough information to calculate take-home pay for each domain.

FAQs on IIT Delhi DMS MBA Placements

1. What is the average package at IIT Delhi DMS for the latest reported MBA batch?

For the MBA Class of 2023–25, the reported average CTC was ₹22.53 LPA. The same report lists a median CTC of ₹22.51 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹43.55 LPA. These figures describe that graduating batch, not a guaranteed offer for incoming students.

2. What is the median package at DMS IIT Delhi?

The latest report (Class of 2023–25) records a median CTC of ₹22.51 LPA. The median is the midpoint of the reported salary distribution, so it is useful to consider alongside the average and highest CTC. It still does not show the full spread of individual offers.

3. What is the highest package offered at IIT Delhi DMS?

The highest CTC reported for the MBA Class of 2023–25 was ₹43.55 LPA. The preceding batches reported ₹40.5 LPA (2022–24) and ₹41.13 LPA (2021–23). The highest package is one outcome and should not be treated as the expected salary for all graduates.

4. What was the placement rate for the latest DMS MBA batch?

DMS reported a 98% placement rate for 2023–25. The 2022–24 and 2021–23 reports each recorded 100%. Placement rates are historical outcomes; they do not guarantee the same result for future batches.

5. Which domain had the largest share of placements in 2023–25?

Strategy & Consulting had the largest reported share at 30.07%, followed by IT at 23.78% and Sales & Marketing at 19.58%. The report also lists Finance at 10.49%, HR & General Management at 8.39%, and Operations & Supply Chain at 7.69%. These percentages show the placement mix, not domain-wise salary or the likelihood that a particular applicant will be hired.

6. Which companies recruit from IIT Delhi DMS MBA?

Across the supplied reports, recruiter examples include Bain & Company, McKinsey & Company, Amazon, Tata Consumer Products, Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, BlackRock, Barclays, EXL Service, Wipro, Capgemini, Paytm, Yes Bank and ICICI Bank, among others. The 2023–25 report names Bain & Company, McKinsey & Company, Amazon and Tata Consumer Products among its regular recruiters. Recruiter participation can change by year.

7. What jobs can students get after an MBA from DMS IIT Delhi?

The placement reports mention roles such as Associate Consultant, Strategy and Management Consultant, Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Marketing Analyst, Product Specialist, Product Manager, B2B Sales Consultant, Finance Associate, Wealth Management, Supply Chain Management Trainee and General Management Trainee. Actual roles depend on the openings, recruiter requirements and the candidate's skills and selection performance.

8. Does DMS IIT Delhi offer analytics and product management placements?

Yes, the supplied placement reports list analytics and product-related roles. Examples include Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Marketing Analyst and Product Specialist under IT & Analytics, as well as Product Manager under Sales & Marketing. The reports do not publish a separate average CTC for analytics or product roles, so a domain-specific salary cannot be inferred from the overall batch average.

9. Are the placement packages fixed salary or take-home salary?

The reports publish CTC (cost to company), not monthly take-home salary. CTC can include fixed and variable components, bonuses, benefits or equity depending on the employer. Students should review the individual offer's fixed pay, variable pay and other components before estimating take-home income.

10. Can I expect the same package if I join DMS IIT Delhi?

No report can guarantee an individual's future salary. Your outcome can depend on the hiring market, role availability, previous experience, skills, interview performance and recruiter-specific criteria. Use the three-year data as context, compare median and average CTC, and evaluate the roles available in your intended domain rather than relying only on the highest package.

11. Does the placement report publish domain-wise salary packages?

The supplied reports publish domain-wise placement shares and examples of roles and recruiters, but they do not provide a complete domain-wise salary table. It would therefore be misleading to assign a separate average package to consulting, finance, analytics or marketing without an official figure.

12. Where can I verify the official placement data?

Use the official DMS IIT Delhi placement report archive and check the graduating batch printed on the report cover. The page links the archive and the three supplied reports so that you can compare the underlying statistics directly.
