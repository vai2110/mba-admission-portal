Summer Placements — Separate from Final Placements

DMS maintains a separate summer-placement archive. The 2023–25 summer report lists an average stipend of ₹2.10 lakh and a highest stipend of ₹5.24 lakh. These are internship figures and are not final-placement CTC.

Recruiter & Role Context

DMS reports roles across consulting, IT/analytics, finance, sales and marketing, strategy, operations/supply chain and general management. The Corporate Relations & Placement Team coordinates final placements alongside summer, winter and corporate-engagement activities.
