IIT Delhi DMS Placements 2026: Latest MBA Placement Statistics

The latest reported placement data for the IIT Delhi Department of Management Studies (DMS) MBA Class of 2023–25 records an average CTC of ₹22.52 LPA, median CTC of ₹22.50 LPA and highest CTC of ₹43.55 LPA. The reported placement outcome for the cycle was 98%.

IIT Delhi DMS also publishes separate reports for MBA final placements and summer internships. For the MBA Class of 2023–25 summer placement cycle, the official report recorded an average two-month internship stipend of ₹2.43 lakh and a highest stipend of ₹5.24 lakh.

IIT Delhi DMS Placements FAQs: MBA Package, Recruiters and Summer Placements

What is the latest average package at IIT Delhi DMS for MBA?

The latest reported MBA placement cycle for the IIT Delhi DMS Class of 2023–25 recorded an average CTC of ₹22.52 LPA. The reported median CTC for the same cycle was ₹22.50 LPA.

What is the highest package offered at IIT Delhi DMS?

The latest reported MBA placement data for the 2023–25 cycle records a highest CTC of ₹43.55 LPA.

What is the median package at IIT Delhi DMS?

The latest reported median MBA package at IIT Delhi DMS is ₹22.50 LPA for the 2023–25 placement cycle.

Which MBA roles have the highest placement share at IIT Delhi DMS?

In the latest reported placement distribution, Strategy & Consulting accounted for the largest share at 30.07%, followed by IT & Analytics at 23.78% and Sales & Marketing at 19.58%.

Which companies recruit MBA students from IIT Delhi DMS?

Companies mentioned in official DMS placement reporting include Bain & Company, McKinsey & Company, Reckitt, GE Healthcare, Medtronic, Tata Consumer Products and Synergy Consulting. The summer placement report also mentioned Myntra, GE Vernova and HSBC as new recruiters.

What is the IIT Delhi DMS summer internship stipend?

For the MBA Class of 2023–25, IIT Delhi DMS reported an average two-month summer internship stipend of ₹2.43 lakh and a highest stipend of ₹5.24 lakh.

How have IIT Delhi DMS MBA placement packages changed over the years?

Recent reported placement data shows an average CTC of ₹25.82 LPA for the 2021–23 cycle, ₹23.40 LPA for the 2022–24 cycle and ₹22.52 LPA for the latest reported 2023–25 cycle. The highest CTC increased to ₹43.55 LPA in the latest reported cycle.
