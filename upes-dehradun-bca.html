UPES BCA Overview

The three-year BCA at UPES is offered through the School of Computer Science and covers programming, data structures, web technologies, databases and emerging computing areas. The programme can be a pathway to software and IT roles or further study such as MCA.

UPES BCA Eligibility

UPES lists 50% marks in Class X and XII with Mathematics, Computer Science or Information Technology as one of the major subjects in Class XII.

UPES BCA Fees 2026

A current 2026 listing reports approximately ₹7.16 lakh for the three-year BCA, with year-wise fees of about ₹2.84 lakh, ₹2.09 lakh and ₹2.24 lakh. Another current fee source lists tuition-only figures differently, so students should verify the final payable amount against the official 2026-27 fee structure.

UPES BCA Admission 2026

UPES lists UPESEAT, CUET and board merit among BCA selection routes. Admission can also be subject to programme-specific entrance or interview requirements.

BCA Career Scope

SoftwareDeveloper and application roles
DataEntry-level analytics and database roles
ITSupport, cloud and operations pathways

Students planning a technical career should build projects, coding skills, internships and relevant certifications alongside the degree.

UPES BCA Placements

UPES placement reporting is generally school- and programme-dependent. The School of Computer Science publishes separate B.Tech and school-level figures. Students should ask for BCA-specific recruiter and role data rather than assuming B.Tech CSE outcomes apply directly to BCA.

UPES BCA FAQs

What is BCA eligibility at UPES?

50% in Class X and XII with Mathematics, Computer Science or IT as a major subject in Class XII.

What is the BCA fee?

Current listings indicate around ₹7.16 lakh for three years, but the official 2026-27 fee structure should be checked before payment.

Can BCA students pursue MCA?

Yes. A BCA can provide a foundation for MCA and other postgraduate computing programmes, subject to the eligibility rules of the institution selected.
