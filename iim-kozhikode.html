IIM Kozhikode MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility, CAT Cutoff & Selection

For the 2026–28 PGP cycle, IIM Kozhikode uses a three-stage process. The official policy makes it clear that CAT is the entry point, but the eventual shortlist and final merit list are based on multiple factors.

What the cutoff actually means

These are minimum eligibility thresholds. A qualifying percentile does not guarantee a Stage 2 shortlist, WAT/PI call or final admission. The official policy also requires a positive scaled score in all CAT sections and specifies separate academic eligibility requirements.

How the selection process works

Stage 1: Eligible CAT applicants complete the required IIM Kozhikode registration. Stage 2: A category-wise shortlist is prepared using the Aggregate Index Score, which considers CAT, Class X and XII performance, work experience and gender/academic diversity. Stage 3: Shortlisted candidates take the WAT and PI; the final merit list considers CAT, PI, WAT and resume-related components under the applicable policy.

How to interpret your admission chances

For a realistic assessment, students should consider sectional performance, academics and profile strength together. The published cutoff is only the first filter; it is not the score at which every candidate can expect an interview call.

What students study

The flagship PGP covers major management areas including economics, finance and accounting, information technology and systems, marketing, organisational behaviour and human resources, quantitative methods and operations, strategy and area-neutral courses. The programme structure supports a broad management foundation before students make more informed elective choices.

Learning beyond the classroom

IIMK's PGP ecosystem includes student councils, committees, interest groups, clubs, international exchange opportunities and institute-wide academic and extracurricular activities. For applicants, the large and academically diverse cohort is also a significant part of the peer-learning experience.

Who Should Choose IIM Kozhikode?

Strong Fit

  • You want a broad MBA with access to consulting, finance, marketing, analytics, operations and general-management pathways.
  • You value a large, academically diverse residential cohort and peer learning.
  • You are comfortable with a selection process that evaluates more than CAT performance.
  • You want to keep career options open before deciding on a specific management function.

Think Carefully If

  • You are applying only because you meet the published minimum CAT cutoff.
  • You have not estimated the full financial and opportunity cost of a two-year residential MBA.
  • You want a narrowly specialised degree rather than a broad management foundation.
  • You have not identified the roles or sectors you want to evaluate against the placement mix.

Student decision: IIM Kozhikode is particularly compelling for candidates who want a top-tier general-management MBA and value career optionality. The strongest applications and enrolment decisions will be those where programme fit, target roles and financial planning are considered together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IIM Kozhikode MBA fee for 2026–28?

The published two-year fee for the flagship PGP is ₹23.50 lakh. The same 2026–28 fee schedule is linked from the PGP-Finance and PGP-LSM programme pages. Students should separately review applicable deposits and other charges in the official fee schedule.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Kozhikode?

For the 2026–28 PGP policy, the General category minimum is 85 overall and 75 sectional percentile. These are eligibility thresholds; meeting them does not guarantee a shortlist or admission.

Does IIM Kozhikode consider Class 10 and Class 12 marks?

Yes. The Stage 2 Aggregate Index Score considers academic performance in Class X and XII along with CAT, work experience and gender or academic diversity under the official selection process.

Is work experience compulsory for IIM Kozhikode MBA admission?

Work experience is not a compulsory eligibility condition for the flagship PGP, but it is one of the factors considered in the Stage 2 shortlist process under the applicable admission policy.

What was the median package at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The official Final Placements Report 2025 for the PGP, PGP-Finance and PGP-LSM placement process reported a median CTC of ₹27.50 LPA.

Is IIM Kozhikode good for consulting and finance careers?

The official 2025 placement report shows recruitment across consulting and finance, alongside sales and marketing, general management, IT and analytics, and operations. Students should still compare the detailed sector mix with their own target role.

What is the difference between IIM Kozhikode PGP, PGP-Finance and PGP-LSM?

PGP is the broad flagship MBA. PGP-Finance is designed around finance and financial-services careers, while PGP-LSM integrates liberal studies with management for a more interdisciplinary approach.
