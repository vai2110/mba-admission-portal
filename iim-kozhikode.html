IIM Kozhikode MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility, CAT Cutoff & Selection

For the 2026-28 PGP, IIM Kozhikode uses a three-stage selection process. Candidates must first meet the minimum criteria and complete the institute's Stage 1 registration before being considered for the Stage 2 shortlist.

Minimum eligibility and CAT thresholds

CategoryOverall CATSectional CATClass XClass XII
General857560%60%
OBC-NCL / EWS756560%60%
SC655555%55%
ST / PwD554555%55%
Important: the cutoff is an eligibility threshold, not an admission guarantee.

The 2026-28 policy also requires a positive scaled score in every CAT section. A graduation route requires 60% aggregate in a degree involving at least three years of education after 10+2; CA/CMA/CS candidates without a graduation degree can qualify with 50% in the professional qualification subject to the policy.

Three-stage admission process

Stage 1 — Registration: CAT candidates who meet the minimum criteria and have opted for IIMK receive instructions to complete online registration. Stage 2 — Shortlist: IIMK prepares a category-wise shortlist using an Aggregate Index Score based on CAT, Class X/XII performance, work experience and gender/academic diversity. Stage 3 — WAT & PI: Shortlisted candidates appear for Written Ability Test and Personal Interview; final merit uses CAT, PI, WAT and resume components under the applicable policy.

Source: IIM Kozhikode PGP Admissions Policy 2026-28 and official PGP Selection Process.

For the 2024-26 batch, the official recruiter guide reported 55% female students, 27 months average work experience and 53% engineering background.

IIM Kozhikode Campus, Hostel & Facilities

IIM Kozhikode's campus covers about 112.5 acres. The institute has 20 hostels with capacity for about 1,400 students, and hostel rooms have LAN and Wi-Fi connectivity. The campus also provides sports and recreation facilities, including badminton, basketball, football, volleyball, squash, swimming and a gymnasium.

Students have access to the library, computer facilities, canteens and campus food outlets. The institute states that classrooms, the computer centre and student canteens are within walking distance of the hostels.

Who Should Apply to IIM Kozhikode?

Potentially strong fit

  • You want a broad full-time MBA rather than a narrowly specialised degree.
  • Your target roles include consulting, finance, marketing, analytics, operations or general management.
  • You are comfortable with a process where academics, diversity and work experience matter alongside CAT.
  • You want a large residential peer-learning environment.

Check before applying

  • Do not use the 85 percentile minimum as a prediction of an interview call.
  • Calculate the complete cost of attendance, including mess and personal expenses.
  • Compare the 2025 placement domain mix with your intended function rather than looking only at the ₹81 LPA highest CTC.
  • Review the applicable admission policy for your own category and academic profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IIM Kozhikode PGP fee for 2026-28?

The published two-year programme fee is ₹23.50 lakh, or ₹11.75 lakh per academic year. Mess and specified deposits/usage-based charges are additional.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Kozhikode PGP?

For the 2026-28 policy, the General-category minimum is 85 overall and 75 sectional percentile. Category-wise thresholds are lower. These are eligibility thresholds, not admission guarantees.

What graduation percentage is required?

The regular graduation route requires 60% aggregate in a degree involving at least three years after 10+2. CA/CMA/CS candidates have a separate 50% professional-qualification route under the policy.

How does IIM Kozhikode shortlist candidates?

Stage 2 uses an Aggregate Index Score based on CAT, Class X and XII performance, work experience and gender/academic diversity. Shortlisted candidates then take WAT and PI.

What was the median CTC at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The official 2025 Final Placements Report reported a ₹27.50 LPA median CTC and ₹28.18 LPA mean CTC for the combined PGP, PGP-Finance and PGP-LSM placement process.

What was the highest CTC in 2025?

The official Final Placements Report 2025 reported a ₹81.00 LPA highest CTC. It should not be interpreted as the typical salary outcome.

Does IIM Kozhikode have a management quota?

The 2026-28 admission policy states that admission communication is handled solely through the IIM Kozhikode admissions office and offers are made through the prescribed merit process and reservation norms.
