Quick Answer

The MBA is a 2-year postgraduate programme. The official School of Management page lists a graduate degree with at least 50% aggregate as the academic requirement and accepts KIITEE Management, CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT; the page also lists GMAT and ATMA on its MBA eligibility information. Selection and fee details should be checked for the active 2026 admission cycle.

MBA Programme Overview

KIIT School of Management offers a two-year MBA programme and positions the school around management education, industry exposure and placement preparation. The programme should be evaluated separately from engineering placement statistics because the relevant recruiter mix, roles and compensation are different.

Current school information also highlights specialised management learning in areas such as Business Analytics and Operations and Supply Chain Management.

MBA Admission Dates 2026

The current management admission cycle is published through the School of Management and its admission process. Applicants should check the active application window, exam dates and interview/selection schedule before applying because these can change during the cycle.

MBA Eligibility 2026

The official school page requires graduation in any discipline with 50% aggregate marks. It also states that candidates need 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA in both the 10th and 12th board examinations.

MBA Admission Process

  1. Check the current eligibility and accepted test route.
  2. Apply through the applicable management admission channel.
  3. Submit academic records and entrance-test details.
  4. Complete the institute's applicable evaluation stages.
  5. Receive and accept the admission offer if selected.
  6. Complete fee payment and document verification.

MBA Selection Criteria

The institute evaluates candidates through the applicable entrance score and its selection process. The exact 2026 weightage should be treated as cycle-specific; a fixed percentage split is not reproduced unless it is published in the current admission notification.

MBA Cutoff

A single official public cutoff should not be assumed across CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT, ATMA and KIITEE Management. The effective shortlist depends on the accepted exam route and the current selection cycle. No fabricated “expected cutoff” is provided.

MBA Fees

The active 2026 programme fee should be checked in the current KSOM admission communication before payment. The official school page confirms the programme duration and eligibility but does not provide a single fee figure in the source used for this page. Students should therefore avoid relying on older fee figures circulating on third-party portals.

MBA Curriculum & Specialisations

The management school offers broad management education with exposure to contemporary business areas. Current school positioning specifically highlights Business Analytics and Operations and Supply Chain Management. Applicants should review the latest curriculum for elective baskets, analytics tools, experiential learning, live projects and industry interaction.

Relevant for students targeting data-driven business, consulting and analytics roles.

General management

Industry exposure

Compare live projects, internships, corporate sessions and placement roles before choosing the programme.

MBA Placements & Career Outcomes

The official 2025 placement page reports that around 161 companies visited for KSOM placements, generating around 401 offers. The highest CTC was ₹20 LPA and average CTC was ₹7.25 LPA for MBA graduates in that report.

Who Should Apply for MBA?

The programme is best considered by graduates who want a full-time management qualification and can compare the total cost with role quality and placement outcomes. Your decision should depend on entrance score, preferred function, target industry, previous work experience, fee affordability and alternatives available at the same score range.

MBA FAQs

What is the MBA duration?

The programme duration is 2 years.

What is the MBA eligibility?

The school lists graduation in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate and 50% in both 10th and 12th examinations.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

The school lists KIITEE Management, CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT; its current eligibility information also lists GMAT and ATMA.

What is the MBA average package?

The 2025 placement page reports an average CTC of ₹7.25 LPA for KSOM MBA graduates.

What is the MBA highest package?

The 2025 KSOM placement report lists ₹20 LPA as the highest CTC.
