Nirma University B.Tech CSE: Quick Overview

The B.Tech programme at Nirma's Institute of Technology is four years. CSE is one of the listed branches, alongside CSE (AI & ML), ECE, Electronics Engineering (VLSI), Electrical, Mechanical, Chemical and Civil Engineering.

What is the key admission point?

For All-India institute seats, merit is prepared using the JEE Main Paper-I 2026 CRL. Gujarat state seats are handled through ACPC under Gujarat admission rules.

B.Tech CSE Eligibility

Applicants must pass Class 12 Science with Physics and Mathematics plus Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science or a Technical Vocational subject, subject to the marks prescribed by ACPC/Gujarat rules. The published 2026 criteria specify 45% aggregate for General and 40% for applicable reserved categories.

Nirma B.Tech CSE Admission Process

All-India seats

Appear for JEE Main Paper-I, register on Nirma's admission portal and submit the required documents. Institute merit is based on JEE Main CRL for the applicable All-India seats.

Gujarat state seats

Government/ACPC seats are filled through the Admission Committee for Professional Courses, Government of Gujarat. Students must follow the ACPC application and counselling process.

B.Tech Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

For the 2026-27 cycle, the official page lists ACPC vacant-seat applications from July 29 to August 3, 2026, with merit declaration on August 4 and institute counselling on August 6-7 if required. Other All-India milestones are published separately by the institute.

2027 planning: expect JEE Main and Nirma application activity around the first half of 2027, but do not treat an exact date as official until Nirma publishes the 2027 notice.

B.Tech CSE Curriculum & Career

The programme develops foundations in computing and engineering with practical laboratory learning. Students should use the current institute curriculum for semester-wise subjects and electives.

Career directions

Typical CSE pathways include software engineering, data/AI roles, cybersecurity, cloud, product engineering, analytics and higher studies. Actual outcomes depend on skills, internships, projects and the hiring cycle.

Nirma B.Tech CSE Placements

The official 2024-25 Institute of Technology statistics report 90% placement for CSE and a ₹51.75 LPA highest salary. The institute's overall UG highest salary rose from ₹46.47 LPA in 2020-21 to ₹88.42 LPA in 2021-22, ₹50.15 LPA in 2022-23, ₹52.69 LPA in 2023-24 and ₹72.04 LPA in 2024-25. These are highest-salary figures, not average salaries.

Do not confuse the headlines

The institute now reports ₹94.13 LPA as its 2025-26 highest package, while the 2024-25 CSE-specific highest was ₹51.75 LPA. Always match the package to the batch and branch.
