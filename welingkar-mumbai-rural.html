PGDM Rural Management: What Is It?

Rural Management is a specialised management programme focused on businesses and organisations operating in rural and emerging markets. It can be relevant to FMCG, rural finance, development, agriculture-linked businesses, supply chains and social enterprises.

Rural Management FAQs

What is the placement average?

The 2022-24 interim placement report shows ₹10.37 LPA domestic average and ₹18.16 LPA highest; the 2023-25 page reports ₹11.47 LPA average and ₹19 LPA highest.

Who should choose Rural Management?

Students interested in rural markets, development, FMCG, financial inclusion, agriculture-linked businesses and social enterprise can consider it.
