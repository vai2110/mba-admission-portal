IIM Shillong: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Indian Institute of Management Shillong (IIM Shillong) is an Institute of National Importance offering a two-year full-time residential PGP leading to an MBA degree. The institute also runs a five-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM), a 12-month PGP for Executives and a two-year MBA for Working Executives.

The flagship PGP is designed for fresh graduates and young professionals. It combines core management courses, electives, a two-month summer internship after the first year, capstone learning and opportunities for international exchange. The PGP is fully residential and students must stay in institute hostels.

Is IIM Shillong worth considering?

For a student comparing established IIMs, IIM Shillong stands out for its residential learning environment, strong consulting and finance presence, high recent median salary and a distinctive campus in Meghalaya. The right decision still depends on your CAT profile, total cost, preferred career function and the opportunity cost of the two-year MBA.

Which IIM Shillong programme is relevant to you?

If you are completing graduation or have limited work experience, the two-year PGP is the main route. If you are coming directly after Class 12, IPM is the relevant integrated route. Experienced professionals should compare PGPEx and PGP Working Executives separately rather than treating them as the same MBA.

IIM Shillong MBA Admission 2026-28: CAT Cutoff & Selection

IIM Shillong's PGP 2026-28 domestic admission process uses CAT 2025 for preliminary screening and a subsequent Personal Interaction process. The institute's published minimum sectional percentiles for PI consideration are 75 in VARC, DILR and QA for the General/EWS/other unreserved group, 60 each for SC and 50 each for ST and DA. These are minimum screening thresholds, not the percentile that guarantees an interview or admission.

How shortlisting works

IIM Shillong first applies the minimum CAT sectional requirements. Eligible candidates are then evaluated through a composite score using CAT percentile and a normalized application rating score (NARS). NARS considers Class X, Class XII, graduation and work-experience components. Therefore, the published CAT threshold should not be read as an expected-call percentile.

Important for CAT aspirants:

IIM Shillong's shortlist is independent of other IIMs. A candidate who clears the published minimums can still miss the PI shortlist because the institute uses composite scoring and profile information.

Scholarships and financial support

IIM Shillong lists a Need Based Financial Assistance scheme for economically weaker PGP participants. Government scholarships are also available for eligible SC, ST and PwD participants. The institute states that PGP participants have been eligible for collateral-free education loans from banks, subject to bank procedures.

Financial support

  • Need Based Financial Assistance scheme.
  • Government scholarships for eligible categories.
  • Education-loan access through banks.

Budget beyond headline fee

  • Mess and personal expenses should be planned separately where applicable.
  • Optional international exchange can add travel and related costs.
  • Do not compare colleges using tuition alone.

IIM Shillong MBA Curriculum, Campus & Student Life

The PGP runs across six terms over two years. The curriculum includes core courses in areas such as financial reporting, managerial economics, marketing, statistics, operations, finance, human capital, strategy, business law and information systems, followed by electives and integrative learning. A two-month summer internship is conducted after the first year.

What the academic experience looks like

Hostel and campus

The PGP is fully residential. IIM Shillong's handbook states that first-year accommodation may be allotted at Umsawli or Nongthymmai; the locations are about 12 km apart and institute buses connect participants to the main campus for classes and activities. The institute aims to provide single accommodation, although double sharing can be used when required.

Student-life reality:

The Shillong location is part of the experience: the campus setting, residential cohort and relatively contained environment can be attractive if you value community and campus life. It can be less convenient for students who strongly prefer a large metro-city ecosystem.

IIM Shillong Placements: Latest 2024-26 Outcomes

The latest completed final placement report is for the PGP 2024-26 batch. It reports 393 graduating students, 147 recruiters including 51 new recruiters, 102 PPO/PPI offers, a highest CTC of ₹71.50 LPA, average CTC of ₹27.03 LPA and median CTC of ₹26.00 LPA.

The previous 2023-25 report recorded ₹26.44 LPA average and ₹25 LPA median, with 141 recruiters and 75 PPO/PPI offers. This gives students useful context: the 2024-26 batch improved the average and median over the immediately previous reported batch.

What should a student focus on?

Do not use ₹71.50 LPA as the expected salary. The median of ₹26 LPA is a much better indicator of the middle of the graduating cohort. Also inspect function and sector mix on the detailed placements page before choosing IIM Shillong for a particular career path.

Who Should Choose IIM Shillong?

Strong fit

  • Students targeting consulting, finance, general management, sales & marketing or analytics roles.
  • Candidates who value a fully residential IIM environment.
  • Students comfortable with Shillong's location and campus-led lifestyle.
  • Aspirants who want an IIM with strong recent median CTC and diverse academic backgrounds.

Think carefully if

  • You are choosing only on the IIM label without comparing fees and career outcomes.
  • You need a metro-city campus ecosystem for personal or career reasons.
  • Your target role is highly specialised and requires a specific recruiter mix.

IIM Shillong FAQs

What is IIM Shillong MBA fees?

What is the IIM Shillong CAT cutoff?

For PGP 2026-28, the published minimum sectional percentiles for the unreserved/other group are 75 in VARC, DILR and QA. These are screening thresholds, not guaranteed interview or admission percentiles.

What is the latest IIM Shillong placement package?

Is IIM Shillong fully residential?

Yes. The PGP is fully residential and students are required to stay in institute hostels.

Does IIM Shillong offer IPM?

Yes. IIM Shillong offers a five-year Integrated Programme in Management combining BBA/BBA (Honours) stages with an MBA in the fifth year.
