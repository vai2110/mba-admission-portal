How were BIMTECH placements?

BIMTECH publishes two useful official views of placements: NIRF management data and its own final placement reports. NIRF provides a standardised institution-level view, while the institute report gives programme-wise highest, average, median CTC and placement percentage.

Which BIMTECH programme had the strongest placement numbers?

For the 2023-25 batch, PGDM had the highest reported maximum Indian CTC at ₹22 LPA. PGDM-IB, IBM and RM each reported 100% placement, while their highest CTCs ranged from ₹14.50 LPA to ₹16.42 LPA.

Which companies recruit from BIMTECH?

BIMTECH's placement ecosystem spans banking and financial services, consulting, IT/ITES, retail, FMCG/FMCD, insurance, manufacturing and other services. The institute maintains a recruiter list and placement reports on its official placement page.

The current placement page states that more than 100 companies visit the campus for talent acquisition.

What should students check in BIMTECH placement data?

NIRF median vs institute average

NIRF median and institute average are different statistics and come from different reporting frames. Do not present the NIRF median as BIMTECH's average package.

Programme matters

The 2023-25 report shows differences between PGDM, IB, IBM and Retail Management. Compare the programme you intend to join.

Highest package is not typical salary

₹22 LPA is the PGDM highest Indian CTC in the report, not the salary received by the typical PGDM graduate.

BIMTECH placements FAQs

What is the BIMTECH average package?

For the 2023-25 batch, the official report gives ₹10.90 LPA for PGDM, ₹10.61 LPA for PGDM-IB, ₹10.59 LPA for PGDM-IBM and ₹10.13 LPA for PGDM-RM.

What is BIMTECH's highest package?

The 2023-25 official report records ₹22 LPA as the highest Indian CTC for PGDM. BIMTECH's current website also highlights ₹22 lakh as the highest domestic package.

What is BIMTECH's NIRF median salary?

BIMTECH's NIRF 2025 Management submission reports a ₹10.50 LPA median salary for the 2023-24 graduating cohort.

Which BIMTECH programme had 100% placements?

PGDM-IB, PGDM-IBM and PGDM-RM each reported 100% placement in the 2023-25 final placement report.
