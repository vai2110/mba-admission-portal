Graphic Era BCA: What Students Should Know

BCA at Graphic Era is an undergraduate computer applications pathway for students interested in software, applications and technology careers. GEU also offers specialised BCA variants in areas such as AI & Data Science and Cyber Security.

Quick answer

Choose BCA if you want a computing-focused undergraduate degree but do not specifically need the engineering structure of a B.Tech.

BCA Eligibility

The 2026 fee structure is linked to Class 12 aggregate or CUET NTA score slabs. Confirm the latest programme-level academic eligibility before application.

BCA Admission Process

GEU's programme admission framework uses merit based on the qualifying examination. The 2026 BCA fee table also uses CUET NTA score bands for scholarship/fee categorisation.

BCA Curriculum & Learning

The course is designed around computer applications and technology skills. Students should compare the regular BCA with GEU's AI & Data Science and Cyber Security variants if they already have a specific technology career target.

BCA Careers & Placement Context

Possible paths include software development, application support, web development, testing, database and technology operations. GEU reports university-wide placement activity, but course-specific outcomes can vary by batch and role.

BCA FAQs

What is the BCA fee?

The 2026 published tuition is ₹65,700 per semester in Year 1 and ₹67,150 per semester in Year 2, before applicable scholarship/payment adjustments.

Can I choose a specialised BCA?

Yes. GEU's official programme list includes BCA options in AI & Data Science and Cyber Security in addition to the general BCA.

Is CUET required?

GEU's 2026 fee structure uses CUET NTA score bands alongside Class 12 percentage bands. Verify the admission route applicable to your application.
