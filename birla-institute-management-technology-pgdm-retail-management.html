What is BIMTECH PGDM Retail Management?

PGDM Retail Management is a two-year full-time residential programme launched by BIMTECH in 2002. It combines general management with retail-specific learning in areas such as mall management, buying and merchandising, category management, retail analytics and quick commerce.

Who is eligible for PGDM-RM?

A graduation degree in the 10+2+3 pattern with at least 50% marks is required. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's conditions.

Which exams are accepted for PGDM-RM?

For 2027-29, BIMTECH accepts CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT 2025/2026-27, CMAT 2027 and MAT 2026-27. Shortlisting considers entrance score and past academic record, followed by Extempore and PI.

What is the BIMTECH Retail Management cutoff?

BIMTECH does not publish a single final CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT closing cutoff on the current admission page. Admission is based on a composite assessment rather than a single score.

What do you study in Retail Management?

The curriculum covers retail concepts, mall management, retail consumer behaviour, visual merchandising, sales and distribution, supply chain, buying and merchandising, global retailing, category management, retail analytics, e-commerce, quick commerce, omnichannel and franchising.

Experiential components include on-the-job training, summer internship, retail simulation and alumni mentoring.

What are PGDM-RM placements?

The official 2023-25 report records 100% placement for PGDM-RM. Highest Indian CTC was ₹16.42 LPA, average CTC ₹10.13 LPA and median CTC ₹10 LPA.

PGDM-RM FAQs

Is BIMTECH PGDM-RM only for retail careers?

No. The programme is retail-focused but includes core management and graduates are recruited across retail, IT, consulting, banking, edtech and other sectors.

What is the PGDM-RM average package?

The 2023-25 official placement report records ₹10.13 LPA average CTC and ₹10 LPA median CTC.

Is PGDM-RM equivalent to MBA?

BIMTECH states that its PGDM programmes have AIU equivalence to MBA.
