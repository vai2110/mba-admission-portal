KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science: Is This the Right Engineering Branch?

KLU's B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science is a four-year programme that combines computer science foundations with statistics, data analytics, machine learning and AI. The official programme page describes a mix of classroom learning, hands-on labs, research exposure, industry collaboration and experiential learning.

The key student decision is whether you want to specialise earlier in AI and data or prefer a broader computer-science route. AI & DS can suit students who are already interested in machine learning, analytics, intelligent systems and data-driven problem solving.

4 yearsUndergraduate engineering programme
AI + DataMachine learning, analytics and intelligent systems
ProjectsDesign projects and capstone work
Do I need coding experience before joining?

No prior coding experience is required according to KLU's programme FAQ. The curriculum starts with computational thinking and programming foundations and progressively moves into data structures, AI, ML and advanced data topics.

KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science Admission 2026

What is the eligibility?

KLU's official programme page states that applicants should have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination from a recognised board, generally with at least 60% aggregate, and should have studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM).

Which entrance exams are accepted?

KLU lists KLEEE, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, recognised state-level engineering entrance examinations such as Andhra Pradesh/Telangana EAMCET and a valid Uni-GAUGE score where applicable.

What should you do after qualifying?

Complete the current application, submit the required documents, participate in the applicable counselling/selection process and confirm the programme/campus before paying the admission amount. The 2026-27 schedule can change by phase, so the live admission portal takes priority.

Are hostel and other charges included?

No. KLU states that hostel, mess, examination and other charges are separate. These should be included when calculating the total cost of attendance.

Are scholarships available?

KLU states that merit-based and need-based scholarships are available. The programme page says scholarship consideration can depend on entrance examination performance and academic scores. Students should confirm the exact slab, continuation condition and application process before treating a scholarship as part of their budget.

Projects, Labs, Innovation and Internships: How Practical Is the Course?

KLU describes AI & DS as a practical programme with hands-on labs, research exposure, industry collaboration, semester-wise design projects, an annual AI prototype exhibition and capstone projects. For a student, these opportunities are valuable only when they result in demonstrable skills and work that can be shown in interviews.

Build during the degree

  • Python and SQL proficiency
  • ML and statistics projects
  • Data visualisation portfolio
  • One end-to-end AI/data application
  • Internship and capstone experience

Ask before enrolling

  • Which labs are available to your batch?
  • How are internships facilitated?
  • Are projects individual or team-based?
  • What proportion of learning is hands-on?

B.Tech AI & Data Science Placements at KL University

KLU reports university-wide placement figures including ₹75 LPA highest package and ₹8.1 LPA average package. The figures are not presented as AI & DS-specific, so they should be used only as university-level context.

For AI & DS, students should additionally evaluate whether recruiters are hiring for software engineering, data engineering, analytics, ML or AI roles, because the same degree can lead to very different job profiles.

Which should you choose?

Choose AI & DS if you want deeper exposure to AI, ML and data from the undergraduate level. Choose CSE if you want a broader foundation and may decide your specialisation later. Neither degree automatically guarantees a better placement.

KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science FAQs

What is the B.Tech AI & Data Science fee at KLU?

The official 2025-26 page lists ₹1,53,500 for the first semester at Vijayawada and ₹1,61,000 at Hyderabad, including the ₹16,000 admission fee. KLU states that the fee is subject to revision for 2026-27.

What is the eligibility for B.Tech AI & Data Science?

The programme page states 10+2/equivalent with generally 60% aggregate and PCM subjects.

Does KLU accept JEE Main for AI & Data Science?

Yes. JEE Main is listed among the accepted engineering entrance routes, along with KLEEE, JEE Advanced, recognised state-level exams and Uni-GAUGE where applicable.

Do I need coding knowledge before joining AI & DS?

No prior coding knowledge is required according to KLU's programme FAQ. The curriculum starts with foundational programming and progressively becomes more technical.

What jobs can I get after B.Tech AI & Data Science?

Potential roles include Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Engineer, Business Intelligence Developer and AI/research roles, depending on the skills you build.

Is AI & Data Science better than CSE?

AI & DS is more specialised in AI, ML and data, while CSE is broader. Choose according to the kind of subjects and career direction you want.
