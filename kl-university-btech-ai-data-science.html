KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science: What You Study

KLU's B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science is a four-year undergraduate programme covering AI, machine learning, data science, analytics and related computing foundations. The official programme page describes practical learning through labs, projects, research exposure and industry collaboration.

4 yearsUndergraduate programme
AI + DataMachine learning, analytics and intelligent systems
ProjectsDesign projects and capstone work
Who is this course best suited to?

Students who are interested in mathematics, programming, AI, machine learning, analytics and data-driven problem solving are a natural fit. Prior coding experience is not stated as a compulsory requirement; the programme starts with foundational computing.

B.Tech AI & Data Science Curriculum and Specialisations

The published curriculum progresses from computational thinking and Python to data science, machine learning, data analytics, deep learning, big data engineering, computer vision, NLP and capstone projects.

StageExamples from the published curriculum
FoundationDiscrete Mathematics, computational thinking, Python-based data structures and AI fundamentals
Core AI & dataData Science and Statistics, Machine Learning, Data Analytics and Visualisation, Data Management and Warehousing
Advanced topicsDeep Learning, Big Data Engineering, Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing
Final stageEngineering Capstone Projects and professional/open electives

The official page also lists specialised tracks such as healthcare data analytics, distributed-ledger analytics, AI-driven language technologies, visual intelligence, data engineering for AI and edge AI applications.

KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science Admission 2026

Eligibility

The programme page states that applicants should have passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognised board with generally at least 60% aggregate and should have studied PCM.

Entrance exams

KLU lists KLEEE as its own engineering entrance exam and also mentions JEE Main, JEE Advanced, recognised state-level engineering examinations such as EAMCET and Uni-GAUGE, where applicable.

Selection

Students should complete the current application and counselling process for the 2026-27 intake. The university can revise exam dates, selection routes and scholarship rules, so the live admission notice takes priority.

Are hostel and mess included?

No. The official programme page says hostel, mess, examination and other charges are separate.

Scholarships

KLU states that merit-based and need-based scholarship opportunities are available, with eligibility linked to academic performance and entrance examination performance in relevant cases.

B.Tech AI & Data Science Placements at KL University

KLU's university-wide placement page reports ₹75 LPA as the highest package and ₹8.1 LPA as the average package. These are not presented as AI & DS-specific figures, so students should use them as university-level context rather than as a promised salary for this branch.

Which should you choose?

Choose AI & DS if the curriculum itself matches your interest in ML, analytics and intelligent systems. Choose CSE if you want a broader computing foundation and greater freedom to specialise later.

KL University B.Tech AI & Data Science FAQs

What is the B.Tech AI & Data Science fee at KLU?

The official page currently publishes 2025-26 fees of ₹16,000 admission fee plus ₹1,37,500 semester fee at Vijayawada, or ₹1,53,500 for the first semester. It states that the fee is subject to revision for 2026-27.

What is the eligibility for B.Tech AI & Data Science?

The programme page states 10+2/equivalent with generally 60% aggregate and PCM subjects.

Does KLU accept JEE Main for AI & Data Science?

Yes. KLU's programme page lists JEE Main among the entrance qualifications it accepts, alongside KLEEE and other recognised routes.

Is AI & Data Science better than CSE?

It depends on your intended career. AI & DS is more specialised from the beginning, while CSE offers a broader computing base.
