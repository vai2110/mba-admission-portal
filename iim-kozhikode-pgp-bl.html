Programme Overview

IIM Kozhikode's Post Graduate Programme in Business Leadership (PGP-BL) is a one-year, full-time residential management programme designed for young professionals with relevant work experience who want to move towards broader leadership responsibilities.

The programme is intentionally different from a conventional two-year MBA. IIMK describes it as a high-intensity and holistic programme that combines condensed management learning, leadership development, industry engagement and global exposure. The programme structure is organised into five terms of approximately eight to nine weeks each.

What makes PGP-BL different?
The programme is built for experienced professionals, not fresh graduates. Its value proposition is based on accelerating an existing career through an intensive management and leadership experience, rather than providing an entry-level management foundation.

Eligibility for IIM Kozhikode PGP-BL 2026–27

For the current PGP-BL admission process, IIM Kozhikode requires candidates to meet all three core conditions: at least 50% or equivalent CGPA in graduation, post-graduation or an eligible professional qualification; at least four years of qualifying work experience after graduation; and a valid CAT, GMAT or GRE score.

Only full-time remunerative work experience after graduation is counted. Part-time work, projects, internships, articleship and pre-graduation experience are not treated as qualifying work experience under the published admission requirements.

Entrance Exam Requirements & Profile Fit

IIM Kozhikode requires a valid CAT, GMAT or GRE score for PGP-BL, but the programme's admission process is not based on an entrance-test percentile alone. The application also includes programme-specific written components and Personal Interaction.

For working professionals:
The most relevant admission question is not simply “What CAT percentile is required?” PGP-BL evaluates an experienced-professional profile through eligibility, application content and personal interaction in addition to the accepted test score.

IIM Kozhikode PGP-BL Admission & Selection Process

IIM Kozhikode follows a two-stage selection process for PGP-BL. The admission cycle is conducted through multiple mutually independent phases, with separate application and interaction dates published by the institute.

Stage 1: Online Application

Eligible candidates complete the online application, including registration against the minimum criteria and programme-specific written components such as the Statement of Goals and Alternative Essay.

Stage 2: Personal Interaction

Selected candidates participate in Personal Interaction. The application and interaction together form the core of the programme-specific selection process.

Multiple Admission Phases

For the PGP-BL 08 admission cycle, IIMK has published multiple application phases, so applicants should track the phase-specific deadline that applies to them.

Current 2026–27 schedule:
The official PGP-BL page for the current cycle lists three application phases and a planned class commencement in the first week of April 2027. Applicants should check the live IIMK admission page for the latest phase status before applying.

IIM Kozhikode PGP-BL Fees & Fee Bifurcation

How the 2024 and 2025 outcomes differ

In 2024, consulting accounted for 41.9% of offers and general management & operations for 25.8%. The 2025 batch saw consulting rise to 48.14% of offers, while strategy, marketing and business development together accounted for 27.77%. This makes PGP-BL particularly relevant for experienced professionals looking to use prior work experience for leadership, consulting and strategic roles—but the exact opportunity mix changes by batch.

The 2025 PGP-BL batch profile reported an average pre-programme work experience of 4.9 years. Placement interpretation for a one-year experienced-professional programme should therefore be considered alongside the candidate's own prior role, industry and work experience.

Who Should Apply for IIM Kozhikode PGP-BL?

Professionals with 4+ years of experience

The clearest fit because qualifying work experience is a core eligibility requirement.

High-performing specialists

Suitable for professionals who have developed depth in a function or industry and now want broader management exposure.

Leadership-track professionals

Relevant for candidates preparing for roles involving larger teams, cross-functional decisions or broader business ownership.

Career accelerators

Useful for professionals seeking an intensive one-year transition rather than stepping away for a conventional two-year MBA.

Who Should Think Twice Before Choosing PGP-BL?

  • Fresh graduates and candidates without the required four years of qualifying full-time work experience.
  • Professionals who are primarily looking for an entry-level management qualification rather than accelerated leadership development.
  • Candidates unwilling to take a one-year intensive residential programme after an established professional career.
Realistic view: PGP-BL is not simply a shorter MBA. Its strongest value is for candidates who can connect their existing professional experience with a credible next step into broader management or leadership responsibilities.

FAQs

What is IIM Kozhikode PGP-BL?
PGP-BL is a one-year, full-time residential Post Graduate Programme in Business Leadership designed for young professionals with relevant work experience.
How much work experience is required for PGP-BL?
IIM Kozhikode currently requires a minimum of four years of qualifying full-time remunerative work experience after graduation.
Is CAT compulsory for PGP-BL admission?
No. IIMK accepts CAT, GMAT or GRE for the programme, subject to the official admission conditions for the relevant cycle.
What is the PGP-BL fee at IIM Kozhikode?
The institute currently lists a programme fee of ₹22.50 lakh and a non-refundable application fee of ₹3,000. Applicants should verify the latest fee notice before applying.
Is PGP-BL a one-year MBA?
PGP-BL is a one-year postgraduate management programme focused on business leadership. Students should use IIMK's official degree and programme documentation for the exact qualification nomenclature rather than assuming it is identical to a conventional two-year MBA.
Does IIM Kozhikode provide placements for PGP-BL?
IIMK states that it facilitates final placements for PGP-BL students and maintains a dedicated PGP-BL placement-report section.
