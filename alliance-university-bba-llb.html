Alliance University BBA LL.B.: Why Students Choose an Integrated Law Degree

The B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) combines business administration with professional legal education over five years. Alliance describes the programme as a route that integrates management, finance and legal studies, with corporate-law exposure and opportunities to build both commercial and legal expertise.

BBA LL.B. vs B.A. LL.B.: what is the key difference?

BBA LL.B. is more directly aligned with business, management and corporate-law subjects. B.A. LL.B. combines law with liberal-arts disciplines. Students targeting corporate legal roles may prefer the business-law orientation of BBA LL.B.

Alliance University BBA LL.B. Admission 2026

The programme page states that students can use the university's law admission route and specifically notes that candidates who have not appeared for CLAT can take the Alliance University Common Law Aptitude Test (ACLAT) followed by an online personal interview. BBA LL.B. Curriculum, Majors & Practical Exposure

The programme blends management and finance with professional legal studies. The current curriculum framework also lists legal domains such as international law and constitutional law and provides interdisciplinary electives across business, law, liberal arts and other schools.

Business side

  • Marketing
  • Finance
  • Human resources
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Financial institutions and markets

Legal side

  • Business and corporate law
  • International law
  • Constitutional law
  • Intellectual property
  • Legal research and drafting
  • Moot courts and internships

The combination is particularly relevant for students considering corporate legal practice, in-house counsel, compliance, commercial contracts, legal consulting or law-firm careers.

BBA LL.B. Careers, Internships & Higher Studies

Graduates can pursue corporate law, law-firm practice, in-house legal roles, compliance, legal consulting, arbitration, policy and related areas. The programme's practical learning model includes internships, moot courts and legal clinics.

Is BBA LL.B. useful for corporate law?

Yes. The business component gives students exposure to management, finance and commercial decision-making alongside legal education, which can be relevant for corporate legal work.

After graduation, students can also pursue LL.M., specialised legal certifications, competitive examinations or management-oriented higher studies depending on their career goal.

Alliance University BBA LL.B. FAQs

What is the duration of BBA LL.B. at Alliance University?

It is a five-year integrated programme spread across ten semesters.

What is the BBA LL.B. fee?

The current programme page publishes a total programme fee of ₹17.5 lakh for Indian nationals, including registration and all listed instalments.

Can I get BBA LL.B. admission without CLAT?

Yes. Alliance states that candidates who have not appeared for CLAT can take ACLAT followed by the university's personal interview process.

What is the minimum eligibility?

The current programme information states a minimum 50% aggregate requirement for applicants, with the university's stated relaxation for eligible reserved categories.

Is the Alliance School of Law BCI approved?

Yes. Alliance states that its School of Law is approved by the Bar Council of India.
