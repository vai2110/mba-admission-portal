KL University MBA Admission 2026

Eligibility

Eligibility can vary by MBA format. Applicants should check the current programme notice for graduation requirements, minimum marks and any specific entrance-test conditions.

Entrance and selection

KLU's 2026-27 admissions portal lists KLMAT for management admissions. The broader admission system also uses counselling and programme-specific selection procedures.

Application

Applicants complete the online registration, verify contact details, submit the application and pay the applicable fee. The admissions portal states a ₹1,000 non-refundable application fee for the online application.

KL University MBA Placements

KLU's headline placement figures are university-wide. The admissions site currently reports ₹75 LPA highest package and ₹8.1 LPA average package, but these numbers should not be presented as MBA-specific salaries without a course-level placement report.

KL University MBA FAQs

Which entrance exam is required for MBA at KL University?

KLU's current admissions portal identifies KLMAT as the management entrance test. Check the programme-specific notice for the applicable route for your intake.

Does KL University offer an MBA in FinTech?

Yes. MBA FinTech is listed among KLU's management programmes.

Does KL University offer a Global MBA?

Yes. KLU lists a Global MBA option with an international semester structure; foreign-university tuition and travel/living expenses need to be considered separately.

What is the average MBA package at KL University?
