BMU BBA & B.Com Overview

BMU offers BBA/BBA (Hons.) and B.Com (Hons.) pathways designed around business knowledge, practical learning and industry exposure. The admissions portal currently lists BBA focus areas such as Business Analytics, Strategy, Family Business & Entrepreneurship, Finance, Marketing, HR, International Business & Global Trade and AI applications in business.

Which is better: BBA or B.Com?

BBA is generally more management-oriented, while B.Com can suit students seeking stronger exposure to commerce, accounting, finance and related areas. Your preferred career and professional qualification plans should guide the choice.

Career Scope

BBA graduates can target management, marketing, finance, analytics, HR, sales and entrepreneurship roles, while B.Com graduates can pursue finance, accounting, banking, fintech and related careers or professional qualifications.

BBA & B.Com FAQs

What is BMU BBA fee?

The published BBA (Hons.) tuition is ₹3.50 lakh per year, with a four-year published tuition total of ₹14 lakh.

Does BMU offer B.Com?

Yes. BMU lists B.Com (Hons.) and B.Com (Hons.) with Research.

Does BMU have an integrated BBA MBA?

Yes. BMU offers a five-year integrated BBA-MBA programme.
