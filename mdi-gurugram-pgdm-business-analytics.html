About MDI Gurgaon PGDM-Business Analytics

MDI says the programme is designed to develop holistic business analytics managers who can address business problems through analytics, apply emerging analytical technologies, identify patterns and present logic-based solutions.

Programme fit

Business analytics. Compare the curriculum, eligibility, cost and programme-specific placement data before selecting it in the MDI application form.

Programme structure published by MDI

  • Six terms of three months each.
  • Core courses in the first four terms.
  • Compulsory eight-to-ten-week internship after Term 3.
  • Eight electives from Operations Management and Information Management in Terms 5 and 6, with up to two electives from other functional areas.
  • MDI states that 20% of the course is taught by industry practitioners.

PGDM-Business Analytics Eligibility & Admission 2027–29

At least 50% in Class 10 and 12 and at least 50% in a recognised bachelor’s degree of minimum three years. CAT 2026 is required for Indian applicants.

Cutoff note

MDI does not publish a fixed numeric CAT cutoff in the current admission notice. MDI’s admissions FAQ says aggregate and sectional CAT cut-offs are considered.

PGDM-Business Analytics: Student FAQs

What is the MDI Business Analytics fee?

The published 2026–28 fee for PGDM-Business Analytics is ₹26,55,310. It includes the listed tuition, boarding, lodging, learning material, activities, library, alumni fee and refundable security deposit.

How is the MDI Business Analytics programme structured?

MDI describes a six-term programme. Core courses are covered in the first four terms; students complete a compulsory eight-to-ten-week internship after term 3. In terms 5 and 6, students can choose eight electives from the OM and IM areas and up to two electives from other functional areas. MDI states that 20% of the course is taught by industry practitioners.

What was the latest PGDM-Business Analytics placement average?

The 2024–26 report calls the programme PGDM-BN and records ₹26.20 LPA average CTC and ₹25.70 LPA median CTC.
