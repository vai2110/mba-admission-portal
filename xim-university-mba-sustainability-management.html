What Is XIM MBA-SM?

MBA-Sustainability Management is a specialised two-year management programme for students interested in sustainability strategy, responsible business, environmental and social issues, impact and development. It sits within XIM University's School of Sustainability.

Best fit

This programme is particularly relevant if you want sustainability, ESG, responsible-business, impact, development or related consulting and corporate roles.

MBA-SM Eligibility 2026

The published requirement is a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 55% aggregate marks. Rank holders can also apply under the stated special route.

MBA-SM Admission 2026

XIM's 2026 information lists XAT, CAT, GMAT, XGMT and NMAT as admission-test options for MBA-SM. The application and selection route should be checked against the latest programme bulletin.

MBA-SM Career Options

Potential career areas include ESG and sustainability, responsible supply chains, impact consulting, CSR, development organisations, sustainability reporting and corporate sustainability strategy. The specialised nature of the degree makes career-goal clarity important.

Relevant interests

  • ESG and responsible business
  • Sustainability consulting
  • CSR and impact
  • Development sector

Compare before choosing

If you want a conventional general-management role without sustainability focus, compare MBA-SM with MBA-BM.
