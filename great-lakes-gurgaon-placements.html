Great Lakes Gurgaon Placements 2026: Latest Outcomes

The latest PGPM placement report for the Class of 2026 records an average CTC of ₹16.2 LPA and highest CTC of ₹34.5 LPA. Great Lakes also reports a 2.6x average post-MBA CTC compared with the average incoming CTC.

For the latest available PGDM report, the Class of 2025 recorded an average CTC of ₹11.8 LPA and highest CTC of ₹22.7 LPA.

Which Roles and Industries Recruit at Great Lakes Gurgaon?

PGPM 2026

For PGPM 2026, IT/ITES accounted for 41% of offers, Consulting 30% and Analytics 11%. Functionally, Analytics accounted for 21%, Functional Consulting 17%, Business Consulting 11% and Marketing & Sales 11%. Roles included Consultant, Business Transformation Lead, Decision Analytics Consultant, Associate Product Manager, Product Analyst, Assistant Brand Manager and Operations Manager.

PGDM 2025

For PGDM 2025, Finance accounted for 28% of roles, Business/Digital/Functional Consulting 16%, and Marketing, Presales, Sales and Distribution 13%. IT/ITES led industries at 26%, Consulting at 25% and BFSI at 23%. Roles included Consultant, Credit Analyst, Associate Product Manager, Customer Success Manager, Sustainability Consultant and Finance Consultant.

Great Lakes Gurgaon Placement Recruiters

The institute's latest reports list recruiters including Deloitte, Accenture, Infosys, Cognizant, KPMG, Tiger Analytics, ZS Associates, Bank of America, EY, HCL Technologies and others. The recruiter list is partial, as explicitly noted by Great Lakes.

Important

Recruiter participation does not mean every company hires every student or every programme. Roles, eligibility and hiring numbers vary by batch and placement cycle.

How Should Students Read Great Lakes Placement Data?

Useful signals

  • PGPM average CTC reached ₹16.2 LPA in 2026.
  • PGDM average CTC reached ₹11.8 LPA in 2025.
  • Both programmes show consulting, technology/IT and analytics opportunities.
  • PGPM shows career-transition evidence through its post-MBA CTC comparison.

Do not overread

  • Average CTC is not a guaranteed individual salary.
  • Highest CTC represents one outcome, not the typical student outcome.
  • PGPM and PGDM should not be compared without considering candidate experience and programme duration.
