IIM Sirmaur MBA Placements 2025: Latest Snapshot

The institute's 2024-25 annual report records an average CTC of ₹13.7 LPA for the MBA 2023-25 cohort. The cohort had 295 students, approximately 78.3% freshers and 64 students with prior work experience.

Roles a student may target

The sector mix points toward careers across banking and financial services, consulting, analytics, technology, sales/marketing, operations and general management. The institute also notes that experienced students secured roles in consulting, fintech, analytics and strategy.

Who Gets the Most Value from IIM Sirmaur Placements?

Potential advantage

  • Students with a clear target sector can use the broad recruiter mix strategically.
  • Experienced candidates can leverage prior work experience for role fit and compensation growth.
  • Students interested in BFSI, consulting, technology and analytics have visible opportunities in the reported mix.

Do not assume

  • ₹13.7 LPA is the salary every student receives.
  • The highest offer represents the typical outcome.
  • A strong CAT percentile alone guarantees a high-paying placement.

IIM Sirmaur MBA ROI: How Should You Read the Placement Numbers?

The published 2026-28 programme fee is ₹21 lakh, excluding the ₹30,000 refundable caution money. Against the latest programme-specific average CTC of ₹13.7 LPA, students should evaluate ROI using the full two-year cost, financing cost, opportunity cost, role quality and individual pre-MBA salary—not by dividing fee by average CTC mechanically.

Student takeaway

IIM Sirmaur's placement data shows a reasonably diversified recruiter base and a three-year MBA average-CTC series. If you are deciding between IIMs, compare this trend with the fees, your expected role, location preference and the probability of getting the role you actually want.
