IIM Jammu MBA Healthcare Overview

IIM Jammu's MBA Healthcare Management is a specialised two-year management programme with a 2026-28 batch size of 90. It is designed for students interested in healthcare organisations, hospital administration, health services and related management careers.

Why choose MBA Healthcare?

The programme is better suited to candidates who want management careers connected to healthcare rather than a general MBA with no sector focus. IIM Jammu's first MBA HA & HM batch was created through collaboration involving IIM Jammu, AIIMS Jammu and IIT Jammu.

IIM Jammu MBA Healthcare Fees

The 2026-28 admission policy states that the programme follows IIM Jammu's fee framework. Students should confirm the programme-specific payment schedule with the institute before payment; the flagship MBA fee page currently lists ₹22,80,756 for 2026-28. A refundable ₹50,000 caution deposit and actual mess bills are additional.

Offer acceptance

The ₹50,000 offer acceptance amount is adjusted against Term-I fee. The institute also specifies withdrawal and refund rules in the admission policy.

IIM Jammu MBA Healthcare Placements: Latest Available Report

IIM Jammu currently lists a 2023-25 MBA HA & HM final placement report and a 2024-26 brochure on its official placement archive. The earlier 2022-24 report should therefore be treated as historical rather than the latest outcome. The first completed cohort reported 100% placement, 56+ companies, ₹32 LPA highest domestic CTC, ₹14.01 LPA average and ₹13 LPA median.

Students’ Discussions & Practical Inference

Student discussions around MBA Healthcare usually centre on one question: will the programme lead to healthcare-specific careers or a broader MBA-style role set? The placement data shows a mix of healthcare and general management functions, so candidates should examine recruiter names and role descriptions before joining.

Practical inference:

Choose this programme primarily when healthcare is a genuine career interest. If your only objective is general consulting, finance or marketing flexibility, compare the flagship MBA and other options before paying the programme fee.

Career Fit: Who Should Choose MBA Healthcare?

Good fit

  • Students targeting healthcare management and hospital administration.
  • Candidates interested in healthcare consulting and healthcare product roles.
  • Applicants who want management skills with a sector-specific lens.

Think carefully if

  • You want unrestricted general-MBA career flexibility.
  • Your target role is unrelated to healthcare.
  • You are selecting only for the IIM brand.

IIM Jammu MBA Healthcare FAQs

What is the batch size?

The 2026-28 MBA Healthcare batch size is 90.

What is the General CAT cutoff?

The published overall PI-shortlisting cutoff is 88 for General male candidates and 86 for General female/transgender candidates.

What was the first batch placement outcome?

The 2022-24 first batch reported 100% placement, with ₹32 LPA highest domestic CTC, ₹14.01 LPA average and ₹13 LPA median.
