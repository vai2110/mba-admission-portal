SOA B.Tech: Quick Answer

SOA B.Tech is offered by the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER), Bhubaneswar. The programme covers CSE and multiple CSE specialisations along with ECE, EEE, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.

Which entrance exam is accepted?

SOA publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates. The university also has its own admission route for applicable programmes; check the current admission round before applying.

SOA B.Tech Eligibility 2026

Eligibility is branch-specific. CSE and its listed specialisations require 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics plus the prescribed third subject and aggregate. Civil, Electrical and Mechanical branches follow their applicable PCM requirements.

AreaRequirement
Qualification10+2 / equivalent
Core subjectsPhysics and Mathematics for applicable CSE/technology branches
AggregateProgramme-specific minimum; category relaxation applies where prescribed

SOA B.Tech FAQs

Does SOA accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. SOA publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates.

Is SAAT required for B.Tech?

The applicable admission route depends on the round and candidate category. SOA also publishes a JEE Main route for B.Tech.

What is the SOA B.Tech placement salary?
