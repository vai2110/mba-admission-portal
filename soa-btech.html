SOA B.Tech: Quick Answer

SOA's B.Tech programmes are offered through the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER), Bhubaneswar. The portfolio includes CSE, CSE specialisations such as AI/ML, Cyber Security and Data Science, plus CSIT, IoT, ECE, EEE, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.

Who is this page for?

Students comparing SOA B.Tech branches, fees, admission routes, eligibility and placement outcomes before applying for the 2026 intake.

Why Consider SOA B.Tech?

The main decision factors are branch availability, published fee, admission route, curriculum, engineering infrastructure and reported placement outcomes. SOA's Engineering category was ranked 22 in NIRF 2025.

SOA B.Tech Eligibility 2026

SOA's published eligibility is branch-specific. CSE and the listed CSE technology specialisations require Physics and Mathematics with one prescribed additional subject. Core branches such as Civil, Electrical and Mechanical follow their published PCM requirements.

Branch groupPublished academic requirement
CSE / CSIT / AI-ML / Cyber Security / Data Science / IoT10+2 with Physics and Mathematics plus one prescribed subject and the required aggregate.
Civil / Electrical / Mechanical10+2 with the prescribed PCM combination and aggregate.
Category relaxationSOA publishes lower aggregate thresholds for eligible reserved categories.

SOA B.Tech Curriculum & Subjects

The B.Tech curriculum is structured around foundational engineering/science subjects followed by branch-specific technical courses, laboratories, projects and practical learning. CSE-focused branches progressively move into programming, data structures, computer systems, software-oriented subjects and their respective specialisations.

SOA B.Tech FAQs

Does SOA accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. SOA publishes a JEE Main route for applicable B.Tech admissions.

Is SAAT compulsory for B.Tech?

The applicable route depends on the admission round and candidate category. Follow the current B.Tech admission instructions on SOA's official portal.

What is the SOA B.Tech fee?

Published annual academic fees range from ₹2.35 lakh for Civil/Electrical/Mechanical to ₹3.35 lakh for listed CSE specialisations.

Which SOA B.Tech branch has the best placement?

SOA's NIRF submission is discipline-level and does not provide a reliable branch-wise median for every CSE specialisation. Compare branch curriculum, skills, internships and the latest institute-level placement disclosures.

What is the SOA B.Tech median salary?

₹6 lakh for the 2023-24 engineering graduating cohort in NIRF 2025.
