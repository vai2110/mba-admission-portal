SOA B.Tech: Fees, Admission 2026, Eligibility & Placements

SOA B.Tech is offered by the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER). The programme list includes Civil, CSE, CSE-AI & ML, CSE-Cyber Security, CSE-Data Science, CSE-IoT, CSIT, Electrical, EEE, ECE and Mechanical Engineering.

SOA B.Tech Eligibility 2026

Eligibility is branch-specific. CSE and its listed specialisations require the prescribed Physics/Mathematics requirements and minimum aggregate; other branches have their own subject combinations.

How to Get Admission to SOA B.Tech

SOA publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates. SAAT is another route used for applicable programmes. Check the specific 2026 round before submitting documents.

SOA B.Tech Fees 2026

The published SOA fee structure is branch-specific. The latest 2026 admission portal should be treated as the controlling source for academic fee, hostel, mess and other charges.

FAQs

Does SOA accept JEE Main for B.Tech?

Yes. SOA publishes B.Tech admission rounds for JEE Main candidates.

Which B.Tech branches are available?

SOA offers CSE and multiple CSE specialisations along with ECE, EEE, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.

What is the SOA B.Tech placement salary?

The latest NIRF figure is a ₹6 lakh median salary for the 2023-24 graduating cohort.
