How much does ABV-IIITM Gwalior cost in 2026?

For new 2026 admissions:

The official fee notice gives a ₹1,68,750 grand total for the B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG group and ₹1,53,750 for MBA. These totals combine institute fee, hostel, mess, one-time admission payments and refundable hostel-mess security.

Because the one-time and security components are included in the admission-year grand total, students should not interpret the entire amount as annual tuition.

ABV-IIITM hostel and mess fees

For new 2026 admissions, hostel room and basic electricity charges are ₹15,000 per semester, while hostel mess is ₹20,000 per semester. The total hostel component is therefore ₹35,000 per semester in the official fee notice.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior Fees FAQs

What is the B.Tech fee at ABV-IIITM Gwalior in 2026?

For new 2026 admission, the official notice lists ₹1,68,750 as the grand total for the B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG group. This includes institute fee, one semester hostel and mess, one-time payments and refundable hostel-mess security as shown in the notice.

What is the MBA fee at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The official 2026 fee notice lists ₹1,53,750 as the grand total for a new MBA admission under the stated structure. This includes ₹90,000 institute fee, ₹35,000 hostel fee, ₹23,750 one-time payment and ₹5,000 refundable hostel-mess security.

What is the tuition fee at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For new 2026 admissions, tuition is ₹95,000 per semester for the B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG group and ₹80,000 per semester for MBA. Other institute charges are added to tuition.

Is hostel compulsory at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The institute provides separate hostels for boys and girls, and the official new-admission fee notice includes hostel and mess charges in its grand total. Students should follow the institute's current hostel allotment rules for their programme and batch.

Is the security deposit refundable?

The 2026 fee notice identifies the ₹5,000 institute security deposit and ₹5,000 library security deposit as refundable. The hostel mess security is another refundable ₹5,000 item in the admission-year total.

Does ABV-IIITM Gwalior fee include mess?

Yes, the admission-year structure includes a separate ₹20,000 per-semester hostel mess fee. It is shown separately from the ₹15,000 hostel room/basic electricity component.
