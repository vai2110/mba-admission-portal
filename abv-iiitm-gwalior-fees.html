How much does ABV-IIITM Gwalior cost in 2026?

Quick answer:

ABV-IIITM Gwalior's official 2026 fee notice lists an admission-year grand total of ₹1,68,750 for the B.Tech/BEE/BMS/IMT/IPG group and ₹1,53,750 for MBA. These are not annual tuition figures: they combine one semester of institute fee, one semester of hostel and mess, one-time charges and refundable security deposits.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior is an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, located on Morena Link Road in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. The institute offers undergraduate, integrated, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across technology and management. For students comparing colleges, the most important distinction is between the official admission-year amount and the estimated total programme cost at current rates.

What are the hostel and mess fees at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For new 2026 admissions, hostel room and basic electricity charges are ₹15,000 per semester and hostel mess is ₹20,000 per semester, making the hostel component ₹35,000 per semester. The institute is residential and provides separate hostels for boys and girls. Students should follow the current hostel-allotment rules for their batch.

Campus connectivity is practical for students and visitors: the institute is about 7 km from Gwalior railway station and about 12 km from the airport, with local transport and an institute bus service.

What is ABV-IIITM Gwalior's latest NIRF ranking?

In NIRF 2025, ABV-IIITM Gwalior was ranked #96 in Engineering and #93 in Management. These are category-specific ranks, so students should compare the category relevant to their programme rather than treating one rank as a universal college ranking.

The institute's older prospectus states NAAC accreditation with an A grade, but that source is from 2019. Because accreditation status can change, this page does not present the old grade as a current 2026 accreditation claim without a newer official confirmation.

What are the placements at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The institute's 2024-25 placement material reports 80+ companies visiting for B.Tech, M.Tech and MBA placements, a ₹67.5 LPA highest package, and a 90% placement percentage for students meeting the stated 6 CGPA+ eligibility condition. Programme-wise average packages reported by the institute were ₹27.23 LPA for BCS, ₹29.59 LPA for IPG-M.Tech and ₹28.49 LPA for IPG-MBA.

Examples of companies named in the institute's placement material include Google, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Uber, Intuit, Samsung, Nutanix, DE Shaw and Curefit. The placement report also mentions PPOs from companies including Google, Amazon and Goldman Sachs.

For a more conservative comparison, NIRF-submitted data provides median salary figures for graduating batches. For 2023-24, the Engineering UG 4-year group reported a ₹22 lakh median salary with 54 of 73 graduates placed; the Management PG 2-year group reported an ₹8 lakh median salary with 2 of 16 graduates placed. These NIRF figures and the institute's placement-season figures use different reporting definitions and should not be mixed.

What is campus life like at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

ABV-IIITM has a large residential campus with academic buildings, laboratories, hostels, a library and student facilities. Its Learning Resource Centre has nearly 30,000 books plus journals, theses and digital resources.

Sports infrastructure includes a swimming pool, gymnasium, lawn-tennis court, basketball court, billiards room, squash courts, badminton courts, table tennis facilities, cricket ground and football field. The campus also has a health centre; the institute states that ambulance service is available 24×7.

For a student, the practical takeaway is that ABV-IIITM offers a residential, technology-focused campus rather than a commuter-college experience. Gwalior railway station is about 7 km away, while the airport is about 12 km from campus.
