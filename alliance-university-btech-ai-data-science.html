Alliance University B.Tech AI & Data Science: What You Learn

Alliance University's B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is designed for students who want a technology degree with a stronger focus on artificial intelligence, data and analytics. The programme sits close to the CSE ecosystem but is more directly aligned to data-driven applications.

Choose this programme when you are comfortable building strong mathematics/programming fundamentals and want your projects and electives to move toward AI, machine learning and data-centric careers.

AI & DS vs CSE

Pick AI & DS for deeper early focus on AI/data. Pick CSE if you want the broadest software and computing base with the freedom to specialise later.

B.Tech AI & Data Science Eligibility

The programme is a regular four-year B.Tech. Alliance's current engineering information specifies a 10+2 qualifying background with Physics and Mathematics plus one of the listed subjects and a minimum marks requirement. Reserved-category relaxation is also stated on the programme page.

Do I need JEE for AI & Data Science?

No. The current programme page lists JEE Main, JEE Advanced/state-level routes and AUEET depending on the applicable admission route. Students without JEE can therefore explore AUEET if they meet the current criteria.

Is this course suitable for non-maths students?

AI and data science is mathematics-heavy. Students should be comfortable with quantitative subjects, statistics and logical problem-solving before choosing the programme.

Alliance University B.Tech AI & Data Science FAQs

What is the duration of B.Tech AI & Data Science?

The regular programme is four years across eight semesters.

Can I do B.Tech AI & Data Science without JEE?

Yes. AUEET and other accepted routes are available subject to the current eligibility and selection rules.

What is the AI & Data Science fee?

The current official page lists ₹25,000 registration fee and ₹20 lakh total programme fee for Indian nationals.

What is the B.Tech placement package at Alliance?

Alliance reports ₹60.1 LPA highest, ₹5.7 LPA average and ₹5 LPA median for B.Tech for academic years 2024-25 and 2025-26. It does not currently publish a separate AI & DS salary table on the placement page.

Should I choose AI & DS or CSE?

Choose AI & DS for an earlier focus on AI/data. Choose CSE if you want broader software and computing flexibility.
