BBAU MBA Specialisations

The Management Studies page lists MBA options including Rural Management, Financial Management, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Innovation Entrepreneurship & Venture Development and Defence Studies & Strategic Management.

MBA Eligibility

The department page lists a graduate degree with 50% marks from a recognised university, with 45% for SC/ST/PH candidates. Students should verify the 2026-27 prospectus for the exact admission route and any entrance-score requirement applicable in the current cycle.

MBA Admission 2026

BBAU publishes a 2026-27 PG prospectus, score lists, merit phases and spot-round notices. Because PG processes can vary, candidates should follow the latest MBA/Management notice rather than a generic third-party admission schedule.

See admission process →

MBA Fees

The Management Studies page currently lists MBA Rural Management at ₹5,000 per semester. The same page displays ₹45,000 per semester alongside a USD 500 foreign-candidate figure for the other listed MBA specialisations; students should verify the current domestic fee in the 2026-27 prospectus before payment.

MBA Placement Evidence

BBAU's NIRF Management disclosure provides a much better benchmark than a single advertised highest package. For the 2024-25 graduating batch, 74 of 76 reported two-year management students were placed and the median salary was ₹15.135 lakh. The previous batches reported 72 placed with ₹12.78 lakh median and 70 placed with ₹10.40 lakh median.
