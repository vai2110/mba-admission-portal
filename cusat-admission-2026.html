CUSAT Admission 2026: Identify Your Route Before Applying

CUSAT does not have one universal admission formula. The route changes by programme. B.Tech applicants, MBA applicants, MCA applicants and M.Tech applicants must follow different combinations of entrance tests, eligibility rules and option registration.

Eligibility: Course-Specific Rules Apply

Eligibility is not interchangeable across CUSAT courses. Engineering requires the prescribed higher-secondary subject combination; MBA requires a bachelor's degree and the prescribed qualifying marks; MCA uses mathematics/aptitude requirements; M.Tech rules depend on the specialisation.

B.TechCheck Physics/Mathematics and other subject requirements in the 2026 prospectus for the exact branch and category.
MBASMS states a bachelor's degree from a recognised university with minimum qualifying marks under CUSAT norms.
MCAThe CAT test includes substantial mathematics and logical-reasoning content; the qualifying degree rules must also be checked.
M.TechRelevant engineering/science qualification plus GATE or departmental route depending on programme.

Application and Selection Process

  1. Identify the programme and category. Confirm whether the course requires CAT, CUET, GATE or another test.
  2. Register on the official CUSAT admissions portal. Do not rely on a separate coaching-site form.
  3. Upload/enter the required academic and category information.
  4. Take the applicable test or update the accepted score.
  5. Track rank list, option registration and allotment notices.
  6. Pay the prescribed fee within the published window and complete document verification.

For MBA, SMS explicitly states that CAT/CMAT/KMAT scores are considered and that the final rank uses test score, interview and Class XII performance. citeturn1search0

CUSAT Admission FAQs

Can I get CUSAT admission without CAT?

Some programmes use other routes or accept specific tests, and some categories may have CUET/GATE/departmental routes. You must check the exact programme in the 2026 prospectus.

Is CUSAT CAT compulsory for MBA?

SMS accepts CAT, CMAT and KMAT for MBA programmes. The final selection also includes a personal interview and Class XII marks.

Where should I check CUSAT 2026 admission dates?

Use the official CUSAT admissions portal because it publishes allotments, real-time admission notices and programme-specific updates.
