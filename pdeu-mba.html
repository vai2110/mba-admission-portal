PDEU MBA: What does the programme offer?

Quick answer

PDEU's School of Management offers a two-year full-time MBA. Published programme material lists areas such as Finance, Marketing, Operations, Human Resources, Business Analytics and Energy & Infrastructure Management.

The School of Management evolved from the university's petroleum-management heritage and retains a strong energy/infrastructure orientation. It also positions itself around analytics, general management, industry interaction and corporate exposure.

PDEU MBA Admission 2026

Eligibility

Published MBA rules require a bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate marks, with a 5 percentage-point relaxation for reserved categories. Final-year students can apply subject to the relevant cycle conditions.

Entrance exams

Published School of Management material has considered CAT, XAT and NMAT. Some older cycles mention additional tests, so the current admission notice should be treated as the final authority.

Selection

Entrance performance is followed by institute-level evaluation. Published criteria include Written Ability Test, Group Discussion, Personal Interview, work experience and academic performance.

PDEU MBA FAQs

Is PDEU MBA worth it?

It can be, especially for students interested in energy, infrastructure, analytics and general management in the Gujarat market. The latest NIRF management data reports a ₹8.50 LPA median salary for 2023-24, but you should compare the current fee and role mix with alternatives.

What is PDEU MBA eligibility?

Published rules require a recognised bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate marks, with 45% for reserved categories. Final-year students can apply subject to the admission cycle's conditions.

Which exams are accepted for PDEU MBA?

Published School of Management material has considered CAT, XAT and NMAT. Because exam acceptance can change by cycle, check the current 2026 policy.

Does PDEU MBA have WAT and GDPI?

Yes. Published selection criteria include Written Ability Test, Group Discussion and Personal Interview after entrance-score shortlisting.

What is the PDEU MBA fee?

A 2025 secondary listing reported around ₹8.1 lakh tuition. Older official policies show different structures, so this should not be presented as the confirmed 2026 fee. Check the current School of Management offer document.

What is PDEU MBA average package?

The most comparable official figure is the NIRF median: ₹8.50 LPA for the 2023-24 graduating cohort. Median is not the same as average.

What is PDEU MBA placement percentage?

NIRF 2025 reports 117 placements among 121 graduates in minimum stipulated time for 2023-24, or about 96.7%.

Which MBA specialisation is best at PDEU?

There is no single best specialisation. Finance suits finance roles, Marketing suits brand/customer roles, Business Analytics suits data-led roles, and Energy & Infrastructure suits sector-specific careers. Match it to your target job.

Is PDEU MBA residential?

The School of Management's MBA has been described as a full-time residential programme in published material. Hostel availability and charges should be checked for the current batch.

What is PDEU NIRF Management rank?

PDEU ranked 89 in NIRF 2025 Management with a score of 46.81.

When was the previous PDEU MBA application deadline?

The 2025 MBA/PGDM application deadline was reported as 28 February 2025. Treat it as a planning reference rather than a fixed future deadline.

Where is PDEU School of Management?

The School of Management is at PDEU's Raisan campus in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
