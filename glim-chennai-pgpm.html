Great Lakes Chennai PGPM: One-Year MBA for Experienced Professionals

The Great Lakes PGPM is a one-year residential management programme designed for professionals with prior work experience. The institute positions it as its flagship one-year MBA programme, with a strong emphasis on applied management, leadership, analytics, consulting and industry exposure.

1 yearResidential programme

Is PGPM better than PGDM for an experienced candidate?

Not automatically. PGPM is the faster one-year route for experienced professionals, while PGDM provides two years of management education and is intended mainly for freshers and early-career candidates. Great Lakes itself says candidates with 2-3 years of experience may be eligible for either programme, so the choice should depend on career goals, experience and the value you place on a longer academic experience.

Great Lakes PGPM Eligibility 2027-28

For the 2027-28 PGPM admission process, Great Lakes requires a recognised bachelor's degree and considers candidates with 2+ years of work experience. The institute also publishes entrance-test requirements for the applicable admission cycle.

RequirementPGPM 2027-28
EducationBachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised institution
Academic performanceMinimum 50% across Class 10, Class 12 and graduation
Work experience2+ years considered
Entrance examsCAT 2025/2026, GMAT after 1 Jan 2024, XAT 2026/2027, NMAT 2025 onwards
FreshersGreat Lakes directs freshers and candidates with less than 24 months of experience to PGDM

Great Lakes does not state a single mandatory percentile cutoff for PGPM. It says that entrance scores are evaluated together with academics, work experience, essays, achievements and interviews.

Great Lakes PGPM Admission Process

How does PGPM selection work?

StageWhat students should expect
Online applicationSubmit the Great Lakes application with academic, test and profile details.
Initial screeningApplications are assessed using test scores, academics, work experience and overall profile.
Early / video assessmentSelected applicants may be evaluated through an AI-proctored/video interview process.
Interview roundsGreat Lakes uses interview stages to assess thought process, communication, goals, business awareness and fit.
Final selectionThe admissions committee considers the complete profile before releasing offers.

What does Great Lakes look for?

  • Consistent academic performance and ability to handle an intensive programme.
  • Relevant professional experience and demonstrated performance.
  • Management aptitude, communication and clarity of career goals.
  • Achievements, leadership and personal/professional initiatives.
  • Diversity of background and potential contribution to the class.
Important for applicants

Great Lakes states that CAT is used only for screening and that IIMs have no role in conducting the Great Lakes programme. Similarly, XAT is used only for screening and Great Lakes is not an associate institute of XLRI.

Great Lakes PGPM Fees 2026-27

The official fee structure for Indian students totals ₹23.50 lakh. Because the programme is residential, accommodation is included in the published total and is mandatory.

What does the PGPM fee include?

  • Tuition and programme charges.
  • Reading material, case studies, textbooks and online resources.
  • Single AC accommodation, subject to allocation; twin sharing may be provided if single allocation is unavailable.
  • Vegetarian meals are included. Non-vegetarian food is optional and charged extra.
  • Refundable caution deposit and alumni subscription.

PGPM scholarships

Great Lakes currently lists 40 Diversity & Merit Scholarships worth ₹5 lakh each for PGPM students, with at least 30% reserved for female candidates. The Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship can provide a 100% tuition fee waiver to exceptional candidates.

Great Lakes PGPM Curriculum & Specialisations

The one-year PGPM is structured across seven terms. The first terms focus on core management subjects, followed by electives and applied learning. Great Lakes highlights experiential learning, data-driven decision making and industry relevance.

AnalyticsStatistical Methods in Decision-Making, Making Sense of Data, Storytelling with Data

Students should check the latest curriculum before choosing a specialisation because elective baskets and course structures can change with each cohort.

Who Should Choose Great Lakes PGPM?

Strong fit

  • Professionals with 2+ years of experience who want a one-year management qualification.
  • Candidates targeting consulting, analytics, product, technology or strategy roles.
  • Applicants looking for a residential, intensive programme with strong industry interaction.
  • Professionals who want to accelerate their career rather than take a two-year break.

Think carefully if

  • You are a fresher; PGDM is the more natural Great Lakes route.
  • You need a low-cost programme and the ₹23.50 lakh total cost is difficult to finance.
  • You want a relaxed academic schedule; the one-year PGPM is highly intensive.
  • You are choosing the programme solely on average or highest CTC.

Great Lakes PGPM FAQs

What is the Great Lakes PGPM fee?

The official 2026-27 fee for Indian students is ₹23.50 lakh, including tuition, programme fee, single AC accommodation, refundable caution deposit and alumni subscription.

What is the eligibility for Great Lakes PGPM?

Candidates need a bachelor's degree from a recognised institution, minimum 50% across Class 10, Class 12 and graduation, and 2+ years of work experience is considered.

Which exams does Great Lakes accept for PGPM?

For the 2027-28 process, Great Lakes lists CAT 2025/2026, GMAT scores after 1 January 2024, XAT 2026/2027 and NMAT 2025 onwards.

Does Great Lakes PGPM have a CAT cutoff?

Great Lakes does not publish a fixed minimum CAT, XAT, GMAT or NMAT cutoff for PGPM and states that it follows a holistic evaluation.

What is the PGPM average package?

The latest official PGPM Class of 2026 report lists an average CTC of ₹18.1 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹43 LPA.

Is PGPM equivalent to an MBA?

Great Lakes describes PGPM as its one-year MBA programme. Applicants should still verify the exact qualification wording and equivalence requirements relevant to their target employer, higher studies or government application.
