XLRI Jamshedpur Placements: Student Decision Snapshot

The official XLRI final placement report for the 2024-26 batch covers 576 students from the Jamshedpur and Delhi-NCR campuses. It records 145 recruiting organisations and 25 new recruiters. More than 576 domestic offers and 2 international offers were made. The average salary was ₹31.4 lakh and the median was ₹29 lakh. The highest domestic offer was ₹59 lakh and the highest international offer was ₹1.10 crore. The top 10% and top 25% average salaries were ₹49.2 lakh and ₹44.18 lakh respectively. Consulting accounted for 36% of roles, BFSI 18%, ITES 15% and sales and marketing 12%.

Recruiters, Job Roles & Career Functions

The official report identifies Accenture Strategy and Consulting, Accenture Technology, Aditya Birla Group, Amazon, American Express, Bajaj Auto, Boston Consulting Group, Deloitte USI, EY Parthenon, HUL, ITC, Kotak Mahindra Bank and PwC among recruiters making high numbers of offers. Functions and roles reported

XLRI's official report records hiring across consulting, BFSI, sales and marketing, ITES, e-commerce, general management, HR, manufacturing, FMCG, pharma and energy-related sectors. BFSI roles included investment banking, asset management, portfolio management, global and corporate banking, wealth management, global markets, equity research and retail banking. HR roles included HR consulting, recruitment, compensation and benefits, HR analytics and chief-of-staff roles.

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.
