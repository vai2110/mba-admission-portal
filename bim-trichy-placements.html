BIM MBA Placements vs Fees: what does the data suggest?

The 2026-28 institute fee is ₹18.71 lakh before mess, while BIM 40 recorded a ₹10.46 LPA average and ₹10 LPA median CTC. The highest offer was ₹35.26 LPA.

This does not mean the fee is recovered in one year. CTC includes components that are not take-home salary. Loan interest, tax, variable pay, relocation and the actual role all matter. The strongest case for BIM is therefore based on career fit, recruiter quality and median/average outcomes, not only the highest package.

BIM Placements FAQs

What is the median package at BIM?

The BIM 40 median CTC was ₹10 LPA. Median is a useful benchmark because it is less influenced by a few very high offers.

Does BIM have 100% placement?

BIM states that it consistently achieves 100% placement of qualified graduates. Its placement reporting has been audited by CRISIL since 2022 under IPRS2.

Which companies recruit from BIM?

Recruiting partners listed by BIM include Accenture, Deloitte, EY, CRISIL, Barclays, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Infosys, Tiger Analytics, ITC, Nestlé, TVS, BMW and others.

What is the BIM internship stipend?

The latest annual report reports an average stipend of ₹19,000 per month for BIM 41's Corporate Immersion Programme, with 36 firms participating.

Is BIM placement only for finance and marketing?

No. Recruiter participation spans finance, banking, consulting, technology, analytics, FMCG, automotive, manufacturing and other sectors. The actual role depends on specialisation, profile and recruiter requirements.
