GGSIPU Admission Process 2026

The 2026-27 official admission portal provides separate UG, PG and PhD brochures and directs applicants to programme-specific counselling and admission notices. The first step is to identify the exact programme and programme code.

StepWhat students should do
1. Select programmeIdentify course, programme code and preferred institutes.
2. Check eligibilityRead the programme-specific academic and entrance requirements.
3. ApplyComplete the applicable university/CET/national-test process.
4. Track merit/resultMonitor score, merit list, result and counselling notices.
5. CounsellingComplete choice filling, document verification and seat allocation as applicable.
6. AdmissionPay the required fee and complete reporting/verification.

GGSIPU Admission Routes

GGSIPU does not use one entrance test for every course. The 2026 UG brochure explicitly provides programme-wise information covering CET, NEET-based admissions, national tests and other admission criteria. The current counselling notices also show programme-specific routes such as CAT/CMAT/CET for MBA and NIMCET/CET for MCA.

Eligibility: Why You Must Check the Programme Code

Eligibility varies by course. UG programmes may require Class 12 subjects and minimum marks; PG programmes generally require a relevant bachelor's degree and may specify a national entrance score. Professional programmes can have additional subject, age or qualifying-exam requirements.

Important

Do not use a generic GGSIPU eligibility percentage for every programme. Always match the course name and programme code with the 2026-27 official brochure.

GGSIPU Counselling 2026

Counselling may be online or offline depending on the programme. The official 2026 notices include round-wise seat status, spot-round schedules, choice filling, reporting and programme-specific merit/cutoff updates. Applicants should keep documents, category certificates and fee-payment records ready.

Before choice filling

Compare institute, branch/programme, location, fee, hostel, placement evidence and previous closing ranks.

Before paying

Check the latest allotment notice, fee amount, refund rules and reporting instructions for your round.

GGSIPU Admission FAQs

When should I check the official GGSIPU website?

Check it before application, before choice filling, after every result/round and before reporting because schedules and seat status can change during counselling.

Is CET compulsory for MBA?

Not necessarily. Current 2026 counselling notices show MBA routes involving CAT, CMAT and CET, subject to the applicable programme rules.

Does GGSIPU conduct spot rounds?

Yes, programme-specific spot-round counselling notices are issued when applicable and may have separate registration, seat and reporting conditions.
