Welingkar E-Business: What Is It?

PGDM E-Business is a two-year management programme designed around the intersection of business and technology. It is aimed at students who want management fundamentals with stronger exposure to digital business and technology-enabled decision making.

Is E-Business the same as an MCA or technical degree?

No. It remains a management programme. Students should choose it for digital-business, product, e-commerce, analytics or technology-management interests rather than pure software engineering.

Who Should Choose E-Business?

Good fit

  • Digital transformation interests you.
  • You want product/e-commerce/business analytics exposure.
  • You want management rather than coding as your core career.

Think twice if

  • You want pure software development.
  • You prefer traditional finance/HR specialisation.
  • You have a stronger analytics-focused B-school option.
