Is SRMIST Good for Placements?

SRMIST has a large recruiting ecosystem, but the correct answer depends on the programme. The university-level 2023 figures show scale, while NIRF engineering data gives a separate view of engineering outcomes.

Do not use the ₹1 crore number as an expected package

₹1 crore is the reported maximum salary, not the average. The official 2023 university-level average was ₹10.5 LPA.

Why Is the Highest Package So Different from the Median?

A highest salary is one top outcome. An average is calculated across a group. A median is the middle value of a reported group. These numbers answer different student questions.

How Do You Read an SRMIST Placement Report?

Before quoting any package, identify the year, programme, cohort size and statistic type. Then ask whether the number is university-wide or branch-specific.

What Should You Ask Before Joining?

  • How many students were eligible and how many were placed?
  • Is the salary average, median or maximum?
  • Is the number university-wide or course-specific?
  • Which roles were actually offered to my branch?
  • How recent is the report?

FAQs Students Ask

Is ₹1 crore the average SRMIST package?

No. ₹1 crore per annum is the maximum salary reported in the official 2023 university-level figures. The reported average was ₹10.5 LPA.

What is the 2024-25 engineering median salary?

NIRF 2026 reports a ₹6.32 lakh median salary for the reported four-year UG engineering population.

Can I call ₹10.5 LPA the CSE average?

No. It is a university-level average, not a CSE-only statistic.

Why are the NIRF and university figures different?

They cover different reporting scopes and use different statistics. NIRF gives programme-category data; the older brochure gives university-level figures.

What should I check instead of the highest package?

Check median/average salary, placement count, eligible-student count, role mix and the year and programme covered.
