SRMIST Engineering Placements: 2024-25 Data

Official NIRF 2026 engineering data for 2024-25 reports 4,144 placements out of 4,408 graduates in four-year UG programmes. The median salary was ₹6.32 lakh. For two-year PG programmes, 595 of 621 graduates were placed and the median salary was ₹6.35 lakh.

Why is median salary lower than the older ₹10.5 LPA average?

The figures measure different cohorts and use different metrics. ₹10.5 LPA is an older university-level average; NIRF reports programme-category median salary for the specified graduating cohort. They should not be compared as if they were the same statistic.

What Do SRMIST Placement Numbers Actually Tell You?

  • Average salary: useful for a broad university-level view but can be influenced by high salaries.
  • Median salary: the middle salary of the reported cohort and often more useful for judging a typical outcome.
  • Highest package: shows the top offer, not the normal outcome.
  • Companies/offers: indicate recruitment activity but do not guarantee an offer for every branch.

For a course decision, use the programme-specific information first and the university placement figures as context.

SRMIST Placement FAQs

Is ₹1 crore the average SRMIST package?

No. ₹1 crore per annum is the highest salary in the official 2023 university-level figures. The reported average was ₹10.5 LPA.

Is ₹10.5 LPA the current salary for every SRMIST course?

No. It is a university-level average from the official 2023 figures and is not a programme-wise guarantee.

What is the latest official engineering placement figure available here?

NIRF 2026 data for 2024-25 reports 4,144 placed out of 4,408 four-year UG graduates, with a ₹6.32 lakh median salary.
