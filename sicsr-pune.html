SICSR Pune MBA IT: key details at a glance

SICSR (Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research) is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University), located at Model Colony, Pune. Its MBA-IT is a full-time, four-semester postgraduate programme combining management subjects with information technology and technology-enabled business applications.

What does SICSR's MBA in Information Technology offer?

The programme is designed for graduates who want to work where business decisions, software, data and IT operations meet. It is not simply a software-development degree and it is not a conventional general MBA without a technology focus.

What will students study?

The published programme structure includes technology and management foundations such as relational database management, software engineering and agile practices, IT-enabled management, business statistics for data analysis, digital marketing, enterprise resource planning, Python, PHP, web development frameworks and research methodology. SICSR MBA IT eligibility and selection process

Who is eligible?

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university or an Institution of National Importance.
  • At least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent grade.
  • 45% aggregate marks or equivalent grade for Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe applicants, as stated on SICSR's eligibility page.

The institute's published MBA-IT intake is 60 students. Applicants should read the current university and institute rules for final-year status, documents and category-specific conditions before submitting the application.

How does SICSR select MBA-IT students?

  1. Register for SNAP: Complete the SNAP test application and appear for the test.
  2. Register separately for SICSR: Select SICSR and the MBA-IT programme in the institute/programme registration process and pay the applicable programme fee.
  3. Check the shortlist: SICSR publishes the list of candidates called for the next stage based on SNAP performance.
  4. Attend GE and PI: Shortlisted candidates book a slot and participate in the Group Exercise and Personal Interaction.
  5. Check the merit list: Follow the merit-list, offer acceptance and fee-payment instructions issued by SICSR.

The published 2025–27 programme document described the final composite score as SNAP 50%, GE 10% and PI 40%. Treat that weighting as the previous published batch's criterion; confirm the 2027 intake's selection weightage in the current admission notice.

What is the SICSR Pune MBA IT SNAP cutoff?

SICSR's official MBA-IT pages state that candidates are shortlisted through SNAP and then called for GE-PI. The official pages reviewed do not publish a fixed numeric SNAP cutoff or a guaranteed percentile for MBA-IT.

For that reason, this page does not present an unofficial percentile as an official cutoff. Your shortlist depends on the admission cycle and the institute's selection process. A SNAP score or percentile by itself does not guarantee an offer: the institute also considers the subsequent selection stages and final merit list.

Student takeaway: Apply to SICSR through the separate institute registration even after registering for SNAP. Track the official shortlist rather than relying on coaching-site cutoff predictions.

Is SICSR Pune MBA IT worth considering?

It may suit you if…

  • You want a management qualification with a clear IT, analytics, infrastructure or software-process orientation.
  • You are interested in roles such as business analyst, data analyst, quality analyst, application/software process roles or IT project coordination.
  • You are comfortable with a technology-heavy curriculum that includes databases, programming foundations and IT-enabled business subjects.

Think carefully if…

  • You want a broad general-management MBA with no technology specialisation.
  • Your main decision depends on a high salary outcome: the latest official MBA-IT average reported is about ₹5.98 LPA, not a guaranteed individual salary.
  • You need a confirmed all-inclusive hostel cost: the institute lists different accommodation types and revises hostel/mess charges separately.

SICSR Pune MBA IT: frequently asked questions

1. What is the total academic fee for SICSR MBA IT 2026–28?

The official fee schedule lists ₹2.85 lakh for each of four instalments, making the two-year academic fee ₹11.40 lakh. A refundable institute deposit of ₹20,000 is charged with the first instalment, so the academic fee plus institute deposit is ₹11.60 lakh. Hostel, mess, taxes and personal expenses are separate.

2. What is the first instalment payable after admission?

The first academic instalment is ₹2.85 lakh and the refundable institute deposit is ₹20,000. The total shown for the first instalment is therefore ₹3.05 lakh. Check the offer letter for the actual payment deadline and payment instructions.

3. Is hostel and mess fee included in SICSR MBA IT fees?

No. SICSR lists hostel and mess charges separately. For the Model Colony campus, the published 2026–27 three-sharing hostel fee is ₹1,23,200, mess is ₹1,19,600 and refundable hostel deposit is ₹20,000. These add up to ₹2,62,800 for the first year under that accommodation option. Other sharing types cost differently.

4. What is the SNAP cutoff for SICSR Pune MBA IT?

SICSR's official MBA-IT admission pages do not state a fixed numeric cutoff. The institute says SNAP is used for shortlisting candidates for GE-PI. Avoid treating unofficial predicted percentiles as a confirmed cutoff or admission guarantee.

5. Is SNAP compulsory for SICSR MBA IT admission?

Yes. SICSR's stated selection route uses the Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP), followed by Group Exercise and Personal Interaction for shortlisted applicants. You must also complete SICSR's separate programme registration; SNAP registration alone does not complete the institute application.

6. Can I apply to SICSR MBA IT without separately registering with SICSR?

No. The institute schedule separately lists the SICSR registration and payment closing date. Complete the SNAP form and the SICSR programme application, selecting MBA-IT, within the relevant deadlines.

7. What is the eligibility for SICSR MBA IT?

A graduate from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade is eligible under the published criteria. The stated minimum is 45% for SC/ST applicants. Check the institute's current eligibility notice for documentation and any updated university rules.

8. What is the intake for SICSR MBA IT?

SICSR's MBA-IT eligibility page lists an intake of 60 students. The same page outlines reservation provisions; applicants should consult the official page for the exact category-wise rules applicable to their application.

9. What are the specialisations in SICSR MBA IT?

The current SICSR programme overview highlights Banking and Financial Markets, Data Analytics, IT Infrastructure Management and Software Process Management. The institute may update course offerings, so verify the specialisation choices and availability for your joining batch before deciding.

10. What is the average package of SICSR MBA IT?

SICSR's Placement Report Batch 2025 reports average annual compensation of ₹5,97,622 (about ₹5.98 LPA) for MBA-IT under PG Placement Details 2023–2025. This is the published programme-specific average in that report, not a salary guarantee for every student or the current incoming batch.

11. What is the highest package of SICSR MBA IT?

The same official report lists the highest annual compensation for MBA-IT as ₹8,40,000 (₹8.40 LPA) for the 2023–2025 PG placement data. Compare this with the average and the programme fee; the highest offer is not representative of every student's outcome.

12. What is the placement percentage at SICSR MBA IT?

The latest MBA-IT table in SICSR's 2025 placement report gives average and highest compensation but does not show the eligible-student and placed-student counts for MBA-IT. Therefore, a reliable MBA-IT placement percentage cannot be calculated from that report. Do not substitute the institute-wide 100% placement assistance statement for a verified MBA-IT placement rate.

13. What kind of jobs can I get after SICSR MBA IT?

SICSR's stated programme outcomes refer to application programmer, business analyst, quality analyst and data analyst roles, along with technology and project-management capabilities. Actual roles depend on your specialisation, technical skills, internships, interview performance and the recruiters hiring in your graduating year.

14. Does SICSR MBA IT require a technical or engineering background?

The published eligibility criterion asks for a recognised bachelor's degree and the required marks; it does not restrict admission to engineering or computer-science graduates. However, the curriculum includes IT and programming-related subjects, so applicants from non-technical backgrounds should be prepared to build comfort with databases, software concepts and quantitative subjects.

15. Is SICSR MBA IT the same as SICSR MBA Digital Transformation?

No. MBA-IT and MBA-DT are distinct programmes at SICSR. Their curriculum, fee structure, admission details and placement reporting can differ. This page's ₹11.40 lakh academic fee and MBA-IT placement figures should not be used as the MBA-DT figures.

16. When does the SICSR MBA IT 2027 programme start?

The currently published MBA-IT admission schedule lists 1 June 2027 as the programme commencement date. SICSR describes its dates as tentative, so confirm the date in the final admission communication before making travel or accommodation arrangements.
