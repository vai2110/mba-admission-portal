What is SICSR Pune's MBA IT?

Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research (SICSR), Pune offers an MBA in Information Technology. It suits applicants seeking management education connected to IT and technology-enabled organisations. Compare its course structure with a general MBA before choosing.

The official 2026–28 fee page lists academic fees of ₹2,85,000 per instalment across four instalments and a refundable institute deposit of ₹20,000 with the first instalment. Hostel and mess charges are listed separately.

How do you apply?

SICSR states that SNAP and institute registration are mandatory. The institute's stated shortlisting criterion for Group Exercise and Personal Interaction is based on SNAP score; registering for SNAP alone is not enough if the programme application is incomplete.

  1. Register for the applicable SNAP test.
  2. Select SICSR and complete programme registration.
  3. Pay the institute application fee.
  4. Track the official shortlist and GE/PI schedule.
  5. Follow the final merit list and offer instructions.

Do not treat an unofficial percentile range as a guaranteed cutoff.

What eligibility and costs should you verify?

Review the current notice for degree requirements, minimum marks and final-year rules. For the total budget, add academic fees across two years, refundable deposits, hostel, mess, travel, laptop and personal expenses. Accommodation charges can vary by sharing type and may change.

If comparing SICSR with other Symbiosis programmes, remember that common use of SNAP does not mean identical curriculum, fees or placement outcomes.

Which careers align with this programme?

Investigate IT consulting, business analysis, technology management, systems, project coordination and digital-business roles. These are role families, not guaranteed placements. Use SICSR's latest official placement report to check actual job functions and salary definitions.
