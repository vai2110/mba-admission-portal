SITM Pune / SIDTM: College Overview

Symbiosis Institute of Digital and Telecom Management (SIDTM), formerly known as Symbiosis Institute of Telecom Management (SITM), is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The institute was established in 1996 and is based at the Lavale campus in Pune. It focuses on the intersection of digital technology, telecom and management. citeturn2search3

The current programme is a two-year full-time MBA in Digital and Telecom Management. The institute describes its objective as developing technology business leaders who can understand business requirements and deliver commercially viable technology solutions. citeturn3view0

What is the key difference from a general MBA?

SIDTM is a niche technology-management MBA. Its curriculum combines management with digital/telecom subjects, making it more relevant for students targeting technology consulting, digital business, analytics, systems, product, IT and related management roles.

MBA-DTM Courses & Specialisations

For the 2027-29 batch, SIDTM states that its two-year MBA-DTM will have three specialisations: Systems and Finance, Marketing and Finance, and AI and Analytics. This is an important change from the 2026-28 structure, which listed Analytics and Finance instead of AI and Analytics. citeturn2search1

Systems & Finance

Technology, systems, digital infrastructure and finance with career paths in consulting, cyber security, IT advisory, project and process roles.

Marketing & Finance

Technology-led marketing and finance with B2B/B2C marketing, digital business, product and communication-oriented career paths.

AI & Analytics

New 2027-29 specialisation focused on AI, analytics and technology-enabled decision making.

Eligibility

Applicants must be graduates of a recognised university or institution of national importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the stated minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Foreign university qualifications require the applicable AIU equivalence process. citeturn3view0

Who Should Apply to SIDTM Pune?

Good fit if
  • You want a specialised MBA rather than a general management MBA.
  • You are interested in digital business, IT, telecom, analytics or technology consulting.
  • You want to combine management with a technical/digital foundation.
  • You are comfortable with Pune's Lavale residential campus and the fee structure.
Consider alternatives if
  • You want a conventional MBA with broad specialisations such as HR, finance or general marketing.
  • Your target role does not benefit from technology/digital exposure.
  • Your budget is significantly below the published SIDTM fee structure.
  • You prefer an admission route other than SNAP.
Decision rule

Compare SIDTM with other SNAP colleges on your target role, total two-year cost, specialisation, placement trend and location—not only the highest package.
