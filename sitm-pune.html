SIDTM Pune MBA: key details at a glance

Symbiosis Institute of Digital and Telecom Management (SIDTM), formerly Symbiosis Institute of Telecom Management (SITM), is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). Established in 1996, the institute is based at Village Lavale in Pune and focuses on management education for the digital, telecom and technology ecosystem.

Its flagship programme is the two-year full-time MBA in Digital and Telecom Management (MBA-DTM). The course combines core management learning with technology-management areas such as digital systems, telecom, analytics, fintech, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, IoT, digital marketing and blockchain.

What makes SIDTM different from a general MBA? It is a domain-focused MBA, not a broad general-management programme. Its value is strongest when your target roles involve digital business, IT, telecom, technology consulting, analytics, product/process management or technology-led marketing.

SIDTM Pune MBA specialisations for 2027-29

For the 2027-29 batch, SIDTM lists three dual-specialisation tracks. Each has a stated intake of 60 students. The first semester is common; specialisation teaching starts from the second semester.

Systems and Finance

Combines business and technology for students interested in digital systems, business processes, product development and project management. SIDTM mentions industry-oriented training in cybersecurity, DevOps, internal audit and AWS cloud.

Possible career directions: IT advisory, technology consulting, process/project roles, cybersecurity-related management and systems roles.

Marketing and Finance

Blends marketing with technology applications. The institute highlights brand management, sales force and channel management, and marketing communication for B2B and B2C contexts.

Possible career directions: growth marketing, digital marketing, presales consulting, marketing communications and technology-led business development.

AI and Analytics

For 2027-29, AI and Analytics replaces the earlier Analytics and Finance track. The institute lists R, Python, Tableau, Power BI, IBM Cognos, data mining, social media analytics, big data, Generative AI tools and AI/ML applications.

Possible career directions: business analytics, analytics consulting, data-led decision support and AI-enabled business roles.

How is the specialisation allotted?

SIDTM states that students enter a common first semester. Allocation is merit-based using combined internal marks in Business Statistics, Network Concepts and Components, and Essentials of Marketing Management. Students are ranked by combined marks for allocation; the institute says the Specialisation Allocation Process/Test is announced in advance.

Important for applicants: the 2027-29 specialisation names are not identical to the 2026-28 tracks. AI and Analytics is the new track; do not use older “Analytics and Finance” information when planning for 2027-29.

SIDTM MBA eligibility: who can apply?

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance.
  • Minimum 50% marks or equivalent grade.
  • Minimum 45% marks or equivalent grade for SC/ST applicants.
  • Applicants with foreign university degrees must obtain the required equivalence certificate from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

SIDTM's FAQ states that graduates from disciplines such as BSc, Commerce, BBA and Biotechnology can apply if they meet the minimum graduation criteria. It also states that final-year students may apply provisionally, subject to submitting qualifying documents within the university's prescribed timeline. The official FAQ says part-time/correspondence degree candidates are not eligible.

Reservation and intake information

The institute lists a sanctioned intake of 180. Its published SIU reservation policy includes SC 15%, ST 7.5%, PwD 3% and children/wards of defence personnel 5% within sanctioned intake, with additional provisions for specified categories. Candidates applying under a reserved category must check the current policy and submit valid supporting documents.

SIDTM Pune admission process: SNAP to final merit list

  1. Register for SNAP 2026: Complete the common Symbiosis entrance test registration and choose the required test attempt(s).
  2. Register separately for SIDTM: Complete the institute/programme registration and fee payment. SIDTM says SNAP registration alone does not make a candidate eligible for its shortlist.
  3. Appear for SNAP: The 2026 test pattern published by SIDTM has 60 questions for 60 marks across General English, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, Quantitative/Data Interpretation/Data Sufficiency, and Ethics, Morality and Values.
  4. Shortlisting: Candidates who have applied, registered and paid for SIDTM are considered for GE-PI shortlisting after SNAP results.
  5. Attend GE and PI: Shortlisted candidates must attend the prescribed Group Exercise and Personal Interaction components.
  6. Final merit and offer: SIDTM publishes merit/wait lists; candidates must meet eligibility and pay fees by the stated deadline.

Is there a direct admission route? The official admission information describes SNAP plus institute registration followed by GE-PI and merit selection. It also mentions discretionary quota seats in the fee section, but says these are considered on academic record and entrance-test scores, with double academic fees. SIDTM/SIU explicitly states that no donation or capitation fee is charged. Do not treat agents or paid admission guarantees as authorised routes.

SIDTM Pune SNAP cutoff: is an official percentile published?

SIDTM's current official programme page explains that candidates are shortlisted based on SNAP performance after completing institute registration, but it does not provide a fixed overall SNAP percentile cutoff in the published admission information reviewed for this page.

What should you use instead of an unverified cutoff? Track your SNAP score/percentile, complete the SIDTM application before its deadline, and monitor the institute's official shortlist and category-wise merit/wait lists. A predicted cutoff from a coaching or admissions website is not an official SIDTM cutoff and should not be treated as an admission guarantee.

SIDTM states that final merit is based on SNAP (50%), Group Exercise (10%) and Personal Interaction (40%). A strong SNAP score is important, but it is not the only component of final selection.

Is SIDTM Pune the right MBA for you?

Consider SIDTM if…

You want a specialised MBA linking business with digital technology, telecom, systems, AI/analytics or technology-led marketing, and you are comfortable with the Lavale campus and residential costs.

Think carefully if…

You want a conventional MBA with broad standalone HR, operations or general finance tracks, or your career goal has little connection to technology-enabled businesses.

Before accepting an offer…

Compare the actual specialisation allocation process, total two-year living cost, latest placement report, role profiles and location—not just the highest package.

SIDTM Pune (SITM) MBA FAQs

Is SITM Pune now called SIDTM?

Yes. The institute changed its name from Symbiosis Institute of Telecom Management (SITM) to Symbiosis Institute of Digital and Telecom Management (SIDTM) as its focus expanded to digital technology and telecom management. Students may still search for the older SITM name.

Which MBA programme does SIDTM offer?

SIDTM offers a two-year full-time MBA in Digital and Telecom Management (MBA-DTM). The programme combines core management subjects with applied learning in digital, telecom and technology-management areas.

What are the SIDTM Pune specialisations for 2027-29?

The 2027-29 tracks are Systems and Finance, Marketing and Finance, and AI and Analytics. The institute lists 60 seats for each track and says the first semester is common before specialisation teaching begins.

Has SIDTM replaced Analytics and Finance with AI and Analytics?

Yes. The official programme page says AI and Analytics replaces the earlier Analytics and Finance specialisation from the 2027-29 batch. The older name applies to earlier batches such as 2026-28.

Is SNAP compulsory for SIDTM MBA admission?

Yes. Candidates must appear for SNAP and also complete separate SIDTM programme registration and payment. SIDTM states that only candidates who apply, register and pay for the institute are considered for GE-PI shortlisting.

What is the SIDTM SNAP cutoff for 2027 admission?

The official page reviewed for this guide does not publish a fixed overall percentile cutoff. It explains that shortlisting follows SNAP performance and institute registration. Check SIDTM's shortlist and category-wise merit/wait lists rather than treating unofficial predictions as confirmed cutoffs.

What is the SIDTM MBA eligibility?

A recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade is required. The stated minimum is 45% for SC/ST applicants. Foreign degrees require AIU equivalence. SIDTM's FAQ says final-year students may apply provisionally and correspondence/part-time degrees are not eligible.

What is the SIDTM MBA fee for Indian students?

The published 2026-28 fee schedule lists ₹8.45 lakh academic fee per annum and a refundable institute deposit of ₹20,000. Hostel, mess, hostel deposit and applicable taxes are additional. The 2027-29 fee should be confirmed when its official notice is published.

How much are SIDTM hostel and mess fees?

For the Lavale Hill Top campus, the 2026-28 schedule lists hostel fees of ₹1,24,100 for four sharing, ₹1,57,300 for three sharing, ₹1,79,700 for twin sharing and ₹92,000 for dormitory, plus mess fees of ₹1,19,600 per year. A refundable hostel deposit of ₹20,000 is also listed. Second-year hostel and mess charges are communicated later.

What is the latest SIDTM Pune average package?

SIDTM's official placement page reports an average package of ₹13.32 LPA for the 2024-26 batch. It also lists a highest package of ₹25.41 LPA.

What is the highest package at SIDTM Pune?

The official 2024-26 final placement statistics list ₹25.41 LPA as the highest package. The same page reports top-15 average of ₹24.74 LPA and top-25 average of ₹21.32 LPA.

Does SIDTM provide 100% placements?

The institute describes a 100% placement record for eligible students. Its placement committee also states that placement is a facility and not a right. Students should read the placement report and eligibility terms for the relevant batch.

How are SIDTM specialisations allocated?

All students have a common first semester. SIDTM says specialisation allocation is based on combined internal marks in Business Statistics, Network Concepts and Components, and Essentials of Marketing Management, with allocation in descending order of marks.

Where is SIDTM Pune located?

SIDTM is at Village Lavale, Taluka Mulshi, Pune - 412115, Maharashtra, on the Symbiosis Lavale campus.

Is SIDTM suitable for non-engineering students?

SIDTM's FAQ says graduates from BSc, Commerce, BBA and Biotechnology backgrounds can apply if they meet the graduation marks requirement. The programme is a management degree with a technology focus, so applicants should be interested in digital and technology-enabled business roles.

Is there a management quota or guaranteed direct admission at SIDTM?

SIDTM's published process requires SNAP, institute registration and GE-PI selection. The fee page mentions discretionary quota seats subject to academic record and entrance-test scores, with double academic fees; it also states that no donation or capitation fee is charged. No agent can guarantee admission.
