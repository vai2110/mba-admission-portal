MBBS at SRMIST

SRMIST offers MBBS through its medical and health sciences ecosystem at Kattankulathur, including the SRM Medical College Hospital & Research Centre.

Eligibility

MBBS admission follows the applicable national medical eligibility rules, including the required Class 12 subjects and NEET-UG qualification.

Admission Through NEET-UG

NEET-UG is the entrance examination for MBBS. Admission is completed through the applicable medical counselling process and seat allocation.

MBBS Fees

Use the current SRMIST medical admission fee notice for the exact 2026-27 MBBS fee. A non-medical programme fee should not be used as an MBBS fee.

MBBS Cutoff

The effective cutoff depends on NEET rank, category, counselling authority, seat type and year. No single SRMIST closing rank is presented here as an official universal cutoff.

FAQs

Which exam is required for MBBS?

NEET-UG.

Which SRMIST campus has the medical college?

Kattankulathur.

Is there one fixed MBBS cutoff?

No. It depends on counselling, seat type, category and the admission year.
