What Is MBBS at SRMIST?

SRM Medical College Hospital and Research Centre at Kattankulathur is the medical institution associated with the SRMIST MBBS pathway. The decision here is very different from choosing a B.Tech or MBA because clinical training and medical postgraduate pathways are central to the outcome.

Who Can Apply for MBBS?

MBBS admission follows the applicable medical eligibility rules and NEET-UG. Students should match their Class 12 PCB qualifications and current counselling requirements with the 2026-27 medical admission information.

How Does SRMIST MBBS Admission Work?

The practical route is NEET-UG → applicable counselling → choice filling → seat allotment → institute reporting and fee/document formalities.

StageWhat the student does
NEET-UGAppear and obtain the relevant rank/score

What Is the SRMIST MBBS Cutoff?

There is no single closing rank that applies to every MBBS applicant. The closing position depends on NEET performance, counselling authority, category, seat type and the seats available in that round.

Do not use an unlabelled internet cutoff

Check the counselling authority, category and seat type before comparing a NEET closing rank.

Where Is the Medical Campus?

The SRM Medical College Hospital and Research Centre is at Kattankulathur. Students should evaluate the medical campus separately from SRMIST's engineering and management campuses because the academic environment and clinical requirements are different.

How Long Is MBBS and What Happens After It?

The published duration is 4.5 years followed by a 1-year internship. After MBBS, students commonly consider clinical practice, postgraduate medical education and other healthcare pathways subject to the applicable professional rules.

Who Should Choose MBBS?

Choose MBBS if you want a clinical medical career and are prepared for the academic load, clinical training and internship. Do not choose it based on university-wide engineering placement packages.

How Should You Judge MBBS Career Outcomes?

For MBBS, look at clinical exposure, hospital training, internship, postgraduate entrance pathways and the medical career route you want. Standard campus-placement metrics such as an engineering average package are not meaningful substitutes.

FAQs Students Ask

Which entrance exam is required?

NEET-UG is the relevant entrance examination for MBBS admission.

What annual tuition is listed?

₹25 lakh annual tuition is listed on the official SRM medical admissions page.

How long is the course?

4.5 years plus 1-year internship.

Is there one fixed SRMIST MBBS cutoff?

No. Closing position depends on NEET performance and the applicable counselling category and seat type.

Can SRMIST engineering placement figures be used for MBBS?

No. MBBS career outcomes should be judged through clinical training, internship and postgraduate pathways.
