MBBS at SRMIST

SRMIST offers MBBS through its medical and health-sciences ecosystem at Kattankulathur. This page covers the admission journey rather than repeating the university-wide course catalogue.

MBBS Eligibility

Applicants must meet the applicable medical admission eligibility, including the prescribed Class 12 PCB requirements and NEET-UG conditions.

How Does SRMIST MBBS Admission Work?

MBBS admission follows NEET-UG and the applicable medical counselling process. Keep the counselling authority's current rules alongside SRMIST's programme information.

MBBS Fees

MBBS fee information is course- and admission-category specific. No unsupported consolidated fee is inserted here. Use the current SRMIST medical admission document for the exact payable amount.

SRMIST MBBS Cutoff

Do not confuse an unofficial internet cutoff with an official SRMIST closing rank. MBBS admission depends on NEET-UG rank, counselling category, seat availability and applicable regulations.

Where Is SRMIST MBBS Offered?

The SRM Medical College Hospital & Research Centre is located at the Kattankulathur campus. The exact seat matrix and current admission document should be checked for the applicable year.

Placements After MBBS

MBBS is a regulated medical programme and its career path differs from campus placement-led programmes. Use the placement page for engineering and management placement statistics, not as an MBBS salary benchmark.

SRMIST MBBS FAQs

Which entrance exam is required for SRMIST MBBS?

NEET-UG is the relevant entrance examination for MBBS admission.

Is SRMJEEE used for MBBS?

No. SRMJEEE is the engineering entrance route; MBBS follows the NEET-UG medical admission process.

Can engineering placement figures be used to judge MBBS outcomes?

No. Engineering and management placement statistics measure campus recruitment and should not be used as an MBBS salary benchmark.
