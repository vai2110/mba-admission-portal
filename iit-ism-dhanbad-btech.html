IIT (ISM) Dhanbad BTech: Course Structure

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad offers undergraduate engineering education through its JEE Advanced/JoSAA intake. The institute's academic portfolio is particularly strong in mining, petroleum, mineral, earth-science and engineering domains, alongside computer science and other disciplines.

Who should consider IIT (ISM) BTech?

Students who want an IIT education and are comfortable choosing among engineering branches based on career interest, curriculum, placements, research exposure and long-term domain fit.

For the 2026-27 undergraduate cycle, admission is linked to JEE Advanced and JoSAA. The official UG admission portal publishes reporting and fee instructions for allotted candidates.

BTech Fees 2026-27

The official 2026-27 fee structure lists ₹1,00,000 tuition for General/OBC-NCL/GEN-EWS candidates, with other fees, JoSAA processing and mess charges added. Eligible SC/ST/PwD categories receive tuition waiver as specified by the institute.

BTech Placements

Placement outcomes vary considerably by branch. The institute's 2025 departmental material for Computer Science and Engineering reported a highest package above ₹50 LPA and average package of ₹22.6 LPA for that department; this should not be treated as the institute-wide BTech average.

Students should compare branch-specific recruiters, average/median compensation, internship conversion and the nature of roles before choosing a branch.

Hostel & Campus Life

IIT (ISM) is a fully residential institute. Official information lists multiple hostels with Wi-Fi, common rooms, sports facilities, messes and student support. The campus also has cultural, technical and sports activities.

Student Discussion: Choosing the Right Branch

The most useful comparison is not simply “IIT vs non-IIT”. Applicants should evaluate the specific BTech branch, curriculum, placement profile, research opportunities and their own interest in the subject.

  • Do not select a branch only from the highest package headline.
  • Check department-level placement trends.
  • Consider mining, energy and earth-science strengths if those sectors interest you.
  • Look at internships and higher-study options as well.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad BTech FAQs

How can I get BTech admission to IIT (ISM) Dhanbad?

You need to qualify through the JEE Advanced route and participate in JoSAA seat allocation, subject to the applicable eligibility rules.

What is the BTech tuition fee?

For 2026-27, the official fee page lists ₹1,00,000 tuition for General/OBC-NCL/GEN-EWS candidates and NIL tuition for specified SC/ST/PwD categories, before other charges.

Is hostel available?

Yes. IIT (ISM) is a residential institute with multiple student hostels.
