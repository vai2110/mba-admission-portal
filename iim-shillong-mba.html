IIM Shillong MBA: Programme Overview

IIM Shillong's flagship PGP is a two-year, full-time residential programme leading to the Master of Business Administration degree. The institute describes the programme as suitable for fresh graduates and young professionals. It runs across six terms and combines core management courses, electives, a summer internship and integrative learning.

What degree do you get?

The programme awards an MBA degree. IIM Shillong's official FAQ confirms that its two-year full-time PGP leads to the Master of Business Administration degree.

IIM Shillong MBA Eligibility 2026-28

For the PGP 2026-28 domestic admission cycle, a candidate must have a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and Differently Abled candidates. The degree must be from a recognized university/institution under the eligibility rules specified by the institute.

IIM Shillong CAT Cutoff 2026-28 & Admission Process

IIM Shillong does not publish a single CAT percentile as a guaranteed admission cutoff. Instead, the 2026-28 policy establishes minimum sectional thresholds and then uses a composite score for PI shortlisting. This distinction is important when interpreting online “cutoff” searches.

Shortlisting formula

After preliminary screening, IIM Shillong calculates a composite score using CAT percentile with 65% weight and the Normalized Application Rating Score (NARS) with 35% weight. NARS incorporates Class X, Class XII, graduation and work-experience information. Shortlisted candidates then participate in the Personal Interaction stage.

Final admission

The final merit list is prepared after the PI stage using the institute's published final-selection criteria. The exact final score structure can change by admission cycle, so applicants should always use the current policy document rather than older coaching-site formulas.

CAT strategy:

Do not target only the minimum 75 sectional threshold. IIM Shillong's composite shortlisting means a stronger CAT percentile and stronger academic/profile scores can materially improve the probability of a PI call.

Scholarships and loans

IIM Shillong offers a Need Based Financial Assistance scheme. The institute also lists government scholarships for eligible SC/ST/PwD participants and states that PGP students have been able to obtain collateral-free educational loans through banks, subject to bank procedures.

IIM Shillong MBA Curriculum: What You Study

The programme is designed around core courses in the first three terms and deeper elective/integrative learning later. The published curriculum includes financial reporting and analysis, managerial economics, marketing management, statistics for decision making, financial management and markets, managerial accounting, operations management, leadership, business ethics, corporate finance, MIS, operations research, human capital management, strategic management, sustainability and business law.

Summer Internship, International Exposure & Learning Beyond Class

The PGP includes a two-month summer internship at the end of the first year. The internship is important because it can lead to a PPO/PPI and also helps students test a function before final placements.

IIM Shillong also offers Study Abroad and Student Exchange opportunities. The institute's handbook describes the Study Abroad Program as an optional international academic experience. Students should budget for airfare, food, visa, insurance and personal expenses separately from the estimated academic/accommodation cost.

Why this matters for students:

If your goal is consulting, finance, analytics or general management, use the internship to build role-specific evidence. The final placement headline is not the only outcome that matters; the internship and PPO route can materially change your two-year ROI.

IIM Shillong MBA FAQs

Is IIM Shillong MBA residential?

Yes. The two-year PGP is fully residential and students are required to stay in institute hostels.

Is work experience required for IIM Shillong MBA?

No. The PGP is intended for fresh graduates and young professionals, so work experience is not a mandatory eligibility requirement.

What is the minimum CAT cutoff for IIM Shillong?

For PGP 2026-28, the published minimum sectional percentiles for the unreserved/other group are 75 in VARC, DILR and QA. This is only a screening threshold.

What is IIM Shillong MBA average package?

The latest completed PGP 2024-26 placement report records an average CTC of ₹27.03 LPA and median CTC of ₹26 LPA.

Does IIM Shillong offer education loans?

The institute states that PGP participants have been eligible for collateral-free education loans from banks, subject to bank procedures.
