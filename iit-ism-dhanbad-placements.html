IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Placements: Latest Detailed Official Data

For MBA applicants, the relevant figures are the management programme's own placement outcomes—not the highest package reported for B.Tech CSE or the institute overall. The latest detailed official placement report located for this page is the Career Development Centre report dated 23 September 2025, covering the 2024–25 cycle.

MBA placement metric2024–25 official report
Students placed41
Average CTC₹10.48 LPA
Median CTC₹10.38 LPA

The report's MBA sector split shows 15 placed in core engineering, 14 in banking and financial services, 10 in IT and 2 in education. This sector mix reflects the batch covered by the report; it is not a promise that the same sectors or numbers will recruit in a future year.

The department announced that the final placement season for the MBA and MBA (Business Analytics) batch of 2024–26 was completed in 2026. The announcement did not include a complete statistical table. Until a newer official report with batch-level data is published, the 2024–25 report remains the latest detailed official dataset used here.

How should an MBA applicant interpret these numbers?

Compare average and median CTC together, check how many students were placed, examine sector and role mix, and confirm the graduating batch. A highest package alone does not show the typical outcome.

MBA Recruiter Sectors and Career Roles

The official 2024–25 report groups MBA placements into core engineering, banking and financial services, IT and education. This is consistent with the institute's wider industrial and technology ecosystem. Applicants should verify current recruiter names and role descriptions from the latest placement office material rather than treating a historical recruiter list as a current hiring commitment.

When evaluating the MBA, ask whether roles match your target function—such as operations, finance, analytics, IT/business roles or industrial-sector management. The published sector split is useful context, but it does not provide a guaranteed role or salary for an individual student.

B.Tech and Other Programme Placement Highlights

Placement figures vary by department and batch. The cited 2024–25 official report lists B.Tech average CTC of ₹19.73 LPA and median CTC of ₹15.00 LPA for 650 placed students. It reports different outcomes for integrated M.Tech, M.Tech and M.Sc programmes. These are not MBA figures and should not be used to represent MBA career outcomes.

How to Read IIT (ISM) Placement Data

  • Check the report year and graduating batch.
  • Separate MBA, MBA Business Analytics, B.Tech and other programme outcomes.
  • Use average and median together; the median is less affected by a few unusually high offers.
  • Check placed students and participating students before calculating a placement rate.
  • Review sector mix and job roles, not just recruiter logos or highest CTC.
  • Remember that CTC is not the same as fixed in-hand salary.
