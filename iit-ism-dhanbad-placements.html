How to Read IIT (ISM) Placement Data

Placement numbers at an IIT can differ substantially by department and programme. A headline highest package is not a typical salary. Students should compare average or median compensation, percentage placed, recruiter quality, job roles and the number of students participating.

Important

The ₹50+ LPA and ₹22.6 LPA figures below are from a 2025 department-level CSE highlight in IIT (ISM)'s UG admissions material, not an institute-wide BTech average.

BTech Placement Highlights

The institute's CSE department material reports a highest package above ₹50 LPA and an average package of ₹22.6 LPA, with recruiters including Google, Microsoft, Adobe, Amazon, Goldman Sachs and Atlassian. These figures are department-specific.

Recruiters & Career Ecosystem

Official departmental material names recruiters such as Google, Microsoft, Adobe, Amazon, Goldman Sachs and Atlassian for CSE. IIT (ISM)'s broader industry ecosystem also reflects its engineering, mining, energy and technology orientation.

Recruiter lists change every year; do not assume a past recruiter will hire in the next cycle.

Student Discussion: Placement Reality Check

Students should ask three questions: what roles are offered in my branch, what is the median/average outcome, and how many eligible students actually receive offers? These are more useful for decision-making than a single record package.

  • Compare branch-wise rather than only institute-wide numbers.
  • Check role quality and location along with CTC.
  • Factor in internship conversion and higher-study options.
  • Use the latest official placement material whenever available.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad?

Placement figures vary by year and programme. The 2025 CSE departmental material reports a highest package above ₹50 LPA.

Is ₹22.6 LPA the IIT (ISM) average?

No. That figure is reported for the CSE department in the cited UG material and should not be treated as an institute-wide average.

Which recruiters hire from CSE?

The department material names Google, Microsoft, Adobe, Amazon, Goldman Sachs and Atlassian among recruiters.
