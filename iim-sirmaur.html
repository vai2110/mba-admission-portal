IIM Sirmaur MBA: What Students Should Know

IIM Sirmaur is a third-generation IIM established in 2015. Its permanent campus is at Dhaula Kuan in Sirmaur, Himachal Pradesh. The institute describes its flagship MBA as a two-year, full-time residential programme focused on developing principled leaders and capable decision-makers.

The MBA begins with three terms of compulsory core courses covering management concepts, analytical frameworks and functional disciplines. A structured summer internship follows the first year, giving students an opportunity to apply classroom learning to an industry assignment.

Quick answer: What is the biggest thing to understand before applying?

Your CAT percentile is only the entry point. For the 2026 admission cycle, IIM Sirmaur's final composite score gave 35% weight to CAT, 20% to PI, 15% to academics, 20% to work experience, 5% to gender diversity and 5% to the Trailblazer score. This makes the profile and interview important even after clearing the shortlist cutoff.

IIM Sirmaur MBA Admission Process

Phase 1: CAT-based PI shortlist

IIM Sirmaur uses CAT as an evaluation criterion. Candidates meeting the applicable category-wise cutoff are considered for the personal interview stage under the institute's 2026 admission policy and the Common Admission Process.

Phase 2: Final composite score

What does this mean?

A candidate who clears the CAT cutoff but has a weak interview or profile does not automatically secure an offer. Conversely, the final formula rewards more than CAT percentile alone. IIM Sirmaur also applies Government of India reservation norms.

Does IIM Sirmaur use a management quota?

Admission offers are made in order of merit according to the applicable selection parameters and composite score. The institute's policy also says applicants should regularly check official communications for admission updates.

Scholarships and education loans

IIM Sirmaur says enrolled students can apply for scholarships available through the National Scholarship Portal, subject to each scheme's eligibility. The institute also says it has empanelled leading banks for education loans and provides loan information with admission offers.

What should I budget beyond ₹21 lakh?

The published fee should not be treated as your entire two-year personal budget. Keep a separate buffer for laptop, travel, clothing, laundry, personal purchases, club activities and other individual expenses. Exact personal spending varies widely.

IIM Sirmaur MBA Curriculum

The first year is common and core-heavy. The official curriculum includes courses such as Behaviour in Organisations, Communication and Critical Thinking, Financial Reporting & Analysis, Marketing Management, IT Concepts, Legal Aspects in Management, Managerial Economics, Quantitative Analysis and Data Visualization. Later terms add areas such as Financial Management, HRM, Strategic Analysis, Supply Chain Management, Corporate Governance and Ethics.

What you build

  • Finance and accounting fundamentals
  • Marketing and operations understanding
  • Quantitative and analytical skills
  • Strategy and leadership perspective
  • Technology and information-systems exposure
  • Ethics, governance and sustainability awareness

Student reality

The curriculum is broad rather than a narrow job-training bootcamp. Your internship, electives, projects, competitions and placement preparation will matter when converting that breadth into a specific career profile.

IIM Sirmaur Campus & Student Life

IIM Sirmaur's permanent campus is at Dhaula Kuan in Sirmaur, Himachal Pradesh. The institute describes its setting as the foothills of the Himalayas and highlights modern infrastructure, a residential environment and connectivity through Dehradun and Chandigarh airports.

Campus advantages

  • Permanent residential campus
  • Academic and learning resources
  • Student clubs and co-curricular activities
  • Sports and student events
  • Himalayan setting and lower-density environment

Location trade-off

If your priority is being physically inside a major metro's corporate district every day, compare the location with your alternatives. If you value a residential campus and a quieter setting, Sirmaur's location can be a positive.

IIM Sirmaur MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Sirmaur MBA fee?

The published 2026-28 programme fee is ₹21 lakh, plus ₹30,000 refundable caution money.

What is the IIM Sirmaur CAT cutoff?

For the 2026-28 process, the published General cutoff was 90 overall for male candidates and 85 overall for female candidates, with 65 sectional percentiles in each section. Cutoffs vary by category and admission cycle.

Is CAT enough for admission?

No. CAT had 35% weight in the 2026 final composite score. PI, academics, work experience, gender diversity and Trailblazer score also contributed.

What is the latest MBA average CTC at IIM Sirmaur?

The 2024-25 annual report reports ₹13.7 LPA for the MBA 2023-25 cohort.

Is IIM Sirmaur a residential MBA?

Yes. The institute describes the MBA as a two-year, full-time residential programme.

Does IIM Sirmaur offer MBA Tourism?

Yes. It offers MBA Tourism Management and MBA Tourism, Travel and Hospitality Management as separate sector-focused programmes.

Are the 2027 admission dates official?

No. The 2027 dates on this page are explicitly tentative planning estimates because the complete 2027-29 calendar is not yet published.
