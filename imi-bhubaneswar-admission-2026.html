IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM eligibility 2026

Applicants need a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent from a recognised university. Candidates graduating in 2026 were eligible to apply for the 2026-28 cycle.

There is no requirement for a specific undergraduate stream; applicants can come from different academic backgrounds.

IMI Bhubaneswar Admission FAQs

How can I get admission to IMI Bhubaneswar?

For the PGDM, apply to IMI Bhubaneswar with a valid accepted test score, meet the 50% graduation requirement, and clear the institute's selection stages. The process includes holistic shortlisting, video assessment and personal interview.

What is the IMI Bhubaneswar CAT cutoff?

IMI Bhubaneswar does not publish a fixed cut-off in its official FAQ. It says shortlisting considers academics, work experience, test scores and other profile factors. A third-party predicted cutoff should therefore not be treated as an official cutoff.

Can I apply to IMI Bhubaneswar without CAT?

Yes. For the 2026-28 cycle, the institute accepted XAT, CMAT and GMAT in addition to CAT.

What is the minimum percentage required for IMI Bhubaneswar?

A minimum of 50% in a recognised three-year bachelor's degree is required for PGDM eligibility. Candidates graduating in 2026 could also apply in the 2026-28 cycle.

Does IMI Bhubaneswar conduct GD?

The current official selection description focuses on a video assessment followed by a personal interview. It does not list a conventional group discussion as a mandatory stage in the current FAQ.

Is work experience compulsory for IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM?

No. The eligibility requirement is based on a bachelor's degree and test score. Work experience is considered as part of the profile, but the institute accepts fresh graduates.

When does the IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM session start?

The 2026-28 programme was scheduled to begin in July 2026. Future batches should be checked against the institute's new admission calendar.
