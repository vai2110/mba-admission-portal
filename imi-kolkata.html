IMI Kolkata: Is It a Good B-School for You?

IMI Kolkata is a two-year, full-time PGDM institute established in 2011 and part of the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group. The PGDM is AICTE approved, NBA accredited, AMBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. IMI Kolkata says it is ranked 42nd in Management in NIRF 2025.

Quick student verdict

IMI Kolkata is worth evaluating if you want a Kolkata-based residential PGDM with a broad management curriculum, established accreditations and a placement ecosystem across BFSI, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG and other sectors. The key decision is ROI: compare the complete residential cost with the realistic salary band for your target role rather than relying only on the highest package.

Reasons to consider

  • AIU-equivalent-to-MBA PGDM.
  • AMBA, NBA and NAAC accreditation.
  • Strong Kolkata industry ecosystem.
  • Mandatory residential setup and campus clubs.
  • Broad functional electives.

Questions to answer before joining

  • What role do you realistically target?
  • What is the total hostel and mess cost?
  • How does your expected salary compare with total cost?
  • Which companies recruit for your preferred function?
  • Is IMI Kolkata your best alternative at this fee?

Does meeting 50% guarantee a call?

No. The 50% requirement is minimum eligibility. IMI Kolkata explicitly states that meeting minimum eligibility does not ensure shortlisting; the effective shortlist depends on the applicant pool and the institute's selection process.

IMI Kolkata PGDM Fees & Scholarships

The latest detailed official fee published for PGDM 2026-28 is ₹14.98 lakh for the academic programme, including tuition and listed institutional charges. Hostel and mess are additional and mandatory because IMI Kolkata mandates hostel accommodation.

Fee componentPublished 2026-28 amount

Student activities & welfare₹43,000
Library & information resources₹75,000
IT infrastructure₹32,000
Alumni life membership₹20,000
Security deposit₹15,000 refundable
Academic programme total₹14.98 lakh

The institute states that hostel and mess charges depend on room category and are added separately. Students should therefore budget above ₹14.98 lakh for the actual residential cost.

Scholarship to know about

IMI Kolkata states that students with total family income below ₹8 lakh per annum may apply for an EWS tuition fee waiver, subject to documentation and availability; the published policy limits this support to up to 5% of institute seats.

IMI Kolkata PGDM Curriculum, Specialisations & Learning

The PGDM runs for two years across six trimesters. The first three trimesters build a common management foundation; students then develop deeper functional expertise through electives and advanced courses.

Functional areas

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Operations Management
  • Economics
  • Strategy, Innovation & Entrepreneurship
  • Organisational Behaviour & HR
  • Information Systems & Analytics

Experiential learning

  • 8-10 week summer internship
  • Company-based live projects
  • New Venture Development
  • Local Business Accelerator
  • Design Thinking for Rural Innovation
  • Hands-on courses and industry exposure

The redesigned programme also emphasises career-aligned, application-driven learning and day-one readiness.

IMI Kolkata Campus, Hostel & Student Life

IMI Kolkata is located at 2/4C Judges Court Road, Alipore, Kolkata. The institute describes a fully Wi-Fi-enabled campus with modern classrooms, computing facilities and student activity spaces. Student life includes student-led academic and professional clubs, events, symposia and lectures.

IMI Kolkata Placements: What Should You Expect?

The latest widely reported 2025 placement figures show 100% placement for the graduating batch, with a median salary of about ₹12.49 lakh. Published placement sources report a highest package of ₹40.31 lakh and an average around ₹12.7 lakh for the 2025 cycle; the institute's placement page also provides process and report resources.

Should You Choose IMI Kolkata?

Strong fit if

  • You want a full-time residential PGDM in Kolkata.
  • You value AMBA/NBA/NAAC accreditation and AIU MBA equivalence.
  • You want broad functional choices rather than a narrow specialisation.
  • You are comfortable evaluating ROI using median/average outcomes.

Compare alternatives if

  • Your target is a highly specialised role with a stronger placement pipeline elsewhere.
  • You are uncomfortable with mandatory hostel and additional living costs.
  • You are joining only because of the highest-package headline.
  • You have another B-school with materially stronger outcomes at a similar total cost.

IMI Kolkata FAQs

Is IMI Kolkata worth it?

It can be, particularly for students seeking a residential PGDM in Kolkata with established accreditations and a broad corporate-facing curriculum. ROI should be judged against the full residential cost and the realistic salary/role you target.

What is the IMI Kolkata PGDM fee?

The latest detailed official 2026-28 academic programme fee is ₹14.98 lakh, excluding mandatory hostel and mess charges.

What entrance exams does IMI Kolkata accept?

The published admission process lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT for shortlisting.

Is IMI Kolkata PGDM equivalent to MBA?

Yes. IMI Kolkata states that its PGDM has AIU equivalence to an MBA.

Is hostel compulsory at IMI Kolkata?

Yes. The institute states that hostel accommodation is mandatory for students, with room-category-based charges plus mess fees.
