Law Career Scope

Career options can include law firms, litigation, corporate legal teams, compliance, policy, legal operations and higher legal education. Internships and practical exposure are important when evaluating a law programme.

Student Discussion: Choosing a Private Law School

Compare internship access, moot-court exposure, faculty, alumni network, total cost, location and placement transparency. Avoid selecting a law programme solely on the university brand.
