SIBM Bengaluru Overview

Quick answer

SIBM Bengaluru is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University) offering a two-year full-time residential MBA, a two-year MBA in Business Analytics and an MBA Executive programme. The flagship MBA and Business Analytics programmes use SNAP followed by GE-PI for selection.

The institute is located at Electronic City Phase I on Hosur Road, Bengaluru. Its official programme pages describe the MBA as a compulsory residential programme and the Business Analytics programme as a two-year full-time residential MBA.

What makes the Bengaluru campus relevant for MBA applicants?

The campus is positioned in Bengaluru's technology and corporate ecosystem. The official institute material emphasises industry exposure, case-based learning, corporate interaction and analytics-oriented management education. These are useful considerations for applicants comparing SIBM Bengaluru with other private MBA institutes.

How is the SIBM Bengaluru MBA selection calculated?

For the 2027-29 batch, the official selection weightage is 50 marks for SNAP score out of 60, 10 marks for Group Exercise and 40 marks for Personal Interaction. The institute therefore does not select candidates on SNAP percentile alone.

What is the eligibility for SIBM Bengaluru MBA?

The official MBA programme requires graduation from a recognised university/institution with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade, with 45% for SC/ST candidates. Candidates with foreign qualifications require an AIU equivalence certificate.

Who Should Consider SIBM Bengaluru?

Strong fit

Applicants looking for a residential MBA in Bengaluru, willing to use SNAP as the entrance route and interested in a curriculum combining management foundations with industry exposure.

Check before applying

Applicants should compare the complete residential cost, understand that SNAP is only one component of final selection, and verify the latest fee and admission dates before payment.

SIBM Bengaluru FAQs

Does SIBM Bengaluru accept CAT for the flagship MBA?

The current official flagship MBA admission route specifies SNAP. Candidates should not treat CAT as the stated entrance route for this programme.

What is the official SIBM Bengaluru MBA cutoff?

The institute's current page explains SNAP-based shortlisting and final selection but does not publish a fixed SNAP percentile cutoff. Any external percentile should therefore be labelled an estimate, not an official cutoff.

Is SIBM Bengaluru MBA residential?

Yes. The official MBA page describes it as a compulsory residential programme.

Where is SIBM Bengaluru located?

The institute is at 95/1, 95/2, Electronic City Phase I, Hosur Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560100.
