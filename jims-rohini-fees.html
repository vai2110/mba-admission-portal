JIMS Rohini PGDM Fees 2026-28

Official fee

JIMS lists ₹10,75,000 as the programme fee for PGDM, PGDM-IB and PGDM-RM. The published structure separately lists ₹2,000 lifetime alumni membership and a ₹5,500 refundable security deposit.

This means the listed payment obligation including those two separate charges is ₹10,82,500, although the security deposit is refundable subject to institute rules.

What is the practical cost of studying at JIMS Rohini?

Known institute charges

₹10.75 lakh programme fee + ₹2,000 alumni membership + ₹5,500 refundable security deposit.

Budget separately

Students should also plan for commuting, books, laptop/software, personal expenses and any accommodation requirement. These are not the same as the published programme fee.

Because JIMS is located in Rohini, students living elsewhere in Delhi-NCR should compare daily travel cost and time against accommodation options before calculating their real two-year budget.

Is JIMS Rohini worth the fee?

Do not judge the fee against the highest package alone. A better comparison uses total academic cost, scholarship eligibility, median salary, placement coverage, target role and your financing cost.

Decision rule: If your target is a management career in Delhi-NCR and you can access a scholarship or manageable financing, JIMS can be worth considering. Compare the programme against competing B-schools using median outcomes rather than only headline salary figures.

JIMS Rohini Fees FAQs

What is the JIMS Rohini PGDM fee for 2026-28?

The published programme fee is ₹10.75 lakh for PGDM, PGDM-IB and PGDM-RM.

What extra charges does JIMS have?

The published structure separately lists ₹2,000 lifetime alumni membership and ₹5,500 refundable security deposit.

What is the first instalment at JIMS?

The first listed payment is ₹2.90 lakh plus ₹2,000 alumni membership and ₹5,500 security deposit.

Does JIMS offer scholarships?

Yes. The published 2026-28 scheme includes merit-cum-means awards, with some CAT/XAT categories offering up to ₹3 lakh.

Is the JIMS security deposit refundable?

The published fee structure identifies ₹5,500 as a refundable security deposit, subject to institute rules.

Is hostel fee included in JIMS PGDM fee?

The ₹10.75 lakh programme fee should not be assumed to include personal accommodation or living expenses. Students should confirm any separate accommodation arrangement with the institute.

Can scholarship reduce the JIMS fee?

Yes, eligible students may receive the applicable scholarship, subject to the published category and continuation conditions.

Should I compare JIMS fee with highest package?

No. Compare fee with median salary, placement coverage, scholarship, financing cost and your intended career role.
