Jaipuria Noida Placement ROI: Is ₹16.50 Lakh Worth It?

ROI depends heavily on the role you secure. At ₹16.50 lakh tuition, plus hostel and living costs, students should compare the total two-year cost with the realistic salary band for their target role.

For example, the ₹24.11 LPA highest CTC is not the right benchmark for an average student. The top 50% average of ₹11.08 LPA gives a more grounded view of the stronger half of the batch. Students should also check fixed salary, variable pay, location and joining conditions.

Use this checklist before accepting the offer

  • Compare total fee after scholarship.
  • Ask for batch-specific placement report.
  • Check median/average and not only highest package.
  • Separate domestic and international offers.
  • Check actual job function and variable pay.
