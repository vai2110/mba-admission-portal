Jaipuria Noida Recruiters & Hiring Sectors

Jaipuria highlights hiring across BFSI, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, manufacturing and retail. Recruiter names displayed across current institute material include Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, Accenture, BlackRock, HDFC Bank, Aditya Birla Capital, Genpact, Nestlé, Flipkart, Dabur, Tata Power, HCL, ANZ, Adani, IDBI Bank and Mahindra Finance.

Role families to evaluate

  • Finance, credit and risk
  • Consulting and business analysis
  • Marketing, sales and business development
  • Operations and supply chain
  • HR and people functions

Questions to ask

  • How many students got non-sales roles?
  • What is fixed pay versus variable pay?
  • How many PPOs converted?
  • What was the median salary?
  • Which roles were open to freshers?

Jaipuria Noida Placement ROI: Is ₹16.50 Lakh Worth It?

For 2027-29, the published Noida PGDM fee is ₹16.50 lakh. Add hostel, mess, personal expenses and opportunity cost. ROI should be calculated against the realistic role and salary band you expect, not the highest package.

ROI factorStudent interpretation
Tuition₹16.50 lakh published for 2027-29 PGDM/Marketing
Hostel₹1.65 lakh/year listed for 2026-27 AC double occupancy; subject to revision
Top 50% average₹11.08 LPA latest published centralised figure
Highest CTC₹24.11 LPA; not a typical outcome
Bottom line

Jaipuria Noida can offer reasonable ROI for students who secure strong roles, scholarships and controlled living costs. If another B-school offers materially better median/average outcomes at similar total cost, compare that option carefully.

Jaipuria Noida Placements FAQs

What is the highest package?

The latest published centralised placement highlights show ₹24.11 LPA highest CTC.

What is the average package?

The current highlight panel reports segmented averages: ₹14.70 LPA for the top 10%, ₹13.18 LPA for the top 20% and ₹11.08 LPA for the top 50%.

Which companies recruit?

Recruiters span BFSI, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, manufacturing and retail, with names including Deloitte, KPMG, Accenture, BlackRock, HDFC Bank and Genpact.

Are placements guaranteed?

No. Use final batch-specific reports and understand that outcomes vary by profile, role and recruiter.
