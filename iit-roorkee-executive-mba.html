Quick answer

IIT Roorkee's Executive MBA is a two-year master's degree designed for working professionals. The official site describes a blended model, 73 credits and the option to complete the degree within 24 to 60 months. The published total fee is ₹11,67,500 including a ₹25,000 non-refundable registration fee.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Overview

The Executive MBA is designed for professionals who want a formal management degree while continuing to work. IIT Roorkee describes a flexible, technology-focused degree combining live and prerecorded teaching with simulations, case studies and project work.

The official programme site highlights more than 40 electives and the opportunity to earn a double specialisation, with areas including Operations, Finance, Marketing, HR, Analytics and IT.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Eligibility

The official admissions page specifies three core conditions:

  1. A bachelor's degree from an accredited school or university with at least 65% marks or equivalent; the stated threshold is 60% for SC/ST candidates.
  2. At least four years of relevant full-time work experience after graduation within the programme's specified eligibility window.
  3. At least one of the programme's stated qualifying conditions: graduation from a university within the specified NIRF/QS ranking bands, employment with a qualifying large organisation/government department/reputed NGO, or qualification in the latest CAT, GMAT or XAT.
The official admissions page also requires documentation such as bachelor's marksheets, work-experience proof and an NOC from the current employer.

How to Apply for IIT Roorkee Executive MBA

  1. Check the current cohort's eligibility and application deadline.
  2. Submit the application through the official route.
  3. Upload the required academic and professional documents.
  4. Complete screening. The official admissions page states that shortlisted applicants are invited for an online interview and that there is no written exam.
  5. After selection, complete registration and course enrolment under the pay-as-you-go fee structure.

Executive MBA Programme Structure & Specialisations

The degree comprises eight terms and 73 credits: 37.5 core-course credits, 25.5 elective-course credits and 10 project/seminar credits.

Students can choose from 40+ electives and customise the degree around professional goals. The official site highlights Operations, Finance, Marketing, HR, Analytics and IT.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Format

  • Live weekend classes plus prerecorded lectures.
  • Case studies, simulations and project work.
  • Annual campus immersion at IIT Roorkee.
  • Access to IIT Roorkee campus resources.
  • Option to complete the programme within 24 to 60 months.

This format is materially different from IIT Roorkee's two-year residential MBA: EMBA is designed around continued employment and flexible study.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA FAQs

What is the IIT Roorkee Executive MBA fee?

₹11,67,500 including the ₹25,000 non-refundable registration fee, according to the official tuition-financing page.

How much work experience is required?

The official admissions page states a minimum of four years of relevant full-time work experience after graduation.

Is the programme fully online?

No. It is blended, with online learning and annual campus immersion at IIT Roorkee.

Is there a written entrance exam?

The official admissions page states that shortlisted applicants are invited for an online interview and that there is no written exam.
