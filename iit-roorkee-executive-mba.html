Quick answer

IIT Roorkee's Executive MBA is a blended programme for working executives. The official EMBA site currently shows Autumn 2026 admissions open. The published total fee is ₹11,67,500 including a non-refundable ₹25,000 registration fee, for 73 credits. The fee is pay-as-you-go and is the same across categories and nationalities.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Overview

The programme is positioned for working executives who want cross-dimensional expertise across business and technology without leaving employment for a conventional full-time residential MBA.

The official programme site highlights experiential and participative learning through simulations, business case studies, project work, prerecorded lectures and live lectures. It also connects the programme to contemporary areas including automation, data science and sustainability.

Who Should Apply for IIT Roorkee Executive MBA?

The programme is designed around the needs of working professionals. Candidates should use the current official admission notice for the exact eligibility and documentation requirements of the intake they are applying to.

  • Working executives seeking management education alongside employment.
  • Professionals wanting stronger business-and-technology exposure.
  • Managers who need analytical and cross-functional decision-making skills.
  • Professionals who can participate in blended learning and campus immersion.

Do not substitute the two-year MBA's CAT-based eligibility/selection rules for EMBA. The Executive MBA has its own admission process.

IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Admission 2026

The official EMBA site currently states Admissions open for Autumn 2026. Applicants should use the official EMBA application route and follow the programme-specific instructions for eligibility, documents, selection and payment.

  1. Review the official programme and admission requirements.
  2. Complete the EMBA application through the official admission route.
  3. Submit the required academic/professional documents.
  4. Complete the programme's selection/admission requirements as instructed by IIT Roorkee.
  5. Upon selection, register for courses and pay the applicable fee under the pay-as-you-go structure.

Executive MBA Programme Structure

The published fee table shows 73 credits: 37.5 core-course credits, 25.5 elective-course credits and 10 project/seminar credits.

The programme's stated learning direction combines management with technology-oriented applications, including automation, data science, sustainability, manufacturing, consulting, finance and logistics.

How the IIT Roorkee Executive MBA Is Delivered

The official site describes a blended model using live and prerecorded lectures alongside case studies, simulations and project work. It also highlights a campus immersion at IIT Roorkee every year.

  • Blended learning format
  • Business case studies and simulations
  • Project-based learning
  • Live and prerecorded lectures
  • Annual campus immersion
  • Access to IIT Roorkee campus resources
