IIM Jammu MBA Overview

The IIM Jammu MBA is a two-year full-time residential management programme. For 2026-28, the institute expects a batch of around 300 students and uses CAT 2025 for PI shortlisting.

Is IIM Jammu MBA worth considering?

It can be a strong option for candidates seeking an IIM environment with a broad management curriculum, relatively accessible published CAT thresholds and a growing recruiter base. Students should evaluate the full cost against median/average placement outcomes rather than only the highest CTC.

IIM Jammu MBA Eligibility

Candidates should satisfy the eligibility conditions specified in the official admission process and appear for CAT 2025. The institute's work-experience scoring specifically counts full-time remunerative experience after graduation; internships, projects, training, apprenticeships, articleship and pre-graduation work are not counted as qualifying work experience for the profile score.

Profile factors that matter

  • CAT performance
  • Class 10 and Class 12 marks
  • Graduation performance
  • Relevant post-graduation work experience
  • Gender diversity

Work-experience caution

Only completed months of eligible full-time remunerative post-graduation experience are counted. Documentary proof is required.

IIM Jammu MBA Placements: Latest 2023-25 Outcomes

The latest completed MBA final-placement report listed by IIM Jammu is for the 2023-25 batch. It reports ₹26.60 LPA highest CTC, ₹16.04 LPA average CTC and ₹15.80 LPA median CTC, with 200 recruiters, 250 offers and 24 PPOs. IIM Jammu also lists a 2024-26 placement brochure, but that should be treated as the current brochure cycle rather than a completed outcome report.

For students, the median is the better first benchmark. The next question is role quality: finance, consulting, analytics, product, marketing and general-management opportunities can have very different day-to-day work even when their CTC is similar.

Students’ Discussions & Practical Inference

Recent aspirant and student discussions raise a recurring question: are the available roles broad enough for someone who does not want a sales-heavy career? Discussions are mixed and anecdotal, so they should be used as a prompt for due diligence rather than as a substitute for placement data.

If you want finance/consulting/analytics

  • Check the actual job descriptions, not just sector names.
  • Use summer internships to build domain credibility.
  • Strengthen Excel, SQL, analytics, finance or case-interview skills as relevant.

If you are open to sales/marketing

The recruiter universe can be broader, particularly across BFSI, consumer and business-development roles. Your decision should therefore depend on your target function, not only the IIM label or highest package.

IIM Jammu MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Jammu MBA fee?

The official 2026-28 programme fee is ₹22,80,756, with mess bills payable as actuals and a refundable ₹50,000 caution deposit.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Jammu MBA?

The published General male overall PI-shortlisting cutoff is 91 percentile and General female/transgender cutoff is 89 percentile for 2026-28.

How is final selection calculated?

CAT carries 42%, PI 30%, academic profile 10%, work experience 10% and gender diversity 8%.
