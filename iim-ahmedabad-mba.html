IIM Ahmedabad MBA: At a Glance

Programme PGP in Management
Degree MBA
Duration 2 Years
Mode Full-Time Residential

IIMA's Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is its flagship two-year MBA programme. The class includes students from different academic backgrounds, including engineering, commerce, science, arts and professional disciplines.

What matters for an applicant: IIMA does not select candidates only on CAT percentile. Academic performance, work experience, gender diversity and other Application Rating components are also considered during shortlisting.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA Eligibility 2026–28

Requirement Eligibility
Graduation Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. For SC/ST/PwD candidates, the minimum is 45%.
Duration of degree Bachelor's/equivalent qualification should involve at least three years of education after 10+2.
Final-year students Candidates appearing in the final year can apply, subject to IIMA's prescribed completion and document submission requirements.
Minimum age Candidates for the 2026–28 PGP must be at least 19 years old as on June 30, 2026.
Work experience No minimum work experience is required to apply for the PGP. However, work experience contributes to the Application Rating used during shortlisting.
Student takeaway: A fresher can apply. Having work experience is not an eligibility requirement, but the number of months of work experience can influence the Application Rating.

How IIM Ahmedabad Shortlists MBA Candidates

This is one of the most important parts of the admission process. A high CAT percentile alone does not decide whether you receive an AWT-PI call.

Step 1: CAT Screening

You first need a valid CAT 2025 score, must satisfy the eligibility conditions and clear IIMA's overall and sectional CAT thresholds.

Step 2: Application Rating

IIMA calculates an Application Rating using your academic record, work experience and gender-diversity component.

Component What IIMA considers
A Class 10 performance
B Class 12 performance. The scoring thresholds vary according to Science, Commerce and Arts/Humanities backgrounds.
C Bachelor's degree performance. The scoring system differs according to the academic category of the candidate.
D Work experience. Less than 12 months receives 0 points; 12–36 months is calculated using IIMA's formula; more than 36 months receives 5 points.
E Gender diversity component. IIMA's 2026–28 criteria assign 0 to male candidates and 3 to all other candidates.

Step 3: Shortlist Composite Score

65% Normalised CAT Score
35% Normalised Application Rating
CAT Entrance performance
AR Academic + profile score
In simple terms: IIMA's shortlist uses 65% CAT + 35% Application Rating , along with the institute's academic-category and screening conditions.

If you have a very high CAT score

A strong CAT score improves your shortlist component, but it does not erase the effect of academic performance and other Application Rating components.

If you have work experience

Work experience can add to the Application Rating. The 2026–28 criteria give the maximum work-experience component after more than 36 months.

IIM Ahmedabad Final Selection Criteria

Getting an AWT-PI call is not the final step. After AWT and PI, IIMA calculates the Final Composite Score.

50% Personal Interview
10% Analytical Writing Test
25% CAT Score
15% Application Rating
What this means for you: Once you reach the interview stage, PI becomes the largest component of the final score at 50%. CAT contributes 25%, AWT contributes 10% and Application Rating contributes 15%.

Fee information needs to be handled carefully because IIMA's published fee for one batch should not automatically be presented as the fee for the next batch.

2026–28 fee Use IIMA's latest fee notice once published. Do not assume that the 2025–27 fee is the confirmed 2026–28 fee.

Financial Aid

IIMA offers a Need-Based Scholarship Scheme. The institute states that factors such as annual gross family income, assets and number of dependants are considered when evaluating financial need.

Student takeaway: Do not calculate your IIM Ahmedabad budget using tuition alone. Check the current programme fee plus accommodation, living expenses and other personal costs.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA Batch Profile

IIMA's PGP is not restricted to one academic background. The institute describes students from arts, commerce, science and professional backgrounds including engineering, technology and medicine.

Profile Factor What It Means for Applicants
Fresh graduates Eligible to apply if they meet the academic requirements. Work experience is not compulsory.
Work experience Contributes to Application Rating. The 2026–28 framework gives the highest work-experience component after more than 36 months.
Engineers Eligible, but bachelor's performance is evaluated within IIMA's academic-category framework.
Non-engineers Eligible. IIMA has separate academic categories covering commerce/management, science, medicine and arts/humanities.

How to Apply for IIM Ahmedabad MBA

Step What You Need to Do
1. Check eligibility Verify your graduation marks, degree status and other IIMA eligibility requirements.
2. Take CAT Appear for CAT and ensure that you meet both sectional and overall requirements.
3. Submit information Ensure your academic and work-experience details are entered accurately because they feed into the Application Rating.
4. Wait for shortlist IIMA applies its preliminary screening and composite-score process.
5. AWT + PI If shortlisted, attend the Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview.
6. Final selection IIMA calculates the Final Composite Score and releases admission offers.
Practical tip: Do not treat the CAT application as a formality. Academic scores, degree discipline and work-experience information can affect your Application Rating.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA FAQs

Is 95 percentile enough for IIM Ahmedabad?

No. 95 percentile is the published overall qualifying cutoff for General/EWS non-PwD candidates for the 2026–28 PGP. It does not guarantee an AWT-PI call.

Does IIM Ahmedabad accept CAT for MBA?

Yes. CAT 2025 is the entrance examination route for domestic applicants to the 2026–28 PGP/MBA batch.

Does work experience matter for IIM Ahmedabad?

Yes. It contributes to the Application Rating used during shortlisting, although it is not a minimum eligibility requirement.

Can a non-engineer apply to IIM Ahmedabad?

Yes. IIMA admits students from multiple academic backgrounds. The shortlisting framework has separate academic categories for different undergraduate backgrounds.

Is ₹27.50 lakh the confirmed IIM Ahmedabad MBA fee for 2026–28?

No. ₹27.50 lakh is the published PGP fee for the 2025–27 batch. The 2026–28 fee should be taken from IIMA's current fee notice when published.

What carries the highest weight in final selection?

Personal Interview carries the highest weight at 50% in the Final Composite Score.
