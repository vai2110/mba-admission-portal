IIM Ahmedabad MBA / PGP 2026–28

Programme Overview

The two-year full-time Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is the flagship MBA programme of IIM Ahmedabad. It is a residential programme designed for graduates from different academic disciplines who want to build careers in management.

The 2026–28 admission cycle for domestic candidates was based on CAT 2025. IIMA's process included preliminary screening, shortlisting for the Analytical Writing Test (AWT) and Personal Interview (PI), followed by final selection using a Final Composite Score.

Important: this is the 2026–28 admission cycle

The admission process has concluded. This page explains how IIMA evaluated candidates for the 2026–28 PGP batch and is intended as a reference for students preparing for future IIMA admission cycles.

Who Is the Programme For?

The PGP is intended for graduates from diverse academic backgrounds. IIMA's official admission information notes that the class can include students from arts, commerce, science, engineering, technology, medicine and other professional streams, along with both fresh graduates and candidates with work experience.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. For candidates belonging to SC, ST or PwD categories, the minimum is 45%.

Minimum Duration of Graduation

The bachelor's degree must involve at least three years of education after completing higher secondary schooling (10+2) or equivalent.

Final-Year Students

Candidates appearing for their final-year bachelor's degree examination, or candidates who have completed their degree requirements but are awaiting results, can also apply subject to IIMA's prescribed conditions.

Work Experience

Prior work experience is not a mandatory eligibility requirement for the PGP. IIMA's PGP cohort includes both fresh graduates and candidates with work experience.

Age Requirement for 2026–28

Selected candidates for the 2026–28 two-year PGP were required to be at least 19 years of age as on June 30, 2026.

Key takeaway

You do not need prior work experience simply to be eligible for the IIMA PGP. However, work experience can form part of the Application Rating used during the selection process.

Step 1 — Preliminary Screening

Candidates needed a valid CAT 2025 score, had to satisfy the programme's eligibility requirements and meet IIMA's minimum sectional and overall CAT screening thresholds.

Step 2 — AWT & Personal Interview Shortlisting

IIMA used a Composite Score combining the normalized Application Rating and normalized CAT score. For the 2026–28 process, the Composite Score used a 35% Application Rating factor and a 65% CAT factor.

Step 3 — Analytical Writing Test & Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates appeared for the Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview. These stages became important inputs into the final composite score.

Step 4 — Final Selection

Candidates who appeared for AWT and PI were considered for final selection within their respective categories.

Application Rating

What Is Application Rating?

Application Rating, or AR, is a score derived from multiple parts of a candidate's academic and professional profile. For the 2026–28 process, IIMA used rating scores based on Class 10, Class 12, bachelor's degree performance, work experience and gender diversity.

Component What IIMA Considered
Rating A Class 10 percentage
Rating B Class 12 percentage and stream
Rating C Bachelor's degree performance and academic discipline
Rating D Work experience
Rating E Gender diversity score

Work Experience in Application Rating

For the 2026–28 selection process, less than 12 months of work experience received a Rating D of zero. Experience from 12 to 36 months received a score based on IIMA's specified formula, while more than 36 months received the maximum Rating D score of 5.

What this means for applicants

Work experience can improve the Application Rating, but it is only one part of the overall process. A fresher is not automatically excluded from the PGP.

Final Selection Criteria

Final Composite Score — 2026–28

For candidates who appeared in AWT and PI, IIMA calculated the Final Composite Score using four components.

50% Personal Interview
10% Analytical Writing Test
25% CAT Score
15% Application Rating
65% CAT + AR in PI shortlist composite
2-Step Shortlisting followed by final selection
The biggest takeaway

For final selection, the Personal Interview carried the largest individual weight at 50%. CAT remained important, but the final decision was not based on CAT percentile alone.

What Else Can Matter?

IIMA states that the broader selection process considers academic performance, co-curricular and extracurricular achievements, work experience and performance in CAT, AWT and PI.

Who Can Apply for IIM Ahmedabad MBA?

Fresh Graduates

Freshers are eligible for the PGP. Prior work experience is not mandatory for eligibility.

Working Professionals

Candidates with work experience can apply. Work experience is considered within the Application Rating.

Engineers

Engineering and technology degrees fall within IIMA's academic classification framework and engineers are eligible to apply.

Non-Engineers

Candidates from commerce, economics, arts, humanities, science, medicine and other disciplines can also apply.

What Makes a Profile More Competitive?

  • Strong CAT performance across sections
  • Strong academic record
  • Good bachelor's performance
  • Relevant work experience where applicable
  • Strong performance in AWT and PI
  • Co-curricular and extracurricular achievements
Important

Eligibility and competitiveness are different questions. Meeting IIMA's minimum eligibility or CAT screening cutoff does not mean that a candidate is likely to receive an interview call or admission offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is work experience mandatory for IIM Ahmedabad MBA?

No. Prior work experience is not mandatory for the PGP. The programme includes both fresh graduates and candidates with work experience.

What was the CAT cutoff for IIM Ahmedabad 2026–28?

For General/EWS non-PwD candidates, the minimum screening threshold was 95 overall and 85 in each of VARC, DILR and QA.

Does a 95 percentile guarantee an IIM Ahmedabad call?

No. The 95 percentile figure was a minimum screening cutoff. IIMA also considered academic performance, Application Rating, CAT performance and other selection criteria.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes. Final-year bachelor's students can apply subject to IIMA's conditions regarding completion and submission of degree requirements.

What is the IIM Ahmedabad MBA selection process?

For the 2026–28 PGP, the process involved CAT 2025, preliminary screening, AWT and PI shortlisting, followed by final selection using the Final Composite Score.

What is the weight of the Personal Interview?

The PI carried 50% weight in the Final Composite Score for the 2026–28 PGP selection process.

What is the latest published IIM Ahmedabad MBA fee?

The current public PGP admission page lists ₹27.50 lakh for the 2025–27 batch, including ₹20.10 lakh tuition. IIMA says future-batch fees will be announced later.

Is IIM Ahmedabad MBA residential?

Yes. IIMA describes the two-year PGP as a residential programme.
