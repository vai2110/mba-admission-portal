Latest Placement Highlights

The official 2025 graduating-batch report states that overall campus placement conversion across the listed schools was around 92.50%. More than 757 companies visited for around 5,000 eligible students and approximately 4,621 offers were generated. The highest reported CTC was ₹51 LPA, offered by Microsoft to a student in the placement programme.

Important:

These are campus-level figures. A university-wide highest package should not be treated as the expected salary for every course. Use the school/programme-level figures wherever available.

School-wise Placement Outcomes

KIIT School of Technology

For the 2025 graduating batch, KSOT reported 3,800+ offers from 451 companies, an average CTC of ₹8.50 LPA, 1,500+ dream offers and 1,209 paid internship opportunities. Microsoft offered the highest CTC of ₹51 LPA in the university's reported placement figures.

KIIT School of Management

The official 2025 placement page reports around 161 companies, around 401 offers, a highest CTC of ₹20 LPA and an average CTC of ₹7.25 LPA for MBA graduates.

KIIT School of Rural Management

KSRM reported 51 companies, a batch size of 152, highest package of ₹20.71 LPA and average CTC of ₹5.75 LPA, with 100% placement conversion reported for the batch.

KIIT School of Biotechnology

KSBT reported 85% placement, 42 participating companies and a highest CTC of ₹8 LPA. The school also reports substantial higher-study progression.

KIIT School of Law

The 2025 report states that more than 52 firms and companies participated, with average CTC of ₹4.50 LPA and highest package of ₹9.60 LPA.

Internships & Career Preparation

For KSOT, the 2025 placement report records 1,209 paid internship opportunities. KIIT's training and placement ecosystem also provides aptitude, communication, technical and soft-skill preparation and company-specific training. Students should evaluate internship conversion and role quality alongside the final placement package.

How Should Students Read KIIT Placement Data?

B.Tech applicants

Focus on branch-wise roles, average CTC, internship access, multiple offers and recruiter mix instead of only the ₹51 LPA highest offer.

MBA applicants

Use the KSOM-specific average and highest CTC rather than the engineering placement figures shown on the university-wide page.

BBA applicants

The official annual report has combined KSOM placement information for MBA and BBA in some reporting. Confirm the current BBA-specific cohort data before making an ROI calculation.

ROI comparison

Compare total programme cost, average/median compensation where available, internship outcomes and career function—not just the highest package.
