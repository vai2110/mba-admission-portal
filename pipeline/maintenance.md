# Monthly SEO maintenance job

Run once per month and before each major admission cycle.

1. Re-crawl every indexable canonical URL and record HTTP status, canonical, robots directive and sitemap membership.
2. Re-fetch official institute admission, fee, placement and programme sources. Update facts only when the source has changed.
3. Keep average, median and highest placement figures separate and label each with its batch/year.
4. Re-check cutoffs, exam dates and eligibility against the official institute or exam-conducting-body source.
5. Update the page's Last updated date only when substantive content changes.
6. Check for new duplicate slugs, orphan pages, empty FAQ answers, missing sources and thin content.
7. Refresh CAT, XAT, SNAP, CMAT and NMAT pages when the official exam authority changes dates or notices.
8. Run the independent content audit, technical SEO audit, structured-data validation and mobile audit.
9. Log unresolved source gaps or conflicting facts in pipeline/needs-human.md. Do not guess or silently remove pages.
