How BBAU Admission Works in 2026

BBAU does not use one entrance test for every programme. The 2026-27 notices show CUET-based UG admissions, CUET and separate processes for PG programmes, and JEE-based counselling for B.Tech. Merit lists, vacant-seat notices and spot counselling are published during the cycle.

UG Admission 2026: CUET and Other Routes

BBAU's 2026 UG cycle includes CUET-based admissions. The university is also publishing notices for B.Tech through JEE and for CUET and non-CUET candidates. The correct route therefore depends on the course.

PG Admission 2026: CUET and Non-CUET

The official PG page lists prospectus, registration, score lists, multiple merit phases and spot rounds for 2026-27. BBAU also publishes separate notices for PG skill-based non-CUET programmes.

B.Tech Admission 2026

BBAU has published a separate 2026-27 B.Tech admission notice through JEE. Programme-wise score lists, merit lists and vacant-seat details are available for Computer Science, Electrical, Civil, Mechanical & Automobile and Electronics & Communication Engineering.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Entrance score/rank details where applicable.
  • Class 10 and Class 12 records for UG.
  • Graduation records for PG.
  • Photo identity proof.
  • Category/EWS/PwD certificates where applicable.
  • Photographs and application records.
  • Migration, transfer or character documents where required.

Merit Lists, Cutoffs and Spot Rounds

BBAU publishes score lists, phase-wise merit lists and vacant-seat details rather than one permanent cutoff. Admission chances depend on programme, category, route, phase and seats remaining.

BBAU Admission FAQs

Is BBAU admission open for 2026-27?

The official site is actively publishing 2026-27 merit, vacant-seat and spot-round notices. Availability is programme-specific.

Does BBAU accept CUET?

Yes. CUET is used for multiple UG and PG admissions, while B.Tech uses JEE and some PG skill-based programmes have non-CUET routes.

Does BBAU conduct spot counselling?

Yes. The 2026-27 cycle includes spot counselling and vacant-seat notices.

How can I contact the admission helpdesk?

The official helpdesk lists 1800-180-5789 and admission@bbau.ac.in.
