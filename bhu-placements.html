How good are BHU placements?

BHU placement data should be read at two levels: university-level NIRF reporting and programme/faculty-level placement reports. NIRF provides a broader view of students placed and median salary, while BHU's Annual Report provides more detailed examples of recruiters and salary ranges from individual academic units.

This distinction matters because BHU has hundreds of programmes. A placement number from one faculty cannot be presented as the salary of every BHU student.

What does BHU's official placement report show?

The BHU Annual Report 2024-25 gives a detailed postgraduate placement example. In the reported academic unit, 115 male and 45 female students appeared for campus recruitment, and 110 male and 41 female students were placed.

Which companies recruit from BHU?

The 2024-25 Annual Report names recruiters participating in the reported campus drive, including Infosys, Wipro, EY, ICICI Bank, Federal Bank, SBI Life, Adani Wilmar, VECV and DCM Shriram.

BHU also maintains an official placement system and publishes placement-drive information through its website. Recruiter participation can vary by faculty, programme and year.

What do BHU placement numbers mean for a student?

Median salary is not the highest package

A ₹9 LPA median means half of the reported salaries are at or below that value and half are at or above it. It does not mean every BHU graduate earns ₹9 LPA.

University data is broader than course data

The NIRF numbers cover broad programme categories. A BA, BSc, BCom, law or management student should not treat the university-wide median as their course-specific salary guarantee.

Annual Report figures can be more specific

The 2024-25 Annual Report's 151 placements and ₹6–19.8 LPA range come from a particular reported academic unit. They are valuable recruiter evidence but should not be labelled as the average BHU package.

BHU placements FAQs

What is the BHU placement average package?

BHU's official NIRF data cited here reports median salaries, not one university-wide average package. The medians are ₹8 LPA for UG 3-year, ₹9 LPA for UG 4-year, ₹16 LPA for UG 5-year and ₹9 LPA for PG 2-year programmes.

What is the BHU highest package?

The official sources used here do not support one current university-wide highest package. The Annual Report example reports a salary range up to ₹19.8 LPA for the specific unit.

How many students are placed at BHU?

The official NIRF-based 2025 material reports 626 UG students and 1,341 PG students placed.

Which companies recruit from BHU?

The 2024-25 Annual Report names Infosys, Wipro, EY, ICICI Bank, Federal Bank, SBI Life, Adani Wilmar, VECV and DCM Shriram among recruiters for the reported placement drive.
