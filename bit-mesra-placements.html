BIT Mesra Placements: latest year-wise picture

The institute's current placement page reports that the 2025-26 recruitment season recorded 1,167 offers across full-time and internship roles, with more than 250 recruiters participating. The Mesra campus reported an ₹11.15 LPA average CTC and ₹1.40 crore highest package.

BIT Mesra B.Tech Placements 2024-25

The official B.Tech placement statistics report ₹1.45 crore highest CTC, ₹11.66 LPA average CTC and ₹8.8 LPA median CTC. The institute reported 550+ FTE offers and 300+ internship offers in the 2024-25 placement brochure.

The sector mix included IT services (22%), IT products (19%), core engineering (17%), consulting & analytics (13%) and education (13%). Other sectors included BFSI, e-commerce, automobile, telecom, oil/gas, FMCG and infrastructure/power.

BIT Mesra MBA Placements 2024-25

The official MBA placement brochure for 2024-25 reports a batch size of 125, with 118 registered for placement and 90+ students placed. The highest package was ₹15.12 LPA, average package ₹7.90 LPA and median package ₹8 LPA.

These MBA figures should not be mixed with the much higher B.Tech highest package because the programmes have different recruiter pools and salary distributions.

Top recruiters at BIT Mesra

The official 2024-25 placement material lists a broad recruiter base including Microsoft, NVIDIA, Samsung, Oracle, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, Walmart Global Tech, Adobe, Tata, Reliance, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, Accenture, TCS, Wipro and Tech Mahindra, among many others.

Recruiters vary by year, branch and role. A company appearing in a historical recruiter list should not be interpreted as a guaranteed recruiter for every future batch.

BIT Mesra Placements FAQs

What is the average package at BIT Mesra?

The institute's latest Mesra-campus placement page reports an ₹11.15 LPA average CTC for 2025-26. For B.Tech specifically, the 2024-25 placement statistics report ₹11.66 LPA average.

What is the highest package at BIT Mesra?

The 2025-26 Mesra-campus headline reports a ₹1.40 crore highest package. The 2024-25 B.Tech placement statistics reported ₹1.45 crore.

What is the B.Tech median package at BIT Mesra?

The official 2024-25 B.Tech statistics report a ₹8.8 LPA median CTC.

What is the MBA average package at BIT Mesra?

The official MBA placement brochure reports a ₹7.90 LPA average package for 2024-25, with ₹8 LPA median and ₹15.12 LPA highest.

Does BIT Mesra have good placements?

The official data shows a broad recruiter base and strong outcomes, particularly for engineering. Students should judge the result by branch, average/median CTC and the type of role, not only the highest package.
