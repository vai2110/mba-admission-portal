Which BCA should you choose?

Choose the general BCA if you want flexibility across software and IT roles. Choose BCA with AI & ML if you specifically want an AI/data-oriented curriculum and are comfortable with a higher fee.

What You Study in Chitkara BCA

The general BCA covers programming, data structures, networking, cybersecurity and software development. The AI & ML route adds AI technologies, data analysis, deep learning, neural networks, automation and algorithm design.

BCA Career Options

Chitkara lists career possibilities including AI Developer, Machine Learning Engineer, Software Developer, Data Scientist, Systems Analyst, Systems Administrator and Project Manager. Students should also build a portfolio, internship experience and practical technical skills to compete for these roles.

Chitkara BCA FAQs

What is the BCA eligibility at Chitkara?

The current BCA page states XII passed with a minimum aggregate of 50%, any stream, followed by a personal interview.

What is the BCA fee?

The current page lists ₹70,000 for Sem I and II and ₹65,000 for Sem III-VI, including the stated ETS/CAS charges in the first two semesters.

Is BCA with AI & ML available?

Yes. Chitkara's Department of Computer Applications lists a three-year BCA with AI & ML.

Can I become a data scientist after BCA?

Yes, but the degree alone is not enough. Build Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, data visualisation and project skills, and consider postgraduate study where appropriate.
