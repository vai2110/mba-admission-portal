Chitkara B.Tech Admission 2026

The 2026 engineering admission document states that B.E. seats are allocated through either JEE Main 2026 performance or Class 12 marks. The two routes have different qualifying thresholds.

JEE Main route

  • Appear in JEE Main 2026 January/April sessions.
  • Meet the prescribed Class XII aggregate/PCM requirement.
  • Have passed XII in 2025 or appear in 2026.
  • Allocation is based on JEE Main AIR and preferences during counselling.

Class 12 route

  • Meet the published 70% aggregate or 70% PCM threshold.
  • Pass XII in 2025 or appear in 2026.
  • Follow the university counselling schedule and programme preference process.
Can I get Chitkara B.Tech without JEE?

Yes. The 2026 engineering document provides a Class 12 marks-based route, subject to its eligibility and counselling rules.

Chitkara B.Tech FAQs

Is JEE Main compulsory for Chitkara B.Tech?

No. The 2026 document provides both a JEE Main-based route and a Class 12 marks route.

What is the eligibility for Chitkara B.Tech?

It depends on the admission route. The JEE route and Class 12 route have different published thresholds; check the current 2026 engineering document for the exact branch.

Does Chitkara B.Tech require a laptop?

Yes. The 2026 engineering admission document says candidates granted admission to B.E. are required to have a laptop.

Is Chitkara CSE good?

It is relevant for students seeking a broad CSE curriculum with emerging-tech options, industry exposure and a large private-university placement ecosystem. Compare course-level outcomes with your alternatives before deciding.
