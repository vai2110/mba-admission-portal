IBS Hyderabad MBA Fees 2026

The published IBS Hyderabad MBA fee is ₹17.01 lakh for two years, or ₹8.505 lakh per year. A refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit is additional. Living expenses are extra.

Hostel and Additional Costs

Hostel facilities are available for MBA students, while day scholars can use campus transport on payment basis. IFHE's fee information also lists a refundable hostel security deposit and mess deposit. Exact accommodation charges should be confirmed for the intake because room availability and fee schedules can change.

Budget beyond tuition

Include hostel, mess, transport, caution/security deposits, laptop, books/materials and personal expenses when estimating your actual cost.

How to Evaluate IFHE Fee vs ROI

For IBS Hyderabad MBA, compare the ₹17.01 lakh programme fee plus living costs with the typical placement outcome rather than the highest package. The 2025 reported average CTC was ₹9.82 LPA and the NIRF-based median was ₹9 lakh.

Useful numbers

  • Programme fee: ₹17.01 lakh
  • 2025 average CTC: ₹9.82 LPA
  • 2025 median: ₹9 LPA

Do not ignore

  • Taxes and take-home salary.
  • Living expenses.
  • Loan interest.
  • Role quality and career growth.

Student Discussion: Is the Fee Worth It?

The most useful way to answer the ROI question is to compare the exact fee you will pay with the roles you are likely to target. A candidate aiming for finance, consulting, analytics or marketing should check role mix rather than relying on a single average CTC.
