What is FORE School of Management?

FORE School of Management offers four main two-year full-time PGDM programmes: PGDM, PGDM (International Business), PGDM (Financial Management) and PGDM (Big Data Analytics). The institute's 2026 admission material accepts CAT, XAT and GMAT for these programmes.

What is the eligibility for FORE PGDM?

A bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification with at least 50% marks in aggregate is required. A three-year graduation programme after 10+2 is accepted. Final-year students can apply subject to completing graduation with the required marks.

  • 2026-28 routes: CAT 2025, XAT 2026 or valid GMAT score.
  • For the 2026-28 process, FORE's FAQ accepted GMAT scores taken between 1 January 2024 and 31 January 2026.

How much does FORE PGDM cost?

The 2026 programme fee is ₹23.24 lakh for PGDM, PGDM(IB), PGDM(FM) and PGDM(BDA). A ₹23,000 one-time registration fee is payable separately.
