What is FORE School of Management?

FORE School of Management (FSM) offers two-year full-time PGDM programmes in New Delhi and Gurugram. Its core four programmes are PGDM, PGDM (International Business), PGDM (Financial Management) and PGDM (Big Data Analytics). PGDM(BDA) is offered exclusively at the Gurugram campus.

The institute's 2026 admission material lists a 50% bachelor's-degree requirement and accepts CAT, XAT and GMAT for the four main programmes. The institute also uses an extempore and personal interview in its selection process.

The common programme fee is ₹23.24 lakh for the four programmes. The fee includes tuition, examination, co-curricular activities, library, books, IT services and the complimentary international immersion component; a ₹23,000 one-time registration fee is payable separately.

What is the eligibility for FORE PGDM?

You need a bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification with at least 50% marks in aggregate. A three-year graduation programme after 10+2 is accepted. Final-year graduation students can also apply, subject to completing graduation with the required 50% marks.

  • Accepted entrance routes for the 2026-28 full-time programmes: CAT-2025, XAT-2026 or a valid GMAT score.
  • FORE's FAQ states that GMAT scores taken between 1 January 2024 and 31 January 2026 were valid for the 2026-28 process.
  • All four main programmes are AICTE approved.

What is the FORE School of Management cutoff?

FORE does not publish one universal final CAT/XAT/GMAT cutoff. Its official brochure gives the median entrance percentiles of students admitted to the 2025-27 batch, which is a more useful programme-wise benchmark for students than an invented single cutoff.

Within academics, the published split is Class X 5%, Class XII 5%, graduation 12%; the graduation component includes an additional 5% for candidates with a STEM background within the stated 12% academic framework.

The selection process includes Extempore and Personal Interview. The institute can add or remove selection criteria and informs candidates of the final criteria before the selection process.

What are FORE placements like?

FORE's latest published final placement material for the 2023-25 batch reports 138 corporate partners, including 45 new recruiters, with 466 students across PGDM, PGDM(IB), PGDM(FM) and PGDM(BDA). The reported average CTC was ₹16.4 lakh, median ₹15.2 lakh and highest ₹29 lakh.

The largest offer share came from BFSI at 33%, followed by IT/ITES at 23% and MR & Consulting at 23%.

FORE School of Management FAQs

Which FORE PGDM has the lowest published admitted-student median CAT percentile?

PGDM(BDA) had the lowest published median at 74.75 percentile for the 2025-27 admitted batch. This is not a guaranteed cutoff.

Can I apply to more than one FORE programme?

Yes. The 2026 application fee increased from ₹1,500 for one programme to ₹3,000 for all four programmes when selected together in the same application.

Is PGDM(BDA) offered in Delhi?

No. FORE's current admissions information states that PGDM(BDA) is offered exclusively at the Gurugram campus.

Does FORE accept only CAT?

No. For the 2026-28 main full-time programmes, FORE accepted CAT-2025, XAT-2026 and valid GMAT scores. The current admissions portal also lists CMAT, GRE and the FORE Aptitude Test for certain current programmes, so students should check the exact programme's current notice.

Is the ₹23.24 lakh fee the total amount?

It is the published programme fee for the four main full-time PGDMs. A separate ₹23,000 one-time registration fee is payable. Hostel and personal living expenses are outside this programme fee.
