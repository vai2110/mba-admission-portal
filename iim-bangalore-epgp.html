IIM Bangalore EPGP: Who the One-Year MBA Is Designed For

The Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) is IIM Bangalore's one-year, full-time residential MBA for experienced professionals. For 2027-28, IIMB requires at least five years of full-time work experience after completing a bachelor's degree. The programme is therefore structurally different from the two-year PGP: applicants are expected to bring substantial professional experience into an accelerated MBA environment.

1 yearFull-time residential MBA
5+ yearsMinimum full-time work experience as on March 31, 2027
GMAT / GREValid score required for EPGP 2027-28
Decision point: EPGP is better suited to a professional who wants to accelerate a career transition, broaden management capability or move toward senior leadership without spending two years away from the workplace. It is not intended as a substitute for the two-year PGP for fresh graduates.

IIM Bangalore EPGP Eligibility 2027-28

IIMB's current 2027-28 admission process gives a clear three-part eligibility test: academic qualification, qualifying work experience and a valid standardized test score.

Academic eligibility

Requirement2027-28 criterion
Bachelor's degreeBachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA.
SC / ST / PwDAt least 45% marks or equivalent CGPA.
Professional qualification routeApplicants who do not have a bachelor's degree but completed CA/ICWA/CS after Class XII may also apply, subject to IIMB's stated minimum percentage/CGPA requirement.
Work experienceMinimum 5 years of full-time work experience after bachelor's degree, calculated as on March 31, 2027. IIMB says less than 12 years is preferable.
TestValid GMAT or GRE score on the application date for the relevant round.

What work experience counts?

The requirement is specifically full-time work experience after completion of the bachelor's degree. Applicants should document joining dates and employment history through work-experience letters and/or first and last payslips as required by IIMB. The admissions page also requires a statement of purpose and two online reference letters.

Documents required

  • SSC, HSC and bachelor's degree transcripts
  • Bachelor's degree certificate
  • GMAT/GRE scorecard
  • Statement of purpose describing achievements and future goals
  • Work-experience letters / first and last payslips showing joining date
  • Applicable category or disability certificates

IIM Bangalore EPGP Admission Process 2027-28

The EPGP selection process is conducted in two rounds. Candidates may apply in either Round 1 or Round 2, but IIMB states that applicants should apply in only one round.

Confirm the five-year experience requirementCheck your qualifying post-graduation work experience as on March 31, 2027 before choosing EPGP.
Prepare a valid GMAT or GRE scoreThe score must be valid for the application round. Online GMAT/GRE tests taken at home are not accepted for EPGP 2027.
Submit the application and referencesUpload academic, test, work-experience and SOP documents. Two online references are mandatory and must be completed before the application can be submitted.
Clear Stage I screeningOnly complete applications satisfying the minimum academic and other criteria move forward to the shortlist stage.
Attend the interviewShortlisted candidates are called for an interview. The current schedule lists Bangalore, New Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata as interview cities.
Final composite scoreIIMB evaluates academic track record, GMAT/GRE, quality of work experience, writing ability test performance and interview performance before making offers based on the composite score.

IIM Bangalore EPGP GMAT/GRE & Selection Criteria

IIMB does not publish a single fixed GMAT or GRE percentile cutoff for EPGP 2027-28 on its current admission process page. Instead, the standardized test score is one component of a broader composite evaluation.

What the final evaluation considers

Selection factorHow it is used
Academic track recordAssesses the candidate's academic foundation.
GMAT / GREStandardized test performance contributes to the composite score.
Quality of work experienceRelevant professional impact and experience quality matter beyond simply counting years.
Writing Ability TestEvaluates written reasoning and communication in the selection stage.
InterviewAssesses fit, goals, experience and potential for success in management.
Do not chase a number in isolation: Because IIMB does not publish a single guaranteed GMAT/GRE score for admission, a competitive application should demonstrate career progression, leadership exposure, quantified impact, clear post-MBA goals and strong communication alongside the test score.

IIM Bangalore EPGP Curriculum & International Immersion

The one-year EPGP is built for experienced professionals, so the curriculum is delivered at an accelerated pace. IIMB describes the programme as a rigorous MBA experience and the international immersion component is compulsory.

What students study

The curriculum covers the major management functions through core and elective learning, with emphasis on applying management concepts to complex organisational situations. Students should expect a high-intensity schedule because the programme compresses a broad MBA experience into one academic year.

International immersion

The international immersion is a compulsory component of EPGP. It adds an international business-learning experience and is separately charged in the published fee structure.

Who benefits most from the format?

Professionals with clear career goals, substantial work context and an ability to translate classroom frameworks into organisational decisions are likely to benefit more from the accelerated format than candidates who are still exploring their career direction.

Who Should Apply for IIM Bangalore EPGP?

Strong fit if you

  • Have at least five years of post-graduation full-time experience and want a one-year MBA.
  • Want to move toward consulting, technology/product, strategy, finance or senior management.
  • Can articulate a clear career goal and show progression or impact in your professional experience.
  • Want an intensive residential MBA with compulsory international immersion.

Think carefully if you

  • Have less than five years of qualifying work experience.
  • Want a two-year MBA with a longer internship and exploration period.
  • Are applying mainly because of the IIMB brand without a defined post-MBA objective.
  • Need a distance or part-time format; EPGP is full-time and residential.
Student decision rule: EPGP makes the most sense when the value of an accelerated IIMB MBA and career transition outweighs the opportunity cost of stepping away from work for one year.

Frequently Asked Questions About IIM Bangalore EPGP

What is IIM Bangalore EPGP?

EPGP is IIM Bangalore's one-year full-time residential MBA for experienced professionals.

What is the IIM Bangalore EPGP eligibility for 2027-28?

A bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required, 45% for SC, ST and PwD applicants, along with at least five years of full-time work experience after graduation as on March 31, 2027 and a valid GMAT or GRE score.

Does IIM Bangalore EPGP accept CAT?

No. The 2027-28 EPGP admission process requires a valid GMAT or GRE score.

What is the IIM Bangalore EPGP GMAT cutoff?

IIMB does not publish a single fixed GMAT cutoff on the current 2027-28 admission process page. GMAT/GRE is evaluated as part of a composite selection process.

What is the latest IIM Bangalore EPGP fee?

The latest published 2026-27 reference is ₹26.5 lakh tuition, with additional international immersion, accommodation, mess and deposit components. The confirmed 2027-28 fee should be checked when IIMB publishes it.

What was the EPGP average salary in 2025-26?

IIMB's 2026 placement report records an average salary of ₹37.10 lakh and median salary of ₹36.50 lakh for EPGP 2025-26.
