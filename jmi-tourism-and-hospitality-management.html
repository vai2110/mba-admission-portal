Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management at JMI

The Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management at Jamia Millia Islamia was established in 2012. It offers academic and professional training in tourism, travel, hotel management and food production. The department combines classroom learning with applied and industry-oriented skills relevant to travel operations, destination development and hospitality services.

Who should explore this department? Students interested in tourism management, travel operations, hotel administration, hospitality services, food production and related service-sector careers.

JMI BTTM: Duration, Eligibility and Intake

The official programme page for Bachelor of Tourism and Travel Management (BTTM), self-financed, lists a four-year programme with an intake of 40. The published eligibility requires a Senior Secondary School Certificate or equivalent from a recognised board, with at least 45% marks in the aggregate of the best five papers. The displayed fee on the official page is ₹30,185; applicants should confirm the amount in the prospectus for their admission year because fee schedules can be revised.

JMI Tourism and Hospitality Course Fees

Fees vary by course and may differ for regular and self-financed programmes. The BTTM programme page currently displays ₹30,185, but students should confirm the fee for the relevant admission session in the latest prospectus. Do not apply the BTTM fee to BHM, MTTM or other programmes.

Career Options after Tourism and Hospitality Courses

Depending on the programme and practical experience, graduates may explore roles in travel operations, tour planning, destination management, airlines and travel services, hotel operations, guest relations, food production, event operations and hospitality administration. Career outcomes depend on the course, internships, communication skills, operational exposure and employer requirements.

Which course should you choose?

  • Choose BTTM/MTTM if your interest is tourism, travel and destination operations.
  • Choose BHM if you want hotel and hospitality operations training.
  • Choose B.Voc. Food Production if you prefer hands-on culinary and food operations training.
