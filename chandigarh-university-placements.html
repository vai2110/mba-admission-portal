Chandigarh University Placements: Latest Official Highlights

For the 2024-25 batch, Chandigarh University's placement pages report more than 10,000 placement offers and more than 1,300 companies visited for recruitment. The university reports a highest national package of ₹59.9 LPA and a highest international package of ₹1.7 crore.

Is ₹59.9 LPA the average Chandigarh University package?

No. ₹59.9 LPA is the highest national package reported by the university. It should never be described as the average or median salary. Students should look at salary bands and course-level outcomes when estimating their own likely result.

Chandigarh University Recruiters and Placement Roles

CU's official website showcases a broad recruiter network and highlights names including Google, Amazon and Microsoft on its placement pages. Joint placement drives also list employers such as Sify Technologies, CtrlS, JBM Group, Daikin Industries and BL Kashyap & Sons for specific engineering cohorts.

Why recruiter names alone are not enough

A recruiter appearing on campus does not mean every programme or student is eligible for every role. The useful comparison is the combination of recruiter, role, eligible branch, salary band and number of offers. Students should therefore use the placement tracker and department-specific information alongside the recruiter logo wall.

How to Judge Chandigarh University Placements Before Taking Admission

Positive signals

  • Large recruiter participation and 10,000+ offers reported for 2024-25.
  • Multiple salary bands rather than only one headline package.
  • Dedicated placement analysis for several academic areas.
  • Joint campus drives that expand recruiter access for selected cohorts.

What needs caution

  • Highest package is not average or median salary.
  • University-wide figures do not represent every branch.
  • “Company visited” is different from number of offers made.
  • Students should compare role quality and salary distribution, not only recruiter count.
What is the realistic way to compare CU placements with another college?

Use the same year and the same course, then compare average/median salary where available, number of placed students, recruiter and role mix, salary bands, internships and the proportion of students receiving offers. Comparing one college's highest package with another college's average produces a misleading conclusion.

Chandigarh University Placement FAQs

What is the highest package at Chandigarh University?

CU currently reports ₹59.9 LPA as its highest national package and ₹1.7 crore as its highest international package for its placement ecosystem.

How many students were placed at Chandigarh University in 2024-25?

The university's current placement pages state 10,000+ placement offers for the 2024-25 batch. This is an offers figure; it should not automatically be treated as the exact number of unique students placed.

How many companies visit Chandigarh University for placements?

The university currently reports 1,300+ companies visited for recruitment. The exact set of eligible companies varies by course, branch and graduating cohort.

Is Chandigarh University's ₹59.9 LPA package realistic for B.Tech CSE students?

It is a reported highest national outcome, not a typical CSE salary. A CSE applicant should examine CSE-specific recruiter roles and salary bands before using the figure in a personal ROI calculation.

Does Chandigarh University have MBA placements?

Yes. CU publishes management-specific placement information, and its MBA admissions portal currently highlights a ₹28 LPA highest package, 400+ MNCs recruiting management students and 1,427 offers for management students.
