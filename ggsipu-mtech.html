GGSIPU M.Tech Overview

GGSIPU offers multiple postgraduate technology programmes through University Schools and participating institutes. Current 2026 notices show programme-specific admission and counselling schedules, including separate notices for M.Tech Mechanical Engineering and technology programmes.

Admission 2026

The admission route depends on the M.Tech programme. Applicants should check the current PG brochure and programme-specific notice for GATE/CET/other criteria, application dates, test requirements and counselling mode.

Eligibility

Applicants generally need a relevant engineering/technology or allied qualifying degree, but the exact discipline and marks requirements are programme-specific. Check the applicable 2026 notice.

Fees

M.Tech fees vary by programme and institute. Include tuition, examination/university charges, hostel and other living costs in the final budget.

GGSIPU M.Tech FAQs

Is GATE required for every GGSIPU M.Tech?

The applicable admission route varies by programme and notification. Check the exact 2026 programme notice rather than assuming one rule for all M.Tech courses.

Does GGSIPU conduct M.Tech counselling?

Yes, programme-specific counselling/admission notices are issued, including notices for 2026 M.Tech programmes.
