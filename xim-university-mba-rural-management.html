What Is XIM MBA-RM?

MBA-Rural Management is a specialised two-year management programme focused on rural management, development and the intersection of business, communities and the development sector. It is suited to students who want careers beyond conventional corporate management.

Best fit

Consider MBA-RM if you are genuinely interested in rural markets, development organisations, social impact, livelihoods, agriculture-linked ecosystems, CSR or inclusive business.

MBA-RM Eligibility 2026

The published eligibility requires a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 55% aggregate marks. XIM also states that undergraduate rank holders can apply under the stated route.

MBA-RM Admission & Entrance Tests

The 2026 admission information lists XAT, CAT, GMAT, XGMT and NMAT as test routes for MBA-RM. Applicants should check the current XIM bulletin because test windows and eligibility conditions can change by intake.

MBA-RM Fees 2026

The published 2026 course fee is ₹7 lakh in Year 1 and ₹7 lakh in Year 2. Other applicable charges include development fund, course materials, IT, alumni activities, career advisory services, field learning and refundable deposit.

MBA-RM Career Options

Career paths can include development consulting, rural marketing, microfinance, livelihoods, CSR, social enterprises, NGOs, government-linked projects and inclusive-business roles. The fit is strongest when you want the rural/development sector rather than a generic MBA job profile.

Good fit for

  • Development-sector careers
  • CSR and social impact
  • Rural markets
  • Microfinance and livelihoods

Think twice if

  • You only want conventional investment banking.
  • You want a broad corporate MBA without sector focus.
  • You are not interested in field-oriented learning.

FAQs

What is the MBA-RM fee?

The 2026 course fee is ₹7 lakh in each year, before other applicable fees and residential expenses.

Is MBA-RM only for rural-background students?

No. The published eligibility is open to bachelor's graduates from any discipline meeting the marks requirement.

Which tests are accepted?

XAT, CAT, GMAT, XGMT and NMAT are listed in the 2026 admission information.
