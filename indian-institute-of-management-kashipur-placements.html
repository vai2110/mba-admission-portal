IIM Kashipur Placement Highlights 2025

Quick answer

IIM Kashipur Salary Analysis

The audited report classifies salary outcomes by sector, function and location. The institute states that salary data is presented as Cost to Company and that the auditor validated remuneration, function, sector and location data using communication received from recruiting companies.

Recruiters and Hiring Sectors

The 2023-25 report records more than 190 participating organizations. Hiring covered Consulting, Sales & Marketing, Business Analytics, General Management, Finance, HR, Operations and Product Management.

IIM Kashipur Placement Process

The official placement FAQ states that student recruitment happens through an 8–10 week full-time summer internship process and final recruitment. The final placement process for final-year students tentatively starts in October, while summer placements tentatively start in the first week of September.

What recruiters assess

Role fit, academic profile, prior experience, functional skills, interview performance and the requirements of individual recruiting organizations can influence outcomes. The institute also highlights corporate interactions, live projects and industry workshops as part of its broader industry interface.
