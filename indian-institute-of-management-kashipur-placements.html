Overview

IIM Kashipur is a premier management institute offering full-time residential MBA and MBA (Analytics) programmes, alongside Executive MBA and Executive MBA (Analytics) options. The institute focuses on academic excellence, industry-aligned curriculum, and international exposure through student exchange partnerships.

Programmes

MBA (Analytics)

A two-year, six-term residential programme. The curriculum includes core management and analytics courses, electives, a summer internship, and a dissertation.

Executive MBA (Analytics)

A two-year, six-term blended learning programme for working professionals. Eligibility requires a bachelor's degree (min 45% marks) and 3 years of full-time work experience. Total fees: ₹12,00,000 (Year 1: ₹6,60,000; Year 2: ₹5,40,000).

Placement Process

Placement activities are managed by the institute's dedicated Placement Office. The process includes corporate interactions, live projects, and industry workshops. Official year-wise placement outcome data is available via the official IIM Kashipur placement reports portal.

FAQs

What is the duration of the MBA (Analytics) programme?
It is a two-year, six-term full-time residential programme.
What is the eligibility for Executive MBA (Analytics)?
Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree with at least 45% marks and possess a minimum of 3 years of full-time post-bachelor work experience.
Does IIM Kashipur offer international exchange?
Yes, the institute facilitates student exchange programmes with various international partner universities.
