Jamia Hamdard

What is Jamia Hamdard known for?

Quick answer

Jamia Hamdard is a deemed-to-be university in Hamdard Nagar, New Delhi. It has a particularly strong identity in pharmacy and health sciences, while also offering management, engineering, computer applications, law, nursing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, Unani medicine and other programmes.

For students comparing Delhi universities, the most important distinction is that Jamia Hamdard is not simply a general management or engineering college. Its strongest national academic positioning is in pharmacy: the Ministry of Education's NIRF 2025 Pharmacy ranking placed Jamia Hamdard at Rank 1 with a score of 86.59.

The university also appears in NIRF 2025's Overall ranking at Rank 74 and Medical ranking at Rank 40. These rankings are category-specific and should not be interpreted as a universal ranking of every programme.

Students should therefore judge Jamia Hamdard programme-by-programme. Pharmacy, health sciences and allied areas have a different value proposition from MBA, engineering or general UG programmes.

Jamia Hamdard FAQs

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA fee?

The official fee sheet currently linked by the university lists ₹2.60 lakh per year for MBA in all disciplines, before one-time and annual examination/e-governance charges. The fee sheet is labelled 2025-26 and the university has a 2026 fee corrigendum, so students should verify the current 2026-27 amount before payment.

What is the Jamia Hamdard B.Pharm fee?

The published fee sheet lists ₹1.60 lakh per year for B.Pharm General and ₹2.40 lakh per year for B.Pharm SFS, before applicable one-time and examination/e-governance charges.

What are the additional Jamia Hamdard fees?

The published fee sheet lists ₹5,000 admission, ₹5,000 enrolment, ₹3,000 alumni fund and ₹5,000 refundable library deposit as one-time charges. For all other schools it lists ₹10,000 examination and ₹1,000 e-governance fees annually.

Is hostel included in Jamia Hamdard fees?

Hostel and mess are separate costs and should be added to the academic fee when calculating the total cost of attendance.

Is Jamia Hamdard good for pharmacy?

Yes. Pharmacy is one of Jamia Hamdard's strongest areas. NIRF 2025 placed Jamia Hamdard Rank 1 in Pharmacy nationally.

Are Jamia Hamdard admissions open for 2026?

The official 2026 admission notice page says applications for UG and PG programmes are live and lists programme-specific 2026 notices.
