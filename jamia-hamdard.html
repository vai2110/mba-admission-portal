What is Jamia Hamdard known for?

Quick answer

Jamia Hamdard is a deemed-to-be university in Hamdard Nagar, New Delhi. It has a particularly strong identity in pharmacy and health sciences, while also offering management, engineering, computer applications, law, nursing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, Unani medicine and other programmes.

For students comparing Delhi universities, the most important distinction is that Jamia Hamdard is not simply a general management or engineering college. Its strongest national academic positioning is in pharmacy: the Ministry of Education's NIRF 2025 Pharmacy ranking placed Jamia Hamdard at Rank 1 with a score of 86.59.

The university also appears in NIRF 2025's Overall ranking at Rank 74 and Medical ranking at Rank 40. These rankings are category-specific and should not be interpreted as a universal ranking of every programme.

Students should therefore judge Jamia Hamdard programme-by-programme. Pharmacy, health sciences and allied areas have a different value proposition from MBA, engineering or general UG programmes.

Jamia Hamdard FAQs: 12 questions students actually search

Is Jamia Hamdard a good university?

It depends on the programme. Jamia Hamdard has particularly strong academic positioning in pharmacy and health sciences. NIRF 2025 placed it Rank 1 in Pharmacy, while it was Rank 74 in the Overall category and Rank 40 in Medical. Students should not use the Pharmacy ranking as a proxy for MBA, engineering or every other course. Compare the specific programme's fees, curriculum, placement outcomes and admission route before deciding.

What is Jamia Hamdard famous for?

Jamia Hamdard is best known nationally for pharmacy, pharmaceutical education and health sciences. Its School of Pharmaceutical Education & Research offers B.Pharm, D.Pharm, M.Pharm specialisations and PhD programmes, and the university's Pharmacy ranking is a major differentiator. It also offers management, engineering, computer applications, nursing, allied health, law and other disciplines.

Is Jamia Hamdard admission open for 2026-27?

Yes. The university's current 2026 admission notice page says applications for UG and PG programmes are live. The same page lists programme-specific notices including MBA GD/PI rounds, UG NEET-based admissions, M.Sc Nursing and other admission updates. Because dates vary by programme, students should check the current 2026-27 notice rather than relying on one university-wide deadline.

How can I apply for Jamia Hamdard admission 2026?

Applications are submitted through the Jamia Hamdard admission portal. The university's admissions support page directs applicants to the portal and says selection criteria should be checked in the 2026-27 prospectus. After applying, students should monitor the admission notices and selection-list pages for programme-specific updates. Admission confirmation is communicated by the JH Admission Cell.

Which entrance exams are accepted by Jamia Hamdard?

There is no single entrance exam for every Jamia Hamdard course. The applicable route is programme-specific. The university's prospectus has used routes such as CUET, NEET-UG, JEE Main and CLAT wherever applicable, while other programmes can use qualifying-exam merit, university entrance tests, interviews or other approved selection methods. Always check the 2026-27 prospectus and course notice for the exact programme you want.

What is the eligibility for B.Pharm at Jamia Hamdard?

The university's pharmacy information states that B.Pharm is an eight-semester programme. The published eligibility requires 10+2 with PCB and at least 50% marks in the aggregate, along with the applicable age requirement under PCI norms. The university has also described selection through the applicable entrance/merit route. Since admission rules can change by session, candidates should use the 2026-27 programme notice before applying.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA admission process?

For MBA, the 2026-27 admission cycle includes a university application followed by a GD/PI process. The official 2026 admission notices include MBA interview and GD/PI schedules, including a third round for MBA General. Students should check the current prospectus and MBA notice for eligibility, accepted score/selection components and the applicable deadline for their round.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA fee?

The university's fee PDF currently linked from its admission portal lists ₹2.60 lakh per year for MBA before applicable additional charges; that PDF is labelled 2025-26. However, a 2026-27 MBA General selection list shows an SFS semester fee of ₹1.40 lakh plus listed admission, enrolment, library, alumni, examination and e-governance charges. This means students should check the exact 2026-27 fee applicable to their MBA category/offer instead of assuming one number applies to every route.

What is the Jamia Hamdard B.Pharm fee?

The published fee structure currently linked by the university lists ₹1.60 lakh per year for B.Pharm General and ₹2.40 lakh per year for B.Pharm SFS. Students also need to account for applicable one-time admission, enrolment, alumni and refundable library-deposit charges plus annual examination/e-governance charges. The 2026-27 fee corrigendum should be checked before payment because the currently accessible fee PDF is labelled 2025-26.

Does Jamia Hamdard provide hostel facilities?

Yes, Jamia Hamdard has separate boys' and girls' hostels with options including AC rooms, non-AC rooms and dormitories. However, admission to the university does not guarantee hostel allotment because accommodation is limited. The university says hostel applicants should apply through the student/hostel process, and allotted students must pay hostel charges in advance and join the mess. Hostel and mess costs should therefore be budgeted separately from academic fees.

What is Jamia Hamdard's placement record?

Placement outcomes vary considerably by programme. NIRF 2025 Management data reports 202 of 230 graduating-in-minimum-time students placed in 2023-24, with a ₹5.95 lakh median salary. NIRF 2025 Engineering data reports 197 of 337 UG engineering graduates placed with a ₹5.45 lakh median, while the two-year engineering PG category recorded 42 of 60 placed with a ₹6.20 lakh median. These are category-specific official submissions, not a single university-wide package.

What is Jamia Hamdard's NIRF ranking for Pharmacy?

Jamia Hamdard was ranked 1st in Pharmacy in NIRF 2025, with a score of 86.59. The ranking is specifically for the Pharmacy category and should be interpreted as evidence of the university's strength in pharmacy education/research; it does not mean every Jamia Hamdard programme has the same national standing.

Does Jamia Hamdard offer scholarships or fee concessions?

Jamia Hamdard's official FAQ says fee-concession queries are handled under the supervision of the Dean Students' Welfare and directs scholarship applicants to the National Scholarship Portal. Eligibility and availability depend on the applicable scheme, so students should not subtract a scholarship from their budget until they confirm that they qualify and receive approval.
