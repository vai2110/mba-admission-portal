Methodology & data note

XLRI's official information identifies XAT as the route for its PGDM BM/HRM/GM/LSCM programmes. XIM University lists XAT among the admission test options for MBA-BM, while IMT Ghaziabad accepts XAT for its full-time PGDM. CollegeDecoded deliberately avoids carrying older third-party XAT lists forward when a current official route is unclear.
