About MBA Marketing

Marketing is one of the specialisations offered within GEU's MBA structure. The pathway is relevant to students interested in customer behaviour, brand building, market research, digital channels and business growth.

Who should choose Marketing?

Choose it if you enjoy understanding customers, building campaigns, analysing markets and combining creativity with commercial decision-making.

Eligibility

Applicants need a bachelor's degree of at least three years from a recognised university. Final-year students are eligible to apply. Recognised CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/NMAT scores may be used under GEU's management admission policy.

Admission Process

Complete the online application, upload documents, pay the application fee and undergo the applicable assessment/interview process. The management department states that recognised entrance-test scores may provide exemption from the aptitude test.

MBA Marketing Fees 2026

Marketing is included in the general MBA fee structure. GEU's 2026 fee schedule varies by graduation percentage/entrance percentile and scholarship. The displayed total fee-per-year-installment ranges from ₹6,70,795 to ₹8,28,875 across the listed slabs.

Career After MBA Marketing

Career pathways can include marketing management, brand management, digital marketing, customer experience, market research, sales strategy, product marketing and growth roles. Practical campaign projects and internships can make the profile stronger.

Marketing Placement Signals

GEU's official MBA placement records list marketing students with employers such as Bal Media AID, Edoofa, Indigo Paints and Infoedge. The listed offers range from ₹6.75 LPA to ₹10 LPA in the current records and represent individual offers rather than an average salary.

MBA Marketing FAQs

Is Marketing available as an MBA specialisation at GEU?

Yes. Marketing is explicitly listed in GEU's MBA specialisation/fee structure.

What should I learn besides the MBA?

Build practical capability in market research, Excel/analytics, digital marketing, CRM, presentation and campaign measurement. These skills complement the academic programme.

Which companies are visible in current MBA marketing records?

The current official placement records include Bal Media AID, Edoofa, Indigo Paints and Infoedge among listed marketing offers.
