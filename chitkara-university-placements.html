Chitkara Placement Statistics

University-published material cited in current Chitkara content reports 1,000+ students placed on Day 1, 640+ super-dream offers above ₹10 LPA and a highest package of ₹1.11 crore. These are university-reported figures and should not be treated as a guaranteed package for every student.

Recruiters & Industry Exposure

University content references employers such as Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Deloitte, IBM, Infosys and Adobe in the context of internships or industry exposure. Recruiter participation varies by programme and hiring cycle.

Internships

Chitkara emphasises internships and practical industry exposure. Students should ask for programme-specific internship participation, duration, paid/unpaid status and conversion outcomes when evaluating the placement ecosystem.

How to Evaluate Placement ROI

Look beyond highest package

Compare median or average CTC, role quality, number of students placed and recruiter mix.

Match it to your programme

University-wide figures may combine many disciplines. Use the data for your exact course and graduating batch whenever possible.

Placement FAQs

Is ₹1.11 crore the average package?

No. It is a highest package figure reported in university material and should not be interpreted as the average salary.
