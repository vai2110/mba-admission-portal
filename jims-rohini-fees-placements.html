JIMS Rohini ROI: should you pay ₹10.75 lakh?

Positive signal

The current programme fee is ₹10.75 lakh before separate listed charges, while the reported PGDM median salary is ₹9.83 LPA. A Delhi location may also be advantageous for NCR-based students.

Do not oversimplify

Salary is not the same as take-home pay. Also account for accommodation, travel, living costs, taxes, loan interest and the opportunity cost of two years.

Bottom line: JIMS looks strongest for students who want a Delhi/NCR management career and can use the institute's corporate access. Compare it with alternatives using median salary, placement coverage, total cost and target-role fit, not only the ₹24 LPA headline.
