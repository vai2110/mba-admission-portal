IIM Jammu Summer Placements

IIM Jammu conducts summer internships after the first year. Its official placement archive lists summer reports for recent MBA and MBA HA & HM batches. The institute describes the internship as an opportunity to connect classroom concepts with real organisational situations.

Why summer placement matters

For MBA students, the summer internship can influence final-placement readiness, provide industry exposure and help test whether a chosen function or sector is the right fit.

How to Read IIM Jammu Placement Numbers

Use these metrics

  • Median CTC for a typical-outcome signal.
  • Average CTC for cohort-level comparison.
  • Highest CTC only as an upside indicator.
  • Recruiter and role mix for career-fit assessment.

Avoid this mistake

Do not assume that the highest package is what most students receive. Also avoid comparing a small specialised healthcare cohort directly with the much larger flagship MBA cohort without considering programme structure.

What is the IIM Jammu MBA average package?
