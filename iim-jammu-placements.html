How to Read IIM Jammu Placement Numbers

Use these metrics

  • Median CTC for a typical-outcome signal.
  • Average CTC for cohort-level comparison.
  • Highest CTC only as an upside indicator.
  • Recruiter and role mix for career-fit assessment.

Avoid this mistake

Do not assume that the highest package is what most students receive. Also avoid comparing a small specialised healthcare cohort directly with the much larger flagship MBA cohort without considering programme structure.

Students’ Discussions: What Aspirants Are Actually Worried About

Recent Reddit discussions show that applicants are less interested in the headline package alone and more concerned about role quality, sales-heavy hiring, finance/consulting opportunities, placement transparency and actual outcomes for freshers versus work-ex candidates. These are anecdotal discussions, not official statistics, but they reveal the questions students should ask before joining.

IIM Jammu Placements FAQs

What is the IIM Jammu MBA average package?

The latest completed 2023-25 MBA report recorded an average CTC of ₹16.04 LPA and median CTC of ₹15.80 LPA.

What is the highest package at IIM Jammu?

The latest completed 2023-25 MBA report recorded ₹26.60 LPA as the highest CTC. The earlier 2022-24 report had a higher peak, but it is no longer the latest completed cycle.

What is the IIM Jammu MBA average package in 2025?

The latest completed 2023-25 report records an average CTC of ₹16.04 LPA and median CTC of ₹15.80 LPA.

How many recruiters visited IIM Jammu for MBA placements?

The 2023-25 final placement report lists 200 recruiters, including 50 new recruiters.

How many PPOs were offered at IIM Jammu?

The 2023-25 report lists 24 PPOs.

Are IIM Jammu placements suitable for students who do not want sales?

There are finance, consulting, analytics, product, IT and general-management opportunities, but student discussions also raise concerns about sales/relationship-oriented roles. Candidates who want to avoid sales completely should inspect role descriptions and recruiter openings rather than rely on sector labels.

Does IIM Jammu have healthcare placements?

Yes. The MBA HA & HM programme has its own placement reports and the first batch recorded 100% placement with healthcare and healthcare-management roles.
