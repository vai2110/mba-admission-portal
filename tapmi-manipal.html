TAPMI Manipal: What Students Should Know Before Applying

T A Pai Management Institute (TAPMI) is a constituent unit of Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) located in Manipal, Karnataka. Established in 1981, TAPMI offers MBA programmes in core management as well as banking and financial services, HR, marketing, international business and newer technology/data-focused areas. TAPMI holds AACSB and AMBA accreditations, and its MBA, MBA-BKFS, MBA-HRM and MBA-Marketing programmes are NBA accredited.

For a student, the important question is not simply whether TAPMI is a known private B-school. The real decision depends on the programme you want, your entrance-score and academic profile, the total cost after hostel and mess, the roles available through placements and how comfortable you are with a residential campus in Manipal.

Is TAPMI Manipal worth considering?

TAPMI is particularly relevant for students who want a structured, residential MBA with strong experiential learning and specialised options. Its 2025 MBA Core placement report recorded 100% placement among students who opted in, with 340 of 340 interested students placed. The same report shows a strong BFSI, consulting and IT/analytics presence. Compare the total cost and role mix against the alternatives you can realistically convert before making the final decision.

TAPMI MBA Admission: Eligibility, Cutoff & Selection Process

TAPMI requires a graduate degree from a recognised university with at least 60% marks; final-year students can apply but admission is cancelled if their final graduation score falls below 60%. For the 2026 cycle, TAPMI accepted CAT 2025, XAT 2026 and GMAT scores, while NMAT 2025 was accepted for the relevant sectoral programmes. TAPMI says the highest submitted valid score can be considered when multiple test scores are provided.

Is there a fixed TAPMI cutoff?

TAPMI does not publish one permanent cutoff for every programme. The institute states that cutoffs can vary by year and programme depending on the applicant pool and programme fit. For the 2026 cycle, it stated a cutoff above 85 percentile for applicants with less than 12 months of work experience for MBA Technology Management; the core and other programmes use profile-based shortlisting rather than presenting one universal guaranteed-call percentile.

TAPMI application fee

The published application fee for 2026 ranged from ₹2,000 for one programme to ₹5,750 for seven programmes. The fee is non-refundable.

What does this mean for a student with a good CAT/XAT score?

A strong entrance score helps, but TAPMI explicitly evaluates the broader profile. If your academics, work experience, achievements and interview performance are weak, do not assume that a single percentile automatically guarantees selection.

TAPMI MBA Fees 2026-28, Hostel, Mess & Scholarships

For Indian domicile candidates, TAPMI currently publishes ₹9.75 lakh tuition fee per year for MBA, MBA-BKFS, MBA-HRM, MBA-IB, MBA-Marketing, MBA-Tech and MBA-AI & DS. That means the published two-year tuition component is ₹19.50 lakh before hostel, mess and other charges. TAPMI's admissions page currently says hostel and mess fees will be updated, so students should not present ₹19.50 lakh as the final all-inclusive cost.

TAPMI Manipal Curriculum, Campus & Student Life

TAPMI's MBA ecosystem is strongly experiential. The 2026-28 handbook shows a six-term structure and a summer internship, with courses designed around functional foundations, analytics, strategy and application. The flagship MBA also uses cross-functional experiential courses such as BrandScan, SEVA, SMIC, SAMNIDHY, Integrative Thinking Simulation and OMEGA.

Campus and hostel experience

TAPMI's Manipal campus is residential. The institute describes five hostel blocks with attached bathrooms, 24-hour hot water, Wi-Fi, lifts and backup power, with separate men's and women's hostels and both air-conditioned double and single rooms. The campus also has classrooms, lecture halls and a multi-cuisine canteen/mess.

International exposure

TAPMI has internationalisation partnerships supporting student and faculty exchange. The institute lists partners such as University of Akron, Montpellier Business School and Loughborough University, with opportunities depending on nomination, screening and programme arrangements.

What is the Manipal location like for an MBA student?

Manipal offers a university-town environment rather than a metro-city corporate ecosystem. That can work well if you want a contained residential campus, peer interaction and academic focus. Students who strongly prefer daily access to a large corporate hub should compare this lifestyle with city-based B-schools before deciding.

TAPMI Manipal FAQs

What is TAPMI MBA fees?

TAPMI currently publishes ₹9.75 lakh tuition per year for the 2026-28 MBA programmes for Indian domicile students, or ₹19.50 lakh tuition for two years. Hostel and mess charges are listed as to be updated, so the final residential cost should be confirmed from the official fee notice.

What is the TAPMI CAT cutoff?

TAPMI does not publish one permanent universal cutoff for all MBA programmes. It states that cutoffs can vary by year and programme and that shortlisting also considers the overall profile. For some programmes, such as MBA Technology Management, TAPMI published an above-85-percentile threshold for candidates with less than 12 months of work experience for the 2026 cycle.

Does TAPMI accept XAT and GMAT?

Yes. TAPMI's 2026 admission process accepted CAT, XAT and GMAT, with NMAT applicable to relevant sectoral programmes. Candidates could submit more than one valid test score.

Is TAPMI Manipal residential?

Yes. TAPMI's Manipal campus provides residential hostel facilities. The institute describes five hostel blocks with single and double air-conditioned rooms and attached bathrooms.

Is TAPMI accredited?

TAPMI is accredited by AACSB and AMBA. Its MBA, MBA-BKFS, MBA-HRM and MBA-Marketing programmes are also NBA accredited.

What is the latest TAPMI MBA placement package?

For the 2025 MBA Core placement report, the highest CTC was ₹32.77 LPA and the average CTC was ₹13.99 LPA. MBA-BKFS reported ₹26.25 LPA highest and ₹14.73 LPA average, while MBA-HRM reported ₹19.59 LPA highest and ₹12.86 LPA average.
