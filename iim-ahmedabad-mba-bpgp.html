What is IIM Ahmedabad MBA-BPGP?

BPGP is a two-year long-duration, degree-granting management programme primarily delivered online in synchronous mode, with distinct on-campus modules. IIMA designed the format for professionals who want to continue their careers while pursuing an MBA-level management programme.

The programme uses IIMA's case-based pedagogy and peer learning. It is therefore not simply a self-paced online course or a short certificate programme.

Important terminology: “IIM Ahmedabad online MBA”, “IIM Ahmedabad blended MBA” and “IIM Ahmedabad BPGP” are often used interchangeably by students. The official name is Blended Post Graduate Programme in Management (BPGP).

How Does the Blended Format Actually Work?

The programme is primarily delivered through live online synchronous sessions, so students can continue working while studying. IIMA also conducts distinct on-campus modules that create face-to-face interaction with faculty and peers.

Part of programmeWhat it means for students
Online synchronous learningScheduled live academic sessions; students need to manage work commitments around classes.
On-campus modulesStudents travel to IIMA for immersive classroom modules and peer interaction.
Case methodLearning includes business cases, discussion and application rather than only recorded lectures.
Peer learningInteraction with working professionals and entrepreneurs is an important part of the experience.

Is BPGP fully online? No. The programme is blended because it combines online synchronous learning with on-campus modules.

BPGP Curriculum: What Will You Study?

The 2026–28 programme has a first-year management core and a second-year elective phase. IIMA describes the curriculum as covering around 900 hours of classroom sessions and project work and 36 credits.

Pre-programme preparation

Students complete preparatory learning in Statistics, Advanced Excel for Business and Accounting.

BPGP Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

The latest published 2026–28 criteria require all of the following:

  • Work experience: Working professionals and entrepreneurs with at least 3 years of full-time work experience after graduation as on April 30, 2026.
  • Age: Minimum 24 years as on April 30, 2026; there is no upper age limit.
  • Education: Bachelor's degree/CA/CS/ICWA or equivalent in any discipline, with at least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA.
  • Education duration: Minimum 15 years of formal study under the applicable education system.
2027–29 note: The criteria above are the latest published 2026–28 criteria reviewed for this page. Do not assume the dates or cutoffs will remain identical for the next batch until IIMA releases the new notification.

What is the IIMA Admission Test?

For the 2026–28 BPGP process, IAT was a 90-minute online assessment with 45 questions across Quantitative Aptitude, Analytical Reasoning and Verbal Aptitude. The exact test structure should be rechecked for the future batch.

Application fee

For the 2026–28 portal, the application fee was ₹2,000 for CAT/GMAT/GRE applicants and ₹5,000 for IAT applicants; the Dubai IAT route had a separate fee. Future-batch fees may change.

BPGP Admission Process

The exact shortlisting and interview procedure depends on the applicable admission notification. Students should distinguish the eligibility route from the selection process: having a valid test score only makes you eligible to be considered.

  1. Check the current eligibility and choose CAT/GMAT/GRE or IAT route.
  2. Submit the online application and supporting information within the deadline.
  3. Complete the applicable test requirement if using IAT.
  4. Participate in the institute's subsequent selection stages if shortlisted.
  5. Complete fee/commitment and enrolment formalities after receiving an offer.

IIMA may update selection parameters for a future batch, so the latest admission notification should override older cycle information.

BPGP Fees and Total Budget

For the 2026–28 batch, IIMA lists a total programme fee of ₹20 lakh.

What is included?

The ₹20 lakh figure is the published programme fee for the academic programme and is payable according to the schedule specified by IIMA.

What is not included?

IIMA explicitly states that travel and accommodation for campus modules and all International Immersion costs are excluded. On-campus accommodation, when available, is separately chargeable.

What should I budget overall?

Planning estimate: ~₹22–25 lakh+, depending on travel frequency, accommodation choices and international immersion costs. This is a budgeting estimate, not an official IIMA cost-of-attendance figure.

Fee alert: ₹20 lakh is the published 2026–28 figure reviewed for this page. It should not be presented as a confirmed 2027–29 fee until IIMA publishes the next batch schedule.

Can You Do BPGP Along With a Full-Time Job?

The programme is specifically designed around the needs of working professionals, but “online” does not mean “low workload”. Live synchronous classes, case preparation, assignments, group work and campus modules require regular time commitment.

  • Plan work meetings and travel around scheduled academic sessions.
  • Keep time for pre-class case reading and preparation.
  • Expect group assignments and peer interaction.
  • Plan separately for travel and stay during campus modules.

The right question is therefore not simply “Is BPGP online?” but “Can I consistently allocate the required time while continuing my job?”

Degree, Alumni Status and Campus Access

BPGP is a degree-granting programme. IIMA states that participants are bona fide students of IIMA and are eligible for alumni status on successful completion, subject to the institute's applicable rules.

Campus participation is an integral part of the programme. Students should not assume that every campus facility or residential arrangement is automatically included in the ₹20 lakh programme fee.

Career Scope After BPGP

BPGP is designed for working professionals and entrepreneurs who want to strengthen general management, leadership and business decision-making while remaining connected to their careers. The curriculum covers the major business functions rather than preparing students for one fixed occupation.

Because participants are already working, the value proposition is different from a two-year residential MBA where students typically use internships and campus placements as the main transition mechanism. Students should separately verify what career/placement services are available for the applicable BPGP batch rather than assuming the same placement structure as PGP.

Who Should Consider BPGP?

Working Professionals

You want an IIMA degree while continuing your professional career.

Entrepreneurs

You want structured management learning, peer exposure and a broader business toolkit while running a venture.

Career Accelerators

You want to strengthen finance, marketing, operations, strategy, leadership and digital/business decision-making.

If your goal is a traditional two-year campus MBA immediately after graduation, compare the PGP. If you have 4+ years of post-graduation experience and can take a full-time career break, compare PGPX.

BPGP FAQs Students Usually Ask

Is BPGP fully online?

No. It is primarily online synchronous but includes distinct on-campus modules.

Can I continue my job during BPGP?

The programme is designed for working professionals, but students still need to manage live classes, case preparation, group work and campus modules alongside their jobs.

How much work experience is required?

The latest published 2026–28 criteria require at least three years of full-time post-graduation work experience. Check the next batch notification for any change.

Can I apply without CAT?

Yes, the published BPGP process provides an IIMA Admission Test (IAT) route as well as CAT/GMAT/GRE routes.

What is the IAT syllabus?

For the 2026–28 process, IAT covered Quantitative Aptitude, Analytical Reasoning and Verbal Aptitude in a 90-minute, 45-question format.

Is BPGP an MBA degree?

Yes. IIMA describes BPGP as a degree-granting programme and awards an MBA degree on successful completion.

Does BPGP provide alumni status?

IIMA states that participants receive alumni status on successful completion, subject to the institute's applicable rules.

Does ₹20 lakh include travel and accommodation?

No. IIMA explicitly excludes travel and accommodation for campus modules and International Immersion costs from the programme fee.

Is BPGP the same as IIMA's regular MBA?

No. PGP is a two-year full-time residential MBA; BPGP is a two-year blended programme primarily delivered online with campus modules.
