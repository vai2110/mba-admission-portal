Programme Overview

MBA-BPGP is IIMA's two-year blended postgraduate management programme, combining live online sessions with on-campus modules. The published 2026–28 brochure lists a total programme fee of ₹20 lakh.

Eligibility & Admission

  • Format: Designed for a blended learning model rather than a fully residential MBA.
  • Entrance: IIMA's published admission information provides CAT/GMAT/GRE and IIMA Admission Test routes, subject to the applicable batch rules.
  • Application fee: ₹2,000 for CAT/GMAT/GRE applicants and ₹5,000 for the IAT route under the published schedule.
  • Selection: Follow the current BPGP admission notification for exact eligibility and selection requirements.

Fees & Other Costs

For the 2026–28 BPGP batch, the total programme fee is ₹20 lakh. IIMA states that the fee excludes travel and accommodation for campus modules and costs related to international immersion. On-campus accommodation, when available, is separately chargeable.

Important: The ₹20 lakh figure is for the published 2026–28 batch; verify the applicable fee before applying to a later batch.

Who Should Consider BPGP?

This programme is relevant to working professionals who need a blended format. If you want the standard two-year residential CAT-based MBA, use the MBA/PGP page.

FAQs

Is MBA-BPGP fully online?

No. It combines live online learning with on-campus modules.

Does the ₹20 lakh fee include travel and accommodation?

No. IIMA's published information states that these costs are excluded.
