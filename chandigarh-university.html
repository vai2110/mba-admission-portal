Chandigarh University: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Chandigarh University (CU) is a private university located on the Chandigarh-Ludhiana Highway in Mohali, Punjab. Its current academic portfolio spans engineering, management, computing, law, pharmacy, architecture, design, hospitality, media, basic sciences and allied health programmes. The university's 2026 admission portal lists more than 150 bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes across its portfolio.

Is Chandigarh University good for students comparing private universities?

CU is worth shortlisting when a student wants a large multi-disciplinary campus, a wide choice of industry-linked programmes and a broad placement network. The right decision still depends on the specific course, fee, scholarship, admission route and course-level career outcome rather than the university-wide headline package.

Chandigarh University Admission 2026: Process, Eligibility and CUCET

For 2026-27, Chandigarh University is accepting applications across UG and PG programmes. Students should check the programme-specific rule before applying because CUCET requirements and exemptions vary by programme.

How does Chandigarh University admission work?

The university's standard online flow is: create a CUCET account, complete the profile and take CUCET where applicable, then use the result for admission and scholarship consideration. The university also publishes programme-specific exceptions; for example, its CUCET policy states exemptions for certain programmes for candidates with more than 90% in the qualifying examination or a JEE rank up to 50,000.

Does every Chandigarh University course require CUCET?

No. The university's policy specifies CUCET as mandatory for a defined set of programmes and lists exemptions. Students should not assume that one admission rule applies to every UG or PG course.

Chandigarh University Fees and Scholarships 2026

CU does not have one university-wide fee. The official mandatory disclosure lists programme-wise annual or semester fee figures, so students should compare the exact programme rather than using a generic “Chandigarh University fees” number.

What scholarship can a student get?

For CUCET Phase-II 2026, the published scholarship slab gives 100% scholarship for scores of 90.01–100, with lower slabs for lower CUCET scores. The university separately publishes rules for specialised management programmes and other categories. Scholarship should therefore be calculated against the exact programme and current phase, not against a generic maximum claim.

Chandigarh University Campus, Hostel and Student Experience

CU's main campus is in Mohali, Punjab, on the Chandigarh-Ludhiana Highway. The university promotes a large residential campus environment, multiple academic institutes, laboratories, student activities and career services. Hostel availability, room type and current charges should be checked in the latest hostel information before budgeting.

What makes the campus relevant to the decision?

For students choosing between private universities, the campus matters mainly because the programme experience depends on labs, industry exposure, clubs, internships, peer mix and access to career support. CU also states that it has students from more than 68 countries, making international student diversity a visible part of its campus proposition.

Who Should Apply to Chandigarh University?

Strong fit

  • Students who want many programme and specialisation choices within one university.
  • Candidates interested in industry-linked programmes and a large placement network.
  • Applicants who can use CUCET performance to improve scholarship eligibility where applicable.
  • Students comfortable evaluating course-level outcomes rather than choosing on university-wide claims alone.

Check carefully before applying

  • Do not assume the highest university package is representative of your course.
  • Do not use one fee figure for every programme; CU's fee varies significantly by course and specialisation.
  • Confirm the current CUCET requirement and exemption for your exact programme.
  • Check scholarship conditions before treating a headline percentage as your final award.

Chandigarh University FAQs Students Actually Search

What is the Chandigarh University admission process for 2026?

For programmes covered by CUCET, students register, create their profile, take CUCET where applicable and use the result for admission and scholarship consideration. Some programmes have published exemptions, so the exact course rule should be checked before applying.

What is Chandigarh University's highest placement package?

The university currently reports a highest national package of ₹59.9 LPA and a highest international package of ₹1.7 crore for its placement ecosystem. These are peak outcomes, not the typical salary of a Chandigarh University graduate.

Is Chandigarh University good for B.Tech CSE?

It can be a relevant option for students seeking a broad CSE programme portfolio, specialised pathways and a large placement ecosystem. The more useful comparison is the CSE fee, curriculum, specialisation, admission route and engineering-specific placement outcome against the alternatives the student is considering.

Does Chandigarh University offer scholarships through CUCET?

Yes. CU publishes CUCET-linked scholarship slabs, including up to 100% scholarship for qualifying scores in the published Phase-II structure. The exact scholarship depends on the programme, score and applicable policy.
