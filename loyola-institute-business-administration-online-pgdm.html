LIBA Online PGDM: Overview

LIBA's online PGDM is positioned for working professionals seeking flexible management education. The official online programme site describes it as AICTE-approved and MBA-equivalent, with online classes and learning materials designed for flexible study.

LIBA Online PGDM Eligibility

Applicants should check the current online programme admission page for the exact academic eligibility and intake-specific requirements before applying. Requirements can differ from the full-time PGDM route.

What You Get from the Online PGDM

The official page highlights flexibility, experienced faculty and industry leaders, access to online classes and learning materials, and an industry-connected learning environment. It is suited to candidates who need a programme compatible with work commitments.

Career Support

The official online programme page states that it provides placement assistance for students and alumni. Candidates should distinguish placement assistance from the campus-placement outcomes reported for the full-time PGDM.
