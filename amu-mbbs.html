MBBS at AMU: Overview

The MBBS programme at AMU is offered through the Faculty of Medicine and Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College (JNMC). The 2026-27 Guide to Admissions lists a duration of 4½ years followed by one year of compulsory rotatory internship and an intake of 145 students.

Unlike programmes such as B.Tech and MBA that use AMU's own admission tests, MBBS selection is through NEET-UG for registered candidates. Selected candidates also have to undergo a medical fitness examination before completing admission formalities.

AMU MBBS Eligibility 2026

The 2026-27 admission guide requires the Senior Secondary School Certificate Examination of AMU or an equivalent recognised examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. Candidates must have obtained at least 50% marks in aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

The minimum age is 17 years as on December 31 of the year of admission. Candidates must also satisfy the applicable NEET-UG eligibility and counselling requirements.

AMU MBBS Admission 2026 Through NEET-UG

MBBS selection at AMU is through NEET-UG among registered candidates. The AMU admission guide does not prescribe a separate AMU MBBS entrance test.

Admission workflow

  1. Meet the 10+2 PCB and English eligibility requirements.
  2. Appear for NEET-UG and obtain a valid result/rank.
  3. Register for the applicable medical counselling process and indicate AMU/JNMC where eligible.
  4. Follow the AMU admission/verification instructions after allotment.
  5. Complete medical fitness examination and remaining admission formalities.

AMU MBBS Fees 2026-27

The 2026-27 AMU admission guide lists ₹55,900 as the fee payable at the time of admission for M.B.B.S. This should be read as the admission-time amount, not automatically as the total cost of the full MBBS programme.

Students should separately verify recurring university charges, hostel and mess expenses and any other applicable fees from the current AMU schedule.

Clinical Training & Medical Learning

Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College is the clinical teaching environment associated with AMU's medical education. AMU's Department of Medicine states that its department was established in 1963 and that the annual intake of MBBS students increased from 50 to 150 historically; the current 2026-27 admission guide lists 145 seats.

The clinical environment is important for medical students because MBBS outcomes depend not only on classroom teaching but also on patient exposure, clinical rotations, internship quality, faculty, hospital infrastructure and postgraduate preparation.

AMU MBBS Cutoff 2026

There is no single safe cutoff number without specifying the NEET year, counselling route, category, quota and round. AMU's 145-seat intake should also not be interpreted as a guaranteed NEET closing rank.

AMU MBBS FAQs

How many MBBS seats are there at AMU?

The 2026-27 Guide to Admissions lists 145 MBBS seats.

Is AMU MBBS admission through NEET?

Yes. The 2026-27 guide specifies selection through NEET-UG for registered candidates.

What is the MBBS duration at AMU?

The academic duration is 4½ years followed by one year of compulsory rotatory internship.

What is the eligibility for MBBS at AMU?

Applicants need Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English in the qualifying examination, with at least 50% aggregate in PCB, plus the applicable age and NEET requirements.

Is there an AMU-specific MBBS entrance test?

No separate AMU MBBS admission test is specified in the 2026-27 guide; selection is through NEET-UG.

What is the admission-time MBBS fee?

The 2026-27 guide lists ₹55,900 as the fee payable at admission.
