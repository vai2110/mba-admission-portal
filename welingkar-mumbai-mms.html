Welingkar Mumbai MMS: Quick Answer

The MMS is a two-year full-time management programme offered under the Maharashtra admission framework. Unlike the institute's AICTE PGDM route, MMS admissions are tied to the state-regulated process and eligible entrance tests.

Why consider MMS instead of PGDM?

The MMS can be substantially lower in published tuition for the open category than the two-year PGDM, while still providing access to WeSchool's Mumbai campus and placement ecosystem. Students should compare admission route, fee, curriculum and role outcomes.

Welingkar MMS FAQs

Is Welingkar MMS different from PGDM?

Yes. MMS follows the Maharashtra admission framework, while the two-year PGDM follows WeSchool's national entrance-test and profile-based process.

What is the MMS fee?

The 2025-26 open-category institute fee reference was ₹4.31 lakh plus ₹4,000 refundable library deposit; confirm the 2026-27 fee before admission.

Which entrance exam is important?

The institute's 2025-26 disclosure lists MH-CET, CMAT, MAT, ATMA and XAT.
