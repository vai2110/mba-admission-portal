Jain University Bangalore: What Students Should Know

JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) is a multidisciplinary university in Bengaluru offering programmes across engineering and technology, management, commerce, design, humanities, sciences, law, allied healthcare and other areas. The official university site currently lists 58 undergraduate programmes, including 33 in innovative or emerging areas, and more than 50 postgraduate programmes.

50+PG programmes listed by the university
6,700Students admitted in the 2025-26 class, per the admissions page
Is Jain University a university or a college?

JAIN is a deemed-to-be university. Students apply to different schools/faculties depending on the programme, so the admission process, campus, fee and entrance requirements can vary substantially by course.

Jain University Admission 2026: Eligibility & JET

JAIN University follows programme-specific admission routes. The university uses the JAIN Entrance Test (JET) for many programmes. For PG programmes other than MBA, the stated JET process includes a written test and personal interview. MBA selection includes a written test, essay writing, presentation and personal interview.

Jain University Campus & Student Life

JAIN operates multiple academic locations in and around Bengaluru. The MBA programme is listed at the Sheshadri Road campus, while the Faculty of Engineering and Technology operates at the JAIN Global Campus on the Bengaluru–Kanakapura Main Road. Students should therefore check the exact campus before applying.

Academic diversity

Students can choose from engineering, management, commerce, computing, design, sciences, law and other disciplines.

Industry exposure

Official programme pages highlight internships, practical learning, industry interactions, projects and recruiter engagement.

Is Jain University Bangalore Worth It?

JAIN can be a strong option if you want programme variety and a Bengaluru location, but the right decision depends heavily on the exact school and course.

For B.Tech, MBA, BBA, BCA and B.Com, compare curriculum, total fees, campus, entrance route, placement statistics and your intended career. Avoid judging the university only from a single headline package.

Jain University FAQs Students Actually Ask

Is Jain University Bangalore good for placements?

Placement outcomes are programme-specific. Official pages report strong outcomes for B.Tech, MBA and BBA, but students should compare the exact course's placement rate, average/median package and highest package rather than using one university-wide number.

Does Jain University accept JEE Main?

For B.Tech, the Faculty of Engineering and Technology states that it accepts UNIGAUGE, JEE and COMEDK scores, along with its own admission route. Check the exact 2026 programme page before applying.

Is JET compulsory for MBA at Jain University?

MBA applicants normally go through the JET selection process, but candidates with specified valid scores in MAT, NMAT, CAT, GMAT, CMAT or XAT can be exempted from the written test subject to the published thresholds.

What is the minimum eligibility for PG admission?

The university's PG eligibility page states an aggregate of 50% in a bachelor's degree in the respective discipline or equivalent 10+2+3 qualification. Programme-specific requirements can apply.

Does Jain University have BCA and B.Com?

Yes. The university lists BCA under its computing/science offerings and B.Com programmes under its commerce school, with multiple specialised and honours options.

Which Jain University campus is used for MBA?

The current MBA admission page lists the campus as Sheshadri Road, Bengaluru. Other programmes can be based at different Jain campuses, so students should verify the campus for their selected course.
