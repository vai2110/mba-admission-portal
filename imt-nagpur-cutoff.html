Does IMT Nagpur have a fixed cutoff?

Quick answer:

No. IMT Nagpur's official admissions FAQ explicitly says there is no pre-decided cutoff. It uses a consolidated assessment of entrance score, past academics, work experience and Personal Interview.

The institute nevertheless publishes historical stopping points that help applicants understand competitiveness. These should not be treated as guaranteed thresholds for the next batch.

What percentile should you target for IMT Nagpur?

A student should not prepare to merely touch the historical cutoff. Because selection also considers academics, work experience and PI, a higher entrance score gives you more room when other parts of the profile are average.

Do not confuse cutoff with final admission. Meeting a shortlisting threshold does not mean that an offer is guaranteed.

What happens after meeting the cutoff?

IMT Nagpur's selection process considers the entrance score together with academic record, work experience and Personal Interview performance. The institute says work experience is not compulsory, and there is no management quota.

For PI preparation, focus on your graduation subjects, current affairs, career goals, reasons for choosing the programme, work/internship experiences and questions connected to your preferred specialization.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff FAQs

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no fixed pre-decided cutoff. The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT.

What is the CMAT cutoff for IMT Nagpur?

The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of 90+ percentile for CMAT.

Is 75 percentile enough for IMT Nagpur?

It has been a historical stopping reference, but it should not be treated as a safe or guaranteed score. Final selection also depends on academics, work experience and PI.

Does IMT Nagpur have sectional cutoffs?

The official FAQ discussed overall stopping points rather than a published sectional-cutoff matrix. Applicants should not invent sectional thresholds that the institute has not published.

Can a fresher get shortlisted at IMT Nagpur?

Yes. Work experience is not compulsory, although relevant experience receives additional selection weight according to the institute.
