Does IMT Nagpur have a fixed cutoff?

Quick answer

No. IMT Nagpur's official FAQ says there is no pre-decided cutoff. Admission is based on a consolidated assessment of entrance score, academics, work experience/profile and PI.

Therefore, a webpage showing one number as the “official IMT Nagpur cutoff 2026” is misleading unless it clearly identifies the number as a historical or third-party reference.

What percentile should you target?

Do not prepare for the minimum. A higher score gives you more buffer because IMT Nagpur considers more than the entrance exam.

ProfilePractical target strategy
Strong academics + work experienceTarget materially above 75 CAT/XAT so the test score strengthens an already good profile.
Fresher / average academicsAim higher than the historical threshold to reduce profile risk.
CMAT routeAim comfortably above the 90+ historical stopping point.
GMAT routeTreat 550 as a historical floor reference, not a safe score.

What happens after meeting the cutoff?

Meeting a historical cutoff does not guarantee admission. IMT Nagpur evaluates the broader profile and admission-process performance. Prepare for questions around academics, current affairs, business awareness, career goals, work experience and why you want a particular PGDM route.

Decision rule: if you are comparing IMT Nagpur with another B-school, compare the actual selection process and placement outcomes, not just the lowest percentile at which someone received an interview call.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff FAQs

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no fixed public cutoff. The institute's official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT.

What was the CAT cutoff before 2025?

Third-party historical datasets commonly report 85 percentile for CAT in 2022, 2023 and 2024. These are not an official fixed cutoff policy.

What is the IMT Nagpur CMAT cutoff?

The institute's official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of 90+ percentile. Treat it as historical.

Is 75 percentile enough for IMT Nagpur?

It can be a historical screening reference, but it is not a safe-score guarantee. The institute also evaluates academics, work experience/profile and PI.

Does IMT Nagpur have sectional cutoffs?

The current official FAQ does not publish a verified sectional-cutoff matrix. Avoid treating third-party sectional figures as official unless the institute confirms them.
