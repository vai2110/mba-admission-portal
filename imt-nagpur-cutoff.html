Does IMT Nagpur have a fixed cutoff?

Quick answer:

No. The official admissions FAQ says there is no pre-decided cutoff. Selection considers CAT/XAT/CMAT/GMAT, academics, work experience and Personal Interview.

The historical stopping points are useful only as reference points.

What score should you target?

Do not target the minimum historical stopping point. A higher score gives you more buffer because final selection is not based on the entrance score alone.

Applicant profilePractical approach
Strong academics + work experienceAim materially above 75 CAT/XAT to strengthen the composite profile.
Fresher / average academicsTarget a stronger percentile to reduce profile risk.
CMAT applicantAim comfortably above the historical 90+ reference.

What happens after meeting the cutoff?

Shortlisting is followed by the institute's broader selection process, which considers academics, work experience and Personal Interview performance. Meeting a historical cutoff does not guarantee admission.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff FAQs

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no fixed pre-decided cutoff. The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT.

What is the CMAT cutoff?

The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of 90+ percentile.

Is 75 percentile enough?

It is a historical reference, not a safe-score guarantee. Final selection also depends on academics, work experience and PI.

Are there sectional cutoffs?

The official FAQ does not publish a sectional-cutoff matrix. Do not present an unverified sectional cutoff as official.
