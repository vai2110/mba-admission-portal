Great Lakes Chennai: What Students Should Know

Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai is a management institute offering full-time residential programmes led by its flagship one-year PGPM and two-year PGDM. The institute positions the programmes around industry exposure, experiential learning, analytics, technology and leadership.

The Chennai campus currently highlights 100+ CXO interactions annually and a curriculum designed with academic and industry inputs. Great Lakes is ranked 37th in the NIRF 2025 Management ranking. Official Chennai campus information →

Is Great Lakes Chennai suitable for you?

It is worth considering if you want a career-focused management programme with strong exposure to consulting, technology, analytics, finance, product, marketing and operations. The right programme depends mainly on your work experience: PGDM is designed for freshers and candidates with less than three years of experience, while PGPM is the one-year option for candidates with 2+ years of experience.

Great Lakes Chennai Admission 2026-27: Eligibility & Selection

Great Lakes follows a holistic selection process rather than publishing a fixed minimum CAT, XAT or GMAT percentile. The institute evaluates entrance scores along with academics, work experience, essays/profile and interview performance.

ProgrammeBasic eligibilityAccepted testsExperience
PGPMBachelor's degree with minimum 50% across Class 10, Class 12 and graduationCAT, GMAT, XAT, NMAT2+ years considered
PGDMBachelor's degree with minimum 50% across Class 10, Class 12 and graduationCAT, GMAT, XATFreshers and <36 months

How does the selection process work?

Applicants submit the online form and test details. Shortlisted candidates may go through an AI-proctored/video assessment followed by a Personal Interview. Great Lakes also considers academic consistency, work experience, communication, business awareness, career clarity, achievements, diversity of background and overall programme fit.

Does Great Lakes have a fixed CAT cutoff?

No fixed CAT/GMAT/XAT/NMAT cutoff is published for PGPM. Great Lakes states that it uses a holistic evaluation and that test scores are considered along with academics, experience, essays and interviews.

Great Lakes Chennai Fees 2026: What the Fee Includes

The latest official fee pages list separate programme and accommodation components. Both flagship programmes are residential, and hostel accommodation is mandatory.

What is included?

  • Tuition/programme and academic expenses.
  • Reading material, case studies, textbooks and online resources.
  • Residential accommodation as specified for the programme.
  • Vegetarian meals are included; optional non-vegetarian food is charged separately.
  • Refundable caution deposit and alumni subscription are included in the published total.
  • Students need to arrange a laptop as per institute specifications.

Scholarships

Great Lakes currently publishes Merit and Diversity Scholarships worth ₹5 lakh each for eligible PGPM and PGDM candidates, with at least 30% reserved for female candidates. The institute also lists the Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship, which can provide a 100% tuition fee waiver to exceptional candidates.

Who Should Choose Great Lakes Chennai?

Strong fit

  • Freshers or early-career candidates seeking a two-year PGDM.
  • Professionals with 2+ years of experience seeking a one-year PGPM.
  • Students targeting consulting, analytics, technology, finance, product, marketing or operations.
  • Candidates who value industry interaction and experiential learning.

Think carefully if

  • You want a low-cost MBA and the residential fee is beyond your budget.
  • You specifically need a traditional university MBA degree rather than a PGDM qualification.
  • You are choosing only on the highest package without checking the role mix for your target career.
  • You have no interest in a residential, intensive management programme.

Great Lakes Chennai FAQs

Is Great Lakes Chennai good for an MBA?

Great Lakes is a well-known management institute with flagship PGPM and PGDM programmes. The right fit depends on your experience, career target, budget and preference for a residential programme.

What is the Great Lakes Chennai PGPM fee?

The official 2026-27 fee page lists a total of ₹23.50 lakh for Indian students, including academic fees, single AC accommodation, refundable caution deposit and alumni subscription.

What is the Great Lakes Chennai PGDM fee?

The official 2026-28 fee page lists a total of ₹22.50 lakh, including academic/programme fees, twin AC accommodation, refundable caution deposit and alumni subscription.

Who is eligible for PGDM at Great Lakes?

Freshers and candidates with less than 36 months of work experience can apply, subject to the applicable academic and entrance-test requirements.

How much work experience is needed for Great Lakes PGPM?

Great Lakes considers candidates with 2+ years of work experience for PGPM. Candidates with 2-3 years may also be eligible for the two-year PGDM.

Does Great Lakes have a fixed CAT cutoff?

Great Lakes does not publish a fixed minimum CAT/GMAT/XAT/NMAT cutoff for PGPM and states that it uses a holistic evaluation. PGDM selection also considers the complete candidate profile.
