Great Lakes Chennai: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai (GLIM Chennai) is a residential management institute offering the one-year PGPM for experienced professionals and the two-year PGDM for freshers and early-career candidates. The institute was founded in 2004 and currently highlights industry-oriented management education, global exposure, analytics, AI and experiential learning.

Great Lakes is ranked 37th in the NIRF 2025 Management category. Its Chennai campus is a 30-acre green campus and Great Lakes states that it was the first campus in South Asia to receive LEED Platinum recognition. The institute also lists AACSB, AMBA, SAQS and NBA accreditations/approvals alongside AICTE approval.

Why Consider Great Lakes Chennai?

Great Lakes is best evaluated as a career-focused B-school rather than only through its headline package. Its differentiators include a one-year flagship PGPM, a two-year PGDM, industry-linked curriculum, analytics and AI exposure, experiential learning, international partnerships and a large corporate-facing ecosystem.

PGPM or PGDM—which one should you choose?

If you are a fresher or have less than three years of experience, PGDM is normally the relevant route. If you have 2+ years of experience, PGPM becomes the one-year accelerated option; candidates with 2-3 years may be eligible for both and should compare opportunity cost, curriculum depth and career goals.

Great Lakes Chennai Admission 2027: Eligibility, Exams & Selection

Great Lakes uses a holistic selection process. It does not publish a single fixed CAT/XAT/GMAT/NMAT cutoff for PGPM and evaluates entrance scores alongside academics, work experience, essays, achievements and interviews.

How does Great Lakes selection work?

  1. Online application and entrance-test submission.
  2. Application/profile screening.
  3. AI-proctored video assessment for shortlisted applicants.
  4. Personal Interview.
  5. Final holistic evaluation and offer.

What does the admissions panel evaluate?

  • Academic consistency and ability to handle an intensive programme.
  • CAT/GMAT/XAT/NMAT performance.
  • Work experience and professional achievements.
  • Career goals, business awareness and communication.
  • Leadership potential, initiatives and diversity of background.
  • Integrity, originality and ethical values.

Application fee and application route

Great Lakes states that applications are submitted only through its official online application. The current PGPM/PGDM admission pages list an application fee of ₹2,200. Applicants can choose Chennai, Gurgaon or both campuses through the common application flow where applicable.

Is there a management quota?

Great Lakes explicitly states that it does not admit students through management quota or another reservation-based seat route and warns applicants against third parties claiming to offer such seats.

Academics, Campus, Internships & Global Exposure

PGDM academics

The PGDM moves from management fundamentals into electives and applied learning. Current subjects include Business Analytics, AI for Managers, Data Visualization, Python Workshop, Design Thinking, Strategic Management and Business Storytelling. The programme also includes a summer internship, Karma Yoga, empirical studies and boardroom simulation.

PGPM academics

PGPM 2.0 is structured across seven terms, with core courses followed by concentration/elective learning. Current curriculum elements include Gen AI, Business Analytics, Storytelling with Data, Innovation Sandbox, Finance Management Lab, Supply Chain Simulation, Product Lab, Business Consulting Lab and GenAI Models Lab.

International exposure

Great Lakes lists short-term immersion and exchange opportunities with partner institutions including IÉSEG, EDHEC, SKEMA, HHL Leipzig, NUCB, Manchester Metropolitan University, IPAG, Ajman University and others. Some opportunities can involve additional costs and eligibility conditions.

Campus and residential life

The Chennai campus is a 30-acre green residential campus. Since accommodation is mandatory for the flagship programmes, students should treat the residential environment as part of the academic experience and total cost rather than as an optional add-on.

Great Lakes Chennai ROI: Should You Spend ₹22-24 Lakh?

A student should not decide on ROI using “highest package ÷ fee”. A more useful evaluation combines the programme's average CTC, your likely role, prior salary, career mobility, financed cost and opportunity cost.

QuestionWhat to check before accepting the offer
Can I afford it?Calculate tuition + accommodation + laptop + travel + personal expenses + loan interest.
Will the placement fit my goal?Compare role/function and sector mix, not just average or highest CTC.
Which programme fits?PGPM for experienced professionals; PGDM for freshers/early-career candidates.
What is my opportunity cost?PGPM: one-year career break. PGDM: two-year break plus internship period.
Can scholarship reduce my cost?Check Merit/Diversity Scholarship and Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Scholarship eligibility.

Good fit if

  • You value consulting, analytics, technology, finance, marketing, operations or product roles.
  • You want a residential, industry-facing learning environment.
  • Your career stage matches the programme.
  • You can comfortably finance the total cost.

Think carefully if

  • You are choosing only because of the highest CTC.
  • You need a low-cost MBA with minimal residential expenses.
  • You want a conventional university MBA degree rather than a PGDM/PGPM qualification.
  • Your target career is poorly represented in the published placement mix.

Great Lakes Chennai FAQs Students Actually Search

Is Great Lakes Chennai a good B-school?

Great Lakes Chennai is a recognised management institute with AACSB/AMBA accreditation, AICTE-approved flagship programmes, NIRF 2025 Management rank 37, a residential Chennai campus and established corporate recruitment. Whether it is right for you depends on programme fit, career target, fees and expected role outcomes.

What is the Great Lakes Chennai fee?

The published total is ₹23.50 lakh for PGPM 2026-27 and ₹22.50 lakh for PGDM 2026-28 for Indian students, including specified accommodation and mandatory charges.

What is the Great Lakes Chennai CAT cutoff?

Great Lakes does not publish a single fixed CAT cutoff for PGPM. It uses a holistic process considering test scores, academics, work experience, essays, achievements and interviews. Applicants should not treat an unofficial percentile as an official cutoff.

What is the eligibility for Great Lakes PGPM?

For the 2027-28 process, applicants need a bachelor's degree with minimum 50% across Class 10, Class 12 and UG, a valid accepted entrance score and 2+ years of work experience considered by the institute.

Can a fresher apply to Great Lakes?

Yes. Freshers are eligible for the two-year PGDM, subject to the published academic and entrance-test requirements. The one-year PGPM is intended for experienced candidates.

Which is better, Great Lakes PGPM or PGDM?

PGPM is the accelerated one-year option for experienced professionals; PGDM is the two-year route for freshers and early-career candidates. Candidates with 2-3 years may be eligible for both, so the decision should be based on career goals, opportunity cost and curriculum.

What is the average package at Great Lakes Chennai?

The latest official completed reports currently available list ₹18.1 LPA average CTC for PGPM Class of 2026 and ₹15 LPA for PGDM Class of 2025.

Does Great Lakes provide scholarships?

Yes. Great Lakes currently lists ₹5 lakh Merit & Diversity Scholarships for incoming PGPM/PGDM students and a Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship offering a full tuition fee waiver to exceptional candidates, subject to the published rules.

Does Great Lakes have hostel facilities?

Yes. The flagship PGPM and PGDM programmes are residential and hostel accommodation is mandatory. The published fee structures include accommodation charges.
