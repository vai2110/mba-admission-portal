IIM Amritsar Placements 2024-26: Latest Snapshot

IIM Amritsar's latest published final-placement update for the MBA Batch of 2024-26 reports a highest CTC of ₹43 LPA. The institute reports an average CTC of ₹18.60 LPA and median CTC of ₹18 LPA for the top 50 students. For the top 50% of the batch, average CTC was ₹16.30 LPA and median CTC was ₹15.25 LPA. IIM Amritsar Placement Year-wise Context

The institute's placement archive shows that reported placement outcomes and salary metrics have varied by batch and reporting format. For example, IIM Amritsar reported an overall average CTC of ₹16.51 LPA for the 2021-23 batch and ₹14.63 LPA for 2020-22; older releases also reported 100% placement for several early batches.
