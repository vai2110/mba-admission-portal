IIM Amritsar Placements 2024-26: Latest Snapshot

The latest official final-placement update reports a ₹43 LPA highest CTC for the MBA 2024-26 batch. The institute reports ₹18.60 LPA average and ₹18 LPA median for the top 50 students. For the top 50% of the batch, average CTC was ₹16.30 LPA and median CTC was ₹15.25 LPA. The graduating cohort comprised 256 students.

How should a student interpret IIM Amritsar placements?

The right question is not “What is the highest package?” It is “What salary outcome and role mix should I realistically expect for my profile?” The current report is strongest on recruiter breadth and top-segment outcomes; it is less useful for estimating a single overall-batch average.

IIM Amritsar Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at IIM Amritsar?

The latest MBA 2024-26 final-placement update reports ₹43 LPA.

What is the average package at IIM Amritsar?

For 2024-26, the institute reports ₹18.60 LPA for the top 50 students and ₹16.30 LPA for the top 50% of the batch. These are not overall-batch averages.

Which sectors recruit from IIM Amritsar?

BFSI, IT/ITeS, consulting, manufacturing, FMCG/FMCD, fintech, analytics, logistics, energy, EdTech, hospitality and media were among the sectors represented.

Does IIM Amritsar have 100% placement?

Older official releases reported 100% placement for several batches, but the latest 2024-26 public update does not state an overall placement percentage in the same format. It should not be assumed without a current batch-specific figure.
