What changed?

From the 2020–22 report to the 2026 report, average CTC moved from ₹31.03 LPA to ₹33.2 LPA, while median CTC moved from ₹29 LPA to ₹32.9 LPA. The figures did not increase every year, so compare the full trend rather than treating one batch as a continuous year-on-year rise.

How to use this table: Average CTC gives the arithmetic mean, while median CTC is the midpoint of the reported salary distribution. Highest CTC is an individual top figure and does not represent the salary most students receive.

IIM Lucknow Placements: Student FAQs

What is the average package at IIM Lucknow for the 2026 batch?

The official final placement report for the 2024–26 batch records an average CTC of ₹33.2 LPA. This headline figure covers the PGP and PGP-ABM graduating cohort together. Average CTC is the arithmetic mean reported by the institute and should be read alongside the median CTC of ₹32.9 LPA.

What is the median package at IIM Lucknow in 2026, and why does it matter?

The median CTC for the 2026 batch is ₹32.9 LPA. Median salary is useful because it represents the midpoint of the reported salary distribution, so it helps students assess outcomes beyond the highest package. It is still a CTC figure, not a guarantee of monthly take-home pay.

What is the highest domestic and international package at IIM Lucknow in 2026?

The 2026 final placement report lists the highest domestic CTC as ₹1 crore per annum and the highest international CTC as ₹65 LPA. These are the top reported offers; they should not be interpreted as typical salaries for the batch. For a more balanced comparison, check the average, median and domain-wise mean CTC as well.

Which MBA placement domain has the highest average salary at IIM Lucknow?

Among the domains listed in the 2026 report, consulting has the highest mean CTC at ₹36.9 LPA. Finance reports a mean CTC of ₹34.8 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹1 crore. The highest mean and the highest individual offer are different metrics, so compare them separately when evaluating career paths.

What percentage of IIM Lucknow students get consulting placements?

The 2026 report shows consulting at 39% of the placement distribution, the largest share among the listed domains. General Management accounts for 20%, Finance 17%, IT/Tech/Analytics/Product 13%, Sales and Marketing 8%, and Operations 3%. These percentages describe the reported distribution for that batch, not an individual student's probability of receiving a consulting offer.

How has the IIM Lucknow average package changed from 2020–22 to 2026?

The average CTC moved from ₹31.03 LPA for the 2020–22 batch to ₹33.2 LPA for the 2026 batch. The series is not a straight year-on-year rise: the average was ₹32.23 LPA in 2021–23, ₹30 LPA in 2022–24 and ₹32.3 LPA in the 2025 report before reaching ₹33.2 LPA in 2026.

Are IIM Lucknow MBA and MBA-ABM placement packages reported separately?

The headline placement figures used on this page are published for the combined PGP and PGP-ABM graduating cohorts in the relevant reports. The cited reports do not provide separate headline average and median figures for each programme in the statistics summarised here. Therefore, these figures should not be labelled as PGP-only or PGP-ABM-only outcomes.

Which companies visited IIM Lucknow for MBA placements?

Recruiters named across the reports include Accenture Strategy, Bain & Company, BCG, Deloitte, EY-Parthenon, Kearney, KPMG, McKinsey & Company, PwC, American Express, Citi, Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Adobe, Amazon, Microsoft, Hindustan Unilever and P&G, among others. Recruiter lists change by batch, so check the relevant official report for the complete list for a specific year.

Does IIM Lucknow's highest package mean that most students earn that salary?

No. The highest CTC is the top offer reported for a placement cycle and does not describe the salary earned by most students. For a more representative view, compare the median and average CTC, then review domain-level mean CTC and placement distribution to understand the range of career functions represented.
