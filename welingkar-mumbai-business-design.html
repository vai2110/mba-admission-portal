What Is PGDM Business Design?

WeSchool's PGDM Business Design combines management education with design and innovation. The programme is built for students interested in solving business problems through customer understanding, design thinking, innovation, prototyping and business-model development.

Is it an MBA in graphic or product design?

No. The programme is management-led. Design is used as a problem-solving and innovation discipline, making it relevant to consulting, product, entrepreneurship, strategy and innovation roles.

Who Should Choose Business Design?

Good fit

  • You enjoy solving ambiguous business problems.
  • You want innovation, product or consulting exposure.
  • You are interested in entrepreneurship and customer-centric strategy.

Not ideal if

  • You want a conventional finance-only specialisation.
  • You want a pure design degree.
  • You dislike project-based and interdisciplinary learning.
