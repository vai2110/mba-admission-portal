Christ University MBA: Quick Answer

What matters most

For the 2026 MBA admission route, Christ requires a recognised bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate and a valid score in an accepted test such as CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT Focus or GRE. The selection process then uses GD, Micro Presentation, Written Assessment, Personal Interview and academic performance.

What is the Christ University MBA?

The MBA is a two-year management programme offered through Christ's School of Business and Management across its campuses. Students can pursue areas including Finance, Marketing, Human Resource, Lean Operations & Systems and Business Analytics.

The programme is not a CAT-only admission. Meeting the test-score requirement gets a candidate into the eligibility/selection process; the final decision also considers the university's selection stages.

Who Is Eligible for Christ University MBA?

  • A recognised undergraduate degree with 50% aggregate marks or equivalent grade.
  • Final-year UG students can apply if they meet the stated academic condition.
  • A valid score is required from the accepted 2025/2026 tests for the 2026 cycle.

The published 2026 minimums are MAT 600, CAT 60 percentile, XAT 60 percentile, CMAT 60 percentile, ATMA 60 percentile, GMAT Focus 450 and GRE 295.

What Is the MBA Selection Process?

Christ's 2026 MBA selection process included four assessed stages plus academic performance:

StageWhat happens
GDGroup discussion; the official process lists 25 minutes for each group.
Micro Presentation90-second presentation on a topic given by the panel.
Written AssessmentWrite on a given topic for up to 10 minutes/one A4 sheet.
PIPersonal interview with an expert panel.
Academic performanceClass 10, Class 12 and undergraduate performance is assessed.

Christ explicitly states that the minimum CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/ATMA/GMAT/GRE score is an eligibility condition and the actual test score has no bearing on the selection process.

What Is the Christ University MBA Cutoff?

There is no published single "final cutoff" for selection. The official 2026 eligibility thresholds are CAT 60 percentile, XAT 60 percentile, CMAT 60 percentile, ATMA 60 percentile, MAT 600, GMAT Focus 450 and GRE 295.

Important:

Do not report 60 percentile as a guaranteed admission cutoff. Christ calls it an eligibility requirement and separately conducts its selection process.

The official fee differs by domicile/category and programme/campus. For the standard MBA fee table currently published, the annual programme fee is ₹6.50 lakh for Karnataka students and ₹6.70 lakh for other Indian states, plus the ₹8,000 non-refundable admission registration fee.

What Do You Study in the MBA?

Christ's MBA follows a six-trimester structure. Students study common management foundations before moving into specialisation areas. The published programme structure covers finance, marketing, human resource, operations and business analytics, with projects and industry-oriented learning.

Choose the specialisation based on the role you want after graduation rather than selecting only on the course title.

Christ University MBA Placements

Christ's 2024-25 annual report records 327 companies participating in MBA recruitment. Of 946 eligible students, 911 received offers, giving a 99% placement rate. The reported average salary was ₹10.7 lakh and the highest domestic package was ₹33 lakh.

The same official report notes 12 international offers. These numbers are MBA-specific and should not be presented as the university-wide placement average.

Christ University MBA FAQs

Is CAT mandatory for Christ University MBA?

No. Christ's 2026 eligibility page accepted MAT, CAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA, GMAT Focus and GRE, subject to the stated minimum score.

What CAT percentile is required for Christ MBA?

CAT 60 percentile is the published 2026 eligibility threshold. It is not a guaranteed final-selection cutoff.

Does the CAT score decide the final selection?

Christ states that the minimum test score is only an eligibility criterion and the actual score has no bearing on the selection process. GD, MP, Written Assessment, PI and academic performance are assessed.

What is the latest official MBA average package?

The 2024-25 annual report records an average salary of ₹10.7 lakh and highest domestic package of ₹33 lakh for the MBA programme.

How much is the Christ MBA fee for non-Karnataka students?

The published standard fee table lists ₹6.70 lakh per year for other Indian states, plus ₹8,000 admission registration fee.
