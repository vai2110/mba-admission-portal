XIM University Admission Dates 2026

Always use the programme-specific deadline. The MBA-BM January deadline should not be applied to later-opening XIM programmes.

XIM University Eligibility 2026

Eligibility differs by programme. Most management programmes require a bachelor's degree with at least 55% aggregate. MMS-BF has a defined list of eligible academic backgrounds. Other PG and UG programmes have their own criteria.

ProgrammeKey eligibility
MBA-BM3-year bachelor's degree, any discipline, 55%.
MBA-HRM / RM / SM / UMG / PPMGenerally 55% bachelor's; programme-specific provisions apply.
MMS-BFSpecified finance/economics/quantitative/business backgrounds, 55%.
Other PG programmesProgramme-specific academic criteria.

How to Apply to XIM University

StepWhat to do
1Create an XIM application login.
2Enter personal, academic and work-experience details.
3Select programme and test option(s).
4Upload required documents/photo/signature and complete the form.
5Pay the application fee online.
6Track shortlist, interview and offer communication.

For MBA-BM, the 2026 application fee was ₹1,800 for the first option and ₹400 for each subsequent option, as specified in the application instructions.

XIM Selection Process

Selection varies by programme. MBA-BM's 2026 process included shortlisting from the admission-test score followed by Writing Ability Test, Group Discussion and Personal Interview, with past academic record and relevant work experience considered. Specialised programmes can use different processes.

Do not prepare only for the exam

For XIM, interview readiness, academic consistency, communication, motivation and programme fit can matter after the test score stage.

Student Discussion: Questions Worth Checking Before You Decide

Student discussions about XIM often go beyond rankings and brochures. Applicants commonly compare the programme's career outcomes, total cost, campus experience and the value of the specific specialisation they are considering. These discussions are useful for spotting questions to investigate, but they are opinions rather than official university data.

Questions students commonly ask

  • Is this programme a good fit for my target career?
  • What roles do students actually pursue after graduation?
  • How much should I budget beyond the published tuition fee?

What to verify independently

  • Latest programme-specific placement outcomes.
  • Current fees, hostel and mess charges.
  • Entrance tests, eligibility and selection rules.
