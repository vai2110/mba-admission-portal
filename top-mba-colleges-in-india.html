How the NIRF 2025 Management ranking works

NIRF's Management ranking is published by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The official ranking uses parameters including Teaching, Learning & Resources, Research & Professional Practice, Graduation Outcomes, Outreach & Inclusivity and Perception. MBA Portal does not recalculate NIRF scores.

Top 100 MBA Colleges FAQs

Which is the No. 1 MBA college in India according to NIRF 2025?

IIM Ahmedabad is ranked #1 in the NIRF 2025 Management ranking.

How many institutions are included in the NIRF 2025 Management Top 100?

NIRF publishes a Top 100 Management ranking for 2025.

Does the NIRF Management ranking include IITs?

Yes. IIT Delhi, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Kanpur and IIT Roorkee appear in the Management ranking.

Does NIRF rank only MBA programmes?

No. NIRF's Management category is an institutional management ranking. Students should check the specific MBA, PGDM or other management programme offered by each institution.

Is NIRF rank enough to select an MBA college?

No. Entrance route, total fees, placements, programme fit, location and your career goals should also be considered.
