STUDENT DECISION GUIDE

How to choose from NIRF-ranked MBA colleges

A higher NIRF rank can help you build a shortlist, but it should not be the only decision factor. Compare the same criteria across every college you are seriously considering.

1. Entrance routeCheck CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT, institute tests and the complete selection process.
2. Total costLook beyond tuition. Check hostel, mess, deposits, one-time charges and other compulsory costs.
3. PlacementsPrefer average and median outcomes over a highest-package headline alone.
4. Programme fitCompare MBA, PGDM and specialisations against your target role and industry.
5. LocationConsider industry access, internships, living costs and your preferred work market.
6. Official evidenceVerify fees, eligibility, dates and placement numbers from the institution before applying.
RANKING CONTEXT

What does the NIRF Management ranking measure?

NIRF Management is an institutional ranking published by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The 2025 Management framework uses Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR), Research & Professional Practice (RPC), Graduation Outcomes (GO), Outreach & Inclusivity (OI) and Perception. CollegeDecoded does not recalculate or modify NIRF scores.

ANSWERS

MBA Colleges in India FAQs

Which is the No. 1 MBA college in India according to NIRF 2025?

Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad is ranked #1 in the NIRF 2025 Management ranking.

How are NIRF-ranked institutions and additional profiles shown?

The list includes institutions in the NIRF 2025 Management ranking. Additional CollegeDecoded profiles are marked separately and do not carry an NIRF rank.

Does the NIRF Management ranking include IITs?

Yes. IIT Delhi, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Kanpur and IIT Roorkee are among the institutions appearing in the 2025 Management ranking.

Does NIRF rank only MBA programmes?

No. NIRF Management is an institutional category. Students should check the individual institution's MBA, PGDM or other management programmes before comparing them.

Is NIRF rank enough to select an MBA college?

No. Compare entrance requirements, total cost, placements, programme structure, location and career fit along with the ranking.
