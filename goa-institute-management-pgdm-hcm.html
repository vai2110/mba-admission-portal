GIM PGDM HCM: What Is Healthcare Management?

GIM's PGDM in Healthcare Management is a two-year full-time residential programme designed around the management needs of the healthcare sector. The institute describes it as a blend of management and healthcare functions intended to develop professionals for a sector with growing organisational and managerial complexity.

Is HCM only for doctors?

No. GIM's stated eligibility allows graduates from any discipline meeting the programme criteria. MBBS graduates can additionally be considered for the GIM-MBBS Scholarship, but an MBBS degree is not the general eligibility requirement.

HCM Curriculum & Learning Focus

The HCM programme combines management concepts with healthcare-sector context. Students can expect learning around healthcare organisations, operations, strategy, finance, marketing, human resources and sector-specific management challenges.

Management foundation

  • Finance and accounting.
  • Marketing and strategy.
  • Operations and supply chain.
  • Organisational behaviour and HR.
  • Business analytics and decision-making.

Healthcare context

  • Healthcare organisations and systems.
  • Healthcare operations.
  • Industry-specific case learning.
  • Healthcare business and service delivery.
  • Exposure to healthcare-sector organisations.

Who Should Choose GIM HCM?

Good fit if

  • You want management roles in healthcare and allied sectors.
  • You are interested in healthcare business, operations or consulting.
  • You want a residential management programme with sector context.

Compare alternatives if

  • You want a conventional general-management career with no healthcare focus.
  • You are choosing HCM only because of the healthcare label.
  • Your preferred role is outside the programme's strongest recruiter sectors.

GIM HCM FAQs

What is the HCM fee at GIM?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh. The 2027-29 fee is not yet published.

Can non-medical graduates apply?

Yes. GIM's general eligibility is based on a bachelor's degree and accepted entrance examination; MBBS is not the general requirement.

How many students are admitted to HCM?

GIM lists an intake of 120 students for HCM.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. Freshers are welcome to apply.
