IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Hostels

Official hostel information lists seven male hostels—Aquamarine, Sapphire, Amber, Topaz, Emerald, Diamond and Jasper—two female hostels, Ruby & Rosaline and Opal, and a dedicated international hostel.

Hostel Facilities

Facilities vary by hostel, but official descriptions mention high-speed Wi-Fi, LAN connectivity, common rooms, badminton/table tennis or other sports areas, gyms in some hostels, canteens, laundry and mess facilities. Some hostels also have dedicated lifts, cycle stands and 24-hour support facilities.

Campus Life

IIT (ISM) describes itself as a fully residential campus. The institute's student-welfare office supports cultural, sports, technical and student-led activities. The campus also has health, library, sports and other everyday facilities.

Hostel & Other Charges

Hostel and related semester charges appear in institute fee schedules and can vary by programme and category. Students should not assume that a hostel amount from one academic year remains unchanged.

Student Discussion: What Is Hostel Life Like?

The residential model means that academic life, clubs, sports and hostel life overlap heavily. Students choosing IIT (ISM) should consider whether they prefer a residential campus environment and should check hostel rules and allotment procedures for their batch.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Hostel FAQs

Is IIT (ISM) Dhanbad residential?

Yes. The institute describes itself as a fully residential campus.

Are hostels available for girls?

Yes. Official hostel information lists Ruby & Rosaline and Opal among the female hostels.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Official hostel descriptions list high-speed Wi-Fi and, in several hostels, LAN connectivity.
