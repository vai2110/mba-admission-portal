IIM Kashipur MBA Overview

Quick answer

The IIM Kashipur MBA is a two-year full-time residential management programme. Admission is through CAT, with eligibility aligned to the CAT/JAP admission process.

The programme is designed around management foundations, functional areas and applied learning. IIM Kashipur also maintains academic calendars, curriculum resources, experiential learning initiatives and industry workshops for MBA students.

IIM Kashipur MBA Eligibility

Candidates must satisfy the institute's published eligibility and CAT requirements for the relevant admission cycle. The current 2026-28 admission page states that MBA admission is through CAT and that the eligibility criteria are the same as those for CAT.

What to keep ready

Applicants should maintain Class 10 and 12 records, graduation documents, CAT score details and category/other supporting documents where applicable. The final requirements should be checked against the current admission policy.

IIM Kashipur MBA FAQs

Is IIM Kashipur MBA residential?

Yes. The programme is a two-year full-time residential MBA.

Does IIM Kashipur accept CAT?

Yes. The institute states that MBA admission is through CAT.

What is the 2026-28 MBA fee?

The official published grand total is ₹20.80 lakh including admission fee and caution deposit.
