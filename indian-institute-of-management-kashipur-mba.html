Quick Answer

IIM Kashipur offers a two-year, full-time residential MBA (Analytics) programme and an Executive MBA (Analytics) for working professionals. The institute also facilitates international student exchange programmes with various global partner universities.

Programme Overview

IIM Kashipur provides a range of management programmes designed to foster leadership and analytical skills. The flagship MBA (Analytics) is a two-year, six-term residential programme. For working professionals, the institute offers the Executive MBA (Analytics), a two-year blended learning programme.

MBA (Analytics)

The MBA (Analytics) is a full-time residential programme. The first year consists of core management and analytics courses, followed by an 8–10 week summer internship. The second year includes mandatory analytics courses, electives, and a dissertation spanning three terms.

Executive MBA (Analytics)

The Executive MBA (Analytics) is designed for working executives. It requires a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 45% marks (42% for NC-OBC, 40% for SC/ST/DAP) and at least 3 years of full-time work experience.

Student Exchange Programme

IIM Kashipur facilitates international exposure through student exchange programmes. Partner universities include institutions in Greece, South Korea, USA, Morocco, Israel, China, UK, and Latvia, among others.
