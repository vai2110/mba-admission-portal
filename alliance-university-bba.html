Alliance University BBA: What the Programme Offers

Alliance University's BBA (Hons.) is a four-year undergraduate management programme designed around business fundamentals, experiential learning, internships and contemporary business skills. The current programme page highlights AI-driven insights, analytical decision-making, professional communication and industry interaction.

Who is BBA at Alliance best suited for?

It is suited to students who want a broad management foundation before entering business roles or pursuing an MBA, while keeping options open across finance, marketing, HR, operations, analytics, fintech and entrepreneurship.

Scholarships

Alliance lists merit, AUSAT, CUET, IPMAT and category-based scholarships. The published BBA page includes tuition waivers up to 50% for eligible academic performance and additional awards linked to entrance performance and inclusion criteria.

BBA Careers, Placements & Higher Studies

A BBA degree can lead to entry-level roles in business operations, marketing, finance, HR, analytics and related functions. Students who want deeper specialisation can also pursue an MBA, professional qualifications or domain-specific postgraduate study.

Should you choose BBA if you want an MBA later?

Yes. BBA provides a direct business foundation for an MBA, but students should still compare the curriculum, cost, internship exposure and placement outcomes of the undergraduate programme before joining.

Alliance's university-level placement reporting should not be treated as a guaranteed BBA outcome. For a programme decision, compare BBA-specific placement information wherever available and review the role mix rather than only the highest package.

Alliance University BBA FAQs

What is the duration of BBA at Alliance University?

The current BBA (Hons.) programme is four years. Alliance also lists a three-year BBA exit option in its programme information.

What is the BBA eligibility?

General applicants need 10+2 with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognised Board/Council; the university states a 5% relaxation for SC/ST candidates.

What is the BBA fee at Alliance University?

The current BBA (Hons.) page publishes a total programme fee of ₹12 lakh for Indian nationals, including registration and listed instalments.

Which entrance exams are accepted for BBA?

The current programme page lists CUET, IPMAT, UGAT, SAT and AUMAT among accepted routes.

Does Alliance offer BBA scholarships?

Yes. Merit, entrance-score and category-based scholarships are listed, subject to the university's conditions.
