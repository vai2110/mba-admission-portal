What is BIMTECH PGDM-IB?

PGDM International Business is a two-year full-time residential management programme focused on international trade and cross-border business, while retaining core management areas such as marketing, finance, economics, IT, HR and operations.

BIMTECH states that the programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

Who can apply for BIMTECH PGDM-IB?

A graduation degree in the 10+2+3 pattern with at least 50% marks is required. Final-year candidates in their first attempt may also apply subject to the institute's result deadline and 50% requirement.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

For 2027-29, BIMTECH lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT 2025/2026-27 and CMAT 2027 for PGDM-IB. Shortlisting considers entrance score and academics, followed by Extempore and Personal Interview.

What are the important BIMTECH admission dates for 2027-29?

Officially confirmed: BIMTECH says the final selection list will be declared in March 2027, and the academic session is likely to start in June/July 2027. Final-year graduation candidates must submit their final result with at least 50% marks by 31 October 2027.

What is the BIMTECH PGDM-IB cutoff?

BIMTECH does not state a single final CAT/XAT closing percentile for PGDM-IB on the current admission page. The institute uses a composite selection process, so meeting a minimum eligibility condition does not guarantee admission.

The CAT/XAT merit scholarship begins at 85 percentile, but that threshold is a scholarship criterion, not an admission cutoff.

What will you study in PGDM-IB?

The programme builds a foundation in management before moving to international business concepts, analytics, case-based learning and experiential activities. Students can build depth around global business while retaining exposure to mainstream management functions.

What are PGDM-IB placements?

The official BIMTECH 2023-25 placement report records 100% placement for PGDM-IB, with highest Indian CTC of ₹16.42 LPA, average CTC of ₹10.61 LPA and median CTC of ₹9.50 LPA.
