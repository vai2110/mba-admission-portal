Why is Jamia Hamdard B.Pharm popular?

Quick answer

Jamia Hamdard is particularly strong in pharmacy. In NIRF 2025 Pharmacy rankings, it was ranked No. 1 in India with a score of 86.59. That makes the university especially relevant for students who want pharmacy education, research exposure and access to a health-sciences ecosystem.

The B.Pharm programme is a four-year undergraduate route. Students should still evaluate the programme using the current 2026 prospectus, fee structure, admission rule and their own career goal rather than relying on the ranking alone.

What do you study in B.Pharm?

A B.Pharm curriculum typically builds knowledge across pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmaceutics, pharmacology, pharmacognosy, pharmaceutical analysis, microbiology, pharmacy practice and related areas. At Jamia Hamdard, the broader pharmacy ecosystem includes specialised postgraduate and research work across pharmaceutical disciplines.

B.Pharm Career Scope & Placements

Career paths after B.Pharm include pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality control, quality assurance, regulatory affairs, clinical research, medical writing, pharmacovigilance, hospital pharmacy, sales and marketing, and higher studies.

For placement expectations, do not treat Jamia Hamdard's NIRF Pharmacy Rank 1 as a salary guarantee. NIRF ranking measures a broader institutional category. Students should ask for B.Pharm-specific placement data, role mix and median/average salary before making an ROI decision.
