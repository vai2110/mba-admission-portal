IIM Kozhikode Placements: Student Decision Snapshot

The official reports show a rise in the highest CTC from ₹67.02 lakh in 2023 to ₹72.02 lakh in 2024 and ₹81 lakh in 2025. Mean CTC moved from ₹31.02 lakh in 2023 to ₹28.05 lakh in 2024 and ₹28.18 lakh in 2025. Median CTC remained at ₹27 lakh in 2023 and 2024 before increasing to ₹27.50 lakh in 2025. The domain mix also changed. Consulting remained the largest domain, while finance declined as a share of offers from 28% in 2024 to 19.2% in 2025.

Recruiters, Job Roles & Career Functions

The 2025 report records 169 total recruiters and 57 new recruiters. The report lists new recruiters across technology, manufacturing, consumer, logistics and financial services. Examples include HP, IndianOil, LAVA, Michelin, NSE, Swiggy, Target, UKG, Vivo and Zinnov.

Functions and roles reported

The official 2025 report groups hiring into consulting, finance, general management, sales and marketing, IT and analytics, and operations. The placement mix indicates that students were recruited across strategy, finance, analytics, technology, marketing, operations and general management functions.

Placement Analysis: What the Official Data Shows

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.
