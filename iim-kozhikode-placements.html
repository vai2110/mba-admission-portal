IIM Kozhikode Placements 2025: Key Stats

If you are evaluating IIM Kozhikode for consulting, finance, analytics, marketing or general management, the 2025 report gives you two useful starting points: the median CTC was ₹27.50 LPA, and consulting accounted for the largest share of offers. Finance recorded the highest individual package, but that headline should not be mistaken for the salary most students receive.

The three-year pattern is more informative than the highest package alone. Median CTC stayed at ₹27 LPA in 2023 and 2024, then rose to ₹27.50 LPA in 2025. The highest CTC increased each year, while mean CTC declined after 2023 and recovered only slightly in 2025.

What does the trend mean for an applicant?

  • Use the median as an anchor, not a promise. It is more useful than the highest offer for understanding the centre of the reported salary distribution.

IIM Kozhikode Placements: What Students Should Know

If you are choosing between B-schools

  • Compare median CTC and the domain mix, not just highest CTC.
  • Check whether your intended function appears consistently in the reports.
  • Look at the programme you are applying to; these placement figures are combined across three programmes.
  • Compare fees and your likely loan repayment against a realistic salary expectation, not the top offer.

If you are choosing a career domain

  • Consulting held the largest offer share in all three years.
  • Finance's offer share was lower in 2025 than in 2023–2024, while its highest CTC reached ₹81 LPA.
  • General Management and Sales & Marketing gained share in 2025 compared with 2024.
  • Review the actual job description and selection criteria because domain labels can cover different responsibilities.

Bottom line: IIM Kozhikode's reported median CTC moved from ₹27 LPA in 2023 and 2024 to ₹27.50 LPA in 2025, while consulting remained the largest domain. Your own outcome will still depend on the role you secure, recruiter requirements, interview performance and fit.

IIM Kozhikode Placements: Career Support

The 2025 Final Placements Report describes IIM Kozhikode's Corporate Access, Readiness and Engagement (CARE) Committee as an initiative designed to connect employment with employability. This is useful context for applicants because campus placement outcomes depend not only on recruiter participation, but also on preparation for the roles being offered.

Corporate Access

The report says CARE facilitates sector-specific and role-specific preparatory workshops to help students understand industry landscapes and career pathways.

Corporate Readiness

Students are supported in building profiles aligned with their career goals. The report describes one-to-one executive coaching from professionals or alumni to help develop relevant skills and competencies.

Corporate Engagement

CARE works on maintaining sustainable relationships with recruiters and encouraging deeper engagement with organisations to create career opportunities.

What applicants should take from this

Use the placement report to understand which domains and roles are present, then prepare for the specific selection process and skill expectations of the roles you want. The report describes the CARE initiative, but does not quantify its individual impact on placement outcomes.

Source: IIM Kozhikode Final Placements Report 2025, page 8, “Corporate Access, Readiness and Engagement (CARE) Committee”.

IIM Kozhikode Placements: Understanding CTC

  • Highest CTC: the largest package reported in that placement season. It is not the expected salary for every student.
  • Mean CTC: the arithmetic average of reported packages. A small number of high offers can pull the mean upward.
  • Median CTC: the middle value in the reported salary distribution; it is often a more useful reference point than the highest package.
  • Domain offer share: the proportion reported for a career domain in the placement report. It is not automatically the percentage of students placed in that domain unless the report explicitly says so.
  • Recruiter count: the number of recruiters recorded in the report. It does not show how many offers each company made or whether every student had access to every role.

CTC may include multiple salary components and benefits. These reports label the metric as CTC and do not provide a consistent role-by-role breakdown of fixed pay, variable pay and other components. Students should request the offer structure before estimating take-home pay or loan affordability.

IIM Kozhikode Placements FAQs

What was the median package at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The official 2025 Final Placements Report reported a median CTC of ₹27.50 LPA. The median was ₹27 LPA in both 2023 and 2024, so the 2025 figure represents a ₹0.50 LPA increase over those two seasons. It is a campus-level combined figure for the programmes covered by the report, not a guaranteed salary for an individual student.

What was the average package at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The 2025 report recorded a mean CTC of ₹28.18 LPA. The mean was ₹31.02 LPA in 2023 and ₹28.05 LPA in 2024. Since the mean can be affected by high offers and changes in role mix, compare it with the median and the domain data instead of using it alone.

What was the highest package at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The highest CTC reported was ₹81 LPA, up from ₹72.02 LPA in 2024 and ₹67.02 LPA in 2023. The 2025 domain section attributes the ₹81 LPA highest CTC to Finance. This is the highest reported offer, not a typical or guaranteed outcome.

Which domain had the most offers at IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

Consulting had the largest share at 32%, followed by Finance at 19.2%, General Management at 16.4%, Sales & Marketing at 14.4%, IT & Analytics at 13.5% and Operations at 4.5%. These are offer shares as reported by IIMK; they should not be relabelled as student percentages without further evidence.

Which domain had the highest average CTC in 2025?

Among the six domains shown in the 2025 report, Consulting had the highest average CTC at ₹33.13 LPA. IT & Analytics averaged ₹28.54 LPA and Operations ₹28.33 LPA. Finance had the highest individual CTC at ₹81 LPA, but its reported domain average was ₹24.97 LPA. This illustrates why the highest package and average package answer different questions.

Which companies recruit from IIM Kozhikode?

The reports name employers including Accenture Strategy, Bain, BCG, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase, Citi, Deutsche Bank, Microsoft, Walmart, Amazon, HUL, ITC, PepsiCo, Asian Paints and Tata Steel, among many others. The list varies by domain and year. These examples do not mean every company hires in every season or makes the same number of offers.

Are IIM Kozhikode placement statistics separate for PGP, PGP-Finance and PGP-LSM?

The 2023, 2024 and 2025 final placement reports describe a combined placement process covering the flagship PGP, PGP-Finance and PGP-Liberal Studies & Management. The headline mean, median and highest CTC figures on this page are therefore combined outcomes. Do not use them as programme-specific salary claims unless IIMK publishes a separate, comparable report.

How many recruiters visited IIM Kozhikode in 2025?

The 2025 report recorded 169 total recruiters, including 57 new recruiters, and 561 participating students. The report also recorded 13 international offers. Recruiter count indicates the breadth of employer participation, but does not disclose how many offers each recruiter made.

Did IIM Kozhikode placements improve from 2024 to 2025?

Some measures improved: median CTC rose from ₹27 LPA to ₹27.50 LPA, mean CTC rose slightly from ₹28.05 LPA to ₹28.18 LPA, highest CTC increased from ₹72.02 LPA to ₹81 LPA and recruiters increased from 156 to 169. Domain shares also changed: General Management increased from 8% to 16.4% and Sales & Marketing from 10% to 14.4%, while Finance fell from 28% to 19.2%. A balanced assessment should consider all of these measures rather than calling the entire placement season better based on one metric.

Does the ₹27.50 LPA median mean a student will earn ₹27.50 LPA in take-home salary?

No. The figure is reported as CTC, not monthly take-home pay. CTC can include fixed salary, variable pay and other components, and the reports do not provide a consistent component-wise breakdown for every role. Check the offer letter's fixed pay, variable component, benefits and deductions before estimating take-home salary.

Can I use the overall placement package to estimate my likely salary in a specific role?

Use it as a broad reference only. Domain-level averages help narrow the comparison, but they are not role-specific medians and do not show how salary varies by prior experience, skills, recruiter or job location. For a decision, compare the relevant domain's average and offer share with the actual role descriptions and your own target function.
