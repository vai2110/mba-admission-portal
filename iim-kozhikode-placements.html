How Students Should Read the Placement Data

Use median CTC to understand the middle of the reported salary distribution. Use the mean CTC to understand the arithmetic average, and use the domain mix to check whether your target function is represented.

Do not mix datasets: final placements, summer placements and one-year programme placements are separate datasets. The figures on this page are specifically the final placement outcomes and NIRF Management data identified above.
