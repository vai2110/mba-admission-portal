IIM Mumbai Placements 2026: Average CTC, Highest Package & Sector-wise Hiring

IIM Mumbai Placements: Student Decision Snapshot

The official 2026 report covers 505 students across MBA, MBA-OSCM and MBA-SM. It records 208 recruiting companies, 66 new companies and 179 PPOs. The average CTC was ₹31.4 lakh and the highest CTC was ₹71.4 lakh. The top 10%, top 20% and top 50% average CTCs were ₹47.5 lakh, ₹41.4 lakh and ₹35.92 lakh respectively. The report does not publish an overall median CTC, so this page does not infer one. The 2025 report recorded a highest CTC of ₹54 lakh and 198 recruiters, while its report presents a PPO average of ₹30.11 lakh.

The 2026 official report lists recruiters across BFSI, consulting, e-commerce and retail, FMCG, IT and analytics, manufacturing, pharma and other sectors. Named recruiters include Accenture, Alvarez & Marsal, Bain Capability Network, Deloitte, EY, Kearney, KPMG, PwC, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan Chase, Axis Bank, Amazon, Flipkart, Microsoft, Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Nestlé, PepsiCo, Reliance Industries, Tata Administrative Services and many others.

Functions and roles reported

The official 2026 report groups roles into consulting, supply chain, sales and marketing, BFSI, IT/tech/analytics and other functions. The report also identifies roles related to operations, supply chain, analytics, product and technology, finance and commercial functions.

Placement Analysis: What the Official Data Shows

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.
