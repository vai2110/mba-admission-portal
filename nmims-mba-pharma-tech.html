Programme Overview

The MBA Pharma Tech programme at NMIMS is an integrated course designed to equip students with a unique combination of pharmaceutical knowledge and management proficiency. The programme aims to develop professionals capable of impacting the global healthcare system.

Admission Process

Admissions at NMIMS are merit-based. The selection process for the MBA Pharma Tech programme typically involves the NMAT by GMAC entrance exam, followed by a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).
