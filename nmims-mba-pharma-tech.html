NMIMS MBA Pharma Tech Overview

Quick answer

MBA Pharma Tech combines pharmaceutical education with management training, making it relevant for students targeting the business side of healthcare, pharma and related industries.

The programme sits at the intersection of domain knowledge and management. Students can use the combination to understand pharmaceutical products and processes while developing commercial, operational and leadership capabilities.

MBA Pharma Tech Curriculum

The value proposition is the combination of pharmaceutical domain knowledge and management. Relevant learning areas can span healthcare/pharma operations, commercial strategy, marketing, finance, supply chain and organisational management, subject to the institute's current curriculum.

Pharma domain

Builds understanding of the pharmaceutical and healthcare ecosystem and its operating environment.

Business management

Develops managerial capability for commercial, operational and strategic decision-making.

Career Scope After MBA Pharma Tech

Potential career directions include pharmaceutical marketing, healthcare business development, product and brand management, operations, supply chain, consulting and other commercial functions within healthcare and life sciences.

Actual placement roles and packages depend on the programme, campus and batch. Use official programme-specific placement data wherever available.
