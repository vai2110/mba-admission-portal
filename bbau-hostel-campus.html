BBAU Lucknow Campus Location

BBAU's main campus is at Vidya Vihar, Raebareli Road, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226025. The university's official contact information lists toll-free number 1800-180-5789.

Hostel Facilities and Purpose

The official hostel page says the university hostel system is intended to help students from different parts of the country live together, support study time and create an environment for co-curricular and community activities. BBAU publishes a hostel manual and student code of conduct.

Hostel Admission and 2026-27 Allotment

The hostel procedure requires a student to submit the prescribed form with documentary evidence of registration in a regular university programme and Aadhaar as residential proof to the Office of the Dean Students' Welfare within the prescribed date. The university is publishing 2026-27 boys' and girls' hostel allotment lists.

Hostel Rules and Documents

  • Follow the university hostel manual and code of conduct.
  • Maintain proof of regular programme registration.
  • Keep Aadhaar or accepted residential proof ready.
  • Submit the hostel form within the notified schedule.
  • Follow warden and hostel administration instructions.

Student Life on Campus

BBAU's campus ecosystem includes academic departments, central library, student activities and university-level events. Students should evaluate campus life alongside hostel availability, commute, mess arrangements and department workload.
