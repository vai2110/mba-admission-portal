SOA BDS: Quick Answer

SOA's Institute of Dental Sciences offers BDS in Bhubaneswar. Admission is through NEET and the prescribed counselling route; SAAT is not the BDS admission test.

SOA BDS Admission 2026

Qualify NEET and participate in the counselling process applicable to SOA's BDS seats. Follow the current counselling authority's schedule for registration, choice filling and reporting.

SOA BDS Eligibility

Candidates must meet the applicable 10+2 PCB requirements and qualify NEET under the prevailing dental-admission rules.

SOA BDS Seats

NIRF 2025 reported an approved intake of 100 UG seats for 2023-24. Treat this as the reported NIRF-year intake, not automatically as the 2026 sanctioned intake.

SOA BDS Internship

The published 2026-27 fee information lists an internship stipend of ₹13,000 per month for one year.
