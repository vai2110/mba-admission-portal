SOA BDS: Quick Answer

SOA's Institute of Dental Sciences offers BDS in Bhubaneswar. Admission is through NEET and the prescribed counselling process. The BDS programme is separate from SOA's SAAT-based programmes.

Why Consider BDS at SOA?

Students should compare dental colleges on clinical exposure, patient flow, faculty, facilities, fee, internship support, postgraduate options and dental-category recognition. SOA's Dental category ranked 9 in NIRF 2025.

SOA BDS Admission 2026

To seek BDS admission, qualify NEET and participate in the counselling route applicable to SOA's dental seats. Choice filling, seat allotment, reporting and document rules are governed by the current counselling authority.

  1. Qualify NEET.
  2. Register for the applicable counselling.
  3. Select SOA/dental preferences where eligible.

SOA BDS Seats

NIRF 2025 reported an approved intake of 100 UG seats for the 2023-24 reporting year. This is a year-specific NIRF disclosure and should not be presented as the confirmed 2026 sanctioned intake without current confirmation.

BDS Course Structure & Learning

BDS combines dental and medical foundation subjects with pre-clinical laboratory work and clinical dental training. Students should expect a progression from basic sciences to dental specialities and patient-facing clinical learning.

Foundation

Basic sciences

Core medical and dental sciences that support later clinical learning.

Pre-clinical

Laboratory training

Practical work develops clinical and procedural foundations before full patient care.

Clinical

Dental practice

Clinical training covers diagnosis, treatment and dental specialities.
