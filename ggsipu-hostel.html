GGSIPU Hostel Facilities

GGSIPU maintains multiple hostel systems, including East Delhi Campus and other university hostels. The official hostel portal publishes separate boys and girls hostel brochures, allotment lists and notices.

GGSIPU Hostel Allotment 2026-27

Current 2026 notices include allotment lists for East Delhi Campus hostels, Aravali Boys Hostel, Shivalik Boys Hostel and Satpura Girls Hostel. Hostel allotment is therefore a separate process from academic admission and should be tracked independently.

GGSIPU Hostel Fees

Hostel charges vary by hostel and room type. Official hostel brochures can list annual room charges, admission charges, mess maintenance, refundable mess amounts and welfare charges. Do not use an old hostel fee as the current 2026-27 amount.

Budgeting tip

Calculate hostel + mess + security/refundable deposits + academic fee together when comparing residential and non-residential options.

Should You Choose a GGSIPU Hostel?

Hostel can make sense when

You live far from Delhi, have an inconvenient commute, or want more predictable access to campus activities.

Commuting may work when

Your institute is well connected and daily travel costs less than the complete residential cost.

GGSIPU Hostel FAQs

Does every GGSIPU student automatically get hostel?

No. Hostel allotment is separate and depends on the applicable hostel rules, application process and available seats.

Are hostel fees the same across campuses?

No. Room types and charges can differ by hostel and campus.
