BTech Fees 2026-27

The official 2026-27 UG fee page lists tuition of ₹1,00,000 for General/OBC-NCL/GEN-EWS candidates. Eligible SC/ST/GEN-PwD and other specified PwD categories have NIL tuition.

Other Semester Charges

Institute fee documents can include medical, sports, hostel seat rent, water, registration, examination, computer and internet, electricity/maintenance, library, training and placement support and gymkhana charges. These should be checked in the latest applicable fee notice.

Fee Waivers & Concessions

The institute states that the top 10 candidates admitted with a JEE Advanced CRL rank up to 1000 can receive a full tuition fee waiver for the UG programme subject to the stated conditions. Low annual family income concessions are also listed on the official fee page.

Student Discussion: Calculate Total Cost, Not Just Tuition

When comparing IIT options, students should add hostel, mess, registration and other recurring charges to tuition. Category-based waivers can materially change the total cost, so compare your own payable amount rather than a generic fee figure.
