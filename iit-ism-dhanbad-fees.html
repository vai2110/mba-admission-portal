Hostel, Mess and Other Charges

Hostel seat rent, hostel management, electricity, water, deposits and other recurring charges can be separate from tuition or programme fees. The applicable components differ by programme and fee schedule. Do not use the B.Tech fee table to estimate MBA costs or vice versa.

Fee Waivers and Financial Assistance

Fee concessions are programme- and category-specific. The 2026–27 MBA brochure publishes different semester totals for UR/OBC/EWS and SC/ST/PwD candidates. For undergraduate students, the institute's fee notices separately describe tuition waivers and income-linked concessions. Applicants should read the current scheme conditions and submit valid supporting certificates; do not assume a B.Tech waiver automatically applies to MBA.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Fees FAQs

What is the MBA fee at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad?

The 2026–27 brochure lists four semester fees totalling ₹3,85,500 for UR/OBC/EWS and ₹85,500 for SC/ST/PwD, plus mess charges of ₹19,500 per semester. The institute says the fee may change.

What is the total MBA fee including mess?

Adding the brochure's stated four semester mess charges gives an indicative subtotal of ₹4,63,500 for UR/OBC/EWS and ₹1,63,500 for SC/ST/PwD. Hostel, deposits and other costs may be additional.

Is MBA mess included in the semester fee?

No. The cited brochure states mess charges separately at ₹19,500 per semester.

Are the fees fixed for 2027–29?

No 2027–29 fee schedule was confirmed in the material reviewed. The 2026–27 brochure itself notes that fees were under revision.

Does MBA have the same tuition waiver as B.Tech?

Do not assume so. MBA fee categories and B.Tech tuition-waiver rules are published separately. Check the MBA brochure and the current fee notice for the programme you are joining.
