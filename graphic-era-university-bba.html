Graphic Era BBA: What Students Should Know

Graphic Era offers a three-year BBA pathway and multiple management variants, including finance, marketing, HR, entrepreneurship, aviation, hospital administration, AI & Data Science, fintech and e-commerce/digital marketing.

Quick answer

BBA is a practical starting point for students targeting business, management, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship or related roles and who may later pursue an MBA or professional qualification.

BBA Eligibility

GEU's published BBA criteria state 10+2 with any stream as the core academic requirement, with applicable regional criteria or quotas and minimum qualifying marks under university norms.

BBA Admission Process

The official programme page states that merit is prepared on the basis of the qualifying examination. Provisional admission may be offered under the university's stated process.

BBA Specialisations at GEU

The university lists BBA options in Finance, Marketing, HR, Financial Technology, Business Analytics, Entrepreneurship, Aviation Management, Hospital Administration, AI & Data Science and E-Commerce & Digital Marketing, among others.

BBA Careers & Placement Context

Career directions include marketing, sales, business development, finance operations, HR, analytics, entrepreneurship and management-trainee roles. Course-specific outcomes depend on the programme and student profile; university-wide placement figures should not be read as a BBA average.

BBA FAQs

Can students from Science or Arts take BBA?

Yes. GEU's published BBA criteria allow 10+2 with any stream, subject to university norms.

What is the BBA fee at GEU?

The 2026 general BBA tuition is ₹90,000 per semester in Year 1, ₹92,000 in Year 2 and ₹96,600 in Year 3 before scholarship/payment adjustments.

Does GEU offer specialised BBA programmes?

Yes. The official programme list includes several specialised BBA pathways.
