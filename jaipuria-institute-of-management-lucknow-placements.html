Placement Roles and Hiring Sectors

Jaipuria students are prepared for roles across functions such as finance, marketing, sales, consulting, human resources, operations, business analytics and general management. The institute's recruiter ecosystem includes organisations from BFSI, FMCG, retail, consulting, technology and other sectors.

What should students check beyond the highest package?

  • Median or typical compensation, not only the highest CTC.
  • Number and quality of offers relative to the graduating batch.
  • Role profile and location of the offer.
  • Sector mix and repeat recruiters.
  • Placement status at the time of your admission decision.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placement Process

The placement ecosystem is supported by the institute's career development and corporate-relations function. Students typically prepare through aptitude and interview training, industry interaction, internships, live projects, skill development and recruiter-facing processes.

Career Preparation

Students build employability through academic learning, communication, business skills, projects and structured career preparation.

Corporate Interaction

Industry sessions, corporate engagements and recruiter interactions help students understand role expectations.

Recruitment

Companies participate in campus recruitment across multiple sectors and functional areas.

Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

The PGDM curriculum incorporates summer internships and live projects. These experiences matter because they allow students to test classroom concepts in real business settings and can strengthen their profile before final placements.

For students comparing B-schools, internship quality should be evaluated alongside final placement statistics. A relevant internship in the desired sector can be particularly valuable for candidates targeting finance, analytics, consulting, retail or marketing.

How to Evaluate Jaipuria Placement Data

The current official data highlights a ₹24.11 LPA highest CTC, but the more useful question for an applicant is what type of role they are likely to secure. The institute separately publishes averages for the top 10%, 20% and 50%, which gives more context than the highest package alone.

Decision tip

If your target is a specific function, compare role-wise and sector-wise outcomes wherever the latest official placement report provides them. Do not assume the highest CTC represents the typical outcome for every student.

For the latest placement report and official updates, use the institute's Jaipuria Lucknow downloads page.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at Jaipuria Lucknow?

The current official ongoing placement update reports a highest CTC of ₹24.11 LPA.

What is the average package at Jaipuria Lucknow?

Jaipuria currently reports average CTC figures of ₹13.52 LPA for the top 10%, ₹12.16 LPA for the top 20% and ₹11.08 LPA for the top 50% in its ongoing placement update.

How many companies recruit at Jaipuria Lucknow?

The current official update lists 275+ companies participating in recruitment.

Is placement guaranteed?

The institute advertises placement support, but students should distinguish placement assistance from a guaranteed individual job offer. Outcomes depend on profile, skills, interview performance, role availability and market conditions.
