Jaipuria Lucknow Placement Status & Salary Distribution

A placement percentage and a package figure answer different questions. The current 74% status indicates the stage of the placement season, while the salary figures describe reported compensation benchmarks.

Why top 10%, 20% and 50% figures matter

The highest package is achieved by a small subset of students. Separate averages for the top 10%, top 20% and top 50% provide more context than the highest CTC alone.

Highest CTC

₹24.11 LPA is the current highest reported CTC and represents the upper end of outcomes.

Top 20% Average

₹12.16 LPA provides a broader benchmark than the single highest offer.

Top 50% Average

₹11.08 LPA provides another reference point for understanding compensation distribution.

The public dashboard does not provide a single overall-batch average. The top-segment averages should not be described as the average salary of every student.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placement Process

The placement ecosystem combines career preparation, corporate interaction and campus recruitment. Individual recruiter processes vary.

What students should prepare

  1. Resume and profile positioning for the target function.
  2. Aptitude and assessment preparation.
  3. Communication and case-solving skills where relevant.
  4. Domain knowledge for finance, analytics, marketing or other specialised roles.
  5. Personal interview preparation and business awareness.
  6. Strong examples from internships, projects and extracurricular work.

Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

Jaipuria's PGDM structure includes summer internships and live projects. These experiences provide practical exposure before final placements and can help students identify the function or sector they want to pursue.

How to make the internship placement-relevant

  • Choose a function aligned with the target final-placement role.
  • Track measurable business outcomes.
  • Develop at least one demonstrable technical or functional skill.
  • Convert the project into quantified resume achievements.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placements FAQs

What is the highest package?

The current interim placement communication reports ₹24.11 LPA as the highest CTC.

What is the average package?

The current report gives separate averages: ₹13.52 LPA for the top 10%, ₹12.16 LPA for the top 20% and ₹11.08 LPA for the top 50%.

What is the placement percentage?

The current interim report states that placements stand at 74% at the time of reporting.

How many recruiters participate?

Which companies recruit?

Current hiring-partner communication includes PwC, KPMG, Accenture, HDFC Bank, Nestle, Deloitte, Wipro, Blackstone, Flipkart, Dabur, HCL, Tata Power, Genpact and others.

Is placement guaranteed?

Placement support should not be interpreted as a guaranteed individual job. Outcomes depend on profile, skills, interview performance, recruiter requirements and market conditions.

Is the highest package the average package?

No. The highest CTC is the upper-end offer. Separate top-segment averages provide additional context.
