Jaipuria Lucknow Placement Status

A placement percentage and a package figure answer different questions. The current 74% placement status indicates the stage of the ongoing placement season, while the salary figures show compensation among reported groups.

Why the top 10%, 20% and 50% figures matter

The highest package is usually achieved by a small subset of students. The separate top-10%, top-20% and top-50% averages provide additional context and help a candidate avoid treating the highest CTC as a typical outcome.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placement Process

The placement ecosystem is built around career preparation, corporate interaction and campus recruitment. Students should expect to work on both technical/business knowledge and communication skills.

Typical preparation areas

  1. Resume and profile development.
  2. Aptitude and assessment preparation.
  3. Communication and group-discussion skills.
  4. Personal interview preparation.
  5. Domain knowledge and role-specific preparation.
  6. Corporate interactions and recruiter processes.

The exact process and eligibility for individual recruiters can vary by company and year.

Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

Jaipuria's PGDM programmes include summer internship and live-project exposure. These components allow students to work with business problems before final placements and can help them decide which sector or function fits their career goals.

Why internship quality matters

  • It adds practical evidence to a resume.
  • It can improve domain understanding.
  • It provides exposure to workplace expectations.
  • It can support final-placement preparation.
  • It helps students test whether they actually enjoy a chosen function.

What is the highest package at Jaipuria Lucknow?

How many companies recruit at Jaipuria Lucknow?

Are Jaipuria placements guaranteed?
