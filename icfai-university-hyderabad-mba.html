IBS Hyderabad MBA: Programme Overview

The IBS Hyderabad MBA is a two-year full-time management programme offered by ICFAI Business School under IFHE. The programme uses case-based learning and exposes students to functional areas such as finance, marketing, human resources, operations and IT systems.

Who is it for?

The programme is worth considering if you want a broad management MBA, are comfortable with a large cohort and want access to a Hyderabad-based residential business-school ecosystem. Your decision should ultimately depend on target roles, total cost and placement outcomes.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Eligibility 2026

IFHE states that applicants should have graduation in any discipline with 50% or above marks, with English as the medium of instruction. Candidates with a different medium may need to satisfy the applicable language requirement.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Admission 2026

Applicants can seek admission through IBSAT 2026 or qualifying scores from CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT, according to the current IBS admission route. IBSAT is the institute's own management admission test.

Application and test timelines can change by intake. Students should use the official IBS/IFHE admission portal for the active cycle rather than relying on an older deadline.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Selection Process

Shortlisted candidates attend a selection process comprising Micro Presentation and Personal Interview. The institute states that the final admission decision considers academic record along with performance in these selection components.

Prepare for the Micro Presentation

  • Structure an argument quickly.
  • Use clear examples rather than generic statements.
  • Keep the presentation focused and time-bound.
  • Show awareness of business and current affairs.

Prepare for the PI

  • Know your academic and work background.
  • Explain why MBA and why IBS Hyderabad.
  • Be ready for career-goal questions.
  • Understand your chosen functional area.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Fees 2026

The current published programme fee for IBS Hyderabad is ₹17.01 lakh for two years, equivalent to ₹8.505 lakh per year. A refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit is additional, and living expenses are extra. The university's mandatory disclosure also reports ₹17.01 lakh as the total MBA programme fee.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Placements 2025

Publicly reported 2025 placement data puts IBS Hyderabad's average CTC at ₹9.82 LPA, highest domestic CTC at ₹21 LPA and placement rate at about 93%. The top 10% average was reported at ₹13.70 LPA and top 25% average at ₹11.76 LPA.

IBS Hyderabad MBA Curriculum and Career Fit

The broad MBA structure is useful for students who have not yet committed to a single functional career. Finance, marketing, HR, operations and IT systems can lead to different placement pools, so students should use electives, internships, live projects and skills development to build a coherent profile.

Student Discussion: What Should You Evaluate?

Public applicant discussions around IBS Hyderabad often revolve around fee-versus-placement ROI, large batch size, role quality, campus experience and whether the average package justifies the total cost. Treat these as individual opinions, not official facts.

  • Compare average and median outcomes instead of focusing only on the highest offer.
  • Ask which roles are actually available to the majority of the batch.
  • Calculate your personal two-year cost including living expenses.
  • Compare the programme with the alternatives you would realistically convert.

IBS Hyderabad MBA FAQs

What is the IBS Hyderabad MBA fee?

The published fee is ₹17.01 lakh for two years, plus a refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit and living expenses.

Can I apply through CAT or XAT?

Yes. IBS currently lists CAT and XAT among the qualifying score routes, along with IBSAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT.

What is the minimum graduation percentage?

The current eligibility states 50% or above in graduation, with the applicable English-medium requirement.

What is the IBS Hyderabad average package?

Reported 2025 data puts the average CTC at ₹9.82 LPA. The highest domestic CTC was ₹21 LPA.
