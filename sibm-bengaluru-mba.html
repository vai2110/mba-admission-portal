SIBM Bengaluru MBA Eligibility

The official MBA eligibility requires a graduate degree from a recognised university/institution with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the stated threshold is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Candidates with foreign qualifications need an AIU equivalence certificate.

The MBA is a full-time residential programme. The institute's 2027-29 page states an intake of 240 students.

How SIBM Bengaluru MBA Selection Works

For the 2027-29 batch, the official final-merit structure assigns 50 marks to SNAP score out of 60, 10 marks to Group Exercise and 40 marks to Personal Interaction. The published structure makes clear that SNAP is the first filter, not the entire final-selection score.

Is there an official SNAP percentile cutoff?

The current official MBA page does not state a fixed SNAP percentile cutoff. Therefore, this page does not present an external coaching-site percentile as an official SIBM Bengaluru cutoff. Applicants should distinguish the institute's shortlisting practice from unofficial “expected cutoff” estimates.

How to apply

Applicants first register and pay for SNAP. They then register separately for the SIBM Bengaluru MBA programme and pay the institute application fee. The current official page lists ₹2,550 per SNAP test and ₹1,000 for the SIBM-B MBA programme.

What You Study in the SIBM Bengaluru MBA

The first year introduces foundation areas such as Economics, Accounting, Statistics, Strategy, Marketing, Operations, Organisational Behaviour, Finance and HR. The institute states that students then undertake an eight-week Summer Internship Programme after the first year.

The curriculum is enhanced through Harvard Business School cases and simulations. Applicants should use the official programme structure for the detailed semester-wise subject list.

SIBM Bengaluru MBA Placements

The institute publishes placement outcomes on a dedicated page, including specialization-level figures. The current official page reports 2025 average CTC of ₹15.36 LPA for Operations, ₹14.87 LPA for Finance, ₹14.38 LPA for Marketing, ₹12.75 LPA for Human Resources and ₹14.32 LPA for Business Analytics.

SIBM Bengaluru MBA FAQs

Can a candidate get admission to SIBM Bengaluru MBA with SNAP alone?

No. SNAP contributes 50 marks to the final merit list, while GE and PI contribute the remaining 50 marks.

Is SIBM Bengaluru MBA residential?

Yes. The official programme describes the MBA as a compulsory residential programme.

What is the total SIBM Bengaluru MBA fee?

The 2026-28 published schedule can be used to understand the components, but the final 2027-29 fee should be taken from the institute's new fee schedule when published.

Does SIBM Bengaluru publish placements by specialisation?

Yes. Its current placement page reports 2025 outcomes separately for Marketing, Finance, Operations, HR and Business Analytics.
