Which Roles Recruit at SIBM Pune?

Which Companies Recruit from SIBM Pune?

The official recruiter page lists companies across banking, consulting, consumer goods, technology, healthcare, manufacturing and other sectors. The placement reports also identify new associations and continuing recruiters for individual years.

Examples appearing in the institute's official recruiter information include Accenture, Asian Paints, Bajaj Auto, Deloitte Consulting, Goldman Sachs, Google, HDFC Bank, Hindustan Unilever, ICICI Bank, IBM, and Colgate-Palmolive. Recruiter participation can change by placement cycle.

What the Placement Trend Means for MBA Applicants

The official three-year figures show two useful patterns. First, average CTC has been relatively stable: ₹28.16 lakh in 2023, ₹28.83 lakh in 2024 and ₹28.18 lakh in 2025. Second, the highest CTC increased substantially in 2025 to ₹74.84 lakh.

Students should therefore evaluate the placement report using the median and average together. The highest package represents the top end of outcomes and should not be used as the expected salary for every graduate.

SIBM Pune Placement FAQs

What is the latest average package at SIBM Pune?

The latest official 2025 placement report for the MBA 2024–26 batch records an average CTC of ₹28.18 lakh.

What is the highest package at SIBM Pune?

The 2025 official placement report records a highest CTC of ₹74.84 lakh.

What is the median package at SIBM Pune?

The 2025 report records a median CTC of ₹27 lakh. The corresponding figures were ₹26.04 lakh in 2024 and ₹25 lakh in 2023.

How many recruiters visited SIBM Pune in 2025?

The official 2025 report states that more than 110 recruiters participated in the Campus Recruitment Programme.

Does the SIBM Pune placement report apply to MBA I&E?

The cited placement reports are explicitly presented as reports for the MBA batch. They should not be treated as MBA I&E-specific placement statistics unless SIBM Pune publishes a separate I&E placement report.
