Amity Noida Hostel Rooms

The official accommodation information describes apartment-style AC accommodation with four single rooms, attached bathrooms and a shared sitting lounge. The university also references non-AC accommodation on its accommodation pages.

Hostel Facilities

Official information lists air conditioning, attached bathrooms, sitting lounge, television/cable, refrigerator, Wi-Fi, essential furniture and laundry services. Dedicated wardens and security systems support residential operations.

Hostel Life

Living on campus can reduce daily travel and makes it easier to access academic, library, sports and student activities. Students should still review hostel rules, reporting requirements and the refund policy before paying.

Important Hostel Considerations

The university communicates hostel reporting separately through Amizone. Hostel cancellation and refund rules depend on the timing of withdrawal, so students should read the current 2026 withdrawal guidelines before committing.

Amity Noida Hostel FAQs

How much is Amity Noida hostel fee in 2026?

The official FAQ lists ₹1.95 lakh per annum for AC rooms plus a ₹15,000 one-time security deposit.

Is food included in the hostel fee?

The official 2026 FAQ says the listed hostel fee is inclusive of boarding and laundry charges.

Are hostels available for boys and girls?

Yes. Amity provides separate hostel facilities for boys and girls within the university campus.
