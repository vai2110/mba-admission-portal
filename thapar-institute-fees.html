Thapar BTech Fees

UG engineering fees are published in the applicable 2026-27 fee schedule and can include tuition and institute charges in addition to accommodation. Students should use the current programme fee notice rather than an older year.

MTech, MCA and MSc Fees

Postgraduate fees vary by programme. M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc. and MA applicants should verify the exact current fee schedule for their programme before accepting an offer.

Hostel Fees and Additional Costs

Hostel accommodation is separate from academic tuition. TIET currently operates 17 on-campus hostels with different room types and facilities. Accommodation charges depend on the hostel and room category.

How to Calculate Your Total Thapar Cost

Tuition is only one part of the cost. Add hostel, mess, security/deposits, books, laptop, travel and personal spending. For MBA students, also consider the opportunity cost of two years away from full-time employment.

Budget for

  • Academic fee
  • Hostel and mess
  • Laptop and study material
  • Travel and personal expenses

Verify

  • Latest fee circular
  • Refund rules
  • Hostel category
  • Additional institute charges

Student Discussion: Is Thapar Worth the Cost?

The answer depends on programme and alternative admits. Compare the complete cost with branch-specific or programme-specific outcomes, not just the university's highest package.

Thapar Fees FAQs

What is the Thapar MBA fee?

The published 2026-28 academic fee totals ₹14.10 lakh across four semesters.

Are hostel charges included?

No. Accommodation and related living expenses are separate.

Are BTech and MBA fees the same?

No. Each programme has its own fee schedule.
