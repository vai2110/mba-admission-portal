How much is the PGDM fee at IMI Bhubaneswar?

2026-28 published programme fee: ₹14,98,000.

This is the academic programme fee before hostel and mess. The published structure includes tuition, learning material, student activities and welfare, library resources, IT infrastructure, alumni membership and security deposit.

Is the IMI Bhubaneswar security deposit refundable?

The published PGDM fee includes a ₹15,000 security deposit. The institute's fee documents state that the deposit is refundable subject to clearance of dues and damages to institute property/assets.

IMI Bhubaneswar Fees FAQs

What is the IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM fee for 2026-28?

The published two-year programme fee is ₹14.98 lakh. This excludes hostel and mess charges.

Is hostel included in IMI Bhubaneswar fees?

No. Hostel and mess are charged separately. For 2026-27, hostel charges range from ₹1.45 lakh to ₹1.60 lakh depending on room and bathroom type, while mess is ₹80,000.

What is the IMI Bhubaneswar hostel fee?

For 2026-27, boys' twin AC common-bathroom accommodation is ₹1.45 lakh. Girls' twin AC accommodation is ₹1.45 lakh with common bathroom or ₹1.60 lakh with attached bathroom.

What is the total cost of PGDM at IMI Bhubaneswar including hostel?

Using the published 2026-28 programme fee plus two years of hostel and mess, the approximate residential academic cost is around ₹19.83–20.13 lakh, depending on hostel option. Personal expenses are extra.

Does the ₹14.98 lakh IMI fee include tuition?

Yes. Tuition is the largest component at ₹12.08 lakh. The remaining amount covers learning materials, welfare, library, IT, alumni membership and security deposit.

Is the IMI Bhubaneswar security deposit refundable?

The published fee structure marks the ₹15,000 security deposit as refundable, subject to clearance of dues and damages to institute property/assets.

Is IMI Bhubaneswar expensive compared with other PGDM colleges?

The useful comparison is total residential cost, not tuition alone. IMI Bhubaneswar's published programme fee is ₹14.98 lakh, and hostel/mess take the approximate two-year residential cost to about ₹19.8–20.1 lakh before personal expenses.
