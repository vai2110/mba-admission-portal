What placement figures are published?

Amrita's Coimbatore School of Business placement page displays a “best of 3 years” summary with ₹24.84 LPA highest CTC, ₹8.34 LPA average CTC and ₹7.60 LPA median CTC. Separately, an Amrita 2024–25 newsletter reports MBA 2023–25 cross-campus figures: ₹7.95 LPA average salary, ₹7.60 LPA median and 166 offers.

These are different scopes: a campus-specific multi-year summary is not the same as a single batch across campuses. Do not combine them into one “Amrita MBA placement” statistic.

For your decision: ask for the latest report for the exact campus and programme you plan to join.

Which roles and sectors should you investigate?

Amrita School of Business describes MBA internships and placements across Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics. Check the role/function distribution in the report rather than assuming a recruiter name means the company hired for your preferred function.

  • Finance: verify whether roles are analytical, sales-oriented or operations-focused.
  • Marketing: distinguish brand/product roles from sales/business development.
  • Operations: check supply-chain, process and procurement positions.
  • Analytics: confirm the job actually uses analytical methods/tools.
  • HR: review role descriptions and growth path.

Do not use B.Tech or M.Tech placements as evidence for MBA outcomes.

How do internships connect to final placements?

The Coimbatore School of Business describes an internship period after the first year, generally 8–10 weeks between May and July, with faculty guidance and the possibility of pre-placement offers. Internship data and final-placement data should be kept separate.

Check the number of students eligible, internships secured and whether stipend values are average, median or highest. Do not infer that every student receives a PPO or that every internship is paid unless the report confirms it.

How should you evaluate ROI?

  1. Confirm campus and batch.
  2. Use median salary where available.
  3. Check eligible students and offer counts.
  4. Review actual role and industry mix.
  5. Compare outcomes with the campus-specific fee and living costs.

Where a current official metric is unavailable, state that limitation rather than filling the gap with a third-party estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What average salary does the Coimbatore page display?

₹8.34 LPA in its “best of 3 years” summary.

What median salary is shown?

₹7.60 LPA in the Coimbatore summary and in Amrita's cross-campus MBA 2023–25 newsletter.

Are all campuses' placements identical?

No. Check the campus, programme and batch before comparing.

Are internships guaranteed to become PPOs?

No. The official page describes the possibility of PPOs, not a guarantee.

Should highest CTC decide my choice?

No. Compare median, roles, batch, campus and total cost.
