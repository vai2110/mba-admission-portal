Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multi-campus, multi-disciplinary research institution accredited with an 'A++' grade by NAAC. The university is recognized for its research-intensive environment and commitment to holistic education.

MBA Programmes

The university offers the following management degree programmes:

  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • MBA - MS in Business Analytics (Dual Degree Programme with University at Buffalo, USA)
