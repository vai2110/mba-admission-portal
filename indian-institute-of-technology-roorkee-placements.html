What does IIT Roorkee’s MBA placement report say?

The Department of Management Studies (DoMS), IIT Roorkee published a Final Placement Report for the MBA Class of 2025. The report says more than 60 organisations participated and notes growth in offers from PSUs and government organisations. It describes opportunities in general management, marketing and finance, alongside the department’s strengths in operations, IT and analytics.

Use the official PDF for batch-specific salary figures and recruiter/role details. Do not transfer engineering placement data or a figure from an older MBA batch to the current MBA cohort.

First checks: batch year, students who sought placement, metric definition, and whether the data is MBA-only.

Which functions should applicants investigate?

  • Finance: check whether roles are corporate finance, banking, analysis, sales or operations.
  • Marketing: distinguish sales/business development from brand or product roles.
  • Operations: look for supply chain, process, procurement and service operations positions.
  • IT and analytics: verify the actual job description and required analytical/technical work.
  • PSUs and government organisations: read the report's eligibility and recruitment context for these offers.

Recruiter participation does not mean every student received an offer from every organisation, and a logo list alone does not show the number of roles available.

How should you compare salary figures?

Median salary is usually more informative about the middle outcome than the highest package. Average salary can be pulled upward by a small number of large offers. Check the official report's denominator and whether international offers, PPOs or students opting out are treated separately.

  1. Use the latest DoMS MBA report.
  2. Confirm the number of students seeking placement.
  3. Compare median and average only when both are published.
  4. Read the function and sector distribution.
  5. Compare total programme cost with realistic career outcomes.

Who should use this placement page?

This page is for applicants comparing the DoMS two-year MBA with other management programmes. If you are considering IIT Roorkee's Executive MBA, assess its separate online format, fee and working-professional fit instead of applying full-time MBA placement data to it.
