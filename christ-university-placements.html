Christ University Placements: Quick Answer

Latest official MBA snapshot

For 2024-25, Christ's annual report records 327 companies, 911 offers among 946 eligible MBA students, a 99% placement rate, ₹10.7 LPA average salary and ₹33 LPA highest domestic package.

How Should Students Read the Placement Data?

The placement rate, average salary and highest salary answer different questions. A 99% placement rate does not mean every student earns ₹10.7 LPA, and a ₹33 LPA highest package does not represent the typical offer.

MetricWhat it tells you
Placement rateShare of eligible students receiving offers in the reported process.
Average salaryMean salary across the reported placed cohort.
Highest packageTop reported domestic package; not a typical outcome.
OffersTotal offers received, which can differ from the number of students.

What Salary Can MBA Students Expect?

The latest annual report gives a ₹10.7 LPA average and ₹33 LPA highest domestic package. The official MBA placement brochure also reports distribution figures for the 2022-24 batch: median CTC ₹10.8 LPA, top 50% ₹12 LPA, top 25% ₹15.3 LPA and top 10% ₹17.4 LPA.

Because the two sources describe different batches, do not combine them into a single-year salary distribution.

Which Companies Recruit MBA Students?

The official placement material identifies recruiting activity across consulting, finance, BFSI, technology, IT/ITES, retail and manufacturing. The 2024-25 annual report also reports participation from multinational and Fortune 500 organisations.

Examples appearing in Christ's official MBA placement material include Deloitte, EY, KPMG, HSBC and Wells Fargo. Recruiter lists can change by batch and should not be treated as guaranteed recruiters for the next intake.

What Does This Mean for MBA ROI?

Using the 2024-25 average salary of ₹10.7 LPA against the published two-year programme fee of ₹13.0 lakh for Karnataka students or ₹13.4 lakh for other Indian states gives a simple fee-to-average-salary comparison. It is not a guaranteed ROI calculation because salary is gross CTC, while students also incur living, hostel and other costs.

For a realistic decision, compare the fee, living costs, your expected role, placement rate and the salary distribution rather than using the highest package alone.
