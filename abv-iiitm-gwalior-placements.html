Recruiters and internships at ABV-IIITM Gwalior

The institute's annual report notes that 165 students completed summer training at organisations including Google, Microsoft, D. E. Shaw, Cisco, Amazon and Curefit. This is internship/training exposure and should not be interpreted as a placement guarantee.

The placement report also notes 4 international offers for the detailed programme data shown.

How should students read ABV-IIITM placement data?

Check three things before comparing numbers: programme, graduating session and denominator. “Placement % of eligible students” is not the same metric as “students placed”, and average CTC is not the same as highest CTC.

The institute also reports multiple offers for some students. Therefore, total offers should not automatically be interpreted as the number of unique students placed.

ABV-IIITM Gwalior Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The institute's 2023–24 annual report lists ₹65 LPA as the highest CTC for B.Tech CSE, B.Tech+M.Tech and B.Tech+MBA. It is a programme/session-specific highest CTC, not an assured salary for all students.

What is the average package at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For 2023–24, the reported average CTC was ₹22.11 LPA for B.Tech CSE, ₹22.68 LPA for B.Tech+M.Tech and ₹16.18 LPA for B.Tech+MBA. PG M.Tech and PG MBA averages were ₹9.86 LPA and ₹8.15 LPA respectively.

Does ABV-IIITM Gwalior have 100% placement?

Not across every programme. The 2023–24 report shows placement percentages of 92.72% for BCS, 90% for IMT and 84.31% for IMG based on eligible students, while PG MBA reported 100% for its four-student cohort. These figures are programme-specific.

Which branch has the best placement at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

For the 2023–24 detailed data, B.Tech+M.Tech had the highest reported average CTC at ₹22.68 LPA, slightly above B.Tech CSE at ₹22.11 LPA. However, students should also compare curriculum, duration and career goal rather than choosing only on average package.

What is the B.Tech CSE average package at ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The 2023–24 annual report lists ₹22.11 LPA average CTC for B.Tech CSE, with 51 students placed and ₹65 LPA highest CTC.

Which companies recruit from ABV-IIITM Gwalior?

The institute's annual report records summer training at organisations including Google, Microsoft, D. E. Shaw, Cisco, Amazon and Curefit. Recruiter participation varies by placement year, so students should not treat an internship/training list as a guaranteed placement list.

How should I compare ABV-IIITM placement statistics with other colleges?

Compare the same programme and year, then check whether the source reports eligible students, total graduates, students placed or offers. Also compare average versus median salary and on-campus versus off-campus highest packages where those distinctions are reported.
