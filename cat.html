CAT 2026: What Students Need to Know

The Common Admission Test (CAT) 2026 is the national-level management entrance examination conducted by IIM Indore. CAT scores are used for admission to postgraduate management programmes of the IIMs and are also accepted by several non-IIM institutions.

For students planning an MBA, this page brings together the latest CAT 2026 dates, eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus, registration and correction updates, fees, admit card, result, percentile and cutoff guidance, IIM admission process and preparation strategy in one place.

CAT 2026 Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

For CAT 2026, a candidate should have a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. For candidates belonging to SC, ST and PwD categories, the minimum requirement is 45%. Candidates in the final year of a bachelor's degree or those awaiting the result can also apply, subject to the conditions in the CAT notification and the admission requirements of the IIM concerned.

Important: CAT eligibility is not the same as IIM admission eligibility. Individual IIMs can apply additional programme-specific conditions and selection criteria. Always read the current admission policy of the IIM/programme you are targeting.

CAT 2026 Exam Pattern: 68 Questions, 2 Hours

The CAT 2026 examination is a 2-hour computer-based test with three sections. The latest CAT 2026 information reported from the official examination notice specifies 68 questions, with candidates getting 40 minutes per section. Section switching is not permitted while a section is active.

CAT 2026 Marking Scheme: MCQ vs TITA

The CAT 2026 marking structure gives 3 marks for a correct answer and 1 mark is deducted for an incorrect MCQ. Non-MCQ/TITA questions do not carry negative marking for an incorrect response.

ResponseMarks
Correct answer+3
Incorrect MCQ-1
Incorrect TITA / non-MCQ0
Unattempted0
What this means for preparation:

Do not chase attempts alone. Question selection, accuracy and time spent per question matter because each section has its own fixed 40-minute window.

CAT 2026 Syllabus: What Should You Prepare?

IIMs do not publish a chapter-by-chapter CAT syllabus in the same way as many school examinations. The practical preparation areas below are based on the three CAT sections and the types of skills tested in the examination. Students should use official CAT information for the exam structure and use previous CAT papers for topic-level practice.

VARC — Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension

  • Reading comprehension across different subjects and writing styles
  • Para summary and paragraph-based questions
  • Para jumbles / sentence arrangement
  • Odd sentence out and verbal reasoning
  • Reading speed, inference and author-intent questions

DILR — Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning

  • Tables, charts and graphs
  • Caselets and data-based reasoning
  • Arrangements and distribution sets
  • Games, tournaments and scheduling
  • Sets, Venn diagrams and logical conditions

QA — Quantitative Aptitude

  • Arithmetic: percentages, ratios, averages, profit-loss, mixtures, time-work and time-speed-distance
  • Algebra: equations, inequalities, functions and progressions
  • Geometry and mensuration
  • Number system and related applications
  • Modern mathematics topics that appear in CAT-level questions

CAT 2026 Cutoff: Qualifying Percentile vs Actual Shortlist

There is no single CAT percentile that guarantees admission to an IIM. Each IIM publishes its own admission policy, and the minimum qualifying percentile can be different from the percentile at which shortlists are actually seen in a particular year.

What students seeWhat it means
Qualifying percentileMinimum CAT percentile stated in an institute's admission policy for consideration.
Shortlisting percentileThe effective level at which candidates may receive calls; it can be higher and depends on the applicant pool.
Final admissionDepends on CAT plus academics, work experience, PI/WAT or other institute-specific components.

Students should therefore avoid treating an unofficial “99+ required” number as an official cutoff. For an accurate target, compare the current admission policy of each IIM with your academic profile, category, work experience and other selection components.

CAT Selection Process: What Happens After the Exam?

CAT is the entrance test, not the complete IIM admission process. After CAT results, individual IIMs conduct their own shortlisting and selection processes according to their published admission policies.

  1. CAT score and percentile: Your CAT performance is used as a major screening/selection input.
  2. Shortlisting: Each IIM applies its own rules using CAT and other profile factors.
  3. Written assessment / PI: Depending on the institute and programme, candidates may face a personal interview and/or written assessment.
  4. Profile factors: Academic performance, work experience, diversity and other published components may contribute.
  5. Final merit: The institute combines the components using its own notified formula.
Important:

A high CAT percentile does not automatically guarantee an IIM call or final admission. Conversely, the minimum qualifying percentile is not necessarily the level needed to receive a shortlist.

How to Prepare for CAT 2026: A Practical Student Strategy

1. Start with a diagnostic mock

Take one full-length mock before building your timetable. Record section-wise accuracy, unanswered questions and time spent. Your preparation plan should be based on these gaps.

2. Build section-wise fundamentals

Strengthen arithmetic and algebra for QA, learn set-selection and data-reading approaches for DILR, and build reading stamina plus question-selection discipline for VARC.

3. Practise timed sets

CAT is not only a knowledge test. Practise under a 40-minute sectional limit so that you learn when to skip a difficult question or abandon an unproductive DILR set.

4. Analyse every mock

Maintain an error log covering conceptual mistakes, calculation errors, misreading, poor question selection and time-management problems. Re-solving without understanding the cause of an error gives limited improvement.

5. Use the final weeks for revision

Reduce new-topic learning as the exam approaches. Prioritise mocks, revision, error-log review, sectional tests and a stable exam-day strategy.

CAT 2026 FAQs: Questions Students Actually Search

When is the CAT 2026 exam?

CAT 2026 is scheduled for November 29, 2026. IIM Indore is conducting the exam in three sessions. Candidates should follow the exact session, reporting time and test-centre details printed on the admit card.

Is CAT 2026 registration still open?

No. The CAT 2026 registration window has closed. The latest update is the limited correction window scheduled for October 5, 2026, 10:00 AM to October 6, 2026, 5:00 PM for candidates who completed registration and paid the fee.

Can I apply for CAT 2026 now if I missed the registration deadline?

No. The correction facility is for already registered candidates; it is not a fresh registration window. Students who did not complete CAT 2026 registration will need to track the next CAT cycle.

What is the CAT 2026 correction window?

The correction window is scheduled from 10:00 AM on October 5 to 5:00 PM on October 6, 2026. IIM Indore has specified limited editable fields, including photograph, signature and test-city preferences.

Can I change my CAT 2026 category, name or academic marks during correction?

Candidates should not assume that these fields can be changed. The latest correction information states that the facility is limited to selected fields such as photograph, signature and test-city preferences; personal and academic details remain locked. Check the candidate dashboard and official notice for the exact fields available to you.

What is the CAT 2026 exam pattern?

CAT 2026 has three sections—VARC, DILR and QA—with 68 questions in two hours. Candidates receive 40 minutes for each section and cannot switch between sections during the section window.

How many questions are there in CAT 2026?

The CAT 2026 paper has 68 questions: 24 in VARC, 22 in DILR and 22 in QA, based on the latest reported examination structure.

Is there negative marking in CAT?

Yes. CAT awards 3 marks for a correct answer and deducts 1 mark for an incorrect MCQ. Incorrect TITA/non-MCQ responses do not attract negative marking.

What is the minimum percentage required for CAT eligibility?

For CAT 2026, the standard requirement is a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates, subject to the conditions in the official notification.

Can final-year students apply for CAT?

Yes. Final-year bachelor's degree students can apply subject to the conditions specified by CAT. If selected by an IIM, they must subsequently satisfy the institute's document and degree-completion requirements within the prescribed timeline.

What is a good CAT percentile for IIM admission?

There is no single “good” percentile that applies to every IIM or every student. IIMs publish different qualifying percentiles and selection policies, while actual shortlisting depends on the applicant pool and profile. Students should compare their target IIMs' current policies rather than rely on one generic cutoff number.

Does 99 percentile guarantee an IIM call?

No. A percentile by itself does not guarantee a shortlist. IIM admission policies can consider academics, work experience, diversity, written assessment, interview and other institute-specific factors in addition to CAT performance.

Is CAT compulsory for all MBA colleges in India?

No. CAT is required or accepted by the IIMs and many other B-schools, but other institutes use exams such as XAT, CMAT, MAT, NMAT, SNAP, GMAT or their own entrance tests. Always check the programme's current admission notice.

When will the CAT 2026 admit card be released?

The CAT 2026 admit card is scheduled to be available from November 4, 2026. Candidates should download it from the official CAT portal and check the assigned test centre, session and reporting instructions.

When will CAT 2026 results be declared?

The CAT 2026 result is scheduled for the first week of January 2027. The result/scorecard should be accessed through the official CAT portal using the candidate's login credentials.

Which IIM conducts CAT 2026?

IIM Indore is the conducting institute for CAT 2026.
