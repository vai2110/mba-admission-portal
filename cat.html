CAT 2026 Overview

The Common Admission Test (CAT) is a national-level computer-based management entrance examination conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management.

CAT scores are used for admission to various postgraduate, fellow and doctoral programmes of participating IIMs and are also accepted by a number of non-IIM institutions.

CAT Exam Pattern

CAT is conducted as a computer-based examination. The examination includes multiple sections designed to assess quantitative ability, verbal ability, reading comprehension and logical/data interpretation skills.

Section Area
VARC Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension
DILR Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning
QA Quantitative Ability
The exact CAT 2026 section structure, duration, question distribution and marking scheme should be updated from the official CAT 2026 notification.

CAT Syllabus

VARC

Reading comprehension, para-based questions, verbal ability and related language skills.

DILR

Data interpretation, logical reasoning, arrangements, tables, graphs, sets and analytical reasoning.

Quantitative Ability

Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, number systems, modern mathematics and related quantitative topics.

CAT Preparation Strategy

1. Build fundamentals

Start with basic concepts in arithmetic, algebra, reading comprehension and logical reasoning.

2. Practise regularly

Solve topic-wise questions and gradually move towards mixed sets and timed practice.

3. Take mock tests

Use mock tests to improve time management, question selection and exam strategy.

4. Analyse mistakes

Maintain a record of incorrect questions and revisit the concepts behind repeated mistakes.

CAT FAQs

What is CAT?

CAT stands for Common Admission Test and is a national-level management entrance examination conducted by the IIMs.

Is CAT compulsory for IIM admission?

CAT is the entrance examination used for the relevant IIM postgraduate and other programmes, subject to each programme's admission policy.

Can non-IIM colleges accept CAT scores?

Yes. The official CAT website publishes a list of non-IIM institutions that use CAT scores.

Where should I register for CAT?

Candidates should use the official CAT website for registration and official updates.
