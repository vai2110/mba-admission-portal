CAT 2026 Overview

The Common Admission Test (CAT) is a national-level computer-based management entrance examination conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management.

CAT scores are used for admission to postgraduate, fellow and doctoral programmes of participating IIMs and are also accepted by a number of non-IIM institutions.

Why is CAT important?

Students targeting IIMs and other CAT-accepting MBA programmes should understand the examination pattern, eligibility, registration process, cutoff requirements and institute-specific admission policies before applying.

CAT 2026 Eligibility

Candidates should meet the academic eligibility requirements specified in the official CAT notification for the relevant admission cycle.

  • Candidates should check the required qualifying examination and academic conditions specified in the official notification.
  • Final-year candidates should verify the conditions applicable to them in the official CAT notification.
  • Candidates belonging to reserved categories should check the applicable eligibility provisions and academic requirements.
  • Programme-specific admission requirements may differ across IIMs, so candidates should also check the admission policy of each institute.
Important:

Do not treat the eligibility requirements of one IIM as automatically applicable to every IIM programme. Check the relevant institute's current admission policy.

CAT Selection Process

CAT is the entrance examination, but admission to an IIM programme is determined through the individual institute's selection process. The exact criteria and weightages differ across IIMs and programmes.

  • CAT score / percentile
  • Academic profile
  • Work experience, where applicable
  • Personal interview and other institute-specific stages
  • Additional factors specified in the individual IIM admission policy
Important:

Do not assume that one IIM's selection criteria or weightage applies to another IIM. Check the official admission policy of the institute and programme you are targeting.

How to Prepare for CAT 2026

1. Build strong fundamentals

Start with basic concepts in arithmetic, algebra, reading comprehension and logical reasoning before moving to advanced questions.

2. Practise section-wise

Solve topic-wise questions and gradually move towards mixed sets, timed practice and full-length tests.

3. Take mock tests

Use mock tests to improve time management, question selection, accuracy and exam strategy.

4. Analyse every mock

Track incorrect answers, questions where you spent too much time, and concepts that repeatedly cause mistakes.

CAT 2026 FAQs

What is CAT?

CAT stands for Common Admission Test and is a national-level management entrance examination conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management.

Is CAT compulsory for IIM admission?

CAT is the entrance examination used for the relevant IIM postgraduate and other programmes, subject to each programme's admission policy.

Can non-IIM colleges accept CAT scores?

Yes. CAT scores are also accepted by a number of non-IIM management institutions, subject to their individual admission requirements.

Where should I register for CAT?

Candidates should use the official CAT website for registration and official updates.

Is the CAT 2026 exam pattern final?

The final CAT 2026 section structure, duration, question distribution and marking scheme should be checked against the official CAT 2026 notification.
