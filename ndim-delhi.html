NDIM Delhi

What is NDIM Delhi?

Quick answer

New Delhi Institute of Management (NDIM) is an AICTE-approved management institute in Tughlakabad Institutional Area, New Delhi. Its core offering is a two-year full-time PGDM, including general PGDM, PGDM Finance and PGDM Marketing routes. NDIM states that its PGDM was granted MBA equivalence by AIU and that its programmes are AICTE approved.

NDIM is primarily relevant to students looking for a Delhi-NCR management institute with a strong industry-interface model rather than a university campus offering a wide range of undergraduate disciplines. The institute also runs a Fellow Programme in Management (Business Management).

The official 2026 mandatory disclosure lists sanctioned intakes of 240 for PGDM, 180 for PGDM (F), 180 for PGDM (M) and 10 for FPM. These are sanctioned programme capacities, not placement counts.

NDIM Admission Dates: What is Current?

NDIM's website currently states that applications are invited for the PGDM 2027-29 batch, with admission counselling/GDPI conducted at the Delhi campus and online. The exact GDPI schedule can change, so students should use the institute's current admission notice before planning a deadline.

NDIM Admission: Eligibility, Entrance Exams & Selection

NDIM's published eligibility requires a bachelor's degree from a UGC/AIU-recognised university. For the 2026-28 batch, the institute stated 50% graduation marks for General-category candidates and 45% for reserved-category candidates. Final-year graduation students who met the stated timing condition could also apply.

NDIM Fees: What Will the PGDM Cost?

The institute's published fee page currently gives the 2026-28 PGDM course fee as ₹6.90 lakh for Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh for Year 2, or ₹13.75 lakh for the two-year course fee. This fee is stated to include all charges except optional hostel and international study tours.

NDIM Placements: What the Institute Reports

NDIM states that more than 250 companies have recruited in each of the last two years and describes its placement record as 100%. Its current admissions page also reports a ₹16.1 lakh highest domestic placement and ₹32.5 lakh highest international placement. These are institute-reported figures and should not be read as the typical salary received by every student.

NDIM Delhi FAQs

Is NDIM Delhi AICTE approved?

Yes. NDIM states that its PGDM programmes are approved by AICTE. Its official disclosure also records the sanctioned programme intakes.

Is NDIM PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

NDIM states that its PGDM was granted MBA equivalence by the Association of Indian Universities. Students should still check the latest official recognition wording for the specific admission cycle.

Which entrance exams does NDIM accept?

NDIM's published eligibility page lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT and state common entrance examinations for shortlisting.

What is the NDIM PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 fee is ₹6.90 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh in Year 2, totalling ₹13.75 lakh for the course fee. Hostel and international study tours are separate/optional.
