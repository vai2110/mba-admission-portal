NDIM Delhi

What is NDIM Delhi?

Quick answer

New Delhi Institute of Management (NDIM) is an AICTE-approved management institute in Tughlakabad Institutional Area, New Delhi. Its main management offering is a two-year full-time PGDM, with PGDM, PGDM Finance and PGDM Marketing routes. NDIM states that its PGDM has been granted MBA equivalence by AIU.

The institute is particularly relevant for students seeking a Delhi-NCR management programme with industry interaction. Its 2026 mandatory disclosure lists sanctioned intakes of 240 for PGDM, 180 for PGDM Finance, 180 for PGDM Marketing and 10 for FPM. These are approved capacities, not placement figures.

NDIM Admission Process: Eligibility, Exams, GDPI & Selection

In short:

NDIM's published process combines an accepted management entrance score with academic profile assessment and Group Discussion + Personal Interview. There is no need to treat an unofficial coaching-site cutoff as an official NDIM cutoff.

1. Check eligibility

Applicants need a bachelor's degree from a recognised/UGC or AIU-recognised university. For the 2026-28 cycle, NDIM published a minimum of 50% graduation marks for General-category candidates and 45% for reserved-category candidates. Final-year students can apply subject to the cycle's result/deadline conditions.

2. Submit the application

Complete the NDIM online application and submit the published ₹1,000 non-refundable application fee. Keep academic records and entrance-score details ready.

3. Use an accepted entrance exam score

NDIM lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT and state common entrance examinations among the scores accepted for shortlisting.

4. Attend GDPI

Shortlisted applicants go through the institute's Group Discussion and Personal Interview process. NDIM currently states that this may be conducted at its Delhi campus or online.

5. Understand final selection

Selection is not based only on the entrance score. NDIM states that it considers Class 10, Class 12, graduation, entrance performance, GD/PI, work experience, achievements, leadership, teamwork, innovation, decision-making and social/community contributions.

Cutoff caution:

NDIM's current eligibility page does not present one universal fixed percentile cutoff. An older mandatory disclosure contains cycle-specific qualifying figures, so those numbers should not be presented as a guaranteed current cutoff.

NDIM Fees 2026-28: PGDM Fee, Hostel & Scholarships

NDIM's published fee page lists a ₹13.75 lakh two-year PGDM course fee: ₹6.90 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh in Year 2. Optional hostel and international study tours are separate.

NDIM Delhi FAQs

Is NDIM Delhi AICTE approved?

Yes. NDIM states that its PGDM programmes are AICTE approved.

What is the NDIM admission process?

Check eligibility, apply online, submit an accepted entrance score, attend GDPI if shortlisted, and complete the final selection process based on academics, entrance performance, GD/PI and profile factors.

Which exams does NDIM accept?

NDIM lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT and state common entrance examinations.

What is the NDIM PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 course fee is ₹13.75 lakh: ₹6.90 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh in Year 2. Hostel and international study tours are separate.

What is the latest NDIM placement salary?

The latest NIRF series reviewed reports a ₹10.12 LPA median salary for the 2024-25 minimum-time graduating cohort, with 500 of 500 students placed.
