NMIMS MBA Overview

Quick answer

The SBM Mumbai MBA is a flagship full-time management programme using NMAT by GMAC as the entrance route. The programme uses case-based learning supported by lectures, group activities, discussions and simulations.

SBM NMIMS describes its pedagogy as participant-centred and case-based, with cases from national and international sources. The school also highlights industry interaction and a large alumni network.

NMIMS MBA Eligibility

Applicants must follow the eligibility stated in the current MBA admission handout. The school requires candidates to complete the relevant NMAT and NMIMS registration process and satisfy the programme's academic eligibility before applying.

Do not use old eligibility pages blindly

NMIMS updates programme handouts and admission information by cycle. The current 2026 handout is the final authority for the exact academic percentage, category and other eligibility rules.

NMIMS MBA Admission Process

STEP 1

NMAT by GMAC

STEP 2

STEP 3

Selection

The school communicates the applicable shortlisting and WAT/competency/PI requirements for the admission cycle.

NMIMS MBA Curriculum & Pedagogy

The programme uses participant-centred, case-based learning supplemented by lectures, problem solving, group activities, discussions and simulations. The approach is intended to connect management frameworks with real business situations.

Case method

Cases are used to place students in decision-making situations and develop analytical and managerial judgement.

Industry relevance

SBM combines academic learning with corporate interaction and practical exposure to business contexts.

NMIMS MBA Fees

Fee amounts can change by admission cycle and campus. The portal therefore does not carry an old fee as a current 2026-27 payable amount unless the current official handout confirms it.

Applicants should verify the programme fee, refundable deposits, hostel/residential charges where applicable and payment schedule from the current NMIMS offer/admission documentation.
