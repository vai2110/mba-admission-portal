NMIMS MBA Overview

Quick answer

The SBM Mumbai MBA is a flagship full-time management programme using NMAT by GMAC as the entrance route. The programme uses case-based learning supported by lectures, group activities, discussions and simulations.

SBM NMIMS describes its pedagogy as participant-centred and case-based, with cases from national and international sources. The school also highlights industry interaction and a large alumni network.

NMIMS MBA Specialisations

Official information regarding specific MBA specialisation tracks for the current academic cycle is unavailable on the public domain. Candidates are advised to refer to the official SBM Mumbai programme brochure for the latest list of electives and major/minor specialisation options.

NMIMS MBA Eligibility

Applicants must follow the eligibility stated in the current MBA admission handout. The school requires candidates to complete the relevant NMAT and NMIMS registration process and satisfy the programme's academic eligibility before applying.

Do not use old eligibility pages blindly

NMIMS updates programme handouts and admission information by cycle. The current 2026 handout is the final authority for the exact academic percentage, category and other eligibility rules.

The programme uses participant-centred, case-based learning supplemented by lectures, problem solving, group activities, discussions and simulations.

Industry relevance

Is NMIMS MBA the right fit?

The SBM Mumbai MBA is designed for candidates seeking a high-intensity, case-driven management education in a metropolitan corporate hub. It is best suited for those who thrive in competitive, industry-integrated environments and are prepared for the NMAT-based selection process.

NMIMS MBA FAQs

Which exam is required for NMIMS MBA?

NMAT by GMAC is the entrance route stated for the applicable SBM MBA programmes.

What is the latest MBA Core average package?

SBM Mumbai reports ₹24.6 LPA average CTC for 2025-26.

Does NMIMS publish one fixed cutoff?

No universal cutoff should be assumed. Shortlisting is programme- and cycle-specific, so use the current official admission information.
