Overview

SVKM's NMIMS is a Deemed to be University offering a range of management programmes. With a legacy of 44 years, the university operates across multiple campuses including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Indore, Navi Mumbai, Shirpur, and Chandigarh.

Admission Process

Admissions at NMIMS are merit-based. The primary entrance test for management programmes is the NMAT by GMAC. The selection process typically includes a Group Discussion (GD) and a Personal Interview (PI).

Quick Answer: How do I get into an MBA at NMIMS? Admission is merit-based, requiring a valid NMAT by GMAC score followed by participation in the university's Group Discussion and Personal Interview rounds.

Programmes

NMIMS offers several management-focused degrees, including:

  • MBA
  • MBA Tech (Integrated Engineering & Management)
  • MBA Pharma Tech (Integrated Pharmacy & Management)
