What MBA programmes does BHU offer?

BHU's current admission portal lists MBA and MBA-IB routes, while the 2026 admission notices show GDPI schedules for MBA Financial Management, MBA Foreign Trade and MBA Risk and Insurance. BHU therefore has multiple management programmes rather than one single MBA admission route.

What is the BHU MBA eligibility?

The official CUET-PG eligibility material states that a candidate needs a bachelor's degree in any discipline or equivalent with a minimum 50% aggregate for MBA.

How does BHU MBA admission work?

  1. Complete the required CUET-PG examination/application route.
  2. Register through the BHU PG admission/CAP process when notified.
  3. Submit programme preferences and documents.
  4. For programmes with GDPI, attend the notified group discussion/personal interview process.
  5. Track allotment/selection and complete fee payment.

The current BHU portal shows separate GDPI schedules for several MBA specialisations, so students should not assume every MBA variant has identical selection steps.

What is the BHU MBA cutoff?

BHU does not publish one universal MBA percentile cutoff in the sources used here. The applicable score depends on the programme, category and admission process. The official PG cutoff portal should be checked for the relevant round.

Because MBA programmes can include GDPI, a cutoff should be read as an eligibility/shortlisting reference rather than a guarantee of admission.

What is the BHU MBA fee?

MBA fee varies by programme and admission category. The official PG bulletin provides programme-level fee figures; students should use the current MBA-specific fee notice rather than a generic BHU PG fee.

What are BHU MBA placements like?

BHU does not publish one consolidated university-wide MBA average package in the official sources used here. The 2024-25 Annual Report provides unit-level placement evidence: 156 postgraduate students appeared for campus recruitment, 151 were placed, packages ranged from ₹6 LPA to ₹19.8 LPA, and 57 organisations participated.

Recruiters listed include Infosys, Wipro, EY, ICICI Bank, Federal Bank, SBI Life, Adani Wilmar, VECV and DCM Shriram. These figures are unit-level, not an MBA-wide average.

BHU MBA FAQs

Is BHU MBA through CUET-PG?

The official eligibility material lists CUET-PG paper COQP12 for MBA, and BHU's admission portal runs a separate MBA/PG admission route.

What is the minimum MBA eligibility at BHU?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate is listed in the official eligibility material.

Does BHU MBA have GDPI?

Yes for the MBA variants for which BHU publishes GDPI schedules; the 2026 portal shows GDPI schedules for Financial Management, Foreign Trade and Risk and Insurance.
