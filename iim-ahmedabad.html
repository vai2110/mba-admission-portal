IIM Ahmedabad MBA Overview

IIM Ahmedabad offers management education and postgraduate programmes through its academic programmes. Its flagship two-year postgraduate management programme is commonly referred to as the MBA/PGP.

Admission to the flagship programme involves the Common Admission Test (CAT) followed by the institute's selection process.

Important: Admission criteria, fees, cutoff requirements and selection policies can change between admission cycles. Students should verify the latest information from the institute's official admission documents before applying.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA FAQs

Which entrance exam is accepted for IIM Ahmedabad MBA admission?
CAT is used for admission to the flagship MBA/PGP programme, subject to the institute's applicable admission policy.
How long is the IIM Ahmedabad MBA programme?
The flagship MBA/PGP is a two-year full-time postgraduate management programme.
Where is IIM Ahmedabad located?
IIM Ahmedabad is located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
Does the CAT qualifying requirement guarantee admission?
No. Meeting a qualifying requirement does not by itself guarantee admission. The institute applies its complete selection criteria.
