IIM Ahmedabad MBA Overview

IIM Ahmedabad (IIMA) is a management education institute located in Vastrapur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and was founded in 1961. The institute describes its two-year full-time Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) as its flagship MBA programme. The programme is residential and accepts graduates from different academic backgrounds. The PGP awards the Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree.

For students comparing MBA colleges, the two numbers that should be separated are the qualifying CAT cutoff and the actual competitiveness of the profile. For the 2027–29 PGP, the minimum CAT 2026 cutoff is 95 overall with 85 in each section for General/EWS candidates, but IIMA also uses Class 10, Class 12, undergraduate marks, work experience, gender-diversity points and CAT performance in its shortlisting composite. Meeting the minimum percentile therefore does not itself create an admission guarantee.

IIMA's placement reporting also needs careful reading. The institute reports Maximum Earning Potential (MEP) rather than conventional CTC, and the 2025 audited report recorded a mean MEP of ₹35.50 lakh and median MEP of ₹34.59 lakh. The 2026 final-placement media release confirms the completion of the process with all students placed across 26 cohorts, but says the audited salary report would be published separately.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA Admission Process 2027–29

The IIM Ahmedabad PGP admission process for domestic applicants is not a simple CAT-score admission. IIMA's 2027–29 policy uses CAT 2026, academic profile, work experience, gender-diversity points, a composite shortlisting score, Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview.

  1. Check bachelor's eligibility: You need a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is relaxed to 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates under the 2027–29 policy.
  2. Check final-year eligibility: Final-year bachelor's students can apply. If selected provisionally, they must complete the degree requirements by June 30, 2027 and submit the required marksheet/certificate within the deadlines specified by IIMA.
  3. Meet the age condition: For PGP 2027–29, selected candidates must be at least 19 years old by June 30, 2027.
  4. Register for CAT 2026: Domestic applicants apply through CAT 2026 and must select IIM Ahmedabad's PGP in the CAT application where required.
  5. Enter accurate academic details: IIMA uses Class 10, Class 12 and bachelor's marks in the application rating. Enter marks exactly as required and keep marksheets ready.
  6. Enter work-experience details correctly: The 2027–29 application rating gives points for full-time work experience. Less than 12 months receives zero work-experience points; 12–36 months is scored progressively; more than 36 months receives the maximum work-experience rating of 5 points.
  7. Clear the CAT 2026 minimum cutoff: For General/EWS candidates, the minimum is 95 overall and 85 in each section. NC-OBC requires 90 overall/80 sectional; SC 85/75; ST 75/65. PwD candidates have separate lower thresholds in the policy.
  8. Understand that cutoff is only a screening threshold: IIMA calculates a normalised application rating and combines it with CAT performance. For 2027–29, the raw composite uses 35% normalised application rating and 65% CAT total percentile before discipline-level normalisation.
  9. Wait for the AWT & PI shortlist: IIMA states that the shortlist will be available tentatively in the second week of January 2027.
  10. Attend AWT and PI: Shortlisted candidates take the Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview. IIMA lists Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and New Delhi as interview locations for the 2027–29 process.
  11. Final selection: The final composite score for candidates appearing in AWT & PI is calculated using 50% PI, 10% AWT and 40% normalised composite score.
  12. Check the admission offer: IIMA states that final admission status will be available tentatively in the second week of April 2027.
  13. Accept the offer: Successful candidates must complete acceptance formalities tentatively by the first week of May 2027.
  14. Pay the applicable fee: Once the offer is accepted, follow IIMA's fee-payment schedule for the relevant batch. Do not use the 2025–27 fee as the final 2027–29 payable amount.

For the 2027–29 PGP, IIMA states that reservations follow Government of India requirements: 27% NC-OBC, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 5% PwD and up to 10% EWS. How to read IIM Ahmedabad's placement numbers

  • Mean salary is not the same as CTC: IIMA reports Maximum Earning Potential under its IPRS methodology.
  • Median is useful for comparison: it is less affected by a small number of very high offers than the mean.
  • Highest package is not a typical salary: it represents the top end of the reported offers and should not be used to estimate the salary of an average graduate.
  • Placement percentage needs context: students who do not participate or opt out of the institute placement process can affect the denominator.

IIM Ahmedabad Campus and Facilities

Hostel and student housing

IIMA is a residential programme environment. The institute's campus information states that 27 student dormitories accommodate 740 students, along with 120 flats for married students. The campus page does not publish a current standalone PGP hostel fee in the material reviewed for this page.

The Vikram Sarabhai Library is open 24 hours and provides print and digital resources. IIMA's library information states that its digital collection includes 115+ databases, 28,220+ e-journals and more than 2 lakh e-books. The campus also has classrooms, academic buildings, computing infrastructure and collaborative spaces.

Sports, health and student services

The campus provides outdoor facilities including tennis, volleyball, basketball, football, cricket, jogging and cycling tracks, along with indoor sports such as swimming, squash and badminton. IIMA also has on-campus residential doctors and a dispensary for routine healthcare and referrals. Banking, ATMs, pharmacy, stores, courier and other utility services are available on campus.

Location and connectivity

IIMA is located in Vastrapur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Ahmedabad is a major urban and commercial centre, giving students access to city transport, healthcare, accommodation for visitors and a broader business ecosystem. Exact travel time from the airport or railway station varies with traffic, so students should use live maps when planning an interview or campus visit.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA FAQs: Student-Intent Questions

What is the fee structure of IIM Ahmedabad?

The latest officially published domestic fee for the two-year PGP is ₹27.50 lakh for the 2025–27 batch, including ₹20.10 lakh in tuition. IIMA has not yet published the final PGP 2027–29 fee in the information reviewed for this page. The published fee also does not cover several personal expenses, including a laptop/personal computer, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry. Therefore, a student should treat ₹27.50 lakh as the latest official fee benchmark, not the confirmed 2027–29 all-in cost.

Is IIM Ahmedabad good for placements?

The most recent audited PGP placement report available from IIMA is for 2025. It reported a mean Maximum Earning Potential of ₹35.50 lakh and a median of ₹34.59 lakh. The 2026 final-placement release states that all students were placed across 26 cohorts, with recruiters spanning consulting, finance, technology, consumer businesses, healthcare and other sectors. The 2026 audited salary figures should be added only after IIMA publishes the detailed IPRS report.

What is the NIRF ranking of IIM Ahmedabad?

IIM Ahmedabad was ranked #1 in the NIRF Management category in 2025. IIMA also held the #1 position in 2024 and 2023. NIRF rank measures the institute at the management-category level, so it should not be interpreted as a programme-specific placement or salary ranking.

How to get admission in IIM Ahmedabad?

For the domestic PGP 2027–29, you need a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates under the published policy, and you must take CAT 2026. IIMA then applies its application-rating and CAT-based shortlisting process. Shortlisted candidates attend the Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview, after which IIMA calculates a final composite score using 50% PI, 10% AWT and 40% normalised composite score. A qualifying CAT cutoff alone does not guarantee a PI call.

Does IIM Ahmedabad offer scholarships?

Yes. IIMA offers need-based financial assistance as well as industry, donor and government-linked scholarships. The need-based process considers factors such as family income, assets and number of dependants. Published examples include the Mirae Asset Foundation Scholarship, which provides ₹5 lakh each to three PGP/PGP-FABM students under the stated scheme, the Aditya Birla Group Scholarship of ₹3 lakh per year under its published rules, and the OP Jindal Engineering and Management Scholarship of ₹1.5 lakh. Availability and selection conditions can change, so students should check the scholarship rules for their admission year.

What courses are offered at IIM Ahmedabad?

IIMA's current programme portfolio includes the two-year MBA/PGP, MBA-FABM, MBA-PGPX, MBA-BPGP, a blended MBA in Business Analytics & AI, ePGD-ABA and doctoral programmes, along with executive education. For a graduate seeking a standard two-year MBA through CAT, the flagship PGP is the primary programme to evaluate. Other programmes have different eligibility, work-experience, entrance and fee rules.

What CAT percentile is required for IIM Ahmedabad MBA admission 2027?

For the 2027–29 PGP, IIMA's minimum CAT 2026 cutoff is 95 overall and 85 in each section for General/EWS candidates. NC-OBC requires 90 overall and 80 sectional, SC requires 85 overall and 75 sectional, and ST requires 75 overall and 65 sectional. PwD candidates have separate lower thresholds. These are minimum screening cutoffs, not guaranteed PI-call or admission percentiles.

Does work experience matter for IIM Ahmedabad MBA admission?

Yes, but it is not a mandatory eligibility condition for the two-year PGP. Under the 2027–29 admission policy, full-time work experience contributes to the application rating used in shortlisting. The policy awards zero work-experience points below 12 months, progressively increasing points from 12 to 36 months, and the maximum work-experience rating of 5 points above 36 months.

Can final-year students apply for IIM Ahmedabad MBA?

Yes. The 2027–29 policy allows candidates appearing for their final-year bachelor's examination or awaiting results to apply. If selected, they may join provisionally only after meeting IIMA's documentation conditions, including completion of degree requirements by June 30, 2027. The final marksheet and degree certificate must then be submitted within the specified deadline.

Does IIM Ahmedabad have a separate application after CAT?

For the domestic PGP, candidates apply through the CAT process and indicate the relevant IIMA programme. IIMA then conducts its own independent shortlisting and selection process. Shortlisted candidates receive instructions for AWT and PI; therefore, students should monitor IIMA's website and their registered contact details after CAT results.

Is IIM Ahmedabad MBA worth the ₹27.50 lakh fee?

The answer depends on the student's own financial position, expected career path and opportunity cost. The latest published 2025 PGP fee benchmark is ₹27.50 lakh, while the 2025 audited mean and median MEP were ₹35.50 lakh and ₹34.59 lakh respectively. Those placement figures are not guarantees for an individual student, and the 2027–29 fee has not yet been published, so students should compare the full cost of attendance, financing terms, likely career outcomes and alternative MBA programmes before deciding.
