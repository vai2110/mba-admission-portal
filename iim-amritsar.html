IIM Amritsar Overview

IIM Amritsar is the 15th Indian Institute of Management and was established in 2015 under the aegis of the Ministry of Education. The institute offers management programmes from its campus in Amritsar, Punjab. Its flagship full-time MBA is a two-year residential programme, while the institute also offers specialised MBA programmes and an Executive MBA.

For the MBA 2026-28 cycle, IIM Amritsar used a two-stage process: a pre-PI shortlist followed by Personal Interview. The institute explicitly states that selection criteria and weights are decided annually, so the 2026-28 criteria should not be treated as a guaranteed template for 2027-29. citeturn0search0

IIM Amritsar Admission & Selection Process

The flagship MBA admission process begins with CAT and proceeds through institute-level shortlisting and Personal Interview. For the 2026-28 batch, the first shortlist required minimum section-wise and overall CAT percentiles, after which a composite pre-PI score was calculated using CAT, Class 10, Class 12, graduation, gender diversity and work experience. The final aggregate score used 70% pre-PI score and 30% post-PI score. citeturn0search0

Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy the CAT eligibility requirements applicable to the admission cycle. Final-year candidates can also be considered subject to the institute's stated degree-completion and minimum-mark conditions. Always check the admission document for the relevant CAT year because IIM Amritsar states that criteria can change annually. citeturn0search0

Selection Process

  1. Appear for CAT.
  2. Meet the applicable section-wise and overall minimum percentile requirements.
  3. Compete for the pre-PI shortlist using the institute's composite criteria.
  4. Attend the Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  5. Final offers are made using the applicable aggregate selection score.

IIM Amritsar CAT Cutoff

IIM Amritsar publishes minimum percentile requirements for its first shortlist rather than a single guaranteed final admission cutoff. For the MBA 2026-28 cycle, the General-category minimums were 80 percentile in QA, 75 in DILR, 80 in VARC and 90 overall. EWS also required 90 overall, while the published minimums were lower for NC-OBC, SC, ST and PwD categories. citeturn0search0

These are minimum eligibility/shortlisting thresholds, not a promise of admission. The institute ranks candidates using multiple factors, so a candidate should not interpret the published overall percentile as the likely final admission percentile.

What actually matters beyond CAT?

The 2026-28 process gave substantial weight to CAT but also considered Class 10 and 12 performance, graduation, work experience and diversity factors before the PI. The PI then carried 70% of the post-PI score, with work experience, gender diversity and academic diversity also contributing. citeturn0search0

IIM Amritsar Fees & Scholarships

IIM Amritsar's official fee page currently provides fee structures for the 2024-26, 2025-27 and 2026-28 MBA batches. The institute also states that educational loans are available and describes merit-based and need-based financial assistance. citeturn0search1

The detailed 2026-28 fee document should be treated as the current reference for students applying in that cycle. For 2027-29, the final fee should be confirmed from the institute when the new batch fee structure is released rather than carrying forward an old amount.

Scholarships

IIM Amritsar states that scholarships include awards based on scholastic performance, including tuition-fee-equivalent awards for eligible top performers, along with a need-based scholarship buffer and external scholarship guidance. citeturn0search1

IIM Amritsar Placements

IIM Amritsar's latest published final-placement update for the MBA 2024-26 batch reports a highest CTC of ₹43 LPA. The institute reports an average CTC of ₹18.60 LPA for the top 50 students, with a median of ₹18 LPA, and an average of ₹16.30 LPA for the top 50% with a median of ₹15.25 LPA. For the MBA 2026-28 cycle, the published General-category minimum was 90 overall with section-wise minimums of 80 QA, 75 DILR and 80 VARC. These are minimum shortlisting thresholds, not guaranteed final admission cutoffs. citeturn0search0
