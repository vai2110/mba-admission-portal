What is IIM Amritsar?

IIM Amritsar is the 15th IIM established under the Ministry of Education. Founded in 2015, it has grown from a flagship MBA institute into a multi-programme management school offering MBA, MBA Business Analytics, MBA-HR, IPM, the joint MSDSM with IIT Ropar and an Executive MBA.

The flagship MBA is a full-time two-year residential programme. The institute says each academic year normally has three terms and includes a summer internship. Official MBA overview.

IIM Amritsar Admission 2027: How will selection work?

The latest published flagship MBA policy uses two stages: a pre-PI shortlist and a Personal Interview. For 2026-28, IIM Amritsar used CAT, Class 10, Class 12, graduation, gender diversity and work experience in the pre-PI composite. The final aggregate was 70% pre-PI + 30% post-PI. The institute explicitly says admission criteria are decided annually, so students should not assume the 2026-28 formula will remain identical for 2027-29.

What is the IIM Amritsar CAT cutoff?

For the latest published MBA 2026-28 policy, the General-category minimum for the first shortlist was 90 overall, with 80 in QA, 75 in DILR and 80 in VARC. EWS also required 90 overall; NC-OBC required 80; SC 55; ST 40; and PwD 40.

How much does IIM Amritsar cost?

The official 2026-28 fee notice dated 10 April 2026 covers MBA, MBA-BA and MBA-HR together. It lists ₹9.75 lakh programme fee in each academic year, or ₹19.50 lakh over two years. The document separately lists a ₹50,000 caution deposit and ₹75,000 mess deposit in each academic year; the caution deposit is refundable subject to dues and actual mess expenses are adjusted against the mess deposit.

What do IIM Amritsar placements actually look like?

The latest official final-placement update for MBA 2024-26 reports a ₹43 LPA highest CTC. It reports ₹18.60 LPA average and ₹18 LPA median for the top 50 students, and ₹16.30 LPA average with ₹15.25 LPA median for the top 50% of the batch. The graduating cohort comprised 256 students.

The reporting format matters: the current official update does not state an overall-batch average in the same way. So this page does not misleadingly call ₹18.60 LPA the “average package of IIM Amritsar”.

IIM Amritsar FAQs

Is 90 percentile enough for IIM Amritsar?

90 overall was the published General-category minimum for the 2026-28 first shortlist. It is not a guaranteed PI call or admission score.

What is IIM Amritsar MBA fees?

The latest official 2026-28 notice lists ₹19.50 lakh as the two-year programme fee. The document shows ₹21.50 lakh total payment including listed caution and mess deposits, with the caution deposit refundable subject to dues.

What is the latest IIM Amritsar highest package?

₹43 LPA is the highest CTC reported by the institute for the MBA 2024-26 batch.

Does IIM Amritsar have MBA Business Analytics and MBA HR?

Yes. Both have dedicated programme pages, admission criteria and fee structures.
