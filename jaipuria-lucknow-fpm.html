Jaipuria Lucknow FPM: Overview

The Fellow Programme in Management is Jaipuria's doctoral-level management programme. It is designed for candidates seeking research-intensive careers in academia, management research and research-oriented professional roles.

The current FPM page lists a four-year programme, weekend classes, diverse management specialisations and full scholarships/fee waivers for eligible full-time scholars subject to the institute's terms.

Who is FPM for?

FPM is fundamentally different from a two-year PGDM. It is appropriate for candidates who want to build research capability, pursue doctoral work and potentially move into academic, research or high-end analytical careers.

Jaipuria Lucknow FPM Eligibility 2026

The institute states that candidates should hold a postgraduate degree with first-class standing in fields such as Engineering, Technology, Management, Economics, Social Science or Commerce.

Final-year students may apply provisionally, subject to completion of the qualifying degree and submission of proof within the institute's stated timeline.

What kind of candidate fits FPM?

  • Postgraduates interested in management research.
  • Professionals considering a transition into research or teaching.
  • Candidates interested in specialised management domains.
  • Students willing to commit several years to doctoral-level study.

Strategy, competitiveness, innovation, transformation and organisational-level research.

Research Support & Academic Resources

Jaipuria's FPM offering highlights access to academic databases, statistical tools, financial software and research resources. The programme also provides opportunities for conference participation and research exposure.

Research support highlighted by the institute

  • Access to academic and financial databases.
  • Statistical and data-analysis tools.
  • Conference participation support linked to research output.
  • Research guidance and thesis supervision.
  • Interdisciplinary research opportunities.
  • Access to management research resources.

Career Scope After FPM

An FPM is primarily designed for research-oriented career paths rather than conventional MBA placement outcomes.

Academia

Faculty, teaching and academic research positions in management institutions, subject to eligibility and recruitment requirements.

Research

Research roles in think tanks, consulting, policy, corporate research and specialised research organisations.

Industry Research

Research-heavy roles in analytics, strategy, consumer insights, finance and business intelligence.

Jaipuria Lucknow FPM FAQs

What is the duration of the FPM?

The institute's current FPM page states a four-year programme.

What is the FPM fee?

The published 2026-30 Lucknow fee structure lists ₹4.3525 lakh in programme fees plus a ₹10,000 admission fee, with separate financial-assistance provisions.

Is scholarship available?

Yes. The institute advertises 100% fee-waiver and scholarship opportunities for eligible full-time scholars, subject to terms.

What stipend is offered?

The current programme page advertises a stipend of up to ₹45,000 per month, subject to conditions.

Is FPM the same as PGDM?

No. PGDM is a two-year management programme aimed primarily at managerial careers, while FPM is a doctoral-level research programme.
