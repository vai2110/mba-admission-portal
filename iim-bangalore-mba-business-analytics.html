IIM Bangalore MBA Business Analytics: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) is a two-year, full-time residential programme leading to a Master of Business Administration in Business Analytics. IIMB describes it as an MBA built to develop leaders who can use analytics and artificial intelligence to solve complex management problems.

The programme runs across six trimesters. The first year emphasises intensive coursework in analytical and mathematical skills followed by an eight-week summer internship. In the second year, students can choose electives in business analytics across management domains and may opt for exchange opportunities at select international business schools.

Who Is Eligible for IIM Bangalore PGPBA?

The programme is a bachelor's-degree-entry MBA. IIMB's admission information specifies the academic and CAT requirements for domestic applicants. Candidates should check the live admission document for the applicable category-wise eligibility and documentation before applying.

2026 batch context

The current PGPBA programme page reports 77 students enrolled in 2026, including students from engineering, management, commerce, science and other academic backgrounds.

What Is the IIM Bangalore PGPBA Admission Process?

1. Take CAT

Domestic PGPBA admission uses CAT. The CAT score is considered along with the institute's broader selection process.

2. Submit the IIMB application

Applicants complete the IIMB application and provide the required academic and supporting information.

3. Shortlisting and interview

IIMB evaluates applicants for the next stage and conducts interviews according to the published admission process.

4. Final selection

The final decision is based on the institute's prescribed selection methodology. Meeting a minimum requirement alone does not guarantee admission.

What Will You Study in IIM Bangalore PGPBA?

The programme is designed around management education with a stronger analytical and mathematical emphasis. IIMB says students develop an analytical mindset and learn current methodologies in analytics so that they can apply them across functional areas of management.

Academic focus

  • Analytical and mathematical skills
  • Business analytics across management functions
  • Artificial intelligence and emerging methodologies
  • Summer internship and experiential learning
  • Second-year analytics electives

Student decision point

PGPBA is not simply a technical analytics degree. It leads to an MBA and is designed around applying analytics to business and management decisions.

IIM Bangalore PGPBA FAQs

Is PGPBA an MBA?

Yes. It leads to the degree of Master of Business Administration in Business Analytics.

What is the PGPBA duration?

Two years, full-time and residential.

What is the PGPBA fee?

The published 2026-28 fee is ₹26 lakh for two years, plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit and ₹30,000 mess advance per term.
