Student FAQs

What percentile do I need for a good MBA college?

It depends on the college, exam, programme and admission criteria. Use your percentile above to see the colleges whose listed cutoff threshold you meet.

Is 90 percentile enough for an MBA?

90 percentile can make you eligible to explore several MBA colleges, but the options vary by entrance exam and institute. Check the cutoff results for your specific exam.

What is the difference between cutoff and final admission?

A cutoff is a threshold used to shortlist or consider applicants. Meeting it does not guarantee admission because institutes may use other selection criteria as well.

Do MBA colleges consider sectional cutoffs too?

Many institutes can have sectional as well as overall requirements. This page focuses on overall percentile, so check the college's current admission criteria before applying.

Are MBA cutoff percentages the same for CAT, XAT, SNAP and CMAT?

No. Each entrance exam has its own score or percentile system and each institute can set different thresholds. Select your exam above to check the relevant results.

Can the cutoff change from one year to another?

Yes. Cutoffs can vary by admission cycle, programme, applicant pool and institute policy. Always verify the latest admission information before applying.
