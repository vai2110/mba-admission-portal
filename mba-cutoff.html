How to read these results

01 · Your percentile meets the listed threshold

If your percentile is equal to or higher than the stored threshold, that college appears in the result set.

02 · Official vs expected is shown

Each card labels whether the threshold is an official institute cutoff, an expected/indicative figure, or a score-converted threshold.

03 · Always verify before applying

Cutoffs can change by programme, category, year and applicant pool. Open the college page and source before making a shortlist.

Research methodology

The dataset prioritises institute admission policies and cutoff announcements. Where institutes do not publish a fixed percentile, the tool uses a clearly labelled expected/indicative threshold from current 2026 cutoff research. We do not present those estimates as official cutoffs.

Important before you shortlist

75 percentile is the floor of this tool, not a promise. A result means only that your entered percentile meets the stored overall threshold. It does not mean you will receive a call or convert the programme. Check the college's current admission notice, sectional requirements, eligibility, academics, work-experience rules and selection process before applying.

Student FAQs

Can I use this tool below 75 percentile?

No. This version intentionally starts at 75 because the goal is to compare 75+ cutoff opportunities rather than create a broad low-cutoff directory.

Why does the same college appear more than once?

A college can have different cutoffs for different programmes, so programme-specific rows are kept separate where the source provides them.

Why are some cutoffs marked expected?

Many institutes do not publish a fixed percentile before the admission cycle. Those rows are shown as expected/indicative rather than being presented as official.

Why is NMAT different?

NMAT institutes frequently publish score thresholds. The tool converts those scores to the current GMAC percentile-ranking reference so you can still use one percentile filter across exams.

Editorial rule: We will not turn an estimate into an “official cutoff”. Every result carries its research status and a source link.
