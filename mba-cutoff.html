Why the result is only a starting point

01 · Minimum ≠ guaranteed call

Meeting a published minimum may only make you eligible for the next stage. Actual shortlist thresholds can be higher.

02 · Sectionals can matter

Some institutes require minimum VARC, DILR, QA or XAT sectional percentiles in addition to the overall score.

03 · Profile matters

Academic record, work experience, gender or academic diversity and interview performance can influence selection.

Cutoff data used on this page

We prioritise institute-published admission policies and cutoff announcements. Where a fixed threshold is not available, the exam is kept out of the college-match results rather than using an invented number.

Student FAQs

Can I get a college at exactly its cutoff?

Not necessarily. A minimum cutoff is generally a screening threshold, not a guaranteed interview or admission score.

Should I use overall or sectional percentile?

Start with overall percentile here, then check the college page for sectional requirements before applying.

What if my percentile is below the cutoff?

Use the exam and location hubs to find alternative colleges and routes instead of relying on one shortlist.

Editorial rule: We will not show a college as matching a percentile unless the underlying threshold is supported by a published admission/cutoff source. This keeps the filter useful without turning it into a misleading “college predictor”.
