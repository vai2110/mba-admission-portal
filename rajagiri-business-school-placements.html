Rajagiri Business School Placements: latest outcome

Quick answer

The latest NIRF 2026 data for the 2024-25 graduating cohort reports 201 students placed out of 215 graduates, with a ₹7.50 LPA median salary and 8 students selecting higher studies.

RBS's separate 2023-25 placement report uses a different denominator: 272 students opted for placements and 249 were placed. It also reports 170 recruiters, ₹16.13 LPA for the top 10 placements, ₹11.26 LPA for the top 50, ₹9.28 LPA for the top 100 and 21 international placements.

Student takeaway: For comparing RBS with another B-school, use the same reporting basis. The NIRF figures are cohort-level outcomes; the 2023-25 placement report is based on students who opted for placements.

What changed over these years?

  • Median salary increased from ₹5.50 LPA in 2021-22 to ₹7.50 LPA in 2024-25.
  • The reported placed-student count increased from 87 to 201 across these cohorts, although cohort size and reporting conditions changed.
  • The 2024-25 median is higher than the 2023-24 median by ₹0.50 lakh.
  • The 2022-23 cohort shows a lower median of ₹4.50 LPA, so students should look at the multi-year trend rather than relying only on the latest figure.

RBS's top-10/top-50/top-100 numbers are useful for understanding salary distribution, but they should not be called the average or highest package. The median salary is the cleaner comparison metric because it represents the middle of the placed-student salary distribution.

How should students interpret ₹7.50 LPA median?

A ₹7.50 LPA median means roughly half of the reported placed salaries are at or below the median and half are at or above it. It does not tell you the exact salary for a particular specialisation, recruiter or job role, and it is not the same as monthly take-home pay.

RBS Placement Support and Internship Process

The Office of Corporate Relations coordinates final placements, summer internships, industry interactions, professional-body associations and career support. The placement cell also supports career counselling and pre-placement training and works on recruiter relationships.

RBS describes its placement ecosystem as being supported by experiential learning, mentoring and an Individual Development Plan. Students therefore have career preparation running alongside the academic programme rather than only during the final placement season.

What students should do before placement season

  • Choose electives around the target career rather than choosing a specialisation only because of a recruiter name.
  • Use the summer internship to build a demonstrable project or business outcome.
  • Prepare a role-specific CV before final placements.
  • Build interview readiness around business awareness, communication and functional concepts.
  • Compare fixed pay, variable pay, role, location and growth—not just CTC.

RBS Placement ROI: is the PGDM worth the cost?

With current PGDM tuition referenced at ₹7.50 lakh and the latest NIRF median salary at ₹7.50 LPA, the tuition-to-median comparison is attractive. However, total cost includes hostel, academic charges, living expenses and potentially loan interest.

Positive ROI signals

₹7.50 LPA latest median, 201 placements in the 2024-25 NIRF cohort, 170 recruiters in the 2023-25 placement report and scholarship opportunities.

What can change your ROI

Scholarship received, loan rate, variable CTC, role quality, location, living costs and how quickly you progress after graduation.
