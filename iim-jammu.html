What Should Students Know About IIM Jammu?

Indian Institute of Management Jammu (IIM Jammu) is a public management institute established by the Government of India in 2016. Its main campus is at Jagti, Jammu. The institute's flagship two-year MBA is supported by specialised programmes including MBA in Hospital Administration & Healthcare Management, the five-year IPM and other management and doctoral programmes.

Student takeaway

IIM Jammu is no longer only a new-IIM option. Its 2025 NIRF Management rank is 35, and the institute has added multiple entry routes and specialised programmes. For MBA applicants, however, the published CAT threshold is only a PI-shortlisting floor; final selection also considers PI and profile components.

MBA in Hospital Administration & Healthcare Management

The 2026–28 batch size is 90 and the fee is ₹22,80,756. The programme uses CAT, PI and profile criteria under a separate policy and is intended for students targeting healthcare management.

IIT Jammu + IIM Jammu Dual Degree

The pathway combines a four-year B.Tech at IIT Jammu with a two-year MBA at IIM Jammu. Entry is through JEE Advanced and JoSAA. The programme policy provides an MBA transition route subject to the required B.Tech CGPA.

Dual MBA in AI & Business Analytics with AIT Thailand

Year 1 is at IIM Jammu and Year 2 is at AIT Thailand. The programme is designed for students who want an international analytics and AI-focused management pathway.

Executive MBA & PhD

The blended Executive MBA is aimed at working professionals, while the PhD is intended for research careers. Each has its own eligibility, entrance and application policy.

What should students budget?

The published programme fee is not the same as an all-in personal budget. Add mess, travel, personal expenses and loan interest according to your circumstances. The page should therefore present ₹22.81 lakh as the official programme fee rather than an invented total cost of attendance.

Who Should Choose IIM Jammu?

Choosing IIM Jammu depends on the programme, fee, admission route and the outcomes you are targeting. The published data should be read alongside your own CAT/JIPMAT profile, budget, preferred career path and campus preferences.

What the numbers show

The MBA 2026–28 programme fee is ₹22,80,756, the published General Male CAT PI-shortlist floor is 91 overall percentile, and the latest completed MBA placement report cited here records ₹16.04 LPA average CTC and ₹15.80 LPA median CTC.

What not to assume

A shortlist cutoff is not a final-admission cutoff, and the highest CTC is not the typical outcome. Review the complete admission policy, placement report and fee structure before making an application or accepting an offer.

For students comparing IIM options, useful dimensions include CAT percentile, profile weightage, programme specialisation, total fee, placement distribution, work-experience rules and location. These factors can be compared without relying on a single ranking or headline package.

What Are the Frequently Asked Questions About IIM Jammu?

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Jammu MBA 2026?

The published PI-shortlist cutoff is 91 overall for General Male candidates with 72 in each section. General Female and Transgender candidates need 89 overall and 71 in each section.

What is the IIM Jammu MBA fee?

The official MBA fee for 2026–28 is ₹22,80,756, plus a ₹50,000 refundable caution deposit. Mess is charged as per actuals.

What is the average package at IIM Jammu?

For the MBA 2023–25 batch, the reported average CTC was ₹16.04 LPA and the median CTC was ₹15.80 LPA.

What is the NIRF ranking of IIM Jammu?

IIM Jammu was ranked 35th in the NIRF Management category in 2025, up from 42nd in 2024.

How can I get into IIM Jammu after Class 12?

Through the five-year IPM using JIPMAT. For 2026–31, the composite is 70% JIPMAT percentile, 15% Class 10 and 15% Class 12.

Does IIM Jammu have a permanent campus?

Yes. The main campus is at Jagti, Jammu-181221. The institute also has a Srinagar off-campus and announced a Leh satellite campus for BBA (Hons.).

How many MBA seats does IIM Jammu have?

The 2026–28 MBA policy states that the batch is around 300.
