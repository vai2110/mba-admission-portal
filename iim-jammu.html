About IIM Jammu

Why do students shortlist IIM Jammu in 2026?

Students shortlist IIM Jammu because it now combines an IIM degree, a 35th NIRF Management rank, twin international accreditations, and more than one entry route. It is no longer only “a new IIM in Jammu and Kashmir”.

The ranking and quality signals moved together in 2025. NIRF Management rank rose from 42 to 35. In the same year the institute received a five-year BGA institutional accreditation under AMBA, London, and a three-year EFMD accreditation for the flagship MBA. Among 21 IIMs, it is one of five with both. It is the first second- and third-generation IIM to hold this pair.

The 2026–28 MBA policy also changed the convert math. CAT is 42 marks, PI is 30, profile is 28. Women and transgender candidates get 8 profile marks. Work experience of 24.01–36 months gets the full 10 work-ex marks. A higher CAT score can still lose to a stronger interview and profile.

What courses does IIM Jammu offer?

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Jammu?

The official IIM Jammu CAT 2025 PI shortlist cutoff for General Male candidates is 91 overall percentile, with 72 in VARC, 72 in DILR and 72 in QA. General Female and Transgender candidates need 89 overall and 71 in each section.

These numbers are shortlist floors published in the MBA 2026–28 admission policy. They are not final-admission cutoffs. The institute can revise them after CAT results. A PI call does not mean a convert.

How does IIM Jammu MBA admission work?

You need a valid CAT 2025 score, a bachelor’s degree with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD), and a place on IIM Jammu’s PI shortlist. Final merit is 42 marks from CAT, 30 from the Personal Interview and 28 from profile.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree with at least 50% or equivalent CGPA; 45% for SC/ST/PwD.
  • CAT 2025. Final-year students can apply provisionally.
  • Reservation: SC 15%, ST 7.5%, NC-OBC 27%, EWS 10%, PwD 5%.

How profile marks are given

Academics: Class 10 is 2 marks, Class 12 is 3, graduation is 5. Full marks start at 95.01% in each exam. Graduation at 90.01–95 gets 4 marks.

Work experience: only full-time paid work after graduation counts. Internships and part-time work do not. 24.01–36 months gets 10. Under 12 months gets 0. Over 60 months gets 0. The institute asks for letterhead certificates and bank-credited salary proof. Cash salary is not counted.

Gender diversity: female and transgender candidates get 8 marks. Male candidates get 0.

Offer fee

Pay ₹50,000 to accept an offer. It is adjusted against Term I fee if you join. Withdraw by 5:30 pm on 21 June 2026 for a refund minus ₹1,000. After that date, or after registration, the published policy says no refund of offer-acceptance and term fee. Queries: mba.admissions@iimj.ac.in or 0191-2741414.

IPM fee

IPM years 1–3 cost ₹6,77,424, ₹6,77,424 and ₹6,83,424. Years 4–5 follow the MBA fee then in force. Caution deposit is ₹50,000. Hostels are shared in the first three years. Single rooms in years 4–5 are given if available.

Executive MBA fee

The official blended EMBA fee for 2026–28 is ₹11,72,650. That includes two campus-immersion fees of ₹60,000. Travel to campus is extra. There is a 20% concession for Indian military and paramilitary personnel and 10% for ICMA/ICAI/ICSI qualified candidates, subject to documents.

BFSI and consulting took the largest share of the latest drive, followed by IT and analytics, FMCG/FMCD and manufacturing. That batch had about 255 students, average age 25, average work experience 29 months, 54% freshers and a gender mix close to 53% male and 47% female.

Fee of ₹22.81 lakh against a median CTC of ₹15.80 LPA is roughly an 18–20 month pre-tax payback on tuition and bundled campus charges alone. Add mess, loan interest and living costs and a clean payback is closer to 2–2.5 years for a median student. How can I get into IIM Jammu after Class 12?

Apply to the five-year Integrated Programme in Management through JIPMAT, conducted by NTA. You need 60% in Class 10 and Class 12 (55% for SC/ST/PwD). For the 2026–31 batch, merit is 70% JIPMAT percentile, 15% Class 10 marks and 15% Class 12 marks.

  • Class 12 in 2024, 2025 or appearing in 2026.
  • Class 10 in 2022 or later.
  • All streams are eligible.
  • Official likely batch size for 2026–31: around 140.

The previous cycle gave JIPMAT 95% weight and a gender-diversity bonus. That bonus is gone. Board marks now decide 30% of the composite. A strong school record can offset a mid-high JIPMAT score. IPM students enter the MBA phase on campus and use the same placement process after year five.

What other programmes can students apply to?

Beyond the flagship MBA and IPM, students can apply to the healthcare MBA, the IIT Jammu dual degree, the AIT Thailand analytics dual MBA, the blended Executive MBA and the PhD. Each has its own policy. Do not assume one convert covers another programme.

MBA in Hospital Administration and Healthcare Management

Official 2026–28 batch size is 90. Fee is the same ₹22,80,756. Admission is CAT, PI and profile under a separate MBA (HC) policy. The programme is run with academic collaboration involving AIIMS Jammu and IIT Jammu. Choose it only if healthcare is the intended career. Check that year’s healthcare placement report before treating it as a substitute for the flagship MBA.

IIT Jammu + IIM Jammu dual degree

IIT Jammu reserves 20% of B.Tech seats, category-wise, for this track. Entry is JEE Advanced and JoSAA. After four years at IIT Jammu, a CGPA of 7.0 or above lets the student enter the IIM Jammu MBA without CAT. Students who do not transit still receive the IIT Jammu B.Tech. Fees follow IIT Jammu during engineering and IIM Jammu during the MBA years.

Dual MBA in AI and Business Analytics with AIT Thailand

Year 1 at IIM Jammu follows the same term grid as the flagship MBA: ₹11,37,378 for Indian nationals, plus a ₹6,000 alumni fee. Year 2 at AIT Thailand is billed at THB 373,500 as listed on the official policy page, after the stated fellowships and campus charges. Use this only if you want the international year and the analytics degree.

PhD 2026

The policy lists 29 April 2026 as a tentative application close and interviews around June. Applicants need a master’s or equivalent professional qualification, 50% in Class 10 and 12, and must be no older than 40 on 1 June 2026. Tests depend on area: CAT, GMAT, GRE, GATE or JRF. The General CAT guide in that policy is 70 sectional and 80 overall.

Where is IIM Jammu and how is campus life?

The permanent campus is at Jagti, about 14 km from Jammu city, 16 km from Jammu Tawi railway station and 18 km from Jammu airport. Address: Indian Institute of Management Jammu, Jagti, Jammu-181221. Phone: 0191-2741400.

Facilities listed across official pages include academic and MDP blocks, hostels, sports facilities, a medical centre, cafeteria, shopping complex, incubation centre and centres of excellence. The Srinagar off-campus opened on 24 January 2020.

In September 2026, the Ladakh administration and IIM Jammu signed an MoU for a satellite campus at Leh. The first announced programme is a four-year BBA (Hons.) with 60 seats. That campus is not the location of the flagship MBA.

The student calendar includes HR Conclave, leadership talks, Empyrean, Inter-IIM sports, Innovation Day and the alumni meet Samagam. Winters in Jammu are cold. Budget clothing and occasional travel delay. That is a living fact, not a reason to reject the institute.

Is IIM Jammu worth it in 2026?

Yes, if you want an IIM degree, can fund about ₹23–25 lakh, and accept a median outcome near ₹15.80 LPA. No, if a 25 LPA+ first job is a hard condition, or if a metro campus is non-negotiable for family reasons.

Join if your CAT is likely to sit in the early-to-mid 90s, or if your reserved-category profile clears the published bars, and you prefer a growing campus over an old alumni book.

Compare IIM Amritsar, IIM Bodh Gaya, IIM Sambalpur and IIM Sirmaur before you lock the ₹50,000 offer fee. IIM Jammu is relatively kinder to women (8 diversity marks) and to candidates with 2–3 years of clean full-time work (peak 10 work-ex marks).

How should you apply?

Register for CAT at iimcat.ac.in and keep IIM Jammu in the CAP / IIM preference flow that year. Class 12 applicants register for JIPMAT when NTA opens the form.

  1. Scan Class 10, 12 and graduation marksheets. Profile bands use exact percentages.
  2. If you have a job, collect letterhead certificates, joining and relieving letters, and salary proof with bank credit.
  3. Prepare the interview for why this campus, your work or internship stories, and one honest gap. Clarity beats jargon.
  4. IPM applicants must submit board marks on the IIM Jammu form when it opens. Missing that form can drop a good JIPMAT score off the list.
  5. Pay the ₹50,000 offer fee only after you compare other converts. The withdrawal cut-off is 21 June 2026.
