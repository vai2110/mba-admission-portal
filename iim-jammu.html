IIM Jammu: What Students Should Know

Indian Institute of Management Jammu is an Institute of National Importance offering management programmes from its Jammu campus. Its current portfolio includes the flagship two-year MBA, MBA in Healthcare Management and a dual-degree MBA in AI & Business Analytics with AIT Thailand.

What makes IIM Jammu different?

The institute combines the IIM brand with a relatively young campus, experiential learning and a placement mix spanning consulting, finance, marketing, product management and general management. Its specialised programmes also connect management with healthcare and AI/business analytics.

IIM Jammu Admission: CAT Cutoff at a Glance

For the 2026-28 MBA, IIM Jammu uses CAT 2025 for PI shortlisting, followed by a final merit process based on CAT, PI and profile. The institute notes that the displayed cutoffs can be adjusted depending on the number of candidates available.

IIM Jammu MBA Fees 2026-28

The official 2026-28 MBA fee is ₹22,80,756. This covers tuition and listed academic/non-academic expenses. Mess bills are payable as per actuals, and a refundable ₹50,000 caution deposit is payable separately at admission.

Important cost point

The ₹50,000 offer-acceptance amount is adjusted against Term-I fee. The refundable caution deposit is additional, while mess bills are based on actuals.

Who Should Choose IIM Jammu?

Strong fit

  • Students seeking an IIM-brand residential MBA.
  • Candidates interested in consulting, finance, marketing, product or general management.
  • Applicants who can benefit from work-experience and profile weightage.
  • Students considering specialised healthcare or AI/analytics pathways.

Think carefully if

  • You are choosing only because of the IIM label.
  • Your career goal requires a very specific recruiter mix.
  • You cannot comfortably fund the full residential programme cost.

IIM Jammu FAQs

What is the IIM Jammu MBA fee for 2026-28?

The official programme fee is ₹22,80,756, excluding the refundable ₹50,000 caution deposit and mess bills payable as actuals.

What is the IIM Jammu CAT cutoff?

For MBA 2026-28, the published overall PI-shortlisting threshold is 91 for General male candidates and 89 for General female/transgender candidates, with sectional thresholds also prescribed.

Does IIM Jammu have specialised MBA programmes?

Yes. It offers MBA Healthcare and a dual-degree MBA in AI & Business Analytics with AIT Thailand in addition to the flagship MBA.
