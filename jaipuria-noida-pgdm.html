Jaipuria Noida PGDM: Quick Answer

Jaipuria Noida's flagship PGDM is a two-year full-time management programme. The institute describes the PGDM as AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA. The programme starts with core business subjects and allows students to build functional depth through electives and dual specialisation options.

Who is the PGDM best suited for?

It is most suitable for graduates who want a broad management degree and are not yet fixed on one narrow function. Students can explore finance, marketing, HR, operations, business analytics, strategy and related areas before choosing a specialisation path.

PGDM Curriculum & Specialisations

The curriculum combines core business subjects with electives and industry-oriented learning. Jaipuria's programme information lists areas such as finance and accounting, marketing, HR and organisational behaviour, operations and decision sciences, business analytics, economics and international business, and general management and strategy.

Students should verify the final elective basket for their batch because the institute notes that electives can change with academic and industry requirements.

Jaipuria Noida PGDM FAQs

What is the PGDM fee at Jaipuria Noida?

₹16.50 lakh for 2026-28, excluding the refundable security deposit and hostel/mess costs.

Is CAT compulsory for Jaipuria Noida PGDM?

No single exam is compulsory. The institute lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT as valid aptitude tests.

Does Jaipuria Noida PGDM require work experience?

No. Work experience is only one 3% component of the published selection framework.

Is Jaipuria PGDM equivalent to MBA?

Jaipuria states that its PGDM is AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.
