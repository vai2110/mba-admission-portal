Jaipuria Noida PGDM: What You Actually Study

The flagship PGDM starts with core management subjects and allows students to customise their second year through a dual-specialisation structure. The current curriculum includes Accounting, Economics, Statistics, Organisational Behaviour, Marketing, GenAI for Managers, Corporate Finance, HR, Operations, Business Analytics, Research and Strategic Management.

Best suited for

Students who want broad management exposure and flexibility across finance, marketing, HR, operations, analytics and strategy rather than a single narrow functional route.

PGDM Curriculum & Dual Specialisation

Students choose six courses from Specialisation-I, six from Specialisation-II, one Liberal Arts course and one open elective. The institute lists specialisation areas including Finance & Accounting, Marketing, HR & OB, Operations & Decision Sciences, Business Analytics, Economics/IB and General Management & Strategy.

Learning, Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

Jaipuria's PGDM combines classroom learning with case discussions, live projects, workshops, industry interactions and a Summer Internship Programme. The current curriculum also includes GenAI for Managers and business analytics foundations.

International exposure is supported through the institute's global academic alliances, subject to programme rules and selection.

Who Should Choose Jaipuria Noida PGDM?

Good fit

  • You want broad management exposure.
  • You value dual specialisation.
  • You are considering analytics, finance, marketing, HR or operations.
  • You want a Delhi-NCR campus.

Think twice

  • You have a stronger call at similar total cost.
  • You are choosing only for the highest package.
  • You need a very high average salary to justify the investment.

Jaipuria Noida PGDM FAQs

What is the Jaipuria Noida PGDM fee?

The published 2027-29 programme fee is ₹16.50 lakh, excluding refundable security deposit and separate hostel/mess costs.

Which exams are accepted?

CAT 2026, XAT 2027, CMAT, MAT and GMAT.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. Fresh graduates can apply.

Does PGDM have dual specialisation?

Yes. The current curriculum provides a dual-specialisation structure.
