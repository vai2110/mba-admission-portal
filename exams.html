Which MBA Entrance Exam Should You Take?

The right entrance exam depends on the colleges you want to target, your academic profile, preferred location and the type of management programme you want to pursue.

CAT is particularly important for students targeting the IIMs and many leading management institutes. XAT is important for aspirants targeting XLRI and other XAT-accepting institutes.

CMAT and MAT can provide access to a broad range of MBA and PGDM institutes, while SNAP is relevant for Symbiosis institutes and NMAT is used by NMIMS and other participating institutions.

MBA Entrance Exams FAQs

Which is the most important MBA entrance exam in India?
CAT is one of the most widely considered MBA entrance examinations, particularly for admission to IIMs and many leading management institutes.
Is CAT required for all MBA colleges?
No. Different MBA colleges accept different entrance examinations. Some institutes accept CAT, while others may accept XAT, CMAT, MAT, SNAP, NMAT or other exams.
Which exam is accepted by XLRI?
XLRI uses XAT as its major entrance examination for its relevant management admissions.
Which exam is used for Symbiosis MBA admissions?
SNAP is the entrance examination associated with admissions to participating Symbiosis institutes.
Can I take more than one MBA entrance exam?
Yes. MBA aspirants can appear for multiple entrance examinations if they meet the respective registration and eligibility requirements.
