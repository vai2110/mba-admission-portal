Quick answer

IIM Rohtak PGPx is a one-year Post Graduate Programme in Management for Executives designed for working professionals. Applicants need a bachelor's/postgraduate degree with at least 50% marks and three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience. Admission is based on a valid CAT/GMAT/GRE/GATE score from the past three years or the IIMR Entrance Test, followed by screening and Personal Interview. The institute currently lists a total programme fee of ₹7,43,650, plus a ₹2,500 non-refundable application fee and a separate on-campus module fee.

IIM Rohtak's PGPx is the institute's one-year Post Graduate Programme in Management for Executives. It is positioned for working professionals who want a structured management-learning experience without leaving employment for a conventional two-year full-time MBA.

The current programme combines online synchronous classes with on-campus sessions and project work. The institute highlights general management orientation, practical knowledge, case-based pedagogy, simulations, live projects, leadership development and peer learning.

Important qualification distinction

PGPx should not be presented as a two-year MBA degree. IIM Rohtak describes it as a one-year executive postgraduate diploma programme and states that successful participants receive a Post Graduate Diploma in Management for Executive with executive alumni benefits.

IIM Rohtak PGPx Eligibility 2026-27

The programme is intended for candidates who already have substantial professional exposure. The current official PGPx admission page specifies both an academic qualification and a minimum experience requirement.

Academic requirement

A Bachelor's Degree or Post Graduation Degree in any discipline with a minimum 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA.

Experience requirement

At least 3 years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience at the time of admission.

Who is this entry route actually meant for?

  • Working professionals who want formal management education without entering a full-time two-year MBA.
  • Managers preparing for broader responsibilities, team leadership or cross-functional roles.
  • Entrepreneurs seeking structured management frameworks alongside practical experience.
  • Professionals who can continue working while attending the hybrid programme format.
Application check

Experience should be assessed against the institute's stated definition and timing requirement. Do not assume internships, short-term training or unrelated academic projects satisfy the three-year professional-experience condition.

IIM Rohtak PGPx Admission Process

The admission route is different from the CAT-based two-year PGP process. PGPx applicants first satisfy the eligibility conditions, qualify through an accepted entrance-score route or IIMR Entrance Test, and then go through institute screening and Personal Interview.

Do not confuse the routes

PGPx uses an executive-programme selection route. Its qualifying exams, experience requirement and interview stage should not be copied from IIM Rohtak's two-year PGP admission process.

PGPx Entrance Exams & Selection Criteria

For the current programme, candidates can qualify for consideration through a valid score in CAT, GMAT, GRE or GATE obtained within the past three years, or through the IIMR Entrance Test.

Accepted score

CAT / GMAT

A valid score from the past three years can be used as the qualifying examination route for PGPx consideration.

Accepted score

GRE / GATE

Applicants can also qualify through a valid GRE or GATE score from the past three years.

Institute route

IIMR Entrance Test

Candidates without an accepted qualifying score can use the institute's entrance-test route, subject to the current admission instructions.

How selection works

  1. Candidate satisfies the minimum eligibility conditions.
  2. Candidate submits the application through the PGPx admission process.
  3. Candidate qualifies through an accepted test score or IIMR Entrance Test.
  4. IIM Rohtak screens the eligible pool and shortlists candidates.
  5. Shortlisted candidates appear for the Personal Interview.
  6. Final offers are made after the institute's evaluation and merit process.
Important:

The institute's current PGPx admission page describes the accepted-score/test route and PI process, but it does not publish a simple CAT-percentile-style cutoff for PGPx. Do not create a numerical cutoff table from unofficial estimates.

PGPx Learning Experience & Class Schedule

The programme is designed around a hybrid learning model rather than a conventional residential MBA timetable. IIM Rohtak highlights case-based pedagogy, simulations, live projects, experienced faculty and peer learning with working professionals.

How teaching and evaluation are structured

  • Case-based learning: the institute highlights cases and practical management situations rather than only classroom theory.
  • Simulations: strategic decision-making and practical application are built into the programme.
  • Live/project work: project work is part of the programme from the terms rather than being restricted to a final assignment.
  • Online platform: synchronous classes can be attended through the institute's Direct-to-Device technology.
  • LMS access: participants receive access to study material and assessment submission through the learning-management system.
  • Evaluation: the institute lists end-term examinations, live projects, online assessments, quizzes, assignments and class participation as evaluation components, with final discretion resting with the course instructor/faculty.

PGPx is structurally different from a conventional campus-placement MBA. The programme is designed for professionals who already have work experience and can continue participating in employment while studying. Should You Choose IIM Rohtak PGPx?

The programme can be relevant when the candidate's priority is management upskilling and career progression while continuing as a working professional. The right comparison is therefore not simply “PGPx vs MBA package”; it is programme fit versus the opportunity cost of leaving employment for a full-time programme.

PGPx may fit you if...

  • You already have at least three years of professional, managerial or entrepreneurial experience.
  • You want general-management education rather than a narrow technical certification.
  • You need a hybrid schedule that can coexist with a full-time job.
  • You value peer learning with other experienced professionals.
  • You want an IIM Rohtak executive credential and alumni benefits.

Compare alternatives carefully if...

  • You are primarily seeking a conventional campus-placement MBA.
  • You need a two-year MBA degree specifically for eligibility in a target role.
  • You cannot commit to the Friday-Sunday schedule and project workload.
  • Your main objective is a specialised technical programme rather than broad management capability.
  • The total cost plus travel/on-campus expenses does not fit your career-return calculation.

Questions to answer before applying

  1. Will the programme improve my next role or responsibility level?
  2. Can I realistically manage the weekend schedule alongside my current job?
  3. Does a one-year executive diploma meet the qualification requirement of my target employers?
  4. Have I budgeted for the programme fee plus the separate on-campus module and travel?
  5. Am I choosing PGPx for career progression rather than only for the IIM brand?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Rohtak PGPx a degree programme?

No. IIM Rohtak describes PGPx as a one-year Post Graduate Diploma in Management for Executives. Successful participants are awarded the executive postgraduate diploma and executive alumni benefits.

What is the eligibility for IIM Rohtak PGPx?

You need a Bachelor's Degree or Post Graduation Degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA, along with three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience at the time of admission.

Which entrance exams are accepted for PGPx?

IIM Rohtak currently accepts valid CAT, GMAT, GRE or GATE scores from the past three years, or the IIMR Entrance Test route.

Does PGPx have a CAT percentile cutoff like the two-year PGP?

The current official PGPx admission page does not publish a CAT-percentile cutoff comparable to the two-year PGP. Candidates should not use the PGP CAT cutoff as a PGPx cutoff.

What is the IIM Rohtak PGPx fee?

The institute currently lists a total programme fee of ₹7,43,650 inclusive of academic charges and a separate ₹2,500 non-refundable application fee. A separate fee is applicable to the on-campus module.

What is the duration of PGPx?

The programme duration is one year, with 450 hours of interactive sessions and 180 hours of project work, for a published total of 630 hours.

Is PGPx online or offline?

It is hybrid. The published structure includes 375 hours of online virtual-classroom sessions and 75 hours of offline on-campus sessions.

Does IIM Rohtak PGPx provide campus placements?

The current official PGPx page does not publish a comparable campus-placement report. It is an executive programme for working professionals, so students should evaluate it through career progression, learning, network and executive-alumni value rather than a PGP-style placement package.
