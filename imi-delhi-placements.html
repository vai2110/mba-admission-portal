IMI Delhi Placements 2025: What the Latest Report Says

IMI Delhi's official 2023-25 Final Placement Report covers 412 students across PGDM, PGDM (B&FS) and PGDM (HRM). The overall placement statistics show a highest CTC of ₹40.31 LPA, average CTC of ₹17.91 LPA and median CTC of ₹16.60 LPA.

₹40.31 LPAOverall highest CTC
₹17.91 LPAOverall average CTC
₹16.60 LPAOverall median CTC

The placement report also shows a diverse batch: 288 PGDM students, 63 B&FS students and 61 HRM students. Average work experience was 19 months, while students came from engineering, commerce and other academic backgrounds.

Which programme has the strongest salary numbers?

In the 2023-25 report, B&FS has the highest programme-wise average, median and highest CTC. That does not mean every B&FS student earns more than every PGDM or HRM student; it reflects the distribution reported for each programme.

The placement report also publishes average CTC for the top portions of each programme. These figures are useful for understanding upside among stronger-performing students, but they should not be treated as the expected salary for the full batch.

How Students Should Read IMI Delhi Placement Data

Do not compare colleges using highest package alone. The ₹40.31 LPA highest CTC belongs to the top end of the B&FS distribution. For judging the typical outcome, median and average are more informative.

What does this mean for ROI?

With a published programme fee of ₹23.54 lakh, students should compare the fee with the median/average salary, expected living costs and the specific role they want. A strong salary number is useful only when the role, function and long-term career direction match your goals.
