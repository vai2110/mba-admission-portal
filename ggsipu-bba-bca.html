GGSIPU BBA/BCA FAQs

Are BBA and BCA available in affiliated colleges?

Yes. GGSIPU's programme ecosystem includes University Schools and affiliated institutes, with availability depending on the programme and academic session.

Should I compare only the cutoff?

No. Compare fees, location, infrastructure, placement support, batch size and programme curriculum as well.
