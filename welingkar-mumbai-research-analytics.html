Research & Business Analytics: What Is the Programme?

This specialised PGDM combines management education with business research and analytics. It is designed for students who want to use data, research methods and analytical thinking to support business decisions.

Is it a pure data-science course?

No. It is a management programme. The strongest fit is for students targeting business analytics, consulting, research, insights and data-backed management roles rather than pure software/data engineering.

Who Should Choose Research & Business Analytics?

Good fit

  • You enjoy data and business problem solving.
  • You want analytics/insights/consulting roles.
  • You want management context alongside analytical skills.

Compare alternatives if

  • You want a pure data-science/engineering programme.
  • You dislike quantitative work.
  • A stronger analytics B-school call is available at similar cost.
