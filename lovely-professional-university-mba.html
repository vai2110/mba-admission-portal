LPU MBA: What Should You Know Before Applying?

LPU offers a two-year full-time MBA with multiple pathways beyond a conventional general-management degree. Students can choose from areas such as business analytics, data science and AI, financial markets, fintech, international business, hospital and healthcare management, supply chain and logistics, and digital and social media marketing, subject to the current programme portfolio.

Quick student verdict

LPU MBA is most relevant if you want a large university ecosystem, a residential campus, multiple specialisation choices, scholarship opportunities and a high-volume recruiter environment. The decision should still be based on the exact MBA variant, total cost after scholarship, internship quality and the placement outcomes relevant to your target role.

Why students consider it

  • Two-year full-time MBA structure.
  • Multiple industry-linked specialisations.
  • Large campus and student ecosystem.
  • Scholarships through LPUNEST and other routes.
  • Placement preparation, internships and live-project opportunities.

What to verify

  • Exact fee for the specialisation you choose.
  • Whether your preferred entrance test is accepted for that variant.
  • Role-wise placement outcomes rather than only the highest package.
  • Scholarship renewal conditions.
  • Hostel and living expenses if you are relocating.

LPU MBA Internships: What Students Should Expect

For an MBA, the internship can matter almost as much as the classroom curriculum because it gives recruiters evidence of practical business exposure. LPU highlights internships, live projects, industry visits and interactions with corporate professionals.

Use the internship to build

  • One measurable business project.
  • Industry-specific knowledge.
  • Excel, analytics or presentation skills.
  • A strong resume bullet with measurable impact.
  • Professional references and networking.

Do not assume

  • Every internship will be paid.
  • Every student gets the same recruiter.
  • An internship automatically converts into a final placement.
  • A brand name alone guarantees a strong MBA outcome.

LPU MBA Placements: What Students Actually Want to Know

LPU's university-level placement page reports 2,225+ recruiters, 6,000+ Fortune 500 offers and an average salary of ₹10.23 lakh for the top 25% of placed students. A 2025-26 placement update also reported more than 2,000 students securing ₹10 lakh+ offers, with a top-10% average of ₹14 lakh and an MBA offer of ₹40.85 lakh. These are headline outcomes and should not be interpreted as the average package for every MBA student.

Student questionPractical answer
What is the average MBA package?LPU's public university-level figures include top-cohort metrics; a single MBA-wide average should not be inferred from the highest package.
What is the highest package?Recent LPU placement communication reported an MBA offer of ₹40.85 lakh; highest offers are exceptional outcomes.
Are placements guaranteed?No. Placement support and eligibility do not equal a guaranteed job.
Do all MBA students get the same roles?No. Roles depend on specialisation, skills, internship, academic profile, interview performance and recruiter requirements.
What improves placement chances?Internships, communication, Excel/analytics, domain knowledge, projects, aptitude preparation and interview practice.
How should you read LPU placement data?

Use highest package only as an upper-bound indicator. For your decision, ask for the median or typical salary for your school/specialisation, recruiter list, number of students placed, role mix and the share of students entering core versus generic sales/support roles.

Is LPU MBA Worth the Investment?

It can be a good fit if

  • You want a broad MBA ecosystem with many specialisations.
  • You are proactive about internships and projects.
  • You can secure a meaningful scholarship.
  • You value residential campus life.
  • Your target roles match the available recruiter ecosystem.

Think twice if

  • You are choosing only because of a headline package.
  • You expect a guaranteed placement.
  • Your budget cannot comfortably support the full cost.
  • You have stronger offers from institutes with better outcomes for your target role.

The right comparison is total two-year cost versus the realistic role and salary you can target, not fee versus highest package.

Who Should Choose LPU MBA?

LPU MBA is worth shortlisting for students who want a large private-university environment, multiple management pathways, scholarship options and a broad corporate ecosystem. If you already have a strong offer from a more specialised management institute, compare placement quality, alumni outcomes, fees and role fit before deciding.

Best next step

Shortlist the exact MBA variant, calculate your net tuition after scholarship, add hostel/living expenses and then compare the expected roles with two or three alternative colleges.
