What should students include in their actual MBA budget?

The programme fee is not always the same as the total cost of attendance. Before accepting an offer, check these items in the latest fee letter or programme brochure:

  • Tuition/programme fee and the batch to which it applies.
  • Hostel or accommodation charges, mess/meal costs and refundable caution deposits.
  • Travel to campus, campus-module stays and international immersion expenses.
  • Laptop, books, course materials, utilities, laundry and personal expenses.
  • Application/commitment fees, instalment dates, refund rules and late-payment charges.
  • Scholarships, need-based financial aid and education-loan options—eligibility and availability may differ by programme.

FAQs on IIM Ahmedabad courses and fees

Is ₹27.50 lakh the confirmed fee for IIM Ahmedabad MBA 2026–28?

No. It is the latest published 2025–27 PGP fee benchmark identified for this page. The 2026–28 fee should be updated only after IIMA publishes the applicable official figure.

Is IIM Ahmedabad PGPX suitable for fresh graduates?

No. PGPX is intended for experienced professionals and the published eligibility information requires at least four years of full-time work experience after graduation.

Does the ₹20 lakh BPGP fee include travel and accommodation?

No. IIMA states that travel and accommodation for campus modules and costs related to International Immersion are excluded from the programme fee.

Can working professionals pursue an IIM Ahmedabad MBA?

IIMA offers blended programmes including BPGP, designed for working professionals. Check the current experience, age, admission-test and campus-module requirements before applying.

Are scholarships available?

Financial aid and scholarship provisions vary by programme. Check the relevant official programme page and current admission communication rather than assuming the same support applies across all programmes.
