Quick Answer

IMT Ghaziabad's official placement communication published in January 2026 reported placement activity up to 31 December 2025. It stated that 400 students had been placed with 110 participating companies, including 25 new recruiters. The highest CTC reported was ₹35.00 LPA, with an average CTC of ₹18.89 LPA and median CTC of ₹17.56 LPA.

Summer Internships

Summer internships form part of the institute's career and industry interface. The latest placement communication emphasises recruiter participation across sectors and the role of internship performance in pre-placement offers. CollegeDecoded is not adding an unsupported stipend figure where the current official disclosure does not provide one.

Recruiters & Hiring Sectors

The latest official placement update describes hiring across BFSI, Consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, Investment Banking, Global Capability Centres and E-commerce. Functional roles reported by the institute include strategy, management consulting, business analytics, product management, sales leadership, investment banking, wealth management, analytics, risk and fraud.

The institute's communication names recruiters including Aditya Birla Capital, Axis Bank, Bajaj Finserv, CRISIL, Federal Bank, HDFC Ergo, HSBC Bank, ICICI Bank, Kotak Mahindra Life and Tata Capital, among others.
