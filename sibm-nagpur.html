SIBM Nagpur MBA Admission 2027: SNAP, Eligibility, Fees & Placements

Symbiosis Institute of Business Management (SIBM) Nagpur is a constituent institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The institute offers a two-year MBA and an MBA in Food and Agri Business Management on its residential campus near MIHAN, Nagpur.

Does SIBM Nagpur accept SNAP?

Yes. SIBM Nagpur's official admission page states that SNAP is part of the selection process for the MBA programmes. For the 2027-29 batch, the final merit list is weighted using SNAP, Group Exercise (GE) and Personal Interaction (PI).

SIBM Nagpur MBA Eligibility

For the MBA programmes, the official institute page states that a candidate must be a graduate from a recognised university or an institution of national importance. A minimum of 50% marks or equivalent grade is required for general candidates, while the stated threshold for Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe candidates is 45%. Final-year students can apply subject to meeting the required marks condition at the time of admission.

SIBM Nagpur Admission Dates 2027

For the 2027-29 batch, the institute currently lists SNAP 2026 registration/payment closing on November 25, 2026. The SNAP tests are scheduled in December 2026, with the SNAP result listed for January 12, 2027. SIBM Nagpur programme registration/payment is listed to close on January 20, 2027, followed by shortlisting, GE-PI and merit-list stages.

  • SNAP registration/payment closes: November 25, 2026

  • SNAP result: January 12, 2027
  • SIBM Nagpur registration/payment closes: January 20, 2027

Important: Admission dates can be revised. The institute's current 2027-29 admission page should be checked before relying on any date.

Courses at SIBM Nagpur

The institute's current academic information lists the Master of Business Administration and the Master of Business Administration (Food and Agri Business Management). The flagship MBA covers areas including Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations and Business Analytics.

SIBM Nagpur Placements

SIBM Nagpur maintains a dedicated placement function for internships and final placements. The institute's official website publishes placement information and recruitment updates. Placement outcomes should be read by batch and placement cycle rather than treated as a permanent package guarantee.

Who should consider SIBM Nagpur?

  • Students who are comfortable taking SNAP as the common Symbiosis entrance test.
  • Candidates looking for a residential MBA environment in Nagpur.
  • Aspirants interested in general management specialisations or Food and Agri Business Management.
  • Students who want the final selection to include SNAP performance as well as GE and PI.
