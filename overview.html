Overview of SVKM's NMIMS

Established in 1981, SVKM's NMIMS is a research-focused, multi-disciplinary, multi-campus university. It holds A++ accreditation from NAAC with a 3.67 CGPA and is recognized as a Category 1 University by the MHRD.

Quick Facts

  • Legacy: 45 Years
  • Students: 32,000+
  • Faculty: 900+
  • Schools: 17 Specialized Schools
  • Campuses: 7 Off-Campuses/Centres

Academic Excellence

NMIMS offers a wide range of programs across disciplines including Management, Engineering, Pharmacy, Law, Commerce, Economics, and more. The university emphasizes industry-integrated curriculum, research, and global insights.

FAQs

Does NMIMS have a management quota?

No, SVKM’s NMIMS Deemed-to-be University does not have any management quota for admissions. All admissions are merit-based.

Are there any authorized agents for admission?

No, the university does not authorize any third-party agency or individual to represent NMIMS for direct admissions.
