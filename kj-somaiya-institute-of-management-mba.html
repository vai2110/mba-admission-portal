KJ Somaiya MBA: Quick Answer

For 2027-29

KJSIM's flagship MBA is a two-year full-time programme. The current cycle accepts CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT and CMAT 2027. MBA selection uses entrance score, academics, work experience, PSA, WAT and PI. The 2026-28 published programme fee totals ₹23.875 lakh before separately listed hostel costs.

When Does KJ Somaiya MBA 2027–29 Application Close?

The current official deadline for the flagship MBA application is 20 January 2027 at 11:59 PM. Shortlist, WAT, PSA and PI schedules are round-specific; rely on the institute portal for slot selection and reporting instructions.

KJ Somaiya MBA Eligibility

A minimum three-year graduation in any discipline with 50% aggregate marks from a recognised university is required. Final-year candidates appearing for exams in April-May 2027 can also apply under the institute's stated conditions.

How Does KJSIM Shortlist and Select MBA Applicants?

KJSIM uses two shortlisting routes for the flagship MBA: merit-based shortlisting against institute cutoffs and profile-based consideration for eligible candidates. Both routes use the same application form.

  1. Register on the official KJSIM application portal and choose the MBA programme.
  2. Submit CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT or CMAT 2027 score details. For NMAT, the best of two attempts is considered; GMAT validity is subject to the institute's published date window.
  3. Complete merit-based or profile-based shortlisting. Profile-based consideration looks for consistent academics, valid exam score and 12–42 months of full-time paid work experience after graduation.
  4. If shortlisted, select the available assessment slot and submit the candidate profile/documents.
  5. Complete the Written Ability Test (online) and Problem Situation Analysis plus Personal Interview as scheduled.
  6. Track the online merit result and seat allotment; offers depend on merit position and seat availability.
  7. After receiving the offer, pay the first-year fee through the candidate portal within the stated deadline.

Documents to prepare before the shortlist stage

Entrance scorecard and admit card; Class 10 and 12 marksheets; graduation marksheets for all semesters/years; degree or provisional certificate; CGPA conversion letter if applicable; work-experience certificates; photo ID; recent photographs; and any achievement or extracurricular certificates you want considered.

KJ Somaiya MBA Cutoff

KJSIM states that merit-based candidates must meet or exceed the institute's entrance-exam cutoffs. A fixed numeric CAT/XAT/NMAT/CMAT/GMAT cutoff is not published on the current official page reviewed, so no unofficial percentile is presented here as an official cutoff.

KJ Somaiya MBA Curriculum

The programme combines core management learning with the freedom to select a major and minor in the second year. KJSIM highlights areas including business analytics and data science alongside the broader management curriculum.

The pedagogy includes classroom interaction, cases, assignments, simulations and industry interface. Students should review the current curriculum before choosing a major because the institute can revise course structures.

KJ Somaiya MBA: Questions Applicants Usually Ask

Which entrance exams are accepted for KJSIM MBA 2027–29?

The official admission page lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT and CMAT 2027. NMAT considers the best of two attempts; GMAT scores must fall within the validity window stated by KJSIM.

Is KJSIM MBA admission possible without work experience?

Work experience is one selection parameter, not a universal eligibility requirement. The profile-based shortlisting route specifically refers to 12–42 months of full-time paid work experience after graduation; merit-based applicants are evaluated under the published merit criteria.

What is the difference between merit and profile-based selection?

For the flagship MBA, merit-based selection assigns 25% to entrance score and 30% to PI. Profile-based selection assigns 15% to entrance score and 40% to PI, while also considering academics and work experience.

What is the official KJSIM MBA cutoff?

KJSIM confirms that it sets cutoffs for merit-based shortlisting but does not publish one fixed numeric cutoff on the official page reviewed. Do not treat online expected percentiles as guaranteed calls.

What is the MBA fee and does it include hostel?

The published 2026–28 flagship MBA programme total is ₹23.875 lakh. Hostel is listed separately and should be added to the total cost of attendance.

What is a realistic salary benchmark after KJSIM MBA?

The latest official MBA 2024–26 highlight reports ₹12 LPA median CTC and ₹29.30 LPA highest CTC. Median is a more useful reference for planning than the highest offer.
