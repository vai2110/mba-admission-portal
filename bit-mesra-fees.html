BIT Mesra B.Tech Fees 2026-27

BIT Mesra's official 2026 fee structure gives separate figures by branch group. For CSE, AI & ML and ECE, the institute lists an approximate total expenditure of ₹17.88 lakh across eight semesters, before hostel charges. The first-semester institutional total is ₹1.89 lakh plus ₹45,000 admission-time charges.

BIT Mesra Hostel Fees

The official fee schedule explicitly states that hostel fees are extra and payable during registration for each semester. This means the programme totals above should not be treated as the complete residential cost.

Students should add the current hostel seat-rent/hostel circular and mess/personal expenses when calculating their full four-year B.Tech or two-year MBA budget.

How to calculate your actual BIT Mesra budget

Total budget = academic fee + admission-time charges + hostel + mess/personal expenses. For B.Tech CSE/AIML/ECE, the published academic expenditure is about ₹17.88 lakh before hostel; for MBA, the four semester totals plus admission charges come to ₹7.84 lakh before hostel.

BIT Mesra Fees FAQs

What is the B.Tech CSE fee at BIT Mesra?

For the 2026 batch, the official schedule gives an approximate total expenditure of ₹17.88 lakh for B.Tech CSE/AIML/ECE across eight semesters, before hostel. Admission-time charges of ₹45,000 are included in that approximate expenditure figure.

What is the BIT Mesra MBA fee?

The four semester institutional totals for the 2026 main-campus MBA are ₹1.815 lakh, ₹1.815 lakh, ₹1.955 lakh and ₹1.955 lakh. Adding ₹30,000 admission-time charges gives ₹7.84 lakh before hostel.

Is hostel included in BIT Mesra fees?

No. The official 2026 fee schedule says hostel fees are extra and payable during registration for each semester.

Is caution money refundable?

The fee schedule identifies the ₹10,000 amount as caution money. Students should follow the institute's refund rules for the applicable batch when leaving the programme.
