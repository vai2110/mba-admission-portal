Is B.Tech at CU the right choice?

CU offers a very broad engineering portfolio, from conventional branches such as Civil, Electrical and Mechanical to CSE-linked options in AI/ML, Data Science, Cyber Security and Cloud Computing. The advantage is choice; the challenge is deciding whether the extra specialisation actually matches your target career.

Don't choose a branch only because its name sounds modern

Compare curriculum, projects, faculty/labs, recruiter eligibility, total cost and your own interest. A strong CSE foundation can matter more than a fashionable specialisation title if your fundamentals are weak.

Eligibility and admission 2026

CUCET is mandatory for B.E. under the current scholarship/admission policy, with exemptions for candidates above 90% in the qualifying examination or up to 50,000 JEE rank. Programme-specific eligibility still applies, so students should check the current admission criteria for the exact branch.

CUCET

Used as an entrance/eligibility and scholarship route for applicable programmes.

JEE route

The current policy provides an exemption threshold up to 50,000 JEE rank for applicable mandatory programmes.

Academic eligibility

Requirements vary by programme; check the official admission-criteria table before applying.

What does B.Tech cost?

The mandatory disclosure lists ₹1.46 lakh for B.E. Computer Science and Engineering. Other branches vary: Civil and Electrical are listed at ₹1.10 lakh, ECE at ₹1.14 lakh, Chemical at ₹95,000 and specialised CSE variants can be higher.

Use these as programme-level reference figures, not a universal B.Tech fee

Specialised collaborations and programmes have different fee structures. Hostel, other charges and scholarship benefits can change the total cost.

What should you expect academically?

CU's academic framework highlights flexible choice-based credits, 70+ electives, advanced technology integration and project-based/experiential learning. Engineering-specific programmes also include industry collaborations in some specialisations.

Build fundamentals

Mathematics, programming, engineering science and core subjects remain important even in specialised CSE tracks.

Build projects

Use projects to demonstrate actual technical capability rather than relying only on certificates.

Use electives strategically

Choose electives that reinforce your intended role rather than collecting unrelated subjects.

What about engineering placements?

CU reports 10,000+ offers across the university for batch 2024-25, including 60+ MNCs recruiting engineering students according to its placement page. The highest national package reported by the university is ₹59.9 LPA and the highest international package is ₹1.7 crore.

Because the university has many engineering branches, a better decision is to check which employers recruit your specific branch and what technical skills their job descriptions demand. See the placement analysis.

Which B.Tech branch should you choose?

CSE / IT

AI/ML / Data Science

Core engineering

ECE

Useful for students interested in electronics, communication, embedded systems and adjacent technology careers.

FAQs

Is CUCET compulsory for B.Tech?

CU identifies B.E. as a mandatory CUCET programme, with stated exemptions for qualifying marks/JEE rank.

What is the CSE fee?

The mandatory disclosure currently lists ₹1.46 lakh for B.E. CSE; verify the current programme fee before payment.

Which B.Tech branch is best?

There is no universal best branch. The right choice depends on career target, aptitude, curriculum and recruiter eligibility.
