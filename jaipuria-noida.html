Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida: What Students Should Know

Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida is a management school in Sector 62, Delhi NCR. The campus offers full-time PGDM programmes that are AICTE approved, NBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. Jaipuria Noida was ranked 41st in NIRF Management 2025. The institute also highlights AACSB accreditation across the Jaipuria network.

The Noida campus currently lists PGDM, PGDM (Marketing) and PGDM (Service Management), along with an Online PGDM and FPM. The full-time PGDM is the broadest option, while Marketing and Service Management are designed for students who already have a stronger functional or sector preference.

Is Jaipuria Noida worth considering?

It is worth shortlisting if you want a Delhi-NCR B-school with a relatively accessible admission process, AACSB accreditation, dual-specialisation flexibility, a current AI-oriented curriculum and a centralised corporate placement ecosystem. The important question is whether the ₹16.50 lakh academic fee plus hostel, mess and living costs makes sense for your target role and expected salary—not simply whether the college has a high headline package.

Jaipuria Noida Admission 2027: Eligibility, Exams & Selection

For the 2027-29 cycle, Jaipuria states that applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year students graduating by June 30, 2027 can also apply provisionally. Accepted aptitude tests listed by the institute are CAT 2026, XAT 2027, CMAT, MAT and GMAT.

Does Jaipuria Noida publish a fixed CAT cutoff?

The institute does not present a single guaranteed CAT cutoff on its current 2027-29 admissions page. Students should therefore treat percentile-based estimates found on third-party portals as competitive indicators, not guaranteed call or admission thresholds.

How does the application work?

The official process has three broad phases: register your profile and campus preference, pay the ₹1,000 non-refundable admission-process entrance fee, and complete the application with academic, extracurricular and work-experience details.

For the 2027-29 batch, Jaipuria lists merit-based scholarships linked to entrance performance. For Noida PGDM and PGDM Marketing, the published slabs are ₹5 lakh for 90% and above, ₹3.50 lakh for 80% and above, and ₹2.50 lakh for 70% and above. A sibling scholarship of ₹1 lakh is also listed.

Jaipuria Noida Campus, Location & Student Experience

The Noida campus is a four-acre green, Wi-Fi-enabled campus in Sector 62. Official campus information highlights an air-conditioned academic complex, theatre-style and lecture halls, auditoriums, library, computer labs, faculty offices, a central green court and an open-air amphitheatre.

Why Sector 62 matters

Sector 62 places the campus within the wider Delhi-NCR corporate and technology ecosystem. For students, this can be useful for industry events, guest sessions, internships and recruiter interaction. Location alone, however, should not be treated as a placement guarantee.

International exposure

Jaipuria highlights 15+ global academic alliances across countries including the UK, France, Germany, Canada, Malaysia, New Zealand and others. Exchange and immersion opportunities are subject to the applicable programme and selection rules.

Learning beyond classrooms

The institute's current positioning emphasises an AI-native, student-first learning environment, individual development plans, industry training, live projects and structured career preparation. The PGDM curriculum also includes GenAI for Managers and Business Analytics foundations.

Jaipuria Institute of Management Placements: What Students Should Actually Read

Jaipuria's latest public placement reporting is centralised across its four campuses, so students should not automatically interpret the headline figures as Noida-only outcomes. The 2024-26 placement report currently shows ₹24.11 LPA highest CTC, ₹14.70 LPA average for the top 10%, ₹13.18 LPA for the top 20%, ₹11.08 LPA for the top 50%, 350+ companies and 1,100+ students placed.

Who Should Choose Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida?

Good fit

  • You want a Delhi-NCR management school with an accessible entrance route.
  • You value dual specialisation and cross-functional learning.
  • You are targeting finance, analytics, marketing, HR, operations or consulting.
  • You want a lower published fee than many premium private B-schools.
  • You want a campus with strong corporate and international exposure.

Think twice

  • You are targeting only the very top consulting/IB roles and have stronger options available.
  • You are choosing the college solely because of the ₹24.11 LPA headline package.
  • You need a very high average salary rather than a broad recruiter ecosystem.
  • You dislike a large, centrally managed placement structure.

What is Jaipuria Noida PGDM fees for 2027-29?
