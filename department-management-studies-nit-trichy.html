What is DoMS NIT Trichy?

The Department of Management Studies (DoMS), NIT Tiruchirappalli offers a full-time, two-year MBA. The 2026-28 admission brochure states that the department was established in 1978 and that the 2026 intake was 115 seats, including reservations.

The programme follows a trimester system with six trimesters. Students study core subjects, electives and complete a summer internship. Major areas listed by DoMS include Human Resource Management, Marketing, Finance, Production and Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management.

The key admission point is simple: DoMS NIT Trichy accepts CAT for MBA admission. MAT, XAT, CMAT and other management tests are not listed as alternative entrance routes by the department.

Who Can Apply for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

For the 2026-28 intake, candidates needed a full-time regular undergraduate degree in any discipline. The minimum requirement was 60% marks or 6.5 CGPA for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL candidates and 55% marks or 6.0 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Final-year undergraduate students could also apply if they had CAT 2025 and met the prescribed academic requirement after completing the degree. There is no age limit according to the department FAQ.

What Is the DoMS NIT Trichy Selection Criteria?

The official MBA page states that CAT overall percentile, personal interview, academic performance and professional work experience form the final selection parameters. Shortlisting is based on CAT overall percentile, with undergraduate academic achievement also specified in the 2026 brochure.

This means CAT is important for getting shortlisted, but CAT percentile alone does not decide the final offer. Your academics, work experience and interview performance also matter.

What CAT Percentile Is Needed for DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS NIT Trichy does not publish a single fixed CAT percentile as a guaranteed final admission cutoff. Its official admission information says candidates are shortlisted based on CAT overall percentile, while final selection also considers academics, work experience and the personal interview.

So, if you are targeting DoMS, do not read the CAT number as a guaranteed admission score. A stronger CAT percentile improves your shortlisting position, but the final decision depends on the complete selection profile.

For the 2026-28 batch, the official eligibility requirement was a CAT 2025 score plus the prescribed graduation marks. The department did not publish a category-wise final CAT closing percentile on its MBA admission page.

How Much Does DoMS NIT Trichy MBA Cost?

NIT Trichy's official MBA FAQ gives an approximate annual cost of ₹1,58,700, comprising ₹1,08,700 institute fee and about ₹50,000 for hostel plus mess. Which Specializations Are Available?

The 2026 MBA FAQ lists these areas: Human Resource Management, Marketing, Finance, Production and Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis and IT Consulting, and General Management.

DoMS also describes the MBA as a residential-style academic experience with core courses, electives and a summer internship. The programme uses a trimester pattern rather than a semester pattern.

How Are DoMS NIT Trichy MBA Placements?

DoMS states that it has maintained a 100% placement record in recent years. The department's official placement section currently provides the MBA placement brochure for 2025-26.

Do Students Have to Stay in the Hostel?

No. DoMS's official FAQ says hostel accommodation is not compulsory, although hostel stay is recommended.

Does DoMS NIT Trichy accept XAT, MAT or CMAT?

No. The department FAQ specifically says it accepts CAT scores only for MBA admission.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes. The 2026 brochure allowed final-year undergraduate students to apply if they had CAT 2025 and met the academic eligibility conditions.

How many MBA seats are there?

The official 2026 admission FAQ states 115 seats, inclusive of reservations.

Is there an age limit?

No. The department FAQ states that it does not prescribe a minimum or maximum age limit.

When does the MBA academic session normally start?

The department FAQ says the academic session normally commences in the second week of July.
