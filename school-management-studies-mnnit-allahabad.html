What is School of Management Studies MNNIT Allahabad?

The School of Management Studies (SMS) at MNNIT Allahabad offers a two-year full-time MBA, Executive MBA and PhD programmes. The School was established in 1996. The flagship full-time MBA has an intake of 77 students and shortlists candidates on the basis of CAT percentile for Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

The MBA offers dual-specialisation options across Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Operations Management, Systems Management and International Business.

How does MNNIT MBA admission work?

  1. Appear for the accepted management entrance examination and obtain a valid score.
  2. Apply to the School of Management Studies within the institute's application window.
  3. Shortlisting is based on CAT percentile as stated by SMS MNNIT.
  4. Shortlisted candidates participate in Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
  5. Final admission follows the institute's published selection and reservation rules.

For 2026-27, the institute's admission material announced applications in the 2026 cycle and GD/PI in late May. The next cycle's exact dates should be treated as unannounced until MNNIT publishes them.

What CAT percentile is needed for MNNIT MBA?

MNNIT does not publish a permanent, single CAT closing percentile that guarantees MBA admission. The School states that candidates are shortlisted on the basis of CAT percentile and then called for GD and PI.

Student takeaway

Do not treat a reported CAT percentile as a guaranteed admission cutoff. The practical target should be higher than the minimum level used for shortlisting because seats are limited to 77 and the final process includes GD and PI.

Searches for MNNIT MBA cutoff often mix official shortlisting information with third-party estimates. The page will use official MNNIT figures where available and clearly label any student-reported or external reference separately.

What do MNNIT MBA students study?

The SMS curriculum combines management fundamentals with research-based projects, practical computer applications, market and industry surveys, electives and a summer internship. The curriculum includes Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations, Systems and International Business areas.

Examples of advanced electives include investment analysis, financial analytics, marketing analytics, HR analytics, supply-chain analytics, data analytics, business intelligence, e-business and international marketing.

What are MNNIT MBA placements like?

The latest placement highlight published by SMS for the 2024-26 MBA batch reports a batch size of 67 students, highest package of ₹13.96 LPA, median package of ₹9 LPA and average package of ₹7.76 LPA. The top 10% average was ₹9 LPA.

The same official highlight shows sector-wise recruitment led by BFSI, followed by IT & ED Tech and FMCG/Consulting, and names recruiters including ICICI Bank, EY, HDFC Bank, TCS, Accenture, Volvo, IndiaMART and others.
