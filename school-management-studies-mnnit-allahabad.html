What is School of Management Studies MNNIT Allahabad?

The School of Management Studies (SMS) at MNNIT Allahabad was established in 1996 and offers a two-year full-time MBA, Executive MBA and PhD programmes. The full-time MBA intake is 77. SMS states that MBA candidates are shortlisted on the basis of CAT percentile for Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

The MBA offers dual-specialisation choices across Finance, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Operations Management, Systems Management and International Business.

How does MNNIT MBA admission work?

  1. MNNIT's central admission information lists CAT, MAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA and XAT for regular MBA admission.
  2. Complete the School of Management Studies application within the notified window.
  3. SMS states that candidates are shortlisted on CAT percentile for GD and PI.
  4. Attend Group Discussion and Personal Interview if shortlisted.
  5. Complete document verification, fee payment and registration after selection.

The official MNNIT website confirms that the MBA admission process for AY 2026-27 started in 2026; the next cycle should be followed through the institute's admission notices.

What CAT percentile is needed for MNNIT MBA?

MNNIT does not publish a permanent single CAT closing percentile that guarantees admission. SMS officially says CAT percentile is used for shortlisting candidates for GD and PI.

What is the MNNIT MBA fee?

For the 2026 admission cycle, the reported semester-wise MBA fee schedule is ₹84,000 in Semester 1 and ₹79,000 in Semesters 2, 3 and 4. This gives an academic/institute-fee total of ₹3.21 lakh across four semesters.

What do MNNIT MBA students study?

The SMS curriculum combines management fundamentals with research-based projects, computer applications, industry surveys, electives and summer internship. It covers Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations, Systems and International Business, with analytics-oriented areas such as financial analytics, marketing analytics, HR analytics, data analytics, business intelligence and supply-chain analytics.

MNNIT MBA FAQs
