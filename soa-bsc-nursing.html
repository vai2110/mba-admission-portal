SOA B.Sc Nursing: Quick Answer

SOA offers B.Sc Nursing (Basic) and also lists Post Basic B.Sc Nursing and M.Sc Nursing. The basic B.Sc Nursing programme is included in SOA's SAAT admission system.

Why Study B.Sc Nursing at SOA?

Nursing applicants should compare clinical exposure, hospital access, faculty, practical training, fee and progression options. SOA's multidisciplinary health-science ecosystem can be relevant for students who want to study nursing within a larger university and medical environment.

B.Sc Nursing Subjects & Course Structure

The programme combines nursing foundation subjects with anatomy and physiology, nutrition, psychology, microbiology, pharmacology, medical-surgical nursing, community health, child health, mental health and practical clinical training.

Foundation

Nursing sciences

Fundamentals of nursing, communication and patient-care principles.

Medical

Health sciences

Anatomy, physiology, microbiology, nutrition and pharmacology foundations.

Clinical

Specialised nursing

Medical-surgical, child, community and mental-health nursing.

Practical

Clinical practice

Hands-on training develops patient-care and clinical decision skills.

Clinical Training & Practical Exposure

Nursing education is highly practice-oriented. Students should evaluate the availability of clinical departments, patient exposure, supervised practice, skills labs and internship/clinical posting arrangements when comparing colleges.

Higher Studies After B.Sc Nursing

SOA lists M.Sc Nursing and Post Basic B.Sc Nursing among its nursing programmes. Students can also consider postgraduate nursing specialisations, public health and healthcare-management routes depending on their goals.

SOA B.Sc Nursing FAQs

Is B.Sc Nursing at SOA through SAAT?

Yes. B.Sc Nursing is included in SOA's SAAT programme list.

Is NEET required for SOA B.Sc Nursing?

No. B.Sc Nursing is placed under SOA's SAAT admission system rather than the MBBS/BDS NEET route.

What subjects are required for B.Sc Nursing?

SOA's 2026 eligibility requires PCB and English with the prescribed aggregate.

Does SOA offer M.Sc Nursing?

Yes. SOA's programme list includes M.Sc Nursing.

What can I do after B.Sc Nursing?

Common pathways include hospital nursing, community health, specialised clinical roles and higher study.
