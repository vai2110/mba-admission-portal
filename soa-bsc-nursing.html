SOA B.Sc Nursing: Quick Answer

SOA offers B.Sc Nursing (Basic) and also lists Post Basic B.Sc Nursing and M.Sc Nursing programmes. B.Sc Nursing is included in SOA's SAAT admission system.

SOA B.Sc Nursing Eligibility 2026

SOA's 2026 criteria require 10+2 with PCB and English and the prescribed aggregate. The published criteria also specify age conditions for the 2026 batch.

SOA B.Sc Nursing Admission 2026

B.Sc Nursing is admitted through SOA's SAAT process. The 2026 admission updates include results from later SAAT rounds, so applicants should use the current official schedule for the applicable round.

SOA B.Sc Nursing FAQs

Is B.Sc Nursing at SOA through SAAT?

Yes. B.Sc Nursing is included in SOA's SAAT programme list.

Is NEET the admission route for SOA B.Sc Nursing?

No. SOA places B.Sc Nursing under its SAAT admission system rather than the MBBS/BDS NEET route.

What percentage is required for B.Sc Nursing?

SOA's 2026 criteria specify the required PCB and English aggregate, with category-wise thresholds. Check the official eligibility page before applying.
