NDIM PGDM: Quick Answer

What is the course?

NDIM's PGDM is a two-year full-time management programme approved by AICTE. NDIM positions it around management fundamentals, industry interaction and corporate skill development. The published 2026-28 course fee is ₹13.75 lakh, excluding optional hostel and international study-tour costs.

For admission eligibility and the complete process, see the NDIM admission section on the overview page.

NDIM PGDM Eligibility

Applicants need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. For the 2026-28 cycle, NDIM stated a 50% minimum graduation score for General-category candidates and 45% for reserved-category candidates. Final-year students may apply subject to the relevant cycle's conditions.

NDIM PGDM Specialisations & Curriculum

NDIM lists general PGDM, PGDM Finance and PGDM Marketing routes. The broad PGDM is suited to students who want flexibility, while Finance and Marketing provide a clearer functional direction.

GENERAL

PGDM

Broad management route for students who want flexibility across business functions.

FINANCE

PGDM Finance

Aligned with banking, financial services, corporate finance and finance-oriented roles.

MARKETING

PGDM Marketing

Aligned with brand, digital, sales and market-facing career interests.

NDIM also highlights exposure to areas such as AI, machine learning, data science, digital marketing, cybersecurity, blockchain, Power BI and data visualisation. These should be understood as learning/exposure areas, not separate degrees.

NDIM PGDM Career Scope

Career outcomes vary by specialisation, internships, profile and role. Potential functions include finance, marketing, sales, consulting, operations, HR and business analysis.
