NDIM PGDM: Quick Answer

What is the course?

NDIM's PGDM is a two-year full-time management programme approved by AICTE. NDIM positions the programme around dual specialisations, industry interaction and corporate skill development. The institute's published 2026-28 course fee is ₹13.75 lakh, excluding optional hostel and international study-tour costs.

NDIM PGDM Programme Overview

The PGDM is designed to build management capability across core business functions while allowing students to develop specialist interests. NDIM also separately lists PGDM Finance and PGDM Marketing as programme routes.

NDIM PGDM Eligibility

Applicants need a bachelor's degree from a UGC/AIU-recognised university. For the 2026-28 admission cycle, NDIM stated a 50% minimum graduation score for General-category candidates and 45% for reserved-category candidates.

Students in their final year of graduation can apply subject to the institute's deadline and result conditions. Always check the current batch's admission notice before submitting the application.

NDIM PGDM Admission Process

  1. Submit the NDIM online application.
  2. Provide an accepted management entrance score such as CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT or a state common entrance score.
  3. Attend the institute's GD and PI process if shortlisted.
  4. Selection considers academics, entrance performance, GD/PI and relevant achievements or experience.

NDIM PGDM Curriculum & Specialisations

The programme is positioned around functional management areas and dual-specialisation opportunities. NDIM's programme pages separately identify Finance and Marketing routes.

GENERAL

PGDM

Broad management route suited to students who want flexibility across business functions.

FINANCE

PGDM Finance

Better aligned with banking, financial services, corporate finance and finance-analyst career interests.

MARKETING

PGDM Marketing

Designed for students targeting sales, brand, marketing, digital and market-facing roles.

NDIM also highlights exposure to emerging areas such as AI, machine learning, data science, digital marketing, cybersecurity, blockchain, Power BI and data visualisation in its current MBA-admission content. These should be treated as learning components/exposure areas rather than separate degrees.

NDIM PGDM Career Scope

Career outcomes depend heavily on specialisation, prior profile, internships, role type and individual performance. NDIM's placement ecosystem covers a wide range of companies and functions, but students should compare actual roles and salary distributions rather than assume every PGDM graduate receives the highest reported package.

Good fit for

  • Management careers in Delhi-NCR
  • Students seeking corporate exposure during the programme
  • Applicants wanting multiple accepted entrance exams
  • Students choosing Finance or Marketing as a direction

Compare before paying

  • Total cost including living expenses
  • Median/average salary, not just highest package
  • Role quality and sector mix
  • Placement eligibility and batch-level outcomes

NDIM PGDM FAQs

Is NDIM PGDM a two-year course?

Yes. NDIM describes the PGDM as a two-year full-time programme.

What is the NDIM PGDM fee?

The published 2026-28 course fee is ₹13.75 lakh: ₹6.90 lakh in Year 1 and ₹6.85 lakh in Year 2.

Does NDIM offer PGDM Finance and Marketing?

Yes. NDIM lists PGDM Finance and PGDM Marketing alongside its general PGDM offering.

Is NDIM PGDM equivalent to MBA?

NDIM states that its PGDM was granted MBA equivalence by AIU.
