How to Apply to BMU

BMU uses an online application system. Students should select the programme, review its eligibility and selection rules, complete the application, upload the required information and track the next stage through the university admissions system.

Important

BMU states that application timelines differ by programme. Its admission FAQ says UG applications generally become available from November and MBA applications from October.

Programme-wise Eligibility

Eligibility is programme-specific. For example, the MBA page requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50%, while undergraduate programmes have Class 12-based requirements. Candidates should always verify the exact programme page before applying.

Entrance Tests & Selection

BMU uses different selection routes across programmes. The MBA currently lists CAT, NMAT, GMAT, CMAT, XAT and GRE, followed by the university's selection process. UG programmes have their own admission criteria and may consider academic performance and programme-specific assessments.

Scholarships

BMU advertises merit, sportsperson, Haryana domicile, equity and defence ward scholarships or fee waivers. The university states that eligible students can receive scholarships of up to 100%, subject to programme and scholarship conditions.

Admission FAQs

When does BMU admission begin?

BMU's FAQ states that UG application forms are generally available from November and MBA forms from October.

Can I apply online?

Yes. BMU provides an online admissions application process.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. BMU lists multiple scholarship and fee-waiver categories, including merit and sports scholarships.
