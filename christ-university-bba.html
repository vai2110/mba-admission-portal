Christ University BBA: Quick Answer

For applicants

The standard BBA (Honours/Honours with Research) is a 4-year, 8-semester programme. The central eligibility rule is a pass in Class 12, while the published 2026 entrance-test route uses ET, SA, MP and PI for BBA at listed campuses.

What Does the Christ BBA Cover?

The BBA gives students a foundation in management, business, communication, decision-making and entrepreneurship. The programme can include specialisation choices such as Human Resources, Marketing, Finance and Business Analytics depending on the campus/programme variant.

Christ also offers specialised BBA variants such as Strategy and Business Analytics, Finance and International Business, Finance and Marketing Analytics and Decision Science. These should not be treated as identical programmes because eligibility, duration and fees can vary.

Who Can Apply for BBA?

For the standard BBA, the official eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12 (10+2). International-board applicants must follow the university's AIU-related eligibility rules.

Specialised BBA programmes can have additional academic conditions. For example, BBA Decision Science requires 60% at +2 in Science or Commerce with Mathematics, Statistics or Computer Science as one subject.

What Is the BBA Selection Process?

Christ's 2026 UG entrance-test document lists ET + SA + MP + PI for BBA at Bangalore Central and several other listed campuses. The SA tests written skills, MP is a 90-second topic presentation and PI is an interview. Academic performance is also considered in the published process material.

The exact process can vary for a specific BBA variant or campus, so the programme's selection notice should be checked before applying.

What Will You Study in BBA?

The programme combines core business subjects with areas such as finance, marketing, HR and analytics. The Honours/Honours with Research structure extends the academic pathway beyond the conventional degree and can include research-oriented work.

Students interested in analytics should compare the standard BBA with specialised options such as BBA Strategy and Business Analytics and BBA Finance and Marketing Analytics before applying.

If you want an MBA later

A BBA is a direct foundation for management studies, but the quality of internships, projects, quantitative skills and entrance-test preparation can matter more than the degree title alone.

Christ University BBA FAQs

Is Christ University BBA 3 years or 4 years?

The standard BBA (Honours/Honours with Research) listing is a 4-year, 8-semester programme. Some other programme variants can have different structures.

Is the Christ BBA entrance test compulsory?

For BBA in the published Bangalore Central entrance-test list, ET is followed by SA, MP and PI. The exact route depends on the programme and campus.

What is the minimum Class 12 percentage for standard BBA?

The central eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12 for BBA. Specialised BBA programmes can have higher or subject-specific requirements.

What is the BBA fee for students from other states?

The 2026 standard BBA fee table lists ₹2.75 lakh per year for other Indian states, plus the ₹8,000 non-refundable admission registration fee.
