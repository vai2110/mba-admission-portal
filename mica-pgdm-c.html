What is MICA PGDM-C?

MICA's PGDM-C is its flagship postgraduate management programme focused on strategic marketing and communications. The programme is designed around brand, consumer, culture, product, media and relationship-led business decisions.

Who is PGDM-C best suited for?

It is a strong fit for students who want careers in brand management, marketing strategy, communications, consumer insights, media, entertainment, sports business, sales and customer relationship management, especially where creative and cultural thinking matters.

MICA PGDM-C Eligibility and Admission Process

Applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree under the 10+2+3 pattern or equivalent qualification in any discipline, with the published minimum academic requirement. Final-year candidates can also apply subject to MICA's conditions.

Selection componentRole in the process
CAT / XAT / GMATValid management entrance score used in the School of Management evaluation.
MICATAssesses psychometric attributes, descriptive ability, aptitude and creative/analytical capabilities.
GEGroup Exercise for shortlisted candidates.
PIPersonal Interview as part of the final assessment stage.
Final meritHolistic evaluation determines the final selection.
Is a high CAT score alone enough for MICA PGDM-C?

No. MICA's process is multi-stage. CAT/XAT/GMAT is one input, while MICAT performance and the later GE/PI stages also matter.

PGDM-C Specialisations and Curriculum

MICA currently lists four major focus areas for PGDM-C:

Brand Strategy & Management

For students interested in brand positioning, consumer understanding and strategic brand building.

Product Innovation & Management

For students who want to connect consumer insight with product development and innovation.

Media, Entertainment & Sports Business

For careers spanning media businesses, entertainment ecosystems and sports marketing.

Sales & Customer Relationship Management

For students interested in customer-led growth, sales strategy and relationship management.

What makes the curriculum different?

MICA describes its PGP pedagogy as interdisciplinary, experiential and industry-oriented. The programme combines conceptual rigour with cultural intelligence, strategic thinking and practical exposure.

MICA PGDM-C Fees and Financial Aid

The published fee for the complete two-year residential PGDM-C programme is ₹28 lakh. Optional international immersion is separate.

MICA also provides merit-cum-means scholarships for eligible students. The institute's scholarship criteria include financial need, academic performance and attendance conditions.

MICA PGDM-C Placements and Career Roles

MICA's placement ecosystem spans marketing, advertising, consumer technology, FMCG, BFSI, consulting, media and other sectors. Reported recruiters include Google, Accenture Strategy & Consulting, Flipkart, IBM, Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Mastercard, L'Oréal, Nivea, PUMA India, Coca-Cola, Titan and others across its placement reports.

Who Should Choose MICA PGDM-C?

Good fit

  • You want a marketing, brand or communication-led career.
  • You enjoy consumer psychology, culture and storytelling alongside business.
  • You are interested in media, entertainment, sports or advertising ecosystems.
  • You want a specialised management programme rather than a broad generalist MBA.

Reconsider if

  • You want a heavily finance, operations or general-management oriented programme.
  • You dislike presentations, communication-heavy work or creative problem solving.
  • You are choosing MICA only because of the institute's placement headline.
  • The residential programme fee is outside your realistic financing capacity.

MICA PGDM-C FAQs

What is the MICA PGDM-C fee?

The published fee for the complete two-year residential PGDM-C programme is ₹28 lakh, excluding separately charged optional international immersion.

What is the MICA PGDM-C eligibility?

Candidates need a recognised bachelor's degree under the 10+2+3 pattern or equivalent qualification. MICA publishes a minimum academic requirement and conditions for final-year applicants.

Does MICA PGDM-C require CAT?

The School of Management admission process uses CAT, XAT or GMAT scores alongside MICAT and the GE/PI stages. A candidate should therefore plan for the full selection process rather than relying on one test.

What jobs can I get after MICA PGDM-C?

Career paths include brand management, marketing strategy, consumer insights, advertising, communication, media, account management, sales and customer relationship roles.

Is MICA PGDM-C better than a general MBA?

It depends on career goals. PGDM-C is especially suited to marketing, brand, communication and consumer-focused careers, while a general MBA may offer broader exposure across management functions.
