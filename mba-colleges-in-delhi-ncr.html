Which entrance exam do you need?

CAT is accepted by several leading full-time programmes in the region; some programmes may accept XAT, GMAT or other tests. Requirements differ between flagship, executive and specialised tracks.

  • Check the programme-specific admission page.
  • Confirm whether a separate institute application is required.
  • Review final-year rules and work-experience requirements.
  • Separate minimum qualifying cutoffs from actual interview-call outcomes.

Should you choose Delhi, Gurugram or Noida?

Compare commute, accommodation, public transport and internship/placement travel. A lower tuition fee can be offset by rent and daily transport. Also compare actual curriculum and placement functions for your target role; proximity to a corporate hub alone does not prove that a programme delivers the roles you want.

How should you compare total fees and placements?

  1. Calculate tuition plus housing, food, travel and loan costs.
  2. Use the latest official report for the exact programme and batch.
  3. Prioritise median salary and role distribution over highest CTC.
  4. Confirm whether the credential is an MBA degree or PGDM and check current approvals.
  5. Match the admission route to your exam score and profile.
