Department of Hospital Management and Hospice Studies at JMI

The Department of Hospital Management and Hospice Studies (DHMHS) at Jamia Millia Islamia focuses on management education for healthcare, hospitals and pharmaceutical organisations. The department aims to prepare graduates for administrative, operational, analytical and managerial responsibilities in healthcare delivery and allied sectors.

Important for applicants: The department's MBA programmes are management degrees focused on healthcare/hospital or pharmaceutical settings. The official programme page should be used to verify the current intake, eligibility, entrance-test structure and fee before applying.

JMI MBA Healthcare and Hospital Management

The official course page lists the MBA (Healthcare and Hospital Management) as a regular, four-semester programme with 40 seats. It is designed for graduates who want to develop management skills for hospitals, healthcare providers and related institutions.

Eligibility

The published course page requires a bachelor's degree or equivalent in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate from a recognised institution.

Selection process

The published selection framework includes a JMI entrance test followed by Group Discussion/Personal Interview or aptitude assessment for shortlisted candidates. The displayed weightage is 170 marks for the written test and 30 marks for GD/PI/aptitude assessment. The entrance paper covers health and environmental awareness, English comprehension and verbal ability, quantitative aptitude and numerical ability, reasoning and data analysis.

JMI MBA Pharmaceutical Management

The department also lists MBA Pharmaceutical Management, including an industry-sponsored route and a self-financed route. This programme area is relevant to students interested in business and management roles across pharmaceutical companies, healthcare products, distribution and related commercial functions. Do not assume the Healthcare and Hospital Management eligibility, seats or selection process applies unchanged to Pharmaceutical Management; check its own course page and current admission notice.

JMI MBA Healthcare and Hospital Management Fees

The official programme page states that the programme fee should be checked in the prospectus of the current session. It also displays a total programme fee of ₹2,10,000 payable yearly, and ₹2,60,000 per year for industry-sponsored candidates. Treat these as figures displayed on the programme page and verify the current admission-year fee schedule before payment.

Career Opportunities after MBA Healthcare and Hospital Management

Graduates may explore roles in hospital administration, healthcare operations, quality and process improvement, medical services coordination, healthcare project management, hospital finance, patient services, healthcare consulting and health-sector business development. MBA Pharmaceutical Management graduates may target commercial, product, sales, distribution, market access and operations roles in the pharmaceutical ecosystem. Actual opportunities depend on employer requirements and the candidate's experience.

Is a medical background compulsory?

The published MBA Healthcare and Hospital Management eligibility accepts a bachelor's degree in any discipline, subject to the stated minimum marks. Applicants from non-medical backgrounds should still review the course content and career expectations to understand the healthcare context.
