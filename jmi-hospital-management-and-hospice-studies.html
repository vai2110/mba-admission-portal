Quick Answer

The Department of Hospital Management and Hospice Studies at Jamia Millia Islamia offers specialized academic programs in healthcare administration and palliative care. Admissions are conducted through the centralized JMI portal, and students are advised to consult the official JMI Prospectus for specific eligibility and cutoff details.

Course Overview

The Department of Hospital Management and Hospice Studies is a specialized academic unit at Jamia Millia Islamia. It is dedicated to the interdisciplinary study of healthcare administration and palliative care, preparing students for leadership roles in hospital systems and hospice organizations. The department operates under the university's Academic Council guidelines.

Admission & Selection Logic

Admission is conducted through the centralized Jamia Millia Islamia admission process. Selection is based on merit, typically determined by entrance examinations as specified in the annual university prospectus.
