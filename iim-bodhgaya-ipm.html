Who should choose the IPM?

IPM is not simply a five-year version of an MBA. It changes the timing of the management decision: you commit to an integrated management education immediately after Class 12 instead of first completing a separate bachelor's degree.

Choose IPM if…

You are already confident that management is your preferred career direction and want early exposure to business, analytics and leadership.

Think twice if…

You are unsure about management or want maximum freedom to change your academic discipline during undergraduate study.

What is the IPM eligibility for 2026?

The official 2026-31 admission policy requires candidates to have passed or be appearing in Class 12 in the specified 2025/2026 cycle with 60% or more, with 55% for SC/ST/PwD/Transgender candidates. Class 10 marks of 60% or 55% for the specified reserved categories are also required, and the Class 10 passing year must not be before 2023.

How does JIPMAT 2026 selection work?

Admission to the Indian 2026-31 IPM route is through JIPMAT 2026, conducted by NTA on behalf of IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu. The admission policy states that JIPMAT performance is used for the merit list, with candidates needing to meet the specified category-wise sectional percentile threshold to be considered.

Preparation implication

Do not prepare for JIPMAT only by chasing an overall score. The policy explicitly includes sectional eligibility, so your preparation should protect performance across all test areas.

What should a JIPMAT aspirant work on?

  • Quantitative aptitude and speed-based arithmetic problem solving.
  • Logical reasoning and pattern recognition.
  • Verbal ability, reading comprehension and vocabulary.
  • Timed mocks with sectional analysis rather than only total-score review.

How is the five-year academic journey structured?

The first three years follow a semester structure, while the final two years follow the prevailing MBA-style trimester structure. This lets students move from broad academic foundations into progressively deeper management education.

What degree do you receive after IPM?

Students who complete the full programme requirements are awarded a Bachelor of Business Administration and Master of Business Administration by IIM Bodh Gaya. The programme also provides an exit option after completing the first three years, under which a Bachelor of Business Administration is awarded.

Why this matters

The exit provision creates a meaningful fallback compared with committing to an undifferentiated five-year course. However, students should still verify the current academic and exit requirements before joining.

What can you do after IPM?

The integrated route is designed to prepare students for the same broad management ecosystem that includes consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, product and general management. The advantage is continuity: career preparation begins earlier and the final two years follow the MBA structure.

Consulting & strategy

Strong fit for students who build structured problem-solving, communication and business-analysis skills.

Finance & analytics

Students can build quantitative and financial capabilities before entering the MBA-style phase.

Marketing & general management

Broad management exposure can support customer, sales, marketing and cross-functional roles.

Do not automatically apply the flagship MBA placement average to IPM. Programme-specific audited outcomes should be used when available for the relevant IPM graduating cohort.

IPM FAQs

What is the duration of IPM?

Five years.

Which exam is used for 2026?

JIPMAT 2026 is the admission route for the Indian 2026-31 IPM batch.

What are the Class 10 and 12 marks requirements?

The 2026 policy lists 60% in both, with 55% for SC/ST/PwD/Transgender candidates, subject to the detailed policy conditions.

How many seats are there?

The 2026 policy states that intake will be around 120.

What happens after three years?

Students meeting the programme's exit requirements can receive a BBA; those completing the full requirements receive BBA and MBA.

Should I choose IPM over a regular bachelor's degree?

Choose IPM when you are comfortable committing early to management. Choose a regular bachelor's degree if flexibility and exploration matter more.
