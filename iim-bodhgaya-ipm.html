Who should choose IPM?

The IPM is fundamentally a choice about timing. Instead of completing a separate undergraduate degree and then competing for a postgraduate management programme, you commit to an integrated five-year management pathway immediately after Class 12.

Choose IPM if…

You are already confident that management is the direction you want and value continuity, a structured cohort and early management exposure.

Think twice if…

You are unsure about your career direction or want the flexibility of exploring multiple disciplines through a conventional bachelor's degree.

How does the five-year journey work?

The value of an integrated programme comes from continuity. Students can progressively move from foundational quantitative, communication and social-science learning into management concepts, analytics, strategy and advanced business subjects.

StageTypical objective
Early stageBuild quantitative, communication, analytical and broad academic foundations.
Middle stageDevelop business fundamentals and understand organisations, markets and economic systems.
Advanced stageMove towards higher-level management, analytics, strategy, projects and career preparation.

What can you do after IPM?

An integrated management programme can lead towards the same broad management ecosystem that attracts MBA graduates: consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, product and general management. The key advantage is that career preparation starts earlier.

However, placement expectations should be evaluated from the relevant IPM cohort once programme-specific audited outcomes are available. Do not automatically apply the flagship MBA placement average to the IPM cohort.

FAQs

How long is IPM?

Five years.

Which exam is used for 2026?

IIM Bodh Gaya lists JIPMAT 2026 for the 2026-31 IPM admission cycle.

Can international students apply?

Yes. The institute publishes an international IPM admission route through SAT in addition to its JIPMAT route.

Should I choose IPM over a regular bachelor's degree?

Choose IPM when you are comfortable committing early to management. Choose a regular bachelor's degree if academic/career flexibility is more important.
