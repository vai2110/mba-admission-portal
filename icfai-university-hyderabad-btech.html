IFHE Hyderabad B.Tech Overview

IcfaiTech is IFHE's technology-focused school. Current listings include B.Tech routes in Computer Science Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, IoT and related technology areas, along with Electronics & Communication and Mechanical Engineering with Robotics and AI.

Best fit

These programmes are most relevant if you want a private-university engineering degree with strong exposure to computing and emerging technologies. Compare curriculum, labs, internships and actual placement outcomes before choosing a specialisation.

B.Tech Career Scope

Career outcomes depend heavily on specialisation, technical depth, projects, internships and placement performance. CSE, AI, data science, cloud and cyber security can lead to software, data, cloud, security, testing and technology-support roles. Students should build demonstrable projects rather than relying on the degree title alone.

Student Discussion: Questions Before Choosing B.Tech

Students commonly compare private engineering colleges on total cost, coding culture, placements, internships, peer group and campus infrastructure. These are decision themes rather than guaranteed outcomes.

  • How much time will you spend on coding and projects?
  • What is the latest programme-specific placement data?
  • Does the campus support the technical specialisation you want?
  • What is the full four-year cost including hostel?

IFHE Hyderabad B.Tech FAQs

What is the B.Tech fee at IFHE?

The current schedule lists ₹20,000 admission fee and ₹1.40 lakh per semester, plus a refundable caution deposit and living expenses.

Which B.Tech specialisations are available?

Current listings include CSE, AI, AI & Data Science, AI & ML, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, IoT, ECE and Mechanical Engineering with Robotics & AI.

Where is the campus?

The IFHE campus is at Donthanapally, Shankarapalli Road, Hyderabad, Telangana.
