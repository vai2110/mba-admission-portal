How to Apply to Jamia Hamdard in 2026

  1. Open the Jamia Hamdard admission portal and select the programme.
  2. Read the programme eligibility and entrance requirement before paying the application fee.
  3. Enter academic and personal details exactly as shown on official documents.
  4. Upload the required marksheets, entrance score and category/supporting documents where applicable.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee and submit the form.
  6. Track the university notice page for merit lists, interview/GDPI, entrance tests or counselling.
  7. After selection, pay the programme fee by the stated deadline and complete document verification.

Important: Filling CUET or another national exam form does not by itself complete Jamia Hamdard admission. The university's prospectus states that applicants for CUET-related UG courses must also fill the Jamia Hamdard application form.

NEET-Based Admissions at Jamia Hamdard

Jamia Hamdard's 2026 admission notices specifically include an important notice for UG NEET-based applicants. For MBBS and other regulated health programmes, the applicable national counselling/regulatory process must be followed in addition to the university's own requirements.

Do not use old course-specific NEET cutoffs as the 2026 cutoff. Cutoffs depend on the programme, counselling round, category, seat type and year.
