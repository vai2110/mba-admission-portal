How to Apply to Jamia Hamdard in 2026

  1. Open the Jamia Hamdard admission portal and select the programme.
  2. Read the programme eligibility and entrance requirement before paying the application fee.
  3. Enter academic and personal details exactly as shown on official documents.
  4. Upload the required marksheets, entrance score and category/supporting documents where applicable.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee and submit the form.
  6. Track the university notice page for merit lists, interview/GDPI, entrance tests or counselling.
  7. After selection, pay the programme fee by the stated deadline and complete document verification.

Important: Filling CUET or another national exam form does not by itself complete Jamia Hamdard admission. The university's prospectus states that applicants for CUET-related UG courses must also fill the Jamia Hamdard application form.

Jamia Hamdard Admission 2026 FAQs: 12 High-Intent Questions

Is Jamia Hamdard admission open for 2026-27?

Yes. The current university admission notices state that applications for UG and PG programmes are live. The same notice page lists programme-specific updates for MBA GD/PI, NEET-based UG admissions, MPT/MOT entrance tests and PhD. Deadlines differ by programme and round, so students should follow the latest notice for their course rather than assume one common closing date.

How can I apply for Jamia Hamdard admission 2026?

Apply through the official Jamia Hamdard admission portal. Select your programme, read the 2026-27 eligibility and selection conditions, complete the application, upload the required documents and pay the applicable fee. After submission, monitor admission notices and selection-list updates. The university says admission confirmation is communicated by the JH Admission Cell.

Which entrance exams are accepted by Jamia Hamdard?

There is no single entrance exam for the entire university. Depending on the programme, the published admission framework has used CUET, NEET-UG, JEE Main and CLAT, while other programmes can use qualifying-exam merit, university entrance tests, interviews or another approved route. The exact 2026 requirement must be checked for the specific programme.

Does Jamia Hamdard accept CUET for admission?

Yes, CUET is used for applicable programmes, but it is not a universal requirement for every course. Subject combinations, minimum marks and the university application requirement vary by programme. A CUET candidate should therefore read the Jamia Hamdard programme notice in addition to the CUET rules.

Is the Jamia Hamdard application form mandatory after appearing for CUET?

For CUET-linked UG admissions, the university's admission guidance requires the Jamia Hamdard application process in addition to the national entrance process where applicable. A CUET score alone should not be treated as a completed Jamia Hamdard application.

What is the eligibility for Jamia Hamdard UG courses?

UG eligibility is programme-specific. Applicants generally need a recognised 10+2 qualification, but required subjects, minimum marks, entrance examination and other conditions differ. Pharmacy and regulated health programmes have their own subject requirements. The 2026-27 prospectus should be used for the exact course.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA admission process?

The 2026-27 MBA process includes the university application followed by the applicable shortlisting and GD/PI/interview process. The university has issued MBA General GD/PI and interview notices, including multiple rounds. Final admission involves the selection list, fee payment and document formalities.

What was the first-round MBA 2026 application deadline?

An official February 2026 MBA notice specified 31 March 2026 as the application deadline for its first GD/PI round. Later notices introduced further rounds, so students should use the latest MBA notice rather than treating the first-round date as the final deadline for the entire cycle.

Is NEET required for Jamia Hamdard courses?

NEET is required for programmes for which the university and applicable regulator/counselling authority prescribe it. The 2026 admission page specifically carries a notice for UG NEET-based applicants. NEET should not be assumed to be required for unrelated management, commerce or other non-medical programmes.

How does Jamia Hamdard selection work?

Selection is programme-specific and can involve national entrance scores, qualifying-exam merit, university tests, interviews, GD/PI or counselling. The 2026-27 prospectus is the controlling document. Students should check both eligibility and selection criteria before applying.

Does Jamia Hamdard provide hostel to all admitted students?

No. The university explicitly states that hostel accommodation is limited and university admission does not guarantee hostel allotment. Students apply through the hostel/student process; allotted residents pay hostel charges in advance and join the mess. Hostel should therefore be treated as a separate allocation and cost decision.

How can I get a fee refund after cancelling Jamia Hamdard admission?

Jamia Hamdard's admissions support information says fee refunds are handled according to UGC fee-refund norms. Students should submit the cancellation/refund request through the prescribed university process and retain payment and application records for follow-up.
