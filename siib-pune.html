SIIB Pune: College Overview

Symbiosis Institute of International Business (SIIB), Pune is a constituent of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The institute is located at G. No. 174/1, Hinjawadi, Taluka-Mulshi, Pune, and focuses on management education across International Business, Agri Business and Sustainability Management.

SIIB's flagship MBA in International Business is a two-year residential programme with specialisations in Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain Management and Business Analytics. The institute also offers MBA programmes in Agri Business and Sustainability Management, giving students a choice of sector-focused career paths.

Is SIIB Pune worth considering?

SIIB can be a strong option if you want a SNAP-based MBA and are specifically interested in international business, supply chains, finance, analytics, agribusiness or sustainability. The right choice depends on your SNAP performance, preferred programme, total residential cost and target career.

SIIB Pune Courses & Eligibility

MBA – International Business

Two-year residential programme with Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain Management and Business Analytics specialisations. Intake listed by SIIB's current admissions material: 150.

MBA – Agri Business

Sector-focused management programme covering agribusiness marketing, finance, supply chain, taxation and agriculture/WTO-related areas.

MBA – Sustainability Management

Focuses on sustainability, energy, ESG, renewable energy, sustainable finance and environmental management.

Eligibility

For SIIB's full-time MBA programmes, candidates generally need a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the applicable reserved-category minimum is 45%. Candidates should always verify the programme-specific eligibility in the current admission notice before applying.

For the regular domestic route, appearing for SNAP and registering for the SIIB programme are mandatory parts of the admission process.

SIIB Pune Fees, Hostel & Mess

For the 2026-28 MBA International Business batch, SIIB lists academic fees of ₹5,15,500 for each of four academic installments. Hostel and mess charges are additional. Shared hostel accommodation is stated to be mandatory for the entire programme duration.

Who Should Apply to SIIB Pune?

SIIB Pune FAQs

Does SIIB Pune accept SNAP?

Yes. SIIB requires candidates to appear for SNAP and separately complete SIIB programme registration. SNAP-based shortlisting is followed by GE and PI for shortlisted candidates.

What is the SIIB Pune SNAP cutoff?

SIIB does not publish a fixed overall percentile cutoff on its current admission pages. The portal should therefore treat any percentile benchmark as expected or indicative, not official.

What programmes does SIIB Pune offer?

SIIB offers MBA programmes in International Business, Agri Business and Sustainability Management. MBA International Business includes specialisations in Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain Management and Business Analytics.

What is the SIIB Pune MBA fee?

For the 2026-28 MBA International Business batch, academic fees are ₹5,15,500 per installment. Hostel, mess and refundable deposits are additional.

What is the highest package at SIIB Pune?

The latest official MBA International Business placement report for the 2024-26 batch reports a highest CTC of ₹27.60 LPA.

Is hostel compulsory at SIIB Pune?

SIIB states that shared hostel accommodation is available on campus and is mandatory for the entire duration of the programme in its published fee structure.

What is the SIIB Pune admission process?

Register and appear for SNAP, select SIIB in the SNAP form, complete SIIB registration and payment, get shortlisted based on SNAP performance, and participate in Group Exercise and Personal Interview.

Where is SIIB Pune located?

SIIB is located at G. No. 174/1, Hinjawadi, Taluka-Mulshi, Pune, Maharashtra.
