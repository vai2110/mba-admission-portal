Programme Overview

IIM Kozhikode's Post Graduate Programme in Finance (PGP-Finance) is a two-year, full-time master's-level programme designed for students who want to build careers in core finance and allied financial-services domains. IIMK specifically identifies areas such as corporate finance, investment banking and wealth management among the programme's intended career directions.

The programme is positioned as a specialised finance qualification rather than a general MBA with a finance concentration. Its design combines finance theory and application, with industry interaction and exposure intended to connect global finance practices with the Indian context.

What makes IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance different?
The programme is built around an exclusive focus on finance. It is therefore most relevant for candidates who already know that they want finance, fintech, banking or financial-services roles and prefer a specialised two-year management qualification over a broad general-management programme.

Eligibility for IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance 2026–28

For the 2026–28 admission cycle, IIM Kozhikode's official policy requires candidates with a graduation degree to have at least 60% aggregate marks. Candidates without a graduation degree but with CA, CMA (ICWA) or CS must have at least 50% aggregate marks in the relevant professional qualification.

Candidates may use a valid CAT, GRE or GMAT score from the test window specified in the 2026–28 policy. CAT applicants must obtain a positive scaled score in all CAT 2025 sections. Final-year candidates may also apply subject to the conditions in the official admission policy.

IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance Admission & Selection Process

IIM Kozhikode follows a three-stage process for PGP-Finance admissions. The process is programme-specific and should not be confused with the selection process for IIMK's flagship PGP.

Stage 1: Registration

Eligible candidates complete the programme-specific online registration process. Registration is a pre-interview stage and does not itself mean that a candidate has received an interview call.

Stage 2: Shortlisting

IIMK prepares a shortlist using an Aggregate Index Score (AIS), which considers aptitude-test performance, academic performance, work experience, suitability score and gender diversity score.

Stage 3: WAT & PI

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Written Ability Test and Personal Interview. The final merit list considers CAT/GRE/GMAT performance, PI, WAT, work experience and suitability, as specified in the official selection process.

Finance relevance in the combined placement process

Finance accounted for 19.2% of the combined final-placement outcomes in 2025. In the 2024 report, finance accounted for 28% of offers, with the official report naming recruiters such as Axis Bank, BNP Paribas, Citi, D. E. Shaw, Deutsche Bank, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan Chase & Co. and Standard Chartered among the finance recruiters. The 2024 report also highlighted front-end investment banking and PE/VC roles from firms including Ambit Capital, Equirus, GEF Capital Partners, Lodha Capital Markets, Nomura, Pavestone, Piramal Alternatives and Xponentia Capital.

Summer internship context

IIMK's 2024 summer placement report for PGP, PGP-LSM and PGP-Finance recorded 147 recruiters, a highest stipend of ₹7 lakh and an average stipend of ₹2.68 lakh for the participating batch. As with final placements, these are combined figures and should not be interpreted as PGP-Finance-only statistics.

For a finance aspirant, the more useful takeaway is not just the overall mean CTC. Review the official placement report's finance-domain roles, recruiter mix and offer share alongside the combined salary statistics before judging the programme's fit.

Who Should Apply for IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance?

Finance-focused graduates

A strong fit for graduates who want to enter finance rather than pursue a completely generalist management path.

Quantitative and analytical candidates

The programme is particularly relevant for candidates comfortable with analytical problem-solving and finance-oriented career decisions.

Early-career professionals

Work experience is not a minimum eligibility requirement, although relevant experience is considered during the selection process.

Career switchers into finance

Suitable for professionals who can clearly justify a move into banking, financial services, fintech or core finance roles.

Who Should Think Twice Before Choosing PGP-Finance?

  • Candidates who are unsure whether they want finance or general management and are choosing the programme only for the IIM Kozhikode brand.
  • Students looking for a broad MBA experience without a strong preference for finance-oriented careers.
  • Candidates who do not enjoy quantitative, analytical or finance-related problem-solving.
Realistic view: PGP-Finance offers a specialised career direction. The right comparison is not simply “IIM Kozhikode versus another college”; students should also compare a specialised finance programme with a general MBA based on the role and industry they want after graduation.

FAQs

What is IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance?
PGP-Finance is a two-year, full-time master's-level programme with an exclusive focus on financial theory and application. IIMK positions it for careers in core finance, fintech, banking and financial services.
What is the PGP-Finance fee for 2026–28?
IIM Kozhikode's current fee structure for the 2026–28 batch lists a total programme fee of ₹23.50 lakh for PGP-Finance. Students should check the latest official fee document for the detailed payment schedule and components.
Can freshers apply for IIM Kozhikode PGP-Finance?
Yes. Work experience is not a minimum eligibility criterion. IIMK's official programme information also states that the admission process aims to admit a mix of experienced professionals and fresh graduates.
Which exams are accepted for PGP-Finance admission?
For the 2026–28 cycle, IIMK's official admission policy allows CAT, GRE or GMAT scores subject to the score-window and programme-specific eligibility conditions.
Does PGP-Finance have a separate placement report?
Students should rely only on programme-specific official reports when comparing outcomes. IIMK's current placement ecosystem includes PGP-Finance, but institute-wide placement figures should not automatically be treated as PGP-Finance-specific.
Is PGP-Finance the same as an MBA in Finance?
It is a specialised postgraduate programme in finance, while the exact degree nomenclature and academic structure should be interpreted from IIMK's official programme documentation. Its core positioning is distinct from a general MBA with a finance specialisation.
