IMT Nagpur PGDM Marketing: Overview

PGDM-Marketing is one of the three programme labels listed in IMT Nagpur's official admissions material. It is a two-year, full-time residential management programme designed for students who want deeper exposure to marketing and related business functions.

The programme sits inside the institute's broader management curriculum, so students still develop foundations in economics, analytics, operations, finance, HR and general management before building functional expertise.

Who should choose PGDM Marketing?

Choose it if you want careers in brand management, digital marketing, sales, customer strategy, market research, growth or related commercial roles and want a structured management-school environment.

Admission to IMT Nagpur PGDM Marketing

Selection is based on a consolidated assessment of CAT/XAT/CMAT/GMAT, academic record, work experience and PI performance. Work experience is not compulsory. The institute says there is no pre-decided cutoff.

PGDM Marketing Curriculum & Electives

IMT Nagpur's published elective material includes marketing subjects such as brand management, digital marketing, marketing analytics, advanced marketing research, customer relationship management, integrated marketing communication, sales and distribution, services marketing, international marketing, pricing and retail management.

PGDM Marketing Career Opportunities

Typical career paths include brand management, product marketing, digital marketing, sales, business development, category management, market research, customer experience and growth roles.

IMT Nagpur's broader placement process is common across its programmes. Students should therefore compare role mix and sector-wise opportunities in the latest placement report instead of assuming that the highest package is specific to marketing.

PGDM Marketing Fees

The official 2025-27 fee sheet lists the same ₹15 lakh total for PGDM, PGDM-Marketing and PGDM-Financial Management. The fee sheet includes admission, academic, alumni and refundable caution components; the FAQ says the total includes hostel and mess charges.

IMT Nagpur PGDM Marketing FAQs

What is the PGDM Marketing fee at IMT Nagpur?

The official 2025-27 fee reference is ₹15 lakh including the listed residential cost components.

Is work experience required?

No. Freshers can apply.

What exams are accepted?

CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT are considered in the selection process.

Is the cutoff fixed?

No. IMT Nagpur says there is no pre-decided cutoff.
