IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA: Programme and Department

The Department of Management Studies and Industrial Engineering (DMSIE) at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad offers a two-year MBA and a two-year MBA in Business Analytics. The department's MBA began in 1997 and its MBA in Business Analytics was introduced in 2020. Its distinctive context is management education inside a technology-focused IIT, with department activity and industry/research connections spanning mining, minerals, energy, petroleum, industrial engineering and wider business functions.

Who should consider this MBA?

Applicants interested in management careers connected to industrial and technology-led sectors, operations, energy, mining, BFSI, IT or business analytics should examine the programme. The institute name alone is not enough to decide: compare the latest MBA-specific placement report, curriculum, fee, role mix and your own career goal.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Eligibility

Regular MBA: A bachelor's degree in any discipline, or recognised equivalent professional qualification, from a recognised university, with first class or at least 60% marks/6.0 CGPA. The 2026–27 notice specified 55% marks/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

MBA in Business Analytics: The 2026–27 notice required a B.E./B.Tech degree in any discipline from a recognised university/institute, with at least 60% marks/6.0 CGPA, or 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Final-year candidates were permitted to apply provisionally, subject to completing the qualifying degree requirements and producing the required proof by the deadline in the admission notice. Both programmes required a valid CAT score for the relevant cycle.

Can a non-engineering graduate apply for MBA?

Yes, the regular MBA eligibility in the 2026–27 brochure accepted graduates from any discipline. The MBA in Business Analytics had a separate B.E./B.Tech eligibility requirement in that cycle.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Curriculum: What Do Students Study?

The institute's published Management Studies curriculum includes core management and quantitative subjects such as Organisational Behaviour, Decision Modelling, Managerial Economics, Management Principles and Practice, Financial Accounting and Reporting, Corporate Finance, Project Management, Research Methodology and Statistics, Operations Management, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management and a Business Analytics Lab.

The department's published elective list includes Consumer Behaviour, Financial Analytics, Sales and Distribution Management, Materials Management, Investment Analysis and Portfolio Management, Management of Banks and Financial Institutions, Advertising and Promotions Management, Human Resource Development, Service Operations Management and Management of Self & Business Analytics. Elective availability can change by semester and cohort; this is a sample of published courses, not a promise that every elective runs every year.

This curriculum makes the programme relevant to students exploring finance, marketing, HR, operations, industrial management and analytics. Applicants should compare the regular MBA and MBA Business Analytics curriculum directly with the latest official brochure before choosing.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Scholarships

The Office of the Dean of Students' Welfare lists Merit-cum-Means (MCM) scholarship for regular two-year MBA students. The institute describes the award as merit-cum-means based for the top 35% of enrolled students: tuition-fee refund is listed for the top 35%, and the top 25% are additionally eligible for ₹1,000 per month for up to 10 months. A Benevolent Fund Scholarship is also listed for eligible two-year MBA students, offering partial tuition-fee refund subject to income criteria.

These schemes are conditional and competitive; they are not automatic fee waivers. The Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) entry on the same institute page is for regular UG courses, so MBA applicants should not assume it applies to them. Confirm current eligibility, income criteria, CGPA conditions, application deadlines and availability with the DSW office.

What Makes IIT (ISM) Dhanbad's MBA Different?

DMSIE's institutional profile describes consultancy projects, executive development programmes, research collaborations and industry interaction, including work connected to mining, minerals, energy and petroleum. That setting can be relevant to management applicants targeting industrial operations, energy, natural resources, infrastructure, BFSI or technology businesses. It does not mean every MBA student receives a particular project, internship or recruiter offer; access depends on the live academic and placement cycle.

The 2025 NIRF India Rankings placed IIT (ISM) Dhanbad at rank 48 in the Management category. This is the institute's Management-category rank, not a ranking exclusively for its MBA course.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA FAQs

Does IIT (ISM) Dhanbad accept CAT?

Yes. The 2026–27 admission notice required a valid CAT 2025 score. Check the next official notice for the CAT year accepted for 2027–29.

Can a BA, BCom or BSc graduate apply for the MBA?

The 2026–27 regular MBA eligibility accepted a bachelor's degree in any discipline if the candidate met the marks/CGPA and CAT requirements. MBA Business Analytics had a B.E./B.Tech requirement.

What is the MBA CAT cutoff at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad?

The official notice says CAT percentile is used for shortlisting but does not state a fixed universal percentile cutoff. No guaranteed cutoff should be inferred.

What is the MBA fee for two years?

The 2026–27 brochure's four semester fees total ₹3,85,500 for UR/OBC/EWS and ₹85,500 for SC/ST/PwD, plus mess charges stated at ₹19,500 per semester. The institute said fees were under revision; verify the next intake's schedule.

What is the average MBA placement package?

The official 2024–25 report lists ₹10.48 LPA average CTC and ₹10.38 LPA median CTC for 41 MBA students placed.

How many seats are there in MBA and MBA Business Analytics?

The 2026–27 brochure listed 90 regular MBA seats and 30 MBA Business Analytics seats.

Is MBA Business Analytics open to all graduates?

In the 2026–27 admission notice, MBA Business Analytics required a B.E./B.Tech degree in any discipline. Check the next brochure for any change.

When will IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA 2027–29 applications open?

The next official schedule was not available in the institute material reviewed as of 17 November 2026. Follow the official MBA admission portal for dates.
