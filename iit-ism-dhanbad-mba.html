MBA Eligibility 2026

For the 2026-27 intake, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad states that candidates need a graduation degree or equivalent recognised qualification in any discipline with first class or at least 60% marks/6.0 CGPA. The threshold is 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates as specified by the institute.

Which entrance exam is accepted?

The 2026-27 MBA admission notice requires a valid CAT 2025 score for MBA and MBA in Business Analytics.

MBA Selection Process

The current admission cycle uses the CAT score as the qualifying test route followed by the institute's selection process, including personal interview. Candidates can apply for both MBA programmes through the institute portal.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA FAQs

Does IIT (ISM) Dhanbad accept CAT for MBA?

Yes. The 2026-27 admission notice requires a valid CAT 2025 score.

What is the MBA eligibility?

Graduation in any discipline with first class or 60%/6.0 CGPA; 55%/5.5 CGPA is specified for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Can I apply for both MBA programmes?

Yes. The institute states that candidates can apply for both MBA and MBA in Business Analytics.
