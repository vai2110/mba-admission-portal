IIM Calcutta MBA Placements: What Do the Latest Numbers Tell Students?

IIM Calcutta's flagship MBA placement outcomes are best understood through the scale of recruiter participation, the number of offers generated and the distribution of opportunities across consulting, finance and management roles. In the latest detailed final placement report currently reflected on this page, the MBA batch received 573 offers for 465 students, with 190 firms participating in the placement process.

The reported salary outcomes show the level of opportunities available across the batch. The placement cycle recorded an average yearly compensation of ₹35.07 LPA and a median compensation of ₹33.67 LPA. For students comparing MBA colleges, the median is particularly useful because it gives additional context beyond the highest package or a small number of exceptional offers.

  • 573 offers were made during the reported final placement process for the flagship MBA batch.
  • 190 participating firms created opportunities across consulting, finance, technology, marketing and general management.
  • ₹35.07 LPA average and ₹33.67 LPA median compensation provide the key salary benchmark for the reported batch.
  • Consulting led recruitment with 232 offers, making it the largest career destination for MBA graduates.
  • Finance generated 86 offers, reinforcing IIM Calcutta's strong position for students targeting financial services careers.
  • The top salary quartile averaged ₹50.86 LPA, reflecting the premium available for highly competitive roles.
How students should read these numbers

The total offer count can be higher than the number of students because of placement policies that may allow students to explore or receive additional opportunities during the process. Therefore, students should consider salary trends, recruiter diversity and sector-wise hiring alongside the total number of offers.

Why MBA, MBAEx and PGPEX-VLM Placement Figures Should Not Be Mixed

IIM Calcutta offers programmes designed for different career stages. The flagship two-year MBA primarily serves students building or transitioning into management careers, while MBAEx and PGPEX-VLM cater to experienced professionals with different industry backgrounds and career objectives.

The course-wise comparison below separates these outcomes so that students can benchmark the programme that matches their own experience level and career objective instead of relying on one combined "IIM Calcutta placement package" figure.

What the year-wise trend means for MBA aspirants

The reported average compensation increased from ₹28 LPA in the 2020 placement data to ₹35.07 LPA in the 2023 placement report, while the reported median increased from ₹25.5 LPA to ₹33.67 LPA. The narrowing difference between average and median in the later data also provides useful context on salary distribution across the batch.

Important while comparing placement years

Batch size, recruiter mix, economic conditions and the methodology used to report compensation can vary across placement cycles. Students should use year-wise figures to understand the direction and scale of outcomes rather than treating historical figures as a guaranteed salary prediction.

What IIM Calcutta Placement Data Means for Students

For CAT aspirants targeting the flagship MBA

Consulting is the strongest recruitment destination in the reported placement data, followed by finance and general management. Students targeting these sectors can use the recruiter mix and year-wise salary trends to assess career fit.

For experienced professionals considering MBAEx

The programme's placement outcomes should be evaluated alongside a candidate's existing work experience. The latest data shows opportunities across IT, consulting, BFSI, Data & AI and other experienced-hire career paths.

For manufacturing and operations professionals

PGPEX-VLM provides a more specialised placement route. Its recruitment outcomes should be assessed in relation to careers in consulting, operations, supply chain, manufacturing and business transformation.

Do not choose a college based only on the highest package

Average and median compensation, recruiter participation, sector-wise hiring and year-wise consistency provide a more useful picture for students than a single highest salary figure.

IIM Calcutta Placements FAQs

What is the average package for the flagship IIM Calcutta MBA?

The detailed placement data reflected on this page reports an average yearly compensation of ₹35.07 LPA and a median of ₹33.67 LPA for the reported flagship MBA placement batch.

How many offers were made during IIM Calcutta MBA placements?

The reported flagship MBA placement process recorded 573 offers for 465 students. The total number of offers can be higher than the number of students because of the placement process and policies governing multiple opportunities.

Which sector recruits the most from IIM Calcutta MBA?

Consulting was the largest recruitment sector in the placement data reflected on this page, with 232 offers. Finance and general management were also major career destinations.

What is the latest MBAEx average package shown on this page?

The latest detailed MBAEx statistics reflected here report an average CTC of ₹35.37 LPA and a median CTC of ₹32.88 LPA.

What is the PGPEX-VLM average package in 2026?

The PGPEX-VLM placement statistics reflected on this page report an average CTC of ₹24.57 LPA, a median CTC of ₹22 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹38.55 LPA for 2026.

Should MBA, MBAEx and PGPEX-VLM placement packages be compared directly?

No. These programmes cater to students at different career stages and have different candidate profiles and recruiter mixes. Students should primarily compare placement outcomes within programmes designed for candidates with similar levels of work experience.
