How to Apply to Chitkara University in 2026

Chitkara uses an online application process. The official admissions portal states that applicants must enter their name as it appears on the qualifying-examination marks card and that an application is not considered complete until the required application fee is paid.

StepWhat to do
1. Choose programmeCheck the exact programme, eligibility, selection route and 2026 fee document.
2. RegisterCreate an account on the official Chitkara admissions portal.
3. Fill applicationEnter personal, academic and contact information exactly as required.
4. Upload/submit documentsKeep qualifying marksheets and identification documents ready.
5. Pay application feeThe current portal states ₹1,100 for other programmes and ₹2,100 for Global or Doctorate programmes.
6. Selection/counsellingFollow the programme-specific merit, interview, entrance or counselling instructions.
Important:

There is no single Chitkara entrance examination applicable to every programme. Always check the exact course rule before assuming that JEE Main, an interview or Class 12 merit applies.

Chitkara Engineering Admission 2026: JEE Main vs Class 12

The 2026 engineering fee/admission document is unusually clear: seats in B.E. programmes are allocated using either JEE Main 2026 score or Class 12 marks. For the JEE route, candidates need the prescribed Class XII threshold and a JEE Main 2026 appearance; the university then uses the JEE Main All India Rank for allocation. For the Class 12 route, a higher qualifying threshold is specified.

JEE Main route

  • Appeared in JEE Main 2026.
  • Passed XII in 2025 or appearing in 2026.
  • Meet the prescribed aggregate/PCM eligibility.
  • Seat allocation based on JEE Main AIR and preference.

Class 12 route

  • Meet the published 70% aggregate or 70% PCM threshold.
  • Pass XII in 2025 or appear in 2026.
  • Seat allocation follows the university's counselling process and preferences.

Documents Required for Chitkara Admission

  • Class X certificate and marksheet.
  • Class XII certificate/marksheet.
  • Migration certificate where applicable.
  • Entrance/rank card where the programme requires it.
  • Aadhaar or accepted identification.
  • Character certificate.
  • Medical fitness certificate where required.
  • Category certificate if applicable.
  • Recent photographs.
  • Application form and programme-specific supporting documents.

Engineering counselling documents additionally include JEE Main 2026 rank information and other prescribed proofs. Candidates with a study gap may need the prescribed affidavit.

Chitkara University Admission FAQs

Is Chitkara University admission open for 2026?

The official admissions portal is active for the current admission cycle. Programme availability and deadlines can change, so applicants should use the live portal for the final status.

Can I get B.E. admission without JEE Main?

Yes. The 2026 engineering document provides a Class 12 marks route alongside the JEE Main route, subject to the published eligibility and counselling process.

What is the Chitkara application fee?

The current official portal states ₹1,100 for other programmes and ₹2,100 for Global or Doctorate programmes.

Does Chitkara conduct interviews?

Some programmes use personal interviews as part of selection. This is programme-specific rather than a university-wide rule.

Where can I apply?

Use the official Chitkara admissions portal linked at the top of this page.
