IIM Udaipur Executive MBA: Programme Overview

IIM Udaipur's Executive MBA is a blended programme designed for working professionals. The current programme combines online coursework with campus immersion. The institute describes it as a two-year programme with multiple-entry and stage-wise completion options.

Working-professional format

Online classes are designed to allow participants to continue working while studying.

Campus connection

The programme includes two on-campus modules to connect participants with faculty, peers and the IIM Udaipur ecosystem.

Degree outcome

Participants who meet the programme requirements receive a Master of Business Administration degree from IIM Udaipur and alumni status.

Who is the programme designed for?

The programme is intended for professionals who already have substantial post-graduation work experience and want to develop broader management, leadership and strategic capabilities without taking a conventional full-time residential MBA break.

How to Get Admission to IIM Udaipur Executive MBA

The current admission process combines the application, qualifying test score, professional and academic profile, and an online personal interview.

Step 1: Submit the online application

Candidates complete the online application and pay the applicable non-refundable application fee. Candidates choosing the IIMU Test also pay the separate test fee.

Step 2: Submit or take the qualifying test

Applicants can use a valid GMAT/GMAT Focus, GRE or eligible CAT score. Candidates without one of these scores can take the online IIMU Test.

Step 3: Personal interview

Shortlisted candidates are called for an online personal interview. The interview forms part of the final admission decision.

Step 4: Final admission decision

Offers are made using a combination of the candidate's academic profile, work experience, test score and personal-interview performance.

IIM Udaipur Executive MBA Fees

The Executive MBA fee should be checked against the latest admission offer because the institute can revise programme charges between admission cycles. Candidates should also distinguish the programme fee from personal expenses related to technology, travel and campus attendance.

Fee planning

The official admissions page used for this update does not expose a complete term-wise fee table in the searchable admission content. Therefore, this page does not invent a total programme cost. Applicants should use the fee amount stated in the current official offer/application portal before making a financial decision.

Additional costs to plan for

  • Laptop and reliable internet connection
  • Travel and accommodation connected with campus immersion
  • Personal study material and other individual expenses

IIM Udaipur Executive MBA Programme Structure

The programme is delivered primarily online and is structured around weekend and selected weekday sessions. The official programme page states that there are typically seven sessions each week, with each session lasting about 90 minutes followed by Q&A/tutorial interaction.

Online learning

Core academic delivery is online, making the programme compatible with continued employment.

Multiple entry/exit

The programme provides stage-wise completion and multiple-entry/exit flexibility as described by IIM Udaipur.

Credit-based progression

The programme uses a credit structure and provides stage-wise academic recognition.

Management learning focus

The programme is designed to strengthen business fundamentals, managerial effectiveness, leadership, strategic thinking and decision-making in digitally evolving business environments.

Executive MBA Online Classes and Campus Immersion

The Executive MBA is not a fully residential programme. IIM Udaipur states that the format combines online coursework with two on-campus modules.

Why the campus modules matter

The immersion component provides face-to-face interaction with faculty and peers and gives working professionals access to the institute environment without requiring a two-year residential break from employment.

Balancing work and study

Because the programme is designed for working executives, students should assess their weekly workload before enrolling. Live online sessions, assignments, examinations and campus attendance require sustained time commitment.

IIM Udaipur Executive MBA FAQs

Is IIM Udaipur Executive MBA online?

It is a blended programme. Coursework is delivered online and the programme includes two on-campus modules.

How much work experience is required for IIM Udaipur Executive MBA?

The 2026 admission policy requires at least 36 months of full-time remunerative work experience after graduation as of May 20, 2026.

Can I apply without CAT, GMAT or GRE?

Yes. Candidates without a valid GMAT, GMAT Focus, GRE or eligible CAT score can take the IIMU Test.

Is the IIM Udaipur Executive MBA a degree programme?

Yes. On successful completion of the programme requirements, participants are awarded a Master of Business Administration degree by IIM Udaipur and receive alumni status.

Does the Executive MBA require leaving a job?

No. The programme is specifically structured for working professionals, with online classes and limited campus immersion rather than a full-time residential format.
