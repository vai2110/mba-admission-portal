Where is Chitkara University Punjab?

The Punjab campus is located on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway near Rajpura in Patiala district. Chitkara's institutional material places the campus around 30 km from Chandigarh and describes it as equidistant from several nearby cities including Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Ambala and Patiala.

What does the location mean for students?

The campus is residential and outside the dense city centre, so hostel and campus transport become more important considerations than they would be at an urban commuter university.

Chitkara University Hostel

Chitkara operates a Punjab-campus hostel system. The official U-Hostel portal is used for hostel management, and university policy material covers accommodation, safety and conduct.

Room type, availability and current charges can change by academic year. Students should use the current hostel portal and admission material for the final amount rather than relying on older third-party hostel tables.

Before booking

  • Confirm room type and occupancy.
  • Check the current academic-year hostel fee.
  • Ask what is included in the fee.
  • Read refund and cancellation rules.

Read the rules

  • Campus exit and return rules apply.
  • Students are responsible for securing personal belongings.
  • Hostel conduct rules can be updated by the university.

Student Life at Chitkara

Student life combines academic work with clubs, sports, events, cultural activities, competitions and professional development. The annual Chit Utsav and university activities provide opportunities beyond classrooms.

For students choosing a residential private university, campus culture can materially affect the experience. It is worth speaking to current students about workload, attendance, hostel life, food, clubs and placement preparation before finalising admission.

Chitkara Hostel & Campus FAQs

Does Chitkara University have hostel facilities?

Yes. The Punjab campus has a university hostel system and a dedicated U-Hostel management portal.

Where is Chitkara University located?

The Punjab campus is on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway near Rajpura, Patiala.

Is hostel compulsory at Chitkara?

Hostel requirement can depend on programme, residence and university rules. Check the current admission and hostel policy for your batch.

Are hostel fees included in tuition?

No. Hostel/accommodation is a separate cost and should be added to your total education budget.
