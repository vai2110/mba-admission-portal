What is IIM Calcutta PGDBA?

PGDBA is a two-year, full-time residential Post Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics jointly offered by IIM Calcutta, ISI Kolkata and IIT Kharagpur. ISI contributes statistics and machine-learning depth, IIT Kharagpur covers technology, and IIM Calcutta focuses on business applications.

Best fit: You want a technical career in business analytics, data science, machine learning or quantitative analytics.

Eligibility & Admission 2026

The published eligibility framework requires the prescribed graduate/postgraduate qualification structure with at least 60% marks or CGPA 6.5 in the qualifying degree; the threshold is 55% or CGPA 6.0 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. The entrance test covers Verbal Ability, Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation/Data Visualization and Quantitative Aptitude. For Batch 12 (2026-28), IIM Calcutta states that admissions are being handled by ISI.

Curriculum & Learning: What You Actually Study

The programme covers computing for data science, database systems, inference, stochastic processes, algorithms and machine learning, regression and time-series models, data science, business data mining, economics and management.

Why this matters for your career

Business analytics, data science, machine learning, product analytics, risk analytics, quantitative analytics and analytics consulting are natural career directions.

Fees & ROI

₹25 lakh is the published programme fee for the 2025-27 batch. It covers tuition, on-campus lodging, some books and study materials, examination materials and laboratory charges. Food-related expenses, caution money and certain travel expenses are additional.

Placements & Career Outcomes

The published PGDBA placement report records ₹26.31 LPA average remuneration, ₹24.59 LPA median CTC and ₹41.29 lakh maximum salary for the reported batch. BFSI accounted for 57% of offers; recruiters named include Adani, UGRO Capital, ZS Associates, Envestnet Yodlee, EXL Analytics and ICICI Bank.
