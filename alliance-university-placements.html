Alliance University Placements: What Students Should Know

Alliance University's current placement page publishes programme-wise placement outcomes for academic years 2024-25 and 2025-26. The headline numbers are strong at the top end, but the average and median packages are more useful for understanding typical outcomes.

For B.Tech, the university reports ₹60.1 LPA highest, ₹5.7 LPA average and ₹5 LPA median. For MBA, it reports ₹36 LPA highest, ₹7.8 LPA average and ₹7.2 LPA median.

Which placement number matters most?

Look at median first, then average, then highest package. Also check the programme category, recruiter mix, internships and your target role. A single highest package is not a realistic salary expectation for every student.

Internships and Live Projects at Alliance University

The placement office describes internships and live projects as important components of its career-readiness model. The stated benefits include applying classroom learning to real business challenges, building professional networks, developing workplace-ready skills and strengthening profiles before final placements.

Practical workApply academic concepts to business problems.
NetworkingBuild connections through industry exposure.
PPO potentialInternships can create opportunities for pre-placement offers.

For students, the quality and relevance of an internship can matter more than simply having the word “internship” on a resume. Try to target work that aligns with your intended post-graduation role.
