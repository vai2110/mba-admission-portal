SNAP 2026 Overview

SNAP is the Symbiosis National Aptitude Test conducted for admission to postgraduate management programmes at Symbiosis International (Deemed University).

Candidates seeking admission to participating Symbiosis MBA programmes generally need to register for SNAP and separately complete the application process required by the relevant institute or programme.

Official update:

The official SNAP website is the primary source for the latest registration dates, test dates, fees and programme information.

SNAP 2026 Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognised university or institution.
  • For many MBA programmes, a minimum of 50% marks is required.
  • The minimum may be 45% for SC/ST candidates for applicable programmes.
  • Programme-specific eligibility can vary across Symbiosis institutes.
  • Final-year candidates may apply subject to programme requirements.
Important:

Always check the eligibility requirements of the specific Symbiosis MBA programme you intend to apply for.

SNAP Admission Process

  1. Register for SNAP.
  2. Appear for the SNAP test.
  3. Receive the SNAP score/percentile.
  4. Apply to the relevant Symbiosis MBA programmes.
  5. Meet the programme-specific shortlist criteria.
  6. Participate in further selection stages.
  7. Complete admission formalities after selection.

How to Prepare for SNAP

Focus on Speed

SNAP has a short test duration, so solving questions quickly is critical.

Practise Logical Reasoning

Build speed through regular practice of arrangements, puzzles and analytical reasoning.

Strengthen Arithmetic

Focus on percentages, ratios, averages, profit and loss, time-work and data interpretation.

Take Mock Tests

Use timed mocks to develop section selection and question-selection strategies.
