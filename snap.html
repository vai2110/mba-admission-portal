SNAP 2026: What Students Need to Know

SNAP 2026 is the entrance test for selected MBA programmes offered by participating institutes of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The score is for the 2027–28 academic session.

The test is computer-based, lasts 60 minutes and has 60 objective questions. You can appear for up to three tests; if you take more than one, the higher score is considered for the final percentile calculation and there is no normalisation between attempts.

What changed in 2026?

SNAP has added a fourth section — Ethics, Morality and Values — with 10 questions. The complete paper now has four sections and 60 questions.

SNAP 2026 Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

Eligibility depends on the MBA programme. For many Symbiosis MBA programmes, the official requirement is graduation from a recognised university/institution with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; for applicable SC/ST candidates, the requirement is 45%.

QualificationGraduate from a recognised University/Institution of National Importance
Marks50% for many programmes; 45% for applicable SC/ST programmes
Programme-specific rulesSome programmes have additional requirements, such as engineering background or work experience
Final-year candidatesCheck the specific programme's current admission rules

Do not use one eligibility rule for every Symbiosis MBA. For example, some specialised programmes have additional academic or work-experience requirements.

SNAP 2026 Exam Pattern: 60 Questions in 60 Minutes

The latest official SNAP 2026 test structure has four sections. Each correct answer carries 1 mark and every incorrect answer attracts a penalty equal to 25% of the marks assigned to that question.

SNAP 2026 Syllabus: What Should You Prepare?

General English

  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary and word usage
  • Grammar and sentence correction
  • Para-based questions

Analytical & Logical Reasoning

  • Arrangements and puzzles
  • Series and coding-decoding
  • Analytical reasoning
  • Critical/logical reasoning

Quantitative, DI & Data Sufficiency

  • Arithmetic, percentages, ratios and averages
  • Algebra and number systems
  • Geometry and mensuration
  • Tables, charts and graphs

Ethics, Morality and Values

  • Ethical judgement
  • Honesty and integrity
  • Fairness and responsibility
  • Accountability and respect
  • Situation-based decision making
New-section note:

SNAP's official material does not provide a long chapter-wise syllabus for Ethics, Morality and Values. Focus on understanding principles and applying them to situations rather than memorising a fixed topic list.

SNAP 2026 Registration: Fee & How to Apply

The official SNAP portal currently lists a ₹2,550 test registration fee per attempt, plus applicable government taxes. Programme registration is separate at ₹1,000 per programme, plus applicable taxes.

SNAP test registration₹2,550 per test + applicable taxes

How to Apply

  1. Register on the official SNAP website.
  2. Verify your contact details and complete the form.
  3. Choose the SNAP test attempt(s) you want to take.
  4. Pay ₹2,550 per test plus applicable taxes.
  5. Select the Symbiosis MBA programme(s) you want to apply to.
  6. Pay the separate programme registration fee for each selected programme.
  7. Save your confirmation and check the admit-card dates for your selected tests.

SNAP Cutoff & Shortlisting: How It Works

There is no single SNAP cutoff applicable to every Symbiosis MBA programme. Each institute/programme publishes its own shortlisting criteria and GE-PI schedule.

StageWhat happens
SNAP scoreYour best eligible SNAP test score is used for the final percentile calculation when multiple tests are taken.
ShortlistingEach selected programme uses its own shortlist/cutoff criteria.
GEGroup Exercise is part of the selection process for shortlisted candidates, as applicable to the programme.
PIPersonal Interaction is conducted separately by the relevant institute/programme.
Final meritThe institute prepares its merit/wait/reject lists under its published selection rules.

SNAP Admission Process: From Test to MBA Seat

  1. Register for SNAP: Pay the test fee and choose your test attempt(s).
  2. Register for programmes: Pay the separate programme fee for each MBA programme you want.
  3. Take SNAP: You can attempt up to three tests.
  4. Check your score: If you take multiple tests, the higher score is considered for final percentile calculation.
  5. Shortlisting: Each institute/programme publishes its own shortlist.
  6. GE + PI: Attend the required selection components for shortlisted programmes.
  7. Final merit: Admission is based on the programme's published selection process and merit list.
Important:

A high SNAP score does not by itself guarantee admission. The official SNAP portal states that final admission also depends on programme eligibility, GE-PI performance, applicable category rules and the institute's merit list.

How to Prepare for SNAP 2026: Student-Friendly Strategy

1. Practise for speed from day one

You get only 60 minutes for 60 questions. Build the habit of solving easy questions quickly and moving on from time-consuming ones.

2. Do not ignore the new Ethics section

Add Ethics, Morality and Values to your weekly preparation instead of leaving it for the end.

3. Try different section orders

SNAP lets you attempt sections in any order. Test different sequences in mocks and compare your accuracy and time usage.

4. Use short timed sets

Practise 10–15 question sets under strict time limits before moving to full-length mocks.

5. Analyse mistakes, not just scores

Track questions lost because of concepts, calculation errors, reading mistakes, overthinking or poor time allocation.

6. Prepare specifically for your target institutes

SNAP is only the first stage. Keep the eligibility and selection process of your preferred Symbiosis programmes in view while preparing.

SNAP 2026 FAQs: Frequently Asked Questions

When is SNAP 2026 conducted?

SNAP 2026 is scheduled on December 13, December 19 and December 26, 2026.

What is the last date to register for SNAP 2026?

The official registration and payment deadline is November 25, 2026.

How many times can I take SNAP 2026?

You can appear for up to three SNAP tests. If you take more than one, the higher score is considered for final percentile calculation and there is no normalisation between attempts.

What is the SNAP 2026 exam pattern?

SNAP has 60 questions, 60 marks and 60 minutes across four sections: General English; Analytical & Logical Reasoning; Quantitative, Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency; and Ethics, Morality and Values.

What is new in SNAP 2026?

Ethics, Morality and Values has been added as a dedicated section with 10 questions.

Is there negative marking in SNAP?

Yes. Every incorrect answer attracts a penalty equal to 25% of the marks assigned to that question, i.e. 0.25 mark for a one-mark question.

What is the SNAP 2026 registration fee?

The official test fee is ₹2,550 per attempt, plus applicable government taxes. Programme registration is separate at ₹1,000 per programme plus applicable taxes.

Who is eligible for SNAP?

Eligibility is programme-specific. Many Symbiosis MBA programmes require graduation with at least 50% marks/equivalent grade, with 45% for applicable SC/ST candidates. Some programmes have additional requirements.

Can I attempt sections in any order?

Yes. The official instructions allow candidates to attempt sections in any order.

Is SNAP compulsory for every Symbiosis postgraduate programme?

No. SNAP is mandatory only for the MBA programmes listed through the SNAP admission process.

Does paying the SNAP test fee apply me to an MBA programme?

No. You must separately select the MBA programme(s) you want and pay the applicable programme registration fee.

When will SNAP 2026 results be announced?

The official schedule lists January 12, 2027 for the SNAP 2026 result.
