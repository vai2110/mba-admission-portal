UPES BBA Overview

UPES School of Business offers BBA programmes across business, digital, events, finance, marketing, aviation and other emerging areas. Students can also consider integrated BBA-MBA routes.

Who should choose BBA at UPES?

It can suit students who want a specialised undergraduate business degree and exposure to industry-oriented learning. Compare the exact specialisation and its career outcomes before enrolling.

Popular UPES BBA Specialisations

BBA Global

International-business oriented pathway with global progression options.

BBA Business Analytics

Business education with an analytics and data-oriented focus.

BBA Events, PR & Corporate Communications

Communication and event-industry focused programme.

BBA Aviation Management

Business management pathway focused on aviation and allied services.

UPES BBA Eligibility

UPES lists minimum 50% marks in Class X and XII for BBA programmes. Board-merit pathways and other selection routes can have additional conditions.

UPES BBA Fee Breakdown & Placement Comparison

Current listings place BBA tuition at approximately ₹11.92–13.42 lakh, depending on programme. BBA Global has a separately listed first-year UPES fee of ₹3.74 lakh before partner-university costs.

What is usually NOT part of the headline tuition?

Hostel, transport, technology, programme-specific consumables/training, insurance, I-Card and other applicable charges can be additional. UPES also lists a ₹31,300 one-time training/certification fee for applicable full-time programmes, ₹9,000 PDC fee for PG programmes and ₹23,300 for UG programmes, subject to programme exclusions.

UPES BBA Fees

Current listings put tuition for UPES BBA programmes at approximately ₹11.92 lakh to ₹13.42 lakh, depending on the programme. The international BBA (Global) pathway has a separately listed first-year UPES fee of ₹3.74 lakh before partner-university costs.

Important:

Hostel, transport, technology, consumable and programme-specific charges can be additional. Verify the exact 2026-27 fee for your selected BBA.

UPES BBA Admission 2026

Selection routes include UPESMET-UG, UGAT, CUET and board marks. UPES also lists a non-exam board-merit route with 60% in Class X and XII for eligible BBA candidates.

UPES BBA Placements

UPES publishes BBA-specific placement figures under its School of Business. The current placement page identifies a dedicated BBA cohort and 2025-26 placement reporting. Students should use BBA-specific figures rather than overall university placement headlines when comparing colleges.
