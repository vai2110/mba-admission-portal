Chitkara MBA Career Scope

Career pathways vary by specialisation. Finance can lead to corporate finance, banking and analyst roles; marketing to brand, digital and sales roles; HR to people and talent roles; DS & AI to analytics and technology-enabled business roles; logistics to supply-chain and operations careers.

95%+Placement rate claims appear in some Chitkara Business School materials; verify the latest audited/course-level figure before using it for ROI.

Is Chitkara MBA Worth the Fee?

The answer depends on the student's alternative options, scholarship, work experience, specialisation and expected role. At a headline level, the 2026 fee can be substantial, so ROI should be judged using the likely salary range and role quality rather than a single highest package.

Potential strengths

  • Large specialisation portfolio.
  • Industry-oriented learning and practical projects.
  • Global and corporate exposure opportunities.
  • Dedicated career services.

Questions to ask

  • What is the latest median salary for my specialisation?
  • Which recruiters hired for my target role?
  • What is the total two-year cost including hostel?
  • How much scholarship is actually applicable to me?

Chitkara MBA FAQs

What is the Chitkara MBA fee in 2026?

It varies by specialisation. The published 2026 structure ranges from ₹1.65 lakh to ₹2.25 lakh per semester for the listed MBA groups.

Which MBA specialisation is best at Chitkara?

There is no universal best option. Finance, Marketing, HR, DS & AI, Healthcare and other tracks serve different career goals.

Does Chitkara MBA have Data Science and AI?

Yes. Data Science & Artificial Intelligence is listed in the 2026 MBA portfolio.

Does Chitkara MBA offer healthcare management?

Yes. Healthcare, Digital Health and Public Health-related programmes are listed in the 2026 portfolio.
