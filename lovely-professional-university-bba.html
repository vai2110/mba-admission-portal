LPU BBA: Programme Overview

LPU's Bachelor of Business Administration is a three-year, six-semester undergraduate management programme. The curriculum is designed to move from business fundamentals to applied management, electives, projects and skill enhancement. LPU also offers several specialised and industry-integrated BBA routes.

Quick student verdict

LPU BBA can work well for students who want a broad business foundation, large campus exposure, internships and flexibility to build skills in marketing, finance, HR, analytics or emerging areas. Students should not select BBA only because it is cheaper than B.Tech; career outcomes depend heavily on skills, internships and whether you pursue higher education.

Why students shortlist LPU BBA

  • Three-year undergraduate degree.
  • Business foundation plus electives.
  • Industry projects and internship opportunities.
  • Large campus and student clubs.
  • Scholarship routes through LPUNEST and academic performance.

What to plan early

  • Build Excel and communication skills.
  • Choose a career direction by second year.
  • Complete internships before final year.
  • Consider CFA, CMA, professional certifications or MBA based on your target career.

LPU's published BBA learning structure progresses from foundation subjects to core business application and advanced electives. Typical areas include economics, accounting, communication, business mathematics, HRM, marketing, research methodology, finance, e-commerce and project work.

LPU BBA Internships: How to Get Real Career Value

BBA is a generalist degree, so internships are particularly important. By graduation, aim to have at least one meaningful internship plus a project that demonstrates a business skill.

Good internship targets

  • Marketing and digital agencies.
  • Business development and operations.
  • Finance operations and banking.
  • HR and talent acquisition.
  • Business analytics and reporting.

Build proof, not just certificates

  • Show revenue, leads, cost, time or efficiency impact.
  • Maintain a project portfolio.
  • Ask for a reference or internship certificate.
  • Convert experience into measurable resume bullets.

Is LPU BBA Worth It?

BBA works best as a foundation degree when you use the three years to build a specialisation and professional profile. The degree alone is less likely to create a strong differentiated profile than BBA plus internships, analytics/finance/marketing skills and a clear post-graduation plan.

Good fit if

  • You want broad business exposure.
  • You plan to pursue MBA or a professional qualification.
  • You want a large residential university.
  • You can access a scholarship.

Compare alternatives if

  • You are certain you want a technical career and prefer B.Tech.
  • You already have a lower-cost option with stronger role-specific outcomes.
  • You expect BBA alone to guarantee a high-paying job.

LPU BBA FAQs

What is the duration of LPU BBA?

Three years divided into six semesters.

What is the BBA fee at LPU?

The published 2026 standard BBA fee is ₹80,000 per semester, before scholarship and other charges.

Is BBA at LPU good for MBA preparation?

Yes, it can provide a business foundation, but students should separately prepare for aptitude tests and build quantitative, communication and analytical skills.

Can BBA students get placements?

Yes, placement opportunities are available across disciplines, but offers depend on student eligibility, skills and recruiter requirements.

Is hostel available?

Yes, LPU has a large residential ecosystem with multiple room categories and separate mess charges.
