Nirma University Placements: What the Numbers Actually Mean

Nirma provides placement support through dedicated cells across institutes. The university says it has corporate linkages with more than 450 companies across institutes; the Institute of Technology separately reports more than 300 companies regularly conducting campus recruitment.

Most important point

There is no single salary number that represents every Nirma student. Technology, management, pharmacy, law, commerce, architecture and design have different recruiters, roles and placement processes.

Nirma B.Tech Placements

The Institute of Technology reports a highest package of ₹94.13 LPA for 2025-26 and ₹72.04 LPA for 2024-25. For 2024-25, branch-wise placement included CSE 90%, ECE 94%, Electrical 89%, Mechanical 96%, Chemical 97%, Civil 83% and Electronics & Instrumentation 91%.

Nirma University Recruiters & Hiring Sectors

Across institutes, Nirma reports hiring from IT, FMCG, manufacturing, telecom, banking, insurance, advertising, construction, automobile, pharmaceuticals, healthcare, infrastructure, power, petroleum, consulting and other sectors. Institute-specific recruiter lists should be used when comparing a course.

The Institute of Commerce highlights Bank of America, Federal Bank, Karnataka Bank, KPMG, PwC and EY. Management reports a broad corporate network and regular campus recruitment.

Placement Reality Check for Students

Use these numbers for

  • Understanding recruiter reach.
  • Comparing course-level placement strength.
  • Checking the difference between maximum and average.
  • Assessing whether your target role is represented.

Do not use them for

  • Assuming ₹94.13 LPA is a typical salary.
  • Comparing B.Tech and MBA maximum packages directly.
  • Assuming 100% placement means every student gets the same offer.
  • Predicting your salary without considering profile and role.

Nirma University Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at Nirma University?

The Institute of Technology currently reports ₹94.13 LPA for 2025-26. It is an institute-level highest package, not an average across the university.

What is the Nirma B.Tech CSE placement percentage?

The official 2024-25 figure is 90% for CSE, with a highest salary of ₹51.75 LPA.

What is the Nirma MBA average package?

The published 2024-25 average package is ₹11.10 LPA, with top-100 and top-50 averages of ₹14.74 LPA and ₹17.08 LPA respectively.

Does Nirma provide placement support outside MBA and engineering?

Yes. The university states that placement support is provided across Architecture, Commerce, Design, Law, Management, Pharmacy, Science and Technology through dedicated placement cells.

Does Nirma publish three-year placement data?

Comparable multi-year highest-salary data is available for Institute of Technology UG programmes. A single university-wide three-year average CTC series across all disciplines is not published in the official sources reviewed.
