IIM Udaipur Two-Year MBA: Programme Overview

The Two-Year MBA is IIM Udaipur's flagship full-time residential management programme. It runs across six terms and includes a compulsory summer internship after the first year. The revised curriculum combines management fundamentals with flexi-core, experiential and elective courses so students can shape the second half of the programme around their career interests.

6 termsTwo academic years with a structured first year and elective-led second year
375Planned batch size stated in the 2026–28 admission policy
Summer internshipMandatory internship after the first year

What is distinctive about the curriculum?

IIM Udaipur's revised curriculum introduces flexi-core courses in the first year and experiential courses in Term III. The institute states that the curriculum was reviewed using feedback from faculty, alumni and recruiters and benchmarked against reputed Indian and global business schools.

Skill-based learning includes Spreadsheet Modelling, Managerial Oral Communication and Written Analysis and Communications. Knowledge-based courses cover functional management areas, strategy, economics, operations, analytics and organisational subjects.

Does work experience matter?

Work experience is not a mandatory eligibility requirement for the Two-Year MBA. However, IIM Udaipur includes work experience as part of the 20% profile component in the final composite score for the 2026–28 process. This means a candidate can apply without work experience, while relevant experience can affect the profile score.

IIM Udaipur MBA Admission Process

The 2026–28 process is institute-specific. IIM Udaipur moved away from the Common Admission Process and uses its own sequence of CAT screening, online registration, Personal Interview and final merit-list preparation.

Step 1: Take CAT

Domestic applicants must appear for CAT. IIM Udaipur uses both overall and sectional CAT thresholds to determine eligibility for the PI process.

Step 2: Complete IIM Udaipur online registration

Candidates who meet the published minimum CAT and eligibility requirements receive an invitation to complete the mandatory online registration after CAT results. Registration is a pre-interview requirement and does not itself mean that an interview call has been received.

Step 3: Personal Interview shortlisting

The Admission Committee determines the number of candidates to call for PI in each category. The institute can increase or reduce the stated minimum CAT scores if required to reach the desired shortlist size.

Step 4: Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates participate in the PI process. IIM Udaipur states that candidates who do not obtain the minimum PI score decided by the institute will not be considered for the final merit list.

Step 5: Final merit list

The final composite score uses the normalised CAT score, PI and profile. The CAT component is based on CAT score rather than CAT percentile at this stage.

What is included in the published fee?

The published schedule separately identifies tuition, hostel charges, mess charges, the refundable caution deposit and alumni fee. The institute's programme fee structure should therefore be used as the primary reference when planning the academic and residential cost.

What should students budget separately?

  • Laptop or computer and related software, unless already owned.
  • Travel to and from Udaipur.
  • Personal purchases, clothing, stationery and laundry.
  • Medical or personal expenses beyond the items covered by the institute's applicable arrangements.

Important fee calculation

The ₹20,000 caution deposit is refundable subject to the institute's rules. Therefore, the cash outlay and the non-refundable academic/residential cost are not identical. The ₹21,51,258 figure includes the refundable deposit and offer-acceptance payment because both appear in the published payment schedule.

IIM Udaipur MBA Curriculum: Year-Wise Structure

The revised curriculum is designed so that students build management fundamentals first and then use flexi-core and elective choices to align learning with career interests.

First year: core management foundation

Term IIIFlexi-core and experiential choices include areas such as Corporate Strategy and Structure, Strategic Corporate Finance, Strategic Cost Management, Marketing in the Digital World, Data Science for Business Analytics, Strategic HR, Cross-cultural Management, Design Thinking, Entrepreneurship and Rural Immersion.

Second year: electives and career-oriented choices

The second year allows students to select electives across major functional areas. Published areas include Business Policy and Strategy, Finance, Marketing, Operations, Organisational Behaviour and Human Resources, and other multidisciplinary options. A compulsory Capstone course emphasises application and simulated decision-making.

IIM Udaipur MBA Summer Internship

The Two-Year MBA includes a mandatory summer internship at the end of the first year. The internship is designed to connect classroom learning with organisational work before students enter the second-year elective phase.

Why it matters

  • Provides exposure to a business function before final placements.
  • Creates an opportunity to apply first-year management concepts in a company setting.
  • Can help students test a preferred career function before selecting deeper second-year electives.

What to plan for

  • Prepare for summer internship recruitment during the first year.
  • Use the internship to build evidence of functional skills and business problem-solving.
  • Do not treat the internship as a substitute for final-placement preparation.

Who Should Choose the IIM Udaipur Two-Year MBA?

Strong fit

  • You want a full-time residential MBA rather than a specialised one-year programme.
  • You want to build a broad management foundation before choosing deeper electives.
  • You are comfortable with CAT-based admission and a PI-led final selection process.
  • You want a programme with early experiential learning and a mandatory summer internship.

Think carefully if

  • You already have substantial work experience and specifically want a one-year programme such as GSCM or DEM.
  • You are applying only because of the published CAT minimum and have not considered the full selection process.
  • You need a highly specialised course from the first term rather than a broad management curriculum.
  • You have not budgeted for residential living and personal expenses beyond the published fee schedule.

IIM Udaipur MBA FAQs

Is 94 percentile enough for IIM Udaipur MBA?

No. 94 overall with 75 in each CAT section was the published General-category minimum for PI eligibility for 2026–28. It is not a guaranteed admission percentile. IIM Udaipur also uses PI and profile components in final selection.

Does IIM Udaipur require work experience for its Two-Year MBA?

No. Work experience is not listed as a basic eligibility requirement. However, it carries 6% weight within the 20% profile component of the final composite score for the 2026–28 process.

What is the total IIM Udaipur MBA fee for 2026–28?

The published payment schedule adds up to ₹21,51,258, including the ₹1 lakh offer-acceptance payment and ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit. Students should separately budget for personal expenses and other items not included in the published schedule.

What is the IIM Udaipur MBA admission process after CAT?

Eligible candidates are invited to complete mandatory online registration after the CAT result. IIM Udaipur then shortlists candidates for Personal Interview. The final merit list uses 55% normalised CAT score, 25% PI and 20% profile for the 2026–28 cycle.

Is IIM Udaipur MBA residential?

Yes. The Two-Year MBA is a full-time residential programme. The published fee schedule includes hostel and mess charges for the six terms.

What is the latest official IIM Udaipur MBA placement package?

For the Two-Year MBA 2022–24 batch, IIM Udaipur's current admissions portal reports ₹18.77 LPA average maximum earning potential and ₹47.66 LPA highest maximum earning potential.
