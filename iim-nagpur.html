Quick Answer

The IIM Nagpur MBA 2026-28 is a two-year full-time residential programme with a batch size of 340. The total fee is Rs. 21,00,000. Admission is based on CAT 2025 scores, personal interviews, and a composite score including academic performance and diversity factors.

Programme Overview

IIM Nagpur offers a two-year full-time residential Master in Business Administration (MBA). The pedagogy includes case studies, simulations, projects, and industry interactions. The curriculum covers areas such as Finance & Accounting, Marketing, Strategy & Entrepreneurship, and Decision Science.

Admissions Policy

Admission for the 2026-28 batch follows a three-stage process:

  • Stage 1: CAT 2025 eligibility screening.
  • Stage 2: Shortlisting for Personal Interview (PI).
  • Stage 3: Final selection based on a composite score.
