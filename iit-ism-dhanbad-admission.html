Important 2026 Dates

Dates vary by programme. The MBA application ran through 31 January 2026; MTech applications ran through 19 April 2026. Executive MTech applications opened in May 2026. Undergraduate admissions follow the JEE Advanced and JoSAA calendar.

How to Apply

  1. Identify the exact IIT (ISM) programme.
  2. Read the current eligibility and entrance-test requirement.
  3. Complete the relevant national counselling or institute application.
  4. Upload the required academic and category documents.
  5. Pay the application or counselling fee.
  6. Track shortlist, counselling, interview or seat-allocation updates.
  7. Complete institute reporting and fee payment after selection.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Academic marksheets and certificates.
  • Entrance scorecard/rank details where applicable.
  • Valid category/EWS/PwD certificate where applicable.
  • Photo ID and recent photographs.
  • Application and payment confirmations.

Programme-specific notices can require additional documents, so treat this as a preparation checklist rather than a final document list.

Student Discussion: Avoid These Admission Mistakes

Most avoidable problems come from using old dates or applying against the wrong programme rules. IIT (ISM) runs separate portals and calendars for different programmes, so always verify the exact notice before payment.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Admission FAQs

How can I get BTech admission?

Through JEE Advanced followed by JoSAA counselling, subject to current eligibility rules.

Which exam is required for MBA?

The 2026-27 MBA and MBA Business Analytics cycle requires CAT 2025.

Is GATE required for MTech?

The standard MTech route uses GATE and COAP, while sponsored candidates can have separate requirements.
