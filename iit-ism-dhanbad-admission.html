IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Admission 2027–29

The Department of Management Studies and Industrial Engineering offers a two-year MBA and MBA in Business Analytics. The latest fully published admission cycle located is 2026–27: it required a valid CAT 2025 score, an institute application and a personal interview. Applications for that cycle closed on 31 January 2026. As of 17 November 2026, the 2027–29 admission notice, accepted CAT year and application calendar were not yet confirmed in the official material reviewed.

Eligibility in the latest published 2026–27 notice

  • MBA: Recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline with first class or at least 60% marks/6.0 CGPA; 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates as specified in the notice.
  • MBA Business Analytics: B.E./B.Tech in any discipline with at least 60%/6.0 CGPA; 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
  • Both programmes required a valid CAT score for the relevant admission cycle. Final-year candidates could apply provisionally subject to the institute's completion deadline.

How to Apply to IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

  1. Choose the exact programme: MBA, MBA Business Analytics, B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc or another programme.
  2. Open the current official admission notice and confirm the academic year and eligibility.
  3. Check the accepted entrance exam and score year. For MBA, wait for the 2027–29 notice to confirm the accepted CAT year.
  4. Complete the correct institute application or national counselling registration.
  5. Upload the required academic, entrance-score and category documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee and retain the acknowledgement.
  7. Track shortlisting, interview, counselling, seat allocation and reporting notices.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • 10th, 12th and graduation marksheets/certificates as applicable.
  • CAT/GATE/JEE Advanced or other required scorecard.
  • Valid category, EWS or PwD certificate where applicable.
  • Photo ID, recent photograph and signature file.
  • Work-experience proof if claimed in an MBA application.
  • Application and payment confirmation.

This is a preparation list, not a substitute for the document checklist in the relevant admission brochure.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Admission FAQs

How can I get admission to IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA?

In the latest published 2026–27 cycle, applicants needed a valid CAT 2025 score, had to submit the institute application and were shortlisted for a personal interview. Confirm the next cycle's CAT year and dates from the official portal.

What is the MBA eligibility?

The 2026–27 MBA accepted graduates from any discipline meeting the published marks/CGPA requirement; MBA Business Analytics required a B.E./B.Tech degree. Category-specific relaxations were specified by the institute.

What is the MBA CAT cutoff?

The institute says CAT percentile is used for shortlisting but the official pages reviewed do not state a fixed universal percentile cutoff.

When will MBA 2027–29 applications open?

The official 2027–29 dates were not yet available in the material reviewed as of 17 November 2026.

How can I get B.Tech admission?

Through JEE Advanced followed by JoSAA counselling, subject to the applicable year's eligibility and seat-allocation rules.

Is GATE required for M.Tech?

The standard M.Tech route uses GATE and institute/COAP processes where applicable; always check the department's current notice and exceptions.
