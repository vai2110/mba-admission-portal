College comparison

Verified comparison values are shown with their stated placement cycle or fee scope. “Not yet verified” means the portal has not established the figure from an acceptable source.

Data integrity rule: No fee, placement, intake, entrance exam, programme or location number is invented. A college can be selected even when its core data is still pending official verification.
About cutoff: Qualifying cutoff and competitive shortlist benchmarks are different. We leave cutoff blank when a defensible official/primary benchmark is not established. A cutoff figure does not guarantee an interview call or admission.

What should you compare?

The table gives the facts; your priorities determine how you interpret them.

1. Total programme cost

Read the fee note carefully. Some institutes publish tuition/course fees separately from deposits, hostel or mess costs.

2. Average + median together

Average can be pulled upward by high offers. Median gives a different view of the same placement cycle.

3. Intake and programme scope

Check whether the number refers to the MBA only, a combined batch, or participants in a placement report.
