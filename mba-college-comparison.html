College comparison

Data integrity: Every college in the 109-college universe is selectable. Green records satisfy the portal's mandatory comparison fields. Amber records have some official/reliable fields loaded but still have core data pending. Grey/pending records remain selectable while research is pending. Missing values are never guessed.
Cutoff: Qualifying cutoff and competitive shortlist benchmarks are different. Cutoff is optional and does not decide whether a college appears in the comparison list.

How to read the comparison

All colleges stay available

The comparison universe is the portal's full 109-college created-page list, not only the colleges whose data research is complete.

Average + median

Both placement measures are shown together for the stated placement cycle. Highest package is not used as the primary comparison metric.

Pending does not mean excluded

If a fee, median, intake or other field has not yet been verified, it is shown as “Not yet verified” instead of inventing a number.
