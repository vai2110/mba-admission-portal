LPU Design: What Is the Programme Like?

LPU offers design programmes across fashion, graphics, interior and furniture, product and industrial design, UI/UX, animation and VFX, gaming, multimedia and film/TV production. Most B.Des programmes are four years and eight semesters.

Quick student verdict

Design education rewards students who are willing to create, iterate and build a portfolio. LPU's large campus and broad design portfolio can be useful, but your portfolio quality, software skills, internships and ability to explain design decisions will matter greatly.

LPU B.Des Eligibility 2026

LPU's current design programme pages commonly state 60% aggregate in 10+2 with the specified qualification and an applicable entrance route, while current third-party course listings may show different programme-specific criteria. Check the exact design specialisation before applying.

Why Your Design Portfolio Matters

For design careers, the portfolio can be more persuasive than a long list of certificates. Show the problem, research, iterations, sketches/wireframes, final output and your specific contribution.

Strong portfolio

  • 6–10 carefully selected projects.
  • Process, not only final images.
  • Clear problem statement.
  • Original work and measurable outcomes.
  • Good presentation and case-study writing.

Weak portfolio

  • Only copied inspiration.
  • No process explanation.
  • Too many unrelated projects.
  • Poor presentation or missing context.

LPU Design Internships

Internships should be selected according to your design track: agencies and brands for communication/fashion, product teams for UI/UX, studios for animation/film and architecture/interior practices for spatial design.

LPU Design Career Scope

Design graduates can work in agencies, technology companies, consumer brands, studios, media houses, manufacturing, fashion and independent creative businesses. Freelancing and entrepreneurship are also realistic routes for strong portfolios.
