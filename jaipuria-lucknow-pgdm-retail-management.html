Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Retail Management: Overview

PGDM Retail Management is a two-year full-time specialised management programme at Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow. It is designed for students who want management training with a stronger orientation towards retail, consumer businesses and e-commerce. The institute describes its PGDM programmes as AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

The programme combines management fundamentals with retail-oriented learning. This makes it different from the general PGDM: students still study core areas such as marketing, finance, operations and business analytics, but the application is more closely connected to consumers, channels, merchandise, stores and digital commerce.

Important for applicants

The programme is specialised, but it does not restrict a student to only store-management jobs. Retail companies hire across marketing, category, analytics, supply chain, e-commerce, operations and customer-experience functions.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Retail Management Curriculum

The curriculum is designed around contemporary retail and e-commerce requirements while retaining a management foundation. The institute highlights an industry-centric pedagogy using real-world cases, projects and industry connections.

Core management foundation

Students build fundamentals in accounting, economics, statistics, organisational behaviour, marketing, finance, operations, business analytics, human resources, research methods and strategic management. These subjects matter because retail decisions increasingly require financial, analytical and cross-functional understanding.

Retail-focused learning areas

  • Consumer behaviour and retail marketing.
  • Product, merchandise and category management.
  • Store operations and visual merchandising.
  • Distribution and channel management.
  • E-commerce and omnichannel retail.
  • Retail analytics and data-driven decision-making.
  • Customer relationship and experience management.
  • Digital marketing and contemporary consumer engagement.

Learning through projects and cases

Students can use live projects and case-based learning to connect classroom frameworks with practical retail problems such as pricing, assortment, customer acquisition, channel performance and store productivity.

PGDM Retail Management Career Scope

The programme can lead to careers in retail companies, e-commerce businesses, consumer brands, marketplaces, consulting firms and companies operating retail distribution networks.

Category Management

Category managers analyse products, pricing, assortment, demand and category performance. This is one of the more analytical paths within retail.

E-commerce

Roles can span marketplace operations, online merchandising, growth, digital commerce, customer acquisition and e-commerce strategy.

Retail Marketing

Students can target brand, digital, consumer insights, campaign and customer-engagement roles in consumer-facing businesses.

Operations & Supply Chain

Retail requires efficient inventory, fulfilment, distribution and store operations, creating opportunities beyond marketing.

Typical role families to explore

Depending on skills and recruiter requirements, students can explore roles such as category executive/manager, retail operations executive, merchandising analyst, e-commerce executive, business development, sales, marketing, customer experience, supply-chain and business analytics.

Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

Jaipuria's PGDM model incorporates summer internships and live projects. For Retail Management students, the internship can be particularly useful because the retail industry involves practical exposure to customers, channels, products and operational decisions.

What to look for in a retail internship

  • Exposure to a measurable business problem rather than only administrative work.
  • Interaction with category, marketing, e-commerce, operations or analytics teams.
  • Clearly defined deliverables and business metrics.
  • Opportunity to convert the internship into a pre-placement opportunity where applicable.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Retail Management Admission 2026

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required. Final-year candidates may apply subject to the institute's conditions regarding completion of graduation.

Accepted entrance exams

Jaipuria accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT scores for the PGDM admission process.

Selection process

  1. Complete the online application and select the preferred programme/campus.
  2. Submit entrance-test and academic information.
  3. Provide relevant work-experience and extracurricular details where applicable.
  4. Participate in the institute's selection stages, including case analysis and personal interview components.
  5. Receive the admission decision and complete fee/payment formalities as per the offer.

The selection process means entrance percentile is important, but it is not the only factor considered.

Who Should Choose PGDM Retail Management?

This programme is worth considering if you are interested in consumer businesses and want your management education to be closely connected to retail and digital commerce.

  • Choose it if: you are interested in retail, e-commerce, consumer brands, category management or retail operations.
  • Choose general PGDM instead if: you want maximum flexibility across consulting, finance, HR, operations and marketing without a sector-specific orientation.
  • Choose PGDM Financial Services instead if: your career target is clearly banking, insurance, investments or financial services.
