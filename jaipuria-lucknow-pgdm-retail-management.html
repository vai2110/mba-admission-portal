Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Retail Management: Overview

PGDM (Retail Management) is a two-year full-time specialised management programme offered at Jaipuria Lucknow. The programme combines management fundamentals with retail-focused learning covering marketing strategy, customer behaviour, e-commerce and contemporary retail practices.

The programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA. Its pedagogy includes industry illustrations, case studies, internships and live projects.

PGDM Retail Management Curriculum

The curriculum is designed around the changing retail and e-commerce landscape. Official programme information includes retail-focused learning in areas such as consumer behaviour, retail marketing, product and category management, distribution and channel management, store operations, omnichannel retail and retail analytics.

Key learning areas

  • Retail marketing and consumer behaviour.
  • Product, merchandise and category management.
  • Store design, store operations and visual merchandising.
  • E-commerce and omnichannel retail strategy.
  • Retail analytics and supply-chain concepts.
  • Digital marketing, brand management and customer relationship management.

The elective pool is indicative and can change based on academic and industry requirements.

Career Scope After PGDM Retail Management

The programme is suited to students interested in retail, e-commerce, consumer businesses and related marketing and operations roles.

Category & Product

Potential roles include category management, product roles, merchandising and assortment planning.

E-commerce & Digital

Useful for e-commerce operations, digital marketing, online retail strategy and customer-growth roles.

Retail Operations

Relevant to store operations, supply chain, distribution, customer experience and retail service functions.

Admission 2026

The eligibility requirement is a bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year graduation students can apply subject to the institute's stated conditions.

CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT scores are accepted. The selection process also considers factors beyond the aptitude score, including case analysis, personal interview, academics, work experience and extracurricular profile.
