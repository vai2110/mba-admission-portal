LPU Law: What Is the Right Programme for You?

LPU's School of Law offers undergraduate and integrated law pathways including B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) and BBA LL.B. (Hons.), while a three-year LL.B. route is also available for eligible graduates. The right choice depends on whether you are entering law after Class 12 or after graduation.

Quick student verdict

LPU Law is relevant if you want an integrated law degree, campus-based legal education and exposure to practical activities. For legal careers, internships, drafting, mooting, research and professional networking matter as much as the classroom curriculum.

LPU Law Internships: What Should You Target?

Internships are critical in law. Students should seek experience with litigation chambers, law firms, corporate legal teams, compliance functions, NGOs, policy organisations or courts as appropriate to their career direction.

Build these skills

  • Legal research.
  • Drafting and contract review.
  • Case briefing.
  • Oral arguments and presentation.
  • Professional communication.

Do not wait until final year

Use semester breaks to build a sequence of internships rather than relying on one certificate at graduation.

LPU Law Career Scope

Choose the integrated programme based on career direction: BBA LL.B. can be attractive for corporate/business law interests, B.A. LL.B. offers broader humanities exposure, and LL.B. is the graduate-entry route. Higher legal qualifications and bar enrolment requirements should be considered separately.

LPU Law FAQs

How long is BA LLB at LPU?

The B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) programme is five years and ten semesters.

How much is LPU LLB fee?

Current reporting places the three-year LL.B. at around ₹80,000 per semester; confirm the current official fee before payment.

Does LPU offer BBA LLB?

Yes, LPU offers BBA LL.B. (Hons.) as an integrated law pathway.

Are law placements guaranteed?

No. Placement support does not guarantee a job.
