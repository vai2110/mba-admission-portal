How to Calculate Your LPU Net Fee

Use this simple calculation: tuition + academic charges + hostel + mess + personal costs − scholarship benefit. Do not subtract scholarship from hostel unless the specific scheme says so.

Example

If a course costs ₹8 lakh in tuition and you receive a 40% tuition scholarship, the tuition component becomes ₹4.8 lakh. Hostel, mess and other costs remain separate unless specifically covered.

LPU Fee Payment & Education Loans

Students who cannot fund the entire programme upfront should compare education-loan interest, moratorium, collateral requirements and scholarship-adjusted principal. Keep all fee receipts and scholarship communications.

Is LPU Fee Worth Paying?

ROI depends on programme, scholarship and career outcome. A ₹4.8 lakh BCA or BBA has a different ROI equation from a ₹12.8 lakh B.Tech CSE or ₹8.8 lakh MBA. Compare total cost with realistic—not exceptional—salary outcomes.

LPU Fees & Scholarships FAQs

What is the LPU B.Tech fee?

Many B.Tech branches are reported around ₹1.40–2.00 lakh per semester; CSE is ₹1.60 lakh per semester.

What is LPU MBA fee?

The standard MBA is currently reported around ₹2.20 lakh per semester; specialised routes can differ.

What is LPU hostel fee?

2026-27 hostel rates vary by room type, starting around ₹83,000 for a four-seater air-cooled room under the early-decision schedule.
