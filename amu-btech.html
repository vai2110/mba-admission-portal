B.Tech at AMU: Overview

Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) offers a four-year B.Tech programme through Zakir Husain College of Engineering & Technology. The 2026–27 admission guide lists 11 programmes, with selection through the AMU Admission Test. This page brings together eligibility, branch-wise seats, admission dates, fees, cutoff guidance and published placement outcomes so you can compare the key details in one place.

Important

AMU B.Tech admission is through the university's admission test for the 2026-27 cycle. Do not assume JEE Main is the primary route simply because the programme is B.Tech; follow the AMU admission guide for the current cycle.

AMU B.Tech Eligibility 2026

The 2026-27 guide requires the Senior Secondary School Certificate examination of AMU or an equivalent examination with at least 50% marks in aggregate of English, Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. An eligible Diploma in Engineering with at least 50% marks in aggregate is also listed as a qualifying route.

Applicants must also satisfy the applicable course and category conditions under AMU's admission rules. The university specifically directs candidates to check the Allowed Courses under Important Information & Rules.

AMU B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission Test

Selection is through the AMU Admission Test. The 2026-27 guide specifies one objective-type paper of 100 marks with 100 questions covering Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics. The notified test centres are Aligarh, Lucknow, Srinagar, Kolkata, Patna and Kozhikode.

Application and counselling workflow

  1. Read the current 2026-27 Guide to Admissions and identify the B.Tech course code.
  2. Complete the AMU Controller of Examinations application form during the published application window.
  3. Prepare Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics according to the published admission-test syllabus.
  4. Appear for the AMU B.Tech admission test at the notified centre.
  5. Check the result, rank/selection status and counselling-cum-admission schedule.
  6. Complete document verification and fee payment if selected.

AMU B.Tech FAQs

These answers summarise the published 2026–27 admission rules and available university-level outcomes. Where AMU has not published a separate figure, the answer says so rather than guessing.

Is AMU B.Tech admission through JEE Main?

No. For the 2026-27 admission cycle, AMU's official Guide to Admissions specifies selection through the AMU Admission Test for B.Tech. The notified paper is objective type and carries 100 marks with 100 questions covering Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics. Students should therefore follow the AMU Controller of Examinations schedule and admission notices for B.Tech rather than assuming JEE Main is the selection route.

What is the eligibility for B.Tech at AMU?

The 2026-27 guide requires the Senior Secondary School Certificate examination of AMU or an equivalent examination with at least 50% marks in aggregate of English, Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. It also lists an eligible Diploma in Engineering of AMU or an equivalent qualification with at least 50% aggregate as an alternative route. Candidates must also satisfy the applicable admission rules and course conditions published by AMU.

What is the AMU B.Tech entrance exam pattern and where is it conducted?

The official 2026-27 guide specifies one objective-type paper of 100 marks comprising 100 questions on Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics. The listed test centres are Aligarh, Lucknow, Srinagar, Kolkata, Patna and Kozhikode. For the 2026 cycle, the guide scheduled the test for 19 April 2026 at 10:00 A.M. with a duration of two hours.

What are the AMU B.Tech fees for 2026-27?

The official fee appendix gives the fee payable at the time of admission, and the amount differs by branch and, for some programmes, by Self-Finance Scheme (SFS) seat. For example, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical and regular ECE/Mechanical admissions are listed at ₹25,500, while Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence), Automobile Engineering (Electric & Hybrid Vehicles) and ECE (VLSI Design & Technology) are listed at ₹70,500. These are admission-time amounts; students should not treat them as the complete four-year academic cost.

How many B.Tech branches and seats does AMU offer?

The 2026-27 guide lists 11 B.Tech programmes at ZHCET: Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Technology (Refining & Petrochemicals), Chemical Engineering (Food Technology), Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence), Automobile Engineering (Electric & Hybrid Vehicles), and ECE (VLSI Design & Technology). Intake varies by programme, with some branches also carrying seats under the Self-Finance Scheme.

Does AMU B.Tech have placements and internships?

Yes. ZHCET has a dedicated Training & Placement Office that publishes placement announcements, visited-recruiter information and internship/training data. Current 2026 notices show recruitment drives for specific groups such as Computer/AI, Electrical/Mechanical, Computer Engineering and other eligible engineering branches, while some drives are open to all B.Tech/B.E. branches. AMU also maintains branch-wise higher-studies data, so students can review placement, internship and higher-study information through official ZHCET resources.

What does the AMU B.Tech cutoff depend on?

AMU cutoff or selection outcomes depend on the branch, applicable category, selection list and admission round. A single university-wide cutoff cannot accurately represent every programme. Compare the relevant branch and category against the published selection outcome for the same cycle. Previous-year ranks are a reference only and do not guarantee admission in a later year.

Are hostel and mess charges included in AMU B.Tech admission fees?

The course-wise amount in the 2026–27 admission guide is an admission-time fee, not a confirmed four-year residential budget. The published course fee table does not separately itemise hostel, mess, deposits or all recurring charges. These costs should not be assumed to be included in the amounts shown above. The page therefore keeps them separate rather than quoting an unverified hostel total.

Are scholarships available for AMU B.Tech students?

Scholarship eligibility and award amounts depend on the scheme and the student's circumstances. The 2026–27 B.Tech course-fee table does not establish a single scholarship amount applicable to every student. Do not subtract an assumed scholarship from the fees shown on this page. Any award should be counted only after its eligibility conditions and approval are confirmed.

Which documents should I prepare for AMU B.Tech admission?

Keep your qualifying examination marksheets and certificates, identity details, photographs and any category or eligibility documents relevant to your application ready. The exact list can vary with the qualification route and the admission stage. The admission process can also require original documents during counselling or reporting. Use the requirements stated in the applicable admission notice and selection communication; do not assume an incomplete document set will be accepted.
