Who should choose the MBA?

The two-year MBA is the broadest management route at IIM Bodh Gaya. It is the logical starting point for a graduate who wants to build a general management foundation before specialising through electives, career choices and placements.

Strong fit

Graduates targeting consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, sales or general management.

Why choose a general MBA?

You keep more functional and sector choices open than with a programme built around one sector or technology theme.

Who should look elsewhere?

Students with a very specific healthcare or digital-AI career goal should compare the specialised programmes before deciding.

What is the MBA eligibility for 2026?

The official MBA programme page describes the route as graduation plus CAT. The 2026-28 admission cycle has a separate admission policy, so the policy should be treated as the controlling source for marks, category rules, shortlist conditions and documents.

Do not confuse eligibility with shortlisting

Meeting the minimum academic and test requirements only makes you eligible to be considered. The actual shortlist and final merit depend on the current admission policy and candidate pool.

What should you keep ready?

  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets and graduation records.
  • CAT score details and the information submitted through the relevant admission process.
  • Category certificates where applicable.
  • Work-experience or other profile documents if you are claiming them in the application.

Use the official 2026 admissions page for the current policy, notices and application information.

How does the selection process work?

The 2026 policy should be read as a selection funnel: CAT performance brings the candidate into consideration, followed by the institute's personal-interaction process and profile-based evaluation as specified for the cycle. The exact scoring rules can change between admission years.

What will you study in the MBA?

The programme begins with the core disciplines required to understand a business and then gives students access to more advanced functional and strategic subjects. The official curriculum covers accounting, economics, statistics, marketing, finance, operations, organisational behaviour, IT and systems, entrepreneurship, analytics, strategy and business ethics.

Which functional areas can you explore?

Finance & accounting

Advanced options include areas such as portfolio management, derivatives, valuation, financial statement analysis, banking, risk, fixed income and investment banking.

Marketing

Students can explore consumer behaviour, brand management, digital marketing, marketing analytics, pricing, B2B and services marketing.

Strategy & leadership

Advanced strategy, organisational leadership, international business, economics and problem-solving subjects support broader managerial decision-making.

The programme is therefore broader than a single specialisation label. Your actual career direction will depend on electives, projects, internship and final placement choices.

What is the MBA fee and what else should you budget for?

IIM Bodh Gaya publishes a term-wise fee structure. The available official MBA fee document shows tuition, hostel rent, academic charges, admission/registration, student activity, career development and other components separately. A newer batch should always be budgeted from the latest official document rather than an old fee table.

Are scholarships available?

The MBA programme lists Government of India scholarships for eligible students, including Top Class Education schemes for SC/ST students, minority merit-cum-means support and scholarships for students with disabilities. Eligibility and application are separate from the institute's admission decision.

What career outcomes can you expect after the MBA?

The current placement page reports 100% placement, 200+ recruiting companies, ₹14.80 LPA average CTC and ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC, with additional top-25/50/75 percentile averages. These are institute-level placement indicators and should be read with the latest batch report.

Is the MBA worth the cost?

ROI depends on more than the average CTC. A sensible calculation compares the complete programme cost with realistic post-MBA compensation and considers the student's opportunity cost.

Positive signal

A ₹14.80 LPA current average CTC provides a stronger reference point than the highest ₹45.63 lakh offer when estimating broad ROI.

Do not ignore

Living costs, foregone salary, financing cost and the time required to reach the role you actually want.

Best comparison

Compare the same metrics across competing B-schools: total cost, average, distribution, role mix and recruiter quality.

Personal fit

A lower-cost programme can still be the better decision if it provides the role, geography or specialisation you value more.
