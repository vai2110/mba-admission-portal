Who should choose the MBA?

The two-year MBA is the broadest management route at IIM Bodh Gaya. It is designed for graduates who want exposure to the core functions of business rather than committing to a narrow sector at the point of admission.

Best for

Graduates targeting consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, sales or general management.

What you gain

Core management foundations followed by deeper functional and strategic subjects.

What to check

CAT competitiveness, total cost, role preferences and the placement distribution for your target career.

Eligibility and admission route

The official programme page describes the MBA as a two-year programme with graduation and CAT as the eligibility route. For the 2026-28 cycle, the institute publishes a dedicated MBA admission policy and participates in the CAP 2026 process.

Do not use one generic “IIM cutoff”

Admission is policy-specific. Check the current 2026 admission policy and CAP communication for qualifying criteria, EOI requirements, shortlist rules and subsequent stages.

Open the official 2026 admissions page for the current policy and application notices.

What does the selection journey involve?

The selection process should be understood as a funnel rather than a single CAT-score decision. The current cycle publishes an admission policy and CAP process; students should therefore track the official notices from registration through shortlist and final admission.

What will you study?

The curriculum starts with the foundations needed to understand organisations and markets and then moves into advanced functional and strategic subjects. The official curriculum includes accounting, organisational behaviour, marketing, economics, statistics, IT and systems, finance, operations, entrepreneurship, analytics, strategy and business ethics.

Year 1

Core management: accounting, economics, statistics, marketing, finance, operations, OB, IT, analytics, entrepreneurship and strategy.

Finance & Accounting

Portfolio management, derivatives, valuation, financial statement analysis, banking, risk, fixed income and investment banking.

Marketing

Consumer behaviour, brand, product, digital marketing, marketing analytics, pricing, B2B and services marketing.

Fees and ROI

IIM Bodh Gaya publishes a separate fee structure for the 2026-28 MBA batch. The correct comparison is not simply “IIM fee versus salary”; calculate the full programme cost and compare it with realistic post-MBA compensation.

Include

Tuition, academic/service charges, hostel, deposits, insurance or other institute charges and living expenses.

Compare

Average CTC, percentile-level outcomes, target-role availability and the salary trajectory you expect after graduation.

Use the official 2026-28 fee document before making a financial decision because fees are batch-specific.

Career outcomes and placements

The current official placement page reports 100% placement, 200+ recruiting companies, ₹14.80 LPA average CTC and ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC. It also reports ₹21.00 LPA, ₹17.95 LPA and ₹16.24 LPA average CTCs for the top 25%, 50% and 75% respectively.

These numbers should be read as cohort-level evidence, not as an individual salary promise. Your function, recruiter, academic profile, interview performance and market conditions all matter.

Read the dedicated IIM Bodh Gaya placements analysis before comparing the institute with other B-schools.

Quick decision test

Strong fit

You want a full-time general management MBA, are comfortable preparing through CAT and value an IIM environment with a growing recruiter base.

Think twice

You already know you want a specialised healthcare or digital-AI management path; the specialised programmes may be more aligned.

FAQs

How long is the MBA?

Two years.

What is the eligibility?

The official programme page lists graduation plus CAT as the route; the detailed 2026 admission policy should be used for current eligibility and selection conditions.

What is the average package?

The current official placement page reports ₹14.80 LPA average CTC.

Does the MBA have specialisations?

The curriculum provides advanced functional domains such as finance and accounting and marketing, alongside economics and broader management subjects.
