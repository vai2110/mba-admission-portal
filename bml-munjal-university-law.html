BMU Law Overview

BMU's School of Law offers integrated five-year BBA LLB (Hons.) and BA LLB (Hons.) programmes along with a three-year LLB. The programmes can suit students interested in corporate law, litigation, policy, compliance and interdisciplinary legal careers.

Law Courses

5 YEARS

BBA LLB (Hons.)

Combines business education with legal studies and can suit students targeting corporate and commercial law.

5 YEARS

BA LLB (Hons.)

Combines liberal arts and legal education with broader social-science exposure.

3 YEARS

LLB

Graduate-entry law programme for students who already hold a bachelor's degree.

Admission 2026

BMU publishes programme-specific law eligibility and application requirements. Candidates should confirm the current accepted entrance route, academic qualification and interview or assessment requirements before applying.

Career Scope

Law graduates can pursue litigation, corporate legal teams, compliance, consulting, policy, legal operations and higher legal education. Integrated programmes can be particularly useful for students interested in business-law intersections.

Law FAQs

Does BMU offer BA LLB?

Yes. BMU offers BA LLB (Hons.) as a five-year integrated law programme.

Does BMU offer BBA LLB?

Yes. BMU offers BBA LLB (Hons.) as a five-year integrated programme.

What is BMU LLB fee?

The current published tuition is ₹2.50 lakh per year, or ₹7.50 lakh for three years.
