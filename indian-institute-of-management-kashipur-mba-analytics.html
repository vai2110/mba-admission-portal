IIM Kashipur MBA Analytics Overview

Quick answer

IIM Kashipur's MBA (Analytics) is a two-year full-time residential programme combining management education with descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics for business decision-making.

The institute positions the programme for managers and future leaders working in increasingly technology-oriented and data-driven business environments. The curriculum blends analytical and managerial skills with industry exposure and co-curricular learning.

Eligibility for MBA Analytics

For 2026-28, the institute requires a full-time bachelor's degree in any discipline or equivalent 10+2+3 qualification with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The published relaxed thresholds are 47% for NC-OBC/EWS and 45% for SC/ST/PwD.

Candidates must also have either a valid CAT-2025 score or a GMAT score where the exam was not taken earlier than May 1, 2024.

MBA Analytics Admission Process

STEP 1

Application

Submit the online application, required documents and applicable application fee.

STEP 2

Shortlisting

Shortlisting considers qualifying exam score, X/XII/graduation marks, work experience, discipline diversity and gender diversity.

STEP 3

Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates appear for a Personal Interview. The institute conducts online PIs for this admission process.

MBA Analytics Scholarships

IIM Kashipur states that full yearly tuition fee waivers are provided under need-based, merit-based and need-cum-merit scholarships to 10% of both the first-year and second-year batches of the MBA (Analytics) programme.

Candidates in financial need can also explore education-loan facilities listed by the institute.

MBA Analytics Placements

The latest audited final placement report covers both MBA and MBA (Analytics) for the 2023-25 batch. It reports ₹14.95 LPA average CTC, ₹14.46 LPA median CTC, ₹20.92 LPA top-25% average and ₹33.12 LPA highest CTC across the audited placement pool.

MBA Analytics FAQs

Is MBA Analytics residential?

Yes. It is a two-year full-time residential programme.

What exams are accepted?
