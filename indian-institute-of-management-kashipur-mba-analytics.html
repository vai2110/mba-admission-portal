Quick Answer

The MBA (Analytics) at IIM Kashipur is a two-year, full-time residential programme. It consists of six terms, including core management and analytics courses, an 8–10 week summer internship, and a dissertation project in the second year.

Programme Overview

The MBA (Analytics) is designed to build a strong foundation in both management principles and advanced data analytics. The programme aims to develop strategic decision-makers capable of navigating complex business environments through data-driven insights.

Programme Structure

The programme is spread over two years and six terms:

  • First Year: Three terms of mandatory core courses covering management and analytics, followed by an 8–10 week summer internship.
  • Second Year: Three terms (IV, V, and VI) featuring mandatory analytics courses, diverse electives, and a comprehensive dissertation conducted across all three terms.
