LIBA PGDM: Programme Overview

LIBA's full-time PGDM is a two-year residential programme spread across six terms. The current official programme site states that the curriculum carries 126 credits, including core courses, electives and a summer project.

LIBA states that its AICTE-approved PGDM is recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. The programme combines management fundamentals with functional specialisations and industry exposure.

LIBA PGDM Admission 2027

LIBA's admissions portal currently shows registration open for the 2027-2029 full-time PGDM. Applicants should use the live admissions portal for the applicable deadline and selection-stage communication.

How to apply

  1. Check eligibility and entrance-test requirements.
  2. Prepare CAT 2026 or XAT 2027 details.
  3. Complete the LIBA application form.
  4. Upload the requested academic and supporting documents.
  5. Track institute communication for the next selection stage.

LIBA PGDM Curriculum

The official PGDM site describes a 126-credit structure covering core courses, electives and a summer project. The six-term structure gives students time to build foundational management knowledge before moving deeper into functional areas.

LIBA PGDM Fees

The current official PGDM website lists the total programme fee at ₹18,89,700. LIBA's official 2024-26 mandatory disclosure provides the component-level fee structure: ₹35,000 admission fee, ₹14,46,900 tuition, ₹1,38,600 establishment, ₹92,400 library, ₹92,400 computer, ₹1,400 insurance, ₹8,000 alumni, ₹36,000 foundation course, ₹14,000 student activities, ₹5,000 NPTEL and ₹20,000 hybrid learning platform fee. Refundable library and retention deposits are ₹10,000 each.

LIBA PGDM Placements

For 2024-25, LIBA's official mandatory disclosure reports ₹11 LPA average salary, ₹21 LPA maximum salary and ₹7.5 LPA minimum salary. It also states that LIBA has secured 100% placements since the inception of the full-time PGDM programme.
