SIBM Bengaluru MBA Executive Overview

Quick answer

The SIBM Bengaluru MBA Executive is a two-year programme designed for working executives. The official page requires graduation with at least 50% marks and a minimum of one year of full-time work experience after graduation.

The programme is designed to strengthen general management capability through lectures, case studies, action learning and coaching. The current official page lists an intake of 30 students.

SIBM Bengaluru MBA Executive Eligibility

Candidates must be graduates of a recognised university/institution with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the official page specifies 45% for SC/ST candidates. In addition, applicants need at least one year of full-time work experience after graduation in a registered firm, company, industry, educational, government or autonomous organisation.

This work-experience requirement makes the Executive MBA materially different from the flagship MBA and Business Analytics programmes.

How SIBM Bengaluru MBA Executive Selection Works

Selection is based on the candidate's performance in the Personal Interaction process conducted by SIBM Bengaluru. The official page states that the PI lasts approximately 12-15 minutes.

Is SNAP required for MBA Executive?

No SNAP requirement is stated on the official Executive MBA page. The programme uses its own application and Personal Interaction route.

What the Executive MBA Offers

The official programme describes a pedagogy based on contemporary course content, technology-enabled learning, LMS access, Harvard cases and simulations, CMIE Prowess, Bloomberg Terminal and a well-equipped library.

The programme outcomes include analytical capability, financial decision-making, technology-enabled problem solving, ethical behaviour and general management capability.

SIBM Bengaluru MBA Executive FAQs

Who is the SIBM Bengaluru Executive MBA for?

It is designed for working executives and requires at least one year of full-time work experience after graduation.

What is the Executive MBA intake?

The official page lists 30 students.

Is there an official CAT or SNAP cutoff?

The Executive MBA page does not state a CAT/SNAP cutoff because selection is based on Personal Interaction.

What is the two-year fee?

The published academic fee is ₹3 lakh per annum for 2026-28, plus a ₹10,000 refundable institute deposit.
