What can you study in BHU BA Hons?

BHU's 2026 UG admission notice lists BA Hons subjects in the Faculty of Arts and Faculty of Social Sciences. Examples include English, Hindi, History, Geography, Economics, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, Sanskrit, Philosophy, Education and several language subjects.

The programme is a four-year NEP undergraduate degree. The exact major/minor combination and seat structure depend on the subject and location.

What is the BHU BA Hons eligibility?

For the 2026 admission cycle, BA Hons groups used English or Hindi + General Aptitude Test as the required CUET-UG combination. Candidates must also satisfy the Class 12 eligibility for the selected programme.

Do not assume that every BA subject has identical eligibility. Check the subject combination and programme bulletin before selecting CUET papers.

How do you get admission to BHU BA Hons?

  1. Appear for CUET-UG with the required language and General Aptitude Test.
  2. Wait for BHU's undergraduate admission registration to open.
  3. Register through BHU CAP-UG.
  4. Enter programme preferences and complete document verification requirements.
  5. Participate in allotment rounds and pay the fee after seat allotment.

CUET participation alone does not complete the BHU admission process.

What is the BHU BA Hons cutoff?

BHU does not have one BA Hons cutoff. The score changes by subject, faculty/college, category and round.

What is the BHU BA Hons fee?

BHU does not use one common BA fee across every subject and college. The payable amount depends on the programme/location and whether the seat is regular or paid-fee. Use the programme-specific fee notice during CAP admission.

What are BA placement opportunities at BHU?

BHU's official NIRF-based university data reports median salaries by programme duration rather than a single BA package. The university-level 2025 material reports a ₹8 LPA median for UG 3-year programmes and ₹9 LPA for UG 4-year programmes. These are not BA-only salary figures.

For the complete university placement data, see BHU Placements: NIRF + Official Placement Data.

What can you do after BA Hons from BHU?

Career options depend heavily on the subject. Graduates can move toward civil services and other competitive examinations, teaching and education, research, media and communication, policy, social-sector roles, analytics and corporate functions. A master's degree can be useful for academic or specialised careers.

BHU BA Hons FAQs

Is BA Hons at BHU based on CUET?

Yes. The 2026 UG admission route used CUET-UG followed by BHU CAP-UG.

Which CUET subjects are needed for BHU BA Hons?

The 2026 notice specifies English or Hindi plus General Aptitude Test for the listed BA Hons groups.

Is there one BA cutoff for BHU?

No. Cutoffs vary by subject, faculty/college, category and round.
