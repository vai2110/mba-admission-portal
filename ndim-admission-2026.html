NDIM Admission: Quick Answer

What you need to know

NDIM is currently inviting applications for the PGDM 2027-29 batch. Applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree; the published eligibility page states 50% graduation marks for General-category candidates and 45% for reserved-category candidates. NDIM considers CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT and state common entrance examination scores for shortlisting, followed by GD and PI.

NDIM PGDM Eligibility 2027-29

NDIM Admission Process: Application to Offer

  1. Complete the NDIM application: submit the online application and the published non-refundable ₹1,000 application fee.
  2. Submit entrance-score details: provide the accepted management entrance examination information used for shortlisting.
  3. Attend GD and PI: shortlisted applicants are invited to NDIM's Group Discussion and Personal Interview process, conducted at the Delhi campus and/or online depending on the schedule.
  4. Final assessment: NDIM considers academics, entrance score, GD/PI and other achievement factors.
  5. Admission offer: selected candidates receive the institute's admission communication and complete the fee/payment formalities.

NDIM does not publish a single simple “entrance percentile = admission” formula on its current eligibility page. Instead, it describes selection as an overall-performance assessment.

NDIM Cutoff: Is There an Official Percentile?

NDIM does not state a fixed official CAT/XAT/CMAT percentile cutoff on the current eligibility page. It says scores from multiple entrance exams are considered for shortlisting and that final selection uses overall performance.

Important for applicants

Do not publish or rely on a coaching site's “NDIM cutoff” as an official institutional cutoff unless NDIM itself releases that figure for the relevant batch.

NDIM Admission FAQs

Can I apply to NDIM without CAT?

Yes. NDIM's published list includes XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT and state common entrance examinations in addition to CAT.

What is the NDIM application fee?

The published admission procedure states a non-refundable application fee of ₹1,000.

Does NDIM conduct GD and PI?

Yes. Shortlisted applicants are invited to the institute's Group Discussion and Personal Interview process.

What is the official NDIM cutoff?

NDIM does not publish a single fixed percentile cutoff on its current eligibility page. It considers multiple entrance scores and overall performance.
