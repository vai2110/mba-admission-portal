IIM Shillong Placement Recruiters

The official placement material lists a broad mix of recruiters. Prominent names appearing in recent reports include Accenture Strategy, Accenture Technology Consulting, Bain & Company, BCG, Deloitte USI, EY, Goldman Sachs, HSBC, ICICI Bank, ITC, JPMorgan Chase & Co., McKinsey & Company, Microsoft, Nomura, Pidilite, Tata Steel, Tiger Analytics, TVS Motors, WNS, Wells Fargo and Zomato, among many others.

New recruiter expansion

The 2024-26 report highlights 51 new recruiters. The 2023-25 report had 54 new recruiters. Recent new/recently participating names across the reports include Alvarez & Marsal, CAMS, FedEx, Flipkart, IDFC First Bank, Indigo, Pharmeasy, PwC India, Swiggy, TresVista Financial Services, United Airlines, V-Guard and Zomato.

How to use the recruiter list:

Do not choose a B-school because a single famous company appears in the recruiter list. Check whether that company hires for the function you want, whether the role is consulting/finance/analytics/marketing/etc., and whether the hiring is recurring.

Internships, PPO/PPI Offers & Career Conversion

IIM Shillong's PGP includes a two-month summer internship after the first year. The 2024-26 final placement report records 102 PPO/PPI offers, while the 2023-25 report records 75. This makes the internship an important part of the placement journey rather than a separate academic formality.

What can improve PPO chances?

  • Strong internship deliverables and measurable business impact.
  • Good manager/team feedback.
  • Clear communication and problem-solving.
  • Role alignment with the firm's final hiring needs.

Do not assume

  • Every internship converts to a PPO.
  • A PPO automatically gives the highest CTC.
  • The placement report's average salary predicts your personal package.

IIM Shillong ROI: How Should Students Evaluate the Salary?

Using the latest reported ₹26 lakh median CTC against the latest detailed official fee schedule of ₹26.18 lakh for the 2024-26 batch gives a useful first comparison, but it is not a payback calculation. CTC is not take-home salary, and a student's true cost also includes living, travel, opportunity cost and financing interest.

QuestionWhat to check
CostCurrent batch fee, mess, personal expenses and financing cost.
SalaryMedian and average CTC rather than highest package alone.
Role qualityFunction, location, role responsibilities and growth path.
Career fitWhether recruiter mix matches your target industry.
Opportunity costSalary you give up during two years plus exam/preparation costs.
Bottom line:

IIM Shillong's recent placement outcomes make it a serious option for students targeting consulting, finance, general management and analytics. The final decision should compare your actual alternatives, not only the headline package.
