What should you know about IPE Hyderabad?

Quick answer

Institute of Public Enterprise (IPE), Hyderabad offers two-year PGDM programmes and accepts several national management entrance scores. Its current official fee disclosure for the 2026–28 batch totals ₹9.15 lakh before hostel charges, while the institute reports 2026 placements as ongoing with an average salary of ₹7.41 LPA.

IPE is located at its Shamirpet campus in Hyderabad and offers a portfolio spanning general management, marketing, banking and financial services, international business, human resource management and business analytics.

How does IPE Hyderabad MBA admission work?

For the 2026–28 PGDM cycle, IPE states that applicants can use CAT, XAT, ATMA, CMAT, GMAT or MAT scores. Candidates with TG-ICET scores can also apply according to the institute's current admissions page.

Eligibility

  • At least 50% in graduation for the standard route.
  • IPE's FAQ states 45% for SC/ST candidates.
  • Selection also considers academic record, entrance-test performance and Personal Interview performance.

Selection flow

Step 1 — Application

Submit the application with academic and entrance-test details.

Step 2 — Shortlisting

IPE evaluates the applicant profile and relevant entrance score.

Step 3 — Personal Interview

Shortlisted applicants participate in the institute's interview process.

Step 4 — Offer

Final admission is subject to the institute's applicable selection and documentation rules.

Source: IPE admissions and FAQ pages.

What is the IPE Hyderabad cutoff?

CollegeDecoded currently uses a 75 percentile MAT reference for IPE Hyderabad in its cutoff dataset. This is an expected/indicative research reference, not an official admission cutoff or guarantee.

Important distinction

IPE's admission pages focus on eligibility, entrance-score consideration and interview-based selection. Applicants should not treat a single percentile figure as a guaranteed shortlist threshold.
