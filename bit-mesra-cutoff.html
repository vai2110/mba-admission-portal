BIT Mesra B.Tech cutoff: what should students track?

Important:

For BIT Mesra B.Tech, the useful number is the JEE Main closing rank for the exact branch, category, quota and round. A lower numerical closing rank means stronger competition.

What the BIT Mesra cutoff trend tells you

1. CSE remains the benchmark. CSE closes at a substantially stronger rank than most other B.Tech branches, so students should not treat the BIT Mesra cutoff as one number.

2. AI & ML is close behind. Its 2025 closing rank of 27,324 was only about 3,700 ranks deeper than CSE.

3. ECE provides a meaningful alternative. The 2025 General AI closing rank of 37,550 puts it in a different but still competitive band.

4. Rank flexibility increases in core branches. Mechanical, Chemical, Civil and related branches generally close at deeper ranks.

5. Round matters. A Round 1 cutoff and a final/special-round cutoff can be very different. Never compare one year's Round 1 number with another year's final-round number without labelling the difference.

How to use BIT Mesra cutoff for choice filling

Use your expected JEE Main rank as a starting point, then build a preference list with dream, realistic and safer branches. Keep CSE/AI/ML/ECE together only if those branches actually match your career goal; do not select a branch only because the college name is attractive.

Also compare fees + hostel cost + placement outcomes + branch curriculum. A lower cutoff branch is not automatically a better or worse choice; it simply has a different demand profile.

BIT Mesra cutoff FAQs

What is the BIT Mesra CSE cutoff?

For General All India candidates, the last reported 2025 closing rank was 23,596 in the published round-wise dataset; the exact cutoff changes by round, quota, gender pool and category.

Which BIT Mesra branch has the highest cutoff?

CSE is generally the most competitive major B.Tech branch, followed by AI & ML and ECE in the recent cutoff data.

Is BIT Mesra cutoff based on JEE Main percentile or rank?

Seat allocation is based on JEE Main rank/CRL under the applicable admission route. Students commonly convert their percentile to an estimated rank for planning, but the actual cutoff should be read as rank.

Does BIT Mesra have Home State quota?

Published cutoff datasets distinguish Home State and All India pools. The exact seat matrix and eligibility for each admission year should be checked in the current JoSAA/CSAB and BIT Mesra documents.
