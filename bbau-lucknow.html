BBAU Lucknow: What Students Should Know

Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University is a Central University located at Vidya Vihar on Raebareli Road, Lucknow. Its practical attractions include central-university status, comparatively low published tuition for many programmes, a broad academic mix and programme-specific admission routes.

#23NIRF 2025 Pharmacy category

The university is currently publishing UG, PG and Ph.D. admissions for 2026-27. Students should not assume that every course follows the same entrance test: UG admissions include CUET routes, B.Tech has a JEE route, and some PG skill-based programmes have separate non-CUET processes.

BBAU FAQs Students Actually Ask

Is BBAU a central university?

Yes. BBAU is a Central University located in Lucknow and its official website identifies it as such.

What is BBAU's NIRF 2025 rank?

BBAU is ranked 37th in the NIRF 2025 University category. It is also ranked 69 overall, 79 in Management, 12 in Law, 23 in Pharmacy and 21 in Agriculture and Allied Sectors.

Does BBAU accept CUET?

Yes. The 2026-27 cycle includes CUET-based UG and PG admissions, while certain programmes use JEE or non-CUET routes.

Where is BBAU Lucknow located?

The main campus is at Vidya Vihar, Raebareli Road, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226025.
