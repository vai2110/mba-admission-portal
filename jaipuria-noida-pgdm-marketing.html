Jaipuria Noida PGDM Marketing: What You Get

PGDM Marketing is a two-year full-time management programme built around marketing strategy, consumer behaviour, branding, digital marketing and related business functions. It remains a management degree rather than a narrow digital-marketing certificate.

Who should choose it?

Students targeting brand, digital, category, sales, customer insights, marketing analytics or business-development roles who still want exposure to broader management disciplines.

PGDM Marketing Curriculum & Electives

The current programme page lists marketing electives such as Consumer Behaviour, B2B Marketing, Brand Management, CRM, Digital Marketing, Distribution and Channel Management, Global Marketing, Integrated Marketing Communication, Marketing Analytics, Marketing Research, Marketing with AI for Non-Coders, Pricing, Product and Category Management, Retail Marketing and Strategic Marketing.

Marketing capabilities

  • Consumer and customer insights
  • Brand and product strategy
  • Digital and social marketing
  • Marketing research and analytics

Business-facing skills

  • B2B and channel management
  • Sales and business development
  • Category and retail management
  • Strategy and communication

The programme also offers dual specialisation, so students can combine Marketing with another functional domain from the approved elective pool.

Who Should Choose PGDM Marketing?

Good fit

  • You enjoy consumer-facing business problems.
  • You want brand, digital, category or sales careers.
  • You like a combination of communication and analytics.
  • You want a management degree rather than a narrow marketing course.

Think twice

  • You want a purely technical analytics programme.
  • You strongly dislike sales-oriented career paths.
  • Your alternative B-school has stronger marketing roles at a similar cost.

Jaipuria Noida PGDM Marketing FAQs

What is the fee for PGDM Marketing at Jaipuria Noida?

The published 2027-29 programme fee is ₹16.50 lakh, excluding refundable security deposit and separate hostel/mess costs.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

CAT 2026, XAT 2027, CMAT, MAT and GMAT are listed for 2027-29.

Is PGDM Marketing a digital-marketing course?

No. It is a full management programme with marketing as the specialist area and includes broader business subjects.

What jobs can I target?

Brand, digital marketing, sales, business development, category management, customer insights, marketing analytics and research are potential career areas.
