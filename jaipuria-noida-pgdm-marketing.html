Jaipuria Noida PGDM Marketing: What You Get

The PGDM (Marketing) at Jaipuria is a two-year full-time programme designed around marketing strategy, consumer behaviour, branding, digital marketing and related business functions. The institute states that the programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

Is this a pure digital-marketing course?

No. It is a management programme with marketing as the specialist area. Students study core business subjects and then build marketing depth through electives such as brand management, digital marketing, marketing analytics, marketing research, B2B marketing and category management.

PGDM Marketing Curriculum & Electives

The official programme page lists marketing electives including consumer behaviour, B2B marketing, brand management, CRM, digital marketing, distribution and channel management, global marketing, integrated marketing communication, marketing analytics, marketing research, marketing with AI for non-coders, pricing, product and category management, retail marketing, social media marketing and strategic marketing.

Core marketing capabilities

  • Consumer and customer understanding
  • Brand and product strategy
  • Digital and social marketing
  • Marketing research and analytics

Business-facing capabilities

  • B2B and channel management
  • Sales and business development
  • Category and retail management
  • Strategy and communication

Who Should Choose PGDM Marketing?

Good fit

  • You enjoy consumer-facing business problems.
  • You want brand, digital, category or sales careers.
  • You are comfortable with communication and data.
  • You want management depth rather than a narrow marketing certificate.

Not the best fit if

  • You want a purely technical analytics degree.
  • You strongly dislike sales-oriented career paths.
  • You have a better MBA/PGDM option at comparable cost.

PGDM Marketing FAQs

What is the fee for PGDM Marketing at Jaipuria Noida?

₹16.50 lakh for 2026-28, plus the refundable security deposit and separate hostel/mess costs.

What entrance exams are accepted?

CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT are listed for the 2026 admission cycle.

What jobs can I get after PGDM Marketing?

Potential roles include brand, digital marketing, sales, business development, category management, marketing analytics and research.

Is PGDM Marketing equivalent to MBA?

Jaipuria states that its AIU-recognised PGDM is equivalent to MBA.
