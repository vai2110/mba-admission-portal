What is the DoMS NIT Trichy placement record?

DoMS NIT Trichy states on its official placement page that the department has maintained a 100% placement record in recent years. NIT Trichy also lists a dedicated MBA Placement 2025-26 brochure on the MBA admissions page.

The 100% figure is the department's stated recent placement record. It should not be read as a guarantee that every student will receive the same salary or role.

What does 100% placement mean for an MBA student?

It means DoMS reports that students have been placed in recent years, but placement outcomes still vary by role, recruiter, student profile, interview performance and market conditions.

When comparing DoMS with another MBA college, look at the median or average salary, number of students placed, number of recruiters and the year of the placement data together. A placement percentage alone does not tell you the salary level.

What roles can DoMS NIT Trichy MBA students target?

The programme's academic areas support careers across Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management. The department's research areas also cover digital business, information systems, investment banking, consumer insights, supply chain and machine learning.

Which recruiters hire from DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS maintains a recruiter section on its official placement site and separately publishes placement material for the MBA batch. Recruiter participation can change from one placement cycle to another, so students should use the latest department placement brochure rather than assuming an old recruiter list will repeat.

What salary should you expect after DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

The official DoMS placement page available for this guide confirms the department's recent 100% placement record, but the searchable official page does not expose a current 2025-26 average, median and highest salary table. I have therefore not inserted an unverified salary number here.

For a salary comparison, use the latest placement brochure linked by NIT Trichy and check whether a figure is average, median or highest before comparing it with another B-school.

Is DoMS NIT Trichy MBA good for ROI?

DoMS is positioned as a relatively low-fee public-institute MBA. The department's own FAQ gives an older approximate annual institute fee of ₹1,08,700 and about ₹50,000 for hostel and mess, while NIT Trichy separately publishes the fee structure for each admitted batch. Because the current 2026 fee notice is the controlling document, students should use that notice for the final payable amount.

The combination of a public NIT environment, a two-year MBA and the department's stated recent 100% placement record makes ROI an important part of the DoMS value proposition. ROI still depends on the actual fee paid and the salary/role secured by the student.

DoMS NIT Trichy Placements FAQs

Does DoMS NIT Trichy have 100% placement?

Yes. The department states that it has a 100% placement record in recent years.

Is the placement data MBA-specific?

The DoMS placement section is for the management department, and NIT Trichy lists an MBA Placement 2025-26 brochure specifically. Students should distinguish this from placement figures for engineering or other NIT Trichy programmes.

Does DoMS publish a highest salary every year?

The department publishes placement material, but the currently searchable official page does not provide a current 2025-26 salary table. Do not treat an older or third-party salary number as the latest official figure.

Which MBA specialisations connect with analytics careers?

Business Analytics and Business Analysis & IT Consulting are the most directly aligned options, while Finance, Marketing and Operations also contain analytics-heavy coursework and applications.
