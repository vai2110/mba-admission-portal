ICFAI Executive MBA Overview

The IFHE Executive MBA is designed for working professionals and has a published duration of 21 months. It is different from the regular two-year IBS Hyderabad MBA and should be evaluated based on work experience, class schedule, peer group and career objectives.

Executive MBA Eligibility 2026

IFHE states that applicants should be graduates in any discipline with 50% or above marks, have at least two years of work experience in managerial or supervisory positions and have completed at least 15 years of education. English-medium requirements also apply, subject to the institute's rules.

Executive MBA Selection Process

Applicants are shortlisted using academic qualifications and work experience. The selection process includes Micro Presentation and Personal Interview.

Executive MBA Fees

The current programme fee is ₹5.25 lakh, payable in seven equal instalments of ₹75,000. A refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit is additional.

Who Should Choose the Executive MBA?

It is more suitable for professionals who already have meaningful work experience and want structured management education without taking the same path as a fresh graduate. Compare the programme's format and expected career benefit with your current role before enrolling.

Student Discussion: Regular MBA vs Executive MBA

The key question is not which programme is universally better. A fresher generally needs a full-time early-career MBA, while an experienced professional may benefit more from a programme that values existing work experience. Compare peer group, opportunity cost and expected post-MBA role.

ICFAI Executive MBA FAQs

How much work experience is required?

IFHE states a minimum of two years of managerial or supervisory work experience.

What is the Executive MBA fee?

The programme fee is ₹5.25 lakh, plus a refundable ₹10,000 caution deposit.

What is the selection process?

Shortlisted candidates go through Micro Presentation and Personal Interview.
