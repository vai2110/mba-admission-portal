What is DoMS NIT Trichy?

Department of Management Studies (DoMS), NIT Tiruchirappalli is the management school of NIT Trichy. DoMS was established in 1978 and offers a full-time, two-year MBA programme. The 2026 admission brochure describes the MBA as the department's flagship programme and the 48th MBA batch.

The MBA follows a trimester system with six trimesters. Students study management fundamentals, functional areas, analytics, IT consulting and entrepreneurship, followed by a two-month summer internship after the first year.

Who can apply for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

For MBA admissions 2026, GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL candidates needed a full-time regular undergraduate degree in any discipline with at least 60% marks or 6.5 CGPA. SC/ST/PwD candidates needed at least 55% marks or 6.0 CGPA. Applicants also had to have a CAT 2025 percentile. Final-year UG students could apply if they met the stated conditions.

DoMS does not use IIMs to conduct its selection process. The institute manages its own MBA admission process.

What CAT percentile is needed for DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS NIT Trichy does not publish a single guaranteed CAT percentile that assures MBA admission. CAT overall percentile is used for shortlisting, and the final selection also considers undergraduate academics, work experience and the personal interview.

For the 2026-28 intake, the official brochure required candidates to have a CAT 2025 percentile but did not state a fixed minimum CAT percentile in the eligibility section. So, a CAT score should be judged against the complete selection profile rather than treated as a guaranteed admission cutoff.

Practical reading: a higher CAT percentile improves the shortlisting position, but meeting the eligibility requirement alone does not guarantee a seat.

What do students study in the MBA?

The programme uses six 10-week trimesters. The 2026 department brochure describes a mix of lectures, case discussions, group work, presentations, simulations, guest sessions, industrial visits and mini projects. Students complete a two-month summer internship after the first year.

Areas covered include leadership, communication, strategy, finance, marketing, operations, HR, analytics, IT consulting and entrepreneurship. Major specialisation areas listed by DoMS include HR, Marketing, Finance, Production & Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management.

What are DoMS NIT Trichy MBA placements like?

DoMS states that it has maintained a 100% placement record in recent years. The institute also publishes a separate MBA placement guide for the 2025-26 placement cycle.

For a student comparing colleges, check the placement page for the latest available salary figures, recruiters and placement scope rather than using an institute-wide NIT Trichy figure for the MBA.

DoMS NIT Trichy MBA FAQs

Does DoMS NIT Trichy accept MAT or XAT?

No. The department's official FAQ says CAT is the entrance score accepted for MBA shortlisting.

How many MBA seats are there?

The MBA Admission 2026 FAQ states 115 seats, inclusive of reservations as proposed by the Ministry of Education.

Is hostel compulsory for DoMS MBA?

No. The department FAQ states hostel accommodation is not compulsory, although hostel stay is recommended.

What specialisations are available?

DoMS lists HR Management, Marketing, Finance, Production & Operations, Business Analytics, Business Analysis & IT Consulting and General Management.

Is the MBA trimester-based?

Yes. The programme has six trimesters over two years.
