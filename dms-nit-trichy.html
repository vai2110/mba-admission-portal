What is DoMS NIT Trichy?

The Department of Management Studies (DoMS), NIT Tiruchirappalli was established in 1978. Its flagship programme is a full-time, two-year MBA. The 2026 admission brochure identifies the 2026-28 programme as the department's 48th MBA batch.

The programme is organised into six trimesters. The 2026 department brochure describes case discussions, group work, presentations, simulations, guest lectures, industrial visits and mini projects, along with a two-month summer internship after the first year.

How do you apply for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

The application is completely online; the brochure says no hard copy is required. The process for the 2026 batch was:

StepStudent action
1Check eligibility and make sure you have CAT 2025.
2Pay the application fee through the Samarth e-Gov Portal.
3Upload the required documents in the prescribed format.
4Review the application carefully and submit it.
5Keep the auto-generated application number for reference.

Application fee: ₹1,550 for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL and ₹1,050 for SC/ST/PwD. The fee is non-refundable. The brochure also says correction requests after submission are not entertained.

What is the CAT cutoff for DoMS NIT Trichy?

DoMS did not publish a fixed numeric CAT cutoff in the 2026-28 admission brochure. CAT 2025 overall percentile was required, but the institute states that shortlisting uses the CAT overall percentile together with undergraduate academic achievement.

How should you judge your CAT score?

Do not treat 80, 85 or 90 percentile as an official DoMS cutoff. A higher CAT percentile can strengthen your position, but the institute does not publish a guaranteed percentile for admission.

Recent applicant discussions provide only a student-reported reality check. Reddit threads include individual applicants reporting 84.51, 85.83 and 73.63 percentile while discussing DoMS/NIT Trichy. These figures are not official cutoff data.

How much is the MBA application fee?

For 2026, the application fee was ₹1,550 for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC-NCL and ₹1,050 for SC/ST/PwD. This is separate from the programme fee and is non-refundable.

How is the final MBA selection made?

DoMS uses different factors at the shortlist and final-selection stages.

Shortlisting

  • CAT 2025 overall percentile
  • Undergraduate academic achievement

Final selection

  • CAT 2025 overall percentile
  • Undergraduate academic achievement
  • Professional work experience
  • Personal interview performance

The 2026 brochure does not publish percentage weightages for these factors, so no artificial score split is shown here.

DoMS NIT Trichy MBA FAQs

Is CAT compulsory for DoMS NIT Trichy MBA?

Yes. CAT is the entrance route used for MBA shortlisting. The 2026-28 brochure specifically required CAT 2025.

Can I apply with a CAT score from before 2025?

No. The 2026 brochure says candidates with a previous-year CAT percentile before 2025 were not eligible for that admission cycle.

What is the official CAT cutoff?

No fixed numeric CAT cutoff is stated in the 2026-28 brochure. Shortlisting uses CAT overall percentile and undergraduate academic achievement.

How many MBA seats are there?

The 2026 MBA admission FAQ states 115 seats, inclusive of reservations as proposed by the Ministry of Education.

What is the full MBA fee for Open category students?

The four semester totals in the 2026 fee notice add up to ₹2,91,700 for the Open/Open-EWS/OBC-NCL and listed equivalent categories. Hostel and mess are not part of this institute fee total.

What do SC/ST students pay?

The 2026 fee notice shows no tuition fee for SC/ST and semester totals of ₹95,650 in Semester I and ₹56,050 in Semester III, giving ₹1,51,700 across four semesters.

Are MAT, XAT or CMAT accepted?

No. The DoMS FAQ identifies CAT as the entrance score for MBA shortlisting.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes. They can apply with CAT 2025, subject to meeting the required UG percentage/CGPA when the degree is completed and verified.

Do I need to submit a hard copy?

No. The 2026 admission brochure says a hard copy of the application is not required.

Where were the 2026 interviews proposed?

Noida/Delhi NCR, Nagpur, Kolkata, Chennai and Tiruchirappalli were the five proposed interview locations.
