What is BIMTECH PGDM-IBM?

PGDM Insurance Business Management is a two-year, six-trimester residential programme focused on insurance, risk management and related financial-services functions. BIMTECH introduced the programme in 2000.

The current curriculum includes actuarial science, InsurTech, insurance analytics, risk management, reinsurance, underwriting and claims.

Who can apply for PGDM-IBM?

Graduation in the 10+2+3 pattern with at least 50% marks is required. Final-year students can apply subject to BIMTECH's stated result deadline and minimum marks.

Which exams are accepted for PGDM-IBM?

For 2027-29, BIMTECH lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT 2025/2026-27, CMAT 2027 and MAT 2026-27. Shortlisting is followed by Extempore and Personal Interview.

What are the important BIMTECH admission dates for 2027-29?

Officially confirmed: BIMTECH says the final selection list will be declared in March 2027, and the academic session is likely to start in June/July 2027. Final-year graduation candidates must submit their final result with at least 50% marks by 31 October 2027.

What is the BIMTECH Insurance Management cutoff?

No fixed final CAT/XAT/MAT closing cutoff is published on the current admission page. The final decision is based on a composite assessment including entrance score, academics, PI, work experience and applicable parameters.

What makes PGDM-IBM different?

The course includes life and non-life insurance, health insurance, actuarial science, catastrophe risk modelling, reinsurance, InsurTech, cyber risk and insurance analytics. BIMTECH also cites industry collaborations and professional recognition through the Insurance Institute of India and Chartered Insurance Institute UK.

What are PGDM-IBM placements?

The official 2023-25 placement report records 100% placement for PGDM-IBM. Highest Indian CTC was ₹14.50 LPA, average CTC ₹10.59 LPA and median CTC ₹10.45 LPA.

BIMTECH lists its PGDM programmes as AIU-equivalent to MBA.
