Rajagiri Business School PGDM Fees 2026

Current fee reference

Current 2026 course listings report ₹7,50,000 as the total PGDM tuition fee for two years. A commonly reported payment split is ₹4,00,000 in the first instalment and ₹3,50,000 in the second.

Fee componentAmountPeriod
PGDM tuition₹7,50,000Entire 2-year programme
First instalment₹4,00,000Reported fee schedule
Second instalment₹3,50,000Reported fee schedule

The RBS website does not currently surface a detailed fee table in the public PGDM page captured for this guide. The ₹7.50 lakh figure is corroborated by current 2026 course listings; confirm the final fee notice from RBS before payment.

Rajagiri Business School Hostel Fees

Current 2026 course listings report hostel charges of about ₹1.88 lakh for two years, split as approximately ₹95,000 and ₹93,000. Meal-plan inclusion/exclusion and the final hostel allocation should be confirmed with the institute.

RBS PGDM ROI: is the fee justified?

The latest NIRF 2026 data reports a ₹7.50 LPA median salary for the 2024-25 graduating cohort. That median is close to the reported tuition amount, but students should not interpret this as a one-year payback because salary is gross CTC, while education cost is paid upfront and students also incur living costs.

What supports ROI

₹7.50 lakh tuition, scholarship opportunities, 201 placements reported for 2024-25 and 170 recruiters in the 2023-25 placement report.

What to calculate

Loan interest, hostel/living costs, taxes, scholarship, relocation and the actual role offered.

RBS Fees FAQs

What is the RBS PGDM fee?

Current 2026 listings report ₹7.50 lakh tuition for the two-year PGDM.

How much is the RBS hostel fee?

Current 2026 listings report about ₹1.88 lakh for two years.

Does RBS offer scholarships?

Yes. RBS lists Diversity, Career & Talent and Director/Dean's scholarships.

Can a non-Keralite get a scholarship?

Yes. The Diversity Scholarship specifically targets eligible non-Keralite students and provides a 25–50% tuition waiver.

What is the maximum scholarship mentioned by RBS?

The Director/Dean's Scholarship lists a 75% tuition-fee waiver for eligible candidates with CAT 2025 percentile of 95 or above, subject to the stated conditions.
