Rajagiri Business School PGDM Fees 2026

Quick answer

The current fee reference used for the 2026 PGDM intake is ₹7.50 lakh for the two-year programme. The amount should be distinguished from hostel, books, examination, organisational/project-study and other student expenses.

RBS's public website currently does not expose a new 2026-27 detailed fee PDF in the same format as its older fee structure. Therefore, the page shows the current ₹7.50 lakh tuition reference separately from the official RBS fee bifurcation published for 2023-24/2024-25, rather than presenting the older instalment schedule as a 2026 promise.

Important year label: The ₹4 lakh + ₹3.5 lakh bifurcation comes from RBS's official PGDM fee-structure PDF for 2023-24 and 2024-25. The total matches the current ₹7.50 lakh tuition reference, but students joining in 2026 should confirm the final instalment dates and any revised charges in their admission offer.

In the official older schedule, the first ₹4 lakh tuition payment was allocated to Trimesters I–III, while ₹3.5 lakh was allocated to Trimesters IV–VI.

Rajagiri Business School Scholarships 2026

Scholarships can substantially change the effective tuition cost. RBS lists multiple scholarship routes, with the final award subject to the institute's eligibility and selection conditions.

ScholarshipBasisPublished benefit
Diversity ScholarshipEligible non-Keralite candidates + merit/profile25–50% tuition waiver
Career & Talent ScholarshipCAT performance + admission + profile25–50% tuition waiver
Director/Dean's ScholarshipEligible CAT 2025 candidates with 95 percentile or above, subject to conditions75% tuition waiver

RBS says scholarship decisions can consider academic achievement, entrance score, GD/PI performance, extracurricular activity, educational experience and work experience. A scholarship is not an automatic reduction simply because a student crosses a percentile threshold.

RBS PGDM ROI: fee versus placement outcome

The latest NIRF data for the 2024-25 graduating cohort reports a ₹7.50 LPA median salary, while the current tuition reference is also ₹7.50 lakh. That makes the tuition-to-median comparison attractive at first glance, but it should not be interpreted as a one-year payback.

Students should compare total education cost, scholarship, loan interest, living expenses and the actual compensation structure of the offer. Median CTC also does not represent take-home salary.

Rajagiri Business School Fees FAQs

What is the RBS PGDM fee in 2026?

The current fee reference is ₹7.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM. Confirm the final 2026 fee notice before payment.

How is the RBS PGDM fee divided?

RBS's official published fee structure for 2023-24/2024-25 split ₹7.50 lakh into ₹4 lakh for the first three trimesters and ₹3.5 lakh for the final three. The 2026 instalment dates should be confirmed with RBS.

What other academic charges should I budget for?

The official older schedule separately listed organisational study, project study, placement, books and stationery, industrial visits and examination fees. These amounts should not automatically be treated as the 2026 schedule.

How much is RBS hostel fee?

The official published schedule lists ₹95,000 for the first year and ₹93,000 for the second year, or ₹1.88 lakh over two years.

What is the maximum RBS scholarship?

RBS's scholarship information lists a 75% tuition waiver under the Director/Dean's Scholarship for eligible candidates meeting its CAT 2025 and other conditions.

What is the approximate tuition plus hostel cost?

Using ₹7.50 lakh tuition and the ₹1.88 lakh published hostel reference gives approximately ₹9.38 lakh before personal and other expenses.
