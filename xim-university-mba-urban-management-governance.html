What Is MBA-UMG?

MBA-Urban Management & Governance is a specialised management programme for students interested in the systems that shape cities, urban services, public institutions, infrastructure and urban development. It is offered through XIM University's School of Human Settlements.

Best fit

Consider MBA-UMG if you want to work in urban development, city management, infrastructure, public-sector consulting, urban programmes or related policy and development organisations.

MBA-UMG Eligibility 2026

XIM's published information states that bachelor's graduates with at least 55% can apply, with additional encouragement for candidates with 60% or more and a keen interest in the urban sector. Rank holders can also apply under the stated criteria.

MBA-UMG Admission 2026

The programme had a separate 2026 application window, and XIM's admission updates listed June 30, 2026 as the application deadline for MBA-UMG and MBA-PPM. The exact selection route should be checked in the current programme instructions.

Use the XIM admission guide for the university-level process.

MBA-UMG Career Options

Potential directions include urban consulting, infrastructure and development projects, urban local bodies, public-sector organisations, NGOs, policy research, city-focused programmes and development consulting. This is a specialised programme, so career fit matters more than generic MBA comparisons.

Relevant interests

  • Urban policy and governance
  • Infrastructure and development
  • City management
  • Public-sector consulting

Compare if needed

If your goal is conventional corporate management, compare MBA-UMG with the broader MBA-BM.
