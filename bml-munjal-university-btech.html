BMU B.Tech Overview

BMU offers B.Tech programmes in Computer Science and Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Electronics & Computer Engineering. The university positions engineering education around experiential learning, industry exposure and practical projects.

Who should choose BMU B.Tech?

Students looking for a private engineering programme in Gurugram with a residential campus and industry-oriented learning can consider BMU, while comparing fees and branch-specific placement outcomes.
