TSM MBA

TSM MBA: Degree, Duration and Intake

TSM's MBA is a two-year full-time residential management programme affiliated with Madurai Kamaraj University. The current programme page lists an intake of 60 students. It is designed as a university-affiliated degree route, which is the main structural difference from TSM's autonomous PGDM.

The MBA is particularly relevant for students who prefer a formal university degree while still wanting TSM's specialist B-school environment, residential learning, internships and placement support.

MBA Curriculum: What You Actually Study

The current curriculum combines management fundamentals with quantitative decision-making, finance, marketing, operations, HR, research and newer business topics. Subjects include Data Analysis for Management, Data Modelling for Decision Making, Financial Reporting & Analysis, Marketing Foundations, Operations Management, Financial Markets, AI Essentials for Business and Applied Business Analytics.

TSM MBA Specialisation Areas

TSM lists Marketing, Finance, HR and Operations among its functional areas, while advanced electives cover business analytics, family business and entrepreneurship, public policy, ESG and strategic management.

MBA Admission 2026

TSM's current MBA programme page requires a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks and specifies TANCET/CAT for admission. The page describes document verification, counselling and seat allotment as part of the process. Applicants should follow the current admission notice because counselling timelines can change each cycle.

MBA Internship and Industry Exposure

The current MBA programme describes an eight-week mandatory summer internship, regular interaction with industry practitioners, case-study learning and a final-semester project with industry guidance. This is important when evaluating ROI because the programme is designed to combine classroom learning with workplace exposure.

MBA Career and Placement Support

TSM's placement system includes training in soft skills, resume writing and interview preparation, supported by industry relationships. The career mapping initiative also provides one-to-one counselling to help students connect strengths and aspirations with career paths.

TSM MBA FAQs

What is TSM MBA intake?

The current MBA programme page lists 60 students.

Is TSM MBA affiliated with a university?

Yes. TSM's MBA is affiliated with Madurai Kamaraj University.

Does TSM MBA accept TANCET?

Yes. The current MBA programme page lists TANCET and CAT as admission routes.

How is TSM MBA different from PGDM?

The MBA is a university-affiliated degree, while PGDM is an autonomous diploma with additional PGDM-only features such as a mandatory international study tour, capstone simulation and longer internship.
