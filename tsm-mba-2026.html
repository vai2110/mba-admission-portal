TSM MBA: Degree, Intake and Academic Structure

TSM's MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme affiliated with Madurai Kamaraj University. The current programme page lists an intake of 60 students. That smaller intake is one of the practical differences from TSM's 180-seat PGDM.

The MBA is suited to students who want a university-affiliated management degree while remaining within TSM's specialist B-school environment. The programme combines management foundations, quantitative analysis, finance, marketing, operations, HR and newer topics such as AI and business analytics.

TSM MBA Curriculum: Subject-Level Information

The curriculum is not limited to conventional management subjects. Early semesters include Data Analysis for Management, Financial Reporting & Analysis, Data Modelling for Decision Making, Cost Management, Operations Management, Financial Markets and AI Essentials for Business. Later study adds Applied Business Analytics and advanced functional electives.

TSM MBA Specialisations and Elective Areas

TSM lists Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics among its specialisation areas. Its detailed curriculum also shows Family Business & Entrepreneurship and General Management & Public Policy-related electives. Students should therefore choose based on target roles rather than simply choosing the most popular specialisation.

TSM MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility and Route

The current MBA programme page requires a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks from a recognised university. It lists a valid TANCET/CAT score and describes counselling, document verification and seat allotment.

The published key-date framework on the programme page places TANCET around March, counselling around May–June and classes around July. These are indicative programme-page dates and should be checked against the current admission notification before planning.

Mandatory Internship and Industry Learning

TSM's current MBA page describes an eight-week mandatory internship with leading organisations, regular sessions with industry experts, case-study learning and a final-semester project with industry guidance. This creates a direct bridge between academic subjects and workplace application.

The internship should be evaluated by project quality, function, organisation, mentor exposure and measurable work delivered—not simply by the fact that an internship exists.

TSM MBA Placements and Career Support

TSM provides placement preparation through soft-skills training, resume writing, interview preparation, industry engagement and career mapping. Its placement section currently publishes 2025 placement records. TSM also identifies role categories such as business development, operations, marketing, finance and HR on the MBA programme page.

For ROI, students should inspect the latest placement record and ask whether their target function appears in the recruiter and role mix. A headline highest package is not enough to estimate an individual student's likely outcome.
