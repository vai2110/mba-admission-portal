AMU Placements: What Students Should Know

Aligarh Muslim University's placement system is decentralised by academic area. The General Training & Placement Office supports non-engineering courses, while the Training & Placement Office of Zakir Husain College of Engineering & Technology (ZHCET) serves engineering students. Individual departments also publish their own placement notices and records.

Important data caution

AMU does not present one consistently updated university-wide placement figure that can be applied to every course. A credible comparison should therefore use the exact department/programme placement brochure or placement record instead of treating one headline figure as representative of the entire university.

How the AMU Placement System Works

The General TPO says its work includes inviting student registrations, receiving recruiter requirements, circulating eligible opportunities and coordinating recruitment either directly or through departmental TPOs.

Engineering Placements: ZHCET

The ZHCET Training & Placement Office describes itself as the primary interface between the college and the professional world. Its current portal provides dedicated sections for placement brochures, policies, placement announcements, internships/training, higher studies and visited recruiters.

Why engineering placement data should be checked branch-wise

AMU's engineering portfolio includes Computer Engineering, Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence), Electronics & Communication Engineering, ECE with VLSI, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical Engineering, Chemical Technology, Food Technology and Automobile Engineering. Recruiter eligibility and career outcomes can vary substantially by branch.

Computer/AI

Evaluate software, data, AI and technology roles, along with internship conversion and higher-study outcomes.

ECE/VLSI

Look for semiconductor, electronics, embedded, communications and technology recruiters rather than only generic IT roles.

Core engineering

Compare PSU, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, consulting and engineering-services opportunities by branch.

Higher studies

AMU departments also report students progressing to competitive examinations and higher education; placement should not be the only outcome metric.

MBA Placements & Management Career Outcomes

The Department of Business Administration maintains a dedicated Training & Placement page. Its published archive includes placement brochures for MBA batches, placement-week activity and an earlier MNC hiring update that reported a 14 LPA offer in 2023.

The AMU ecosystem also includes management programmes at its centres. For example, the Murshidabad centre currently lists an MBA placement brochure for the 2024-25 batch and records multiple campus drives. These centre-level outcomes should not be merged with Aligarh-campus MBA outcomes without checking the source document.

Recruiter Activity at AMU

The General TPO's current notices show recruitment activity from companies and organisations across IT, business development, consulting, services and other sectors. Recent 2026 notices include DXC Technology, Skillflow Learning, Silverskills, CodeYoung, TalentRecruit, Central Square Foundation and WSP India, among others.

Engineering recruitment is managed separately through ZHCET, while individual departments can have their own recruiter relationships. Students should therefore ask: Which recruiters hired from my programme and in which roles? rather than simply counting the university's total recruiter names.

Internships, Training & Career Preparation

Placement preparation at AMU includes recruiter coordination, student registration, job notifications, workshops, counselling and personality-development activities. ZHCET separately provides internship/training resources, and the General TPO coordinates opportunities for non-engineering courses.

What students should build before placements

  1. A one-page resume tailored to the target role.
  2. At least one demonstrable project or internship aligned with the programme.
  3. Quantitative, logical, communication and interview preparation where relevant.
  4. Technical skills for engineering and analytics-oriented roles.
  5. Case, GD, PI and business-awareness preparation for management roles.
  6. Awareness of role-specific eligibility criteria before registering for each drive.

For AMU, the most reliable comparison is therefore programme-specific: identify the department, obtain the latest placement brochure or published placement record, then compare roles, median/average if available, internship outcomes and recruiter quality.

AMU Placements FAQs

Does AMU have a placement cell?

Yes. AMU has a General Training & Placement Office and separate placement infrastructure such as the ZHCET TPO for engineering.

Does AMU publish one university-wide placement percentage?

Not in a consistently updated format that can safely be applied to every course. Placement evidence is distributed across the General TPO, ZHCET and individual departments.

What is the highest package at AMU?

There is no single current university-wide highest package that should be quoted for every programme. Historical departmental notices include high-value offers, but students should use the latest programme-specific placement report for a current figure.

How are engineering placements handled?

ZHCET has a dedicated Training & Placement Office that manages engineering placement activity and provides resources for recruiters, internships, higher studies and placement brochures.

Are MBA placement figures available?

Yes, the Department of Business Administration publishes placement-related material including brochures and campus-drive records. Historical notices include an MNC hire at 14 LPA, but that should not be treated as a 2026 average or highest package.
