ICFAI University Hyderabad BBA Overview

IFHE's management school offers BBA along with newer technology-oriented variants including BBA in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science and BBA in Cloud Computing & Cyber Security. The programmes are intended for students who want an undergraduate foundation in business with different levels of technology exposure.

Choose based on your career direction

Choose the conventional BBA if you want broad management exposure. Choose the AI/Data Science or Cloud/Cyber Security variant if you specifically want technology integrated into your business degree.

ICFAI Hyderabad BBA Fees

IFHE's current fee schedule lists BBA at ₹20,000 admission fee and ₹1.40 lakh per semester. A refundable caution deposit is also applicable. Hostel, mess and other campus expenses are separate.

BBA Career Scope

A BBA can lead to entry-level roles in sales, marketing, operations, finance support, business development, HR and analytics, or provide a base for an MBA. The AI/Data Science and Cloud/Cyber variants can make sense if you want a stronger technology-business intersection.

Student Discussion: Questions Before Choosing BBA

Students comparing BBA programmes commonly discuss the value of a specialised BBA versus a general BBA, the cost of private undergraduate education, campus exposure and whether an MBA will still be needed for the target career. These are decision themes, not official outcomes.

  • What skills will you graduate with beyond the degree?
  • Will you pursue an MBA after BBA?
  • Does the specialisation match your genuine interest?
  • Have you budgeted hostel and living costs?

ICFAI Hyderabad BBA FAQs

What is the BBA fee?

The current schedule lists ₹20,000 admission fee and ₹1.40 lakh per semester, with a refundable caution deposit and living costs extra.

Does IFHE offer specialised BBA programmes?

Yes. Current listings include BBA AI & Data Science and BBA Cloud Computing & Cyber Security in addition to BBA.

Can BBA students do an MBA later?

Yes. A BBA provides an undergraduate business foundation and can be followed by an MBA, subject to the admission requirements of the target institution.
