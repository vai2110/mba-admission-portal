MUJ B.Tech 2026: Engineering Options

MUJ's B.Tech portfolio extends beyond CSE. Current programmes include CSE, CSE & Biosciences, Electrical & Computer Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, VLSI, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Chemical, Civil, Robotics & AI and Fashion Technology among other specialised routes.

Branch choice changes the curriculum, laboratories, electives and the relevant placement pool. A university-wide compensation figure should never be treated as a CSE-specific salary expectation.

CSE-specific academic signal: the current CSE page lists 120 faculty and allows students from semester IV to use electives in AI, ML, Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Computer Vision, Full Stack Development, Web Technologies and IoT.

MUJ states that merit-based admission is subject to eligibility, merit and availability of seats. Branch allocation is not guaranteed simply because a candidate meets the threshold.

How to Choose Between MET and Merit-Based Admission

MET is the dedicated entrance route for B.Tech candidates who want their admission considered through the university's entrance ranking. Merit-based routes can be useful for students with strong Class XII, SAT or JEE Main performance.

Student positionPractical route to examine
Strong MET preparationMET 2026 route and branch preferences
Strong JEE Main scoreCheck the published CSE merit combination of JEE percentile + PMX
Strong Class XII PMXCheck the 70% PMX CSE merit route
Interested in another branchCheck that branch's own eligibility and merit rule rather than applying CSE criteria to it

The published CSE curriculum includes Statistics and Probability, Data Structures and Algorithms, RDBMS, Computer Organisation, Java/Python programming, Operating Systems and advanced computing subjects. Project-based learning begins from semester III.

B.Tech CSE Dual Degree: MUJ + Deakin University

MUJ publishes a 2+2 dual-degree route with Deakin University, Australia: two years at MUJ followed by two years at Deakin. The Deakin years have a published tuition indication of AUD 42,000 per year, subject to variation.

B.Tech Career Evidence: What Can Be Safely Inferred?

The official CSE page displays alumni placement examples associated with companies such as Shell, Accenture, Celebal Technologies, Siemens and Zscaler. MUJ's university-level admissions page reports 95% campus placement, 200 companies visiting campus, 50 Fortune 500 companies, ₹1.06 crore maximum compensation and ₹9 lakh average compensation.

Do not use the ₹1.06 crore number as an expected CSE package.

It is a university-level maximum compensation headline. For ROI, inspect branch/role mix and salary distribution wherever the latest official placement evidence provides it.

MUJ B.Tech FAQs

What is the MUJ B.Tech CSE fee?

The current CSE page lists ₹4,87,000 tuition per year, ₹10,000 registration and ₹15,000 refundable caution. The displayed amount at admission is ₹5,12,000 before hostel.

Is MET compulsory for MUJ CSE?

No. MUJ also lists merit-based admission routes using Class XII performance or SAT/JEE Main conditions.

What specialisations can CSE students choose?

From semester IV, published elective areas include Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, AI, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Computer Vision, Web Technologies, Full Stack Development and IoT.

Does MUJ have a foreign dual degree?

Yes. MUJ publishes a 2+2 CSE dual-degree route with Deakin University, with two years at MUJ and two years at Deakin.
