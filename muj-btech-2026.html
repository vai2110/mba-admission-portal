MUJ B.Tech 2026: Engineering Options

MUJ's B.Tech portfolio extends beyond CSE. Current programmes include CSE, CSE & Biosciences, Electrical & Computer Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, VLSI, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Chemical, Civil, Robotics & AI and Fashion Technology among other specialised routes.

This matters because branch choice affects curriculum, labs, electives and the relevant placement pool. Students should not use a university-wide average compensation figure as a CSE-specific salary expectation.

MUJ states that merit-based admission is subject to eligibility, merit and availability of seats. Branch allocation is therefore not guaranteed simply because a candidate meets the threshold.

How to Choose Between MET and Merit-Based Admission

MET is the dedicated entrance route for B.Tech candidates who want their admission considered through the university's entrance ranking. Merit-based routes can be attractive for students with strong Class XII, SAT or JEE Main performance. The practical decision is to keep the eligible routes open rather than assume one score automatically converts to CSE.

  • Check your Physics-Mathematics-optional-subject aggregate before applying.
  • Confirm whether your optional subject is among the subjects accepted by MUJ.
  • If using JEE Main/SAT, verify the current score/percentile and PMX combination.
  • Track branch allocation separately from admission eligibility.

B.Tech CSE Curriculum: Where MUJ Adds Career Specialisation

The CSE curriculum includes Statistics and Probability, Data Structures and Algorithms, RDBMS, Computer Organisation, object-oriented programming using Java/Python, operating systems and later advanced computing subjects. Project-based learning begins from semester III.

B.Tech CSE Dual Degree: MUJ + Deakin University

MUJ also publishes a 2+2 dual-degree route in which students spend two years at MUJ and two years at Deakin University in Australia. The Deakin years have a published tuition indication of AUD 42,000 per year, subject to variation. MUJ's first-year fee table for the dual-degree route lists ₹4.51 lakh tuition, ₹10,000 registration and ₹15,000 caution.

This route should be evaluated separately from the standard four-year B.Tech because international tuition, living costs, visa, travel and other overseas expenses materially change the total cost.

B.Tech Career Evidence: What Can Be Safely Inferred?

The official CSE page displays alumni placement examples associated with companies such as Shell, Accenture, Celebal Technologies, Siemens and Zscaler. MUJ's university-level admissions page reports 95% campus placement, 200 companies visiting campus, 50 Fortune 500 companies, ₹1.06 crore maximum compensation and ₹9 lakh average compensation.

Do not use the ₹1.06 crore number as an expected CSE package.

It is a university-level maximum compensation headline. For ROI, students should inspect programme/branch-level role mix and salary distribution wherever the latest official placement evidence provides it.

MUJ B.Tech FAQs

What is the MUJ B.Tech CSE fee?

The current CSE page lists ₹4,87,000 tuition per year, ₹10,000 registration and ₹15,000 refundable caution. The amount shown at admission is ₹5,12,000 before hostel and other costs.

Is MET compulsory for MUJ CSE?

No. MUJ also lists merit-based admission routes for eligible CSE candidates using Class XII performance or SAT/JEE Main conditions.

What specialisations can CSE students choose?

From semester IV, the published elective areas include Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, AI, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Computer Vision, Web Technologies, Full Stack Development and IoT.

Does MUJ have a foreign dual degree?

Yes. MUJ publishes a 2+2 CSE dual-degree route with Deakin University, with two years at MUJ and two years at Deakin.
