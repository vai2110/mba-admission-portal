PGDM Media & Entertainment: What You Study

WeSchool's two-year full-time PGDM Media & Entertainment is designed for management careers in media, entertainment and related creative industries. The programme sits at the intersection of business, content, marketing and media ecosystems.

Who is it for?

Graduates interested in media companies, entertainment businesses, advertising, digital content, marketing, production ecosystems and media strategy.
