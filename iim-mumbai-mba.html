IIM Mumbai MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility, CAT Cutoff, Fees & Placements

IIM Mumbai MBA: Programme Overview

IIM Mumbai's MBA is a two-year, full-time residential postgraduate programme. The 2026–28 admission policy states that the programme is open to graduates from all disciplines who meet the degree and CAT requirements. The institute's current MBA offering follows the legacy of NITIE Mumbai while operating as IIM Mumbai.

IIM Mumbai MBA Eligibility 2026

The official 2026–28 MBA admission policy requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum is relaxed to 45% for candidates belonging to SC, ST and PwD categories. The degree must involve at least three years of education after 10+2 or an equivalent qualification.

Eligibility for final-year students

Final-year bachelor's degree students and candidates who have completed degree requirements but are awaiting results can apply. If selected, they can join provisionally only after submitting the required certificate confirming completion of degree requirements by June 30, 2026. The final marksheet and degree certificate must be submitted by December 31, 2026. Failure to meet these conditions leads to cancellation of provisional admission.

How IIM Mumbai treats CGPA

If the university provides an official CGPA-to-percentage conversion, that conversion is used. If the institution does not have a conversion scheme, IIM Mumbai calculates the percentage by dividing the obtained CGPA by the maximum possible CGPA and multiplying by 100.

Eligibility is not the same as shortlist probability

Important for applicants

The institute explicitly states that meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee shortlisting at any stage. Meeting the CAT minimums only makes a candidate eligible for consideration for the next stage.

What CAT percentile should you target for IIM Mumbai?

The official minimum for the Open category is 85 overall, but the institute itself cautions that the actual section-wise and overall cutoffs may be much higher. For a General/Open-category applicant, a practical preparation target should therefore be set materially above 85. A target of around 97–99+ percentile is a planning benchmark rather than an official cutoff or guarantee of a PI call.

Do not treat 85 percentile as a safe CAT target

85 overall with the required sectional percentiles is the published minimum for Stage I. It does not mean that an 85-percentile candidate will necessarily receive a PI call. Shortlisting depends on the CAT score distribution and the number of candidates to be called.

IIM Mumbai MBA Admission Process 2026

The 2026–28 admission policy divides the process into three stages. CAT 2025 is used for the first-stage threshold and PI shortlisting. Final selection is based on a composite score after the Personal Interview.

Stage 1: Meet CAT 2025 cutoff requirements

Candidates must meet both the sectional and overall CAT 2025 percentile requirements for their category. The minimum thresholds are listed in the CAT cutoff table above.

Stage 2: Shortlisting for Personal Interview

Among candidates satisfying Stage I, IIM Mumbai shortlists candidates for the Personal Interview based on CAT 2025 score, sectional cutoffs and reservation category. The institute does not state that merely crossing the published minimum guarantees a PI call.

Stage 3: Personal Interview and final merit list

Candidates appearing for the PI are evaluated through a final composite score. The 2026–28 MBA policy assigns 60% to CAT 2025, 20% to the Personal Interview and 20% to the Academic Performance & Work Experience score.

How APWE is calculated

The Academic Performance & Work Experience score uses Class 10, Class 12, bachelor's degree and work experience. IIM Mumbai applies rating scales that differ by academic stream and bachelor's discipline. The policy specifies an academic multiplier of 0.70 for Class 10, Class 12 and bachelor's ratings, and a 0.20 multiplier for the work-experience component.

Work experience is useful but not mandatory

The regular MBA eligibility criteria do not prescribe a minimum work-experience requirement. However, relevant work experience contributes to the APWE component of final selection.

Reservation and category rules

The 2026–28 policy states that reservation follows Government of India requirements. It lists 27% for NC-OBC, 15% for SC, 7.5% for ST, 5% for PwD and up to 10% for EWS, subject to applicable rules and conditions.

IIM Mumbai MBA Fees 2026-28

The latest official IIM Mumbai admission page lists the gross programme fee for the two-year MBA at ₹25 lakh per student. A refundable caution money deposit of ₹50,000 is payable at admission. Mess charges and the mess deposit are separate from the programme fee.

What students need to budget beyond the programme fee

  • ₹50,000 refundable caution money deposit.
  • Mess charges and mess deposit, which IIM Mumbai states are payable separately.
  • Personal expenses such as travel, local transport, optional purchases and other individual expenses.
Fee planning point

The ₹25 lakh figure should not be treated as the complete cash outflow for the two-year residential MBA. The caution deposit and mess-related payments are additional to the gross programme fee.

Who Should Apply to IIM Mumbai MBA?

Good fit if

  • You want a full-time residential MBA and are comfortable with a CAT-led admission process.
  • You are interested in consulting, operations, supply chain, analytics, technology, finance or general management roles.
  • You can compete above the published minimum CAT threshold.
  • You want a programme where academic record and work experience also contribute to final selection.

Consider carefully if

  • You are treating the 85 percentile Open-category minimum as a guaranteed PI cutoff.
  • You are comparing only the highest placement package.
  • You have not budgeted for mess charges, mess deposit and the refundable caution deposit.
  • You need a narrowly specialised MBA rather than a broader management programme.

IIM Mumbai MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Mumbai MBA CAT cutoff for 2026-28?

The official minimum for the Open category is 85 overall, with 80 in VARC, 80 in DILR and 75 in QA. IIM Mumbai states that actual cutoffs may be higher.

Is 85 percentile enough for IIM Mumbai MBA?

It is the published minimum for Stage I consideration for the Open category. It should not be treated as a safe or guaranteed PI-call percentile because the institute states that actual cutoffs may be higher.

What is the IIM Mumbai MBA fee?

The latest official admission page lists ₹25 lakh as the gross programme fee for the two-year MBA, plus a refundable ₹50,000 caution money deposit. Mess charges and the mess deposit are separate.

Does IIM Mumbai require work experience for MBA?

No. The regular MBA eligibility criteria do not prescribe minimum work experience. Work experience is considered in the Academic Performance & Work Experience component of final selection.

What is the IIM Mumbai MBA selection process?

The process has CAT-based Stage I screening, PI shortlisting in Stage II and final composite scoring in Stage III. For 2026–28, the stated final weights are CAT 60%, PI 20% and APWE 20%.

What was the IIM Mumbai MBA average package in 2026?

The official 2026 placement report records an average CTC of ₹31.40 lakh for the combined MBA, MBA-OSCM and MBA-SM graduating cohort.
