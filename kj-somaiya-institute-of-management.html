KJ Somaiya Institute of Management: MBA Courses & Key Highlights

K J Somaiya Institute of Management (KJSIM) is a management institute in Vidyavihar, Mumbai. Its current portfolio includes a two-year full-time MBA, MBA Healthcare Management, MBA Sports Management, MBA Marketing Communications, and executive/working-professional programmes.

The flagship MBA is a two-year full-time programme with a broad management curriculum and major/minor choices. KJSIM describes its pedagogy through classroom learning, cases, real-world assignments, simulations and industry interface.

What Is the Last Date to Apply for KJSIM MBA 2027–29?

KJSIM currently lists 20 January 2027, 11:59 PM as the application deadline for MBA, MBA-HCM, MBA-SM and MBA-MC. The institute may publish round-wise shortlist, WAT, PSA and PI schedules separately.

Who Is Eligible for KJ Somaiya MBA Admission 2027?

For the MBA programmes, applicants must have completed or be pursuing a minimum three-year bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks from a university recognised by the institute's stated eligibility rules.

Final-year students appearing for examinations in April–May 2027 may apply, but must submit proof of graduation with the required marks by 31 August 2027. The institute states that the graduation examination process must be completed before joining.

Before applying: Check your university's CGPA-to-percentage conversion method and keep the official conversion letter ready if your marksheet uses CGPA.

How Do You Apply to KJSIM and What Happens After the Form?

  1. Select the programme: confirm whether you are applying for the flagship MBA or a specialised MBA.
  2. Complete the online form: submit the application through the institute portal before the deadline. The official FAQ says MBA and MBA-HCM can be applied for using one form; MBA-SM and MBA-MC require separate forms.
  3. Submit an accepted score: CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT or CMAT 2027 for the flagship MBA; programme-specific accepted tests may differ for specialised programmes.
  4. Shortlisting: MBA candidates can be considered through merit-based or profile-based shortlisting. Profile-based consideration includes consistent academics, a valid score and 12–42 months of full-time paid post-graduation work experience.
  5. Complete assessment: shortlisted candidates complete the applicable PSA, WAT, PI or video-resume stages as specified for their programme.
  6. Check merit and seat allotment: offers depend on merit position, programme preference and seat availability.
  7. Pay fees through the portal: selected candidates receive login details; the institute says payment is made through its portal, not cash, cheque or demand draft.

Does KJSIM Publish a Fixed CAT or XAT Cutoff?

The current official admission page confirms that KJSIM uses institute-set cutoffs for merit-based shortlisting, but it does not publish a fixed numeric CAT, XAT, NMAT, CMAT or GMAT cutoff on the page reviewed.

For the flagship MBA, the institute also describes profile-based shortlisting for candidates with consistent academics, a valid entrance score and 12–42 months of full-time paid work experience after graduation. A predicted percentile from a coaching website is not an official call guarantee.

For the selection weightage and how the profile route works, see the KJSIM MBA admission and selection guide.

KJ Somaiya Institute of Management: Student FAQs

What is the last date for KJ Somaiya MBA 2027–29?

The official admission page currently lists 20 January 2027 at 11:59 PM for MBA and MBA-HCM. The same deadline is listed for MBA-SM and MBA-MC.

Can I apply to MBA and MBA-HCM using one application?

Yes. KJSIM's FAQ states that one application form can be used for MBA and MBA-HCM. MBA-SM and MBA-MC require separate application forms.

What is the minimum graduation percentage for KJSIM MBA?

The current eligibility requirement is at least 50% aggregate in a minimum three-year bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Final-year applicants must meet the institute's result-submission deadline.

Does KJ Somaiya publish an official CAT cutoff?

The official admission page says institute cutoffs are used for merit shortlisting but does not publish one fixed numeric CAT percentile on the page reviewed. Profile-based shortlisting is also available under stated conditions.

Is the ₹23.875 lakh fee for the 2027–29 batch?

No. ₹23.875 lakh is the detailed published fee for the 2026–28 flagship MBA batch. KJSIM's current 2027–29 admission information should be checked for the revised fee before budgeting or paying.

Is hostel included in the MBA fee?

No. KJSIM lists hostel fees separately. Students should budget separately for accommodation, food and personal living costs.

Does KJSIM provide a laptop?

No. The institute's FAQ recommends that students have their own laptop and states that a laptop is not provided as part of the fee.

What is the latest KJSIM MBA median package?

The latest official placement highlight reviewed reports ₹12 LPA median CTC for MBA 2024–26. The ₹29.30 LPA figure is the highest CTC, not the average or median.
