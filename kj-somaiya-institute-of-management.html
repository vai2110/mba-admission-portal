What is KJ Somaiya Institute of Management?

Quick answer

K J Somaiya Institute of Management (KJSIM) is a management institute in Vidyavihar, Mumbai. Its current portfolio includes a two-year full-time MBA, MBA Healthcare Management, MBA Sports Management, MBA Marketing Communications, and executive/working-professional programmes.

The flagship MBA is a two-year full-time programme with a broad management curriculum and major/minor choices. KJSIM describes its pedagogy through classroom learning, cases, real-world assignments, simulations and industry interface.

KJ Somaiya MBA Eligibility

Applicants must have completed or be pursuing a minimum three-year graduate programme in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognised university. Final-year students appearing for their exams in April-May 2027 can also apply, subject to the institute's conditions.

KJSIM accepts CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT, NMAT and CMAT 2027 for the current MBA admission cycle. GMAT scores must fall within the institute's stated validity window.

How does KJ Somaiya MBA Admission work?

  1. Apply through the KJSIM application form.
  2. Submit a valid accepted entrance-exam score.
  3. For MBA, candidates may be considered through merit-based or profile-based shortlisting.
  4. Shortlisted candidates participate in the Problem Situation Analysis (PSA), Personal Interview (PI) and Written Ability Test (WAT), as applicable.
  5. The final result combines the notified selection parameters and seat availability.

What is the KJ Somaiya MBA Cutoff?

KJSIM's current 2027-29 admission page states that merit-based candidates are shortlisted by meeting or exceeding the institute's cutoffs for accepted entrance exams. It does not publish a single fixed numeric CAT/XAT/NMAT/CMAT/GMAT cutoff on the page reviewed.

Do not use an unofficial percentile as the official cutoff

Coaching-site estimates can be useful for planning, but they should not be presented as KJSIM's official cutoff unless the institute publishes that number.

KJ Somaiya Placements: Latest MBA Outcomes

KJSIM's latest public placement highlights for MBA 2024-26 report a ₹29.30 LPA highest package, ₹12 LPA median CTC and 210 total recruiters. For the 2025-27 internship cycle, the institute reports 126 recruiters and a highest stipend of ₹3 lakh.

Because the institute's current public placement page highlights median and highest CTC rather than a batch-wide average, students should avoid inventing an overall average from other sources.
