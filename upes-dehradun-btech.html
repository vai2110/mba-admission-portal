UPES B.Tech Overview

UPES offers B.Tech programmes through its School of Computer Science and School of Advanced Engineering, with options spanning computer science, AI, cybersecurity, petroleum, aerospace, mechanical, electronics and other emerging areas.

Who should consider UPES B.Tech?

Students who want a specialised engineering degree, industry-oriented curriculum and international pathway options should compare UPES with other private engineering universities on specialisation, total cost and programme-level placement outcomes.

Popular UPES B.Tech Specialisations

B.Tech CSE

Core computer science with options including AI/ML, cloud, cybersecurity, data science and full-stack development.

Mathematics & Computing

Higher academic eligibility and a stronger mathematics-computing orientation.

Applied Petroleum Engineering

Energy-focused engineering with petroleum and related specialisations.

Aerospace / Mechanical

Engineering pathways aligned with advanced engineering and mobility sectors.

UPES B.Tech Eligibility

For regular B.Tech programmes, UPES lists a minimum 50% in Class X and XII with 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in Class XII. B.Tech Mathematics & Computing has a higher requirement of 70% in Class X and XII and 70% PCM.

UPES B.Tech Fee Breakdown & Placement Comparison

The currently listed tuition range is ₹8.06–13.46 lakh across B.Tech programmes. International pathways can have different first-year fees; for example, the current global-program listing shows ₹5.16 lakh for B.Tech CSE and ₹3.01 lakh for several engineering pathways before partner-university costs.

What is usually NOT part of the headline tuition?

Hostel, transport, technology, programme-specific consumables/training, insurance, I-Card and other applicable charges can be additional. UPES also lists a ₹31,300 one-time training/certification fee for applicable full-time programmes, ₹9,000 PDC fee for PG programmes and ₹23,300 for UG programmes, subject to programme exclusions.

UPES B.Tech Fees

Current fee listings place UPES B.Tech tuition across programmes at approximately ₹8.06 lakh to ₹13.46 lakh, depending on specialisation. A current international-pathway listing shows first-year fees of ₹5.16 lakh for B.Tech CSE and ₹3.01 lakh for several engineering pathways. Fees can change by specialisation and pathway.

Fee caution:

Use the official 2026-27 fee structure before payment. Hostel, transport, technology and other charges are separate where applicable.

UPES B.Tech Admission 2026

UPES lists UPESEAT, JEE Main, Board Merit, SAT and CUET among selection routes. For 2026-27, its non-exam pathway includes board-merit options subject to programme-specific marks.

UPES B.Tech Placements

UPES reports school-level placement outcomes and identifies B.Tech CSE and other engineering cohorts separately. Its B.Tech course page currently highlights a 17.5 LPA top-10% average CTC and a ₹1.3 crore highest package secured by an alumnus. Treat the alumnus package as an exceptional outcome, not a typical B.Tech salary.

What to check before choosing a specialisation:

Compare the exact B.Tech specialisation's recruiters, role profiles, internship opportunities and top-10%/median-type indicators wherever available.

UPES B.Tech FAQs

What is the UPES B.Tech eligibility?

Generally 50% in Class X and XII plus 50% PCM in Class XII. Mathematics & Computing has higher requirements.

Which exams are accepted?

UPES lists UPESEAT, JEE Main, Board Merit, SAT and CUET depending on the admission route.

What is the B.Tech fee?

Fees vary by specialisation. Current listings show about ₹8.06–13.46 lakh tuition across B.Tech programmes.
