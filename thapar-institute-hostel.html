Thapar Hostel Overview

TIET currently lists 17 on-campus hostels, including 10 boys' and 7 girls' hostels. The institute describes the hostels as residential facilities with Wi-Fi, security and student support.

Rooms and Accommodation

Room configurations vary across hostels. Current listings include single, double and three-seater accommodation, with AC and non-AC options depending on the hostel. Some newer hostels offer attached or shared washrooms.

Hostel Facilities

Connectivity

24×7 Wi-Fi is available in the hostels.

Study

Reading rooms and common study areas are available in several hostels.

Daily Living

Mess, laundry and cleaning services are provided.

Recreation

Many hostels include gyms, TV/common rooms and indoor-game facilities.

Hostel Rules and Student Safety

TIET lists 24×7 security, CCTV, manned gates and identity/security systems. Its parent information also mentions biometric night attendance and RFID-enabled identity cards. Hostel-specific rules can vary, so students should read the current reporting and allotment instructions.

Thapar Hostel Cost

Hostel charges depend on accommodation type and the applicable academic-year fee schedule. Because room categories differ, students should not assume one hostel fee applies to every resident.

Student Discussion: What Matters in Hostel Life?

Students usually care about room occupancy, AC/non-AC choice, food quality, internet reliability, distance from academic blocks, study environment, laundry and hostel rules. These practical factors can materially affect the experience.

Thapar Hostel FAQs

How many hostels does Thapar have?

TIET currently lists 17 on-campus hostels: 10 for boys and 7 for girls.

Is Wi-Fi available?

TIET states that its hostels have high-speed/24×7 Wi-Fi connectivity.

Are AC rooms available?

Yes. AC and non-AC room options exist across different hostels, depending on the accommodation.
