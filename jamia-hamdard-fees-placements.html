Jamia Hamdard Fees & Placements FAQs: 10 High-Intent Questions

What is the Jamia Hamdard fee structure?

Jamia Hamdard uses programme-wise fees rather than one common university fee. The currently accessible official fee sheet lists annual figures such as ₹2.60 lakh for MBA, ₹1.60 lakh for B.Pharm General and ₹2.20 lakh for B.Tech CSE, plus one-time and recurring charges. The accessible PDF is labelled 2025-26, while 2026-27 notices include a fee corrigendum, so the final 2026-27 amount should be verified before payment.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA fee?

The fee PDF currently linked by the university lists ₹2.60 lakh per year for MBA in all disciplines before additional charges. A 2026-27 MBA General selection list records an SFS semester fee of ₹1.40 lakh plus admission, enrolment, library, alumni, examination and e-governance charges. Use the exact fee classification in the current selection/offer document.

What is the Jamia Hamdard B.Pharm fee?

The published fee schedule lists ₹1.60 lakh/year for B.Pharm General and ₹2.40 lakh/year for B.Pharm SFS, before applicable additional charges. The current 2026-27 fee corrigendum should be checked because the directly accessible fee PDF is labelled 2025-26.

What additional fees are charged at Jamia Hamdard?

The accessible fee sheet lists ₹5,000 admission, ₹5,000 enrolment, ₹3,000 alumni fund and ₹5,000 refundable library deposit as one-time charges. For schools other than Humanities & Social Sciences it lists ₹10,000 examination and ₹1,000 e-governance charges annually. Programme-specific documents can differ.

Is hostel included in Jamia Hamdard tuition fees?

No. Hostel and mess are separate from academic fees. The university states that accommodation is limited and university admission does not guarantee hostel allotment. Allotted residents pay hostel charges in advance and must join the mess. Total cost of attendance should therefore include academic, accommodation and living costs.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA median salary?

NIRF 2025 Management data reports a ₹5.95 LPA median salary for 2023-24 graduates in the reported Management category. The previous medians were ₹5.52 LPA and ₹4.63 LPA. These are cohort medians, not a guaranteed salary for every student.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA placement rate?

In the 2023-24 NIRF Management submission, 202 out of 230 students graduating in minimum stipulated time were placed, or roughly 88%. This applies to the reported Management category and should not be extrapolated to pharmacy, engineering or every programme.

What is the Jamia Hamdard engineering placement salary?

NIRF 2025 reports a ₹5.45 LPA median for four-year engineering UG graduates in 2023-24 and ₹6.20 LPA for the two-year engineering PG category. The corresponding placement counts were 197 of 337 for UG and 42 of 60 for PG.

Is Jamia Hamdard Pharmacy really ranked No. 1?

Yes. Jamia Hamdard was ranked 1st in Pharmacy in NIRF 2025 with a score of 86.59. This is specifically the Pharmacy category and should be treated as evidence of strength in pharmacy, not as a universal ranking for MBA, engineering or other courses.

How should I calculate Jamia Hamdard ROI?

Calculate the complete cost of the specific programme, including tuition, one-time charges, recurring examination/e-governance charges, hostel/mess and living expenses. Then compare it with the programme-specific median salary and placement rate, while also checking role quality, recruiters, internships and higher-study outcomes. This is more reliable than comparing only tuition or the highest package.
