For MBA and other schools outside Humanities & Social Sciences, the published schedule therefore adds ₹18,000 in one-time charges plus ₹11,000 annually for examination and e-governance charges.

MBA example: ₹2.60 lakh annual course fee + ₹18,000 one-time charges + ₹11,000 annual exam/e-governance charges = approximately ₹2.89 lakh in the first year, before hostel, mess and any programme-specific extras. The second year is approximately ₹2.71 lakh on the same published schedule.

Hostel and mess are separate. Students should add these costs when calculating total cost of attendance.

Pharmacy: Strong academic positioning, separate placement check needed

Jamia Hamdard ranked 1st in NIRF 2025 Pharmacy. That is a meaningful signal for academic and research positioning in pharmacy, but a ranking is not a placement guarantee.

The published fee sheet lists ₹1.60 lakh per year for B.Pharm General and ₹2.40 lakh per year for B.Pharm SFS, before applicable additional charges. Students evaluating B.Pharm should separately check B.Pharm-specific placement data, roles, recruiters and higher-study outcomes.

How to judge Jamia Hamdard ROI

1Calculate total costInclude tuition, one-time fees, exam/e-governance charges, hostel and living costs.
2Use median salaryMedian is more useful than a highest package for ROI planning.
3Match programmeCompare MBA with MBA, B.Pharm with B.Pharm, not university-wide numbers.
4Check role qualitySalary without job role and recruiter context is incomplete.

Bottom line: Jamia Hamdard's pharmacy reputation is a clear differentiator. For management and engineering, the official NIRF data suggests moderate placement outcomes, so ROI should be assessed against the current fee schedule and alternative colleges.

Fees & Placements FAQs

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA fee?

The official fee sheet currently linked by the university lists ₹2.60 lakh per year for MBA in all disciplines, before one-time and annual examination/e-governance charges.

What is the Jamia Hamdard B.Pharm fee?

The published fee sheet lists ₹1.60 lakh per year for B.Pharm General and ₹2.40 lakh per year for B.Pharm SFS, before applicable additional charges.

What are the additional Jamia Hamdard fees?

The published fee sheet lists ₹5,000 admission, ₹5,000 enrolment, ₹3,000 alumni fund and ₹5,000 refundable library deposit as one-time charges. For all other schools it lists ₹10,000 examination and ₹1,000 e-governance fees annually.

What is Jamia Hamdard MBA median salary?

NIRF 2025 Management data reports a ₹5.95 LPA median salary for the 2023-24 graduating cohort.

What is the Jamia Hamdard engineering placement salary?

NIRF 2025 reports a ₹5.45 LPA median for four-year engineering UG graduates and ₹6.20 LPA for the two-year engineering PG category in 2023-24.
