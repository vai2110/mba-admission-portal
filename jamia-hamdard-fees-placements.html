Jamia Hamdard Fees 2026: Why one number is misleading

Quick answer

Jamia Hamdard has no single university-wide fee. Academic cost varies by programme, school, regular/SFS structure and applicable one-time and examination charges. The official 2026 prospectus and fee corrigendum should be treated as the source of truth.

The university's current public self-disclosure page links its regular-course prospectus and fee structure. Students should build a total-cost estimate that includes tuition + one-time charges + examinations + hostel/living costs, rather than comparing only the headline tuition fee.

MBA Fees vs Placements

The 2024-25 official MBA selection list recorded a semester fee of ₹1.25 lakh and an admission-payment total of ₹1,48,500 after listed additional charges. Because the university has issued a 2026 fee corrigendum, this older figure should be treated only as historical reference.

Engineering Placement Data

NIRF 2025 Engineering data provides a separate picture. For PG two-year engineering programmes, the 2023-24 graduating cohort had 60 students graduating in minimum time, 42 placed and a median salary of ₹6.20 lakh.

Pharmacy: Ranking is strong, but salary must be checked separately

Jamia Hamdard ranked 1st in NIRF 2025 Pharmacy. That is a meaningful signal for academic/research positioning in pharmacy, but a ranking is not a placement guarantee.

Students evaluating B.Pharm should ask for B.Pharm-specific placement data, roles, recruiters and higher-study outcomes. A pharmacy student's ROI can also come through M.Pharm, Pharm.D-related pathways, regulatory affairs, clinical research, pharmacovigilance and other specialised careers rather than only campus salary.

How to judge Jamia Hamdard ROI

1Calculate total costInclude tuition, one-time fees, hostel and living costs.
2Use median salaryMedian is more useful than a highest package for ROI planning.
3Match programmeCompare MBA with MBA, B.Pharm with B.Pharm, not university-wide numbers.
4Check role qualitySalary without job role and recruiter context is incomplete.

Bottom line: Jamia Hamdard's pharmacy reputation is a clear differentiator. For management and engineering, the official NIRF data suggests moderate placement outcomes, so ROI should be assessed against the current 2026 fee and alternative colleges.
