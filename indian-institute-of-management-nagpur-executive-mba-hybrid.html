Who is the Executive MBA Hybrid designed for?

IIM Nagpur's Executive MBA (Hybrid) is intended for employed professionals who want to build management capability while continuing to work. The published 2026–28 admissions policy covers the Nagpur and Pune campus cohorts. It is different from a full-time residential MBA; applicants should confirm the timetable, attendance and travel requirements before applying.

Best fit: a working professional with relevant experience who can reserve time for classes, assessments and required programme activities.

What are the eligibility requirements?

The 2026–28 policy requires a bachelor's degree or equivalent in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA. Professional qualifications such as CA, ICWA or CS can also be considered if the stated marks requirement is met.

Applicants need at least three years of managerial, entrepreneurial or professional experience, with the policy specifying three years in the last five years as of the application deadline. The application portal also says applicants must be currently employed full-time.

  • Keep degree records and proof of employment ready.
  • Ensure experience dates and role descriptions match supporting documents.
  • Check the policy's exact date for calculating experience.

What is the programme fee?

The published 2026–28 policy lists a total fee of ₹9,50,000 plus a ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit, paid across four instalments. Confirm the offer-acceptance amount and due dates in the current fee schedule.

Ask whether campus visits, materials and travel are included. Do not copy the fee of IIM Nagpur's separate Blended MBA for Working Professionals onto this programme.

How does selection work?

The institute describes three main stages: application scrutiny, the IIM Nagpur Admission Test (IIMNAT) screening test and Personal Interview.

  1. Confirm education, experience and employment eligibility.
  2. Submit the online form and documents before the deadline.
  3. Complete the screening step in the current policy.
  4. Attend the interview if shortlisted.
  5. Review offer, fee and programme calendar before accepting.

An application or test score alone is not a guarantee of admission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can freshers apply?

No. The 2026–28 policy requires at least three years of relevant experience and current full-time employment.

What is the minimum graduation score?

50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA under the published policy.

What is the fee?

₹9.50 lakh plus a ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit for the published 2026–28 batch.

Does selection include an interview?

Yes, after application scrutiny and IIMNAT screening.

Is this the Blended MBA?

No. These are separate IIM Nagpur programmes.
