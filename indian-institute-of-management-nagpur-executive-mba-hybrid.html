Programme Overview

The Executive MBA (Hybrid) at IIM Nagpur is a two-year programme specifically designed for mid- to senior-level executives. The hybrid format allows professionals to pursue advanced management education while continuing their current career trajectory.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the Executive MBA (Hybrid) programme, candidates must meet the following requirements:

  • A minimum of 50% marks or equivalent in graduation.
  • A minimum of 3 years of professional work experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of the Executive MBA (Hybrid)?
The programme is a two-year course.
Who is this programme designed for?
It is designed for mid- to senior-level executives seeking to enhance their management skills without leaving their jobs.
What is the mode of delivery?
The programme is conducted in a hybrid mode.
