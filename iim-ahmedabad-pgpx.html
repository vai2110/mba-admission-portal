Programme Overview

MBA-PGPX is IIM Ahmedabad's one-year, full-time residential MBA programme designed for experienced professionals who want to move towards larger management responsibilities, leadership roles or a career transition.

Unlike the two-year PGP, PGPX is built around the experience already brought by the cohort. The programme is designed to develop a general management perspective for experienced managers, with a strong focus on leadership, decision-making and preparation for top-management responsibilities.

The key distinction: PGPX is not simply a shorter version of the two-year MBA. It is specifically designed for professionals who already have substantial post-graduation work experience.

Eligibility for MBA-PGPX 2027–28

Bachelor's Degree

A Bachelor's degree or equivalent in any discipline is required, with a minimum of 15 years of formal education under the Indian education system.

Age Requirement

Candidates must be at least 25 years old as on March 31, 2027. There is no upper age limit.

Work Experience

At least four years of full-time work experience after completion of graduation is required as on March 31, 2027.

Academic marks: IIMA's current PGPX FAQ states that there is no minimum bachelor's-level marks requirement for eligibility. However, selected candidates are likely to have a consistently good academic record.

GMAT / GRE Requirement

A valid GMAT or GRE score is required for the PGPX application. Candidates should follow the test-validity conditions specified by IIMA for their application round.

Test Accepted? Important Condition
GMAT Yes IIMA accepts eligible GMAT scores according to the programme's stated validity conditions.
GRE Yes A valid GRE score within the applicable validity window is required.
CAT No PGPX follows its own GMAT/GRE-based admission process rather than the CAT process used for the flagship two-year PGP.
Do not use a CAT cutoff for PGPX. PGPX has a separate admissions process based on GMAT/GRE, professional experience, application profile and the institute's selection process.

Admission & Selection Process

Stage 1 — Application & Shortlisting

Candidates submit their application with their GMAT/GRE score, academic history, employment history, achievements and other requested information. IIMA then shortlists candidates for the next stage.

Stage 2 — Essays

Shortlisted candidates submit essays on specified topics within the stipulated period. These essays form part of the selection assessment.

Stage 3 — Personal Interview

Shortlisted applicants are interviewed by an IIMA faculty panel. The panel evaluates factors including professional background, quality and progression of experience, academic preparedness, leadership potential, accomplishments, interpersonal skills and programme fit.

What this means for applicants: A high GMAT/GRE score alone does not make a PGPX application strong. The quality and progression of your professional experience and your leadership trajectory matter substantially.

International Immersion Programme costs are excluded from the programme fee, along with related travel and stay expenses. Other personal and utility expenses may also be additional.

Curriculum & Learning Experience

The programme is structured across six terms and combines management fundamentals with a dedicated focus on preparing participants for top-management responsibilities.

Management Foundation

Core management learning strengthens understanding of finance, marketing, operations, economics, data and organisational behaviour.

Top Management

The programme focuses on complex managerial problems, leadership, strategic thinking and change management.

International Immersion

International Immersion provides exposure to different economic and cultural environments.

Electives

Electives allow participants to align learning with their target industry, function and career goals.

Case Method

Case discussions are supplemented by lectures, seminars, role plays, industrial visits and group exercises.

Capstone

The programme culminates in an integrative capstone experience focused on complex decision-making.

Professional Backgrounds

The cohort includes professionals from areas such as BFSI, consulting, energy and utilities, IT, manufacturing, defence and security, e-commerce, FMCG, media and advertising, aerospace and aviation, healthcare and logistics.

What the batch profile tells you: PGPX is built around experienced peers rather than fresh graduates. Classroom value therefore depends heavily on your ability to contribute professional perspectives and learn from people with different industry backgrounds.

Placements & Career Outcomes

PGPX career services are designed around participants who already have several years of professional experience. IIMA states that the placement process is aligned with the profiles and aspirations of individual batches and targets middle and senior management roles.

Recruiter Sectors

Banking, consulting, energy, financial services, IT, manufacturing, logistics, automotive and diversified business groups are among the sectors represented in PGPX recruitment.

Career Support

Career workshops, speaker sessions and individual coaching help participants develop focused career strategies.

Placement Format

PGPX uses a wave-based placement process followed by rolling recruitment, reflecting the experienced nature of the cohort.

Placement reality: IIMA states that placements are not guaranteed or an entitlement of students. For PGPX, your pre-MBA career, target role, industry and experience level remain important when evaluating expected ROI.

Official Placement Data

IIMA publishes audited PGPX placement reports under the Indian Placement Reporting Standards (IPRS). Use the latest official report for salary comparisons instead of relying on unverified third-party placement claims.

Who Should Apply for MBA-PGPX?

Mid-Career Professionals

Professionals with four or more years of experience who now need broader management exposure.

Career Switchers

Professionals targeting a meaningful shift in function, industry or management responsibility.

Future Senior Managers

Professionals whose next career step requires stronger strategy, leadership, finance, operations or general-management capabilities.

Who Should Think Twice Before Choosing PGPX?

  • Professionals with less than four years of post-graduation work experience.
  • Candidates looking for a traditional two-year MBA experience with more time for exploration.
  • Applicants expecting the IIMA brand alone to guarantee a career switch or salary jump.
  • Professionals whose current career trajectory is already strong and who cannot justify the opportunity cost of leaving work for a full-time programme.
Realistic verdict: PGPX makes the most sense when you can clearly explain what you want to do after one year and why an intensive IIMA MBA is the right bridge from your current experience to that next role.

FAQs

Is PGPX a one-year MBA?
Yes. IIMA describes MBA-PGPX as a one-year, full-time residential MBA programme for experienced professionals.
How much work experience is required?
A minimum of four years of full-time work experience after graduation is required for the 2027–28 batch.
Is CAT accepted for PGPX?
PGPX uses GMAT/GRE for its admission process rather than the CAT-based process used for the flagship two-year PGP.
Is there a minimum graduation percentage?
IIMA's current PGPX FAQ states that there is no minimum bachelor's-level marks requirement for eligibility.
What is the PGPX fee?
For 2026–27, IIMA lists ₹35 lakh for Single Student Housing and ₹37.10 lakh for Married Student Housing. The 2027–28 fee should be checked when IIMA publishes it.
Is International Immersion compulsory?
Yes. IIMA describes International Immersion as a compulsory component of PGPX.
Does IIMA guarantee a placement after PGPX?
No. IIMA explicitly states that placements are not guaranteed or an entitlement of students.
