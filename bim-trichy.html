What is Bharathidasan Institute of Management Trichy known for?

Quick answer:

BIM Trichy is an autonomous school of excellence of Bharathidasan University offering a two-year full-time residential MBA. It was established in 1984 and is AICTE-approved and affiliated with Bharathidasan University.

BIM is particularly known for its industry-linked management education, its π-shaped programme architecture, corporate engagement and specialised centres such as FINTRAC with Bloomberg terminals, a real-time contextual marketing lab, operations excellence and AI/ML facilities.

The institute is not a specialist MBA only for commerce graduates. Its published student profiles include graduates from engineering, science, arts, computer applications and other disciplines. For 2027-29, BIM accepts a bachelor's or master's degree in any discipline with 50% marks.

BIM Trichy Admission 2027-29

The current official admission cycle is for the 2027-29 MBA batch. Online registration began on September 7, 2026 and the application window is scheduled to close on January 31, 2027. BIM says CAT 2026, XAT 2027 or GMAT can be used for shortlisting.

Shortlisted candidates are called for Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Final selection considers academic grades, entrance score, work experience and GD/PI performance.

BIM Trichy Fees 2026-28: actual published fee

For the 2026-28 batch, the published institute fee totals ₹9,32,012 in Year 1 and ₹9,38,833 in Year 2, or ₹18,70,845 across two years. Mess charges of ₹75,000 per year on actual basis are additional.

Optional costs include a PG Certificate in SAP and international summer/exchange programmes. Students should therefore distinguish the published academic/residential fee from optional extras.

BIM Trichy Campus, Hostel and Student Experience

BIM operates in Tiruchirappalli and has developed a new campus on the Trichy-Pudukottai Highway in addition to its heritage campus inside the BHEL complex. The new campus is built on about 18 acres with approximately 3.4 lakh sq ft of built-up area.

The MBA is residential. BIM states that students receive furnished twin-sharing accommodation with attached bathrooms. The dining system is managed through a student mess committee.

BIM Trichy FAQs: what students actually want to know

Is Bharathidasan Institute of Management a good MBA college?

BIM is a long-established management school with a two-year residential MBA, industry-linked curriculum, CRISIL-audited placement reporting and specialised facilities such as Bloomberg terminals and analytics labs. Whether it is the right choice depends on your target role, location preference, fee budget and the value you place on a residential MBA.

Is BIM Trichy a government college?

BIM is not a conventional government college. It is an autonomous school of excellence of Bharathidasan University, registered as a society and governed by an independent Board.

Is BIM Trichy an MBA or PGDM college?

BIM offers a two-year MBA approved by AICTE and affiliated with Bharathidasan University.

Who can apply for BIM MBA?

For the 2027-29 cycle, a bachelor's or master's degree in any discipline with at least 50% is required. Final-year students can also apply subject to completing the degree requirements with 50% by June 2027.

Does BIM require work experience?

No. Prior work experience is not mandatory. However, relevant full-time work experience receives weightage during selection.

What is the latest average package at BIM Trichy?

The official annual report gives ₹10.46 LPA average CTC for BIM 40, with a ₹35.26 LPA highest CTC and ₹10 LPA median.

What is BIM Trichy's highest package?

The latest published BIM 40 placement data reports a ₹35.26 LPA highest CTC. This is the highest offer, not the typical salary received by students.

What is the total fee for BIM MBA?

For 2026-28, the published institute fee is ₹9,32,012 for Year 1 and ₹9,38,833 for Year 2, totalling ₹18,70,845. Mess charges of ₹75,000 per year on actual basis are additional.

Is BIM Trichy residential?

Yes. The MBA is a two-year full-time residential programme, and students are required to reside on campus subject to institute rules.

What entrance exams does BIM accept?

For 2027-29, BIM lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027 and GMAT as accepted scores for shortlisting.
