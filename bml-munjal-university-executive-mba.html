Executive MBA Overview

BMU lists Executive MBA among its programmes for working professionals. It is intended for candidates who want management education while continuing to build professional experience.

Eligibility

Eligibility and work-experience requirements can vary by intake. Candidates should verify the current Executive MBA admission page for the exact 2026 requirements before applying.

Admission 2026

Applications are handled through BMU's online admissions ecosystem. Check the current programme page for application dates, assessment stages, fees and required documents.

Career Value

An Executive MBA is most useful when the curriculum can be applied directly to a candidate's current or target management role. Compare learning format, peer profile, employer recognition, networking and total cost before enrolling.

FAQs

Who should pursue an Executive MBA?

Working professionals seeking structured management education and career progression can consider an Executive MBA.

Is BMU Executive MBA different from the regular MBA?

Yes. The Executive MBA is designed for working professionals, while the regular MBA is a full-time two-year programme.
