Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Financial Services: Overview

PGDM (Financial Services) is a two-year full-time, MBA-equivalent management programme at Jaipuria Lucknow designed for students interested in financial-services careers. The institute describes the programme as focused on banking, insurance, investment and related finance domains.

The programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised. It combines the common management foundation with finance-oriented learning and electives.

PGDM Financial Services Curriculum

The programme covers the common management foundation along with finance and financial-services-oriented learning. Official programme information highlights areas such as financial planning, mortgage lending, mutual funds, statistics, economics and financial management.

Possible finance-oriented learning areas

  • Corporate finance and financial management.
  • Financial institutions and markets.
  • Investment banking and portfolio-related concepts.
  • Risk management and derivatives.
  • Financial modelling and analysis.
  • FinTech and data-driven finance.

The elective pool can change with academic and industry requirements, so students should verify the current course list for their batch.

Career Scope After PGDM Financial Services

The programme is best aligned with students targeting banking, insurance, investment, corporate finance, wealth management, risk, financial analysis and other financial-services functions.

Banking

Suitable for roles in retail/corporate banking, credit, relationship management, banking operations and financial products.

Investments

Relevant to investment research, wealth management, portfolio-related and capital-market functions.

Corporate Finance

Can support careers in financial planning, analysis, valuation, treasury and corporate finance.

Admission 2026

Applicants need a bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year candidates may apply subject to the institute's conditions.

Accepted aptitude tests include CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT. The selection process considers the aptitude score and other components such as case analysis, personal interview, academics, work experience and extracurricular profile.
