Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Financial Services: Overview

PGDM Financial Services is a two-year full-time specialised management programme at Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow for students targeting banking, insurance, investments and related financial-services careers. The institute describes the programme as an MBA-equivalent PGDM and highlights finance-focused learning in financial planning, mortgage lending, mutual funds, statistics and economics.

Students still receive a broad management foundation, but the programme provides a stronger finance context. It can therefore be more relevant than the general PGDM for candidates who already have a clear interest in financial services.

Career-fit check

Choose PGDM-FS because you want financial-services exposure, not simply because you expect a higher package. Your eventual role depends on profile, skills, recruiter requirements and the placement cycle.

PGDM Financial Services Curriculum

The programme combines core management education with financial-services-oriented learning. Students need both finance knowledge and management skills because modern BFSI roles increasingly involve analytics, customer understanding, regulation, technology and business strategy.

Core management foundation

The broader PGDM curriculum covers accounting, economics, statistics, organisational behaviour, marketing, corporate finance, HR, operations, business analytics, research methods and strategic management.

Finance-oriented learning areas

  • Financial planning and financial management.
  • Financial institutions and markets.
  • Banking and insurance.
  • Mutual funds and investment products.
  • Investment banking and capital-market concepts.
  • Risk management and derivatives.
  • Financial modelling and analysis.
  • FinTech and data-driven finance.

The exact elective basket can change by batch and academic design; students should verify the latest institute-issued curriculum or brochure.

Career Scope After PGDM Financial Services

PGDM-FS is designed around the financial-services ecosystem, but possible careers are broader than traditional banking sales. Depending on skills and recruiter requirements, graduates can explore analytical, advisory, operations and relationship-oriented roles.

Banking

Potential paths include credit, corporate banking, retail banking, banking operations, relationship management and financial-product roles.

Investment & Markets

Students with strong finance and analytical skills can explore investment research, wealth management and capital-market functions.

Corporate Finance

Relevant areas include FP&A, financial analysis, treasury, valuation and management reporting.

Risk & Analytics

Risk, data, financial analytics and process roles can suit candidates who combine finance knowledge with analytical tools.

Role families to research

Compare job descriptions for financial analyst, credit analyst, relationship manager, investment research, risk analyst, wealth-management, corporate finance, banking operations and financial-services consulting roles before choosing electives.

Summer Internship & Industry Exposure

The PGDM structure includes summer internship and live-project exposure. For finance students, an internship is useful because it shows whether a candidate can apply finance concepts to real business problems.

What to look for in a finance internship

  • Exposure to financial statements, markets, products, credit, risk or analytics.
  • Use of Excel, financial modelling, research or data analysis.
  • Clear project ownership and measurable output.
  • Mentorship from a finance or business team.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Financial Services Admission 2026

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's completion requirements.

Accepted exams

CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT scores are accepted for the PGDM admission process.

Selection process

  1. Complete the application and select the campus/programme.
  2. Submit entrance-test, academic and profile details.
  3. Provide work-experience and extracurricular information where applicable.
  4. Participate in the selection process, including case analysis and personal interview components.
  5. Complete admission formalities after receiving an offer.

Who Should Choose PGDM Financial Services?

  • Good fit: candidates who want banking, insurance, investments, corporate finance, risk or financial analytics.
  • Consider general PGDM: if you want to remain open to consulting, marketing, HR, operations and general management.
  • Build extra skills: finance candidates can strengthen their profile with Excel, financial modelling, accounting, SQL, Python or BI tools depending on the target role.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Financial Services FAQs

What is the fee?

The 2026-28 programme fee is ₹15.25 lakh, excluding applicable taxes, hostel and refundable deposits.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT are accepted.

Is PGDM Financial Services equivalent to MBA?

Jaipuria states that its AIU-recognised PGDM programmes are equivalent to MBA.

What careers can I pursue?

Career paths can include banking, credit, investment research, wealth management, corporate finance, risk, analytics, financial operations and related financial-services functions.

Is the placement data programme-specific?

The current public dashboard is institute-level. The reported finance/financial-services share is useful context, but applicants should seek the latest programme-specific role distribution.
