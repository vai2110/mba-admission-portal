UPES LL.B Overview

The three-year LL.B at UPES School of Law is a postgraduate-entry law programme for graduates. The school also offers five-year integrated law programmes for students entering after Class XII; those have separate eligibility and admission rules.

UPES 3-Year LL.B Eligibility

The current course information lists graduation with 50% as the minimum eligibility for the three-year LL.B. Students should check the current admission notice for the applicable entrance/selection route.

UPES LL.B Fees

Current 2026 listings put the three-year LL.B academic fee at approximately ₹14.35 lakh, including tuition and academic/service components and listed one-time charges. Hostel is additional and optional.

Fee note:

The official UPES 2026-27 fee structure should be treated as the final source because law programme fees and additional charges can be revised.

UPES LL.B Admission 2026

UPES lists ULSAT-LLB for law admissions and also provides non-exam pathways for eligible five-year LL.B applicants through CLAT or board merit. The three-year LL.B has separate eligibility and admission requirements.

UPES School of Law Placements

UPES publishes School of Law placement information separately. Summer internships are part of the university's broader career-development model. Students should examine law-specific recruiters, offers and roles rather than using university-wide placement headlines.

UPES LL.B FAQs

What is the eligibility for the three-year LL.B?

Graduation with 50% is listed for the three-year LL.B.

What is the LL.B fee?

Current listings indicate approximately ₹14.35 lakh for the three-year programme; verify the official 2026-27 fee before payment.

Is the three-year LL.B different from the five-year law programmes?

Yes. The three-year LL.B is for graduates, while UPES five-year integrated law programmes have Class XII-level eligibility and separate selection rules.
