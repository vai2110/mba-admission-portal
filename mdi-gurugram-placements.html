MDI Gurgaon Placements 2026: Average CTC, Highest Package & Programme-wise Salary

MDI Gurugram Placements: Student Decision Snapshot

The official 2024-26 final placement report covers 586 students across PGDM, PGDM-HRM, PGDM-Business Analytics and PGDM-IB. It records 157 participating companies, including 75 new recruiters, and 189 pre-placement offers. For the flagship PGDM programme, the average CTC was ₹29.5 lakh, median CTC was ₹29 lakh and highest CTC was ₹97.5 lakh. The top 10%, top 25% and top 50% PGDM averages were ₹45.7 lakh, ₹40.5 lakh and ₹35.4 lakh respectively. Programme-level averages were ₹24.8 lakh for PGDM-HRM, ₹26.2 lakh for PGDM-Business Analytics and ₹26.3 lakh for PGDM-IB.

Recruiters, Job Roles & Career Functions

The official 2024-26 report covers recruiters across consulting, BFSI, technology, FMCG, e-commerce, manufacturing, healthcare and other sectors. The report records 75 new recruiters. Programme-level recruitment therefore needs to be read separately because the overall 586-student cohort combines four programmes.

Functions and roles reported

The official placement report covers roles across consulting, banking and financial services, technology and analytics, business analytics, human resources, international business, sales and marketing, operations and general management. The page avoids assigning roles to programmes where the official report does not provide a separate role denominator.

Placement Analysis: What the Official Data Shows

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.
