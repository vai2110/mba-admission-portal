Placement Analysis: What the Official Data Shows

Useful metrics to compare

  • Median CTC alongside average CTC.
  • Highest CTC as a separate indicator.
  • Top percentile averages where published.
  • Domain-wise offer distribution.
  • Recruiter count and new recruiter participation.

How to read the data

  • Highest CTC is not a typical salary.
  • Average CTC can be affected by a small number of high offers.
  • Median CTC is useful for understanding the middle of the reported salary distribution.
  • Programme-wise figures should not be merged when the institute reports separate cohorts.
  • Placement data should be compared only when the reporting definitions are similar.

MDI Gurgaon Placements: Student FAQs

What is the average package at MDI Gurgaon in 2026?

The 2024–26 final placement report gives programme-wise averages rather than one combined average: PGDM ₹29.50 LPA, PGDM-HRM ₹24.80 LPA, PGDM-Business Analytics ₹26.20 LPA and PGDM-IB ₹26.30 LPA.

What is the highest package at MDI Gurgaon in the 2024–26 batch?

The report records a highest domestic CTC of ₹97.50 LPA and highest international CTC of ₹80.20 LPA. The highest domestic figure is not the typical salary for the batch.

What is the MDI Gurgaon PGDM median package?

The PGDM median CTC in the 2024–26 report is ₹29.00 LPA. The other programme medians are ₹23.00 LPA for HRM, ₹25.70 LPA for Business Analytics and ₹25.00 LPA for IB.

Which MDI programme had the highest average CTC in 2024–26?

Among the four programmes reported separately, PGDM had the highest average CTC at ₹29.50 LPA. PGDM-IB recorded ₹26.30 LPA, Business Analytics ₹26.20 LPA and HRM ₹24.80 LPA.

How many recruiters participated in MDI Gurgaon placements 2026?

The report records 157 participating companies, including 75 new recruiters, and 189 pre-placement offers.

Which sector made the most offers at MDI Gurgaon?

Consulting was the largest offer contributor in each of the four programme sections in the 2024–26 report: 35.1% for PGDM, 46.2% for HRM, 36.6% for Business Analytics and 41.8% for IB.

Does the 2024–26 report publish an overall average CTC?

No combined overall average or median CTC is stated in the 2024–26 report’s placement overview. It publishes programme-wise average and median CTCs. This page does not calculate a combined figure from those programme averages.

Does MDI publish PGDM-BFSI placement statistics?

The three final placement reports supplied for this audit do not contain a separate PGDM-BFSI cohort. MDI’s programme page describes a separate BFS-focused placement process, but no PGDM-BFSI average or median is stated in these reports.

How have MDI Gurgaon placements changed over the last three batches?

The 2022–24 report recorded ₹25.50 LPA average and ₹24.20 LPA median CTC, with ₹63.30 LPA highest CTC. The 2023–25 report recorded ₹25.60 LPA average and ₹25.00 LPA median, with ₹53.60 LPA highest CTC. The 2024–26 report gives programme-wise averages and medians and an institute-level highest domestic CTC of ₹97.50 LPA.
