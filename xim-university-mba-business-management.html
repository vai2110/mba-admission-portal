XIM MBA-BM: What Is the Programme?

MBA-Business Management is the flagship two-year master's programme offered by Xavier Institute of Management, XIM University. It is the broad management route within XIM's portfolio and is relevant to students targeting corporate management, consulting, finance, marketing, operations, strategy and related business roles.

Who should choose MBA-BM?

Choose MBA-BM if you want a broad management degree rather than a sector-specific management programme. If you already know that you want HR, rural management, sustainability, urban governance, public policy or business finance, compare the specialised XIM programmes before applying.

For the university-level picture, see the XIM University overview.

XIM MBA-BM Eligibility 2026

Candidates need a bachelor's degree of minimum three-year duration, or equivalent, in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 55% marks in aggregate. Final-year degree students who expect to complete the required degree formalities by June 15, 2026 could also apply for the 2026 intake under the stated conditions.

Requirement2026 rule

XIM MBA-BM Entrance Exams: XAT, CAT, GMAT or XGMT

For the 2026 intake, XIM listed four admission-test routes for MBA-BM: XAT, CAT, GMAT or XGMT. Applicants using XAT, CAT or GMAT had to register for those tests separately. XGMT was the university's own management test.

Application deadline

The 2026 MBA-BM application deadline was January 20, 2026, and XGMT-based applications were closed after the relevant cycle.

XIM MBA-BM Selection Process

Shortlisted candidates are evaluated through the selection stages announced by XIM. The 2026 process included the admission-test score plus Writing Ability Test, Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Past academic record and relevant work experience also form part of the final evaluation.

XIM MBA-BM Fees 2026

The published 2026 XIM fee structure lists ₹11.50 lakh course fee for each year of MBA-BM. Additional charges include a development fund, course materials, IT expenses, alumni activities, career advisory services and a refundable deposit. Residential expenses are separate.

XIM MBA-BM Placements

XIM has a dedicated career advisory and recruiter engagement ecosystem. In April 2026, the university announced 100% summer placements for the MBA-BM and MBA-HRM 2025–27 batch. This is a summer-placement outcome, not a guarantee of final placement or salary for every student.

How to read placement data

Focus on role mix, recruiter quality, median/average outcomes where published, summer internship conversion, sector distribution and the number of students placed. A single highest package is not a typical outcome.

FAQs

Is XIM MBA-BM the flagship programme?

Yes. XIMB describes MBA-Business Management as the flagship programme of the institute and XIM University.

Can a final-year student apply?

Yes, subject to completing the degree requirements within the deadline specified by XIM for the relevant intake.

Is work experience mandatory?

No. The published eligibility criterion does not make work experience mandatory, although relevant work experience can be considered in selection.

Is hostel compulsory?

MBA programmes at XIM are residential, so students should budget for the university's residential arrangements.

What is the 2026 MBA-BM course fee?

The published course fee is ₹11.50 lakh in Year 1 and ₹11.50 lakh in Year 2, before other applicable fees and residential costs.
