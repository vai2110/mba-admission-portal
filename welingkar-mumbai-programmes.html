What are the published eligibility and exam rules?

The 2026–28 page lists at least 50% in a bachelor's degree from a recognised university and entrance scores from CAT 2025, XAT 2026, GMAT (2023 onwards), ATMA February 2026 or CMAT 2026 for that cycle. Those dates are not current for future batches.

The page says work experience is preferred but not mandatory. Recheck the latest admissions notice for current cutoffs, deadlines, eligibility and selection phases.

How should you compare programme placements?

WeSchool publishes programme-specific highlights for the 2023–25 batch. Do not copy one programme's average to another. Compare the exact programme and batch, then read the official placement report for roles, cohort size and salary definitions.

Median salary, function mix and internship alignment are more useful for programme choice than a highest-package headline alone.

How do you choose the right specialisation?

  1. Define the roles and industry you want after graduation.
  2. Compare the programme structure and electives with those roles.
  3. Review programme-specific placement functions and median salary, where published.
  4. Calculate total tuition and living cost for Mumbai.
  5. Confirm current exam acceptance and application deadlines.
