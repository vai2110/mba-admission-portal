The 50% requirement is an eligibility condition, not a guaranteed-admission cutoff. Students should separate three questions: “Can I apply?”, “Will I be shortlisted?” and “Will I convert the seat?” They are not the same.

Which Entrance Exam Should You Use for TSM?

For PGDM, the current official programme page lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT and ATMA. TSM's broader admissions FAQ also discusses CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT/ATMA scores, which shows that admissions information has changed across pages and cycles. For the current application, use the programme-specific admission instruction rather than assuming every historical exam list applies unchanged.

For MBA, the current programme page specifically lists TANCET and CAT and describes a counselling-based route. This makes the MBA application process materially different from the PGDM application route.

TSM Selection Process: What Happens Beyond the Entrance Score?

TSM describes a holistic selection philosophy. Its selection guidance emphasises academic consistency, analytical thinking, communication, leadership potential and performance in the interaction stages. The published guidance also advises candidates to prepare for WAT-style essay writing on current affairs and business topics and to clearly explain career goals in the PI.

Entrance performanceProvides the qualifying/shortlisting signal for the relevant programme and examination route.

WAT / written abilityPrepare structured arguments on business and current-affairs themes rather than memorised MBA interview answers.
Personal interviewBe ready to defend your career goal, programme choice, academic decisions and understanding of TSM.

What Can Strengthen a TSM Application?

TSM's selection guidance gives applicants practical signals: maintain a consistent academic record, prepare structured written responses, communicate a clear career goal and mention sports or co-curricular participation where relevant. Work experience is not mandatory for PGDM/MBA, although TSM's FAQ states that relevant post-graduation work experience may receive additional weightage during shortlisting.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Entrance examination scorecard or valid score details.
  • Graduation marksheets and degree/provisional certificate.
  • Class 10 and Class 12 academic records.
  • Government photo ID and recent photographs.
  • Work-experience documents, if applicable.
  • Category or other supporting certificates where applicable.
  • Application confirmation and interview/counselling communication.

The exact document list can differ by programme and counselling route, so the institute's current instructions should be treated as the final checklist.

How to Build a TSM Application That Shows Programme Fit

Do not prepare a generic “Why MBA?” answer. For PGDM, understand why the autonomous curriculum, PAN-India cohort, international study tour, outbound training, capstone simulation and specialisation fit your goal. For MBA, understand the university-affiliated degree, 60-seat intake, TANCET/CAT route and detailed functional/analytics curriculum.

A strong finance candidate, for example, should be able to connect a target role such as financial analyst with subjects such as financial analysis, financial markets, modelling or valuation. An analytics candidate should be able to explain why data analysis, data modelling and applied business analytics are relevant to the role they want.

Best interview formula

Career goal → current skill gap → TSM feature → expected career outcome. This makes your answer specific to the institute instead of interchangeable with any B-school.

TSM Admission FAQs

What is the minimum percentage required for TSM?

The current PGDM and MBA programme pages specify a minimum of 50% in a recognised bachelor's degree.

Does TSM accept CAT?

Yes. CAT is listed for both the current PGDM and MBA admission routes.

Does TSM accept XAT and CMAT?

Yes. The current PGDM page lists XAT and CMAT along with CAT, GMAT and ATMA.

Does TSM accept MAT?

TSM's broader admissions FAQ references MAT among scores considered in its admissions information, but the current PGDM programme page lists CAT/XAT/CMAT/GMAT/ATMA. Applicants should follow the programme-specific current application instructions.

Is work experience compulsory for TSM?

No. TSM's admissions FAQ states that work experience is not mandatory, although relevant post-graduation work experience may receive additional weightage during shortlisting.

Is TSM admission based only on CAT percentile?

No. TSM describes a broader evaluation that considers academic profile, analytical thinking, communication, leadership potential and selection-stage performance.
