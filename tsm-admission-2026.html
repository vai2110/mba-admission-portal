TSM Admission Eligibility 2026

For the full-time PGDM, TSM states that candidates must hold a recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks and submit a valid score from an accepted management entrance examination. The MBA page similarly requires a bachelor's degree with 50%, with the current programme page specifying TANCET/CAT for admission.

Students should not confuse eligibility with selection. Meeting the 50% academic threshold makes a candidate eligible to apply; it does not guarantee a call or admission because TSM uses a broader selection process.

TSM Selection Process: Why the Entrance Score Is Not the Whole Story

TSM describes its selection as a holistic evaluation of academic strength, analytical thinking, communication ability and leadership potential. The process can therefore reward a well-rounded profile rather than only a high percentile.

How to Build a Strong TSM Application

A strong TSM application should connect your academic record, entrance score, career objective and interview story. For example, a finance aspirant should be able to explain why finance, what exposure they already have, and how TSM's finance curriculum, live projects and internship fit that goal.

Do not prepare for the interview as a generic MBA interview. Research TSM's programme structure, PGDM vs MBA distinction, residential format, internships, specialisations and placement roles. Your answers become stronger when they demonstrate that you understand the programme you are applying for.

TSM Admission FAQs

What is the minimum percentage for TSM?

The current PGDM and MBA programme information specifies a minimum of 50% in a recognised bachelor's degree.

Does TSM accept CAT?

Yes. CAT is accepted for both the current PGDM and MBA routes described on TSM's programme pages.

Does TSM accept XAT and CMAT?

TSM's PGDM page lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT and ATMA as valid entrance scores.

Is TSM admission based only on CAT percentile?

No. TSM describes its selection as holistic and considers multiple dimensions including academic record, analytical ability, communication and interview performance.
