GIM PGDM BIFS: What Is the Programme?

GIM's PGDM in Banking, Insurance & Financial Services is a two-year full-time residential programme focused on practical and application-oriented management learning for the BFSI sector. GIM says its course content is industry driven and designed to adapt to changes in banking, financial services, insurance and fintech.

Do you need a commerce or finance background?

No. GIM's BIFS FAQ explicitly says graduates from any background can apply if they meet the programme's general eligibility requirements. The programme does, however, have profile-specific Achiever's Round criteria for candidates with qualifications such as CA, CS, CMA, CFA, ACCA, CIMA, CPA or FRM.

BIFS Curriculum & Industry Focus

The programme is built around practical application in banking, insurance and financial services. Industry engagement, internships and placement support are integrated into the programme's positioning.

Core career areas

  • Banking and financial services.
  • Insurance.
  • Fintech.
  • Consulting and advisory.
  • Risk and financial analysis.

Learning approach

  • Industry-driven content.
  • Applied projects and cases.
  • Industry engagement.
  • Internship exposure.
  • Placement-focused preparation.

GIM BIFS Career Scope & Placements

Who Should Choose GIM BIFS?

Strong fit if

  • You want a career specifically in BFSI.
  • You have a finance qualification or strong interest in financial services.
  • You prefer applied sector-focused learning.

Compare the flagship PGDM if

  • You want maximum flexibility across management functions.
  • You are undecided about finance/BFSI as a long-term career.
  • You want a broader elective mix.

GIM BIFS FAQs

What is the BIFS fee at GIM?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh. The 2027-29 fee is not yet published.

Can students from non-commerce backgrounds apply?

Yes. GIM states that graduates from any background can apply to BIFS subject to eligibility.

What is the BIFS intake?

GIM lists an intake of 120 for BIFS.

What professional qualifications are relevant to the Achiever's Round?

GIM lists qualifications including CA, CS, CMA, CFA (US), ACCA, CIMA, CPA (US) and FRM among programme-specific achievements.
