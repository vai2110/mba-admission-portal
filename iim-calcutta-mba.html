What is the IIM Calcutta MBA?

IIM Calcutta's MBA is its flagship two-year, full-time postgraduate management programme. The first year builds core business fundamentals across areas such as economics, finance, marketing, operations, systems, organisational behaviour and strategy. The second year gives students a wider choice of electives aligned with their career interests. The programme also includes a compulsory summer internship between the first and second year.

Best fit: Students who want a highly competitive general-management MBA with strong access to consulting, finance, marketing, general management and technology-oriented roles.

Who Should Choose This MBA?

Choose it if you want finance-heavy career optionsIIM Calcutta has a long-standing reputation for finance-oriented careers. If investment banking, markets, PE/VC or related roles are on your shortlist, the programme deserves serious consideration.
Choose it if consulting is your targetThe campus attracts major consulting firms, and the curriculum's broad management base is useful for strategy and problem-solving roles.
Choose it if you want a full residential MBAThe programme is designed as a two-year residential experience at the Joka campus, with academics, internships, clubs and peer learning forming one ecosystem.
Think twice if you want a low-cost MBAThe programme requires a substantial financial commitment, and your decision should account for tuition, living costs and the salary you would give up during two years of study.

Eligibility & Admission 2026

Basic eligibility

For the 2026-28 MBA batch, applicants need a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA; the minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates. Final-year bachelor's students can also apply subject to the institute's completion requirements.

Entrance exam

For domestic applicants, the MBA admission route is based on CAT followed by the institute's shortlisting and selection process. The final selection considers multiple components rather than CAT alone.

What this means for an applicant

  • A strong CAT score is necessary, but clearing the minimum eligibility or qualifying threshold does not by itself guarantee an interview call or admission.
  • Academic performance, CAT performance, personal interview performance, academic diversity and work experience can influence the selection process.
  • Freshers are eligible if they satisfy the academic and admission requirements; work experience is not a basic eligibility requirement.

Curriculum & Learning: What You Actually Study

The first year is structured around compulsory business fundamentals. Students study areas including economics, finance, marketing, operations, systems, organisational behaviour, human resources, strategy and public policy. In the second year, electives allow greater specialisation.

Why this matters for career choice

  • Finance: Useful foundation for students targeting investment banking, markets, corporate finance and related roles.
  • Consulting: Broad exposure across functions supports case-based problem solving and general management careers.
  • Marketing: Elective flexibility can help students build depth in consumer, brand and go-to-market areas.
  • General management: The broad core prevents the MBA from locking students into one function too early.

Fees & ROI

Programme fee reference: Publicly reported 2025-27 figures place the two-year IIM Calcutta MBA fee at about ₹27 lakh. Because the currently accessible 2026-28 domestic admission policy does not state the domestic programme fee on the page itself, students should verify the final payable amount before accepting an offer.

Placements & Career Outcomes

IIM Calcutta describes its MBA as a programme aimed at developing general managers and high-level decision makers, with recruitment exposure across finance, consulting, technology, FMCG, retail and other sectors. The institute also makes an eight-week summer internship a compulsory part of the MBA.

Career areas

Consulting, finance, marketing/general management and technology/product-related roles are among the career paths commonly associated with the programme. The institute's recruitment material also highlights investment banking/private equity, consulting, technology, media, FMCG, retail and core industries in its internship ecosystem.

Student Decision Snapshot

Strong choice if:You want a top-tier two-year MBA and are targeting consulting, finance, marketing or general-management careers.
Financially sensible if:You can manage roughly ₹27 lakh in programme fees plus living and opportunity costs without relying solely on the highest placement number.
Less suitable if:You already have a strong career with high compensation and do not need a two-year career reset; compare the opportunity cost with one-year MBA options.
Your biggest variable:Your own CAT performance, academics, interview performance, profile and the role you eventually secure matter more than the IIM brand alone.

FAQs

Is CAT mandatory for IIM Calcutta MBA?

For domestic applicants to the flagship MBA, CAT is the entrance route. IIM Calcutta also has a separate admission procedure for overseas candidates, so international applicants should check the applicable policy.

What is the IIM Calcutta MBA fee?

The widely published 2025-27 programme fee is around ₹27 lakh for two years. The 2026-28 domestic admission policy page does not state the domestic fee figure, so students should confirm the payable amount with the institute before accepting an offer.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. A candidate can apply without prior full-time work experience if the academic and admission requirements are satisfied.

What percentile is needed for IIM Calcutta?

The institute's selection process is not based on a single published “safe percentile”. Shortlisting and final selection use the CAT score along with other profile and interview components. Treat online percentile estimates as planning benchmarks rather than guarantees.

Is IIM Calcutta good for finance?

Yes, it is one of the strongest choices in India for students targeting finance-oriented careers. However, actual role access depends on academic performance, profile, shortlisting and interview performance.

What salary can I expect after IIM Calcutta MBA?

Recent 2023-25 placement reporting puts average CTC around ₹34.23 LPA and median CTC around ₹34 lakh. These are batch-level figures, not guaranteed individual salaries.

Is IIM Calcutta worth the fee?

It can be, especially for candidates targeting consulting, finance and high-end management roles. Make the decision using average/median outcomes, your expected role, loan burden and the salary you would give up during two years.
