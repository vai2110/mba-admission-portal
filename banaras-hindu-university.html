What is BHU?

Banaras Hindu University (BHU) is a central university in Varanasi established in 1916 through the initiative of Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya. The university's official admission portal currently handles UG, PG, MBA, PhD, diploma and other programme admissions through different routes.

For a student, the important point is that BHU does not have one common entrance route for every programme. CUET-UG is the main route for the listed UG programmes, CUET-PG is used for the regular PG admission cycle, while some programmes have separate tests, practical/audition requirements, GD-PI or other selection components.

Current admission status

The 2026 cycle is already underway. BHU's official portal is currently showing UG spot-round activity and other programme-specific admissions. For 2027, official dates should be treated as not announced until BHU publishes the new admission notification.

What is the eligibility for BHU admission?

Eligibility is programme-specific. BHU's 2026 UG bulletin and admission notices specify the required CUET subjects for individual programmes, while the PG bulletin provides separate eligibility rules for each postgraduate programme.

  • UG: Candidates generally need to appear in CUET-UG 2026 for programmes covered by the CUET route and must satisfy the programme-specific academic and subject requirements.
  • PG: Candidates who appeared in CUET-PG 2026 were eligible to register for BHU PG admission, subject to the programme-specific eligibility in the PG bulletin.
  • Special selection programmes: Practical, performance, audition, sports and GD-PI based programmes can have additional selection components.

Do not use a general “BHU eligibility percentage” for every course. The exact programme bulletin is the correct source for the subject combination and academic requirement.

How does BHU admission work?

The admission process changes by level and programme. For the standard 2026 UG route, BHU required candidates who appeared in CUET-UG 2026 to register on the BHU admission portal/Samarth system and participate in the university's counselling/allotment process.

UG route

  • Appear for CUET-UG 2026.
  • Register on the BHU admission portal.
  • Complete the programme/application preferences.
  • Wait for BHU allocation/merit rounds.
  • Pay the admission fee within the deadline after allotment.

PG route

  • Appear for CUET-PG 2026.
  • Register on the BHU CAP (PG) portal.
  • Submit programme preferences and required documents.
  • Participate in seat allotment rounds.
  • Complete fee payment and admission formalities.

For PG programmes, BHU's 2026 CAP guidelines state that seat allotment is based on CUET-PG scores except for practical-based, GD-PI based and other specially assessed programmes, where additional components may be considered.

What is the BHU cutoff?

BHU does not have one university-wide cutoff. Cutoffs are programme-, category-, quota- and round-specific.

The official BHU admission portal publishes separate UG and PG cutoff/allotment information. The 2026 cutoff pages also distinguish C1 (BHU Quota) and C2 (Non-BHU Quota) where applicable.

What should you use as your cutoff benchmark?

Use the latest official cutoff for the exact programme, category and round you are targeting. A general “BHU cutoff” number is not meaningful because B.A., B.Sc., law, agriculture, MBA and PG programmes can have completely different admission routes and score requirements.

The current official 2026 UG and PG cutoff pages show that round-wise admission information is published through the BHU portal, although the searchable cutoff pages do not currently expose a consolidated score table in the page text.

What are BHU placements like?

BHU does not publish one single salary number that represents every faculty, department and programme. Placement outcomes vary significantly by course and academic unit.

The university's 2024-25 Annual Report contains placement and student-progression information for individual faculties/units. For example, one faculty-level report records 22 male and 9 female undergraduate students placed with compensation ranging from ₹2 lakh to ₹3.5 lakh per year, while some postgraduate students progressed through competitive examinations with reported annual packages of ₹4–6 lakh.

Important:

These figures are faculty-specific and should not be presented as BHU's university-wide average or highest package. A dedicated BHU placement page should use placement data programme-by-programme wherever official reports provide it.

Where is BHU located?

Banaras Hindu University is located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The university's main campus is a large residential academic campus with teaching departments, hostels, libraries, healthcare facilities, sports and student-support infrastructure.

BHU should not be confused with IIT (BHU) Varanasi. IIT (BHU) is an institute within the BHU ecosystem but has its own engineering admissions, programmes and placement reporting. Their admission and placement data should be kept separate.

BHU admission FAQs

Is BHU admission through CUET?

For the UG programmes covered by the 2026 BHU admission bulletin, CUET-UG is the admission route. For regular PG admissions, BHU used CUET-PG 2026. Some programmes have additional or separate selection requirements.

Is CUET enough for BHU admission?

Not by itself. You must also register on the BHU admission portal, satisfy the exact programme eligibility, submit preferences/documents and complete the allotment and fee-payment process.

Does BHU have one cutoff for all courses?

No. BHU cutoffs vary by programme, category, quota and admission round. Use the official cutoff for the exact course you are applying to.

What is the BHU C1 and C2 quota?

The official 2026 cutoff material uses C1 for BHU Quota and C2 for Non-BHU Quota in the applicable admission framework. Students should check the programme bulletin for the exact seat and eligibility rules.

Are BHU 2027 admission dates announced?

No. The official 2027 schedule is not available yet. The 2026 dates can be used only as a reference for planning, not as confirmed 2027 dates.

Is IIT BHU the same as BHU?

No. IIT (BHU) Varanasi is an engineering and technology institute within the broader BHU ecosystem and has separate programme, admission and placement information.
