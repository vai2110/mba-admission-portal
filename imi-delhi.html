IMI Delhi: What Students Should Know Before Applying

International Management Institute (IMI) New Delhi was established in 1981 and is described by the institute as India's first corporate-sponsored business school. It is located in Qutab Institutional Area in South Delhi and offers two-year PGDM programmes in general management, human resource management and banking & financial services.

IMI Delhi is AICTE approved and its PGDM programmes are associated with NBA, AMBA and AACSB accreditation credentials. The flagship PGDM offers career-oriented learning with specialisation options across areas such as Marketing, Strategy, Analytics, Finance, Operations and Information Systems.

Is IMI Delhi worth considering?

IMI Delhi makes sense for students who want a Delhi-based, established B-school with strong recruiter access, specialised two-year programmes and a broad corporate network. The decision should be based on your target function, entrance-score/profile, the ₹23.54 lakh published programme fee, living costs and the actual role mix you want from placements—not only the highest package.

IMI Delhi Admission: Eligibility, Cutoff & Selection Process

For the 2026 cycle, IMI Delhi accepted valid CAT 2025, XAT 2026 and GMAT scores. Applicants were required to have a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent. The institute states that there is no fixed cutoff; selection is holistic and considers test scores, academics, work experience and performance in the video assessment and personal interview.

ParameterIMI Delhi requirement / approach

Accepted testsCAT, XAT and GMAT for the recent two-year programme cycle
CutoffNo fixed published cutoff; holistic evaluation
AssessmentVideo assessment followed by personal interview for shortlisted candidates

Does IMI Delhi have a fixed CAT cutoff?

No. IMI Delhi explicitly says its process uses holistic evaluation and that there is no fixed cutoff. This means a student should not treat a single percentile as a guaranteed-call or guaranteed-admission number. Your academic consistency, work experience, achievements and interview performance can affect the outcome.

Important distinction for aspirants

The absence of a fixed cutoff does not mean test scores are unimportant. CAT/XAT/GMAT is one part of the profile assessment, and the institute also evaluates the candidate through its subsequent assessment process.

Scholarships and fee support

IMI Delhi lists an Academic Excellence Scholarship and a Professional Impact Scholarship. The institute's recent programme information describes awards of up to 100% and 50% tuition-fee waivers for eligible students under these schemes, subject to the applicable scholarship policy and number of awards.

IMI also implements the AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver scheme where applicable. A recent institute order confirms full tuition fee waiver for eligible EWS students in PGDM, PGDM (HRM) and PGDM (B&FS), subject to the scheme guidelines and merit.

Financial support to check

  • Academic Excellence Scholarship
  • Professional Impact Scholarship
  • AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver for eligible candidates
  • Education-loan support through bank tie-ups

Budgeting caution

  • Do not treat programme fee as the complete cost of living in Delhi.
  • Check hostel and mess charges separately.
  • Include laptop, travel, personal and opportunity costs.

IMI Delhi Curriculum, Campus & Student Life

IMI's new PGDM curriculum is presented around career readiness, management foundations, track-specific learning and application. The institute highlights foundation courses such as Management Communication, Critical Thinking, Business Statistics, Managerial Economics and Business Ethics & Sustainability, followed by track-core and flexible learning.

Campus and location

The campus is in Qutab Institutional Area, South Delhi. Its location gives students access to a major corporate and institutional ecosystem while remaining a dedicated management campus. Facilities include technology-enabled classrooms, library resources, an auditorium, computer facilities and student activity spaces.

International exposure

IMI Delhi has international academic linkages and a student exchange programme. The institute states that students can study for a term at partner B-schools, along with short-duration global immersion opportunities, subject to programme arrangements and selection.

Why location matters

For a student targeting consulting, BFSI, corporate roles or internships in the NCR ecosystem, South Delhi can be a practical advantage. But location should be considered alongside fees, placement roles and your alternative offers.
