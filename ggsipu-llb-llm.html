GGSIPU LLB and LLM Overview

GGSIPU offers law pathways including three-year LLB and postgraduate LLM options, alongside integrated BA LLB and BBA LLB programmes. The 2026 admission notices identify LLB as code 121 and LLM as code 112.

GGSIPU Law Admission 2026

The university issued a 2026 notice regarding consent on the Consortium of NLUs portal for CLAT UG 2026 and CLAT PG 2026 for integrated law and LLM admissions. Current counselling notices also cover LLB and LLM rounds.

GGSIPU LLB/LLM FAQs

Is CLAT used for GGSIPU LLM?

The university's 2026 notice references CLAT PG 2026 for LLM admission. Always check the current counselling notice for the applicable year.

Is LLB the same as integrated BA LLB?

No. LLB and integrated five-year law programmes have different entry qualifications and programme structures.
