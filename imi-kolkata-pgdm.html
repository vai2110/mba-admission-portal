IMI Kolkata PGDM: What You Get

IMI Kolkata's PGDM is a two-year, full-time programme divided into six trimesters. The first three trimesters establish a common foundation across management functions, followed by deeper functional learning through electives. The institute says the programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited, AMBA accredited, NAAC accredited and AIU-equivalent to an MBA.

Is IMI Kolkata PGDM a general MBA-style programme?

Yes. It is designed as a broad management programme rather than a narrow technical course. Students build a common business foundation and then select electives across areas such as finance, marketing, operations, analytics, strategy and HR.

IMI Kolkata PGDM Selection Process

IMI Kolkata first shortlists candidates using entrance-test performance. Shortlisted applicants then go through the Individual Assessment Process, including a personal interview, with academic record and qualitative factors considered as part of the overall assessment.

What can influence selection?

  • CAT/XAT/CMAT/GMAT performance
  • Past academic record
  • Personal interview performance
  • Quality of work experience
  • Extra-curricular achievements

Important reality check

There is no single guaranteed percentile published on the official page that should be treated as a fixed IMI Kolkata cutoff. Meeting 50% graduation eligibility does not itself ensure a shortlist.

Hostel accommodation is mandatory. Hostel and mess charges are separate and depend on room category, so the real cost of attendance is higher than ₹14.98 lakh.

EWS tuition waiver

The institute states that students with family income below ₹8 lakh per year can apply for an EWS tuition fee waiver, subject to documentation and availability. The published policy says support can cover up to 5% of total seats.

IMI Kolkata PGDM FAQs

What is the IMI Kolkata PGDM fee?

The latest detailed official 2026-28 academic fee is ₹14.98 lakh. Hostel and mess charges are additional and mandatory.

Which exams are accepted for IMI Kolkata PGDM?

The published admission process lists CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT.

Does IMI Kolkata PGDM require work experience?

No. The institute states that both fresh graduates and candidates with relevant work experience can participate.

Is IMI Kolkata PGDM equivalent to MBA?

Yes. IMI Kolkata states that the PGDM has equivalence to an MBA from the Association of Indian Universities.

Is hostel mandatory?

Yes. IMI Kolkata states that hostel accommodation is mandatory for students.
