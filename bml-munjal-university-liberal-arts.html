BMU Liberal Arts Overview

BMU's School of Liberal Studies currently lists four-year BA (Hons.) programmes in Economics, Psychology, Sociology, Literature and History. The curriculum is suited to students who want interdisciplinary learning before moving into postgraduate study or careers across research, business, policy, media and social sectors.

BA (Hons.) Options

Economics

Useful for students interested in economics, analytics, finance, policy and research.

Psychology

Suitable for students interested in behavioural science, HR, research and psychology-related higher study.

Sociology

Focuses on society, institutions and social research.

Literature

Develops reading, writing, critical analysis and communication skills.

History

Builds research and analytical skills through historical study.

Admission 2026

BMU's admissions portal provides the current application pathway for liberal studies programmes. Applicants should confirm the latest eligibility, selection process and application timeline on the university portal.

Fees

The current published tuition for BMU's BA (Hons.) Liberal Studies is ₹5.50 lakh per year, giving a published four-year tuition total of ₹22 lakh before applicable additional charges.

Career Scope

Liberal arts graduates can move into research, consulting, communications, policy, education, human resources, business and higher education. Economics and Psychology can also provide stronger routes into quantitative or specialised postgraduate study.
