MBA at AMU: Overview

The Department of Business Administration at AMU offers a two-year regular MBA programme. The department's current page lists specialisations in Marketing, Human Resource, Finance and Operations & IT, with an intake of 60 for the Aligarh programme.

The 2026-27 Guide to Admissions gives a more detailed admission structure: the Aligarh MBA has 60+9 seats, with the additional seats earmarked under Self-finance. The guide also lists MBA programmes at AMU centres in Malappuram, Murshidabad and Kishanganj.

Who should consider this MBA?

This programme can suit graduates looking for a university-based management degree with a dedicated AMU admission test. The key decision factors are the test-based selection process, specialisation, total cost, campus and the latest programme-specific placement evidence.

AMU MBA Eligibility 2026

The 2026-27 admission guide states that a candidate must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate. The candidate must have undergone a bachelor's programme of at least three years.

The AMU Department of Business Administration page similarly states the 50% minimum and recognises relevant bachelor's degrees including B.A., B.Sc., B.Sc. Engineering and B.Tech from AMU or equivalent recognised examinations.

AMU MBA Admission 2026

Admission Test

Selection for MBA is through the AMU Admission Test. The 2026-27 guide specifies an objective-type written paper of 100 marks with 100 questions.

SectionMarks
Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension25
Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning25
Quantitative Ability25
General Awareness25

The admission test is listed at Aligarh, Kolkata and Kozhikode. Candidates who qualify the written test are called for Interview/Aptitude Assessment/Group Discussion, where physical presence is compulsory to become eligible for selection.

The total merit has two components in the 2026-27 guide:

ComponentWeight
Written Test100 marks

Because the written test is the first screening stage, preparation should cover all four sections rather than focusing only on quantitative ability. Students should also prepare for communication, group discussion and interview questions before the shortlist stage.

AMU MBA Fees

The current 2026-27 guide contains course-wise fee information. The admission-time fee should not be confused with the complete two-year programme cost, which can include later-semester university charges, hostel, mess and other applicable expenses.

For an accurate cost comparison, students should use the latest AMU fee appendix for the exact MBA code and campus, particularly because the guide lists different AMU centres and self-finance seats.

Fee-check rule

MBA Curriculum & Learning

The programme carries 120 credits over two years. The curriculum is designed around management concepts, functional specialisations and applied business learning. Students can use the specialisation choice to build a career-oriented profile rather than treating the MBA as a generic qualification.

For stronger outcomes, students should complement classroom learning with internships, live projects, Excel/data skills, business communication, case analysis and role-specific certifications.

AMU MBA Placements

The Department of Business Administration maintains a dedicated Training & Placement page with placement brochures and campus placement activity. Its archive includes an MNC hiring update that reported a 14 LPA offer in 2023. That figure is historical and should not be presented as the 2026 highest or average package.

The wider AMU placement ecosystem also has a General TPO for non-engineering courses, while some AMU centres maintain separate management placement brochures. Students should compare the exact campus and batch before using a placement figure.

AMU MBA Cutoff 2026

AMU's MBA admission process is test-based. A single public cutoff score should not be treated as guaranteed unless it is tied to the specific admission year, category, programme/campus and selection list.

The practical approach is to prepare for a high written-test score, track the official shortlist and then perform strongly in the interview/aptitude assessment/group discussion.

How to Prepare for the AMU MBA Entrance Test

The 2026-27 written test has four equal sections: Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Ability and General Awareness. Because each carries 25 marks, candidates should avoid preparing only for quantitative aptitude.

SectionPreparation focusCommon student mistake

Aligarh MBA vs MBA at AMU Centres

The admission guide lists MBA opportunities not only at Aligarh but also at AMU centres such as Malappuram, Murshidabad and Kishanganj. These programmes should not be treated as interchangeable simply because they carry the AMU name.

Before choosing a centre, compare campus location, intake, fee, faculty, curriculum, placement brochure, recruiter base and residential arrangements. A student should use centre-specific evidence wherever possible.

AMU MBA ROI: What Should You Compare?

Do not evaluate the MBA only by its admission fee or a single historical highest package. A stronger ROI assessment combines total two-year cost, average/median salary where officially available, placement participation, recruiter roles, internship quality, location and the percentage of students progressing to higher studies or other outcomes.

The Department of Business Administration maintains placement-related material, but older placement announcements should be labelled by year. For 2026 decisions, use the newest available batch-level brochure and distinguish historical individual offers from current batch statistics.

AMU MBA FAQs

What is the eligibility for MBA at AMU?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate is required, and the bachelor's programme must be at least three years in duration.

Is CAT accepted for AMU MBA?

The 2026-27 AMU guide specifies selection through the AMU Admission Test for the MBA programme. Do not assume CAT is the primary selection route for the regular MBA unless a current official notice says otherwise.

What is the AMU MBA entrance test pattern?

The written test has 100 objective questions for 100 marks: 25 each from Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Ability and General Awareness.

How many MBA seats are there at Aligarh?

The 2026-27 guide lists 60+9 seats at AMU Aligarh, with the additional nine earmarked under Self-finance.

What specialisations are offered?

The Department of Business Administration currently lists Marketing, Human Resource, Finance and Operations & IT.

Does AMU MBA have placements?

Yes. The Department of Business Administration maintains a dedicated placement page and publishes placement-related material. Current salary outcomes should be taken from the latest programme-specific brochure.
