Graphic Era BPT: What Students Should Know

Graphic Era's Bachelor of Physiotherapy is a 4.5-year undergraduate professional programme comprising academic education and a mandatory rotatory internship. The programme focuses on movement disorders, rehabilitation, patient safety and clinical practice.

Quick answer

BPT is suitable for students who want a patient-facing healthcare career in physiotherapy rather than a general science degree.

BPT Eligibility

GEU states 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with a minimum age of 17 years. Admission is subject to applicable national/state norms.

BPT Admission Process

The official course page states that merit is prepared on the basis of the qualifying examination. Its FAQ also notes that NEET may be compulsory depending on applicable state/university admission rules, so applicants should verify the current route.

BPT Curriculum & Clinical Learning

The programme includes Anatomy, Physiology, Biomechanics, Exercise Therapy, Electrotherapy and Clinical Physiotherapy, supported by practical demonstrations, clinical postings, simulation, case-based learning and community-based rehabilitation.

BPT Internship & Careers

GEU states that a one-year full-time rotatory internship is mandatory. Career options include clinical physiotherapy, sports physiotherapy, rehabilitation, private practice, academics and research.

BPT FAQs

What subjects are required?

Physics, Chemistry and Biology are required at 10+2 level according to GEU's course page.

Is internship compulsory?

Yes. GEU states that the one-year rotatory internship is mandatory.

Is NEET compulsory?

GEU's FAQ says NEET is compulsory depending on state/university admission rules. Verify the applicable admission notification for your intake.
