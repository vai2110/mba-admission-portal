What is FPM (Rural Management) at IRMA?

IRMA's Fellow Programme in Management (Rural Management) is a doctoral-level programme for candidates seeking research, teaching and specialised knowledge careers in management, rural development and related fields.

Is FPM the right option after MBA?

It can be, if your objective is research, academia, policy research or specialist knowledge creation rather than a conventional corporate-management role.

IRMA FPM Eligibility

IRMA publishes a dedicated eligibility notice for each doctoral admission cycle. The current programme is intended for candidates meeting the prescribed postgraduate/equivalent qualification and academic requirements. Check the current admission notice for the exact percentage and qualifying-test rules.

IRMA FPM Admission & Selection

The FPM admission process is separate from MBA(RM). IRMA's doctoral selection framework evaluates academic background, qualifying credentials, research orientation and interview performance. A research proposal is an important part of the doctoral application process under IRMA's published framework.

FPM Rural Management Research Areas

IRMA's published FPM ecosystem includes areas such as management of collectives, agribusiness/rural marketing, natural-resources management, development management, micro-finance management, governance and livelihoods, rural infrastructure and services management, and public policy/non-profit management.

FPM Fellowship & Funding

IRMA's official programme information provides fellowship support to FPM participants. The exact current fellowship amount and terms should be checked in the admission notice for the relevant batch.

Career Options After FPM(RM)

Academic

  • Universities and business schools
  • Research and teaching
  • Management and rural-development research

Policy & development

  • Government and public-policy institutions
  • Development organisations
  • Cooperatives and specialist research bodies

IRMA FPM FAQs

Is FPM a management degree?

It is a doctoral-level management research programme in the rural-management domain.

Can MBA graduates apply?

Candidates with the prescribed postgraduate/equivalent qualification can apply subject to the current admission notice.

Is FPM residential?

IRMA's programme information specifies a minimum residency requirement; candidates should check the current academic regulations.

What is the career after FPM?

Typical directions include academic research, teaching, policy research, development institutions and specialist knowledge roles.
