NMIMS SBM Mumbai Overview

Quick answer

NMIMS is a multi-school deemed-to-be university, while the School of Business Management (SBM) in Mumbai is its flagship management school. SBM's MBA is a two-year full-time programme spread over six trimesters. NMIMS also offers specialised programmes in HR, Business Analytics, Digital Transformation, Pharmaceutical Management and Healthcare Management, plus MBA-NEx for working executives.

SBM NMIMS was established in 1981. The current official SBM website highlights 45 years of legacy, 29,000+ alumni and 250+ recruiters. The Mumbai campus is at V. L. Mehta Road, Vile Parle West.

Why programme-level comparison matters

NMIMS should not be treated as one admission product. Eligibility, selection, fee, format and career outcomes can differ by programme and campus. The overview therefore separates the flagship MBA from specialised and executive routes.

Academic experience

The flagship MBA uses participant-centred, case-based learning supported by lectures, problem-solving activities, simulations, group work and industry exposure. A six-to-eight-week summer internship is compulsory between the first and second year.

NMIMS MBA Admission 2026

The SBM admissions page currently lists NMIMS MBA NMAT registrations 2026. The flagship MBA uses NMAT by GMAC and a separate NMIMS application.

Eligibility

Candidates should verify the qualifying degree, minimum marks and final-year rules in the 2026 document.

NMIMS MBA Fees & ROI

NMIMS fees are programme- and cycle-specific. The accessible official SBM sources do not provide one consolidated 2026 fee that can safely be applied to all management programmes, so this overview does not invent a universal number.

What applicants should use

Use the fee structure in the current programme admission handout or offer/admission communication for the exact programme and campus.

Do not compare blindly

Do not combine MBA, MBA HR, Business Analytics, Pharma, Healthcare and executive fees into one figure or compare an old fee with a current fee elsewhere.

ROI framework

Compare total academic and living cost with average and median CTC, recruiter mix, role quality and your target career. CTC is not take-home salary, and the highest package is not a typical outcome.

NMIMS Student Experience & Campus Context

SBM Mumbai is located in Vile Parle West. Official programme material describes case-based learning, industry interaction, simulations, group activities, seminars and the compulsory summer internship as parts of the full-time MBA experience.

Why Mumbai matters

The Mumbai location provides proximity to a major corporate and financial ecosystem. Applicants should also budget for Mumbai living costs when assessing the real cost of an MBA.

Student development

SBM's ecosystem includes student activities, case competitions, seminars, industry interactions and social-impact initiatives. Community reviews can supplement this information for workload and culture, but anecdotal opinions should remain separate from official facts.

NMIMS Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Scholarship and financial-assistance rules vary by programme and cycle. The current programme admission handout should be used for eligibility, award amount and deadlines.

Check

  • Programme-specific scholarship availability
  • Merit or need-based eligibility
  • Government scholarship eligibility
  • Application deadline and documents

Do not assume a waiver

A scholarship mentioned for one NMIMS programme or year should not be presented as universal across SBM.

Who Should Apply to NMIMS?

Strong fit

  • Candidates seeking Mumbai-based management education with strong industry orientation.
  • Aspirants comfortable with NMAT-based admission for the applicable full-time MBA.
  • Students wanting multiple management specialisations under one SBM ecosystem.
  • Candidates who can benefit from Mumbai's corporate and financial ecosystem.

Think carefully if

  • You are comparing programmes without checking their individual rules.
  • You rely on an unofficial NMAT cutoff.
  • You compare only highest package instead of average, median and role mix.
  • You have not budgeted for Mumbai living costs.
FreshersCheck programme eligibility, curriculum and target roles rather than assuming work experience is required.
EngineersCompare MBA Core with analytics/digital pathways based on the intended career.
Non-engineersSeveral management programmes have broader academic eligibility, but handbooks must be checked individually.
Working professionalsMBA-NEx is a separate executive route and should not be compared using the full-time MBA admission criteria.
Bottom line: NMIMS is most relevant to applicants who want a Mumbai-based management ecosystem and are prepared to compare programme-specific admission, cost and career outcomes.

NMIMS MBA FAQs

Which entrance exam is used for NMIMS MBA?

NMAT by GMAC is the entrance route for the applicable SBM MBA programmes. Check the current programme handout.

What is the NMIMS MBA cutoff?

Do not use one universal cutoff across programmes and campuses. An external expected score must not be presented as an official cutoff.

What is the latest NMIMS MBA placement average?

₹24.60 LPA for MBA Core in the 2025-26 SBM Mumbai placement disclosure; MBA HR was ₹19.08 LPA.

How many recruiters participated?

The 2025-26 disclosure reports 190 recruiters for MBA Core and 37 for MBA HR.

What is NMIMS MBA fees?

Fees are programme- and cycle-specific. This overview does not insert a universal number without a current official fee schedule applicable to the exact programme.

Does NMIMS offer MBA Business Analytics?

Yes. SBM Mumbai lists MBA Business Analytics as a full-time programme.

Does NMIMS offer MBA HR?

Yes. MBA HR is a separate full-time SBM programme.

Does NMIMS offer an Executive MBA?

Yes. MBA-NEx is a separate two-year programme for working executives with a distinct admission process.

Is NMIMS good for freshers?

Freshers can consider eligible full-time programmes; fit depends on programme, academic background, career target and current criteria.

Which NMIMS campus is best?

Compare the exact programme, fee, placement disclosure, location and career target rather than relying on a generic campus ranking.
