Quick Answer

NMIMS offers various management programmes including MBA, MBA Tech, and MBA Pharma Tech. Admissions are merit-based, primarily utilizing the NMAT by GMAC exam followed by a Group Discussion and Personal Interview. The university operates across multiple campuses including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Indore, Navi Mumbai, and Shirpur.

Overview

SVKM's NMIMS is a Deemed to be University with a 44-year legacy. It offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses. The university is known for its management programmes and maintains a strong industry connection across its seven campuses.

Admissions & Selection

Admissions at NMIMS are merit-based. The process for MBA programmes typically involves:

  • Entrance Test: NMAT by GMAC.
  • Selection Rounds: Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).

Official information regarding specific cutoffs, selection weights, and detailed eligibility criteria beyond the entrance exam requirement is currently unavailable.
