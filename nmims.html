NMIMS Overview

Quick answer

NMIMS is a multi-school deemed-to-be university whose School of Business Management (SBM) in Mumbai offers MBA, MBA HR, MBA Business Analytics and other management programmes. NMIMS also operates SBM campuses in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Indore and Navi Mumbai, among others.

SBM NMIMS was established in 1981 and currently highlights 45 years of legacy, 29,000+ alumni and 250+ recruiters on its official website. The Mumbai school is located at V. L. Mehta Road, Vile Parle West.

Why NMIMS is relevant for MBA applicants

The school combines case-based pedagogy, industry interaction and a large alumni network. Applicants should compare programmes separately because the admission route, eligibility, fee and placement outcomes can vary by programme and campus.

NMIMS MBA Admission 2026

The official SBM admissions page currently lists NMIMS MBA NMAT registrations for 2026. For the flagship MBA route, the school has historically required registration for NMAT by GMAC followed by NMIMS registration and a subsequent selection process. The exact 2026-27 handout should be treated as the final authority for current selection details.

STEP 1

NMAT by GMAC

Take the NMAT by GMAC examination as required for the applicable SBM MBA programme.

STEP 2

NMIMS Registration

Complete the separate NMIMS application/registration process within the notified admission window.

STEP 3

Selection Rounds

Programme-specific shortlisting and competency/WAT/PI requirements are communicated in the current admission handout.

Cutoff caution

NMIMS does not publish one universal MBA cutoff that applies to every programme and campus. Avoid treating an external NMAT percentile as an official cutoff unless the institute explicitly publishes it.

NMIMS FAQs

Which entrance exam is used for NMIMS MBA?

NMAT by GMAC is the stated entrance route for the applicable SBM MBA programmes. Always check the current programme handout.

What is the latest NMIMS MBA placement average?

The SBM Mumbai placement page reports ₹24.6 LPA average CTC for MBA Core in the 2025-26 placement season.

Does NMIMS have only one MBA?

No. SBM Mumbai lists multiple MBA programmes including MBA, MBA HR, MBA Business Analytics, MBA Pharmaceutical Management, MBA Healthcare Management and MBA Digital Transformation.
