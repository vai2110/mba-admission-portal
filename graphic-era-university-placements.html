Graphic Era MBA Placement Snapshot

Graphic Era publishes both university-wide placement statistics and an MBA-specific placement-record table. Why we do not publish a fake 3-year average

A university-wide ₹61.99 lakh package belongs to B.Tech CSE in the university's own placement material. Presenting it as an MBA outcome would mislead an MBA applicant. This page therefore separates verified MBA offers from university-wide claims.

Placement & Career Support

The management department states that MBA students receive support through a dedicated placement cell, internship and final-placement coordination, industry mentoring, career guidance, resume building, mock interviews and soft-skill training. It also states that the Summer Internship Project is mandatory.

How to use the placement ecosystem well

  • Build role-specific skills before the placement season.
  • Use internships to create evidence of practical work.
  • Do not choose a specialisation only because of a recruiter name; compare the role and career path.
  • For analytics/AI pathways, build technical projects alongside management knowledge.

Placement Reality Check for MBA Applicants

GEU's public data supports a strong university-wide placement ecosystem and several concrete MBA offers. It does not support a single MBA-wide average salary number from the current placement-record page. Students comparing GEU with other universities should therefore ask for the latest programme-specific placement report, batch size, median/average CTC, number of students placed, role-wise distribution and internship/PPO data before making an ROI decision.
