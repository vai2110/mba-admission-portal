Executive Post-Graduate Diploma Program in Management (EPGDPM)

Advanced management education designed for working professionals at an Institution of National Importance.

Quick Answer

The EPGDPM is a degree-granting programme at IIM Visakhapatnam tailored for working executives. While the institute offers various executive programmes, specific current intake, fee structures, and cutoff data for the EPGDPM are not explicitly published in the current official domain.
