These are the current fee figures displayed on TSM's fee-structure page. The key comparison is that the PGDM academic fee is ₹1 lakh higher over two years, while accommodation and mess are shown at the same ₹3.5 lakh level for both programmes.

What Does the Fee Cover?

TSM states that academic fees include tuition, books, study material, blazer, examination fee and other miscellaneous academic expenses. Residential accommodation with meals is separately shown in the total. PGDM students additionally receive access to the international outbound programme; TSM says travel and course costs are covered for that programme while personal/incidental expenses are the student's responsibility.

TSM Scholarships

TSM offers scholarships based on academic and entrance-exam performance and also describes diversity-focused support. Its scholarship information references CAT, MAT, XAT and CMAT performance, while the PGDM-vs-MBA page specifically states that PGDM candidates with a CAT percentile of 60 or above are eligible for a merit scholarship.

Scholarship conditions can vary by programme and admission cycle. Students should verify the current scholarship notice before treating a stated waiver as guaranteed financial support.

Education Loan Support

TSM's fee page lists education-loan options through Bank of Baroda and HDFC Credila. The page states loan funding of up to ₹10 lakh and no security requirement for loans up to ₹7.50 lakh, subject to the lender's terms and conditions.

TSM Fees & Scholarship FAQs

What is the total TSM MBA fee?

TSM currently shows ₹14.70 lakh for two years including ₹11 lakh academic fee, ₹3.5 lakh accommodation and mess, and ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit.

What is the total TSM PGDM fee?

TSM currently shows ₹15.70 lakh including ₹12 lakh academic fee, ₹3.5 lakh accommodation and mess, and ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit.

Does TSM provide scholarships?

Yes. TSM provides scholarships linked to entrance performance, academic merit and diversity-related criteria. Specific conditions should be checked for the current admission cycle.

Is hostel included in TSM fees?

Residential accommodation and mess are included in the total cost shown by TSM, but they are itemised separately from the academic fee.
