Christ University BCom: Quick Answer

What you need to know

The standard BCom (Honours/Honours with Research) is listed as a 4-year, 8-semester programme. The central eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12, while the 2026 Bangalore Central selection document places standard BCom and several BCom specialisations in the ET + SA + MP + PI route.

What Does Christ BCom Offer?

The standard BCom builds a foundation in accounting, finance, taxation, marketing and business functions. Christ also offers specialised BCom programmes in areas such as Finance and Accountancy, Finance and Investment, Applied Finance and Analytics, Accountancy and Taxation, International Business Accounting and Strategic Finance.

Do not treat all BCom programmes as the same. Some have different duration, subject requirements and fee structures.

Who Is Eligible for BCom?

The central eligibility page lists a pass in Class 12 for BCom. Some specialised programmes have stricter rules. For example, BCom (Hons) and BCom (Hons) SF are listed at 60%, while BCom (Finance & Accountancy) and other variants can have programme-specific subject conditions.

What Is the BCom Selection Process?

For Bangalore Central, the 2026 UG entrance-test list includes standard BCom and multiple specialised BCom programmes under ET + SA + MP + PI. SA is a written assessment, MP is a 90-second presentation and PI is the interview stage.

The process can differ by campus or programme, so the exact programme selection document remains the final reference.

What Can You Do After BCom?

Career options depend heavily on the chosen specialisation and professional qualifications. Typical paths include accounting, audit, taxation, corporate finance, banking, investment analysis, business analytics and management studies.

For CA aspirants, the Finance and Accountancy route has a specific in-house CA facilitation focus according to Christ's programme page.

Christ University BCom FAQs

Is Mathematics compulsory for every Christ BCom?

No universal Mathematics condition applies to every BCom. Some specialised programmes may have their own academic requirements, so check the exact course page.

What is the BCom fee for other Indian states?

The standard BCom 2026 fee listing shows ₹2.95 lakh per year for other Indian states, plus ₹8,000 registration fee.

Does every Christ BCom programme use the same selection process?

No. The university publishes programme/campus-specific selection lists. The Bangalore Central 2026 ET list includes standard and several specialised BCom programmes under ET + SA + MP + PI.

Which Christ BCom is relevant for CA?

BCom Finance and Accountancy is specifically designed with CA facilitation and accounting, audit and taxation focus.
