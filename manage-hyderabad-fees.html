MANAGE PGDM ABM Total Fee 2026-28

Total listed programme cost:

₹9,75,000 for two years, comprising ₹4,05,000 tuition, ₹4,94,000 boarding and lodging, and ₹76,000 under other listed fee heads.

The 2026 prospectus clearly states that the fee is provisional and subject to upward revision. Students should therefore use ₹9.75 lakh as the published 2026-28 figure, not as a guaranteed final cost for future batches.

Other annual charges include ₹10,000 library, ₹5,000 digital learning, ₹2,000 health insurance, ₹15,000 computer/internet and ₹1,000 student welfare each year.

What should students budget beyond the ₹9.75 lakh fee?

The prospectus says students will separately bear expenses for blazers, laptops and other academic events and activities. The optional foreign-university exposure programme can cost around ₹2–5 lakh depending on the university and student choice.

During summer project periods and summer vacations, students staying in the hostel may have to pay separately for boarding and lodging at approved rates.

MANAGE Hyderabad Fees FAQs

What is the total fee of MANAGE Hyderabad PGDM?

The published 2026-28 total is ₹9.75 lakh for two years.

How much is the tuition fee at MANAGE?

The two-year tuition fee is ₹4.05 lakh, payable in two ₹2.025 lakh instalments according to the prospectus.

Is hostel included in MANAGE fees?

Yes. The published ₹9.75 lakh total includes ₹4.94 lakh boarding and lodging. It is a fully residential programme.

Is the MANAGE caution money refundable?

The prospectus lists ₹10,000 caution money as refundable, subject to recovery of dues if any.

Is ₹9.75 lakh the final cost including laptop?

No. The institute separately mentions laptops, blazers and other academic activities as additional expenses.
