MANAGE PGDM ABM Total Fee 2026-28

Published two-year programme fee:

₹9,75,000, comprising ₹4,05,000 tuition, ₹4,94,000 boarding and lodging and ₹76,000 under other listed fee heads.

The 2026 prospectus describes the fee as provisional and subject to upward revision. So ₹9.75 lakh is the published 2026-28 programme figure, not a guarantee that later batches will pay exactly the same amount.

Is Hostel and Boarding Included in MANAGE Fees?

Yes. The programme is fully residential and the published two-year fee includes ₹4.94 lakh for boarding and lodging. Students should still remember that staying on campus during summer project periods or vacations can attract separate boarding and lodging charges.

MANAGE Hyderabad Fees FAQs: student-intent questions

What is the total fee of MANAGE Hyderabad PGDM ABM?

The published 2026-28 fee is ₹9.75 lakh for two years. It includes tuition, boarding and lodging and other listed programme charges.

What is the tuition fee at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The two-year tuition fee is ₹4.05 lakh. The prospectus splits it into two ₹2.025 lakh payments.

How much does hostel cost at MANAGE Hyderabad?

Boarding and lodging together are listed at ₹4.94 lakh for two years and are included in the published ₹9.75 lakh programme fee.

Is hostel compulsory at MANAGE?

The PGDM ABM is a fully residential programme, so students should plan their finances on the basis of the residential structure rather than assuming they can remove hostel costs.

Is mess included in the MANAGE fee?

The official fee document uses the combined head “Boarding & Lodging Charges” of ₹4.94 lakh rather than publishing a separate mess figure in the total table. Students should therefore use the official combined amount for budgeting.

Is the ₹10,000 caution money refundable?

Yes. The fee table lists ₹10,000 caution money as refundable, subject to adjustment/recovery of dues where applicable.

Is ₹9.75 lakh the complete cost of studying at MANAGE?

It is the published two-year programme fee, but not every personal expense. Students may separately need to pay for a laptop, blazer, academic activities and optional foreign exposure. Summer/vacation hostel stays can also have separate charges.

Is the foreign university programme compulsory?

No. MANAGE describes the foreign-university exposure programme as optional. The prospectus gives an approximate additional cost of ₹2–5 lakh depending on the university and student choice.

Can students take an education loan for MANAGE?

Yes. The prospectus says MANAGE facilitates access to education loans through banks such as UBI, SBI and Canara Bank. It also states that eligible students can use the Government's PM-Vidyalakshmi scheme subject to its rules.

Is MANAGE worth ₹9.75 lakh?

For an eligible agriculture/agri-allied student targeting agribusiness, the placement numbers make it worth serious consideration: the 2023-25 average CTC was ₹12.38 LPA and median CTC ₹12 LPA. But students should evaluate role quality, career goals and loan burden instead of comparing only fee against the highest package.
