Chitkara University Punjab Overview

Chitkara University, Punjab is a private university located on the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway. The university positions industry exposure, applied learning, international pathways and career preparation as important parts of its academic model.

Who should consider Chitkara University?

It can suit students seeking a broad private-university portfolio near Chandigarh, particularly those interested in engineering, management, computing, design, healthcare, law and hospitality.

The university's current 2026 information includes undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral options across multiple schools. Always verify programme-specific eligibility and fees because they differ substantially.
