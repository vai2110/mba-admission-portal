Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida: What Students Should Know

Jaipuria Noida is a management school in Sector 62, Delhi NCR. Its full-time PGDM programmes are AICTE approved, NBA accredited and recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. Jaipuria Noida was ranked 41st in NIRF Management 2025.

The Noida campus lists PGDM, PGDM (Marketing) and PGDM (Service Management). The core PGDM is the broadest route; Marketing is function-focused; Service Management is built around the services economy.

Is Jaipuria Noida worth considering?

It can be a strong shortlist option if you want Delhi-NCR access, dual-specialisation flexibility, AACSB accreditation and a relatively accessible entrance route. Make the final decision using role mix, placement distribution, total cost and alternative calls—not the highest package alone.

Jaipuria Noida Campus, Location & Student Experience

The Noida campus is a four-acre green, Wi-Fi-enabled campus in Sector 62. The institute highlights an air-conditioned academic complex, lecture halls, auditoriums, library, computer labs, faculty offices, a central green court and an open-air amphitheatre.

Delhi-NCR advantage

Sector 62 places students within a major corporate and technology ecosystem. This can help with industry interaction, internships and networking, but location itself is not a placement guarantee.

Global exposure

Jaipuria highlights 15+ global academic alliances and international exposure opportunities, subject to programme and selection conditions.

Who Should Choose Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida?

Good fit

  • You want a Delhi-NCR B-school.
  • You value dual-specialisation flexibility.
  • You are considering finance, analytics, marketing, HR, operations or consulting.
  • You want a lower published academic fee than many premium private B-schools.

Think twice

  • You have a stronger call at a similar total cost.
  • You are choosing only because of the highest CTC.
  • You need a very high average salary to justify your investment.
  • You want a highly selective small-cohort environment.
