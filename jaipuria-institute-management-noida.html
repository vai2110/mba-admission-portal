Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida: What Students Should Know

Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida is a management institute in Sector 62, Noida, offering two-year full-time PGDM programmes. The Noida campus currently offers PGDM, PGDM (Marketing) and PGDM (Service Management). The institute states that its PGDM is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA for the stated purposes.

Is Jaipuria Noida an MBA college?

Students enroll in PGDM programmes rather than a university MBA. Jaipuria states that its PGDM is AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA. The practical decision is therefore to compare the programme, total fee, placement outcomes, role mix and your alternative calls rather than judge the label alone.

Jaipuria Noida Campus & Student Life

The Noida campus is a four-acre green, Wi-Fi-enabled campus with lecture halls, auditoriums, a library, computer labs, faculty spaces, a central lawn and an open-air amphitheatre. Its Sector 62 location places students within the wider Delhi NCR corporate ecosystem.

What works for students

  • Delhi NCR location and corporate access.
  • Industry-linked curriculum, live projects and internships.
  • Residential hostel option on campus.
  • International alliances and industry exposure.

What to evaluate before paying

  • ₹16.50 lakh tuition plus living costs is a significant investment.
  • Placement outcomes vary by student, role and batch.
  • Check the actual role mix, fixed pay and location in offers.
  • Compare your alternative MBA/PGDM calls on total cost and career outcome.

Is Jaipuria Institute of Management Noida Worth It?

Jaipuria Noida can make sense as a Delhi NCR PGDM option when you value location, a recognised PGDM structure, multiple specialisation routes and access to the institute's centralised placement ecosystem. The decision becomes weaker if your alternatives offer materially stronger average/median outcomes at a similar or lower total cost.

Do not make the decision on the highest package alone. Compare your likely role, average salary for the relevant segment, placement percentage, total fee, hostel/mess cost, scholarship and the opportunity cost of two years.

Jaipuria Noida FAQs

What is the fee for Jaipuria Noida PGDM 2026-28?

The official fee is ₹16.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM, split as ₹8.30 lakh in Year 1 and ₹8.20 lakh in Year 2, with a refundable ₹15,000 security deposit.

Which exams are accepted by Jaipuria Noida?

For 2026 admissions, Jaipuria lists CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT, MAT and GMAT.

Is work experience compulsory for Jaipuria Noida?

No. The selection framework awards only 3% weight to work experience, sports and extracurricular activities, so fresh graduates can apply.

Is Jaipuria PGDM equivalent to MBA?

Jaipuria states that its AIU-recognised PGDM is equivalent to MBA for the stated purposes.

What is the highest package at Jaipuria Noida?

The latest published placement highlights show ₹24.11 LPA highest CTC. Older material reports different figures, so students should always check the batch-specific placement report.

Which PGDM should I choose at Jaipuria Noida?

Choose core PGDM for flexibility, Marketing for brand/digital/marketing roles, and Service Management if you want a service-sector orientation. Compare the actual electives and target roles before choosing.
