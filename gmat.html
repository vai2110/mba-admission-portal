GMAT 2026 Overview

The GMAT is a global graduate business school admission examination owned and administered by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC).

It is widely used by MBA, business master's and other graduate management programmes to evaluate quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning and data analysis skills.

GMAT is different from CAT:

GMAT is designed primarily for global graduate business-school admissions, while CAT is primarily used for management admissions in India.

Quantitative Reasoning

The Quantitative Reasoning section evaluates foundational arithmetic and algebra knowledge along with problem-solving and analytical reasoning.

Major Areas

  • Arithmetic
  • Algebra
  • Problem Solving
  • Ratios and Percentages
  • Equations
  • Word Problems

Verbal Reasoning

The Verbal Reasoning section evaluates reading comprehension and the ability to analyse and evaluate arguments.

Major Areas

  • Reading Comprehension
  • Critical Reasoning
  • Understanding Arguments
  • Inference
  • Logical Evaluation

Data Insights

Data Insights measures the ability to analyse data and solve problems using quantitative and logical information.

Major Areas

  • Data Interpretation
  • Data Sufficiency
  • Graphs and Tables
  • Multi-Source Reasoning
  • Two-Part Analysis

GMAT Eligibility

  • The GMAT does not require a specific undergraduate discipline.
  • Business schools determine their own admission requirements.
  • Candidates should check the eligibility criteria of each target programme.
  • GMAC policy currently requires candidates to be at least 18 years old to schedule and take the exam, subject to specified exceptions for younger test takers.
Important:

GMAT eligibility and MBA admission eligibility are not the same thing. Always check the requirements of your target university.

GMAT Admission Process

  1. Research target MBA or business master's programmes.
  2. Check programme-specific requirements.
  3. Take the GMAT.
  4. Prepare the rest of the application.
  5. Submit applications before programme deadlines.
  6. Complete interviews and other selection stages if shortlisted.
  7. Receive the admission decision.

How to Prepare for GMAT

Understand the test first

Become familiar with the three sections and the adaptive nature of the examination.

Build fundamentals

Strengthen arithmetic, algebra, reading comprehension, critical reasoning and data interpretation.

Practise official questions

Use official GMAT preparation material to understand the actual question style.

Analyse mistakes

Track recurring mistakes and identify whether they come from conceptual gaps, reasoning errors or time management.

Take full-length mocks

Simulate the actual examination environment and build a consistent time-management strategy.

GMAT 2026 FAQs

What is the GMAT?

GMAT stands for Graduate Management Admission Test.

How many sections are in GMAT?

The current GMAT has three sections: Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning and Data Insights.

How long is the GMAT?

The examination lasts 2 hours and 15 minutes, plus an optional 10-minute break.

How many questions are in GMAT?

The current GMAT contains 64 questions.

What is the GMAT score range?

The current total score range is 205 to 805.

How long is a GMAT score valid?

GMAT scores are valid for five years from the test date.

Can I take GMAT online?

Yes. GMAT is available through test centres and online delivery, subject to availability and applicable policies.

How many times can I take GMAT?

Candidates can take the GMAT up to five times in a rolling 12-month period, subject to the applicable policies.
