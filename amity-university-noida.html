Amity University Noida Overview

The official 2026 prospectus and programme pages should be used for exact eligibility and fee details because requirements vary by course.

Students who want a large private-university campus in Delhi NCR with many programme choices, extensive infrastructure and an active corporate-placement ecosystem should evaluate the Noida campus programme by programme.

Student Discussion: What Should You Compare?

For a private university decision, students should compare the exact course, total four-year or two-year cost, internship opportunities, placement outcomes for that programme, campus experience and alternatives in Delhi NCR. University-wide headline numbers should not be treated as programme-level guarantees.

  • Check the exact 2026 programme fee.
  • Compare median or typical outcomes where available, not only the highest package.
  • Understand sponsored versus non-sponsored fee structures.
  • Factor hostel, living and transport costs into ROI.

Amity University Noida FAQs

Is Amity University Noida accepting applications for 2026?

The official Noida admissions page currently displays 22 September 2026 as the last date to apply for the current UG and PG admission session.

Does Amity Noida provide hostel accommodation?

Yes. The university provides separate on-campus hostel facilities for boys and girls.

How much is the 2026 Noida hostel fee?

The official FAQ lists ₹1.95 lakh per annum for AC rooms plus a ₹15,000 one-time hostel security deposit.

Does Amity Noida offer BTech and MBA?

Yes. Both BTech and MBA are among the major programme categories offered at the Noida campus.
