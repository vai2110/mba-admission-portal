TSM Placements

TSM Placement Records: What Is Publicly Available?

TSM's official placement section currently publishes placement records for 2025 and provides recruiter/outcome material. The website positions placement as a structured process supported by a dedicated placement team, industry engagement and career mapping. Because the official webpage presents the 2025 record primarily through images, students should verify exact salary statistics from the placement report rather than copy an unverified headline number from third-party websites.

Important for students

Do not assume that the highest package is the salary you should expect. Compare median/average compensation where disclosed, number of students placed, recruiter mix, role type and the programme from which the data was generated.

How TSM Placement Preparation Works

TSM describes a placement roadmap that begins in the first trimester. Students are introduced to placement policy and recruitment expectations early, followed by assessments, training, industry engagement and recruitment stages.

What Roles Can TSM Students Target?

TSM's PGDM page identifies management trainee, business analyst, marketing executive, financial analyst and HR associate among top role examples. The actual role mix depends on specialisation, recruiter demand, student profile and the particular placement cycle.

Internship and Placement Connection

TSM's PGDM includes an 8–10 week internship, while the MBA programme describes an eight-week mandatory internship. Students should treat the internship as an opportunity to build a demonstrable project story and potentially strengthen their final-placement profile.

Career Mapping and Mentorship

TSM's career mapping initiative provides one-to-one counselling around interests, strengths, career goals and available opportunities. The placement ecosystem also includes alumni mentorship. These services matter because students often enter an MBA with a broad career goal but need help narrowing it to a function, industry and target role.

How to Evaluate TSM Placement ROI

  • Separate MBA and PGDM outcomes whenever the report permits.
  • Look at median or average compensation instead of only the highest package.
  • Check whether your target function is represented in the recruiter/role mix.
  • Compare placement rate with batch size and not just number of offers.
  • Consider internship quality and role conversion potential.
  • Compare the outcome against TSM's full residential cost, not only tuition.

Does TSM have a placement cell?

What roles are offered at TSM?

TSM cites roles such as management trainee, business analyst, marketing executive, financial analyst and HR associate.

Does TSM provide career counselling?

Yes. TSM's career mapping initiative provides one-to-one counselling to align student strengths and goals with career paths.
