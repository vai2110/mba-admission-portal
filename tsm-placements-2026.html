TSM Placement Salary: What Can Be Verified?

TSM's current homepage highlights a ₹16.4 lakh highest CTC for PGDM. This is an official institute claim and should be treated as a highest-package figure, not as an average salary or an expected salary for every student.

The official placement-record page currently identifies 2025 placement records and displays outcome/recruiter material through images. Because the numerical record is not exposed as a text table on that page, a responsible student guide should not manufacture average, median or placement-percentage figures from third-party sources.

Important distinction

Highest CTC answers “What did the top offer reach?” It does not answer “What salary will the typical student receive?” For ROI, median/average compensation, placed-student count, recruiter mix and role quality are more useful.

How TSM Placement Preparation Works

TSM's placement roadmap begins early rather than waiting until the final trimester. The institute describes a structured progression involving placement orientation, skill development, assessments, industry interaction and recruitment preparation. Career mapping adds one-to-one counselling so that students can connect interests and strengths to realistic career paths.

Career Mapping, Alumni Mentorship and Placement Support

TSM's career-mapping initiative provides one-to-one counselling around student interests, strengths, goals and career opportunities. The placement ecosystem also includes alumni mentorship and structured preparation. This matters because a student entering an MBA with a broad “management” goal needs help narrowing it to a function, role and target industry.

For example, a finance student should build a role-specific story around financial analysis, modelling, valuation or banking rather than presenting themselves as a generic MBA candidate. The same principle applies to analytics, marketing, HR and operations.

For a student targeting finance or analytics, the strongest comparison is not simply “TSM package vs another college package”. Compare the number and quality of relevant roles, the total cost, internship exposure and the career trajectory those roles can create.
