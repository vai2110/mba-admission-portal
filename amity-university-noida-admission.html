Amity Noida Admission Dates 2026

The official admissions pages currently display 22 September 2026 as the last date to apply for UG and PG programmes for the current Noida admission session. Individual programme notices can change, so students should verify the exact course page before applying.

Eligibility

Eligibility varies widely by programme. Engineering courses may specify Physics, Mathematics and another subject, management courses may require graduation or Class XII depending on level, and professional programmes have their own academic requirements.

How to Apply

  1. Select Noida and the exact programme.
  2. Check the current eligibility and selection requirements.
  3. Complete the online Amity application form.
  4. Upload the requested academic and identity documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Track the application and selection status through the admission microsite.
  7. Complete fee payment after receiving the admission decision.

Selection Process

Selection is programme-specific and can include academic merit, personal interview, written assessment or other stated criteria. The university also describes direct-admission provisions for certain high-performing Class XII applicants, subject to the applicable rules.

Scholarships

Amity provides scholarship schemes based on academic performance and other stated criteria. Scholarship eligibility and continuation conditions should be checked for the exact 2026 programme because scholarship rules can differ.
