Which BSc Hons subjects does BHU offer?

BHU's 2026 UG notice lists BSc Hons subjects across Biology and Mathematics groups, including Chemistry, Zoology, Botany, Geography, Geology, Home Science, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Science.

Students are grouped into PCB and PMC routes, so your Class 12 subjects directly affect the CUET combination you need.

What CUET subjects are required for BHU BSc Hons?

RouteCUET subjectsExamples
PCBBiology/Biotech/Biochem + Chemistry + PhysicsChemistry, Zoology, Botany, Geography, Geology, Home Science
PMCChemistry + Mathematics + PhysicsPhysics, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Geology, Geography

You must also satisfy the Class 12 eligibility for the selected programme.

How does BHU BSc admission work?

  1. Choose the BSc subject and verify its PCM/PCB requirement.
  2. Appear in CUET-UG with the required papers.
  3. Register for BHU CAP-UG.
  4. Fill preferences and complete documents.
  5. Track allotment rounds and pay the admission fee after selection.

What is the BHU BSc Hons fee?

Fee varies by programme and location. BHU does not publish one universal BSc fee applicable to every subject. Check the fee schedule attached to the programme/admission round.

What are BSc placements at BHU?

The official university-level NIRF data reports a ₹9 LPA median salary for UG 4-year programmes. This is a university-level UG figure, not a BSc-only median.

BHU BSc Hons FAQs

Can a PCB student apply for every BSc course?

No. The subject combination depends on the BSc group and selected subject.

Does BHU BSc use CUET?

Yes. The 2026 UG admission route uses CUET-UG followed by BHU CAP-UG.

Is 500 a good BHU BSc score?

It depends on the subject and category. Official 2025 examples ranged from about 479 to 516 for the listed General-category programmes.
