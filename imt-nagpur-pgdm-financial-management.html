IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management: Overview

PGDM-Financial Management is one of the three programme labels in IMT Nagpur's current admissions material. It is a two-year, full-time residential management programme for students who want deeper exposure to finance and related business functions.

The programme sits within a common management foundation and then allows students to build functional expertise through finance-oriented subjects and electives.

Who should choose PGDM Financial Management?

It is suited to students interested in corporate finance, banking, investment, financial analysis, risk, consulting and related BFSI roles.

Admission to IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management

IMT Nagpur considers CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT along with academics, work experience and personal interview performance. Work experience is not compulsory. The institute says there is no pre-decided cutoff.

PGDM Financial Management Curriculum

IMT Nagpur's published elective material includes Financial Statement Analysis & Reporting, Financial Modeling Using Excel, Investment Management, Equity Research, Fixed Income Securities, Derivatives, Financial Planning & Wealth Management, Mergers & Acquisitions, Commercial Bank Management, Credit Risk Analytics and related subjects.

PGDM Financial Management Career Scope

Potential career tracks include financial analyst, corporate finance, investment banking, equity research, credit, risk, banking, wealth management, consulting and finance operations.

IMT Nagpur's placement process is common across programmes. Students should use the latest role and recruiter mix to understand the finance-specific opportunity set rather than applying the institute-wide highest package to every programme.
