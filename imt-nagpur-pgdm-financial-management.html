IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management: Overview

PGDM-Financial Management is one of the three programme labels in IMT Nagpur's current admissions material. It is a two-year, full-time residential management programme for students who want deeper exposure to finance and related business functions.

The programme sits within a common management foundation and then allows students to build functional expertise through finance-oriented subjects and electives.

Who should choose PGDM Financial Management?

It is suited to students interested in corporate finance, banking, investment, financial analysis, risk, consulting and related BFSI roles.

Admission to IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management

IMT Nagpur considers CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT along with academics, work experience and personal interview performance. Work experience is not compulsory. The institute says there is no pre-decided cutoff.

PGDM Financial Management Curriculum

IMT Nagpur's published elective material includes Financial Statement Analysis & Reporting, Financial Modeling Using Excel, Investment Management, Equity Research, Fixed Income Securities, Derivatives, Financial Planning & Wealth Management, Mergers & Acquisitions, Commercial Bank Management, Credit Risk Analytics and related subjects.

PGDM Financial Management Career Scope

Potential career tracks include financial analyst, corporate finance, investment banking, equity research, credit, risk, banking, wealth management, consulting and finance operations.

IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management FAQs

What is IMT Nagpur PGDM Financial Management?

It is a two-year, full-time residential programme focused on finance within IMT Nagpur’s wider management curriculum, for students interested in corporate finance, investment, banking, risk and related financial roles.

Which exams are accepted for PGDM Financial Management?

IMT Nagpur considers CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT, along with academics, work experience and personal interview performance. Work experience is not compulsory and there is no pre-decided cutoff.

What is the fee for PGDM Financial Management?

The official 2025-27 fee sheet lists ₹15 lakh for PGDM, PGDM-Marketing and PGDM-Financial Management. The FAQ states that the total includes hostel and mess charges and is subject to minor revisions.

Is PGDM Financial Management suitable for freshers?

Yes. Work experience is not mandatory. Candidates with relevant experience may receive additional selection weight, while freshers can strengthen their application through entrance performance, academics, PI preparation and clear finance-oriented goals.

What finance subjects are taught?

Published elective material includes Financial Statement Analysis & Reporting, Financial Modeling Using Excel, Investment Management, Equity Research, Fixed Income Securities, Derivatives, Financial Planning & Wealth Management, M&A, Commercial Bank Management and Credit Risk Analytics.

What careers can I pursue after PGDM Financial Management?

Potential tracks include financial analysis, corporate finance, investment banking, equity research, credit, risk, banking, wealth management, consulting and finance operations. Individual outcomes depend on skills, internships, interviews, market conditions and available roles.
