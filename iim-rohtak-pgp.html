Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) Admission Process

Take CAT, complete the IIM Rohtak PGP application/shortlist process, attend the institute's selection stages and accept the final offer if selected.

Step-by-step

  1. Confirm eligibility and the applicable entrance route.
  2. Complete the programme application within the official window.
  3. Upload academic, category and work-experience documents where required.
  4. Attend the institute's test, WAT, reflective writing or PI stage if shortlisted.
  5. Review the final offer, fee schedule and joining requirements before payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) the same as the institute's flagship MBA?

No. Programme names can look similar across management schools, but eligibility, delivery mode, work-experience requirements and selection route can differ. Applicants should use the programme-specific policy.

What should I consider beyond the advertised highest package?

Compare average and median CTC, placement batch size, recruiter participation and role mix. Where official programme-level data is unavailable, do not assume institute-wide figures are exclusive to this course.

Are 2027-29 fees available?

Only if the institute has published them. Otherwise the latest official batch is a reference and the future fee should be marked pending.
