IMI Kolkata Salary: What Is a Realistic Benchmark?

The ₹40.31 LPA highest package can attract attention, but it should not be the benchmark used to estimate your personal ROI. The median salary of ₹12.49 LPA is a more grounded reference for the reported cohort. Current placement reporting puts the average around ₹12.7 LPA.

Use these numbers

  • Median salary for a central tendency benchmark.
  • Average salary to understand the overall cohort.
  • Role-wise salary where available.
  • Fixed pay versus variable pay.

Do not use only this

  • Highest CTC.
  • One exceptional international or niche offer.
  • Recruiter logo lists without role information.
  • Old placement numbers mixed with newer batches.

How Does the IMI Kolkata Placement Process Work?

IMI Kolkata's placement process typically involves companies conducting a pre-placement presentation followed by a company-specific selection process such as GD, case study, test or personal interview. The institute invites companies to campus and also supports virtual processes when required.

Sep-MarTypical final placement campus engagement window described by the institute
PPTPre-placement presentation to explain roles and compensation
GD/Case/Test/PIPossible company-specific selection stages

The institute also runs a rolling summer internship process, with SIP opportunities forming an important bridge between first-year academics and final placements.

IMI Kolkata PGDM ROI: Is the Fee Worth the Placement?

The latest detailed academic fee is ₹14.98 lakh for 2026-28, and hostel plus mess are additional and mandatory. That means a student should calculate ROI using the complete cost of attendance, not tuition alone.

Bottom line

IMI Kolkata can make sense when the student's target role, expected salary and total cost line up. If your only reason for choosing it is the highest package, the placement data is not enough to justify the decision.

Questions to Ask Before Accepting IMI Kolkata

  • What percentage of the batch received an offer by the final placement deadline?
  • What is the median salary for the exact graduating batch?
  • How much of the CTC is fixed and how much is variable?
  • Which roles are available to freshers?
  • How many offers are sales/business-development roles?
  • What is the total hostel and mess cost for my room category?
  • What scholarship or EWS fee waiver can I realistically claim?
  • How does IMI Kolkata compare with my other offers on total cost and role quality?
