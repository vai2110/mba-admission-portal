What Is MBA Business Analytics at SRMIST?

Business Analytics is the management option for students who want to use data for business decisions. It is different from the broader MBA Business Administration route and should be compared on curriculum, roles and fee.

Who Can Apply?

SRMIST lists a minimum 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree for the MBA portfolio. Work experience is given preference in the published management admission information.

How Does Admission Work?

The standard route is SRMJEEM followed by Personal Interview. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

What Entrance Score Is Needed?

SRMIST's AICTE mandatory disclosure lists minimum desirable attainment figures: CAT 65 percentile+, MAT 75 percentile+, XAT 65 percentile+, GMAT 640+, NMAT 180+, CMAT 75 percentile+ and TANCET 80 percentile+.

These are not guaranteed final cutoffs. SRMIST states that selection also depends on profile and pre-admission rounds.

What Will You Study?

The value of Business Analytics lies in combining management decisions with data. Before joining, check how much of the curriculum is devoted to analytics, statistics, data tools, business problem-solving and projects.

Who Should Choose Business Analytics?

Choose it if you enjoy numbers and business problems and want analytics-oriented management roles. If you want wider specialisation freedom, compare the general MBA.

What About Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported average salary ₹10.5 LPA and maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum. These figures are university-level and are not Business Analytics-specific.
