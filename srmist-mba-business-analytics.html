MBA Business Analytics

SRMIST lists MBA Business Analytics among its management programmes. The course is aimed at students who want management training with analytics-oriented decision making.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree with minimum 50% marks is the stated MBA eligibility requirement.

Admission

SRMJEEM plus PI is the standard route. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct PI consideration.

Fees

SRMIST publishes management fees by programme and campus. The current admission page should be used for the exact Business Analytics fee because a generic MBA fee should not be applied to every specialisation.

Placements

Official university-level 2023 figures reported average salary of ₹10.5 LPA and maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum, with 1,311 companies and 13,069 offers.

FAQs

Is Business Analytics a separate MBA?

Yes. SRMIST lists MBA Business Analytics separately from MBA Business Administration.

Can CAT applicants apply?

Eligible CAT scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

Is the university placement figure Business Analytics-specific?

No. The ₹10.5 LPA average is a university-level figure.
