MBA Business Analytics at SRMIST

This programme combines management study with analytics-oriented decision making. It is a separate choice from the general MBA Business Administration programme.

Eligibility

SRMIST's MBA eligibility requires a bachelor's degree with a minimum 50% marks, subject to the programme's current conditions.

Admission Route

SRMIST lists SRMJEEM followed by PI. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

Fees

The general MBA Business Administration fee is ₹4.50 lakh at Kattankulathur, ₹3.25 lakh at Ramapuram and ₹3 lakh at Vadapalani. SRMIST does not provide a single fee that can safely be applied to every specialised MBA, so the exact Business Analytics fee should be taken from its current programme listing.

What Is the Analytics Focus?

The programme is intended for students who want management training alongside data and analytics-based business decision making. Compare its curriculum with the general MBA before choosing.

Placements

See the dedicated SRMIST placements page for university-level placement numbers. Those figures are not a Business Analytics-specific salary guarantee.

MBA Business Analytics FAQs

Is the ₹4.50 lakh fee specifically for MBA Business Analytics?

No. It is the official fee listed for MBA Business Administration at Kattankulathur. A specialised programme should be checked separately.

Can CAT candidates apply for SRMIST MBA Business Analytics?

CAT is among the listed scores eligible for direct consideration for the PI, subject to SRMIST's conditions.

Is this the same as SRMIST MBA AI & Data Science?

No. Business Analytics and AI & Data Science are separately listed MBA programme options.
