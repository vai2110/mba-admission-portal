Programme Overview

BMU currently lists MA Public Policy among its postgraduate programmes. It can suit students interested in governance, public administration, policy research, development and public-sector or consulting careers.

Applicants should use BMU's current admissions portal to verify eligibility, application requirements, selection process and fee details for the 2026 intake. Programme-specific rules should take precedence over generic university information.
