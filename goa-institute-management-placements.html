GIM Recruiters, Sectors & Roles: What Should Students Check?

GIM's published placement ecosystem spans financial services, banking, IT/ITES, consulting, FMCG/FMCD, healthcare, manufacturing, insurance and retail. Recruiter breadth is useful, but students should go deeper than company logos.

Check these before accepting an offer

  • Exact job title and function.
  • Fixed versus variable compensation.
  • Location and transfer conditions.
  • Role progression and learning scope.
  • Eligibility rules for your programme.

Do not judge placement by

  • Highest CTC alone.
  • Recruiter-logo count alone.
  • Older placement data mixed with current batches.
  • Average CTC without role distribution.

GIM ROI: Is ₹21.45 Lakh Academic Fee Worth It?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh, while the 2027-29 fee is not yet published. This means ROI should be calculated from the eventual fee plus living and personal expenses and compared against realistic salary outcomes.

Bottom line

GIM can make financial sense for students who convert the programme into a strong role, particularly in sectors where GIM has meaningful recruiter participation. The correct comparison is total cost versus your realistic post-MBA role and salary—not ₹35 LPA versus the fee.

GIM Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at GIM?

GIM reports ₹35 LPA as the highest package for the 2024-26 PGDM batch.

How many recruiters visited GIM?

GIM reports 110+ recruiters for the 2024-26 PGDM batch.

How many PPOs were reported?

GIM reports 50 PPOs for the 2024-26 batch.

What are the major PGDM placement sectors?

Financial services, IT, banking, consulting and FMCG/FMCD are among the largest published sector categories.

Should I use ₹35 LPA to calculate my expected salary?

No. It is the highest offer. Students should use role-level and central-tendency salary information where available and compare the complete cost of attendance.
