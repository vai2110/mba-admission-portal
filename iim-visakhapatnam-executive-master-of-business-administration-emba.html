Executive Master of Business Administration (EMBA)

A two-year, blended-mode programme for working professionals focused on strategic leadership and AI-integrated management.

On This Page:
  • Quick Answer
  • Programme Overview
  • Admission & Selection
  • Fees & Financial Aid

Quick Answer

  • Duration: 2 Years (Blended Mode).
  • Intake: Twice a year (May and December).
  • Eligibility: Bachelor's degree with 50% marks (relaxations apply).
  • Selection: Composite score (Work experience, entrance exam, personal interview).
  • Fees: Official fee structure is currently unavailable on the institute's portal.
  • Cutoff: Official cutoff data is not published by the institute.

Programme Overview

The EMBA programme at IIM Visakhapatnam comprises 740+ hours of interactive executive learning. It is designed to build managerial depth, digital fluency, and leadership resilience in an AI-native ecosystem.

Admission & Selection Logic

IIM Visakhapatnam uses a multi-parameter approach to identify promising candidates. The selection process is based on a composite score derived from work experience, entrance examination scores, and personal interview performance.
