What should you know about IBS Hyderabad?

Quick answer

IBS Hyderabad offers a two-year full-time MBA through the ICFAI Business School system. For the 2027–29 cycle, the official IBS admissions site allows applicants to apply through IBSAT or submit valid CAT, XAT, NMAT or GMAT scores.

IBS Hyderabad is one of the campuses under the IBS admissions system. The official 2027 cycle lists IBSAT 2026 as the institute's own entrance test and also permits specified external scores.

How does IBS Hyderabad MBA admission work?

The current IBSAT 2026 admissions cycle lists IBSAT, CAT, XAT, NMAT and GMAT score routes. IBSAT 2026 is scheduled for December 2026, with the selection process tentatively planned for February 2027 at IBS Hyderabad.

Eligibility

  • Graduation in any discipline with at least 50% marks.
  • Minimum 15 years of education under the published eligibility structure.
  • Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's completion and documentation requirements.

Selection process

1. Test / Score

Take IBSAT or submit an accepted external score.

2. Selection briefing

Qualified candidates attend the institute's selection briefing.

3. Micro Presentation

The selection process includes a micro-presentation component.

4. Personal Interview

Final selection also considers interview performance and past academics.

What are IBS Hyderabad MBA fees?

For the 2027–29 MBA programme, the official IBS fee page lists ₹8.505 lakh per year, or ₹17.01 lakh for two years. A ₹10,000 refundable caution deposit is additional; living expenses are extra.

IBS does not publish a single official CAT/XAT/NMAT percentile on the current 2027 admissions page as a universal guaranteed cutoff. The institute states that candidates are invited for selection based on qualifying scores.

Does IBS offer scholarships?

For the 2027–29 intake, IBS has announced ₹10 crore worth of merit scholarships, covering 500 scholarships of ₹2 lakh each. The current announcement includes candidates applying through IBSAT and specified CAT/XAT/NMAT/GMAT routes, subject to the scholarship rules.

IBS Hyderabad MBA FAQs

Does IBS Hyderabad accept CAT, XAT, NMAT or GMAT scores?

Yes. For the current MBA/PGPM admission route, candidates can apply through IBSAT or use valid CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC or GMAT scores, subject to the applicable IBS admission rules. IBSAT is the institute's own entrance examination, while the external-score route allows eligible candidates to participate without relying solely on IBSAT.

What is the IBS Hyderabad MBA fee?

For the 2027–29 MBA programme, the published fee is ₹8.505 lakh per year, making the two-year programme fee ₹17.01 lakh. A refundable caution deposit of ₹10,000 is additional, while living and personal expenses are not included in the programme fee. Applicants should use the fee applicable to their admission batch rather than an older fee figure.

What is the IBS Hyderabad cutoff for MBA admission?

IBS Hyderabad has not released a single fixed official percentile cutoff that guarantees admission for CAT, XAT, NMAT or GMAT candidates. Admission is based on the applicable qualifying score route followed by the institute's selection process. Therefore, an unofficial percentile should only be treated as a reference and not as a guaranteed shortlist or admission threshold. Candidates should rely on the official qualifying-score requirements for the relevant admission cycle.

What are the latest IBS Hyderabad placement figures?

For the IBS Hyderabad Class of 2026, the official IBS admission material reports an average CTC of ₹10.01 LPA and a highest CTC of ₹40 LPA. It also reports ₹15.58 LPA for the top 10% average and ₹13.36 LPA for the top 25% average. These figures relate specifically to IBS Hyderabad and should not be confused with placement figures for the other IBS campuses.

What is the IBS Hyderabad MBA selection process?

Candidates qualifying through IBSAT or an accepted CAT, XAT, NMAT or GMAT score are called for the selection process. The published IBS process includes a Micro Presentation and a technical-cum-Personal Interview, along with consideration of past academic performance. Final selection therefore depends on the complete applicant profile rather than only the entrance-test percentile.
