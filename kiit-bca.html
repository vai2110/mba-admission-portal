Quick Answer

The BCA is an undergraduate computer-applications programme. The current KIITEE eligibility criteria require 50% in 10+2 in any stream with Mathematics as a subject.

BCA Programme Overview

The programme is intended for students who want an undergraduate route into computer applications and software-oriented careers without taking the engineering B.Tech route. It can also provide a foundation for MCA, specialised computing certifications and technology roles.

Admission Dates 2026

Applicants should follow the live KIITEE 2026 application and counselling schedule. The official portal publishes phase-wise updates and seat-allocation instructions.

Check KIITEE 2026

BCA Eligibility 2026

The published criteria require 10+2 in any stream with at least 50% marks and Mathematics as a subject.

BCA Admission Process

  1. Check the current KIITEE eligibility and programme information.
  2. Complete the online application.
  3. Follow the applicable entrance and result instructions.
  4. Attend counselling/seat allocation when called.
  5. Complete document verification and fee payment.

BCA Entrance Exam

KIIT's 2026 admission policy states that admission to courses is through KIITEE 2026, with MBBS and BDS as the NEET exceptions. Follow the current BCA instructions in the official portal.

BCA Cutoff

KIIT does not present a single fixed BCA cutoff in the accessible official information used here. Counselling and seat allocation are merit based and continue while seats remain available.

BCA Fees

The accessible current KIITEE information confirms the programme and eligibility but does not provide a single BCA fee figure in the source used for this page. Use the current official prospectus/fee communication before making a financial decision.

BCA Curriculum

A BCA curriculum typically builds foundations in programming, databases, computer networks, web technologies, software development and information systems. Applicants should use the current KIIT curriculum for the exact semester-wise subjects and electives before comparing programmes.

BCA Career Scope

BCA graduates can target entry-level software, web development, testing, technical support, application support, database and junior analyst roles depending on their skills. An MCA or specialised postgraduate qualification can strengthen options for students targeting deeper technical roles.

Software development

Build programming and project skills through internships and practical work.

Data & analytics

Strengthen SQL, Python, statistics and visualisation alongside the degree.

Cybersecurity

Add networking, operating systems and security certifications to the academic foundation.

MCA pathway

Consider MCA if your target role requires deeper computer-science and application development training.

Who Should Apply for BCA?

BCA is best suited to students who want a computing career but do not necessarily want the engineering structure of B.Tech. Compare the curriculum, practical exposure, total cost, internships and the pathway to MCA or employment before choosing.

BCA FAQs

What is the BCA eligibility?

10+2 in any stream with at least 50% marks and Mathematics as a subject.

Is Mathematics compulsory?

Yes. The published eligibility criteria specify Mathematics as a subject at 10+2 level.

What careers can I pursue after BCA?

Depending on skills and experience, graduates can target software, web, testing, application support, database, technical support and junior analytics roles.

Can I do MCA after BCA?

Yes. BCA is a common undergraduate foundation for MCA, subject to the eligibility requirements of the institution offering the MCA.
