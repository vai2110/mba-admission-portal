XIM University Placement Update 2026

In April 2026, XIM University announced that the MBA Business Management and Human Resource Management batches of 2025–27 achieved 100% summer placements. The university linked the outcome to industry alignment, student preparation and recruiter participation.

What this number means

100% summer placements is an internship outcome for the specified batch. It should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every student will receive the same final placement role or salary.

Summer Placements: Why They Matter

For a two-year MBA, summer internships are an important bridge between classroom learning and final recruitment. Students should evaluate whether the internship provides meaningful work, brand exposure, a chance of PPO and relevant functional experience.

XIM Career Advisory Services

XIM maintains a dedicated Career Advisory Services ecosystem for student career preparation and recruiter engagement. Career support can include placement processes, employer interaction, preparation and internship/final-placement coordination.

What students should use

  • Mock interviews and interview preparation
  • Resume and profile refinement
  • Recruiter interactions
  • Alumni and industry engagement

What students control

  • Skill building
  • Internship performance
  • Interview preparation
  • Role and sector selection

FAQs

Does XIM have 100% placements?

XIM announced 100% summer placements for the MBA-BM and MBA-HRM 2025–27 batch in April 2026. That is a summer-placement result, not a blanket guarantee of final placements for every programme and batch.

Does XIM provide career support?

Yes. XIM has a dedicated Career Advisory Services function and recruiter engagement ecosystem.

Should I choose XIM based only on highest package?

No. Role quality, median/average outcomes where available, recruiter mix, internship quality and programme fit are more useful for decision-making.
