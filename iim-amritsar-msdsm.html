What is IIM Amritsar MSDSM?

The M.Sc. in Data Science and Management (MSDSM) is jointly offered by IIM Amritsar and IIT Ropar. It is a full-time two-year residential programme designed around data science and its managerial applications. The programme combines technical data-science learning with management exposure across the two institutions.

This is a very different proposition from an MBA Business Analytics: MSDSM is an M.Sc. programme with stronger technical/data-science positioning.

IIM Amritsar MSDSM Eligibility

  • Minimum 60% marks or 6.0 CPI/CGPA or First Class in the qualifying degree for the latest published cycle.
  • Qualifying degree may be a recognised Bachelor's or Master's degree, preferably involving management, mathematics, computer science or allied fields.
  • Applicants need aptitude for mathematics or computer science.
  • Valid CAT, GATE, GMAT, GRE or JAM score from the validity window specified by the current admission policy.

How is MSDSM selection done?

The latest published process uses merit-based shortlisting followed by Personal Interview. The final composite score gives weight to Class 10, Class 12, qualifying degree, work experience, PI and gender diversity.

IIM Amritsar MSDSM Fees

The official MSDSM fee page confirms that programme fees cover tuition, course material, library, IT/computer, hostel, student activities and placement support; mess and personal expenses are excluded and mess is charged as per actuals.

IIM Amritsar MSDSM Placements 2025-26

The latest official MSDSM final-placement update reports a ₹32 LPA highest CTC, ₹15.26 LPA average CTC and ₹15 LPA median CTC. The top 10% averaged ₹24.97 LPA and the top 50% averaged ₹15.97 LPA.

Roles included Associate Data Scientist, Data Science Consultant, AI Automation Specialist, Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Senior Analyst, Consultant, Software Engineer and Management Trainee – AI & Initiatives.

Would I choose MSDSM over MBA-BA?

IIM Amritsar MSDSM FAQs

Is MSDSM offered jointly with IIT Ropar?

Yes. IIM Amritsar and IIT Ropar jointly offer the M.Sc. in Data Science and Management.

What exams are accepted for MSDSM?

The latest published policy accepts CAT, GATE, GMAT, GRE or JAM within the specified validity window.

What is the latest MSDSM placement package?

The 2025-26 official placement update reports ₹32 LPA highest, ₹15.26 LPA average and ₹15 LPA median CTC.
