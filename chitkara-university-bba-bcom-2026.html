Chitkara B.Com 2026

B.Com is suited to students who want a stronger commerce/accounting foundation with scope to add professional qualifications and business skills. The exact 2026 programme structure and fee should be checked in the latest Business School document.

BBA or B.Com — which should you choose?

Choose BBA when you prefer management, marketing, business analytics, operations or entrepreneurship. Choose B.Com when you prefer accounting, finance, commerce and professional finance pathways. The right choice depends on your target career, not only the degree name.

BBA & B.Com Fees

Chitkara publishes programme-specific fee structures and some programmes can include service charges such as ETS/CAS. Do not reuse an older fee from a third-party article when budgeting for 2026.

Selected global pathway programmes have separate fee structures and transfer conditions. For the current course-by-course fee hub, use the Chitkara Courses & Fees 2026 page.

BBA & B.Com Career Scope

BBA graduates can target business development, marketing, operations, analytics, sales, consulting support, entrepreneurship and management-track roles. B.Com graduates can target accounting, finance, taxation, audit, banking and corporate-finance pathways, especially when combined with professional certifications.

AnalyticsBusiness/data analyst pathways after relevant skill building.
FinanceAccounting, banking, audit and corporate finance routes.
ManagementMarketing, operations, HR and general management roles.

Who Should Choose Chitkara BBA/B.Com?

BBA is a stronger fit if

  • You enjoy business, management and market-facing subjects.
  • You want specialisations such as analytics or FinTech.
  • You plan to pursue an MBA later.

B.Com is a stronger fit if

  • You prefer accounting, finance and commerce.
  • You are considering CA/CMA/ACCA or related professional routes.
  • You want a commerce-focused undergraduate base.

BBA & B.Com FAQs

What is the duration of BBA at Chitkara?

The BBA Professional route is a three-year undergraduate programme.

What are popular BBA specialisations at Chitkara?

Published Chitkara material lists Retail Management, Logistics & Supply Chain, FinTech, Business Analytics, Digital Transformation & Strategy and Aviation among the options.

Is BBA better than B.Com?

Neither is universally better. BBA is more management-oriented, while B.Com is more commerce/finance-oriented. Choose based on your intended career and postgraduate plan.

Can BBA students do an MBA?

Yes. BBA is a common undergraduate pathway into an MBA, subject to the MBA institute's eligibility and selection criteria.
