Quick answer

IIM Rohtak's flagship PGP is a two-year MBA programme. For the 2026-28 batch, the institute's published minimum CAT 2025 threshold was 97 percentile for General category candidates, followed by an independent Personal Interview. Final selection used CAT score (60%), PI (20%) and Academic & Gender Diversity (20%). The latest reported PGP placement cycle (2026) recorded ₹20.03 LPA average CTC and ₹18 LPA median CTC.

IIM Rohtak Overview

Indian Institute of Management Rohtak is an institute of national importance established under the IIM Act, 2017. The institute offers management programmes across postgraduate, integrated, doctoral and executive formats. For MBA aspirants, the two-year Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is the primary graduate-entry programme, while the five-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) is the major school-leaver route.

Important distinction

Do not combine PGP and IPM admission data. PGP is a graduate-entry MBA route using CAT and the institute's own PI process; IPM is a five-year integrated programme for eligible Class X/XII candidates using IPM AT and PI.

IIM Rohtak PGP Admission Process

IIM Rohtak follows an institute-specific process rather than relying on another IIM's PI score. For 2026-28, the process can be understood in five stages.

Should You Choose IIM Rohtak?

The right decision depends on your profile, CAT score, career target, budget and the alternatives available to you. A strong placement headline alone should not decide the choice.

IIM Rohtak can make sense if...

You want an IIM-branded two-year MBA, are comfortable with a CAT-heavy selection process, want broad management roles and value access to consulting, finance, sales & marketing, technology/operations and general management recruiters.

Compare alternatives carefully if...

You already have a stronger offer on role quality, median compensation, programme fit or location. Compare the complete cost, median salary, role distribution and career trajectory rather than highest CTC alone.

What to compare before accepting an MBA offer

  • Admission probability: distinguish minimum cutoff from realistic conversion.
  • Total cost: include academic fees, mess, accommodation and one-time charges.
  • Placement quality: compare median, average, role mix and recruiter depth.
  • Career fit: check whether the functions you want have meaningful placement share.
  • Programme structure: evaluate curriculum, academic load, electives and experiential opportunities.
  • Opportunity cost: compare the two-year cost and lost income against alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Rohtak MBA?

For PGP 2026-28, the published minimum overall CAT 2025 percentile was 97 for General, 93 for EWS, 83 for NC-OBC, 60 for SC and 50 for ST. These are minimum eligibility thresholds, not guaranteed admission scores.

Does IIM Rohtak use CAP for MBA admission?

No. IIM Rohtak conducts its own PGP admission process and independent PI.

What is the final selection weightage?

For PGP 2026-28, CAT carried 60%, Personal Interview 20%, and Academic & Gender Diversity 20%.

What is the latest IIM Rohtak average package?

The reported 2026 PGP average CTC was ₹20.03 LPA, with a median of ₹18 LPA and highest CTC of ₹37.1 LPA.

What is the IIM Rohtak MBA fee?

The published official PGP fee structure shows ₹8.95 lakh per year in programme charges, or ₹17.90 lakh over two years, with additional charges such as mess and placement facility fees. Verify the current 2026-28 payable amount from the institute's fee schedule and offer letter.

How is IIM Rohtak different from its IPM?

PGP is a two-year graduate-entry MBA route through CAT and PI. IPM is a five-year integrated programme for eligible school-leavers through IPM AT, PI and past academics.
