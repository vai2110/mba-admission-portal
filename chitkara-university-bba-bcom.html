BBA & B.Com Overview

Chitkara's business portfolio includes BBA and B.Com options across areas such as business data analytics, AI, fintech, digital transformation, logistics, retail, aviation and sports management. The university also lists B.Com and B.Com with ACCA.

Admission 2026

The official 2026 Business School fee material lists 50% in Class XII for several BBA programmes, with personal interview specified for the listed programmes. Check the exact course page before applying.

Career Options

Graduates can target business analytics, consulting, finance, marketing, operations, digital roles, entrepreneurship and further management education. Career outcomes depend heavily on specialisation, internships and individual skills.

BBA/B.Com FAQs

Does Chitkara offer BBA in FinTech?

Yes. The current programme portfolio includes BBA in FinTech with Deloitte and related technology-focused business options.

Does Chitkara offer B.Com with ACCA?

Yes. B.Com with ACCA is listed in the university's current programme portfolio.
