GGSIPU MBA Fire & Industrial Safety

GGSIPU offers an MBA in Fire and Industrial Safety in a weekend format. The current 2026 admission notices identify the programme as code 185 and also list a separate lateral-entry option.

Admission 2026

Current university notices include online counselling for MBA Disaster Management and MBA Fire & Industrial Safety, as well as interview schedules and application extensions for MBA-FIS. Applicants should follow the current programme notice for the exact process.

Eligibility

Check the current programme notification for qualifying degree, marks, work-experience or other programme-specific requirements. Weekend professional programmes should not be assumed to follow the regular MBA rules.
