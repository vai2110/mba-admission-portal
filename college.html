How selection works

For the 2026–28 PGP, IIMA describes the admission process as a two-step process. Candidates with a valid CAT 2025 score who meet the applicable criteria are considered for shortlisting to the Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview. Final selection considers the candidate's performance and other applicable selection attributes specified by IIMA.

CAT Cutoff vs Realistic Target

The published minimum CAT cutoff should not be confused with the percentile required to remain competitive for an IIMA call. IIMA itself states that the shortlist is based on a composite process involving CAT and Application Rating.

95 General/EWS minimum overall
85 General/EWS sectional minimum
65% CAT weight in Stage-1 composite
Important

A 95 percentile CAT score is a screening threshold, not a realistic promise of an IIMA interview call. The actual shortlist depends on the applicant pool, academic record, academic category and other components of the selection formula.

Student & Aspirant Discussions

Online discussions are useful for understanding questions that official brochures cannot answer. They are anecdotal, however, and should not be treated as official IIMA policy.

Academic intensity

Recent Reddit discussions from students/aspirants repeatedly describe the first year as highly demanding. One recent IIMA student described the first month as hectic, while another discussion specifically asks about the intensity of the first-year curriculum.

Freshers

Community discussions consistently challenge the idea that work experience is mandatory. Freshers do enter IIMA, although the latest 2026–28 batch shows that experienced candidates form the majority.

Interview matters

A recent IIMA student AMA highlighted the importance of understanding your own academics, work and career motivation. Another recent discussion around IIMA admissions emphasised that the interview can materially influence the final outcome.

Our Realistic View: Who Should Apply?

IIMA is especially attractive if you:

  • Have strong academic performance and can compete strongly on CAT.
  • Want consulting, finance, strategy, product, technology, consumer or general-management opportunities.
  • Enjoy analytical problem-solving and discussion-based learning.
  • Are comfortable with a demanding academic environment.
  • Can clearly explain why you need an MBA and why now.
  • Want access to a broad recruiter and alumni ecosystem.

Think carefully if you:

  • Are choosing IIMA purely because of the brand.
  • Already have a strong career and the MBA would not create a meaningful career change or acceleration.
  • Prefer a low-pressure academic environment.
  • Have a very specific career target that may be better served by a specialised programme.
Bottom line

IIM Ahmedabad is not simply a "99.9 percentile college." Its published selection system rewards a combination of CAT performance, academic record, profile factors and interview performance. The latest batch confirms that both freshers and experienced professionals, engineers and non-engineers, are part of the programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is work experience mandatory for IIM Ahmedabad?

No. Freshers are admitted. However, around 77% of the 2026–28 PGP batch had prior work experience, so work experience is common even though it is not mandatory.

2. Is 95 percentile enough for IIM Ahmedabad?

95 percentile is the published minimum overall screening threshold for General/EWS candidates, not a guaranteed shortlist or admission percentile.

3. Can an engineer get into IIM Ahmedabad?

Yes. Engineers make up about 60.87% of the 2026–28 PGP batch. However, engineering applicants compete in a large and highly competitive pool.

4. Can a non-engineer get into IIMA?

Yes. Non-engineering students constitute about 39.13% of the latest PGP batch. IIMA's academic-category system explicitly recognises multiple disciplines.

5. Does IIMA look at Class 10 and Class 12 marks?

Yes. Academic performance in Class 10 and Class 12 forms part of the Application Rating and also affects the shortlisting criteria.

6. Does CAT percentile alone decide admission?

No. For final selection, IIMA currently assigns 50% to PI, 10% to AWT, 25% to CAT and 15% to Application Rating.

7. What is the latest average salary at IIMA?

The latest audited IPRS figure currently published by IIMA for the PGP is ₹35.50 LPA mean salary and ₹34.59 LPA median salary for 2025. IIMA reports these using Maximum Earning Potential rather than conventional CTC.

8. Is IIMA suitable for consulting?

Yes. Consulting is one of the strongest recruiting areas, with BCG, McKinsey, Bain, Kearney, Accenture Strategy, Deloitte, EY-Parthenon and others appearing in the latest official placement release.

9. Is IIMA suitable for finance?

Yes. The latest placement cycle included investment banking, markets and PE/VC/asset-management cohorts with recruiters including Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley, Blackstone, Temasek and General Atlantic.

10. Is IIMA extremely difficult academically?

The case-method curriculum and student discussions suggest that the programme can be academically intense. The institute itself confirms that case discussions, seminars, group exercises, role-plays, lectures and discussions are major instruction methods.
