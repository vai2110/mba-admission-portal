What is SCIT's MBA ITBM?

Symbiosis Centre for Information Technology (SCIT), Pune offers an MBA in Information Technology Business Management. SCIT describes it as building management skills alongside IT/business capability for technology products and services. It is a technology-business management programme, not a general MBA with a single IT elective.

The official page lists Information Security Management, Systems, Digital Transformation and AI in Business among its focus areas. It notes that AI will be offered for the 2027 batch in place of Data Science; check the latest structure for the batch you intend to join.

How does admission work?

SCIT states that SNAP and separate programme registration are mandatory. Its page says shortlisting for Group Exercise and Personal Interaction is based on SNAP score.

  1. Register for SNAP for the applicable cycle.
  2. Select SCIT and pay the separate programme registration fee.
  3. Check the current shortlist criteria and GE/PI dates.
  4. Attend the required stages and follow the merit list.
  5. Use the official offer for fee payment and joining instructions.

What is the fee baseline?

The official 2026–28 fee structure lists academic fees of ₹3,90,000 per instalment across four instalments—₹7.80 lakh in academic fees over two years—plus a ₹20,000 refundable institute deposit with the first instalment. Hostel and mess fees are separate and may change. This is not a confirmed 2027–29 fee.

Budget for accommodation, meals, deposits, travel and personal costs, and check taxes and future-year hostel/mess charges with the institute.

Which careers align with the specialisations?

Information Security Management aligns with security governance and risk; Systems can suit IT consulting and project delivery; Digital Transformation connects to technology-led process change; AI in Business targets business applications of AI. Review the current course structure and official placement report to understand the actual roles, rather than assuming a specialisation guarantees a job title.
