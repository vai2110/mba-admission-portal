MAT 2026: What Students Need to Know

The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is conducted by AIMA to help B-Schools screen candidates for MBA and allied programmes. AIMA says MAT has been administered since 1988 and is currently accepted by 600+ B-Schools across India.

MAT is conducted four times a year — February, May, September and December — and candidates can choose Paper Based Test (PBT), Computer Based Test (CBT), or both, subject to the applicable schedule and fee.

Latest MAT 2026 update:

The September 2026 PBT and CBT registration deadlines have passed. The next official administration listed by AIMA is December 2026: PBT on December 20 and CBT on December 27.

MAT 2026 Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

AIMA's official FAQ states that the minimum qualification for appearing in MAT is graduation in any discipline from a recognised university or equivalent recognised degree. Final-year undergraduate students can also appear provisionally.

MAT 2026 Registration: How to Apply

MAT registration is completed online through mat.aima.in. For December 2026, AIMA lists separate registration deadlines for PBT and CBT.

How to Register

  1. Open the official MAT website.
  2. Create your candidate account.
  3. Enter personal and academic details.
  4. Select PBT, CBT or PBT+CBT, as permitted.
  5. Upload the required photograph and signature.
  6. Pay the applicable fee online.
  7. Submit the application and save the confirmation details.
  8. Download the admit card from the applicant dashboard when available.

Preparation takeaway:

With 150 questions in 120 minutes and negative marking, practise both speed and question selection instead of attempting questions randomly.

MAT Admission Process: What Happens After the Exam?

MAT is an entrance test, not a centralised MBA admission process. Candidates need to apply to the B-Schools they want to join and follow each institute's eligibility, cutoff and selection process.

  1. Take MAT: Appear in the selected test mode and obtain the scorecard.
  2. Shortlist B-Schools: Check current MAT acceptance and programme eligibility.
  3. Apply separately: Complete institute applications within their deadlines.
  4. Meet the cutoff: Satisfy the institute's current MAT score/percentile and academic criteria.
  5. Selection rounds: Participate in the institute's notified selection process.
  6. Final admission: Complete document verification and fee formalities after selection.

How to Prepare for MAT 2026: Practical Strategy

1. Start with a diagnostic mock

Take a full-length MAT mock to measure accuracy, speed and section-wise weaknesses.

2. Build fundamentals

Strengthen arithmetic, reasoning, reading comprehension and data interpretation before focusing heavily on speed.

3. Practise timed sets

MAT gives 120 minutes for 150 questions, so practise solving questions efficiently and moving on when a question is consuming too much time.

4. Read business and current-affairs content

Regular reading can support Language Comprehension and Economic & Business Environment preparation.

5. Analyse every mock

Track conceptual errors, calculation mistakes, misreading and time-management issues instead of looking only at the total score.

6. Recheck the official schedule

Before the December exam, confirm your registration, admit-card date, test mode, centre and reporting instructions on the official MAT portal.
