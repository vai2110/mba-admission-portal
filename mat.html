MAT 2026 Overview

The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is conducted by the All India Management Association (AIMA) for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes.

AIMA states that MAT is conducted multiple times a year and offers candidates flexibility in choosing Paper Based Test (PBT) or Computer Based Test (CBT) modes.

MAT advantage:

MAT gives management aspirants multiple opportunities during the year and allows candidates to choose between PBT and CBT modes.

MAT 2026 Eligibility

  • Candidates should have a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university or equivalent institution.
  • Final-year undergraduate students can also appear provisionally.
  • There is no age bar for appearing in MAT.
  • Work experience is not mandatory for appearing in MAT.
  • Individual management institutes may have their own academic percentage or programme-specific requirements.

AIMA's official MAT FAQ confirms graduation in any discipline as the minimum qualification and states that final-year students can apply provisionally. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

MAT 2026 Registration

MAT registration is completed online through the official MAT website operated by AIMA.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official MAT website.
  2. Create your candidate account.
  3. Enter personal and academic details.
  4. Select PBT, CBT or both modes.
  5. Upload the required photograph and signature.
  6. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  7. Submit the application.
  8. Download the confirmation details for future reference.

MAT 2026 Marking Scheme

  • Each section contains 30 questions.
  • The current official MAT information states a negative marking of 0.25 marks for every incorrect answer.
  • Candidates should avoid random guessing because incorrect responses can reduce the score.
Tip:

MAT rewards a balance between speed and accuracy. Use mock tests to develop a question-selection strategy rather than attempting every question blindly.

MAT Admission Process

Qualifying MAT does not automatically guarantee admission to a management institute.

  1. Appear for MAT and obtain the scorecard.
  2. Shortlist participating B-Schools.
  3. Apply to institutes according to their individual deadlines.
  4. Meet institute-specific cutoff and eligibility requirements.
  5. Participate in the institute's selection process.
  6. Complete admission formalities after selection.

Depending on the institute, selection may include personal interviews, group discussions, written assessments, academic profile and other criteria.

How to Prepare for MAT 2026

1. Build your fundamentals

Complete the core concepts of arithmetic, algebra, reasoning, grammar and data interpretation.

2. Practise with a timer

MAT requires candidates to solve 150 questions in 120 minutes, so speed is an important part of preparation.

3. Read daily

Reading newspapers, business articles and editorials can improve language comprehension and awareness of business developments.

4. Practise Data Analysis

Regularly solve tables, graphs, charts and data-sufficiency problems.

5. Take full-length mocks

Use mocks to improve time management and identify sections where your accuracy or speed needs improvement.

6. Analyse your mistakes

Maintain a mistake log and revisit questions that consumed excessive time or resulted in avoidable errors.
