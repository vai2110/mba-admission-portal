About MBA Finance

Finance is one of the specialisation areas published by Graphic Era for its MBA. The pathway is designed for students who want to build management knowledge around financial decision-making and related business functions.

Who should choose Finance?

Choose it if you enjoy numbers, business performance, financial statements, markets or banking and want management roles where financial understanding is important.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree of at least three years from a recognised university is required for the MBA. Final-year graduation students can apply. Valid CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/NMAT scores may be considered under university policy.

Admission Process

Apply online, submit documents and complete the applicable aptitude/interview process. GEU's management department states that candidates with valid recognised entrance-test scores may be exempted from the aptitude test under university policy.

MBA Finance Fees 2026

The finance specialisation appears within GEU's general MBA fee structure. The 2026 fee page varies the applicable slab according to graduation percentage or entrance percentile. The displayed total fee per year installment ranges from ₹6,70,795 to ₹8,28,875 across the listed slabs, plus one-time charges.

Finance Curriculum

GEU's published MBA curriculum includes financial statement analysis and reporting, corporate finance, financial analytics, behavioural finance and international financial management among its management/specialisation subjects. Exact semester allocation may change with the university syllabus.

Career After MBA Finance

Potential career paths include financial analysis, banking, financial services, corporate finance, investment-related business functions, consulting and fintech. Build Excel, financial modelling, accounting and analytical skills alongside the MBA to improve role readiness.

Finance Placement Signals

GEU's current MBA placement records list finance students with employers including BNY Mellon, Ameriprise Financial, Crescendo and Tech Mahindra. These are individual listed offers, not a guaranteed salary for all Finance students.

MBA Finance FAQs

Is Finance available as an MBA specialisation?

Yes. GEU's official MBA fee page lists Finance among its MBA specialisations.

What jobs can I target after MBA Finance?

Finance, banking, financial services, consulting and fintech-related roles are relevant pathways. Employer requirements vary, so technical finance skills and internships matter.

What is the fee for MBA Finance?

It follows the published general MBA fee structure and varies by the applicable scholarship/merit slab.
