Which entrance exams are accepted?

There is no single exam accepted by every Mumbai college. CAT is relevant to several programmes; NMIMS has a programme-specific NMAT route; other schools may accept XAT, GMAT or additional tests. The accepted exams and application forms can differ between flagship, executive and specialised programmes.

  • Match each exam to the exact programme and intake.
  • Check whether the institute requires a separate application after the exam.
  • Verify the interview/essay/profile stages.
  • Do not treat a minimum cutoff as a guaranteed interview call.

How should you compare fees and placements?

Calculate the full cost: tuition, hostel or rent, food, travel, deposits, laptop, books and loan interest. Mumbai living costs can change the total budget materially.

For placements, compare the same graduating batch and the same metric. Median salary is usually more representative than highest CTC; also review job functions, cohort size and the number of students who sought placement. Do not compare one programme's average with another's highest package.

How can you make a shortlist?

  1. Pick target roles such as finance, consulting, marketing, operations or technology management.
  2. Map your scores to published admission requirements.
  3. Calculate total cost, including living expenses.
  4. Read the latest official placement report for the exact programme.
  5. Check campus location and commute/housing costs.

Use individual college pages for programme-level details; if the institute has not published a figure, treat it as unavailable rather than guessing.
