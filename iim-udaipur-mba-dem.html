IIM Udaipur DEM: Programme Overview

IIM Udaipur's One-Year MBA in Digital Enterprise Management is a full-time residential programme designed for experienced professionals who want to build managerial capability for digital-first organisations. The programme combines management fundamentals with digital strategy, analytics, technology and hands-on business problem solving.

Digital leadership

Leadership, strategy, innovation, decision-making and entrepreneurship in digital environments.

Analytics & technology

Advanced analytics, AI, cloud, blockchain, IoT and data-driven decision-making.

Real business problems

Industry projects, labs, case studies, simulations and prototypes connect theory with application.

IIM Udaipur DEM Admission Process

  1. Submit the online application with the accepted test score and academic/work-experience documents.
  2. Eligible applicants are considered for shortlisting.
  3. Shortlisted applicants appear for a Personal Interview.
  4. Offers are made after comprehensive assessment of the candidate profile and selection performance.

2026–27 cycle

The published cycle ran through four application rounds, with the final application closing on January 31, 2026 and programme commencement scheduled for April 7, 2026.

IIM Udaipur DEM Curriculum

The current curriculum is organised over four terms and is built around management fundamentals, digital enterprise, data analytics and technology management, digital industry practices, and solving business problems through projects.

Term 1: Management Fundamentals

Financial Reporting and Analysis & Cost Management, Business Statistics, Economics for Managers, Marketing Management, Organisational Behaviour, Operations Management, Agile & DevOps, Corporate Finance, Operations Research-I, Managerial Oral Communication and Emerging Technologies for Business workshop.

Term 2: Industry Practices & Transformation

Design Thinking, Strategic Management, Advanced Analytics-I, Digital Product Management, Digital Marketing, IoT, Artificial Intelligence and Future of Work, and Emerging Technologies workshop.

Term 3: Advanced Business, Data Analytics & Projects

Advanced Analytics-II, Multi-sided Platform Business Strategy, Digital Transformation Management, Project Management, Written Managerial Communication, Industry Projects, Building a Data-driven Organization, Data Security and Privacy for Business Managers, Cloud Technology & Security, and AI and Sustainability.

Term 4: Electives, Projects & Simulation

Capstone Enterprise Simulations, Industry Projects, Entrepreneurship and programme-specific electives.

How DEM Learning Works

DEM is not structured as a conventional lecture-only MBA. The learning model includes case studies, simulations, workshops, lab projects, individual and group projects, interaction with practitioners and industry-assigned business challenges. Participants can develop practical solutions demonstrated through demos, prototypes or wireframes.

Case-based

Connect concepts to business decisions.

Project-led

Solve real digital business problems.

Technology-aware

Work with emerging technologies and analytics.

DEM FAQs

Is DEM a one-year MBA?

Yes. It is a full-time residential one-year MBA programme.

Is CAT accepted?

Yes. The current admissions cycle accepts CAT tests taken in 2021 or later, along with eligible GMAT and GRE scores.

Does DEM require 3 years of work experience?

Yes. The current eligibility requirement is at least 36 months of full-time remunerative work experience after graduation as of February 28, 2026.

Does DEM have an internship?

The one-year DEM format is built around intensive coursework, industry projects and experiential learning rather than the compulsory summer internship used in the Two-Year MBA.
