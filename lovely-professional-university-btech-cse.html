LPU B.Tech CSE: Programme Overview

LPU's B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering is a four-year, eight-semester engineering programme. The current programme structure combines foundation and core courses with engineering/open minors, electives, industry interface, study-abroad opportunities, placement pathways and project-based learning.

Quick student verdict

LPU B.Tech CSE is worth considering if you want a large private-university campus, a broad technology curriculum, industry exposure and multiple opportunities for projects, certifications and competitions. Your final decision should depend on the fee after scholarship, coding culture, internship quality and realistic placement outcomes.

Key strengths to explore

  • Four-year CSE degree.
  • Foundation plus core computing curriculum.
  • Minors, electives and specialisation choices.
  • Industry projects, hackathons and certifications.
  • Large campus and recruiter ecosystem.

What you should not assume

  • Every student receives the highest package.
  • A CSE degree alone guarantees a software job.
  • All specialisations have identical fees or placement outcomes.
  • Scholarship continues automatically without meeting renewal rules.

LPU B.Tech CSE Eligibility 2026

The current official CSE page states eligibility as 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics and English or an equivalent qualification, subject to the applicable admission route. What should a CSE student focus on?

Build depth in data structures and algorithms, one programming language, SQL, Git, software development, cloud or AI fundamentals and 2–3 strong projects. Do not rely only on your semester marks.

B.Tech CSE Internships & Projects

For a software career, internships and projects are where students convert the degree into employability. LPU highlights live projects, hackathons, workshops, certifications, industry exposure and training opportunities.

Build before final year

  • DSA problem-solving profile.
  • Two substantial software projects.
  • One internship or industry project.
  • GitHub portfolio and technical resume.
  • Strong communication and interview skills.

Avoid these mistakes

  • Collecting certificates without projects.
  • Learning five languages without depth.
  • Waiting until final year to start DSA.
  • Using copied projects in interviews.

LPU B.Tech CSE Placements: Student-Friendly View

Is LPU B.Tech CSE Worth It?

The ROI depends on your net fee after scholarship and the role you secure. A student who uses the four years to build coding depth, internships and a strong portfolio can extract far more value than a student who relies only on classroom teaching.

Good fit if

  • You want a large campus with many activities.
  • You are ready to build your own coding profile.
  • You can access a meaningful scholarship.
  • You want multiple technology pathways.

Compare carefully if

  • You have a stronger CSE offer elsewhere at similar cost.
  • You expect the highest package to be typical.
  • You prefer a very small, specialised college environment.

LPU B.Tech CSE FAQs

What is the duration of B.Tech CSE at LPU?

Four years divided into eight semesters.

What is the LPU B.Tech CSE fee?

The published 2026 programme fee is ₹1.60 lakh per semester, before scholarship and additional charges.

Does LPU accept JEE Main for B.Tech CSE?

The current CSE programme page lists LPUNEST, JEE Main and CUET as applicable admission routes.

Is LPU good for software placements?

It has a large recruiter ecosystem and many technology recruiters, but individual outcomes depend strongly on coding skills, projects, eligibility and interview performance.

Is hostel compulsory?

Hostel is an additional residential option; the university publishes separate room and mess charges.
