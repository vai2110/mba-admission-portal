Eligibility Criteria

IIFT’s official admissions page states that MBA (IB) is a two-year full-time residential programme and that eligibility is a bachelor’s degree of at least three years in any discipline with 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST/PwD.

What applicants should verify before submitting the form

  • Qualifying degree: confirm the exact degree/discipline requirement for the programme.
  • Minimum marks: use the category-specific percentage or CGPA stated in the current official notice.
  • Entrance score: confirm the accepted test and the relevant examination year.
  • Final-year status: check the deadline for submitting proof of graduation.
  • Work experience: do not assume it is mandatory unless the programme notice says so.

Admission Process: Step-by-Step

The 2026–28 MBA(IB) and MBA(BA) admission portal is the institute’s official application route. Applicants should follow the current prospectus, admission calendar and programme-specific guidelines rather than older IIFT entrance-exam articles.

Application to offer

  1. Read the current programme-specific admission notice.
  2. Check eligibility before paying the non-refundable application fee.
  3. Enter academic, test-score and work-experience information exactly as supported by documents.
  4. Upload certificates and scorecards required by the institute.
  5. Complete the institute’s shortlist, PI, WAT, written test or other notified selection stage.
  6. After an offer, compare the total payable cost and joining deadline before accepting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main admission route for IIFT?

What is the minimum eligibility for the flagship management programme?

Is the published cutoff a guaranteed admission percentile?

No. A screening threshold only establishes eligibility for consideration. Final conversion depends on the institute’s complete selection methodology and competition.

How should I calculate the actual cost of the programme?

Add every mandatory academic and residential charge, then add mess/boarding, insurance, deposits and other recurring charges listed by the institute. Do not treat refundable deposits as permanent academic fees.

Which placement number should I use when comparing colleges?

Prefer median CTC where available, then average CTC and role/sector distribution. Highest CTC should be treated as an exceptional outcome rather than the expected salary.
