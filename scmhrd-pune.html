SCMHRD Pune: College Overview

Symbiosis Centre for Management and Human Resource Development (SCMHRD) is a management institute of Symbiosis International (Deemed University), located at the Symbiosis Infotech Campus in Hinjawadi, Pune. The institute was established in 1993 and offers MBA programmes across Human Resources, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Infrastructure Development & Management and Business Analytics.

SCMHRD describes itself as AACSB accredited and focuses on classroom learning combined with guest lectures, industry interaction and student-led activities. Its official placement material also reports a strong recruiter base across consulting, financial services, IT/ITES, manufacturing, healthcare, power and other sectors.

Is SCMHRD Pune worth considering?

It is worth considering if you are targeting a SNAP-based MBA in Pune and are comfortable with the institute's residential cost. The strongest fit depends on your preferred specialization, SNAP performance, total budget and the placement roles you want.

Eligibility

For the MBA, the institute states that a candidate should be a graduate from a recognised university or institution of national importance with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. For SC/ST candidates, the stated minimum is 45%. Final-year graduation students can also apply, subject to obtaining the required marks.

Indian applicants must appear for SNAP for admission to SCMHRD's MBA programmes.

SCMHRD Pune Fees, Hostel & Scholarships

For the MBA 2026-28 batch, SCMHRD's official fee schedule lists academic fees of ₹6,45,500 per annum. The published first-year schedule also includes refundable institute and hostel deposits, hostel and mess charges.

Component2026-28 published amount
Academic fee₹6,45,500 per annum
Institute deposit₹20,000 refundable
Hostel deposit₹20,000 refundable
Hostel, three sharing₹1,12,500 for the published first-year schedule
Mess fee₹59,800 per listed installment; subsequent-year charges are communicated later
Published first-year total₹8,57,800

The institute states that hostel and mess fees can increase in subsequent years and that government taxes may be additional. Students should therefore use the published installment schedule rather than assuming the first-year total is the final two-year residential cost.

Scholarships

SCMHRD and Symbiosis provide scholarship/financial-assistance provisions subject to the applicable rules and eligibility. The current programme page should be checked for the scholarship category and application process relevant to your batch.

Who Should Apply to SCMHRD Pune?

Good fit if
  • You are targeting a SNAP-based MBA.
  • You want to study in Pune/Hinjawadi.
  • You are interested in HR, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Infrastructure or Business Analytics.
  • You are comfortable with the residential fee structure.
Check alternatives if
  • Your SNAP percentile is materially below the current expected shortlist range.
  • Your primary goal is the lowest possible MBA cost.
  • You need a programme whose admission route is not SNAP-based.
  • You are comparing a specific specialisation and another college has stronger programme-level outcomes for your target role.
Decision rule

Don't decide only from the 98 percentile estimate or highest package. Compare your SNAP score, programme preference, total residential cost, relevant placement outcomes and alternative calls.

SCMHRD Pune FAQs

What is the SNAP cutoff for SCMHRD Pune?

The portal uses 98 percentile as an expected/indicative threshold. SCMHRD's official programme page requires a valid SNAP score but does not publish a fixed percentile cutoff.

What is the SCMHRD Pune MBA fee?

The official 2026-28 schedule lists ₹6,45,500 academic fee per year. Hostel, mess and refundable deposits are additional.

Does SCMHRD accept CAT for its MBA?

The flagship MBA admission route stated by SCMHRD is SNAP. Applicants must have a valid SNAP score.

What is the SCMHRD MBA admission process?

Register for SNAP, select SCMHRD programmes, complete the institute application, appear for GEPI if shortlisted and then proceed through the final merit process.

What is the latest highest package at SCMHRD?

SCMHRD's current placement page reports a highest CTC of ₹35.90 lakh.

Does SCMHRD provide hostel accommodation?

Yes. Hostel charges are included in the published MBA fee schedule, with a three-sharing first-year hostel fee listed separately from academic fees.

What is the MBA intake at SCMHRD?

The official MBA programme page states an intake of 180 for the MBA in Finance, Marketing, HR and Operations.

When does the SCMHRD MBA 2027-29 programme start?

The current official admission schedule lists programme commencement as 3 June 2027.
