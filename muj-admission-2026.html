MUJ Admission 2026: Start With the Correct Route

Manipal University Jaipur does not use one universal admission test for every programme. B.Tech CSE and other engineering programmes have MET 2026 and listed merit-based pathways, while MBA uses national management aptitude tests and programme-specific selection. Other UG and PG courses have their own qualifying-exam criteria.

B.Tech Admission 2026: MET vs Merit-Based Admission

For MET 2026, the official eligibility requires Physics, Mathematics and English as compulsory subjects plus one of the listed optional subjects such as Chemistry, Computer Science, Biotechnology, Biology, Statistics or Engineering Drawing. The combined score in Physics, Mathematics and the optional subject must be at least 50%.

MUJ also lists merit-based admission. For CSE, the page specifies either at least 70% aggregate in PMX subjects in Class XII, or SAT score of at least 75% / JEE Main score of at least 90 percentile together with at least 60% PMX, subject to the stated conditions. Admission remains subject to merit and seat availability.

Important

A 70% PMX or 90 percentile JEE Main criterion is a stated eligibility route, not a guarantee of a particular branch. MUJ explicitly says branch allocation under merit admission is based on merit and availability.

MBA Admission 2026: Eligibility Is Only the First Filter

General MBA applicants need a bachelor's degree with at least 50% and a valid CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT or GMAT score. MUJ states that the merit list considers Class X, XII and graduation academics, entrance-test performance, personal interview, presentation and English comprehension.

MBA Business Analytics requires graduation with at least 50%; MUJ says mathematics at 10+2 and a background in engineering, science, computer applications or economics is preferred. Selection is based on career and interview with equal weightage.

Documents and Application Checks

Applicants should keep their qualifying examination marksheets, identity documents, photographs, entrance-test score details and programme-specific certificates ready. Candidates still pursuing graduation for MBA are required to submit evidence of passing by October 31, 2026 according to the published eligibility table.

  • Verify the exact programme and admission route before paying the application fee.
  • Enter marks exactly as shown in official academic documents.
  • Keep entrance scorecards accessible for MBA applications.
  • Check category, scholarship and special-quota documents separately where applicable.
  • Do not assume hostel booking is included in academic admission; hostel is a separate fee process.

Admission Fee Checkpoints

Programme pages currently publish different one-time charges. For example, B.Tech CSE lists ₹10,000 registration and ₹15,000 refundable caution alongside ₹4.87 lakh annual tuition; general MBA lists ₹10,000 registration and ₹15,000 caution alongside ₹5.10 lakh annual tuition.

MUJ's central fee page says tuition, registration and caution amounts are subject to change and Fee Fixation Committee approval. Therefore, the amount shown on the current programme page should be checked again at the payment stage.

MUJ Admission FAQs

Is JEE Main accepted for MUJ B.Tech CSE?

Yes. MUJ lists a merit-based CSE route using JEE Main 90 percentile together with at least 60% PMX in Class XII, subject to the listed conditions, merit and seat availability.

What entrance exams are accepted for MUJ MBA?

The general MBA page lists CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT and GMAT as valid aptitude tests.

Can a final-year graduate apply for MBA?

Yes, the eligibility table allows candidates who are pursuing graduation, provided evidence of passing is submitted by October 31, 2026.

What is the official admission contact?

MUJ publishes 18001020128 and admissions@jaipur.manipal.edu for admission-related queries. WhatsApp support is also published on the university site.
