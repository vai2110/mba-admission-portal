What is IIM Ahmedabad MBA-FABM?

The Post Graduate Programme in Food and Agri-business Management (PGP-FABM) is a two-year, full-time residential MBA-level programme leading to a Master of Business Administration (Food and Agri-business Management) degree. IIMA describes it as a sector-specific management programme for food, agriculture, agribusiness, rural, developmental and allied sectors.

The important point for applicants is that FABM combines a general management foundation with sector-specific learning. It is therefore broader than a purely agricultural degree, but more sector-focused than the regular PGP.

Student terminology: “IIM Ahmedabad Agri MBA”, “IIM Ahmedabad FABM”, “IIM Ahmedabad Food & Agri Business MBA” and “PGP-FABM” generally refer to this programme. The official programme name is PGP-FABM.

Eligibility: Do You Need an Agriculture Degree?

No, an agriculture degree is not mandatory. IIMA's domestic FABM eligibility allows candidates from agriculture-related as well as non-agricultural disciplines, subject to the applicable academic conditions.

Agriculture / Related Background

A Bachelor's or Master's degree in agricultural sciences or an agriculture-related discipline with the prescribed minimum marks/equivalent CGPA.

Non-Agricultural Background

A recognised Bachelor's or Master's degree in a non-agricultural discipline can also be considered. IIMA requires a strong interest in agriculture, food, rural and allied sectors.

Final-Year Students

Final-year candidates can apply subject to IIMA's conditions for completing the degree and submitting the required documentation by the specified deadlines.

Important: Eligibility is not the same as selection. Meeting the academic requirement does not guarantee a CAT shortlist, AWT/PI call or final admission.

MBA-FABM Admission & Selection Process

Step 1: CAT 2026 and preliminary screening

Domestic applicants need a valid CAT 2026 score and must meet the applicable academic and programme requirements. IIMA then applies its published preliminary screening methodology.

Step 2: AWT and Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are called for the Analytical Writing Test (AWT) and Personal Interview (PI).

MBA-FABM Curriculum: What Will You Study?

The programme starts with management fundamentals common to the broader IIMA management environment and then moves into food, agriculture and agribusiness-specific learning. IIMA describes the second year as a combination of sector-specific compulsory and elective courses.

How Is FABM Taught?

  • Case method: business concepts are applied to real managerial situations.
  • Group work: students work with peers from different educational and professional backgrounds.
  • Guest lectures and seminars: used to connect classroom concepts with industry practice.
  • Industrial visits and field exposure: particularly relevant to understanding sector value chains.
  • Rural Immersion: a mandatory academic component for FABM students.

The programme is designed around the realities of agribusiness such as seasonality, perishability, rural markets, policy, supply chains and uncertainty. That sector context is a major part of what differentiates FABM from a generalist MBA.

Rural Immersion & Summer Internship

Rural Immersion Module (RIM)

IIMA states that Rural Immersion is a mandatory academic requirement. Students spend time in a village environment to understand rural economic and social realities rather than studying the rural market only through classroom cases.

Summer internship

After the first year, students undertake an 8–9 week summer internship. IIMA lists exposure across FMCG, agricultural inputs, agri-banking, agri-consulting, renewable energy and food/agribusiness advisory, with functions such as strategy, marketing, commodity trading, sales and distribution, supply chain, procurement and research/advisory.

Internships can also lead to pre-placement offers when students meet the employer's requirements, but a PPO is not automatic.

International Exposure and Student Exchange

IIMA lists exchange collaborations involving institutions such as ESSEC, UBC, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and NHH Norwegian School of Economics. The exchange opportunity and participating institutions can depend on the applicable academic year and selection/availability conditions.

This is different from saying that every FABM student automatically gets an international exchange. Students should check the current programme rules for eligibility, selection and costs.

What is included?

The ₹27.50 lakh amount is the latest published total programme-fee benchmark, including the stated tuition component and institute/programme charges. The future-batch line-item breakup should be checked when IIMA publishes it.

What is extra?

IIMA identifies laptop/personal computer, personal expenses on boarding, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry as additional expenses.

Overall budget

Planning estimate: ~₹30–32 lakh for the two-year programme. This is a budgeting estimate, not an official IIMA cost-of-attendance figure.

Scholarships

IIMA has a need-based scholarship mechanism for eligible students. Financial-aid decisions consider factors such as family income, assets and number of dependants under the applicable scholarship rules.

Fee alert: ₹27.50 lakh should be treated as the latest published benchmark, not the confirmed 2027–29 payable fee.

Who Should Consider MBA-FABM?

Agriculture / Food Background

Graduates from agriculture, food and related disciplines who want formal management training.

Career Switchers

Non-agriculture graduates with a genuine interest in food, agriculture, rural and allied sectors can also apply.

Agribusiness Career Goals

Candidates interested in marketing, consulting, supply chain, finance, trading, strategy or entrepreneurship within the sector ecosystem.

When FABM May Not Match Your Career Goal

  • If you want a completely generalist MBA and do not want sector-specific food/agri-business coursework.
  • If you are choosing the programme only because of the IIMA brand but have no interest in its sector context.
  • If your intended career path is unrelated to the programme and you do not value rural, food or agribusiness exposure.
Student perspective: Treat FABM as a career-and-curriculum choice, not simply as another name for the regular PGP.

FABM FAQs Students Usually Ask

Is agriculture background compulsory for FABM?

No. Non-agriculture graduates can also be eligible if they meet the academic requirements and the programme's interest/selection conditions.

Is FABM a full MBA degree?

Yes. IIMA describes PGP-FABM as an MBA-level two-year programme leading to a Master of Business Administration (Food and Agri-business Management) degree.

What is the CAT cutoff for FABM?

IIMA publishes minimum overall and sectional screening cutoffs that vary by category and Academic Category. See the CAT cutoff table on this page.

Does meeting the CAT cutoff guarantee an interview?

No. The published minimum cutoff is a screening threshold. Shortlisting and final selection use additional profile and selection components.

Is rural immersion compulsory?

Yes. IIMA identifies the Rural Immersion Module as a mandatory academic requirement for PGP-FABM.

How long is the FABM summer internship?

IIMA's programme information describes an 8–9 week summer internship after the first year.

What kind of jobs can I get after FABM?

Roles can span marketing, sales and distribution, strategy, consulting, supply chain, procurement, commodity trading, agricultural finance and research/advisory across food, agriculture and allied sectors.

What is the latest published FABM fee?

The latest published benchmark used here is ₹27.50 lakh for the 2025–27 batch. A confirmed 2027–29 fee should be checked when IIMA publishes the applicable fee schedule.

Is FABM easier than the regular PGP?

The published information describes FABM as a rigorous two-year IIMA management programme with a sector-specific curriculum. Applicants should compare the programme content and career goals rather than assume one is easier.
