Programme Overview

IIM Ahmedabad's Post Graduate Programme in Food and Agri-business Management (PGP-FABM) is a two-year, full-time residential programme focused on management careers in food, agriculture, agribusiness, rural and allied sectors.

IIMA describes the programme as an MBA-level programme leading to the degree of Master of Business Administration (Food and Agri-business Management). Its management foundation is combined with sector-specific learning.

What makes FABM different from the regular MBA?
The programme is not simply a general MBA with an agriculture elective. Its academic design combines management fundamentals with food, agriculture and agribusiness-focused courses, rural immersion and sector-oriented electives.

Eligibility for MBA-FABM 2026–28

IIMA provides two broad academic routes for domestic candidates.

Agriculture / Related Background

A Bachelor's or Master's degree in agricultural sciences or agriculture-related disciplines with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA.

The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.

Non-Agricultural Background

A Bachelor's or Master's degree or equivalent qualification in a non-agricultural discipline with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA.

The candidate must also have a strong interest in agriculture, food, rural and allied sectors.

Final-Year Candidates

Candidates completing their degree can be considered subject to IIMA's specified conditions and deadlines for completion and submission of degree requirements.

Important: The academic classification and final interpretation of eligibility rests with IIMA's Admissions Committee.

Admission & Selection Process

Step 1: CAT 2025 + Preliminary Screening

Candidates with a valid CAT 2025 score who applied to PGP-FABM and satisfy the programme's eligibility criteria are considered for the first-stage screening.

Step 2: AWT & Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are called for the Analytical Writing Test (AWT) and Personal Interview (PI).

Final Selection Weightage

Component Weightage
Analytical Writing Test (AWT) 10%
Personal Interview (PI) 40%
Composite Score 50%

IIMA states that the PI score can consider interview performance along with factors such as verifiable awards and recognitions, academic performance, exceptional achievements, extracurricular activities, relevant post-degree work experience and post-graduation education.

Key takeaway: FABM admission is not a CAT-percentile-only process. Academic profile, CAT performance, AWT, PI and the broader application profile all matter.

IIMA states that personal expenses such as boarding-related personal expenses, laptop, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry are not covered by the programme fee.

Scholarships

IIMA has a Need-Based Scholarship Scheme for eligible students who require financial assistance. Scholarship decisions consider factors including annual gross family income, assets and number of dependents.

Curriculum & Learning Experience

FABM starts with a strong management foundation and then moves deeper into food and agribusiness-specific learning. The first year includes core management courses, while the second year adds sector-specific compulsory and elective courses.

Management Foundation

Core management learning provides the foundation in areas such as quantitative methods, finance, marketing, operations, strategy and organisational dynamics.

Sector Specialisation

The programme adds food, agriculture, rural and agribusiness-oriented courses designed around the sector's market, policy and operating environment.

Rural Immersion

IIMA states that Rural Immersion is a mandatory academic component for PGP-FABM students.

Summer Internship

Students undertake an 8–9 week summer internship following the first year, providing practical organisational and managerial exposure.

Case-Based Learning

IIMA uses the case method, supplemented by guest lectures, seminars, role plays, industrial visits and group exercises.

International Exposure

IIMA lists exchange collaborations involving institutions including ESSEC, UBC, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and NHH.

Career & Placement Scope

FABM is designed around management careers in food, agriculture, agribusiness, rural and allied sectors. IIMA's career and placement information lists opportunities across a wider range of sectors and functions.

Sector Exposure

FMCG, agricultural inputs, agri-banking, agri-consulting, renewable energy, food and agribusiness advisory and related sectors.

Functions

Strategy, marketing, commodity trading, sales and distribution, supply chain, procurement and research & advisory are among the functions listed by IIMA.

Summer Placements

IIMA reported that the entire 46-student PGP-FABM 2025–27 batch received summer internship offers during the 2025–26 summer placement process.

Placement interpretation: FABM should not be evaluated only by comparing its outcomes with the flagship PGP. Its value is particularly relevant for candidates who want a management career connected to food, agriculture and allied value chains.

Who Should Apply for MBA-FABM?

Agriculture / Food Background

A strong fit if your academic background already connects with agriculture, food, agribusiness or related sectors and you want formal management training.

Career Switchers with Sector Interest

Non-agriculture graduates can also be eligible, but IIMA specifically requires a strong interest in agriculture, food, rural and allied sectors.

Future Agribusiness Leaders

Useful for candidates targeting managerial, consulting, marketing, supply chain, finance, trading or strategy roles within relevant sector ecosystems.

Who Should Think Twice Before Choosing FABM?

  • Candidates who want a completely generalist MBA with no particular interest in food, agriculture or allied sectors.
  • Candidates choosing FABM only because they believe it is an easier route into IIMA than the flagship PGP.
  • Candidates whose career target is unrelated to the programme's sector orientation and who do not value agribusiness-focused learning.
Realistic view: FABM carries the IIMA management environment, but it is still a specialised programme. The programme choice should therefore be driven by career fit, not only by the IIM Ahmedabad brand.

FAQs

Is MBA-FABM the same as IIM Ahmedabad's regular MBA?
No. FABM is a separate two-year MBA-level programme specialised in food and agribusiness management.
Can a non-agriculture graduate apply?
Yes. IIMA's 2026–28 eligibility allows candidates from non-agricultural disciplines if they meet the academic requirement and demonstrate a strong interest in agriculture, food, rural and allied sectors.
Is CAT required for domestic candidates?
The 2026–28 domestic admission process uses a valid CAT 2025 score for the preliminary screening stage.
What is the MBA-FABM fee for 2026–28?
IIMA's current domestic admission page lists ₹27.50 lakh for the 2025–27 batch and states that future-batch fees will be announced later. The confirmed 2026–28 fee should therefore be checked against IIMA's latest official notice.
Does FABM include rural exposure?
Yes. IIMA states that the Rural Immersion Module is a mandatory academic requirement for PGP-FABM students.
What kind of careers can FABM lead to?
IIMA lists opportunities across food and agribusiness, FMCG, agricultural inputs, agri-banking, consulting, renewable energy and advisory, with functions such as strategy, marketing, commodity trading, supply chain, procurement and research.
