Panjab University Management courses

Management is only one part of PU, but it has multiple programmes through UBS and UIAMS. These include regular MBA, MBA International Business, MBA Human Resource, MBA Entrepreneurship, MBA Business Data Analytics and sectoral MBA options.

Science, Pharmacy and interdisciplinary programmes

PU's science ecosystem spans traditional sciences, applied sciences and professional programmes. The university also runs B.Pharmacy and M.Pharmacy pathways. For the 2026 cycle, PU-CET (UG) specifically covers B.Sc (Hons) and B.Pharmacy admissions.

Students comparing science programmes should examine the exact department syllabus, laboratory infrastructure, research opportunities, higher-study pathways and placement/higher-study data rather than relying on university-level averages.

Distance and online courses

The Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), formerly associated with the University School of Open Learning, offers programmes through a separate admission and regulatory framework. The current CDOE portal publishes its own notices, programme approvals and admission information.

Students should verify recognition, programme mode, duration, fee and examination requirements on the current CDOE portal before enrolling.

Panjab University Courses FAQs

What are the most popular courses at Panjab University?

PU has no single official “most popular courses” list. Students commonly compare programmes in engineering, management, law, pharmacy, science, commerce and arts. The right choice depends on the student's career goal, eligibility, admission route, fee and programme-level outcomes.

How many courses are there in Panjab University?

PU has a large and changing programme portfolio spread across 74 teaching and research departments, centres and affiliated institutions. The exact course count can change each admission cycle, so the current UG and PG portals are the reliable source for the active 2026-27 list.

Does Panjab University offer B.Tech or B.E.?

Yes. Engineering and technology programmes are offered through relevant PU institutes, including UIET. Admission is not handled by the general UG process alone; students should follow the current engineering institute and counselling instructions for their programme.

Does Panjab University offer B.Pharm?

Yes. B.Pharmacy is included in the PU-CET (UG) admission route. Students should follow the current PU-CET notification and the subsequent university admission/merit process for eligibility, documents and seat allocation.

Does Panjab University offer LLB after graduation?

Yes. PU offers three-year LLB options along with integrated law programmes. The exact admission route and centre can differ, so applicants should check the current law admission notice before applying.

Does Panjab University offer MBA?

Yes. Management programmes are offered through UBS and UIAMS. The portfolio includes regular MBA and specialised options such as International Business, Human Resource, Entrepreneurship and Business Data Analytics. Each programme can have a different entrance and selection process.

Which Panjab University course is best for placement?

Placement should be compared at programme level. For example, official UIET data reports ₹9.11 LPA average CTC in 2023–24, while NIRF reports a ₹12.42 LPA median for the UBS management cohort graduating in 2022–23. These are different programmes and reporting methods, so they should not be treated as a university-wide ranking.

Can I study Panjab University courses through distance or online mode?

Yes. The Centre for Distance and Online Education offers programmes through a separate admission framework. Before enrolling, check the current programme list, mode, duration, fee, recognition/approval and examination rules on the CDOE's own admission notice.
