BBA at BBAU

BBAU's Management Studies page lists BBA as a three-year programme with intake of 90 and tuition of ₹25,000 per semester. Eligibility is Intermediate from a recognised institution with 50% marks, with 45% for SC/ST/PH candidates as listed.

B.Com (Hons.) at BBAU

The university programme catalogue lists B.Com (Hons.) as a three-year programme with intake of 90 and semester fee of ₹15,000. Eligibility is Intermediate with 50% marks, with 45% for SC/ST/PH candidates as listed.

Choose B.Com ifYou prefer accounting, commerce, taxation, finance and a broader commerce curriculum.
