Both are three-year undergraduate degrees, but they solve different career problems. BBA is more naturally aligned with students who want a management-oriented foundation; B.Com (Hons.) is more suitable for students who want deeper exposure to commerce, accounting, finance and related areas.

BBA at BBAU: What You Actually Study

BBAU lists BBA as a three-year programme with 90 seats and a published tuition fee of ₹25,000 per semester. The degree is designed around business and management fundamentals, so students can expect subjects and learning activities related to management principles, business environment, marketing, finance, human resources, operations, entrepreneurship and quantitative/business skills.

Who is BBA better suited to?

BBA is a sensible starting point for students who already see themselves moving toward management, marketing, consulting, entrepreneurship, sales/operations or an MBA later. It is also useful for students who want to understand how different business functions fit together before specialising at postgraduate level.

B.Com (Hons.) at BBAU: What You Actually Study

BBAU lists B.Com (Hons.) as a three-year programme with 90 seats and ₹15,000 semester tuition. The commerce route is a stronger fit for students interested in accounting, financial reporting, taxation, banking, finance, business law and related commerce disciplines.

B.Com can also work well as a foundation for professional qualifications or postgraduate study. Students considering CA, CMA, CS, M.Com, MBA or finance-related roles should choose based on their long-term plan rather than assuming the cheaper programme is automatically better.

BBAU BBA & B.Com Eligibility 2026

The current programme listing requires Intermediate/Class 12 with 50% marks for both BBA and B.Com (Hons.), with 45% listed for SC/ST/PH candidates. The qualification must be from a recognised institution/board as specified by the university.

Don't confuse eligibility with selection

Meeting 50% makes you academically eligible; it does not by itself guarantee admission. The university's 2026 admission route, CUET score/merit process, category rules and seat availability determine selection.

BBAU BBA & B.Com Admission 2026

The 2026-27 UG admission cycle includes CUET-based admissions and university-side merit/counselling updates. Students should first complete the applicable CUET requirement, then track BBAU's registration and merit process for the chosen programme. Spot/vacant-seat rounds can be announced when seats remain.

BBAU BBA & B.Com Career Scope

A three-year degree is the beginning of a career path, not the career itself. BBA graduates can target entry-level roles in business operations, marketing, customer success, sales/operations and management support, while B.Com graduates commonly build toward accounting, finance, banking, taxation, audit support and business operations. The quality of the first role depends on skills, internships, communication and employer requirements.

Students who want stronger management or finance credentials can consider an MBA after graduation. B.Com students may also pursue professional qualifications such as CA/CMA/CS depending on their interests and eligibility.

BBA vs B.Com: Which Should You Choose?

Choose BBA if

  • You prefer management and business subjects.
  • You are considering an MBA later.
  • You are interested in marketing, operations or entrepreneurship.
  • You want a broad business foundation.

Choose B.Com if

  • You enjoy accounting, finance and commerce.
  • You may pursue CA/CMA/CS or M.Com.
  • You are considering banking, accounting or finance roles.
  • You want a commerce-focused curriculum.

The B.Com fee is currently ₹10,000 per semester lower than BBA in the published listings. That cost difference should be considered, but course fit and career plan are more important than a ₹10,000 semester difference.
