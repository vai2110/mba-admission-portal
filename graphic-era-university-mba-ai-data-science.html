About MBA AI & Data Science

GEU's MBA in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is a two-year management programme designed for students who want to combine business knowledge with advanced data and AI capabilities. The official curriculum includes programming, machine learning, data engineering, business intelligence and generative AI.

Who is it for?

Students interested in analytics, AI-enabled products, digital transformation, business intelligence and technology-led strategy. It is particularly relevant if you want technical exposure without leaving the management career track.

Eligibility

GEU lists the programme as a two-year postgraduate course. The university's MBA admission information requires a bachelor's degree of at least three years from a recognised university, with final-year students eligible to apply.

Admission Process

Admission follows the university's management admission workflow: online application, document submission, fee payment and applicable assessment/interview. Valid CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/NMAT scores may provide aptitude-test exemption under university policy.

Fees

The current public fee page for the general MBA uses merit/entrance percentile slabs. GEU's course page does not publish a separate fixed AI&DS fee in the programme description, so students should use the live fee schedule linked by the university before payment.

Do not assume the general MBA fee is automatically identical.

Confirm the 2026 AI&DS fee and applicable scholarship slab from GEU's live fee portal.

AI & DS MBA FAQs

Is this an MBA or a technical degree?

It is an MBA. The differentiator is the depth of AI and data coursework integrated into the management curriculum.

Does the course teach Python and machine learning?

Yes. The published curriculum includes Foundation of Python, statistics with Python, machine learning, big data analytics and machine-learning projects.

Can this lead to AI product roles?

GEU explicitly lists AI Product Manager and several analytics/digital-transformation roles among possible career pathways.
