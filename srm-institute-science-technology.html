What is SRMIST?

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) is a deemed university with its flagship campus at Kattankulathur in the Chennai region. Its academic portfolio covers engineering, management, medicine and health sciences, science and humanities, law, agriculture and doctoral studies.

The 2025-26 prospectus identifies six SRMIST campuses: Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Modinagar/Delhi-NCR, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai.

The important point for applicants

SRMIST does not have one common admission rule, fee or cutoff. The exact programme and campus decide what you need to apply, what you pay and which admission route you follow.

How Does SRMIST B.Tech Admission Work?

For regular B.Tech admission, SRMIST states a minimum 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects, with Physics and Mathematics mandatory and one prescribed third subject. The published age condition is 16 years 6 months on 31 July of the calendar year in which the Class 12 examination is held.

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal engineering entrance route. In 2026, SRMIST published three application phases. After the exam, counselling is important because programme and campus choice is tied to rank and available seats.

What Is the SRMIST B.Tech Cutoff?

SRMIST does not publish one consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the programme sources used for these pages. Therefore, an internet estimate should not be presented as an official CSE, AI, IT or ECE closing rank.

What should you use?

Use your SRMJEEE rank, the programme/campus options shown during counselling and seat availability. Also keep entrance rank, scholarship rank and counselling closing rank separate; they are not the same metric.

How Does SRMIST MBA Admission Work?

SRMIST lists a minimum 50% aggregate in a bachelor's degree for its MBA portfolio. The standard route is SRM Joint Entrance Examination for Management (SRMJEEM) followed by Personal Interview.

Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores receive direct consideration for the Personal Interview according to SRMIST's management admission information. Preference is given to candidates with work experience.

What Are SRMIST Placements Like?

The official 2023 university-level placement communication reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, 3,970 high-paying offers, maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum and average salary of ₹10.5 LPA.

For engineering, NIRF 2026 reports 4,144 placements among 4,408 four-year UG graduates in minimum stipulated time in 2024-25, with median salary ₹6.32 lakh. These figures have different scopes and must not be merged into one average-package claim.

Which Scholarships Are Available?

SRMIST's scholarship policy includes the Founder's Scholarship and SRM Merit Scholarship. B.Tech scholarship categories include specified SRMJEEE and JEE Main rank bands and other achievement categories. Renewal conditions apply.

JEE Main appearing in the scholarship policy does not make it the principal regular B.Tech admission route. For regular B.Tech admission, SRMJEEE remains the main route.

FAQs Students Ask About SRMIST

Is Kattankulathur the main SRMIST campus?

Yes. Kattankulathur is the flagship SRMIST campus in the Chennai region.

Does SRMIST have the same fee at every campus?

No. Published MBA fees differ across Kattankulathur, Ramapuram and Vadapalani.

Is SRMJEEE compulsory for every SRMIST course?

No. Admission is programme-specific. B.Tech uses SRMJEEE, MBA uses SRMJEEM/eligible national tests plus PI, and MBBS uses NEET-UG and counselling.

Can I use JEE Main rank as the regular B.Tech cutoff?

No. SRMIST uses SRMJEEE as the principal B.Tech route. JEE Main ranks appear in the scholarship framework.

Why are SRMIST placement figures different across sources?

The university brochure and NIRF engineering disclosure cover different populations and use different statistics such as average and median.

What should I verify before paying?

Verify the exact programme title, campus, eligibility, admission route, fee, intake and the latest relevant placement information.
