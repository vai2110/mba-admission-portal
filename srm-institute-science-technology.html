SRMIST Overview

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) is a deemed university with its flagship Kattankulathur campus in the Chennai region. It has six SRMIST campuses: Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Modinagar, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai.

This overview is for comparing the university, campuses and admission routes. Detailed programme information is kept on the dedicated course pages below.

SRMIST Admission 2026-27

B.Tech

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal B.Tech entrance route. The official 2026 FAQ listed application deadlines of April 16, June 4 and June 30, 2026 for its three phases.

MBA

SRMIST lists SRMJEEM followed by personal interview. Eligible CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT and TANCET scores can receive direct consideration for the PI.

MBBS

MBBS admission follows NEET-UG and the applicable medical counselling process.

SRMIST B.Tech Cutoff

SRMIST does not provide a single consolidated official branch-wise B.Tech closing-rank table in the programme sources used here. Therefore, no unofficial CSE, AI, IT or ECE rank is presented as an official cutoff.

What should students use instead?

SRMJEEE rank, programme preference and seat availability are used during counselling. Use the official counselling allotment rather than an unverified internet cutoff.

SRMIST Placements

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement figures reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, 3,970 high-paying offers, a maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum and an average salary of ₹10.5 LPA.

For engineering, NIRF 2026 data for 2024-25 reports 4,144 placements out of 4,408 four-year UG graduates, with a median salary of ₹6.32 lakh.

SRMIST Scholarships

SRMIST offers Founder’s Scholarship and SRM Merit Scholarship. B.Tech scholarship categories include specified SRMJEEE and JEE Main rank bands, along with other achievement categories. Renewal conditions apply.

SRMIST FAQs

Which SRMIST campus is the main campus?

Kattankulathur is the flagship SRMIST campus in the Chennai region.

Does SRMIST accept JEE Main for normal B.Tech admission?

SRMJEEE is the principal B.Tech admission route. JEE Main ranks also appear in SRMIST's scholarship criteria, which is a separate matter.

Does SRMIST publish official B.Tech closing ranks?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used for this guide.

Where can I compare SRMIST placement numbers?

Use the dedicated SRMIST placement page, which separates university-level figures from NIRF engineering data.
