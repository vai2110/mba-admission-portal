SRM Institute of Science and Technology: What Students Should Know

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) is a multidisciplinary deemed university based in the Chennai region of Tamil Nadu. Its main Kattankulathur campus is at SRM Nagar, Kattankulathur, Chengalpattu, while the university also operates campuses at Ramapuram and Vadapalani in Chennai, Modinagar in Ghaziabad, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai in Chengalpattu district.

The university's academic portfolio spans Engineering & Technology, Management, Medicine & Health Sciences, Science & Humanities, Law and Agricultural Sciences. The official 2025-26 prospectus describes a broad multi-disciplinary structure, while the current SRMIST website separately lists admissions across engineering, management, health sciences, science and humanities, law, agriculture, distance and online education.

Quick answer: Is SRMIST only an engineering university?

No. Engineering is one of its largest and most searched academic areas, but SRMIST also offers management, medicine and health sciences, science and humanities, law, agricultural sciences and other programmes. Students should therefore choose the campus and programme first rather than assuming every SRM course is offered at Kattankulathur.

SRMIST Campuses: How Many Campuses Does SRM Have?

SRM Institute of Science and Technology has 6 campuses. The current SRMIST campus listing and the university's UGC annual report identify Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Modinagar, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai. The university's wider ecosystem includes other SRM institutions, so students should not confuse the six SRMIST campuses with the broader SRM group footprint.

SRMIST Courses: UG, PG and Doctoral Programmes

SRMIST offers programmes across multiple academic faculties. For a student researching SRM University courses in Chennai, the most important distinction is between the programme family and the campus offering it.

Engineering & Technology

B.Tech programmes include Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Biotechnology, Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Automation and Robotics, Electrical and Electronics Engineering and several specialised CSE/ECE pathways. M.Tech and integrated options are also offered.

Management

SRMIST offers MBA programmes in Business Administration and specialised areas such as Business Analytics, Banking and Financial Services, Health Care and Hospital Management, Digital Marketing, Financial Services with NSE, Sports Management, Logistics and Supply Chain Management and Project Management.

Medicine & Health Sciences

Major programmes include MBBS, postgraduate medical programmes and super-speciality courses. The SRM Medical College Hospital & Research Centre is located at Kattankulathur.

Science, Humanities, Law & Agriculture

The university also offers B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, BCA, M.Sc., M.Com, MCA, law programmes, agricultural programmes, doctoral programmes and other specialised academic pathways.

SRMIST Admission 2026-27: Entrance Exams & Selection Process

B.Tech admission through SRMJEEE

For B.Tech programmes, SRMIST uses SRMJEEE (UG) as its principal entrance route. The official 2026 FAQ lists three application phases: Phase I application deadline 16 April 2026, Phase II 4 June 2026 and Phase III 30 June 2026. These are 2026 cycle dates and should not be presented as 2027 dates.

MBA admission

For MBA Business Administration and several management specialisations, SRMIST states that admission is through SRM Joint Entrance Examination for Management (SRMJEEM) followed by a Personal Interview. Candidates with eligible scores in CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT or TANCET can receive direct consideration for the Personal Interview, subject to SRMIST's criteria.

Medical admission

MBBS admission follows the NEET-UG and medical counselling framework. SRM Medical College Hospital & Research Centre's official 2026-27 information states that NEET-qualified candidates register for All India Quota counselling through the Medical Counselling Committee for the deemed medical college route.

Other programmes

Admission routes vary across science, humanities, law, agriculture, postgraduate and doctoral programmes. SRMIST's official Admission India page is the correct starting point because it separates programmes by faculty and admission mechanism.

2026-27 date note: The dates above are included only where the official SRMIST material currently provides them. Do not carry 2026 dates forward as confirmed 2027 deadlines.

SRMIST Fees: What Students Should Budget For

SRMIST fees are not a single university-wide amount. They vary substantially by programme, campus and specialisation. The current official management admission page, for example, lists an annual fee of ₹4.50 lakh for the Kattankulathur MBA Business Administration programme, ₹3.25 lakh at Ramapuram and ₹3 lakh at Vadapalani. It also lists ₹4.75 lakh for MBA Logistics and Supply Chain Management and ₹5.75 lakh for MBA Financial Services with NSE at Kattankulathur.

Additional costs: SRMIST's official management fee table notes a one-time first-year registration fee of ₹10,000 for programmes with annual fees of ₹1 lakh or above, with examination fees charged separately according to registered subjects. Hostel, transport and other student-service costs should be checked separately.

How should students compare SRMIST fees?

Compare the complete cost of attendance: tuition + registration/examination charges + hostel + food + transport where applicable. Then compare that total with the programme-specific placement outcomes, location and career target. A lower tuition figure at one campus does not automatically mean better ROI.

SRMIST Placements: Latest Official Placement Numbers

SRMIST publishes placement information at both university and programme/department levels. The official university brochure available for the 2024-25 academic year reports consolidated 2023 placement figures of 1,311 companies visiting, 13,069 offers, 3,970 high-paying offers, a maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum and an average salary of ₹10.5 LPA.

The 2026 NIRF engineering submission gives a more programme-specific lens: for four-year engineering UG students graduating in 2024-25, 4,144 students were placed and the median salary was ₹6.32 lakh. This difference is important: the university brochure's average/highest figures are consolidated placement figures, while NIRF reports programme-category median outcomes.

Placement interpretation: Do not use the ₹1 crore headline package as the expected salary for a typical SRMIST graduate. Students should compare median/average data, programme-specific placement data, recruiter mix and the number of students placed for the exact course they plan to join.

SRMIST Scholarships: What Merit Students Should Know

SRMIST's official scholarship policy includes Founder’s Scholarship, SRM Merit Scholarship and other programme-specific categories. For B.Tech, the Founder’s Scholarship criteria include top SRMJEEE ranks, top IIT JEE Main ranks, state/district toppers and eligible exemplary sports persons, subject to the policy's conditions.

For BBA at Kattankulathur, the current scholarship policy also provides CUET-based tuition fee waivers: 100% at 95 percentile and above, 75% at 90–94.99 percentile, 50% at 85–89.99 percentile and 25% at 80–84.99 percentile, subject to the stated terms. Other scholarships and renewal conditions apply across faculties.

SRMIST FAQs

How many campuses does SRM Institute of Science and Technology have?

SRMIST has six campuses: Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Modinagar/Delhi-NCR, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai. This is the SRMIST campus count and should not be confused with the wider SRM group of institutions.

Which is the main SRMIST campus?

Kattankulathur (KTR) is the flagship/main SRMIST campus. It is located in Chengalpattu district in the Chennai region of Tamil Nadu and hosts programmes across multiple faculties.

Which are the most popular courses at SRMIST?

B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering and related technology programmes are among the major student-search pathways. MBA, MBBS, MCA, ECE, Artificial Intelligence and Information Technology are also important programme categories. Popularity can vary by campus and admission year.

What entrance exam is required for B.Tech at SRMIST?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal entrance route for eligible B.Tech applicants. SRMIST's 2026 FAQ also states that JEE Main/SAT scores are considered for B.Tech international admission, subject to the applicable category.

What entrance exams are accepted for SRMIST MBA?

For MBA Business Administration and several management programmes, SRMIST states that admission is through SRMJEEM followed by a Personal Interview. Eligible scores in CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT or TANCET can receive direct consideration for the Personal Interview.

What is the SRMIST MBA fee?

The fee varies by campus and specialisation. The current official management admission page lists ₹4.50 lakh per year for MBA Business Administration at Kattankulathur, ₹3.25 lakh at Ramapuram and ₹3 lakh at Vadapalani. Specialised MBA programmes have different fees.

What is the latest official SRMIST placement package?

The official SRMIST university brochure available for 2024-25 reports 2023 consolidated placement figures of ₹10.5 LPA average salary and ₹1 crore maximum salary, alongside 1,311 companies visited and 13,069 offers. Students should also examine course-specific and NIRF median outcomes.

Is SRMIST in Chennai or outside Chennai?

SRMIST's flagship Kattankulathur campus is in Chengalpattu district in the Chennai region. SRMIST also has Ramapuram and Vadapalani campuses within Chennai, plus campuses in Modinagar/Ghaziabad, Tiruchirappalli and Baburayanpettai.

Is SRMIST the same as SRM University in other states?

Students should check the exact institution name. SRMIST is the deemed university based in Tamil Nadu with the six campuses described on this page. The wider SRM ecosystem also includes separately named institutions in other locations.
