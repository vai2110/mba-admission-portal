What is BIMTECH known for?

Birla Institute of Management Technology (BIMTECH) is a management institute in Greater Noida offering AICTE-approved, NBA-accredited PGDM programmes. Its PGDM programmes are recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

The current 2027-29 admission cycle lists PGDM, PGDM International Business, PGDM Insurance Business Management and PGDM Retail Management.

How does BIMTECH admission work for 2027-29?

  1. Apply through the official BIMTECH admission process.
  2. Submit eligible CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT 2025/2026-27 or CMAT 2027 scores; MAT 2026-27 is also accepted for IBM and Retail Management.
  3. Shortlisting considers entrance score and past academics.
  4. Shortlisted candidates appear for Extempore and Personal Interview.
  5. Final selection uses entrance score, academics, PI, work experience and other applicable parameters.

The official 2027-29 page says the final selection list is expected in March 2027 and the academic session is likely to begin in June/July 2027.

What are the important BIMTECH admission dates for 2027-29?

Officially confirmed: BIMTECH says the final selection list will be declared in March 2027, and the academic session is likely to start in June/July 2027. Final-year graduation candidates must submit their final result with at least 50% marks by 31 October 2027.

What is the BIMTECH cutoff?

BIMTECH does not publish one fixed CAT/XAT closing cutoff for every programme on its current admissions page. The institute shortlists using entrance scores and past academic record, followed by Extempore and PI.

For planning, the official admission page shows a merit scholarship at CAT/XAT 85+ and higher bands, but scholarship thresholds should not be treated as admission cutoffs.

What are BIMTECH placements like?

The official 2023-25 placement report records 98.04% overall placement across the four PGDM programmes. PGDM recorded 96.79%, while IB, IBM and Retail Management each recorded 100%.

The same report gives PGDM average CTC of ₹10.90 LPA, median ₹10.00 LPA and highest Indian CTC ₹22 LPA. Across the four programmes, the top 25 students had an average salary of ₹16.33 LPA.

See the dedicated BIMTECH Placements page for programme-wise placement data.

Is BIMTECH residential?

Yes. BIMTECH states that its programmes are residential and students are required to stay in the hostel for the full two-year programme. The campus is in Greater Noida in the NCR.

BIMTECH FAQs

Is BIMTECH equivalent to an MBA?

BIMTECH's PGDM programmes are recognised by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

Does BIMTECH accept CAT and XAT?

Yes. The 2027-29 admissions page accepts CAT 2026 and XAT 2027 for all four flagship PGDM programmes.

Does BIMTECH have direct admission?

No. BIMTECH explicitly states that it does not offer direct admissions and warns applicants against admission brokers or agents.

What is BIMTECH's highest package?

The institute currently highlights ₹22 LPA as the highest domestic package. The 2023-25 final placement report records ₹22 LPA for PGDM.
