How to Read Chitkara Fees

The university publishes programme-specific 2026 fee documents. Fees can include programme tuition and additional charges such as Educational Technology Services or Career Advancement Services for some programmes.

Important

Do not use one university-wide fee figure. The exact amount depends on the programme, semester and applicable additional services.

What to Include in Your Budget

  • Tuition/programme fee
  • Educational or career service charges where applicable
  • Hostel and food
  • Books, equipment and travel
  • Internship or international pathway costs where applicable

Scholarships & Financial Planning

Check the current scholarship page and programme-specific eligibility before assuming a waiver. Compare the net payable amount after confirmed scholarship support rather than comparing headline tuition alone.

Fees FAQs

Can Chitkara fees change?

Yes. The official 2026 material states that fees are subject to revision.

Are additional charges included in tuition?

Not always. Some 2026 programmes list ETS or CAS separately.
