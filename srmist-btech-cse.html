B.Tech CSE at SRMIST

SRMIST offers Computer Science and Engineering through its engineering admissions. Kattankulathur is the flagship campus. Exact programme title, campus and intake should be checked in the current SRMIST engineering prospectus.

Eligibility

For B.Tech admissions, SRMIST engineering information requires the prescribed Class 12 PCM background and minimum aggregate for the programme. The current B.Tech CSE-related programme pages should be checked for the exact branch requirement.

How to Get Admission?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal SRMIST entrance route for B.Tech. The 2026 SRMJEEE cycle had three application phases. Candidates complete the application, appear for the entrance test and participate in counselling.

B.Tech CSE Fees

Fees vary by programme/campus. SRMIST's official programme listings publish annual tuition for individual B.Tech programmes. Do not use MBA or another B.Tech specialisation fee as the CSE fee.

SRMIST B.Tech CSE Cutoff

SRMIST does not provide a single official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used for this page. Therefore, no unofficial closing rank is presented as an official cutoff.

B.Tech Placement Data

SRMIST's official 2023 university placement figures reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, a maximum salary of ₹1 crore per annum and average salary of ₹10.5 LPA. These are university-level figures, not CSE-only figures.

FAQs

Is SRMJEEE compulsory for B.Tech CSE?

SRMJEEE is the principal SRMIST UG engineering admission route; always check the current admission notice for applicable exceptions.

Does SRMIST publish an official CSE closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table was not found in the official sources used here, so an unverified rank should not be treated as the official cutoff.

Which campus is the flagship?

Kattankulathur is SRMIST's flagship/main campus.
