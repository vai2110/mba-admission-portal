B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering at SRMIST

SRMIST's engineering portfolio includes core CSE and several CSE-linked specialisations. This page focuses on the CSE pathway; campus and branch availability should be checked in the current engineering prospectus.

Eligibility

For B.Tech admission, SRMIST prescribes a Class 12 PCM background and the required aggregate. The exact current eligibility should be matched to the programme listed in the admission portal.

How to Get Admission to SRMIST B.Tech CSE?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal entrance route. After the examination, counselling uses the candidate's rank, programme/campus preferences and seat availability.

SRMIST B.Tech CSE Fees

The exact CSE fee varies by programme/campus. As an official SRMIST reference point, the Kattankulathur B.Tech CSE with Big Data Analytics page lists ₹4,25,000 per year. This should not be substituted for a different CSE programme without checking its fee page.

SRMIST B.Tech CSE Cutoff

SRMIST does not publish a consolidated official CSE closing-rank table in the sources used for this guide. Unofficial internet ranks are therefore not shown as an official cutoff.

What determines allotment?

SRMJEEE rank, selected programme/campus preferences and available seats during counselling.

What Do You Study in CSE?

The CSE route is centred on computing fundamentals, programming, algorithms, software systems, databases and related technology areas. SRMIST also lists specialised CSE pathways, so the exact electives and specialisation structure depend on the programme selected.

Placements

Use the dedicated SRMIST placements page for university-level salary and NIRF engineering data rather than repeating the full placement dataset here.

B.Tech CSE FAQs

Is ₹4.25 lakh the fee for every SRMIST CSE programme?

No. ₹4.25 lakh is the annual fee listed for the specific Kattankulathur B.Tech CSE with Big Data Analytics programme used as the official reference here.

Does SRMIST publish an official CSE closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Should I compare CSE and AI only by the course name?

No. Compare the exact programme, campus, curriculum, fee and available seats because SRMIST offers multiple related computing programmes.
