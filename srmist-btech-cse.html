What Is B.Tech CSE at SRMIST?

Computer Science and Engineering is one of the main computing choices at SRMIST. The university also offers CSE-linked specialisations, so students should identify the exact programme title and campus before comparing fees or curriculum.

This guide separates what is officially available from what should not be guessed, especially for branch-wise cutoff and fee comparisons.

What Is the B.Tech CSE Eligibility?

SRMIST's regular B.Tech eligibility requires a minimum 60% aggregate in the specified Class 12 subjects. Physics and Mathematics are mandatory, along with one prescribed subject such as Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices or Engineering Graphics, as applicable under the published eligibility.

The published age condition is 16 years 6 months on 31 July of the calendar year in which the Class 12 examination is to be held.

How Do You Get CSE Admission?

SRMJEEE (UG) is the principal regular B.Tech admission route. After the exam, counselling is important because the student chooses from the programme and campus options available for the candidate's rank and the seats remaining.

What Is the SRMIST B.Tech CSE Fee?

The exact fee depends on the CSE programme title and campus. SRMIST's official Kattankulathur listing for B.Tech CSE with Specialization in Big Data Analytics gives an annual fee of ₹4,25,000, sanctioned intake 180 and duration 4 years.

What Is the SRMIST CSE Cutoff?

SRMIST does not publish a consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table in the sources used for this page. Therefore, an internet “CSE cutoff” should not be presented as an official SRMIST closing rank.

What should you use?

Use your SRMJEEE rank, the options visible in counselling and seat availability. Keep entrance rank, scholarship rank and counselling closing rank separate.

What Do You Study in CSE?

A CSE student normally builds the foundation needed for software and computing careers: programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering and related technology subjects. The exact subjects and electives depend on the selected SRMIST CSE variant.

Who Should Choose CSE?

CSE is a sensible choice if you want a broad computing foundation and are comfortable building skills through programming, projects and technical practice. If you already know that you want a stronger AI orientation, compare the exact AI programme rather than assuming CSE and AI are identical.

What About CSE Placements?

SRMIST's official 2023 university-level placement communication reported 1,311 companies, 13,069 offers, maximum salary ₹1 crore per annum and average salary ₹10.5 LPA. For engineering, NIRF 2026 reports a ₹6.32 lakh median salary for the reported four-year UG engineering population in 2024-25.

These are not CSE-only figures. Use the programme/department scope before interpreting them.

FAQs Students Ask

Is ₹4.25 lakh the fee for every SRMIST CSE programme?

No. It is the annual fee listed for the specific Kattankulathur CSE with Big Data Analytics programme.

Does SRMIST publish an official CSE closing rank?

A consolidated official branch-wise closing-rank table is not available in the sources used here.

Is CSE available at every SRMIST campus?

Do not assume that. Programme availability is campus-specific.

Does JEE Main decide CSE admission?

SRMJEEE is the principal regular B.Tech admission route. JEE Main appears separately in the scholarship framework.

What should matter more than an unofficial cutoff?

Your SRMJEEE rank, counselling options, campus, exact CSE variant, fee and curriculum.
