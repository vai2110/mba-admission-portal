What Is MBA-PPM?

MBA-Public Policy & Management is a specialised two-year management programme for students interested in governance, public policy, public institutions and management. It is offered through XIM University's School of Governance and Public Affairs.

Best fit

Choose this route when you want a career at the intersection of management, policy and governance rather than a conventional corporate MBA profile.

MBA-PPM Eligibility 2026

The published requirement is a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 55% aggregate marks. Undergraduate rank holders can also apply under the stated route.

RequirementRule
Bachelor's degreeAny discipline.
Minimum marks55% aggregate.
Rank-holder routeAvailable under XIM's published criteria.

MBA-PPM Admission 2026

XIM announced a separate 2026 application window for MBA-PPM and MBA-UMG with a June 30, 2026 deadline. The selection route for MBA-PPM can differ from the flagship MBA-BM process, so applicants should follow the programme-specific instructions.

See the XIM admission guide for the wider 2026 application picture.

MBA-PPM Career Options

Career directions can include public policy consulting, government programmes, development organisations, think tanks, public-sector projects, CSR and policy research. The programme is especially useful for students who enjoy analysing systems and translating policy into practical management decisions.

Relevant career areas

  • Policy consulting
  • Government and public-sector projects
  • Development organisations
  • Think tanks and research

Compare with

If you want a broader corporate management degree, compare with MBA-BM.
