Recruiters & Career Preparation

BMU's placement ecosystem includes corporate interactions, internships and career development support. Recruiter lists can change by year, so students should use the latest programme-level placement information when making a decision.

Student Discussion: Is BMU Placement ROI Good?

The practical question for applicants is whether the programme's total cost, scholarship and expected role outcomes align with their career target. A high headline package is less useful than a consistent set of outcomes across the batch.

For a stronger decision

Ask for programme-wise salary distribution, median CTC, recruiter roles and internship-to-PPO conversion.

Be cautious

Do not compare one university's highest package with another university's average salary.
