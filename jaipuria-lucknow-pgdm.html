Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM: Overview

The two-year full-time PGDM at Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow is the flagship general-management programme. The institute states that the programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

The programme is designed around management fundamentals, applied learning, live projects, summer internship and specialisation choices. Students can build depth across Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Operations Management, Business Analytics, Business Strategy and Economics.

Who should choose this programme?

PGDM is the broadest choice at Jaipuria Lucknow. It is suitable if you want flexibility across management functions rather than a programme focused specifically on financial services or retail.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Curriculum

The curriculum combines core management courses with workshops, internships, live projects and electives. Official programme information includes subjects such as Accounting for Business, Managerial Economics, Statistics for Management, Organisational Behaviour, Marketing Management, GenAI for Managers, Corporate Finance, Managing Human Resources, Operations Management, Business Analytics and Strategic Management.

Learning model

  • Core management foundation in the initial part of the programme.
  • Industry-oriented case studies and real-world illustrations.
  • Summer internship and live-project exposure.
  • Dual-specialisation structure in the second year.
  • AI and analytics-oriented courses relevant to modern business roles.

PGDM Specialisations

The institute lists specialisation domains including Finance & Accounting, Marketing, Human Resource & Organisational Behaviour, Operations & Decision Sciences, Business Analytics, Business Strategy and Economics, with course availability subject to the institute's academic structure.

Finance & Accounting

Useful for students targeting corporate finance, investment, banking, risk, financial analysis and related roles.

Marketing

Relevant to brand, digital marketing, sales, product, consumer insights and category-oriented careers.

Business Analytics

Useful for candidates interested in data-driven decision-making, business intelligence and analytics roles.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Admission 2026

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's stated completion conditions.

Accepted exams

CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT, MAT and GMAT scores are accepted for the admission process.

Application stages

  1. Register your profile and select campus/programme.
  2. Pay the ₹1,000 non-refundable admission-process entrance fee.
  3. Complete the application with academic, test, extracurricular and work-experience information.
  4. Proceed through the institute's selection process, including aptitude score, case analysis and personal interview components.
