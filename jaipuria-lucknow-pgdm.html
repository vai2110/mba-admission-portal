Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM: Overview

The PGDM at Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow is a two-year full-time management programme and the flagship option on the campus. The institute states that the programme is AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

The programme is designed for graduates who want broad management exposure before choosing functional depth. It combines core management courses, workshops, internships, live projects and a dual-specialisation structure.

PGDM vs specialised PGDM

Choose the flagship PGDM when you want flexibility across management functions. If you already know that you want a finance/financial-services or retail career, compare it directly with the specialised PGDM Financial Services and PGDM Retail Management programmes.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Curriculum

The curriculum begins with a cross-functional management foundation and moves towards specialisation and elective learning. Current programme information includes Accounting for Business, Managerial Economics, Statistics for Management, Organisational Behaviour, Critical Reading and Writing, Marketing Management, GenAI for Managers, Corporate Finance, Managing Human Resources, Operations Management, Macroeconomics and Economic Environment of Business, Essentials of Business Analytics, Business Research Methods, Management Accounting and Control, Sales Management and Business Development, and Strategic Management.

Jaipuria PGDM Specialisations

The programme provides a dual-specialisation option. The institute states that students select six courses from Specialisation-I, six from Specialisation-II, one Liberal Arts course and one open elective. Specialisation availability can change with academic and industry requirements.

Finance & Accounting

Corporate finance, valuation, financial modelling, investment banking, portfolio management, risk, FinTech and related finance subjects.

Marketing

Consumer behaviour, brand management, digital marketing, CRM, marketing analytics, pricing, distribution and strategic marketing.

HR & Organisational Behaviour

Talent acquisition, compensation, HR analytics, learning and development, performance management and organisational change.

Operations & Decision Sciences

Operations research, logistics, inventory, project management, supply chain, lean and operations analytics.

Business Analytics

Python, machine learning, AI, forecasting, business intelligence, data visualisation, NLP and digital transformation.

Business Strategy & Economics

Strategy, economic environment, competitive analysis, business transformation and decision-oriented management learning.

PGDM Learning Model & Industry Exposure

Jaipuria describes its pedagogy around real-world illustrations and case studies. The programme also includes innovative workshops, summer internship and live projects, giving students multiple opportunities to connect academic concepts with business problems.

Learning components

  • Case studies and real-world business illustrations.
  • Design Thinking and problem-solving workshops.
  • Business Ethics and Sustainability.
  • Public Speaking and Persuasion.
  • Summer internship exposure.
  • Live projects and industry interaction.
  • AI and analytics-oriented management learning.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM Admission 2026

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree from a recognised Indian university with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required. Final-year candidates can apply provisionally subject to the institute's completion conditions.

Accepted entrance exams

CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT, MAT and GMAT scores are accepted.

Application process

  1. Register on the common application form and select the preferred campus/programme.
  2. Pay the non-refundable ₹1,000 admission-process entrance fee.
  3. Submit academic and entrance-test information.
  4. Add extracurricular credentials and work experience, if applicable.
  5. Complete the applicable selection stages.

Selection factors

The institute's selection framework includes aptitude performance, case analysis and personal interview components, with academic record, work experience and extracurricular profile contributing to the candidate assessment.

Jaipuria Lucknow PGDM FAQs

Is Jaipuria PGDM equivalent to MBA?

The institute states that its PGDM is AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

What is the PGDM fee?

The 2026-28 total tuition fee is ₹15.25 lakh, excluding applicable taxes, hostel and deposits.

What entrance exams are accepted?

CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT, MAT and GMAT are accepted.

What is the highest package?

The current interim placement report gives a highest CTC of ₹24.11 LPA.

Can students choose two specialisations?

Yes. The programme describes a dual-specialisation structure with six courses from each of two chosen specialisation areas, subject to the institute's academic rules.

Is hostel available?

Yes. Hostel accommodation is available separately from tuition fees.
