Jaipuria Institute of Management Lucknow: Overview

Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow has been offering management education since 1995. The campus offers three two-year full-time PGDM programmes: PGDM, PGDM Financial Services and PGDM Retail Management. It also offers the Fellow Programme in Management at doctoral level.

The Lucknow campus is NIRF-ranked and AACSB-accredited. For students evaluating private B-schools, Jaipuria Lucknow combines a general-management route with specialised finance and retail pathways. The right choice depends on the student's entrance score, budget, preferred career function, desired industry and expectations from placements.

Student decision point

Do not judge Jaipuria only by the highest package. Compare total cost, the latest placement status, role quality, programme-specific opportunities, scholarship eligibility and the alternatives available at your entrance-exam score.

Jaipuria Lucknow Admission 2026

Jaipuria's application process has three broad phases: registering the profile, paying the admission-process entrance fee and completing the application with academic, extracurricular and work-experience information.

How to Apply

  1. Register on the common application form and select the preferred campus and programme.
  2. Pay the non-refundable ₹1,000 admission-process entrance fee.
  3. Submit academic information and valid entrance-test details.
  4. Add extracurricular achievements and work experience, if applicable.
  5. Select the preferred interview venue where applicable.
  6. Participate in the applicable selection stages.

Selection Process

The selection framework considers aptitude-test performance along with case analysis and personal interview components. Academic record, work experience and extracurricular credentials can also contribute to the overall profile.
