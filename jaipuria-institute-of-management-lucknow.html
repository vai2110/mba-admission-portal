Jaipuria Institute of Management Lucknow: Overview

Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow is a management institute established in 1995. The Lucknow campus offers full-time PGDM programmes in general management, Financial Services and Retail Management, along with the Fellow Programme in Management. The PGDM programmes are AICTE approved, NBA accredited and AIU recognised as equivalent to MBA.

The campus was ranked 67th in the NIRF 2025 Management ranking. Jaipuria also highlights AACSB accreditation across its management education ecosystem. For students comparing private B-schools, the key decision points are programme fit, total cost, entrance-score profile, learning model, summer internship exposure and placement outcomes.

What should you check before applying?

Do not evaluate Jaipuria Lucknow only on headline package numbers. Compare the programme you want, the latest placement status, fee and hostel cost, scholarship eligibility, selection process and the roles offered to students.

Jaipuria Lucknow Admission 2026

Admission to the PGDM programmes requires a recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Final-year graduation students can also apply subject to the institute's completion and documentation conditions.

Accepted Entrance Exams

The institute accepts valid scores from CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT and GMAT for the admission process.

Selection Process

  1. Register and select the preferred campus and programme.
  2. Pay the non-refundable admission-process entrance fee of ₹1,000.
  3. Submit academic, test-score, extracurricular and work-experience details.
  4. Participate in the institute's selection stages, including aptitude performance, case analysis and personal interview components.

Jaipuria Lucknow Placements

These figures are presented by the institute as ongoing placement details and can change as the placement cycle progresses.

Campus & Student Life

Jaipuria Lucknow describes itself as a residential management campus with experienced full-time faculty, industry exposure, student clubs, events, internships, live projects and international opportunities. The curriculum also includes AI-oriented learning and business analytics exposure.
