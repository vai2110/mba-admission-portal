Quick answer

IIMA's MBA-PGPX is a one-year, full-time residential MBA for experienced professionals. It is a general-management programme built around core management courses, an International Immersion Programme, Preparing for Top Management, electives and a capstone. This page covers eligibility, GMAT/GRE, application rounds, curriculum, IIP, fees, career use-cases and the practical differences from the two-year PGP.

What is IIM Ahmedabad MBA-PGPX?

MBA-PGPX is IIMA's one-year full-time residential MBA for professionals who already have substantial post-graduation work experience. IIMA describes its orientation as general management and preparation for top management, rather than the more functional/operational orientation of the two-year PGP.

The programme awards a Master of Business Administration degree on successful completion. It is not a weekend or part-time executive programme; students are based at IIMA for the full-time academic year.

Student terminology: When students say “IIM Ahmedabad one-year MBA”, “IIM Ahmedabad Executive MBA” or “IIM Ahmedabad PGPX”, they are generally referring to this programme. The official programme name is MBA-PGPX.

PGPX Curriculum: What Will You Study?

The curriculum moves from management fundamentals to leadership/top-management learning, international exposure, electives and a capstone. IIMA uses the case method along with lectures, seminars, games, role plays, industrial visits and group exercises.

Examples of compulsory courses

Students encounter subjects such as Analysis of Data, Modelling for Decisions, Financial Reporting and Analysis, Firms and Markets, Corporate Finance, Financial Markets, Strategic Cost Management, Strategic Management, Leadership Skills and Corporate Governance. The exact course basket can be updated by IIMA.

Can you specialise in PGPX?

PGPX is officially a general-management programme, not a fixed specialisation MBA. However, IIMA offers a wide elective basket that lets students build depth in areas relevant to their career goals.

How Is the PGPX Learning Experience Different?

  • Case-heavy: students are expected to prepare, analyse and discuss real business situations.
  • Peer learning: the classroom includes experienced professionals, so discussions often draw on participants' own work contexts.
  • Fast pace: a large amount of management learning is compressed into one academic year.
  • Leadership orientation: the Preparing for Top Management component focuses on complex managerial problems and change leadership.
  • Applied work: electives may involve projects, cases, simulations or an Individual Research Project with faculty guidance.

PGPX Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

  • Bachelor's degree: Any discipline with at least 15 years of formal education.
  • Age: Minimum 25 years as on March 31, 2027; there is no upper age limit.
  • Work experience: Minimum 4 years of full-time work experience after graduation as on March 31, 2027.
  • Test: Valid GMAT/GRE score; IIMA specifies the accepted score validity window and test-format rules for the relevant round.
  • Part-time/internship experience: IIMA says undergraduate internships do not count and part-time work generally does not count toward the minimum full-time experience requirement.
  • Management experience: A candidate does not need a job title containing “manager”, but IIMA notes that management experience or related responsibilities and goals are important for successful candidates.
Practical check: Count qualifying full-time post-graduation experience up to the exact cutoff date in the admission notice. Do not count undergraduate internships toward the four-year requirement.

PGPX Selection Process

The 2027–28 process has two stages. First, IIMA shortlists applicants for the Personal Interview. Shortlisted candidates submit essays within the specified window. In the second stage, an IIMA faculty panel interviews candidates.

What is assessed?

IIMA lists factors such as professional background, quality of experience, career progression, academic preparedness, leadership potential, accomplishments and interpersonal skills. There is no single published “GMAT cutoff” that by itself guarantees admission.

Application fee

The current PGPX application portal lists an application fee of ₹10,000 for Round 2. The applicable fee should be checked on the live portal for the round in which you apply.

PGPX Fees and Total Budget

For the 2026–27 batch, IIMA lists ₹35 lakh for Single Student Housing (SSH) and ₹37.10 lakh for Married Student Housing (MSH), plus the International Immersion Programme cost excluding related travel and stay.

What is included?

IIMA describes the programme fee as covering tuition, books/course materials, accommodation, library, computing and network service facilities.

What is not included?

The fee excludes IIP and its travel/stay costs. Electricity, telephone, laundry, sports/gym subscriptions and meals are additional. Other personal expenses also need to be budgeted.

What should a student budget overall?

Planning estimate: ~₹39–43 lakh+ for SSH and ~₹41–45 lakh+ for MSH. These are budgeting ranges, not official IIMA cost-of-attendance figures.

International Immersion Programme (IIP)

The IIP is a compulsory part of PGPX. IIMA describes it as an intensive international academic module designed to expose students to a different economic and business environment. The exact destination/partner school can vary by year based on student interest, active relationships, faculty availability, fees and logistics.

Do not assume the IIP is a holiday or an optional foreign trip: it is an academic component of the programme, and its cost is separate from the published programme fee.

Careers After PGPX

PGPX is aimed at experienced professionals looking to move into broader management, leadership or career-transition roles. The programme does not prescribe one job function; electives allow students to build depth relevant to their post-MBA goals.

IIMA's placement/career process and audited reports should be used for current salary and recruiter data. The dedicated IIM Ahmedabad placements guide summarises the available PGPX placement information by year and metric.

Who Usually Finds PGPX Relevant?

  • Professionals with at least four years of qualifying post-graduation experience.
  • Candidates seeking a one-year full-time MBA rather than a two-year programme.
  • Professionals who want to broaden from a functional role into general management or leadership.
  • Candidates who can take a full-time career break for a residential programme.

If you are a fresher or do not meet the four-year experience condition, the two-year PGP is the relevant IIMA programme to examine. If you need to continue working while studying, also examine the BPGP.

PGPX FAQs Students Usually Ask

Is PGPX an Executive MBA?

IIMA calls it the MBA-PGPX, a one-year full-time residential MBA for experienced professionals. It is not a weekend or part-time programme.

Can a fresher apply for PGPX?

No. The current 2027–28 criteria require at least four years of full-time post-graduation work experience.

Does PGPX accept CAT?

The current PGPX process specifies GMAT/GRE rather than CAT as the standard test requirement.

Is the International Immersion Programme compulsory?

Yes. IIMA describes IIP as a compulsory component of the PGPX curriculum.

Can I choose a specialisation?

PGPX is a general-management programme, but its elective basket allows students to build depth in selected functional or sectoral areas.

Does the programme award an MBA degree?

Yes. IIMA states that successful PGPX participants are awarded a Master of Business Administration degree.

Is the ₹35 lakh fee the full cost?

No. The published programme fee excludes IIP and related travel/stay and also has additional utility/personal expenses. See the fee section for a planning estimate.
