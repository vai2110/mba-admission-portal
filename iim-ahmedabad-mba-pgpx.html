Programme Overview

IIMA's MBA-PGPX is a one-year full-time MBA designed for experienced professionals seeking higher-level management and leadership roles. IIMA describes it as a general-management programme with a strong focus on preparing participants for top management.

Eligibility & Admission

  • Bachelor's degree: Any discipline with at least 15 years of formal education.
  • Age: Minimum 25 years as on March 31, 2027 for the 2027–28 batch.
  • Work experience: Minimum 4 years of full-time work experience after graduation as on March 31, 2027.
  • Test: Valid GMAT/GRE score.
  • Selection: Shortlisting, essays and personal interview. IIMA evaluates professional background, career progression, leadership potential and accomplishments.
  • 2027–28 application rounds: IIMA lists September 2, 2026 and November 23, 2026 as Round 1 and Round 2 application closing dates, subject to change.

Fees & Other Costs

For the 2026–27 batch, IIMA lists ₹35 lakh for single accommodation and ₹37.10 lakh for married student housing, plus the international immersion programme cost excluding related travel and stay. The bundled fee includes tuition, books/course materials, accommodation and specified campus services. Utilities such as electricity, telephone, laundry and sports/gym subscriptions are additional.

Important: Do not use the 2026–27 fee as the confirmed fee for a future batch until IIMA publishes its applicable fee schedule.

Who Should Consider PGPX?

This programme is designed for professionals who already have substantial post-graduation work experience and want a one-year full-time MBA. Freshers and candidates with less than four years of qualifying experience should evaluate the two-year MBA/PGP instead.

FAQ

Can a fresher apply for PGPX?

No. The current 2027–28 criteria require at least four years of full-time work experience after graduation.

Does PGPX accept CAT?

The current admission process specifies GMAT/GRE as the standard test requirement.
