Nirma B.Pharm Overview

Bachelor of Pharmacy at Nirma University's Institute of Pharmacy is a four-year, eight-semester undergraduate programme approved by the Pharmacy Council of India, with an intake of 100 seats.

Where can B.Pharm lead?

The institute lists opportunities in drug manufacturing, quality control/assurance, pharmacy, government roles, pharmaceutical marketing, supply chain, academia and higher education.

B.Pharm Eligibility

The published 2026-27 eligibility requires at least 50% aggregate in HSC/10+2, with English and Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and/or Biology (PCM/PCB/PCMB) as prescribed subjects.

Nirma B.Pharm Admission Process

Admissions are conducted through the prescribed Nirma/ACPC process. The official page states that undergraduate B.Pharm admissions are purely on merit. ACPC and category-specific instructions should be checked for the applicable year.

B.Pharm Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

For 2026 NRI/NRI-sponsored admissions, applications opened May 5 and the last date was August 10, 2026. Vacant-seat dates were listed as TBA and subject to the ACPC process.

2027 planning: do not use the 2026 dates as official 2027 deadlines; wait for the new admission notice.

B.Pharm Career & Higher Studies

Students can target pharmacist roles, manufacturing, QA/QC, regulatory/government roles, pharmaceutical marketing, supply chain and further study. The institute highlights hands-on training, laboratories and industry engagement.

Nirma B.Pharm FAQs

What is the duration of B.Pharm at Nirma?

Four years or eight semesters.

What percentage is required?

The published 2026-27 eligibility states a minimum 50% aggregate in HSC with the required subject combination.

What is the B.Pharm fee?

Tuition is ₹2.80 lakh per year for 2026-27, plus the listed additional charges.

Is Nirma B.Pharm approved?

The programme is approved by the Pharmacy Council of India according to the official programme page.
