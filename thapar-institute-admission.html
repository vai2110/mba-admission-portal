Student Discussion: Common Admission Mistakes

Applicants often make decisions using old cutoff, fee or eligibility information. At TIET, the programme matters: BTech counselling, MBA testing and PG admissions can follow different rules.

  • Use the current 2026-27 prospectus.
  • Verify your exact branch/programme eligibility.
  • Keep entrance score details ready.
  • Save the final application and payment confirmation.
