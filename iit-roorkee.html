Quick answer

DoMS IIT Roorkee offers a two-year full-time residential MBA and a separate blended Executive MBA. For MBA 2026-28, the CAT route lists 95 seats. The latest official MBA placement report records ₹17.91 LPA average CTC, ₹17.64 LPA median CTC and ₹24 LPA highest CTC for the 2023-25 batch.

IIT Roorkee MBA & Executive MBA

DoMS currently lists three programmes: MBA, Executive MBA (EMBA) and PhD. For this management-admissions cluster, the two student-facing management degree pages are the regular MBA and EMBA; the PhD is not treated as an MBA-admission course page.

The regular MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme. The EMBA is a separate blended master's degree designed for working professionals.

IIT Roorkee MBA Admission

  1. Meet the current academic and route-specific eligibility requirements.
  2. Appear for CAT, unless eligible for the stated IIT-graduate CAT exemption.
  3. Submit the DoMS MBA application within the published window.
  4. Receive the CAT-based interview shortlist.
  5. Attend GD/PI at the allotted centre/date.
  6. Compete for final merit under the institute's published selection framework.
  7. Complete admission registration, fee payment and document verification after selection.

IIT Roorkee MBA Eligibility

The current brochure should be treated as the controlling source for eligibility. The 2026-28 cycle uses CAT for the regular route and identifies a separate route for qualifying IIT graduates.

  • Applicants must satisfy the prescribed qualifying-degree and academic requirements.
  • CAT is the principal qualifying route for the regular intake.
  • JEE/JEE Advanced-qualified IIT graduates with CGPA 7 or above on a 10-point scale are stated to be exempt from CAT and can be directly called for Personal Interview, subject to the brochure conditions.

IIT Roorkee MBA Cutoff & Selection Criteria

The admission material used here does not state one final CAT percentile as an official cutoff. IIT Roorkee describes CAT-based initial shortlisting and final merit based on CAT, Interview and Work Experience.

Important: A qualifying CAT condition and the percentile required to receive an interview call are not necessarily the same. Do not publish an estimated competitive percentile as an official IIT Roorkee cutoff.

IIT Roorkee MBA Admission FAQs

How many CAT-route seats are available?

The 2026-28 brochure lists 95 seats through CAT, subject to increase with approval.

Can IIT Roorkee graduates apply without CAT?

Qualifying IIT graduates meeting the stated CGPA condition are described as CAT-exempt and can be directly called for Personal Interview, subject to the institute's conditions.

What is the complete MBA fee?

The published four-semester total for General/GEN-EWS/OBC under Dining & Residing is ₹9,74,200 for 2026-27.

What was the 2025 average MBA package?

The official 2025 final placement report lists an average CTC of ₹17.91 LPA.

Is the Executive MBA the same as the regular MBA?

No. The EMBA is a separate blended master's programme for working professionals with its own eligibility, selection and fee structure.
