IIT Roorkee MBA Admission Process

  1. Check eligibility: confirm the academic qualification, CAT route and institute-specific conditions in the current brochure.
  2. Submit the online application: pay the applicable category-wise application fee and provide the required academic, CAT and personal details.
  3. Initial shortlisting: the institute states that shortlisting for interviews is based on CAT performance. IIT graduates who qualify through JEE/JEE Advanced with CGPA 7 or above on a 10-point scale are exempt from CAT and can be directly called for Personal Interview.
  4. GD/PI: shortlisted candidates attend the institute's Group Discussion and Interview process at the scheduled location.
  5. Final merit: IIT Roorkee states that final offers are based on aggregate performance in CAT, Interview and Work Experience.
  6. Offer and admission: selected candidates complete fee payment, registration and document verification by the institute's deadline.
Meeting the minimum eligibility conditions does not itself guarantee an offer. IIT Roorkee uses a selection/merit process after initial CAT-based shortlisting.

IIT Roorkee MBA Specialisations & Programme Structure

DoMS describes the MBA as an 8-term, 76-credit residential programme. The first year builds core management knowledge; the second year moves toward advanced learning and practice in chosen areas.

Current dual-specialisation options listed by DoMS are:

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Operations
  • Information Systems
  • Human Resource Management

DoMS also states that the programme structure is designed to allow students to pursue dual specialisation in any two of these areas.

The semester totals include institute/semester charges, hostel charges and the published mess components for the Dining & Residing category. The fee sheet also lists refundable deposits and one-time admission/mess charges within the semester totals.

For SC/ST/PWD students under Dining & Residing, the published semester totals are ₹1,55,550, ₹1,39,050, ₹1,40,550 and ₹1,39,050 respectively, giving a published four-semester total of ₹5,74,200.

The published mess amount is an advance subject to adjustment as per actuals. Hostel accommodation shown is a minimum bachelor-hostel charge; married accommodation is as per actual. Therefore, ₹9,74,200 is the published four-semester fee total for the specified category, not a promise that every personal living expense will be exactly this amount.

IIT Roorkee MBA Admission FAQs

How many CAT-route seats are available for IIT Roorkee MBA?

The 2026-28 admission brochure lists 95 seats through CAT, subject to increase with competent-authority approval.

Can IIT Roorkee graduates apply without CAT?

JEE or JEE Advanced-qualified IIT graduates with a CGPA of 7 or above on a 10-point scale are stated to be exempt from CAT and can be directly called for Personal Interview, subject to the institute's conditions.

What is the total IIT Roorkee MBA fee for 2026-27?

For General/GEN-EWS/OBC students under Dining & Residing, the four published semester totals add up to ₹9,74,200. The exact payable amount can vary with applicable category, accommodation status and actual mess adjustment.

What was IIT Roorkee MBA average package in 2025?

The official 2025 final placement report lists an average CTC of ₹17.91 LPA, median CTC of ₹17.64 LPA and highest CTC of ₹24 LPA.

Is IIT Roorkee MBA residential?

Yes. IIT Roorkee describes its MBA as a regular, full-time residential programme spread over two academic years.
