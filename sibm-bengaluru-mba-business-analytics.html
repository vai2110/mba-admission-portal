SIBM Bengaluru MBA Business Analytics Overview

Quick answer

This is a two-year full-time residential MBA designed to develop managers who can apply analytics to business problems. The official 2027-29 intake is 30 students.

The institute says the first year combines general management and analytics subjects, while the second year applies analytics across HR, marketing, operations and finance. Successful completion leads to the MBA degree from Symbiosis International (Deemed University).

SIBM Bengaluru MBA Business Analytics Eligibility

Applicants need a graduate degree from a recognised university/institution with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade; the official page specifies 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year candidates may apply subject to obtaining the required marks. Foreign university qualifications require an AIU equivalence certificate.

How SIBM Bengaluru MBA Business Analytics Selection Works

The official 2027-29 selection weightage assigns 50 marks to SNAP out of 60, 10 to Group Exercise and 40 to Personal Interaction. The programme therefore has a structured institute-stage assessment after SNAP shortlisting.

What is the official SNAP cutoff?

The institute does not publish a fixed SNAP percentile cutoff on the current programme page. This page therefore does not present third-party estimates as an official cutoff.

Application steps

Applicants register for SNAP and pay the test fee, then separately register for the SIBM Bengaluru MBA Business Analytics programme. The official page lists ₹1,000 as the institute programme registration fee.

What You Study in MBA Business Analytics

The programme is designed around analytics-led management. The first year includes subjects such as digital transformation, mathematical models for data sciences, e-commerce, data warehousing and business intelligence. The second year includes applications such as HR analytics, marketing analytics, operations analytics and business forecasting.

The institute also states that students have access to Harvard cases and simulations, CMIE Prowess, Bloomberg Terminal and LinkedIn Learning, along with a well-equipped library.

MBA Business Analytics Placements

SIBM Bengaluru's official placement page reports a 2025 average CTC of ₹14.32 LPA and highest CTC of ₹17.50 LPA for the Business Analytics specialization. The page also identifies roles such as Associate Business Consultant, Business Analyst, Data Analyst and Market Research Analyst in its summer-internship discussion.

MBA Business Analytics FAQs

Is SIBM Bengaluru MBA Business Analytics residential?

Yes. The official programme is described as a two-year full-time residential MBA.

How many seats are offered?

The official intake for the 2027-29 batch is 30 students.

Does MBA Business Analytics accept SNAP?

Yes. SNAP contributes 50 marks to the 2027-29 final merit calculation, followed by GE and PI.

Is the 2027-29 fee officially published?

The current programme page displays the 2026-28 fee schedule. The final 2027-29 fee should be taken from the institute when published.
