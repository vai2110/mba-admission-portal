JMI MBA Placements: Latest Official Data

Latest published package figures:

The official DMS 2025–26 Placement Brochure reports placement statistics for the 2023–25 batch: ₹24 LPA highest CTC, ₹10.8 LPA average CTC and ₹10 LPA median CTC.

₹24 LPAHighest CTC
₹10.8 LPAAverage CTC
₹10 LPAMedian CTC

The brochure presents these figures on the DMS placement-statistics page. It also contains placement and student-profile material for multiple DMS MBA programmes. Accordingly, the figures should be used as the department's published placement benchmark rather than a promise for every student in the regular MBA.

What the JMI Placement Figures Actually Mean

Placement data is most useful when the cohort and reporting scope are clear. The DMS brochure's placement section is department-level material covering its MBA programmes. It should not be converted into an “MBA-only average” unless JMI explicitly labels the figure that way.

MetricPublished resultInterpretation
Highest CTC₹24 LPATop reported CTC in the cited 2023–25 placement statistics
Average CTC₹10.8 LPADepartment brochure's reported average for the cited placement cohort
Median CTC₹10 LPAMiddle salary figure; useful for judging a typical outcome

For admission decisions, median CTC is often more useful than the highest package because it reduces the effect of one or two exceptional offers.

JMI MBA ROI: How to Evaluate It

JMI's regular MBA fee is unusually low compared with many private B-schools: the 2026–27 prospectus lists ₹26,875 as the total fee payable per year. Against the DMS brochure's ₹10.8 LPA average CTC for the 2023–25 placement statistics, the headline fee-to-package relationship looks attractive. However, students should also account for living costs, opportunity cost, taxes, role quality, location and the fact that placement figures cover the department's published cohort scope.

Best way to compare JMI:

Use median salary + total academic cost + placement rate/cohort definition, rather than comparing highest packages alone.
