PGDM in Agri-Business Management: flagship course

The two-year, full-time residential PGDM ABM is the programme most students search for when looking at MANAGE Hyderabad admission, CAT, fees and placements. It is designed around management problems in agriculture and allied value chains and is recognised by AICTE and accredited by NBA.

ParameterDetails
Duration2 years
Intake100 students
ModeFull-time, residential
Entrance examCAT
2026 eligibilityFour-year agriculture/agri-allied bachelor's degree; 50% general, 45% SC/ST/PwD
Published 2026-28 fee₹9.75 lakh
Latest completed placement batch2023-25: 100%, ₹12.38 LPA average, ₹21 LPA highest

The programme is a strong fit for students who want management careers in agri-inputs, procurement, commodity trading, food, BFSI, consulting, supply chain and rural markets.

PGDAEM: Post Graduate Diploma in Agricultural Extension Management

PGDAEM is designed for agricultural extension professionals rather than the same audience as the full-time PGDM ABM. The course uses distance/supported learning and is relevant for professionals working in government and the wider agricultural extension ecosystem.

ParameterDetails
Duration1 year / two semesters
ModeDistance / supported learning
Target learnerAgricultural extension professionals
Typical eligibilityAgriculture/allied graduate with relevant extension employment/experience as specified by the course
Indicative feePublished course-specific fee; varies by sponsorship/support route

This is not the course to choose if your primary objective is a two-year campus-based corporate placement programme. PGDM ABM is the more relevant route for that goal.

PGDAWM: Post Graduate Diploma in Agri-Warehousing Management

PGDAWM is an online programme focused on warehousing and post-harvest management in agriculture. It is more flexible than the residential PGDM and is useful for students or professionals who want specialised knowledge without moving into a full-time residential programme.

MANAGE Hyderabad Courses FAQs

What is the main course at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The flagship course is the two-year residential PGDM in Agri-Business Management. It is the programme most closely associated with MANAGE's CAT admissions and campus placements.

Is MANAGE PGDM ABM an MBA?

No. It is a Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Agri-Business Management), not a degree titled MBA. It is recognised by AICTE and accredited by NBA.

Can any graduate apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

No. The 2026 policy required a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline. It also required 50% marks for general candidates and 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Which entrance exam is required for PGDM ABM?

A valid CAT score is mandatory for the 2026-28 PGDM ABM cycle.

What is the difference between PGDM ABM and PGDAEM?

PGDM ABM is a full-time, two-year residential management programme aimed at corporate agribusiness careers. PGDAEM is a shorter programme designed primarily for agricultural extension professionals and uses a distance/supported-learning model.

What is PGDAWM at MANAGE?

PGDAWM is an online postgraduate diploma in Agri-Warehousing Management. The published eligibility accepts a bachelor's degree in any discipline with 50% marks, making it more broadly accessible than PGDM ABM.

Which MANAGE course has campus placements?

The PGDM ABM is the full-time residential programme associated with MANAGE's campus final placements. Other professional programmes have different purposes and should not be assumed to have the same placement structure.

What is the fee for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

The published 2026-28 total is ₹9.75 lakh for two years, including tuition, boarding/lodging and other listed charges.

Is MANAGE good for a career outside agriculture?

It can lead to some roles outside core agriculture companies. MANAGE placement demand includes BFSI, consultancy, FMCG, procurement and commodity trading in addition to agri-input and allied sectors. However, the programme remains specialised in agribusiness, so students wanting a completely sector-neutral MBA should compare other options too.

Which course should a fresher choose at MANAGE?

If you are an eligible agriculture/agri-allied graduate and want a full-time management career, PGDM ABM is the main course to evaluate. PGDAEM is more relevant to working extension professionals, while PGDAWM is suited to online specialisation.
