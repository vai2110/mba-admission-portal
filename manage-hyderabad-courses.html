PGDM in Agri-Business Management

MANAGE's flagship PGDM ABM is a two-year, full-time residential management programme launched in 1996. It is AICTE recognised and NBA accredited. The programme is designed to develop managers for agriculture and allied value chains.

ParameterDetails
Duration2 years
Intake100 students
Admission testCAT
Eligibility4-year agriculture/agri-allied bachelor's degree; 50% general, 45% SC/ST/PwD for 2026 policy
ModeFull-time residential
RecognitionAICTE recognised; NBA accredited

Career sectors include agri-inputs, animal nutrition, procurement, commodity trading, BFSI, FMCG, consultancy and development.

PGDAEM: Agricultural Extension Management

The Post Graduate Diploma in Agricultural Extension Management is designed mainly for extension functionaries. The official course page says candidates are expected to be graduates in agriculture/allied areas and currently employed in state, central or UT governments, with a minimum five years' agricultural-extension work experience, relaxable for deserving cases.

ParameterDetails
Duration1 year, two semesters
Credits30 credit loads per semester structure
DeliveryDistance mode with contact classes, e-learning and assignments
Typical applicantWorking agricultural extension professional
Indicative fee₹20,000 for sponsoring NGOs/private sector candidates; government support may apply through SEWP

PGDAWM: Agri-warehousing Management

PGDAWM is an online postgraduate diploma in Agri-warehousing Management. The official eligibility page accepts a bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks. There is no stated minimum or maximum age.

EligibilityBachelor's degree in any discipline with 50%

MANAGE Courses FAQs

Is MANAGE PGDM ABM an MBA?

It is a Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Agri-Business Management), not a degree titled MBA. It is recognised by AICTE and accredited by NBA.

Can any graduate apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

No. The 2026 policy specifically requires a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline.

Which entrance exam is required for PGDM ABM?

A valid CAT score is mandatory under the 2026 admission policy.

What is the PGDAEM course at MANAGE?

It is a one-year postgraduate diploma focused on agricultural extension management and is primarily intended for working extension functionaries.

What is the PGDAWM fee?

The official MANAGE page lists the online PGDAWM fee as ₹10,000.
