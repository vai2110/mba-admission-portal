Selection Criteria

Stage 1: Preliminary screening

Candidates need a valid CAT 2025 score, must apply to the programme, satisfy eligibility requirements and clear the published CAT sectional and overall thresholds. A positive raw score in all CAT sections is also required.

Academic and profile-based shortlisting

IIMA's 2026–28 process uses academic performance in Class 10, Class 12 and bachelor's studies, CAT performance and other applicable profile components to create the composite score used for additional AWT/PI shortlisting.

Stage 2: AWT & PI

Shortlisted candidates participate in the Analytical Writing Test (AWT) and Personal Interview (PI). IIMA's admission process then uses the applicable selection components to determine the final offer.

Why this matters: A CAT percentile alone is not the complete IIMA selection formula. Your academic profile and the institute's application-rating/composite-score rules matter as well.

FAQs

Is 95 percentile enough for IIM Ahmedabad?

95 percentile is the published overall qualifying threshold for General/EWS non-PwD candidates for PGP 2026–28, but it does not guarantee an AWT/PI shortlist. Additional academic and profile criteria apply.

Does IIMA accept CAT for MBA?

Yes. CAT 2025 is the entrance route for domestic applicants to the 2026–28 PGP/MBA batch.

Is IIM Ahmedabad PGP the MBA programme?

Yes. IIMA describes its two-year full-time PGP in Management as its flagship MBA programme.

Does work experience matter?

The PGP batch includes both fresh graduates and candidates with work experience, and work experience is one of the profile components considered in IIMA's admission process.
