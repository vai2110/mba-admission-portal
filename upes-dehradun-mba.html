UPES MBA Overview

UPES School of Business offers a broad portfolio of MBA programmes including General, Power Management, International Business, Aviation Management, Business Analytics, Strategy & Consulting, Oil & Gas, Logistics & Supply Chain and industry-partnered programmes.

What makes the UPES MBA different?

The major differentiator is specialisation. Students should choose based on the target function and industry rather than treating every UPES MBA as having identical career outcomes.

Popular UPES MBA Specialisations

MBA General

Broad management pathway for students seeking flexibility across business functions.

MBA Business Analytics

Analytics-oriented management pathway with industry collaboration.

MBA Strategy & Consulting

Strategy-focused programme offered with industry-linked learning.

MBA Strategic Financial Management

Finance-focused programme in collaboration with EY India.

MBA Oil & Gas / Power

Domain-focused management programmes for energy-sector careers.

MBA Logistics & Supply Chain

Operations and supply-chain focused management education.

UPES MBA Eligibility

UPES lists minimum 50% marks in Class X, Class XII and graduation from a recognised university in any stream. MBA Strategic Financial Management and certain other EY-linked programmes require a minimum 60% in graduation.

What is usually NOT part of the headline tuition?

Hostel, transport, technology, programme-specific consumables/training, insurance, I-Card and other applicable charges can be additional. UPES also lists a ₹31,300 one-time training/certification fee for applicable full-time programmes, ₹9,000 PDC fee for PG programmes and ₹23,300 for UG programmes, subject to programme exclusions.

UPES MBA Fees

Current 2026 listings show the General / Power Management / International Business / Aviation Management MBA at ₹4.47 lakh for Semesters 1-2 and ₹4.715 lakh for Semesters 3-4, or approximately ₹18.37 lakh across the four semesters including the listed tuition and academic service components. Fees vary by specialisation.

Earlier official programme material and current third-party fee reproductions also show different structures for KPMG/EY-linked and energy-focused MBAs. Always verify the exact 2026-27 programme fee on the UPES fee page.

UPES MBA Admission 2026

UPES accepts UPESMET and selected national-level entrance exams followed by a personal interview. Current admission alerts also list academic-score pathways for eligible candidates. The exact route depends on the MBA specialisation.

UPES MBA Placements

UPES publishes School of Business placement information separately and identifies MBA outcomes. Its MBA admission page cites 2023-24 placement data of ₹30 LPA highest package, ₹14 LPA top-10% average CTC, 390+ offers and 150+ recruiters for the relevant placement cohort.

Student takeaway:

Do not use the university's ₹1.3 crore alumnus headline to estimate an MBA outcome. Compare MBA-specific recruiters, roles, top-10% outcomes and the specialisation you intend to pursue.

UPES MBA FAQs

What is the UPES MBA eligibility?

50% in Class X, XII and graduation for the general MBA criteria. Some EY-linked programmes require 60% graduation.

What entrance exams does UPES accept?

UPESMET and selected national-level exams are accepted, followed by the applicable selection process.

What is the MBA fee?

It varies by specialisation. Current listings put the common General/Power/International Business/Aviation structure at roughly ₹18.37 lakh across four semesters.
