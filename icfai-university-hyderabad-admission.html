IBS Hyderabad MBA Admission 2026

For the full-time MBA, IFHE currently accepts IBSAT as well as qualifying CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT scores. Shortlisted candidates go through Micro Presentation and Personal Interview. Final admission considers academic record and selection performance.

How to Apply to IFHE Hyderabad

  1. Select the exact school and programme.
  2. Read the programme-specific eligibility and selection rules.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload the requested photograph and academic/document details.
  5. Pay the applicable application/admission amount.
  6. Complete the programme-specific selection process where required.
  7. After selection, complete fee payment and document verification.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Class X and XII certificates/marksheets.
  • Graduation marksheets and degree/provisional certificate for PG applicants.
  • Valid entrance-test score where applicable.
  • Identity proof and recent photograph.
  • Category or other supporting documents where applicable.

2026 Admission Dates: Why You Should Check Programme-wise

IFHE does not use one universal admission calendar for every course. MBA, BBA, B.Tech, law and other programmes can have different deadlines. The safest approach is to check the current programme page and application portal immediately before submitting the form.

Student Discussion: Common Admission Mistakes

Applicants often lose time by assuming that one IFHE admission rule applies to every programme. The more important checks are programme name, campus, entrance test, eligibility, deadline and total fee.

  • Do not confuse IBS Hyderabad MBA with online/distance MBA.
  • Do not use an old deadline from a previous intake.
  • Confirm whether your test score is accepted for the exact programme.
  • Save the application and fee-payment confirmation.

ICFAI University Hyderabad Admission FAQs

Which tests are accepted for IBS Hyderabad MBA?

IBSAT, CAT, XAT, NMAT by GMAC and GMAT are listed as qualifying routes, subject to the applicable admission cycle.

What is the MBA eligibility?

Graduation in any discipline with 50% or above marks and the applicable English-medium requirement.

Is there one deadline for all IFHE courses?

No. Admission timelines vary by programme and school.
