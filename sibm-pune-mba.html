SIBM Pune MBA: Programme Overview

The SIBM Pune flagship MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme with an intake of 240 students. The official programme structure covers Marketing, Human Resource Management, Finance, Operations and Supply Chain Management, and Data Analytics.

The curriculum includes generic management courses, specialisation core courses, electives, a summer internship and a dissertation. The official structure also includes subjects such as Data Driven Decision Making, R Programming, Data Visualization and Modelling, Design Thinking and Business Simulation.

Who Is Eligible for SIBM Pune MBA?

A candidate must hold a graduation degree from a recognised university or Institution of National Importance with 50% marks or equivalent grade. The minimum is 45% for SC/ST candidates. Final-year students can apply, subject to obtaining the required marks in the qualifying examination.

Intake and reservation

The flagship MBA has an intake of 240 students. SIBM Pune lists 15% reservation for SC, 7.5% for ST and 3% for PwD within intake, with specified seats above intake for Kashmiri migrants/pandits and a separate international-student provision.

What Is the SIBM Pune MBA Selection Process?

1. Register for SNAP

Applicants must appear for SNAP. They must also register separately with SIBM Pune and pay the institute application charge of ₹1,000 plus GST.

2. Get shortlisted on SNAP

SIBM Pune states that shortlisted applicants are selected based on SNAP scores. The institute does not publish a fixed official percentile cutoff on the cited page.

3. Complete GE-PI

The current admission information specifies Group Exercise and Personal Interview as the institute selection stage. Shortlisted candidates receive the schedule through the institute's website.

4. Merit list and fee payment

SIBM Pune publishes the merit list. Selected candidates must complete the required fee payment within the specified deadline.

What Will You Study in SIBM Pune MBA?

The official programme structure combines generic management courses with specialisation-specific courses. Core areas include Financial Management, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Operations Management, Microeconomics, Business Statistics, Data Driven Decision Making, Financial Accounting, Research Methodology, Supply Chain Management and R Programming.

Students then move into specialisation courses. Data Analytics, for example, includes Data Preparation and Data Management, Programming for Data Sciences, Data Mining, Predictive Analytics and Machine Learning. Operations and Supply Chain includes Materials Management, Quality Management, Logistics Management and Supply Chain Modelling and Design.

The programme also includes a 10-credit summer internship, Strategic Management, Data Visualization and Modelling and Design Thinking.

SIBM Pune MBA FAQs

Does SIBM Pune MBA accept CAT?

The official flagship MBA admission route is SNAP. Applicants must appear for SNAP and separately register with SIBM Pune.

What is the official SNAP cutoff for SIBM Pune MBA?

SIBM Pune does not publish a fixed SNAP percentile cutoff on its current official admission page. It states that shortlisted candidates are selected based on SNAP scores.

What is the SIBM Pune MBA fee for 2026-28?

The published academic fee is ₹26.20 lakh for two years. Hostel, mess and refundable deposits are additional components.

What is the latest SIBM Pune MBA average package?

The 2025 official placement report records an average CTC of ₹28.18 lakh and median CTC of ₹27 lakh.
