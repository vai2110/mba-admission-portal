TSM Campus

Where Is TSM Madurai?

Thiagarajar School of Management is located at Pamban Swamy Nagar, Thiruparankundram, Madurai District, Tamil Nadu 625005. The campus is outside the dense central-city environment, so applicants should consider commute patterns, access to the city and the fact that the full-time programmes are residential.

TSM Residential Model

TSM's full-time MBA and PGDM are residential programmes. The current fee structure describes twin-sharing residential accommodation, with single occupancy subject to availability. Residential living means academic activities, peer interaction and many co-curricular experiences take place within a shared campus environment.

For students comparing TSM with day-scholar B-schools, this is an important lifestyle difference. It can improve peer interaction and reduce daily commute, but it also means accommodation and mess are part of the total cost of attending.

Accommodation and Mess Cost

TSM currently shows ₹3.5 lakh for accommodation and mess across two years for both MBA and PGDM. This is separate from academic fees but included when TSM presents the total programme cost.

Learning Environment

TSM's academic model uses case studies, live projects, business simulations, industry visits, practitioner interactions and internships. The value of the residential model therefore extends beyond accommodation: students share a concentrated environment with peers working on management assignments, presentations, competitions and placement preparation.

What Students Should Evaluate Before Joining

Food & accommodationAsk about current room allocation, mess arrangements, occupancy and rules rather than relying on old reviews.
Academic workloadSix-trimester PGDM structure can create a faster academic rhythm than a conventional semester model.
Peer networkPGDM is described as PAN-India, while MBA is predominantly Tamil Nadu, which can affect classroom diversity.
City accessConsider how often you need external city access and whether the residential environment suits your lifestyle.

TSM Campus & Hostel FAQs

Is TSM residential?

Yes. TSM describes its full-time MBA and PGDM as residential programmes.

Is accommodation included in TSM fees?

Accommodation and mess are included in the total programme cost shown on TSM's fee page, though itemised separately from academic fees.

What type of room does TSM provide?

TSM's fee page states that twin-sharing accommodation is provided, with single occupancy subject to availability.

Where is TSM campus located?

TSM is at Pamban Swamy Nagar, Thiruparankundram, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625005.
