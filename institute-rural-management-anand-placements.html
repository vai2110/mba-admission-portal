IRMA Placements: What Students Should Know

IRMA's campus placement programme is built to provide meaningful and challenging career opportunities. The institute says organisations from the business sector, PSUs, state and central governments, NGOs and multilateral agencies participate.

Quick answer: Is IRMA placement only for rural jobs?

No. IRMA graduates enter banking and finance, FMCG, retail, agribusiness, consulting, CSR, cooperatives, development and other management roles. The rural-management context influences the sectors and problems students are trained to understand.

What Jobs Do IRMA Graduates Get?

Corporate roles

  • Finance and accounts
  • Operations and production
  • Business/project management

Development roles

  • Rural development
  • Natural-resource management
  • Livelihoods and development programmes
  • Public-policy and nonprofit work

IRMA Placement Sectors

The institute's official placement information highlights business organisations, cooperatives, PSUs, government, NGOs and multilateral/development organisations. Historically published placement material also shows participation from BFSI, FMCG/retail, agribusiness, dairy/cooperatives, consulting and CSR/social-business organisations.

IRMA Top Recruiters & Recruiter Ecosystem

IRMA's official recruiter information includes organisations from diverse sectors. Examples appearing in official placement material include Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation/Amul, ITC, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, NDDB, Samunnati, Mother Dairy, Tata Steel and development organisations.

Recruiter names vary by batch. A company recruiting in one year should not be treated as a guaranteed recruiter for every future batch.

IRMA Placement Trends: How to Read the Numbers

IRMA maintains batch-wise placement pages for its flagship programme. The institute's website currently exposes historical batch pages including 2021-23, 2022-24 and 2023-25. Because some current reports are presented as images and are not consistently machine-readable, this page does not invent a three-year salary series where the official source cannot be directly verified in text.

Salary reality check

Highest salary is an outlier, not the expected salary. For decision-making, compare average/median salary, recruiter mix, role quality, sector fit and the total cost of the programme.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) Placements

IRMA publishes a separate placement page for the executive rural-management programme. For the second self-sponsored batch, the official page reports an average package of ₹13.67 LPA and lists recruiters including Samunnati Financial Services, ITC, GCMMF/Amul Dairy, TVS Srinivasan Service Trust, Banas Dairy and Jai Kisan.

Examples of roles on that official page include Projects Manager, Senior Executive - Sales & Marketing, Deputy Manager - CSR, Executive - IT, Associate Manager Operations, Marketing Manager and Account Manager.

IRMA Placements FAQs

Does IRMA have 100% placement?

IRMA's official placement communication describes a campus placement programme designed to provide meaningful career opportunities, but students should use the batch-specific placement report for the exact placement outcome of the relevant year.

What is the highest package at IRMA?

Do not use a single highest-package number without checking the batch and programme. IRMA maintains batch-wise placement reports, and the flagship programme's historical figures differ by batch.

Does IRMA place students in banks?

Yes. IRMA's placement ecosystem includes banking, financial services and insurance organisations, along with agribusiness, FMCG, cooperatives and development-sector recruiters.

Can IRMA graduates work in FMCG?

Yes. FMCG and retail are part of the recruiter ecosystem documented by IRMA's placement material.

Does IRMA offer placements for PGDM(RM-X)?

Yes. IRMA maintains a separate placement page for RM-X. Its current official page reports ₹13.67 LPA average for the second self-sponsored batch.

What should I compare instead of the highest package?

Compare median/average compensation where officially available, role, sector, recruiter quality, placement coverage and the total cost of the programme.
