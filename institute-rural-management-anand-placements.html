IRMA Placements 2025: Actual Highlights

The 2025 flagship PGDM(RM) placement report records a ₹31.84 LPA highest CTC, ₹15.64 LPA mean CTC, ₹15.80 LPA median CTC and ₹8.00 LPA lowest CTC. Placement coverage was 100% for the students who sought placement.

What does this mean for an applicant?

The median is slightly above the mean, so the typical reported outcome was around ₹15.8 lakh rather than the ₹31.84 lakh headline highest package. Compare median and mean alongside role, sector and recruiter quality.

IRMA Top Recruiters

Recruiters reported across the placement ecosystem include GCMMF (Amul), NDDB, Mother Dairy, Banas Dairy, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, IDFC First Bank, ITC, Maruti Suzuki and organisations from consulting, finance, agribusiness, FMCG and social-business segments.

Recruiter participation changes by batch. A company listed in a previous report should not be treated as a guaranteed recruiter for the next graduating class.

IRMA PGDM(RM-X) Placements

IRMA also reports a separate placement track for its executive rural-management programme. The official material cited in the existing page reports an average package of ₹13.67 LPA for the second self-sponsored batch, with recruiters including Samunnati Financial Services, ITC, GCMMF/Amul Dairy, TVS Srinivasan Service Trust, Banas Dairy and Jai Kisan.

Because RM-X is a separate programme with a different participant profile, its compensation should not be merged with the flagship PGDM(RM) figures.

IRMA Placements FAQs

What is the average package at IRMA?

For the 2025 flagship batch, the mean CTC was ₹15.64 LPA and the median CTC was ₹15.80 LPA.

What is the highest package at IRMA?

The highest CTC reported for the 2025 flagship batch was ₹31.84 LPA.

What is the lowest package at IRMA?

The lowest CTC reported for the 2025 flagship batch was ₹8.00 LPA.

Does IRMA have 100% placements?

The 2025 flagship placement data reports 100% placement coverage for students seeking placement. Students who opt out of the placement process should not be interpreted as placed.

Which sector hires the most IRMA students?

BFSI was the largest sector in the 2025 data, accounting for 40.34% of the batch placed.

Does IRMA offer corporate jobs?

Yes. The 2025 recruiter mix includes BFSI, FMCG, agribusiness, consumer businesses, consulting and other corporate organisations in addition to development-sector employers.
