Alliance University: What Students Should Know Before Applying

Alliance University is a multidisciplinary private university in Bengaluru with programmes across engineering, business, law, computing, design, liberal arts and sciences. The university states that it is recognised by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A+.

Because Alliance offers programmes across very different career tracks, students should not evaluate the university only from a single B.Tech or MBA outcome. The right comparison is course + fee + admission route + curriculum + campus + programme-level career outcomes.

Does Alliance University offer more than B.Tech and MBA?

Yes. Its current programme portfolio includes BBA, BCA, B.Com, integrated law programmes, B.Des, B.Sc., BA and other undergraduate and postgraduate options. Seven high-intent course guides are now linked below for students who want a dedicated programme-level comparison.

Alliance University Eligibility & Entrance Exams

Eligibility varies by programme. Many undergraduate business and computing programmes use 10+2 with around 50% aggregate as the baseline, while engineering and law have additional subject or degree requirements. The dedicated course guides above provide programme-specific eligibility and admission information.

Can you apply to Alliance without JEE?

For several programmes, yes. JEE is relevant to specified B.Tech routes, while BBA, BCA, B.Com and law have separate published admission pathways. Always check the exact course rather than assuming one entrance exam applies to the whole university.

Alliance University Fees 2026: Why Programme-Level Fees Matter

Alliance does not have one common fee for every course. Current programme pages publish different registration fees, instalments and total programme fees. For example, current published references show ₹12 lakh for BBA (Hons.), ₹10 lakh for BCA (Hons.), ₹8 lakh for B.Com (Hons.) and ₹17.5 lakh for BBA LL.B. (Hons.).

Alliance University Rankings, Accreditation & Recognition

Alliance states that it is accredited with NAAC A+ and recognised by the UGC. Its current institutional material also highlights NIRF 2025 positions of #20 in Law and #71 in Management. Students should interpret rankings within the category relevant to their chosen course.

Alliance University FAQs

What are the popular courses at Alliance University?

Seven dedicated guides on this portal now cover B.Tech CSE, B.Tech AI & Data Science, BBA, BCA, B.Com, BBA LL.B. and MBA. Alliance also offers B.Des, B.Sc., BA, LL.B., MCA and other programmes.

Which course is best at Alliance University?

There is no single best course for every student. The right choice depends on your career goal, eligibility, fee budget, curriculum and programme-level outcomes.

The BBA programme offers multiple management specialisations, experiential learning and internships. Students should compare its fee and career outcomes with alternative BBA colleges.

Is Alliance University NAAC accredited?

Yes. Alliance states that it is accredited with NAAC A+.
