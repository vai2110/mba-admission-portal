Who is eligible for Panjab University MBA 2026?

Basic eligibility

The UBS 2026 handbook requires a recognised bachelor's degree with the prescribed minimum marks. The handbook also provides specific alternative qualifications and category-wise concessions under university rules.

RequirementWhat the 2026 handbook says
Academic qualificationBachelor's degree in the prescribed manner from a recognised university; alternative professional qualifications are also specified.
General minimum50% aggregate for the standard bachelor's-degree route.
Reserved-category concession5% concession in eligibility for SC/ST/BC/PwD candidates, subject to rules.
Entrance requirementCandidate must appear in CAT 2025 for the 2026-27 UBS MBA admission cycle.

CAT 2025 for UBS MBA: how the merit is built

The 2026 UBS handbook makes CAT 2025 the basis for admission to its MBA programmes. Candidates should therefore treat CAT preparation as the central pre-admission task rather than assuming that the low fee means an easy admission process.

Important:

UBS does not publish a single guaranteed CAT percentile cutoff in the 2026 handbook. Do not label a coaching-site estimate as the official UBS cutoff.

The post-CAT process includes Group Discussion and Personal Interview. The university also publishes category-specific merit lists and counselling notices, so admission can involve multiple counselling rounds when seats remain vacant.

Panjab University MBA cutoff 2026

There is no single official fixed CAT cutoff percentile published in the 2026 UBS handbook. The university publishes merit lists and counselling outcomes instead. Historical or coaching-site percentile estimates should therefore be treated as indicative, not as an official guarantee.

How to use cutoff information

Use past merit lists, category-wise trends and your CAT score together. Also account for GD/PI performance and the specific programme you are applying to.

What not to do

Do not reject UBS because a third-party website says the cutoff is a particular percentile unless the number is clearly identified as an estimate.

Panjab University MBA Admission FAQs

Is CAT compulsory for UBS MBA?

Yes. The 2026 UBS handbook states that admission to the MBA programmes is based on CAT 2025, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

What is the UBS MBA eligibility?

The standard route requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate, with specified concessions and alternative qualifications under university rules.

What is the UBS MBA application fee?

The 2026 handbook lists ₹3,250 for general candidates and ₹1,625 for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Does UBS conduct GD and PI?

Yes. CAT is followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview for the regular MBA programmes.

Can I apply to multiple UBS MBA programmes?

Programme preferences and application instructions should be followed exactly as stated in the relevant admission notice. Check the current UBS application portal for the applicable preference rules.
