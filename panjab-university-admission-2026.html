How does Panjab University admission work?

Key point

There is no single Panjab University admission process. The route depends on the course, department, level and whether the programme is entrance-based, merit-based or handled by a specialised institute.

For 2026-27, PU operates separate online portals for UG and PG admissions. Individual departments and institutes then publish course-specific entrance, GD/PI, counselling and document-verification notices.

Panjab University UG Admission 2026

The UG portal covers a broad range of courses. The current 2026 portal lists programmes such as BA, BA Hons/Hons with Research, B.Com, B.A.-B.Ed, B.Sc and certificate/diploma options.

Admission through Panjab University regional centres

PU has regional centres including Muktsar, Ludhiana and Hoshiarpur. Some programmes are offered through these centres, so applicants should verify the exact campus/centre in the current admission notice.

Panjab University Admission FAQs

Is CUET compulsory for Panjab University?

No single answer applies to all PU courses. Admission is programme-specific and can be based on merit, PU-CET, JEE/JAC, CAT or another notified route.

What is the Panjab University admission last date 2026?

For the general UG 2026 portal, the published form-completion and fee-payment deadline was 8 July 2026. PG and specialised programme deadlines vary.

Does Panjab University conduct an entrance exam?

Yes, for selected programmes. PU-CET (UG) is one example; several departments and institutes have their own admission routes.

How can I check PU admission merit lists?

Use the official UG or PG admission portal and the department-specific notices linked there.
