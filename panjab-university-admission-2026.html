How does Panjab University admission work?

Key point

There is no single Panjab University admission process. The route depends on the course, department, level and whether the programme is entrance-based, merit-based or handled by a specialised institute.

For 2026-27, PU operates separate UG and PG admission portals. Individual departments and institutes can publish additional entrance, counselling, document-verification and merit-list schedules.

Panjab University Admission FAQs

What is the last date to apply for Panjab University Admission 2026?

For the general 2026 cycle, the published UG form and fee deadline was 8 July 2026, while the general PG deadline was 28 July 2026. A specialised course can have a different deadline, so the programme-specific notice should always be checked.

Is CUET compulsory for Panjab University?

No. CUET is not a universal admission requirement for every PU programme. Different courses use different routes such as merit, PU-CET, JEE/JAC, CAT, MET or other notified processes. The exact course notice determines the applicable route.

Which entrance exam is required for Panjab University B.Pharmacy?

B.Pharmacy is covered through PU-CET (UG). For the 2026 schedule, the entrance test was listed for 10 May 2026. Admission after the test also involves the university's notified admission/merit process.

Which entrance exam is required for Panjab University MBA?

The answer depends on the MBA programme and institute. PU's 2026 entrance schedule includes MET for selected UIAMS MBA programmes, while UBS and other management programmes can have their own eligibility and selection requirements. Students should not assume that one exam applies to every MBA at PU.

When does Panjab University admission usually start?

The recent general cycle shows a consistent early-summer pattern: the 2025 UG form opened on 3 June and the PG form on 10 June; the 2026 UG and PG forms opened on the same dates. For a programme without a separate schedule, early June is a useful planning window, but it is not an official future date.

How do I apply for Panjab University UG admission?

Start with the official UG Admissions portal, identify your programme, read its eligibility and admission route, complete the online form and fee payment, and then follow the merit-list, counselling or document-verification instructions issued for that course. Entrance-based programmes may require an additional test and admission form.

Does Panjab University have separate admission for regional centres?

Some programmes are offered through PU regional centres such as Muktsar, Ludhiana and Hoshiarpur. Applicants should check the programme listing and current notice carefully because the centre, eligibility, fee and admission route can differ from Chandigarh-campus programmes.

Are the tentative Panjab University admission dates official?

No. The tentative windows on this page are planning estimates based on the 2025 and 2026 general admission cycle. They are useful when a programme has not published its complete schedule, but an official department or institute notice always replaces the estimate.
