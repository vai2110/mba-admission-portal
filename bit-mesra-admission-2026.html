BIT Mesra MBA Admission 2026

The MBA route is separate from B.Tech admission. BIT Mesra's MBA notification specifies the accepted management entrance examinations and subsequent selection stages. Depending on the cycle, these may include CAT, XAT, CMAT and MAT; students should use the 2026 notification for the exact accepted-score list.

The institute evaluates candidates through the notified entrance-score and selection process. A valid bachelor's degree and the prescribed minimum marks are required.

How to apply to BIT Mesra?

  1. Choose the exact programme and campus.
  2. Check the current official eligibility rules.
  3. Appear for the required entrance examination where applicable.
  4. Complete the BIT Mesra application/counselling process within the notified window.
  5. Upload documents and pay the prescribed application fee.
  6. Track merit lists, counselling, seat confirmation and fee-payment notices.

Do not rely on a generic BIT Mesra date for every programme: B.Tech, MBA and other courses follow different admission mechanisms.

Documents generally required

  • Class 10 certificate/marksheet
  • Class 12 certificate/marksheet
  • Graduation marksheets for MBA and postgraduate applications
  • Entrance-exam scorecard where applicable
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Photo and signature
  • Identity proof

BIT Mesra Admission FAQs

What entrance exam is required for BIT Mesra B.Tech?

JEE Main is the principal entrance route for BIT Mesra's undergraduate engineering admission, followed by the applicable seat-allocation/counselling process.

Can I get BIT Mesra through JEE Main?

Yes. B.Tech admission uses JEE Main scores. The exact branch and seat depend on the applicable counselling process, category, rank and availability.

Does BIT Mesra accept CAT for MBA?

CAT is one of the management entrance routes used for BIT Mesra MBA admissions. The current notification should be checked for the complete accepted-exam list for the relevant batch.

Is there one admission date for all BIT Mesra courses?

No. Admission schedules differ by programme. Engineering admissions are linked to the undergraduate entrance/counselling calendar, while MBA and other programmes have separate notifications.

What is the eligibility for BIT Mesra MBA?

A bachelor's degree with the prescribed minimum marks is required. The exact percentage, accepted entrance exams and selection rules should be taken from the current MBA admission notification.
