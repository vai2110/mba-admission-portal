MUJ BCA / MCA

MUJ BCA 2026: Three Study Tracks

MUJ's BCA programme is three years and six semesters. The current programme page lists three study options: BCA, BCA-Cyber Security and BCA-Data Science. This makes the degree relevant for students who want a computer-application route without entering a four-year engineering programme.

The published annual tuition is ₹1.77 lakh. Registration is ₹10,000 and refundable caution is ₹10,000, giving a displayed admission amount of ₹1.97 lakh before hostel.

BCA, Cyber Security or Data Science: Which One?

TrackBest aligned withStudent should build
BCAGeneral software/application careersProgramming, databases, projects and internships
BCA Cyber SecuritySecurity operations and cyber rolesNetworking, Linux, security tools, labs and certifications
BCA Data ScienceAnalytics/data-oriented rolesPython, statistics, SQL, visualisation and portfolio projects

The track name alone will not make a student job-ready. Projects, internships, GitHub/portfolio evidence and technical interview preparation remain important.

MUJ MCA 2026: Four-Semester Postgraduate Route

MUJ's MCA has four semesters and is more technically structured than a general postgraduate business degree. The published fee is ₹1.78 lakh tuition per year, with ₹10,000 registration and ₹10,000 refundable caution; the displayed amount at admission is ₹1.98 lakh.

MCA Curriculum: Semester-by-Semester Information

SemesterPublished subjects / work
1Discrete Mathematical Structures, Web Technologies, C, RDBMS, Data Visualization and labs
2Computer Networks, Java, Operating System, Data Structures & Algorithms, elective and labs; aptitude/technical development
3Unix & Shell Programming, Android Development, Software Engineering & Project Management, electives and minor project
4Major Project

The sequence gives MCA students a conventional programming and systems base before project work. Students targeting data or software roles should use electives and projects to develop a visible technical portfolio.

Career Evidence Published by MUJ

The BCA/MCA programme page displays alumni examples associated with companies including Accenture, Planet Spark, CodefyLabs, TCS, EY India and Vinove Software. These examples show recruiter presence but should not be read as a guaranteed placement list for every batch.

For a computer-applications student

Compare the curriculum, project opportunities and internship access first. Then use placement statistics to understand the broader university recruiter ecosystem, rather than assuming the university-level ₹9 lakh average applies to BCA or MCA.

MUJ BCA & MCA FAQs

How long is BCA at MUJ?

BCA is three years and six semesters.

What BCA specialisations are available?

The current programme page lists BCA, BCA-Cyber Security and BCA-Data Science.

What is the MCA fee at MUJ?

MUJ currently lists ₹1.78 lakh annual tuition, ₹10,000 registration and ₹10,000 refundable caution; the displayed admission amount is ₹1.98 lakh.

Does MCA include a major project?

Yes. The published curriculum places the major project in semester IV and a minor project in semester III.
