MUJ BCA / MCA

MUJ BCA 2026: Three Study Tracks

MUJ's BCA programme is three years and six semesters. The current programme page lists BCA, BCA-Cyber Security and BCA-Data Science. This is the computer-application route for students who want programming and application development without choosing a four-year engineering degree.

The published annual tuition is ₹1.77 lakh. Registration is ₹10,000 and refundable caution is ₹10,000, giving a displayed admission amount of ₹1.97 lakh before hostel.

BCA, Cyber Security or Data Science: Which One?

TrackAcademic directionSkills to build
BCAGeneral software/application careersProgramming, databases, projects and internships

Track selection should follow the job you want: the specialisation is a starting point, not a substitute for projects, internships, certifications or technical interview preparation.

MUJ MCA 2026: Four-Semester Postgraduate Route

MUJ's MCA has four semesters and is more technically structured than a general postgraduate degree. The published fee is ₹1.78 lakh tuition per year, with ₹10,000 registration and ₹10,000 refundable caution; the displayed amount at admission is ₹1.98 lakh.

Who should compare MCA with BCA? A graduate who already has a bachelor's degree and wants a postgraduate computing qualification should compare MCA's four-semester curriculum, project work and total cost with specialised alternatives before applying.

MCA Curriculum: Semester-by-Semester Information

4Major ProjectPortfolio-ready capstone work

The sequence matters: students move from programming/database/system foundations into a minor project and then a major project. For software or data roles, those projects should be turned into demonstrable portfolio evidence.

Career Evidence Published by MUJ

The BCA/MCA programme page displays alumni examples associated with companies including Accenture, Planet Spark, CodefyLabs, TCS, EY India and Vinove Software. These examples show recruiter presence but are not a guarantee for every batch.

Do not copy the university-wide ₹9 lakh average onto BCA/MCA.

The published ₹9 lakh average is a university-level figure. For computer-applications students, curriculum, project quality, internships, technical skills and programme-specific outcomes are more useful evidence.
