Jain University BBA Overview

JAIN University's Center for Management Studies offers several BBA formats, including BBA Honours/Honours with Research, Corporate BBA, Entrepreneurship, Sports Management, Branding and Advertising, Events, Entertainment and Media Management, Global Business, Digital Business and Business Analytics and Intelligence.

Is BBA at Jain University only for Commerce students?

No. The official BBA page states that students who have passed 10+2 in Science, Commerce or Arts from a recognised board can apply, subject to the programme's specific requirements.

BBA Eligibility

The official BBA page states that applicants should have passed 10+2 in Science, Commerce or Arts from PUC, ISC, CBSE or an equivalent recognised board. Specific programme rules can vary.

BBA FAQs

Can Arts students apply for BBA at Jain University?

Yes. The current BBA page accepts 10+2 pass students from Science, Commerce or Arts, subject to the chosen programme's rules.

Which BBA specialisation is best?

It depends on the intended career. Business Analytics suits data-led roles, Finance suits accounting/finance pathways, while Branding, Events, Sports and Entrepreneurship are more specialised choices.

What is the BBA placement package?

The current BBA page reports ₹24.80 LPA highest CTC and ₹6.56 LPA average salary for 2024-25.

Is BBA better than BCom?

BBA is generally more management-oriented, while BCom is more commerce/accounting/finance oriented. Choose based on curriculum and career plans rather than the degree name alone.
