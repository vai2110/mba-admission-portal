MTech Admission 2026

The official MTech portal asks candidates to register on COAP 2026 before applying to IIT (ISM) for MTech admission. The 2026 institute application window ran from 24 March to 19 April 2026.

Eligibility

Eligibility varies by MTech department and specialisation. GATE-qualified candidates should check the exact programme requirements and eligible GATE paper before applying. Sponsored candidates may have separate written-test/interview requirements.

2026 status

The 2026 application deadline has passed. Future applicants should use the next official notice for new dates.

MTech Fees

MTech fees can include tuition, hostel, registration, examination, medical, internet and other semester charges. The amount depends on category and the current fee structure.

MTech Career Scope

Career outcomes depend on specialisation. IIT (ISM)'s engineering, energy, mining, earth-science and technology ecosystem can support technical industry roles as well as research-oriented pathways.

Student Discussion: Is MTech Worth It?

MTech is most useful when the specialisation directly improves your technical depth, research profile or eligibility for the roles you want. Compare labs, faculty, thesis opportunities, placements and total cost.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MTech FAQs

Is GATE required?

The standard route uses GATE and COAP, while sponsored candidates can have separate requirements.

When was the 2026 application deadline?

The official portal lists 19 April 2026 as the last date for the 2026 MTech application.

Can I apply after the deadline?

The 2026 application window has closed; applicants should wait for the next official cycle.
