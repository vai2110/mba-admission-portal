What Is MBA IMPACT?

GEU describes MBA IMPACT as its flagship programme of excellence from Graphic Era School of Management. It is a two-year postgraduate programme designed around leadership, strategic thinking, global intelligence, collaboration, critical analysis and creative transformation.

Who should choose IMPACT?

It is suited to students who want a broader leadership-oriented MBA with experiential learning and international exposure rather than a narrowly technical specialisation.

Eligibility & Selection

The programme is a two-year postgraduate MBA. GEU's management admissions information states that graduates and working professionals can apply, with admission prepared on merit under university norms. The programme page also describes provisional admission based on projected scores for meritorious students.

Admission Process

Applicants complete the university's online application, upload documents, pay the application fee and complete the admission process after evaluation. The management department states that recognised CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/NMAT scores may provide exemption from the aptitude test under university policy, followed by interview/assessment as applicable.

Admission Dates 2026 & 2027

GEU currently shows Admissions 2026-27 open. It does not publish a single fixed final MBA IMPACT deadline on the current course page.

MBA IMPACT Curriculum

The programme covers core management disciplines such as finance, marketing, strategy and operations, while also exploring sustainable business practices, corporate social responsibility, entrepreneurship and international business. The university highlights strategic thinking, global intelligence, teamwork, problem-solving and decision-making.

International & Industry Exposure

GEU describes MBA IMPACT as offering an international industrial tour/IIM Indore immersion opportunity, internships and interaction with experts from leading business schools and industry. The official programme page also highlights collaboration with top B-schools and summer internship exposure.

Career Prospects

GEU positions MBA IMPACT graduates for management and leadership roles and entrepreneurial paths. The programme's placement material discusses internships, PPOs and placements, but the university's ₹61.99 lakh highest-package figure is university-wide and specifically associated with B.Tech CSE, not MBA IMPACT.

MBA IMPACT FAQs

Is MBA IMPACT a two-year MBA?

Yes. GEU's official course page lists MBA IMPACT as a two-year postgraduate programme.

What makes IMPACT different from the regular MBA?

IMPACT is positioned as a flagship programme with emphasis on creative transformation, leadership, global exposure, industry interaction and an international/IIM Indore immersion opportunity.

Is ₹61.99 lakh the MBA IMPACT package?

No. GEU's placement material identifies that ₹61.99 lakh figure with B.Tech CSE under the university brand.
