B.Arch at AMU: Overview

The Bachelor of Architecture programme at AMU is offered by the Department of Architecture under the engineering and technology ecosystem. The current department page describes it as a five-year undergraduate programme and lists 24 seats, including NRI/foreign national and diploma-topper seats.

The programme is designed for students interested in architectural design, building systems, professional practice and higher studies in architecture and planning. AMU's department notes that graduates can pursue private-sector, government/semi-government or self-employment pathways after meeting professional registration requirements.

AMU B.Arch Eligibility 2026

The department's current undergraduate page requires the Senior Secondary School Certificate of AMU or an equivalent examination with at least 50% marks in aggregate of English, Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. A Diploma in Engineering of AMU with at least 50% marks in aggregate is also listed.

Candidates should also check the current Council of Architecture and AMU admission rules applicable to the admission year, especially for diploma-based eligibility.

AMU B.Arch Admission 2026

AMU's admission system has a course-specific architecture aptitude requirement. The admission guide states that B.Arch selection is through the prescribed admission test and that candidates, including eligible diploma rank holders, need to qualify in NATA to become eligible for selection.

The 2026 admission cycle also included an official AMU notice specifically for B.Arch aspirants and subsequent admission/counselling activity. Candidates should follow the current Controller of Examinations notice rather than relying on an older exam pattern.

Application workflow

AMU B.Arch Fees 2026-27

The 2026-27 fee appendix lists ₹25,500 as the fee payable at admission for B.Arch. This is the admission-time amount and should not be represented as the full five-year cost.

Students should separately verify subsequent university charges, hostel, mess, studio/material expenses and any other applicable costs.

B.Arch Curriculum & Practical Learning

Architecture education combines design studios with building construction, services, planning, structures, graphics, history and professional practice. AMU's Department of Architecture publishes a current B.Arch syllabus for students admitted from the 2025-26 session onwards.

The department's programme outcomes emphasise professional architecture capability, higher education and research readiness, and employability in private and public-sector architecture and allied fields.

Career Scope After B.Arch

Architecture practice

Graduates can work with architectural firms and design practices and build professional experience toward registration and practice.

Government / PSU / public sector

Architecture graduates can explore government, semi-government and public-sector opportunities subject to recruitment rules.

Design & allied fields

Possible directions include urban design, interior-related work, building services coordination, visualisation and allied construction roles.

Higher studies

AMU graduates can pursue postgraduate study in architecture, planning, construction management and related disciplines.

AMU B.Arch Cutoff 2026

Because admission depends on the applicable AMU process, aptitude qualification and selection lists, a single cutoff mark should not be quoted without the relevant year, category and stage.

Students should follow AMU's admission lists and notices for the exact session and compare previous-year outcomes only as trend indicators.

B.Arch Aptitude & Admission Preparation

The B.Arch admission route requires students to satisfy the prescribed qualifying-examination conditions and the architecture aptitude requirement specified by AMU, including the NATA qualification condition stated in the admission guide. Candidates should prepare for architecture aptitude rather than treating the process like a conventional PCM-only engineering admission.

What to build before joining

Develop observation, visualisation, basic drawing, spatial understanding, design thinking and awareness of buildings and the built environment. These skills also help later when studio-based learning becomes a major part of the programme.

B.Arch Curriculum: Why Studio Work Matters

Architecture is a studio-heavy discipline. Along with history and theory, students work on design problems, drawings, models, building construction, structures, services, planning and professional practice. AMU's Department of Architecture publishes a syllabus for students admitted from the 2025-26 session onward.

Students should therefore evaluate an architecture programme through studio culture, faculty guidance, software and visualisation exposure, construction learning, site visits, portfolio development and professional practice opportunities—not simply through the admission fee.

Career Path After B.Arch

Architectural practice

Work with architecture and design firms while developing professional experience and the requirements applicable to architectural practice.

Urban planning

Higher study can open routes into urban and regional planning and related built-environment disciplines.

Construction & project roles

Graduates can explore construction management, coordination, design documentation and allied roles.

Higher studies

M.Arch, M.Plan and allied postgraduate pathways are relevant for specialisation and academic careers.

B.Arch Cost, Studio Expenses & ROI

The admission-time B.Arch fee in the 2026-27 guide is ₹25,500, but architecture students should also plan for studio materials, printing, model-making, software and project-related expenses. The practical cost of the degree is therefore broader than the university fee alone.
