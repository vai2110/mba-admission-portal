GIM PGDM: What Does the Programme Offer?

GIM's flagship PGDM is a two-year full-time residential programme with six terms. The institute describes the curriculum as a blend of theory and practice, reviewed with input from an Academic Advisory Committee of industry and academic experts. The programme is designed for broad management careers rather than a single functional niche.

Who is the PGDM best suited for?

It suits students who want a general management foundation, access to multiple functional electives, live projects and a strong residential campus experience. It is also a practical option for candidates who want the flexibility to explore finance, marketing, operations, strategy, HR and analytics before specialising through electives.

GIM PGDM Curriculum, Electives & Learning

The six-term structure combines compulsory management foundations with elective learning. Students can develop depth in areas such as finance, marketing, operations, strategy, organisational behaviour, economics and analytics.

Academic strengths

  • Six-term residential management programme.
  • Core management foundation.
  • Functional and cross-functional electives.
  • Industry-informed curriculum review.
  • Course of Independent Study option with faculty guidance.

Experiential exposure

  • Summer projects.
  • Live industry assignments.
  • International Consulting Bootcamp.
  • Cross-cultural projects with University of Antwerp students.
  • Student-led clubs and competitions.

GIM PGDM FAQs

What is the GIM PGDM fee?

The latest published 2026-28 academic fee is ₹21.45 lakh. The 2027-29 fee is not yet published.

What is the GIM PGDM intake?

GIM lists an intake of 300 students for the flagship PGDM.

Which exams are accepted?

GIM lists CAT 2026, XAT 2027 and eligible GMAT scores for the full-time PGDM admission process.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. Freshers can apply. Work experience is considered as part of evaluation.

Does GIM have an Achiever's Round?

Yes. The Achiever's Round is profile-based and considers achievements in academics, sports, co-curricular activities and professional experience. Exam scores are incorporated later.
