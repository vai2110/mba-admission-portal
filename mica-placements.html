MICA Placements: What the Numbers Actually Tell You

MICA's placement outcomes are shaped by its specialist positioning in strategic marketing, communication, consumer insight and newer analytical management roles. The current official admissions page reports a ₹40.91 LPA highest CTC and ₹19.22 LPA average CTC for its 2025 placement snapshot.

Should you judge MICA only by the highest package?

No. MICA has a concentrated programme and role mix. Students should compare average CTC, role type, sector, recruiter quality and the fit between PGDM-C/PGDM specialisation and the target career.

MICA Recruiters and Placement Sectors

MICA's official reports show recruiter participation across BFSI and fintech, consumer technology, FMCG, consulting, media, advertising, healthcare, manufacturing and other sectors.

Consumer & Technology

Recruiters reported include Google, Flipkart, CleverTap, Ola and other consumer or technology businesses.

FMCG & Consumer Brands

Recruiters across reports include Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Coca-Cola, Titan, L'Oréal, Nivea and PUMA India.

BFSI & Fintech

Reported names include Mastercard, Kotak Mahindra Bank, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, ICICI Bank, TATA AIA and others.

Advertising & Communication

Placement reports include agencies and creative organisations such as DDB Mudra, Leo Burnett, Saatchi & Saatchi, BBDO and Edelman.

MICA Placements FAQs

What is the highest package at MICA?

MICA's current admissions page reports a highest CTC of ₹40.91 LPA in its 2025 placement snapshot.

What is the average package at MICA?

The current admissions page reports an average CTC of ₹19.22 LPA for its 2025 placement snapshot.

Does MICA have 100% placements?

MICA states that its PGP students have consistently had 100% placements. Students should read the detailed placement report for the exact cohort and programme context.

Which companies recruit from MICA?

MICA's reports list recruiters across Google, Flipkart, Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Coca-Cola, Titan, Mastercard, L'Oréal, Nivea, PUMA India, Accenture Strategy & Consulting and many others.

Which is better for placements, MICA PGDM-C or PGDM?

Neither is universally better. PGDM-C is more aligned with brand, marketing and communication careers, while PGDM is more aligned with analytics, AI, consulting, fintech and technology-driven strategy.
