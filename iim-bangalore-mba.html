IIM Bangalore MBA: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is IIM Bangalore's flagship two-year, full-time residential MBA. It lasts six trimesters over two years and includes a summer project between the first and second year.

IIMB describes the programme as a combination of strong fundamentals, contemporary management issues, analytical reasoning, experiential learning and leadership development. In the second year, students can choose from a range of electives and may opt for an exchange programme at select international business schools.

Who Is Eligible for IIM Bangalore MBA?

A candidate needs a bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. For SC, ST and PwD candidates, the minimum is 45%. IIMB also specifies eligibility for candidates who have completed CA, ICWA or CS after Class XII under the conditions in the admission document.

Final-year students

Applicants studying in the final year can apply, subject to the institute's requirements and submission of the required qualifying documents.

What Is the IIM Bangalore MBA Selection Process?

1. Apply through CAT

Domestic applicants apply through the Common Admission Test (CAT). The CAT score forms a major part of the initial assessment.

2. First shortlist

IIMB uses a multi-parameter pre-PI score. For the 2026-28 cycle, the published weights were CAT 55, Class 10 10, Class 12 10, bachelor's 10, work experience/professional course 10 and gender diversity 5.

3. WAT and Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates attend a Written Ability Test and Personal Interview. The WAT and PI are conducted as the second stage of selection.

4. Final composite score

The post-PI score combines PI, WAT, CAT, school and graduation performance and work experience/professional course as prescribed by IIMB. Final offers are made strictly on the post-PI score within the applicable category process.

What Will You Study in IIM Bangalore MBA?

The first year builds a broad management foundation. IIMB's programme design combines conceptual learning with case-based and experiential learning and covers major management domains. The second year gives students more flexibility through electives.

Learning focus

  • Conceptual and analytical reasoning
  • Contemporary management issues
  • Leadership and critical thinking
  • Experiential and action-oriented learning
  • Summer project and elective depth

Student decision point

The PGP is broad rather than a single-function MBA. Your second-year electives and projects become important when aligning the programme with consulting, finance, product, marketing, operations or entrepreneurship goals.

Questions to Resolve Before Choosing IIM Bangalore’s PGP MBA

Can a high CAT percentile guarantee an IIM Bangalore interview call?

No. CAT is one component of a composite screening process. IIMB also evaluates school, higher-secondary and graduation academics, work experience and other factors specified in that year's policy. The published minimum percentile is an eligibility threshold for screening, not a prediction tool or a guaranteed call.

Is the two-year PGP MBA suitable for someone with three to five years of experience?

It can be, but compare the career outcome you want with the opportunity cost of leaving work for two years. The PGP is a full-time residential programme; the one-year EPGP requires at least five years of post-bachelor's full-time work experience, while PGPEM is a weekend option requiring at least six years for the 2027–29 cycle. Eligibility alone does not decide which format is better—career-switch goals, current role, financing and whether you can pause employment matter.

What does the PGP curriculum actually cover?

IIMB's first year includes core subjects such as accounting, marketing, managerial economics, decision sciences, organisational design, corporate finance, operations, competition and strategy, and managing people. The second year offers electives across functions, including finance, marketing, analytics, operations and strategy. Students should check the current term-wise curriculum rather than assuming every elective runs every year.

What do student discussions say about profile-based shortlisting and workload?

Public Reddit discussions from applicants and people identifying as current students repeatedly raise two practical concerns: a strong CAT score does not make conversion predictable, and the programme can be academically demanding. One 2026 applicant/student discussion also highlights the trade-off for candidates with several years of work experience: the value of a career pivot must justify two years away from employment. These are individual experiences, not verified institute-wide findings or admissions rules. Read the IIM Bangalore applicant AMA and discussion on joining PGP with 4.5 years of experience as perspectives, not guarantees.

IIM Bangalore MBA FAQs

Is IIM Bangalore MBA a two-year programme?

Yes. The PGP is a two-year, full-time residential MBA spread across six trimesters with a summer project.

What is the minimum CAT cutoff?

For the 2026-28 admission cycle, the published first-shortlist minimum for General candidates was 85 overall with 80 VARC, 75 DILR and 75 QA. Actual interview-call scores were higher.

How much is the IIM Bangalore MBA fee?

The published domestic PGP fee for 2026-28 is ₹26 lakh, plus a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit and ₹30,000 mess advance per term.

Can a non-engineer apply for IIM Bangalore PGP MBA?

Yes. IIMB accepts a bachelor's degree in any discipline, subject to the published minimum marks and the cycle-specific admission policy. The final shortlist is multi-factor, so degree stream alone does not determine admission.

Does IIM Bangalore PGP require work experience?

Work experience is not a universal minimum eligibility requirement for the two-year PGP. Relevant experience can contribute to the selection score, and the exact weightage must be checked in the admission policy for the cycle.

Is the ₹26 lakh PGP fee the complete cost of attendance?

No. The published 2026–28 programme fee is ₹26 lakh, with a ₹30,000 refundable caution deposit and ₹30,000 mess advance each term payable separately. Taxes, personal expenses and any separately notified charges can affect the final amount.

Are PGP placements and PGPBA placements reported separately?

IIMB's 2026 final placement communication reports the PGP and PGPBA cohort together. Do not interpret the combined salary or offer figures as a PGP-only statistic unless the institute publishes a separate programme-wise breakdown.
