The median increased from ₹8.60 lakh in 2022-23 to ₹9.50 lakh in 2023-24 and ₹10.12 lakh in 2024-25. This is a positive trend, not a guarantee for the next batch.

NDIM Recruiters & Industry Exposure

NDIM reports 250+ top companies recruiting each year and highlights corporate mentors, internships and live projects. These are institute-reported ecosystem figures and do not mean every recruiter hires from every programme.

What Jobs Can NDIM PGDM Graduates Target?

Depending on profile and specialisation, students can target roles across finance, marketing, sales, consulting, operations, HR and business analysis. Role quality and functional alignment should be considered alongside salary.
