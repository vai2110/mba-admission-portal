NDIM Placements: Quick Answer

What do the official numbers show?

NDIM's latest NIRF submission reports 500 students placed out of 500 graduates in the minimum stipulated time for academic year 2024-25, with a ₹10.12 lakh median salary. The same disclosure reports 504 placed out of 505 graduates for 2023-24 with ₹9.50 lakh median salary, and 455 of 455 with ₹8.60 lakh median salary for 2022-23.

NDIM Recruiters & Industry Exposure

NDIM states that more than 250 top companies visit for campus placements each year. The institute also highlights 354 active corporate mentors and a large recruiter network across sectors. These are institute-reported ecosystem figures, not a guarantee that every company hires from every programme.

Useful placement ecosystem signals

  • 250+ companies reported visiting annually
  • Industry mentoring and corporate skill development
  • Internships and live projects are part of the institute's career-readiness positioning

What the headline number cannot tell you

  • How many offers each recruiter made
  • Role and function distribution
  • Salary distribution within a batch
  • Programme-specific placement differences

What Jobs Can NDIM PGDM Graduates Target?

Depending on specialisation and profile, NDIM graduates can target roles across finance, marketing, sales, consulting, operations, HR, business analysis and related management functions. NDIM's own career material lists roles such as management trainee, business analyst, consultant, marketing manager, sales manager, operations manager and HR manager.

Students interested in Finance should evaluate analyst, banking, corporate-finance and financial-services roles; Marketing students should compare brand, digital, sales and market-facing roles; general PGDM students can keep a wider functional shortlist.

Student verdict: The official three-year median series is the strongest data point to use when comparing NDIM. The institute's highest domestic/international package claims are useful as context, but they should not replace median salary and placed-student counts in your decision.

NDIM Placements FAQs

What is the latest NDIM median salary?

The latest NIRF data reviewed reports a ₹10.12 lakh median salary for students graduating in minimum stipulated time in academic year 2024-25.

How many students were placed at NDIM?

The latest NIRF submission reports 500 placed out of 500 students graduating in minimum stipulated time for 2024-25.

Does NDIM have 100% placements?

NDIM states a 100% placement record on its website, while its NIRF submission reports 500 placed out of 500 graduates in minimum stipulated time for 2024-25. Students should note the reporting definitions used by each source.

What is NDIM's highest package?

NDIM's current admissions page reports a highest domestic placement of ₹16.1 lakh and highest international placement of ₹32.5 lakh. These are institute-reported highest offers and should not be treated as typical salaries.
