Which sectors recruit from MANAGE Hyderabad?

MANAGE's official placement information shows that the programme is not restricted to one type of agriculture company. Recruiter demand has come from procurement, trading, banking, insurance, agro-chemicals, seeds, agro-processing, fertilizers and retail.

Top recruiters and types of companies

MANAGE's official placement pages list recruiters from agriculture, food, chemicals, finance, retail, consulting and commodity markets. Examples include ITC, KRIBHCO, BASF, Coromandel, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Cargill, MCX and NCDEX.

The exact recruiter mix changes by batch. Students should therefore use recruiter names as evidence of sector reach, not as a promise that every company will recruit every year.

MANAGE Hyderabad Placements vs Fee: what does the data suggest?

The published 2026-28 programme fee is ₹9.75 lakh, while the 2023-25 batch recorded a ₹12.38 LPA average and ₹12 LPA median CTC. On a simple headline comparison, the annual average CTC is above the full two-year programme fee, but this is not the same as a first-year take-home salary or guaranteed ROI.

Students should consider loan interest, taxes, relocation, the actual role, variable pay and career progression. The strongest ROI case is likely for an eligible student who specifically wants agribusiness and related sectors and values the specialised recruiter base.

What is the average package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

What is the median package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The 2023-25 final placement announcement reports a ₹12 LPA median CTC. Median is useful because it shows the middle salary and is less affected by a few unusually high offers.

Does MANAGE Hyderabad really have 100% placement?

MANAGE states that PGDM ABM has achieved 100% final placements since inception in 1996. The current mandatory disclosure also reports 100% final placements for the recent batches.

How many students were placed in the 2023-25 batch?

The MANAGE Placement Cell announced that 100 students were successfully placed in the 2023-25 final placement process.

How many companies recruited in 2023-25?

The 2023-25 placement announcement reported 45 recruiters, including 20 first-time recruiters. The recruiter mix can change every year.

Which companies recruit from MANAGE Hyderabad?

Official recruiter lists include companies such as ITC, KRIBHCO, BASF, Coromandel, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Cargill, MCX and NCDEX, among many others.

Which sector hires the most MANAGE students?

The exact mix changes by batch. In the official 2022-24 recruiter guide, agri-inputs accounted for 33% of final placements, followed by BFSI at 25% and consultancy at 17%.

Are MANAGE placements only in agriculture companies?

No. The official recruiter mix includes BFSI, consultancy, procurement, FMCG, commodity trading and food processing in addition to agri-input companies.

What was the summer internship stipend at MANAGE?

For 2022-24, the official recruiter guide reported a ₹2 lakh highest stipend and ₹58,000 average stipend for two months. These figures should not be assumed for every newer batch.

Is MANAGE worth it for placements?

It is worth considering if your career target is agribusiness or closely related sectors. The programme combines a ₹9.75 lakh published fee with a 2023-25 average CTC of ₹12.38 LPA and 100% placement. Students seeking completely sector-neutral management careers should compare MANAGE with general MBA/PGDM institutes as well.

Does the highest package mean every student gets a similar salary?

No. The ₹21 LPA highest CTC is the top reported offer. The average was ₹12.38 LPA and median ₹12 LPA for 2023-25, which are better measures for understanding the broader outcome.
