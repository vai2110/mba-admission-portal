MANAGE 2023-25 Placement Report: key numbers

The 28th batch concluded with 100% placement. The highest CTC was ₹21.00 LPA and the average CTC was ₹12.38 LPA. MANAGE reported that the batch size had increased from 66 to 100 students.

The institute's 2024 bulletin for the previous batch reported ₹20 LPA highest, ₹12.33 LPA average and ₹11.60 LPA median. The 2023-25 average therefore improved modestly over 2022-24.

MANAGE Placement Recruiters

Official MANAGE placement pages list recruiters across agri-business and allied sectors. Examples include ITC, Godrej Agrovet, Olam, EY, KPMG, BASF, Coromandel, PI Industries, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, IndusInd Bank, Ujjivan Small Finance Bank, Cargill, MCX and NCDEX.

The institute also reports recruiters from animal nutrition, seeds, agrochemicals, food, procurement, retail and development.

What do MANAGE placement numbers mean for students?

  • 100% placement means the institute reports placement for all eligible students in the stated batch; it should not be interpreted as every student receiving the highest package.
  • Average CTC ₹12.38 LPA is more useful for comparing typical outcomes than the ₹21 LPA highest package.
  • The sector mix shows that MANAGE is not limited to traditional agriculture companies; BFSI, consultancy, procurement and FMCG also recruit.
  • Because the programme is specialised, students should compare MANAGE with other agri-business programmes rather than only general MBA colleges.

MANAGE Placements FAQs

What is the average package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The official 2023-25 placement figure is ₹12.38 LPA.

What is the highest package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The highest CTC reported for the 2023-25 batch was ₹21 LPA.

Does MANAGE have 100% placement?

Yes. MANAGE states that PGDM ABM has achieved 100% placements since inception, and the 2023-25 batch also recorded 100% placement.

Which companies recruit from MANAGE?

Recruiters listed by MANAGE include ITC, Godrej Agrovet, Olam, EY, KPMG, BASF, Coromandel, PI Industries, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and other companies across agri-business and allied sectors.

Is MANAGE placement good for non-agriculture jobs?

The programme is specialised in agri-business, but placements include BFSI, consultancy, FMCG, procurement and commodity trading in addition to agri-input and allied sectors.
