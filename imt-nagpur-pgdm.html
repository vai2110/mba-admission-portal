IMT Nagpur PGDM: Programme Overview

IMT Nagpur's PGDM is a two-year, full-time, fully residential programme conducted through a trimester system. The official brochure describes a common first year followed by specialization and elective choices in the second year.

Students can build expertise in areas such as Finance, Marketing, HR & OB, Operations Management, Economics, General Management and Business Analytics & IT. The PGDM is designed around managerial skills, analytical thinking, technical knowledge, soft skills and industry exposure.

Who should choose the core PGDM?

The core PGDM is the most flexible option for students who have not finalized a single functional area or who want to combine two management domains.

IMT Nagpur PGDM Eligibility & Admission 2026

IMT Nagpur's selection process considers CAT, XAT, CMAT or GMAT scores along with past academic record, work experience and personal interview performance. Work experience is not compulsory.

FactorWhat to know
Entrance examsCAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT considered.
Work experienceNot mandatory; relevant experience can add weight.
SelectionConsolidated score + academics + experience + PI.
CutoffNo pre-decided cutoff; historical stopping point was 75 percentile for CAT/XAT and 90+ for CMAT.

IMT Nagpur PGDM Curriculum & Specializations

The first year provides common management foundations. In the second year, students can choose functional expertise and electives. Official curriculum material lists subjects across finance, marketing, analytics, HR, operations, economics and general management.

AreaExamples of learning
FinanceFinancial statement analysis, investment management, derivatives, banking and financial modelling.
MarketingBrand management, digital marketing, marketing analytics, consumer and sales subjects.
Business Analytics & ITData analytics, visualization, business intelligence, big data and Python-based analytics.
HR & OBTalent management, performance management, leadership and strategic HRM.
OperationsSupply chain, operations strategy, quality and project management.

IMT Nagpur PGDM Placements & Career Scope

Career outcomes depend on specialization, role and batch. The official admissions FAQ reports ₹36.33 LPA as the highest package for the 2022-24 batch, with Top-10 and Top-20 averages of ₹17.16 LPA and ₹14.47 LPA respectively.

Career paths can span consulting, BFSI, marketing, sales, analytics, operations, HR, product and general management. Summer internships are an important part of the residential PGDM experience.

IMT Nagpur PGDM FAQs

Is IMT Nagpur PGDM equivalent to MBA?

IMT Nagpur states that its PGDM programmes are equivalent to MBA for higher studies.

Is CAT mandatory?

The institute considers CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT scores. Check the current admission notice for the applicable cycle and route.

What is the PGDM fee?

The official 2025-27 total fee reference is ₹15 lakh.

Can freshers apply?

Yes. Work experience is not compulsory.
