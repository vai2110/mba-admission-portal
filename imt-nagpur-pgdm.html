IMT Nagpur PGDM: Programme Overview

Quick answer

IMT Nagpur's PGDM is a two-year, full-time residential management programme conducted through a trimester system. The core route gives students flexibility across Finance, Marketing, HR & OB, Operations, Economics, General Management and Business Analytics & IT.

The programme is designed for students who want a broad management foundation before building functional depth. The first year focuses on common management learning, while later terms provide elective and specialization choices.

IMT Nagpur PGDM FAQs

Is IMT Nagpur PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

IMT Nagpur offers PGDM rather than an MBA degree. The institute states that its PGDM programmes have AIU equivalence to MBA for higher studies.

What is the IMT Nagpur PGDM fee?

The official 2026-28 total is ₹16 lakh. The fee sheet lists ₹15.27 lakh academic fees, ₹50,000 admission fee, ₹8,000 alumni membership and ₹15,000 refundable caution money.

Can a fresher apply?

Yes. Work experience is not compulsory. Freshers should focus on entrance performance, academics, PI preparation and a clear career narrative.

What specializations are available?

The PGDM ecosystem covers Finance, Marketing, HR & OB, Operations, Economics, General Management and Business Analytics & IT. Separate PGDM Marketing and PGDM Financial Management programmes are also offered.

What is the IMT Nagpur cutoff?

There is no fixed pre-decided cutoff. The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT, 90+ for CMAT and 550 for GMAT.

Does the PGDM include hostel and mess?

Yes. The institute's current fee information says the ₹16 lakh residential total includes hostel and mess, along with books and admission fee.
