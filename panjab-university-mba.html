1. UBS MBA — flagship programme

Best for

Students who want the broadest management route and flexibility to target finance, marketing, consulting, operations, analytics or general management roles.

The regular MBA is the flagship UBS programme. The 2026 admission route uses CAT 2025 followed by GD and PI. The programme is designed around management fundamentals and elective areas rather than restricting students to one narrow function from the beginning.

FactorUBS MBA
Duration2 years
EntranceCAT 2025
Post-CAT stagesGD + PI
Regular tuition reference₹31,675/year in 2026 handbook, excluding separately listed charges

2. MBA International Business

UBS's MBA in International Business is aimed at students interested in international markets, trade, cross-border business and global corporate roles. The official course listing shows a two-year duration and a published intake of 30 plus category-specific seats.

Good fit for

Students interested in export/import, global supply chains, international marketing, trade finance and multinational business.

Check before choosing

Do not assume the programme guarantees an international job. Placement outcomes depend on role, recruiter and individual performance.

4. MBA Entrepreneurship

MBA Entrepreneurship is designed for students interested in venture creation, entrepreneurial management and building or scaling businesses. The 2026 handbook lists ₹1,35,135 per annum as the tuition component, subject to university revision and additional charges.

This route can also suit students who want entrepreneurial thinking within a conventional management degree rather than immediately starting a venture.

5. MBA Business Data Analytics

Best fit for analytics-oriented students

UBS's MBA(BDA) combines management education with a stronger data/analytics orientation. It is a particularly relevant option for students targeting business analytics, BI, data-led decision support and analytics-enabled management roles.

What do students study at UBS?

UBS course material includes core management areas such as management accounting, marketing management, organisational behaviour, operations management, strategic management and legal aspects of business, along with electives.

Published elective/course material also includes management information systems, management of financial services, managing global enterprise, business transformation, marketing research, mobile commerce, strategic HR and performance/compensation management.

For BDA aspirants, the decision should be based on the actual current curriculum and elective availability rather than assuming every data-science topic is included. Check the latest syllabus before admission.

UBS MBA Programme FAQs

Which is the flagship MBA at Panjab University?

The regular MBA at University Business School is the flagship broad-based management programme.

Does UBS offer MBA in Business Analytics?

Yes. UBS offers MBA (Business Data Analytics), listed as a two-year programme with 25 seats plus specified NRI/foreign seats.

Which UBS MBA is best for HR?

MBA Human Resource is the dedicated choice for students targeting HR and people-management careers.

Is MBA International Business different from the regular MBA?

Yes. MBA IB has a specific international-business orientation, while the regular MBA is broader.

What is the UBS MBA duration?

The regular UBS MBA programmes discussed here are two-year programmes.
