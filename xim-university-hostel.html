Is Hostel Compulsory at XIM University?

XIM's PG FAQ states that MBA and MMS-BF programmes are residential, so hostel/residential living is compulsory for students in these programmes. Other postgraduate programmes, executive programmes and bachelor's programmes can have different residential requirements.

For MBA applicants

Do not compare XIM tuition alone with a non-residential MBA. Add room, residence administration and board expenses when calculating your total cost.

XIM Hostel Facilities & Residential Life

XIM describes separate residences for men and women, student-managed mess arrangements, internet connectivity, laundry and basic campus services. The university also lists health, security, recreation and other residential support infrastructure.

Published facilities

  • 24-hour internet connectivity
  • Washing machines and water facilities
  • Indoor games and multipurpose spaces
  • Campus doctor and medical support
  • Security and CCTV surveillance
  • Banking / ATM and essential services

Daily costs to plan

  • Board / mess
  • Personal food outside the mess
  • Laundry and personal items
  • Travel and local expenses

How to Budget for XIM Hostel

Use the official room-rent figure plus residence administration fee and the approximate monthly board amount. Then add a separate personal-expense buffer. The official university fee page says the figures are indicative and subject to change.

Budget tip

For a new-campus PG double room, the published room rent is ₹40,000 per year plus ₹20,000 residence administration fee and approximately ₹5,000 monthly board. This is only a base residential estimate; personal spending is extra.
