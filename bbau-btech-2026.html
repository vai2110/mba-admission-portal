B.Tech Eligibility

The official programme catalogue requires Intermediate/Class 12 with Science subjects Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and 50% marks, with 45% for SC/ST/PH candidates as listed. Students should also follow the 2026 admission notice for the exact JEE and counselling conditions.

B.Tech Admission 2026 Through JEE

BBAU published a specific notice for B.Tech admission through JEE for 2026-27. The university then published branch-wise score lists, merit lists and vacant-seat details. Admission is therefore merit/counselling driven rather than a university-wide B.Tech entrance exam.

B.Tech Fees

The current BBAU programme catalogue lists ₹55,000 per semester for the five listed B.Tech branches. Other university charges may be payable separately.

Merit Lists and Vacant Seats

BBAU's 2026 merit page publishes programme-specific provisional merit lists, score lists and vacant-seat updates. Check the exact branch because seat availability can change between rounds.

BBAU B.Tech FAQs

How many B.Tech branches does BBAU offer?

The current programme listing shows five: CSE, Electrical, Civil, Electronics & Communication, and Mechanical & Automobile Engineering.

What is the BBAU B.Tech fee?

The current programme listing shows ₹55,000 per semester for the listed B.Tech programmes.

Does BBAU B.Tech require JEE?

The university has a specific 2026-27 admission route through JEE and publishes JEE-based merit and counselling updates.

Can I check branch-wise vacant seats?

Yes. BBAU publishes branch-wise vacant-seat and merit updates on its official 2026 UG merit page.
