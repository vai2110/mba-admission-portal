BBAU B.Tech 2026: Quick Snapshot

BBAU's engineering programmes are four-year undergraduate degrees offered through the University's engineering institute. The current course catalogue lists five branches, each with an intake of 60 seats and a published tuition fee of ₹55,000 per semester. The important admission distinction is that B.Tech is not simply treated like every other CUET UG programme: BBAU has published a specific JEE-based admission route for the 2026-27 cycle.

BBAU B.Tech Eligibility 2026

The current BBAU course listing requires Intermediate/Class 12 with Science subjects including Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and 50% marks. A 45% threshold is listed for SC/ST/PH candidates. Because admission rules can include additional counselling conditions, students should read the 2026 B.Tech notice alongside the course catalogue.

Before you apply, verify three things

  1. Your Class 12 subjects satisfy the PCM requirement.
  2. Your marks meet the applicable minimum for your category.
  3. You satisfy the current JEE/admission counselling conditions notified for 2026-27.

BBAU B.Tech Admission 2026: JEE, Merit and Counselling

BBAU has issued a separate B.Tech admission process for 2026-27 based on JEE information. The university subsequently publishes score/merit information and vacant-seat updates. This means students should not rely on a generic “BBAU admission cutoff”; the useful question is your JEE position, chosen branch, category and the round in which seats are available.

BBAU B.Tech Fees 2026

The current official programme listing shows ₹55,000 per semester for the listed B.Tech branches. On an eight-semester basis, that published tuition figure corresponds to ₹4.40 lakh in semester tuition before considering other university, examination, hostel, mess, deposit or living expenses. Do not interpret ₹4.40 lakh as the complete cost of attendance.

Important cost point

For budgeting, ask for the current fee schedule for your admission year and seat type. The payable amount at admission can include charges that are not represented by the semester tuition number alone.

What to Expect Academically

A B.Tech student should expect a combination of foundational science and engineering subjects in the early semesters followed by branch-specific technical subjects, laboratories, projects and practical exposure. The value of the degree will depend heavily on how actively you use labs, projects, internships, coding platforms and faculty guidance.

For CSE/ECE students, building demonstrable technical projects can be particularly important when competing for software and technology roles. For core branches, internships and applied engineering projects can help bridge the gap between classroom theory and industrial work.

BBAU B.Tech Placements: Don't Use a University-Wide Number Blindly

BBAU's official NIRF disclosures provide useful university-level placement data, but they combine programmes and should not be presented as a B.Tech-specific package. The overall NIRF 2026 submission reports 4-year UG cohorts with 135 students placed at a ₹9.846 lakh median in 2022-23, 151 at ₹11.851 lakh in 2023-24 and 160 at ₹12.735 lakh in 2024-25.

These figures are useful context but do not mean that every B.Tech graduate receives the median salary. Students should ask which companies recruit the engineering batch, how many students participate, which roles are offered, and whether the result is branch-specific.

BBAU B.Tech FAQs

How many B.Tech branches are offered by BBAU?

The current course catalogue lists five: Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, and Mechanical & Automobile Engineering.

What is the BBAU B.Tech fee?

The published tuition is ₹55,000 per semester for the listed B.Tech branches. Additional institutional and living expenses should be budgeted separately.

What is the BBAU B.Tech eligibility?

Students need 10+2 science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and 50% marks; the current listing specifies 45% for SC/ST/PH candidates.

Is BBAU B.Tech admission through CUET?

BBAU has a specific 2026-27 JEE-based B.Tech admission route. Students should follow the current B.Tech notice rather than assuming the general CUET UG process applies.

Which is the best B.Tech branch at BBAU?

There is no universally best branch. CSE may suit students targeting software and technology careers, while ECE, Electrical, Civil and Mechanical & Automobile fit different technical interests. Compare your JEE position, interest, curriculum, facilities and placement opportunities before choosing.
