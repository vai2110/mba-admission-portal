What is BHU BA LLB?

BHU offers the five-year integrated B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) programme. Students combine law with humanities/social-science subjects in an integrated undergraduate course.

What is the BHU BA LLB eligibility?

The official 2026 UG notice requires English or Hindi + General Aptitude Test in CUET-UG for B.A. LL.B. (Hons.). Candidates must also satisfy the programme's qualifying-examination conditions in the current bulletin.

How do you get BHU BA LLB admission?

  1. Appear in CUET-UG with the required papers.
  2. Register in BHU CAP-UG when applications open.
  3. Select B.A. LL.B. and submit preferences/documents.
  4. Track allotment and pay the prescribed fee.
  5. Complete document verification/reporting.

What is the BHU BA LLB cutoff?

BHU's cutoff is programme-, category- and round-specific. The university's 2026 cutoff portal is the place to check current allotment scores; older official cutoff PDFs can be used only as historical references.

Unlike BCom or BSc examples, the current source set does not expose a single reliable BA LLB 2025 score in the page text used here, so no number is invented on this page.

What is the BHU BA LLB fee?

The fee is programme-specific and should be taken from the latest Faculty of Law/BHU admission fee notice. Do not use the low regular-fee figures of general Arts programmes to estimate BA LLB cost.

What are law placements at BHU?

BHU does not publish a single university-wide BA LLB salary figure in the sources used here. The NIRF data is reported by broad programme duration, not as a BA LLB-specific package.

For the university-level placement evidence, see BHU Placements.

BHU BA LLB FAQs

Is BHU BA LLB through CUET?

Yes. The 2026 UG notice lists CUET-UG with English/Hindi plus General Aptitude Test.

How long is BHU BA LLB?

It is a five-year integrated B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) programme.

Is there a fixed BHU BA LLB cutoff?

No. The applicable cutoff changes by category and admission round.
