Amity Noida Course Fees 2026

The 2026 prospectus lists fees programme by programme and distinguishes non-sponsored and sponsored categories where applicable. Because Amity Noida offers a very large number of courses, there is no single university-wide tuition fee.

Hostel Fee

The official 2026 FAQ lists AC hostel accommodation at ₹1,95,000 per annum with a one-time ₹15,000 hostel security deposit. The fee includes boarding and laundry charges. Accommodation is apartment-style with single rooms, shared lounge and facilities.

Scholarships

Amity offers scholarship schemes for eligible students based on stated academic and other criteria. Scholarship conditions, continuation requirements and eligible programmes should be verified for the 2026 intake.

How Much Should You Budget?

Students should calculate total cost as academic fee + hostel or rent + food where not included + books and academic materials + transport + personal expenses. This is particularly important for comparing private university programmes.

Student Discussion: Avoid Comparing Only First-Semester Fees

A lower first-semester figure does not necessarily mean a lower total programme cost. Compare all semesters, fee escalation if stated, hostel, security deposit, scholarships and likely living expenses before deciding.
