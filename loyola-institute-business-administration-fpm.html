LIBA FPM: Programme Overview

LIBA's Fellow Programme in Management is a doctoral-level management programme. LIBA's official website states that its FPM is AIU-recognised as equivalent to a doctoral programme. It is intended for candidates interested in advanced research and academic or research-led management careers.

LIBA FPM Eligibility

The current LIBA admissions portal lists eligibility as a two-year Master's degree with at least 55% marks from a recognised university. Candidates should verify the current admission notice for discipline-specific, entrance and other requirements before applying.

LIBA FPM Admission Process

LIBA currently lists admission to the FPM as open. The exact application deadline, selection stages and research-area requirements should be taken from the current admission notice on LIBA's admissions portal because these details can change by intake.

FPM Career Direction

FPM is a different proposition from LIBA's placement-oriented PGDM. The logical outcomes are academic, teaching, research, policy and research-intensive business roles. Applicants should choose it for research interest rather than as a substitute for a conventional MBA.
