MUJ Hostel: What Students Actually Get

MUJ's official hostel page describes separate boys' and girls' hostels, twin-sharing AC rooms and single rooms subject to availability. Rooms are furnished with bed, mattress, wardrobe, table, chair, AC, hot water and basic fittings. Hostel allotment is first-come-first-served after fee payment.

The hostel is a material part of the university budget. Because MUJ is a residential university, hostel, food and daily living costs should be included when comparing total cost with another college.

Hostel Rules Students Should Know Before Paying

MUJ's hostel FAQ states that hostel accommodation is effectively a one-year contract covering two semesters. Leaving during the year generally does not result in a refund, subject to published exceptional provisions.

The hostel FAQ also states that fees are paid yearly in advance rather than monthly. Room booking is first-come-first-served, and existing students may request the same room subject to availability.

MUJ Hostel FAQs

What is the MUJ hostel fee for 2026–27?

For double occupancy, MUJ publishes ₹1,67,372 accommodation, ₹15,378 housekeeping and ₹25,475 refundable security, totaling ₹2,08,225 including security.

Does MUJ offer single rooms?

Yes, but single rooms are subject to availability.

Can hostel fees be paid monthly?

No. The official FAQ says hostel fees are paid in advance on a yearly basis.

Can I leave the hostel mid-year and get a refund?

The official FAQ treats hostel accommodation as a one-year contract and says fees are generally non-refundable if a student leaves during the year, subject to published exceptional provisions.
