MUJ Hostel: What Students Actually Get

MUJ's official hostel page describes separate boys' and girls' hostels, twin-sharing AC rooms and single rooms subject to availability. Rooms are furnished with bed, mattress, wardrobe, table, chair, AC, hot water and other basic fittings. Hostel allotment is on a first-come-first-served basis after fee payment.

The hostel is not a small add-on to the academic campus budget. Because MUJ is a residential university, hostel, food and transport/living expenses should be included when comparing total cost with another college.

Rooms, Food and Daily Living

  • Twin-sharing AC rooms are the standard published option; single rooms are subject to availability.
  • Rooms are furnished with bed, mattress, wardrobe, study table and chair, AC, hot water and basic fittings.
  • Food is professionally managed through hostel mess/catering arrangements.
  • The hostel page says vegetarian food is served in the mess, with separately cooked non-vegetarian food on selected days.
  • Medical support includes a doctor, nursing staff and 24×7 ambulance service according to the hostel page.

Hostel Rules Students Should Know Before Paying

MUJ's hostel FAQ states that hostel accommodation is effectively a one-year contract covering two semesters. Leaving during the year generally does not result in a refund, subject to the university's published exceptional-case provisions. Hostel rooms are allotted annually and may change because rooms are vacated for maintenance.

The hostel FAQ also states that fees are paid yearly in advance rather than monthly. Room booking is first-come-first-served, and existing students may request the same room subject to availability.

Students should read the hostel handbook and undertaking before payment because operational rules can change and the hostel management communicates updates through official notices, email and the relevant portals.

MUJ Hostel FAQs

What is the MUJ hostel fee for 2026–27?

For double occupancy, MUJ publishes ₹1,67,372 accommodation, ₹15,378 housekeeping and ₹25,475 refundable security, totaling ₹2,08,225 including security.

Does MUJ offer single rooms?

Yes, but single rooms are subject to availability.

Can hostel fees be paid monthly?

No. The official FAQ says hostel fees are paid in advance on a yearly basis.

Can I leave the hostel mid-year and get a refund?

The official FAQ says the hostel is a one-year contract and fees are non-refundable if a student leaves during the year, subject to the published exceptional provisions.
