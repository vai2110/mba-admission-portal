Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose an MBA college?

Start with your exam, shortlist colleges, then compare programme fit, fees, cutoff, location and placement outcomes.

Which MBA colleges accept CAT?

Many management institutes use CAT as an admission route. Start with the CAT page and then verify each college's current requirements.

How much does an MBA cost in India?

Fees vary widely by institute and programme. Use the fee hub to begin by budget and verify the detailed fee structure on each college page.

What should I check before applying?

Check eligibility, accepted exam, application deadline, cutoff, complete programme cost and the latest placement information.
