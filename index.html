Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose an MBA college?

Start with your exam, shortlist colleges, then compare programme fit, fees, cutoff, location and placement information.

Which MBA colleges accept CAT?

Many management institutes use CAT. Start with the CAT guide and verify each college's current admission requirements.

How much does an MBA cost in India?

Fees vary by institute and programme. Use the fee hub to start by budget and then verify the detailed fee structure.

What should I check before applying?

Check eligibility, accepted exam, application deadline, cutoff, total programme cost and latest placement information.
