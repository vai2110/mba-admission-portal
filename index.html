Frequently Asked Questions

How should I choose an MBA college?

Start with your entrance exam and eligibility, then shortlist colleges using programme fit, fees, cutoff, location and placement information.

Which entrance exam should I take for an MBA?

It depends on the colleges you want to target. Check the admission route of your preferred colleges first and identify the exams they accept.

How much does an MBA cost in India?

MBA fees vary widely by institute and programme. Use the fee range as a starting point and verify the current fee on the relevant college or programme page.

What should I check before applying?

Check eligibility, accepted exam, application deadline, fees, cutoff expectations, selection process and available placement information.
