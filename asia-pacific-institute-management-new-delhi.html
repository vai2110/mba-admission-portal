Admission

AIM's current admission information states that applicants need a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks. The institute lists CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT and ATMA among accepted entrance scores, followed by its selection process.

Important: The cutoff shown on CollegeDecoded's CMAT matcher is an expected/indicative threshold, not an official guaranteed admission cutoff.

Fees

AIM's current PGDM programme information lists the total fee for its two-year PGDM General programme at ₹11.50 lakh. Students should verify the latest fee structure, instalments and scholarship terms before applying.

Placements

AIM's current official website highlights placement support and reports a 35 LPA highest package on its current programme pages. Placement figures can vary by batch and reporting methodology, so students should check the latest institute placement information before making comparisons.
