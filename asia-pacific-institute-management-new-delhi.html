What should you know about Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi?

Quick answer

Asia-Pacific Institute of Management (AIM), New Delhi offers AICTE-approved PGDM programmes with multiple specialisation pathways. The institute publishes ₹11.50 lakh as the academic fee for the 2026–28 PGDM batch, while its placement pages publish different headline metrics depending on the reporting scope.

₹11.50LPublished 2026–28 academic fee
₹22LHighest salary in AIM's placement page

AIM's current programme information lists General, Marketing, Banking & Financial Services and Big Data Analytics PGDM options. Applicants should verify the exact programme, fee schedule and placement cohort before comparing offers.

How does AIM Delhi PGDM admission work?

AIM's current admission pages describe a merit-driven process using academic record, management entrance scores and selection-stage assessment. Current programme information mentions CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and ATMA.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks according to current programme information.
  • Final-year candidates may apply subject to completion and documentation conditions.

Selection

Application

Submit academic details and the relevant entrance-score information.

Shortlisting

Applicants are evaluated on academic and aptitude-related factors.

GD/PI

Shortlisted candidates participate in the institute's group discussion and personal interview process where applicable.

Offer

Final admission follows the institute's applicable merit and documentation requirements.

Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi FAQs

What is the Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi PGDM fee?

For the 2026–28 PGDM batch, AIM's published fee schedule lists a total academic fee of ₹11.50 lakh under the self-sponsored category. The schedule includes an initial registration payment of ₹75,000, a further ₹75,000 for seat confirmation and four subsequent instalments of ₹2.50 lakh each. Students should also budget for living and personal expenses that are outside the academic fee.

Which entrance exams does AIM Delhi accept?

AIM's current programme information mentions CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT and ATMA as accepted management entrance routes. Eligibility and the exact score considered can depend on the programme and admission cycle, so applicants should confirm the latest programme-specific admission notice before applying.

What is the Asia Pacific Institute of Management Delhi cutoff?

AIM Delhi has not released a single fixed official cutoff for MBA/PGDM admission that guarantees selection. As a reference for the CMAT route, the percentile requirement is around 85 percentile as a minimum reference. This is not a guaranteed admission cutoff or an assured interview call; the final outcome also depends on eligibility, academic record, entrance-test performance and the institute's selection process.

What are the placement figures at AIM Delhi?

AIM's placement page reports a 98% placement record, around ₹8.75 LPA average salary and ₹22 LPA highest salary, with 62% repeat recruiters. Some other current AIM pages publish different highest-package figures under different reporting scopes. For this reason, students should ask for the placement report for their specific graduating batch instead of combining figures from different cohorts or pages.

What is the AIM Delhi PGDM selection process?

AIM describes its admission process as a combination of academic performance, national-level entrance examination performance and selection-stage assessment. Its published material also states that shortlisted candidates may be invited for an assessment such as a written test before admission confirmation. The exact process can vary by programme and admission cycle, so applicants should follow the current official admission notice.
