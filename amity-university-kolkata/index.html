Overview

Amity University Kolkata is a private state university in New Town, Rajarhat, Kolkata. Established in 2015, it offers undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated and doctoral programs across engineering, management, law, architecture, sciences, commerce, psychology, design, media and humanities. Popular choices include MBA, BTech, BBA, BCA and law. The university lists BTech branches such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, AI & Machine Learning, AI & Data Science, Cyber Security, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology. Non-sponsored first-year tuition examples for 2026 range from ₹1.44 lakh for BCA to ₹4.89 lakh for MBA. NIRF 2025 places it in several category bands, including Architecture at 25. Latest NIRF 2026 disclosures report placement medians by program duration. Applicants should compare total costs, curriculum and program-specific career outcomes before paying.

Amity University Kolkata Admission

Amity Kolkata admissions use academic merit, entrance scores and university selection processes, depending on the program. The university regulations list AMCAT, national tests, qualifying marks, written tests, interviews and Group Discussions as possible methods.

UG admission

UG applicants generally need Class 12 with program-specific subjects and minimum marks. BTech applicants must check Physics, Mathematics and other subject requirements. Law applicants must meet relevant Class 12 eligibility. Merit-based fast-track conditions must be checked for the current program and intake.

PG admission

PG applicants need a relevant bachelor’s degree. MBA applicants may submit CAT, MAT or GMAT scores and may face further selection steps. Other PG courses may use qualifying marks, university assessments or interviews.

Selection and cutoff

Amity Kolkata does not publish one fixed cutoff for all courses. Minimum eligibility is not the same as the selection cutoff. Exact selection thresholds, interview dates and category rules should be confirmed with admissions.

Application steps

  1. Choose the program and review current eligibility.
  2. Complete the official application and pay the applicable application fee.
  3. Submit qualifying scores and attend assessments/interviews if required.
  4. Review the admission offer and fee category.
  5. Submit documents, pay fees and complete enrolment.

Sources: admission regulations, admission portal and prospectus.
