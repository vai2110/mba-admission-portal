Overview

Amity University Kolkata is a private state university in New Town, Rajarhat, Kolkata. Established in 2015, it offers undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated and doctoral programs across engineering, management, law, architecture, sciences, commerce, psychology, design, media and humanities. Popular choices include MBA, BTech, BBA, BCA and law. The university lists BTech branches such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, AI & Machine Learning, AI & Data Science, Cyber Security, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology. Non-sponsored first-year tuition examples for 2026 range from ₹1.44 lakh for BCA to ₹4.89 lakh for MBA. NIRF 2025 places it in several category bands, including Architecture at 25. Latest NIRF 2026 disclosures report placement medians by program duration. Applicants should compare total costs, curriculum and program-specific career outcomes before paying.

Amity University Kolkata Admission

Amity Kolkata admissions use academic merit, entrance scores and university selection processes, depending on the program. The university regulations list AMCAT, national tests, qualifying marks, written tests, interviews and Group Discussions as possible methods.

UG admission

UG applicants generally need Class 12 with program-specific subjects and minimum marks. BTech applicants must check Physics, Mathematics and other subject requirements. Law applicants must meet relevant Class 12 eligibility. Merit-based fast-track conditions must be checked for the current program and intake.

PG admission

PG applicants need a relevant bachelor’s degree. MBA applicants may submit CAT, MAT or GMAT scores and may face further selection steps. Other PG courses may use qualifying marks, university assessments or interviews.

Selection and cutoff

Amity Kolkata does not publish one fixed cutoff for all courses. Minimum eligibility is not the same as the selection cutoff. Exact selection thresholds, interview dates and category rules should be confirmed with admissions.

Application steps

  1. Choose the program and review current eligibility.
  2. Complete the official application and pay the applicable application fee.
  3. Submit qualifying scores and attend assessments/interviews if required.
  4. Review the admission offer and fee category.
  5. Submit documents, pay fees and complete enrolment.

Sources: admission regulations, admission portal and prospectus.

Amity University Kolkata FAQs

Is Amity University Kolkata admission 2026 still open?

No. The 2026–27 admission cycle is past. Amity Kolkata’s official admissions page is currently advertising the 2027 intake, but course-wise deadlines should be checked on the live application portal. Do not rely on an old 2026 date copied from a third-party page.

Does Amity University Kolkata have a fixed cutoff?

Amity Kolkata does not publish one universal cutoff for all courses. Eligibility and selection vary by program and may use board marks, entrance scores, interviews or other selection stages. Ask the admission office for the current program-specific criteria and the previous intake’s selection range, if available.

Can students get direct admission at Amity Kolkata?

The university advertises a Scholars’ Fast Track pathway, but the published page includes older scholarship and eligibility conditions. Confirm whether the fast-track route applies to your chosen program and 2027 intake, including whether the pathway remains active, before treating it as a direct-admission guarantee. Request the policy in writing.

Are sponsored and non-sponsored fees different?

Yes, fee categories may differ. The fee figures on this page are non-sponsored examples where specified. Ask the university for the exact category assigned to your application and the semester-wise payment schedule, including deposits, charges beyond tuition, and fee revisions across years.

Is hostel food included in Amity Kolkata hostel fees?

The official FAQ lists a separate AC hostel fee and a one-time security deposit. The sources reviewed do not confirm a fixed mess package included in that room tariff. Ask for the current hostel invoice breakdown, mess options, refund terms and room allotment details.

Is Amity Kolkata hostel compulsory?

A current compulsory-residence rule was not verified. Students should ask whether hostel occupancy is optional for day scholars, how allotment works for outstation students, and whether the university offers bus routes or other transport support for students living off campus.

Does Amity Kolkata guarantee placements?

No campus can guarantee a job for every student based on a placement headline. Amity Kolkata’s NIRF disclosures report placed-student counts by program duration. Ask for the eligible cohort, placement participation, job roles and salary distribution for your specific course.

Are Amity Kolkata placements course-wise available?

The latest NIRF data is grouped by program duration in the public disclosure reviewed. A complete course-wise breakdown for MBA specializations, BTech branches and Law was not verified. Request a recent program-specific report and clarify whether offers or unique students are counted.

What is the application fee and is it refundable?

The current 2027 application fee and refund conditions were not confirmed in the sources reviewed. Before submitting the form, check the payment page and written refund policy. Keep the receipt and review withdrawal rules for admission fee, academic security and any hostel payment separately.

What documents should students keep ready?

Prepare Class 10 and 12 certificates and marksheets, graduation documents for postgraduate admission, entrance scorecards where relevant, ID proof, photographs and any category or scholarship documents. The university may request originals or additional certificates during verification. Ask whether documents must be submitted in original form and whether copies need attestation.

Does Amity Kolkata have a strict attendance requirement?

The exact attendance requirement for the 2027 program was not verified in a current official regulation during this research. Ask the department for the attendance policy, medical leave rules, shortage condonation procedure and consequences before enrolling. Request a written handbook or department notice and confirm how approved leave is handled.

How can students contact Amity Kolkata admissions?

The official website lists the Kolkata admission helpline as 1800 102 3320 and admissions@kol.amity.edu. Confirm the latest contact details on the official admissions page before calling or emailing, especially during application season. Ask for the correct department or extension, expected response hours, and whether a callback is available.
