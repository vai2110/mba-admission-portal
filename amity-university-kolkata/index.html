Amity University Kolkata is a private state university in New Town, Kolkata. It was established under the West Bengal Act XXIV of 2014. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, management, law, architecture, pharmacy, psychology, sciences, design, media and humanities. Popular choices include MBA, BTech, integrated Law, BBA and BCA. The official 2025 NIRF University category places it in the 151–200 rank band. Its published MBA and BTech fees vary by programme and fee category. The university reports a highest salary of ₹32.21 lakh, but its placement page does not identify the batch or programme. NIRF placement disclosures provide a clearer view of median salaries and placed-student counts.

Quick answer: Amity University Kolkata is a multi-disciplinary private university in New Town. Students comparing it for MBA, BTech or Law should check programme-level fees, eligibility and placement data. A course-wise admission cutoff and average salary are not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed.

Amity University Kolkata Admission

Admission to Amity University Kolkata is programme-specific. Applicants submit an online application and then follow the academic-merit, entrance-score or university selection route stated for their chosen course.

UG admission

UG eligibility depends on the programme and Class 12 subject combination. For example, the published BTech CSE non-sponsored criteria require 50% aggregate in Class 12 and at least 55% across Physics, Mathematics and an eligible third subject. BA LLB (Hons.) lists 50% in Class 12.

PG admission

The MBA programme lists graduation with at least 50% and a Class 12 requirement. CAT, MAT and GMAT are among the management entrance routes referenced by the university. Applicants should confirm the accepted exam and selection stages for their chosen MBA variant.

Law admission

The integrated BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB programmes list 10+2 with at least 50%. The three-year LLB requires graduation. The LLM (Business Law) listing requires an LLB or equivalent qualification.

Application steps

  1. Select the Kolkata campus and exact programme on the official portal.
  2. Check subject, aggregate and category-specific eligibility.
  3. Complete the online application and upload the requested records.
  4. Submit entrance scores or attend any assessment/interview required for that programme.
  5. Track the application portal and email for the selection decision.
  6. Verify the offer, fee category, refund terms and payment deadline before paying.

Cutoff and selection criteria

Amity University Kolkata does not publish a single common cutoff for all courses in the sources reviewed. A course-wise closing rank or confirmed MBA CAT percentile cutoff was not officially disclosed. Meeting the minimum eligibility percentage should not be treated as a guarantee of selection.

Documents to keep ready

  • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets/certificates
  • Graduation marksheets and degree/provisional certificate for PG applicants
  • Entrance exam scorecard, where applicable
  • Government photo identity document
  • Recent photograph and signature
  • Transfer/migration certificate and other records if requested
  • Scholarship or category documents, if applying under a relevant scheme

Sources: Official admissions portal; MBA eligibility; BTech eligibility; Law eligibility.

Amity University Kolkata FAQs

Does Amity University Kolkata have a separate admission cutoff for sponsored and non-sponsored seats?

The university lists different eligibility conditions and fee categories for some programmes, but a separate published closing cutoff for sponsored and non-sponsored seats was not found. Ask admissions for the programme code, applicable category, minimum subject marks and selection stages before submitting the application.

Can a student change their course after joining Amity University Kolkata?

A public, universal course-transfer rule was not verified in the sources reviewed. Course changes may depend on seat availability, academic prerequisites, timing and university approval. Obtain the current written transfer policy from the registrar before accepting an offer based on a possible later change.

Are Amity University Kolkata placements compulsory for every student?

The university's placement cell provides career support and employer engagement. The public placement page does not state that every student must participate or that every eligible student receives an offer. Students should ask how placement eligibility, opt-in status, backlogs and higher-study plans affect reported placement numbers.

Does Amity University Kolkata provide lateral entry in BTech?

The current course catalogue should be checked for the exact branch and lateral-entry intake. Do not assume that every BTech specialization accepts diploma holders. Ask for the current lateral-entry eligibility, semester of entry, seat availability and applicable fee before applying.

Are Amity Kolkata's NIRF placement figures only for MBA and BTech?

No. NIRF reports broad programme-duration categories, such as four-year undergraduate and two-year postgraduate programmes. Those groups can include multiple degrees. The published median should not be presented as the MBA-only or BTech-only median unless a separate programme disclosure confirms it.

Does Amity University Kolkata offer international study opportunities?

The university advertises a five-week study-abroad programme at Amity campuses in London, New York, Singapore or Dubai. Participation rules, additional costs, credit transfer and availability may vary. Confirm whether the opportunity is open to your programme and intake.

Is the Amity Kolkata campus the same as Amity University Noida?

No. Amity University Kolkata and Amity University Uttar Pradesh at Noida are separate campus entities with different locations, programme details and disclosures. Do not use Noida fees, rankings or placement statistics to describe the Kolkata campus.

Where can applicants verify the exact programme code?

Use the Kolkata campus course-detail page and check the programme code displayed beside the degree title. Keep that code when contacting admissions. It helps avoid confusion between similarly named programmes, specializations and campuses within the wider Amity network.

Does Amity University Kolkata publish a student review score?

The official sources reviewed do not provide an independently audited student-review score. Applicants should read multiple student accounts and ask current students about their own department, class size, faculty access, timetable, internships, hostel experience and course-specific placement support.

How can parents verify the fee before paying the admission amount?

Ask for the current written fee schedule with the programme code and fee category. It should separate tuition, one-time charges, refundable deposits, examination charges, hostel and mess. Confirm refund rules and payment deadlines from the university's official admission office, not an agent.
