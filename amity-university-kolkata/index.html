Amity University Kolkata is a private state university in New Town, Kolkata. It was established under the West Bengal Act XXIV of 2014. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, management, law, architecture, pharmacy, psychology, sciences, design, media and humanities. Popular choices include MBA, BTech, integrated Law, BBA and BCA. The official 2025 NIRF University category places it in the 151–200 rank band. Its published MBA and BTech fees vary by programme and fee category. The university reports a highest salary of ₹32.21 lakh, but its placement page does not identify the batch or programme. NIRF placement disclosures provide a clearer view of median salaries and placed-student counts. The campus page lists 60+ labs, a 15,000 sq ft library and a 748-resident hostel.

Quick answer: Amity University Kolkata is a multi-disciplinary private university in New Town. Students comparing it for MBA, BTech or Law should check programme-level fees, eligibility and placement data. A course-wise admission cutoff and average salary are not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed.

Amity University Kolkata Admission

Admission to Amity University Kolkata is programme-specific. Applicants submit an online application and then follow the academic-merit, entrance-score or university selection route stated for their chosen course.

UG admission

UG eligibility depends on the programme and Class 12 subject combination. For example, the published BTech CSE non-sponsored criteria require 50% aggregate in Class 12 and at least 55% across Physics, Mathematics and an eligible third subject. BA LLB (Hons.) lists 50% in Class 12.

PG admission

The MBA programme lists graduation with at least 50% and a Class 12 requirement. The university’s latest admission blog lists CAT, MAT, XAT, NMAT or its own written test followed by a personal interview for MBA. For BTech, it lists JEE Main or Amity JEE. These routes must be reconfirmed for the exact 2027–28 programme.

The university admission blog lists a ₹1,500 application fee. It also says applicants with 80% or above in Class 12 may qualify for merit-based direct admission to BCA, BBA, BBA LLB and MA. Confirm both rules on the live 2027–28 portal; they do not apply automatically to MBA or BTech.

Law admission

The integrated BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB programmes list 10+2 with at least 50%. The three-year LLB requires graduation. The LLM (Business Law) listing requires an LLB or equivalent qualification.

Application steps

  1. Select the Kolkata campus and exact programme on the official portal.
  2. Check subject, aggregate and category-specific eligibility.
  3. Complete the online application and upload the requested records.
  4. Submit entrance scores or attend any assessment/interview required for that programme.
  5. Track the application portal and email for the selection decision.
  6. Verify the offer, fee category, refund terms and payment deadline before paying.

Cutoff and selection criteria

Amity University Kolkata does not publish a single common cutoff for all courses in the sources reviewed. A course-wise closing rank or confirmed MBA CAT percentile cutoff was not officially disclosed. Meeting the minimum eligibility percentage should not be treated as a guarantee of selection.

Amity University Kolkata FAQs

Does Amity University Kolkata have sponsored and non-sponsored fee categories?

Yes. The university uses sponsored and non-sponsored categories for some programmes. The MBA course page lists ₹2.445 lakh per semester for first-year non-sponsored study. Its admission blog separately says MBA annual fees can reach ₹7.33 lakh. Ask admissions to confirm your category and full payable amount in writing.

Does Amity University Kolkata publish course-wise admission cutoffs?

No course-wise closing rank or confirmed MBA CAT percentile cutoff was found in the official material reviewed. The university publishes minimum eligibility, but that is not the same as a competitive cutoff. Ask admissions whether the selected programme uses merit ranking, entrance scores or an interview.

Can I get direct admission to Amity University Kolkata without an entrance exam?

There is no blanket direct-admission guarantee for every course. The university's admission blog says 80% or above in Class 12 may qualify applicants for merit-based entry to BCA, BBA, BBA LLB and MA. MBA and BTech have separate selection routes. Confirm the current course rule.

Is hostel accommodation guaranteed for every Amity Kolkata student?

The university does not state that every applicant is guaranteed a hostel seat. Its published capacity is 748 residents, split between boys and girls. Allocation depends on availability. The 2026 hostel tariff is not a confirmed 2027–28 quote, so request the current allotment and payment terms.

Are Amity Kolkata's NIRF placement figures only for MBA and BTech?

No. NIRF reports programme-duration groups, such as four-year undergraduate and two-year postgraduate programmes. These groups can include several degrees. The median salary should not be treated as the MBA-only or BTech-only average. Request the latest department-level placement report before comparing offers.

Is Amity University Kolkata good for students moving from another state?

It may suit students who want a New Town campus and on-campus accommodation. Shiksha displays 4.0/5 from 251 reviews, but its placement sub-rating is lower than its infrastructure score. Read recent reviews for your course and verify hostel availability, costs and placement roles.

Is Amity University Kolkata UGC approved?

Amity University Kolkata is a state private university established under West Bengal Act XXIV of 2014, and its disclosures state UGC recognition. Professional approvals can differ by course. The NIRF 2025 self-disclosure reported no valid NAAC accreditation at submission; ask for any later certificate.

Does Amity University Kolkata offer merit scholarships?

Yes. The university's admission blog refers to merit-based tuition waivers of 25%–50%. Exact 2027–28 marks, entrance-score thresholds and renewal conditions were not fully published in the reviewed material. Ask for written eligibility, covered fee components and continuation rules before budgeting.

Can graduates from any discipline apply for the Amity Kolkata MBA?

The MBA course page lists graduation with at least 50% and Class 12 with at least 50%. It does not restrict the degree to one undergraduate stream in the displayed eligibility. Applicants should still confirm subject-specific or category conditions for the current intake.

What is the last date to apply to Amity University Kolkata for 2027–28?

A consolidated final date for all programmes was not found. The university advertises the 2027–28 intake, and deadlines can differ by course and seat availability. Check the live admission portal and applicant dashboard. Treat any date from the 2026–27 cycle as historical.

Is 100% placement guaranteed at Amity University Kolkata?

No. The university FAQ describes placement assistance and states a minimum 6 CGPA requirement for recruitment eligibility. Assistance does not guarantee an offer, salary or role. Ask for the eligible-student count, placed-student count and course-wise recruiter list for the latest graduating batch.

What attendance is required at Amity University Kolkata?

The university's admissions FAQ states that students need at least 75% attendance to appear in end-semester examinations. It says attendance is counted from the programme start date or fee-payment date, whichever is later. Confirm whether any programme-specific attendance rules apply.
