Amity University Kolkata Placements 2026

Amity Kolkata’s latest NIRF 2026 disclosure reports placement outcomes for the 2024–25 graduating cohort. It reports medians and placed-student counts by program duration. A single average salary for the entire university was not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed.

Official placement disclosure

Amity’s official placement page highlights placement activity and testimonials. Current course-wise MBA, BTech branch and Law reports were not verified. Request a cohort report from the placement office.

Salary context

Do not use package headlines without course and cohort year. Request median, average, roles, recruiters and placement denominator.

Recruiters and placement support

Official recruiter testimonials and examples mention Deloitte, TCS, HDFC Bank, Nestlé, Byju’s, Oberoi, Marks & Spencer, Wunderman Thompson, Anmol Biscuits, EPTISA, Simplex Infrastructures and Tosoh India. The current number of recruiters visited, offers made and branch-wise recruiter counts were not officially disclosed.

The Amity Centre for Career Development (ACCD) offers counselling, resume and cover-letter workshops, interview preparation, soft-skill training, aptitude preparation, internship support and employer engagement. Placement participation and results depend on program, eligibility, performance and market conditions.

Source: Official Amity Kolkata placement page.

What students should ask the placement office

  • Can I see the latest placement report for my course and branch?
  • What is the eligible batch size and number placed?
  • What were the median and average salary figures, and how were they calculated?
  • Which job roles and recruiters hired from my program?
  • Are internships guaranteed, facilitated or student-arranged?

Amity Kolkata FAQs

Are course-wise placement reports available?

A current official report for MBA specializations, BTech branches and Law was not verified. Request it directly from the placement office.

Which placement metrics should students ask for?

Ask cohort year, eligible/placed counts, offers, median/average salary, roles, recruiters, internships and the percentage denominator.

Is the highest package a useful comparison?

It is one offer; compare median, cohort, course, roles and participation.

What placement support is provided?

The university describes career support. Confirm which services apply to your course and any participation conditions.

Is the placement rate available for each course?

Course-level reports may not be published on the same page. Request a report for the exact programme and batch, with eligible students, placement-seeking students, placed count and salary measure.

Is the highest package a useful comparison?

It is only one outcome and can be atypical. Compare median salary, placed/eligible counts, roles, recruiter list and cohort year. Confirm whether salary figures include variable pay or other components.

Do placements include off-campus jobs?

Ask how the report separates campus offers, off-campus outcomes, internships, PPOs and students who did not participate. Use the university’s defined reporting denominator.

Which roles should I check for my course?

Ask for actual job titles and functions for MBA specializations, BTech branches or Law students. Recruiter names alone do not show the roles or how many students were hired.
