Quick Answer: Amity Kolkata Placements

Amity Kolkata's official NIRF 2025 report gives the 2023–24 median salary as ₹9.235 Lakhs for UG 4-year programmes (299 placed of 332 graduating in minimum time) and ₹10.65 Lakhs for PG 2-year programmes (349 of 388). These duration-group figures are not MBA-, BTech- or Law-specific. Compare course-level roles, placement counts and median salary before estimating ROI.

How to Read These Placement Numbers Before Choosing a Course

The NIRF figures shown above describe broad programme-duration cohorts. They cannot tell you what an MBA specialization, a BTech branch or Law student is likely to earn. A median is more useful than a highest-package headline, but only when the cohort and denominator match the course you plan to join.

Ask the placement team for this course-specific evidence

  • Eligible students, students actively seeking placement and students placed
  • Median and average salary, with fixed and variable components separated
  • Job roles and recruiters for the programme—not just a university-wide list
  • Internship conversion, PPO counts and whether off-campus offers are included
  • Year and cohort size of the report, and how multiple offers are counted

Calculate your personal return on investment

Compare total degree cost (tuition + hostel/mess + living + loan interest) against realistic starting-role salary ranges for the career you want. Do not use the university-wide median as your personal salary forecast. If course-specific outcomes are not provided, treat placement ROI as unknown rather than assuming it is strong.

Reported Highest Package and Recruiting Sectors

The official placement page reports a ₹32.21 Lakhs highest package for a 2023 record. This is a separate headline and cohort scope from the NIRF median tables. Highest salary is a single outcome and is not a typical salary.

Official placement materials mention activity across IT/ITES, consulting, BFSI, FMCG, e-commerce, biotech, manufacturing, healthcare, media, hospitality and legal-related sectors. Treat sector mentions as university-level context; recruiter names and roles can vary by course and graduating year.

Amity Kolkata Career Development Support

University career-support materials describe counselling, résumé and cover-letter workshops, interview preparation, aptitude and soft-skills training, internship/co-op support and employer engagement. These services help prepare students; they do not guarantee an offer.

Questions to ask for your programme

  • How many students were eligible and actively seeking placement?
  • How many received offers and what is the reporting denominator?
  • What roles and job functions were offered to my programme?
  • What are median and average salaries, and are variable components included?
  • Are internships, PPOs and off-campus outcomes counted separately?

Is Amity Kolkata Worth the Cost for Placements?

Evaluate MBA by specialization-level roles and salary distribution, BTech by branch-specific technical roles and internships, and Law by legal internships, firms, in-house roles and practical exposure. Compare those outcomes against tuition, hostel, living costs, loan interest and opportunity cost. University-wide NIRF figures provide context only and should not be used as a guaranteed outcome for your course.

Amity Kolkata Placements FAQs

What is the median salary at Amity Kolkata?

NIRF 2025 reports ₹9.235 Lakhs for UG 4-year and ₹10.65 Lakhs for PG 2-year cohorts in 2023–24. The report includes 299 placed of 332 in UG 4-year and 349 placed of 388 in PG 2-year. These are duration groups, not individual course outcomes; ask for your programme's report and denominator.

What is the highest package reported?

The university placement page reports ₹32.21 Lakhs for a 2023 record. This highest-offer headline is not comparable to the NIRF 2023–24 median tables. Ask what cohort and programme it represents, whether salary is fixed or variable, and how the offer was counted.

Are MBA placements course-specific?

Current MBA specialization-wise outcomes were not verified in official reports reviewed. Request eligible, participating and placed counts, median and average salary, job roles, recruiters, internships and PPOs for the latest MBA graduating cohort. Do not use the mixed PG median as an MBA salary.

What are BTech placements?

Branch-level BTech figures were not verified. The UG 4-year NIRF median includes multiple programmes. Ask separately for CSE, AI, ECE, Civil, Mechanical and Biotechnology reports with placed/eligible counts, salary distribution, job functions, recruiters and internship conversion.

Which recruiters visit the campus?

Recruiter activity can vary by programme and batch. The university materials describe several industry sectors, but a sector mention is not a verified hiring list. Ask for the names of companies that recruited your course in the latest year, roles offered, number of offers and selection criteria.

Is the highest package representative?

No, it is one offer and may be an outlier. Compare median salary, placement-seeking and eligible counts, number placed, role quality, recruiter mix, cohort year and fixed/variable salary. Confirm whether off-campus placements, internships and PPOs are included in the published statistics.

What placement support is provided?

Career materials describe counselling, resume support, interview preparation, aptitude/soft-skills training and employer engagement. These are preparation services, not guarantees. Ask which activities are scheduled for your programme, whether internships are supported, and whether students must meet conditions to participate in placements.

How do I judge placement ROI?

Use course-specific outcomes and total course cost. Include tuition, residential costs, living expenses and financing, and compare salary distribution and job roles. If course-level data is missing, treat the return as uncertain rather than assuming the university-wide median applies to your degree.
