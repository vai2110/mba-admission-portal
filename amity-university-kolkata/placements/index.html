Placement record reported on the university page

The official placement page describes a 2023 record across 63 UG & PG disciplines and reports a highest salary of ₹32.21 Lakhs per annum. The page also mentions employers/sectors spanning FMCG, IT/ITES, BFSI, biotech, e-commerce, EdTech, law firms, manufacturing, consulting, biopharma, media, hospitality, fashion/retail and healthcare.

Placement support

Amity Centre for Career Development (ACCD) describes career counselling, résumé and cover-letter workshops, interview prep, soft-skills and aptitude training, internship/co-op support and employer engagement. These are support services—not placement guarantees.

Amity Kolkata FAQs

Are course-wise placement reports available?

A current official report for MBA specializations, BTech branches and Law was not verified. Request it directly from the placement office.

Which placement metrics should students ask for?

Ask cohort year, eligible/placed counts, offers, median/average salary, roles, recruiters, internships and the percentage denominator.

Is the highest package a useful comparison?

It is one offer; compare median, cohort, course, roles and participation.

What placement support is provided?

The university describes career support. Confirm which services apply to your course and any participation conditions.

Is the placement rate available for each course?

Course-level reports may not be published on the same page. Request a report for the exact programme and batch, with eligible students, placement-seeking students, placed count and salary measure.

Is the highest package a useful comparison?

It is only one outcome and can be atypical. Compare median salary, placed/eligible counts, roles, recruiter list and cohort year. Confirm whether salary figures include variable pay or other components.

Do placements include off-campus jobs?

Ask how the report separates campus offers, off-campus outcomes, internships, PPOs and students who did not participate. Use the university’s defined reporting denominator.

Which roles should I check for my course?

Ask for actual job titles and functions for MBA specializations, BTech branches or Law students. Recruiter names alone do not show the roles or how many students were hired.
