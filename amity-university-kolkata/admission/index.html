Amity University Kolkata Admission FAQs

When will Amity University Kolkata admission applications for 2027 close?

A single university-wide closing date is not verified. The 2027 intake is being promoted, but deadlines can vary by programme and may be rolling. Check the course page and applicant portal for the application deadline, assessment date, offer-acceptance deadline and reporting date before planning around a date.

How do I apply to Amity University Kolkata?

Apply through Amity’s official admission portal and select the Kolkata campus and exact programme. Complete the form, provide the requested academic details, upload documents and pay any application charge shown at checkout. Save your application number and receipt, then check the portal for assessment instructions or status updates.

What is the eligibility for MBA admission at Amity University Kolkata?

The official MBA listing reviewed states 50% in graduation and 50% in Class XII. Confirm whether the current 2027 programme accepts your degree, final-year status, entrance score and any specialization-specific conditions. These minimum marks are eligibility criteria, not a promise of shortlisting or admission.

What is the eligibility for BTech admission at Amity Kolkata?

BTech eligibility varies by branch and fee category. The official non-sponsored CSE example lists 50% overall in Class XII and 55% in the specified subject aggregate including English, Physics, Mathematics and an approved third subject. Confirm the exact branch-specific criteria for the current intake before applying.

Is JEE Main compulsory for Amity University Kolkata BTech?

The accepted entrance route must be confirmed for the chosen BTech branch and intake. Do not assume JEE Main, Amity JEE or a single exam is mandatory or sufficient across every programme. Check the current official notice for accepted scores, exemptions, whether an assessment is required and how the score is used in selection.

Can I apply to Amity Kolkata before my Class XII or graduation results are declared?

Provisional or final-year applications depend on the current programme rules. Ask whether you can apply with pending results, what minimum marks you must ultimately secure, and the deadline for submitting final marksheets or degree proof. If an offer is conditional, keep the condition and completion date in writing.

What are the steps after submitting the Amity Kolkata application?

Follow the programme’s instructions in the application portal after submission. Depending on the course, the next step may be an entrance score review, university assessment, interview, group discussion, portfolio or document check. Track status messages and complete each required step by the stated deadline; not every programme uses the same selection stages.

Does meeting Amity Kolkata’s minimum eligibility guarantee admission?

No, meeting the minimum marks requirement does not guarantee a seat. Eligibility allows you to be considered; the programme may have additional tests, assessments, interviews, merit criteria or seat availability conditions. Confirm the complete selection process and any published criteria for your specific programme and intake.

Which documents are required for Amity University Kolkata admission?

Prepare academic marksheets, identity proof and any programme-specific documents. UG applicants should keep Class X and XII records; PG applicants should also prepare graduation marksheets and degree/provisional proof. Entrance scorecards, photographs and scholarship/category documents may be requested. Use the current portal checklist as the final list.

How can I check my Amity Kolkata application or admission status?

Use the official applicant portal and your application credentials. Check for status changes, assessment invitations, missing documents, offer letters, payment instructions and reporting notices. If a status is unclear or no update appears, contact the admissions office using contact details on Amity’s official website and include your application number.

Are scholarships available during Amity Kolkata admission, and how do I apply?

Scholarship availability, amounts, eligibility and renewal rules must be checked for the 2027 intake. Do not rely on older percentage thresholds as current policy. Ask whether scholarship consideration is automatic or requires a separate application, what documents are needed, which fee components are covered and whether the award can be renewed.

Follow the dates and conditions in the official offer and reporting notice.
