Entrance Exams and Selection Criteria

Amity Kolkata does not use one entrance exam for every course. MBA applicants may submit accepted management entrance scores such as CAT, MAT or GMAT, subject to the current programme rules. Engineering and Law applicants should follow the selection instructions on their specific course page. Do not assume JEE Main, CLAT or any other score is compulsory unless the current Kolkata programme notice says so.

A programme-wise competitive cutoff or closing score was not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed. Minimum eligibility is not a guaranteed admission cutoff.

How to Apply to Amity University Kolkata

  1. Open the official Kolkata admission portal and choose the correct campus.
  2. Select the programme and read its programme code, eligibility and fee category.
  3. Register with valid contact details and complete the application form.
  4. Upload academic records and entrance scorecards, where required.
  5. Complete the assessment, interview or other selection stage stated for the programme.
  6. Track the portal for the selection decision and written offer.
  7. Verify the full fee schedule and refund policy before paying.

Documents to prepare

  • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets
  • Graduation records for postgraduate applicants
  • Entrance scorecard where applicable
  • Photo ID and recent photograph
  • Transfer/migration certificate if requested
  • Scholarship documents where relevant

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a common Amity Kolkata cutoff for every course?

No. Eligibility and selection rules differ by programme. The university does not publish one common cutoff for MBA, BTech, Law and other degrees. Applicants should check the exact course code and selection notice.

Can I apply to Amity Kolkata without CAT?

CAT is one MBA entrance route, not necessarily the only route. The university also lists MAT and GMAT in its management admission information. Confirm the current accepted-score list for the exact MBA programme.

Are admission dates for 2027–28 fully announced?

The official portal advertises the 2027–28 intake, but a consolidated course-wise application calendar was not available in the reviewed sources. Check the portal and your applicant dashboard for the last date and selection schedule.

Sources are linked beside the relevant data. Programme availability and fees can change by intake. Verify the final offer and fee schedule with Amity University Kolkata before payment.
