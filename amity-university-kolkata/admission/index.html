UG admission requirements

Most undergraduate programs require Class 12 completion with course-specific subjects and marks. BTech applicants should verify Physics, Mathematics and the accepted third-subject combination. Integrated Law programs require Class 12 eligibility. Architecture applicants must check Council of Architecture requirements and the applicable NATA/JEE Paper 2 route.

PG admission requirements

Postgraduate applicants need a bachelor’s degree in the required discipline. MBA candidates may use CAT, MAT, GMAT or other routes listed for the program, and selection may include interview or assessment. Requirements differ by specialization.

How to apply

  1. Open the official admission portal and choose the campus and program.
  2. Review eligibility, fees, entrance requirements and selection stages.
  3. Submit the online form and application fee.
  4. Upload academic details and qualifying exam scores.
  5. Complete the test, interview or other selection stage if required.
  6. Read the offer letter, fee category and payment deadlines carefully.
  7. Complete document verification and enrolment.

Documents

  • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets/certificates
  • Graduation marksheets and degree certificate for PG applicants
  • Entrance scorecard, if applicable
  • Photo ID and passport-size photographs
  • Migration/transfer certificate, if required
  • Scholarship or category documents, where applicable

Cutoff and direct admission

No single cutoff is published for all programs. Eligibility percentages are not selection cutoffs. The university advertises a Scholars’ Fast Track route for some high-scoring Class 12 applicants, but the webpage includes older criteria. Confirm program eligibility and current admission terms in writing.

Amity Kolkata FAQs

When should I apply?

The official portal advertises 2027 intake, but course-specific deadlines were not verified. Apply early and check the portal frequently.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

Accepted tests and selection routes vary. Confirm test list, score validity and interview/GD requirements.

Can I get direct admission?

Verify whether Scholars’ Fast Track applies to your program and intake. Older criteria are not a current guarantee; ask for written terms.

Which documents should I prepare?

Prepare Class 10/12 certificates, graduation records for PG, scorecards where needed, ID, photos and scholarship documents. Follow the official checklist.

Is admission merit-based or entrance-based?

The route depends on the programme. Confirm whether your course uses academic merit, an accepted entrance score, university assessment, interview or a combination of these criteria.

Can I apply before my final result?

Final-year eligibility and provisional admission rules vary by programme. Confirm whether pending results are accepted and the deadline to submit final marks or degree proof.

Does Amity Kolkata publish a guaranteed cutoff?

A single guaranteed cutoff is not verified. Minimum eligibility or entrance requirements should not be interpreted as guaranteed selection; confirm the programme’s actual shortlist and selection process.

What happens after an offer?

Review the offer conditions, fee instalments, scholarship terms, refund policy and reporting schedule. Keep receipts and submit original documents for verification as instructed by the university.
