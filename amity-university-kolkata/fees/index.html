Amity Kolkata FAQs

Where are 2027 fees published?

Use the official prospectus and offer letter. Current course-wise 2027 fees were not verified; third-party estimates are excluded.

Does tuition include hostel or mess?

Confirm tuition, hostel, deposits, mess/food, transport and academic charges separately.

Can fees change during the course?

Check official fee terms for revisions; do not assume a semester amount remains constant.

What should I ask before paying?

Request itemized fees, fee category, deposits, refund deadlines, hostel/mess costs and payment schedule in writing.

What is the complete course cost?

The complete cost can include tuition, registration, examination, lab, security deposit, hostel, mess, transport and personal expenses. Ask for a written year-wise/semester-wise breakdown and identify refundable deposits.

Are hostel and mess charges included in tuition?

Inclusion was not verified for the current intake. Confirm hostel room type, mess plan, annual rate, deposit, utilities, transport and whether residence is optional or compulsory.

Can sponsored and non-sponsored fees differ?

Fee categories may differ by programme and admission offer. Confirm your category, applicable semester rate, total course cost and whether the rate can change during the programme.

How should I check scholarship impact on fees?

Verify the scholarship amount, qualifying criteria, renewal conditions, exclusions and whether it is applied to tuition each semester or credited separately. Obtain the terms for your admission year.
