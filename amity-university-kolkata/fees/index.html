Quick Answer: Amity Kolkata Fees

Amity Kolkata tuition varies by programme and category. Official non-sponsored examples include MBA ₹2.445 Lakhs per semester, BTech CSE ₹1.345 Lakhs and BTech CSE AI & ML ₹1.485 Lakhs. These are published examples, not verified 2027 totals. Hostel, mess, deposits and other academic charges must be confirmed separately.

Amity Kolkata Fees FAQs

What is Amity Kolkata MBA tuition?

The official example is ₹2.445 Lakhs for the first semester. Two semesters at the same rate would equal ₹4.89 Lakhs, but that is a calculation, not a verified 2027 tariff. Confirm the second-semester rate, later-year charges, fee category, and any registration or exam charges in your offer.

What is the BTech CSE fee?

The non-sponsored example is ₹1.345 Lakhs per semester. First-year tuition estimates ₹2.69 Lakhs if the same rate applies twice. This is not a confirmed 2027 fee. Ask for an itemized branch fee schedule and separately verify hostel, mess, deposits and additional academic costs.

Is hostel included in tuition?

Hostel and mess are not included in the tuition examples shown. The current tariff was not verified in the official source set. Ask for room options, annual charges, security deposit, mess plan, utilities, transport and refund terms. Add these costs to your budget if you plan to live on campus.

What does the fee include?

The verified figures are tuition examples; a full inclusions list is not available here. Ask whether registration, examination, library, labs, workshops, consumables, activities, transport or insurance are charged separately. Get one-time, semester-wise and refundable costs in writing.

What is BTech AI & ML tuition?

The official semester example is ₹1.485 Lakhs. Two semesters at that rate would be ₹2.97 Lakhs first-year tuition. Confirm the 2027 amount, sponsored/non-sponsored category, and additional costs; the estimate assumes no change between semesters.

Are scholarships available?

Current 2027 scholarship terms were not verified. An older official scholarship notice contains historic academic thresholds, not current guarantees. Ask for scholarship amounts, eligibility, renewal, exclusions and how the award affects each semester's payable fee.

How do I calculate total degree cost?

Use the full semester-wise tariff and add non-tuition expenses. Include tuition, registration, examinations, labs, hostel/mess, transport, materials and living costs. Check fee revision terms and refundable deposits. Do not extrapolate an old semester rate across the entire course without confirmation.

Do sponsored and non-sponsored students pay the same?

The listed examples are non-sponsored rates. Sponsored fees may differ by course and intake. Confirm the category on your admission offer, the exact rate for each semester, any fee revision rule and the payment schedule before accepting the seat.
