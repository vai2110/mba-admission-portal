How to Calculate the Total Course Cost

For MBA, multiply the confirmed semester tuition by four semesters only if the university confirms the same fee applies across all four semesters. For BTech, use eight semesters; for five-year integrated Law, use ten semesters. These calculations are not official total-course quotes because future-year fee revisions and additional charges may apply.

Ask the admission office for a year-wise written fee schedule, sponsored/non-sponsored category, hostel and mess tariff, refundable deposit, examination charges and cancellation/refund policy. Keep the written schedule with the admission offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the published MBA fee the total two-year cost?

No. The cited figure is the first-year non-sponsored semester tuition. A full two-year estimate requires confirmation of all four semester charges, future-year revisions, hostel, mess, deposits and other applicable fees.

Do sponsored and non-sponsored fees differ?

The university's programme pages identify fee categories for some programmes. Confirm which category applies to your offer. Do not compare a sponsored fee with another university's standard tuition.

Does the listed tuition include hostel and mess?

The reviewed course-detail pages show programme fees but do not establish that hostel and mess are included. Ask for the current written residential fee schedule before calculating your total budget.
