Quick Answer: Amity Kolkata MBA

Amity Kolkata lists a two-year MBA and eligibility of 50% in graduation and Class XII. The official non-sponsored semester tuition example is ₹2.445 Lakhs; first-year tuition would be ₹4.89 Lakhs if the same rate applies twice. Amity Kolkata MBA FAQs

What is MBA eligibility?

The official MBA page lists 50% in graduation and 50% in Class XII. Check qualifying degree rules, final-year applicants, entrance tests, interviews and any specialization-specific requirements for 2027 before submitting your application.

What is the MBA fee?

The first-semester non-sponsored tuition example is ₹2.445 Lakhs. A first-year estimate is ₹4.89 Lakhs if that rate applies to both semesters. Confirm the official fee for each semester, academic charges, hostel/mess and deposits for your offer category.

Which entrance exams are accepted?

The 2027 accepted test list was not verified. Confirm external tests, score validity, university assessment, interview/GD and exemptions for your selected MBA variant. Do not rely on test requirements from other campuses or older admission cycles.

Which MBA specializations are listed?

Official listings include Business Analytics, Financial Technology and Logistics & Supply Chain variants. Confirm current intake availability, specialization code, seats, syllabus, electives and whether the option appears in the 2027 Kolkata application.

What are MBA placements?

MBA-specific salary outcomes were not verified. The PG 2-year NIRF median is ₹10.65 Lakhs for 2023–24 across a group of programmes. Request MBA median/average, placed/eligible counts, job roles, recruiters, internships and PPOs for the latest cohort.

Is the MBA worth its fee?

ROI depends on your career target and total cost. Include tuition, hostel, living expenses, financing and lost income, and compare these with MBA-specific roles and salary distributions. Highest-package headlines do not represent typical outcomes; seek the latest cohort report before deciding.

Is an interview or GD part of selection?

Confirm assessment stages from the current policy. Ask whether entrance scores, university tests, interviews, GD, written assessments or other criteria are used and whether they carry weightage. Meeting academic eligibility is not the same as receiving an offer.

Does the MBA include a summer internship?

The official curriculum mentions a summer internship. Confirm duration, credit, assessment, internship support, whether students source placements independently and how internship/PPO outcomes are reported for your batch.
