Amity University Kolkata MBA Overview

Amity Business School, Kolkata lists a two-year full-time MBA. The official course page identifies the programme code as 19019 and shows core management subjects such as managerial economics, marketing management, organisational behaviour, financial management, business analytics and operations management.

The university also lists MBA variants such as Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Financial Technology and Logistics & Supply Chain Management in its Kolkata programme catalogue. Check the current 2027–28 intake status for the exact variant.

Sources: MBA programme code 19019; University admission Q&A; Official programme list.

MBA Eligibility and Entrance Exams

The MBA course page specifies at least 50% in graduation and 50% in Class 12. The university's admission blog says graduates from any stream may apply. It lists CAT, MAT, XAT, NMAT or the university's written test followed by a personal interview. Confirm the accepted route for your MBA variant.

A confirmed MBA CAT percentile cutoff or course-wise closing score was not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed. Meeting the minimum marks does not guarantee selection. Meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee selection.

Amity Kolkata MBA Fees

The official MBA course page lists a first-year non-sponsored semester fee of ₹2.445 lakh. That equals approximately ₹4.89 lakh for two semesters if both are billed at the same listed rate. It is not a confirmed two-year total. The university also uses sponsored and non-sponsored fee categories. Its admission blog reports MBA annual fees up to ₹7.33 lakh, while the course page shows the non-sponsored semester rate. Confirm your category and full 2027–28 quote in writing.

MBA Placements and Career Outcomes

The latest NIRF 2026 placement figures summarized by Careers360 report 216 placed students among 254 graduates in the Management and Business Administration category for 2024–25, with a median salary of ₹9.50 LPA. This category is not necessarily MBA-only. Request the university's MBA-specific batch report before treating it as an MBA outcome.

The university's placement page lists ₹32.21 LPA as its highest salary but does not identify the programme or batch year. An MBA-specific average salary and placement rate were not officially disclosed in the reviewed sources.

Sources: NIRF 2025 disclosure; Placement page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amity Kolkata MBA a two-year programme?

Yes. The Kolkata MBA course page lists a two-year duration under Amity Business School, Kolkata. The programme code shown on the official listing is 19019. Verify the active 2027–28 intake before applying.

Is CAT compulsory for Amity Kolkata MBA?

CAT is one of the management entrance routes referenced by the university. MAT and GMAT are also listed. Confirm the current accepted-exam list and selection stages for the specific MBA variant.

Does the MBA placement median equal the MBA average package?

No. The ₹10.65 LPA figure comes from the broad NIRF PG two-year category for 2023–24. It is a median, not an MBA-only average. Ask the university for the programme-specific batch report.
