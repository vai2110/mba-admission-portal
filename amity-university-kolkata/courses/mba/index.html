Amity University Kolkata MBA

Amity University Kolkata offers a two-year MBA and listed specializations such as Business Analytics, Financial Technology, and Logistics & Supply Chain Management. The program is designed for graduates pursuing management careers, but students should compare specialization, fees and reported outcomes before enrolling.

Who should consider this MBA?

Consider the MBA if you want a structured management degree, access to career development support, and a specialization aligned with your goals. It may be relevant for careers in finance, marketing, analytics, technology services, operations, HR and business development. Your work experience, internships, communication, analytical skills and projects will influence job outcomes.

Admission and selection

Applicants generally need a bachelor’s degree with the prescribed marks. The official regulations list management entrance tests such as CAT, MAT or GMAT and may include an interview, GD, AMCAT or other university process depending on the course. There is no verified universal MBA cutoff published for all applicants.

Check current entrance exam acceptance, score validity, interview format, sponsored/non-sponsored fee category and application deadline before submitting your application. Useful CollegeDecoded exam guides: CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT and GMAT. For current dates, use the admission guide.

Specializations

The official course catalogue lists MBA options including Business Analytics, Financial Technology and Logistics & Supply Chain Management. Check the course page for curriculum, elective availability, tools, project work and whether the specialization is offered for your intake.

MBA placement details to request

Ask for specialization-wise placed/eligible counts, median and average salaries, roles, recruiters and internships. University-wide PG figures are not MBA-specific.

Is the MBA worth the cost?

Compare the estimated tuition with realistic salary outcomes, living expenses, loan interest and opportunity cost. Ask for MBA specialization-wise placement outcomes and the roles offered. A high university-wide package is not enough to estimate your personal return on investment.

See the university overview, fee page and placement details for the full context.

Amity Kolkata FAQs

Which MBA specializations are listed?

Official catalogue lists Business Analytics, Financial Technology and Logistics & Supply Chain options. Confirm 2027 availability.

What is MBA eligibility?

Check current degree marks, accepted tests and selection stages in the official prospectus.

Which entrance tests are accepted?

Confirm the current MBA test list and interview/GD requirements with admissions.

Is MBA-specific placement data available?

Request specialization-wise placed/eligible counts, median, roles, recruiters and internships. University-wide PG data is not MBA-specific.

How do I confirm MBA specializations for the new batch?

Use the official MBA catalogue and admission notice to verify the specialization list, intake availability, curriculum, electives and any prerequisites. Specializations can change between admission cycles.

Which entrance scores are accepted for MBA?

Accepted test scores and selection stages can change by intake. Confirm the 2027 MBA policy for external scores, university tests, interviews and any additional assessment.

What placement information should MBA applicants request?

Request MBA-specific median and average salary, placed/eligible/participating counts, recruiter roles, internship and PPO outcomes, and specialization-level breakdown for the latest graduating cohort..

What should I include in MBA ROI?

Include tuition for both years, hostel/mess and living costs, financing interest, current income/opportunity cost and course-specific placement outcomes. Use median outcomes, not the highest salary headline.
