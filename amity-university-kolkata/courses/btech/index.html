Amity Kolkata BTech Eligibility and Admission

Subject requirements vary by branch

For CSE, the official non-sponsored example requires 50% aggregate and 55% in the stated aggregate including English, Physics, Mathematics and an approved third subject. Sponsored category criteria differ. Do not apply the CSE rule to other branches without checking their programme entries.

Entrance tests and assessment

The accepted test, score rules and selection route for 2027 should be verified branch-wise. Confirm whether entrance score, merit, university assessment or interview is used and what stage is decisive.

Application sequence

  1. Confirm exact branch and eligibility.
  2. Check current entrance route and application dates.
  3. Submit application and retain receipt.
  4. Complete assessments/interview if required.
  5. Review offer and full fee schedule.

What to Evaluate in the BTech Curriculum

CSE and computing branches

Compare programming, data structures, mathematics, core computing, branch electives, AI/ML or security modules, software projects and final-year capstone credits. Review actual modules rather than relying on a specialization label.

ECE, Civil and Mechanical

Check lab hours, design projects, workshop and field exposure, equipment, software/tool training, electives and capstone requirements. Ask about access to facilities outside scheduled practicals.

Biotechnology

Verify the lab course list, instrumentation, practical hours, research projects, consumables and internship options. Ask whether any lab or project costs are billed separately.

Amity Kolkata BTech Placements

Amity Kolkata BTech FAQs

Which BTech branches are offered?

The official catalogue lists CSE, AI, AI & ML, AI & Data Science, Cyber Security, ECE, Civil, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology. Availability and intake can change. Confirm the exact branch title and seats for the 2027 Kolkata intake.

What is CSE tuition?

The non-sponsored semester example is ₹1.345 Lakhs. First-year tuition estimates ₹2.69 Lakhs at the same rate for two semesters. This is not a confirmed 2027 schedule; request the branch and category fee sheet, plus extra charges.

What is CSE eligibility?

The official example lists 50% overall and 55% in the specified subject aggregate. The subject set includes English, Physics, Mathematics and an approved third subject. Sponsored criteria may differ; verify current eligibility and exact calculations.

Is JEE Main required?

Confirm the current accepted admission route for your branch. The 2027 test list was not verified for all branches. Ask whether JEE Main, Amity JEE or another assessment is accepted and whether it affects shortlisting or selection.

What is AI & ML tuition?

The official example is ₹1.485 Lakhs per semester. Two semesters at the same rate estimate ₹2.97 Lakhs for year one. Confirm the current batch tariff, category and non-tuition charges.

Are branch-wise placements published?

Branch-specific outcomes were not verified in the official report set. The NIRF UG 4-year median combines programmes. Request the exact branch's placed and eligible counts, salary distribution, roles, recruiter list and internships for the latest batch.

What should I check in labs?

Request the branch lab and equipment list and student access details. Ask about practical hours, project facilities, software/tool licences, equipment availability and any lab consumables or charges not covered in tuition.

Does tuition include lab charges?

Fee inclusions were not confirmed from a full official schedule. Ask whether lab, workshop, examination, project materials, software, hostel, mess, transport and refundable deposits are included or billed separately before finalizing your budget.
