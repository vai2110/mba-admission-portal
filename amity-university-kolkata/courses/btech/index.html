Placement data

NIRF 2025 UG 4-year group reports 299 placed among 332 students graduating in minimum time for the 2023–24 cohort, with median salary ₹9.235 Lakhs. This includes multiple programmes and is not BTech branch-specific. Request CSE/AI/ECE/Civil/Mechanical/Biotechnology reports separately.

Student takeaway: Compare the branch curriculum, labs, total cost and branch-level placement evidence—not the campus-wide figure.

Amity Kolkata FAQs

Which BTech branches are listed?

Official catalogue lists Computer Science, AI, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology, among others. Confirm exact titles and intake.

What subjects and marks are required?

Verify branch-specific subjects and minimum marks in the official 2027 prospectus.

Is JEE Main required?

Confirm whether JEE Main, Amity JEE or another route applies to the selected branch.

Are branch-wise placement reports available?

Ask for branch-specific cohort counts, salary distribution, roles, recruiters and internship data. University-wide UG data is not branch-specific.

How do I confirm the exact BTech branch?

Check the current official BTech catalogue for the branch title, intake availability, specialization options, curriculum and eligibility. Branch labels and intake can change; confirm for 2027 before submitting an application.

Are labs available for every BTech branch?

Ask for the lab and equipment list for your branch, access schedule, project facilities, software/tools, practical coursework and whether sessions are conducted on campus..

Does BTech admission require JEE Main?

The accepted route depends on the programme and current admission policy. Confirm whether JEE Main, other scores, merit or a university assessment is accepted for the selected branch and intake.

What placement data should engineering applicants compare?

Compare branch-wise median salary, roles, recruiter names, eligible/placed counts, internships and PPOs for the same graduating batch. Campus-wide placement claims do not guarantee branch outcomes.
