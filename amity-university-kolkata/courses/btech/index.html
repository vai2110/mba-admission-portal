Amity University Kolkata BTech Overview

Amity School of Engineering and Technology, Kolkata lists a four-year BTech degree. Its branches include Computer Science & Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, AI & Machine Learning, AI & Data Science, Cyber Security with Blockchain Technology, Cloud Computing, Electronics & Communication, Civil Engineering, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology. Branch availability can change by intake.

BTech Eligibility and Admission

For the non-sponsored BTech CSE category, the official page specifies 50% aggregate in Class 12 and at least 55% in Physics, Mathematics and an eligible third subject. Sponsored-category eligibility may be relaxed, as stated on the programme page. Other branches can have different subject requirements; Civil and Mechanical listings, for example, state PCM minimums.

Follow the live Kolkata engineering admission notice for the current selection route. JEE Main may be relevant to engineering applications, but do not assume it is compulsory for every branch unless the current course notice confirms it. A published BTech closing cutoff was not found.

BTech Placement Data

The NIRF 2025 submission reports 299 placed students among 332 graduates in the UG four-year category for 2023–24, with a median salary of ₹9.235 LPA. This group covers multiple four-year undergraduate programmes, not only BTech. A separate current BTech CSE average package or placement rate was not officially disclosed in the sources reviewed.

Source: Amity Kolkata NIRF 2025 submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of BTech at Amity University Kolkata?

The official engineering course listings show a four-year duration for regular BTech programmes at the Kolkata campus. Branch availability and lateral-entry options should be confirmed for the specific admission year.

What is the BTech CSE eligibility at Amity Kolkata?

The non-sponsored CSE listing specifies 50% aggregate in Class 12 and at least 55% in Physics, Mathematics and an eligible third subject. Check the live course page for the current intake and sponsored-category conditions.

Is the NIRF median salary the BTech CSE average package?

No. The ₹9.235 LPA figure is the median for the broad four-year UG category in the 2023–24 NIRF disclosure. It is not a BTech CSE-only average or guaranteed salary.
