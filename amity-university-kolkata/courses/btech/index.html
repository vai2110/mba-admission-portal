Eligibility for BTech

Applicants generally need Class 12 with Physics, Mathematics and an accepted third subject. Minimum marks and subject combinations vary by branch and category. Check the current prospectus for exact eligibility before applying.

Admission and cutoff

Engineering admissions may use qualifying marks, JEE Main, Amity JEE or other university selection routes as applicable. The exact cutoff for 2027 is not officially disclosed in the reviewed sources. A cutoff can vary by branch, category and seat availability.

Curriculum and learning

Review the branch curriculum, programming and math requirements, lab work, electives, internships and capstone projects. Amity’s official admissions page advertises 60+ hi-tech laboratories and learning studios across campus. Applicants should ask which facilities are available to their specific BTech branch.

Placement data

The latest NIRF 2026 Overall disclosure reports 2024–25 median salary of ₹4.20 LPA for UG four-year programs, with 290 students placed among 368 graduates. It is not a BTech CSE-only result. The 2023 highest salary reported by the university was ₹32.21 LPA across its placement record.
