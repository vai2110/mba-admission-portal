Amity Kolkata FAQs

What programs are listed?

Official program catalogue lists engineering, management, law, computer applications, sciences, psychology, commerce, design, media, architecture, hospitality and education. Confirm the exact program and intake.

Are specializations open in 2027?

Confirm course, specialization, campus and intake on the current official catalogue.

How do I check eligibility?

Review the current prospectus for subjects, marks, duration and selection steps.

How do I compare course fees?

Compare official semester schedules, deposits and other charges for the same intake.

Which course page should I use for details?

Use the MBA, BTech or Law course pages for course-specific admission, fee, curriculum and placement questions. The university’s official programme catalogue remains the final source for current availability and eligibility.

How do I confirm a course is offered in 2027?

Check the official programme catalogue and admissions portal for the current intake. Course titles, specializations and seats can change; do not rely only on an older page or listing.

Are fees the same for every course?

No. Fees can vary by degree, specialization, fee category, semester and admission year. Request the complete course-specific schedule and any compulsory additional charges.

Can students compare placements by course?

Yes, but only using reports for the same graduating year and programme. Ask for eligible, participating and placed counts, median salary, roles and recruiter details. Campus-wide statistics may not represent a particular course.
