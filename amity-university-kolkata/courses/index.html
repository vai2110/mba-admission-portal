Frequently Asked Questions

Does Amity University Kolkata offer MBA and BTech?

Yes. The Kolkata campus lists a two-year MBA and four-year BTech programmes. Engineering branches include Computer Science and related specialisations, alongside Electronics & Communication, Civil, Mechanical & Automation and Biotechnology.

Does Amity Kolkata offer integrated Law courses?

Yes. Its Kolkata Law School lists BA LLB (Hons.), BBA LLB (Hons.) and BCom LLB (Hons.), along with three-year LLB and LLM options. Check the current course page for programme code and admission status.

Are all listed courses open for the 2027–28 intake?

Not necessarily. The university's catalogue includes programmes whose admission status can vary. Confirm that the exact programme is open for the 2027–28 session before paying an application fee.
