BA LLB vs BBA LLB vs BCom LLB: How to Choose

  • BA LLB: inspect social science, policy, humanities and law foundations.
  • BBA LLB: review business, management and corporate-law subjects.
  • BCom LLB: compare commerce, finance, accounting and commercial-law modules.
  • Across all: evaluate core legal subjects, electives, practical credits, internship access, faculty, fees and career fit.

Student takeaway: Choose the foundation subjects you want to study alongside law, then confirm course cost and practical training in writing.

Amity Kolkata Law FAQs

Which law courses are listed at Amity Kolkata?

The official catalogue lists BA LLB (Hons.), BBA LLB (Hons.), BCom LLB (Hons.), LLB and LLM (Business Law). Verify that each programme is open for the 2027 Kolkata intake, and confirm exact title, seats, duration and approvals before applying.

How does BA LLB differ from BBA LLB?

The integrated foundation is different—arts/social sciences compared with business/management. Compare official module lists, electives, credits and practical requirements. Choose the foundation that matches your interests, but do not infer a career or salary outcome from the degree label alone.

Is BCom LLB available?

BCom LLB (Hons.) appears in the official programme catalogue. Confirm current intake availability, eligibility, duration, tuition, syllabus and approved seat count. Use the current application listing to ensure the programme is open for your admission year.

What is the Law fee?

A current official 2027 Law fee schedule was not verified. Request semester tuition, course duration, one-time and recurring charges, practical or moot-court fees if applicable, hostel/mess, security deposits and refund rules for your exact degree.

What eligibility applies to integrated Law?

Eligibility depends on the exact degree and current prospectus. Verify Class XII marks, subjects or stream, entrance score, assessment and interview requirements. Do not use graduate-entry LLB or LLM eligibility for integrated programmes.

What practical training is provided?

Confirm the practical curriculum from the current official syllabus. Ask about moot courts, drafting, research, counselling, legal aid, internships and court visits, including which are compulsory, how they are assessed and whether opportunities are arranged by the department.

Are Law placements published?

How do I choose between integrated degrees?
