Law Courses at Amity University Kolkata

Amity Law School, Kolkata lists five-year integrated degrees, a three-year LLB and a one-year LLM (Business Law). Students should choose the programme based on whether they are applying after Class 12 or graduation.

Amity Kolkata Law Fees

The BA LLB (Hons.) course page lists a first-year non-sponsored semester fee of ₹1.09 lakh in the 2026 listing. The 2027–28 fee is not separately confirmed. Two semesters at that rate would be approximately ₹2.18 lakh for the first academic year. The current full five-year fee, sponsored-category fee, hostel and other charges should be confirmed in the 2027–28 fee schedule.

Source: Official BA LLB fee listing.

Law Admission and Selection

Applicants to integrated Law programmes need to meet the Class 12 eligibility requirement. LLB applicants must hold a qualifying bachelor's degree, while LLM applicants need an LLB or equivalent. The reviewed public pages do not provide a confirmed 2027–28 CLAT cutoff or course-wise closing score. Check the current admission notice for any entrance test, interview or selection stage.

The university's admission blog states that applicants with 80% or above in Class 12 may qualify for merit-based direct admission to BBA LLB. Do not extend that rule to BA LLB, BCom LLB or LLB without confirmation. Verify the programme code and current BCI-related regulatory details before accepting an offer.

Law Curriculum and Career Considerations

Compare the course's core legal subjects, moot-court practice, legal research, internships, drafting exercises and exposure to litigation or corporate legal work. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study Law at Amity Kolkata after Class 12?

Yes. The university lists five-year integrated programmes including BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB. The published eligibility for these courses is 10+2 with at least 50%, subject to the current admission notice.

Does Amity Kolkata offer a three-year LLB?

Yes. The Law programme listing includes a three-year LLB for graduates. The non-sponsored eligibility shown is graduation with at least 50%. Confirm the current programme code and admission status before applying.

Is CLAT compulsory for Amity University Kolkata Law admission?

The sources reviewed do not establish a compulsory CLAT cutoff for all Kolkata Law programmes. Check the current programme-specific selection notice. Do not treat a third-party expected cutoff as an official university requirement.
