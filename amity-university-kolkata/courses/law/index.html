Which law programs are listed?

How do integrated law degrees differ?

They combine law with arts/social sciences, business or commerce foundations. Compare official curricula and electives.

Can integrated law be joined after Class 12?

Confirm the current eligibility and selection rules in the 2027 prospectus.

What practical learning should I check?

Ask about moot court, legal aid, internships, research, court visits, mentoring and whether opportunities are curricular or optional.

How do I confirm the Law degree approval?

Check the current official programme entry and applicable regulatory approvals for the exact course, intake and duration. Confirm that the degree title and approved intake match the admission offer.

What practical training is part of the law programme?

Ask about moot court, legal drafting, research exercises, counselling simulations, internships, court visits and seminars. Confirm which are compulsory, how often they occur and how the department supports participation.

Which integrated law course should I choose?

Compare BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB by their subject mix, electives, eligibility, career interests, practical exposure and full-course cost. Choose the curriculum that fits your intended legal pathway.

What should law applicants check about placements?
