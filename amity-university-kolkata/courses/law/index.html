Amity University Kolkata Law Courses

Amity Law School Kolkata offers integrated undergraduate law degrees and postgraduate options. Students can study law after Class 12 through five-year integrated programs or after graduation through the three-year LLB route. Program availability and current admission rules should be confirmed.

Law programs

The official catalogue lists BA LLB (Hons.), BBA LLB (Hons.), BCom LLB (Hons.), LLB and LLM (Business Law). Confirm 2027 availability, duration, eligibility, approvals and current fees through the prospectus.

  • Integrated BA/BBA/BCom LLB: confirm entry requirements, duration and fee schedule.
  • LLB: verify prior degree requirements and current intake.
  • LLM (Business Law): verify current specialization, duration and fees.

BA LLB vs BBA LLB vs BCom LLB

BA LLB combines law with arts and social science subjects. BBA LLB includes business and management foundations. BCom LLB connects legal studies with commerce and finance. The choice should match your interest in corporate practice, litigation, public policy, commercial law or other legal career areas.

Admission and eligibility

Integrated law applicants need Class 12 with the minimum percentage specified for the program. LLB applicants must hold a bachelor’s degree. Admission may use academic merit, entrance assessment, written tests or interviews as specified by the university. A universal cutoff score was not officially disclosed.

Practical learning

Ask Amity Law School about moot court, legal aid, internships, research, court visits and faculty mentoring. Confirm which opportunities are part of the curriculum and which are optional.

Career paths

Law graduates can pursue litigation, corporate legal teams, compliance, contracts, legal research, policy, government exams, judiciary preparation where eligible, or further study. Employment outcomes depend on practical exposure, internships, writing and research ability, and the path chosen after graduation.

Use the admission page for application steps and the fee guide for cost information.
