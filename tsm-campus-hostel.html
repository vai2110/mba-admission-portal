TSM Campus

Where Is TSM Madurai?

Thiagarajar School of Management is located at Pamban Swamy Nagar, Madurai District, Thiruparankundram, Tamil Nadu 625005. The institute's location is relevant for applicants because TSM's core MBA and PGDM programmes are residential rather than commuter-only programmes.

Students moving from another city should therefore budget for the complete residential cost and consider travel to and from Madurai during breaks. The campus setting also creates a concentrated peer-learning environment because students live and study around the same academic schedule.

TSM Hostel and Accommodation

TSM's published fee structure states that the residential programmes use twin-sharing accommodation. Single occupancy is available subject to availability. Accommodation is shown as a separate component in the official two-year fee table rather than being hidden inside tuition.

Hostel, Mess and Total Cost: What Students Should Budget

TSM currently displays ₹3.50 lakh for accommodation and mess over two years for both MBA and PGDM. The total shown is ₹14.70 lakh for MBA and ₹15.70 lakh for PGDM after adding academic fees and the ₹20,000 refundable caution deposit.

This is more useful for a student than quoting tuition alone. A residential B-school decision should include accommodation, food, academic materials and the opportunity cost of leaving a job. Personal spending, travel and incidental expenses should be treated separately because they are not the same as the published programme fee.

What Student Life at TSM Is Built Around

TSM's programme model combines classroom learning with case studies, live projects, industry interactions, simulations, internships and co-curricular activities. The residential setting makes these activities part of a shared student environment rather than isolated classroom events.

Case-based learningStudents analyse business situations and defend recommendations rather than relying only on textbook theory.
Live projectsAcademic concepts are connected to practical business problems and industry exposure.
Industry interactionGuest sessions, corporate interactions and recruiter engagement help students understand workplace expectations.
Peer learningThe PGDM's stated PAN-India diversity can create a broader peer mix than a predominantly regional cohort.

Why the Residential Model Matters for TSM's PGDM

The PGDM includes several experiential components that go beyond regular classroom teaching. TSM's programme comparison identifies a mandatory international study tour, domestic outbound training, capstone simulation and a longer summer internship for PGDM students. These components are part of the programme's value proposition and should be considered when comparing the ₹15.70 lakh published PGDM total with another B-school.

The MBA also provides residential learning, industry interaction and a mandatory internship, but the PGDM-specific global and outbound components do not apply to MBA students.

TSM Campus and Hostel FAQs

Is TSM MBA residential?

Yes. TSM's MBA is a full-time residential programme, and accommodation and mess are included as a separate component in the published fee structure.

Is TSM PGDM residential?

Yes. The PGDM is a full-time residential programme, with accommodation and mess included in the published two-year cost.

Does TSM provide twin-sharing hostel rooms?

Yes. TSM's fee page states that students are accommodated in twin-sharing rooms; single occupancy is subject to availability.

How much are accommodation and mess at TSM?

The current official fee table shows ₹3.50 lakh for accommodation and mess over two years for both MBA and PGDM.

Where is TSM located?

TSM is at Pamban Swamy Nagar, Madurai District, Thiruparankundram, Tamil Nadu 625005.
