Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Program in Management (PGPM) at Great Lakes Gurgaon is a one-year full-time MBA designed for professionals with work experience. The institute positions it as an accelerated programme for career growth, transitions and leadership roles.

The programme combines an industry-oriented curriculum with faculty mentorship, live projects, experiential learning and global exposure. Great Lakes reports an average CTC of ₹16.2 LPA and highest CTC of ₹34.5 LPA for the 2026 class.

Who is PGPM really for?

PGPM makes the most sense if you already have meaningful professional experience and want to make a career move without spending two years away from work. The trade-off is a much more compressed academic and placement cycle.

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM Admission Process

1. Submit the online application

Eligible candidates complete the Great Lakes online application and pay the applicable application fee.

2. Profile and application screening

The institute evaluates standardised test scores, academic performance, work experience, personal and professional track record, management aptitude and demonstrated competencies.

3. AI-proctored video interview

The AI-proctored interview evaluates thought process and communication before shortlisted candidates proceed to the personal interview.

4. Personal Interview

The PI assesses areas such as general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, people skills, communication and personal, extracurricular or professional initiatives.

5. Final selection

Final decisions are based on holistic evaluation and programme fit.

Can I apply to both PGPM and PGDM?

Yes. Great Lakes states that eligible candidates can submit separate applications for both programmes. The admissions panel may recommend a specific programme based on assessment and programme fit.

What is included?

Tuition includes reading material such as case studies, textbooks and online resources. Hostel accommodation is mandatory and twin-sharing AC accommodation is provided. Vegetarian meals are included; non-vegetarian food is optional and charged extra.

Scholarships

Great Lakes currently lists Diversity & Merit Scholarships for PGPM, including 40 scholarships worth ₹5 lakh each, with at least 30% reserved for female candidates. It also lists the Dr. Bala V. Balachandran Merit Scholarship with a 100% tuition fee waiver for exceptional candidates. Scholarship availability and terms can change.

Fee vs ROI

The ₹22.50 lakh figure is the published programme fee, not a guarantee of personal ROI. Compare it with the PGPM placement trend, your expected role, your pre-MBA salary and the opportunity cost of taking a year away from work.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM?

The PGPM uses a compressed one-year format, so students should expect an intensive academic schedule. Great Lakes highlights live projects, industry interaction, experiential learning, faculty mentorship and global learning opportunities.

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Live business projects
  • Industry and CXO interactions
  • Career-focused experiential learning
  • Faculty mentorship
  • Optional international learning

What the one-year format means

You have less time to experiment. Enter with a reasonably clear target role and use projects, mentorship and industry exposure to strengthen that direction quickly.
