Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Program in Management (PGPM) at Great Lakes Gurgaon is a one-year full-time MBA designed for professionals with work experience. The institute positions it as a fast-track programme for career acceleration, transitions and leadership roles.

The current Gurgaon PGPM highlights live projects, industry interaction and optional international learning opportunities. The institute's latest programme page reports an average package of ₹16.2 LPA, highest CTC of ₹34.5 LPA and an average post-MBA salary that is 2.6 times the average incoming CTC for the 2026 class. citeturn0search8

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM Admission Process

1. Submit the online application

Applicants complete the Great Lakes application and pay the published application fee of ₹2,200. Applications are made online through the institute's website. citeturn1search2

2. Application screening

Great Lakes considers standardised test scores, academic performance, work experience, leadership potential and diversity of experience.

3. AI-proctored video interview

The AI-proctored interview assesses thought process and communication skills before shortlisted candidates proceed to the personal interview.

4. Personal Interview

The personal interview evaluates areas including general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, people skills, communication and professional/personal initiatives. citeturn1search2

5. Final selection

The final decision is based on the candidate's overall profile and fit with the programme rather than only the entrance-test score.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM?

The PGPM uses a compressed one-year format, so students should expect an intensive academic schedule. Great Lakes currently highlights live projects in the Gurgaon PGPM 2.0, including six-month projects that address real business problems with partner organisations. citeturn0search8

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Live business projects
  • Industry and CXO interactions
  • Career-focused experiential learning
  • Optional international learning

What the one-year format means

You get less calendar time than a conventional two-year MBA. Candidates should therefore enter with a clear career objective and be ready for an intensive academic and placement-preparation cycle.

Who Should Choose Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM?

Good fit

  • 2+ years of work experience
  • Want a one-year MBA
  • Seeking career acceleration or transition
  • Comfortable with an intensive academic schedule

Consider PGDM instead if

  • You are a fresher or have less than 36 months of experience.
  • You want a two-year MBA with a longer internship and specialisation journey.
