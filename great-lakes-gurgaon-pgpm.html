Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM: Programme Overview

The Post Graduate Program in Management (PGPM) at Great Lakes Gurgaon is a one-year full-time MBA designed for professionals with work experience. The institute positions it as a fast-track programme for career acceleration, transitions and leadership roles.

The current Gurgaon PGPM highlights live projects, industry interaction and optional international learning opportunities. The institute reports an average CTC of ₹16.2 LPA and highest CTC of ₹34.5 LPA for the 2026 class. citeturn0search8turn0search3

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM Admission Process

1. Submit the online application

Applicants complete the Great Lakes application and pay the published application fee. Applications are made online through the institute's website. citeturn0search7

2. Application screening

Great Lakes considers standardised test scores, academic performance, work experience, leadership potential and diversity of experience.

3. AI-proctored video interview

The AI-proctored interview assesses thought process and communication skills before shortlisted candidates proceed to the personal interview.

4. Personal Interview

The personal interview evaluates general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, people skills, communication and professional/personal initiatives. citeturn0search7

5. Final selection

The final decision is based on the candidate's overall profile and fit with the programme.

What Will You Study in Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM?

The PGPM uses a compressed one-year format, so students should expect an intensive academic schedule. Great Lakes highlights live projects, industry interaction and experiential learning.

Learning exposure

  • Management fundamentals
  • Live business projects
  • Industry and CXO interactions
  • Career-focused experiential learning
  • Optional international learning

What the one-year format means

Candidates should enter with a clear career objective and be ready for an intensive academic and placement-preparation cycle.

Who Should Choose Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM?

Good fit

  • 2+ years of work experience
  • Want a one-year MBA
  • Seeking career acceleration or transition
  • Comfortable with an intensive academic schedule

Consider PGDM instead if

  • You are a fresher or have less than 36 months of experience.
  • You want a two-year MBA with a longer internship and specialisation journey.

Great Lakes Gurgaon PGPM FAQs

What is the PGPM fee?

The published 2026-27 total fee is ₹22.50 lakh.

What extra costs should students budget for?

Laptop, personal travel, personal expenses and optional non-vegetarian food are additional considerations. International learning can also require separate personal travel costs.

What is the latest average PGPM CTC?

₹16.2 LPA for the 2026 class.

Are the 2027 dates official?

No. The 2027 dates on this page are clearly marked tentative and are planning estimates until Great Lakes publishes its official calendar.
