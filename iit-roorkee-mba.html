Quick answer

IIT Roorkee MBA is a 2-year, full-time residential programme. For 2026-28, the CAT route has 95 seats. The admission process uses CAT-based initial shortlisting followed by GD/PI, with final merit based on CAT, Interview and Work Experience. The 2026-27 fee sheet gives a published four-semester total of ₹9,74,200 for General/GEN-EWS/OBC students under Dining & Residing.

IIT Roorkee MBA: Programme Overview

DoMS describes the MBA as an 8-term, 76-credit residential programme over two academic years. The first year focuses on core management concepts and techniques; the second year moves to advanced learning and practice in chosen specialisation areas.

Students can pursue dual specialisation in any two of Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Systems and Human Resource Management.

IIT Roorkee MBA Eligibility 2026-28

The exact eligibility conditions should be checked against the current admission brochure. The 2026-28 brochure specifies the CAT-based admission route and also provides a separate route for qualifying IIT graduates.

  • Applicants must satisfy the institute's prescribed qualifying-degree and academic requirements.
  • CAT is the principal qualifying route for the regular MBA intake.
  • JEE/JEE Advanced-qualified IIT graduates with CGPA 7 or above on a 10-point scale are stated to be exempt from CAT and can be directly called for Personal Interview, subject to the brochure conditions.

Do not use a generic “50% graduation” rule for IIT Roorkee unless the current official brochure supports that exact figure for the relevant applicant category.

How to Get Admission to IIT Roorkee MBA

  1. Appear for CAT and satisfy the applicable qualifying conditions, unless eligible for the IIT-graduate CAT exemption.
  2. Submit the IIT Roorkee MBA application within the published application window.
  3. Wait for the institute's CAT-based shortlist for GD/PI.
  4. Attend the GD/PI process at the allotted centre/date.
  5. Compete for the final merit list, which IIT Roorkee states is based on CAT, Interview and Work Experience.
  6. After an offer, complete fee payment and admission/document verification.

IIT Roorkee MBA Selection Criteria

IIT Roorkee's published admission information states that initial shortlisting for interviews is based on CAT 2025 performance. The final merit for offers is based on aggregate performance in CAT 2025, Interview and Work Experience.

The institute does not publish a single CAT-percentile number on the admission information used here as an “official final cutoff”. Therefore this page does not invent a cutoff percentile.

Important: A qualifying CAT threshold and the actual shortlist level are not necessarily the same. Use the official shortlisting notice for the relevant cycle.

IIT Roorkee MBA Curriculum & Specialisations

The programme is 76 credits over eight terms. DoMS currently lists dual specialisation choices in Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Systems and Human Resource Management.

The structure is deliberately split between a common management foundation and advanced specialisation-oriented learning. Students should use the official syllabus linked by DoMS for the detailed course-by-course paper list because individual course offerings can change.
