Quick answer

IIT Roorkee's MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme. For 2026-28, the CAT route lists 95 seats. The institute uses CAT-based shortlisting followed by GD/PI, with final merit based on CAT, Interview and Work Experience. The 2026-27 published four-semester total is ₹9,74,200 for General/GEN-EWS/OBC students under Dining & Residing.

IIT Roorkee MBA Overview

DoMS describes the MBA as an 8-term, 76-credit residential programme over two academic years. The programme combines a common management foundation with advanced learning in chosen areas and permits dual specialisation.

DoMS currently lists Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Systems and Human Resource Management as specialisation areas. Students can pursue dual specialisation in any two of these areas.

IIT Roorkee MBA Eligibility 2026-28

The current admission brochure should be treated as the controlling document for academic eligibility. The 2026-28 cycle uses CAT for the regular route and provides a separate CAT-exemption route for qualifying IIT graduates.

  • Applicants must satisfy the qualifying-degree and academic conditions stated in the current brochure.
  • CAT is the principal route for the regular MBA intake.
  • JEE/JEE Advanced-qualified IIT graduates with a CGPA of 7 or above on a 10-point scale are stated to be exempt from CAT and can be directly called for Personal Interview, subject to the institute's conditions.
Do not insert a generic 50% graduation rule unless IIT Roorkee's current brochure explicitly supports it for the applicant category being described.

How to Get Admission to IIT Roorkee MBA

  1. Qualify through the applicable route: normally CAT; eligible IIT graduates may use the stated CAT-exemption route.
  2. Apply to DoMS: submit the institute's MBA application with the required academic, CAT and personal information.
  3. Shortlisting: IIT Roorkee uses CAT performance for initial interview shortlisting.
  4. GD/PI: shortlisted candidates attend the institute's scheduled selection process.
  5. Final merit: the published process considers CAT, Interview and Work Experience in aggregate.
  6. Admission: selected candidates complete fee payment, registration and document verification.

IIT Roorkee MBA Selection Criteria & CAT Cutoff

IIT Roorkee's published admission material states that initial shortlisting for interviews is based on CAT performance and that final merit is based on aggregate performance in CAT 2025, Interview and Work Experience.

The material used for this page does not state one final CAT percentile as an official cutoff. Therefore, this page does not convert an estimated competitive percentile into an official cutoff.

Student takeaway: a qualifying CAT requirement and the percentile needed to receive an interview call are different concepts. Use the institute's current shortlisting notice for the relevant cycle.

IIT Roorkee MBA Curriculum & Specialisations

The MBA comprises 76 credits across eight terms. DoMS currently identifies five specialisation areas: Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Systems and Human Resource Management.

The first year establishes the common management foundation, while later terms provide advanced and specialisation-oriented learning. For a course-by-course paper list, students should use the latest syllabus published by DoMS because individual offerings can change.
