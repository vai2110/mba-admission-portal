IMI Bhubaneswar course eligibility

For PGDM, applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree of at least three years with 50% marks. Candidates graduating in 2026 were also eligible for the 2026-28 cycle.

PGDM selection is not based on an entrance score alone. The institute considers academic record, test score, work experience where applicable, video assessment and personal interview.

How many courses does IMI Bhubaneswar offer?

The institute's current academic portfolio includes the PGDM and FPM, along with a separate online PGDM offering. The flagship full-time PGDM has an intake of 180.

What is the duration of PGDM at IMI Bhubaneswar?

The full-time PGDM is a two-year residential programme.

What is the eligibility for IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM?

You need a recognised bachelor's degree of at least three years with 50% marks. Students graduating in 2026 could also apply for the 2026-28 cycle.

Which entrance exams are accepted for IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM?

The 2026-28 cycle accepted CAT 2025, XAT 2026, CMAT 2026 and GMAT within the institute's specified validity window.

Is IMI Bhubaneswar PGDM equivalent to an MBA?

The institute offers a PGDM rather than a university MBA. For career comparison, students should check the programme's AICTE approval and AIU equivalence information alongside accreditation and placement data.
