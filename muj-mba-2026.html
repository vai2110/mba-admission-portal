MUJ MBA Eligibility 2026

For the general MBA, candidates need a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 50% aggregate. Candidates still pursuing graduation must submit evidence of passing by October 31, 2026. A valid score in CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT or GMAT is required.

MBA Business Analytics requires graduation or equivalent with at least 50%. MUJ says students with mathematics at 10+2 and graduates from engineering, science, computer applications or economics are preferred, although the eligibility is not restricted to those backgrounds.

MUJ MBA Selection: What Happens Beyond the Entrance Score?

For the general MBA, MUJ says the merit list is drawn using Class X, XII and graduation academics, entrance-test performance, personal interview, presentation and English comprehension. This means a strong entrance percentile does not by itself describe the selection decision.

For MBA Business Analytics, MUJ states that selection is based on career and interview with equal weightage. Applicants should therefore be able to explain their quantitative preparation, software exposure, target analytics role and reason for choosing a management-plus-analytics degree.

General MBA: Curriculum and Career Orientation

The general MBA is designed around functional management areas including Marketing, HR, Finance and Management of Technology. Students who are not committed to a pure analytics pathway can use the broader curriculum to build a functional management profile.

FinanceBetter fit if your target is corporate finance, financial services or finance-linked management roles.

MUJ MBA FAQs

Which exams are accepted for MUJ MBA?

MUJ lists CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT and GMAT for the general MBA.

What is the fee difference between MBA and MBA Business Analytics?

General MBA tuition is currently ₹5.10 lakh per year, while MBA Business Analytics is ₹5.73 lakh per year. The latter is ₹63,000 higher annually.

Is MUJ MBA Business Analytics suitable for a non-engineer?

Yes. The published eligibility accepts any discipline with 50%, while engineering, science, computer applications, economics and mathematics backgrounds are preferred.

Does MUJ offer MBA scholarships?

Yes. MUJ currently publishes 45 MBA and 15 MBA Business Analytics scholarships with tuition waivers ranging from 20% to 50% under the specified percentile/CGPA criteria.
