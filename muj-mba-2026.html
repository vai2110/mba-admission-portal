The Business Analytics option costs ₹63,000 more in annual tuition on the currently published programme pages. Its value therefore depends on whether the student wants analytics-heavy coursework rather than a broad management curriculum.

MUJ MBA Eligibility 2026

For the general MBA, candidates need a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 50% aggregate. Candidates still pursuing graduation must submit evidence of passing by October 31, 2026. A valid score in CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT or GMAT is required.

MBA Business Analytics requires graduation or equivalent with at least 50%. MUJ says students with mathematics at 10+2 and graduates from engineering, science, computer applications or economics are preferred, although the eligibility is not restricted to those backgrounds.

MUJ MBA Selection: What Happens Beyond the Entrance Score?

For the general MBA, MUJ says the merit list uses Class X, XII and graduation academics, entrance-test performance, personal interview, presentation and English comprehension. A high percentile is therefore only one part of the published evaluation.

For MBA Business Analytics, MUJ states that selection is based on career and interview with equal weightage. Applicants should be able to explain their quantitative preparation, software exposure, target analytics role and reason for choosing a management-plus-analytics degree.

Interview preparation: Prepare one clear story connecting your degree, work/internship exposure, quantitative skills and the role you want after the MBA. For Business Analytics, be ready to discuss Excel/Python/SQL or statistics exposure if you have it.

General MBA: Curriculum and Career Orientation

The general MBA is designed around functional management areas including Marketing, HR, Finance and Management of Technology. It is the broader option for students who want management depth without committing their coursework primarily to analytics.

MUJ MBA FAQs

Which exams are accepted for MUJ MBA?

MUJ lists CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT and GMAT for the general MBA.

What is the fee difference between MBA and MBA Business Analytics?

General MBA tuition is ₹5.10 lakh per year, while MBA Business Analytics is ₹5.73 lakh. The analytics option is ₹63,000 higher annually.

Is MUJ MBA Business Analytics suitable for a non-engineer?

Yes. The published eligibility accepts any discipline with 50%, while engineering, science, computer applications, economics and mathematics backgrounds are preferred.

Does MUJ offer MBA scholarships?

Yes. MUJ currently publishes 45 MBA and 15 MBA Business Analytics scholarships with tuition waivers ranging from 20% to 50% under the specified criteria.
