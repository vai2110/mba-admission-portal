What is IIM Amritsar MBA-HR?

IIM Amritsar's MBA-HR is a full-time two-year residential programme designed around HR strategy, workforce planning, digital HR tools and analytics, labour-law awareness and leadership. It is a better fit for a student who already sees HR as a target function than for someone simply looking for another generic MBA.

The institute publishes a separate MBA-HR admission policy and fee page.

IIM Amritsar MBA-HR Admission 2027

The 2026-28 policy used CAT for the first shortlist and Personal Interview for the second stage. The institute combined CAT with academics, work experience, gender diversity and academic diversity.

IIM Amritsar MBA-HR FAQs

Is IIM Amritsar MBA-HR residential?

Yes. IIM Amritsar describes the MBA-HR as a full-time two-year residential programme.

What is the IIM Amritsar MBA-HR cutoff?

The latest published 2026-28 General-category minimum was 90 overall with section minimums of 80 QA, 75 DILR and 80 VARC.

What is the MBA-HR fee?

The official 2026-28 programme fee is ₹19.50 lakh over two years; the fee notice separately shows caution and mess deposits.
