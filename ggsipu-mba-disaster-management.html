GGSIPU MBA Disaster Management

GGSIPU offers an MBA in Disaster Management as a weekend programme. Current 2026 notices show a separate counselling and interview process for the programme, making it distinct from the regular MBA admission route.

Admission 2026

The university issued a 2026 schedule for online counselling for MBA Disaster Management and a separate interview schedule. Applicants should follow the latest programme-specific notice for dates, documents and selection stages.

Eligibility

Check the current programme notification for the qualifying degree, minimum marks and any work-experience or professional requirements. Weekend programmes should not be assumed to have identical eligibility to the regular MBA.

Fees

Use the current GGSIPU fee notification for the programme rather than the regular MBA fee. Add examination, university and commuting costs to the programme budget.
