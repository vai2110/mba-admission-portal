NMIMS MBA Tech Overview

Quick answer

MBA Tech is designed around the intersection of engineering/technology and management. It is relevant for students who want business education without giving up a technical foundation.

The programme sits within NMIMS's broader multidisciplinary ecosystem and is intended to develop graduates who can understand technology-led businesses as well as management decisions.

NMIMS MBA Tech Admission

The admission route and eligibility should be checked against the current programme-specific NMIMS handout because integrated programmes can have rules that differ from the flagship SBM MBA.

Check qualifying subjects

Verify the required school/engineering background and minimum marks in the current official handout.

MBA Tech Curriculum

The programme is built around a dual perspective: technical/engineering understanding and management capability. That combination can help students evaluate technology products, operations, innovation and business strategy together.

Technical foundation

Engineering and technology knowledge supports problem solving and understanding of technical systems.

Management layer

Business and management learning develops skills in decision-making, finance, marketing, operations and organisational leadership.

Career Scope After MBA Tech

The combination of technical and management skills can be relevant to technology consulting, product roles, operations, business development, technology strategy, project management and other tech-enabled business functions.

Actual placement opportunities vary by campus, batch and recruiting company. Programme-specific placement data should be used where the institute publishes it.

NMIMS MBA Tech Fees

Fees should be taken from the current MBA Tech admission handout rather than carried forward from an older cycle. NMIMS changes fees and payment schedules by admission year.

MBA Tech FAQs

Is MBA Tech the same as the flagship MBA?

No. It is an integrated technology and management pathway and should be evaluated using its own programme rules.

What careers can MBA Tech lead to?

Potential areas include technology consulting, product, project management, operations, technology strategy and business development.
