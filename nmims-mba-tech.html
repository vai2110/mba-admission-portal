Programme Overview

The MBA Tech programme at NMIMS is an integrated management and engineering/technology management course designed to provide students with a competitive edge through a dual-degree approach. The programme combines technical engineering expertise with management proficiency.

Admission Process

Admissions at NMIMS are merit-based. The selection process for the MBA Tech programme typically involves the NMAT by GMAC entrance exam, followed by a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).
