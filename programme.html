Academic Programmes

NMIMS offers a diverse suite of programmes across 17 specialized schools.

Programme Levels

  • Undergraduate: Available in 14 disciplines including Engineering, Commerce, Law, Economics, and Design.
  • Postgraduate: Diverse management, science, and technology courses for graduates.
  • Integrated: Dual-degree options combining undergraduate and postgraduate studies.
  • PhD: Research-oriented doctoral programmes across various fields.
  • Diploma/Certificate: Specialized programmes for working executives and students.

Admission Basis

Admissions are merit-based, considering past academic records, accomplishments in sports/arts, and relevant admission test scores.
