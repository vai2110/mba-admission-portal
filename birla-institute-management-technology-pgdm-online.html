What is BIMTECH Online PGDM?

BIMTECH's Center for Online Learning offers a 24-month online PGDM with dual specialisation. The official programme material describes a mix of live sessions, recorded videos, graded assessments, projects, reading material, self-study and exam preparation.

The programme is designed for working professionals as well as freshers who want a flexible online management programme.

Who is eligible for BIMTECH Online PGDM?

The official online programme page states eligibility of any graduate with 50% marks for General/OBC and 45% for SC/ST.

What is the BIMTECH Online PGDM fee?

The current public programme pages reviewed for this build do not provide a single current 2027-29 fee figure that can be safely reproduced here. The fee should therefore be checked on the active BIMTECH Online admissions portal rather than estimated from the residential PGDM fee.

Does BIMTECH Online PGDM offer career support?

The official online programme material mentions career counselling and placement assistance, industry SME workshops and webinars, alumni status, projects, case studies and on-campus immersion.

This should not be confused with the residential PGDM campus placement statistics on the dedicated BIMTECH placements page.

BIMTECH Online PGDM FAQs

Is BIMTECH Online PGDM equivalent to MBA?

BIMTECH's programme listing describes PGDM Online as equivalent to MBA. Students should check the current AICTE/UGC recognition applicable to the specific admission cycle.

Can working professionals apply?

Yes. BIMTECH's online programme material specifically describes the programme as suited to working professionals as well as freshers.

Is there a dissertation?

Yes. The programme structure includes a dissertation.
