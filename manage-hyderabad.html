What is MANAGE Hyderabad known for?

Quick answer:

MANAGE is the National Institute of Agricultural Extension Management, an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. Its flagship programme is the two-year residential PGDM in Agri-Business Management (PGDM ABM).

The key difference from a general management college is specialisation. MANAGE prepares students for management roles across the agricultural and food value chain, including agri-inputs, procurement, trading, food processing, banking, insurance, FMCG and consulting. The programme has been running since 1996 and the institute states that it has achieved 100% final placements since inception.

For a student, the biggest eligibility filter is important: the 2026-28 policy required a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline and a valid CAT 2025 score. Therefore, MANAGE is not a conventional option for every graduate.

MANAGE Hyderabad Admission 2026: CAT, eligibility and selection

For the 2026-28 PGDM ABM batch, MANAGE required a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline, with 50% marks for general candidates and 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. A valid CAT 2025 score was mandatory.

The final selection formula is not based on CAT alone. CAT carries 55%, while essay writing, GD, PI, work experience and academic record make up the remaining 45%. This makes academic consistency and interview performance relevant even after clearing the entrance stage.

MANAGE Hyderabad Fees 2026: actual two-year cost

The published 2026-28 PGDM ABM fee is ₹9.75 lakh for two years. The amount is not only tuition: it includes ₹4.05 lakh tuition, ₹4.94 lakh boarding and lodging, and ₹76,000 under other listed heads.

Students should also budget separately for a laptop, blazer and other academic activities. The optional foreign-university exposure programme is estimated at ₹2–5 lakh and is not part of the ₹9.75 lakh mandatory programme fee.

MANAGE Hyderabad Campus and Hostel

The campus is at Rajendranagar, Hyderabad and covers 17 hectares. The PGDM ABM is fully residential. The official mandatory disclosure lists one boys' hostel with 140 double-occupancy beds and one girls' hostel with 47 double-occupancy rooms.

Students should compare the programme on total cost and career fit, not tuition alone. Boarding and lodging form a substantial part of the published ₹9.75 lakh fee.

MANAGE Hyderabad FAQs: questions students actually ask

Is MANAGE Hyderabad a government institute?

Yes. MANAGE is an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. It was established in Hyderabad in 1987 and was elevated to National Institute status in 1992.

Is MANAGE Hyderabad good for an MBA?

MANAGE does not offer a programme titled MBA as its flagship course. Its main management programme is the two-year PGDM in Agri-Business Management. It is a strong-fit option for students who specifically want careers connected to agriculture, food, rural markets and allied businesses. It is less suitable if your main goal is a completely general management programme with no sector specialisation.

Who is eligible for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

For the 2026-28 batch, candidates needed a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline with at least 50% marks, relaxed to 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. A valid CAT 2025 score was also mandatory.

Can a student with a three-year BSc apply to MANAGE PGDM ABM?

Not under the published 2026-28 eligibility rule if the degree does not meet the required minimum four years of education after Class 12. Students should check the next admission policy because eligibility rules can change by batch.

Is CAT compulsory for MANAGE Hyderabad?

Yes, for the 2026-28 PGDM ABM cycle a valid CAT 2025 score was mandatory. The institute's selection formula then used CAT along with essay writing, GD, PI, academics and work experience.

What CAT percentile is required for MANAGE?

MANAGE does not publish an official fixed CAT cutoff in the admission policy. Students should therefore avoid treating an unofficial percentile such as 80, 85 or 90 as a guaranteed call. The published policy makes CAT 55% of final selection, so a stronger score can materially help, but the final decision also considers the rest of the profile.

What is the selection process at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The 2026 policy used CAT score, essay writing, group discussion, personal interview, work experience and academic record. The weightage was 55% CAT, 5% essay, 12% GD, 17% PI, 5% work experience and 6% academics. The 2026 final-selection process was scheduled for April 6–23.

What is the total fee of MANAGE Hyderabad PGDM?

The published 2026-28 total is ₹9.75 lakh for two years. It includes ₹4.05 lakh tuition, ₹4.94 lakh boarding and lodging and ₹76,000 under other listed heads. The fee is stated as provisional and may be revised.

Is hostel included in the MANAGE fee?

Yes. The published ₹9.75 lakh total includes ₹4.94 lakh boarding and lodging. However, students staying in the hostel during summer project/vacation periods can have additional boarding and lodging charges.

What is the average package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The latest official mandatory disclosure lists ₹12.38 LPA average CTC for 2023-25. The same disclosure gives ₹12.33 LPA for 2022-24 and ₹12.16 LPA for 2021-23.

What is the highest package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The 2023-25 batch recorded a ₹21 LPA highest CTC. The official three-year disclosure lists ₹20 LPA for both 2022-24 and 2021-23.

Does MANAGE Hyderabad have 100% placement?

MANAGE states that the PGDM ABM programme has achieved 100% final placements since inception. Its current mandatory disclosure also records 100% final placements for the recent batches.

Which companies recruit from MANAGE Hyderabad?

Recruiters listed by MANAGE include organisations from agri-inputs, food, banking, chemicals, procurement, commodity trading and other sectors. Examples include ITC, KRIBHCO, BASF, Coromandel, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Cargill, MCX and NCDEX. The exact recruiter list changes by batch.

Is MANAGE placement only for agriculture companies?

No. Agriculture and agri-input companies are important recruiters, but the institute also reports demand from BFSI, consultancy, procurement, FMCG, commodity trading, food processing and retail. Students should still remember that the PGDM is specialised in agri-business, so most roles have some connection with the sector or its value chain.

Is MANAGE worth the fee?

For an eligible agriculture/agri-allied graduate targeting agribusiness, the published numbers make the programme worth evaluating: the 2026-28 fee is ₹9.75 lakh and the 2023-25 average CTC is ₹12.38 LPA, with 100% placement reported. But students should compare the total cost, their preferred career sector and the actual role offered, rather than comparing only highest packages.
