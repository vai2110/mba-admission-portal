What is MANAGE Hyderabad known for?

Quick answer:

MANAGE is the National Institute of Agricultural Extension Management, an autonomous organisation of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. It was established in 1987 and is known especially for its PGDM in Agri-Business Management, launched in 1996 and recognised by AICTE and accredited by NBA.

Unlike a general MBA institute, MANAGE is built around agriculture, agri-business, extension management and allied sectors. That makes its flagship PGDM particularly relevant for students with a four-year agriculture or allied-sector bachelor's degree who want management careers in agri-inputs, procurement, commodity trading, BFSI, food and related businesses.

MANAGE PGDM ABM Admission 2026

For the 2026-28 PGDM ABM batch, applicants needed a bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates, from a recognised institution. The bachelor's programme must involve at least four years of education after Class 12.

A valid CAT 2025 score was mandatory. MANAGE's published selection policy gives 55% weight to CAT score, with the remaining weight distributed across essay writing, GD, PI, work experience and academic record.

MANAGE Hyderabad Fees 2026: PGDM ABM

The 2026-28 prospectus lists a ₹9.75 lakh total two-year fee for PGDM ABM: ₹4.05 lakh tuition, ₹4.94 lakh boarding and lodging and ₹76,000 under other fee heads. The institute notes that the fee is provisional and can be revised.

Students should also budget for laptops, blazers and academic activities. An optional foreign-university exposure programme may cost around ₹2–5 lakh.

MANAGE Hyderabad Campus and Student Life

The campus is at Rajendranagar, Hyderabad and covers about 17 hectares. The 2026 prospectus describes academic, hostel, sports, health and recreation facilities. The library has agriculture and management resources and access to more than 8,000 e-journals and databases.

The PGDM is fully residential with twin-sharing accommodation. MANAGE also provides sports facilities including tennis, badminton, squash, volleyball, table tennis, snooker, carrom, chess and a gymnasium.

MANAGE Hyderabad FAQs

Is MANAGE Hyderabad a government institute?

Yes. MANAGE is an autonomous organisation of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

What is the main course at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The flagship full-time management programme is the two-year PGDM in Agri-Business Management (PGDM ABM).

Who can apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

The 2026 policy required a four-year bachelor's degree in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline, with 50% marks or 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates, plus a valid CAT 2025 score.

Does MANAGE accept CAT?

Yes. CAT is mandatory for the 2026-28 PGDM ABM admission cycle.

What is the total fee at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The 2026-28 PGDM ABM prospectus lists ₹9.75 lakh for two years, including tuition, boarding/lodging and other listed fee heads.

What is the average package at MANAGE Hyderabad?

The official mandatory disclosure lists an average pay package of ₹12.38 LPA, matching the 2023-25 placement outcome.

Does MANAGE have 100% placement?

MANAGE reports 100% final placements since inception of the PGDM ABM programme in 1996.

Is MANAGE good for students without an agriculture background?

For the flagship PGDM ABM, the 2026 eligibility rules specifically required agriculture or agriculture-related disciplines. Students from unrelated bachelor's backgrounds therefore do not meet the published PGDM ABM eligibility.
