IIM Sambalpur MBA: Programme Overview

The IIM Sambalpur MBA is a two-year full-time residential management programme. The first year builds a common foundation across functional areas, while the second year allows students to select electives across finance, marketing, operations, HR, information systems, economics and policy/strategy.

2 YearsFull-time residential MBA
~320Approximate 2026-28 batch size
8-10 weeksSummer internship duration described by the institute
Who is the IIM Sambalpur MBA for?

It suits students who want a broad management foundation before choosing a functional direction, and who value experiential learning, summer internships and exposure to multiple management disciplines.

IIM Sambalpur MBA Eligibility

For the regular MBA admission process, candidates need a recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification. The 2026-28 process is built around CAT 2025 and the institute's published admission policy.

RequirementWhat the 2026-28 policy says
Academic qualificationBachelor's degree or equivalent qualification from a recognised institution.
Minimum academic marks50% for General category candidates; applicable relaxation is provided for reserved categories as per the admission rules.
Entrance testCAT 2025 with a positive raw score in all sections for the published Stage-1 consideration.
Final-year studentsApplicants completing the qualifying degree can be considered subject to the institute's conditions and documentary requirements.

IIM Sambalpur CAT Cutoff and Admission Process

The 2026-28 admission policy uses three stages: CAT-based shortlisting, a Pre-PI score and Personal Interview/final merit.

*The policy states that the General-category minimum is reduced to 85 for male candidates with at least one year of relevant work experience and for all female candidates. The institute also states that these are minimum eligibility criteria and may be revised.

How does the admission process work?

StageWhat happens
1. CAT shortlistingEligible CAT 2025 candidates who selected IIM Sambalpur are considered against the published minimum overall percentile and positive section-wise raw scores.
2. Pre-PI scoreCAT score, Class X, Class XII, graduation, relevant work experience and gender diversity contribute to a 75-mark pre-PI score.
3. PI & final meritPersonal Interview contributes 25 marks to the 100-mark final merit score.

IIM Sambalpur MBA Curriculum and Specialisations

The first year provides a common management foundation. The summer internship follows the third term. In the second year, students can choose electives from several functional areas.

AreaFocus
EconomicsEconomic reasoning and business environment.
Financial Management & AccountingCorporate finance, accounting and financial decision-making.
Information Systems ManagementTechnology and systems in business.
Production & Operations ManagementOperations, supply chain and process decisions.
OB & HR ManagementPeople, behaviour and organisational effectiveness.
Marketing ManagementCustomer, market and commercial decision-making.
Policy & Strategy ManagementStrategic choices, policy and competitive positioning.
How is learning delivered?

The institute describes a mix of lectures, case methods, problem-solving exercises, simulations, projects, presentations and technology-enabled learning, with emphasis on self-learning and peer learning.

IIM Sambalpur MBA Fees

IIM Sambalpur has published a dedicated fee structure for the 2026-28 MBA batch. The official fee document should be treated as the controlling source because the payable amount includes multiple components beyond tuition.

Fee componentWhat students should account for
Course/tuition feeCore programme charges payable term-wise.
Other programme chargesCareer development, student activities and alumni-related components may be included as specified in the official fee document.
Mess depositCharged separately as a deposit and subject to service-provider variation.
Refundable cautionRefundable component subject to institute rules.

Do not compare the MBA fee with another IIM using tuition alone. Compare the full payable amount, accommodation/mess components and any one-time deposits.

The work-experience component is not simply a yes/no criterion. The published policy allocates the highest work-experience score around the 24-30 month range, with lower scores outside that range and no score beyond 48 months.

IIM Sambalpur MBA Career Outcomes

The programme is generalist in structure, so career outcomes span multiple management functions. Recent placement data shows roles and opportunities across general management, sales and marketing, finance, IT, consulting, product management, HR and operations.

Good career fit for

  • General management and leadership-track roles.
  • Finance, consulting and business analysis pathways.
  • Marketing, sales and customer-facing management roles.
  • Operations, HR, product and technology-enabled management roles.

Before choosing

  • Review the median and average CTC, not just the highest package.
  • Check recruiter mix and role quality for your target function.
  • Compare the general MBA against MBA-BA if analytics is your primary target.
  • Factor the complete residential cost into ROI calculations.

IIM Sambalpur MBA FAQs

What is the IIM Sambalpur MBA cutoff?

The published 2026-28 minimum overall CAT percentile is 90 for General, 80 EWS, 75 NC-OBC, 55 SC and 40 ST/DAP, with the policy-specific General-category relaxation described on this page.

Is IIM Sambalpur MBA residential?

Yes. The institute describes the flagship MBA as a two-year full-time residential programme.

What is the IIM Sambalpur MBA highest package?

The latest official final placement report for the 2023-25 batch records ₹48.60 LPA as the highest offered CTC.

What is the IIM Sambalpur MBA average package?

The latest official final placement report records an average CTC of ₹15.65 LPA and median CTC of ₹15.03 LPA.

Does IIM Sambalpur consider work experience?

Yes. Relevant post-graduation work experience carries 20 marks in the final merit score for the 2026-28 MBA admission process.
