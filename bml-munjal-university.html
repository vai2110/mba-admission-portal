BML Munjal University Overview

BML Munjal University (BMU) is a private university in Gurugram founded by the Hero Group. Its academic portfolio spans engineering, management, commerce, law and liberal studies. BMU positions experiential learning, industry interaction, international exposure and entrepreneurship as important parts of its academic model.

Is BML Munjal University good?

BMU can suit students looking for a private university in the Delhi-NCR region with a practical, interdisciplinary learning model. The decision should be based on the exact programme, fee, scholarship, placement outcome and career goal.

The university currently lists B.Tech, BBA, B.Com, integrated BBA-MBA, MBA, law, liberal studies and other programmes. Its official site also reports an A NAAC grade and a 2025 NIRF management rank of 77.

Student Discussion: What Should You Compare?

Students considering BMU commonly need to balance private-university fees against scholarship support, Gurugram's location advantage, campus residential experience, programme-specific placements and the value of experiential learning. These are decision themes rather than official claims.

Potential advantages

Delhi-NCR location, Hero Group association, interdisciplinary programmes, experiential learning and industry exposure can be attractive for students seeking a career-focused private university.

What to verify

Compare your programme's complete cost, scholarship eligibility, placement data, hostel cost and target-sector outcomes before deciding.

BMU FAQs

Where is BML Munjal University located?

BMU is located in Gurugram, Haryana, in the Delhi-NCR region.

Is BMU a private university?

Yes. BML Munjal University is a private university founded by the Hero Group.

What MBA entrance exams does BMU accept?

BMU's MBA page lists CAT, NMAT, GMAT, CMAT, XAT and GRE among accepted entrance tests, followed by the university's selection process.

Does BMU offer scholarships?

Yes. BMU lists merit, sports, Haryana domicile, equity and defence-related scholarship or fee-waiver categories.
