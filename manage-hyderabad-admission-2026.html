MANAGE PGDM ABM Eligibility 2026

The 2026-28 admission policy required a four-year bachelor's degree after Class 12 in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline from a recognised university/institute. The minimum marks were 50%, relaxed to 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Requirement2026 policy
DegreeBachelor's degree in Agriculture or specified allied disciplines
Education durationMinimum 4 years after XII/10+2
Marks50% general; 45% SC/ST/PwD
Entrance scoreValid CAT 2025 score mandatory

Examples of accepted allied areas listed by MANAGE include agricultural engineering, agricultural information technology, dairy technology, fisheries, food technology, food processing engineering, forestry, horticulture, sericulture and veterinary science/animal husbandry, subject to the published policy.

How to Apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM

  1. Appear for the required CAT examination and keep the scorecard available.
  2. Read the MANAGE admission notification and confirm that your degree and duration satisfy the eligibility rule.
  3. Register through the prescribed MANAGE application route.
  4. Enter academic, category and work-experience information accurately.
  5. Upload the required documents and submit the application fee.
  6. Submit the completed form before the deadline.
  7. If shortlisted, attend the prescribed essay writing, GD and PI process.

MANAGE's 2026 admissions page provided online registration as well as downloadable/requested application-form routes. Students should use the official notification for the next cycle because application instructions can change.

How should students prepare for MANAGE selection?

First, focus on CAT. It carries 55% of the final selection weightage, so a strong score gives the biggest direct advantage.

Second, prepare for agriculture and management questions. The interview is likely to test why you want agri-business management, what you know about agriculture and allied sectors, and whether you understand the business side of the sector.

Third, prepare your own profile. Be ready to explain your graduation subject, internships, projects, work experience, career goal and why a specialised PGDM ABM is a better fit for you than a general MBA.

Do not rely on an unofficial CAT cutoff. MANAGE's published policy does not specify a fixed CAT percentile cutoff. A third-party estimate should not be presented as an official closing percentile.

MANAGE Admission FAQs: high-intent student questions

Is CAT compulsory for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

Yes. For the 2026-28 batch, a valid CAT 2025 score was mandatory. Students without the required CAT score were not eligible for that admission route.

What is the minimum percentage required for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

The 2026 policy required 50% marks in the qualifying bachelor's degree for general candidates. The requirement was 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Can a three-year degree holder apply to MANAGE?

For the 2026-28 PGDM ABM cycle, the bachelor's education had to involve at least four years after Class 12. Therefore, a standard three-year graduation degree did not satisfy the published eligibility rule.

Can BSc Agriculture students apply to MANAGE?

Eligibility depends on the exact degree duration and discipline. The 2026 policy accepted agriculture and specified allied disciplines, but it also required a minimum four-year bachelor's education after Class 12. Students should therefore check their exact degree against the next official policy rather than relying only on the degree title.

What is the MANAGE Hyderabad application last date for 2026?

The published 2026-28 application deadline was 10 February 2026. That cycle is closed now.

When was the MANAGE GD PI held in 2026?

MANAGE's official notice scheduled the final selection process, including essay writing, GD and PI, from April 6 to April 23, 2026.

What is the CAT cutoff for MANAGE Hyderabad?

MANAGE does not publish a fixed official CAT cutoff in its 2026 admission policy. Students should be careful with websites quoting a specific percentile as a guaranteed cutoff. CAT contributes 55% to final selection, so a stronger score is beneficial, but there is no official closing percentile published in the policy.

What is the MANAGE selection process after CAT?

The 2026 final selection used CAT, essay writing, GD, PI, work experience and academics. CAT had 55% weightage, PI 17%, GD 12%, academics 6%, essay 5% and work experience 5%.

Does work experience matter for MANAGE?

Yes, but it is not the largest component. The 2026 selection policy assigned 5% weightage to work experience. A fresher can still be considered because CAT, PI, GD, academics and essay together carry most of the remaining weight.

Can a non-agriculture graduate apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

The flagship PGDM ABM is specialised and the 2026 eligibility policy required an agriculture or agriculture-related bachelor's degree of the required duration. Therefore, students from unrelated graduation backgrounds should not assume they are eligible.

Is the MANAGE admission process only based on CAT?

No. CAT is the largest component at 55%, but the final selection also evaluates essay writing, GD, PI, work experience and academic record. So students should prepare for the complete selection process, not just the entrance exam.
