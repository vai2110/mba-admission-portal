MANAGE PGDM ABM Eligibility 2026

The 2026-28 admission policy requires a four-year bachelor's degree after Class 12 in agriculture or an agriculture-related discipline from a recognised university/institute. The minimum marks are 50%, relaxed to 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Requirement2026 policy
DegreeBachelor's degree in Agriculture or listed allied disciplines
Duration of bachelor's educationMinimum 4 years after XII/10+2
Marks50% general; 45% SC/ST/PwD
Entrance scoreValid CAT 2025 score mandatory

Eligible allied disciplines include agricultural engineering, agricultural information technology, dairy technology, fisheries, food technology, food processing engineering, forestry, horticulture, sericulture and veterinary science/animal husbandry, among others specified by MANAGE.

How to Apply for MANAGE PGDM ABM

  1. Take the relevant CAT examination and retain the scorecard.
  2. Register through the MANAGE PGDM ABM application route when the next admission window opens.
  3. Upload academic/category/work-experience documents as applicable.
  4. Pay the application fee through the prescribed MANAGE payment route.
  5. Submit the application and attend the prescribed essay writing, group discussion and personal interview stages if shortlisted.

The 2026 policy provided both online and offline application options. The published 2026 cycle is closed; use the official admissions page for the next cycle.

Documents Required

The published policy asks applicants to upload academic documents including SSC, Class 12 and graduation records. Final-year students can upload latest marksheets along with a bonafide certificate. Category and work-experience certificates are required when claimed.

MANAGE Admission 2026 FAQs

Is CAT compulsory for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

Yes. The 2026-28 policy required a valid CAT 2025 score.

What is the minimum percentage for MANAGE PGDM ABM?

For 2026, the minimum was 50% in the qualifying bachelor's degree, with 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

Can a three-year BSc graduate apply?

For the 2026 PGDM ABM cycle, the bachelor's degree was required to entail at least four years of education after Class 12. A three-year degree therefore did not satisfy the published requirement.

What is the MANAGE PGDM ABM application last date?

The published 2026-28 last date was 10 February 2026. That cycle is now closed.

What is the CAT weightage at MANAGE?

CAT carries 55% weightage in the published final selection formula.

Does work experience matter?

Yes. The 2026 selection policy assigns 5% weightage to work experience.
