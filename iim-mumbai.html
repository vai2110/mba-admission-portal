College Overview

About IIM Mumbai

IIM Mumbai, formerly the National Institute of Industrial Engineering (NITIE), is a public management institute in Powai, Mumbai. Its current MBA portfolio includes General Management, Operations & Supply Chain Management and Sustainability Management.

Academic and industry context

The institute continues to build on its long-standing focus on operations, industrial management and supply chain while expanding its management curriculum. Its 2026 placement report also describes activity across consulting, BFSI, IT & analytics, FMCG, manufacturing, supply chain and sales & marketing.

Admission & CAT Cutoff

Eligibility for the MBA

For the 2026–28 MBA batch, IIM Mumbai required a bachelor's degree of at least three years after 10+2 with 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. The minimum was 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates. Final-year candidates and candidates awaiting results could apply subject to the institute's completion and documentation conditions.

Selection process

  1. Appear for the applicable CAT examination.
  2. Meet the category-wise Stage-I sectional and overall minimums.
  3. Clear the institute's PI shortlisting stage based on CAT score and reservation category.
  4. For final selection, IIM Mumbai's 2026–28 policy used CAT 60%, PI 20% and Academic Performance & Work Experience 20% in the composite score, with the stated weightages subject to the policy's provisions.

How to assess the total cost

The ₹25 lakh programme fee is not an all-inclusive living-cost figure. Budget separately for mess and any personal, travel or other expenses applicable to your stay. For ROI analysis, compare the total academic and living cost with the placement outcomes for the relevant cohort rather than using the highest CTC alone.

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

IIM Mumbai's 2024–25 annual report records scholarships awarded to first-year students under the Rangan Scholarship, Madala Scholarship, Singhal Scholarship and Financial Assistance Scholarship through the IIM Mumbai Alumni Association.

What to verify for your batch

The annual report confirms the existence of these scholarship initiatives but does not establish a single fixed amount or universal eligibility rule for every MBA student. Check the latest institute/alumni scholarship notice for eligibility, number of awards and amount before budgeting for them.

Campus & Hostel

IIM Mumbai's MBA, MBA-OSCM and MBA-SM are residential programmes. The institute states that hostel rooms are furnished and double occupancy, and its campus infrastructure includes academic facilities, library, sports and fitness facilities, health services and multiple hostel blocks.

Campus facilities

  • Library and academic buildings
  • Sports and fitness facilities
  • 24/7 on-campus health centre and emergency ambulance support
  • Banking and ATM facilities

Accommodation planning

  • Residential programme requirement
  • Hostel and mess costs are separate from the ₹25 lakh programme fee
  • Check the latest hostel and mess instructions before joining

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IIM Mumbai MBA fee for the 2026–28 batch?

The gross programme fee is ₹25 lakh for two years, plus a refundable ₹50,000 caution money deposit. Mess charges and the mess deposit are separate.

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Mumbai MBA?

For 2026–28, the Stage-I minimum for Open candidates was 85 overall, with 80 VARC, 80 DILR and 75 QA. IIM Mumbai states that actual cutoffs may be higher depending on the number of candidates called for PI.

What is the eligibility for IIM Mumbai MBA?

A bachelor's degree of at least three years after 10+2 with 50% marks or equivalent CGPA is required; the threshold is 45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. Final-year candidates can apply subject to the institute's conditions.

What is the average package at IIM Mumbai?

For Batch 2026, the institute's final placement report gives an average CTC of ₹31.4 lakh. It also reports top-10%, top-20% and top-50% averages of ₹47.5 lakh, ₹41.4 lakh and ₹34.5 lakh respectively.

What should students compare before evaluating the ₹25 lakh fee?

Compare the total programme cost including separate mess and living expenses with the placement outcomes for the relevant cohort, the functions represented in recruitment and your own target career path.

IIM Mumbai vs IIM Indore: what should students compare?

Compare programme structure, admission criteria, total cost, campus location, batch profile, recruiter mix, placement statistics and the functions you intend to target. These factors can differ across programmes and cohorts.

MBA vs MBA-OSCM: what is the difference at IIM Mumbai?

MBA is the general-management programme, while MBA-OSCM is specifically focused on operations and supply chain management and has a more restricted eligibility route. Both are two-year residential programmes.

Is IIM Mumbai suitable for freshers?

Freshers are eligible for the MBA if they meet the academic and CAT requirements. The Batch 2026 placement report shows 14% freshers in the combined MBA, MBA-OSCM and MBA-SM cohort.
