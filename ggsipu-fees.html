How GGSIPU Fees Are Structured

GGSIPU does not have one fee for all courses. The 2026-27 UG brochure includes a dedicated fee chapter for University Schools, government institutes and self-financing institutes/colleges affiliated with GGSIP University. PG and professional programmes can have separate fee notifications.

Why does the number vary?

The same university can have substantially different costs depending on programme, institute, course type, academic year and residential requirement.

GGSIPU Hostel Costs

Hostel charges are campus-specific. Current official hostel notices include separate boys and girls hostel brochures and room-category charges, while allotment lists are issued separately for different campuses/hostels.

How to Calculate Total Cost

For a realistic budget, add academic fee for all semesters, hostel or rent, mess/food, transport, books and personal expenses. Also check the university's refund policy before paying a large admission amount.

GGSIPU Fees FAQs

Does every GGSIPU college have the same fee?

No. Fees vary by programme and institute.

Are hostel fees included in tuition?

Usually hostel and mess are separate campus-specific charges. Check the current hostel brochure.
