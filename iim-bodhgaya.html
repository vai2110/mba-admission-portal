What should a student know before choosing IIM Bodh Gaya?

IIM Bodh Gaya is a relatively young IIM, established in 2015, that combines management education with experiential learning and a stated focus on mindful and ethical leadership. The institute currently reports NIRF 2025 Management rank 31 and AACSB accreditation.

31stNIRF 2025 Management ranking shown by the institute
₹14.80 LPACurrent official average placement package
₹45.63 LCurrent official highest CTC
The important point:

The IIM tag alone should not decide your choice. Compare the specific programme, admission route, total cost, placement evidence and career direction.

What makes the institute different?

Official programme material highlights cases, simulations, live industry projects, internships, ethics, sustainability, mindfulness and international exposure. The institute also highlights 20+ international academic partnerships.

How does admission work in 2026?

There is no single admission process for every programme. The 2026-28 MBA, MBA-DBAI and MBA-HHM routes use programme-specific policies within the CAP framework, while IPM 2026-31 follows a separate JIPMAT-based policy.

What should you prepare before applying?

  • Keep academic marksheets and profile/category documents ready.
  • For CAT routes, prepare VARC, DILR and QA alongside interview readiness.
  • Read the exact programme policy instead of relying on a generic IIM cutoff.
  • For IPM, verify Class 10/Class 12 eligibility and prepare specifically for JIPMAT.

Do not add refundable deposits to the course fee

The MBA fee schedule separately lists a ₹25,000 caution deposit. Mess and laundry deposits are also separate and can be adjusted/refunded according to the institute's terms. For ROI, calculate tuition + institute charges + actual living/mess costs, then treat refundable deposits separately.

Which programme is right for you?

Choose MBA if…

You want maximum flexibility across management functions and industries after graduation.

Choose MBA-DBAI if…

You want management careers at the intersection of digital transformation, analytics, AI and business strategy.

Choose MBA-HHM if…

You have a clear healthcare-sector goal and want healthcare context built into your management education.

Choose IPM if…

You are entering after Class 12 and are comfortable committing early to an integrated management path.

What if you are undecided?

If you are unsure about your function or industry, the general MBA offers the broadest postgraduate positioning. If you are unsure about management itself, a conventional undergraduate degree may preserve more flexibility than IPM.

IIM Bodh Gaya FAQs

Is IIM Bodh Gaya an IIM?

Yes. It is an Indian Institute of Management established in 2015.

What is the NIRF 2025 rank?

The institute currently displays NIRF 2025 rank 31 in Management.

Which is the flagship programme?

The institute identifies the two-year MBA as its flagship management programme.

Is admission through CAT?

Graduate-entry MBA routes use CAT-based policies, while IPM uses JIPMAT for 2026.

What is the latest average placement package?

The current official placement page reports ₹14.80 LPA average CTC.

Does IIM Bodh Gaya offer IPM?

Yes. IPM is a five-year integrated programme for eligible students entering after Class 12.
