Is IIM Bodh Gaya worth considering?

IIM Bodh Gaya is a young IIM with a deliberately distinctive academic identity: rigorous management education combined with experiential learning, mindfulness, sustainability and ethical leadership. The institute was established in 2015 and its current official site lists NIRF 2025 rank 31 in management and AACSB accreditation. x

The useful way to evaluate it

Do not judge the institute only by the IIM label. Compare the programme you want, its admission route, total cost, placement evidence, cohort profile and the kind of career you want after graduation.

Academic identity

The MBA curriculum mixes core management subjects with analytics, sustainability, design thinking, ethics and mindfulness.

Global orientation

The institute highlights international academic partnerships and a global outlook in its programme material.

Career orientation

The Career Development Cell reports audited placement outcomes and structured recruitment processes across sectors.

Which programmes are available?

The current long-duration portfolio gives students several very different entry points. Your choice should depend on whether you are a graduate, a school-leaver, or looking for a specialised management path.

Flagship

MBA

Two-year full-time management programme for graduates, with CAT-led admission and broad functional coverage.

Best fitGraduates targeting consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics or general management.
New-age

MBA – Digital Business & AI

A two-year management programme built around digital business and AI-oriented capabilities.

Best fitStudents wanting management careers with a stronger digital, analytics and AI orientation.
Sector-focused

MBA – Hospital & Healthcare Management

A two-year full-time programme focused on management in hospitals and healthcare ecosystems.

Best fitStudents interested in healthcare administration, hospital operations and healthcare management.
5-year route

Integrated Programme in Management

A five-year integrated management pathway for students entering directly after school.

Best fitStudents who already know they want a management education without taking the conventional graduation-first route.

How does admission work in 2026?

The admission route changes substantially by programme. For the 2026-28 MBA, MBA-DBAI and MBA-HHM intake, IIM Bodh Gaya publishes separate admission policies and a CAP 2026 process. The IPM 2026-31 route has a separate policy and JIPMAT-based admission process, with an international SAT route also published.

Fees: what should you actually budget?

Fee structures vary by programme and batch. IIM Bodh Gaya has published separate 2026-28 fee documents for the MBA, MBA-DBAI and MBA-HHM and a separate 2026-31 fee structure for IPM. The published schedules include tuition as well as academic, hostel, career-development and other institute charges.

Do not compare only the headline tuition fee

For a realistic ROI comparison, add hostel, academic/service charges, deposits, living costs and the opportunity cost of the programme. Also check the exact fee document for your batch because charges can change.

For example, the official 2026-28 MBA-DBAI fee document lists ₹1.60 lakh tuition per term, plus separate hostel, academic, career-development and other charges; the published two-year course total is ₹16.70 lakh before refundable deposits and other variable items. x

What do placements look like?

The institute's current placement page reports 100% placement, 200+ recruiting companies, a ₹45.63 lakh highest CTC and ₹14.80 LPA average package. It also reports top-percentile averages of ₹21.00 LPA for the top 25%, ₹17.95 LPA for the top 50% and ₹16.24 LPA for the top 75%.

What is the learning experience like?

IIM Bodh Gaya positions experiential learning at the centre of its model. Official programme material highlights case studies, simulations, live industry projects, industry-crafted courses, internships and a strong emphasis on mindfulness and ethical decision-making.

Experiential learning

Cases, simulations and live projects connect classroom concepts with business decisions.

Industry exposure

The institute highlights internships and industry interaction as part of the learning journey.

Mindfulness

Mindfulness is positioned as part of leadership development rather than as an isolated extracurricular theme.

Which programme should you choose?

Choose the MBA if…

You want the broadest management route and have a graduate degree. It keeps your functional career options relatively open.

Choose MBA-DBAI if…

You specifically want management roles shaped by digital transformation, data and AI rather than a conventional general-management positioning.

Choose MBA-HHM if…

You already have a clear interest in hospitals, healthcare delivery or healthcare administration and prefer sector specialisation.

Choose IPM if…

You are entering after Class 12 and want a continuous five-year management pathway instead of completing a separate undergraduate degree first.
