How is the Executive MBA delivered?

IIT Roorkee's Department of Management Studies describes an Executive MBA for working professionals with live weekend classes and pre-recorded lectures. The degree is designed for two years, with flexibility to complete it within 24 to 60 months under the programme rules.

This is not a full-time residential MBA. Check class timings, attendance, assessment, projects and any live or campus requirements before deciding whether it fits alongside your job.

What does the 73-credit curriculum cover?

The official fee table lists 37.5 core-course credits, 25.5 elective credits and 10 credits for projects and seminars. Elective areas include Operations, Finance, Marketing, HR, Analytics, IT, Economics, Strategy and Public Policy.

Elective availability can vary by term. Review the current course catalogue and do not assume every listed elective runs every year.

What is the published fee?

The official tuition page lists ₹11,67,500, including a non-refundable ₹25,000 registration fee. The fee table allocates charges by credit type and describes a pay-as-you-go model where fees are paid when courses are registered. The page states that fees are the same irrespective of category or nationality and that the programme does not offer a scholarship.

Recheck the live fee page before applying and budget separately for any travel, technology or personal expenses.
