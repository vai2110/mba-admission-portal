What is the total BIM MBA fee?

Published 2026-28 institute fee:

₹18,70,845 for two years, before the separate mess charge. BIM lists ₹9,32,012 for Year 1 and ₹9,38,833 for Year 2.

The fee covers academic, residential and several student-service heads. Mess is additional at ₹75,000 per year on actual basis. Therefore, students should not treat ₹18.71 lakh as the complete two-year cash budget.

Education Loans for BIM MBA

BIM's 2026 brochure states that its Scholar Loan Scheme is available through State Bank of India, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India and Union Bank of India without collateral, subject to the applicable bank rules. Students should confirm the current sanctioned amount, interest rate and repayment terms directly with the bank before borrowing.

BIM Fees FAQs

What is the total BIM Trichy MBA fee?

The published 2026-28 institute fee is ₹18,70,845 over two years, before separate mess charges.

How much is the BIM MBA tuition fee?

Tuition is ₹7,19,992 in Year 1 and ₹7,48,813 in Year 2, totalling ₹14,68,805.

Is mess included in BIM fees?

No. BIM lists mess fees of ₹75,000 per year on actual basis separately from the institute fee.

How much does BIM hostel/accommodation cost?

The 2026-28 fee table lists ₹53,800 per year for accommodation. Mess is separate.

Is BIM fee refundable?

The brochure includes a caution deposit of ₹10,000 and a mess deposit of ₹7,000. Refunds and deductions are subject to institute rules and applicable fee-refund policy.

Does BIM offer education loans?

Yes. BIM lists a Scholar Loan Scheme through SBI, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India and Union Bank of India, subject to bank norms.

What optional costs should students know about?

The brochure lists an optional ₹2.36 lakh SAP certificate and an optional international summer/exchange programme at about ₹5 lakh.
