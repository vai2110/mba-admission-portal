IBS Hyderabad MBA Placements 2025: Key Highlights

Publicly reported placement data for IBS Hyderabad's 2025 MBA cycle shows an average CTC of ₹9.82 LPA, highest domestic CTC of ₹21 LPA and placement rate around 93%. The top 10% average was ₹13.70 LPA and the top 25% average was ₹11.76 LPA.

NIRF-Based Placement Data: Why Median Matters

NIRF-based reporting for the management category shows 1,230 students graduating in the minimum stipulated time, 1,078 placed and a median salary of ₹9 lakh for the relevant reporting cycle. Median and average are different measures and should not be mixed.

Why median matters

The highest CTC shows the ceiling. The average shows the arithmetic mean. The median shows the middle salary in the distribution. For ROI decisions, median can be more informative about the typical outcome.

Recruiting Sectors and Career Roles

IBS Hyderabad Placement ROI

The MBA programme fee is ₹17.01 lakh before living costs. Against the 2025 reported average CTC of ₹9.82 LPA and median of ₹9 lakh, the programme should be assessed as a multi-year career investment rather than by a simple first-year salary calculation.

Positive indicators

  • Average CTC close to ₹10 LPA.
  • Highest domestic CTC of ₹21 LPA.
  • Strong representation of BFSI and IT/ITES.
  • Hyderabad location with a broad corporate ecosystem.

Questions before enrolling

  • What roles are available to the majority?
  • What is your personal total cost?
  • What loan repayment would look like?
  • Does the role mix match your career target?

Student Discussion: What Placement Numbers Do Not Tell You

Applicants commonly focus on the highest package, but discussions around IBS Hyderabad also raise questions about batch size, role distribution, location, sales-heavy roles and ROI. These are discussion themes and individual experiences, not official placement claims.

  • Ask for function-wise or role-wise information where available.
  • Look at median as well as average.
  • Separate internship outcomes from final placement outcomes.
  • Compare the fee with realistic take-home salary, not headline CTC.
