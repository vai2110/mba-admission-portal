Is Jamia Hamdard MBA worth considering?

Quick answer

Jamia Hamdard offers a two-year MBA through its Department of Management. The published programme includes specialisations such as Marketing, Finance, Human Resource, Operations Management, Information Technology, International Business and Entrepreneurship.

The MBA is a separate value proposition from Jamia Hamdard's nationally strong pharmacy programmes. If your target is management, you should compare the MBA's current fee, GD/PI process, median salary, recruiter mix and Delhi location with other Delhi-NCR MBA options.

Jamia Hamdard MBA Admission 2026

The official 2026 admission page lists both an MBA interview notice and a third-round MBA (General) GD/PI schedule. This confirms that the 2026 MBA admission cycle is active and that GD/PI is a meaningful part of the current process.

Jamia Hamdard MBA Eligibility

The published admission prospectus lists MBA as a two-year programme. Eligibility should be checked against the current 2026 prospectus and programme notice because minimum marks, qualifying degree and selection rules are cycle-specific.

Do not rely on a generic “MBA eligibility” article: confirm the exact 2026 Jamia Hamdard requirement before submitting the application.

Jamia Hamdard MBA Fees

An official 2024-25 MBA selection list recorded a ₹1.25 lakh semester fee, plus one-time admission, enrolment, library and alumni charges and semester examination/e-governance charges. The historical selection list showed a total of ₹1,48,500 for the listed admission payment.

This is historical evidence, not the 2026 fee. Jamia Hamdard's 2026 admission page includes a fee-structure corrigendum, so students should use the latest 2026 prospectus/fee schedule before budgeting.

Jamia Hamdard MBA Placements

The best official comparable evidence is the NIRF 2025 Management submission. For the 2023-24 graduating cohort, 230 students graduated in minimum stipulated time and 202 were placed, with a median salary of ₹5.95 lakh. The previous two reported cohorts had median salaries of ₹5.52 lakh and ₹4.63 lakh.

Jamia Hamdard MBA FAQs

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA fee?

The current 2026 fee should be checked in the latest prospectus/fee corrigendum. A 2024-25 official selection list recorded ₹1.25 lakh per semester plus other charges, but that figure should not be presented as the 2026 fee.

What is the Jamia Hamdard MBA placement package?

NIRF 2025 Management data reports a ₹5.95 LPA median salary for the 2023-24 graduating cohort, with 202 of 230 graduates placed.

Does Jamia Hamdard MBA have GD/PI?

Yes. The university's 2026 admission notices include MBA GD/PI and interview schedules.

What MBA specialisations are offered?

The published programme lists Marketing, Finance, Human Resource, Operations Management, Information Technology, International Business and Entrepreneurship.
