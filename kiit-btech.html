Quick Answer

The B.Tech programme is a four-year undergraduate route with multiple engineering branches. The 2026 prospectus publishes semester fees from ₹1.75 lakh to ₹2.20 lakh for the listed mainstream B.Tech groups. Eligibility and the applicable entrance route must be checked against the current KIITEE 2026 criteria.

B.Tech Programme Overview

The programme portfolio includes CSE, IT, Computer Science & System Engineering, Computer Science & Communication Engineering, electronics and electrical branches, mechanical, civil, aerospace, chemical, mechatronics and other engineering options. CSE-linked specialisations include AI/ML, AI, Cyber Security, Data Science and IoT.

Students should choose the branch first and then compare the fee, curriculum, internship opportunities, recruiter mix and career outcomes.

Admission Dates 2026

KIIT runs admissions through the KIITEE 2026 cycle. The current portal publishes phase-wise application, examination, result and counselling information. Dates can change during the live cycle, so applicants should use the official KIITEE portal for the current phase schedule.

B.Tech Eligibility 2026

The published criteria require candidates to have passed 10+2 in 2024 or 2025 or be appearing in 2026, through regular full-time formal education. For B.Tech, the applicant must have at least 60% in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics together. The published birth-date condition is that the candidate should be born on or after 1 July 2005.

B.Tech Admission Process

  1. Register through the KIITEE 2026 application portal.
  2. Complete the required academic and personal details.
  3. Appear through the applicable KIITEE admission process.
  4. Check result/rank and counselling instructions.
  5. Attend counselling and document verification when called.
  6. Pay the applicable admission amount and report as instructed.

The university states that counselling and seat allocation are merit-based on entrance-examination performance and continue until seats are filled.

B.Tech Entrance Exam

KIIT's 2026 admission policy states that admission to courses is through KIITEE 2026, while MBBS and BDS are routed through NEET. Applicants should follow the current B.Tech-specific KIITEE instructions rather than relying on older admission-cycle information.

B.Tech Cutoff & Seat Allocation

KIIT publishes merit-based counselling and seat-allocation information rather than a single fixed public branch cutoff on the main admission page. The effective branch closing level depends on entrance performance, preference and seat availability. Therefore, a fabricated “expected cutoff” is not shown here.

B.Tech Curriculum & Learning

The exact curriculum varies by branch and specialisation. Students should review the current school curriculum for core engineering subjects, laboratory work, projects, electives, industry exposure and specialisation-specific courses before selecting a branch.

CSE/IT

Best suited to students targeting software, data, AI, cybersecurity and technology roles.

Electronics

Relevant to embedded systems, semiconductor, electronics, communication and VLSI pathways.

Core engineering

Mechanical, civil, electrical, chemical and related branches align with infrastructure, manufacturing, energy and engineering operations.

Interdisciplinary options

AI, Data Science, IoT and other technology specialisations suit students seeking newer computing applications.

B.Tech Placements & Internships

KIIT School of Technology reported 3,800+ job offers from 451 companies for the 2025 graduating batch, an average CTC of ₹8.50 LPA, 1,500+ dream offers and 1,209 paid internship opportunities. The university-level highest CTC was ₹51 LPA, but that figure should not be interpreted as the typical B.Tech outcome.

Who Should Apply for B.Tech?

B.Tech is a strong fit for students who have a clear engineering/technology career direction and are willing to compare branches rather than choosing solely by the CSE label. Before accepting a seat, compare total four-year cost, branch-specific curriculum, internship access and the placement outcomes relevant to the chosen branch.
