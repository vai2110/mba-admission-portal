LIBA Executive Diploma: Overview

LIBA describes its executive programmes as one-year programmes for freshers and working professionals who want to expand their knowledge, acquire new expertise or strengthen existing skills. Teaching combines academicians and practitioners, with case studies and real-world business examples.

LIBA Executive Diploma Selection Process

LIBA's official programme information describes a three-step selection route: preliminary screening of applications, assessment and interview for shortlisted candidates, followed by selection notification through SMS/email and the LIBA website.

Who Should Choose an Executive Diploma?

These programmes make more sense when the student has a clear functional objective—such as analytics, finance, HR, logistics or marketing—and wants focused management learning rather than a conventional two-year full-time PGDM. Working professionals should check the latest schedule, class mode and programme-specific fee before applying.

LIBA Executive Diploma Fees

LIBA runs multiple executive diploma programmes, so there is no single universal fee that should be applied to every specialisation. The official programme page should be treated as the source of truth for the current batch fee, schedule and taxes.

LIBA Executive Diploma FAQs

How long is the Executive Diploma?

LIBA describes the executive diploma programmes as one-year programmes.

Who can apply?

LIBA states that its executive programmes are designed for freshers and working professionals.

Which specialisations are available?

LIBA's official pages list areas including Business Analytics, Financial Management, HRM, International Business, Investment Analysis, Logistics & Supply Chain, Marketing, Healthcare, Insurance and Media & Entertainment.
