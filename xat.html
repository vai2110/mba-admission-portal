XAT 2027: Exam Overview

The Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) is a national-level entrance examination for admission to MBA, PGDM and other management programmes. XLRI – Xavier School of Management, Jamshedpur conducts the test on behalf of the Xavier Association of Management Institutes (XAMI). XAT scores are accepted by more than 250 B-schools, according to the official exam website.

XAT 2027 is scheduled for Sunday, January 3, 2027, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM. It is a computer-based test held in a single slot. The paper has approximately 95 questions across four sections, with Decision Making being its most distinctive component.

XAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualification

  • Applicants must hold a recognised bachelor’s degree of at least three years in any discipline, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Final-year undergraduate students can apply if they complete their final graduation examinations by June 11, 2027.
  • XAT does not prescribe a minimum graduation percentage for appearing in the exam. Individual institutes may set their own academic requirements for admission.

Age, attempts and work experience

  • There is no prescribed age limit or cap on the number of XAT attempts.
  • Work experience is not compulsory to appear for XAT or apply to XLRI’s BM and HRM programmes.
  • XLRI’s PGDM (General Management) is designed for experienced professionals and requires at least three years of full-time managerial or supervisory experience, subject to the programme’s 2027 criteria.

Nationality

Indian candidates apply through XAT. NRI, foreign, PIO and OCI applicants may have additional application routes, including GMAT, depending on the programme. Check the relevant XLRI programme page before applying.

XAT 2027 Exam Pattern

The exam is divided into two parts. Part 1 contains the three sections used to calculate the overall percentile. Part 2 is the General Knowledge section and has a separate 10-minute duration.

XAT 2027 Marking Scheme

The marking scheme rewards correct answers and penalises incorrect answers. XLRI has revised the unanswered-question allowance for XAT 2027: the first 12 unanswered questions in Part 1 carry no penalty.

There is no negative marking for General Knowledge. GK marks are not included in the overall XAT percentile or cutoff score, but XLRI may consider them during its own final selection process.

XAT 2027 Syllabus

XLRI does not publish a fixed, exhaustive syllabus. Its official exam description identifies the broad skills tested in each section. The topic lists below are useful areas to practise, not a guaranteed list of questions.

1. Verbal Ability and Logical Reasoning (VALR)

VALR tests reading comprehension, interpretation and reasoning in written English. XAT passages may include poems, cartoons and other less conventional material.

  • Reading comprehension and inference-based questions
  • Critical and analytical reasoning
  • Vocabulary, word usage and English grammar
  • Para jumbles, odd sentence out and cloze tests
  • Sentence correction and fill-in-the-blanks

2. Decision Making (DM)

DM presents personal, professional or business situations and asks candidates to choose a practical course of action. Questions assess judgement, prioritisation and the ability to balance competing interests.

  • Ethical and workplace dilemmas
  • Business and managerial caselets
  • Stakeholder conflicts and prioritisation
  • Data-based decisions, allocations and constraints
  • Personal and social situations

3. Quantitative Aptitude and Data Interpretation (QA & DI)

This section tests mathematical fundamentals and the ability to interpret information presented in different formats.

  • Arithmetic: percentages, ratios, averages, mixtures, interest, time and work, and speed-distance-time
  • Algebra: equations, inequalities, functions, progressions and logarithms
  • Geometry and mensuration: triangles, circles, polygons, areas and volumes
  • Modern maths and number systems: probability, permutations, combinations, sets, divisibility and remainders
  • Data interpretation: tables, charts, graphs and caselets

4. General Knowledge (GK)

The GK section has about 20 questions: 8 on static GK and 12 on current affairs.

  • Current affairs in India and internationally
  • Business, economy and government schemes
  • Science, technology, sports and awards
  • History, geography, polity and important organisations
  • Books, authors and notable personalities

XAT 2027 Registration and Application Fee

Applications are submitted online through the official XAT website. Keep your own email ID and mobile number ready; XLRI advises applicants to enter these carefully because they cannot be changed after registration.

Documents and details required

  • Recent passport-size photograph and scanned signature
  • Class 10 and Class 12 academic details
  • Graduation details and final-year status, where applicable
  • Work experience details, if relevant to the programme
  • Valid email ID, mobile number and online payment method

How to apply for XAT 2027

  1. Visit xatonline.in and create a registration account using your name, email ID and mobile number.
  2. Verify your email and save the XAT ID generated for your application.
  3. Log in and complete your personal, academic and work experience details. Ensure that names match your certificates and ID proof.
  4. Select the XLRI programmes you wish to apply for and choose your preferred test cities.
  5. Upload your photograph and signature in the format and size specified in the application portal.
  6. Pay the registration fee online, review the form and submit it. Save the confirmation and payment receipt.

XAT 2027 Admit Card

The XAT 2027 admit card is expected to be available from December 20, 2026 (tentative) through the official website. Log in using your XAT ID and password, open the admit-card link and download the document.

Check your name, photograph, signature, exam date, reporting time and test-centre address. Carry a printed admit card and a valid original photo ID to the centre. If you find an error, contact XLRI through the official dashboard or the helpdesk before exam day.

XAT 2027 Answer Key

XLRI has not announced the answer-key release date or objection window. After the examination, check xatonline.in for the response sheet, question paper or official answer key, if released.

You can estimate your Part 1 score using the final marking rules: +1 for each correct answer, −0.25 for each incorrect answer, and −0.10 for every unanswered question beyond the first 12. GK has no negative marking and is not included in the overall percentile. Follow XLRI’s notification for the process and deadline to raise any objection.

XAT 2027 Result and Scorecard

The XAT 2027 result is expected in the last week of January 2027. The published scorecard download window is January 31 to June 30, 2027. Candidates can log in to xatonline.in to view and download their scorecards.

The scorecard reports overall and sectional scores and percentiles. XAT percentiles are calculated using Part 1 performance across VALR, Decision Making and QA & DI. The GK score is excluded from the overall percentile; XLRI may use it in its own final selection. Since XAT is conducted in a single slot, there is no slot-wise normalisation.

XAT 2027 Selection Process

An XAT score does not guarantee admission. XLRI and other participating institutes conduct their own shortlisting and selection processes, and applicants must complete each institute’s application requirements.

XLRI BM and HRM

For the Business Management (BM) and Human Resource Management (HRM) programmes, XLRI shortlists candidates using XAT performance and may consider academic background, work experience and other profile factors. Shortlisted applicants are invited to the next selection stages, which may include a personal interview and group discussion or other assessments. Final offers are based on the institute’s overall evaluation.

XLRI PGDM (General Management)

PGDM (GM) is intended for experienced professionals. Applicants may use the accepted XAT or GMAT/GRE route, subject to the test-date and work-experience conditions in XLRI’s programme prospectus. The programme requires at least three years of managerial or supervisory experience as on March 31, 2027, according to the supplied 2027 information.

Other XAT-accepting institutes

Each B-school sets its own cutoff, application form, selection weightage and interview process. XLRI does not conduct admissions on behalf of these institutes. Check the target college’s official admission page for its current requirements.

What Is a Good XAT 2027 Score or Percentile?

A good XAT percentile depends on the programme and institute you are targeting. As a broad planning guide, a percentile of 95 or above is generally considered a strong target for XLRI BM and HRM shortlisting. A range of 85–95 may be competitive at several well-known B-schools, while 75–85 can provide options at other private institutes.

These are indicative ranges, not official cutoffs or admission guarantees. XLRI does not publish a single universal “good score”; cutoffs can vary by programme, applicant profile and admission year. Compare your score with the latest official shortlist criteria of each target institute.

XAT 2027 Preparation Tips

Preparation should cover the common MBA entrance-test areas while giving Decision Making and GK dedicated attention.

  • VALR: Practise reading comprehension, critical reasoning and para jumbles. Read editorials and varied long-form writing, including poems, to become comfortable with different passage styles.
  • Decision Making: Solve caselets from previous XAT papers. Identify the stakeholders, constraints and ethical issues before comparing the answer choices.
  • QA & DI: Strengthen arithmetic, algebra and geometry before moving to mixed problem sets and timed data-interpretation caselets.
  • GK: Follow national, international, business and economic news. Revise static GK and monthly current-affairs notes; there is no negative marking in this section.
  • Mock tests: Use previous XAT papers and a small number of reliable mock-test series. Review errors, time spent and question selection after every mock.

Time management strategy

Part 1 has no sectional time limits, so choose an order that suits your strengths and move on when a question is consuming too much time. The first 12 unanswered questions carry no penalty, but every additional unanswered Part 1 question attracts a deduction. Keep the separate 10-minute GK section in mind and practise in the full 180-minute format.

XAT 2027 Frequently Asked Questions

When will XAT 2027 be conducted?

XAT 2027 is scheduled for January 3, 2027, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, as a single-slot computer-based test.

What is the last date to register for XAT 2027?

The registration deadline listed by XLRI is December 6, 2026. Check the official portal for any update.

How many questions are there in XAT 2027?

The paper is expected to have about 95 questions: 75 in Part 1 and 20 in the General Knowledge section.

How many unanswered questions are allowed without penalty?

The revised XAT 2027 rule allows the first 12 unanswered questions in Part 1 without penalty. Each unanswered question after the first 12 attracts a 0.10-mark deduction.

Does General Knowledge affect the XAT percentile?

No. GK is not included in the overall percentile or cutoff score. XLRI may consider the GK score in its own final selection, and the section has no negative marking.

Is work experience compulsory for XAT 2027?

No. Work experience is not required to appear for XAT or apply to XLRI BM and HRM. XLRI PGDM (GM) has a separate managerial work-experience requirement.

What is a good XAT percentile for XLRI?

A percentile of 95 or above is generally used as a strong planning target for XLRI BM and HRM, but actual shortlisting cutoffs vary by programme and applicant profile. This is not an official cutoff.

How many colleges accept XAT scores?

XLRI states that more than 250 B-schools accept XAT scores. Each institute follows its own application and selection process.

Can I prepare for CAT and XAT together?

Yes. CAT preparation builds much of the foundation for VALR, QA and DI. Add regular Decision Making practice and a GK revision routine for XAT, then use XAT-specific mocks to practise its format.
