XAT 2027: Exam Overview

The Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) is a national-level entrance examination for admission to MBA, PGDM and other management programmes. XLRI – Xavier School of Management, Jamshedpur conducts the test on behalf of the Xavier Association of Management Institutes (XAMI). XAT scores are accepted by more than 250 B-schools, according to the official exam website.

XAT 2027 is scheduled for Sunday, January 3, 2027, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM. It is a computer-based test held in a single slot. The paper has approximately 95 questions across four sections, with Decision Making being its most distinctive component.

XAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualification

  • Applicants must hold a recognised bachelor’s degree of at least three years in any discipline, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Final-year undergraduate students can apply if they complete their final graduation examinations by June 11, 2027.
  • XAT does not prescribe a minimum graduation percentage for appearing in the exam. Individual institutes may set their own academic requirements for admission.

Age, attempts and work experience

  • There is no prescribed age limit or cap on the number of XAT attempts.
  • Work experience is not compulsory to appear for XAT or apply to XLRI’s BM and HRM programmes.
  • XLRI’s PGDM (General Management) is designed for experienced professionals and requires at least three years of full-time managerial or supervisory experience, subject to the programme’s 2027 criteria.

Nationality

Indian candidates apply through XAT. NRI, foreign, PIO and OCI applicants may have additional application routes, including GMAT, depending on the programme. Check the relevant XLRI programme page before applying.

XAT 2027 Registration and Application Fee

XAT 2027 Exam Pattern

The exam is divided into two parts. Part 1 contains the three sections used to calculate the overall percentile. Part 2 is the General Knowledge section and has a separate 10-minute duration.

XAT 2027 Marking Scheme

The marking scheme rewards correct answers and penalises incorrect answers. XLRI has revised the unanswered-question allowance for XAT 2027: the first 12 unanswered questions in Part 1 carry no penalty.

There is no negative marking for General Knowledge. GK marks are not included in the overall XAT percentile or cutoff score, but XLRI may consider them during its own final selection process.

XAT 2027 Syllabus

XLRI does not publish a fixed, exhaustive syllabus. Its official exam description identifies the broad skills tested in each section. The topic lists below are useful areas to practise, not a guaranteed list of questions.

1. Verbal Ability and Logical Reasoning (VALR)

VALR tests reading comprehension, interpretation and reasoning in written English. XAT passages may include poems, cartoons and other less conventional material.

  • Reading comprehension and inference-based questions
  • Critical and analytical reasoning
  • Vocabulary, word usage and English grammar
  • Para jumbles, odd sentence out and cloze tests
  • Sentence correction and fill-in-the-blanks

2. Decision Making (DM)

DM presents personal, professional or business situations and asks candidates to choose a practical course of action. Questions assess judgement, prioritisation and the ability to balance competing interests.

  • Ethical and workplace dilemmas
  • Business and managerial caselets
  • Stakeholder conflicts and prioritisation
  • Data-based decisions, allocations and constraints
  • Personal and social situations

3. Quantitative Aptitude and Data Interpretation (QA & DI)

This section tests mathematical fundamentals and the ability to interpret information presented in different formats.

  • Arithmetic: percentages, ratios, averages, mixtures, interest, time and work, and speed-distance-time
  • Algebra: equations, inequalities, functions, progressions and logarithms
  • Geometry and mensuration: triangles, circles, polygons, areas and volumes
  • Modern maths and number systems: probability, permutations, combinations, sets, divisibility and remainders
  • Data interpretation: tables, charts, graphs and caselets

4. General Knowledge (GK)

The GK section has about 20 questions: 8 on static GK and 12 on current affairs.

  • Current affairs in India and internationally
  • Business, economy and government schemes
  • Science, technology, sports and awards
  • History, geography, polity and important organisations
  • Books, authors and notable personalities

XAT 2027 Preparation Strategy

Prepare for all three Part 1 sections, but give Decision Making dedicated practice. A useful plan is built around previous XAT papers, timed practice and careful review—not just the number of mocks attempted.

Verbal Ability and Logical Reasoning

  • Practise reading comprehension with inference, tone, argument and author-intent questions.
  • Include critical reasoning, para jumbles, grammar and vocabulary in your weekly practice.
  • Review why each incorrect option is wrong; this improves elimination rather than relying on guesswork.

Decision Making

  • Solve caselets from previous XAT papers and identify the decision-maker, stakeholders, constraints and likely consequences.
  • Prioritise fair, practical and sustainable decisions; avoid options that ignore a key stakeholder or introduce an unrealistic assumption.
  • After each set, compare your reasoning with the official answer key where available. Do not memorise a single formula for every case.

Quantitative Aptitude and Data Interpretation

  • Build accuracy in arithmetic, algebra, geometry and modern mathematics before attempting mixed timed sets.
  • Practise interpreting tables, charts and caselets; write down the data you need before calculating.
  • Maintain an error log for concept gaps, calculation mistakes and questions that took too long.

General Knowledge

  • Revise current affairs, business and economy, government initiatives, awards, sports, science and important static-GK topics.
  • Use short, regular revision sessions rather than leaving GK until the final few days.

How to review mocks and previous papers

  1. Attempt a full-length paper under the current 170-minute Part 1 plus 10-minute GK structure.
  2. Classify every lost mark as a concept gap, misread question, calculation error, poor question selection or time-management issue.
  3. Re-solve questions you got wrong or left unanswered, then note the lesson in your error log.
  4. Track accuracy and time by section across several mocks before changing your section order or attempt strategy.
Use the unanswered-question rule strategically: the first 12 unanswered Part 1 questions do not attract a penalty; each additional unanswered Part 1 question attracts a 0.10-mark deduction. This is not a reason to leave questions blank automatically—use mocks to decide when to move on.

Practise with the official XAT portal and use the latest question papers and answer keys released by XLRI.

XAT 2027 Admit Card

The XAT 2027 admit card is expected to be available from December 20, 2026 (tentative) through the official website. Log in using your XAT ID and password, open the admit-card link and download the document.

Check your name, photograph, signature, exam date, reporting time and test-centre address. Carry a printed admit card and a valid original photo ID to the centre. If you find an error, contact XLRI through the official dashboard or the helpdesk before exam day.

XAT 2027 Answer Key

XLRI has not announced the answer-key release date or objection window. After the examination, check xatonline.in for the response sheet, question paper or official answer key, if released.

You can estimate your Part 1 score using the final marking rules: +1 for each correct answer, −0.25 for each incorrect answer, and −0.10 for every unanswered question beyond the first 12. GK has no negative marking and is not included in the overall percentile. Follow XLRI’s notification for the process and deadline to raise any objection.

XAT 2027 Result and Scorecard

XLRI has not listed a separately confirmed result-publication date in its published admission timeline. Check xatonline.in for the result notification and scorecard download link rather than relying on an unconfirmed date.

Once released, log in with your XAT credentials and download the scorecard. Review your overall score, overall percentile and sectional scores/percentiles. The General Knowledge score is not included in the overall percentile; XLRI may use the GK score in its own final selection process.

What to do after the result

  1. Download and save your scorecard; check that your details are correct.
  2. Compare your overall and sectional performance with the published criteria of each target programme.
  3. Confirm that you have submitted a separate application to every B-school you want to consider.
  4. Track shortlist announcements and complete interview or assessment registrations within each institute’s deadline.
  5. Compare final offers, programme fit, fees, location and career outcomes before accepting an admission offer.

XAT 2027 Cutoff and Percentile

There is no single XAT cutoff that applies to every MBA college. Each institute sets its own shortlist criteria, and requirements may differ by programme, applicant category and admission year.

For XLRI, shortlist decisions consider XAT performance alongside factors such as academic background and relevant work experience. Shortlisted candidates then take part in the institute’s interview process; final selection also considers interview performance and other profile factors. Other B-schools may use different sectional thresholds, overall percentiles, profile factors and selection stages.

How to assess your XAT score

  • Check both overall and sectional percentiles; a strong overall percentile may not offset a programme-specific sectional requirement.
  • Read the current admission criteria on the institute’s official website, not just an aggregate cutoff list.
  • Separate an official shortlist threshold from an estimated target or a coaching-site prediction.
  • Use previous-year cutoffs only as historical context. They are not a guarantee of the next admission cycle.

As of the latest published XAT 2027 information, candidates should wait for institute-specific shortlist criteria rather than treating an unofficial percentile range as a confirmed cutoff.

XAT 2027 MBA Admission Process

Your XAT score is one part of the admission process; it does not by itself confirm admission. XLRI and every other participating B-school manage their own applications, shortlists and final offers.

What happens after you take XAT?

  1. Complete college applications: Submit a separate form to each institute. Check whether the form must be submitted before the XAT result.
  2. Institute shortlisting: Colleges assess XAT performance against their own overall and sectional criteria. Some may also consider academics, work experience or other profile factors.
  3. Interview and assessments: Shortlisted candidates may be invited for a personal interview, group discussion, written assessment or another programme-specific evaluation.
  4. Final merit list: Institutes combine entrance-test performance with the components in their published selection policy.
  5. Offer and admission: Check the offer deadline, fee payment schedule, document verification and waitlist movement before accepting a seat.

XLRI selection

For XLRI BM and HRM, the published process includes shortlisting followed by interviews. XLRI may consider XAT performance, academic background and relevant work experience at shortlisting; final selection also considers interview performance and other profile factors. Applicants to multiple XLRI programmes may be considered for more than one interview or group discussion.

XLRI PGDM (General Management) has a separate eligibility and application route for experienced professionals, including XAT/GMAT/GRE options subject to the programme rules. Check the official XLRI XAT admission page for the latest programme-specific conditions.

Other XAT-accepting B-schools

Do not assume that applying for XAT automatically applies you to another college. Track each institute’s form, shortlist date, interview instructions and fee-payment deadline independently.

XAT 2027 Frequently Asked Questions

When will XAT 2027 be conducted?

XAT 2027 is scheduled for 3 January 2027, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, as a computer-based test.

What is the last date to register for XAT 2027?

The published registration deadline is 6 December 2026. Check the official portal in case XLRI announces a change.

How many questions are there in XAT 2027?

The published pattern lists 95 questions across four sections: VALR, Decision Making, QA & DI and General Knowledge.

How many unanswered questions are allowed without penalty?

For Part 1, the first 12 unanswered questions do not attract a penalty. Each additional unanswered question attracts a 0.10-mark deduction. General Knowledge has no negative marking.

Does General Knowledge affect the XAT percentile?

No. GK is excluded from the overall XAT percentile. XLRI may use the GK score in its own final selection process.

Is work experience compulsory for XAT 2027?

No. Work experience is not required to appear for XAT or apply to XLRI BM and HRM. XLRI PGDM (General Management) has a separate work-experience requirement.

Does XAT have one common cutoff for all colleges?

No. Each institute and programme sets its own shortlist criteria. Check overall and sectional requirements on the relevant college’s official admission page.

Do I need to apply separately to colleges accepting XAT?

Yes. XAT registration is not a common application for all participating B-schools. Submit a separate application to each institute and track its deadline.

Can I prepare for CAT and XAT together?

Yes. CAT preparation supports much of VALR, QA and DI. Add regular XAT Decision Making practice, GK revision and full-length XAT papers to prepare for the differences in the exam.
