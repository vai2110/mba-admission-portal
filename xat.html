XAT 2027 Overview

Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) is conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur on behalf of XAMI members for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes.

The official XAT website states that the examination is accepted by 250+ premier B-schools and is conducted in 100+ test cities across India.

Why is XAT important?

XAT is particularly known for its Decision Making section, which tests candidates on critical thinking, problem solving and prioritisation in practical situations.

XAT 2027 Eligibility

Candidates applying for XAT should fulfil the eligibility requirements specified by XLRI and the participating institutes.

  • Candidates should possess a recognised Bachelor's degree of at least three years in any discipline.
  • Final-year undergraduate students can also apply subject to the conditions specified by XLRI.
  • There is no restriction based on a specific undergraduate stream for the general XAT examination.
  • Candidates should separately check programme-specific eligibility requirements of the B-school they wish to apply to.

XAT 2027 Marking Scheme

  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • 0.25 marks are deducted for an incorrect answer.
  • Candidates can leave up to 8 questions unattempted without the additional unattempted-question penalty.
  • A penalty of 0.10 marks applies for every question left unanswered after the first 8 unanswered questions.
  • There is no negative marking for General Knowledge questions.
Important:

The General Knowledge section does not contribute to the overall percentile/cutoff score, but it can be considered in the selection process for XLRI programmes.

How to Prepare for XAT 2027

1. Build strong fundamentals

Start with the basics of quantitative aptitude, verbal ability, logical reasoning and data interpretation.

2. Give special attention to Decision Making

Decision Making is a distinctive part of XAT. Practise caselets and learn to evaluate situations from multiple stakeholder perspectives.

3. Read regularly

Reading editorials, business news and long-form articles can improve reading comprehension and general awareness.

4. Take mock tests

Use full-length mocks to improve time management, question selection and accuracy.

5. Analyse every mock

Don't only track your score. Identify questions where you lost time, made avoidable errors or selected the wrong approach.

XAT Selection Process

The admission process after XAT varies across B-schools. Candidates may be shortlisted based on their XAT performance and subsequently evaluated through institute-specific selection stages.

  • XAT score / percentile
  • Academic profile
  • Work experience, where applicable
  • Personal interview
  • Written assessment or other institute-specific components

Always check the individual B-school's current admission policy before assuming that a particular component or weightage applies.

XAT 2027 FAQs: Frequently Asked Questions

When will XAT 2027 be conducted?

XAT 2027 is scheduled for January 3, 2027 from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, according to the official XAT website.

When does XAT 2027 registration close?

The official registration page currently lists December 6, 2026 as the registration closing date.

What is the XAT 2027 exam duration?

The examination duration is three hours.

How many questions are there in XAT?

The current official pattern lists 95 questions.

Is there negative marking in XAT?

Yes. There is a 0.25-mark penalty for incorrect answers. An additional 0.10-mark penalty applies for unanswered questions after the first eight skipped questions. GK questions do not have negative marking.

Is there a Decision Making section?

Yes. Decision Making is one of the main XAT sections and is a distinctive feature of the examination.
