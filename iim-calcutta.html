College Overview

About IIM Calcutta

IIM Calcutta is a two-year, full-time residential management institute at Joka, Kolkata. The flagship MBA combines compulsory first-year courses, a summer internship and second-year electives across multiple functional areas.

Academic structure

The official 2026–28 admission policy describes first-year coursework across economics, finance, marketing, operations, systems, organisational behaviour, human resources, strategy and public policy. The second year provides a broad elective basket.

How to use this page

Admission, fee, placement and batch figures are labelled by cycle where possible. A minimum CAT percentile is an eligibility/shortlisting threshold and does not by itself guarantee a PI call or admission.

Admission & CAT Cutoff

Eligibility for the MBA

For the 2026–28 batch, candidates needed a bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA; the threshold was 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates. Final-year bachelor's candidates and candidates awaiting results could also apply subject to the institute's completion and documentation conditions.

IIM Calcutta states that actual section-wise and overall cutoffs may be much higher depending on the number of candidates called for PI.

Selection process

  1. Stage I: Meet the minimum CAT 2025 sectional and overall percentiles and the non-negative raw-score requirement.
  2. Stage II: PI/WAT shortlisting uses CAT 56%, Class 10 marks 10%, Class 12 marks 15% and gender diversity 4% in the stated composite score.
  3. Stage III: Final selection uses CAT 30%, PI 48%, WAT 8%, academic diversity 6% and work experience 8%.

These weightages are specific to the 2026–28 MBA admission policy and should not be carried forward to 2027–29 unless the next policy retains them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Calcutta MBA?

For the 2026–28 MBA batch, the Stage-I minimum for Open candidates was 85 overall, with 80 VARC, 80 DILR and 75 QA. IIM Calcutta states that actual cutoffs may be higher depending on the number of candidates called for PI.

What is the eligibility for IIM Calcutta MBA?

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA was required for the 2026–28 batch, with 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates. Final-year candidates could also apply subject to the institute's completion conditions.

What is the IIM Calcutta MBA fee?

The 2026–28 MBA admission offer letter states a two-year programme fee of ₹27 lakh, plus a separate refundable caution deposit of ₹20,000.

What is the average salary at IIM Calcutta?

The 2026 final placement report records an average salary of ₹36 lakh and median salary of ₹35 lakh for the 61st MBA batch, with 542 offers for 458 students from 202 participating firms.

Does IIM Calcutta consider Class 10 and Class 12 marks?

Yes. In the 2026–28 admission policy, Class 10 and Class 12 marks contributed 10% and 15% respectively to the Stage-II PI/WAT shortlisting composite score.

Does IIM Calcutta require work experience for the MBA?

No. Prior work experience is not a basic eligibility requirement for the flagship MBA. Work experience contributes to the final-selection composite score under the 2026–28 policy.

What should students compare when evaluating IIM Calcutta and another IIM?

Compare the admission process, academic structure, total cost, location, batch profile, placement data, recruiter domains and the functions relevant to your career plans. The applicable admission and fee policies can differ by institute and year.

What should students check before accepting an IIM Calcutta MBA offer?

Check the current fee notice, admission conditions, hostel and other charges, financing options, programme calendar and the latest placement report before making the final enrolment decision.
