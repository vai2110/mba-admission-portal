College Overview

About IIM Calcutta

IIM Calcutta is India's first IIM and has a particularly strong reputation in finance, consulting and analytical management education. Its two-year MBA combines a compulsory first-year core with a wide second-year elective basket.

Why students shortlist it

Particularly strong for finance and consulting ambitions, or students who value a mature corporate ecosystem and extensive second-year elective choice.

Decision lens

Use ranking as a starting point; make the final decision using programme fit, fees, career outcomes, admission probability and personal preference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Calcutta MBA 2026?

The official minimum for Open candidates is 85 overall, with 80 VARC, 80 DILR and 75 QA. IIM Calcutta explicitly notes that the actual cutoff can be much higher because the shortlist is composite. So 85 is an eligibility gate, not a safe CAT percentile.

What is the fee of IIM Calcutta MBA?

The incoming 2026–28 fee should be taken from IIM Calcutta's current fee notice. For comparison, the latest official programme pages also show ₹33.50 lakh for the one-year MBAEx and ₹25 lakh for the two-year PGDBA 2025–27 batch. Students should not use MBAEx or PGDBA fees as the MBA fee.

Is IIM Calcutta good for finance?

Yes. Finance is one of IIM Calcutta's clearest differentiators. If you are targeting investment banking, markets, corporate finance, consulting or finance-heavy roles, its curriculum, alumni network and recruiter ecosystem make it particularly relevant.

What is the average salary at IIM Calcutta?

The 2026 final placement cycle reported ₹36 lakh average and ₹35 lakh median salary, with ₹1.45 crore highest domestic and ₹1.10 crore highest international offer. Median salary is the better number for judging typical outcomes; the highest package should be treated as an outlier.

IIM Calcutta vs IIM Lucknow: which is better?

For finance and a finance-heavy consulting path, IIM Calcutta is usually the more natural fit. IIM Lucknow remains extremely strong for broad consulting, finance and general management. The final choice should depend on your target function, admission profile, location preference and cost.

Does IIM Calcutta consider Class 10 and 12 marks?

Yes. IIM Calcutta's admission policy uses past academics as part of the composite selection process. This matters for candidates with uneven school marks: a very high CAT percentile does not erase every weakness in the profile.

Is IIM Calcutta worth the high fee?

For students targeting finance, consulting and high-value corporate roles, the latest placement outcomes make the economics compelling. For a student choosing primarily for the IIM tag without a clear target role, the fee and opportunity cost deserve much more scrutiny.
