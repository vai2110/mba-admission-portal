Welingkar Mumbai PGDM: Quick Answer

WeSchool's flagship PGDM is a two-year full-time programme, started in 1994, with a multidisciplinary approach across Marketing, Finance, Human Resources and Operations. The current published intake is 180.

What makes this PGDM different?

It combines general management foundations with functional specialisation and WeSchool's broader ecosystem of industry projects, experiential learning and specialised programmes.

PGDM Curriculum & Specialisation

The programme combines general management with specialised expertise in Marketing, Finance, Human Resources and Operations. Students should also evaluate electives, live projects, internships and industry exposure while comparing offers.

Who Should Choose Welingkar PGDM?

Good fit

  • You want broad management rather than a narrow degree.
  • You value Mumbai's corporate ecosystem.
  • You want to explore marketing, finance, HR or operations.

Compare alternatives if

  • You have a stronger B-school call at similar cost.
  • You are choosing only for the ₹40 LPA headline.
  • You need a highly specialised analytics/technology programme.

Welingkar PGDM FAQs

What is Welingkar PGDM fee?

Is work experience compulsory?
