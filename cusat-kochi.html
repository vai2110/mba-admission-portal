CUSAT Overview 2026: Courses, Admission, Fees, Ranking & Campus

Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), commonly searched as CUSAT Kochi, is a public university in Kerala with its main campus at Thrikkakara, Kalamassery, Kochi. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated, doctoral and specialist programmes across engineering, science, computer applications, management, law, marine studies and applied research. Its 2026 prospectus states that the university is re-accredited with NAAC A+ grade and that its academic programmes are approved by UGC/AICTE, as applicable.

For 2026-27 applicants, the most important point is that CUSAT does not have one common admission rule or one common fee for every course. B.Tech, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, law and science programmes can use different entrance tests, eligibility rules, seat categories and fee schedules. Check the exact department and campus before filling options.

Quick answer for students

Consider CUSAT if you want a public-university option in Kochi with specialist engineering, marine, science, management or law programmes. Before deciding, compare your programme's entrance route, latest last rank, first-year payable fee, campus location and course-specific placement data—not only the university name.

CUSAT Admission 2026: CAT Exam, Eligibility, Application & Last Rank

For most programmes covered by the Common Admission Test (CAT), candidates apply online through the official CUSAT admissions portal, select the relevant test code and programme, complete the required details and fee payment, and then follow the university's instructions for exam, rank list, option registration and allotment. The prospectus instructs registered candidates to check the portal and their profile regularly for notices and action items.

How to apply for CUSAT admission step by step

Keep these details ready before starting the form:

  • Working email address and mobile number
  • Class 10/12 or qualifying-degree details and marks
  • Entrance-test information and the correct programme/test code
  • Documents required for the category or concession you claim
  1. Open the official CUSAT admissions portal and read the prospectus for your admission year.
  2. Register with a working mobile number and email address, verify the details and activate the account.
  3. Complete the online application and choose the correct programme/test code and applicable seat category.
  4. Upload the required documents and pay the applicable application fee shown in the current notification.
  5. Download or save the confirmation page and monitor your registered account for admit-card, exam and rank-list updates.
  6. After results, complete option registration and allotment steps when notified; pay the fee and report as instructed if allotted a seat.

2026 prospectus application-fee note: it lists ₹1,500 for up to two test codes and ₹700 for Kerala SC/ST candidates for up to two test codes. Additional charges can apply to particular categories, including NRI applicants. These are prospectus figures for CAT 2026, not a guarantee of the fee for a future admission cycle.

CUSAT B.Tech eligibility: what Class 12 students should check

For several regular B.Tech programmes, the 2026 prospectus requires a recognised Plus Two/Class 12 qualification with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, generally with at least 50% in Mathematics and 50% in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry taken together. Programme-specific exceptions and higher thresholds exist: for example, Naval Architecture and Ship Building lists a first-class/60% requirement along with the Mathematics conditions. Kerala SC/ST and eligible non-creamy-layer SEBC relaxations are stated in the prospectus. Do not assume that the same percentage rule applies to Marine Engineering, lateral entry or every branch.

For regular B.Tech programmes, CAT Test Code 101 is described as a three-hour test with 225 objective questions: 90 Mathematics, 75 Physics and 60 Chemistry. The prospectus says candidates generally need a minimum normalised combined score of 10 across Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry; the stated SC/ST exemption and requirement to attempt at least one question should be read in the official rules. B.Tech Marine Engineering and lateral entry to the third semester are listed as exceptions to this compulsory test route.

Are there All India, NRI or reserved seats at CUSAT?

Yes, but the seat allocation is not identical across courses. The 2026 prospectus says 45% of approved B.Tech seats are earmarked as All India Merit (AIM) seats, which carry a higher fee and can be opted for by Keralite and non-Keralite candidates. It also describes NRI seats for applicable programmes and All India Quota (AIQ) seats for specified UG/PG categories. For PG programmes other than M.Tech, 50% of General category seats are earmarked as AIQ; the prospectus also states a 25% tuition-fee hike for AIQ seats. Check the programme's seat table and fee category rather than applying these percentages to every course.

The prospectus describes supernumerary seats/categories such as EWS, differently abled candidates, transgender candidates, sports quota and other specified groups. The exact categories and seat count vary by programme. Do not infer a separate management-quota or direct-admission route unless the current official notice explicitly provides it.

Where should I check CUSAT cutoff or closing rank?

Use the official admissions portal's Last Rank Details and allotment notices. Compare the same programme, campus, category and seat type. A CSE rank at the School of Engineering, Thrikkakara, should not be treated as interchangeable with CSE at CUCEK Kuttanad or another seat category. Historical closing ranks are planning references, not guaranteed cutoffs for the next round.

Who is eligible for CUSAT MBA?

For MBA Full Time and Evening-Regular, the prospectus requires a bachelor's degree in any subject with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA. Kerala SC/ST candidates may apply with the minimum pass marks, and a 5% relaxation is listed for eligible Kerala SEBC non-creamy-layer candidates. The 2026 prospectus accepts a valid score in one of the specified management tests: IIM-CAT (November 2025), CMAT conducted after November 2025, or KMAT Kerala conducted after November 2025, subject to the result being available before the application closes.

How is the CUSAT MBA final rank calculated?

The prospectus specifies a 70:20:10 weighting: management aptitude test score (70%), personal interview (20%) and Class 12 marks (10%). This means a test score is important, but the final ranking is not based on the entrance test alone. The prospectus records 30 April 2026 as the application closure date for the 2026 MBA process; check the current portal for any later cycle rather than reusing this date.

The 2026 fee schedule lists MBA Regular General tuition at ₹35,200 and common fees at ₹18,642, for a stated total payable of ₹53,842. The AIQ tuition listed is ₹44,000, with total payable of ₹62,642. These figures are category-specific; NRI fees and one-time charges differ.

CUSAT MCA Admission 2026: Eligibility, Fees & Regular vs Cost Sharing

CUSAT offers MCA in Regular and Cost Sharing formats. Related postgraduate computing options include M.Sc Computer Science specialisations in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. The entrance route and fee depend on the exact programme entry.

  • Confirm the qualifying degree and any Mathematics requirement in the MCA prospectus entry.
  • Check the prescribed test code and whether a CUET-PG provision applies to your programme.
  • Compare Regular and Cost Sharing tuition before submitting programme options.
  • Confirm the teaching campus; do not assume all computing courses are offered at the same location.

Before choosing between MCA Regular and Cost Sharing, compare the total course fee and campus, not just the programme name. Also check whether the selected programme is at the Thrikkakara campus or Cochin University College of Engineering Kuttanad. Use the CUSAT MCA guide for the programme-level breakdown.

CUSAT FAQs: Admission, Fees, CAT Cutoff, Placements & Hostel

Is Cochin University of Science and Technology a government university?

CUSAT is a public university in Kerala. Its 2026 prospectus describes it as a science and technology university with programmes across engineering, applied sciences, management, law, marine studies and research.

What is the CUSAT admission process for 2026-27?

Read the prospectus, register on the official admissions portal, select the relevant test code and programme, submit the application and fee, follow the test/rank-list notifications, and complete option registration and allotment when announced. Programme-specific exceptions apply.

Can I get CUSAT B.Tech admission without CUSAT CAT?

The prospectus says CAT 2026 Test Code 101 is compulsory for regular B.Tech programmes except B.Tech Marine Engineering and lateral entry to the third semester. Those exceptions have their own admission rules; check the exact programme entry.

What percentage is required for CUSAT B.Tech?

For several regular branches, the prospectus states at least 50% in Mathematics and 50% in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry together. Some programmes have higher or different requirements, and category-based relaxations may apply. Check the specific branch before applying.

Does CUSAT accept CAT, CMAT or KMAT for MBA?

For the 2026 MBA Full Time and Evening-Regular process, the prospectus lists IIM-CAT, CMAT and KMAT Kerala, subject to the specified test/result timing and application deadline. MBA ranking uses test score, interview and Class 12 marks in a 70:20:10 ratio.

What are the CUSAT B.Tech fees for 2026?

The uploaded fee schedule lists ₹48,842 as total payable for General/State Merit School of Engineering B.Tech and ₹75,042 for All India Merit in the shown fee context. Other programmes, campuses and NRI categories can have different fees.

What is the CUSAT MBA fee for 2026?

The uploaded 2026 schedule lists ₹53,842 total payable for MBA Regular General category and ₹62,642 for AIQ. These are category-specific totals, not a hostel-inclusive two-year cost.

What is the latest CUSAT placement package?

The documents supplied do not verify one latest highest package across all courses. CUSAT's NIRF 2025 submission reports a ₹7,70,417 median salary for a specified four-year UG cohort graduating in 2023-24, with 151 of 155 placed in minimum stipulated time. Compare course-wise data and batch year before using it for ROI.

Does CUSAT provide hostel facilities?

The prospectus lists hostels at the main Thrikkakara/Kalamassery campus and a men's hostel at the Lakeside campus. Accommodation is subject to availability and hostel admission rules; confirm current room and mess charges with the hostel office.

Are NRI or All India seats available at CUSAT?

The 2026 prospectus lists All India Merit seats for B.Tech and NRI seats in applicable programmes, along with specified AIQ provisions. Fees and eligibility differ by category, so check the seat matrix and fee schedule for your exact course.

Where can I find the official CUSAT prospectus, fees and last rank?

Use the official admissions portal for the latest prospectus, notices, fee schedules and Last Rank Details. Always rely on the current cycle's official notices for deadlines and allotment steps.
