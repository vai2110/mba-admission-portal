CUSAT Admission 2026: CAT, CUET, GATE & Programme Routes

Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) is a public university in Kochi with a strong concentration in science, engineering, technology, marine sciences, law, management and applied research. Its official academic structure includes the School of Engineering, School of Management Studies, School of Legal Studies, Department of Computer Applications and specialist science and marine departments.

Why CUSAT is different from a typical private university

The academic offering is organised around specialist schools and departments rather than one broad private-university model. For students, this means the value proposition is strongest where low-to-moderate published academic fees, specialised departments and entrance-based selection matter.

The admissions portal is the authoritative source for current programme availability, CAT 2026 notices, last-rank information and the 2026–27 fee structure. The portal is currently publishing real-time admission updates, including B.Tech rounds in September 2026.

CUSAT Courses & Fees: What Students Should Compare

The School of Management Studies lists MBA Full Time as a 2-year, 4-semester, campus-based programme with 120 seats. It is designed for fresh graduates and early-career applicants.

For the 2026 process, SMS states that accepted management tests are CAT, CMAT and KMAT. The final MBA rank is prepared using 70% aptitude-test score, 20% personal interview and 10% Class 12 marks.

Student takeaway

CUSAT MBA is particularly worth comparing when your priority is management education at a public-university fee level and you are willing to compete through an entrance-score-led selection process.

Is CUSAT Good? Who Should Choose CUSAT in 2026

The 2026 prospectus lists MCA alongside M.Sc Computer Science programmes with specialisations in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. MCA admission uses a dedicated aptitude test where about half the questions are from mathematics and the rest test logical reasoning.

The MCA option also exists in both Regular and Cost Sharing formats and at both the Thrikkakara campus and Cochin University College of Engineering Kuttanad. Students should therefore compare fee category and location before selecting options.

CUSAT 2026 FAQs

Is CUSAT a government university?

CUSAT is a public university in Kerala with a broad academic structure spanning engineering, science, management, law, marine sciences and other disciplines.

Is CUSAT admission through CAT?

For many programmes, yes, but not every programme follows exactly the same route. The 2026 prospectus includes CAT, CUET, GATE and programme-specific tests depending on the course.

Which CUSAT course is best?

There is no single best course. CSE/AI & Data Science suit technology-oriented students, MBA suits management aspirants, MCA suits postgraduate computing applicants and specialist marine/science programmes suit students targeting those sectors.

Where can I check CUSAT 2026 last ranks?

The official admissions portal provides a Last Rank Details section. Students should use the exact programme and category rather than relying on coaching-site estimates.
