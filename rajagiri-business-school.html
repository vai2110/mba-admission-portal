What is Rajagiri Business School, Kochi known for?

Quick answer

Rajagiri Business School (RBS) is an AICTE-approved standalone management institute in Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi. Its flagship two-year full-time PGDM is recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU. RBS was ranked 91st in Management in NIRF 2025.

The school has international accreditations including AACSB, while its PGDM is accredited by EFMD and ACBSP. The programme uses core management courses, electives, dual specialisation options, cases, simulations, summer internship and international exposure opportunities.

RBS is particularly relevant for students who want a management programme in Kochi with a relatively moderate tuition fee and access to employers across consulting, financial services, FMCG, IT/ITES and other sectors.

Sources: RBS official website and RBS rankings & accreditations.

Rajagiri Business School Cutoff: what percentile is needed?

RBS's current admission page says shortlisting is based on eligibility criteria and entrance examination scores, but it does not publish a fixed 2026 CAT/CMAT/XAT cutoff on the admission page. Therefore, an exact “2026 cutoff” should not be invented.

Students should treat published or third-party percentile figures as indicative only and focus on submitting the strongest valid score among the exams RBS accepts.

Rajagiri Business School Fees & Scholarships

Current fee reference

Current 2026 course listings report the two-year PGDM tuition at ₹7.50 lakh. Hostel is separately reported at about ₹1.88 lakh for two years. Confirm the final payable amount in the RBS offer/fee communication before payment.

RBS also offers scholarships. The Diversity Scholarship provides a 25–50% tuition-fee waiver for eligible non-Keralite students. Career and Talent Scholarships are linked to CAT performance, with the Director/Dean's Scholarship offering a 75% tuition-fee waiver for CAT 2025 scores of 95 percentile or higher, subject to the stated selection conditions.
