What is Rajagiri Business School, Kochi known for?

Quick answer

Rajagiri Business School (RBS) is an AICTE-approved management institute in Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi. Its flagship two-year full-time PGDM is recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU. RBS was ranked 91st in Management in NIRF 2025.

RBS combines core management learning with electives, dual-specialisation flexibility, cases, simulations, experiential learning, summer internship and international exposure opportunities. The institute also highlights AACSB accreditation at school level and EFMD/ACBSP accreditation for its PGDM.

For students, the main decision is not simply whether RBS is “good”; it is whether the combination of ₹7.50 lakh tuition reference, Kochi location, programme structure and placement outcomes matches their target career.

Rajagiri Business School Admission 2026

RBS requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks or a master's degree. For PGDM 2026, the institute lists CAT, CMAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT and GMAT. Shortlisted candidates are invited for GD, PI and Business Awareness Test, and previous academic performance, relevant work experience and non-academic achievements can also be considered.

Rajagiri Business School Cutoff 2026

RBS's current admission page does not publish a fixed official 2026 cutoff percentile. It says shortlisting is based on eligibility and entrance-examination scores. Therefore, an exact “official RBS cutoff 2026” should not be invented.

Practical approach: submit your strongest accepted score and prepare seriously for GD/PI/BAT. A high percentile alone does not complete the RBS selection process.

Rajagiri Business School Fees & Scholarships

The current tuition reference is ₹7.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM. RBS's official older fee schedule shows the tuition as ₹4 lakh for the first three trimesters and ₹3.5 lakh for the final three. That bifurcation is clearly labelled as the older published schedule on the dedicated fee page.

RBS also lists scholarships, including Diversity, Career & Talent and Director/Dean's scholarships. Eligible candidates can receive substantial tuition waivers.

Rajagiri Business School FAQs

Is Rajagiri Business School good for PGDM?

RBS is an AICTE-approved management institute with a two-year PGDM, NIRF 2025 Management rank of 91 and international accreditation credentials. Whether it is the right fit depends on the student's target role, budget and preferred location.

What is the RBS PGDM fee?

The current tuition reference is ₹7.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM. Hostel and other expenses are additional.

How is the RBS PGDM fee divided?

RBS's official older fee structure divided ₹7.50 lakh into ₹4 lakh for the first three trimesters and ₹3.5 lakh for the final three. Confirm the 2026 instalment schedule before payment.

What is the latest RBS placement median?

The latest NIRF data for the 2024-25 graduating cohort reports ₹7.50 LPA.

Which exams does RBS accept?

For PGDM 2026, RBS lists CAT, CMAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT and GMAT.

Does RBS offer scholarships?

Yes. RBS lists Diversity, Career & Talent and Director/Dean's scholarship routes, subject to their eligibility and selection conditions.
