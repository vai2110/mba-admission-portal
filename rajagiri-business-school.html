What is Rajagiri Business School, Kochi known for?

Quick answer

Rajagiri Business School (RBS) is an AICTE-approved management institute in Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi. Its flagship two-year full-time PGDM is recognised as equivalent to an MBA degree by AIU. RBS was ranked 91st in the NIRF 2025 Management category; its official rankings page lists a 2026 data submission, but a 2026 rank should not be inferred from the submission document. See the official rankings and accreditation record ↗

RBS combines core management learning with electives, case studies, simulations, experiential learning and a summer internship. It also highlights international exposure opportunities.

  • Programme: two-year full-time PGDM.
  • Location: Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi.
  • Accreditations: AACSB at school level; EFMD and ACBSP for the PGDM.

To judge fit, compare the ₹7.50 lakh tuition reference with the total residential cost, the latest median salary and the roles you want after graduation.

Rajagiri Business School Admission 2027: Eligibility and Process

For PGDM admission, applicants need a bachelor’s degree with at least 50% aggregate marks or a master’s degree. The latest published admission process reviewed is for 2026; confirmed 2027 dates are not yet available.

The 2026 notice listed these accepted entrance exams:

  • CAT
  • CMAT
  • MAT
  • KMAT
  • XAT
  • GMAT

Accepted exams can change by cycle, so check the current notice before applying.

Selection stages: shortlisted candidates are invited for a Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI) and Business Awareness Test (BAT). Academic performance, relevant work experience and non-academic achievements may also be considered.

How to apply

  1. Check the latest eligibility rules and accepted entrance tests.
  2. Complete the online application and choose the relevant programme(s).
  3. Pay the application fee shown in the current notice.
  4. Upload academic records, entrance-test scores and supporting documents.
  5. Check your registered email for the GD/PI/BAT invitation and document checklist.
  6. Follow the official rank-list and admission instructions.

Rajagiri Business School Cutoff 2026

RBS's current admission page does not publish a fixed official 2026 cutoff percentile. It says shortlisting is based on eligibility and entrance-examination scores. Therefore, an exact “official RBS cutoff 2026” should not be invented.

Practical approach: submit your strongest accepted score and prepare seriously for GD/PI/BAT. A high percentile alone does not complete the RBS selection process.

Cutoff interpretation: a shortlist threshold, if published for a future cycle, is not the same as a guaranteed admission percentile. Final selection may consider entrance scores alongside the interview, business-awareness assessment and other profile information. RBS does not provide a fixed official 2027 cutoff in the reviewed admission notice, so third-party percentile predictions should be treated as estimates rather than official criteria.

Before applying, compare your score with the requirements in the current admission notice, but do not wait for a predicted cutoff to begin interview preparation. Also check whether your chosen test and test session are accepted for the specific programme you want; PGDM and other management programmes may not share identical test rules.

Rajagiri Business School Fees, Total Cost & Scholarships

The current tuition reference is ₹7.50 lakh for the two-year PGDM. An older RBS fee schedule split this into ₹4 lakh for the first three trimesters and ₹3.5 lakh for the final three. Confirm the latest instalment schedule before paying.

Scholarships listed by RBS include:

  • Diversity Scholarship: the official page describes tuition waivers of 25–50% for eligible non-Keralite students.
  • Career and Talent Scholarship: the published criteria link awards to CAT performance.
  • Director/Dean’s Scholarship: the published page describes a 75% tuition waiver for qualifying CAT performance.

Scholarship rules can change by admission cycle. Review the official scholarship criteria ↗ and obtain confirmation before budgeting.

Remember: ₹7.50 lakh is a tuition reference, not the confirmed total residential cost. Hostel, mess, deposits, travel, study materials and personal expenses may be additional. Ask RBS for the current complete fee schedule and refund policy.

Campus, Location and Student Fit: Is RBS Right for You?

RBS is located at Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi, near the Infopark and SmartCity business hubs. Its PGDM is described as a fully residential programme; proximity to business hubs does not guarantee an internship or job.

The NIRF 2026 institutional submission reports the following accessibility provisions:

  • Lifts or ramps in more than 80% of buildings.
  • Wheelchair or walking-aid support for movement between buildings.
  • Specially designed toilets in more than 80% of buildings.

Before accepting an offer, confirm hostel availability, room and mess charges, medical support, sports facilities, Wi-Fi, library access and transport options directly with RBS. Current prices and all facility details were not established in the reviewed sources.

Good fit if…You want a two-year PGDM with dual-specialisation flexibility and a moderate tuition reference.
Good fit if…You want Kochi-based management education near Infopark and SmartCity, with a residential PGDM experience.
Think carefully if…Your decision depends only on the highest package. The latest NIRF median is ₹7.50 LPA; average and highest package figures should be checked against the relevant official placement report.
