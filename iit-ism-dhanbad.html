IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA: Programme Overview

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad offers a two-year MBA and a separate two-year MBA in Business Analytics through its Department of Management Studies and Industrial Engineering (DMSIE). The department launched its MBA in 1997 and MBA in Business Analytics in 2020. Its distinctive context is the institute's engineering and applied-science ecosystem, with department-led industry interaction, consultancy and research connections in mining, minerals, energy and petroleum as well as broader management areas.

Is IIT (ISM) Dhanbad good for an MBA?

It is worth considering if you want a management degree within an IIT environment and are interested in sectors such as core engineering, energy, mining, oil and gas, operations, banking/financial services, IT or analytics. The decision should be based on the programme you qualify for, the latest MBA placement outcomes, total fee, role mix and your career target—not the IIT name alone. The latest detailed official placement report located for this page reports MBA average CTC of ₹10.48 LPA and median CTC of ₹10.38 LPA for the 2024–25 placement report; these are historical reported outcomes, not a promise for a new batch.

2025 NIRF Management ranking: IIT (ISM) Dhanbad was ranked 48th in the Management category in India Rankings 2025 (score 54.34). This is an institutional category ranking, not a ranking of the MBA programme alone.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad MBA Admission 2027–29

The latest fully published admission cycle available in the institute's MBA portal is 2026–27. It required a valid CAT 2025 score, an online application to IIT (ISM), and a personal interview. The application deadline for that cycle was 31 January 2026 and has passed. As of 17 November 2026, the 2027–29 MBA admission notification, accepted CAT year, application dates and interview calendar should be treated as awaiting official confirmation; candidates must not reuse the previous cycle's dates as the next schedule.

MBA eligibility in the latest published 2026–27 notice

  • MBA: A recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline, with first class or at least 60% marks/6.0 CGPA. The notice specifies 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
  • MBA in Business Analytics: B.E./B.Tech in any discipline from a recognised university/institute, with at least 60%/6.0 CGPA; 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
  • Final-year candidates could apply provisionally subject to completing the qualifying degree requirements within the institute's stated deadline.
  • A valid CAT score for the relevant admission cycle was required for both programmes.

How IIT (ISM) selected MBA candidates in 2026–27

The published final-selection matrix assigned 40% to the personal interview, 35% to CAT percentile, 10% to past academic records, 10% to industrial experience and 5% to female-candidate diversity. The institute stated that CAT percentile was used for shortlisting. It did not publish a guaranteed universal CAT percentile cutoff on the pages reviewed; therefore, CollegeDecoded does not claim a fixed cutoff. Meeting minimum graduation eligibility or having a particular CAT percentile does not guarantee an interview or admission.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad Ranking and Is It Worth Joining?

In NIRF India Rankings 2025, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad was ranked 48th in the Management category with a score of 54.34. The same institute is also evaluated in other NIRF categories; do not confuse its Management rank with its Engineering or Overall rank. A ranking is one input, not a substitute for comparing the MBA curriculum, fees, placements, role mix and location.

Who may find the MBA relevant? Applicants interested in management roles connected to engineering-led industries, energy, mining, minerals, operations, BFSI, IT and analytics should examine the department's industry connections and placement sector mix. Applicants seeking a particular consulting, product, marketing or finance career should verify recent role-level outcomes rather than assume a specific specialisation or recruiter pipeline.

For an IIT Dhanbad vs another IIT comparison, compare the same programme and batch: admission process, fee, average/median CTC, number placed, role mix, curriculum and location. An institute-wide engineering package comparison is not a fair MBA comparison.

MBA Campus Life, Hostel and Location

MBA students study at IIT (ISM)'s residential campus in Dhanbad, Jharkhand. The campus environment combines management studies with a much larger engineering, science and research community. Students should verify current hostel allocation, room-sharing, mess charges, internet, medical support and residential rules with the institute's hostel/Dean of Students' Welfare notices before admission; availability and charges can change by batch.

The department describes industry interaction, executive development programmes, consultancy assignments and research collaboration in areas including mining, minerals, energy and petroleum. This is a distinctive institutional context for management students. For clubs, cultural activities, sports and festivals, consult current Students' Gymkhana and institute event information rather than relying on generic campus-life descriptions.

Dhanbad is in Jharkhand. Applicants travelling for interview or joining should use the institute's current reporting instructions and verify rail/road connections and arrival arrangements for their reporting date.

Research, Industry Exposure and Career Context

DMSIE's stated activities include consultancy projects, executive development programmes and research collaborations involving mining, mineral, energy and petroleum organisations and agencies. The department also offers programmes in management and industrial engineering. This creates opportunities to study management problems in industrial and technology-intensive settings; individual live projects, internships, electives and company access depend on faculty, batch and current partnerships, so applicants should confirm specific opportunities directly with the department.

Does IIT (ISM) Dhanbad offer an MBA?

In the 2026–27 notice, MBA applicants needed a recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline with first class or 60%/6.0 CGPA, with 55%/5.5 CGPA for SC/ST/PwD candidates. MBA Business Analytics required a B.E./B.Tech degree with the corresponding marks threshold.

What is the MBA selection weightage?
