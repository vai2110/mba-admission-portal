Anna University ROI: How to Calculate It Properly

Why ROI can be attractive

  • Public-university fee structure
  • Strong engineering brand
  • Low academic cost for many regular seats
  • Chennai location and industry ecosystem

What to include

  • Hostel/mess and living cost
  • Laptop and travel
  • Opportunity cost
  • Your actual campus/programme placement outcome

For example, comparing a ₹32,000 first-year CEG fee benchmark with an expensive private college's fee is incomplete unless you also compare all four years, hostel/living costs and realistic placement outcomes. The official CEG fee sheet itself varies by category and self-supporting status.

Anna University Fees & Placements FAQs

What is Anna University B.Tech fee?

The official CEG 2025-26 fee sheet shows ₹32,000 as the first-year total for a General Tamil Nadu regular programme. Other categories and self-supporting programmes have different totals.

Is Anna University B.Tech cheap?

Many regular public-university seats have relatively low academic fees compared with private engineering colleges, but the exact amount depends on campus, programme and category.

What is Anna University hostel fee?

For CEG UG students, the 2025-26 hostel fee sheet listed ₹42,625 as the combined total of specified admission, deposits and recurring hostel/mess components.

What is Anna University placement package?

The latest NIRF 2025 overall submission reports an ₹8.00 LPA median for UG 4-year programmes in 2023-24. This is not a highest package and not a CSE-only number.

What is the Anna University CSE package?

Anna University's NIRF overall report does not isolate CSE. Use the UG 4-year university benchmark of ₹8.00 LPA as context, not a CSE guarantee.

What is Anna University MBA placement salary?

The NIRF overall data gives ₹6.00 LPA median for the PG 2-year category in 2023-24, which covers more than MBA.

Is hostel included in Anna University fees?

No. Academic fees and hostel/mess charges are separate. CEG publishes separate hostel fee information.

Does Anna University provide scholarships?

Fee concessions and scholarships can apply to eligible categories such as First Graduate and Tamil Nadu SC/ST/SCA students. The exact benefit depends on the current government/university rules and required certificates.

Which is better for ROI, Anna University or a private college?

It depends on the alternative. Compare total cost, median salary, placement count, branch, campus and career outcomes. A low fee alone does not guarantee the best career result.

Are the fee figures on this page for 2026-27?

The detailed CEG fee figures cited here are from the official 2025-26 fee document and are clearly labelled as a benchmark. Students should replace them with the current 2026-27 or next-cycle fee notice before payment.
