What is IIM Ahmedabad’s MBA (PGP)?

The Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is IIMA’s flagship two-year, full-time residential MBA. Domestic applicants apply through CAT and then go through IIMA’s shortlist and Analytical Writing Test–Personal Interview (AWT–PI) process. The programme accepts graduates from different academic disciplines; an MBA-specific undergraduate degree is not required.

Use this page for PGP-specific admission, cutoff, fee and placement information. For institute-wide courses, rankings, scholarships and campus information, see the IIM Ahmedabad overview. For full salary, recruiter and function-wise data, go to the IIM Ahmedabad MBA placements guide.

Who is eligible to apply for the 2027–29 PGP batch?

  • You need a bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification recognised under IIMA’s admission policy.
  • You must appear for CAT 2026 under the domestic applicant route and satisfy the applicable category-wise overall and sectional minimums.
  • IIMA applies its academic/application-rating rules in shortlisting; clearing graduation eligibility and CAT minimums alone does not ensure an AWT–PI call.

Eligibility and shortlist conditions can differ by applicant category and current policy. Read the official IIMA admissions page before applying.

What are the expected IIM Ahmedabad MBA admission dates for 2027?

IIMA’s published 2027–29 policy milestones indicate an AWT–PI shortlist around the second week of January 2027, final admission status around the second week of April 2027 and acceptance formalities around the first week of May 2027. Treat these as policy-indicated dates, not a substitute for the latest official notice.

Planning point: Complete CAT registration and exam formalities using the CAT 2026 portal, then monitor IIMA’s admission page for shortlist and offer updates. Do not use last year’s calendar as a confirmed deadline for this batch.

What CAT percentile actually gets an IIM Ahmedabad interview call?

There is no single public “actual-call percentile” that can be applied to every candidate. IIMA’s screening uses more than the minimum CAT percentile. Academic record, the policy-defined Application Rating, applicant pool and category can affect whether a candidate is shortlisted. A 99+ percentile is not a universal guarantee, and a community post describing one candidate’s outcome cannot establish a category-wide cutoff.

What candidate discussions can—and cannot—tell you

  • Reddit: discussion of IIMA’s 2027–29 admission policy works through how AR factors may interact with CAT percentile. Treat its calculations and conclusions as a candidate’s interpretation, not an official shortlist rule.
  • Reddit: candidates discussing IIM Ahmedabad interview calls includes self-reported profile and call experiences. These reports illustrate variation, but they are not a verified cutoff dataset.
  • Quora search: IIM Ahmedabad CAT cutoff and interview-call discussions can surface additional self-reported profiles. Check the year, category, academic record and source of any claim before using it.
How to use these discussions: Use Reddit and Quora to understand candidate experiences and questions to ask—not to set your target percentile from a single anecdote. The official policy is authoritative for minimum cutoffs and selection rules; online reports are unverified unless independently supported by published data.

For a realistic profile review, compare CAT percentile with Class 10, Class 12, graduation, discipline/category and any policy-recognised work experience or diversity factors. Do not infer a guaranteed call from percentile alone.

How does IIM Ahmedabad select PGP candidates after CAT?

  1. Minimum screening: the candidate must meet the applicable overall and sectional CAT minimums and policy conditions.
  2. Shortlisting for AWT–PI: IIMA uses a Composite Score incorporating the policy-defined Application Rating and CAT performance to identify candidates for the next stage.
  3. Analytical Writing Test and Personal Interview: shortlisted applicants are assessed through the AWT–PI process.
  4. Final selection: the final composite score uses the weightages specified in the admission policy. The 2027–29 policy gives the Personal Interview the largest share, followed by AWT and the composite score component.

Use the current policy document for the exact formula, normalization method and category-specific rules. Do not apply a previous batch’s formula to CAT 2026 applicants.

What is the IIM Ahmedabad MBA fee for 2027–29?

The latest published domestic PGP fee benchmark available for reference is ₹27.50 lakh for the 2025–27 batch, including tuition and programme-related charges specified for that batch. This is not a confirmed fee for 2027–29; IIMA may revise the amount before the new cohort joins.

  • Budget separately for personal expenses such as laptop, travel, clothing, stationery and laundry where these are not covered by the official fee schedule.
  • Check the official fee notice for instalment dates, deposits, mess charges and the exact list of inclusions.
  • Explore need-based financial assistance and scholarships through the IIM Ahmedabad overview and official institute website; eligibility depends on the applicable scheme.

How important is the summer internship in the PGP?

The summer internship falls between the first and second academic years. It gives students a chance to test a role and industry before choosing second-year electives and pursuing final placements. Recruiter participation and outcomes vary by batch, so use the institute’s latest placement materials rather than assuming every student receives the same role or compensation.

Who should consider this MBA—and what should applicants compare?

  • Consider PGP if you want a full-time, residential, two-year general-management MBA and can commit to the campus programme.
  • Compare total cost, not only tuition: account for personal expenses and fee-payment timing.

  • Compare programmes carefully: IIMA’s PGP is different from its one-year PGPX and blended BPGP. Start with the overview of IIM Ahmedabad courses if you are unsure which programme fits your experience and study format.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA PGP: frequently asked questions

Is 95 percentile enough for an IIM Ahmedabad interview call?

No. For General/EWS candidates, 95 is the published overall minimum screening percentile for the 2027–29 cycle, not a guaranteed interview-call threshold. IIMA also considers its Application Rating and other policy conditions.

Does IIM Ahmedabad publish one actual interview-call cutoff?

The minimum thresholds are published, but a single percentile should not be treated as a guaranteed actual-call cutoff for every profile. Use the current official policy and treat online candidate reports as anecdotal.

Is work experience compulsory for the two-year PGP?

No. The PGP cohort includes fresh graduates as well as candidates with work experience. Follow the current admission policy for all eligibility requirements.

Is ₹27.50 lakh the confirmed fee for 2027–29?

No. It is the latest published 2025–27 benchmark used here for planning. Check IIMA’s official fee notice for the 2027–29 amount.
