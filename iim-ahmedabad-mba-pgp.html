What is IIM Ahmedabad MBA (PGP)?

The PGP is IIMA's flagship two-year MBA programme. It is a full-time residential programme and is open to graduates from different academic disciplines. IIMA says the class includes both fresh graduates and candidates with work experience. The programme is designed to build general management capability first and then allow students to develop deeper interests through second-year electives.

In simple terms: if you are looking for the standard two-year IIM Ahmedabad MBA through the domestic CAT route, this is the programme you are looking for. It is different from PGPX, which is a one-year MBA for experienced professionals, FABM, which is sector-focused on food and agribusiness, and BPGP, which is primarily online/blended for working professionals.

Programme Structure: What Will You Study?

The first year is the common management foundation. IIMA groups the curriculum into foundational subjects, tools and techniques, functional courses and courses that provide a broader business perspective. The first year has about 30 core courses across three terms. In the second year, students choose electives from areas such as finance, marketing, strategy, HR, information systems, operations and decision sciences, entrepreneurship and public systems.

Year 2: Electives and specialisation

Second-year electives allow students to build a course basket around their interests. IIMA lists elective areas including Finance & Accounting, Marketing, Strategy, Human Resources, Information Systems, Operations & Decision Sciences, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Public Systems and Agri Management. The exact elective basket can change by academic year.

Student perspective: PGP is not a programme where you study only one specialisation from day one. The first year is broad and intensive; the second year gives you more choice in what you want to explore.

How Are PGP Classes Conducted?

IIMA's core pedagogy is the case method. Students analyse business situations, discuss alternatives and defend decisions rather than only learning concepts through lectures. IIMA also uses group work, seminars, games, role plays, industrial visits and project-based activities.

What does this mean for a student?

  • You should expect regular preparation before class, not only classroom attendance.
  • Class participation matters: students are expected to analyse cases and contribute to discussions.
  • Group work is an important part of the learning experience.
  • Quantitative subjects, presentations, written analysis and case preparation form part of the academic workload.

IIMA states that each credit requires a minimum of 80 hours of work; actual workload varies by course and term.

IIM Ahmedabad MBA Admission Dates 2027

The PGP 2027–29 policy provides indicative milestones. CAT 2026 follows the CAT examination schedule; IIMA has indicated a shortlist around the second week of January 2027, final admission status around the second week of April 2027 and acceptance formalities around the first week of May 2027. Always check the applicable IIMA notification before relying on a date.

Eligibility: Who Can Apply?

  • Academic qualification: A recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent with at least 50% marks/equivalent CGPA; the applicable category relaxation is 45% for SC/ST/PwD under the 2027–29 policy.
  • Final-year students: Candidates awaiting final-year results can apply subject to IIMA's prescribed completion and documentation conditions.
  • Academic background: Engineering, commerce, arts, science, medicine and other recognised disciplines can be considered; there is no requirement to have studied business at graduation.
  • Work experience: Prior work experience is not a basic eligibility requirement for the PGP. The class includes fresh graduates as well as experienced candidates.
  • Entrance exam: Domestic applicants use CAT 2026 for the 2027–29 admission cycle.
Common doubt: You do not need a BBA/B.Com degree to do the PGP. A recognised bachelor's degree from another discipline can also make you academically eligible if the applicable conditions are met.

IIMA does not publish a single guaranteed CAT percentile for an interview call.

How IIM Ahmedabad PGP Selection Works

The PGP process is not CAT-percentile-only. After the CAT stage, IIMA applies its published shortlisting methodology and calls selected candidates for Analytical Writing Test (AWT) and Personal Interview (PI). The final selection then combines multiple components.

Final-selection componentWeightage for 2027–29
Personal Interview50%
Analytical Writing Test10%
Normalised composite score40%

The broader profile considered by IIMA can include academic performance, CAT performance, work experience, co-curricular/extracurricular achievements and performance in AWT/PI. Therefore, two candidates with the same CAT percentile can have different selection outcomes.

Fees and Other Costs

The latest published domestic PGP fee benchmark on this page is ₹27.50 lakh for 2025–27, including ₹20.10 lakh tuition. IIMA has not published a confirmed 2027–29 fee in the sources reviewed.

What is included?

The published amount represents the total programme-fee benchmark including the stated tuition component and institute/programme charges. The exact line-item breakup for the 2027–29 batch should be checked when IIMA releases the applicable fee schedule.

What is extra?

IIMA identifies laptop/personal computer, personal expenses, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry as additional expenses.

How much money should a student budget overall?

Planning estimate: ~₹30–32 lakh for the two-year programme, combining the latest published fee benchmark with a reasonable allowance for personal/incidental expenses. This is not an official IIMA cost-of-attendance figure.

Fee alert: Do not present ₹27.50 lakh as the confirmed 2027–29 payable fee. It is the latest published benchmark currently used on this page.

Summer Internship: What Happens After Year 1?

Students undertake summer training/internships with corporate partners between the first and second year. IIMA describes the internship as an opportunity to gain first-hand organisational exposure, validate classroom concepts and understand business practices.

The summer internship is particularly important because it gives students an opportunity to test a career direction before returning for the second year and choosing electives. The exact internship process, participating recruiters and outcomes vary by batch.

Careers After MBA PGP

PGP is a general-management MBA, so career options are spread across multiple functions and sectors rather than being restricted to one industry. Students can use second-year electives and internships to build depth in areas such as consulting, finance, marketing, product/business roles, operations, strategy, HR, analytics, technology and entrepreneurship.

For audited salary figures, recruiters, roles and year-wise placement outcomes, use the dedicated IIM Ahmedabad placements guide. Placement figures should be read with their year, batch and metric because IIMA's audited reports use Maximum Earning Potential (MEP).

Academic Life and Student Experience

  • Residential format: students live and study on the IIMA campus during the programme.
  • Peer learning: group cases and team assignments expose students to classmates from different academic and professional backgrounds.
  • Clubs and activities: students can participate in academic, cultural, sports and other student activities.
  • Exchange/field opportunities: second-year academic choices can include exchange programmes, projects and intensive field courses depending on the offering.

The programme is academically demanding and requires consistent preparation. Students comparing PGP with a conventional low-intensity MBA should account for the residential format, case preparation and group workload.

PGP FAQs Students Usually Ask

Is work experience compulsory for IIM Ahmedabad PGP?

No. Work experience is not a basic PGP eligibility requirement. IIMA's PGP class includes fresh graduates as well as candidates with work experience.

Is 95 percentile enough for an IIM Ahmedabad interview call?

95 is the published overall minimum screening threshold for General/EWS candidates for the 2027–29 PGP cycle, but meeting the minimum does not guarantee an interview call. Shortlisting also uses other application-rating components.

Do I need an MBA-related bachelor's degree?

No. Graduates from recognised disciplines can apply if they meet the applicable academic and CAT requirements.

Is PGP the same as an Executive MBA?

No. PGP is a two-year full-time residential MBA. IIMA's PGPX is the one-year full-time programme for experienced professionals, while BPGP is a blended programme.

How many years is the PGP?

The PGP is a two-year full-time residential programme.

Where can I find the latest PGP placement numbers?

See the dedicated IIM Ahmedabad placements page, where placement metrics are separated by programme and year.

Is the ₹27.50 lakh fee confirmed for the 2027–29 batch?

No. It is the latest published 2025–27 benchmark used on this page. The applicable 2027–29 fee should be checked in IIMA's future fee schedule.
