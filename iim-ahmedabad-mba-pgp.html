Programme Overview

The PGP is IIMA's flagship two-year full-time residential programme and leads to an MBA degree. IIMA describes it as a programme for graduates from different academic disciplines who want to build careers in management. The class includes fresh graduates as well as candidates with work experience. See the IIM Ahmedabad overview for institute-level rankings, campus facilities and placement history.

Eligibility and Admission

  • Degree: A recognised bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA; 45% for SC/ST/PwD under the 2027–29 policy.
  • Final-year students: Eligible to apply, subject to completing degree requirements by the prescribed deadline.
  • CAT: Domestic applicants take CAT 2026.
  • Minimum CAT 2026 cutoff: General/EWS 95 overall and 85 sectional; NC-OBC 90/80; SC 85/75; ST 75/65. PwD thresholds are separately specified.
  • Selection: IIMA uses application rating and CAT performance for shortlisting, followed by AWT and PI for shortlisted candidates. The 2027–29 final composite uses 50% PI, 10% AWT and 40% normalised composite score.

Fees and Other Costs

The latest officially published domestic PGP fee benchmark is ₹27.50 lakh for 2025–27, including ₹20.10 lakh tuition. IIMA has not yet published the final 2027–29 PGP fee in the information reviewed. Personal expenses such as laptop, travel, clothes, stationery and laundry are additional.

Planning point: Do not treat ₹27.50 lakh as the confirmed 2027–29 payable amount. Use it as the latest official benchmark until IIMA publishes the new batch fee.

Who Should Consider This Programme?

This is the relevant programme for a graduate looking for IIMA's standard two-year MBA through the CAT-based domestic admission route. If you already have substantial work experience and want a one-year format, compare the MBA-PGPX. If your career interest is specifically food and agri-business, see the MBA-FABM.

Key Questions Students Ask

Is the CAT cutoff the same as the percentile needed for an interview call?

No. The published CAT figures are minimum screening cutoffs. Meeting them does not guarantee an interview call because IIMA also uses academic and application-rating components.
