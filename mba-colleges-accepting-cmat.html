Methodology & data note

CMAT is used across a broad set of management programmes, but acceptance can be programme-specific. IMT's current admission information is a useful example: CMAT 2027 is applicable only for IMT Nagpur, not IMT Ghaziabad. CollegeDecoded will keep exam acceptance tied to the relevant programme rather than treating an institute-wide label as universal.
