What Roles and Sectors Recruit from DoMS IIT Roorkee?

The 2025 report describes roles across General Management, Marketing and Finance, alongside the department's established strengths in Operations, IT and Analytics. It also notes increased opportunities from PSUs and government organisations.

The 2025 report's sector discussion should be read as a description of that specific placement season rather than a guarantee that every future batch will receive the same role mix.

How Students Should Read IIT Roorkee Placement Data

  • Average vs median: median is useful for understanding the middle outcome and can prevent the highest offers from distorting the average.
  • Highest CTC: this is an individual maximum, not the salary most students receive.
  • Top-batch metrics: Top 10% and Top 50% averages give additional context about distribution.
  • Recruiter count: participating organisations are not the same as number of offers or number of students placed.
  • Summer stipend: do not compare a two-month stipend directly with annual CTC.
