Quick answer

For the MBA 2023-25 batch, DoMS IIT Roorkee's official 2025 final placement report records ₹17.91 LPA average CTC, ₹17.64 LPA median CTC and ₹24 LPA highest CTC, with 60+ participating organisations. The same report also publishes top 10% and top 50% averages.

Roles and Sectors in IIT Roorkee MBA Placements

The 2025 report describes roles across General Management, Marketing and Finance in addition to established opportunities in Operations, IT and Analytics. It also notes participation from PSUs and government organisations.

These are placement-season observations, not a promise that every future batch will receive the same role mix or recruiter list.

How Students Should Read IIT Roorkee Placement Data

  • Average vs median: median gives a middle-outcome reference and is less influenced by the highest offer.
  • Highest CTC: this is the maximum reported CTC, not the typical salary.
  • Top 10% / Top 50%: these figures provide additional distribution context.
  • Recruiter count: participating organisations are not the same as offers made or students placed.
  • Summer stipend: a two-month stipend should not be compared directly with annual CTC.
