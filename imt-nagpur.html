What is IMT Nagpur known for?

Quick answer:

IMT Nagpur is an AICTE-approved management institute established in 2004. Its current two-year, full-time residential portfolio comprises PGDM Core, PGDM Marketing and PGDM Financial Management.

The institute states that its programmes are AICTE-approved, accredited by NBA and SAQS, and have AIU equivalence to MBA for higher studies. Its PGDM Core route provides flexibility across Finance, Marketing, HR & OB, Operations and Business Analytics & IT.

IMT Nagpur Admission: eligibility and selection

The latest official admissions information requires a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate. CAT, XAT, CMAT and GMAT are accepted for IMT Nagpur. Final selection uses the entrance score, academics, work experience and Personal Interview; work experience is not compulsory.

As of September 2026, the official admissions page still displays the 2026-28 cycle. The site does not yet provide a verified 2027-29 schedule, so this portal does not invent application deadlines.

IMT Nagpur Fees 2026-28

The current official fee information states that the two-year PGDM fee is ₹16 lakh, including hostel, mess, books and admission fee under the published residential structure.

Scholarship examples:

The official page lists ₹9,89,200 total payable after a 40% academic-fee merit scholarship for eligible high scorers, ₹12,94,600 for the 20% academic-fee female scholarship, and ₹2,89,000 under the published TFW structure.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff: CAT, XAT and CMAT

IMT Nagpur says there is no pre-decided cutoff. Its official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of 75 percentile for CAT/XAT, 90+ for CMAT and 550 for GMAT. Third-party historical datasets also report CAT 75 as the 2025 closing reference.

IMT Nagpur Campus and residential life

The campus is on Katol Road, about 35 km from Nagpur city. The PGDM is residential and the official FAQ says hostel stay is compulsory. The institute also reports international exchange opportunities through MOUs with 28 international institutes.

IMT Nagpur FAQs

Does IMT Nagpur offer an MBA?

It offers PGDM programmes. The institute states that its PGDM programmes have AIU equivalence to MBA for higher studies.

What is the IMT Nagpur fee for 2026-28?

The latest official page states ₹16 lakh for the two-year PGDM, including hostel, mess, books and admission fee under the published residential structure.

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no pre-decided cutoff. The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT and 90+ for CMAT.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. Work experience is not mandatory, although candidates with relevant experience can receive additional selection weight.

Is hostel compulsory?

Yes, the PGDM is residential and the official FAQ says hostel stay is compulsory.

What is the latest average package?

The current official admissions page reports ₹12 LPA average for Batch 2024-26, marked ongoing, and ₹24 LPA as the highest package.

Is there a management quota?

No. The official FAQ says there is no management quota and selection is based purely on merit.
