IMT Nagpur: What Students Should Know

Institute of Management Technology, Nagpur was established in 2004. The institute describes itself as an AICTE-approved management institute accredited by SAQS, and states that its PGDM programmes are equivalent to an MBA for higher studies.

IMT Nagpur is a focused management school rather than a multidisciplinary university. Its current flagship offering is a two-year, full-time, fully residential PGDM ecosystem with three programme labels: PGDM, PGDM-Marketing and PGDM-Financial Management.

Does IMT Nagpur offer an MBA?

Its core management qualification is the PGDM. IMT Nagpur states that its PGDM programmes are equivalent to MBA for higher studies. Students should therefore compare the programme on curriculum, accreditation, placements, fees and career outcomes rather than on the degree label alone.

IMT Nagpur Admission 2026: Eligibility & Selection

IMT Nagpur's admission FAQ says selection uses a consolidated assessment of CAT/XAT/CMAT/GMAT scores, past academic record, work experience and personal interview performance. Work experience is not compulsory, although relevant work experience can add weight in the selection process.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff: Is There a Fixed CAT/XAT Cutoff?

IMT Nagpur explicitly says there is no pre-decided cutoff. In its 2025-27 admissions FAQ, the institute says the previous stopping point was 75 percentile for CAT/XAT, 90+ percentile for CMAT and 550 for GMAT. These are historical reference points, not a guaranteed cutoff for 2026.

What percentile should you target?

Do not treat the historical stopping points as a safe score. A stronger percentile can improve competitiveness, but the final outcome also depends on academics, work experience, entrance score and PI performance.

IMT Nagpur Fees & Scholarships

The official 2025-27 fee sheet lists the total programme fee at ₹15 lakh, comprising admission fee, academic fees, alumni membership and refundable caution money. The institute's FAQ says the ₹15 lakh figure includes hostel and mess charges and is subject to minor revisions.

IMT Nagpur Campus & Student Life

IMT Nagpur follows a residential model. The institute's official material describes its campus as located around 35 km from Nagpur city on Katol Road, in a green and relatively quiet setting. The 2025-27 FAQ says hostel stay is compulsory for the residential programme.

Residential experience

Hostel, mess, student clubs, committees, events and peer learning are part of the management-school experience.

Industry exposure

The curriculum uses projects, summer internships and industry-facing activities to connect classroom concepts with business problems.

Is IMT Nagpur Worth It?

IMT Nagpur can make sense for students looking for a focused, residential PGDM with specialization flexibility and a strong management-school environment.

The decision should be made after comparing your entrance score, total cost, specialization preference, placement outcomes, location and alternative B-schools. Its three-programme structure also means students do not need to navigate a very large and fragmented course catalogue.

IMT Nagpur FAQs

Is IMT Nagpur an MBA college?

IMT Nagpur primarily offers PGDM programmes. It states that its PGDM programmes are equivalent to an MBA for higher studies.

What is the IMT Nagpur fee?

The official 2025-27 fee reference is ₹15 lakh including the listed hostel and mess component in the FAQ; the fee sheet breaks the total into admission, academic, alumni and refundable caution components.

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no fixed pre-decided cutoff. The 2025-27 FAQ gives previous stopping points of 75 percentile for CAT/XAT and 90+ for CMAT.

Is work experience mandatory?

No. IMT Nagpur says work experience is not compulsory, although it can add weight during selection.

Is hostel compulsory at IMT Nagpur?

The institute's FAQ says the residential programme requires students to stay in the hostel.
