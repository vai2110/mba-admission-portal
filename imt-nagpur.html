What is IMT Nagpur known for?

Quick answer

Institute of Management Technology, Nagpur is a management institute offering two-year, full-time residential PGDM programmes. The current portfolio comprises PGDM, PGDM Marketing and PGDM Financial Management.

For a student comparing private B-schools, IMT Nagpur stands out for its multi-programme PGDM structure, a residential campus and a placement pipeline covering BFSI, consulting, IT/ITES, FMCG, manufacturing and other sectors.

The latest NIRF-linked placement data reports 345 students placed out of 353 graduates in 2024-25, with a ₹11.96 LPA median salary. This is more useful for ROI evaluation than looking only at the highest package.

IMT Nagpur Fees 2026-28

The latest official fee sheet lists ₹16,00,000 for the two-year PGDM, PGDM Marketing and PGDM Financial Management programmes. The breakup is ₹15.27 lakh academic fee + ₹50,000 admission fee + ₹8,000 alumni membership + ₹15,000 refundable caution money.

The institute's FAQ states that the residential total includes hostel, mess and books. Scholarship and TFW schemes can materially reduce the academic-fee burden for eligible students.

IMT Nagpur Cutoff: CAT, XAT, CMAT & GMAT

IMT Nagpur says there is no pre-decided cutoff. Its official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT, 90+ for CMAT and 550 for GMAT. Treat these as historical screening references, not guaranteed current cutoffs.

Practical strategy: target comfortably above the historical threshold because final selection also considers academics, work experience/profile and PI performance.

IMT Nagpur FAQs

What is the IMT Nagpur PGDM fee?

The official 2026-28 total is ₹16 lakh, including the listed academic, admission, alumni and refundable caution components. The institute's FAQ says hostel, mess and books are included in the residential total.

What is the IMT Nagpur CAT cutoff?

There is no fixed published cutoff. The official FAQ cites a previous stopping point of about 75 percentile for CAT/XAT, 90+ for CMAT and 550 for GMAT.

Does IMT Nagpur accept CMAT?

Yes. CMAT is one of the accepted entrance exams for IMT Nagpur.

Is work experience compulsory?

No. Freshers can apply. Relevant work experience can receive additional consideration in the selection process.

What is the latest IMT Nagpur median salary?

The latest NIRF-linked 2024-25 figure is ₹11.96 LPA, with 345 students placed out of 353 graduates.

Does IMT Nagpur offer B.Tech?

No. IMT Nagpur is a management institute and its main full-time offerings are PGDM programmes. A separate B.Tech/cutoff page is therefore not required for this college cluster.
