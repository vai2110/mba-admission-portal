IIM Bangalore Placement Overview 2026

IIM Bangalore's PGP and PGPBA Class of 2026 completed the final and lateral placement cycle with strong participation across consulting, technology, product management, finance, e-commerce, manufacturing, FMCG, conglomerates and healthcare.

The scale of recruitment is important for students because the placement process is not dependent on a single sector. Consulting remains the largest career destination, but the presence of technology, product, investment banking and other management roles provides meaningful alternatives for students with different career goals.

177 Recruiting firms in the latest official placement cycle
665 Offers listed in the 2026 final placement report
45% Share of offers in management consulting in the 2026 placement update
What students should focus on

The placement headline is strong, but the most useful comparison is the role mix. Students targeting consulting, product management, technology, finance or investment roles should compare the relevant functional opportunities rather than relying only on the total number of offers.

Why role diversity matters

A student entering an MBA may not have the same career preference at graduation as they had before joining the programme. The breadth of recruiters at IIM Bangalore can therefore be particularly useful for candidates who want to explore different career paths through coursework, internships, competitions and the placement process.
