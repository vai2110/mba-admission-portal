About the Graphic Era MBA

Graphic Era's general MBA is a two-year postgraduate management programme. The university says the Department of Management Studies has been delivering management education since 2003 and its MBA is designed around industry-oriented learning.

Who should consider it?

Students who want broad management training but also want to choose a functional or sector-oriented specialisation. The programme is particularly useful if you want flexibility rather than a narrowly technical MBA.

MBA Admission Process

Apply online, upload documents, submit the application and complete the application-fee payment. Management admissions may include an aptitude test and personal interview/self-introduction video. Candidates with recognised entrance-test scores may receive exemption from the aptitude test as per policy.

MBA Fees & Scholarships

For 2026, the general MBA fee schedule varies by graduation percentage/entrance percentile and scholarship slab. The official fee page shows a total fee-per-year-installment range of ₹6,70,795 to ₹8,28,875 across the displayed slabs.

GEU states that 25% of seats are available for scholarship on a first-come, first-served basis. The fee page also lists a 5% defence-personnel ward scholarship and an additional 10% scholarship on net tuition fee for girls, subject to policy.

MBA Curriculum

The curriculum spans strategy, economics, business environment, analytics, finance, marketing, HR, operations and other management disciplines. The university also describes exposure through summer internships, real-life projects and dissertation work.

MBA Specialisations

FinanceFinancial management and analytical decision-making

Other published specialisation areas include Banking & Finance, Branding & Advertising, International Business, Retail Management, Entrepreneurship, Hospital Administration and Aviation Management.

Career After MBA

GEU identifies career pathways across consulting, banking, fintech, marketing, branding, retail, HR, operations, analytics, aviation, hospital administration and entrepreneurship. The actual role and compensation depend on specialisation, skills, internship experience and employer.

MBA FAQs

Can freshers apply for the Graphic Era MBA?

Yes. The management department states that fresh graduates as well as working professionals are eligible.

Is CAT compulsory for the MBA?

The university considers multiple recognised entrance tests and may exempt candidates with valid scores from its aptitude test under university policy.

What is the MBA fee?

The 2026 fee varies by scholarship/merit slab. The official page displays annual fee structures ranging from ₹6,70,795 to ₹8,28,875 per year installment across the shown slabs, plus one-time charges.
