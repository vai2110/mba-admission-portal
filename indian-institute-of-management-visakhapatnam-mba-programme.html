Post Graduate Programme (PGP)

The flagship two-year, full-time, residential Post Graduate Programme at IIM Visakhapatnam is designed to equip students to take on leadership roles in a rapidly changing global scenario.

Overview | Curriculum | Admissions | Fees | FAQs

Is the PGP at IIM Visakhapatnam a full-time program?

Yes, the Post Graduate Programme (PGP) is a two-year, full-time, residential program leading to a Post Graduate Degree in Management. It is accorded equivalence with a Master’s degree of an Indian University by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Duration2 Years
ModeFull-time, Residential
DegreePost Graduate Degree in Management

Overview

The PGP at IIM Visakhapatnam focuses on conceptual and analytical reasoning, providing students with deep insights into the dynamics of the business environment. The programme is designed to prepare students for leadership roles in business, government, and society.

Curriculum & Pedagogy

The programme follows a structure of three terms per year. Students undergo a summer internship upon completion of the first year. The pedagogy includes:

  • Lectures and classroom discussions
  • Case studies and business games
  • Individual and group projects
  • Term papers and role plays

Admissions & Selection

Admission to the PGP is based on a multi-phase process. Candidates are evaluated based on their performance in the Common Admission Test (CAT), academic background, work experience, and performance in personal assessment rounds.

Entrance ExamCAT (Common Admission Test)
