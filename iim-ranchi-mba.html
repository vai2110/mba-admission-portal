MBA Eligibility Criteria

Follow the IIM Ranchi MBA eligibility in the applicable admission policy. For 2026-28, final selection after PI used 65% CAT, 20% profile and 15% PI.

Before applying

  • Verify the qualifying degree and minimum marks against the latest programme notice.
  • Check whether final-year applicants are permitted and what proof must be submitted later.
  • Check category-specific relaxations and document requirements.
  • For executive programmes, verify the work-experience requirement separately.

MBA Admission Process

Submit the relevant CAT/application information, complete IIM Ranchi's shortlist/PI process and then compete on the consolidated merit list.

Step-by-step

  1. Confirm eligibility and the applicable entrance route.
  2. Complete the programme application within the official window.
  3. Upload academic, category and work-experience documents where required.
  4. Attend the institute's test, WAT, reflective writing or PI stage if shortlisted.
  5. Review the final offer, fee schedule and joining requirements before payment.

MBA Cutoff & Selection Criteria

The 2026-28 final merit formula was 65% CAT, 20% profile and 15% PI. This is more useful than treating one unofficial CAT percentile as a safe conversion score.

How to use cutoff data: A published minimum or screening threshold only tells you whether an application can be considered. It does not establish a guaranteed conversion percentile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MBA the same as the institute's flagship MBA?

No. Programme names can look similar across management schools, but eligibility, delivery mode, work-experience requirements and selection route can differ. Applicants should use the programme-specific policy.

What should I consider beyond the advertised highest package?

Compare average and median CTC, placement batch size, recruiter participation and role mix. Where official programme-level data is unavailable, do not assume institute-wide figures are exclusive to this course.

Are 2027-29 fees available?

Only if the institute has published them. Otherwise the latest official batch is a reference and the future fee should be marked pending.
