Alliance University B.Com: What Students Should Know

Alliance University's B.Com (Hons.) is designed to build knowledge in commerce, accounting and business while developing broader managerial and analytical skills. The official programme page positions the degree for careers in commerce, accountancy and related analyst roles and as a foundation for higher studies such as M.Com and MBA.

Who should consider B.Com at Alliance?

Students interested in accounting, taxation, finance, business analytics or professional qualifications can use B.Com as a foundation. Compare the regular B.Com (Hons.) with the CA Integrated, ACCA or CMA-linked options before selecting the exact programme.

Alliance University B.Com Admission 2026

The current B.Com (Hons.) programme information lists AUMAT, the Alliance Undergraduate Management Aptitude Test, followed by a personal interview assessment. Alliance University B.Com Fees 2026

The current B.Com (Hons.) page publishes a ₹25,000 registration fee and four instalments, with a total programme fee of ₹8,00,000 for Indian nationals. B.Com Career Options & Higher Studies

B.Com graduates can pursue accounting, finance, taxation, banking, business analysis and related roles depending on their specialisation and professional qualifications. The degree can also lead to M.Com, MBA and other postgraduate programmes.

B.Com or BBA: which is better?

B.Com is generally the more natural choice for students who want stronger commerce, accounting and finance exposure. BBA is broader for students focused on management functions such as marketing, HR, operations or general business. Compare the specialisation and career goal rather than choosing only by degree name.
