Who should choose BBA?

BBA is useful when you want an early foundation in business rather than a technical or engineering degree. It can lead toward marketing, finance, HR, analytics, sales, operations and entrepreneurship, but students should understand that internships, communication, analytical ability and postgraduate study can significantly affect long-term outcomes.

Choose the career direction before the specialisation

A specialisation should strengthen a career goal. Don't pick Business Analytics, FinTech or Digital Marketing simply because the title sounds current.

Which BBA options are available?

How does admission work?

BBA is listed among CU's current open programmes. The university follows programme-specific admission criteria, and CUCET requirements differ by programme. Students should therefore verify the exact 2026 eligibility and admission route for their selected BBA variant rather than applying a blanket rule.

The official admissions portal currently provides programme status and the active application process.

What does BBA cost?

The mandatory disclosure currently lists ₹84,000 for the broad BBA Hons./Hons. with Research route. Business Analytics and Digital Marketing are listed at ₹89,000, FinTech at ₹89,000 and Logistics & Supply Chain Management at ₹88,000.

Scholarship can change your effective cost

CU offers merit-based scholarships under its 2026-27 policy. Check the applicable phase and programme conditions before calculating ROI.

What should a strong BBA experience include?

A good BBA should connect business concepts with real decisions. CU highlights experiential learning, industry orientation and flexible academic options. Students should actively use that environment for projects, internships, competitions and industry exposure.

Business fundamentals

Accounting, finance, marketing, HR, operations and business strategy create the base.

Analytics

Excel, statistics, data visualisation and basic SQL can make a business graduate more employable.

Communication

Presentation, writing, interviews and professional communication matter across management roles.

What can you do after BBA?

Marketing

Brand, digital marketing, sales, growth and customer roles.

Finance

Entry-level finance, banking and business-finance roles, depending on skills and employer requirements.

Analytics

Business analyst and reporting-oriented roles when supported by quantitative and technical skills.

MBA

Many BBA students use an MBA to deepen specialisation and improve access to higher-level management roles.
