How Much Should You Budget for XIM?

Build your budget in four layers: course fee, other programme charges, residence charges and monthly living/personal expenses. Your actual cost depends on programme, room type, field learning and lifestyle.

Budget checklist

  • Tuition/course fee
  • Development fund
  • Materials and IT
  • Career services
  • Hostel and residence administration
  • Board/mess
  • Personal expenses

Fee verification

XIM explicitly states that published figures are indicative, subject to change and confirmed through the admission offer letter. Verify before payment.

FAQs

What is the XIM MBA-BM fee?

The 2026 course fee is ₹11.50 lakh per year, or ₹23 lakh over two years, before other applicable charges.

Is hostel included in the course fee?

No. Residential expenses are listed separately.

Is the XIM fee structure fixed?

The university says the published figures are indicative, subject to change and confirmed with the admission offer letter.
