Amity Placement Data 2025-26

Amity's official placement data page currently reports 8,000 placements in 2025-26 and 1,200+ companies visiting Amity campuses for placements. The university also reports more than 4,500 students placed a year before passing out.

These are broad university-level placement figures and should not be interpreted as the average salary or placement rate of every individual programme.

Recruiters and Placement Sectors

The official placement statistics page lists sectors including IT/ITES/software, BFSI, consulting, e-commerce, FMCG, healthcare, manufacturing, energy, logistics, media, retail and research. Historical recruiter participation includes Fortune 500 and other major employers.

How to Read Amity Placement Data

Useful comparisons

Look for programme-specific placement reports, median or average compensation, recruiter quality, role profile, internship conversion and the percentage of the eligible batch participating.

Do not assume

A university-wide placement count or an individual highest package does not represent the typical outcome for every course.

Programme-wise Placement View

Engineering, management, law and other disciplines have different recruiter pools and career paths. Students should compare outcomes for the exact programme and graduating batch wherever the university provides a dedicated report.

Student Discussion: Is the Placement Claim Enough?

Placement numbers are useful only when combined with the total batch size, role quality, salary distribution and programme fee. For a high-fee private programme, ROI should be evaluated using realistic expected outcomes rather than the maximum package.
