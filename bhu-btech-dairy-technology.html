What is BTech Dairy Technology at BHU?

BHU lists B.Tech. in Dairy Technology among its undergraduate programmes. The course is aimed at the technical side of dairy processing, production, quality, food safety, plant operations and related industry functions.

What CUET subjects are needed?

The official 2026 UG registration notice lists the Dairy Technology route under the required CUET subject structure in the programme bulletin. Candidates should verify the current bulletin for the exact qualifying subjects and marks before applying.

How do you get admission?

  1. Check the current BHU BTech Dairy Technology eligibility.
  2. Appear in the required CUET-UG subjects.
  3. Register in BHU CAP-UG.
  4. Submit preferences and documents.
  5. Track allotment and complete fee payment/reporting.

What is the BHU BTech Dairy Technology cutoff?

BHU cutoffs are programme- and round-specific. The current official cutoff portal should be checked for the latest round. A fixed 2027 cutoff is not officially published in advance.

What is the BTech Dairy Technology fee?

The exact fee should be taken from the current B.Tech programme/admission fee notice. It should not be estimated from the regular Arts or Commerce fee structures.

What are BTech placements at BHU?

BHU's university-level NIRF figures report a ₹9 LPA median for UG 4-year programmes. This is not a BTech Dairy Technology-specific salary figure.

See BHU Placements for the latest university-level NIRF and official Annual Report data.

What jobs can you get after Dairy Technology?

Career areas include dairy processing, quality assurance, food safety, production, plant operations, procurement, cold-chain operations, product development and technical roles in dairy and food companies.

BHU BTech Dairy Technology FAQs

Is BTech Dairy Technology through CUET?

Yes. It is listed among BHU's UG programmes under the CUET-UG admission system.

Is there one BHU BTech cutoff?

No. The applicable score changes by programme, category and round.
