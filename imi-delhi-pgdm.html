IMI Delhi PGDM: Is This the Right Programme for You?

IMI Delhi's flagship PGDM is a two-year management programme designed for students seeking broad exposure across business functions. The programme offers learning in Marketing, Strategy, Analytics, Finance, Operations and Information Systems while allowing students to shape their career direction through electives and track-oriented courses.

Best suited for

Students who are not completely locked into one function and want the flexibility to explore consulting, BFSI, analytics, marketing, operations, general management and technology-linked business roles.

IMI Delhi PGDM Admission: Eligibility, Exams & Selection

For the recent 2026 cycle, candidates needed a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent. CAT 2025, XAT 2026 and GMAT were accepted. Final-year candidates were also eligible subject to the institute's conditions.

ParameterRecent IMI requirement
Graduation50% minimum

TestsCAT / XAT / GMAT
CutoffNo fixed published cutoff
SelectionProfile + test score + video assessment + PI

What happens after applying?

IMI Delhi evaluates academic consistency, standardised test performance, work experience and broader profile factors. Shortlisted candidates go through the institute's video assessment and personal interview. The PI evaluates areas such as general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, communication and personal or professional initiatives.

Do not chase an assumed cutoff

Because IMI Delhi says there is no fixed cutoff, a candidate should focus on a balanced profile and strong interview preparation rather than treating a single CAT/XAT percentile as a guarantee.

IMI Delhi PGDM Curriculum & Specialisations

The new IMI curriculum is structured around management foundations, track core learning and flexibility. Foundation areas include management communication, critical thinking, business statistics, managerial economics and business ethics & sustainability. Students then develop deeper capability aligned with career tracks.

Career areaExamples of learning direction
FinanceCorporate finance, financial analysis, valuation and related electives
MarketingConsumer, brand, digital and market-facing management roles
StrategyCompetitive advantage, corporate and business strategy
AnalyticsData-driven decision-making and business analytics
OperationsService, process and supply-chain oriented management

The programme also uses case discussions, projects, presentations and industry-linked learning. A summer internship between the first and second year is an important bridge between classroom learning and final placements.

IMI Delhi Campus, Location & Student Experience

The campus is located at B-10, Tara Crescent, Qutab Institutional Area, New Delhi. The location provides access to the wider Delhi-NCR business ecosystem. IMI highlights technology-enabled classrooms, library resources, computer facilities, auditorium and student activities.

International exposure

Students can access international exchange and short global immersion opportunities through IMI's partner-school network, subject to the programme and selection requirements.

Delhi advantage

The location can be valuable for students targeting corporate events, internships and recruiter interaction in NCR. It should still be weighed against the programme fee and the placement outcomes relevant to your chosen career function.

Who Should Choose IMI Delhi PGDM?

Good fit

  • You want a general-management programme with multiple career paths.
  • You want to study in Delhi-NCR.
  • You are interested in finance, consulting, analytics, marketing or operations.
  • You want a structured two-year residential management programme.

Think twice

  • You are certain about HR or BFSI and would benefit more from the dedicated programmes.
  • The total cost is uncomfortable relative to your alternatives.
  • You are choosing only because of a headline package.
