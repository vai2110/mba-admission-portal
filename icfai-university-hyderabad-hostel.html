IFHE Hyderabad Hostel Facilities

IBS Hyderabad states that hostel facilities are available to MBA students. Accommodation is on the campus, and students should confirm room availability and the applicable accommodation charges for their intake before reporting.

Important

Hostel availability does not mean hostel charges are included in the academic programme fee. Treat accommodation, mess and deposits as separate budget items.

Campus Facilities and Student Life

IBS Hyderabad's campus resources include academic databases, sports and recreation facilities and a health centre. Reported sports facilities include cricket, football, volleyball, basketball, tennis, badminton, squash, chess and carrom. The campus health centre has medical and paramedical support.

Academic resources

  • Business and research databases.
  • Technology-enabled learning infrastructure.
  • Industry and business resources.

Student facilities

  • Sports and recreation.
  • Health support.
  • Residential campus environment.

Transport for Day Scholars

Day scholars can use bus transport from the city to the IFHE campus on a payment basis. Students living off campus should compare daily commute time and cost with the residential option.

Hostel and Living Cost Planning

IFHE's published fee information lists a refundable hostel security deposit of ₹10,000 and a mess deposit of ₹30,000. The actual accommodation charge should be confirmed from the current intake-specific schedule.

Student Discussion: What Should You Check?

Before choosing hostel, students should ask about room type, occupancy, food schedule, rules, internet reliability, laundry, visitor policy, curfew and the actual annual cost. These practical details can matter as much as the room itself.

IFHE Hyderabad Hostel FAQs

Is hostel available for MBA students?

Yes. IBS Hyderabad states that hostel facilities are available to MBA students.

Is the hostel fee included in MBA tuition?

No. Living and accommodation expenses are additional to the academic programme fee.

Can day scholars use transport?

Yes. IFHE states that day scholars can avail city-to-campus bus transport on payment basis.
