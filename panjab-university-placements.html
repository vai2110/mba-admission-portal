Does Panjab University have 100% placement?

There is no single university-wide placement percentage

Panjab University is a multidisciplinary university. Placement and higher-study outcomes vary sharply by department, programme and graduating cohort. A single “PU average package” would therefore be misleading.

The most reliable comparable source is the university's NIRF data, which reports programme-category outcomes separately. Institute-level placement reports can then provide additional recruiter and package detail.

UBS / Management placements

UBS is one of PU's most placement-oriented schools. For 2024-25, reported UBS placement coverage was 85% with 105 students placed, ₹12.95 LPA average CTC and ₹25.11 LPA highest CTC.

Engineering placements

Engineering outcomes should be evaluated at the institute and branch level rather than using UBS management figures. Students should compare CSE/IT, electronics, mechanical and other branches separately because recruiter demand and placement rates can differ substantially.

What to compare

Branch-wise placement percentage, average/median package, highest package, recruiter list, internship conversion and batch size.

What to avoid

Do not use a management-school package as the “Panjab University engineering package”.

How should students read Panjab University placement data?

  1. Identify the exact programme. “PU placement” is too broad.
  2. Check the denominator. NIRF may report graduates and placed students, while institute reports may report students who opted for placements.
  3. Prefer median/average over highest. The highest package is a top-end outcome.
  4. Check higher studies. Low placement counts can coexist with large higher-study cohorts.
  5. Look at recruiter quality and role. A salary number without job function gives incomplete career information.

This is especially important for a multidisciplinary university like PU, where career pathways differ substantially between management, engineering, pharmacy, law and academic disciplines.

Panjab University Placements FAQs

Does Panjab University provide placements?

Yes, placement support exists across various institutes and programmes, but there is no single university-wide placement outcome applicable to every PU student.

What is the highest package at Panjab University?

There is no single university-wide highest package. For UBS, the latest reported 2024-25 highest CTC is ₹25.11 LPA.

What is the average package at Panjab University?

There is no meaningful single average for the entire university. Salary outcomes must be evaluated programme-wise.

Does Panjab University have 100% placement?

Not across the entire university. Recent programme-level data shows different placement coverage and significant higher-study participation in some fields.
