UBS Chandigarh placements 2024-25: latest reported outcome

What students should know

UBS reported 105 students placed in the 2024-25 placement season, representing an 85% placement rate. The highest CTC was ₹25.11 LPA, the average CTC was ₹12.95 LPA, and more than 55 companies participated.

What the trend suggests

  • Average salary remained around ₹12–13 LPA in the recent published years.
  • The exceptional ₹58.48 LPA figure in 2022-23 should not be treated as a normal salary benchmark.
  • The 2024-25 highest offer of ₹25.11 LPA is a better current top-end reference.
  • Placement coverage has been below 100% in the latest reported season, so students should not assume every admitted student gets placed through campus recruitment.

How UBS placements work

UBS maintains a dedicated placement ecosystem and its published material highlights a Placement Cell along with alumni, business, consulting and entrepreneurship activities. Companies participate through campus recruitment, and students go through company-specific screening and selection.

  1. Placement registration and student profile preparation.
  2. Company job descriptions and eligibility criteria shared with students.
  3. Shortlisting based on company requirements.
  4. Tests, GDs, case discussions or interviews depending on recruiter.
  5. Offer communication and placement completion.

Students should also build their profile before the placement season through internships, certifications, live projects, Excel/SQL/analytics skills and role-specific preparation.

UBS MBA placement ROI

The biggest financial advantage of UBS is the combination of low published tuition for the regular MBA and a recent reported average CTC of ₹12.95 LPA. This creates a potentially strong ROI profile compared with high-fee private B-schools.

Why ROI can be strong

Low tuition reduces the amount students need to recover through post-MBA salary. This can materially reduce financial pressure even if the student does not receive the highest package.

What ROI does not include

Living costs, lost income, education loans, taxes, relocation and the difference between CTC and take-home salary. Placement rate also matters.
