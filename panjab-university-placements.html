Panjab University Placements FAQs

What are the latest UIET placement statistics for 2026?

Panjab University's official IQAC update for the UIET batch of 2026 reports a 71.5% placement rate, ₹8.74 LPA average package, ₹39.52 LPA highest off-campus package, ₹21.93 LPA highest on-campus package and 319 offers secured by 283 students across 100+ companies. The update also reports branch placement rates of 84.2% for CSE, 81.6% for IT and 65.7% for ECE.

What is the average package at Panjab University?

There is no single university-wide average package. For example, the latest official UIET 2026 update reports ₹8.74 LPA average, while the older UIET 2023–24 institute data on this page reports ₹9.11 LPA. NIRF reports a ₹12.42 LPA median for the UBS management cohort graduating in 2022–23. These figures belong to different programmes and years and should not be combined.

What is the highest package at Panjab University?

It varies by institute and year. The latest official UIET 2026 update reports ₹39.52 LPA as the highest off-campus package and ₹21.93 LPA as the highest on-campus package. UIET's 2023–24 data reports ₹42 LPA off-campus and ₹25 LPA on-campus. Students should always check the year and whether the figure is on-campus or off-campus.

Does Panjab University have 100% placement?

No university-wide 100% placement claim is supported by the data used here. The latest UIET 2026 update reports a 71.5% placement rate, while the NIRF UBS cohort for 2022–23 reported 113 placed out of 121 graduates. Placement outcomes differ by programme and institute.

Which Panjab University course has the best placement?

There is no fair university-wide ranking because the reporting formats differ. Students should compare the exact course using placement rate, median/average salary, number of offers, recruiter mix and higher-study outcomes. For engineering, branch-wise placement rates are more useful than a single university average.

What is the UBS MBA placement package at Panjab University?

The NIRF management data used on this page reports a ₹12.42 LPA median salary for the UBS cohort graduating in 2022–23, with 113 students placed out of 121 graduates. NIRF median salary should not be described as an average package.

What is the UIET placement average at Panjab University?

The latest official 2026 UIET update reports ₹8.74 LPA average package. It also reports 319 offers secured by 283 students across 100+ companies, with a 71.5% placement rate.

Which companies recruit from UIET Panjab University?

UIET's published recruiter data includes ZS Associates, Sagacious, L&T Technology Services, Amdocs, KPMG, Incedo, GreyB and Policy Bazaar in the 2023–24 dataset. Recruiter participation changes by year, so students should treat the list as a historical indicator rather than a guaranteed 2026 recruiter list.

Does B.Pharm at Panjab University have good placements?

The latest NIRF pharmacy data used here reports 1 placed out of 44 B.Pharm graduates for 2024–25, with 43 students reported in higher studies and a ₹4 LPA median salary for those placed. This means a B.Pharm student should evaluate higher-study opportunities along with placements rather than looking only at a package figure.

How should I compare Panjab University placement data?

First compare the same programme and same graduating year. Then check whether the source reports graduates placed, students opting for placement, or total offers. Also distinguish median salary from average CTC and on-campus from off-campus packages. Mixing these measures can produce a misleading placement percentage.
