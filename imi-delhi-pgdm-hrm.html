IMI Delhi PGDM HRM: What Makes It Different?

IMI Delhi's PGDM (HRM) is a two-year specialised management programme designed around human resource management and people-centric business decisions. The programme combines management foundations with HR-specific learning and practical exposure.

A distinctive feature is the institute's 8-week NGO internship in Delhi-NCR, in addition to the dedicated summer internship between the first and second year. The programme also uses case studies, group assignments, simulations and live projects.

Best suited for

Students who already know that HR, people analytics, talent, organisational development, HR consulting or related people functions are their intended career direction.

IMI Delhi PGDM HRM Admission: Eligibility & Selection

For the recent admission cycle, applicants needed a minimum three-year bachelor's degree with 50% marks or equivalent. Valid CAT, XAT and GMAT scores were considered. IMI Delhi states that there is no fixed cutoff and uses holistic evaluation.

ParameterRecent requirement
GraduationMinimum 50%
Entrance testsCAT / XAT / GMAT
CutoffNo fixed published cutoff
AssessmentVideo assessment + personal interview
Profile factorsAcademics, test score, work experience and overall profile

What is evaluated in the interview?

The video assessment is intended to test thought process and communication. The final PI evaluates general and business awareness, clarity of goals, attitude, people skills, communication and personal, extracurricular or professional initiatives.

HRM-specific decision point

If you are unsure whether you want HR, choose the flagship PGDM instead. HRM is most useful when the people function is already a meaningful part of your career plan.

IMI Delhi PGDM HRM Curriculum, Internships & Skills

The curriculum covers contemporary HR issues while retaining core management knowledge. The programme includes HR analytics, HR systems and processes, compensation, competency management, employee relations, learning and development, recruitment and selection, performance management, organisational planning and design, strategic HRM and related subjects.

Learning componentStudent takeaway
HR coreRecruitment, compensation, performance, employee relations and strategic HR
HR analyticsData-informed people decisions and analytical thinking
Summer internship2-month industry internship between Year 1 and Year 2
NGO internship8-week Delhi-NCR experience focused on empathy and social exposure
PedagogyCases, simulations, group work and live projects
Why the NGO internship matters

It adds an exposure dimension that is different from a conventional corporate internship, helping students understand people, communities and social contexts—useful perspectives for HR professionals.

IMI Delhi HRM Campus & Student Experience

Students study at IMI's Qutab Institutional Area campus in South Delhi. The institute highlights technology-enabled classrooms, library resources, student activities and an industry-linked academic environment.

International exchange and short global immersion opportunities are available through IMI's partner network, subject to selection and programme arrangements.

Who Should Choose IMI Delhi PGDM HRM?

Good fit

  • You are committed to an HR career.
  • You want exposure to HR analytics and modern people practices.
  • You value the NGO + corporate internship combination.
  • You want to study in Delhi-NCR.

Think twice

  • You are undecided between HR and finance/marketing/consulting.
  • You want the broadest possible placement flexibility.
  • You are comparing only on the highest package rather than HR-specific outcomes.

IMI Delhi PGDM HRM FAQs

What is IMI Delhi PGDM HRM fees?

The current published programme fee is ₹23.54 lakh, excluding separately applicable living costs.

What is the PGDM HRM cutoff?

IMI Delhi says there is no fixed cutoff and uses holistic profile evaluation.

What is the PGDM HRM average package?

The 2023-25 average CTC is ₹16.32 LPA and median is ₹14.74 LPA.

Does HRM include internships?
