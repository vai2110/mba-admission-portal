Graphic Era B.Tech CSE: What Students Should Know

Graphic Era's B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering is a four-year undergraduate programme covering computing fundamentals and emerging areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, cybersecurity, cloud computing, IoT, big data and generative AI.

Quick answer

This course is best suited to students who want a broad technology degree and are willing to build coding, problem-solving and project skills alongside the formal curriculum.

B.Tech CSE Eligibility

GEU's course page states that eligibility includes 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics and applicable third-subject requirements. Minimum marks and subject combinations should be checked against the current university admission norms before applying.

B.Tech CSE Admission Process

The official course page states that merit is prepared on the basis of the qualifying examination. GEU also publishes admission/fee slabs referencing JEE Main and CUET for specified routes. Do not assume that a JEE score is compulsory for every GEU admission route.

B.Tech CSE Curriculum & Learning

Core areas include programming, data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, computer networks and software engineering, with advanced domains including AI/ML, data science, cybersecurity, cloud, IoT and big-data analytics.

B.Tech CSE Careers & Placement Context

Potential career paths include software engineering, data science, cybersecurity, cloud, DevOps, database administration, AI/ML and systems roles. GEU's university-wide placement page currently highlights 6,200+ offers, 200+ companies and a ₹61.99 LPA highest package; this is not a typical B.Tech CSE salary.

B.Tech CSE FAQs

Is JEE Main compulsory?

GEU's course page states that admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination. JEE Main is referenced in specific published admission/fee routes, so students should verify the route applicable to their profile.

Is B.Tech CSE suitable for PCB students?

Mathematics is an important eligibility subject for CSE. Students without the required mathematics background should not assume eligibility.

What is the duration?

The regular B.Tech CSE programme is four years.
