Why does Chitkara show more than one fee number?

Some official structures separate programme fee from Educational Technology Services (ETS) and Career Advancement Services (CAS). Always read the semester/year total for the exact programme.

Chitkara University Courses & Fees FAQs

What is the fee of BCA at Chitkara University?

The current BCA page lists ₹45,000 programme fee plus ₹25,000 ETS/CAS in Sem I and II, making ₹70,000 each for the first two semesters; Sem III-VI are ₹65,000 each.

What is the fee of BCA AI & ML?

The current page lists ₹80,000 programme fee plus ₹25,000 ETS/CAS in the first two semesters, followed by ₹1,00,000 per semester from Sem III to VI.

What is the Chitkara MBA fee?

It depends on the specialisation. The 2026 fee document shows ₹2.25 lakh per semester for Marketing, Finance, HR and Data Science & AI tracks, with lower structures for several other MBA groups.

Are Chitkara fees refundable?

Refunds are governed by the university's current admission/refund rules. Students should read the applicable 2026 admission procedure before paying the programme fee.
