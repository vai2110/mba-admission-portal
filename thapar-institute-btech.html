Student Discussion: Choosing a Thapar Branch

The biggest decision is often branch rather than the university name. Students should compare the curriculum, coding ecosystem, internships, placement pool and their own interest before choosing between CSE, electronics and core branches.

  • Do not select a branch only because of a headline package.
  • Compare the four-year total cost.
  • Check your realistic admission route and score.
  • Consider whether you prefer software, core engineering or interdisciplinary careers.
