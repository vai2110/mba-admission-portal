Jain University BTech CSE: Course Overview

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology offers B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering at the JAIN Global Campus. The official programme description focuses on computational theory, algorithms, data structures and programming languages, while the wider engineering school offers technology-oriented specialisations.

Who should choose BTech CSE?

Choose it if you want a four-year engineering degree with a deeper engineering foundation and are targeting software development, technology, AI/data or related technical roles.

Eligibility for BTech CSE

The current official CSE page states a minimum of 45% in Class 12, with 40% for SC/ST, with Physics and Mathematics and Chemistry/Electronics/Computer Science/Biology as the optional subject. IB and Cambridge pathways are also specified.

Jain University BTech CSE Admission 2026

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology states that B.Tech admissions can consider UNIGAUGE, JEE and COMEDK scores. Students should check the current admission notice for the applicable JET/institutional route and seat availability.

BTech CSE Fees

JAIN publishes B.Tech fees by engineering programme rather than one universal B.Tech fee. The current fee page shows annual fees ranging by branch; CSE should therefore be checked on the live fee table before payment.

Important fee check

Do not copy the fee of Aerospace, ECE or another engineering branch into a CSE article. Confirm the exact CSE fee, one-time charges and hostel cost from the current admission office.

BTech CSE Curriculum & Learning

The programme covers core computing concepts such as algorithms, data structures and programming languages. The engineering school also highlights practical learning, laboratories, internships, projects and industry exposure.

Technical foundation

Programming, computational thinking, algorithms and data structures build the base for software and technology roles.

Career exposure

Projects, internships, laboratories and industry-linked learning help students translate classroom concepts into applications.

BTech CSE FAQs

Does Jain University accept JEE Main for BTech?

Yes. The current Faculty of Engineering and Technology BTech CSE page lists JEE along with UNIGAUGE and COMEDK as accepted scores.

What is the BTech CSE eligibility?

The current CSE page states 45% in Class 12, or 40% for SC/ST, with Physics and Mathematics plus an eligible optional subject.

What is Jain University BTech CSE placement package?

For B.Tech overall, the official 2024-25 page reports ₹81.25 LPA highest and ₹8.24 LPA average. This is B.Tech-wide data, not a CSE-only guarantee.

Is BTech CSE better than BCA at Jain University?

They serve different goals. B.Tech CSE is an engineering degree with a stronger engineering foundation, while BCA is a computer applications route. Compare curriculum, eligibility, cost and target role before choosing.

Where is the BTech campus?

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology lists its study campus at the JAIN Global Campus on Bengaluru–Kanakapura Main Road.
